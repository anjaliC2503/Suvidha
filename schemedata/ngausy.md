## Brief Description

Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme "Nai Hetu Avashyak Upkaran Sahayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 27 January 2018.

## Detailed Description

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &quot;Nai Hetu Avashyak Upkaran Sahayata Yojana&quot; for unorganized worker on  27 January 2018.. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing required equipment for their work.

## Benefits

> Registered barbers will be provided barber kit including scissors, straight razor, comb, hair cutting cape, water spray bottle and shaving brush.

## Eligibility

1. Applicant must be registered Unorganized worker of State Social Security Board. 
1. Applicant age must be between 18 to 60 years. 
1. Applicant must not receiving any other parallel scheme of the state government

## Application Process

### Online

**Unregistered Applicant :**

1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on "[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)" under "Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board".1. Enter the following details

   1. Select Group Name : "Asangathit Marmakaar Mandal"
   1. Select Service : "Asangathit Shramik Panjikaran"
   1. What do you want to do :  "Aavedan"
   1. Click next.

1. Check the eligibility criteria for registration in Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board and click next. 
1. Fill out the online "Registration form".
1. Submit.
