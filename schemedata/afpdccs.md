## Brief Description

The "Assistance for Power Driven Chaff Cutter Scheme" introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, is designed to help farmers reduce fodder wastage and improve their dairy farming efficiency.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Assistance for Power Driven Chaff Cutter Scheme&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to reduce fodder wastage (25-30%) in dairy farming. By using a power-driven chaff cutter, farmers can efficiently chop green and dry fodder, ensuring better utilization and increased profitability.
<br>

<br>

Assistance up to 75% of the purchase cost or ₹18,000/- whichever is less is given to beneficiary.5 Year	<br>

## Eligibility

- The scheme is applicable to General, Scheduled Caste (SC), and Scheduled Tribe (ST) individuals.


- The applicant must be engaged in dairy farming.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Animal Husbandry Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on &quot;Apply New&quot; button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the &quot;Update Application&quot; button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application and submit Related Documents through to concern Taluka office.
**Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Assistance for Power Driven Chaff Cutter Scheme"?

The Assistance for Power Driven Chaff Cutter Scheme aims to reduce fodder wastage (25-30%) in dairy farming. By using a power-driven chaff cutter, farmers can efficiently chop green and dry fodder, ensuring better utilization and increased profitability.

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Assistance for Power Driven Chaff Cutter Scheme"?

The scheme is available to dairy farmers belonging to the General, Scheduled Caste (SC), and Scheduled Tribe (ST) categories who are residents of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### What type of financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Beneficiaries can receive up to 75% of the purchase cost or ₹18,000, whichever is lower, as financial assistance for purchasing a power-driven chaff cutter.
<br>

<br>

### Can an applicant reapply if they have already availed of this scheme in the past?

No, an applicant who has already availed of this scheme within the last 5 years is not eligible to reapply.
<br>

<br>

### Does the beneficiary need to repay the subsidy amount?

No, the financial assistance provided under this scheme is a subsidy, not a loan, and does not need to be repaid.
<br>

<br>

### Is a caste certificate mandatory for all applicants?

No, a caste certificate is required only for applicants from the Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) categories. General category applicants do not need to submit a caste certificate.
<br>

<br>

### Where can an applicant apply for this scheme?

Applications must be submitted online through the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in)portal.

### Where can an applicant find more details or assistance regarding the scheme?

Additional details can be found on the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in)portal or by contacting the local agriculture department.
