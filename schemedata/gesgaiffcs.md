## Brief Description

The scheme aims to generate a greater understanding of India's culture and foster closer friendship with people of other countries. Through this component, financial grants are provided to Indo-Foreign Friendship/Cultural Societies actively functioning in foreign countries.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Global Engagement Scheme: Grant-In-Aid To Indo-Foreign Friendship Cultural Societies" component of the Global Engagement Scheme by the ICR Division, Ministry of Culture, Government of India aims to generate greater understanding of India's culture and foster closer friendship with people of other countries. Through this component, financial grants are provided to Indo-Foreign Friendship/Cultural Societies actively functioning in foreign countries. The component is implemented by the Ministry of Culture through Indian Missions abroad, with grants authorised via the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), and proposals examined by an Expert Committee.
> **Objectives:**


- Generate greater understanding of India's culture.
- Foster closer friendship with people of other countries.
- Promote interaction between eminent Indian scholars and their foreign counterparts through holding of seminars on related topics.

<br>

## Benefits

> **Activities for which the Grant is given to the Cultural Societies recommended by the Indian Mission:** 

Grants under the scheme will be given primarily to cover expenses of the grantees on activities which help projection of India's cultural image and generation of greater understanding of its cultural heritage, and for promoting bilateral cultural contacts by organising the following:

- Dance (classical, folk and workshops).
- Music (vocal, instrumental, Sufi, classical, semi-classical, Carnatic, contemporary, workshops, etc.).
- Photo Exhibitions on culture-related topics.
- Exhibitions on Books and other culture-related topics/material.
- Publication of Books/journals on culture.
- Promotion of Hindi and other Indian languages by organising activities like Workshops, Essay Competition, Dictation and Valedictory Session, Kavi Sammelan and publication of a regional language magazine, etc.
- Furnishing the premises of societies having adequate space with replicas of exhibits sourced from the Indian Mission in lieu of cash grant.
- Visual Arts and Crafts such as such ceramics, drawing, painting, sculpture, etc.
- Contemporary and Modern/Fine Art exhibitions.
- Theatre: Drama, Skit, Mime, Mimicry and workshops.
- Activities to promote Folk Arts viz Mehendi, Puppetry and Rangoli.
- Classes/Courses in Yoga art.
- Organisation of Literary events like Debate, Elocution, Creative Writing workshops, Book reading sessions, etc.
- Organisation of lecture-demonstrations, seminars, symposiums and conferences on culture-related topics.
- Discussions on Indian culture, history, civilisation by inviting eminent scholars, artists, etc. who are interested in Indian affairs can be sponsored.
- Promoting interaction between distinguished visiting Indian academics, artists etc with their local counterparts, etc.
- Celebrations of Indian festivals
- Celebration of National days like India's Republic Day, International Day of Yoga, Indian Independence Day, Hindi Diwas, Gandhi Jayanti, etc. 
- Celebrations of Anniversaries and centenaries of great Indian personalities and other commemorations of National importance. In this regard, the following activities may be organised.



  - Organise plays on the theme
  - Use the theme for activities planned for National Days.
  - The Works of the personality be translated into major world languages and
  - Digital Library promotion through Indian Embassies.
  - Kavi Sammelans in regional languages.
  - Indian Vegetarian Food Festival to be organised by Indian Embassies abroad.
  - Commemorative postage stamp on Gandhi to be released. VII. Release of Commemorative coin.
  - Exhibitions of paintings, rare photographs, books, sculptures, artefacts.
  - National/International seminars, workshops, competitions, lectures.
  - Illumination of iconic buildings.


> **Please Note:** This Scheme does not grant any funds for operational/ maintenance/capital creation cost of the societies.

<br>

> **Amount of Grant:**


- The grant shall not ordinarily exceed ₹10,00,000/- (ten lakh) per annum per society.
- There is no limit specified on funds authorised to the Mission, as this depends on the funds the Mission can utilise for cultural promotion as per the available number of societies.
- An additional amount over and above the maximum limit, up to ₹25,00,000/- (twenty-five lakh), may be granted at the discretion of the Secretary (Culture) in consultation with the Financial Adviser (Culture), where the Ministry proposes to organise the Festival of India/culture weeks to enable expenditure towards hosting cultural troupes, etc.

<br>

## Exclusions

1. The scheme does not grant funds for operational, maintenance, or capital creation costs of the societies.
1. The applicant should not have been sanctioned grant-in-aid for the same activity/purpose from any other source.
1. No other bill for the same purpose and instalment should have already been paid earlier to the grantee.
1. The applicant (society) should not indulge in activities which are sectarian in nature.
1. Grants should normally be utilised for the activities of the society, and not for the direct activities of the Missions.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant (society) should be an Indo-Foreign Friendship/Cultural Society actively functioning in a foreign country, with the object of fostering closer friendship and cultural contacts between India and the foreign country concerned.
1. The applicant should be actively engaged in the task of fostering friendly relations and projecting India's culture in the country concerned.
1. The applicant should have a definite and well-planned programme of activities, for which financial assistance by the Government of India is considered necessary.
1. The applicant should have gainfully utilised the earlier grant, if any, sanctioned by the Government of India.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

1. The Indian Mission ensures the society meets eligibility criteria that it is actively engaged in fostering friendly relations and projecting India's culture, has a well-planned programme of activities, and has gainfully utilised any earlier grant.
1. The Mission compiles and submits the proposal, comprising Details of the Society (Annexure A), Plan of Activities for the Current Financial Year (Annexure B), Certificate of No Corrupt Practices (Annexure C), Certificate of No Sectarian Activities (Annexure D), and the Utilisation Certificate (GFR 12-A) for any grant last sanctioned.
1. The Expert Committee, chaired by the Bureau Head In-charge of the ICR Division, with members from IFD, ICCR, and Sangeet Natak Akademi, and convened by the Director/DS (ICR), examines the proposal and recommends the quantum of grant for each society.
1. The competent authority sanctions the grant to the Bureau Head In-charge of the concerned Division for amounts up to ₹10,00,000/-, or, at the discretion of the Secretary (Culture) in consultation with the Financial Adviser (Culture), up to ₹25,00,000/- in special cases relating to Festival of India/culture weeks.
1. The grant is issued as an authorisation to the Indian Mission through the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), and the amount is placed with the Mission in their accounts.
1. The Mission informally checks the accounts of the grantee to satisfy itself that the grant has been properly utilised.
1. After utilising the grant, the society/Mission furnishes the Event Review Report (Annexure E) and a performance-cum-achievement report (2 copies) to the Ministry; video/photo/newspaper clips/audio material may be sent to icr-culture@nic.in and icrculture@gmail.com.
1. Any unspent balance with the grantee is surrendered to the Government without delay.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Grant-in-aid to Indo-Foreign Friendship Cultural Societies" component?

It is one of the three components of the Global Engagement Scheme, under which grants are sanctioned to Indo-Foreign Friendship/Cultural Societies actively functioning in foreign countries with the object of fostering closer friendship and cultural contacts between India and the foreign country concerned.
<br>

### What are the objectives of this component?

- Generate a greater understanding of India's culture.
- Foster closer friendship with people of other countries.
- Promote interaction between eminent Indian scholars with their foreign counterparts, holding of seminars on related topics, etc.

<br>

### Who is eligible to receive a grant under this component?

Before the allocation of a grant, the Indian Mission ensures that:

- The society is actively engaged in the task of fostering friendly relations and projecting India's culture in the country concerned.
- The society has a definite and well-planned programme of activities for which financial assistance by the Government of India is considered necessary.
- The society has gainfully utilised the earlier grant, if any, sanctioned by the Government of India.

<br>

### What activities can the grant be used for?

Dance (classical, folk, workshops); Music (vocal, instrumental, Sufi, classical, semi-classical, carnatic, contemporary, workshops); Photo Exhibitions on culture-related topics; Exhibitions on Books and other culture-related topics/material; Publication of Books/journals on culture; Promotion of Hindi and other Indian languages (Workshops, Essay Competition, Dictation, Valedictory Session, Kavi Sammelan, regional language magazine); furnishing society premises with replicas of art exhibits (in lieu of cash grant); Visual Arts and Craft (ceramics, drawing, painting, sculpture); Contemporary and Modern/Fine Art exhibitions; Theatre (Drama, Skit, Mime, Mimicry, workshops); Folk Arts (Mehendi, Puppetry, Rangoli); Classes/Courses in Yoga; Literary events (Debate, Elocution, Creative Writing workshops, Book reading sessions); lecture-demonstrations, seminars, symposiums, and conferences; discussions on Indian culture/history/civilization; interaction between visiting Indian academics/artists and local counterparts; celebration of Indian festivals; celebration of National Days; and celebrations of anniversaries/centenaries of great Indian personalities.
<br>

### What special activities can be organized for anniversaries/centenaries of national importance?

- Organise plays on the theme.
- Use the theme for activities planned for National Days.
- Translate the works of the personality into major world languages, with Digital Library promotion through Indian Embassies.
- Kavi Sammelans in regional languages.
- Indian Vegetarian Food Festival organised by Indian Embassies abroad.
- Release of Commemorative Postage Stamp on Gandhi.
- Release of Commemorative Coin.
- Exhibitions of paintings, rare photographs, books, sculptures, artefacts.
- National/International seminars, workshops, competitions, lectures.
- Illumination of iconic buildings.

<br>

### Does the scheme fund the operational or capital costs of the society?

No. This Scheme does not grant any funds for operational/maintenance/capital creation costs of the societies.
<br>

### What is the maximum amount of grant a society can receive?

The grant shall not ordinarily exceed ₹10,00,000/- (ten lakh) per annum per society.
<br>

### Can the grant exceed ₹10,00,000/- in any case?

Yes. An additional amount over and above the maximum limit, up to ₹25,00,000/- (twenty-five lakh), may be granted at the discretion of Secretary (Culture), in consultation with the Financial Adviser (Culture), where the Ministry of Culture proposes to organize a Festival of India/culture weeks, to enable expenditure towards hosting cultural troupes, etc.
<br>

### Is there a limit on the total funds that can be authorized to a Mission?

No. There is no limit specified for funds to be authorized to the Mission, since this would depend on the funds the Mission can utilize for cultural promotion, as per the available number of societies.
<br>

### How is the grant disbursed?

The Grants under this scheme are issued as authorizations to the Indian Missions through the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), and the amount authorized is placed with the Mission in their accounts.
<br>

### Who examines the proposals and recommends the grant amount?

An Expert Committee examines the proposals for Grant-in-aid received from the Indian Missions and recommends the quantum of grant to be sanctioned to each society. It comprises:

- Bureau Head In-charge of ICR Division, Ministry of Culture — Chairman
- Representative from IFD, Ministry of Culture — Member
- Representative from Indian Council for Cultural Relations — Member
- Representative from Sangeet Natak Akademi — Member
- Director (ICR)/DS (ICR), Ministry of Culture — Convenor

<br>

### Who is the competent authority to sanction the grant?

The Bureau Head Incharge of the concerned Division is the competent authority to sanction grant up to ₹10,00,000/- in normal circumstances; sanctioning of the additional amount (up to ₹25,00,000/-) is at the discretion of the Secretary (Culture), in consultation with the Financial Adviser (Culture).
<br>

### Is a submission of audited accounts mandatory for these grants?

The grants sanctioned under this scheme are subject to usual conditions applicable for non-recurring grants to voluntary organizations working in India, but the condition of submission of audited statements of accounts is relaxed. The certificate of utilization (as per GFR 12-A) signed by the authorized officer of the Mission is acceptable instead.
<br>

### What happens if the society does not utilize the grant fully or properly?

- Unspent balance, if any, with the grantee shall be surrendered to the Government without any delay.
- If the grantee fails to utilise the grant for the purpose for which it was sanctioned, the grantee is required to refund the entire amount with interest thereon at 10 per cent per annum.

<br>

### Are the accounts of grantee societies subject to audit?

Yes. The accounts of all grantee institutions or organizations shall be open to inspection by the Ministry and audit — both by the Comptroller and Auditor General of India under the CAG (Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service) Act, 1971, and by Internal Audit by the Principal Accounts Officer of the Ministry, whenever called upon to do so.
<br>

### Can societies indulging in sectarian activities receive this grant?

No. The society receiving the grant should not indulge in activities which can be termed as sectarian.
<br>

### What must be reported after the grant is utilized?

A performance-cum-achievement report (2 copies), for which the grant has been sanctioned, should be forwarded to the Ministry by the Embassies of India, along with the Event Review Report (Annexure E).
<br>

### Where should photographs/videos/news clippings of the funded event be sent?

These may be sent to the ICR Division's email IDs: icr-culture@nic.in and icrculture@gmail.com.
<br>
