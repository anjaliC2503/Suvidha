## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide crop insurance protection to farmers against weather-related risks under the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana. It ensures financial support to farmers affected by adverse weather conditions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme&quot; launched by the Horticulture and Food Processing Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to protect farmers from weather-related losses through insurance coverage for selected crops cultivated during Kharif and Rabi seasons.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Insurance Coverage for Crops:** Provides protection to crops against losses caused by adverse weather conditions such as high or low temperatures, excess or deficient rainfall, and wind speed.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a farmer.
  - The applicant must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The applicant should contact the District Office of the Horticulture Department.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme aims to protect farmers from losses caused by adverse weather conditions.
<br>

### Who implements the scheme in Madhya Pradesh?

The scheme is implemented by the Horticulture and Food Processing Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Which crops are covered under this scheme?

The scheme covers crops such as orange, banana, papaya, onion, chili, brinjal, tomato, potato, cauliflower, cabbage, peas, coriander, garlic, mango, pomegranate, and grapes.
<br>

### What type of risks are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers risks due to high or low temperatures, excess or deficient rainfall, strong wind, and hailstorm.
<br>

### What is the premium amount to be paid by the farmer?

The farmer has to pay 5% of the insured amount as the premium.
<br>

### Who pays the remaining premium?

The remaining premium is shared equally by the central and state governments.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Farmers of Madhya Pradesh are eligible to apply under this scheme.
<br>

### How is the compensation amount calculated?

The compensation is calculated based on weather deviations recorded at established weather stations.
<br>
