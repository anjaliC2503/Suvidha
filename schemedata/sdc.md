## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote solo theatrical performances and encourage talent in the field of drama across Gujarat. A state-level solo drama competition is organized with cash prizes awarded to winners and special category performers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Solo Drama Competition" was launched by the Department of Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote solo theatrical performances and encourage talent in the field of drama across Gujarat by organizing a state-level competition that recognizes excellence in acting, direction, writing, and scene composition. The scheme provides cash prizes to winning participants, with the first prize winner receiving ₹2,000/- and the second prize winner receiving ₹1,500/-, along with special category awards of ₹500/- each for Original Drama, Best Director, Best Writer, and Best Scene Composition. The applicant must be willing to participate in the state-level solo drama competition and perform a solo theatrical act.
<br>

## Benefits

- First prize winner receives ₹2,000/-.
- Second prize winner receives ₹1,500/-.
- Original Drama category winner receives ₹500/-.
- Best Director receives ₹500/-.
- Best Writer receives ₹500/-.
- Best Scene Composition receives ₹500/-.

_*The benefits are disbursed as one-time cash prizes awarded after the competition results are declared._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
- The applicant must be willing to participate in a solo drama competition.
- The applicant must perform a solo theatrical act as per the competition requirements.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Visit the official website of 'Mari Yojana' at [https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in](https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in).
In the search bar provided at the top of the page, type 'Competition'. All the schemes of the Government of Gujarat having the word 'Competition' will be listed.
**Step 2:** From the list, click on the scheme 'Solo Drama Competition'. You will be taken to the schemes page. Navigate and download the application form of the scheme. The application form is available in both Gujarati and English languages, and the applicant should take print of the form in their preferred language.
**Step 3:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 4:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the designated authority at the Gujarat State Sangeet Natak Academy.
**Step 5:** After successful submission, the applicant will receive an acknowledgement or reference number. The applicant should save this reference number for future correspondence and tracking of the application status.
<br>

> ***Post-Application Processes***

**Step 1:** Once the application is approved, the applicant will be notified about the date, time, and venue of the state-level solo drama competition. The applicant must attend the competition at the scheduled time and perform their solo drama act before the judges.
**Step 2:** After the competition concludes, the results will be evaluated and declared by the organizing committee. Winners will be notified through official channels, and prizes will be distributed to the first and second prize winners, as well as special category award recipients as per the scheme guidelines.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can participants from any educational background apply for the state-level competition, or are there specific qualifications that need to be met before submission?

No specific educational qualifications are required to participate in the competition. Applicants from any educational background are eligible to apply and participate in the state-level solo drama competition.
<br>

### What is the total cash reward provided to the participant who secures the top position in the performance category of the competition?

The participant who secures the first position in the competition receives a cash prize of ₹2,000/-. Additionally, special category awards of ₹500/- each are available for Original Drama, Best Director, Best Writer, and Best Scene Composition.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions based on gender for individuals who wish to participate and compete in this state-level theatrical performance event?

No, there are no gender-based restrictions for participation. Both male and female individuals are eligible to apply and compete in the state-level solo drama competition.
<br>

### Is there a minimum or maximum annual income requirement that applicants or their families must satisfy to be considered eligible for participation?

No, there is no minimum or maximum income requirement for applicants or their families. Individuals from any economic background are eligible to participate in the competition.
<br>

### What monetary recognition is given to the participant who performs exceptionally well and achieves the runner-up position in the event?

The participant who achieves the runner-up or second position in the competition receives a cash prize of ₹1,500/-.
<br>

### If someone creates and presents an entirely new theatrical work, what special recognition or prize will they receive for their original creation?

If a participant creates and presents an entirely original drama, they are eligible to receive a special category award of ₹500/- for Original Drama, in addition to any performance-based prizes they may win.
<br>

### Are residents from all districts across the state permitted to submit their applications, or is participation limited to specific geographical areas only?

Residents from all districts across Gujarat are permitted to submit their applications. The competition is open to participants from across the entire state.
<br>

### What amount is awarded as a special prize to the individual who demonstrates exceptional skills in directing the solo theatrical performance?

The individual who demonstrates exceptional skills in directing and receives the Best Director award is given a special prize of ₹500/-.
<br>

### Is any fee required to be paid by applicants when they download or submit the application form for the competition through the portal?

No, the application form is available free of cost. Applicants can download and submit the application form without paying any fee.
<br>

### What financial reward is given to recognize the participant who excels in writing the script or content for their dramatic presentation?

The participant who excels in writing the script and receives the Best Writer award is given a financial reward of ₹500/-.
<br>

### Besides performance prizes, is there any separate award given for excellence in the visual arrangement and staging of scenes during the drama?

Yes, there is a separate award for excellence in scene composition. The participant who receives the Best Scene Composition award is given a special prize of ₹500/-.
<br>

### Do applicants need to belong to any particular profession or occupational category to be eligible for submitting their application for this event?

No, applicants do not need to belong to any particular profession or occupational category. Individuals from all professional backgrounds are eligible to apply and participate.
<br>

### What type of identity and residence verification documents must be submitted along with the application to prove eligibility for the competition?

Applicants must submit proof of residence such as Ration Card, Aadhar Card, Voter Identity Card, or any other government-issued identity document, along with identity proof such as Aadhar Card, Passport, Driving License, or Voter Identity Card.
<br>

### Once the competition is concluded and winners are selected, through what process will the monetary prizes be distributed to the recipients?

After the competition concludes and results are evaluated by the organizing committee, winners will be notified through official channels. The monetary prizes will be distributed to the first and second prize winners, as well as special category award recipients, as per the scheme guidelines.
<br>
