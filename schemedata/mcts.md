## Brief Description

The scheme's objective is to promote youth entrepreneurship and employment in Meghalaya by supporting cinema theatres while preserving the state’s cultural heritage.

## Detailed Description

The **Meghalaya Cinema Theatre Scheme** is a component of the CM-ELEVATE Program launched by the Planning Department of the Government of Meghalaya. It aims to employ, engage, and empower the youth of Meghalaya by promoting entrepreneurship and providing easy access to credit through partnerships with various banks. The scheme offers financial support for establishing new cinema theatres or upgrading existing ones across the state. This initiative is designed to preserve and promote the cultural heritage of Meghalaya through film and cinema, while also serving as community hubs for artistic expression and entertainment. Additionally, it supports value-added facilities such as food courts, gaming zones, childcare services, and retail spaces to ensure the long-term viability and sustainability of theatre establishments.
<br>

## Benefits

> A maximum project cost of ₹1.5 Crores for a 100-seater cinema theatre and INR 3 Crores for a 200-seater cinema theatre will be accepted under this scheme.

<br>

## Exclusions

**Individuals**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for the following:**
A. Pressure groups and organizations that are primarily political in nature shall not be ordinarily considered
B. Only one member from a family is eligible for the scheme
C. Following individuals (currently employed as full-fledged or contractual, or retired and drawing pension) and their immediate family members which includes spouse, parents, and children up to the age of 30:
i. State and Central Government employees
ii. Employees of public sector undertakings, subsidiaries of public sector undertakings, and divisions of public sector undertakings etc.
iii. Any individual whose job is concerned with politics such as MLAs, MLCs, MPs etc. or seeking to hold a similar position in government
iv. Registered class-1 contractors
v. Consultants who are currently engaged with state or central government
**Registered Entities**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for the following**
A. State and Central Government employees
B. Employees of public sector undertakings, subsidiaries of public sector undertakings and divisions of public sector undertakings etc.
C. Any individual whose job is concerned with politics such MLAs, MLCs, MPs etc. or seeking to hold a similar position in government
D. Registered class-1 contractors
E. Pressure groups and organizations that are primarily political in nature shall not be ordinarily considered
F. Consultants who are currently engaged with state or central government
<br>

## Eligibility

**Individual**

1. The applicants must be between 18 and 58 years of age.
1. The applicants must be permanent residents of Meghalaya.
1. The applicants must have ownership of the land on which the theatre is proposed or have leased land.
1. The applicants shall not be credit defaulters of any bank.

**Registered Entity**

1. The applicant must possess land, either self-owned or on lease, duly verified by the competent authority.
1. The applicant shall not be a credit defaulter of any bank.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to promote entrepreneurship, generate employment for the youth of Meghalaya, and preserve the cultural heritage of the state through film and cinema.
<br>

### What is the age requirement for individual applicants?

Individual applicants must be between 18 and 58 years of age to be eligible for the scheme.
<br>

### Do applicants need to be residents of Meghalaya?

Yes, individual applicants must be permanent residents of Meghalaya to qualify for the scheme.
<br>

### Is land ownership required to apply for the scheme?

Yes, applicants must either own the land on which the cinema theatre is proposed or have valid leased land verified by the competent authority.
<br>

### Can registered entities apply for the scheme?

Yes, registered entities can apply if they possess land (owned or leased) and meet the other eligibility conditions set under the scheme.
<br>

### Are credit defaulters eligible to apply?

No, applicants must not be credit defaulters of any bank in order to be eligible for the scheme.
<br>

### What is the maximum project cost supported under the scheme?

The scheme supports a maximum project cost of ₹1.5 Crores for a 100-seater cinema theatre and ₹3 Crores for a 200-seater cinema theatre.
<br>

### Does the scheme support the upgradation of existing cinema theatres?

Yes, the scheme provides financial assistance for both establishing new cinema theatres and upgrading existing ones.
<br>

### How will the scheme benefit the youth of Meghalaya?

The scheme encourages entrepreneurship among youth and creates employment opportunities through the development and operation of cinema theatres and related services.
