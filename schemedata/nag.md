## Brief Description

The "Nalanda Award" implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, recognizes organizations for exceptional work in the field of social welfare, particularly for the socially and educationally disadvantaged sections.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Nalanda Award&quot; implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to honor organizations that have significantly contributed to the upliftment of socially and educationally backward communities. This state-level recognition encourages continued efforts in the field of social welfare and highlights best practices that can be replicated across the state.

## Benefits

- Financial assistance to the selected organization.

## Eligibility

- The organization must have made outstanding contributions in the field of social welfare.
  - The welfare work should benefit socially and educationally backward classes (SEBC).

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Go to the  [eSamajKalyan portal](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/index.aspx?HodID=Mgt3fh4h1dSAcTIdCEHhzQ==) and navigate to the "Nalanda Award" section under the menu option.
**Step 2:** Click on Apply Now.
**Step 3:** Click on "New User? Please Register Here".
**Step 4:** Enter the required details and click Register.
<br>

> **Application Process:**

**Step 1:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, then click "Login".
**Step 2:** Read the scheme details carefully and click OK.
**Step 3:** If your permanent address is correct, confirm it. Otherwise, enter your correct address.
**Step 4:** Click Save & Next to proceed. Upload the relevant documents.
**Step 5:** If you need to make changes, click "No, Cancel". If all details are correct, click "Yes, Submit".
**Step 6:** Click Save Application. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout by clicking Print Application.
<br>

> **Check Application Status:** [Click here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Nalanda Award" and why is it given?

The Nalanda Award is a state-level recognition conferred by the Government of Gujarat to honor organizations that have made outstanding and impactful contributions in the field of social welfare, specifically for the benefit of socially and educationally backward communities.
<br>

<br>

### Who can apply for the Nalanda Award under this scheme?

Only organizations, not individuals, that are actively involved in social welfare activities targeting the upliftment of socially and educationally disadvantaged groups in Gujarat are eligible to apply for the Nalanda Award.
<br>

<br>

### Is an individual or private citizen eligible to receive the Nalanda Award?

No, the Nalanda Award is not intended for individuals; it is exclusively designed to recognize organizations that have collectively contributed to the welfare of SEBC communities through structured initiatives.
<br>

<br>

### Is the Nalanda Award scheme available to organizations across all regions of Gujarat?

Yes, the Nalanda Award is a statewide initiative, and organizations operating in any part of Gujarat are eligible to apply as long as they meet the necessary criteria.
<br>

<br>

### What kind of support does an organization receive upon being selected for the Nalanda Award?

An organization selected for the Nalanda Award receives financial assistance from the Government of Gujarat along with formal recognition for its outstanding work in social welfare.
<br>

<br>

### On what basis is the final selection of an organization made for the Nalanda Award?

The selection of an organization for the Nalanda Award is based on several criteria, including the documented impact of its work, the authenticity and sustainability of its initiatives, and its overall contribution to the welfare of SEBC communities.
<br>

<br>

### Can a newly established organization apply for the "Nalanda Award"?

A newly formed organization can apply for the Nalanda Award provided it has verifiable proof of impactful work carried out in the field of social welfare, even within a short span of time.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the mode of application for the "Nalanda Award"?

The application process for the Nalanda Award is conducted entirely online through the official portal [https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/), where organizations can register, fill out the form, and upload necessary documents.
<br>

<br>

### Which documents must be submitted by an organization to apply for the Nalanda Award?

To apply for the Nalanda Award, an organization must submit essential documents such as a valid Aadhar Card, a bank passbook or a cancelled cheque, recent passport-sized photographs, and the completed application form.
<br>

<br>

### Whom should an organization contact in case they need help or clarification regarding the scheme?

Organizations requiring assistance or further information about the Nalanda Award scheme can reach out to the Social Justice and Empowerment Department through their official website at [https://sje.gujarat.gov.in](https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/).
