## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial security to farmers through direct income support and death/disability benefits. It benefits small and marginal farmers with annual assistance of ₹5,000/- per acre and a one-time payout of ₹2,00,000/- in case of death.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 2019, the scheme &quot;Krishak Bandhu Scheme&quot; by the Department of Agriculture, Government of West Bengal, aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for agricultural sustainability and risk mitigation. The scheme offers ₹5,000/- per acre per year as direct income support and ₹2,00,000/- as death benefit to the family of deceased farmers. Eligible beneficiaries include small and marginal farmers owning up to 2 acres of land and all farmer families in case of death or disability. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Agriculture, Government of West Bengal, and applications are accepted both online and offline.

## Benefits

**Direct Income Support**

  - Farmers with one acre or more of cultivable land receive ₹10,000/- per annum, payable in two equal installments of ₹5,000/- each (one for Kharif and one for Rabi season).
  - Farmers with less than one acre of cultivable land receive assistance on a pro-rata basis, subject to a minimum of ₹4,000/- per annum, paid in two equal installments.

**Death/Disability Benefit**

  - In the event of the death of a farmer between the ages of 18 and 60 years, a one-time lump sum grant of ₹2,00,000/- is provided to the legal heirs of the deceased. The farmer must have been enrolled under the Krishak Bandhu scheme at the time of death.


  - A one-time lump sum grant of ₹2,00,000/- for permanent disability of the farmer due to accident.

**Disbursement Conditions**

  - Income support is linked to land ownership records.
  - Death benefit requires submission of legal heir certificates.
  - Disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the beneficiary&amp;#39;s bank account.

## Eligibility

**For Income Support**

  - The applicant must be a resident of West Bengal.
  - The applicant must own cultivable land (up to 2 acres).
  - The applicant must be actively engaged in farming.

**For Death/Disability Benefit**

  - The deceased/disabled person must have been a farmer aged 18–60 years.
  - The applicant must be the legal heir (for death benefit).

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the nearest Gram Panchayat or the Office of the Assistant Director of Agriculture of the respective block, and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the **Gram Panchayat or the Office of the Assistant Director of Agriculture.**
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Documents Required

- Record of Rights (RoR) of Cultivable Land / RoR recorded with Barga / Patta Record / Forest Patta (Latest).


- Voter Identity Card (Mandatory).
- Aadhaar Card (Optional).
- Bank Passbook (First page and last page) / Cancelled Cheque.
- Passport Size Recent Photograph (Original).
- Self Declaration with Land Documents & Warison Certificate (if needed).
- Bank account and Aadhaar linked mobile number.
- Death Certificate (For Death Benefit).
- Legal Heir Certificate (For Death Benefit).
- Disability Certificate (For Disability Benefit).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the eligibility criteria for receiving direct income support under this agricultural scheme?

To qualify, you must be a resident of West Bengal, own cultivable land (up to 2 acres), and be actively engaged in farming.

### How much financial assistance is provided per acre under the income support component?

Eligible farmers receive ₹5,000/- per acre per year, with a maximum limit of 2 acres.

### Is there a maximum landholding limit to be eligible for the annual income support?

Yes, only farmers owning up to 2 acres of cultivable land can avail of the income support.

### How is the annual income support amount disbursed to eligible farmers?

The amount is paid in two equal installments, corresponding to the Kharif and Rabi seasons.

### What benefits are provided in case of a farmer’s death under this scheme?

The family of the deceased farmer receives a one-time payment of ₹2,00,000/- as financial support.

### Who is eligible to claim the death benefit under this agricultural scheme?

The deceased must have been a farmer aged between 18–60 years, and the claimant must be the legal heir.

### What is the financial assistance provided in case of permanent disability due to an accident?

Farmers who suffer permanent disability due to accidents receive a one-time payment of ₹2,00,000/-.

### What age group is eligible for the death and disability benefits under this scheme?

Only farmers between 18–60 years of age are covered under the death and disability benefits.

### What documents are required to apply for the direct income support component?

You need an Aadhaar card (linked to a bank account), land ownership proof, and a bank passbook copy.

### What proof is needed to verify land ownership for income support eligibility?

A Record of Rights (RoR) or Pattadar Passbook serves as valid land ownership proof.

### What documents must be submitted to claim the death benefit for a deceased farmer?

The applicant must provide a death certificate, legal heir certificate, and the farmer’s Aadhaar and land records.

### Is a disability certificate required to claim the disability benefit under this scheme?

Yes, a valid disability certificate must be submitted to claim the ₹2,00,000/- disability benefit.

### What is the official website where I can apply online for this scheme?

The application can be submitted at https://krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in/users/sign_up.
<br>

### Is there a helpline number available for queries related to this scheme?

Yes, you can call 8336957370 or 6291720406 between 10 AM and 6 PM for assistance.

### What is the official email address for contacting scheme authorities?

You can email your queries to krishak.bandhu@ingreens.in for support.

### Can I track the status of my application after submission?

Yes, you can check your application status on the official website: https://krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in.
