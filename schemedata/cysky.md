## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide free laptops and tablets to eligible students in higher education institutions. The scheme intends to facilitate the use of new technologies among the state's youth, thereby fostering their economic and social development, and enhancing their knowledge and careers.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 2013, the scheme "Chhattisgarh Yuva Suchna Kranti Yojana" by the Technical Education Department, Government of Chhattisgarh, aims to provide free laptops and tablets to eligible students in higher education institutions. The scheme intends to facilitate the use of new technologies among the state's youth, thereby fostering their economic and social development. It also seeks to enhance the knowledge and career prospects of young individuals through technology. The scheme is implemented by the Chhattisgarh Infotech and Biotech Promotion Society (CHIPS).

## Benefits

- Free laptops are provided to eligible final-year undergraduate and postgraduate students of engineering colleges, medical colleges, and other identified technical higher education institutions.
- Free tablets are provided to eligible final-year undergraduate and postgraduate students in universities, colleges, and higher education institutions, specifically those in commerce, arts, and science streams.
- The distribution occurs in each academic session.
- Each eligible student will receive either a tablet or a laptop only once during their academic life in any college/institution within the state.

_*CHIPS will determine the specifications of the laptops and tablets._
_*CHIPS will purchase laptops/tablets according to the specified procurement process and specifications and deliver them to the Department of Technical Education and the Department of Higher Education for distribution. The Department of Technical Education and the Department of Higher Education will inform CHIPS about the receipt and distribution locations of the laptops/tablets each year._

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a student.
- The applicant must be a native resident of Chhattisgarh.
- The applicant must be studying in the final year of a graduate or postgraduate degree program.
- The applicant must be pursuing the program from an institution in Chhattisgarh.
- For laptop, the applicant must be studying in a recognised engineering college, medical college, or other identified technical higher education institution in Chhattisgarh.
- For tablet, the applicant must be studying in a recognised university, college, or higher education institution in Chhattisgarh, in a commerce, arts, or science stream.

## Application Process

### Offline

- The scheme does not require any formal application from the students themselves. Eligible students will be automatically considered for incentives based on the scheme's eligibility criteria as evaluated by their respective institutions.
- The Department of Technical Education and the Department of Higher Education will ensure the distribution of laptops/tablets to eligible students and provide the list of beneficiary students to CHIPS in the prescribed format after distribution.
- The list of beneficiary students under the scheme will be displayed on the CHIPS website (www.chips.gov.in) and the state government website (www.cg.gov.in).
- The list of beneficiary students from the respective educational institution will be displayed on the institution's notice board.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to be a resident of Chhattisgarh to qualify for the laptop or tablet?

Yes, only native residents of Chhattisgarh are eligible to receive the benefits under this scheme.

### Can students from any year of study receive the laptop or tablet?

No, only final-year undergraduate or postgraduate students are eligible for the benefits.

### Are students from private institutions eligible for the scheme?

Yes, if the institution is recognized by the government and falls under the specified categories (technical for laptops, commerce/arts/science for tablets).

### Do I need to apply separately to receive the laptop or tablet?

No, eligible students are automatically considered based on institutional records; no formal application is required.

### How will I know if I have been selected to receive a laptop or tablet?

The list of beneficiaries will be displayed on the institution’s notice board, the CHIPS website (www.chips.gov.in), and the state government website (www.cg.gov.in).

### Can I receive both a laptop and a tablet under this scheme?

No, each eligible student will receive either a laptop or a tablet only once during their academic life.

### What are the specifications of the laptops and tablets provided?

The Chhattisgarh Infotech and Biotech Promotion Society (CHIPS) determines the specifications, which are not detailed in the document.

### Who is responsible for distributing the laptops and tablets to students?

The Department of Technical Education and the Department of Higher Education handle distribution based on lists provided by institutions.

### What happens if the laptop or tablet I receive is faulty?

CHIPS ensures maintenance during the warranty period, with repairs handled by the respective supplier.

### Is there any cost involved for students to receive these devices?

No, the laptops and tablets are provided free of cost to eligible students.

### Can students pursuing diploma courses avail of this benefit?

The document only mentions degree programs (graduate/postgraduate), so diploma students are not covered unless specified otherwise.

### How often are the laptops and tablets distributed under this scheme?

Distribution occurs in each academic session for eligible final-year students.

### Can I transfer my eligibility to another student if I don’t need the device?

No, the benefit is non-transferable and tied to individual eligibility criteria.

### Who purchases the laptops and tablets for distribution?

CHIPS procures the devices as per specifications and delivers them to the concerned departments for distribution.
