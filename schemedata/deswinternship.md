## Brief Description

The scheme aims to engage Indian Youth as interns to gain firsthand experience and understanding of welfare, healthcare, resettlement, and administrative schemes meant for Ex-Servicemen.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "DESW Internship Scheme" by the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare (DESW), Ministry of Defence, Government of India, aims to engage Indian youth as interns to gain firsthand experience and understanding of welfare, healthcare, resettlement, and administrative schemes meant for Ex-Servicemen. Through this scheme, the interns will undertake a structured study and submit a report that may contribute to policy or program improvements. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare (DESW).
<br>

> **Duration of Internship:**


- **Minimum duration:** 2 months- **Maximum duration:** 12 months- Internships are available throughout the year, based on DESW requirements.

<br>

## Benefits

1. Opportunity to gain firsthand experience in welfare, healthcare, resettlement, and administrative schemes meant for Ex-Servicemen.
1. Structured research assignment on specific topics related to Ex-Servicemen welfare.
1. Issuance of a certificate of successful completion upon satisfactory performance.
1. Potential publication of selected reports or excerpts with due credit to the intern.

<br>

> **Scope of Work: Interns will be assigned specific topics related to:-**


- Welfare schemes for Ex-Servicemen and their families.
- Resettlement and rehabilitation programs.
- Healthcare policies for veterans.
- Administrative processes and challenges in veteran affairs.

<br>

> **Note: For Completion & Certification**

> Interns shall submit their final report to the designated officer.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a citizen of India.
1. The applicant should be a graduate or postgraduate student from any recognised university or institution in any academic stream.
1. A No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the parent institution must be submitted along with the application.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:****** Visit the [official website](https://www.desw.gov.in/) of the Department of Ex-servicemen Welfare.
**Step 2:****** Click on "Scheme" from the menu and select "DESW Internship Scheme". 
**Step 3:****** A new tab will appear. In the scheme guidelines, click on the highlighted “Click Here” link for the application form.
**Step 4:** A [Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdn8__EYvlgsvAQoarxqXow5tv-3EFp94BkIMznpaoLssLfWA/viewform) will open as the Proforma of Application for the DESW Internship Scheme.
**Step 5:****** Enter the Name of the Applicant, Father’s/Mother’s Name, Date of Birth, and select Nationality.
**Step 6:****** Provide Address for Correspondence and Mobile Number.
**Step 7:****** Fill 10th, 12th, Graduation and Post-Graduation Class Details: Board/Course Name, Year of Passing, Percentage Obtained and University Name (where required)
**Step 8:****** Mention Current Course Pursuing (if any), Select Internship Duration (2 months to 1 year).
Choose Area(s) of Interest from the given options.
**Step 9:****** State whether you have previously interned with any Government body and provide details if applicable.
**Step 10:****** Write a Statement of Purpose (Why do you want to join this internship?) (maximum 200 words).
**Step 11:****** Agree to the Undertaking by the Applicant, select NOC Option (Not Applicable / Will Submit Before Joining) and review all entered information and click Submit to complete the application.
_*Once approved for the interview, they are required to submit the hard copy of Resume, Statement of purpose (max 200 words), No Objection Certificate from institution._

## Documents Required

1. Resume (Hard Copy).
1. Statement of Purpose (Maximum 200 words).
1. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Parent Institution (only for students currently enrolled in a university/college).
1. **Educational Qualification Proofs:**

   1. Marksheet and Certificate of 10th or Equivalent Class.
   1. Marksheet and Certificate of 12th or Equivalent Class.
   1. Marksheet and Certificate of Graduation or Equivalent Class.
   1. Marksheet and Certificate of Post-Graduation or Equivalent Class (if applicable).

1. Proof of Nationality (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Passport, or other government-issued ID showing Indian nationality).

> **Special Case:** NOC is not applicable for applicants who are not currently studying in any institution.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of this scheme?

The scheme aims to engage Indian youth as interns to gain firsthand experience and understanding of welfare, healthcare, resettlement, and administrative schemes meant for Ex-Servicemen.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

The applicant must be a citizen of India and a graduate or postgraduate student from any recognised university or institution in any academic stream.
<br>

### Is there any requirement from my college or university to apply?

Yes, a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from your parent institution must be submitted along with the application if you are currently enrolled.
<br>

### What is the minimum and maximum duration of the internship under this scheme?

The internship can be for a minimum of 2 months and a maximum of 12 months, based on requirements.
<br>

### Are there any monetary benefits provided under this scheme?

No, this is a non-remunerative internship and no monetary benefits are provided.
<br>

### What kind of work will I be expected to do as an intern?

You will work on assigned topics related to welfare schemes, resettlement programs, healthcare policies, or administrative processes for veterans.
<br>

### Do I need to submit any report during or after the internship?

Yes, each intern must submit a comprehensive research report summarising observations, best practices, challenges, and recommendations.
<br>

### Will I receive any certificate after completing the internship?

Yes, based on satisfactory performance, the Department will issue a certificate of successful completion.
<br>

### Is it possible for my work to be published by the Department?

Yes, selected reports or excerpts may be published by the Department with due credit to the intern.
<br>

### How do I apply for this scheme?

You need to fill out the online Google Form provided in the scheme guidelines and submit all required details.
<br>

### What documents do I need to provide when applying?

You must provide a resume, a statement of purpose (maximum 200 words), and an NOC from your parent institution if you are currently a student.
<br>

### Can I choose the duration of my internship?

Yes, you can select the duration between 2 months to 1 year while applying, based on your availability.
<br>

### Are internships available throughout the year?

Yes, internships are available year-round, depending on departmental requirements.
<br>

### Can I apply if I have already done an internship with a government body?

Yes, you can apply, but you must mention the details of any previous government internship in the application form.
<br>

### What areas of interest can I choose from when applying?

You can choose from welfare schemes for Ex-Servicemen, resettlement programs, healthcare or medical policies, pension/legal issues, and general administration.
<br>
