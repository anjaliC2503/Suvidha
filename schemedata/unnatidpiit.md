## Brief Description

The UNNATI (Uttar Poorva Transformative Industrialization Scheme), 2024, is a Government of India initiative for Northeastern region of India offering various incentives to the industries in both sectors Manufacturing and Service.

## Detailed Description

The UNNATI (Uttar Poorva Transformative Industrialization Scheme), 2024, is a Government of India initiative to boost industrial growth, investment, and employment in the North-Eastern Region (NER) states by offering substantial financial incentives, capital subsidies, and interest subventions for new and expanding manufacturing and service sector businesses, aiming to create a robust industrial ecosystem with simplified processes and support for innovation and skill development.
<br>

## Benefits

- Capital Investment Incentive (CII) -  30% of the eligible P&M in Zone A and 50% in Zone B.
- Capital Interest Subvention (CIS) - 3% interest subvention in Zone A and 5% in Zone B for a period of 7 years. 
- Manufacturing & Services Linked Incentive (MSLI) - All eligible units of Zone A & Zone B will be granted MSLI equal to 100% of the Net payment of GST, i.e. GST Paid less Input Tax Credit, for a maximum period of 10 years from the date of commencement of commercial production/operation or till the validity of the scheme whichever is earlier.

<br>

## Eligibility

> As per detailed scheme guidelines including minimum investment, legal entity status, fulfilling requirements viz. registered lease dead, GST details, various reports such as DPR, BAR etc. 

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

Step 1: Visit the [official ](https://unnati.dpiit.gov.in/)UNNATI Scheme portal and sign up using your email ID or mobile number. Create valid login credentials to access the application form.
Step 2: Log in to your account and complete the online application form with accurate details about your project and business.
Step 3: Upload all required documents, such as the Detailed Project Report (DPR), land or incorporation papers, and bank appraisal documents. Review the guidelines carefully and submit the application online.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the UNNATI Scheme, 2024?

It is a Government of India initiative to promote industrial growth, investment, and employment in the North-Eastern Region (NER) through financial incentives and policy support.
<br>

### Which regions are covered under the scheme?

The scheme applies to all North-Eastern states of India, categorized into Zone A and Zone B.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply?

New and expanding manufacturing and service sector units that meet the scheme guidelines, including minimum investment and legal entity requirements.
<br>

### What is the Capital Investment Incentive (CII)?

It provides subsidies on plant and machinery—30% in Zone A and 50% in Zone B.
<br>

### What is the Capital Interest Subvention (CIS)?

It offers interest subsidy on loans—3% for Zone A and 5% for Zone B for up to 7 years.
<br>

### What is the Manufacturing & Services Linked Incentive (MSLI)?

Eligible units receive incentives equal to 100% of net GST paid for up to 10 years or until scheme validity.
<br>

### What types of businesses are supported?

Both manufacturing and service sector businesses are eligible under the scheme.
<br>

### Is there a minimum investment requirement?

Yes, applicants must meet the minimum investment criteria specified in the detailed scheme guidelines.
<br>

### What documents are required for application?

Key documents include DPR (Detailed Project Report), registered lease deed/land documents, GST details, bank appraisal report, and incorporation certificate.
<br>

### What is the duration of benefits under the scheme?

Benefits like MSLI can be availed for up to 10 years, while interest subvention is available for 7 years.
