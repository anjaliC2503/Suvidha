## Brief Description

The "Mukhymantri Sarthi Yojana" aims to provide industry-relevant skill development training for youth in Jharkhand, aligning with NSQF standards. Implemented by Jharkhand Skill Development Mission Society (JSDMS) across all 24 districts, it offers free training to enhance employability.

## Detailed Description

The "Mukhymantri Sarthi Yojana" was launched by the Department of Labour, Employment, Training and Skill Development, Government of Jharkhand. The scheme aims to provide industry-relevant skill development training for youth in Jharkhand, aligning with NSQF standards. Implemented by JSDMS across all 24 districts, it offers free training to enhance employability and self-employment opportunities. The scheme ensures quality training with a focus on marginalized communities, including SC/ST, women, PwD, and transgender individuals.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Free Training:** Industry-aligned, high-quality skill development training at no cost.- **Holistic Development:** Includes soft skills and IT training for better employability.- **Essential Materials Provided:** Uniforms, participant handbooks, and required tools for free.- **Financial Support:**



   1. Rozgar Protsahan Bhatta ₹1,000/month for boys and ₹1,500/month for girls, transgender, and PwD for one year if unemployed after training.
   1. ₹1,000/month transport allowance for non-residential trainees.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Jharkhand.
1. The applicant's age should be 18-35 years.
1. The applicant must belong to one of the priority groups (SC/ST, women, PwD, transgender).

## Application Process

### Offline

> ***Candidate registration by TSP:***


- The candidate visits the nearest [Training Service Provide Center](https://jsdm.jharkhand.gov.in/jsdm/cms/en/) for registration under the "Mukhymantri Sarthi Yojna" scheme. 
**NOTE:** Candidate registration would be Aadhaar-based & hence Aadhaar number at the time of candidate registration would be mandatory.

### Online

> ***Self-Registration by Candidate:***



  - Visit the [Jharkhand Skill Development Mission](https://jsdm.jharkhand.gov.in/jsdm/cms/en/#) official website.  - Click on ‘Candidate Registration’ under the ‘Registration’ tab.


> ***Create Profile:***



  - Provide basic information such as First Name, Last Name, Username, Password, Identity Type (e.g., Aadhaar), Identity Number, Date of Birth, Gender, Mobile Number, and Email.
  - Submit to receive a User ID and Password via SMS and email.


> ***Login and OTP Validation:***



  - Log in using the provided Username and Password.
  - Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number to activate the User ID.


> ***Manage Profile:***



  - After login, access the ‘Registration’ link.
  - Update and save user profile details such as Personal information and optional bank account details. 


> **Contact and Educational Qualification** **Information:**



  - Enter and save your contact details, including address and communication preferences.
  - Fill in education details if literate, or select preferred languages if illiterate. 
  - Save and submit information to the Training Service Provider (TSP).


> **Registration ID Generation:**



  - On final submission, the system generates a unique Registration ID and notifications for successful registration will be sent via SMS and email.


<br>

**NOTE:** Aadhaar number is mandatory for registration and prevents duplicate entries.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Mukhymantri Sarthi Yojna?

It is a skill development scheme to provide free NSQF-aligned training for employability.

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Residents of Jharkhand aged 18-35 years (50 years for SC/ST under BIRSA).

### Is the training free of cost?

Yes, the training is completely free of cost.

### What is Rozgar Protsahan Bhatta?

An allowance of [?]1000/month for boys and [?]1500/month for girls, transgender, and PwD for one year if unemployed.

### What type of training is provided?

Industry-relevant, NSQF-aligned training, including soft skills and IT.

### How is attendance tracked?

Through Aadhar-enabled Biometric Attendance System (AEBAS).

### Are there residential facilities?

Yes, both residential and non-residential training centers are available.

### Can non-residential trainees get transport allowance?

Yes, [?]1000/month is provided through DBT.

### What are the materials provided?

Uniforms, participant handbooks, and other essentials.

### How to apply for the scheme?

Visit your district training center and fill out the application with required documents.

### What happens if I remain unemployed after the training?

You will receive Rozgar Protsahan Bhatta for up to one year.

### What is the role of NSDC?

NSDC accredits training centers under SMART measures for quality assurance.

### Is IT training mandatory for all courses?

Yes, IT training is included to enhance holistic development.
