## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage and preserve the traditional Khell-Tiatr folk musical drama of Goa by promoting quality productions. It offers financial assistance and infrastructure support for staging shows, including video documentation, benefiting dramatic troupes and individuals.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Financial Assistance for Encouraging Production of Traditional Khell-Tiatr" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to encourage dramatic troupes and individuals to produce Khell-Tiatrs of better quality and preserve this folk musical drama. The scheme provides financial assistance to compensate for expenditures related to staging four shows, including the provision of an auditorium or hall, or financial aid for temporary village setups, along with video filming and a free Digital Versatile Disc copy for the applicant. The applicant must adhere to traditional Khell-Tiatr format, performance guidelines, advertising requirements, and submit detailed proposals with all required documentation in advance. The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted by submission to the Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days prior to the proposed performance date.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Financial Assistance for Staging Performances***

- Financial assistance (as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa) is provided for staging four shows.
- Payment will be made directly to the applicant after a statement of expenditure enclosing all original bills (with stamped receipts if the amount exceeds ₹5,000/-) is submitted to the Tiatr Academy of Goa.

##### ***Infrastructure Support***

- An auditorium or hall is provided to stage the programme.
- For Village Programmes: Financial assistance (as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa) is provided for erecting a temporary pandal or stage, chairs, sound, etc.

##### ***Archival and Personal Use Video Documentation***

- The Khell-Tiatr will be video filmed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa for its library and other uses.
- One copy of the Digital Versatile Disc (DVD) of the video-filmed Khell-Tiatr will be given to the applicant free of cost.
- The dramatic troupe will have the freedom to use the Digital Versatile Disc in any manner, but cannot make use of the logo of Tiatr Academy of Goa on the said Digital Versatile Disc.

<br>

##### ***Conditions***
**Ongoing Compliance regarding Content**

- There must not be any adverse remark made in any form, either written or verbal, against any Government, Individual, Institution, etc., or anything against one’s caste, creed, and religion which may lead to hurting of religious sentiments of anybody. 
- If proved so, Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) reserves the right to disqualify the performance and refrain from making any payment or recall the payment if already made to the concerned.

**Show Access Obligation**
All shows must be presented to the public free of charge.
**Advertising and Branding Obligation**
In all advertisements, whether oral, print, or electronic media, banners, etc., the statement ‘This programme is held in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa’ must be inserted, published, or announced.
**Guest of Honour Coordination**

- The organizing dramatic troupe or individual will need to discuss with Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) and decide about the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour well in advance. 
- The President or Vice President of Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) will serve as the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour at the function.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a dramatic troupe or an individual producing Khell-Tiatrs.
- The Khell-Tiatr produced by the applicant should be a new or old script.
- The Khell-Tiatr produced by the applicant must conform to the traditional format of the Khell-Tiatr.
- The Khell-Tiatr produced by the applicant must be one in which just one-story flows from beginning to end with an interval of 10 to 15 minutes.
- The Khell-Tiatr produced by the applicant must have 12 to 14 cantos which are related to the theme of the play.
- Out of these cantos, at least 7 must be of an interactive nature.
- The band for the Khell-Tiatr produced by the applicant should include at least two blowing instruments, one keyboard, one bass guitar, and a drum set.
- The applicant must not have any adverse remark made in any form, either written or verbal, against any Government/Individual/Institution, etc.
- The applicant must not have anything against one’s caste, creed, and religion which may lead to hurting of religious sentiments of anybody.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. The form may also be obtained directly from the office of the Tiatr Academy of Goa, requiring a physical visit to the office for collection.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the copies of the mandatory documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
**Deadline:** This submission must occur at least 30 days in advance of the proposed performance date.
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
*The application will be considered on a 'First Come First Serve' basis and is subject to the availability of funds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What types of applicants are eligible to apply for financial assistance under this traditional folk musical drama support program?

Both dramatic troupes and individual producers who create Khell-Tiatrs can apply for this assistance. You must fulfill all the conditions outlined in the scheme and ensure your production adheres to the traditional format requirements.
<br>

### How much advance notice do I need to provide when submitting my application for this cultural performance assistance scheme?

Applications must be submitted to the Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days prior to your proposed performance date. This advance submission requirement ensures proper processing and coordination of your application.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions on the content I can include in my traditional folk musical drama performance?

Yes, your performance must not contain any adverse remarks against any government, individual, or institution. Additionally, nothing should be included that targets someone's caste, creed, or religion, as this could hurt religious sentiments and lead to disqualification.
<br>

### What are the specific musical instrument requirements for the band accompanying my Khell-Tiatr performance?

Your band must include at least 2 blowing instruments, 1 keyboard, 1 bass guitar, and 1 drum set. These are the minimum instrumental requirements to qualify for the financial assistance under this scheme.
<br>

### Can I charge admission fees for the shows that receive financial support through this program?

No, all shows must be presented to the public completely free of charge. This is a mandatory condition for receiving financial assistance and infrastructure support under the scheme.
<br>

### What is the required structure regarding the number of cantos that my Khell-Tiatr production must contain?

Your Khell-Tiatr must have between 12 to 14 cantos that are directly related to the theme of your play. Out of these cantos, at least 7 must be of an interactive nature to meet the traditional format requirements.
<br>

### How should I handle the selection of chief guests or guests of honour for my performances?

You must discuss and coordinate with the Tiatr Academy of Goa well in advance to decide about guests. The President or Vice President of Tiatr Academy of Goa will serve as the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour at your function.
<br>

### What specific advertising requirements must I follow when promoting my performances under this assistance scheme?

In all advertisements, whether oral, print, electronic media, or banners, you must include the statement 'This programme is held in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa.' This acknowledgment is mandatory across all promotional materials.
<br>

### How many performances am I required to stage if I receive financial assistance through this program?

You are required to stage exactly 4 shows as part of the financial assistance package. The scheme specifically provides support for staging this fixed number of performances.
<br>

### What documentation do I need to submit regarding my script and artistic team for this application?

You must provide a typed copy of your complete script and lyrics to be presented, including the names of all artistes who will be performing. Additionally, submit a separate list of all participants on a dedicated sheet.
<br>

### Can I use both new and previously written scripts for my Khell-Tiatr production under this scheme?

Yes, your Khell-Tiatr can be based on either a new script or an old script. Both options are acceptable as long as the production conforms to all other traditional format and content requirements.
<br>

### What specific financial documentation must I provide to receive payment after completing my performances?

You must submit a statement of expenditure that includes all original bills with proper documentation. If any single expense exceeds ₹5,000/-, you need to provide stamped receipts along with the original bills for reimbursement.
<br>

### What are the interval timing requirements that my traditional folk musical drama performance must follow?

Your Khell-Tiatr must have one continuous story flowing from beginning to end with a single interval lasting between 10 to 15 minutes. This timing structure is essential for maintaining the traditional format.
<br>

### What photographic evidence am I required to submit after completing my performances under this assistance program?

You must provide 5 copies of photographs from your function, with 2 specific photographs showing the Tiatr Academy of Goa's banner displayed during the performance. These photos serve as documentation of proper acknowledgment and successful completion.
<br>
