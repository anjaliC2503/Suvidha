## Brief Description

The EXCEL program is a skill development initiative by the Jharkhand Government targeting 10+2 pass-out or university-level students. It integrates skill training with traditional education programs in colleges to improve employability.

## Detailed Description

The “Employability Excellence with College Education & Learning (EXCEL)” is a component of the umbrella scheme “Mukhymantri Sarthi Yojna” launched by the Department of Labour, Employment, Training and Skill Development, Jharkhand. It integrates skill training with traditional education programs in colleges to improve employability. The program focuses on life skills, spoken English, computer skills, and domain-specific training, benefitting students with industry-aligned capabilities.

## Benefits

1. Provides training alongside traditional education in college campuses.
1. Enhances employability through life skills, communication, and domain-specific training.
1. Free-of-cost training conducted within existing college infrastructure.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a student.
1. The student must have completed their 12th education.
1. Open to university students, former pass-outs, and non-college-going youth from the community are eligibile.

## Application Process

### Offline

> ***Candidate registration by TSP:***


- The candidate visits the nearest [Training Service Provide Center](https://jsdm.jharkhand.gov.in/jsdm/cms/en/) for registration under the "Mukhymantri Sarthi Yojna" scheme. 
**NOTE:** Candidate registration would be Aadhaar-based & hence Aadhaar number at the time of candidate registration would be mandatory.

### Online

> ***Self-Registration by Candidate:***



  - Visit the [Jharkhand Skill Development Mission](https://jsdm.jharkhand.gov.in/jsdm/cms/en/#) official website.  - Click on ‘Candidate Registration’ under the ‘Registration’ tab.


> ***Create Profile:***



  - Provide basic information such as First Name, Last Name, Username, Password, Identity Type (e.g., Aadhaar), Identity Number, Date of Birth, Gender, Mobile Number, and Email.
  - Submit to receive a User ID and Password via SMS and email.


> ***Login and OTP Validation:***



  - Log in using the provided Username and Password.
  - Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number to activate the User ID.


> ***Manage Profile:***



  - After login, access the ‘Registration’ link.
  - Update and save user profile details such as Personal information and optional bank account details. 


> **Contact and Educational Qualification** **Information:**



  - Enter and save your contact details, including address and communication preferences.
  - Fill in education details if literate, or select preferred languages if illiterate. 
  - Save and submit information to the Training Service Provider (TSP).


**Registration ID Generation:**


  - On final submission, the system generates a unique Registration ID and notifications for successful registration will be sent via SMS and email.


<br>

**NOTE:** Aadhaar number is mandatory for registration and prevents duplicate entries.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the EXCEL program?

To enhance the employability of youth through skill-based training integrated with traditional education.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

10+2 pass-outs, university students, former students, and community youth.

### What kind of skills are taught under EXCEL?

Life skills, spoken English, computer skills, and domain-specific skills.

### Where is the training conducted?

Training is conducted within college campuses.

### Is the program free of cost?

Yes, the program is free of cost for eligible candidates.

### What is the duration of the training?

The minimum course duration is 210 hours.

### Which colleges are part of this initiative?

Government, constituent, and private colleges under state universities.

### How many students are targeted annually?

The program aims to train 10,000 students per year.

### How are TSPs selected?

Through a Request for Proposal (RFP) floated by JSDMS.

### Is the training linked to job placements?

The training is designed to improve employability but may not guarantee placements.

### How is the training managed?

Training is managed by empanelled TSPs in collaboration with JSDMS.

### What is the program's target over four years?

The program targets training 51,000 students in 100 colleges across five state universities.
