## Brief Description

The scheme aims to empower young professionals with technical expertise to address state-level governance challenges through technology. It offers a 2-year M.Tech program in DSAI, hands-on government project experience, and a monthly stipend of ₹50,000/- to eligible youth of Chhattisgarh.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "The CM IT Fellowship Program" was launched by the Electronics & Information Technology Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. The scheme aims to empower and train young professionals in the field of Software-Driven Analysis to bring measurable improvements to governance by deploying skilled professionals. The scheme provides a unique blended learning experience where fellows earn an M.Tech in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI) from IIIT Naya Raipur while working on real-world government projects with a monthly stipend of ₹50,000/-. The applicant must be a GATE-qualified graduate or postgraduate from Chhattisgarh, aged up to 30 years, and willing to work with government departments. The scheme is implemented by CHiPS (Chhattisgarh Infotech Promotion Society) in collaboration with IIIT Naya Raipur. The applications for this scheme are accepted online through the IIIT-NR website.
<br>

## Benefits

**Monthly Stipend:** The scheme provides a stipend of ₹50,000/- per month during the 2-year program duration.
**Academic Qualification:** Fellows are enrolled in a full-time M.Tech in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI) program at IIIT Naya Raipur.
**Professional Experience:** Fellows gain hands-on experience by working directly with government departments on data-driven policy making and digital transformation initiatives.
**Mentorship:** The program offers mentorship from top academicians, IT leaders, and policymakers to help fellows grow as future-ready professionals.
_*Fellows are deployed at CHiPS or assigned departments for 4 months in Semester 2, 6 months in Semester 3, and 5 months in Semester 4 as part of the engagement._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be an Indian National.
- The applicant must hold a Domicile of Chhattisgarh.
- The applicant must be upto 30 years of age as of the application deadline.
- The applicant must be a graduate or postgraduate student.
- The applicant must have qualified for the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) in Computer Science (CS) or Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI).
- The applicant must have secured a minimum CPI of 6.5 or 60% or First Class in their qualifying degree.

<br>

##### ***Relaxations***

- The applicant belonging to SC/ST/PwD categories is eligible for a relaxation of 5% marks or 0.5 CPI.
- The applicant is eligible for age relaxations as per the Chhattisgarh Government reservation rules.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

##### ***Step 1:********* ***Access the Official Website*** 
The applicant must visit the official website of IIIT Naya Raipur at [https://www.iiitnr.ac.in/](https://www.iiitnr.ac.in/) or the [CM Fellowship website](https://cmitfellowship.cgstate.gov.in/) to check for the admission notification regarding the batch schedule.
##### ***Step 2:********* ***Verify Notification Details*** 
The applicant must look for the "CM IT Fellowship" or "M.Tech in DSAI under CM IT Fellowship" application announcement, which is typically released prior to the start of the batch in June.
##### ***Step 3:********* ***Register and Fill the Application*** 
The applicant must register on the designated admission portal and fill out the application form with accurate personal details, academic qualifications, and GATE scores as required by the institute.
##### ***Step 4:********* ***Submit Required Documents*** 
The applicant must upload or submit the necessary documents to substantiate their eligibility, including the GATE score card, domicile certificate, and academic transcripts.
##### ***Step 5:********* ***Await Selection Process*** 
The applicant must participate in the subsequent selection process conducted by IIIT Naya Raipur and CHiPS, which is based on the eligibility criteria and GATE qualifications submitted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Could you please specify the monthly financial assistance or stipend amount provided to selected candidates during the tenure of this program to support their living expenses?

Selected candidates receive a monthly stipend of ₹50,000/- for the entire duration of the two-year program to support their education and living expenses while they work on government projects.
<br>

### Is it mandatory for an applicant to hold a valid domicile certificate of a specific state to be eligible for admission under this initiative?

Yes, the applicant must strictly hold a valid Domicile of Chhattisgarh to be eligible for admission into this fellowship program and submit the necessary proof during the application process.
<br>

### What are the minimum educational qualifications required for a candidate to apply for this Master of Technology program in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence?

The applicant must be a graduate or postgraduate student who has secured a minimum Cumulative Performance Index (CPI) of 6.5, 60% marks, or First Class in their qualifying degree.
<br>

### Is it necessary to have qualified a specific national-level entrance examination to be considered for selection, and if so, in which specific disciplines is this required?

Yes, the applicant must have qualified the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) specifically in the disciplines of Computer Science (CS) or Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI).
<br>

### Are there any relaxations in the minimum marks or Cumulative Performance Index requirements for candidates belonging to the Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, or Persons with Disabilities categories?

Yes, candidates belonging to Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, or Persons with Disabilities categories are eligible for a relaxation of 5% marks or 0.5 Cumulative Performance Index (CPI) in the eligibility criteria.
<br>

### Upon successful completion of the two-year academic and engagement requirements, what specific academic degree will be awarded to the participants?

Participants will be awarded a Master of Technology (M.Tech) degree in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI) by the International Institute of Information Technology, Naya Raipur (IIIT-NR).
<br>

### Is the application process open to international students, or is it strictly restricted to citizens of a specific nationality according to the eligibility guidelines?

The eligibility criteria strictly require that the applicant must be an Indian national to apply for this program, and proof of nationality is required during the admission process.
<br>

### Could you explain the nature of the professional engagement or project work that fellows are expected to undertake during the course of this program?

Fellows will work directly with government departments on real-world governance challenges, contributing to data-driven policy making and digital transformation initiatives to bring measurable improvements to governance.
<br>

### What is the total duration of the fellowship program, and how is the timeline divided between academic sessions and government department engagements?

The program has a total duration of 2 years, divided into 4 semesters, combining academic sessions at the institute and practical engagement with government departments, with varying durations for each phase.
<br>

### Are there any provisions for age relaxation for candidates belonging to reserved categories, and if so, which rules govern these specific relaxations?

Yes, age relaxations are applicable for candidates from reserved categories as per the existing reservation rules of the Chhattisgarh Government regarding age limits for such programs.
<br>

### What are the specific steps a candidate needs to take to submit an application for the upcoming batch, and where can interested candidates find the relevant official notifications?

Interested candidates must apply online through the International Institute of Information Technology, Naya Raipur (IIIT-NR) website, and should check the site typically around June for the admission notification.
<br>

### Which specific government agencies or departments are designated for the deployment of fellows during the practical engagement phase of the program?

Fellows are deployed at the Chhattisgarh Infotech Promotion Society (CHiPS) or other government departments such as Finance, Home, Transport, or Health, as assigned by CHiPS based on strategic needs.
<br>

### On what basis are the candidates shortlisted and selected for admission into this blended learning fellowship program?

Selection is primarily based on the candidate's Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) score in Computer Science or Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, along with their academic record satisfying the eligibility criteria.
<br>

### Could you please specify the maximum age limit prescribed for candidates wishing to apply for this fellowship, and is there a specific cutoff date for calculating this age?

The applicant must be up to 30 years of age as of the application deadline mentioned in the official notification to be eligible for the fellowship program.
<br>
