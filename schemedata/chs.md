## Brief Description

The "Coaching Help Scheme" provides financial support to non-reserved category students pursuing science in Standard-11 and Standard-12 by covering additional tuition costs.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Coaching Help Scheme&quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, offers an annual incentive to eligible students for tuition classes outside their school/college. The aim is to help academically bright students continue their studies without financial constraints.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

**Financial Assistance:** ₹15,000/- per year for coaching expenses outside the school/college.
<br>

## Exclusions

- Education or tuition fees paid in school/college are not eligible for the scheme.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must belong to a non-reserved category.
   1. The applicant must have scored 70% or more in Standard 10th.
   1. The applicant must be currently studying in the science stream in Standard 11th or Standard 12th.
   1. The applicant’s annual family income should not exceed ₹4,50,000/-.

﻿
**Note:** The applicant can avail of the assistance only once per academic year.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Go to the Corporation's [official website](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/) and navigate to the "Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation" section under the Corporation menu.
**Step 2:**Click on "Coaching Help Scheme" and then Apply Now.
**Step 3:** Click on "New User? Please Register Here".
**Step 4:** Enter the required details and click Register.
> **Application Process:**

**Step 1:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, then click "Login".
**Step 2:** Read the scheme details carefully and click OK.
**Step 3:** If your permanent address is correct, confirm it. Otherwise, enter your correct address.
**Step 4:** Click Save & Next to proceed. Upload the relevant documents.
**Step 5:** If you need to make changes, click "No, Cancel". If all details are correct, click "Yes, Submit".
**Step 6:** Click Save Application. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout by clicking Print Application.
> **Check Application Status:** [Click here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx).

> **Post Online Application Procedure:** After submitting the online application, the applicant must take a printout of the application, sign it, and submit it along with all uploaded documents (certificates, mark sheets, etc.) to the concerned Deputy Director (Non-Reserved Category) or the District Social Welfare Officer through courier, post, or by visiting in person.

> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in/contact-us.html).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Students who are studying in the science stream of Standard-11 and Standard-12 are eligible for the Tuition Assistance Scheme.

### What is the minimum percentage required to avail of the Tuition Assistance?

To be eligible for this scheme, a student must have secured 70% or more marks in Standard-10.

### What is the annual family income limit for this scheme?

The annual family income of the applicant should be less than [?]4,50,000/- to qualify for this scheme.

### How much financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Under this scheme, eligible students will receive a financial assistance amount of [?]15,000/- per year.

### What proof is required for tuition assistance?

Students applying for tuition assistance must provide a receipt of the tuition fee payment as proof.

### What proof is needed to show that the student is enrolled in tuition classes?

To prove enrollment in tuition classes, the student must submit a certificate from the tuition class or coaching institute.

### Which authority issues a valid income certificate for this scheme?

The valid income certificate for this scheme can be issued by the Taluka Development Officer (TDO), Mamlatdar, or the Chief Officer.

### Where can the Non-Reserved Category Certificate be obtained?

The Non-Reserved Category Certificate can be obtained from the Taluka Development Officer (TDO), Mamlatdar, District Social Welfare Officer (Non-Reserved Category), or the Deputy Director (Non-Reserved Category).

### What should be done after submitting the online application?

After submitting the online application, the applicant must take a printout of the application, sign it, and submit it along with all uploaded documents (certificates, mark sheets, etc.) to the concerned Deputy Director (Non-Reserved Category) or the District Social Welfare Officer through courier, post, or by visiting in person.

### What happens if the hard copy of the application is not submitted after the online application?

If the hard copy of the application is not submitted after completing the online application, the application will not be considered valid and will be rejected.
