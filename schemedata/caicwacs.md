## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide scholarships to students from minority religious communities studying Chartered Accounts/Cost & Management Accounts/Company Secretaryship courses. Through this scheme, a scholarship is provided to eligible students.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "CA/ICWA CMA /CS Scholarship" by the Kerala State Minority Welfare Department aims to provide scholarships to students from minority religious communities studying Chartered Accounts/Cost & Management Accounts/Company Secretaryship courses. Through this scheme, a scholarship amount is provided to eligible students. The scheme is implemented by the Kerala State Minority Welfare Department.
<br>

## Benefits

1. The scholarship amount of ₹15,000/- is provided.
1. The scholarship is provided to students from minority religious communities.

<br>

## Exclusions

- Applicants who have received this scholarship in previous years are not eligible to apply.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Kerala.
1. The applicant must belong to Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Parsi, or Jain religious communities.
1. The applicant's annual family income should be below ₹8,00,000/-.
1. The applicant must be appearing for intermediate or final year examinations of Chartered Accounts, Cost & Management Accounts, or Company Secretaryship courses.
1. The applicant must have secured 60% marks in Plus Two/B.Com or other degrees.
1. The applicant should have an account in any nationalised bank in their own name.

<br>

> **Preference/Priority/Reservation/Relaxation:**



  - Preference will be given to applicants belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.
  - In the absence of BPL applicants, applicants from minority religious communities with an annual income up to ₹8,00,000/- in the Above Poverty Line (APL) category will be considered.
  - Selection will be based on merit and income.


<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://www.minoritywelfare.kerala.gov.in) of the Directorate of Minority Welfare.
**Step 2:** Locate and click on the "Scholarship".
**Step 3:** Navigate to the "Scholarship" menu link and click on "CA/ICWA (CMA)/CS Scholarship (IWCS)".
**Step 4:** Click on "Apply Online".
**Step 5:** For new scholarship applicants, first complete one-time registration and log in with your candidate details.
**Step 6:** In the Registration Form, carefully enter the details step-by-step under Examination details (register number/roll number), Personal details, and Scholarship details tabs.
**Step 7:** In the Upload details tab, upload the required documents (Photo, Signature, SSLC Certificate, Income Certificate, Ration Card Copy, Allotment Memo) in JPEG format under 100 KB size each.
**Step 8:** Only after uploading all required documents, the registration process will be considered complete.
**Step 9:** After completing the application submission, use the View/Print Application link to take a printout of the application form.
<br>

> **Submission of Application Printout**


- After submitting the scholarship application (either through new registration or login), click on "View/Print Application" to get a printout of your registration form.
- Submit the printout of the registration form along with all the required supporting documents to the head of the institution where you are studying, within the specified deadline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the CA/ICWA CMA /CS Scholarship?

The main objective of this scholarship is to provide financial assistance to students from minority religious communities studying Chartered Accounts, Cost & Management Accounts, or Company Secretaryship courses.
<br>

### Which courses are covered under the CA/ICWA CMA /CS Scholarship?

The scholarship covers Chartered Accounts, Cost & Work Accounts (Cost & Management Accounts), and Company Secretaryship courses.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scholarship?

Students from minority religious communities who are permanent residents of Kerala and are studying Chartered Accounts, Cost & Management Accounts, or Company Secretaryship courses are eligible.
<br>

### Are there any specific religious communities that are eligible for this scholarship?

Yes, the eligible religious communities are Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Parsi, and Jain.
<br>

### Which academic years of the courses are eligible for this scholarship?

Students in their Intermediate and Final years of the eligible courses are eligible to apply.
<br>

### Is there a minimum marks requirement for previous qualifications to apply?

Yes, applicants must have secured 60% marks in Plus Two, B.Com, or other relevant degrees.
<br>

### Is there any preference given to certain categories of applicants?

Yes, preference is given to applicants belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.
<br>

### If I am from an Above Poverty Line (APL) family, can I still apply?

Yes, if there is an absence of BPL applicants, APL applicants from minority religious communities with an annual income up to ₹8,00,000 will be considered.
<br>

### If I received this scholarship in a previous year, can I apply again?

No, applicants who have received this scholarship in previous years are not eligible to apply again.
<br>

### Do I need a bank account to receive the scholarship amount?

Yes, applicants must have an account in any nationalized bank in their own name.
<br>

### How do I apply for the CA/ICWA CMA /CS Scholarship?

You can apply online through the Kerala State Minority Welfare Department's website, www.minoritywelfare.kerala.gov.in.
<br>

### What should I do after applying online?

After applying online, you will receive a User ID & Password. You need to log in and upload your Photo, Signature, and various certificates.
<br>

### Do I need to take a printout of my online application?

Yes, after submitting the application, you should click "View/Print Application" and take a printout of the registration form.
<br>

### Where should I submit the printout of the registration form and other documents?

The printout of the registration form and other supporting documents must be submitted to the Directorate of Minority Welfare.
<br>

### If I am a Final student, what specific certificates do I need to submit?

If you are a Final student, you must submit a copy of your Intermediate pass certificate and proof of joining the Final course.
<br>
