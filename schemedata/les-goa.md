## Brief Description

The scheme aims to equip Class XI students with individual portable computer systems to enhance digital learning, knowledge acquisition, and skill attainment for employment or self-employment. Students receive one entry-level Laptop or Notebook equipped with specific technical requirements.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Laptop-e Scheme" was launched by the Directorate of Education, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to equip students with individual portable computer systems (Laptops). The scheme aims to enhance learning and knowledge acquisition through Information Technology tools such as the Internet, CD-ROMs, and DVDs, and fully exploit the teaching infrastructure in schools. The applications for this scheme are accepted through the Head of the Institution where the student is studying.
<br>

## Benefits

Provision of one (1) entry-level portable computer system (Notebook or Laptop).
##### ***Details of Equipment***

- The system must be equipped with at least mid-level Processors.
- The system must include a DVD-ROM Drive.
- The system must include Wi-Fi connectivity.
- The system must include multimedia speakers.
- The system must have a 15-inch or more screen size.
- The system must have a requisite Preloaded Open Standards Supported Operating System.
- The operating system must be backed by premium or comprehensive technical support for at least three (3) years from reputed software companies such as Windows Operating System.

##### ***Disbursement***

- Benefits are derived immediately after the approval of the Government, depending on the availability of funds and after following all codal formalities.
- The portable computer systems are supplied immediately to the students in various higher secondaries once tenders are decided and supply orders are issued to the various suppliers.

##### ***Conditions***

- _The applicant must deposit a refundable Registration Fee as decided by the Government._
- _The fee, once deposited, is not refunded in any case by the Government._
- _The student, parent, or guardian shall give a valid receipt of the portable computer system received to the Education Department._
- _This receipt must be submitted after obtaining the portable computer system through the respective Head of the Institute._

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a student of 11th standard.
- The applicant must be studying in a recognized Higher Secondary School in Goa.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The student must obtain the prescribed format of the application form of the scheme from the institution where he/she is currently studying.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, to the concerned staff at the institution. The application form should be duly approved and recommended for the supply of the portable system by the Head of the Institution.
##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 1:** **Processing and Government Approval**
The Government processes the application, decides the tenders, and issues supply orders to the various suppliers after following all formalities.
**Step 2:** **Receive Portable Computer System**
The students derive the benefit of the scheme immediately after the approval of the Government, although disbursement is dependent on the availability of funds. The suppliers are directed to supply the Laptops or Notebooks immediately to the students in various higher secondaries across the state.
**Step 3:** **Provide Valid Receipt of Computer System Received**
After receiving the portable computer system, the student, parent, or guardian must submit a valid receipt of the system received to the Education Department through the respective Head of the Institute.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What particular academic document is necessary to attach along with the formal application when submitting it to the institution?

You must submit a self-attested Secondary School Certificate Examination mark sheet along with the application form.
<br>

### If I apply for this benefit, what specific type and quantity of portable technology equipment will be supplied to me?

You will be provided with one (1) entry-level portable computer system, commonly known as a Notebook or Laptop computer system.
<br>

### What minimum period of comprehensive technical support must the provided operating system be backed by from reputed software providers?

The operating system must be backed by premium or comprehensive technical support for at least three (3) years from reputed software companies such as Windows Operating System.
<br>

### Regarding connectivity features, what wireless technology must the provided portable computer system be capable of utilizing?

The system must be equipped with Wi-Fi connectivity.
<br>

### Are there mandatory audio output accessories or hardware components required to be included with the portable computer system?

Yes, the system must be equipped with multimedia speakers.
<br>

### As an applicant, must I be enrolled in a specific type of educational institution to qualify for the benefits of this scheme?

The applicant must be studying in a recognized Higher Secondary School.
<br>

### Does the portable computer system need to include any specific optical drive hardware for loading media such as Compact Disc Read-Only Memory or Digital Versatile Disc?

Yes, the computer system must specifically include a Digital Versatile Disc Read-Only Memory Drive.
<br>

### If the government mandates a registration fee, what happens to that money once I have deposited it during the application procedure?

Once the registration fee has been deposited, it is non-refundable in any case by the Government.
<br>

### What criteria determine my eligibility regarding my current academic standing or level of study to apply for this assistance?

The applicant must be a student of Class XI standard.
<br>

### What specific operating system is required to be preloaded on the portable computer system provided under this educational initiative?

The portable computer system must be equipped with a requisite Preloaded Open Standards Supported Operating System, such as Windows Operating System.
<br>

### What are the minimum requirements for the central processing unit installed in the portable computer system being distributed through this program?

The provided computer system must be equipped with at least mid-level Processors.
<br>

### Does having any academic concessions affect whether or not a student is allowed to apply for or receive the benefits of this equipment scheme?

No, the applicant is eligible irrespective of the concessions in their academics.
<br>

### After receiving the portable computer system, is there any required acknowledgment or administrative step I must complete?

Yes, you, your parent, or your guardian must submit a valid receipt of the portable computer system received to the Education Department.
<br>

### Are there specific guidelines regarding the display hardware, such as the minimum acceptable screen size for the portable computer system I will receive?

Yes, the portable computer system must have a screen size of 15-inch or more.
