## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support and encourage meritorious students by providing them with monetary assistance of ₹30,000/- in their final year of undergraduate studies, thereby reducing financial burdens.

## Detailed Description

The “Dr. Dhyanpal Singh Memorial Award (for Final-Year Undergraduate Students of All Colleges)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to support and encourage meritorious students in their final year of undergraduate studies by reducing financial burdens.
<br>

## Benefits

The selected students will receive an amount of ₹30,000/- per year in their final year of undergraduate.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the final year of an undergraduate course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The award amount is provided once per year.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the students receive their payments.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this award?

The applicant must be a student enrolled in the final year of an undergraduate course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
<br>

### What is the financial assistance provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides monetary assistance of ₹30,000 per year to eligible students.
<br>

### How long is the award granted for?

The award is granted for a duration of one year only.

### How can students apply for the award?

Students are invited to submit applications on the prescribed form, which is made available by the university.
<br>

### Who evaluates the applications?

A committee constituted by the Vice Chancellor reviews and evaluates all applications following a defined procedure.

### Who approves the award?

The Vice Chancellor grants approval for the award based on the committee’s recommendations.
<br>

### When do students receive the award?

Students receive their payments after the Vice Chancellor’s approval of their application.
