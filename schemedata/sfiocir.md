## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote cage fish farming. It provides grants per unit to the individuals and groups for sustainable aquaculture practices.

## Detailed Description

The "Installation of Cages in Reservoirs Scheme" was launched by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India, under the umbrella scheme "Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana". It aims to promote sustainable aquaculture practices by supporting cage fish farming in water bodies. Under this scheme, a grant of ₹3,00,000/- per unit to the individuals and groups for setting up cage fish farms, thereby encouraging entrepreneurship, increasing fish production, and generating employment.
<br>

## Benefits

**Grant Amount:**

- ₹3,00,000/- per unit (per cage).

**Grant Proportion:**

- 40% of unit cost for General Category beneficiaries.
- 60% of unit cost for Scheduled Caste/Women beneficiaries.

**Cage Limit for Assistance:**

- Up to 5 cages for individual farmers.
- Up to 20 cages for registered cooperative societies/self-help groups/joint liability groups (with at least 10 members).

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be an individual farmer, cooperative society, self-help group, or joint liability group. 
1. The cooperative society, self-help group, or joint liability group having a minimum of 10 members. 
1. The applicant must possess a Family Identity Card (Parivar Pehchan Patra).
1. The applicant must obtain prior permission from the Government of Haryana and competent authorities.
1. The cage location must have year-round water availability and a minimum of 8 meters depth.
1. The applicant should take government permission for rearing exotic fish species.

## Documents Required

**General Document:**

- Family Identity Card (Parivar Pehchan Patra).
- Agreement 1 (agreement deed between the beneficiary and the fisheries Department).
- Date of Birth Certificate (Voting Card/Birth Certificate/Aadhar Card/Matriculation Certificate).
- Caste Certificate (from a first-class Magistrate (Tehsildar)).
- Training Certificate.
- Land Record (Land record from Tehsil (own land- Jamabandi, Nakal, Akshizra, Farad) or registered lease deed of minimum 7 years.
- Bills/Receipts/Vouchers.
- Photographs of cages with beneficiary.
- Bank Account Detail.
- Pan Card.

**Detailed Project Report (DPR)/Self-Contained Proposal (SCP) with:**

- Project justification.
- Capital and recurring cost details.
- Source of the beneficiary's contribution.
- Bank consent for loan (if applicable).
- Employment potential and project timeline.
- An undertaking that no other government assistance has been availed for the project.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

An individual farmer, cooperative societies, self-help groups, or joint liability groups with a Family Identity Card and a valid DPR for cage farming.

### How much financial assistance is available?

₹3,00,000/- per cage unit; 40% subsidy for General, 60% for SC/Women.
<br>

### What is the maximum number of cages one can get support for?

5 for individuals, 20 for registered groups/cooperatives.

### Do I need permission from the government to set up cages?

Yes, prior approval from the state government is mandatory.

### Can I apply if I’ve received similar assistance from another scheme?

No, the project must not have received assistance from any other government scheme.

### Is there any special provision for women?

Yes, women beneficiaries get 60% subsidy support.

### What is the application fee charge by the government?

No application fee is charged by the government.

### Where do I apply for this scheme?

Submit the application at the local District Fisheries Office or Antyodaya SARAL portal.

### Is the scheme applicable for exotic fish farming?

Yes, but prior permission from the government is mandatory.

### What documents are required for the application?

Family Identity Card, DPR, permission letters, and training certificate.
