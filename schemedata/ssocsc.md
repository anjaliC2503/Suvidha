## Brief Description

Launched in 1966-67, the "State Scholarship of Class 1st to 5th" scheme is a welfare initiative by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides a scholarship amount of ₹200/- to Scheduled Caste students, which is disbursed until 31st October every year.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 1966-67, the "State Scholarship for Class 1st to 5th" scheme is a welfare initiative by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to encourage Scheduled Caste students in the field of education and provide financial assistance. A regular student of a recognized institution receives a scholarship amount of ₹200 per year, which is disbursed until 31st October for Scheduled Caste students.

<br>

<br>

## Benefits

Scholarship amount of ₹200/- per year.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a native of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.
1. The student should be enrolled in Class 1 to 5 in a recognized educational institution.
1. The student should be a regular student of the institution.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the Departmental District Officer, and request the hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the  concerned authority.
OR
**Step 2**: In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the authority.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).
<br>

**NOTE:** Ensure that the application is submitted within the prescribed period, if any.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "State Scholarship for Class 1st to 5th" scheme?

It is a welfare scheme provided by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, to support SC students in primary education.

### Who is eligible for this scholarship?

Students belonging to the Scheduled Caste category studying in Class 1st to 5th in a recognized educational institution.

### What is the scholarship amount?

The scholarship amount is [?]200 per year.

### What is the last date to apply for this scholarship?

The application must be submitted by 31st October every year.

### Can a student from a non-government school apply?

Yes, students from recognized non-government schools can apply, provided they meet other eligibility criteria.

### What documents are required to apply?

Documents such as caste certificate, income certificate, and proof of school enrollment are typically required.

### Do students need to apply every year?

Yes, students need to apply annually by the deadline to receive the scholarship for that year.

### Is there an income limit to apply for this scholarship?

Yes, the family income should be within the prescribed limit as per government norms (if applicable).

### Is this scholarship for all SC students in Madhya Pradesh?

Yes, it is available for all SC students in recognized institutions within the state.

### Do students from other states of India qualify for this scholarship?

No, only students who are residents of Madhya Pradesh and belong to the Scheduled Caste category are eligible.
