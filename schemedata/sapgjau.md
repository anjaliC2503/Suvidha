## Brief Description

The "Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for Post Graduate Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University" provides monthly scholarships to students pursuing Post Graduate in various faculties at Junagadh Agricultural University.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for Post Graduate Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University&quot; launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide monthly financial assistance to eligible Under Graduate students at Junagadh Agricultural University. The objective is to attract the bright and talented students to seek admission in various faculties of P.G. level, it is necessary to provide financial assistance.

## Benefits

- **Master’s degree students:** ₹2,000/- per month for 2 years.  - **Ph.D. degree students:** ₹3,000/- per month for 3 years.

## Exclusions

- An in-service candidate of the University or a deputee from any other institute/ organization shall not eligible for the scholarship.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be domiciled in Gujarat.
  - Applicant must have obtained a minimum first-class or equivalent grade/OGPA in UG (for Master’s) or Master’s (for Ph.D.).
  - Applicant must be newly admitted (fresh) to the Postgraduate program at JAU.
  - Applicant must have a basic degree in Agriculture or allied sciences.

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Write an application on plain paper addressed to the Director, Junagadh Agricultural University.
**Step 2:** Submit the completed application along with the necessary documents to the Principal of the college.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for Post Graduate Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University"?

The primary objective of this scholarship scheme is to attract academically bright and talented students towards postgraduate education and research in agriculture and allied sciences by providing them with necessary financial assistance, thereby contributing to agricultural development and rural upliftment.
<br>

<br>

### Who can apply for this scholarship and what are the eligibility requirements?

A student who is a permanent resident of Gujarat and has obtained a minimum of first-class marks or an equivalent grade or OGPA in their undergraduate (for Master’s program) or postgraduate (for Ph.D. program) examination from a recognized agricultural university is considered eligible to apply for the scholarship, provided they are newly admitted to the relevant postgraduate course.
<br>

<br>

### What is the monthly financial assistance provided to eligible students under this scholarship?

Under this scholarship scheme, the university offers a financial grant of ₹2,000/- per month to Master’s degree students and ₹3,000/- per month to Ph.D. students, which is disbursed based on satisfactory academic performance and progress.
<br>

<br>

### What is the duration for which the scholarship is awarded to eligible postgraduate students?

The scholarship is awarded for a fixed period of two years for students pursuing a Master’s degree and for three years for students enrolled in Ph.D. programs, and under no circumstances will the duration be extended beyond the prescribed term.
<br>

<br>

### Who can apply for this scholarship and what are the eligibility requirements?

A student who is a permanent resident of Gujarat and has obtained a minimum of first-class marks or an equivalent grade or OGPA in their undergraduate (for Master’s program) or postgraduate (for Ph.D. program) examination from a recognized agricultural university is considered eligible to apply for the scholarship, provided they are newly admitted to the relevant postgraduate course.
<br>

<br>

### Can students from states other than Gujarat apply for the scholarship offered by Junagadh Agricultural University?

No, the scholarship is exclusively meant for students who hold domicile status in Gujarat, and students from other states are not eligible to apply under this scheme regardless of their academic credentials.
<br>

<br>

### Is it permissible for a student to avail this scholarship while also receiving another scholarship or fellowship?

No, the rules of the scheme strictly prohibit the applicant from receiving this scholarship if they are already availing any other financial assistance, fellowship, or scholarship from any government, private, or institutional source.
<br>

<br>

### What are the academic conditions for retaining the scholarship during the study period?

To continue receiving the scholarship each semester, the student must maintain a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 7.0 and must be promoted to the next semester, failing which the scholarship will be suspended until the required academic standard is regained.
<br>

<br>

### What happens if a student fails to pass a subject or receives an unsatisfactory grade during the course?

If a scholarship recipient fails to clear any subject, obtains a grade below the satisfactory level, or withdraws from the academic program midway, the scholarship will be immediately discontinued and cannot be reinstated for the remaining duration.
<br>

<br>

### How is the final selection of scholarship recipients determined by the university?

The final selection of students for the scholarship is carried out by a university-appointed committee comprising the Dean of Post Graduate Studies, the Deans of the respective faculties, the Director of Students' Welfare, and the Registrar, who evaluate the applications strictly on the basis of academic merit following an established procedure.
<br>

<br>

### What criteria are followed in case multiple students have the same CGPA at the qualifying level?

In situations where two or more students have the same CGPA, the committee determines the merit by comparing the grades in descending order, beginning with the most recent semester (8th semester for Master’s and 4th semester for Ph.D.), to ensure a fair and precise ranking of applicants.
<br>

<br>

### Are students required to maintain good behavioral conduct and class attendance to continue receiving the scholarship?

Yes, along with academic performance, students are expected to exhibit exemplary behavior and maintain regular attendance in all their classes, as any disciplinary misconduct or irregularity may lead to immediate cancellation of the scholarship.
<br>

<br>

### Are candidates who are currently employed or deputed by other institutions eligible to apply for the scholarship?

No, candidates who are employed in any capacity, whether as in-service members of the university or as deputees from other organizations or institutions, are not eligible to receive the scholarship, as it is strictly reserved for fresh, full-time students.
<br>

<br>

### Under what circumstances can the scholarship be canceled after it has been awarded?

The scholarship may be canceled at any point during the academic period if the student engages in disciplinary misconduct, fails to maintain the required academic standards, avails another form of financial aid, or voluntarily discontinues their studies before completing the course.
