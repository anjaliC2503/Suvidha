## Brief Description

The scheme aims to honor senior eminent artists through the provision of financial assistance in the form of a monthly fellowship of ₹5,000/- for one year to support their continued contribution to the field of plastic arts.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to recognize and financially assist artists aged 60 years or above who have retired from professional artistic practice and have made distinguished contributions in the field of plastic arts at the national level. The fellowship is intended to support such artists by enabling them to acquire necessary equipment and continue contributing meaningfully to the arts. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy. In this scheme, 10 artists are given a monthly fellowship of ₹5,000/- for one year, and it is extendable for a maximum of two years, depending on the quality and impact of the artist’s continued work.

## Benefits

- **Monthly Fellowship:** ₹5,000/-.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be an artist.
  - The applicant must be 60 years or older.
  - The applicant should be retired from the professional field of plastic arts.
  - The applicant should have attained national-level fame and established a distinct identity through excellence in painting, sculpture, or graphic arts.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should download the prescribed format of the [application form](https://lalitkalaacademy.gujarat.gov.in/writereaddata/Portal/Magazine/Document/1_35_3_Aminent-Application-Form.pdf) for the &quot;Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist&quot; scheme.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, including career details in the field of art, photographs, or color slides of work (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority at the Academy office to whom the application has been submitted.

  - Career Details in the Field of Art.
  - Photographs or Color Slides of Artworks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the “Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist” scheme?

The purpose of the “Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist” scheme is to provide monthly financial assistance to senior artists who have retired from the profession and have made nationally recognized contributions in the field of plastic arts, enabling them to continue their engagement with the arts and support the purchase of necessary equipment.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which department has launched the “Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist” scheme?

The “Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist” scheme has been launched by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department under the Government of Gujarat to encourage and support eminent artists in their post-retirement years.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who implements the “Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist” scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy, which is responsible for receiving applications, evaluating eligibility, and administering the fellowship.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of artists are eligible for the fellowship under this scheme?

The fellowship under the “Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist” scheme is intended for artists who have attained the age of 60 years or above, have retired from professional artistic practice, and have earned national recognition in fields such as painting, sculpture, and graphic arts.
<br>

<br>

### How much financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Under the “Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist” scheme, each selected artist is provided with a monthly fellowship amount of ₹5,000 to support their continued artistic engagement and well-being.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### For how long is the fellowship granted to a selected artist?

The fellowship is granted for a period of one year, starting from the month in which the application is approved and ending with the conclusion of the financial year.
<br>

<br>

### Can the fellowship be extended beyond one year?

Yes, the fellowship under the “Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist” scheme can be extended for an additional year based on a favorable review of the artist’s ongoing work by the Akademi Committee, subject to the availability of funds and approval.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the process to apply for the “Award of Fellowship to Most Eminent Artist” scheme?

To apply, an eligible artist must download the prescribed [application form, ](https://lalitkalaacademy.gujarat.gov.in/writereaddata/Portal/Magazine/Document/1_35_3_Aminent-Application-Form.pdf)complete it with all required personal and professional details, attach the necessary documents, and submit it to the Academy office for evaluation.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Will incomplete or partially filled applications be accepted?

No, incomplete or partially filled application forms or those missing mandatory documents will be rejected and not considered for financial assistance under the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What kind of supporting documents need to be submitted with the application form?

The applicant must submit a detailed record of their professional career in the arts along with visual documentation such as photographs or color slides of their artworks as part of the application for the fellowship.
<br>

<br>

### What is the intended use of the fellowship amount provided to the artists?

The monthly fellowship amount of ₹5,000/- is intended to help senior artists procure essential art-related equipment and continue making valuable contributions to their chosen field of plastic arts.
