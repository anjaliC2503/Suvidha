## Brief Description

The "Training on Artificial Insemination for Self-Employment" by the Animal Husbandry Department, Madhya Pradesh promotes milk production and employment. Trained Gausevaks get an honorarium of ₹4,000 and equipment worth ₹18,000.

## Detailed Description

The "Training on Artificial Insemination for Self-Employment" scheme by the Animal Husbandry Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to increase milk production through employment generation and breed improvement. Under this initiative, trained Gausevaks will receive an honorarium of ₹4,000 along with equipment worth ₹18,000. The program offers three months of free training, including one month of theoretical and two months of practical training, at Government-recognized institutes. Eligible candidates include pre-selected Gausevaks or unemployed youth (10th pass and above 18 years of age). This initiative promotes self-employment and strengthens the dairy sector.

## Benefits

- **Financial Support:** Trained Gausevaks receive an honorarium of ₹4,000 and equipment worth ₹18,000.- **Skill Development:** Provides three months of free training in artificial insemination, including theory and practical sessions.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be an unemployed youth.
1. The applicant should be at least 18 years old.
1. The applicant should have passed the 10th standard.
1. The applicant should have a keen interest in dairy farming and artificial insemination.

## Application Process

### Offline

**For Initial Training:**
The applicant from each Gram Panchayat will be selected on the approval of Janpad Panchayat.
**Applicants with queries or documentation requirements should reach out to:**
The Office of the Deputy Director, Veterinary Services
Government-recognized Artificial Insemination Training Institutes
The Panchayat Offices in their respective areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Training on Artificial Insemination for Self-Employment" scheme?

The scheme offers three months of free training in artificial insemination, aiming to increase milk production and create self-employment opportunities in the dairy sector.

### Who can apply for the training?

The scheme is open to pre-selected Gausevaks and unemployed youth who are 10th pass and 18 years or older.

### What is the duration of the training?

The training lasts for three months, with one month of theoretical training and two months of practical sessions.

### What are the benefits of the training?

Participants will receive an honorarium of [?]4,000 and equipment worth [?]18,000 upon completion of the training.

### Where is the training conducted?

The training is conducted at Artificial Insemination Training Institutes recognized by the Government of India.

### How are the Panchayats selected for the training program?

Panchayats are selected by the Deputy Director of the district as per the set target.

### Is the training free of cost?

Yes, the training is completely free for eligible candidates.

### What qualifications are required to apply for the training?

Applicants should be 10th pass and at least 18 years old.

### What kind of job opportunities are available after the training?

After completing the training, candidates can engage in self-employment by offering artificial insemination services in the dairy sector.

### What is the selection process for the training?

Pre-selected Gausevaks and eligible youth from the targeted Panchayats are selected for the program.

### How can I apply for the training?

Interested candidates can apply through their local Panchayat Office or contact the Deputy Director, Veterinary Services for further details.
