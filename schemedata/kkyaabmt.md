## Brief Description

The scheme “Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)” aims to strengthen agricultural markets by providing financial assistance for the modernization and development of basic facilities to Cooperative Market Committees in tribal areas.

## Detailed Description

the scheme “Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)” by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat aims to enhance the competitiveness of agricultural produce markets affected by globalization and liberalization by enabling infrastructure development, modernization, and improved facilities through financial support to Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs). The scheme seeks to create an environment conducive to direct purchase, contract farming, and private market participation to benefit farmers and stakeholders.
**Objectives of the Scheme:**

  - To strengthen infrastructure facilities in APMCs in tribal areas.
  - To modernize and develop competitive and efficient agricultural markets.
  - To support market committees in coping with the changes brought by private sector participation and e-market integration.

<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance for Modernization:** The scheme provides financial help for the modernization of Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs).  - **Support for Basic Facility Creation:** Assistance is offered for the creation of essential and modern facilities within these market committees.
<br>

## Eligibility

- Co-operative Agricultural Produce Market Committees  (APMC) shall be eligible under this scheme.
  - The applicant APMC must be located in designated tribal areas within Gujarat.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:**The interested applicant should visit the District Registrar's Office or an APMC and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:**Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the District Registrar's Office or an APMC.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the "Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)"?

The primary objective of the "Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)" is to provide financial assistance to Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs) operating in tribal regions of Gujarat in order to strengthen and modernize their market infrastructure, thereby creating a competitive and efficient marketing environment for agricultural produce.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the "Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)"?

Only those Co-operative Agricultural Produce Market Committees that are situated within the notified tribal areas of Gujarat are eligible to apply under the "Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which department is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The "Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)" is implemented and overseen by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department of the Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of benefits are provided under this scheme?

Under the "Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)", eligible APMCs receive financial assistance that can be used to establish new infrastructure or modernize existing facilities such as storage, auction platforms, administrative buildings, and other market-related amenities.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is this scheme applicable to APMCs located outside tribal areas?

No, the "Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)" is specifically targeted towards APMCs located in tribal areas, and therefore, APMCs located outside of these notified regions are not eligible to receive benefits under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### From where can the application form for this scheme be obtained?

The application form or the format for the proposal can be obtained either from the District Registrar Office or from the office of the respective Agricultural Produce Market Committee (APMC).
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where should the application be submitted for availing the scheme benefits?

The completed application along with all required documentation must be submitted to the concerned District Registrar Office, which will review and process the application as per the guidelines of the scheme.
<br>

### Is there any cost associated with obtaining or submitting the application form?

There is no cost associated with either obtaining or submitting the application form, as the application process under the "Kishan Kalpvriksh Yojana (Scheme for Assistance to APMCs to Provide Basic and Modern Facilities) (Tribal)" is entirely free of charge.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any reservation or priority given to certain types of APMCs under the scheme?

While the scheme focuses exclusively on tribal regions, within those regions, no specific reservation or priority has been explicitly mentioned in the guidelines.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is it necessary for the APMC to obtain construction permission before applying for assistance?

Yes, the APMC must secure a permission letter for any proposed construction or development work from the competent authority before applying, as this document is a mandatory attachment for the scheme application.
<br>
