## Brief Description

The scheme's objective is to enrich and promote the writing skills of Tiatr playwrights and to have a healthy and constructive discussion on various aspects of Tiatr script writing amongst the Tiatr writers, participating institutions and artistes. Lunch & refreshments are provided to participants.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Tiatr Script Writing Workshop" is a state-level cultural scheme by the Kala Academy, Department of Art and Culture, Government of Goa. The objective of the scheme is to enrich and promote the writing skills of Tiatr playwrights and to have a healthy and constructive discussion on various aspects of Tiatr script writing amongst the Tiatr writers, participating institutions and artistes. All the Goan tiatr playwrights, artistes and institution representatives, besides experts invited by Kala Academy, are eligible to participate. A hall with infrastructural facilities is made available free of charge. Experts are paid honorarium and travelling expenses. Lunch and refreshments are provided to all participants.

## Benefits

- A hall with infrastructural facilities made available free of charge. 
- Experts are paid honorarium and travelling expenses per Kala Academy's norms / as decided by Kala Academy. 
- Lunch and refreshments for all participants are provided by Kala Academy.

## Eligibility

All the Goan tiatr playwrights, artistes and institution representatives, besides experts invited by Kala Academy, are eligible.

## Application Process

### Offline

- The interested institutions must apply on plain paper/letterhead to the Member Secretary, Kala Academy, providing all the relevant particulars and details. 
- The application must be submitted within the stipulated date declared by Kala Academy every year.

**Authority to Receive Applications:** Member Secretary, Kala Academy.
**Selection Criteria/Procedure:** Experts decided by the Advisory Committee. All participants who have applied in response to the press communique.
**Authority for Redressal of Grievances:** Member Secretary/Chairman, Kala Academy, Goa.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can participate in this workshop aimed at improving script writing skills for tiatr performances?

All Goan tiatr playwrights, artistes, and institution representatives are eligible to participate, along with experts invited by Kala Academy.

### Are there any restrictions on who can attend this workshop as a participant?

Only Goan tiatr playwrights, artistes, institution representatives, and invited experts are eligible to participate; the general public can attend as audience members.

### How can I apply to participate in this script writing workshop for tiatr?

Applications must be submitted on plain paper or institutional letterhead with relevant details before the deadline set by Kala Academy.

### What documents are required when applying to be part of this workshop?

No specific documents are mentioned, but applications must include relevant personal or institutional details and be submitted before the deadline.

### Is there a deadline for submitting applications to participate in the workshop?

Yes, applications must be submitted within the stipulated date declared by Kala Academy for receipt of applications.

### How are participants selected for this workshop focused on tiatr script writing?

Selection is done by experts appointed by the Advisory Committee, considering all applicants who responded to the press announcement.

### Will participants receive any financial assistance or reimbursement for attending the workshop?

No direct financial assistance is provided to participants, but lunch and refreshments are arranged by Kala Academy.

### Are experts conducting or involved in the workshop compensated for their participation?

Yes, experts receive an honorarium and travelling expenses as per Kala Academy’s norms.

### What facilities are provided to participants during the workshop sessions?

A hall with necessary infrastructural facilities is provided free of charge, along with lunch and refreshments.

### Can members of the public attend the workshop as observers or learners?

The general public can attend only as audience members and not as active participants.

### Who is responsible for receiving and processing applications for this workshop?

The Member Secretary of Kala Academy is the designated authority for receiving applications.

### If I face any issues regarding my application or participation, whom should I contact?

Grievances can be addressed to either the Member Secretary or the Chairman of Kala Academy Goa.

### Is there any fee required to participate in this tiatr script writing workshop?

No, participation is free, and infrastructural facilities are provided without cost to eligible applicants.

### Are outstation participants eligible to attend this workshop, or is it limited to locals?

Only Goan tiatr playwrights, artistes, and institution representatives are eligible, suggesting a focus on local participants.
