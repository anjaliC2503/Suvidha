## Brief Description

The scheme aims to enhance the soft skills of students studying in state colleges of Uttarakhand by providing training through Mahindra Pride Classroom and the Nandi Foundation.

## Detailed Description

The “Scheme for Providing Soft Skill Training Courses to Students Studying in State Colleges” scheme is implemented by the Department of Higher Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to enhance the soft skills of students studying in state colleges of Uttarakhand by providing training through Mahindra Pride Classroom and the Nandi Foundation.
<br>

## Benefits

Soft Skill training courses are being organized in various colleges of the state through Mahindra Pride Classroom and Nandi Foundation.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. All students studying in the colleges of Uttarakhand are eligible.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Under the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Government of Uttarakhand and the Nandi Foundation, soft skill courses will be conducted in Satpuli, Kanda, Nainbag, Garud, Lohaghat, Ranikhet, Haldwani, and Raipur. Students from selected government colleges can participate in these courses.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to participate in this scheme?

All students studying in government colleges of Uttarakhand are eligible to participate.
<br>

### In which locations will the soft skill courses be conducted?

The courses will be conducted in Satpuli, Kanda, Nainbag, Garud, Lohaghat, Ranikhet, Haldwani, and Raipur.
<br>

### How were training locations selected?

These locations were identified under the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Government of Uttarakhand and the Nandi Foundation.
<br>

### Are all colleges covered under this scheme?

Selected government colleges from the specified locations will host the training sessions.
<br>

### Who provides the soft skill training under this scheme?

Soft skill training is provided through Mahindra Pride Classroom and the Nandi Foundation.
