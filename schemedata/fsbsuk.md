## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from Classes 1 to 8 studying in all government & government-aided schools for purchasing shoes & bags. An amount of ₹318/- per student for Classes 1 to 5 & ₹462/- per student for Classes 6 to 8 is provided through Direct Benefit Transfer.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Free Shoes and Bags Scheme&quot; is implemented by the Basic Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from Classes 1 to 8 studying in all government and government-aided schools for purchasing shoes and bags.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided for purchasing shoes and bags for boys and girls as follows:

  - ₹318/- per student for Classes 1 to 5
  - ₹462/- per student for Classes 6 to 8

The amount is transferred directly to the students&#39; account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student of Classes 1 to 8 studying in any government or non-government aided school in Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Students will receive the amount after submitting their Aadhaar card and bank account details to the Principal. 
- At the beginning of the academic session, the amount is transferred through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible under this scheme?

Students studying in Classes 1 to 8 in all government and government-aided schools of Uttarakhand are eligible.
<br>

### Are students of non-government aided schools also eligible?

Yes, students of recognized non-government aided schools in Uttarakhand are also eligible.
<br>

### How much financial assistance is given under this scheme?

- ₹318 per student for Classes 1 to 5.
- ₹462 per student for Classes 6 to 8.

<br>

### How is the amount provided to students?

The amount is directly transferred to the bank accounts of eligible students through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
<br>

### What documents are required to receive the benefit?

Students need to provide their Aadhaar card and bank account details to the school principal.
<br>

### When is the amount disbursed?

The amount is paid at the beginning of the academic session through DBT.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable only to boys or also to girls?

The scheme covers both boys and girls equally.
<br>
