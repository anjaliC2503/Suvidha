## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to MSMEs participating in national and international fairs/exhibitions/buyer-seller meets, etc., under the Rajasthan MSME Policy 2024.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs for Market Development&quot; was introduced under the Rajasthan MSME Policy 2024 by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Government of Rajasthan. The scheme aims to promote and financially support the participation of MSMEs in national and international trade fairs, exhibitions, and buyer-seller meets, etc. This scheme will remain in force until 31st March 2029.
***Scheme Implementation:***
This scheme component will be implemented through the concerned DICC under the aegis of Department of Industries &amp; Commerce. Office of the Commissioner, Industries &amp; Commerce will be the nodal agency for the implementation and supervision of the scheme at the state level.

After availing the benefit in a particular category in one financial year, the beneficiary cannot avail it in the same category for the next 2 financial years.

## Eligibility

**All MSMEs existing anywhere in Rajasthan will be required to comply with the following conditions to avail benefits under the scheme:**

   1. The enterprise will be required to have Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) as per MSME Development Act 2006
   1. The selection of MSME units would be done by Department of Industries and Commerce as per display product profile, theme of the fair and its relevance.
   1. Participation in Government/ Government agency/(ies) sponsored/ approved Fairs/Exhibitions as well as Fairs/ Exhibitions approved by Export Promotion Councils (under Ministry of Commerce &amp; Industry, GoI) will be eligible for the claim to be made under the scheme.

Note: Preference shall be given to participants/units who have not availed of the benefits under this scheme.

## Application Process

### Offline

***Application Process:***
**Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the application form (Annexure 5.1) from the official website. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the respective General Manager, District Industries & Commerce Centre (DICC).
**Note:** The application must be submitted within 6 months of attending the fair/exhibition.
***Post Application Process:***
**Step 1:** The GM, DICC will evaluate the application.
**Step 2:** The District Level Task Force Committee (DLTFC) will approve and sanction the incentive.
**Step 3:** Upon approval, an approval letter will be issued.
**Step 4:** The assistance amount will be disbursed to the enterprise’s registered bank account.
<br>

### Online - via CSC

> **Mode of Online Application: e-Mitra**


- The applicant can submit the online application by visiting the nearest [e-Mitra](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in/emitra/home) center or by using their own e-Mitra account.

- The application is free of cost.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for the scheme?

MSMEs registered in Rajasthan with a valid Udyam Registration Certificate.

### Can I apply if I have availed benefits previously?

Yes, but not in the same category for the next 2 financial years.

### What is the deadline to apply?

Within 6 months of attending the trade fair/exhibition.

### What events are eligible?

Events approved by the Government, its agencies, or Export Promotion Councils.
<br>

### Who selects the participating MSMEs?

The Department of Industries & Commerce based on relevance and display profile.

### Who approves the application?

The District Level Task Force Committee (DLTFC).

### How will the assistance be disbursed?

Directly into the enterprise’s registered bank account.

### Is there a nodal agency for the scheme?

Yes, the Office of the Commissioner, Industries & Commerce.

### How do I apply?

Submit the application form and documents to the concerned GM, DICC.
<br>

<br>

### How many events can I attend under the scheme in one year?

Up to 3 in Rajasthan, 2 in other states, and 1 abroad.
