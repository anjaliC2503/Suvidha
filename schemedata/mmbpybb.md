## Brief Description

The "Mukhya Mantri Balika Poshak Yojna" is a government initiative launched to encourage the enrollment and retention of girl students in schools by providing them with school uniforms.

## Detailed Description

Introduced in 2006-07, the &quot;Mukhya Mantri Balika Poshak Yojna&quot; aims to promote education among girl children by addressing barriers to school attendance, such as the lack of proper clothing. Under the scheme, girl students enrolled in Classes VI to VII in government schools receive a pair of school uniforms. This initiative seeks to improve enrollment, retention, and educational outcomes for girls.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- A pair of school uniforms to girls in Classes VI to VII.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Bihar.
  - The applicant must be a girl student.
  - She should be enrolled in Classes VI or VII.
  - Enrollment must be in a government school within the state.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Mukhya Mantri Balika Poshak Yojna"?

It is a government scheme providing school uniforms to girl students in Classes VI-VII.

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Girl students enrolled in government schools in Classes VI-VII are eligible.

### How many uniforms are provided under the scheme?

Each eligible student receives one pair of school uniforms.

### When was the scheme launched?

The scheme was introduced in the academic year 2006-07.

### How to apply for the scheme?

Applications can be submitted through the concerned school.

### Is there any income limit for eligibility?

The scheme does not mention any income-based criteria.

### Can students of higher classes apply for this scheme?

No, it is limited to Classes VI and VII.

Uniforms are distributed through schools.
