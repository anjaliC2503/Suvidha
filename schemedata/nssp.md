## Brief Description

The key objective of the NIDHI- Seed Support Program (NIDHI-SSP) is to ensure availability of the seed support through incubators for providing timely support to potential startups, thereby enabling them to take their ventures to the next level and facilitate their success in the marketplace.

## Detailed Description

The NIDHI Seed Support Program (NIDHI-SSP) is implemented by the Department of Science and Technology under the Ministry of Science and Technology. The Scheme is designed to provide timely financial assistance to the incubators to support potential startups with promising ideas, innovations and technologies. The key objective of the NIDHI- Seed Support Program (NIDHI-SSP) is to ensure availability of the seed support through incubators for providing timely support to potential startups, thereby enabling them to take their ventures to the next level and facilitate their success in the marketplace. The scheme also enables the incubators to widen their pipeline of startups and also share the success of their startups which would also result in ensuring their long term operational sustainability. This seed support is intended to act as a bridge between the development and commercialization of innovative technologies, products, and services, and to facilitate the success of incubatee startups in a relatively hassle-free manner.
<br>

## Benefits

**For Incubators/TBIs:**
The maximum financial support that will be made available to an incubator is INR 1000 lakh, which will be released in phases according to the needs, capacity, and capabilities of the incubator, following a proper process.
**For Startups/Entrepreneurs:**
The maximum support that will be given to a deserving startup is up to INR 100 lakh. This support will be provided to help startups get off the ground and grow, increasing their chances of success in the marketplace.
<br>

## Eligibility

> The scheme is open to entrepreneurs, startups, students, researchers, and innovators with innovative ideas.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

Step 1: The applicant should visit the DST [e-PMS Portal.](https://onlinedst.gov.in/Login.aspx)
Step 2: Click on "New PI Registration" on the homepage.
Step 3:  Create a unique Login ID (minimum 5 characters, using only letters and numbers).
Step 4:  Enter the required personal details, including name, date of birth, gender, department, designation, and identity proof.
Step 5:  Select or add your Institute/Organization/Startup and provide its address.
Step 6:  Enter your contact details, including address, mobile number, and registered email ID.
Step 7:  Enter the captcha and click Submit to complete the registration.
Step 8: A confirmation email containing your Login ID and a Generate Password link will be sent to your registered email address. Click the link to create your password.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the NIDHI Seed Support Program (NIDHI-SSP)?

It is a scheme that provides seed funding to innovative startups through eligible incubators to help them develop and commercialize their products and technologies.
<br>

### Who implements the NIDHI Seed Support Program?

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Science and Technology under the Ministry of Science and Technology.
<br>

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The objective is to provide timely seed support to innovative startups through incubators and help them successfully enter the market.
<br>

### How can startups apply under the scheme?

Startups must apply through a DST-supported Startup Incubator or Technology Business Incubator (TBI) participating in the NIDHI-SSP.
<br>

### Can an individual innovator apply directly to the Department of Science and Technology?

No. Applications are submitted through eligible incubators and not directly to the Department.
<br>

### What is the maximum financial support available to a startup?

A deserving startup can receive financial support of up to ₹100 lakh, subject to the incubator's evaluation and approval.
<br>

### What can the seed support be used for?

The funding may be used for product development, technology validation, scaling up, market entry, mentoring, consultancy, intellectual property (IPR), and other startup-related activities.
<br>

### Is incubation mandatory to receive support?

Yes. The startup must be supported through an eligible DST-recognized incubator or TBI.
<br>

### How often do incubators invite applications?

Participating incubators announce applications periodically, and applicants should regularly check their websites for new calls.
<br>

### Can students and researchers apply for the scheme?

Yes. Students and researchers with innovative ideas or technologies are eligible to apply through participating incubators.
<br>
