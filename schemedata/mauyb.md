## Brief Description

This is a flagship scheme of the Bihar government aimed at promoting entrepreneurship and self-employment among the youth by providing financial assistance of up to ₹10,00,000/- for setting up businesses.

## Detailed Description

The “Mukhyamantri Alpasankhyak Udyami Yojana” is a component of the umbrella scheme “Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana”, launched by the Department of Industries, Bihar. The main objective of the scheme is to promote entrepreneurship and self-employment among individuals from all categories in the state.
Under this component, financial assistance of up to ₹10,00,000/- is provided to applicants from Minority communities for establishing and operating business ventures, thereby fostering economic growth and empowerment in the state.
&lt;br&gt;

## Benefits

- **Substantial Financial Support:** Receive up to ₹10,00,000/- for setting up a business (50% of the total financial assistance (up to ₹5,00,000/-) is provided as a non-repayable grant. The remaining 50% (up to ₹5,00,000/-) is offered as an interest-free loan, with a nominal 1% interest under specific sub-schemes.)  - **Skill Development:** Mandatory training sessions are provided to enhance entrepreneurial skills.

## Exclusions

- Applicants under this scheme are not permitted to apply for the following schemes:



      1. Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Yojana.
      1. Mukhyamantri Anusoochit Jati / Anusochit Janajaati Udyami Yojana.
      1. Mukhyamantri Ati Pichhada Varg Udyami Yojana.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Bihar.
   1. The applicant must belong to the Minority community.
   1. The applicant should have passed at least 10+2, Intermediate, ITI, Polytechnic Diploma, or equivalent.
   1. The applicant must be aged between 18 and 50 years.
   1. The business unit must be a proprietorship or partnership firm.

> **Note:**


  - Women belonging to the minority community have the option to apply for either of the following schemes, based on their preference:


   1. Mukhyamantri Mahila Udyami Yojana
   1. Mukhyamantri Alpasankhyak Udyami Yojana

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official [website ](https://udyamiuser.bihar.gov.in/login)and click on "[Registration/Log in](https://udyami.bihar.gov.in/mmuy)". Select "Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana" as "MMUY".
**Step 2:** A new page will open where you need to enter your Aadhaar number and create a desired password.
**Step 3:** Click on the "Login" option after entering the details.
**Step 4:**A registration form will appear. Fill in the required details carefully.
**Step 5:** Once completed, click on the "Submit" button to finalize your registration.
> **Application Process:**

**Step 1:** After successful registration, [log in](https://udyamiuser.bihar.gov.in/login) to the portal using your Aadhaar number and password.
**Step 2:** The application form will appear on your dashboard. Fill in the form accurately with all the necessary details.
**Step 3:** Upload all the required documents as specified in the instructions.
**Step 4:** Review the application to ensure all information is correct.
**Step 5:** Click on the "Submit" button to complete the application process.
**Step 6:** Download and print the acknowledgment receipt for your records. Keep it safe for future correspondence.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the “Mukhyamantri Alpasankhyak Udyami Yojana”?

The "Mukhyamantri Alpasankhyak Udyami Yojana" is a component of the "Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana" aimed at promoting entrepreneurship and self-employment among individuals from Minority communities in Bihar.

### Who can apply for the scheme?

Applicants must be permanent residents of Bihar, belong to Minority communities, be aged between 18 and 50 years, and meet the educational qualifications.

### What financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

Financial assistance of up to [?]10,00,000/- is provided, with 50% as a non-repayable grant and the remaining 50% as an interest-free loan (1% interest under certain sub-schemes).

### What is the grant component of the financial assistance?

50% of the total financial assistance, up to [?]5,00,000/-, is provided as a non-repayable grant.

### What is the loan component of the financial assistance?

50% of the total financial assistance, up to [?]5,00,000/-, is provided as an interest-free loan with a nominal 1% interest in specific cases.

### Is training mandatory under the scheme?

Yes, training is mandatory to enhance entrepreneurial skills and prepare applicants for successful business operations.

### What types of businesses are eligible under the scheme?

The scheme supports proprietorship or partnership firms.

### What are the educational qualifications required to apply?

Applicants must have passed at least 10+2, Intermediate, ITI, Polytechnic Diploma, or an equivalent qualification.

### Is there an age limit for applicants?

Yes, applicants must be between 18 and 50 years old to qualify for the scheme.

### How does the scheme promote entrepreneurship?

The scheme provides financial support, training, and resources to help individuals from Minority communities start and run successful businesses, contributing to self-employment and economic growth.

### How can applicants use the financial assistance?

The funds can be used for establishing and operating a business, including site preparation, purchasing equipment, and other approved expenses.

### Can individuals from other states apply for this scheme?

No, only permanent residents of Bihar are eligible to apply.
