## Brief Description

"Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra" scheme aims to develop all Jannayak Karpoori Thakur Hostels, other backward class welfare hostels, and OBC girls’ hostels across the state.

## Detailed Description

The "Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra" scheme, under the BC & EBC Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, aims to develop all Jannayak Karpoori Thakur Hostels, other backward class welfare hostels, and OBC girls’ hostels across the state into “Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evam Digital Adhyan Kendra.”
**Objectives**
(a) To ensure the availability of competitive and high-quality books and study material.
(b) To ensure quality education and guidance through digital learning.
(c) To organize inspirational guidance sessions through experts and previously successful candidates in competitive examinations.
Through this initiative, students—especially those in remote areas—will have access to high-quality study material and expert guidance in a modern educational environment.

## Benefits

The scheme provides students with access to quality study materials, digital learning, and expert guidance to enhance their academic and competitive exam preparation.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Bihar.
1. The applicant should belong to the Other Backward Class (OBC) or Extremely Backward Class (EBC) category.
1. The applicant should be residing in a Jannayak Karpoori Thakur Hostel or other OBC/EBC welfare hostel in Bihar.

## Application Process

### Offline

The scheme "Jan Nayak Pustakalaya & Digital Learning Centre" by the BC & EBC Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, does not require any formal registration or application process. Only the students enrolled/admitted to the notified institutions or residing in the notified hostels (like JNKT Hostel , OBC Welfare Hostel & OBC Girls +2 school) can visit the library and/or learning centre and benefit from the scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Jan Nayak Pustakalaya evem Digital Adhyan Kendra scheme?

This scheme aims to convert OBC and EBC hostels into modern libraries and digital learning centers to support students in their academic and competitive exam preparation.

### Who implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Backward Class and Extremely Backward Class Welfare Department, Government of Bihar.

### Who can benefit from this scheme?

Students residing in OBC/EBC hostels in Bihar, including girls' hostels, are eligible to use the facilities.

### What are the main objectives of the scheme?

To provide high-quality study materials, promote digital learning, and offer expert guidance sessions for competitive exams.

### What facilities are available under this scheme?

Competitive books, digital learning tools, computers, internet access, and motivational sessions by experts are provided.

### Is there any cost to use the library and digital center?

No, all services provided under this scheme are free of cost for eligible students.
<br>

<br>

### How can students access the digital learning facilities?

Students can use computers and internet-enabled devices set up in the digital center within their hostels.

### What type of books are available in the library?

The library includes competitive exam books (like UPSC, BPSC, SSC), academic textbooks, and general knowledge resources.

### Are expert sessions conducted regularly?

Yes, motivational and preparatory sessions by subject experts and successful candidates are organized regularly.

### Can day scholars or non-hostel students avail this facility?

No, the scheme is currently limited to students residing in approved OBC/EBC hostels.

### Can students request specific books or study materials?

Yes, students may submit requests to the librarian or hostel authorities for additional resources.

### What is the long-term goal of this scheme?

To enhance academic and competitive exam readiness among socially and economically disadvantaged students in remote areas.
