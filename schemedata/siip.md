## Detailed Description

The scheme “Study in India Programme” has been launched by the Ministry of Education under the Department of Higher Education, Government of India. The scheme aims to position India as a global education destination by providing international students with a single centralized digital platform, access to quality institutions, and end-to-end admission and visa support for pursuing higher education in India.
> **Scheme Implementation**

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education in coordination with the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Ministry of External Affairs through the Study in India portal.
<br>

## Benefits

**Single Centralized Admission Platform:****** 

- One portal for registration, course search, application submission, visa and FRRO processes.

**Access to Quality Institutions:** 

- Admission to NAAC-accredited, NIRF-ranked, autonomous, and globally ranked institutions.

**Unique Study in India Student ID**

- Mandatory unique ID generated at registration.
- Required for admission and visa issuance.

**Wide Range of Courses**

- Access to 8,000+ courses including Engineering, Management, Science, Humanities, Artificial Intelligence, Medicine, Yoga, Buddhist Studies, and Vocational Courses.
- Ability to apply to multiple institutions simultaneously and receive multiple offer letters.

**Financial Assistance through Institutes**

- Tuition fee waivers ranging from 10% to 100%, subject to institute discretion.
- No direct scholarship is provided by the Study in India Programme.

**Visa and Immigration Support**

- Assistance through coordination with Indian Embassies and High Commissions.

**24×7 Student Support**

- Continuous support through toll-free contact center, email, and social media.

<br>

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a foreign national seeking admission in India.
1. The applicant should have completed the required qualifying education as per course norms.
1. The applicant should apply through the Study in India portal.
1. The applicant should possess a valid passport.
1. The applicant should meet academic and language requirements of the chosen institution.
1. Indian nationals are not eligible under the Study in India Programme.
1. Admissions outside the Study in India portal are not covered.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> To join the Study in India programme, the applicant needs to ‘Register’ on the website and apply to the institutes of your choice and preference. All applicant need to do is follow the ‘6 Steps to Study in India’ which are:

**Register Here:** The applicant must register on the [Study in India portal](https://www.studyinindia.gov.in/Index) for the current academic year by creating a user account.
**Plan Your Budget:** The applicant should assess and plan the estimated expenditure, including tuition fees, accommodation, living expenses, and other related costs.
**Submit Application:** The applicant must select the preferred course and institution and submit the online application through the portal.
**Check Result:** The applicant should check the portal for the allotted course and institution based on merit and preferences.
**Prepare for Departure:** The applicant should complete admission formalities and begin preparations for travel and study in India.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply under the programme?

Foreign nationals seeking admission to Indian higher education institutions are eligible to apply.
<br>

### Is registration mandatory on the portal?

Yes, registration on the Study in India portal is mandatory for admission and visa processing.
<br>

### What is a Study in India Student ID?

It is a unique identification number generated during registration and required for admission and visa issuance.
<br>

### Are scholarships provided under the programme?

No scholarships are provided under the programme; however, institutes may offer fee waivers as per their policies.
<br>

### How many institutions are part of the programme?

More than 600 partner institutions are associated with the Study in India Programme.
<br>

### How many courses are available?

Over 8,000 courses across diverse academic disciplines are available under the programme.
<br>

### Can a student apply to multiple institutions?

Yes, students can apply to multiple institutions simultaneously through the portal.
<br>

### Is the admission process transparent?

Yes, the fully online and system-driven process ensures transparency with no human intervention.
<br>

### Does the programme help with visas?

Yes, the programme provides assistance for visa and immigration processes.
<br>

### Are Indian nationals eligible?

No, Indian nationals are not eligible under the Study in India Programme.
<br>

### Is support available after admission?

Yes, post-admission support and alumni engagement are provided.
<br>

### How are updates communicated to students?

Updates are communicated through the website, email, social media platforms, WhatsApp, and notifications.
<br>

### Where can students get help or guidance?

Students can seek assistance through the 24×7 contact center, official helpdesk email, and social media channels.
