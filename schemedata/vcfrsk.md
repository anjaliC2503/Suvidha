## Brief Description

The scheme “Visit of Contract Farming Farmers to the Research Stations in Karnataka” facilitates visits for contract farming farmers to research stations across Karnataka to gain agricultural knowledge. Financial support is provided for a 3-day visit.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Visit of Contract Farming Farmers to the Research Stations in Karnataka” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to enhance the agricultural practices of contract farming farmers by enabling visits to research stations in Karnataka. A batch of 50 farmers will be sponsored for a 3-day visit. The scheme focuses on increasing exposure to advanced techniques and research findings to improve farming outcomes.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,12,500/- per batch of 50 farmers for 3 days.- **Learning Program:** Exposure to the latest agricultural research and innovations.

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary should be a Farmer.
  - The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office.
**Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). 
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office:
> **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer.

> **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer.

> **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the scheme?

The scheme aims to help farmers to learn about new agricultural techniques through research station visits.

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

All farmers of the Tamil Nadu State, with special provisions for SC/ST and women farmers eligible for the scheme.

### How long is the research station visit?

The visit lasts for 3 days.

### Is there any financial support provided under this scheme?

Yes, [?]1,12,500/- per batch of 50 farmers shall be provided as financial support.

### Are SC/ST farmers given priority?

Yes, 24% of the farmers selected will be from SC/ST communities.

### Is the scheme available for women farmers?

Yes, 20% of the beneficiaries will be women farmers or women groups.

### How is the funding utilized?

Funding is used to cover travel, lodging, and other expenses during the visit.

### What documents are required to apply?

Farmer ID, proof of identity, land ownership or contract farming proof, caste certificate (for SC/ST farmers), and documents for women groups.

### Is there any age limit for the scheme?

No, there is no specific age limit mentioned.

### How many farmers can participate in one batch?

A batch consisting of 50 farmers can participate.
