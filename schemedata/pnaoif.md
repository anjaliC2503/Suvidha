## Brief Description

This scheme has been introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Dept., Gujarat. It aims to support fishermen by providing financial assistance for purchasing essential fishing equipment, life-saving gear, and infrastructure to enhance safety, efficiency, and fish preservation.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Providing Navigational Aids and Other Infrastructure Facilities&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support fishermen by providing financial assistance for purchasing essential fishing equipment, life-saving gear, and infrastructure to enhance safety, efficiency, and fish preservation. It also provides financial aid to families of fishermen captured in Pakistan and subsidies for setting up ice plants, cold storage, and fish processing units.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a native of Gujarat.
  - The applicant must own a registered fishing boat under [Real Craft Software](https://fishcraft.nic.in/web/new/index/public_login_registration.php) (registration is mandatory).  - The applicant must possess a valid fishing license.
  - The applicant must have a bank account in their name.
  - The applicant must have obtained in-principle approval to purchase of the floor plan.

> **Note:** 


  - The assistance for infrastructure (cold storage, fish market) is available to private units, entrepreneurs, and fisheries cooperatives.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme aims to support fishermen by providing financial assistance for purchasing essential fishing equipment, life-saving gear, and infrastructure to enhance safety, efficiency, and fish preservation.

### Who is eligible to apply for the Providing Navigational Aids and Other Infrastructure Facilities scheme?

Any fisherman who is a resident of Gujarat, owns a registered fishing boat under Real Craft software, and holds a valid fishing license is eligible to apply.

### What financial assistance is provided to the families of fishermen captured in Pakistan?

The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹300/- per day per article to the families of fishermen captured in Pakistan.

### Can an applicant avail multiple benefits under this scheme?

Yes, an applicant can avail multiple benefits, but some components have time restrictions, such as once in a lifetime or every 2 to 5 years.

### What is the maximum subsidy provided for purchasing a GPS device for a fishing boat?

The scheme provides a 50% subsidy on the purchase price of a GPS device, with a maximum assistance of ₹20,000/-, whichever is lower.

### Does the scheme provide any support for fish storage and refrigeration?

Yes, the scheme offers subsidies for deep freezers, refrigerator vans, ice plants, and cold storage facilities to help fishermen preserve their fish for a longer period.

### What is the maximum assistance provided for setting up a fish processing plant?

The scheme provides financial assistance of up to ₹500 lakh or 50% of the actual cost, whichever is lower, for setting up fish processing plants.

### What is the subsidy amount for purchasing a sea cage culture unit?

The scheme provides a 75% subsidy on the estimated cost of a sea cage culture unit, up to a maximum of ₹4,68,750/-.

### Can a fisherman apply for assistance if their boat is not registered under Real Craft software?

No, only fishermen whose boats are registered under Real Craft software are eligible for the scheme's benefits.

### What kind of safety equipment is covered under this scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance for life-saving equipment, life buoy rings, and hygienic toilets on fishing boats to enhance safety at sea.

### How can an applicant apply for this scheme?

An applicant can apply online through the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/iKhedutPublicScheme/)portal by completing the registration process and submitting the required documents.
