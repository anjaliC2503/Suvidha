## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from BPL students to support their education. Under this scheme, a monthly stipend is provided to students studying in classes 1 to 12 in Haryana Government schools.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Schemes for Welfare of School Children Belonging to BPL and BC(A) families: Monthly Stipend for BPL Students&quot; was launched by the Government of Haryana during the financial year 2009-10. It aims to provide financial assistance to students from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families to support their education. Under this scheme, a monthly stipend is provided to students from BPL families studying in classes 1 to 12 in Haryana Government schools.

## Benefits

**A.  For Girl Students:**

   1. Class 1 to 5: ₹150/- per month
   1. Class 6 to 8: ₹200/- per month
   1. Class 9 to 12: ₹300/- per month
   1. Class 11 to 12 **(Science Students only)**: ₹400/- per month
**A.  For Boy Students:**

   1. Class 1 to 5: ₹75/- per month
   1. Class 6 to 8: ₹100/- per month
   1. Class 9 to 12: ₹150/- per month
   1. Class 11 to 12 **(Science Students only)**: ₹200/- per month
> **Mode of Disbursement:**

> Direct transfer to students' bank accounts.

> **Frequency of Disbursement:**

> Monthly basis.

> **Expected Time of Disbursement:**

> Funds to be transferred at the beginning of each quarter.

> **Validity:**

> Applicable for the duration of the academic session.

> Attendance criteria: Minimum 50% attendance required for next installment

## Exclusions

1. The applicant who does not maintain 50% attendance will not receive the next instalment.
1. The applicant not studying in a Haryana Government school is ineligible.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.
1. The applicant should be a student From Classes 1 to 12.
1. The applicant should be enrolled in a Haryana Government school.
1. The applicant should maintain at least 50% attendance to continue receiving benefits.
1. The applicant should have an active bank account for stipend transfer.

## Application Process

### Offline

- The applicant should contact their respective school authorities.

> For any queries, applicants can contact the school administration or visit the official website of the Education Department, Government of Haryana.

1. Bank Account Details (For Direct Benefit Transfer)
1. Parent/Guardian Identity Proof (For account verification)
1. Any other documents as required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for Schemes for welfare of School Children belonging to BPL and BC(A) families: Monthly Stipend for BPL Students scholarship?

Students belonging to BPL families who are enrolled in Haryana Government schools and meet the minimum attendance requirement can apply.

### What is the amount of stipend provided under this scheme?

The monthly stipend ranges from [?]75 to [?]400, varying by class level, gender, and stream.

### How is the stipend distributed?

The scholarship amount is transferred directly to the student's bank account through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system.

### How frequently is the scholarship amount disbursed?

The stipend is disbursed on a monthly basis, with funds transferred at the beginning of each quarter.

### What is the minimum attendance requirement to receive the stipend?

Students must maintain at least 50% attendance in school to continue receiving the stipend.

### How can a student apply for this scholarship?

Students should visit their school, collect the application form, fill it out, and submit it along with the required documents to the school administration.

### What happens if a student does not maintain 50% attendance?

If a student's attendance falls below 50%, the next instalment will not be released until attendance improves.

### Can a student regain the scholarship if they improve their attendance?

Yes, if a student regains 50% attendance in subsequent months, their pending and current instalments may be released.

### Is this scholarship applicable to private school students?

No, only students studying in Haryana Government schools are eligible for the scholarship.

### Can students from other states apply for this scholarship?

No, the scheme is only applicable to students enrolled in Haryana Government schools.

### Is there a deadline to apply for the scholarship?

The school administration provides deadlines, and students should apply as early as possible for timely processing.

### Who should be contacted for further queries regarding the scholarship?

Students should reach out to their school administration.
