## Brief Description

The scheme provides capital assistance to fish farmers and entrepreneurs for establishing medium-scale Recirculatory Aquaculture Systems (RAS) and Biofloc systems.

## Detailed Description

The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Establishment of Medium RAS / Biofloc Culture System,  introduced by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide capital assistance to fish farmers and entrepreneurs for establishing medium-scale Recirculatory Aquaculture Systems (RAS) and Biofloc systems. These models promote high-density fish production in controlled environments, significantly reducing land and water usage. It is a centrally funded scheme with a 50:50 cost-sharing pattern between the Central and State Governments.

- For RAS: Minimum 6 tanks of 30 m³ each (10 tons/crop)- For Biofloc: 25 tanks of 4 m diameter and 1 m height

## Benefits

- The project cost is ₹25,00,000/-

> **** **Subsidy Distribution:**


1. 40% subsidy for General Category beneficiaries.
1. 60% subsidy for SC and Women category beneficiaries.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Haryana and possess a valid Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).
1. The applicant must have access to own land or hold a registered lease for a minimum period of 10 years.
1. The applicant must ensure that the land is free from any legal and financial encumbrances.
1. The applicant must have access to a reliable water source such as a canal, tube well, river, or similar.
1. The applicant must provide a valid training certificate in fisheries or aquaculture.
1. The applicant must agree to manage and maintain the system at their own cost after setup.
1. The applicant must submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or a Self-Contained Proposal (SCP).
1. The applicant must sign an agreement with the Department of Fisheries to avail benefits under the scheme.

1. Agreement Deed with the Fisheries Department.
1. Date of Birth Certificate (Aadhaar/Voter ID/Matriculation).
1. Caste Certificate (from Tehsildar, if applicable).
1. Training Certificate (in aquaculture or fisheries).
1. Land Records (own land or registered lease deed for 10 years).
1. Bills/Receipts/Vouchers for purchases.
1. Photographs of Biofloc/RAS unit with beneficiary.
1. Bank account details and PAN card.
1. DPR/SCP as per PMMSY guidelines

## Frequently Asked Questions

### If I belong to the General Category, how much subsidy am I eligible for?

As a General Category applicant, you are eligible for a 40% subsidy on the project cost.

### Being a woman applicant, what subsidy benefit can I get under this scheme?

As a woman applicant, you are eligible for a 60% subsidy on the project cost.

### Can I apply if I don’t have a Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP)?

No, having a valid Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) is mandatory for applying.

### Will my leased land be accepted for this scheme?

Yes, if you have a registered lease deed for at least 10 years, your leased land will be accepted.

### I have a borewell on my land. Can I use it for this project?

Yes, water from borewells, open wells, canals, rivers, springs, etc., is acceptable for the project.

### Will the government take care of the maintenance after construction?

No, you will be responsible for operating, managing, and maintaining the unit after construction at your own cost.
<br>

### Do I need to include anything specific in the infrastructure setup?

Yes, your setup must include all essential requirements, especially water treatment units for RAS/Biofloc.

### Are there any service charges I should be aware of?

Yes, you need to pay ₹10/- to the Atal Seva Kendra and an additional ₹10/- as service charges.

### How long will it take for my application to be processed?

Your application will be processed within 50 days as per the Right to Service (RTS) timeline.

### Do I have to prepare a project report while applying?

Yes, you need to submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or a Self-Contained Proposal (SCP) as per the guidelines.
