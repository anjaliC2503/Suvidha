## Brief Description

The scheme "District Level Sports School" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Dept., Government of Gujarat, aims to identify and nurture sporting talent through scientifically designed training, education, and sports facilities in residential and non-residential schools across the state.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 2013-14, the scheme &quot;District Level Sports School&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide intensive sports training and quality education to young athletes by establishing district-level sports schools under a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model. The scheme seeks to identify sporting talent from events such as Khel Mahakumbh and train them using modern infrastructure, scientific methods, expert coaching, and proper nutrition, while ensuring access to formal school education. The initiative began with 9 schools (6 residential and 3 non-residential), and expanded to 24 schools by 2017-18, catering to over 2,500 students in 21 sports disciplines. Selected athletes receive benefits such as accommodation, food, school uniforms, books, sports kits, and equipment. An average of ₹2.25 lakh is spent per player annually, including ₹1 lakh Mediclaim security. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat (SAG).
<br>

## Benefits

> **Monetary Support & Facilities:**


- Players are provided with free education, stationery, books, accommodation, meals, school uniforms, and modern sports equipment and kits.

> **Medical Insurance:**


- A Mediclaim security cover of up to ₹1,00,000/- is provided to each selected athlete.

> **Expert Coaching & Training:**


- Intensive sports training is imparted in morning and evening sessions by expert coaches. In-school players receive additional training in 5 Physical Training (PT) periods.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be below 14 years of age.
1. The applicant must qualify under one of the following categories:


- **Proven Talent (PT):**



   1. The applicant should have already performed at School Games Federation of India (SGFI), State-level, or National-level sports events.
   1. The applicant must participate in a selection camp for final admission.



- **Young Talent (YT):**



   1. The applicant should have participated and secured first place at Taluka/District/Corporation level in Khel Mahakumbh (Under-9 or Under-11 category).
   1. The applicant must register for Khel Mahakumbh and undergo the selection process.



- **In-School Talent:**



   1. The applicant should be identified within a school already running the DLSS program.


<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

> **For Young Talent:**

**Step 1:** The applicant must register under the [Khel Mahakumbh](https://khelmahakumbh.gujarat.gov.in/login) before reaching 13 years of age.
**Step 2:** Upon qualifying, the names are registered automatically by the authorities for the next stage of selection.
> **For Proven Talent:**

**Step 1:** The applicant must send an email to dlss.sag@gmail.com with basic details like age, academic standard, game, and achievements.
**Step 2:** The authorities will process the application and contact the candidate for further steps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

The "District Level Sports School" scheme is a unique initiative launched by the Government of Gujarat through the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department to identify talented young sportspersons and provide them with comprehensive sports training and academic education under a public-private partnership model.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who implements the scheme in Gujarat?

The implementation of the "District Level Sports School" scheme is carried out by the Sports Authority of Gujarat (SAG), which is responsible for managing the training, education, and overall development of the selected young athletes in designated schools.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### When was the "District Level Sports School" scheme launched?

The "District Level Sports School" scheme was officially launched in the academic year 2013-14, beginning with the selection of nine sports schools to initiate the program across various districts in Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the primary objective of the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

The main objective of the "District Level Sports School" scheme is to identify and nurture young sporting talent from across the state, provide them with modern and scientific training, and simultaneously ensure they receive a quality academic education in reputed schools.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the scheme?

Eligibility for the "District Level Sports School" scheme includes children under 14 years of age who either possess proven talent at national, state, or SGFI events; have demonstrated excellence in Khel Mahakumbh competitions; or have been identified within schools already implementing the DLSS program.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of facilities are provided to students under the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

Students selected under the "District Level Sports School" scheme are provided with a comprehensive set of facilities including free academic education, accommodation, nutritious meals, school uniforms, study materials, sports equipment, and advanced training kits.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How many sports schools are currently functioning under the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

As of the most recent update, a total of 24 schools, including both residential and non-residential institutions, are actively functioning under the "District Level Sports School" scheme across the state of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the financial support given to each student?

An average annual expenditure of ₹2,25,000/- is incurred by the Government of Gujarat per student enrolled in the "District Level Sports School" scheme, covering the costs of training, education, living expenses, and equipment.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is any form of health insurance provided to the students under the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

Yes, under the "District Level Sports School" scheme, each selected player is provided with a Mediclaim health insurance cover of up to ₹1,00,000/- to ensure their medical needs and emergencies are addressed during the course of their training.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What types of sports are covered under the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

The "District Level Sports School" scheme offers structured training in a total of 21 different sports disciplines, ensuring that children with varied interests and talents are given equal opportunity and exposure to excel in their chosen fields.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who provides coaching and training to the students under the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

The training and coaching in the "District Level Sports School" scheme are provided by expert coaches who specialize in various sports and follow a scientific and modern approach to enhance the performance and fitness of the young athletes.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any fee or cost involved for the students to join the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

There is no fee charged to the students for participating in the "District Level Sports School" scheme, as all expenses related to training, schooling, accommodation, and other essential services are fully borne by the Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the process for applying under the Young Talent category in the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

Applicants under the Young Talent category must first register and participate in the Khel Mahakumbh competitions, and if they qualify by securing first place at the Taluka or District level, they are considered for selection into the DLSS program.
<br>

### How are players selected under the Proven Talent category in the "District Level Sports School" scheme?

To be considered under the Proven Talent category in the "District Level Sports School" scheme, an applicant must have performed well in recognized sports events and must send an email to dlss.sag@gmail.com with details such as age, education, sport, and achievements for consideration and further selection.
<br>
