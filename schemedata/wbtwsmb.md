## Brief Description

This scheme provides financial assistance to a beneficiary on successful delivery of a child or miscarriage by such beneficiary or his wife.

## Detailed Description

The “West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Maternity Benefit” by the Labour Department, West Bengal, provides financial assistance to a beneficiary on successful delivery of a child or miscarriage by such beneficiary or his wife. The benefit is limited to two instances per beneficiary to promote maternal health and provide support during critical times.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹6,000/-.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be between 18 and 60 years of age at the time of enrolment.
  - The applicant must be an unorganized worker employed in the operation of commercial vehicles, such as cars, taxis, luxury taxis, light commercial vehicles, vans, auto-rickshaws, tempos, lorries, trucks, or buses.
  - The applicant’s vehicle must be used for commercial purposes and not solely for personal or family use.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Visit the “[eDistrict 2.0](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/portal/home)” portal. Click on Log In/Sign Up.
**Step 2:** In the User Login window, scroll down to the bottom and click on “Register”.
**Step 3:** Fill in all the details carefully, enter a username, and click on “Register”.
**Step 4:** Upon entering the One Time Password received on your mobile, you will be successfully registered.
**Step 5:** Now in the User Login window, enter your Username or email and captcha and click on “Sign In”.
**Step 6:** Enter the received O.T.P and click on “Submit”.
**Step 7:** Upon successful Login, You will be redirected to the “Dashboard”. From the right side menu apply for the concerned scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the “Maternity Benefit” scheme under the “West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme”?

The "Maternity Benefit" Scheme is a government initiative that provides financial assistance of [?]6,000/- to beneficiaries for childbirth or miscarriage to support maternal and child health.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

The scheme is available to pregnant women or their spouses who meet the eligibility criteria.

### How many times can a beneficiary avail of the scheme?

A beneficiary can avail of the financial assistance a maximum of 2 times, either for childbirth or miscarriage.

### Does the scheme provide benefits for miscarriages?

Yes, the scheme provides financial support for miscarriages, subject to the submission of a valid medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

### How can a beneficiary apply for the scheme?

Beneficiaries can apply for the scheme through the online e-District portal.

### Is there an income limit to qualify for the scheme?

No, there is no income limit to qualify for the scheme.

### Can both spouses claim the benefit separately?

No, the financial assistance is granted to the family and can only be claimed by one beneficiary per instance.

### What is the process to obtain a medical certificate?

Beneficiaries must visit a registered medical practitioner who will issue the required certificate confirming childbirth or miscarriage.
