## Brief Description

Under this scheme, subsidies on solar systems for fish/shrimp culture are provided to eligible fish and shrimp farmers. The subsidy is given at 60% of the total project cost, or ₹30,000 per kW, whichever is lower. The maximum subsidy is limited to 30 kW for shrimp farmers and 10 kW for fish farmers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Subsidy on Solar System for Fish/Shrimp Culture&quot; is a component of the Intensive Fisheries Development Programme, implemented by the Fisheries Department, Government of Haryana. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to fish and shrimp farmers for the purchase/installation of solar systems. Under this scheme, subsidies for solar systems are offered to farmers who cultivate fish or shrimp on a minimum area of 2.5 acres. The subsidy benefit is granted only once per beneficiary for a single unit.

## Benefits

1. A subsidy of 60% on the total project cost amounting to ₹50,000 per kilowatt (KW) or a subsidy amount of ₹30,000 per KW, whichever is less, is provided.
1. The maximum limit for this subsidy is 30 KW for shrimp farmers.
1. The maximum limit for this subsidy is 10 KW for fish farmers.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should have a Parivar Pehchaan Patra (PPP).
1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Haryana.
1. The applicant's age should be a minimum of 18 years.
1. The applicant should not be a government or semi-government employee.
1. A minimum of 2.5-acre area is required for fish/shrimp culture. 

> **Note:** The benefit/subsidy will be provided only once to a beneficiary on a single unit.

## Documents Required

1. Agreement Deed between Fish Farmer and Fisheries Department (Agreement 1).
1. Date of Birth Certificate (Birth Certificate, Matriculation Certificate, Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card, Voter Card, or Driving License).
1. Identity Proof (Ration Card, Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Voter Card).
1. Training Certificate (Fisheries Training from any Government Institute).
1. Land Record (from Tehsil, including Own Land-Jamabandi, Nakal Akshizra, Farad, or registered lease deed of minimum 7 years).
1. Goods and Services Tax (GST) Bills/Receipts/Vouchers.
1. Photograph of the Site along with Solar System for power generation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to promote intensive fisheries development by offering financial assistance for the installation of solar power systems for fish and shrimp farming.

### Who are the primary beneficiaries of this scheme?

The scheme primarily benefits fish farmers and shrimp farmers who are engaged in aquaculture activities in Haryana.

### What kind of financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a subsidy on the total project cost for setting up solar systems.

### What is the percentage of subsidy offered on the total project cost?

A subsidy of 60% is offered on the total project cost for the solar system.

### What is the maximum capacity of solar system subsidized for shrimp farmers?

For shrimp farmers, the maximum capacity of the solar system eligible for subsidy is limited to 30 KW.

### What is the maximum capacity of solar system subsidized for fish farmers?

For fish farmers, the maximum capacity of the solar system eligible for subsidy is limited to 10 KW.

### Is it mandatory to have a Parivar Pehchan Patra to apply for this scheme?

Yes, the applicant must possess a Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) to be eligible for the scheme.

### Does the applicant need to be a resident of Haryana?

Yes, the applicant must be a permanent resident of Haryana to qualify for the scheme.

### What is the minimum age requirement to apply for the subsidy?

The applicant's age must be a minimum of 18 years to be considered eligible for the scheme.

### What is the minimum land area required for fish or shrimp culture to avail of the subsidy?

A minimum area of 2.5 acres is required for fish or shrimp culture to meet the eligibility criteria.

### What type of agreement deed is required to apply for the subsidy?

An Agreement Deed between the Fish Farmer and the Fisheries Department, referred to as Agreement 1, is required.

### What documents can be submitted as proof of date of birth?

A Birth Certificate, Matriculation Certificate, Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card, Voter Card, or Driving License can be submitted as proof of date of birth.

### Which documents are accepted as identity proof for the application?

Accepted identity proofs include a Ration Card, Aadhaar Card, Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card, or Voter Card.
