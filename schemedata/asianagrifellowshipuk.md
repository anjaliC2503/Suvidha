## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial support to meritorious students pursuing an M.Sc. in Agriculture across all disciplines by awarding a monthly fellowship of ₹1,200/- for one year.

## Detailed Description

The “Asian Agri-History Foundation Research Fellowship (M.Sc. Agriculture - All Disciplines)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support to meritorious students pursuing an M.Sc. in Agriculture across all disciplines by awarding a monthly fellowship for one year.
<br>

## Benefits

**Amount of Fellowship:** ₹1,200/- per month.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in an M.Sc. Agriculture program (all disciplines) at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The fellowship is awarded for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the students receive their payments.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for the fellowship?

Only students enrolled in an M.Sc. Agriculture program (any discipline) at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar are eligible.
<br>

### Is this fellowship open to students from other universities?

No.
<br>

### What is the duration of the fellowship?

The fellowship is awarded for a duration of one year only.
<br>

### What is the amount of financial assistance provided?

Selected students receive a monthly fellowship of ₹1,200 for one year.
<br>

### How are applications for the fellowship submitted?

Eligible students are required to submit their applications in the prescribed form provided by the university.
<br>

### Who evaluates the applications for the fellowship?

A committee constituted by the Vice Chancellor reviews and evaluates the applications following a defined selection procedure.
<br>

### How do selected students receive their fellowship amount?

After approval by the Vice Chancellor, payments are disbursed to the selected students as per university norms.
<br>
