## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the publication of outstanding works, journals, e-publications, online content, and web-based applications related to sports and games, thereby promoting sports knowledge, research, and awareness among stakeholders and the general public.

## Detailed Description

The scheme-component "Publication of Outstanding Works on Sports Related Subjects" under the "Scheme of Human Resources Development in Sports",  is implemented by the Department of Sports, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India under the Scheme of Human Resources Development in Sports (2022-23 to 2025-26). The scheme aims to promote sports knowledge, research, and awareness through financial assistance for the publication of high-quality sports-related works, journals, e-publications, online content, and web-based applications. Assistance is provided to eligible publishing houses, web companies, reputed organizations, authors, content developers, and app developers whose publications or digital content significantly contribute to the sports sector. The scheme also supports dissemination of sports information, development of online learning resources, and promotion of exchange of ideas among sports stakeholders. Applications are considered through a structured appraisal and approval process conducted by the Department of Sports.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Publication Grant:** Financial assistance up to 50% of the publication cost.  - **Maximum Assistance for Publications:** Up to ₹5,00,000/- per publication.  - **Journal Assistance:** Up to ₹5,00,000/- per journal for a period not exceeding 3 years.  - **E-Publication Support:** Assistance for e-publications and digital sports content.  - **Online Content Development:** Financial support for the development and hosting of sports-related online content.  - **Sports Application Development:** Assistance for the development of web-based applications related to sports.  - **Sports Knowledge Dissemination:** Support for the publication of technical and educational sports literature.  - **Regional Language Promotion:** Assistance for sports literature published in regional languages.
<br>

## Eligibility

> **Eligible Publishing House:**


- The applicant must be one of the following:
- A publishing house that is a member of the Association of Publishers in India.
- A publishing house that is a member of the Federation of Indian Publishers.
- A publishing house that is a member of the Federation of Educational Publishers in India.
- A reputed organization is intending to publish outstanding sports-related works.
- A reputed author is intending to publish outstanding sports-related works.
- A web-based company, online content developer, content hosting agency, or app developer undertaking eligible sports-related sports projects.

> **Eligible Publications:**

The proposed publication/content must be in the area of sports and games and may include:

  - Technical papers on sports.
  - Doctoral dissertations.
  - Research reports or journals.
  - Abstracts of international conferences.
  - Biographies of sportspersons.
  - Other sports-related books.
  - Sports literature in regional languages.
  - E-publications.
  - Online sports content.
  - Web-based applications related to sports and games.

> **Application Requirements**


- The applicant must submit complete information in the prescribed application format.
- The applicant must provide a detailed breakup of publication costs.
- The applicant must obtain the author's agreement if the application is submitted by a publisher.
- The applicant must declare that the publication does not violate Intellectual Property Rights.
- The applicant must declare that the publication has not been published previously.
- The applicant must submit the application within the prescribed timeline.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant will open the [website](https://dbtyas-sports.gov.in/) to register and apply for the Online Schemes of the Department of Sports.
**Step 2:** The applicant will click on the ‘[Registration](https://dbtyas-sports.gov.in/Registration)’ tab to start the one-time registration process. Here, the applicant will fill in the required personal details, including verification of their mobile number through OTP.
**Step 3:** After successful registration, the applicant will get a message that the login credentials have been sent to the registered email ID.
**Step 4:** The applicant will use the login credentials received on the registered email ID to log in to the online portal. Please remember to refresh the captcha before clicking on the Log In button.
**Step 5:** After the first time login, the applicant will be required to update their other basic details. After filling in the details, click on the ‘Update’ button and the profile will be successfully updated.
**Step 6:** The applicant will click on the ‘Schemes’ tab and apply for the desired scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme component?

The scheme component aims to support the publication and dissemination of high-quality sports-related literature, journals, e-publications, and digital content.
<br>

### Who implements this scheme component?

The scheme component is implemented by the Department of Sports, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India.
<br>

### Who can apply for assistance under this scheme?

Eligible publishing houses, reputed organizations, authors, web-based companies, content developers, and app developers can apply.
<br>

### What is the maximum assistance available for a publication?

An eligible publication can receive assistance up to 50% of the publication cost, subject to a maximum of ₹5,00,000/-.
<br>

### What is the maximum assistance available for journals?

A journal can receive assistance up to ₹5,00,000/- per journal for a period not exceeding 3 years.
<br>

### Are e-publications eligible for assistance?

Yes, e-publications related to sports and games are eligible for assistance under the scheme.
<br>

### Are sports-related mobile or web applications eligible for support?

Yes, web-based applications and online sports content development projects may be considered for assistance.
<br>

### Can a publisher apply on behalf of an author?

Yes, a publisher can apply on behalf of an author if there is an agreement between the publisher and the author.
<br>

### What types of publications are covered under the scheme?

Technical papers, doctoral dissertations, research reports, journals, conference abstracts, biographies of sportspersons, sports books, and regional-language sports literature are covered.
<br>

### Can assistance be provided for a publication that has already been published?

No, publications that have already been published are not eligible for assistance.
<br>

### Can the same publication receive assistance more than once?

No, assistance is available only once for the first publication.
<br>

### How are applications submitted?

Applications must be submitted online through the [official portal](https://dbtyas-sports.gov.in/Registration) of the Department of Sports.
<br>

### What happens if an applicant does not submit the Utilization Certificate?

The Department may stop further installments and recover the funds already released if the Utilization Certificate is not submitted or funds are improperly utilized.
<br>

### Within what period must the publication be completed?

The publication must be completed within 1 year from the date of grant approval unless an extension is granted.
<br>

### Can the eligibility criteria be relaxed in special cases?

Yes, the Designated Committee may relax the eligibility criteria based on the merit of a particular case.
<br>
