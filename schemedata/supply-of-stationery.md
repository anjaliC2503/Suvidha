## Brief Description

Supply of School Stationery to all Students for IX to XII studying in Govt Schools

## Detailed Description

Provision of essential stationery items, Notebooks, school bags to students in classes IX to XII of Government schools. This includes  Rubber, Pencil, Pen, Sharpner, Scale, Two Line notebook, square line notebook, etc.,. It ensures that every child, regardless of their socio-economic background, has the basic physical tools required for a productive learning environment. The scheme covers all students from Standard IX to XII.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

1. Reduces financial burden on parents by providing a comprehensive school kit.
2. Provision of free stationery kits includes school bag , Notebooks and a complete stationery set 
3. Promotes regular school attendance by supporting foundational needs
	
<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1.	The applicant should be a student.
2.	The applicant should be studying in class 9th to 12th.
3.	The applicant should be studying in a government school in UT of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.
<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

1. No separate application is required.
1. Eligible students enrolled in government schools are automatically covered under the scheme.
