## Brief Description

The scheme aims to simplify the sale process of medicinal and aromatic products produced by cultivators, enabling them to legally and easily sell their produce through authorized markets or buyers.

## Detailed Description

The “Simplification of Sale Process of Herbal Products” is implemented by the Herbal Research and Development Institute (HRDI), mandal gopeshwar chamoli under the Department of Horticulture, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to simplify the sale process of medicinal and aromatic products produced by cultivators, enabling them to legally and easily sell their produce through authorized markets or buyers.
<br>

## Benefits

This policy has been formulated with the objective of simplifying the sale of medicinal and aromatic products produced through cultivation. Farmers can sell the medicinal and aromatic products grown on their own agricultural land to markets operated by the Forest Department or to any other buyer. This arrangement has simplified the marketing process of medicinal and aromatic plant products produced through cultivation.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
- The applicant must be a cultivator producing medicinal and aromatic plants on measured land.
- The applicant must be legally cultivating medicinal and aromatic plants on the measured land.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

The cultivator must have a registration certificate issued by the Institute. Along with a copy of the registration certificate, the cultivator must apply to the Bhesaj Vikas Ikai, the partner organization authorized by the Institute. After physical verification, the permit is issued.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Cultivators producing medicinal and aromatic plants on measured land and legally cultivating such crops are eligible.
<br>

### What type of crops are covered under this scheme?

The scheme covers medicinal and aromatic plants cultivated for commercial sale.
<br>

### Where can cultivators sell their herbal products?

Cultivators can sell their produce in markets operated by the Forest Department or to any other authorized buyer.
<br>

### What benefit does the scheme provide to cultivators?

It provides a simplified, legal, and transparent sale mechanism for their herbal produce.
<br>

### Is registration mandatory for cultivators to avail the benefit?

Yes. The cultivator must have a registration certificate issued by the Institute.
<br>

### Where does the cultivator submit the application?

The application, along with a photocopy of the registration certificate, must be submitted to Bhesaj Vikas Ikai, the authorized partner organization.
<br>

### When is the sale permit issued?

The sale permit or pass is issued after successful physical verification.
<br>
