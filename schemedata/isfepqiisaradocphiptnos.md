## Brief Description

To motivate the pepper growers to produce good quality pepper for export by promoting installation of pepper threshers for hygienic separation of pepper berries from the spikes.

## Detailed Description

The Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom: Post-Harvest Improvement – Pepper Thresher (Nos.) is implemented by the Spices Board, India, under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. The scheme aims to encourage pepper growers to produce high-quality pepper for export by promoting the installation of pepper threshers for the hygienic and efficient separation of pepper berries from spikes. This helps improve product quality, reduce post-harvest losses, and enhance the export potential of Indian pepper. The scheme is being implemented in accordance with the modalities and guidelines approved by the Competent Authority under the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

## Benefits

- All pepper growers having minimum of 100 yielding vines.
- Assistance for groups/FPOs will be as in QGBG

2. Designated Officials will conduct inspection and recommend the eligible cases.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Pepper Thresher scheme?

The scheme aims to encourage pepper growers to produce high-quality pepper for export by promoting the use of pepper threshers for the hygienic and efficient separation of pepper berries from spikes.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

All pepper growers having a minimum of 100 yielding pepper vines are eligible to apply for assistance under the scheme.
<br>

### Can Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and farmer groups avail assistance?

Yes. Farmer groups and FPOs engaged in pepper cultivation are eligible to avail assistance as per the provisions applicable to group beneficiaries under the scheme guidelines.
<br>

### Why is the use of a pepper thresher encouraged?

A pepper thresher helps separate pepper berries from spikes in a hygienic and efficient manner, reducing manual labour, minimizing contamination, and improving the overall quality of pepper.
<br>

### In which areas is the scheme implemented?

The scheme is available in pepper-growing states, regions, and Union Territories across India.
<br>

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for applying under the scheme?

Yes. Aadhaar is a mandatory document for identification and processing of the application.
<br>

### Can growers cultivating pepper on leased land apply?

Yes. Growers with legal possession of land may apply, provided they submit the required documents proving their legal right to cultivate the land.
<br>

### Is there a minimum requirement for pepper cultivation?

Yes. The applicant must have at least 100 yielding pepper vines to be eligible for assistance.
<br>

### Can assistance be claimed for equipment purchased from any manufacturer?

No. The pepper thresher must be purchased from a valid manufacturer empanelled by the Spices Board, and supporting invoices and payment documents must be submitted.
<br>

### How does a pepper thresher improve pepper quality?

The machine ensures hygienic handling of pepper berries, reduces damage during processing, and helps maintain product quality, making the produce more suitable for export markets.
<br>
