## Brief Description

The scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to encourage women's participation in sports by providing financial assistance for diploma courses in sports education.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote and enhance the participation of women in sports through structured academic training by supporting them financially for pursuing diploma courses in sports. It seeks to enable women to take up professional training to become certified coaches and trainers, thereby boosting employment opportunities and representation in the sports sector. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** Eligible female candidates receive financial support to enroll in recognized diploma courses in sports to become trainers or coaches.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
   1. The applicant should be a female.
   1. The applicant should be a student.
   1. The applicant should be pursuing a diploma course in sports from a recognized university or institution such as the Sports Authority of India.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course”?

The objective of the scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” is to promote greater participation of women in sports by providing them with financial assistance to pursue diploma-level courses that train them as professional coaches and trainers in the field of sports.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course”?

Any female student who is a permanent resident of Gujarat and has taken admission in a recognized diploma-level sports training course at a university or a sports authority-recognized institution is eligible to apply under the scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course”.
<br>

<br>

### Is this scheme applicable only for women residing in Gujarat?

Yes, the scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” is exclusively meant for women who are domiciled in Gujarat and intend to pursue sports training courses within recognized institutions.
<br>

<br>

### What kind of financial support is provided through this scheme?

The scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” provides direct financial assistance to eligible women in order to help them cover the fees and other associated costs of pursuing diploma courses in sports coaching and training.
<br>

<br>

### Are there any income limits for eligibility under this scheme?

There is no income limit prescribed for individual or family under the scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course”, thereby making it accessible to women from all socio-economic backgrounds.
<br>

<br>

### What is the application process for this scheme?

To apply under the scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course”, the applicant must download the application form in the prescribed format, fill in all the required details, attach the necessary documents, and submit the completed form to the designated authority.
<br>

<br>

### Which department is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat under the guidance of the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department of the Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### What are the necessary documents required for applying under this scheme?

The applicant is required to submit several important documents while applying under the scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course”, including Aadhaar Card, Domicile Certificate, Bank Passbook or Cancelled Cheque, Certificate of course enrollment, Fee receipt, Mark Sheet, Income Certificate, Identity Card, Food Bill (if applicable), and Passport-size photographs.
<br>

<br>

### Does the scheme provide any reservation or special preference to specific communities?

The scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” is open to all female applicants irrespective of their social category, and it does not provide any specific reservations or community-based preferences.
<br>

<br>

### How does this scheme benefit women in the long run?

The scheme “Assistance to Women for Trainer and Coaches Training Course” contributes significantly to the long-term empowerment of women by enabling them to acquire professional skills in sports coaching and training, thereby opening up new career opportunities and increasing women’s representation in the sports sector.
