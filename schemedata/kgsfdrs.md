## Brief Description

Under this scheme, girls who pass Class 8 from Kasturba Gandhi Schools, secure at least 50% marks in Class 10, & enroll in Class 11 receive an STDR of ₹2,000/- (5-year term). Those who score 50% in Class 12 and enroll in graduation receive an STDR of ₹4,000/- (3-year term).

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Kasturba Gandhi Special Term Deposit Receipt Scheme&quot; was launched by the Department of Education, Government of Rajasthan. Under this scheme, girls who complete Class 8 from Kasturba Gandhi Residential Girls&#39; Schools and continue their regular studies in government schools are eligible for financial support. Those who secure at least 50% marks in Class 10 and enroll in Class 11 will be awarded a Special Term Deposit Receipt (STDR) of ₹2,000/- with a maturity period of five years.
Similarly, girls who achieve a minimum of 50% marks in Class 12 and enroll in the first year of a graduation course will receive an STDR of ₹4,000/- with a maturity period of three years.

## Benefits

> **Monetary Benefits:**


- A Special Term Deposit Receipt (STDR) of ₹2,000/- for girl students enrolled in Class 11.
- A Special Term Deposit Receipt (STDR) of ₹4,000/- for girl students pursuing a graduation course.

> **Mode of Disbursement:**


- STDRs (Special Term Deposit Receipts) in the name of the student.

> **Validity:**


- An STDR of ₹2,000/- is valid for 5 years.
- An STDR of ₹4,000/- is valid for 3 years.

## Eligibility

> **General Eligibility**


1. The applicant should be a native of Rajasthan state.
1. The applicant should be a girl student.
1. The applicant should have passed the 8th class from a Kasturba Gandhi Residential Girls School in Rajasthan.
> **Eligibility Criteria for Class 10****th** **Students**


1. The applicant should have passed the 10th class with a minimum of 50% marks while studying in a government school. 1. The applicant should be enrolled in the 11th class in a government school. 
> **Eligibility Criteria for Class 12****th** **Students**


1. The applicant should have passed class 12th with at least 50% marks from a government school 1. The applicant should be enrolled in the first year of a recognized undergraduate course.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Application Process:**

**Step 1:** To avail of the benefits of the scheme, the applicant needs to contact their respective school.
**Step 2:** Eligible students are identified by the head of their institution.
**Step 3:** Submit copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required) to the respective school.
**Step 4:** The school will visit the [Shala Darpan, Rajasthan](https://rajshaladarpan.rajasthan.gov.in/SD4/Home/Public2/Default.aspx) Portal.
**Step 5:** Using the school&#39;s login credentials (Login ID and Password), the school will fill and submit the application form. 
> **Post-Application Process:**

**Step 1:** The filled applications are forwarded to the District Education Officer (DEO), Primary/Secondary Education.
**Step 2:** The DEO compiles and forwards all verified applications to the Directorate of Secondary Education, Bikaner, Rajasthan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Only girl students who studied in Kasturba Gandhi schools and later enrolled in government schools.

### What is the benefit under this scheme?

A ₹2,000 STDR after Class 10 and ₹4,000 STDR after Class 12 for eligible students.

### What is an STDR?

STDR stands for Special Term Deposit Receipt, a fixed deposit given in the student’s name.

### Can students from private schools apply?

No, only students from government schools are eligible.

### Is there any income limit for this scheme?

No.

### Do I need to apply online myself?

No, the institution head will apply online on your behalf.

### Where is the application submitted finally?

At the Directorate of Secondary Education, Bikaner via District Education Officer.

### When will I receive the STDR?

After application verification and approval by the authorities.

### Is there any relaxation in marks for SC/ST students?

No.

### Can I use the STDR amount for college fees?

The STDR matures after 3 or 5 years and can be used as needed then.
