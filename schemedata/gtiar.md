## Brief Description

The scheme "Gausevak Training (Initial and Refresher)" aims to empower educated unemployed rural youth with self-employment opportunities and the skills to provide primary veterinary services in remote rural areas. A 100% stipend of ₹6,000 for initial training and ₹500 for refresher training.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Gausevak Training (Initial and Refresher)" by the Animal Husbandry Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to empower educated unemployed rural youth with self-employment opportunities and the skills to provide primary veterinary services in remote rural areas. Under this scheme, the department provides a 100% stipend of ₹6,000 for initial training and ₹500 for refresher training. Additionally, a kit worth ₹1,200 for initial training and study material worth ₹100 for refresher training are also fully funded by the department.

## Benefits

**Initial Training:**
Training cost: ₹6,000.
Stipend: ₹1,200 for six months at ₹1,000 per month.
Total: ₹7,200 per Gausevak.
**Refresher Training:**
Stipend: ₹500.
Study material: ₹100.
Total: ₹600 per Gausevak.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be in the age group of 18 to 35 years.
1. The applicant should be unemployed.
1. The applicant should be from a rural area.
1. The applicant should have passed the 10th standard.
1. The applicant should be a cow-helper.

## Application Process

### Offline

**For Initial Training:**
The applicant from each Gram Panchayat will be selected on the approval of Janpad Panchayat.
**For Refresher Training:**
Gausevaks who have received initial training will be selected on the basis of seniority.
<br>

For queries and documentation requirements, applicants should contact:

- Nearest Veterinary Officer of the concerned district
- Incharge of the veterinary dispensary
- Deputy Director, Veterinary Medicine

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Gausevak Training scheme?

The scheme aims to train educated unemployed rural youth for self-employment and equip them to provide primary veterinary services in remote rural areas.

### Who implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Animal Husbandry Department, Madhya Pradesh.

### What are the eligibility criteria for initial training?

Applicants must be educated, unemployed rural youth who have passed 10th standard, are between 18 to 35 years of age, and are residents of Madhya Pradesh.

### Who is eligible for refresher training?

Gausevaks who have previously completed initial training are eligible for refresher training based on seniority.

### What is the duration of the initial training?

The initial training is conducted over six months.

### What stipend is provided during the training?

For initial training, a stipend of [?]6,000.00 is provided at the rate of [?]1,000.00 per month. For refresher training, a stipend of [?]500.00 is given.

### Are training materials provided under this scheme?

Yes, a kit worth [?]1,200.00 is provided during initial training, and study material worth [?]100.00 is provided during refresher training.

### Is there any cost to the applicant for training or materials?

No, all costs, including stipends, training kits, and study materials, are fully funded by the department.

### How are candidates selected for initial training?

Candidates are selected from each Gram Panchayat with the approval of the District Panchayat.

### How are candidates selected for refresher training?

Candidates are selected on the basis of seniority from those who have completed initial training.
