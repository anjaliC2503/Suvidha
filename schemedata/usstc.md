## Brief Description

The Science & Technology Congress aims to foster scientific talent and encourage emerging scientists and researchers in the state. The congress is organized annually by UCOST and attracts significant participation from young researchers and scientists.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Uttarakhand State Science &amp; Technology Congress” is implemented by the Uttarakhand State Council for Science and Technology (UCOST) under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to foster scientific talent and encourage emerging scientists and researchers in the state. The congress is organized annually by UCOST and attracts significant participation from young researchers and scientists.
<br>

## Benefits

The Uttarakhand State Science and Technology Congress (USSTC) is organized annually by UCOST. The USSTC specifically aims to foster scientific talent and provide encouragement to emerging scientists and researchers in the state. The Science Congress attracts considerable attention from young researchers and scientists and has been a great success.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. Individual researchers from research institutes, startup innovators, farmers, and NGOs from Uttarakhand are eligible to participate in the Science &amp; Technology Congress.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

- Proposals for research paper presentations in various science-based disciplines are invited through advertisements on the Council’s official website, [https://www.ucost.in](https://ucost.uk.gov.in/), and other social media platforms.   - The received proposals are screened, and selected entries are invited for presentation, which are then evaluated by subject experts. Based on the evaluation results, outstanding researchers in different disciplines are awarded with the Young Scientist Award.
