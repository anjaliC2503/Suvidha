## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide an ex gratia assistance of ₹50,000/- per deceased to the next of kin of persons who died due to COVID-19 in Haryana.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Ex Gratia Assistance to Next of Kin of the Deceased by COVID-19&quot; is implemented by the Revenue and Disaster Management Department, Haryana to provide financial assistance of ₹50,000/- per deceased to the next of kin of persons who died due to COVID-19 in the State. Eligible family members can submit their claims through the Antyodaya SARAL Portal or by the prescribed offline modes along with the required documents. The Deputy Commissioner-cum-Chairman of the District Disaster Management Authority verifies the claim before releasing the assistance. The benefit is transferred directly to the beneficiary&#39;s Aadhaar-linked bank account through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Ex Gratia Assistance:** Financial assistance of ₹50,000/- per deceased is provided to the next of kin of the deceased.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a family member (next of kin) of a person who died due to COVID-19.
  - The applicant must submit the prescribed application with the required supporting documents.
  - The deceased must have been diagnosed as COVID-19 positive through:


1. RT-PCR test; or
1. Molecular test; or
1. Rapid Antigen Test (RAT); or
1. Clinical investigation by the treating physician while admitted in a hospital or inpatient facility.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

- The applicant should submit the application:


1. Online through Antyodaya SARAL Portal;
1. By hand; or
1. Through a kiosk.

## Documents Required

- Copy of death certificate/es issued by health department for deceased for all family members who died due to COVID-19.
  - Report diagnosing deceased as COVID19+ve (RT-PCR/Molecular Tests/RAT or through clinically investigation by treating physician while admitted in hospital/in-patient facility).

An eligible beneficiary receives ₹50,000/- per deceased.

RT-PCR, Molecular Test, Rapid Antigen Test (RAT), or a clinical investigation report by the treating physician while the patient was admitted in a hospital or inpatient facility are accepted.
