## Brief Description

The scheme aims to create self-employment for women of nomadic and denotified castes and support women entrepreneurs doing independent business. Through this scheme, microfinance is provided to women beneficiaries to pursue a business/occupation of their choice.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Mahila Samriddhi Yojana" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to create a microfinance scheme for women of target groups and women entrepreneurs doing independent business to create self-employment for women of nomadic and denotified castes. Under this scheme, women beneficiaries will be able to pursue a business/occupation of their choice. Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Castes Development Corporation implement the scheme.
<br>

## Benefits

1. The maximum loan limit will be up to ₹1,25,000/-.
1. The interest rate will be 4% per annum.
1. Under this scheme, 100% loan of the business/occupation amount is provided.
1. The loan amount will consist of 95% National Corporation contribution, 5% State Government contribution and zero beneficiary contribution.

> **Repayment:** The loan amount will have to be repaid in 48 equal monthly instalments, including interest.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a woman from a Nomadic or Denotified Caste.
1. The annual income limit of the family for income eligibility will be up to ₹3,00,000/- with effect from 1/4/2018. At least 50 percent of the total loan amount will be allocated for families having an annual income of up to ₹1,50,000/-.
1. The applicant's age should be at least 21 years and not more than 50 years as on the date of application.
1. The applicant must have experience in a technical and skilled trade/business.
1. The applicant will have to provide suitable collateral to get the loan.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1: Visit the Official Portal**


  - Go to the [Official Website](https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation.  - Scroll down on the homepage, find the "Mahila Samriddhi Yojana" and click on "Apply Now".
  - You will be directed to the application form.


**Step 2: Access and Fill Out the Application Form**


  - Complete all sections under "Applicant information," including full name, father/husband's name, gender, complete address details, contact numbers, Aadhaar No., parents/husband/guardian occupation and income details. Also provide Date of Birth, Age, Caste, and Subspecies. Also, if you are disabled and provide a percentage.
  - If you have taken a past loan, select "Yes" or "No". If you selected "Yes", provide loan details (date, amount, paid, remaining) and your account number.
  - Provide bank account number, bank name, branch name, name as per account, account type (Saving/Current), MICR code, and IFSC code.


**Step 3: Upload Documents**


  - Go to "Upload Photo" in the menu, then upload your photograph and signature (each file less than 15 KB). Click "Save Photo and Signature"
  - Navigate to "Upload Document" in the menu and upload all required files (PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG only).


**Step 4: Review and Final Submission**


  - Carefully review each module. Use the preview option to examine the application before final submission. Fill out the provided "CAPTCHA" and Click on “Submit”.


**Track and Review Status**


  - Applicant will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking.
  - Go to the [Official Website](https://gndcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) Gujarat Nomadic and Denotified Tribes Development Corporation.  - Scroll down and locate the Know App Status. Click on "Know App Status".
  - Select the Scheme name, Enter App No., and Birth Date. Fill out the Captcha and click on Submit.
