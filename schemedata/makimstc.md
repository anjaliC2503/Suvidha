## Brief Description

Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme "Mukhyamantri Asangathit Karmakar Ijjat Monthly Season Ticket Card Yojana" for unorganized worker on 27 December 2017.

## Detailed Description

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Mukhyamantri Asangathit Karmakar Ijjat Monthly Season Ticket Card Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 19 June 2017. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing travel pass for train.

## Benefits

> Train pass for daily travel up to a maximum of 150 kilometers.

## Eligibility

1. Unorganized workers must be registered with the board.
1. If the Unorganized workers already received benefits from any other similar scheme of the state government, they will not be eligible for the benefits of this scheme.

## Application Process

### Online

**Unregistered Applicant :**

1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on "[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)" under "Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board".1. Enter the following details

   1. Select Group Name : "Asangathit Marmakaar Mandal"
   1. Select Service : "Asangathit Shramik Panjikaran"
   1. What do you want to do :  "Aavedan"
   1. Click next.

1. Check the eligibility criteria for registration in Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board and click next. 
1. Fill out the online "Registration form".
1. Submit.

<br>

**Already Registered Applicant :**

1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on "[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)" under "Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board".1. Enter the following details

   1. Select Group Name : "Asangathit Marmakaar Mandal"
   1. Select Service : "Yojana"
   1. What do you want to do :  "Aavedan"
   1. Click next.

1. Select your district name and provide old/new registration number and click next.
1. Select the scheme name. 
1. Fill out the online with required details. 
1. Submit.
