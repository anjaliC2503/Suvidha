## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote skill development and entrepreneurship in food processing by enabling individuals and farmers to process fruits and vegetables, add value to their produce, and enhance income opportunities through community-based facilities.

## Detailed Description

The “Community Food Processing Centre” is initiated by the Department of Horticulture, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to promote skill development and entrepreneurship in food processing by enabling individuals and farmers to process fruits and vegetables, add value to their produce, and enhance income opportunities through community-based facilities.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Training:** The Community Food Processing Centre provides training in fruit and vegetable processing for 10 days in urban areas and 15 days in rural areas.  - **Food Processing:** The Community Food Processing Centre facilitates community-based food processing activities.
<br>

## Eligibility

***Training:***

  - Any individual or farmer from Uttarakhand can receive training at the Community Food Processing Centre. A fee of ₹15/- is charged per trainee for the training.

***Food Processing:***

  - Food products can be prepared at the Community Food Processing Centre by providing the required raw materials. A fee of ₹15/- per kilogram is charged for processing.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The beneficiaries are required to visit the nearest Community Food Processing Centre to avail the benefits. 
- Selection is made on a first-come, first-served basis.
