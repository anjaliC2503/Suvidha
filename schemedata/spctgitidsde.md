## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide youth-friendly counseling services in Government Industrial Training Institutes, delivered by appointed Counselors, to assist trainees suffering from mental, physical, or psychological stress. The benefit is the provision of a safe, confidential space.

## Detailed Description

The "Scheme for Providing Counseling to the Trainees of Government Industrial Training Institutes at Directorate of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship" was launched by the Directorate of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to spot the trainees of Industrial Training Institutes undergoing mental, physical related stress and provide solace and a safe, confidential vent to release their stress, anguish, grievances, guilt, or feeling of unrest/complain of neglect. The scheme provides youth-friendly counseling services delivered by appointed Counselors, along with dedicated supervision and data entry support staff.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Provision of Counseling Services***

- Youth-friendly counseling services are provided to assist distressed students to think more clearly and positively and develop the skills required to find their way forward.
- Counselors discuss and assist trainees with various concerns, including Stress Management, adjusting to the Industrial Training Institute environment, life transitions and changes, difficulties in family relationships, depression, anxiety, anger, physical, sexual, and emotional abuse, thoughts of suicide, trauma, and other feelings that cause discomfort.

##### ***Monetary Remuneration (For Contract Staff)***

- Remuneration for Counselors is ₹28,000/-.
- Remuneration for the Supervisor is ₹35,000/-.
- Remuneration for the Data Entry Operator is ₹20,000/-.

##### ***Conditions***

- The appointment of the Counselor, Supervisor, and Data Entry Operator is on a contract basis for a period of 12 months.
- The renewal of the contract shall be strictly based on the performance of the staff member, which will be assessed by the constituted Committee.
- The Directorate of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship will pay the remuneration from the funds provided by the State Government or by the Government of India through any Centrally Sponsored Schemes.

<br>

## Eligibility

##### ***Eligibility for the Trainee (The Applicant)***

- The applicant must be a trainee studying in Government Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) under the Directorate of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship (DSDE) in the State of Goa.

##### ***Eligibility for the Counselor Profile***

- The applicant must not exceed the age limit of 45 years.
- The applicant must possess a Regular Masters degree in Clinical/Counseling Psychology OR Masters in Social Work (MSW) with specialization in medical and psychiatric.
- OR The applicant must possess a Regular Graduation in Psychology (6 units), Social Work, or Home Science (Human Resources) from a University Grants Commission (UGC) recognized College and a Diploma in Guidance and Counseling from a recognized Institution.
- The applicant must have a minimum of 1 year experience in Counseling.
- The applicant must possess a 15 years residence certificate.
- The applicant must possess knowledge of Konkani.
- The applicant should possess knowledge of Marathi.

##### ***Eligibility for the Supervisor Profile***

- The applicant must not exceed the age limit of 45 years.
- The applicant must possess a Regular Masters Degree in Clinical/Counseling Psychology OR Masters in Social Work (MSW) with specialization in medical and psychiatric.
- The applicant must possess a Diploma in Guidance and Counseling from a recognized institution.
- The applicant must have a minimum of 3 years experience in Counselling.
- The applicant must have experience of conducting training and workshops.
- The applicant must possess a 15 years residence certificate.
- The applicant must possess knowledge of Konkani.
- The applicant should possess knowledge of Marathi.
- The applicant should possess a Master of Philosophy (M. Phil) or Doctor of Philosophy (Ph. D.) in Psychology.

##### ***Eligibility for the Data Entry Operator Profile***

- The applicant must not exceed the age limit of 45 years.
- The applicant must have passed the Higher Secondary School Certificate Examination (HSSCE) from a recognized Institution.
- The applicant must possess a certificate confirming completion of a course in Computer for a minimum period of 3 months from a recognized Institute.
- The applicant must possess a 15 years residence certificate.
- The applicant must possess knowledge of Konkani.
- The applicant should possess knowledge of Marathi.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

****The process described below outlines the selection of contract staff (Counselors, Supervisor, Data Entry Operator) to implement the scheme for trainees.***
##### ***Step 1: Committee Constitution***
The Directorate of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship (DSDE) shall constitute a dedicated Committee for the specific purpose of selecting the Counselors, Supervisor, and Data Entry Operator.
##### ***Step 2: Conducting Selection Interviews***
The Committee constituted by the Department shall conduct walk-in interviews as the official method for selecting the eligible candidates for the positions of Counselors, Supervisor, and Data Entry Operator.
##### ***Step 3: Contract Appointment and Renewal***
Selected candidates will be appointed on a contract basis for a period of 12 months. The Directorate of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship may subsequently renew this contract strictly based upon the staff member's performance, which will be assessed by the aforementioned selection committee.

## Documents Required

##### ***DOCUMENTS REQUIRED BY ALL CONTRACT APPLICANTS***
_These documents are essential for all three roles: COUNSELOR, SUPERVISOR, and DATA ENTRY OPERATOR, based on mandatory eligibility criteria (Age, Residency, and Language)._

- Proof Of Age (To Ensure Applicant Is Under The Age Limit Of 45 Years).
- 15 Years Residence Certificate.
- Proof Of Knowledge Of Konkani.
- Proof Of Knowledge Of Marathi (If Claimed As Desirable).

##### ***DOCUMENTS REQUIRED BY COUNSELOR APPLICANTS***

- Regular Masters Degree Certificate In Clinical/Counseling Psychology.
- OR Masters In Social Work (MSW) With Specialization In Medical And Psychiatric.
- OR Regular Graduation Certificate In Psychology (6 Units), Social Work, Or Home Science (Human Resources) From A University Grants Commission (UGC) Recognized College.
- AND Diploma In Guidance And Counseling From Recognized Institution (If Qualification Is Based On The Regular Graduation Route).
- Documentary Proof Of Minimum 1 Year Experience In Counseling.

##### ***DOCUMENTS REQUIRED BY SUPERVISOR APPLICANTS***

- Regular Masters Degree Certificate In Clinical/Counseling Psychology.
- OR Masters In Social Work (MSW) With Specialization In Medical And Psychiatric.
- Diploma In Guidance And Counseling From Recognized Institution.
- Documentary Proof Of Minimum 3 Years Experience In Counselling.
- Documentary Proof Of Experience Of Conducting Training And Workshops.
- Master Of Philosophy (M. Phil) Or Doctor Of Philosophy (Ph. D.) In Psychology (If Claimed As Desirable).

##### ***DOCUMENTS REQUIRED BY DATA ENTRY OPERATOR APPLICANTS***

- Higher Secondary School Certificate Examination (HSSCE) Passed Certificate From Recognized Institution.
- Certificate Confirming Completed Course In Computer For A Minimum Period Of 3 Months From Recognized Institute.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which specific regional language is considered an essential and required element of knowledge for successful fulfillment of the duties in this role?

The applicant must possess essential knowledge of the Konkani language to fulfill the linguistic requirements necessary for successful engagement with the scheme's beneficiaries.
<br>

### If I hold a Masters degree in Social Work, what precise specialization must have been pursued to meet the essential educational requirement for the Counselor role?

If possessing a Masters in Social Work (MSW), the applicant must have specialized in both medical and psychiatric fields to meet the required educational criteria for the Counselor profile.
<br>

### For the Supervisor role overseeing the professional counseling team, what is the minimum length of professional experience that is considered mandatory?

Applicants for the Supervisor role must possess a minimum of 3 years experience specifically in the field of Counselling to qualify for the position.
<br>

### How long must the computer training course certificate cover to satisfy the technical requirement for the Data Entry Operator role?

The required certificate must confirm that the applicant has completed a computer course for a minimum period of 3 months from a recognized institution.
<br>

### What is the maximum age limit prescribed by the official guidelines for individuals seeking to apply for any of the contract positions under this initiative?

Applicants seeking employment for any of the contract positions, including Counselor, Supervisor, or Data Entry Operator, must not exceed the age limit of 45 years.
<br>

### What advanced academic degree specializing in psychological study is required as an essential qualification for the specific Counselor position?

Applicants for the Counselor position must possess a Regular Masters degree in Clinical Psychology or Counseling Psychology from a recognized university or institution.
<br>

### Regarding my prior work history, must I show specific experience in organizing and executing professional skill development sessions?

Yes, it is mandatory for the applicant to possess verifiable experience of conducting training and workshops relevant to the scope of work described under the scheme.
<br>

### Is it absolutely mandatory for all candidates, regardless of their position, to provide official proof of extended residency in the State?

Yes, every applicant must mandatorily possess and submit a 15 years residence certificate of the State of Goa to satisfy the essential residency requirement.
<br>

### Which specific certification or diploma related to guidance services is required as a mandatory supplement to the Masters degree for the Supervisor role?

The applicant must possess a Diploma in Guidance and Counseling issued by an officially recognized institution as an essential criterion for the Supervisor role.
<br>

### What is the basic mandatory educational certificate required for an applicant applying for the supportive Data Entry Operator position?

The applicant must have successfully passed the Higher Secondary School Certificate Examination (HSSCE) from an officially recognized educational institution.
<br>

### Will my application receive favorable consideration if I have pursued doctoral or higher-level research degrees in the field of psychological studies?

Yes, possessing a Master of Philosophy (M. Phil) or a Doctor of Philosophy (Ph. D.) degree in Psychology is listed as a desirable qualification for the Supervisor position.
<br>

### Is having competency or prior knowledge of the Marathi language considered a mandatory prerequisite for securing an employment contract?

No, while knowledge of the Marathi language is considered a desirable qualification, it is not listed as an essential or mandatory prerequisite criterion for application.
<br>

### How much professional experience is required in the field of guidance or psychological services for me to be deemed eligible for the Counselor role?

The applicant must have a minimum of 1 year experience specifically in the field of Counseling to meet the mandatory professional qualification criteria.
<br>

### Are there alternative academic pathways that can be considered sufficient for meeting the educational requirements if I do not hold a Masters degree in Psychology?

Yes, a Regular Graduation degree in Psychology (6 units), Social Work, or Home Science (Human Resources) combined with a Diploma in Guidance and Counseling is also acceptable.
