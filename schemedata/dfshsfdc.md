## Brief Description

Under this scheme, the Corporation provides loans/benefits to individuals from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families, whose annual family income does not exceed ₹1,80,000/-, in both rural and urban areas for self-employment income generating activities such as dairy farming.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Poultry Farming Scheme&quot; is implemented by the Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation (HSFDC) in collaboration with the bank tie-up schemes. The HSFDC is a Company registered under the Companies Act, 1956 on 02.01.1971. It is a wholly Government-owned Corporation with a 51% share of the State Government and a 49% share of the Government of India.
     Under this scheme, the Corporation provides loans/benefits to individuals from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families, whose annual family income does not exceed ₹1,80,000/-, in both rural and urban areas for self-employment income generating activities such as dairy farming. The scheme provides a subsidy of 50% of the total project cost, with a maximum subsidy amount of ₹10,000/-.

## Benefits

**Project Cost Ceiling:**

  - Up to ₹1,50,000 /-.

**Subsidy:**

  - Subsidy @ 50% of the total project cost (Maximum amount of subsidy is ₹10,000/-)  

**Bank Loan:**

  - Besides subsidy, the composite loan i.e. subsidy, and the balance amount of the bank loan is provided by the banks to the seller of the assets on behalf of the beneficiaries.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Haryana.
   1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste Category.
   1. The applicant should belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
   1. The applicant&#39;s annual family income should not exceed ₹1,80,000/- in both rural and urban areas.
   1. The applicant&#39;s annual family income should not exceed the BPL limit.
   1. The applicant&#39;s name should be included in the BPL Survey List.

Note: The eligibility of the applicant is verified by the field staff before sponsoring the same to the bank for sanction.

## Application Process

### Online

&gt; **Registration Process on Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://hscfdc.org.in/appreg) of Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation and select the scheme under ‘Bank Tie-up Scheme’.
**Step 2:** Click on &amp;quot;[Apply for Loan](http://hsfdc.org.in/register.php)&amp;quot; to begin the registration process.
**Step 3:** Fill in your details, including your name, Aadhaar number, email, mobile number, and captcha code, then click &amp;quot;Register&amp;quot;.
**Step 4:** Enter the OTP sent to your mobile and click &amp;quot;Submit&amp;quot;.
**Step 5:** The application form will open.
**Step 6:** Enter your details and upload the required documents.
**Step 7:** Click &amp;quot;Submit&amp;quot; to complete your application, and you will receive your user ID and password.
&gt; **Login to apply for the scheme:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://hscfdc.org.in/appreg) of the Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation and on the home page, click on ‘Login’ option.
**Step 2:** Enter your user ID/Username, password, and captcha code.
**Step 3:** Click &amp;quot;[Login](http://hsfdc.org.in/login/loneeLogin.php)&amp;quot;.
**Step 4:** Once logged in, the dashboard will open in front of you Click on &amp;quot;Edit Profile&amp;quot;.
**Step 5:** Fill in surety details, upload required documents, and update other details.
**Step 6:** Click &amp;quot;Final Submit&amp;quot;.
**Step 7:** After submitting, you can print out your application for your reference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who implements the Dairy Farming Scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation (HSFDC) in collaboration with bank tie-up schemes.

### What is the maximum project cost under the scheme?

The project cost ceiling is up to ₹1,50,000/-.

### Who is eligible for the loan under this scheme?

Only individuals from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families with an annual family income not exceeding ₹1,80,000 in both rural and urban areas are eligible.

### What is the purpose of the loan?

The loan is provided for dairy farming.

### Is there any subsidy provided under the scheme?

Yes, there is a subsidy of 50% of the total project cost, with a maximum amount of ₹10,000.

### How is the loan disbursed?

The composite loan, including the subsidy and the remaining amount, is provided by the banks to the seller of the assets on behalf of the beneficiaries.

### How is the eligibility of the applicant verified?

The eligibility of the applicant is verified by the field staff before sponsoring the loan to the bank for sanction.

### How can one apply for the Dairy Farming Scheme?

Applicants can visit the official website of the Haryana Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation to apply. [https://hscfdc.org.in/appreg](https://hscfdc.org.in/appreg).
<br>

### What happens after the application is submitted?

After submission, the field staff will verify the eligibility and forward the application to the bank for loan sanction.

### Is the scheme available to non-scheduled caste individuals?

No, the scheme is specifically designed for individuals belonging to the Scheduled Caste category.

### Can individuals from urban areas apply for this scheme?

Yes, individuals from both rural and urban areas can apply, provided they meet the eligibility criteria.
