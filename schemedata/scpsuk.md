## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage and motivate students to pursue higher studies in Sanskrit by rewarding those who achieve top positions in the subject at the high school, intermediate, undergraduate, and postgraduate levels.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Sanskrit Chhatra Pratibha Samman” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Sanskrit Academy, Haridwar, under the Department of Sanskrit Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to encourage and motivate students to pursue higher studies in Sanskrit by rewarding those who achieve top positions in the subject at the high school, intermediate, undergraduate, and postgraduate levels.
<br>

## Benefits

**Awards:**
•	First Place: ₹5,100/-
•	Second Place: ₹4,100/-
•	Third Place: ₹3,100/-
<br>

## Eligibility

Students securing First, Second, or Third position in Sanskrit subject in the following examinations will be eligible:

  - High School Examination (Uttarakhand Vidyalaya Shiksha Parishad)
  - Intermediate Examination (Uttarakhand Vidyalaya Shiksha Parishad)
  - Purva Madhyama Examination (Uttarakhand Sanskrit Shiksha Parishad)
  - Uttar Madhyama Examination (Uttarakhand Sanskrit Shiksha Parishad)
  - Shastri and Acharya Examinations (Uttarakhand Sanskrit University, Haridwar and Central Sanskrit University, Campus Devprayag, etc.)
  - Graduate and Postgraduate classes of higher education institutions (on the basis of marks obtained in Sanskrit subject).

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The Academy obtains information about the highest-scoring students in Sanskrit in the relevant High School and Intermediate Examinations through correspondence with the Secretary of the respective Board. Similarly, the list of students securing First, Second, and Third positions in Sanskrit at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels is obtained through correspondence with the Controller of Examinations/Registrar of the respective University.
  - An award ceremony is organized, during which payment is made through cheque, and the students are honoured with certificates and other recognitions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to receive awards under this scheme?

Students securing the first, second, or third position in Sanskrit subject at high school, intermediate, Purva Madhyama, Uttar Madhyama, Shastri, Shiksha Acharya, undergraduate, and postgraduate levels are eligible.
<br>

### How are students selected for the award?

Students are selected based on their marks in the Sanskrit subject, with the first, second, and third highest scorers being awarded.
<br>

### What is the prize amount for the first, second & third position?

- The first-place winner receives ₹5,100/- and a certificate of honor.
- The second-place winner receives ₹4,100/- and a certificate of honor.
- The third-place winner receives ₹3,100/- and a certificate of honor.

<br>

### How does the Academy obtain information about high school and intermediate toppers?

The Academy collects this information through correspondence with the Secretary of the Uttarakhand Board of School Education.
<br>

### How does the Academy obtain information about university-level toppers?

The Academy collects information about undergraduate and postgraduate Sanskrit toppers through correspondence with the Controller of Examinations or Registrar of the relevant university.
<br>

### Are the awards given only in Sanskrit subject?

Yes, the awards are exclusively for achievements in the Sanskrit subject.
<br>

### How is the award distributed to the winners?

The award is distributed through a cheque, and winners are honored with a certificate at an award ceremony.
<br>
