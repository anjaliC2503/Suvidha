## Brief Description

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Vishesh Yogyajan Palanhar Yojana" was implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan, aims to provide financial assistance for the upbringing and education of children of specially-abled parents. Under this scheme, children receive monthly financial support based on their age to ensure their well-being and education. To be eligible for assistance, the child must attend an Anganwadi/school between the ages of two to five years and study in school after the age of 6 years.

## Benefits

1. Financial assistance of ₹500/- per month for children aged 0-6 years.
1. Financial assistance of ₹1,000/- per month for children aged 6-18 years.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Rajasthan.
1. Only children aged 0 to 18 years of specially-abled parents are eligible for assistance.
1. The child must be between 2 to 5 years of age and enrolled in an Anganwadi or school.
1. Children above the age of 6 must be studying in a recognized educational institution.
1. The family's annual income should not exceed ₹1,20,000/-.

## Application Process

### Online - via CSC

**Step 1:** The applicant should gather copies of the necessary documents needed to apply for the concerned scheme.
**Step 2:** The applicant should visit the nearest E-mitra Center and complete the Biometric Verification Process.
**Step 3:** The applicant should ensure that the E-mitra Center agent completes all mandatory fields in the application form and uploads copies of all required documents.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Parents who are specially-abled and residents of Rajasthan with an annual income below [?]1,20,000 can apply.

### What is the financial assistance provided?

[?]500 per month for children aged 0-6 years and [?]1,000 per month for children aged 6-18 years.

### Is there any educational requirement for children?

Yes, children between 2-5 years must be enrolled in an anganwadi/school, and above 6 years in a formal school.

### Where can I apply for the scheme?

Applications can be submitted at the nearest e-Mitra center.

### What documents are required?

Disability Certificate, Domicile Certificate, Income Certificate, and Educational Enrollment Certificate.

### Is there a deadline to apply?

There is no specific deadline; applications are accepted throughout the year.

### Can a guardian apply on behalf of the child?

Yes, a legal guardian can apply if the parents are unable to do so.

### Will the financial assistance continue after 18 years?

No, the support is provided only up to 18 years of age.

### What if my child drops out of school?

The financial assistance will be discontinued if the child does not remain enrolled in school.

### Is there any reservation for specific categories?

No, the scheme is exclusively for children of specially-abled parents.
