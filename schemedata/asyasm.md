## Brief Description

The scheme-component aims to promote AYUSH interventions for the treatment and rehabilitation of sports injuries and to enhance health and fitness among sports persons.

## Detailed Description

The scheme-component &quot;AYUSH for Sports Medicine&quot; under the &quot;Ayurswasthya Yojana&quot; by the Ministry of Ayush, Government of India, aims to provide AYUSH-based treatment and rehabilitation services to sports persons. AYUSH Sports medicine is a unique concept where, the treatment will be provided on sports injuries and promote the health and fitness of sports persons through AYUSH intervention. Fit India Movement is a nation-wide movement in India to encourage people to remain healthy and fit by including physical activities and sports in their daily lives. 
<br>

## Benefits

> **Financial Assistance:**


  - Maximum grant of ₹1,00,00,000/- for one project for 18 months.
  - The funds will be released in two installments of 60% and 40% of the total amount sanctioned. The second installment will be released after receiving expenditure of at least 75% of the released amount and after acceptance of UC thereof.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The AYUSH teaching Institutions and AYUSH hospitals can apply for the scheme.
  - The Institutions/ Organisations should be having collaboration with only recognized National and International sports bodies such as the Sports Authority of India, All India Football Federation, Indian Olympic Association, The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI), International Cricket Council (ICC), etc.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "AYUSH for Sports Medicine" component under the "Ayurswasthya Yojana"?

The AYUSH for Sports Medicine component is a scheme initiative that supports AYUSH-based interventions for prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation of sports-related injuries and for promoting fitness among sports persons.
<br>

### What are the main objectives of the "AYUSH for Sports Medicine" component?

The objectives of this component are to promote AYUSH interventions for health and fitness of sports persons and to support AYUSH therapeutic interventions for treatment and rehabilitation of sports-related injuries.
<br>

### Who can apply for funding under this component?

AYUSH teaching institutions and AYUSH hospitals with collaboration with recognized national or international sports bodies are eligible to apply for the scheme.
<br>

### What type of collaborations are required for eligibility?

The applicant institution must have collaboration with recognized sports bodies such as Sports Authority of India, All India Football Federation, Indian Olympic Association, BCCI, ICC, or other recognized national or international sports organizations.
<br>

### What is the minimum beneficiary coverage under one project?

Each approved project should benefit a minimum of 200 sports persons.
<br>

### What is the maximum financial assistance provided under this component?

The scheme provides financial assistance up to ₹1.00 crore for one project with a duration of up to 18 months.
<br>

### How is the grant amount released to the implementing institution?

The grant amount is released in two installments of 60% and 40% of the total sanctioned amount.
<br>

### What are the conditions for release of the second installment?

The second installment is released after at least 75% utilization of the first installment and submission and acceptance of the Utilization Certificate.
<br>

### What types of interventions can be supported under this component?

The component supports AYUSH interventions for sports injury treatment, rehabilitation, fitness promotion, and preventive health measures for sports persons.
<br>

### Is the "Fit India" Movement linked to this component?

This component aligns with the "Fit India" Movement and supports national efforts to promote physical activity, sports, and healthy lifestyles through AYUSH interventions.
<br>
