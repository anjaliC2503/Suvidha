## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote biotechnology education and research by providing students, researchers, and farmers with exposure to modern laboratories, advanced research activities, and innovative technologies.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Tour Program of Modern Laboratories” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Council for Biotechnology under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote biotechnology education and research by providing students, researchers, and farmers with exposure to modern laboratories, advanced research activities, and innovative technologies.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this tour program, eligible individuals visit modern biotechnology laboratories to learn about new research works.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand. 
1. The applicant must be a student from a government or private school, college, or university, a researcher, or a member of a farmers’ group or self-help group.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

With the aim of promoting biotechnology education, research, and outreach activities, students from schools, colleges, and universities in the state, as well as researchers and farmers, are taken on tours of the modern laboratories established at the Council and its regional centers. During these visits, they are informed about the research and development work being carried out and are motivated to further biotechnology education and research.
After obtaining approval from the Council&#39;s Director or other competent authority, interested candidates, teams, or groups can avail themselves of this opportunity by visiting the Council headquarters or its regional centers to learn about ongoing activities and access relevant technical information.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can participate in this program?

Students from government or private schools, colleges, and universities, as well as groups of researchers, farmers, or self-formed groups interested in biotechnology, can participate.
<br>

### What activities are included during the tour?

Participants are provided with information about new research work, laboratory demonstrations, and technical guidance on ongoing biotechnology projects.
<br>

### Is prior approval required to participate?

Yes. Interested candidates, teams, or groups must obtain approval from the Council’s Director or other competent authority.
<br>

### How can one apply for the tour program?

Applicants can approach the Council headquarters or its regional centers to submit a request for approval and schedule a visit.
<br>

### Are private schools and colleges eligible?

Yes. Students from both government and private educational institutions are eligible to participate.
<br>
