## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide merit-based scholarship to students of Assam studying in first semester degree and master degree courses in recognized institutions. It benefits academically meritorious students who have obtained at least 60% marks in their last qualifying examination.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Combined Merit Scholarship" by the Higher Education Department, Government of Assam, aims to provide financial assistance to students of Assam studying in first semester degree courses and master's degree courses in any recognized colleges or universities in the state and other states of the country, focusing on students with uniformly good academic records and at least 60% marks in last qualifying examinations. The scheme provides access to poor students of the state and helps them achieve the benefits of higher education through merit-based scholarship support for undergraduate and postgraduate studies. The applicant must be selected and admitted in degree or master's degree courses in any recognized universities or colleges and must be domiciled in Assam. The scheme is implemented by Higher Education Department. The applications for this scheme are accepted online.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Financial Assistance**


- For Degree Courses: ₹300/- per month.
- For Master Degree Courses: ₹500/- per month.
- The scheme provides 200 scholarships for degree students (three years duration) and 100 scholarships for master degree students (two years duration).

**Selection:** Based on merit and budget allocation.
**Coverage:** Students pursuing general degree and master degree courses in Arts, Science, and Commerce disciplines.
> **Mode of Disbursement**

**Mode:** Direct Benefit Transfer system.
**Conditions:** Subject to selection by the selection committee and financial sanction approval.
> **Conditions for Availment**


- The applicant must maintain enrollment in the respective degree or master's degree course throughout the scholarship period.
- Academic performance standards must be maintained as per institutional and scheme requirements.
- Regular attendance and compliance with all institutional rules and regulations.
- Bank account must remain active and updated for Direct Benefit Transfer disbursement.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be pursuing general degree or master's degree courses in Arts, Science, or Commerce discipline in any recognized institution.
- The applicant must be domiciled in Assam (Permanent Resident Certificate document mandatory).
- The applicant must have obtained minimum 60% marks in Higher Secondary examination for those applying for Combined Merit Degree Scholarship scheme.
- The applicant must have obtained minimum 60% marks in General Degree (Three Year Degree Course) examination for those applying for Combined Merit Master Degree Scholarship scheme.
- The applicant must possess relevant qualification from any recognized institution.
- The applicant must have a valid bank account in their name (preferably in State Bank of India).

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** _Online Application Submission_
Visit the official website of the [Directorate of Higher Education, Government of Assam](https://directorateofhighereducation.assam.gov.in/portlets/scholarships), and complete the online application form within the specified application period with all required personal, academic, and bank account details.
**Step 2:** _Document Upload and Verification_
Upload scanned copies of all required documents including Permanent Resident Certificate, mark sheets, institutional certificate, photograph, and signature as per the prescribed format and specifications.
**Step 3:** _Application Form Printout_
After successful online submission, take a printout of the completed application form and ensure all information is accurate and complete before proceeding to the next step.
**Step 4:** _Physical Submission to Institution_
Submit the printed application form along with necessary copies of certificates and testimonials to the Registrar, Principal, or Head of the Institution where currently enrolled for verification and forwarding.
**Step 5:** _Institutional Verification and Forwarding_
The Registrar, Principal, or Head of the Institution will collect, verify, and submit the hard copies of online forms along with all relevant certificates and testimonials under their seal and signature to the Directorate of Higher Education, Assam, Kahilipara, Guwahati - 781019.
**Step 6:** _Selection Process_
The selection committee will evaluate applications strictly on the basis of merit in all cases and selection will be restricted as per budget allocation for the respective categories.
**Step 7:** _Benefit Disbursement_
Selected beneficiaries will receive scholarship amounts through Direct Benefit Transfer system directly to their registered bank accounts on a monthly basis.
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment received for future reference and correspondence with the implementing authorities._

## Documents Required

> **At Application Stage**


- Permanent Resident Certificate from District Collector or Sub Divisional Officer as Proof of Domicile of State of Assam (scanned copy)
- Certificate from the Registrar or Head of the Institution in which the candidate is at present studying (scanned copy)
- Final Mark Sheet of Last Qualifying Examinations (Higher Secondary for degree applicants or Three Year Degree Course for master degree applicants) (scanned copy)
- Recent Photograph (scanned copy)
- Signature of the Applicant (scanned copy)
- Valid Bank Account Details (Bank Name, Account Number, Branch Name, Indian Financial System Code)

> **At Document Submission Stage**


- Hard copies of online application form along with all relevant certificates and testimonials
- All documents to be submitted under seal and signature of Registrar, Principal, or Head of the Institution to Directorate of Higher Education, Assam

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for this financial assistance if I am currently enrolled in a master's degree program in Commerce at a recognized university?

Yes, you can apply if you are pursuing master degree courses in Arts, Science, or Commerce discipline in any recognized institution and meet all other eligibility requirements.
<br>

### What is the minimum percentage of marks required in my three-year degree course to be eligible for the master degree scholarship?

You must have obtained minimum 60% marks in General Degree (Three Year Degree Course) examination to apply for the master degree scholarship.
<br>

### How much monthly financial assistance will I receive if I am selected for the undergraduate degree scholarship program?

Selected degree course students receive ₹300/- per month as financial assistance through this merit-based scholarship program.
<br>

### Is it mandatory for me to have a bank account in a specific bank to receive the scholarship amount?

You must have a valid bank account in your name, preferably in State Bank of India, for receiving scholarship disbursements through Direct Benefit Transfer system.
<br>

### What academic performance standards must I maintain throughout the scholarship period to continue receiving the financial assistance?

You must maintain enrollment in your respective course, uphold academic performance standards as per institutional and scheme requirements, and ensure regular attendance with compliance to all institutional rules.
<br>

### Can students from other states of India apply for this scholarship if they are studying in Assam?

No, you must be domiciled in Assam with a Permanent Resident Certificate from District Collector or Sub Divisional Officer as mandatory proof of domicile.
<br>

### How many total scholarships are available for master degree students and what is the duration of financial assistance?

The scheme provides 100 scholarships for master degree students with financial assistance for two years duration.
<br>

### What is the minimum percentage requirement in Higher Secondary examination for students applying for the degree course scholarship?

You must have obtained minimum 60% marks in Higher Secondary examination to be eligible for the Combined Merit Degree Scholarship.
<br>

### Where should I submit the hard copies of my application form and supporting documents after completing the online application process?

Submit printed application form with necessary certificates to your Registrar, Principal, or Head of Institution, who will forward them under seal and signature to Directorate of Higher Education, Assam, Kahilipara, Guwahati - 781019.
<br>

### How much monthly scholarship amount will I receive if I am pursuing a master's degree course and get selected?

Master degree course students receive ₹500/- per month as financial assistance if selected through the merit-based evaluation process.
<br>

### Can I apply for this scholarship if I am studying Engineering or Medical courses at a recognized university?

No, the scheme is specifically for students pursuing general degree and master degree courses in Arts, Science, or Commerce disciplines only.
<br>

### What type of certificate do I need to obtain from my current educational institution during the application process?

You need a certificate from the Registrar or Head of the Institution in which you are currently studying as part of the required documentation.
<br>

### How many scholarship positions are available for undergraduate degree students and what is the total duration of financial support?

The scheme offers 200 scholarships for degree students with financial assistance provided for three years duration.
<br>

### What documents must I upload in scanned format when applying online for this merit-based financial assistance program?

You must upload scanned copies of Permanent Resident Certificate, institutional certificate, final mark sheet of last qualifying examination, recent photograph, signature, and provide valid bank account details.
