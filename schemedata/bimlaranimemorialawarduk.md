## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and encourage meritorious students pursuing Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (B.Sc. Ag.), Bachelor of Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. & A.H.), Bachelor of Home Science (B.H.Sc.), and Bachelor of Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.) courses.

## Detailed Description

The “Smt. Bimla Rani Memorial Award (B.Sc. Ag., B.V.Sc. &amp; A.H., B.H.Sc. &amp; B.F.Sc.)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and encourage meritorious students pursuing Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (B.Sc. Ag.), Bachelor of Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. &amp; A.H.), Bachelor of Home Science (B.H.Sc.), and Bachelor of Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.) courses.
<br>

## Benefits

**Award Amount:** ₹12,000/- per year. 
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (B.Sc. Ag.), Bachelor of Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. &amp; A.H.), Bachelor of Home Science (B.H.Sc.), or Bachelor of Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.) course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The award amount is granted for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the selected students receive the payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which courses are covered under this scheme?

The award covers the following undergraduate courses:

- Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (B.Sc. Ag.)
- Bachelor of Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. & A.H.)
- Bachelor of Home Science (B.H.Sc.)
- Bachelor of Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.)

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this award?

Students enrolled in any of the above undergraduate programs at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What is the amount of the award?

Each selected student receives a financial award of ₹12,000/- per year under this scheme.
<br>

### For how long is the award granted?

The award is granted for a duration of one year only.
<br>

### How can a student apply for the award?

Students need to submit an application on the prescribed form provided by the university within the announced deadline.
<br>

### Who selects the awardees?

A committee constituted by the Vice Chancellor reviews and evaluates all applications and recommends the most deserving candidates for approval.
<br>

### How is the scholarship amount disbursed?

After approval, the award amount is credited or paid to the selected students as per university norms.
<br>
