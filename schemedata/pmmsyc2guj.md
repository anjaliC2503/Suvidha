## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial support for the construction of 20-tonne capacity ice plant/storage units, ensuring hygienic fish storage and improving the income of beneficiaries.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)” with the component of "Construction of Plant/Storage of Minimum 20 Tonne Capacity", introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to strengthen fish storage and cold chain infrastructure for better preservation and delivery of fish in a healthy condition. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided to beneficiaries for the construction of ice plants/storage units with a minimum capacity of 20 tonnes, ensuring hygienic fish availability and higher returns for sellers. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries of the State Governments, with 60% contribution from the Government of India and 40% contribution from the State Government.
<br>

> **Objective of the scheme**


- Marine production in the Gujarat state is one of the leading in the country. The state lags behind in exports due to non-norms. So cold storage/ice plant modernization is necessary to keep the fish in healthy condition.
- Insulated vehicle, three wheeler with ice box including e-rickshaw, and live fish vending center to deliver the fish in hygienic condition to the consumer, so that the consumer (customer) can get fresh and clean fish from the local area and get good returns to the sellers.
- Around 27,000 small and large boats are operating in the state. To strengthen the safety and security of the fishermen and increase the income of the boat owner, replacement of the existing boats is necessary to increase the sea growth of the existing boats.

<br>

## Benefits

> **Construction of Ice Plant/Storage (minimum 20 tonne capacity):** 


- Unit cost is ₹80,00,000/-. The amount of assistance for the beneficiary of general category will be 40% of the unit cost and for the beneficiary of women and ST/SC category the amount of assistance will be available at the rate of 60% of the unit cost.
- The benefit will be available as per the target (category) allocated by the Government of India.

<br>

> **Remarks:** 60% contribution from Government of India and 40% contribution from State Governments is paid.

> **Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits:** Once in a lifetime.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a native of Gujarat state.
1. The applicant should have a fishing boat registered under Real Craft software.
1. The applicant should hold a fishing license.
1. The applicant should complete the online registration on the [iKhedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/).1. The applicant should obtain in-principle approval before purchase of the floor plan.
1. The applicant should have his/her own bank account.

> **Preference/Reservation/Relaxation:**



  - Women, SC, and ST beneficiaries are eligible for 60% assistance of the unit cost.
  - General category beneficiaries are eligible for 40% assistance of the unit cost.


<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on 'Login' from the top right-hand side, then click on "Beneficiary Registration" and select any one from the 'Farmer' or 'Institutional' or 'Except For the Farmer' as the Beneficiary Type.
**Step 3:** Enter the 'Company Name' and Address if you have selected 'Institutional' or 'Except For the Farmer' as the Beneficiary Type. And for all select the District, Taluka, and Village, choose 'what type of account holder you are?', enter your Account Number and Name, solve the Captcha, and click 'Save and Next'.
**Step 4:** Enter your Mobile Number and E-mail address, click 'Send OTP', enter the received OTP, create and confirm a Password, solve the Captcha, and click 'Submit'.
<br>

> **Login Process:** 

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on 'Login' from the top right-hand side of the homepage. On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button, and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on the "Apply New" button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the "Update Application" button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a printout of the confirmed application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum capacity required for setting up a storage unit under this benefit?

The minimum required capacity for the storage unit is 20 tonnes.
<br>

### How much financial assistance can a general category applicant receive?

A general category applicant can receive 40% of the unit cost as assistance.
<br>

### What percentage of financial support is available for women applicants?

Women applicants are eligible for 60% of the unit cost as assistance.
<br>

### Are SC and ST category beneficiaries eligible for higher support?

Yes, SC and ST beneficiaries are eligible for 60% of the unit cost as assistance.
<br>

### Can this benefit be availed multiple times by the same person?

No, this benefit can only be availed once in a lifetime.
<br>

### Who provides the contribution for this benefit?

The contribution is shared, with 60% by the Government of India and 40% by the State Government.
<br>

### Is the assistance given to every applicant or based on certain targets?

The assistance is given as per the target and category allocation decided by the Government of India.
<br>

### What documents are required for applying to this component?

Documents include Aadhaar card, fishing license, quotation, caste certificate (if applicable), bank passbook or cancelled cheque, ration card, and passport-size photo.
<br>

### Can an applicant without a fishing license apply for this benefit?

No, having a valid fishing license is compulsory to apply.
<br>

### Where should the application for this benefit be submitted?

Applications must be submitted online through the iKhedut portal at https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in.
<br>

### Is it necessary to have a personal bank account for receiving the assistance?

Yes, the applicant must have his or her own bank account to receive the assistance.
<br>

### What happens if the applicant is a woman from SC or ST category?

She is eligible for 60% of the unit cost as financial assistance.
<br>

### Does the assistance cover the entire cost of constructing the plant or storage?

No, only 40% or 60% of the unit cost is covered, depending on the applicant’s category.
<br>
