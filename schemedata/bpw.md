## Brief Description

The objective of the scheme is to provide knowledge of appropriate and traditional Bhajan Singing and Pakhawaj playing to Bhajan performers through experts from the respective field. Applications are invited by announcement in local print & electronic media. Certificates are awarded to participants.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Bhajan Pakhawaj Workshops" is a state-level cultural scheme by the Kala Academy, Department of Art and Culture, Government of Goa. The objective of the scheme is to provide knowledge of appropriate and traditional Bhajan Singing and Pakhawaj playing to Bhajan performers through experts from the respective field. Applications are invited by announcement in local print and electronic media. Certificates are awarded to the participants. A Suitable Honorarium is paid to conductors of workshops.

## Benefits

- Knowledge of appropriate and traditional Bhajan Singing and Pakhawaj playing is provided to the Bhajan performers through experts from the respective field. 
- Certificates are awarded to the participants. 
- A suitable Honorarium is paid to conductors of workshops. 

_*The venue of the workshop is decided by Kala Academy._

## Eligibility

**For Trainee:** The applicant should be a Bhajan performer.
**For Workshop Conductor:** The applicant should be an expert in traditional Bhajan Singing or Pakhawaj playing.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applications for this scheme are invited by announcement in local print and electronic media.
**Step 2:** In the provided application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the supporting documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the **Member Secretary, Kala Academy, Goa**.
**Authority for Redressal of Grievances:** Member Secretary/Chairman, Kala Academy, Goa.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the workshops focused on Bhajan singing and Pakhawaj playing?

The workshops aim to provide knowledge of traditional Bhajan singing and Pakhawaj playing to performers, guided by experts in these fields.

### How are applicants informed about the opportunity to participate in these workshops?

Applications are invited through announcements in local print and electronic media.

### Will participants receive any certification after completing the workshop sessions?

Yes, certificates are awarded to all participants who attend the workshops.

### Is there any financial compensation provided to the experts conducting these workshops?

Yes, a suitable honorarium is paid to the conductors of the workshops.

### Which authority should interested individuals contact for suggestions regarding the workshops?

Suggestions can be sent to the Member Secretary of Kala Academy Goa.

### If an applicant faces any issues or grievances, who should they approach for resolution?

Grievances can be addressed to either the Member Secretary or the Chairman of Kala Academy Goa.

### Are these workshops open to all performers interested in Bhajan singing and Pakhawaj playing?

Yes, applications are invited from Bhajan performers interested in learning these traditional art forms.

### What kind of training can participants expect from these workshops?

The training focuses on traditional Bhajan singing and Pakhawaj playing under expert guidance.

### Can applicants suggest workshop topics or areas of focus?

Yes, suggestions can be submitted to the Member Secretary of Kala Academy Goa.

### How can applicants stay updated about future workshop announcements?

They should monitor local print and electronic media for official announcements.
