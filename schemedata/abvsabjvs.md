## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide a platform for scholars engaged in the study, teaching, propagation, and research of Vedas, Astrology, and Vastu Shastra to participate in the conference.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Akhil Bhartiya Veda Sammelan and Akhil Bhartiya Jyotish Vastu Sammelan” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Sanskrit Academy, Haridwar, under the Department of Sanskrit Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a platform for scholars engaged in the study, teaching, propagation, and research of Vedas, Astrology, and Vastu Shastra to participate in the conference.
<br>

## Benefits

In the conference, scholars gain the opportunity to understand the subtle knowledge of Vedic literature, Astrology, and Vastu Shastra; interpret the scriptures from a contemporary perspective; exchange knowledge and receive certificates of participation.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. Scholars engaged in the study, teaching, propagation, and research of the Vedas, Astrology, and Vastu Shastra will be eligible to participate in the respective conference.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

As per the information letter, the application form must be submitted via email, or information about your arrival has to be given on the phone of the coordinator.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can participate in the Veda Sammelan?

Scholars engaged in the study, teaching, propagation, and research of the Vedas are eligible to participate.
<br>

### Who can participate in the Jyotish Vastu Sammelan?

Scholars engaged in the study, teaching, propagation, and research of Astrology and Vastu Shastra are eligible.
<br>

### What are the benefits of participating in the conference?

Participants can understand the subtle knowledge of Vedic literature, Astrology, and Vastu Shastra, interpret scriptures in a contemporary context, exchange knowledge, and receive certificates of participation.
<br>

### How can scholars apply for the conference?

Scholars can submit their application forms via email or inform the coordinator of their arrival by phone as per the information letter.
<br>

### Are certificates provided to all participants?

Yes, scholars attending the conference receive certificates of participation.
<br>

### How will updates about the conference be communicated?

Updates are shared through email or phone communication as per the details in the information letter.
<br>
