## Brief Description

The scheme "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana (8620)" by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote science education by providing financial assistance to students enrolling in the science stream in Class 11th and 12th.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana&quot; by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to create ample opportunities for technologically educated and skilled youth in the state by encouraging students to enter and complete science stream education up to Class 12th. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹25,000/- over two years to students of Class 11th and 12th to support their education in science and help them complete their board examinations successfully.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Class 11th Benefit:** ₹10,000/- per annum, paid as a monthly stipend of ₹1,000/- for 10 months.  - **Class 12th Benefit:** ₹15,000/- per annum. Students will receive a monthly stipend of ₹1,000/- for 10 months (₹10,000)  - **Additional Benefit:** ₹5,000/- after passing the Class 12th board examination.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a student.
  - The applicant must have studied in a government or grant-in-aid school in Class 9th and 10th.
  - The applicant must have scored 50% or more in the Class 10 board examination.
  - The applicant must have taken admission in the science stream in a government, grant-in-aid, or self-financed school recognized by GSHSEB (Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board) or CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education).
  - If the applicant has studied in a recognized self-financed school for Class 9 and/or 10, the family’s annual income must be ₹6,00,000/- or less.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

- **Student Registration:** Schools must register eligible students on the Child Tracking System (CTS) portal of Vidya Samiksha Kendra.  - **Verification:** The Director of Schools will verify applications with the help of the District Education Officer, and upload the final list of eligible students on the dedicated &quot;Namo Saraswati&quot; portal.  - **Payment:** Assistance is disbursed directly into the beneficiary’s mother’s bank account (or the student’s account, if required) through DBT.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana"?

The "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana" is a financial assistance scheme launched by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat to promote science education among students of Class 11 and 12 by providing monetary support to help them complete their studies.
<br>

### Who is eligible for the "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana"?

A student is eligible if they have scored 50% or more in Class 10, enrolled in the science stream in a government, grant-in-aid, or self-financed school recognized by GSHSEB or CBSE, and meet the family income criteria where applicable.
<br>

### What is the total financial benefit under this scheme?

Under the "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana", each eligible student receives a total of ₹25,000 over two years of Class 11 and 12 as a monthly stipend and a final installment after passing Class 12.
<br>

### How is the financial assistance disbursed?

The financial assistance under the "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana" is disbursed through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the mother’s bank account or directly into the student’s account if the mother is deceased.
<br>

### What is the amount given to Class 11 students?

Class 11 students receive ₹10,000 per year, which is paid as ₹1,000 per month for 10 months under the "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana".
<br>

### What is the amount given to Class 12 students?

Class 12 students receive ₹15,000 per year, which includes ₹10,000 as monthly stipend and ₹5,000 after passing the Class 12 board exam.
<br>

### Who is the implementing authority for this scheme?

The "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana" is implemented by the Education Department of Gujarat.
<br>

### How can schools register students under this scheme?

Schools can register eligible students on the Child Tracking System (CTS) portal of Vidya Samiksha Kendra as part of the "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana".
<br>

### What is the minimum attendance requirement?

Students must maintain a minimum of 80% attendance every month, which is updated by schools on the Vidya Samiksha Kendra’s online attendance portal to continue receiving assistance.
<br>

### What happens if a student drops out mid-year?

If a student drops out mid-year, they will not receive the remaining assistance, but they do not have to return the money already received under the "Namo Saraswati Vignan Sadhana Yojana".
<br>

### When are payments released to students?

Payments are released every month by the 10th, with the first payment for June disbursed in June or July along with July’s payment.
<br>
