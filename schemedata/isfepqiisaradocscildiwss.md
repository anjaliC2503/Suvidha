## Brief Description

Under this scheme, to motivate the growers to bring more area under irrigation and land development by constructing water storage structures for increasing the productivity and improving quality of small cardamom.

## Detailed Description

The Small Cardamom Irrigation Land Development: Irrigation / Water Storage Structure Scheme under the Spices Board India, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India, is intended to motivate growers to bring more area under irrigation and land development by constructing water storage structures, thereby increasing productivity and improving the quality of small cardamom, as per the modalities and scheme guidelines approved by the competent authority for the implementation of the “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom” during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

## Benefits

1. 50 % of cost of construction subject to a maximum of Rs 30,000 per unit for General Category and 75% subject to maximum of ₹ 45,000/- per unit for SC/ST.
1. The minimum capacity of irrigation structure should be 25 cubic metre for availing maximum subsidy under the programme. Any farmer who has availed this benefit earlier is not eligible to avail the benefit.
1. Registered cardamom growers having land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha are eligible to avail this scheme.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a small cardamom grower from Kerala, Tamil Nadu, or Karnataka.
1. The applicant should undertake irrigation and land development activities by constructing irrigation or water storage structures.
1. The applicant should ensure that the proposed structure does not obstruct the natural flow of water or create objections from local communities or concerned authorities, subject to final inspection by designated officials.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Irrigation / Water Storage Structure Scheme?

The objective of the scheme is to encourage small cardamom growers to bring more area under irrigation and land development by constructing water storage structures. The scheme aims to improve the productivity and quality of small cardamom cultivation.
<br>

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Registered small cardamom growers having land holdings between 0.10 hectare and 8 hectares are eligible to apply for this scheme. The scheme is applicable only to growers located in Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka.
<br>

### What is the minimum capacity required for the irrigation structure?

To avail the maximum subsidy under the programme, the irrigation or water storage structure should have a minimum capacity of 25 cubic metres.
<br>

### Can a farmer who already received assistance earlier apply again?

No, farmers who have already availed benefits under this scheme earlier are not eligible to receive assistance again under the same programme.
<br>

### Is a technical estimate required for the proposed structure?

Yes, the applicant must submit a plan and cost estimate for the proposed irrigation or water storage structure prepared by a qualified registered/licensed or Government civil engineer using Government-approved construction rates.
<br>

### In which states is the scheme implemented?

The scheme is implemented in the major small cardamom growing states of Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka.
<br>

### How are applications verified under the scheme?

Designated officials conduct a final inspection of the proposed or completed structure and verify whether the applicant satisfies all eligibility conditions before recommending the case for sanction.
<br>

### What kind of constructions are not eligible for assistance?

Structures that block the natural flow of water, including streams, or constructions that are objectionable to local communities or concerned authorities will not be considered for financial assistance under the scheme.
<br>

### Why is irrigation support important for small cardamom cultivation?

Proper irrigation and water storage facilities help maintain adequate soil moisture, especially during dry periods, which improves crop growth, increases productivity, and enhances the quality of small cardamom.
<br>
