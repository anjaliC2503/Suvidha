## Brief Description

The scheme objective is to provide free skill training and self-employment support to widowed, abandoned, and destitute women through voluntary organizations. The scheme is implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department with financial assistance provided to participating NGOs.

## Detailed Description

The "Mahila Swayamsiddha Yojana" implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan, aims to provide skill training and self-employment opportunities to widowed, abandoned, and destitute women. Under this scheme, free vocational training is offered in Nariniketan buildings, operated via voluntary organizations.
<br>

> ***Objective:***


- Free vocational training for skill development in various trades.
- Accommodation and food provisions for enrolled women and their children.
- Financial assistance to NGOs operating training centers.
- The training period ranges from 3 months to 1 year.
- Assistance in securing employment or availing loans for self-employment.
- Monthly stipend for participating women.

<br>

> ***Women should belong to one of the following categories:***


- Widowed, abandoned, or destitute women facing social neglect.
- Women released from imprisonment without family support.
- Women affected by natural disasters, lacking social and financial assistance.
- Women rescued from exploitation, trafficking, or forced labor.
- Women affected by terrorism or extremist activities.
- Women suffering from mental illness, except those requiring institutional psychiatric care.
- Women living with HIV/AIDS who are abandoned by their families.
- Other women facing extreme social and economic distress.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a registered voluntary organization, trust, or NGO.
1. The organization must be operational for at least three years before applying.
1. The organization should be non-profit and focused on women’s welfare.
1. The organization must have a formally structured administrative and management committee.
1. The organization should not be blacklisted or penalized by any government department.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Interested voluntary organizations must submit applications in the prescribed format to the District Collector’s Office. 
**Step 2:** The District Collector’s office reviews and recommends the application.
**Step 3:** The Social Justice and Empowerment Department evaluates the application.
**Step 4:** Eligible organizations receive approval and funding.
**Step 5:** Beneficiary women enroll through the Admission Advisory Committee.

## Documents Required

- Registration Certificate of the organization.
- Inspection Report (from the concerned District Collector with recommendations).
- Copy of the Organization's bylaws.
- Latest certified executive committee member list.
- Work experience proof in women’s welfare activities.
- Chartered Accountant-certified audit reports for the last three years.
- Income-Expenditure statements for the last three years.
- Progress report of the last three years.
- PAN Card.
- Bank Statement.
- Non-judicial affidavit stating that the organization is not blacklisted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply under this scheme?

Registered voluntary organizations with at least three years of experience in women's welfare.

### What type of training is provided?

Vocational training in various trades based on women's skills and interests.

### Is there any fee for training?

No, the training is provided free of cost.

### What happens after training?

Women receive job placement support or assistance in availing loans for self-employment.

### Can an NGO apply if it is blacklisted?

No, blacklisted organizations are not eligible.

### Is there an age limit for beneficiaries?

The scheme primarily targets adult women in distress.

### Who oversees the scheme’s implementation?

The Social Justice and Empowerment Department and Admission Advisory Committee.

### How is financial assistance given to NGOs?

Quarterly after a review by the Admission Advisory Committee.

### What if an NGO does not follow guidelines?

The department can revoke funding and recover financial aid.

### Can women leave before completing training?

Yes, but they may forfeit financial assistance and accommodation.

### How to contact the department for queries?

Contact details of the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Jaipur, are available on their official website.
