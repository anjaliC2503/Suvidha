## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support Scheduled Caste individuals by providing financial assistance for poultry farming.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Subsidy Scheme on Establishment of 25 R.I.R. Bird’s Unit, 100 Broiler Bird’s Unit and Stipend for Poultry Farming Training for Scheduled Caste People&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to offer subsidies for setting up poultry units and provides a stipend for poultry farming training to promote self-employment and economic upliftment.

## Benefits

<br>

**Name of Component**	**Standard of Assistance**	**Minimum Time Limit(In Year) for Reclaiming Benefits**Subsidy Scheme on establishment of 25 R.I.R. birds unit for Scheduled Caste	75% of total Expenditure or maximum ₹6,000/- in kind (25 R.I.R. birds and poultry feed)	Benefit can be given to one member of family. After 3 years the same family can avail benefit.	Subsidy Scheme for establishment of 100 broiler bird farm for Scheduled Caste	90% subsidy or Maximum ₹36,000/- RTGS/NEFT (In Bank Account Direct)	Benefit can be given to one member of family.	Stipend Scheme for Poultry Farming Training for Scheduled Caste	Maximum ₹2,000/- RTGS/NEFT (in Bank Account Direct)	Benefit can be given to one member of family.	<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be residing in Gujarat.
   1. The applicant must be a Scheduled Caste individual.
   1. The applicant must be an adult (18 years or above).
   1. The applicant must have completed a 6-day poultry farming training.

> **Note:** Females and beneficiaries Below Poverty Line (BPL) will be given priority.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Animal Husbandry Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on &quot;Apply New&quot; button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the &quot;Update Application&quot; button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application and submit Related Documents through to concern Taluka office.
**Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) 
> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).

&gt; **Note:** Before application, beneficiaries must complete poultry training at the Intensive Poultry Development Project or District Poultry Extension Centre.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

This scheme aims to support Scheduled Caste individuals by offering subsidies for setting up poultry units and providing a stipend for poultry farming training to promote self-employment and economic upliftment.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Scheduled Caste individuals residing in Gujarat who are 18 years or older and have completed the mandatory 6-day poultry farming training are eligible to apply.
<br>

<br>

### What financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides:

- 75% subsidy (up to ₹6,000/-) for establishing a 25 R.I.R. bird unit (benefit provided in kind).
- 90% subsidy (up to ₹36,000/-) for setting up a 100-broiler bird unit (amount transferred via RTGS/NEFT).
- ₹2,000/- stipend for completing poultry farming training.

### Is training mandatory to apply for the scheme?

Yes, the 6-day poultry farming training from an authorized Intensive Poultry Development Project or District Poultry Extension Centre is mandatory before applying for the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Where can an applicant receive the required poultry farming training?

The training is provided at Intensive Poultry Development Projects and District Poultry Extension Centres across Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### Are female applicants given preference in this scheme?

Yes, female applicants are given priority under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Can more than one member from the same family apply for the scheme?

No, only one member per family can avail of the benefits at a time. However, for the 25 R.I.R. bird unit, a family can reapply after 3 years.

### How is the subsidy amount provided to beneficiaries?

- For the 25 R.I.R. bird unit, the subsidy is provided in kind (birds and poultry feed).
- For the 100-broiler bird unit and the stipend, the amount is transferred directly to the beneficiary’s bank account via RTGS/NEFT.

<br>

<br>

### Can an applicant from outside Gujarat apply for the scheme?

No, only residents of Gujarat belonging to the Scheduled Caste category are eligible for this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### How can an individual apply for the scheme?

An eligible individual can apply online through the [iKhedut portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/) after completing the 6-day poultry farming training.
<br>
