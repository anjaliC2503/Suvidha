## Brief Description

"Incentive Scheme For The Electricity Consumers Under Various Categories In Andaman & Nicobar Islands" aims to reduce power consumption through demand side management and promote green energy adoption. The scheme offers monetary incentives to consumers who achieve at least 30% energy savings.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 2018, the scheme "Incentive Scheme For The Electricity Consumers Under Various Categories In Andaman & Nicobar Islands" by the Electricity Department, Andaman & Nicobar Administration aims to encourage energy conservation among electricity consumers and promote adoption of green energy initiatives. The scheme offers monetary incentives ranging from ₹15,000/- to ₹75,000/- to consumers who achieve significant energy savings. Eligible participants include domestic, commercial, and industrial consumers with properly functioning meters and up-to-date bill payments. The scheme is implemented by the Electricity Department of Andaman & Nicobar Islands through an evaluation committee headed by the Commissioner-cum-Secretary (Power).

## Benefits

**Prize Money for Domestic Consumers (Green Energy & Energy Conservation)**
First Prize: ₹75,000/-; Second Prize: ₹50,000/-; Third Prize: ₹30,000/-
**Prize Money for Domestic Consumers (Energy Conservation)**
First Prize: ₹50,000/-; Second Prize: ₹30,000/-; Third Prize: ₹15,000/-
**Prize Money for Commercial Consumers**
First Prize: ₹75,000/-; Second Prize: ₹50,000/-; Third Prize: ₹30,000/-
**Prize Money for Industrial Consumers**
First Prize: ₹75,000/-; Second Prize: ₹50,000/-; Third Prize: ₹30,000/-

## Exclusions

- Consumers With Stop/tampered Meter.
- Consumers Billed on Average Basis.
- Consumers Having Outstanding Dues.
- Consumers Under Disconnection During the Month of Evaluation.
- Consumers Physically Not Present in Their Residence.
- Consumers With Disputed Ownership of the Electric Connection.

## Eligibility

- The consumers those have installed Solar Water Heating System, Rooftop Solar & Ground Mounted Solar for their domestic requirements will be considered under Green Energy.
- The consumers whose energy meters are in running condition.
- The calculation of saving done by the consumer shall be on the basis of past three months average consumption.
- The consumers having electricity bill paid up to date will only be considered.
- Ownership of the meter should be in the name of the beneficiary who should be present at the time of evaluation, if called by the department.
- Only those consumers will be eligible whose minimum energy savings are more than 30%.
- The highest savings done by the consumers would be eligible for the prizes.
- In case the percentage saving is equal for more than one consumer, say Mr. A & Mr. B both are with 40% saving, then the winner will be selected by drawing of lots.

## Application Process

### Offline

The "Incentive Scheme For The Electricity Consumers Under Various Categories In Andaman & Nicobar Islands" does not require any formal application process. Eligible consumers with functioning meters and paid-up bills will be automatically considered for incentives based on their energy savings as evaluated by the department's committees.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the scheme benefit the environment and the islands as a whole?

The scheme aims to minimize pollution by reducing carbon emissions and encourages switching to clean and green energy sources. This helps in making the Andaman & Nicobar Islands greener and reduces dependence on diesel generator sets.

### What are the prize amounts for the domestic consumers who have installed green energy solutions?

For domestic consumers under the Green Energy & Energy Conservation category, the first prize is [?]75,000/-, second prize is [?]50,000/-, and third prize is [?]30,000/-.

### How much minimum energy saving is required to be eligible for the incentive?

Only those consumers who achieve minimum energy savings of more than 30% compared to their last three months' average consumption will be eligible for the incentives.

### What incentives are available for domestic consumers who practice energy conservation without green energy installations?

Domestic consumers who practice energy conservation without green energy installations can receive [?]50,000/- for first prize, [?]30,000/- for second prize, and [?]15,000/- for third prize.

### How is the energy saving calculated for determining eligibility under the scheme?

The calculation of energy savings is done based on the consumer's past three months' average consumption. The percentage reduction in energy use compared to this baseline determines eligibility.

### What monetary incentives are offered to commercial consumers who achieve significant energy savings?

Commercial consumers who achieve significant energy savings can receive [?]75,000/- for first prize, [?]50,000/- for second prize, and [?]30,000/- for third prize.

### What are the prize amounts for industrial consumers who participate in the scheme?

Industrial consumers can receive [?]75,000/- for first prize, [?]50,000/- for second prize, and [?]30,000/- for third prize based on their energy savings performance.

### What happens if two or more consumers have the same percentage of energy savings?

If the percentage saving is equal for more than one consumer (for example, two consumers both achieving 40% saving), the winner will be selected by drawing of lots.

### What green energy solutions qualify a domestic consumer for the Green Energy category?

Domestic consumers who have installed Solar Water Heating Systems, Rooftop Solar panels, or Ground Mounted Solar systems for their domestic requirements will be considered under the Green Energy category.

### What is the condition of the electricity meter required for eligibility in this scheme?

To be eligible, consumers must have electricity meters that are in running condition. Consumers with stopped or tampered meters are not eligible for the scheme.

### Can consumers with outstanding electricity bills participate in this scheme?

No, only consumers with electricity bills paid up to date will be considered for the incentives. Those having outstanding dues are explicitly excluded from the scheme.

### Who needs to be the owner of the electric meter for eligibility purposes?

The ownership of the meter should be in the name of the beneficiary who should be present at the time of evaluation if called by the department.

### Are consumers with disputed ownership of the electric connection eligible for the incentives?

No, consumers with disputed ownership of the electric connection are not eligible for the incentives under this scheme.

### What happens if a consumer's connection is disconnected during the evaluation month?

Consumers whose connections are under disconnection during the month of evaluation are not eligible for the incentives under this scheme.
