## Brief Description

The scheme aims to popularize award winning tiatrs by organizing annual festivals in various Talukas of Goa. It provides financial remuneration to directors/producers of prize-winning tiatrs for staging performances.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Award Winning Tiatr Festival" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to popularize award winning tiatrs by organizing an annual festival comprising of tiatrs which have won 1st, 2nd and 3rd places in various competitions, and will be organized in various Talukas of Goa with venues decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The scheme provides financial remuneration to directors/producers for staging their award-winning tiatrs and covers payment to service providers for arranging stage, lights, sound etc. when no furnished auditorium is available. The applicant must be a director/producer of a tiatr that has won 1st, 2nd or 3rd place in specified competitions and must submit self-attested copies of Aadhaar and Permanent Account Number Card along with the application. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Financial Remuneration for Tiatr Performance***

- Tiatr Academy of Goa Popular Tiatr winners: ₹40,000/- for each tiatr.
- Kala Academy Group 'A' winners: ₹35,000/- for each tiatr.
- Kala Academy Group 'B' winners: ₹30,000/- for each tiatr.
- Tiatr Academy of Goa Children Tiatr winners: ₹25,000/- for each tiatr.

##### ***Infrastructure Support***

- Payment to service providers for arranging stage, lights, sound etc. at rates fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa (applicable when no furnished auditorium is available).

##### ***Performance Platform***

- Opportunity to showcase award-winning tiatrs in various Talukas of Goa.
- Venues for the festival will be decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be the director/producer of a tiatr that has won 1st, 2nd or 3rd place in any of the following competitions:
- The applicant must be the director/producer of a tiatr that won 1st, 2nd or 3rd prize in Tiatr Academy of Goa Popular Tiatr Competition.
- The applicant must be the director/producer of a tiatr that won 1st, 2nd or 3rd prize in Kala Academy Group 'A' tiatr Competition.
- The applicant must be the director/producer of a tiatr that won 1st, 2nd or 3rd prize in Kala Academy Group 'B' tiatr Competition.
- The applicant must be the director/producer of a tiatr that won 1st, 2nd or 3rd prize in Tiatr Academy of Goa Children's Tiatr Competition.

***Post-Selection Conditions***

- Presentation of tiatr including kantaram should be done exactly the way it was presented in the respective competitions.
- The tiatr should be shown without charging tickets.
- Non-adherence to performance norms may result in withholding the payments.
- Tiatr Academy of Goa may depute an observer to verify whether the performance is as per the norms.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What documents do I need to submit along with my application to participate in this cultural festival?

You must submit self-attested copies of your Aadhaar Card and Permanent Account Number Card along with your application.
<br>

### How much financial remuneration will I receive if my tiatr won first, second, or third place in Kala Academy Group 'B' competition?

You will receive ₹30,000/- for each tiatr that won 1st, 2nd, or 3rd place in Kala Academy Group 'B' competition.
<br>

### Can I charge entrance fees or ticket prices when my award-winning tiatr is being performed at the festival?

No, the tiatr should be shown without charging any tickets to the audience during the festival performance.
<br>

### What happens if I don't perform my tiatr exactly the same way it was presented in the original competition?

Non-adherence to this norm may result in withholding of the payments, as the presentation must be done exactly as it was in the respective competition.
<br>

### Will I receive any additional financial support if there is no proper auditorium available for staging my performance?

Yes, Tiatr Academy of Goa will make payment to service providers towards arranging stage, lights, sound etc. at rates fixed by the academy.
<br>

### How much money will I receive if my children's tiatr won a prize in the Tiatr Academy of Goa competition?

You will receive ₹25,000/- for each tiatr that won 1st, 2nd, or 3rd place in Tiatr Academy of Goa Children's Tiatr Competition.
<br>

### What is the remuneration amount for tiatrs that won prizes in Kala Academy Group 'A' competition category?

The remuneration is ₹35,000/- for each tiatr that won 1st, 2nd, or 3rd place in Kala Academy Group 'A' competition.
<br>

### Can someone monitor or observe my performance during the festival to ensure I am following the guidelines properly?

Yes, Tiatr Academy of Goa may depute an observer to verify whether your performance is as per the established norms.
<br>

### Who will decide the specific locations and venues where the festival performances will take place across Goa?

The venues for the festival will be decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa, and the festival will be organized in various Talukas of Goa.
<br>

### What should I include in my performance besides the main tiatr when presenting at the festival?

The presentation of tiatr including kantaram should be done exactly the way it was presented in the respective competitions.
<br>

### If I have won multiple prizes in Tiatr Academy of Goa Popular Tiatr Competition, how much will I receive per tiatr?

You will receive ₹40,000/- for each tiatr that won 1st, 2nd, or 3rd place in Tiatr Academy of Goa Popular Tiatr Competition.
<br>

### Who has the final authority to make decisions regarding all matters related to this cultural festival program?

The decision of the Tiatr Academy of Goa in all matters related to this Tiatr Festival will be final and binding.
<br>

### Can the organizing academy make modifications to the rules and regulations of this festival in the future?

Yes, Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to make any changes in the rules of the festival.
<br>

### Which specific competitions make my tiatr eligible for participation in this annual award-winning festival organized by the academy?

Your tiatr must have won 1st, 2nd, or 3rd place in Tiatr Academy of Goa Popular Tiatr Competition, Kala Academy Group 'A' or 'B' competitions, or Tiatr Academy of Goa Children's Tiatr Competition.
