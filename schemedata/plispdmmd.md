## Brief Description

The objective of the Scheme is to provide financial incentive to boost domestic manufacturing and attract large investments in the Medical Device Sector.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Production Linked Incentive Scheme for Promoting Domestic Manufacturing of Medical Devices” by the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers aims to boost domestic manufacturing and attract large investments through providing financial incentives to companies engaged in manufacturing medical devices in India. The scheme provides incentives based on incremental sales over the base year and subject to threshold investment conditions. The scheme is applicable only for greenfield projects and is implemented through a Project Management Agency.
> **Target Segments:**

Target Segment shall mean one of the four segments, viz.:

   1. Cancer care / Radiotherapy medical devices
   1. Radiology &amp; Imaging medical devices (both ionizing &amp; non-ionizing radiation products) and Nuclear Imaging devices
   1. Anaesthetics &amp; Cardio-Respiratory medical devices, Oxygen Concentrators, including Catheters of Cardio-Respiratory Category &amp; Renal Care medical devices
   1. All implants, including implantable electronic devices


  - **Tenure of the Scheme:** Financial Year 2020-21 to Financial Year 2027-28.

- **Project Management Agency:** The scheme is implemented through a Nodal Agency, namely Industrial Finance Corporation of India (IFCI), which acts as the Project Management Agency (PMA).
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Incentive:** 5% incentive on incremental sales of manufactured goods over the base year (FY 2019-20).
<br>

## Eligibility

> **General Eligibility:**


- The applicant must be a company registered in India.
- The applicant may also be registered as a Limited Liability Partnership (LLP).
- The applicant must be engaged in manufacturing of medical devices covered under target segments.
- The applicant must establish a greenfield project in India.
- The applicant must meet the threshold investment and incremental sales criteria.
- The applicant must fulfill all conditions specified for disbursement of incentives.

> **Threshold Conditions:**


- The applicant must achieve minimum cumulative investment of ₹1,80,00,00,000 over 3 years.
- The applicant must achieve incremental sales as specified for each year.

> **Additional Conditions:**


- The applicant must submit a complete application within the application window of 120 days.
- The applicant’s eligibility under this scheme does not affect eligibility under other schemes.

## Application Process

### Online

- The eligible applicants (Category-B applicants) may apply through online mode only. The link is: [https:/plimedicaldevices.ifciltd.com/](https://plimedicaldevices.ifciltd.com/)
> **Contact:** [Click here](https://plimedicaldevices.ifciltd.com/contact-us).

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant must prepare the application with all required details.
**Step 2:** The applicant must submit the application within 120 days of the application window.
**Step 3:** The application is appraised by the Project Management Agency.
**Step 4:** Eligible applications are considered by the Empowered Committee for approval.
**Step 5:** Approved applicants submit claims for incentive disbursement.
**Step 6:** The incentive is released after verification of claims.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to boost domestic manufacturing and attract investments in the medical devices sector.
<br>

### Who implements the scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Pharmaceuticals under the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers.
<br>

### Who can apply under this scheme?

Any company registered in India and a Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), is eligible to apply under the scheme for manufacturing of eligible medical devices.
<br>

### What type of projects are eligible?

Only greenfield projects are eligible under the scheme.
<br>

### What is the incentive rate under the scheme?

The incentive rate is 5% on incremental sales over the base year.
<br>

### What is the base year for calculation?

The base year for calculation of incremental sales is FY 2019-20.
<br>

### For how long is the incentive provided?

The incentive is provided for 5 years from FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26.
<br>

### What is the minimum investment required?

The applicant must invest at least ₹1,80,00,00,000 over 3 years.
<br>

### What are the target segments under the scheme?

The scheme covers cancer care, imaging, cardio-respiratory devices, and implants. Oxygen concentrators are included under Target Segment 3 in the category of anaesthetics and cardio-respiratory medical devices.
<br>

### Is there any application time limit?

Yes, applications must be submitted within 120 days of the application window.
<br>

### How are applications evaluated?

Applications are evaluated by the Project Management Agency and approved by the Empowered Committee.
<br>

### How is the incentive disbursed?

The incentive is disbursed after verification of claims submitted by the company.
<br>
