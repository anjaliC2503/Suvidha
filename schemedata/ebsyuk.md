## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote the safe health and well-being of mothers and newborns by encouraging institutional deliveries and ensuring that mothers stay in government hospitals for at least 48 hours after childbirth.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Eja-Boi Shagun Yojana” was launched by the Department of Medical Health and Family Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote the safe health and well-being of mothers and newborns by encouraging institutional deliveries and ensuring that mothers stay in government hospitals for at least 48 hours after childbirth.
<br>

## Benefits

For ensuring the safe health of both mother and child, an incentive amount of ₹2000/- is provided to all eligible mothers who stay in the government hospital for at least 48 hours after delivery.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Uttarakhand.
   1. The applicant must be a pregnant woman who has delivered her child in a government hospital within the state of Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

After the delivery of a pregnant woman in a government hospital, the concerned hospital collects her Aadhaar card and bank account details and initiates the process for transferring the incentive amount of ₹2000/-, which is later credited directly to the woman’s bank account.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to benefit from this scheme?

The scheme is available to women who are permanent residents of Uttarakhand and have delivered their baby in a government hospital within the state.
<br>

### How much financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

An incentive amount of ₹2000/- is provided to each eligible mother who stays in a government hospital for at least 48 hours after delivery.
<br>

### What is the purpose of the 48-hour hospital stay requirement?

The 48-hour stay ensures that both mother and newborn receive proper postnatal care, medical observation, and necessary support to prevent complications after childbirth.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable for deliveries in private hospitals?

No.
<br>

### How can an eligible woman apply for the incentive amount?

After delivery in a government hospital, the hospital authorities collect the woman’s Aadhaar card and bank account details and process the incentive payment on her behalf.
<br>

### How is the incentive amount disbursed to the beneficiary?

The incentive amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary’s bank account after verification by the concerned hospital.
<br>
