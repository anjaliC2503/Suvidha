## Brief Description

The scheme "Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jaati Vishesh Pariyojana Vitt Poshan Yojana" under the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste Department, Madhya Pradesh, aims to provide financial assistance for special project proposals received in the interest of the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.

## Detailed Description

The Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jaati Vishesh Pariyojana Vitt Poshan Yojana under the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Madhya Pradesh, aims to provide financial assistance for special project proposals received in the interest of the Scheduled Caste (SC) category. The scheme's primary objective is to provide financial support to projects that benefit the SC community in the state. It focuses on enhancing welfare initiatives, improving living standards, and providing opportunities for development in various sectors for the SC category.

## Benefits

The scheme offers a 100% grant based on government approval for project proposals from line departments, recommended by the evaluation committee.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be from the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.
1. The applicant should submit project proposals as per the norms set by the respective line departments.
1. The applicant should ensure that the project proposal is recommended by the concerned department and approved by the State Project Implementation Committee.
1. The applicant should meet any additional requirements as specified by the line departments for the specific project.
1. The applicant should ensure that the project aligns with the objectives of the Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jaati Vishesh Pariyojana Vitt Poshan Yojana.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the Chief Executive Officer, District Panchayat, and request the hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the concerned authority.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the authority within the prescribed period (if any).
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).
**NOTE:** Ensure that the application is submitted within the prescribed period, if any.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Mukhyamantri Anusuchit Jaati Vishesh Pariyojana Vitt Poshan Yojana?

This scheme provides financial assistance for special project proposals benefiting the Scheduled Caste (SC) category in Madhya Pradesh.

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Applicants from the Scheduled Caste category who submit project proposals in accordance with the norms of the respective line departments.

### How can I apply for the scheme?

Applicants need to submit their project proposals through the relevant line departments, ensuring that all required criteria are met.

### What type of projects are eligible under the scheme?

Projects that benefit the Scheduled Caste category, in line with the objectives of the scheme, are eligible for funding.

### How is the grant provided under the scheme?

A 100% grant is provided, subject to government approval of the project proposals, based on the recommendation of the evaluation committee.

### Who approves the project proposals?

Project proposals are approved by the State Project Implementation Committee, chaired by the Chief Secretary.

### What are the steps involved in applying for the scheme?

The steps include submitting a project proposal to the relevant line department, which will then be reviewed and recommended for government approval.

### Do I need any additional approvals for the project?

Yes, after departmental recommendation, the project proposal must be approved by the State Project Implementation Committee.

### What types of departments are involved in the scheme?

Various line departments, depending on the nature of the project, will be involved in the proposal submission and approval process.
