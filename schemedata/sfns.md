## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to economically disadvantaged students enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate programs at Uttarakhand Sanskrit University.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Scholarship to Poor Students” is implemented by Uttarakhand Sanskrit University, Haridwar, under the Department of Sanskrit Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to economically disadvantaged students enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate programs at Uttarakhand Sanskrit University.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, one student from each undergraduate and postgraduate class at Uttarakhand Sanskrit University is awarded a scholarship equal to the tuition fee of their respective class.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
1. The student must be enrolled in an undergraduate or postgraduate course at Uttarakhand Sanskrit University.
1. The student must have the lowest annual family income among all students in the same course, class, or subject.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- A Scholarship Cell has been established at the university level to manage scholarship applications. The cell provides application forms to students, who are required to complete the form and submit it along with their high school certificate, Aadhaar card, income certificate, bank account details, and university identity card. 
  - After scrutinizing the applications, the cell publishes a list of eligible students. Upon administrative and financial approval, the scholarship funds are transferred directly to the bank accounts of the eligible students.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scholarship?

Students enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate programs at Uttarakhand Sanskrit University with the lowest annual family income in their class are eligible.
<br>

### How many students are awarded this scholarship per class?

One student per class in both undergraduate and postgraduate programs is awarded the scholarship.
<br>

### What is the value of the scholarship?

The scholarship amount is equal to the tuition fee of the respective class.
<br>

### On what basis is the scholarship awarded?

The scholarship is awarded to the student with the lowest annual family income among applicants in the respective class/course/subject.
<br>

### Who manages the scholarship process?

A dedicated Scholarship Cell has been established at Uttarakhand Sanskrit University to manage the process.
<br>

### How can students apply for the scholarship?

The Scholarship Cell issues application forms to students, which must be completed and submitted along with required documents.
<br>

### Where should students submit the application form?

Applications must be submitted to the Scholarship Cell at Uttarakhand Sanskrit University.
<br>

### How are the scholarship funds disbursed?

After approval, the scholarship amount is transferred directly to the bank account of the selected student.
<br>
