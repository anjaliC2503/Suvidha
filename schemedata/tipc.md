## Brief Description

The scheme aims to enhance irrigation facilities for farmers in Uttarakhand by providing financial assistance for the construction of tubewells and ponds, thereby improving water availability, supporting horticultural development, and increasing agricultural productivity.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Tubewell Installation/ Pond Construction” is implemented by the Department of Horticulture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to enhance irrigation facilities for farmers in Uttarakhand by providing financial assistance for the construction of tubewells and ponds, thereby improving water availability, supporting horticultural development, and increasing agricultural productivity.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, to promote the development of irrigation facilities, farmers are provided a 50% subsidy on the total cost for the construction of new tubewells or ponds, up to a maximum of ₹90,000/- per unit (limited to one unit per farmer).
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant must be a farmer and must hold a valid Udhyan Card.
1. The farmer must either own agricultural land or have land on lease.
1. The farmer must confirm the availability of water in the concerned area.
1. A farmer is eligible for a subsidy for the construction of one tube well/pond.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The application form can be obtained from the officers/personnel working at the Horticulture Mobile team Centres. It can also be downloaded from the department&#39;s website: [https://shm.uk.gov.in](https://shm.uk.gov.in/). Along with the application form/proposal, the applicant must submit land-related documents such as Khasra and Khatauni, a lease certificate (in case of leased land), Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Udhyan Card, bank account details, and mobile number. At present, the application process is offline.  - If the applicant faces any difficulty while completing the application, the personnel at the concerned Horticulture Mobile team Centre will provide assistance. The completed application form must be submitted at the same office. The personnel at the Horticulture Mobile team Centre then forward the proposal to the district level, which further forwards it to the Directorate. After the Directorate approves the farmers’ proposals, an approval/work order is issued to the respective districts.
  - Once a farmer’s proposal is approved, the district officer sends an acceptance letter and also informs the farmer over the phone. The farmer then begins constructing the tube well/pond, after which an on-site inspection is conducted by departmental personnel. Based on the inspection report, the subsidy amount is transferred directly to the farmer’s bank account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Farmers who are residents of Uttarakhand, hold a valid Udhyan Card, and have their own land or leased land are eligible.
<br>

### How much subsidy is provided under the scheme?

Farmers receive 50% subsidy of the total cost, up to ₹90,000 per unit, for constructing one tubewell or pond.
<br>

### How many tubewells or ponds can a farmer construct under the scheme?

A farmer is eligible for subsidy for only one unit (either one tubewell or one pond).
<br>

### Do farmers need to verify water availability?

Yes. The farmer must confirm whether water is available in the proposed area for tubewell installation or pond construction.
<br>

### Where can farmers get the application form?

The form can be obtained from Horticulture Mobile Squad Centres or downloaded from the department’s website.
<br>

### Where should the completed application be submitted?

The completed form must be submitted at the respective Horticulture Mobile Squad Centre.
<br>

### How is the application processed after submission?

The Mobile Squad Centre forwards the proposal to the district level, which then sends it to the Directorate for approval.
<br>

### How will a farmer know if their proposal is approved?

Once approved, the district officer sends an acceptance letter and also informs the farmer over the phone.
