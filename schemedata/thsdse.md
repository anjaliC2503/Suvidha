## Brief Description

The scheme "Training for Horticulture Skill Development for Self-Employment at Centre of Excellence " is aimed at promoting self-employment opportunities across all communities in Gujarat through practical and theoretical learning at the Centre of Excellence.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Training for Horticulture Skill Development for Self-Employment at Centre of Excellence&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat,  aims to offer structured training programs ranging from 10 to 30 days, focusing on horticultural skills to enable self-employment. The program is tailored for individuals across all social categories, with subsidized training costs. Beneficiaries gain access to a complete learning experience, including expert sessions, training literature, educational tours, and practical kits.

## Benefits

- **Stipend:** ₹250/- per trainee.- **Other Facilities:** Dining facility, training literature kit, experiential education tour, trainee kit, etc.
> **Duration of the Training:**


- Minimum: 10 days
- Maximum: 30 days

## Eligibility

The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
The applicant should belong to any of the following:

- Agriculture Labour
- All Colleges
- All Farmers
- All Individuals
- All Students
- Anganwadi Worker
- College Students
- Farm Labourer
- Horticulturists
- Self-Employment
- Skilled Individual
- Small and Marginal Farmers
- Small, Marginal & Other Farmers

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Obtain a plain paper application form from the District Office of the Deputy Director of Horticulture.
**Step 2:** Fill in the application form with accurate details. Attach the required documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the District Office of the Deputy Director of Horticulture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the primary goal of the "Training for Horticulture Skill Development for Self-Employment at Centre of Excellence"?

The primary goal of this scheme is to equip individuals with practical horticulture-related skills through structured training, enabling them to pursue self-employment opportunities and enhance their livelihood in the agriculture sector.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the horticulture skill development training scheme?

Any individual who is a permanent resident of Gujarat, regardless of their gender, occupation, or educational background, is eligible to apply for this training scheme, provided they are interested in acquiring horticulture skills for self-employment or employment purposes.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any application fee required to enroll in this training programme?

No application fee is required to enroll in this scheme, as the application process is entirely free of cost and can be done using a plain paper format submitted to the district horticulture office.
<br>

<br>

### What additional facilities and resources are offered to trainees besides the stipend?

In addition to the stipend, trainees are provided with a dining facility, a comprehensive training literature kit, an experiential educational tour, and a trainee kit to support hands-on learning.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any fixed duration for the training program under this scheme?

While the exact duration of the training may vary depending on the specific module and location, the duration will be communicated to the applicants by the district horticulture office upon selection.
<br>

<br>

### Does the applicant need any prior experience or educational qualification to be eligible for this training?

No prior experience or specific educational qualification is required to be eligible for this training, as the scheme is open to all individuals willing to learn horticulture skills, including beginners.
<br>

<br>

### Are women and individuals from all communities allowed to apply for this scheme?

Yes, the scheme is inclusive and allows individuals of all genders and communities, including women and marginalized groups, to apply without any form of discrimination.
<br>

<br>

### Can the application process be completed online or through any digital platform?

Currently, the application process is conducted offline only, and applicants must submit their applications physically at the district horticulture office as online application options are not available.
<br>

<br>

### From where can an applicant obtain the application form for this training scheme?

Interested applicants can obtain the application form, which is to be prepared on plain paper, directly from the District Office of the Deputy Director of Horticulture located in their respective area.
<br>

<br>

### Where should the completed application form be submitted for consideration under this scheme?

The completed application form, along with the required supporting documents, should be submitted to the District Office of the Deputy Director of Horticulture for further processing and verification.
