- **Nodal Department Name:** Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department

## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial assistance to farmers across Gujarat for constructing water storage tanks to support drip irrigation, with cost-sharing benefits based on farmer category.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Construction of Water Storage Tanks for Drip Irrigation&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat,  aims to offer financial assistance to farmers for setting up cemented water tanks essential for efficient drip irrigation.  It supports sustainable agriculture through water conservation, especially for horticultural practices.

## Benefits

- **Unit cost of construction:** ₹1,00,000/- per tank.  - **For General category farmers:** 50% subsidy, up to ₹50,000/-.  - **For SC/ST farmers:** 75% subsidy, up to ₹75,000/-.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a farmer.
   1.  The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
   1. The applicant must plan to construct a cemented water storage tank of at least 25.50 cubic meters capacity.

> **Note:** Cemented mature water tanks, drip set mandatory.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on &quot;Apply New&quot; button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the &quot;Update Application&quot; button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application.
**Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) 
> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).

## Documents Required

1. 7/12 Certificate.
1. Aadhaar Card.
1. Bank Pass Book/Cancelled Cheque.

> **Note:** For reasonableness of the cost of the tank, a certificate regarding the cost of the tank from the Government Valuer/Taluka Surveyor/ MNREGA surveyor will have to be provided by the beneficiary.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Construction of Water Storage Tanks for Drip Irrigation" scheme?

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to farmers for constructing water storage tanks to support efficient drip irrigation practices.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Any farmer residing in Gujarat who intends to install a cemented water tank for drip irrigation can apply for the scheme.

### What is the subsidy amount offered to general category farmers?

General category farmers are eligible to receive 50% of the tank’s construction cost, up to a maximum of ₹50,000/-.
<br>

<br>

### What financial assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe farmers?

Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe farmers can receive 75% of the cost, limited to a maximum of ₹75,000/-.
<br>

### Is there a minimum size requirement for the water tank?

Yes, the tank must have a minimum storage capacity of 25.50 cubic meters to be eligible for assistance.

### What type of tank is permitted under this scheme?

Only cemented water storage tanks are allowed under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Is the installation of a drip irrigation set mandatory?

Yes, installation of a drip irrigation set is compulsory as part of the scheme requirements.
<br>

<br>

### Can a farmer apply for the scheme more than once?

No, the benefit under this scheme can be availed only once per account on the i-Khedut portal.
<br>

<br>

### Where can a farmer submit an application for the scheme?

Applicants can apply on the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)portal to avail the benefits of the scheme.

### Is it necessary to submit a cost estimation certificate for the tank?

Yes, applicants must submit a certified cost estimate from a Government Valuer, Taluka Surveyor, or MNREGA Surveyor.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit for eligibility under this scheme?

No, there is no income limit for individual or family income under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

Applicants need to submit their Aadhaar card, 7/12 land record, caste certificate (if applicable), bank passbook or cancelled cheque, and tank cost certificate.
<br>

<br>

### Who should the applicant contact for help or more information about the scheme?

The applicant should contact the nearest agriculture office or visit the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/)portal for more information.
