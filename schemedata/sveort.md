## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide exam-oriented residential training for aspiring candidates seeking recruitment in defence and police forces. It offers comprehensive coaching and preparation for Army, Air Force, Navy, Paramilitary and Police recruitment examinations.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Swami Vivekananda Exam Oriented Residential Training" was launched by the Directorate of Employment and Training, Department of Labour and Employment, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide comprehensive residential training to aspiring candidates for recruitment in Army, Air Force, Navy, Paramilitary Forces and Police Forces, addressing the need for specialized coaching and preparation to enhance success rates in defence and police recruitment examinations. The scheme provides exam-oriented residential training programs and coaching facilities specifically designed for defence services examinations and conducts comprehensive preparation sessions covering written examinations, physical fitness training, personality development, and interview preparation. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat state and must be aspiring for recruitment in Army, Air Force, Navy, Paramilitary Forces or Police Forces with appropriate age and educational qualifications as per the respective service requirements. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Employment and Training under the Labour and Employment Department, Government of Gujarat and applications for this scheme are accepted through designated employment exchanges and training centers across the state.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Training and Coaching Support**


- **Residential Training:** Comprehensive exam-oriented coaching in residential format.- **Defence Examination Preparation:** Specialized training for Army, Air Force, Navy recruitment examinations.- **Police Force Training:** Preparation for Police Force recruitment examinations.- **Paramilitary Force Coaching:** Training for Central Armed Police Forces and other paramilitary recruitment.
<br>

> **Training Components**


- **Written Examination Preparation:** Subject-wise coaching and mock tests.- **Physical Fitness Training:** Physical preparation as per service requirements.- **Personality Development:** Communication skills and confidence building.- **Interview Preparation:** Mock interviews and counseling sessions.
<br>

> **Conditions**


- Regular attendance in all training sessions and activities.
- Compliance with residential training center rules and regulations.
- Participation in mock tests and assessment programs.
- Maintenance of discipline and conduct as per training center guidelines.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a permanent resident of Gujarat.
- The applicant must be aspiring for recruitment in Army, Air Force, Navy, Paramilitary Forces or Police Forces.
- The applicant must meet the age criteria as prescribed for respective defence and police services.
- The applicant must possess minimum educational qualifications as required for the specific service examination they are preparing for.
- The applicant must have completed relevant educational requirements as per Central Government norms for defence services.
- The applicant must meet basic physical fitness standards for defence and police services.
- The applicant must be medically fit as per general health requirements.
- The applicant must be willing to undergo rigorous physical training programs.
- The applicant must belong to economically weaker sections or demonstrate financial need for training assistance.
- The applicant must not be receiving similar training assistance from other government schemes simultaneously.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:****** ***Registration with Employment Exchange***
The applicant must first register with the nearest District Employment Exchange office in Gujarat and obtain a valid employment registration number for accessing training programs.
**Step 2:** ***Medical and Physical Fitness Assessment***
The applicant must undergo preliminary medical check-up and physical fitness assessment at the designated center to ensure they meet basic standards for defence training.
**Step 3:** **Training Program Inquiry**
The candidate should visit the Directorate of Employment and Training office / the nearest District Employment Office / the Designated Training Center and request the hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form for the scheme "Swami Vivekananda Exam Oriented Residential Training".
**Step 4:** ***Form Completion and Documentation***
The candidate should fill the application form completely with accurate personal details, educational background, preferred service category (Army/Navy/Air Force/Police/Paramilitary), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 5:** ***Application Submission and Interview***
The completed application with all documents must be submitted at one of the following - 

- Directorate of Employment and Training, Block No. 1, 3rd Floor, Dr. Jivraj Mehta Bhavan, Gandhinagar - 382010.
- District Employment Exchanges across all districts in Gujarat.
- Designated Training Centers (as notified by the Directorate of Employment and Training).

_*The applicant is advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and acknowledgment receipt for future reference._
**Step 6:** ***Selection and Admission***
The candidate may be required to appear for a preliminary interview or counseling session. Based on the evaluation of application, documents verification, and interview performance, selected candidates will be notified about their admission to the residential training program.
**Step 7:** ***Training Commencement***
Selected candidates must report to the designated training center on the specified date with necessary personal items and documents for the commencement of the residential training program.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What types of defence and security forces recruitment examinations does this residential training program prepare candidates for?

The training covers preparation for Army, Air Force, Navy, Paramilitary Forces and Police Forces recruitment examinations with specialized coaching for each service.
<br>

### Where can I submit my application for this exam-oriented residential training program and what are the designated submission locations?

You can submit applications at Directorate of Employment and Training (Block No. 1, 3rd Floor, Dr. Jivraj Mehta Bhavan, Gandhinagar - 382010), District Employment Exchanges, or Designated Training Centers.
<br>

### What preliminary assessments must I undergo before being considered for admission to this residential defence training program?

You must undergo preliminary medical check-up and physical fitness assessment at designated centers to ensure you meet basic standards for defence training.
<br>

### Do I need to register with any employment office before applying for this residential training program for defence services preparation?

Yes, you must first register with the nearest District Employment Exchange office in Gujarat and obtain a valid employment registration number for accessing training programs.
<br>

### What specific training components are included in this comprehensive residential program for defence and police recruitment preparation?

The program includes written examination preparation, physical fitness training, personality development, and interview preparation with mock tests and counseling sessions.
<br>

### Can candidates from other states apply for this residential training program, or is it restricted to Gujarat residents only?

You must be a permanent resident of Gujarat state to be eligible for this exam-oriented residential training program.
<br>

### What educational qualification requirements must I meet to be eligible for this defence services preparation training program?

You must possess minimum educational qualifications as required for the specific service examination you are preparing for, following Central Government norms for defence services.
<br>

### What happens after I submit my application and what is the selection process for admission to this residential training program?

You may be required to appear for a preliminary interview or counseling session, followed by evaluation of application, document verification, and interview performance for selection.
<br>

### Must I demonstrate financial need or belong to specific economic categories to qualify for this free residential training program?

Yes, you must belong to economically weaker sections or demonstrate financial need for training assistance, and must not be receiving similar assistance from other schemes.
<br>

### What physical and medical fitness standards must I meet before being accepted into this defence services preparation training program?

You must meet basic physical fitness standards for defence and police services, be medically fit as per general health requirements, and be willing to undergo rigorous physical training.
<br>

### What type of consent documentation is required if I am selected for this residential training program for defence recruitment preparation?

You need to provide Parents Consent Letter for residential training as part of the documentation required for training admission.
<br>

### How long does the application processing take and when will I be notified about my selection for this training program?

Selected candidates will be notified about their admission to the residential training program after evaluation, though specific processing timelines are determined by the implementing authority.
<br>

### What personal items and documents must I bring when reporting to the training center if I am selected for admission?

Selected candidates must report to the designated training center on the specified date with necessary personal items and documents for commencement of residential training.
<br>

### What ongoing obligations and discipline requirements must I maintain throughout the duration of this residential training program?

You must maintain regular attendance, comply with residential training center rules, participate in mock tests and assessment programs, and maintain discipline as per guidelines.
