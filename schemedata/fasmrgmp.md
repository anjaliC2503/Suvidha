## Brief Description

The scheme aims to honor and support Shaurya Medal recipients by providing financial aid and recognition for their bravery and distinguished service.

## Detailed Description

The “Financial Assistance to Shaurya Medal Recipients by the Government of Madhya Pradesh” scheme is launched by the Home Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to provide monetary support and recognition to the recipients of the Shaurya Medal for their exemplary acts of bravery and service to the nation. 
<br>

## Benefits

Financial assistance is provided to Shaurya Medal awardees. 
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant must be a Shaurya Medal awardee. 

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

##### ***Application Process***
**Step 1:****** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the District Sainik Welfare Office and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District Sainik Welfare Office.
##### ***Post-Application Process***
**Step 1:****** The District Sainik Welfare Office verifies the application and attached documents.
**Step 2:****** The verified application is forwarded to the Directorate of Sainik Welfare, Bhopal, for approval.
**Step 3:****** The Directorate sends the approved case to the General Administration Department (GAD-4), Government of Madhya Pradesh, for sanction.
**Step 4:****** Upon approval, the payment order is issued to the concerned District Collector.
**Step 5:****** The District Collector disburses the sanctioned amount directly into the beneficiary’s bank account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Shaurya Medal awardees who are permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Is there any age limit?

No, there is no age limit for applying.
<br>

### Which department implements the scheme?

The Home Department, through the Directorate of Sainik Welfare, Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### How can I apply for the scheme?

Submit an offline application to your District Sainik Welfare Office.
<br>

### Is there any application fee?

No, the application is free of cost.
<br>

### How is the financial aid disbursed?

Through direct bank transfer by the District Collector.
<br>

### Is online application available?

No, only offline applications are accepted.
<br>

### Who verifies the documents?

The District Sainik Welfare Office verifies and forwards the case.
<br>

### Which authority sanctions the payment?

The General Administration Department (GAD), Government of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Where can I get more information?

At your local District Sainik Welfare Office or Directorate of Sainik Welfare, Bhopal.
<br>
