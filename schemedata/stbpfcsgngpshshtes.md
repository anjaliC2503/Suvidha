## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide concession in bus fare fees to the students studying in Primary, Secondary, Higher Secondary, and Higher Secondary Technical Education in Government and Non-Government schools. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation (GSRTC).

## Detailed Description

The scheme "S.T. Bus Pass Fee Concession Scheme For Government/ Non Government Primary, Secondary & Higher Secondary and Higher Technical Education Students" was launched by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide concession in bus fare fees to the students studying in Primary, Secondary, Higher Secondary, and Higher Secondary Technical Education in Government and Non-Government schools. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation (GSRTC). The application for this scheme is accepted online.
<br>

## Benefits

Concession in Fees of the Buses of Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation (GSRTC).
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
- The applicant must be a Student.
- The applicant must be a Girl.
- The applicant must be studying in Primary / Secondary / Higher Secondary / Higher Secondary Technical Education.
- The applicant must be studying in a Government or Non-Government School.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Visit the Official GSRTC E-Pass System website at [https://pass.gsrtc.in](https://pass.gsrtc.in). Alternatively, use the GSRTC mobile app (available on the Google Play Store).
**Step 2:** From the list of "Pass Type" categories, select "Student Pass". On the next page, click "New Pass Request". 
**Step 3:** The online application form will open. In the form, fill in the following mandatory fields:
***Personal Details***

- Full name, complete postal address, date of birth, and gender.
- Category details (General, SC, ST, SCBC) and educational qualification level.
- Mobile number and email address for communication.

***Organization/Institution Information***

- Name and address of the educational institution or workplace related to the pass application.

***Travel Route and Service Details***

- Journey details including starting and destination points with route code. Add any via places and select the bus pass issue location.
- Class of bus service required options include Express, AC Seater, AC Sleeper, City Service, Volvo, Luxury, etc.
- Duration and number of days for the pass validity.

**Step 4:** Upload the following mandatory attachments in the specified format and size.
A recent passport-size photo (less than 10 KB in size).
**Step 5:** The form displays fees for bus pass, ID card amount if applicable, and any toll charges. The student concession will be applied automatically. Make the payment via UPI or counter payment at bus depots.
**Step 6:** Check the box agreeing to GSRTC's terms and conditions. Authenticate application submission by entering OTP sent to the registered mobile number. Submit the completed application form. An application number will be received for future tracking and status updates.
<br>

***Important Information***

- Valid identity proof and enrollment certification from the educational institute may be required during verification.
- Students are eligible for up to approximately 82.5% fare concession on the pass fare.
- The pass is valid only on GSRTC buses as per the specified routes and class.
- The online system supports registration, new applications, renewals, and application status inquiries.
- Video tutorials and guides in Gujarati are available online to assist applicants throughout the process.
- The GSRTC app facilitates easy access to pass details, bus schedules, and related services.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### If a girl student is currently studying in a private unaided secondary school in Gujarat, can she benefit from reduced bus fare under this program?

Yes, girl students studying in non-government schools are eligible for this concession. The scheme covers students from both government and non-government educational institutions across primary, secondary, and higher secondary levels.
<br>

### Is there any restriction on which bus routes or service classes a student can choose when applying for the concession pass?

The pass is valid only on Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation buses according to the specified routes and service class selected during application.
<br>

### What is the file size limitation for uploading the photograph when submitting the application form through the online portal?

The recent passport-size photograph must be less than 10 kilobytes in size. Applicants must ensure their photograph meets this technical specification before uploading it to the system.
<br>

### Does a girl student need to provide any certification from her educational institution when applying for or verifying the bus pass application?

Yes, a Bonafide Certificate from the educational institution may be required during the verification process. Additionally, valid identity proof and enrollment certification from the institute may also be necessary for verification purposes.
<br>

### Can students renew their existing bus passes through the same online system, or do they need to visit physical offices for renewal?

The online system supports multiple functions including registration, new applications, renewals, and application status inquiries. Students can conveniently renew their passes through the same digital platform without visiting physical offices.
<br>

### If a student belongs to Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, or Scheduled Caste Backward Class category, do they need to submit any specific documentation?

Yes, students must submit a Social Category Certificate to establish their category status. The application form requires category details to be filled, and supporting documentation must be provided accordingly.
<br>

### What authentication method is used to verify the applicant's identity before final submission of the bus pass application form?

An One-Time Password is sent to the registered mobile number provided in the application form. Applicants must enter this One-Time Password correctly to authenticate and complete the submission process.
<br>

### Besides the web portal, is there any mobile-based application facility available for students to apply for or manage their bus passes?

Yes, the Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation mobile application is available on the Google Play Store. This app facilitates easy access to pass details, bus schedules, application submission, and other related services.
<br>

### When making payment for the bus pass, what are the available payment options for students completing their applications?

Students can make payment through Unified Payments Interface or opt for counter payment at bus depots. The system displays the total fees including bus pass charges, Identity Document card amount if applicable, and any toll charges.
<br>

### If a student has previously held a bus pass under this scheme, do they need to submit their old pass documentation?

Yes, the Previous Bus Pass is listed among the required documents if applicable. Students who are renewing or reapplying should submit their previous bus pass as part of the documentation process.
<br>
