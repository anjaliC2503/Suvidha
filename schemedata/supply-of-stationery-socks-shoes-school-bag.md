## Brief Description

Supply of School Stationery, Socks, Shoes, School Bag, etc. to all Students of Std. I to VIII studying in Govt Schools

## Detailed Description

Provision of essential stationery items, socks, shoes and school bags to students in classes I to VIII of Government schools. This includes notebooks, a geometry box, writing instruments, an eraser, a sharpener, along with footwear and a school bag. It ensures that every child, regardless of their socio-economic background, has the basic physical tools required for a productive learning environment. The scheme covers all students from Standard I to VIII.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

1. Reduces financial burden on parents by providing a comprehensive school kit.
2. Provision of free stationery kits includes school bag, pairs of socks, school shoes and a complete stationery set (notebooks, pens/pencils etc).
3. Providing shoes and socks promotes better foot health and hygiene for students commuting to school.
4. Promotes regular school attendance by supporting foundational needs.
<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1.	The applicant should be a student.
2.	The applicant should be studying in class 1st to 8th.
3.	The applicant should be studying in a government school in UT of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.
<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

1. No separate application is required.
1. Eligible students enrolled in government schools are automatically covered under the scheme.
