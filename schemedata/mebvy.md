## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote horticulture development in Uttarakhand by providing farmers with subsidized fruit plants, seeds, pesticides, & horticulture infrastructure such as Cool Houses & Refrigerator Vans, thereby increasing productivity, reducing post-harvest losses, & enhancing farmers’ income.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Mukhyamantri Ekeekrit Bagwani Vikas Yojana” is implemented by the Department of Horticulture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote horticulture development in Uttarakhand by providing farmers with subsidized fruit plants, seeds, pesticides, and horticulture infrastructure such as Cool Houses and Refrigerator Vans, thereby increasing productivity, reducing post-harvest losses, and enhancing farmers’ income.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, the following assistance is provided:

  - 50% subsidy on the cost of fruit plants, open-field vegetable seeds, spice seeds, and flower seeds. No direct monetary assistance is provided; the seeds and plants are supplied at a subsidized rate.
  - 60% subsidy on the cost of pesticides and other plant protection chemicals. No direct cash is given; the medicines are provided at a subsidized rate.
  - 50% subsidy on the total cost of ₹15.00 lakh for Cool Houses with a capacity of 30 MT.
  - 50% subsidy on the total cost of ₹26.00 lakh for Refrigerator Vans with a capacity of 9 MT.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant must be a farmer and hold a valid Udhyan Card.
1. The farmer must either own agricultural land or have land on lease.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The application form can be obtained from the officers or personnel working at the Horticulture Mobile team Centres. It can also be downloaded from the department&#39;s website: [https://shm.uk.gov.in](https://shm.uk.gov.in/). The application should be submitted approximately two months before planting saplings or applying medicines.  - Along with the application form, the following documents are required: land-related documents such as Khasra and Khatauni; for leased land, a lease certificate; and identification documents including Aadhar card, PAN card, Udhyan card, as well as bank account details and mobile number. Currently, the application process is offline.
  - The farmer must submit the completed application form along with the required documents to the Horticulture Mobile team Centre. The proposal should be submitted in the prescribed format of the application form. If the farmer faces any difficulty in filling out the form, the concerned personnel at the Horticulture Mobile team Centre are responsible for assisting him. The centre then forwards the farmer&#39;s proposal to the district level, and from there to the Directorate. Once the Directorate approves the proposal, a government order is issued, and the districts receive the approval/work order accordingly.
  - After approval, the district officer sends an acceptance letter to the farmer and also informs them over the phone. The farmer can then purchase fruit saplings, seeds, or medicines from the Horticulture Mobile team Centre at a 50% subsidy.
  - In the case of Refrigerator Vans and Cool Houses, the subsidy is provided only after the farmer purchases the van or constructs the Cool House, following a site inspection by departmental officials. Beneficiaries under the scheme are selected on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to the availability of the budget.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Farmers who have their own or leased agricultural land/garden and hold a valid horticulture card are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What assistance is provided under the scheme for seeds and plants?

Farmers receive a 50% subsidy on fruit plants, open-field vegetable seeds, spice seeds, and flower seeds.
<br>

### Is any monetary assistance provided for seeds and plants?

No, the seeds and plants are provided at a subsidized rate, not as direct cash.
<br>

### What assistance is provided for pesticides and other chemicals?

Farmers receive a 60% subsidy on the cost of pesticides and other plant protection chemicals.
<br>

### How can farmers obtain the application form?

The application form can be obtained from Horticulture Mobile Squad Centres or downloaded from the department's website.
<br>

### When should the application be submitted?

Applications should be submitted approximately two months before planting saplings or using medicines.
<br>

### Where should the application be submitted?

Applications should be submitted to the Horticulture Mobile Squad Centre along with the required documents.
<br>

### How is the application processed?

The Centre forwards the application to the district level, which then sends it to the Directorate for approval. Once approved, a government order is issued for implementation.
<br>

### How is the subsidy for Cool Houses and Refrigerator Vans disbursed?

The subsidy is provided after the farmer purchases the van or constructs the Cool House, following a site inspection by departmental officials.
<br>

### How are beneficiaries selected?

Beneficiaries are selected on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to the availability of budget.
