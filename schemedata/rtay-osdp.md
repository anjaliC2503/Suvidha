## Brief Description

The scheme aims to make younger generation aware of the contribution and sacrifices of great tiatr personalities of the past. It provides collaboration and financial assistance to produce special dedicated programmes on late artistes.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Remembering Tiatr Artistes of Yesteryears - Organisation of Special Dedicated Programmes" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to make the younger generation aware of the contribution and sacrifices of great tiatr personalities of the past who are no more with us, addressing the problem that today's younger generation does not know much about these pioneers whose contribution to Goan tiatr is immense. The scheme provides collaboration with troupes/institutions/individuals for producing special dedicated programmes on the lives of particular artistes and financial assistance with auditorium/hall facilities for staging programmes. The applicant must be a troupe/institution/individual willing to produce special programmes dedicated to particular artistes and must obtain No Objection Certificate from the nearest family members of the late artiste. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications for this scheme are accepted offline.
<br>

## Benefits

**Financial Assistance:** Tiatr Academy of Goa will provide financial assistance as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for each show.
**Infrastructure Support:** Provision of auditorium/hall to stage the programme.
**Village Performances:** Financial assistance as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for erecting temporary pandal, providing stage, sound, etc. for village performances.
**Additional Shows:** Option for additional 2 shows in villages at an amount as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa plus additional amount as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for Pandal/Hall/Auditorium including Stage, Sound, etc.
**Documentation:** Tiatr Academy of Goa will document the programme and preserve it at Tiatr Academy of Goa's library.
**Marketing Materials:** Provision of banner for tiatr performances and logo for musical shows.
**Number of Shows:** Maximum of 4 shows at different places including 1 show at the area of residence.
_* Tiatr Academy of Goa has no objection if sponsorship is taken by the producer from other sources._
<br>

> ***Conditions***

_*All shows must be shown to the public free without charging any tickets._
_*Programme duration should be at least 2 hours for musical show and 3 hours for tiatr._
_*Display Tiatr Academy of Goa banner compulsorily on front side wing of stage for tiatr or minimum size of 6ft. x 10ft. backdrop with Tiatr Academy of Goa logo for musical show._
_*Return banner to Tiatr Academy of Goa after all shows are staged._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a troupe, institution, or individual.
- The applicant must wish to produce and stage a special programme dedicated to a particular artiste of yesteryears.
- The applicant must obtain No Objection Certificate from the nearest family members of the late artiste.
- The applicant must invite family members compulsorily for the function.
- The applicant must stage the inaugural show preferably in Panjim, Margao or in the village of the artiste where he resided.
- The applicant must submit the proposal well in advance giving all details of the programme including script and lyrics to be presented including the names of the artistes.
- The applicant must comply with all conditions specified by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
- The applicant must not make any adverse remark in any form either written or verbal against any Governments/Individuals/Institutions in the scripts/song lyrics/performances/speeches.
- In all advertisements, oral, print or electronic media, banners, etc., the following must be inserted/published/announced: 'This programme is held in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa'.

_* Tiatr Academy of Goa has no objection if sponsorship is taken by the producer from other sources._
<br>

***Programme Content Requirements***

- The programme must include staging of tiatr/one-act plays written by the artistes of yesteryears.
- The programme can include musical activities such as songs and music composed and sung by the late artiste if he/she was a singer or music composer/musician.
- The programme should include narration of history of the life of the late artiste to whom the programme is dedicated.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. Alternatively, the applicant may visit the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa to collect the form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
_*Applications are considered on a "First come First Serve" basis, subject to the availability of funds._
_*Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to make any changes in the rules._
_*If adverse remarks are proved, Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to disqualify the performance and refrain from making payment or recall the payment made._
<br>

##### **Post Application Processes**
**Step 1:****** ***Coordinate with Tiatr Academy of Goa***
The applicant must discuss and decide about the Chief Guest/Guest of Honour well in advance with Tiatr Academy of Goa, as the President or Vice President of Tiatr Academy of Goa will either be the Chief Guest/Guest of Honour at the function.
**Step 2:** ***Collect Banner and Materials***
The applicant must collect the banner from the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa before the performances (for tiatr) or arrange for backdrop with Tiatr Academy of Goa logo (for musical shows) and ensure proper display during all shows.
**Step 3:** ***Stage Performances***
The applicant must stage the approved programme ensuring all conditions are met including free admission for public, proper acknowledgment of Tiatr Academy of Goa collaboration in all advertisements and media, and maintaining the specified duration for each type of performance.
**Step 4:** ***Documentation and Reporting***
The applicant must document the programme through photographs and submit 5 copies to Tiatr Academy of Goa for records, ensuring two photographs show the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner during performance.
**Step 5:** ***Submit Financial Documentation***
The applicant must prepare and submit a detailed statement of expenditure with all original bills (stamped receipts if amount exceeds ₹5,000/-) to Tiatr Academy of Goa for reimbursement of approved expenses.
**Step 6:** ***Return Materials***
The applicant must return the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner to the office after all shows are completed to maintain the academy's inventory and complete the programme cycle.
<br>

##### ***Application Deadline***
Applications must reach the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days in advance along with typed script/lyrics.
<br>

- 5 Copies Of Photographs Of The Function For Records (Two Of Which Should Show The Tiatr Academy of Goa Banner During Performance)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum duration requirement for performances under this cultural programme dedicated to late artistes?

The programme duration should be at least 2 hours for musical shows and 3 hours for tiatr performances to qualify for support.
<br>

### Can individual artists apply for financial assistance to organize memorial programmes for deceased tiatr personalities?

Yes, individuals can apply along with troupes and institutions. All three categories are eligible to produce and stage special programmes dedicated to particular artistes of yesteryears.
<br>

### Is it mandatory to obtain permission from the deceased artiste's family members before organizing such commemorative programmes?

Yes, applicants must obtain a No Objection Certificate from the nearest family members of the late artiste and also invite them compulsorily for the function.
<br>

### What specific documentation must be submitted along with the application for organizing these memorial cultural performances?

Required documents include prescribed application form, self-attested Aadhaar and Permanent Account Number card copies, complete script and lyrics, No Objection Certificate from family members, and typed script or lyrics.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions on the content that can be included in performances honoring late tiatr personalities?

Applicants must not make any adverse remarks in written or verbal form against any governments, individuals, or institutions in scripts, song lyrics, performances, or speeches.
<br>

### What is the maximum number of shows permitted under this scheme for commemorating deceased artiste contributions?

Maximum of 4 shows are allowed at different places, including 1 show that must be staged at the area of residence of the late artiste.
<br>

### Can organizers charge admission fees from audiences attending these special programmes dedicated to late cultural personalities?

No, all shows must be shown to the public free of cost without charging any tickets from the audience members.
<br>

### What type of financial support is provided for village performances under this memorial programme scheme?

Financial assistance is provided for erecting temporary pandal, stage setup, sound systems, and other requirements as amounts fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
<br>

### Where should the inaugural show preferably be staged when organizing programmes in memory of deceased artistes?

The inaugural show should preferably be staged in Panjim, Margao, or in the village where the late artiste resided during their lifetime.
<br>

### What promotional materials and infrastructure support are provided to organizers of these commemorative cultural programmes?

Provision includes banners for tiatr performances, logos for musical shows, auditorium or hall facilities, and marketing materials for promoting the events.
<br>

### Are organizers allowed to seek additional sponsorship from external sources while participating in this memorial scheme?

Yes, Tiatr Academy of Goa has no objection if sponsorship is taken by the producer from other sources beyond the provided assistance.
<br>

### What mandatory display requirements must be followed during performances honoring late tiatr personalities from yesteryears?

Tiatr Academy of Goa banner must be displayed compulsorily on front side wing of stage for tiatr or minimum 6 feet x 10 feet backdrop with logo for musical shows.
<br>

### What type of content must be included in programmes dedicated to commemorating contributions of deceased cultural artistes?

Programmes must include staging of tiatr or one-act plays written by the artistes, musical activities if applicable, and narration of the late artiste's life history.
<br>

### What financial documentation is required to be submitted after completion of all shows under this commemorative scheme?

Statement of expenditure enclosing all original bills must be submitted, with stamped receipts required if any amount exceeds ₹5,000, along with 5 copies of function photographs.
<br>
