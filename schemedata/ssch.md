## Brief Description

The Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs Department, Government of Mizoram launched the State Senior Citizens Home for the homeless aged persons. It provides shelter and facilities to homeless elderly individuals aged 60 and above, who lack family support.

## Detailed Description

The Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs Department, Government of Mizoram launched the State Senior Citizens Home for the homeless aged persons. It provides shelter and recreational facilities to homeless elderly individuals aged 60 and above, who lack family support. This Home is the only one of its kind in the state and has a capacity of 25 residents.

## Benefits

- Safe and secure living space for homeless elderly residents.
- Recreational and wellness programs to enhance quality of life.
- Access to essential amenities and supportive care.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Mizoram.
1. The applicant's age should be 60 years or older.
1. The applicant should have no supporter/relative. 

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://socialwelfare.mizoram.gov.in/uploads/attachments/2024/03/bc18c650024fda5381c0bb4a22975010/pages-15-ssc-home-a-awm-dilna-form.pdf) from the Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs Mizoram official website.
**Step-2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step-3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Director of Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs, Mizoram.
**Step-4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the Director of Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs, Mizoram to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the State Senior Citizens Home?

The State Senior Citizens Home is a shelter in Aizawl for homeless elderly residents of Mizoram.

### Who can apply for admission to the Home?

Mizoram residents aged 60 or above without family support are eligible to apply.

### How many residents can the Home accommodate?

The facility has a capacity of 25 residents.

### Where is the Home located?

It is situated in Ainawn Veng, Aizawl, Mizoram.

### What amenities are provided at the Home?

Residents receive housing, recreational activities, and essential support services.

### Is there any cost for staying at the Home?

The Social Welfare Department covers all resident expenses.

### How do I apply for admission?

Complete the application form and submit it to the Director of Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs, Mizoram.

### Are non-residents of Mizoram eligible?

No, only residents of Mizoram can apply for admission.

### Is medical care provided?

Basic medical care is available, and arrangements can be made for additional needs.

### What kind of recreational facilities are available?

The Home offers social activities and programs for mental and physical well-being.

### Who administers the Home?

The Home is managed by a Welfare Officer and assisted by subordinate staff.

### Can someone with family support apply for admission?

No, only homeless elderly without family support are eligible.
