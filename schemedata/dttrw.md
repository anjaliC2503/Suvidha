## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage research work in the field of Tiatr and facilitate writers to take up research work in different aspects of Tiatr, its Song & Music. Research work will be published in book form with remuneration provided to researchers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Documentation of Tiatr through Research Work" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to encourage research work in the field of Tiatr and facilitate writers to take up research work in different aspects of Tiatr, its Song & Music. The scheme provides publication of research work in book form with a minimum of 500 copies printed and remuneration as fixed by TAG as per the researcher's experience. The applicant must be a researcher interested in writing books on tiatr after doing thorough research and must submit three typed copies of the manuscript for approval. The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG). The applications for this scheme are accepted offline.
<br>

## Benefits

> ***Publication Benefits***


- TAG will publish the research work in book form after thorough research on the subject.
- The publication may be in English or Konkani (Roman Script).
- TAG will print a minimum of 500 copies of the book.
- 100 copies will be given to the writer.
- The remaining copies will be used by TAG for various purposes.

> ***Financial Benefits***

The author/researcher will be given remuneration as fixed by TAG as per his/her experience for the research work undertaken.
> ***Editorial Support***


- The manuscript approved by the Recommendation Board shall be edited by a member of Editorial Board.
- Editing will include checking for spelling and orthography.

***Conditions***
_*The manuscript received from the author/researcher will be considered for publication only after getting approval from the Recommendation Board appointed by TAG._
_*The author/researcher will have to provide TAG, 3 (three) typed copies of the manuscript._
_*If approved, the author/researcher will have to submit a soft copy of the manuscript on a Compact Disc/Digital Versatile Disc/pen drive._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a researcher interested in writing books on tiatr after doing thorough research on the subject.
- Preference will be given to any researcher who has already done research on any aspect of Tiatr, its Song & Music.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields: name of the applicant, complete address, contact number, email address, Aadhaar Card number, Permanent Account Number Card number, name of the research, and aspects of tiatr on which research is done. Attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment_ 
<br>

> ***Post-Application Processes***

**Step 1:** _Recommendation Board Review_
The submitted manuscript will be reviewed by the Recommendation Board consisting of 2 members appointed by TAG. The board members will be paid a suitable honorarium as fixed by TAG for the work undertaken.
**Step 2:** _Approval and Soft Copy Submission_
If the manuscript is approved by the Recommendation Board, submit a soft copy of the manuscript on a Compact Disc/Digital Versatile Disc/pen drive to the Tiatr Academy of Goa.
**Step 3:** _Editorial Process_
The approved manuscript will undergo editing by a member of the Editorial Board, which includes checking for spelling and orthography.
**Step 4:** _Publication and Remuneration_
Upon completion of the editorial process, TAG will proceed with printing a minimum of 500 copies of the book and provide remuneration to the author/researcher as per the fixed rates based on experience.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the essential documents I need to submit at the initial application stage for manuscript consideration and approval?

You must submit self-attested copy of Permanent Account Number Card, self-attested copy of Aadhaar Card, and three typed copies of the manuscript for the approval process.
<br>

### How many copies of my research book will be printed, and how many will I receive as the author?

A minimum of 500 copies of the book will be printed, and you will receive 100 copies while the remaining copies will be used by Tiatr Academy of Goa for various purposes.
<br>

<br>

### Will my approved manuscript undergo any editorial review process before publication, and what specific aspects will be checked during editing?

Yes, the approved manuscript will be edited by a member of Editorial Board, which includes checking for spelling and orthography to ensure publication quality.
<br>

### In what languages can I submit my research manuscript for consideration under this documentation and publication initiative?

The publication may be in English or Konkani using Roman Script, allowing flexibility for researchers comfortable with either language option.
<br>

### Who will review my submitted manuscript to determine whether it meets the standards for publication under this research initiative?

The submitted manuscript will be reviewed by the Recommendation Board consisting of 2 members appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for approval consideration.
<br>

### What additional materials must I provide if my manuscript receives approval from the reviewing committee for publication purposes?

If approved, you must submit a soft copy of the manuscript on a Compact Disc/Digital Versatile Disc/pen drive to the Tiatr Academy of Goa.
<br>

### Are there any specific researcher qualifications or backgrounds that receive preference during the manuscript evaluation and selection process?

Preference will be given to any researcher who has already done research on any aspect of Tiatr, its Song and Music.
<br>

### What type of research work and subject matter should I focus on to be eligible for this publication opportunity?

You must be a researcher interested in writing books on tiatr after doing thorough research on different aspects of Tiatr, its Song and Music.
<br>

### How many physical copies of my typed manuscript do I need to submit initially for the review and approval process?

You will have to provide Tiatr Academy of Goa 3 three typed copies of the manuscript for the initial approval process.
<br>

### Will I receive any financial compensation for my research work, and how is the remuneration amount determined for authors?

Yes, you will be given remuneration as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa as per your experience for the research work undertaken.
<br>

### What is the complete submission address where I should send my application form along with all required documents and manuscript copies?

Submit to Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001.
<br>

### What essential information about my research must I include in the application form to complete the mandatory fields properly?

You must fill in name of the applicant, complete address, contact number, email address, Aadhaar Card number, Permanent Account Number Card number, name of the research, and aspects of tiatr on which research is done.
<br>

### What happens to my manuscript after I submit it, and what is the sequence of evaluation and approval processes?

The manuscript undergoes Recommendation Board review by 2 members, and if approved, proceeds to editorial process before publication with remuneration provided.
<br>

### Do the board members who review manuscripts receive any compensation for their evaluation work, and how is this process structured?

Yes, the Recommendation Board members will be paid a suitable honorarium as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for the manuscript review work undertaken.
