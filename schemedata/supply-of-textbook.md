## Brief Description

Supply of Textbook to all Students for IX to XII studying in Govt Schools

## Detailed Description

Provision of providing Textbooks to all students of CBSE Board and Gujarat Board in classes IX to XII of Government schools. It ensures equitable access to quality education by providing the basic books required for a productive learning environment. The aim is to support students from diverse socio-economic backgrounds by ensuring the timely availability of prescribed textbooks required for academic learning and classroom activities. Textbooks are provided free of cost to all students studying in Classes IX to XII in Government Schools, thereby reducing the financial burden on parents and promoting continuity in education. The availability of textbooks at the beginning of the academic session helps create a conducive and effective learning environment, enhances students’ participation in classroom teaching, and improves overall academic performance.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

1. Ensures timely availability of prescribed textbooks to all students.
2. Reduces the financial burden on parents by providing textbooks free of cost.
3. Promotes equal learning opportunities for students from all socio-economic backgrounds.
4. Enhances classroom participation, regular attendance, and overall academic performance.
<br>

	
<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1.	The applicant should be a student.
2.	The applicant should be studying in class 9th to 12th.
3.	The applicant should be studying in a government school in UT of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.
<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

1. No separate application is required.
1. Eligible students enrolled in government schools are automatically covered under the scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of this scheme?

### Which students are eligible?

<br>

### Are private school students eligible?

No, the scheme is only for government school students.
<br>

### Is there any application fee?

No, the application is completely free.
<br>

### Is the scheme for both boys and girls?

Yes, it benefits both boys and girls equally.
