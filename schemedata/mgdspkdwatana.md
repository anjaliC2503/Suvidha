## Brief Description

The scheme provides ₹51,000 as a wedding gift to the sister or daughter of a martyred soldier to honor their sacrifice and support the dependent family.

## Detailed Description

The “Marriage Grant to Daughter/Sister of Personnel Killed/ Disabled in War/ Action/Terrorist Attack/Naxalite Attack/IS Duty” scheme was launched by the Home Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to honor and support the families of soldiers who sacrificed their lives for the nation. Under this scheme, a monetary assistance is provided as a wedding gift to the sister or daughter of a martyred officer/soldier. 
<br>

## Benefits

A gift amount of ₹51,000 is provided for the marriage of the sister or daughter of a martyred soldier/officer.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a dependent (sister or daughter) of a martyred officer or soldier.
1. The deceased officer/soldier must be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The martyrdom must have occurred during war, military operation, Naxalite attack, terrorist attack, or internal security duty.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant (sister or daughter of the martyr) must obtain and fill out the application form available at the District Sainik Welfare Office.
**Step 2:** Attach all required documents including verification from the Sainik Welfare Officer and Battle Casualty Report.
**Step 3:** Submit the completed application form along with the affidavit and supporting certificates to the District Sainik Welfare Office.
**Step 4:** The District Sainik Welfare Officer verifies the application and forwards it to the Directorate of Sainik Welfare, Bhopal.
**Step 5:** After approval, the sanctioned amount is transferred directly to the applicant’s bank account.

## Documents Required

- Identity Proof
- Proof of Relationship with Martyr (Birth Certificate/Family Tree/Legal Affidavit)
- Residence Certificate of the Martyred Officer/Soldier (Madhya Pradesh Domicile Proof)
- Death Certificate of the Martyred Officer/Soldier
- Battle Casualty Report or Daily Order Part-II (Issued by the Defense Unit)
- Certificate of Posting in CI Grid/Operation Area (as applicable)
- Marriage Certificate or Proof of Marriage (Invitation Card or Declaration)
- Medical Report and Inquiry Report Related to Death (if applicable)
- Self-Declaration Affidavit Stating Non-Receipt of Similar Benefit
- Bank Account Details of the Applicant
- Recommendation from the District Sainik Welfare Officer

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for this scheme?

The sister or daughter of a martyred officer or soldier who was a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### What is the amount of assistance provided?

Financial assistance of ₹51,000 as a one-time gift.
<br>

### Is there any application fee?

No, the application process is completely free.
<br>

### What documents are required for application?

Proof of relationship, domicile, death certificate, battle casualty report, and marriage proof.
<br>

### Can the benefit be claimed if the martyr’s family already received Central Government aid?

Not for the same purpose; a self-declaration affidavit must confirm this.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable to all types of martyrs?

Yes, including those who died in war, terrorist attacks, Naxalite operations, or internal security duties.
<br>

### Where should the application be submitted?

The application is submitted at the District Sainik Welfare Office of the applicant’s district.
<br>
