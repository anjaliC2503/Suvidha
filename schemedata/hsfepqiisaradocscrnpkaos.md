## Brief Description

To motivate growers in Karnataka and other states to improve production and productivity through systematic replanting of diseased, old, and senile plantations, and to promote area expansion under cultivation.

## Detailed Description

Under the “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom” implemented by the Spices Board India during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26), assistance is provided for Small Cardamom Replanting/New Planting in Karnataka and other states to motivate growers to improve production and productivity through systematic replanting of diseased, old, and senile plantations, as well as to promote area expansion, in accordance with the modalities and scheme guidelines approved by the Competent Authority.
<br>

## Benefits

1. 33.33% of cost of cultivation of subject to a maximum of ₹75,000/- per Ha for General Category and 75% of cost of cultivation ₹1,68,000/- per Ha for SC/ST payable in two annual installments of ₹37,500/- and 84,000/- each year respectively.
1. Individual registered cardamom growers having a land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha.
1. Minimum area to be replanted under the programme is 0.10 Ha in a continuous block and the maximum area is limited to 2 Ha per beneficiary per year and a maximum of 8 Ha. in block year of 5 years from 2021-2026.The plant population is minimum 3000 Nos. per ha for closer planting [pure crop] and 1500 Nos in intercropped plantations.
1. Replanting cycle is considered as 8 years and a second time benefit for the same area replanted by a beneficiary will be available only on completion of 8 years except on exclusive circumstances like natural calamities, devastating pest and disease attacks as jointly certified by a committee constituted by Board.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicants from Karnataka and other states having potential areas suitable for Small Cardamom cultivation are eligible to apply under the scheme.
1. The applicants undertaking replanting/new planting as a pure crop using suckers will be given preference, subject to final inspection and recommendation by the designated officials.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom, particularly for small cardamom replanting and new planting activities?

The objective of the scheme is to motivate growers in Karnataka and other cardamom-growing states to improve production and productivity through systematic replanting of diseased, old, and senile plantations, as well as to encourage area expansion under scientifically managed cultivation practices.
<br>

### Who are the eligible beneficiaries under this scheme for undertaking small cardamom replanting or new planting activities?

Individual registered cardamom growers having land holdings ranging from 0.10 hectares to 8 hectares are eligible under the scheme, provided they meet all prescribed guidelines and documentation requirements.
<br>

### What is the minimum and maximum land area allowed for availing assistance under the replanting and new planting programme for small cardamom cultivation?

The minimum eligible area for replanting is 0.10 hectares in a continuous block, while the maximum limit is 2 hectares per beneficiary per year, with an overall ceiling of 8 hectares during the 5-year block period from 2021–2026.
<br>

### What types of land and plantation conditions are considered eligible for assistance under the scheme?

The scheme supports replanting of diseased, old, and senile plantations, as well as new planting activities in suitable areas, with preference given to pure crop cultivation using healthy suckers under recommended agronomic practices.
<br>

### What is the financial assistance or subsidy pattern provided under this scheme for general category and SC/ST beneficiaries?

The scheme provides 33.33% of the cost of cultivation subject to a maximum of Rs. 75,000 per hectare for General Category beneficiaries, and 75% of the cost of cultivation subject to a maximum of Rs. 1,68,000 per hectare for SC/ST beneficiaries.
<br>

### In what manner is the subsidy amount disbursed to the beneficiaries under the scheme guidelines?

The eligible subsidy is released in two annual installments based on compliance with scheme conditions, field verification, and recommendations of designated officials after inspection.
<br>

### What is the required plant population density for small cardamom cultivation under this scheme?

The scheme mandates a minimum plant population of 3,000 plants per hectare for pure crop (closer planting) and 1,500 plants per hectare for intercropped plantations to ensure optimum productivity.
<br>

### What type of planting method is encouraged or given preference under this replanting and new planting programme?

Preference is given to replanting or new planting as a pure crop using healthy suckers, as this method ensures better plant establishment, higher productivity, and improved crop quality.
<br>

### How is the selection of eligible beneficiaries finalized under the scheme implementation process?

Final selection is carried out after field inspection by designated officials who verify compliance with scheme norms and recommend eligible cases for assistance.
<br>

### Are farmers from all states eligible to participate in the scheme, or is it limited to specific regions only?

Farmers from Karnataka and other states having suitable agro-climatic conditions for small cardamom cultivation are eligible, subject to fulfillment of state-specific documentation requirements.
<br>
