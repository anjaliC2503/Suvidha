## Brief Description

The "Production of Certified Seeds of Maize" scheme, aims to promote the production of certified maize seeds by providing subsidy to farmers in select districts of Tamil Nadu.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Production of Certified Seeds of Maize” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, is designed to support maize farmers in specific districts of Tamil Nadu. By offering a subsidy of ₹1,000/- per quintal, the scheme encourages farmers to produce certified maize seeds, ensuring a steady supply of high-quality seeds.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹,1000/- per quintal.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary should be a Farmer.
  - The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.
  - The beneficiary should be a maize-growing farmer.
  - The beneficiary should belong to any of the following district of Tamil Nadu: Coimbatore, Dindigul, Erode, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, Namakkal, Perambalur, Virudhunagar, Theni, Villupuram, Vellore, Salem, Pudukkottai, Madurai, Sivagangai, Trichy, Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office.
**Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). 
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office:
> **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer.

> **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer.

> **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Production of Certified Seeds of Maize” scheme?

The "Production of Certified Seeds of Maize" scheme, aims to promote the production of certified maize seeds by providing subsidy to farmers in select districts of Tamil Nadu.

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Maize farmers in select districts of Tamil Nadu are eligible to apply.

### What is the benefit under this scheme?

Farmers receive a subsidy of [?]1,000/- per quintal for certified maize seeds.

### Which districts are covered under this scheme?

Coimbatore, Dindigul, Erode, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, Namakkal, Perambalur, Virudhunagar, Theni, Villupuram, Vellore, Salem, Pudukkottai, Madurai, Sivagangai, Trichy, Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi districts are covered.

### Is this scheme available for all farmers?

Preference is given to small/marginal farmers, SC/ST farmers, and women farmers or groups.

### How to  apply for the scheme?

Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer at the village level, Deputy Agricultural Officer at the block level, or the Joint Director of Agriculture at the district level.

### What documents are required to apply?

Identity proof, land ownership proof, bank passbook, caste certificate (if applicable), and filled application form are required.

### Is there any age or income limit to apply?

No. Specific age or income limit is not required to apply for this scheme.

### Is there a quota for women farmers?

Yes, 20% of the scheme benefits are reserved for women farmers and women's groups.

### Is there any reservation for SC/ST farmers?

Yes, 24% of the scheme's benefits are reserved for SC/ST farmers.

### Whom to contact for more information?

Farmers can contact their local Assistant Agricultural Officer, Deputy Agricultural Officer, or Joint Director of Agriculture for further details.
