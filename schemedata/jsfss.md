## Brief Description

The "Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme (JSFSS)" by the Department of Food, Public Distribution and Consumer Affairs, Government of Jharkhand, aims to ensure food security by providing subsidized/free rice to eligible Green Card holders.

## Detailed Description

The "Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme (JSFSS)" by the Department of Food, Public Distribution and Consumer Affairs, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide subsidized/free food grains to eligible beneficiaries. Through this scheme, food grain, primarily rice, is provided at a subsidized rate or free of cost to individuals holding a Green Card. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Food and Consumer Affairs, Ranchi.

## Benefits

1. Subsidized/free rice is provided to Green Card holders beneficiaries under JSFSS.
1. Benefits are delivered directly through the Directorate of Food and Consumer Affairs.

<br>

**NOTE****:** Priority is given to individuals from economically weaker sections.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Jharkhand.
1. The applicant must possess a Green Card issued under JSFSS.

## Application Process

### Offline

1. Visit the nearest ration shop or designated enrollment center with the required documents.
1. If an Aadhaar is not available, apply for Aadhaar enrollment at an authorized center.
1. Submit the Aadhaar number or Aadhaar Enrolment Identification Slip during registration.
1. Verify the eligibility and documents with the designated officer.
1. Collect the food grains from the distribution center as per the monthly schedule.

## Documents Required

1. Aadhaar Card or Aadhaar Enrolment Identification Slip.
1. Bank or Post Office Passbook with Photo.
1. Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card.
1. Passport (if available).
1. Ration Card.
1. Voter Identity Card.
1. MGNREGA Card.
1. Kisan Photo Passbook.
1. Driving License issued under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
1. Certificate of Identity with a photo issued by a Gazetted Officer or Tehsildar on official letterhead.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Households with Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) cards, Priority Household (PHH) cards under NFSA, and Green Cards under JSFSS are eligible.

### What documents are required to apply?

Aadhaar Card, Ration Card, and other valid identity documents like Voter ID or PAN Card are needed.

### Can I apply if I don’t have an Aadhaar Card?

Yes, you can apply using an Aadhaar Enrollment Identification Slip along with other valid documents.

### Where can I collect the food grains?

Food grains can be collected from the designated Public Distribution System (PDS) ration shops.

### What is the frequency of benefit distribution?

Benefits under this scheme are distributed on a monthly basis.

### What should I do if Aadhaar authentication fails?

Alternative mechanisms like iris scan, face authentication, or OTP-based verification will be used for authentication.

### Is there a fee for Aadhaar enrollment?

No, Aadhaar enrollment is free of cost.

### What happens if I miss collecting food grains on the designated date?

The applicant should contact your local ration shop or the designated authority for assistance with missed collections.

### Are the food grains provided free of cost?

Food grains are provided at highly subsidized rates under this scheme.

### Can I avail benefits if I move to another state?

No, the scheme is applicable only within the state of Jharkhand.
