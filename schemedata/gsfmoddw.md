## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹50,000/- per daughter for the marriage of up to two daughters of destitute widows who are receiving widow pension and whose total monthly income from all sources does not exceed ₹4,000/-, or who belong to BPL families.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Grant for the Marriage of Daughters of Destitute Widow&quot; has been implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the marriage of up to two daughters of destitute widows who are receiving widow pension from the State Government.
<br>

## Benefits

**Grant Amount:** ₹50,000/- per daughter, for the marriage of up to two daughters.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant must be a destitute widow.
1. The widow must be receiving widow pension.
1. The family’s total monthly income from all sources must not exceed ₹4,000/-, or the family must belong to the BPL category.
1. At the time of marriage, the bride’s age must be between 18 and 45 years.
1. At the time of marriage, the groom’s age must be between 21 and 45 years.
1. The grant is applicable for the marriage of up to two daughters.

**Note:** The Social Welfare Department verifies whether the widow is receiving widow pension or not.
<br>

## Application Process

### Online

- The applicant can apply online either by themselves or through a Common Service Center (CSC) via the Department’s Pension Portal ([https://ssp.uk.gov.in](https://ssp.uk.gov.in)), the [UMANG](https://web.umang.gov.in) Mobile App, or the Apuni Sarkar Portal ([https://eservices.uk.gov.in](https://eservices.uk.gov.in)).  - **The following documents are mandatory at the time of applying:** Applicant&amp;#39;s mobile number, Aadhaar card, Passport-size Photo, Caste Certificate, Domicile/permanent residence certificate of Uttarakhand, CBS Bank Account Details (linked/seeded with Aadhaar), Valid Income Certificate or BPL Certificate, Ration Card, Copy of Family Register (only for rural areas), Bride and groom&amp;#39;s date of birth certificate (High School certificate/Birth certificate/Family register/Aadhaar card), Marriage registration certificate, Affidavit (Applicant&amp;#39;s family has taken benefit only once before or has not taken benefit at all)  - It is mandatory to apply between 1st March to 28th February of the same year in which marriage takes place. If someone&amp;#39;s marriage takes place in January or February and the documents are not complete at that time, then in such a situation, one can apply before marriage but the benefit will be available only after uploading the marriage registration certificate on the portal. 
  - After applying online, the Assistant Social Welfare Officer will check it and on the basis of the investigation report/recommendation, the District Social Welfare Officer will approve the application form as per rules and the grant amount will be paid to the beneficiary&amp;#39;s account.

&gt; Whether the widow is receiving a pension from Social Welfare or not, the Social Welfare Department will confirm it, and a caste certificate is not required for availing benefits under this scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply?

The applicant must be a permanent resident of Uttarakhand, a destitute widow receiving widow pension, with a monthly income up to ₹4,000/- or belonging to a BPL family.
<br>

### How much financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

A grant of ₹50,000/- is provided per daughter.
<br>

### Is there an age limit for the bride and groom?

Yes.

- Bride: Minimum 18 years and maximum 45 years at the time of marriage.
- Groom: Minimum 21 years and maximum 45 years at the time of marriage.

<br>

### Can the benefit be availed for more than two daughters?

No. The benefit is available for the marriage of a maximum of two daughters only.
<br>

### Can a widow who is not receiving widow pension apply?

No. Only widows receiving widow pension from the State Government are eligible.
<br>

### How can one apply for the scheme?

You can apply online through the Department’s pension portal h[ttps://ssp.uk.gov.in/](https://ssp.uk.gov.in/), Umang mobile app, Apni Sarkar portal [https://eservices.uk.gov.in](https://eservices.uk.gov.in), or by visiting a Common Service Centre (CSC).
<br>

### Is there any verification process?

Yes. The Social Welfare Department verifies whether the widow is receiving pension and other eligibility conditions.
<br>

### Is there any restriction on family income?

Yes. The total monthly family income from all sources must be up to ₹4,000/- or the family should belong to the BPL category.
<br>

### Can widows living outside Uttarakhand apply?

No. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Uttarakhand.
<br>
