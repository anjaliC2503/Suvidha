## Brief Description

The PRL Award, instituted by the late Prof. Devendra Lal, former Director of the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), is awarded biennially to recognize outstanding Indian scientists for original work in Earth & Planetary systems, including areas like Oceanography, Atmospheric Sciences & Meteorology.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Physical Research Laboratory Award&quot;, instituted by the late Prof. Devendra Lal, former Director of the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), is awarded biennially to recognize outstanding Indian scientists for original work in Earth and Planetary systems, including areas like Oceanography, Atmospheric Sciences, and Meteorology. The award consists of a cash prize of ₹50,000 and a medal. 
**Discipline Areas:** 

  - Earth and Planetary systems, including areas of oceanography, atmospheric sciences, and meteorology.

<br>

## Benefits

- **Cash Prize:** An amount of ₹50,000/- and a medal.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be an Indian citizen.
  - The applicant should be a scientist. 
  - The applicant&#39;s age below 45 years as of 1st January 2021 (born on or after 02.01.1976) 
  - The applicant&#39;s contribution to Earth and Planetary Systems, Oceanography, Atmospheric Sciences, or Meteorology.

> **Note:**

Nominations may be made by the Head of Academic & Research Institutions/ Vice-Chancellors/ Deans of Universities/ Fellows of the Indian Academies of Science and Engineering/ and past recipients of these awards. 
<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant visits the [PRL website](https://www.prl.res.in/prl-eng/) and navigate to the "Opportunities" section.
**Step 2:** Click on "Awards Administered By PRL" and select scheme "PRL Award" 
**Step 3:** The candidate downloads the prescribed format of the [application form](https://view.officeapps.live.com/op/view.aspx?src=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.prl.res.in%2Fprl-eng%2Fsites%2Fdefault%2Ffiles%2Fdocuments%2Fnational-awards%2Fperforma-for-nomination.doc&wdOrigin=BROWSELINK). 
**Step 4:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 5:** Nominations should be submitted via email to the Director: DIRECTOR@PRL.RES.IN with the subject line "Nomination for The PRL Award".
<br>

## Documents Required

1. [Nomination Proforma](https://www.prl.res.in/prl-eng/sites/default/files/documents/national-awards/performa-for-nomination.doc).1. Significant contributions to the area of specialization by the nominee (two pages) during his/her research career.
1. Impact of the contributions in the field concerned, basic and/or applied
1. Specific contributions for which the nomination is being made
1. Complete list of Publications (with the names of authors in the order in which they appear in the publications).
1. List of TEN BEST publications, of which five should preferably be from the last five years (A separate listing of this is essential)
1. Reprints of five important publications in the last five years (e-file only).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the PRL Award?

It is a biennial award recognizing outstanding contributions in Earth and Planetary systems, Oceanography, Atmospheric Sciences, and Meteorology.

### Who is eligible for the award?

Indian scientists under 45 years of age (born on or after January 2, 1976).

### What is the prize for this award?

The award includes [?]50,000 and a medal.

### What areas of research are covered by the award?

Contributions in Earth and Planetary Systems, Oceanography, Atmospheric Sciences, and Meteorology.

### What documents are required for the nomination?

The nomination form, a two-page summary of contributions, an impact statement, specific contributions, a complete list of publications, and five key publications (in electronic format).

### Who can submit nominations?

Nominations can be submitted by Heads of academic and research institutions, Vice-Chancellors, Deans, Fellows of Indian Academies of Science and Engineering, and past recipients.

### Can previous nominees be re-nominated?

Yes, re-nominations are allowed by submitting a fresh nomination as per the updated guidelines

### How should nominations be submitted?

Nominations should be emailed to DIRECTOR@PRL.RES.IN.

### Is there a specific format for the nomination?

Yes, the nomination must be submitted in the prescribed format available on the PRL website.

### How are the award winners selected?

Selections are based on the originality and impact of the nominee's work, with special consideration given to contributions made in India.

### Is there a limit to the number of nominations that can be made?

There is no stated limit, but nominations must meet the eligibility and document requirements.
