## Brief Description

The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs" aims to empower the state's dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&quot; and &quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&quot;
The component &quot;Financial Support to MSMEs in ZED Certification&quot; aims to promote Zero Defect and Zero Effect (ZED) manufacturing among MSMEs. It focuses on quality improvement, energy efficiency, environmental sustainability, and the adoption of advanced manufacturing standards to support the &quot;Make in India&quot; campaign.

## Benefits

> **Financial Assistance:**


  - Subsidy of 50% of all charges after deducting assistance from the Government of India.
  - Maximum subsidy capped at ₹50,000 during the scheme&#39;s operative period.

> **Quality and Sustainability Enhancement:**


  - Encourages MSMEs to adopt ZED manufacturing practices.
  - Promotes energy-efficient manufacturing and eco-friendly production.

> **Professional Development:**


  - Develops professionals skilled in ZED certification and manufacturing.

> **Support for Prior Expenses:**


  - Eligible enterprises can claim assistance for expenses incurred during the previous scheme’s operative period.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The firm must be a Micro/Small/Medium enterprise.
   1. The enterprise must have obtained acknowledgment or registration from the Ministry of MSME or the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), as applicable.
   1. New manufacturing enterprises or existing ones undertaking expansion or diversification are eligible.
   1. The enterprise must commence commercial production during the scheme&#39;s operative period.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on "New Investor Registration" to access the registration form.
**Step 2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on "Submit". 
**Step 3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID.
**Step 4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.
<br>

> **Post-Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on "Login".
**Step 2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration.
**Step 3:** Click the "Login" button to access your account.
**Step 4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.
<br>

> **Helpdesk:** [Contact Here.](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/IFP-pages/contact.jsp?pagedisp=static)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs"?

The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs" is a state initiative designed to support and strengthen the MSME sector in Gujarat. It aims to enhance growth, encourage investments, and contribute to the vision of self-reliant Gujarat and India.

### When is the scheme operative?

The scheme is operative from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, covering a period of five years.

### What are the objectives of the scheme?

The scheme aims to boost MSME growth, enhance the industrial ecosystem, encourage investments, and contribute to Gujarat's and India's self-reliance vision.

### What are some initiatives included in the scheme?

The scheme includes the establishment of new industrial estates, sector-specific parks, and infrastructure projects, along with amendments to the Gujarat Industrial Policy 2020 to further support MSME growth.

### What is the objective of this component?

The objective is to promote Zero Defect and Zero Effect (ZED) manufacturing among MSMEs to enhance quality, energy efficiency, and environmental sustainability.

### Who can apply for the financial support under this scheme?

MSMEs engaged in manufacturing and registered under the ZED Certification scheme can apply.

### What is the financial assistance provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides a subsidy of 50% of ZED Certification charges, capped at [?]50,000/-.

### Is the subsidy available for expenses incurred during a previous scheme’s period?

Yes, eligible enterprises can claim assistance for expenses incurred during the previous scheme's operative period as per the guidelines.

### Can enterprises engaged in trading or services apply for this scheme?

No, the scheme is only for MSMEs engaged in manufacturing activities.

### What is ZED Certification?

ZED Certification focuses on Zero Defect and Zero Effect manufacturing, ensuring quality production without defects and minimal environmental impact.

### Can enterprises apply for this subsidy if they have not yet completed ZED Certification?

No, the subsidy is only available to enterprises that have completed the ZED Certification process.

### How does this scheme-component benefit MSMEs?

The scheme helps MSMEs enhance their product quality, adopt energy-efficient practices, and improve sustainability while reducing certification costs.
