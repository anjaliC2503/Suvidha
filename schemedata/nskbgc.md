## Brief Description

Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme "Nirman Shramikon Ke Bacchon Hetu Nishulk Ganvesh Evam Pustak Copy Hetu Sahaayata Raashi Yojana" for construction worker on 09 December 2022.

## Detailed Description

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Nirman Shramikon Ke Bacchon Hetu Nishulk Ganvesh Evam Pustak Copy Hetu Sahaayata Raashi Yojana&amp;quot; for Construction Workers on 09 December 2022. The main objective of the scheme to provide financial assistance for the purpose of providing educational aids like uniforms and stationery.
&lt;br&gt;

## Benefits

The proposed assistance amount for school uniform and book copy will be payable in lump sum as mentioned below:
ClassesAmountClass 1 to 8₹ 1,000/-Class 9 to 12₹ 2,000/-<br>

## Eligibility

1. Applicant must be a registered construction worker under the Chhattisgarh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
1. Applicant's children must be studying in classes 1 to 12 in any recognized school in Chhattisgarh.

## Application Process

### Online

1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on "[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)" under "Chhattisgarh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board".1. Enter the following details

   1. Select Group Name : "Bhavan Evan Koi Sannirmaan"
   1. Select Service : "Yojana"
   1. What do you want to do : "Aavedan"
   1. Click next.

1. Select your district name and provide old/new registration number and click next.
1. Select the scheme name. 
1. Fill out the online with required details. 
1. Submit.
