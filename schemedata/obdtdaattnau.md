## Brief Description

The scheme "Organizing Block Demonstration Through Department And Also Through TNAU" offers grants to farmers for maize cultivation. Farmers can avail a subsidy for demonstrating cultivation practices on a 1-hectare plot.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Organizing Block Demonstration Through Department And Also Through TNAU” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu is designed to promote maize cultivation through block demonstrations conducted by the Department of Agriculture and Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU). Farmers in select districts of Tamil Nadu can participate. It provides ₹4,000/- per demonstration for 1-hectare plots. Special focus is given to small, marginal, SC/ST, and women farmers.

## Benefits

**Financial Assistance:** ₹4,000/- per demonstration of 1 Hectare plot each.

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary should be a Farmer.
  - The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.
  - The beneficiary should grow maize in the following districts: Coimbatore, Dindigul, Erode, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, Namakkal, Perambalur, Virudhunagar, Theni, Villupuram, Vellore, Salem, Pudukkottai, Madurai, Sivagangai, Trichy, Tirunelveli, and Thoothukudi.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office.
**Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). 
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office:
> **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer.

> **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer.

> **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Organizing Block Demonstration Through Department And Also Through TNAU" scheme?

The scheme "Organizing Block Demonstration Through Department And Also Through TNAU" offers grants to farmers for maize cultivation. Farmers can avail a subsidy for demonstrating cultivation practices on a 1-hectare plot.

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

All maize growing farmers are eligible to avail of the subsidy under this scheme in the Districts of Coimbatore, Dindigul, Erode, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, Namakkal, Perambalur, Virudhunagar, Theni, Villupuram, Vellore, Salem, Pudukkottai, Madurai, Sivagangai, Trichy, Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi., with preference given to small, marginal, SC/ST, and women farmers, will be eligible for this scheme.

### What is the benefit provided under the scheme?

Farmers receive [?]4,000/- per 1-hectare demonstration plot under the scheme.

### How to apply for this scheme?

Farmers can apply through the Assistant Agricultural Officer at the village level or the Joint Director of Agriculture at the district level.

### What documents are required for the application?

Documents like proof of land ownership, identity proof, residence proof, and bank details are required.

### Is the scheme only for maize cultivation?

Yes, it is specifically for maize cultivation demonstrations.

### Can small and marginal farmers apply?

Yes, small and marginal farmers are given priority.

### Is there a quota for SC/ST farmers?

Yes, 24% of the scheme's benefits are reserved for SC/ST farmers.

### Can women farmers apply?

Yes, 20% of the benefits are reserved for women farmers or women farmer groups.

### What is the role of TNAU in this scheme?

TNAU, along with the Department of Agriculture, helps organize and oversee block demonstrations.
