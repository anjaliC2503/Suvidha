## Detailed Description

The **Logistics Excellence, Advancement, and Performance Shield** (LEAPS) scheme under the **Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India,** is a flagship initiative designed to acknowledge and celebrate best practices and innovations within the logistics industry. In line with the National Logistics Policy 2022, LEAPS aims to create a new benchmark in India as well as globally by recognizing outstanding leadership and innovative endeavours across various sectors of the logistics industry, including air, road, sea, rail freight, warehousing, and multimodal transportation. It also recognizes MSMEs, startups, and academia that provide innovative solutions across the logistics ecosystem.
LEAPS also raises awareness about sustainability, ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) practices, and green logistics, while promoting collaboration between government, industry, and academia to drive progress and efficiency. Building upon the foundation laid by the PM GatiShakti National Master Plan and the National Logistics Policy, LEAPS endeavours to further enhance India’s logistics infrastructure and efficiency. It aims to create a technologically enabled, integrated, and sustainable logistics ecosystem. This is the 4th edition of LEAPS.
>  **The key guiding principles for “LEAPS” are:**


1. Comprehensive coverage of all logistics sectors and ecosystem enablers
1. Special focus on MSMEs and Startups
1. Showcase of diversity, inclusion and ESG best practices in the logistics industry
1. Alignment with the objectives of PM Gati Shakti
1. Alignment with the mission of Aatmanirbhar Bharat
1. Process innovation, efficiency aided by technology adoption.

## Eligibility

**A. Core logistics and Special categories**
1. The applicant must be a company registered in India under the Companies Act of 2013 or a partnership firm registered under the Limited Liability Partnership Act of 2008.
2. The entity must possess all relevant trade-specific registrations, such as GST Registration, etc.
3. The entity, its promoters, or any of their affiliated entities must not have incurred any defaults in the last three fiscal years (FY 2022-23, 23-24, 24-25).
4. The entity must submit audited financial statements (balance sheet, profit & loss account) for the last three financial years (FY 2022-23, 23-24, 24-25).
**B. Startups**
1. The startup should be a DPIIT recognized startup. The entity must submit their certificate of incorporation or partnership deed.
2. The entity must have a hardware or software product or a process solution that is present in the market.
3. The entity must have all applicable trade or trade-specific registrations (Example: FSSAI, MSME, GST Registration, etc.)
4. The entity, its promoters, or any of their affiliated entities must not have incurred any defaults in the last three fiscal years (FY 2022-23, 23-24, 24-25).
5. The entity must submit audited financial statements (balance sheet, profit & loss account) for the last three financial years (FY 2022-23, 23-24, 24-25).
**C. MSMEs**
1. The entity should be a recognized MSME entity (Udyam Aadhar). The entity must submit their certificate of incorporation or partnership deed.
2. The entity must have all applicable trade or trade-specific registrations (example: MSME, GST Registration, etc.)
3. The entity, its promoters, or any of their affiliated entities must not have incurred any defaults in the last three fiscal years (FY 2022-23, 23-24, 24-25).
4. The entity must submit audited financial statements (balance sheet, profit & loss account) for the last three financial years (FY 2022-23, 23-24, 24-25).
**D. Institutions:** 
1. Institution must also be officially registered with the appropriate educational or governmental authorities. 
2. This should include accreditation from national or regional educational bodies.

## Application Process

### Online

**Registration:**
**Step-1:** Visit the [official website](https://awards.gov.in/).
**Step-2:** Click on the [Registration ](https://awards.gov.in/Registration/SignUp)button.
**Step-3:** Choose type Individual or  Organization.
**Step-4.1:** Under Individual category provide the following details. 
               - Name as per Aadhaar/Valid Identity Proof.
               - Date of Birth.
               - Nominator/Applicant Type.
               - Mobile No.
               - Email ID.
               - Aadhaar Number.
**Step-4.2:** Under Organization category provide the following details.
               - Type of Organization.
               - Name of the Authorized Person as per Aadhaar/Valid Identity Proof
               - Date of Birth.
               - Brief Description about the Organization (100 Words)
               - Designation
               - Mobile No.
               - Email ID.
               - Aadhaar Number.
**Step-5:** Submit.
            
**Apply:**
**Step-1:** Visit the [official website](https://awards.gov.in/).
**Step-2:** Click on l[ogin button on](https://awards.gov.in/Login) portal and login using the credentials. 
**Step-3:** From the list of awards, select scheme name, then click ["Nominate / Apply Now".](https://awards.gov.in/Home/AwardLibrary)
**Step-4:** On the next page, fill in all the mandatory fields in the online application form, and then click "Submit".

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Logistics Excellence, Advancement, and Performance Shield (LEAPS) and why was it created?

LEAPS is a flagship initiative by DPIIT to recognize and celebrate best practices, innovation, and leadership in India’s logistics sector. It promotes sustainability, technology adoption, and efficiency across the ecosystem.
<br>

### Which government department is responsible for organizing the LEAPS awards?

The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry organizes and manages the LEAPS scheme.
<br>

### Which edition of the LEAPS awards is being conducted this time?

This event marks the 4th edition of the LEAPS awards, building on the success of previous editions in recognizing logistics excellence.
<br>

### What sectors and areas of the logistics industry are eligible to participate in LEAPS?

The scheme covers air, road, sea, and rail freight, warehousing, multimodal transport, startups, MSMEs, and academic institutions offering innovative solutions.
<br>

### What are the primary objectives of the LEAPS initiative?

LEAPS aims to acknowledge outstanding leadership, promote innovation, encourage ESG practices, and enhance India’s logistics infrastructure and efficiency.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the LEAPS awards?

Eligible applicants include core logistics companies, recognized startups, MSMEs, and institutions accredited by national or regional educational or governmental authorities.
<br>

### Are Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) allowed to apply for the LEAPS awards?

Yes, recognized MSMEs with valid Udyam Aadhar, relevant trade registrations, and financial statements for the last three years are eligible.
<br>

### What financial documents are required to apply for the LEAPS awards?

Applicants must submit audited financial statements, including balance sheets and profit & loss accounts, for the last three financial years.
<br>

### Who is allowed to nominate organizations for the LEAPS awards?

Only the organization itself can nominate its application; self-nomination is required to participate in the process.
<br>

### How many awardees are selected under LEAPS and how are the categories divided?

A total of 13 awardees are selected, with one award for each category across core logistics, startups, MSMEs, and institutions.
<br>

### Is diversity and inclusion considered while evaluating applicants for LEAPS?

Yes, the scheme encourages diversity, inclusion, and showcases organizations that implement ESG best practices in logistics.
