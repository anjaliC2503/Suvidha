## Brief Description

The "Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training" aims to promote poultry rearing skills among persons with disabilities through a ₹2,000/- stipend for attending a 7-day training program conducted by the Intensive Poultry Development Unit.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance and capacity building to physically disabled individuals of the state by equipping them with poultry rearing skills. Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive a stipend of ₹2,000/- for participating in a 7-day training session on poultry farming conducted by the Intensive Poultry Development Unit. The initiative is designed to enhance income opportunities and livelihood development for the physically disabled population across Gujarat.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Stipend Amount:** ₹2,000/- per beneficiary.
<br>

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be a person with physical disabilities, holding a valid PH Identity Card.
  - The applicant should express interest in poultry rearing and be willing to attend the full 7-day training session.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the primary objective of the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training”?

The primary objective of the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training” is to empower persons with physical disabilities in Gujarat by equipping them with poultry farming skills and providing them with financial assistance in the form of a stipend to support their participation in a specialized 7-day training program.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training”?

Any person with physical disabilities who is a permanent resident of Gujarat and holds a valid PH (Physically Handicapped) Identity Card is eligible to apply under the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training”.
<br>

### What kind of financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Under the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training,” eligible beneficiaries receive a one-time stipend amount of ₹2,000/- as financial support for attending the 7-day poultry rearing training program.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the duration of the training provided through this scheme?

The training provided through the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training” is conducted over a period of 7 consecutive days by the Intensive Poultry Development Unit.
<br>

### Is there any cost associated with applying for this scheme?

There is no cost associated with the application process, as the application form for the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training” is available free of charge on the official iKhedut portal.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the steps to apply for the scheme?

To apply for the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training,” the applicant must visit the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, fill out the online application form, upload the required documents, and submit the form electronically.
<br>

### Can individuals from outside Gujarat apply for this scheme?

No, individuals who are not residents of Gujarat are not eligible to apply for the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training,” as the scheme is exclusively for residents of the state.
<br>

### Is possession of a PH Identity Card mandatory to receive benefits under this scheme?

Yes, possessing a valid PH Identity Card is mandatory for receiving the benefits under the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training,” as it serves as proof of disability eligibility.
<br>

### Are there any income restrictions for applying to this scheme?

There are no income restrictions or income limits imposed under the “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training”; individuals from all economic backgrounds may apply if they meet the disability and residency criteria.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the geographic coverage of the scheme?

The “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training” is applicable across the entire state of Gujarat and is intended to benefit eligible persons residing in any district of the state.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which department is responsible for implementing the scheme?

The “Scheme to Provide Stipend to the Persons With Disabilities of the State for Poultry Training” is implemented by the Intensive Poultry Development Unit under the Animal Husbandry sector, functioning within the Agriculture and Co-operation Department of the Government of Gujarat.
<br>
