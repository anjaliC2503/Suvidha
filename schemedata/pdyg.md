## Brief Description

"Pashupalan Dhiran Yojana" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department provides loan assistance for animal husbandry to Rabari and Bharwad castes from the socially and educationally backward classes.

## Detailed Description

&quot;Pashupalan Dhiran Yojana&quot; is a welfare initiative by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat. The scheme aims to support economically disadvantaged individuals from the Rabari and Bharwad communities through financial assistance in the form of low-interest loans up to ₹1,30,000/- for animal husbandry activities.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** Loan up to ₹1,30,000/- for animal husbandry.
> **Note:**


- **Loan Amount:** 95%- **Beneficiary Contribution:** 5%

## Eligibility

- The applicant must belong to the Rabari or Bharwad caste, classified under socially and educationally backward classes (SC).
  - The applicant must be between 21 and 45 years of age.
  - The applicant must have a family annual income not exceeding ₹3,00,00/-.
  - The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant must be engaged in or intending to undertake animal husbandry activities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Pashupalan Dhiran Yojana"?

The objective of the "Pashupalan Dhiran Yojana" is to provide financial support in the form of low-interest loans to individuals from the Rabari and Bharwad communities, enabling them to engage in animal husbandry and thereby promote self-employment and economic upliftment among socially and educationally backward classes in Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Pashupalan Dhiran Yojana"?

The scheme is specifically designed for applicants who belong to the Rabari or Bharwad caste, fall within the age group of 21 to 45 years, reside in Gujarat, and have a family income not exceeding ₹3.00 lakh per annum.
<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum loan amount offered under this scheme?

Under the "Pashupalan Dhiran Yojana", the eligible applicant can avail of a loan amount of up to ₹1,30,000/- for the purpose of undertaking animal husbandry activities.
<br>

<br>

### What is the rate of interest applicable to the loan provided through this scheme?

The loan granted under this scheme carries an interest rate of 6% per annum, making it an affordable and accessible source of credit for economically weaker sections.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any contribution required from the applicant under this scheme?

Yes, the applicant is required to contribute 5% of the total project cost, while the remaining 95% is covered by the loan under the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What type of activities can the loan amount be used for under this scheme?

The loan amount provided through this scheme must strictly be utilized for animal husbandry-related activities, such as purchasing livestock, setting up cattle sheds, or acquiring necessary equipment.
<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant apply for the Pashupalan Dhiran Yojana?

The applicant can submit their application online by visiting the official [DAADC ](https://daadconline.gujarat.gov.in)portal and filling out the required details along with uploading necessary documents.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any application fee that needs to be paid while applying for the scheme?

No, the application process for the Pashupalan Dhiran Yojana is completely free, and there is no fee charged for filling or submitting the application form.
<br>

<br>

### Can women applicants also avail benefits under this scheme?

Yes, the scheme is open to all genders, and women applicants who fulfill the eligibility criteria can also apply and benefit from the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to be submitted while applying for this scheme?

The applicant needs to submit documents such as Aadhaar Card, caste certificate, income certificate, age proof, PAN card, bank passbook or cancelled cheque, school leaving certificate, electricity bill, ration card, and birth certificate.

### Where can an applicant find more information or assistance regarding the scheme?

For additional details, the applicant can visit the Social Justice and Empowerment Department's [website  ](https://sje.gujarat.gov.in)or access information directly on [DAADC ](https://daadconline.gujarat.gov.in)portal.
<br>

<br>
