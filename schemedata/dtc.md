## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote theatrical arts and drama performances across Gujarat by organizing competitive events at divisional and state levels. Individual performers and theatre organizations receive cash prizes based on their performance rankings in state-level competitions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Divisional Tripartite Competition" was launched by the Department of Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to promote theatrical arts and drama performances across the state by organizing competitive events at divisional and state levels, encouraging cultural participation and excellence in dramatic arts among youth and theatre organizations. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities, Government of Gujarat. The applications for this scheme are accepted Online.
<br>

## Benefits

- The first prize winner of the drama competition will receive a cash prize of ₹3,000/-.
- The second prize winner will receive a cash prize of ₹2,500/-.
- The third prize winner will receive a cash prize of ₹2,000/-.
- The participating organization will receive cash prizes based on their ranking.
- The first prize winning organization will receive ₹10,000/-.
- The second prize winning organization will receive ₹7,000/-.
- The third prize winning organization will receive ₹5,000/-.

_*The first and second prize winners of the divisional tri-series drama competition also become eligible to participate at the state level competition._
_*The mode and frequency of benefit disbursement details are not specified in the provided reference material._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What type of financial assistance is provided to individual winners who achieve top positions in the state-level drama performance event organized under this cultural initiative?

Individual winners receive cash prizes based on their ranking: ₹3,000/- for first position, ₹2,500/- for second position, and ₹2,000/- for third position at the state-level competition.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions based on income levels, either personal or familial, that would prevent someone from participating in this competitive theatre program?

There are no personal income limits or family income limits applicable for participation. Applicants from any economic background are eligible to participate in the competition.
<br>

### What specific documentation related to banking and financial transactions must be submitted along with the application for this cultural performance scheme?

Applicants must submit bank details including bank passbook (or bank passbook of the heir), as well as a bank passbook or cancelled cheque to facilitate direct transfer of prize money.
<br>

### If our theatre group or dramatic organization performs well in the state-level competition, what monetary rewards can we expect to receive?

Participating organizations receive cash prizes: ₹10,000/- for first position, ₹7,000/- for second position, and ₹5,000/- for third position based on their performance ranking at the state level.
<br>

### Is there any registration fee or application processing charge that needs to be paid when applying for participation in this drama competition program?

There is no application fee required for this scheme. The cost of the application form is ₹0/-, making participation accessible to all interested applicants without any financial barrier.
<br>

### What are the preliminary achievements or qualifications needed at the divisional level before being allowed to compete at the state-level drama event?

Participants must have secured either first or second position at the divisional tri-series drama competition to qualify for participation at the state-level competition.
<br>

### Which identity verification documents are mandatory for submission to establish the applicant's credentials and eligibility for receiving benefits under this scheme?

Mandatory documents include Aadhaar Card, Proof of Age, and a self-attested copy of Permanent Account Number Card for identity verification and establishing eligibility.
<br>

### Does the scheme impose any limitations based on gender, social category, or professional background that would restrict certain individuals from applying?

The scheme is open to applicants of any gender, any social category, and any professional background. There are no restrictions based on these parameters for scheme eligibility.
<br>

### What is the complete geographical coverage area within which this theatrical competition and cultural promotion scheme is currently being implemented and operated?

The scheme is implemented across the entire state of Gujarat, providing opportunities for participation to theatre artists and organizations from all regions within the state.
<br>

### Through which mode or medium is the application submission process conducted, and where should interested participants go to initiate their registration?

Applications are accepted online through the official portal at https://sangeetnatak.gujarat.gov.in/. Interested participants should visit this website to initiate the registration and application process.
<br>

### Which government department or administrative body has been designated as the implementing authority responsible for managing and overseeing this drama competition initiative?

The Department of Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities under the Government of Gujarat is the implementing authority responsible for managing this scheme.
<br>

### What is the primary objective or purpose that this state-level competitive drama program seeks to achieve through its implementation?

The scheme aims to promote theatrical arts and drama performances across Gujarat by organizing competitive events at divisional and state levels, encouraging cultural participation and excellence in dramatic arts.
<br>
