## Brief Description

The Mukhyamantri Yuva Karya Prashikshan Yojana scheme was launched by the Department of Skill, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation to enhance candidates' employability through practical training with entrepreneurs with a budget of ₹5,500 crore.

## Detailed Description

The Mukhyamantri Yuva Karya Prashikshan Yojana scheme was launched by the Department of Skill, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation to enhance candidates' employability through practical training with entrepreneurs. The scheme budget of ₹5,500 crore, and the training programs for approximately 10 lakh candidates per year.  
<br>

***Training Duration:*** Job Training will be for 6 months.
<br>

## Benefits

**Stipend:**
12th Pass - 6,000/-
ITI / Diploma - 8,000/-
Degree / Post Graduation - 10,000/-
<br>

**Note:** Stipends are transferred directly to trainees' bank accounts linked with Aadhaar.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The candidate should be a resident of Maharashtra.
1. The candidate's age should be between 18 to 35.
1. The candidate should have minimum education criteria of 12th Pass/ITI/Diploma/Graduation/Post Graduation.
1. The candidate should be registered with Aadhaar.
1. The candidate's bank accounts should be linked with Aadhaar.

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

***Registration Process for Candidate:***

- Go to the official [CMYKPY website](https://cmykpy.mahaswayam.gov.in/Index.aspx) for job seeker registration and click on “**Intern Login**”.- Use your Aadhaar number as the username for your account.
- Fill in your full name, date of birth, gender, email, and mobile number.
- Create a secure password and confirm it.
- After completing all required fields, submit the registration form to finish the process.

<br>

***Log In Process:***

- Fill Your Aadhar Number as a User Name and Password.
- Complete your profile details, and fill in the personal, address, qualification, and bank details.
- Upload the photo, qualification document, birth certificate, etc.
- Click on “Submit” and apply for the job.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the scheme?

The scheme provides practical job training to improve employability among Maharashtra's youth.

### Who is eligible to apply as a candidate?

Candidates aged 18-35 with a minimum qualification of 12th pass who are residents of Maharashtra.

### How much stipend can candidates receive?

Stipend ranges from [?]6,000 to [?]10,000 per month, depending on the candidate's educational qualification.

### How long does the training last?

Each training period is six months.

### Where can candidates register for the scheme?

Candidates can register on the cmykpy.mahaswayam.gov.in portal.

### What documents do candidates need to submit?

Candidates need an Aadhaar, educational certificates, proof of residence, and a bank passbook copy.

### How will candidates receive their stipend?

Stipends are transferred directly to the Aadhaar-linked bank account of each trainee.

### How many training opportunities are available per year?

Approximately 10 lakh training placements are provided each year.

### Is registration mandatory for candidates?

Yes, both candidates must register on the portal to participate in the scheme.

### Who manages the scheme?

The scheme is managed by the Commissionerate of Skill Development, Employment, & Entrepreneurship, Maharashtra.

### Can candidates outside Maharashtra apply?

No, only Maharashtra residents are eligible for the scheme.

### What support is available for application or grievance?

Candidates can visit their nearest District Skill Development, Employment & Entrepreneurship Guidance Center or call 1800 120 8040 for assistance.
