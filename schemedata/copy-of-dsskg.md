## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote the use and sale of Khadi garments by providing a 10% discount to consumers on purchases made at Khadi production and sales centres registered with the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC).

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Discount Scheme on Sale of Khadi Garments” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Khadi and Village Industries Board under the Department of Industries, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote the use and sale of Khadi garments by providing a 10% discount to consumers on purchases made at Khadi production and sales centres registered with the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC).
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, a 10% discount on Khadi garments is provided to customers from Gandhi Jayanti (2nd October) to Republic Day (26th January).
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The discount is provided to production/sales centres in Uttarakhand that are registered with the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (Government of India), which then pass it on to the customers.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

All consumers receive a 10% discount on the purchase of garments at Khadi Village Industries production and sales centres in Uttarakhand.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the discount?

All consumers purchasing Khadi garments from registered production and sales centres in Uttarakhand are eligible.
<br>

### What is the value of the discount offered?

A 10% discount is provided on all eligible Khadi garments.
<br>

### When is the discount available?

The discount is available from Gandhi Jayanti (2nd October) to Republic Day (26th January) each year.
<br>

### Where can consumers avail the discount?

Where can consumers avail the discount?
<br>

### How is the discount provided to customers?

The production and sales centres receive the discount from the scheme and directly pass it on to the customers at the point of sale.
