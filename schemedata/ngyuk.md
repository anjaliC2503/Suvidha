## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide free uniforms to all girl students studying in Classes 1 to 8 of government and government-aided schools, as well as to all boys belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, and boys from the General category who fall under the BPL category.

## Detailed Description

The “Nishulk Ganavesh Yojana” is a scheme under the Samagra Shiksha Programme, implemented by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free uniforms to all girl students studying in Classes 1 to 8 of government and government-aided schools, as well as to all boys belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, and boys from the General category who fall under the Below Poverty Line category.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, two sets of uniforms per student are provided through DBT/SMC to students studying in classes 1 to 8 of government and government-aided schools, covering all girls, all boys belonging to Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe categories, and boys from the General category belonging to BPL families.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant must be either a girl student, or a boy student belonging to the Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, or General Category BPL family.
1. The applicant must be studying in Classes 1 to 8.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

There is no selection process; all eligible students from Classes 1 to 8 are provided free uniforms or the corresponding financial assistance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

All girl students of Classes 1 to 8, all boys belonging to Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) categories, and boys from General Category BPL families studying in government and government-aided schools are eligible.

### Which classes are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers students studying in Classes 1 to 8.

### How many sets of uniforms are provided under this scheme?

Each eligible student receives two sets of uniforms every year.

### Is there any selection process for the scheme?

No, there is no selection process. All eligible students from Classes 1 to 8 receive uniforms or related financial assistance.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable only to government schools?

The scheme is applicable to students studying in government and government-aided schools.
<br>

No.
