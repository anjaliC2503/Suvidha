## Brief Description

The scheme aims to help Roman Script Konkani writers and publishers who face difficulties in marketing their books related to Tiatr, its song and music, while promoting Tiatr culture among enthusiasts. The scheme provides bulk purchase of books at 80% of the fixed rate and organizes book exhibition.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Exhibition of Books on Tiatr, Its Song and Music" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to help Roman Script Konkani writers and publishers who face difficulties in marketing their books related to Tiatr, its song and music, while promoting Tiatr culture among enthusiasts. The scheme provides bulk purchase of books at 80% of the fixed rate and organizes book exhibitions to facilitate better market reach for publishers. The applicant must be a writer or publisher of books related to Tiatr, its song and music, and must submit 3 copies of their published book for evaluation. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy Goa through its appointed Recommendation Committee. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline.
<br>

## Benefits

**Book Purchase:** Tiatr Academy Goa will purchase books at 80% of the rate fixed as per Tiatr Academy Goa conditions.
**Marketing Support:** Books are sold through book exhibitions organized by Tiatr Academy Goa and participation in exhibitions by various Book Houses and Institutions.
**Promotional Platform:** Books receive exposure to Tiatr lovers and enthusiasts through organized exhibitions.
<br>

***Conditions***
_*Tiatr Academy Goa reserves the right to sell books at discounted rates during exhibitions._
_*Payment will be made only after Tiatr Academy Goa's final decision to purchase the books._
_*Books will be purchased on 'First-come-First' serve basis._
_*The final purchase decision remains at the discretion of Tiatr Academy Goa even after Committee recommendation._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a writer or publisher of books related to Tiatr, its song and music.
- The applicant should have published books in any script or language (not limited to Roman Konkani script).

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa to collect the prescribed application form for the scheme.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). The application should express the intent to avail benefit under this scheme. Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
<br>

> ***Committee Review & Recommendation***

**Step 1:** The Tiatr Academy Goa appointed Recommendation Committee will scrutinize the submitted book and evaluate its relevance to Tiatr, its song and music.
**Step 2:** The Recommendation Committee will recommend to Tiatr Academy Goa whether the book can be purchased or not based on their evaluation.
**Step 3:** Tiatr Academy Goa will make the final decision on book purchase, reserving the right not to purchase even if recommended by the Committee.
<br>

> ***Price Negotiation, Book Purchase and Exhibition***

**Step 1:** If the book is approved, the price will be fixed as per Tiatr Academy Goa conditions, with Tiatr Academy Goa paying 80% of the agreed rate.
**Step 2:** If Tiatr Academy Goa decides to purchase the books, the applicant will be informed in writing about the decision and further procedures.
**Step 3:** Tiatr Academy Goa will purchase the books on 'First-come-First' serve basis and include them in their book exhibitions and promotional activities.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I submit books written in languages other than Konkani for consideration under this scheme?

Yes, books related to Tiatr, its song and music can be submitted in any script or language, not just Roman Konkani script.
<br>

### What percentage of the book price will I receive if my publication is selected for purchase?

You will receive 80% of the rate that is fixed as per the conditions set by Tiatr Academy Goa.
<br>

### Is there any guarantee that my book will be purchased even if the Recommendation Committee approves it?

No, Tiatr Academy Goa reserves the right not to purchase the book even if it has been recommended by the Recommendation Committee.
<br>

### How many copies of my published book do I need to submit with my application?

You must submit exactly 3 copies of your published book along with your application for evaluation.
<br>

### Will I be notified in writing about the decision regarding my book purchase application?

Yes, if Tiatr Academy Goa decides to purchase your books, you will be informed in writing about their decision.
<br>

### What happens to my books once they are purchased under this scheme by the organization?

The books are sold through book exhibitions organized by Tiatr Academy Goa and through participation in exhibitions by various Book Houses and Institutions.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions on the topics my book can cover to be eligible for this scheme?

Your book must be related to Tiatr, its song and music to qualify for consideration under this scheme.
<br>

### Can the organization sell my books at prices lower than the original rate during exhibitions?

Yes, Tiatr Academy Goa reserves the right to sell the books at discounted rates in its book exhibitions or when participating in other exhibitions.
<br>

### Who decides whether my submitted book meets the criteria for purchase under this scheme?

The Tiatr Academy Goa appointed Recommendation Committee will scrutinize your book and make recommendations about its suitability for purchase.
<br>

### What is the selection process followed when multiple applications are received for book purchases?

Books are purchased on a 'First-come-First' serve basis, meaning applications are processed in the order they are received.
<br>

### Can the rules and regulations of this scheme be modified by the implementing organization?

Yes, Tiatr Academy Goa reserves the right to make any changes in the rules and regulations of this scheme.
<br>

### What is the primary objective behind launching this book purchase and exhibition scheme?

The main aim is to help Roman Script Konkani writers and publishers who face marketing difficulties and to promote Tiatr culture among enthusiasts.
<br>

### Will my book be exhibited only by the implementing organization or through other platforms as well?

Your book will be exhibited both through Tiatr Academy Goa's own book exhibitions and through their participation in exhibitions organized by various Book Houses and Institutions.
<br>

### Do I need to be a professional publisher to apply for this scheme or can individual writers also participate?

Both writers and publishers who have published books related to Tiatr, its song and music can apply for this scheme.
<br>
