## Brief Description

The scheme provides a financial incentive of ₹5,000/- to the meritorious boys from Scheduled Castes who have secured 80% or above marks in the HSSLC examination. The objective is to motivate and encourage SC students to pursue higher studies and bring them at par with the rest.

## Detailed Description

The 'Financial Incentive to SC Meritorious Boy Students' scheme, administered by the Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, provides financial assistance to deserving boy students from Scheduled Castes (SC). Eligibility is extended to those who have achieved 80% or higher marks in the HSSLC examination. The scheme's objective is to motivate and encourage SC boy students to pursue higher education, thereby fostering their academic parity with the broader population through a substantial incentive. Assistance is contingent upon the applicant satisfying the stipulated eligibility criteria and the successful submission of required documentation. An incentive of ₹5,000/- will be disbursed to students as approved by the Selection Committee.
<br>

**The applications will be accepted through the myScheme platform from 04/07/2025 to 31/10/2025.**
***A DigiLocker account is required for application. Please refer to the Application Process section for details.***
<br>

## Benefits

A financial incentive of ₹5,000/- will be provided.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be currently enrolled as a student.
- The applicant must be male.
- The applicant must belong to a Scheduled Caste (SC) community of Assam.
- The applicant must have secured a minimum of 80% marks in the HSSLC examination held in 2025.
- The annual income of the applicant's parents/guardians must not exceed ₹5,00,000/-.


_*In instances where multiple applicants achieve an identical percentage in the HSSLC examination, preference will be given to those with a higher percentage in the HSLC examination._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum percentage required in the HSSLC examination to qualify for this financial assistance?

Applicants must have secured 80% or above in their HSSLC examination under SEBA, CBSE, or ICSE boards.

### Is there any income limit for the applicant’s family to be eligible for this scheme?

Yes, the annual income of the applicant’s parents should not exceed ₹5,00,000/-.

### How much financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

A one-time incentive of ₹5,000/- is given to selected meritorious students.

### Who is eligible to apply for this financial incentive scheme for meritorious students?

Only male students belonging to the Scheduled Caste (SC) community of Assam who have scored 80% or above in their HSSLC (SEBA/CBSE/ICSE) examination are eligible.

### Which communities are eligible to apply for this financial incentive?

Only students belonging to the Scheduled Caste (SC) community of Assam can apply.

### What happens if multiple applicants have the same percentage in the HSSLC examination?

In case of a tie, preference will be given to the student who scored a higher percentage in the HSLC (Class 10) examination.

### Which education boards are considered eligible for this scheme?

The scheme recognizes HSSLC results from SEBA (Assam Board), CBSE, and ICSE.

### Is this scheme applicable to both male and female students?

No, this scheme is only for male students from the Scheduled Caste (SC) community.

### What is the purpose of this financial incentive scheme?

The scheme aims to motivate and encourage SC students to pursue higher studies by providing financial support.

### What are the mandatory documents required to apply for this financial incentive?

Required documents include a valid SC caste certificate, parents’ income certificate, Class 10 admit card, HSLC & HSSLC mark sheets, passbook front page, Voter ID/PAN card, and Aadhaar card.

### Who issues the income certificate required for this scheme?

The Circle Officer must issue the parents’ annual income certificate.

### Is self-attestation required for the documents submitted with the application?

Yes, applicants must self-attest documents such as photographs and photocopies where required.

### What is the role of the Selection Committee in this scheme?

The Selection Committee approves the final list of eligible students for the incentive.

### Which government department is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, oversees the scheme’s implementation.

### When is the application window open for the 2025-26 cycle, and how can I apply?

Applications will be accepted online via the myScheme platform from 1st July 2025 to 31st August 2025.

### Is a DigiLocker account mandatory to apply?

Yes, a DigiLocker account is compulsory for application submission.

### What are the technical specifications for uploading photographs in the application?

Photos must be in JPEG/JPG format, with a file size between 50KB and 3MB.

### What file type and size are acceptable for uploading supporting documents?

Documents must be in PDF format, with each file sized between 300KB and 3MB.

### How do I create a DigiLocker account if I don’t have one?

Visit the DigiLocker portal/app, provide your Aadhaar number, mobile, email, and a 6-digit PIN, and submit the details to register.

### What credentials do I need to sign in to the myScheme platform?

Use your DigiLocker-registered mobile number and the 6-digit PIN created during DigiLocker setup to log in.

### Where can I download the DigiLocker mobile app for account creation?

The app is available on Google Play (Android) and the iOS App Store.

### What happens after I submit the application on myScheme?

You’ll receive a "Form Submitted Successfully!" confirmation and can print/download the form as a PDF.
