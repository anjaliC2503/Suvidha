## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial support to the wards of recognized Goan artists who are pursuing higher education, thereby addressing the economic challenges faced by artist families in supporting their children's educational aspirations and ensuring continuity of educational opportunities.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Kalakar Kritadnyata Nidhi" was launched by the Directorate of Art & Culture, Government of Goa. The "Scholarships/Incentives" component of the scheme aims to provide financial support to the wards of recognized Goan artists who are pursuing higher education, thereby addressing the economic challenges faced by artist families in supporting their children's educational aspirations and ensuring continuity of educational opportunities despite financial constraints. The applications for this scheme are accepted Offline.
<br>

## Benefits

- A monthly scholarship of ₹750/- is sanctioned to eligible wards during the currency of the academic year.
- The vacation period is excluded from the scholarship disbursement period.
- The scholarship is disbursed on a half-yearly basis.

<br>

## Eligibility

##### ***For the Artist***

- The artist must be a Goan, i.e., born and brought up in Goa.
- The artist must be recognized by the Directorate of Art & Culture, Government of Goa.
- The income of the family of the artist (from all sources) must not exceed ₹2,00,000/- per annum.
- The artist must not be less than 50 years in age. (The Government may relax the age criteria for deserving cases under special circumstances.)

##### ***For the Ward of the Artist***

- The ward must be enrolled in a recognized educational institution.
- The ward must be pursuing Postgraduate, Degree, or Professional courses.
- The ward may be enrolled in educational institutions within the state or outside the state.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Visit the office of the Directorate of Art & Culture, Sanskruti Bhavan, Patto, Panaji, Goa, and collect the prescribed application form for the scheme.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the completed application form along with all required documents directly to the ***Directorate of Art & Culture, Sanskruti Bhavan, Patto, Panaji, Goa.***
<br>

##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 1:** The Directorate shall forward the application to the sub-committee for scrutinizing the application thoroughly. The Director shall give its recommendations to the sub-committee.
**Step 2:** The sub-committee shall consider the received applications and recommend the names considered fit by the sub-committee to avail the benefits under this scheme. The committee shall decide the quantum of assistance to be released.
**Step 3:** After the committee decides on the quantum of assistance, the approval from the Government shall be obtained for disbursement of the financial assistance.
**Step 4:** After obtaining Government approval, the Director shall disburse the one-time financial assistance amount in the form of Electronic Clearing System mode to the bank account.
<br>

## Documents Required

##### ***For the Artist***

- Birth Certificate.
- Recognition Certificate, from the Directorate of Art & Culture, Government of Goa.
- Income Certificate, from the Competent Authority.

##### ***For the Ward of the Artist***

- Birth Certificate.
- Proof of Admission.
- Dependency Certificate (or a document establishing the relation with  the artist).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the monthly scholarship amount that will be provided to eligible students under this financial assistance program?

Eligible wards receive a monthly scholarship of ₹750/- during the academic year, excluding vacation periods. The scholarship is disbursed on a half-yearly basis to support their educational expenses.
<br>

### Is there any specific requirement regarding the type of educational courses for which the scholarship can be availed?

Yes, the ward must be enrolled in Post Graduate, Degree, or Professional courses at recognized educational institutions. These courses can be pursued either within the State of Goa or outside the state.
<br>

### What is the minimum age criterion that must be satisfied by the parent or guardian who is an artist applying for this benefit?

The parent or guardian artist must be at least 50 years of age at the time of application. However, the Government may relax this age criterion for deserving cases under special circumstances.
<br>

### Are there any income limitations that need to be considered when applying for this educational financial support?

Yes, the annual family income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,00,000/- per annum. An income certificate from the competent authority must be submitted along with the application.
<br>

### Is there any restriction on the location or state where the ward can pursue their higher education?

No, there is no geographical restriction. The ward can be enrolled in recognized educational institutions either within the State of Goa or outside the state for pursuing eligible courses.
<br>

### Will the scholarship be provided during the vacation periods or breaks in the academic calendar?

No, the scholarship of ₹750/- per month is provided only during the currency of the academic year. The vacation period is explicitly excluded from the scholarship disbursement.
<br>

### What is the frequency or periodicity with which the scholarship amount will be transferred to the beneficiary?

The scholarship amount is disbursed on a half-yearly basis. This means payments are made twice during the academic year to cover the eligible period.
<br>

### Is it mandatory for the parent or guardian artist to have official recognition from any specific government department?

Yes, the parent or guardian artist must be recognized by the Directorate of Art & Culture, Government of Goa. The recognition certificate must be submitted along with the application.
<br>

### Where should the completed application form along with all supporting documents be submitted physically?

The completed application form with all required documents should be submitted directly to the Directorate of Art & Culture, Sanskruti Bhavan, Patto, Panaji, Goa during working hours on any working day.
<br>

### Through which mode will the scholarship amount be transferred to the beneficiary after approval?

After obtaining Government approval, the Director of Art & Culture disburses the scholarship amount through Electronic Clearing System mode to the bank account provided during the application process.
<br>
