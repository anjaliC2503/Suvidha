## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage sports across the country by strengthening sports infrastructure, coaching, talent development, and sports academies, thereby promoting excellence in sports and holistic development of children and youth.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Khelo India Centres and Sports Academies&quot; is a component of the &quot;Khelo India Scheme&quot; implemented by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India, aimed at strengthening the sports ecosystem through support for sports centres, centres of excellence, and sports academies. The component seeks to improve utilization of existing sports infrastructure, provide coaching and sports science support, and promote grassroots as well as elite sports development. It includes Khelo India State Centres of Excellence, Khelo India Centres, and support to eligible residential sports centres. The scheme also supports talent identification, athlete development, sports academies, and online sports coaching through e-Khel Pathshala. Need-based financial assistance is provided for infrastructure, equipment, coaches, support staff, sports science services, and athlete development activities.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Khelo India State/UT Centre of Excellence:**


- Need-based financial support for recurring and non-recurring expenditure.
- Support available for up to 3 "One State One Game" disciplines and sports science facilities.
- Funding amount determined through viability gap assessment.
- Support for coaches, support staff, sports science services, and sports equipment.

> **Khelo India Centres:**


- Annual recurring grant of up to ₹5,00,000/- per discipline.
- ₹3,00,000/- per year earmarked for remuneration of Past Champion Athletes at ₹25,000/- per month.
- Support for sports equipment, sports kits, competition exposure, physiotherapists, and recurring expenditure.
- Support for repair and maintenance and beneficiary-related expenses.

> **One-Time Assistance for New Centres/Disciplines**

One-time grant of up to ₹5,00,000/- per discipline.
Assistance for preparation or upgradation of sports fields.
Assistance for purchase of sports equipment and sports kits.
> **Other Residential Centres:**


- Grants-in-aid for boarding expenses.
- Support for education expenses.
- Support for competition exposure.
- Support for consumable sports equipment.
- Funding capped at ₹1,50,000/- per trainee per year, subject to revision by the Departmental Project Approval Committee (DPAC).

> **Sports Academies:**


- Need-based support for establishment, operation, and maintenance.
- Support for recurring and non-recurring expenditure.
- Support for engagement of High-Performance Directors, coaches, foreign coaches, sports scientists, and support staff.
- Support for monitoring and performance measurement systems.
- Support for sports science and sports medicine facilities.
- Support for equipment and critical infrastructure requirements.

> **Online Sports Coaching**


- Sports coaching and education through e-Khel Pathshala.

<br>

## Eligibility

> **For Khelo India State Centres of Excellence:**

The organisation must be an existing sports training facility of a State Government or Union Territory Government.
The organisation must possess sports infrastructure and training facilities.
The organisation must have coaching and support staff.
The organisation must demonstrate past performance of athletes at national or international levels.
The organisation must be selected through the prescribed appraisal and approval process.
> **For Khelo India Centres:**

The organisation must be a sports training facility located within a State or Union Territory.
The organisation may belong to a State Government.
The organisation may belong to a Public Sector Undertaking.
The organisation may belong to an autonomous body.
The organisation may be a school.
The organisation may be a university, including a private university.
The organisation may be another eligible organisation approved by the State or Union Territory Government.
The organisation must be shortlisted and recommended by the State or Union Territory Government.
> **For Other Residential Centres:**

The organisation must be an existing residential sports centre.
The organisation must have a good track record in academics and sports.
The organisation must have adequate boarding and lodging facilities.
The organisation must have sufficient space for sports development.
The organisation must have the capacity and willingness to develop sports, especially Olympic disciplines.
> **For Sports Academies:**

The organisation may be the Sports Authority of India.
The organisation may be a State Government entity.
The organisation may be a Union Territory Government entity.
The organisation may be a National Sports Federation or other eligible sports organisation.
The organisation may be a public entity.
The organisation may be a private entity.
The organisation may be established by an eminent sportsperson.
The organisation must satisfy the evaluation criteria approved by the Departmental Project Approval Committee.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The State or Union Territory Government invites applications or identifies eligible facilities, as applicable.
**Step 2:** The applicant submits the prescribed proposal through the designated authority.
**Step 3:** The State or Union Territory Government scrutinizes and shortlists eligible proposals.
**Step 4:** The shortlisted proposals are forwarded to the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports.
**Step 5:** The Project Appraisal Committee examines and evaluates the proposals.
**Step 6:** Eligible proposals are recommended to the Departmental Project Approval Committee for approval.
**Step 7:** Site inspections, verification, and viability gap assessments may be conducted where applicable.
**Step 8:** Funding requirements are finalized and approved by the competent authority.
**Step 9:** The Ministry issues the sanction order and releases financial assistance to the approved implementing agency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme-component?

The component aims to strengthen sports infrastructure, coaching, and athlete development systems across the country.
<br>

### Who implements this component of the Khelo India Scheme?

The component is implemented by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports through State Governments, Union Territory Governments, the Sports Authority of India, and other eligible agencies.
<br>

### What are the main sub-components under this component?

The main sub-components are Khelo India Centres and Sports Academies.
<br>

### What is a Khelo India State Centre of Excellence?

A Khelo India State Centre of Excellence is an existing sports training facility supported to achieve higher standards through financial assistance and sports science support.
<br>

### What is the annual grant available for a Khelo India Centre?

A Khelo India Centre can receive up to ₹5,00,000/- per discipline annually as recurring support.
<br>

### How much remuneration is earmarked for a Past Champion Athlete?

₹3,00,000/- per year is earmarked for remuneration of a Past Champion Athlete at ₹25,000/- per month.
<br>

### Is any one-time grant available for new Khelo India Centres?

Yes, a one-time grant of up to ₹5,00,000/- per discipline is available for newly approved centres or disciplines.
<br>

### What expenses can be covered under Khelo India Centres?

The assistance can cover coaches, sports equipment, sports kits, competition exposure, physiotherapists, recurring expenditure, and maintenance requirements.
<br>

### What support is available for residential sports centres?

Support is available for boarding, education, competition exposure, and consumable sports equipment.
<br>

### What is the funding limit for trainees in residential centres?

Funding may be provided up to ₹1,50,000/- per trainee per year, subject to revision by the DPAC.
<br>

### What is e-Khel Pathshala?

e-Khel Pathshala is an initiative for online sports coaching and sports education under the scheme.
<br>

### Who can establish or operate supported sports academies under the scheme?

Sports academies may be supported when operated by the Sports Authority of India, State Governments, Union Territory Governments, public entities, private entities, or eligible sportspersons.
<br>

### What types of support are available for sports academies?

Sports academies may receive support for coaches, sports scientists, support staff, equipment, infrastructure, and athlete development activities.
<br>

### Is preference given to any specific sports disciplines?

Yes, preference is given to sports included among the priority Olympic disciplines and sports selected under the One State One Game initiative.
<br>

### What condition must be fulfilled for receiving successive grants

The centre must maintain the prescribed number of trainees and comply with the scheme guidelines and performance requirements.
<br>
