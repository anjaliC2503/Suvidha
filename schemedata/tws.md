## Brief Description

"Tap Water Scheme" (नल जल योजना) by the Panchayat and Rural Development Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to provide clean water in Gram Panchayat areas in an accessible manner. The application has to be made to the concerned Gram Panchayat.

## Detailed Description

"Tap Water Scheme" (नल जल योजना) by the Panchayat and Rural Development Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to provide clean water in Gram Panchayat areas in an accessible manner. The application has to be made to the concerned Gram Panchayat. After the approval of the application by the applicant in the Gram Panchayat, tap connection will be given and the amount of water tax per month will be payable by the beneficiary to the Panchayat. The application can be made online.

## Benefits

Tap Water Connection is provided by the Gram Panchayat

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should belong to a Gram Panchayat area where the scheme is implemented.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://mppanchayatdarpan.gov.in/Default.aspx) of "Panchayat Darpan 2.0" by the Panchayat & Rural Development Department.
**Step 2:** Under "Online Services" click "[Water Connection Application](https://mppanchayatdarpan.gov.in/Public/Online_Services/TapConnectionMgmt/Pages/Application_For_New_Tap_Connection.aspx?1)".
**Step 3:** In the form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size.
**Step 4:** Carefully review all the information provided. Make any necessary corrections. Click "Submit Application". Note the Application Number for future reference.
<br>

**Post-Application Processes**
**Step 1:** After examining the Registered application by the Gram Panchayat, notice will be issued to the applicant (if it is found correct).
**Step 2:** As per the rules, the prescribed fee will have to be deposited before the due date.
**Step 3:** After depositing the prescribed fee, the Connection Permission will be issued by the Gram Panchayat.
<br>

**Track Application Status**
You can check your application status '[here](https://mppanchayatdarpan.gov.in/Public/Online_Services/TapConnectionMgmt/Pages/TapApplication_Status.aspx?2)' by providing your Application Number and Mobile Number.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Tap Water Scheme?

The scheme aims to provide clean and easily accessible water in Gram Panchayat areas.

### Who is eligible for the Tap Water Scheme?

Residents of Gram Panchayat areas in Madhya Pradesh with an approved household water connection are eligible.

### Is there any fee for availing of water supply under this scheme?

Yes, beneficiaries must deposit water tax as per the rules set by the Panchayat.

### How can I apply for a household water connection under the scheme?

You can apply through your local Gram Panchayat office by submitting the required documents.

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme?

Proof of residence, identity proof, and any additional documents required by the Panchayat are necessary.

### Can I avail of this scheme if I already have a private water connection?

No, the scheme is applicable only for Panchayat-approved household connections.

### What happens if I fail to pay the water tax on time?

Non-payment of water tax may result in the suspension of your water supply services.

### Can businesses or commercial establishments apply for this scheme?

No, the scheme is intended for household water connections only.

### Is there a limit to the amount of water provided under this scheme?

The water supply limit may vary based on Panchayat regulations and infrastructure.
