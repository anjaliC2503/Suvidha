## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Haryana Domiciled Widow, Divorced, and Legally Separated Women. Through this scheme, a subsidy on the total loan is provided.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Subsidized Scheme for Widows" by the Women & Child Development Department, Haryana Government, aims to provide financial assistance to Haryana-domiciled widows, divorced, and Legally Separated Women. Through this scheme, a subsidy on the total loan is provided. The scheme is covered without collateral security by the bank. The scheme is implemented by the Haryana Women Development Corporation.
<br>

## Benefits

1. A 25% flat subsidy, subject to a maximum of ₹50,000/-, will be provided on the total loan (not as a capital subsidy).
1. The beneficiary's share is 10% of the total loan.
1. The maximum loan limit is ₹3,00,000/-.
1. The scheme will be covered under without collateral security by the bank.
1. The balance amount will be covered from commercial/nationalised banks.

<br>

> **Mode of Disbursement:** The balance amount will be covered from commercial/nationalised banks.

> **Moratorium Period:** The moratorium period will be three months after disbursement of the loan.

<br>

## Exclusions

1. No subsidy for interest accrued on a delayed period will be allowed in case of default of payment of EMI.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a Haryana-domiciled widow, divorced, or a legally separated Woman.
1. The applicant's annual income should not exceed ₹3,00,000/- per annum.
1. The age of the prospective beneficiary should be between 18 and 55 years at the time of applying for the loan.

<br>

## Documents Required

1. Bank Account Statement 
1. Family ID/PPP (Parivar Pahchan Patra) 
1. Domicile Certificate/Haryana Residence Certificate 
1. Interest Subvention Document 
1. Photograph of the Applicant 
1. Husband Death Certificate (for widows) 
1. Divorce Legal Paper (for divorced women) 
1. Legally Separated Document (for legally separated women)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme by the Women & Child Development Department, Haryana?

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Haryana-domiciled widows, Divorced, and Legally Separated Women. It offers a subsidy on loans to help beneficiaries.
<br>

### What is the age limit for beneficiaries applying for this loan scheme?

The age of the prospective beneficiary should be between 18 and 55 years at the time of applying for the loan.
<br>

### What kind of women are considered "legally separated" for the purpose of this scheme?

The scheme includes women who are legally separated, as long as they meet the other eligibility criteria. Specific legal definitions are not provided within the document.
<br>

### Is the subsidy a capital subsidy?

No, the 25% flat subsidy is not provided as a capital subsidy.
<br>

### Do I need to provide any collateral security for the loan under this scheme?

No, this scheme will cover loans without collateral security from the bank.
<br>

### Which banks will disburse the balance of the loan?

The balance of the loan will be disbursed from commercial/nationalised banks.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme if I am a widow?

You will need your Bank Account Statement, Family ID/PPP, Domicile Certificate/Haryana Residence Certificate, Interest Subvention Document, Photograph of the applicant, and Husband Death Certificate.
<br>

### What documents are needed if I am a divorced woman?

You will need your Bank Account Statement, Family ID/PPP, Domicile Certificate/Haryana Residence Certificate, Interest Subvention Document, Photograph of the applicant, and Divorce Legal Paper.
<br>

### What documents do I need if I am a legally separated woman?

You will need your Bank Account Statement, Family ID/PPP, Domicile Certificate/Haryana Residence Certificate, Interest Subvention Document, Photograph of the applicant, and Legally Separated Document.
<br>

### What happens if I default on my EMI payments?

No subsidy for interest accrued on a delayed period will be allowed in case of default of payment of EMI.
<br>

### Is there a period before I have to start repaying the loan?

Yes, the moratorium period will be three months after the disbursement of the loan.
<br>

### Can I apply if I am not domiciled in Haryana?

No, only Haryana Domiciled Widows, Divorced, and Legally Separated Women are eligible.
<br>

### Will the bank provide the entire loan amount, or do I need to contribute?

You need to contribute 10% of the total loan as beneficiary share, and the balance will be from commercial/nationalised banks.
<br>
