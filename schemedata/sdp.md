## Brief Description

The Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs Department, Government of Mizoram launched the State Disability Pension scheme. It provides a monthly pension of ₹350 to 200 eligible individuals who are completely blind or bedridden.

## Detailed Description

The Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs Department launched the State Disability Pension scheme is funded by the Mizoram government. It provides a monthly pension of ₹350 to 200 eligible individuals who are completely blind or bedridden.

## Benefits

- **Financial assistance of ₹350/- per month for disabled individuals.**

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Mizoram.
1. The applicant must be entirely blind or bedridden due to a disability.
1. The applicant should be from the Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant writes the application on plain paper. 
**Step 2:** In the application form attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application along with the documents to the District Social Welfare Office
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Documents Required

- Passport-size Photograph
- UDID/Disability Certificate
- Proof of BPL Status (Family Ration Card)
- Photocopy of Bank Passbook (first page showing account holder's name and account number)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the State Disability Pension Scheme?

Residents of Mizoram who are fully blind or bedridden and fall under the BPL category.

### What is the monthly pension amount provided?

Eligible recipients receive [?]350 per month.

### How many people can benefit from this scheme?

The scheme assists up to 200 qualified individuals.

### How do I apply for the scheme?

Applications can be submitted on plain paper to the local DSWO with the required documents.

### What documents are needed to apply?

UDID/Disability Certificate, passport photo, BPL status proof, and a photocopy of the bank passbook.

### Can people with partial disabilities apply?

No, only those who are fully blind or bedridden are eligible.

### Is there an age limit for eligibility?

The scheme primarily focuses on individuals with disabilities, not age restrictions.

### What if the district quota is already filled?

Applications will be kept on hold and processed as soon as there's availability within the quota.

### How is the application verified?

The DSWO verifies each applicant's documents and eligibility status.

### Is the pension transferred directly to my bank?

Yes, funds are typically transferred to the bank account linked to the application.

### How long does the approval process take?

The time varies based on the verification process and district quota availability.

### What is the grievance redressal process?

Issues can be directed to the grievance officer assigned to each district.

### Can more than one family member receive the pension?

The scheme is intended for individual applicants who meet the criteria.
