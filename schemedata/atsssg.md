## Brief Description

The scheme aims to assist Sugarcane Growers to take up sugarcane cultivation in the State of Goa. It provides financial support to farmers cultivating sugarcane, paid based on actual tonnage supplied at the factory weighbridge as per the Fair & Remunerative Price.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Assistance Towards Sugarcane Supplied By The Sugarcane Growers" was launched by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to assist the Sugarcane Growers to take up sugarcane cultivation in Goa. The scheme provides financial assistance based on the actual tonnage of canes weighed at the Sugar factory weighbridge, determined by the Fair & Remunerative Price (FRP) fixed by the National Federation of Co-operative Sugar Factories Limited, New Delhi. The applicant must be a farmer cultivating sugarcane in the State of Goa, possess a valid Krishi Card, and bear the cost of harvesting and transporting the harvested cane to Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana, Dayanandnagar Dharbandora. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Agriculture. The applications for this scheme are accepted via registration through Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana (SSSKL).
<br>

## Benefits

- Financial assistance is provided based on the Fair & Remunerative Price (FRP) fixed by the National Federation of Co-operative Sugar Factories Limited, New Delhi, from time to time.
- Farmers will be paid based on the actual tonnage of canes weighed at the Sugar factory weighbridge.

> ***Conditions***


- The farmers must harvest and supply the sugarcane on their own to Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana (SSSKL).
- The harvested cane must be loaded and brought to Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana, Dayanandnagar Dharbandora by the farmer on his own cost.
- The farmer must duly sign the GAR 32 form, which is used in the submission process by Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana to the Zonal Agricultural Office.
- The Government or Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana will not take any responsibility to harvest and transport the sugarcane.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a farmer.
- The applicant must be cultivating sugarcane in Goa.
- The applicant must possess a valid Krishi Card issued by the Directorate of Agriculture.
- The applicant must ensure the sugarcane area is verified by the representatives of the Zonal Agriculture Office of the respective Taluka, along with the Fieldman of Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana (SSSKL).
- The applicant must be issued an area certificate following verification of the sugarcane area.
- The applicant must be present for the inspection of the harvested cane by the Fieldman of Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana (SSSKL) or any representative of the Agriculture Department.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:****** ***Registration By Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana (SSSKL)***
The Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana (SSSKL) shall register the farmers cultivating sugarcane.
**Step 2:****** ***Harvest And Supply Of Sugarcane***
The farmer must harvest the sugarcane and supply it on their own to Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana (SSSKL), Dayanandnagar Dharbandora. The farmer must bear the cost for loading and bringing the harvested cane to the Karkhana.
Verification and Sanction Process
**Step 3:****** ***Submission Of Supply Details***
The Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana (SSSKL) shall submit the details of the farmer who supplied cane, along with the weigh slips and the duly signed GAR 32 form of the farmer, to the respective Zonal Agricultural Office.
**Step 4:****** ***Verification And Recommendation By Zonal Agricultural Officer***
The Zonal Agricultural Officer shall verify the submitted details and forward the list with a recommendation to the respective District Agricultural Office.
**Step 5:****** ***Verification And Sanction Order Issuance***
The District Agricultural Officer shall verify the claims received and issue the Sanction Order after completing the verification process.
<br>

##### ***Disbursement***
The financial assistance shall be drawn and disbursed to the farmers by the respective Zonal Agricultural Officer, based on the Sanction Order issued by the respective District Agricultural Officer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific criteria must be fulfilled regarding my agricultural activities in order to qualify as an eligible participant under this support program?

To be considered eligible, you must be a farmer actively cultivating sugarcane within the geographical limits of the State of Goa.
<br>

### When is the specific calendar timeframe during which the processing factory is expected to carry out the formal registration process for eligible sugarcane growers?

Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana Limited (SSSKL) is mandated to register the farmers cultivating sugarcane during the month of May.
<br>

### If I have a genuine and unique case that does not strictly meet all the established scheme clauses, which specific official has the power to grant an exception?

The Minister of Agriculture is empowered to relax any or all clauses or conditions of the scheme in genuine cases.
<br>

### Who specifically is responsible for inspecting the area where I am growing sugarcane, and what is the outcome of a successful physical check of the cultivation site?

The sugarcane area must be verified by representatives of the Zonal Agriculture Office, along with the Fieldman of Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana Limited (SSSKL). After verification, an area certificate will be issued to you.
<br>

### What is the total operational period established for this financial assistance program, starting from its introduction year?

The scheme came into force starting from the year 2025-26 and is scheduled to remain operational for two years, until the end of 2026-27.
<br>

### Is there a mandatory document required from the Directorate of Agriculture that I must possess and ensure is up-to-date at the time of application for this financial support?

Yes, all participating farmers must possess a valid Krishi Card that has been duly issued by the Directorate of Agriculture.
<br>

### On what specific metric or tonnage measure will the final financial assistance amount be calculated and subsequently provided to me?

The payment of financial assistance will be strictly based on the actual tonnage of canes recorded when weighed at the Sugar factory weighbridge.
<br>

### Which national authority determines the rate or price formula used to calculate the financial assistance I receive for the tonnage of sugarcane supplied?

The financial assistance is provided according to the Fair & Remunerative Price (FRP) which is fixed from time to time by the National Federation of Co-operative Sugar Factories Limited, New Delhi.
<br>

### Am I personally responsible for covering the expenses associated with the logistics, such as loading and transporting the harvested cane to the designated processing location?

Yes, the harvested cane must be loaded and brought to Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana, Dayanandnagar Dharbandora, entirely at the farmer's own cost.
<br>

### Who is primarily responsible for performing the activities of harvesting the sugarcane crop and ensuring its subsequent delivery to the processing facility?

The farmers themselves are responsible for harvesting and supplying the sugarcane on their own initiative to Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana Limited (SSSKL).
<br>

### Will the state government or the sugar factory facility assume responsibility or liability for managing the logistics, such as harvesting or transporting the sugarcane to the mill?

No, neither the Government nor the Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana Limited (SSSKL) will take any responsibility for harvesting and transporting the sugarcane.
<br>

### What is the process regarding the specific accounting form, known as GAR 32, and what action must I take to ensure proper submission of my cane supply details?

You must duly sign the GAR 32 form, which Sanjivani Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana Limited (SSSKL) shall submit, along with the weigh slips and cane supply details, to the respective Zonal Agricultural Office.
<br>
