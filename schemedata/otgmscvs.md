## Brief Description

Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance/grant ranging from a minimum of ₹75,000/- to a maximum of ₹2,00,000/- is provided to serving soldiers and ex-servicemen of the state who have been conferred with the ‘Vishisht Seva Medal Award’.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “One-Time Grant to Soldiers Conferred with the Vishisht Seva Medal Award” is implemented by the State Sainik Board under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance/grant is provided to serving soldiers and ex-servicemen of the state who have been conferred with the &#39;Vishisht Seva Medal Award&#39;.
<br>

## Benefits

- A one-time grant ranging from a minimum of ₹75,000/- to a maximum of ₹2,00,000/- is given to serving soldiers and ex-servicemen of the state.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
   1. The applicant must be a serving soldier or an ex-serviceman of the state who has been conferred with the &#39;Vishisht Seva Medal Award&#39;.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- After obtaining the application form from the District Sainik Welfare Office, it must be submitted to the concerned office.
  - The following documents are required for the application: Aadhaar Card of the applicant, PAN Card, Photocopy of bank passbook and cancelled cheque, Identity card of the dependent (if the applicant is a dependent), Domicile certificate of Uttarakhand, Affidavit (on ₹10/- stamp paper) declaring that the applicant has not received, and will not receive in the future, the one-time grant/annuity payable on the gallantry medal from any other state, Photocopy of discharge book (if the applicant is an ex-serviceman), a photocopy of the Gazette Notification regarding conferment of &#39;Vishisht Seva Medal Award&#39;
  - After verification of the applications at the District Sainik Welfare Office level, eligible applicants are paid the approved amount through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Under this, the one-time grant is disbursed immediately, while the annuity amount is released annually.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Serving soldiers or ex-servicemen of Uttarakhand who have been conferred with the Vishisht Seva Medal are eligible.
<br>

### Is residency in Uttarakhand mandatory for applying?

Yes. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Uttarakhand.
<br>

### What is the financial benefit provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides a one-time financial grant ranging from a minimum of ₹75,000/- to a maximum of ₹2,00,000/-.
<br>

### Where can the application form be obtained?

The application form can be obtained from the District Sainik Welfare Office.
<br>

### Where should the application form be submitted?

The filled application form along with required documents must be submitted at the concerned District Sainik Welfare Office.
<br>

### Is an affidavit required with the application?

Yes. An affidavit on ₹10 stamp paper is required stating that the applicant has not received and will not receive the one-time grant/annuity from any other state.
<br>

### How is the grant amount disbursed?

The grant amount is disbursed directly to the applicant’s bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
