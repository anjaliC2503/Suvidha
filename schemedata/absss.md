## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide a platform (Sanskrit Conference) for scholars, professors, lecturers, teachers, and students engaged in teaching, learning, and research to present their findings.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Akhil Bhartiya Sanskrit Shodh Sammelan” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Sanskrit Academy, Haridwar, under the Department of Sanskrit Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a platform (Sanskrit Conference) for scholars, professors, lecturers, teachers, and students engaged in teaching, learning, and research to present their findings.
<br>

## Benefits

At the Sanskrit Shodh Sammelan, participants gain knowledge through new research in Sanskrit literature, exchange ideas, learn the methodology of writing research papers, and receive certificates.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. Acharyas, co-acharyas, assistant acharyas, lecturers, assistant professors, teachers, and research scholars from all states of the country who are engaged in study, teaching, or research are eligible to participate.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

In accordance with the rules and regulations of the Academy, researchers must complete the application form and submit their research paper on the prescribed subject via email by the due date. Those whose papers are found eligible by the Research Paper Evaluation Committee will be invited to participate.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can participate in the Sammelan?

Acharyas, co-acharyas, assistant acharyas, lecturers, assistant professors, teachers, and research scholars from all states engaged in study, teaching, or research.
<br>

### How should participants submit their research papers?

Participants must submit the application form and research paper via email by the specified due date.
<br>

### Who evaluates the submitted research papers?

The Research Paper Evaluation Committee of the Uttarakhand Sanskrit Academy evaluates all submissions.
<br>

### How are participants selected for the Sammelan?

Only those whose research papers are found eligible by the Evaluation Committee are invited to participate.
<br>

### Can students participate in the Sammelan?

Yes, research scholars and students engaged in Sanskrit research are eligible.
<br>

### What type of research papers are accepted?

Research papers should be academically sound, relevant to Sanskrit literature, and socially useful as per the scheme’s guidelines.
<br>

### Are there any certificates for participants?

Yes, eligible participants whose papers are accepted receive certificates from the Academy.
<br>
