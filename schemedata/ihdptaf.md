## Brief Description

The scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance the horticulture sector through financial assistance and subsidies to tribal area farmers across the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to strengthen horticulture-based livelihoods and promote modern horticultural practices among tribal farmers through financial assistance for farm mechanization, processing units, crop protection, post-harvest management, and export-related infrastructure. The scheme provides targeted subsidies on equipment, units, and inputs to ensure sustainable horticultural growth in tribal areas.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Capacity Building & Promotion:**


- Assistance for publication and promotional materials related to horticultural crops.
- Financial support for organizing and participating in exhibitions, competitions, and seminars.
- Subsidy for organizing or participating in Krishi Melas and payment for stall rentals.

> **Farm Mechanization Support:**


- Subsidy for purchase of power tillers and mini tractors to enhance horticultural productivity.
- Financial aid for harvesting equipment and crop protection equipment.

> **Processing & Value Addition:**


- Support for establishment of new distillation units for medicinal and aromatic crops.


- Subsidy for setting up new processing units for cashew and other fruit crops.


- Assistance for processing equipment for value addition.

> **Export Promotion:**


- Sea fridge subsidy for export of fruits, vegetables, flowers, and plants.
- Air fridge subsidy to promote export of horticultural produce.

> **Price Stabilization:**


- Minimum Support Price (MSP) assistance to ensure stable market prices for horticultural crops.

> **Infrastructure & Utilities Support:**


- Subsidy on electricity bills for operating greenhouses and tissue culture laboratories.
- Support for hi-tech greenhouses (with fan and pad system).
- Support for naturally ventilated polyhouses and net houses.

> **Post-Harvest Management:**


- Subsidy for packing materials used in post-harvest handling of horticultural produce.
- Support for setting up kaccha mandaps for tomato/chilly and other vegetable marketing.
- Construction of vegetable trellises and half-lined pedestrian paths for climbing vegetable crops.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be a farmer.
  - The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category.
  - The applicant should be practicing horticulture in tribal areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3"?

The main objective of the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" is to enhance the income and livelihood opportunities of tribal farmers in Gujarat by promoting horticultural development through financial assistance for farm mechanization, post-harvest management, export promotion, and infrastructure support.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is responsible for implementing the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3"?

The implementation of the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" is carried out by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department under the Government of Gujarat, which oversees all applications, disbursements, and monitoring activities related to the scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which category of farmers can apply for the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3"?

The scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" is exclusively targeted at farmers who belong to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category and are engaged in horticultural activities within the tribal regions of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of financial assistance is provided under the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3"?

Under the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3", farmers receive a wide range of financial assistance including subsidies for farm equipment, construction of greenhouses, establishment of processing units, electricity bills, packing materials, and export refrigeration support.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is support provided for exporting horticultural produce under this scheme?

Yes, the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" provides significant support for promoting the export of horticultural produce through financial subsidies for sea fridges and air fridges used in the transportation of fruits, vegetables, flowers, and plants.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Does the scheme cover infrastructure development for horticultural crops?

The scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" offers financial support for setting up advanced infrastructure such as hi-tech greenhouses with fan and pad systems, naturally ventilated polyhouses, net houses, and kaccha mandaps to facilitate better crop production and marketing.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit applicable for farmers to qualify for this scheme?

No, there is no individual or family income limit specified as part of the eligibility criteria for the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3", making it accessible to all tribal farmers regardless of their income status.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Does the scheme provide any support for post-harvest management activities?

Yes, the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" includes specific financial assistance for post-harvest management by offering subsidies on packing materials and post-harvest equipment to reduce wastage and improve the shelf life of horticultural produce.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of crop protection support is available under this scheme?

Under the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3", tribal farmers are eligible to receive subsidies for crop protection equipment, which helps them safeguard their horticultural crops from pests, diseases, and adverse weather conditions.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is electricity bill reimbursement provided under the scheme?

Yes, the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" offers a subsidy on electricity bills specifically for farmers operating greenhouses and tissue culture laboratories, thereby reducing their operational costs and encouraging technology adoption.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How can eligible farmers apply for the scheme?

Eligible farmers can apply for the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" by visiting the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, filling out the application form online, uploading the required documents, and submitting it for departmental verification.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents are necessary to apply under this scheme?

The documents required to apply under the scheme "Integrated Horticulture Development Program for Tribal Area Farmers (TASP) HRT-3" include the Aadhaar Card for identity proof, 7/12 Certificate for land ownership, and a bank passbook or cancelled cheque to facilitate direct benefit transfer.
