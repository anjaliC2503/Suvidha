## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide Scheduled Caste farmers with free and adequate irrigation by constructing small irrigation projects such as water tanks and pipelines in Scheduled Caste-dominated areas, thereby helping them improve their farming.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Construction of Minor Irrigation Projects for Scheduled Caste Beneficiaries” was launched by the Department of Minor Irrigation, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide Scheduled Caste farmers with free and adequate irrigation by constructing small irrigation projects such as water tanks and pipelines in Scheduled Caste-dominated areas, thereby helping them improve their farming. It is a 100% state government-funded scheme.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, farmers will be provided with free irrigation facilities. The following projects are constructed for collective irrigation in Scheduled Caste-dominated areas:

  - Hauz (Water Storage Tank)
  - Construction of Pipelines / Water Channels (Gool)

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiary must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
   1. The beneficiary must be a farmer engaged in agricultural activities.
   1. The beneficiary must belong to the Scheduled Caste category.
   1. Collective irrigation projects will be constructed under the scheme.
   1. The name of the proposed village must be included in the list of Scheduled Caste-dominated villages issued by the Department of Social Welfare.
   1. Sufficient irrigated area must be available for the project.
   1. The cost of the project must not exceed ₹4.00 lakh per hectare, and the benefit-cost ratio must be greater than 1.0.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- For construction of the project, project&#39;s proposal may be submitted to the Division, Sub-Division, or other departmental offices through the Gram Sabha, Gram Panchayat, District Panchayat, or other authorized channels. Based on site inspection and the suitability of the received proposals, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) will be prepared.
  - After examination by the departmental TAC, the DPR is forwarded to the headquarters. After which the proposal is sent to the government for approval. The construction work of the projects is initiated after receiving financial approval from the Government.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to benefit from this scheme?

Residents of Uttarakhand who are Scheduled Caste farmers engaged in agricultural activities are eligible.
<br>

### Is the scheme fully funded by the government?

Yes, the scheme is funded with 100% State Share.
<br>

### What types of irrigation structures are constructed under the scheme?

Two major structures are constructed:

- Hauz (Water Storage Tanks)
- Pipelines / Water Channels (Gool)

<br>

### Are the irrigation facilities provided free of cost?

Yes, all irrigation facilities under this scheme are provided completely free to eligible farmers.
<br>

### Is there a cost limit for each project?

Yes, the project cost must not exceed ₹4.00 lakh per hectare.
<br>

### How can project proposals be submitted?

Proposals can be submitted through the Gram Sabha, Gram Panchayat, District Panchayat, or other authorized bodies to the Division, Sub-Division, or concerned departmental offices.
<br>

### When does construction begin?

Construction begins only after the Government grants financial approval.
