## Brief Description

The scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading" aims to prevent post-harvest spoilage in horticultural crops by providing financial assistance for constructing storage infrastructure and purchasing sorting-grading equipment for farmers across the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading" by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat aims to reduce post-harvest losses in horticultural crops and improve value addition by providing financial assistance to farmers for establishing on-farm infrastructure, including sorting, grading, packing, and primary value addition. The support includes both construction-related infrastructure and modern equipment/machinery for on-farm operations.
<br>

## Benefits

- **For Construction of On-Farm Infrastructure:** Financial aid will be 50% of the unit cost or ₹1,50,000/- per unit, whichever is lower.- **For Machinery or Equipment Purchase:** Financial aid of 50% of the actual cost, subject to a maximum of ₹50,000/- per unit, whichever is less.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be a farmer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the primary objective of the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading"?

The primary objective of the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading" is to support farmers in Gujarat by reducing post-harvest losses in horticultural crops through financial assistance for constructing storage infrastructure and purchasing equipment required for sorting, grading, and other primary value addition processes.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading"?

Any individual who is a farmer and a resident of Gujarat is eligible to apply under the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What financial assistance is available for constructing on-farm infrastructure under this scheme?

Under the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading", a farmer is eligible to receive financial assistance amounting to 50% of the unit cost for constructing on-farm storage infrastructure, subject to a maximum limit of ₹1,50,000 per unit, whichever amount is lower.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### How is the financial assistance for equipment or machinery calculated under this scheme?

For purchasing machinery or equipment under the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading", the farmer will be provided financial assistance equal to 50% of the actual cost of the equipment, with a cap of ₹50,000 per unit, and whichever amount is less will be applicable.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit applicable for farmers to qualify for this scheme?

No, there is no individual or family income limit specified as part of the eligibility criteria for the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading", making it accessible to all tribal farmers regardless of their income status.
<br>

### How can a farmer apply for the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading"?

Eligible farmers can apply for the scheme by visiting the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, filling out the application form online, uploading the required documents, and submitting it for departmental verification.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

To apply for the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading", the farmer must submit a copy of the Aadhaar Card for identification, the 7/12 land certificate for ownership verification, and either a bank passbook or a cancelled cheque for enabling direct benefit transfer.
<br>

### What types of equipment are supported under this scheme?

Under the scheme "Assistance for Establishment of On-Farm Storage for Sorting-Grading", equipment that facilitates post-harvest activities such as grading, sorting, weighing, packing, washing, primary value addition, and storage (like crates and pallets) is supported.
<br>
