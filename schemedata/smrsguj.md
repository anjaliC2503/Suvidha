## Brief Description

The scheme provides awards in the field of mountaineering is in place to enthusiastically engage and inspire the youth of the state towards risky and adventurous activities like summiting, mountaineering and rock climbing and to honour and commend the youth of the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "State Mountaineering Reward Scheme" was launched by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide awards in the field of mountaineering is in place to enthusiastically engage and inspire the youth of the state towards risky and adventurous activities like summiting, mountaineering and rock climbing and to honor and commend the youth of the state who have achieved exceptional achievements in the field of mountaineering. Under the scheme, a cash award, a memento, and a certificate will be awarded to each of the adventurers of the state every year for their outstanding achievements in the field of mountaineering.
<br>

## Benefits

A cash award of ₹25,000/-, a memento, and a certificate will be awarded to each of the adventurers of the state every year for their outstanding achievements in the field of mountaineering.
<br>

## Exclusions

A person who has previously received an award in this field will not be eligible for the award.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a native of Gujarat.
- The applicant should have climbed a height of 7,000 meters once or a height of more than 6,000 meters twice.
- The climbing achievement of the applicant must be confirmed by the Director of the Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF).
- The applicant should not be involved in any criminal activity.

****This award can also be given posthumously, for which the legal heir of the awardee will have to apply.***
<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Visit to [official website](https://sycd.gujarat.gov.in/) of the Sports, Youth & Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat. 
**Step 2:** Click on the "Schemes" section on the website. A list of available schemes will appear. From the list of schemes, select "State Mountaineering Reward Scheme."
**Step 3:** A new page will display the scheme details. Scroll down on this page to find and download the "Application Form for State Mountaineering Award [(Annexure-A](https://commi-synca.gujarat.gov.in/writereaddata/Portal/Images/pdf/State-Mountaineering-Award-form.pdf)).
**Step 4:** Fill out the application form completely and clearly. Ensure all correspondence details, including telephone/mobile number, are clearly mentioned.
**Step 5:****** Gather all necessary supporting documents as listed in the Documents Required section. Submit the application form along with the supporting documents in duplicate.
**Step 6:****** Ensure the application is submitted within the stipulated time frame. Incomplete or unclear applications will not be accepted.
**Step 7:****** The submitted applications will be scrutinised by the State Level Selection Committee. The final selection will be made by the state-level selection committee from the received applications.
<br>

## Documents Required

1. Aadhaar Card.
1. Address Proof.
1. Qualification Details.
1. Photographs of the Sport/Adventurous Activity.
1. Certificate from the Director of the Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) (confirming climb of 7000 meters once or more than 6000 meters twice).
1. Certificate of Domicile (being a native of Gujarat).
1. Police Certificate (stating no involvement in criminal activity).
1. Application Form (in [Annexure-A](https://commi-synca.gujarat.gov.in/writereaddata/Portal/Images/pdf/State-Mountaineering-Award-form.pdf), in duplicate).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the primary aim of this scheme for the youth of the state?

The scheme aims to enthusiastically engage and inspire the youth towards risky and adventurous activities like summiting, mountaineering, and rock climbing. It also seeks to honour and commend those who have achieved exceptional feats in mountaineering.
<br>

### What kind of achievements are recognized under this scheme?

Exceptional achievements in the field of mountaineering by the youth of the state are recognised and commended under this scheme.
<br>

### Who are the intended beneficiaries of this award scheme?

The intended beneficiaries are adventurers of the state who have demonstrated outstanding achievements in the field of mountaineering.
<br>

### What types of activities does this scheme encourage youth participation in?

This scheme encourages youth participation in risky and adventurous activities such as summiting, mountaineering, and rock climbing.
<br>

### What specific recognition is provided to the awardees each year?

Each year, awardees receive a cash award, a memento, and a certificate for their outstanding achievements.
<br>

### What height must I have climbed to be eligible for this award?

You must have climbed a height of 7000 meters once or a height of more than 6000 meters twice to be eligible.
<br>

### Is a police certificate required as part of the application process?

Yes, a police certificate stating that the applicant is not involved in any criminal activity must be submitted with the application.
<br>

### Who makes the final selection of the awardees?

The final selection will be made by a state-level selection committee from the applications received.
<br>

### What are the documents required to be submitted with the application form?

The necessary supporting documents, along with the prescribed application form attached in Annexure-A, must be submitted. These include a certificate from IMF, a native of Gujarat certificate, and a police certificate.
<br>

### Are there specific individuals who form part of the State Level Selection Committee?

Yes, the committee includes the Secretary/Principal Secretary of Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Joint Secretary/Deputy Secretary, Commissioner of Youth Services and Cultural Activities, Principal of Swami Vivekananda Mountaineering Training Institute, and Assistant Director of Youth Services and Cultural Activities.
<br>

### Who chairs the State Level Selection Committee?

The Secretary, Principal Secretary, Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Secretariat, Gandhinagar, serves as the Chairman of the committee.
<br>

### What kind of details are important to include in the application for communication purposes?

It is crucial to clearly mention all correspondence details and telephone/mobile number in the application for communication.
<br>
