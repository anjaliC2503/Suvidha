## Brief Description

The scheme "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" aims to promote structured vegetable farming through the provision of financial assistance for constructing kachha/semi-pucca/pucca trellis mandaps to all farmers across the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote sustainable and structured vegetable crop cultivation by extending financial assistance to eligible farmers for building kachha, semi-pucca, and pucca trellis mandaps using materials like wood, bamboo, GI wire, and plastic wire. This initiative not only supports effective vegetable cultivation techniques but also improves the productivity and longevity of vegetable crops grown on trellises. 
<br>

## Benefits

> **Monetary Assistance:**


- **General Farmers:** 50% of unit cost or ₹80,000/- per hectare- **SC/ST Farmers:** 75% of unit cost or ₹1,20,000/- per hectare- **Devipoojak Farmers:** 90% of cost or ₹46,800/- per hectare
> **Input Support:**


- **Wood/Bamboo Supports:** 1,600 units per hectare- **GI Wire (12–18 gauge) or Plastic Wire:** 400 kg per hectare- **Recommended Plant Spacing:** 2.5 x 2.5 meters (approximate)
> **Coverage Limit:**


- **Maximum Area:** 2.00 hectares per Khata
**Validity:**

- **Assistance duration:** 3 years
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a farmer
  -  The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be engaged in vegetable cultivation.
  - The applicant should be willing to construct trellis mandaps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the scheme "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops"?

The main objective of the scheme is to encourage the adoption of structured farming methods by supporting farmers with financial assistance for constructing kachha, semi-pucca, or pucca trellis mandaps to improve vegetable crop productivity and quality.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the scheme "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops"?

All farmers residing in the state of Gujarat who are engaged in vegetable cultivation are eligible to apply under the "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the financial benefits available to farmers under this scheme?

Under the "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" scheme, General category farmers are entitled to receive 50% of the unit cost or ₹80,000/- per hectare, SC/ST category farmers receive 75% of the cost or ₹1,20,000/- per hectare, and Devipoojak farmers are eligible for 90% of the cost or ₹46,800/- per hectare.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there a limit on the area of land for which assistance can be availed?

Yes, the assistance under the scheme "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" is limited to a maximum of 2.00 hectares per Khata, and farmers cannot claim benefits for land beyond this limit.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the materials that farmers are expected to use for constructing the trellis mandap?

Farmers availing benefits under the "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" scheme are expected to use approved materials such as bamboo or wooden supports (1,600 units per hectare), GI wire of 12–18 gauge or plastic wire (400 kg per hectare) to ensure durable and efficient structures.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any specific spacing required for the trellis structure under the scheme?

Yes, the scheme "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" recommends a spacing of approximately 2.50 meters by 2.50 meters for the trellis structure to ensure optimal crop growth and structural balance.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the validity period of the assistance once it is approved?

Once approved, the financial assistance under the "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" scheme remains valid for a period of three years, within which the farmer is expected to construct and utilize the trellis structure.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Are farmers from Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, or Devipoojak communities given any special benefit?

Yes, farmers belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, or Devipoojak communities are given higher subsidy percentages under the "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" scheme, thereby ensuring inclusive and equitable access to agricultural infrastructure support.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can farmers from all districts of Gujarat apply for this scheme?

Yes, the "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" scheme is applicable across the entire state of Gujarat, allowing farmers from any district to apply, provided they meet the eligibility conditions.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the steps to apply for the scheme?

To apply for the “Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops,” the applicant must visit the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, fill out the online application form, upload the required documents, and submit the form electronically.
<br>

### Is there any income limit or financial background criteria for applying under this scheme?

There is no individual or family income limit specified in the guidelines for eligibility under the "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" scheme, making it accessible to all farmers irrespective of their economic background.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents are required for applying under this scheme?

Farmers applying for the "Assistance in Trellise Mandap for Cultivation of Vegetable Crops" scheme must submit a copy of their Aadhaar Card, 7/12 Certificate, and either a Bank Passbook or a Cancelled Cheque for bank account verification.
<br>
