## Brief Description

Under this scheme, free bicycle assistance is provided to economically backward class girls studying in Class 9 in government and grant-in-aid schools across Gujarat.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Saraswati Sadhana Yojana to Economically Backward Class Girls studying in Class 9&quot;  is a welfare scheme by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of Gujarat. The scheme offers free bicycles to girls enrolled in Class 9 in government and grant-in-aided schools, facilitating easier access to education and encouraging regular school attendance. This physical aid helps in improving educational participation and empowering female students from economically weaker backgrounds.

## Benefits

- Free bicycle provided to each eligible girl student.

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a permanent resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant must be a female student.
  - The applicant must be studying in Class 9.
  - The applicant must be enrolled in a government or grant-in-aided school in Gujarat.
  - The applicant must belong to the Economically Weaker Section (EWS).
  - The applicant&#39;s family income must not exceed ₹6,00,000 per annum.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the primary objective of this scheme?

The primary objective of the scheme is to encourage education among economically backward class girls by providing them with free bicycles, thereby reducing school dropouts and promoting regular school attendance.

### Who is eligible to apply for the Saraswati Sadhana Yojana?

The scheme is specifically designed for girl students who are currently studying in Class 9 in government or grant-in-aided schools and belong to the Economically Backward Class with a family income not exceeding ₹6,00,000/- per annum.
<br>

<br>

### What type of benefit does the scheme provide to the beneficiaries?

Under the Saraswati Sadhana Yojana, eligible girl students are provided with a free bicycle as a form of physical assistance to support their educational journey and help them travel to school conveniently.
<br>

<br>

### What is the rationale behind providing bicycles to Class 9 girls under this scheme?

The provision of bicycles is intended to ease transportation challenges faced by girls from economically weaker sections, thus reducing barriers to education and encouraging continued enrollment and attendance in secondary education.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit that determines the eligibility of an applicant for this scheme?

Yes, the scheme sets a maximum family income limit of ₹6,00,000 per annum, and only applicants from families with income equal to or below this threshold are eligible to apply.
<br>

<br>

### Can the application for the Saraswati Sadhana Yojana be submitted online?

Yes, applicants have the convenience of submitting their application online through the official [Digital Gujarat portal](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/frmMain1.aspx).
<br>

<br>

### Can male students apply for benefits under this scheme?

No, male students are not eligible to apply for this scheme, as it is exclusively designed for female students as part of the state’s initiative to promote girls’ education.
<br>

<br>

### Is submission of a caste certificate mandatory when applying for the scheme?

Yes, applicants are required to submit a valid caste certificate as one of the supporting documents to establish their eligibility under the Economically Backward Class category.

### Is the scheme applicable across all regions of Gujarat?

Yes, the scheme is implemented throughout the state of Gujarat and is applicable to all eligible applicants residing in any district or taluka within the state.

### Whom should applicants contact if they need more information or assistance regarding the scheme?

For further assistance or information, applicants are advised to visit the [Digital Gujarat](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/SchemPortal/ContactUs.aspx) portal.
