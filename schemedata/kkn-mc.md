## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to aged and needy Goan artists. It offers marriage assistance of up to ₹20,000/- for the first daughter and ₹15,000/- for the second daughter of eligible artists.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Kalakar Kritadnyata Nidhi: Marriage Component" was launched by the Directorate of Art & Culture, Government of Goa. The "Marriage Component" of the scheme aims to provide financial assistance to aged and needy Goan artists to address their daughter's marriage-related expenses. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Art & Culture, Government of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted through prescribed proforma directly at the Directorate of Art & Culture, Sanskruti Bhavan, Patto, Panaji, Goa.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Financial Assistance***

- Financial assistance of ₹20,000/- shall be sanctioned for the first wholly dependent daughter's marriage.
- Financial assistance of ₹15,000/- shall be sanctioned for the second wholly dependent daughter's marriage.
- Maximum two wholly dependent unmarried daughters shall be eligible for this benefit.

##### ***Disbursement***

- The financial assistance shall be disbursed through Electronic Clearing System mode.
- The assistance is a one-time grant per eligible daughter's marriage.

##### ***Conditions***

- The details of the expenditure incurred along with the vouchers shall be furnished to the department.
- The unutilized amount out of the sanctioned amount if any shall be refunded to the Government within 2 months from the date of marriage.

<br>

## Eligibility

##### ***For the Artist***

- The applicant must be a Goan aged needy artist, born and brought up in the state of Goa.
- The applicant must be recognized by the Directorate of Art & Culture, Government of Goa.
- The applicant must be not less than 50 years of age.
- The applicant's annual family income from all the sources should not exceed ₹2,00,000/- per annum.

##### ***For Dependent Daughter(s)***

- The daughters must be wholly dependent on the eligible Goan artist.
- The daughters must be unmarried at the time of application.
- Maximum two wholly dependent unmarried daughters shall be eligible.

##### ***Relaxation***

- The Government may relax the age criteria for the artist for deserving cases under special circumstances.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Visit the office of the Directorate of Art & Culture, Sanskruti Bhavan, Patto, Panaji, Goa, and collect the prescribed application form for the scheme.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the completed application form along with all required documents directly to the ***Directorate of Art & Culture, Sanskruti Bhavan, Patto, Panaji, Goa.***
<br>

##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 1:** The Directorate shall forward the application to the sub-committee for scrutinizing the application thoroughly. The Director shall give its recommendations to the sub-committee.
**Step 2:** The sub-committee constituted by the Government shall meet as and when required and shall consider the applications received for this purpose. The committee shall recommend the names of the artists which are considered fit by the sub-committee to avail the benefits under this scheme. The committee shall decide the quantum of assistance to be released to the needy artist.
**Step 3:** After the committee decides on the quantum of assistance, the approval from the Government shall be obtained for disbursement of the financial assistance.
**Step 4:** After obtaining Government approval, the Director shall disburse the one-time financial assistance amount to the eligible applicant in the form of Electronic Clearing System mode to the bank account.
<br>

## Documents Required

##### ***At the Time of Application***

- Dependency Certificate from the Authorized Authority.
- Birth Certificate of the Daughters.
- Marriage Certificate or any other document indicating the relationship.
- Income Certificate from the Competent Authority.
- Recognition Certificate from the Directorate of Art & Culture, Government of Goa.

##### ***After Benefit Disbursal***

- Details of the Expenditure Incurred.
- Vouchers for Expenses Incurred on the Marriage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum amount of financial support provided for marriages under this assistance program for artists' families?

The financial assistance provided is ₹20,000/- for the first wholly dependent daughter's marriage and ₹15,000/- for the second daughter's marriage.
<br>

### If my annual family earnings from all sources are ₹2,50,000/-, would I still qualify for the marriage benefit?

No, you would not qualify as the annual family income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,00,000/- per annum to be eligible.
<br>

### Can I receive assistance for the marriages of three daughters if all of them are wholly dependent on me?

No, the scheme provides assistance for a maximum of two wholly dependent unmarried daughters only.
<br>

### What happens to the financial assistance amount that remains unused after my daughter's wedding expenses are completed?

The unutilized amount out of the sanctioned amount must be refunded to the Government within 2 months from the date of marriage.
<br>

### Is there any specific age requirement that I need to meet as an artist to apply for this marriage assistance?

Yes, the age limit for grant of benefits shall be not less than 50 years in case of the eligible artist, though the Government may relax this in deserving cases.
<br>

### What type of certificate do I need to provide to establish that my daughter is wholly dependent on me?

You must submit a dependency certificate from the authorized authority to establish that your daughter is wholly dependent on you.
<br>

### After receiving the marriage assistance amount, what documentation am I required to submit to the department?

You must furnish the details of the expenditure incurred along with the vouchers to the department after the completion of the marriage.
<br>

### Can I apply for this assistance if my daughter is already married or if the wedding ceremony has already taken place?

The scheme is meant for wholly dependent unmarried daughters, so they must be unmarried at the time of application for eligibility.
<br>

### Do I need to have formal recognition from any government body to be considered eligible for this marriage assistance?

Yes, you must be recognized by the Directorate of Art & Culture, Government of Goa to be eligible for this assistance.
<br>

### Where exactly should I go to obtain and submit the application form for this marriage assistance program?

You should visit the Directorate of Art & Culture, Sanskruti Bhavan, Patto, Panaji, Goa during working days to obtain and submit the application form.
<br>

### What is the consequence if I fail to attach all the required certificates and documents with my application?

If all relevant documentation like birth certificate, marriage certificate, dependency certificates, and income certificates are not enclosed, the application shall be summarily rejected.
<br>

### If I am born in Goa but currently reside outside the state, can I still apply for this assistance?

The applicant must be a Goan aged needy artist, born and brought up in the state of Goa.
<br>

### Through what method will the approved financial assistance amount be transferred to my account?

The Director of Art & Culture shall disburse the one-time financial assistance amount to the eligible applicant through Electronic Clearing System mode.
<br>

### Who has the final authority to resolve any disputes or grievances that may arise during the implementation of this scheme?

The Minister for Art & Culture shall hear and decide such matters, and the decision of the Minister for Art & Culture shall be final.
<br>
