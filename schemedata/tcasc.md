## Brief Description

The scheme "Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Dept., aims to promote cultural awareness and coastal resource understanding by organizing guided trekking tours for Scheduled Caste youths aged 15 to 35 across the coastal region of Sagarkantha.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to offer Scheduled Caste youth a direct introduction to the culture of Sagarkantha, increase awareness about coastal industries and marine wealth, and motivate them to engage in ocean-related developmental activities.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Cultural and Industrial Exposure:** The program provides 100 Scheduled Caste youth annually with a 10-day trekking tour to explore the culture of Sagarkantha, learn about coastal wealth, and understand local industries.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste category.
  - The applicant should be between 15 and 35 years of age.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should download the prescribed format of the application form.

**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the office of the District Youth Development Officer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme?

The objective of the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme is to provide Scheduled Caste youth of Gujarat with a valuable opportunity to directly experience and understand the culture of the Sagarkantha region, gain knowledge about marine resources and coastal industries, and encourage their participation in activities related to ocean development and conservation.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to participate in the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme?

Any youth who belongs to the Scheduled Caste category, resides in Gujarat, and falls within the age group of 15 to 35 years is eligible to apply for participation under the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme.
<br>

### What is the age limit specified under the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme?

The “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme is open exclusively to Scheduled Caste individuals who are at least 15 years old and not older than 35 years at the time of application.
<br>

### Is there any income-based eligibility condition for availing the benefits of the scheme?

No, there is no income-based eligibility criterion under the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme, as the scheme is open to all Scheduled Caste youth irrespective of their individual or family income.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Does the applicant need to be from a specific educational or professional background to apply?

The scheme does not require the applicant to have any specific level of education or professional background, making it accessible to all Scheduled Caste youth within the eligible age range in Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How many beneficiaries are selected annually under this scheme?

Each year, a total of 100 Scheduled Caste youth are selected to participate in the 10-day coastal trekking program organized under the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme.
<br>

### What is the duration of the trekking tour conducted under the scheme?

The trekking tour conducted under the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme is designed as a 10-day program during which participants explore and learn about various aspects of the Sagarkantha coastal region.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of benefits do the participants receive under this scheme?

The participants under the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme receive non-monetary, physical benefits in the form of a guided trekking experience aimed at enhancing their cultural understanding, marine awareness, and personal motivation.
<br>

### Is the benefit provided under this scheme financial in nature?

No, the benefit provided under the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme is not financial or monetary in nature; it consists of an educational and experiential field program.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any application fee or cost for obtaining the application form?

There is no application fee associated with this scheme, and the application form for the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme is made available free of cost to all eligible applicants.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is online application facility available under this scheme?

As of now, there is no provision for online application submission under the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme, and applicants are required to obtain and submit the form through offline channels.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme?

The applicant is required to submit a duly filled application form in the prescribed format along with a valid Aadhar Card as identity proof while applying for the “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which department is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The “Trekking in Coastal Area for Scheduled Caste” scheme is implemented and managed by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department of the Government of Gujarat.
<br>
