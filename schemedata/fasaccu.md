## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial support or grants to recognized sports associations, clubs, and committees in the state for organizing sports competitions and for purchasing sports equipment for associations and players at the national, state, and district levels.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Financial Assistance and Grants to Sports Associations/Clubs/Sports Committees of Uttarakhand” was launched by the Department of Sports, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support or grants to recognized sports associations, clubs, and committees in the state for organizing sports competitions and for purchasing sports equipment for associations and players at the national, state, and district levels.
<br>

## Benefits

Recognized associations, clubs, and sports committees will receive financial assistance as follows:

- National-level competitions: ₹3.50 lakh
- State-level competitions: ₹2.50 lakh
- District-level competitions: ₹1.00 lakh

Additionally, there is a provision to provide ₹2.50 lakh to players selected to represent at the international level.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The recognized Sports Associations, Clubs, and Sports Committees in Uttarakhand are eligible to receive financial assistance or grants under this scheme.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Recognized associations, clubs, sports societies, and selected international-level players may apply for grants using the prescribed format, as per departmental rules.
  - Sports associations, clubs, and committees in Uttarakhand are provided grants and financial assistance for organizing sports competitions and purchasing sports equipment, benefiting approximately 20 associations, committees, and players throughout the year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Recognized sports associations, clubs, sports committees, and players selected for international-level competitions can apply.
<br>

### Is there any grant for individual players?

Yes, players selected for international-level competitions can receive Rs. 2.50 lakh under the scheme.
<br>

### How is the grant applied for?

Applications are submitted in the prescribed departmental format, as determined by departmental rules.
<br>

### What is the purpose of these grants?

Grants are meant for organizing competitions, training programs, and purchasing sports equipment for the development of sports in Uttarakhand.
<br>

### Is there any yearly limit on how many associations benefit?

Approximately 20 associations, committees, or players benefit from the scheme each year.
<br>

### Who determines the grant amount?

The Department of Sports, Government of Uttarakhand determines the grant amount based on the level of competition.
<br>
