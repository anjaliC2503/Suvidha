## Brief Description

The scheme “Financial Assistance to Inter-Caste Married Couples (Except SC/ST Category)” was launched by the Department of Social Justice, Government of Kerala.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Financial Assistance to Inter-Caste Married Couples (Except SC/ST Category)” was launched by the Department of Social Justice, Government of Kerala. Under the scheme, the Government of Kerala provides financial assistance to inter-caste married couples (except SC/ST category) who suffer from financial problems.

## Benefits

1. Under the scheme, a one-time financial assistance of ₹30,000/- will be given to inter-caste married couples.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Kerala State.
   1. The inter-caste married couples who are legally married are eligible for financial assistance.
   1. The combined family income of the couple should not exceed ₹1,00,000/-.
   1. The couples are entitled to financial assistance one-time only.
   1. The couples must apply for financial assistance after one year of marriage but not exceeding 2 years from the date of marriage.

**Note 01:** The financial assistance should be utilized for purposes like starting a business, for the purchase of land, or for the construction of a house. The Local Self Government Institutions (LSGIs) should ensure that funds are being utilized for the said purposes.
**Note 02:** While receiving financial assistance, couples will be required to sign an agreement and submit it to the LSGIs.

## Documents Required

1. Identity proof i.e. copy of Election ID Card/ Aadhaar card
   1. Passport size photos
   1. Income Certificate (Original) from the concerned Village Officer
   1. Certificate proving that Inter-caste marriage is registered (certificate has to be issued by Sub Registrar’s office, Panchayath President, certificates issued from organizations like NSS, and SNDP are acceptable).
   1. Caste certificate of the couple from the concerned Village Officer
   1. Original certificate (from Gazetted Officer/MLA/ MP) proving that the married couple has been together for the past one year.
   1. Copy of the Ration card of the couple
   1. Bank account details
