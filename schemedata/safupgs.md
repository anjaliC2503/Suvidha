## Brief Description

The "Scheme for Awarding Fellowships for Undergraduate and Postgraduate Students" provides financial support in the form of monthly scholarships to students pursuing higher education in various faculties at Anand Agricultural University.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme for Awarding Fellowships for Undergraduate and Postgraduate Students&quot; launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide monthly financial assistance to qualified undergraduate, postgraduate, and Ph.D. students at Anand Agricultural University.

## Benefits

- **Undergraduate Students:** ₹500/- per month for 12 months.  - **Postgraduate Students:** ₹1,500/- per month for 12 months.  - **Ph.D. Student:** ₹2,000/- per month for 12 months.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a regular full‑time student enrolled in an undergraduate/ postgraduate or Ph.D. program at Anand Agricultural University.
  - The student should not avail any other Scholarship/ Fellowship concurrently with this one.
  - The family income of the student should not exceed ₹6,00,000/- per year.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Write an application on plain paper addressed to the Director, Anand Agricultural University.
**Step 2:** Submit the completed application along with the necessary documents to the Principal of the college.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Scheme for Awarding Fellowships for Undergraduate and Postgraduate Students"?

The "Scheme for Awarding Fellowships for Undergraduate and Postgraduate Students" is a state government scholarship program that provides monthly financial assistance to qualified undergraduate, postgraduate, and Ph.D. students at Anand Agricultural University.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Any full-time undergraduate, postgraduate, or Ph.D. student currently enrolled at Anand Agricultural University is eligible to apply.
<br>

<br>

### How much financial assistance is provided to Under Graduate students?

Under Graduate students receive ₹500/- per month under this fellowship scheme for a period of 12 months.

### How much financial assistance is provided to Post Graduate students?

Post Graduate students receive ₹1,500/- per month under this fellowship scheme for a period of 12 months.
<br>

<br>

### What amount is granted to Ph.D. students through this scheme?

Ph.D. students studying at Anand Agricultural University are eligible for a monthly scholarship of ₹2,000/- for 12 months.
<br>

<br>

### Is there an income limit for students to be eligible for this fellowship?

Yes, the family income of the student should not exceed ₹6,00,000/- per year.

### Is the scheme applicable only to specific categories or communities?

No, the scheme is open to students of all categories, genders, and communities.
<br>

<br>

### Is a student allowed to receive another scholarship or fellowship along with this one?

No, a student should not avail any other scholarship or fellowship concurrently with this one. An undertaking confirming the same must be submitted each year to continue receiving the benefits under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the duration for which the fellowship is granted?

The fellowship is awarded for a continuous period of 12 months, subject to satisfactory academic progress.
<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant apply for the scheme?

Applicants must submit their application on plain paper with all required details and supporting documents to the Principal of the college.
