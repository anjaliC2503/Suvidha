## Detailed Description

The "Vice Chancellor Internship Scheme (VCIS)" by the University of Delhi aims to provide students with experiential learning opportunities, integrating academic knowledge with practical work experience. This initiative aligns with the objectives of "Samagra Shiksha" (holistic education) as outlined in the NEP 2020. The VCIS allows students to work in various departments, centers, and institutes within the university, offering a unique platform to broaden their skills and gain real-world experience.
> **Kinds of Internships under VCIS:** 


- Internship
- Summer Internship

> **Duration of the Internship:**


- Summer internships are 8 weeks long with 15-20 flexible hours per week. 
- Regular internships during the academic session are 8-10 hours per week, with a maximum duration of six months.

<br>

## Benefits

> **Stipend:** 


- ₹5,000/- per month for interns
- ₹10,500/- per month for summer interns.

**NOTE:** Stipend will be enhanced by 5% each financial year. 
> **Certificate:**


- Receive a certificate of experience from the Dean of Students' Welfare.

<br>

## Exclusions

- Those students who have already availed VCIS (Summer/ Part-time) are not eligible for the VCIS.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a student.
  - The students pursuing Final year/ Final Semester are not eligible.
  - All bonafide full-time regular students of the University of Delhi, studying in any course or stream at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels, are eligible.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Apply Procedure:**

**Step-01:** The interested applicant visits the [official website](https://www.du.ac.in/index.php?mact=News,cntnt01,detail,0&cntnt01articleid=14844&cntnt01returnid=219#:~:text=Vice%20Chancellor%20Internship%20Scheme%3A%20Summer,Cell%2C%20Dean%20Students'%20Welfare%20Office).

**Step-03:** Fill in the required information and upload all the essential documents.
**Note:** The list of selected candidates for the Vice Chancellor Internship Scheme is shown on the official website.
> **Selection Procedure:**


- Based on assessment and interaction with the selection committee, comprising members nominated by the Vice-Chancellor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the duration of the internship?

Summer internships are 8 weeks long with 15-20 flexible hours per week. Regular internships during the academic session are 8-10 hours per week, with a maximum duration of six months.

### How many interns are selected per session?

The total proposed number of interns is 200, subject to periodic review and approval.

### What are the working hours for interns?

Summer internships: 15-20 hours per week. Regular internships: 8-10 hours per week.

### Is this internship considered employment?

No, this internship is not employment nor an assurance of employment with the university.

### What happens if I perform unsatisfactorily during the internship?

Unsatisfactory performance may lead to discontinuation of the internship.

### Can I terminate the internship early?

Yes, interns can terminate the internship with prior notice.

### Will the university provide accommodation?

No, interns need to make their own accommodation arrangements.

### What is the selection process?

Based on assessment and interaction with the selection committee, comprising members nominated by the Vice-Chancellor.

### Where will the selected interns be placed?

In various departments/centers/institutes, including the Vice Chancellor's Office, Central Reference Library, Examination Branch, etc.

### What kind of tasks will I be doing?

Tasks include data collection, document streamlining, content creation, research, and community engagement.

### Can I apply if I have already done an internship under VCIS?

No, a student can avail of VCIS only once during their course of study at the University of Delhi.
