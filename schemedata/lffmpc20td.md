## Brief Description

The scheme aims to strengthen aquaculture through fish feed production. It provides a grant per unit to the beneficiaries under PMMSY.

## Detailed Description

The "Large Fish Feed Mill with Production Capacity of 20 Ton/Per Day" scheme was launched by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY), it aims to strengthen post-harvest infrastructure and enhance fish feed production to support sustainable aquaculture. Under this scheme, a grant is provided to beneficiaries for setting up a large fish feed mill, with 40% subsidy for general category and 60% for Scheduled Caste/Women beneficiaries. 
<br>

## Benefits

**Monetary Assistance:**

- Grant of ₹20,00,000/- per unit for establishing a fish feed mill with 20-ton/day capacity.

**Subsidy Provisions:**

- 40% subsidy on unit cost for General Category beneficiaries.
- 60% subsidy on unit cost for Scheduled Caste and women beneficiaries.

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1. Beneficiary must have Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).
1. The Beneficiaries will submit an undertaking in the DPR to the effect that all operational, maintenance and post construction management costs of the infrastructure facilities will be borne by them and the Fish Feed Plant will be kept in operational condition.
1. Central assistance for Fish Feed Mill will be as per actual cost within the overall ceiling indicated in the respective sub-components/activities under PMMSY.
1. The Beneficiary will ensure supply of feed produced from the government assisted Fish Feed Plant to the fishers and fish farmers at affordable price.
1. The projects having land on long term registered lease shall also be considered for Central financial assistance under the scheme. However, the registered lease period/agreement shall not be less than a minimum period of 10 (ten) years.

## Documents Required

- Date of Birth Certificate (Voting Card/Birth Certificate/Aadhar Card/Matriculation Certificate).
- Caste Certificate (Caste Certificate from first-class Magistrate (Tehsildar/SDM)).
- Agreement 1 (Agreement deed between the beneficiary and the Fisheries Department).
- Training Certificate of the Beneficiary (related to the operation/maintenance of fish feed mill).
- Land Record (issued from Tehsil (Land-Jamabandi, Nakal Akshizra/Fard/Registered Patta Nama) and the land title should be clear. Additionally, the latest .Jamabandi/Fard/ Akshizra should be attached
- GST Bills.
- Photographs of Fish Feed Mill with the Beneficiary.
- Details of Bank Account and Pan Card.
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) or Self Contained Proposal (SCP) (as per the guidelines issued under the Centrally Sponsored.
- Scheme PMMMSY).
- All bills of amount more than ₹50,000/- should be as per E-way/Income Tax/GST rules.
- Certificate from the C.A. with the bills submitted for the subsidy case, certifying that the payments for the goods purchased by the beneficiary comply with Income Tax/GST regulations.
- CLU/NOC/Pollution/Fire Certificate (from the concerned department).
- NOC (from the Gram Sabha/Municipal Corporation/Authority of the area where the unit is located).
- Site Plan/Area Site Plan must be attached with the subsidy case.

> ***Note******:*** All the documents should be in readable form.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for the fish feed mill subsidy?

Any individual with a Family Identity Card and relevant fisheries training.

### What is the grant amount?

The grant amount is ₹20,00,000 per unit.

### What is the production capacity supported under this scheme?

The 20 tons per day production capacity is supported.

### How many units can one applicant apply for?

Only one unit is allowed per applicant.

### Is leased land eligible?

Yes, with a registered lease of at least 10 years.

### What documents are mandatory?

DPR, land records, training certificate, CA certificate, PAN, and bank details.

### Is GST mandatory for bills submitted?

Yes, for bills exceeding ₹50,000.

### What if the unit is already constructed?

The subsidy is only applicable if the DPR and proposal are pre-approved.

### What is the timeline for application processing?

Within 50 working days.

### Where can I apply for this scheme?

The applicant can apply at the local District Fisheries Office or the Antyodaya SARAL portal.
