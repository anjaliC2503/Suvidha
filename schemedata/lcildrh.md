## Brief Description

The scheme aims to motivate the growers to bring more area under irrigation by constructing water storage structures, rain water harvesting structures and installation of irrigation equipments for increasing the productivity and improving quality of large cardamom

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Large Cardamom Irrigation Land Development: Rainwater Harvesting Structures (Nos)” is a component of the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion and Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom, implemented during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26) under the Export-Oriented Production component by the Spices Board, India, under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. The scheme aims to motivate growers to bring more area under irrigation by supporting the construction of water storage structures, rainwater harvesting systems, and the installation of irrigation equipment, thereby increasing productivity and improving the quality of large cardamom.
<br>

## Benefits

1. 33.33 % of cost of construction subject to a maximum of ₹18,000 per structure for General and 75% subject to maximum of ₹ 40,000 /- per structure for SC/ST
1. The above assistance will be for a structure of 200 cu. Mt. capacity lined with silpauline sheet. For lesser capacity the assistance will be limited proportionate to the water holding capacity.
1. Large cardamom growers having land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha Devices can be for harvesting water from direct rainfall as well as surface run off without blocking the natural flow of water including streams.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a large cardamom grower located in Sikkim, Darjeeling and Kalimpong districts of West Bengal, or other North Eastern States.
1. The applicant should undertake construction activities in a manner that does not block the natural flow of water, including streams, and is not objectionable to the local community or concerned authorities.
1. The applicant should ensure that the proposed rainwater harvesting or irrigation structure complies with all local regulations and environmental norms.
1. The applicant should allow inspection by designated officials, who will conduct the final inspection and recommend only eligible cases for assistance.
1. The applicant should ensure that the Inspecting Officer can verify that no violations of the prescribed conditions have occurred before recommending the case for sanction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Rainwater Harvesting Structures scheme?

The scheme aims to encourage large cardamom growers to expand irrigation coverage by constructing rainwater harvesting and water storage structures, thereby improving productivity and crop quality.
<br>

### Who is eligible to avail benefits under this scheme?

Large cardamom growers with landholding sizes ranging from 0.10 hectares to 8 hectares are eligible to apply.
<br>

### Which geographical areas are covered under the scheme?

The scheme is applicable in Sikkim, Darjeeling and Kalimpong districts of West Bengal, and other North Eastern States.
<br>

### Hat type of structures are supported under this scheme?

The scheme supports the construction of rainwater harvesting structures designed to collect water from direct rainfall and surface runoff.
<br>

### Is there a standard size or capacity requirement for the structure?

Yes, assistance is provided for structures with a capacity of 200 cubic meters, typically lined with silpauline sheets.
<br>

### What happens if the structure has a smaller capacity than prescribed?

If the structure has less than 200 cubic meters capacity, the financial assistance will be provided on a proportionate basis.
<br>

### Can the structure block natural water flow such as streams?

No, structures must not block natural water flow. Any such construction will be considered ineligible.
<br>

### Are there environmental or community-related restrictions?

Yes, constructions that are objectionable to the local community or authorities will not be considered for assistance.
<br>

### What is the role of the Inspecting Officer?

The Inspecting Officer ensures that all scheme conditions are met and that the construction does not violate any guidelines before recommending the case for sanction.
