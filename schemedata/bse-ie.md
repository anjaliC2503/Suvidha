## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage Brahmin students of Andhra Pradesh to continue their education. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided to eligible poor Brahmin students.

## Detailed Description

The "Bharati Scheme for Education - Intermediate or Equivalent (ITI, Polytechnic, D.Ed, TTC, D.Pharmacy, etc.)" introduced by the Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation aims to encourage Brahmin students of Andhra Pradesh to continue their education. Under this scheme, eligible poor Brahmin student applicants will receive financial assistance for the academic year. This scheme applies to the Brahmin students of Andhra Pradesh, covering all 13 districts. The Andhra Pradesh Brahmin Welfare Corporation implements the scheme.
<br>

## Benefits

1. Financial Assistance: ₹10,000/-.
1. Intended Use: To encourage Brahmin students to continue their education.

<br>

> **Mode of Disbursement:** The amount of Financial Assistance granted will be credited through online to the SB Accounts of the students.

<br>

## Exclusions

The applicant should not have availed himself/herself any amount under any other Government scheme for the same purpose.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The student and his/her parents should belong to the Brahmin community.
1. The parents should reside in Andhra Pradesh.
1. The student's parents/guardians should stay in Andhra Pradesh. 
1. The student should be enrolled in the Praja Sadhikara Survey (PSS).
1. The annual family income of Parents/guardians should not be more than ₹3,00,000/- (Rupees Three lakhs only).
1. The student should be pursuing a regular course in a School/College/Institute/University for the Academic year 2019-20.
1. The student should have passed in all the subjects till the previous year of study (i.e., students who do not have any backlogs in their previous semesters/years are only eligible).
1. The students availing the benefit from Archaka Welfare Trust are eligible under this scheme, in addition to the assistance they get from Archaka Welfare Trust.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration**

**Step-01:** Applicants have to visit the [official website](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/) and click on [Scheme](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/schemes/schemes.aspx#).
**Step-02:** Click on "Registration" under the concerned scheme name.
**Step-03:** Fill out the application form with required details like:


  - Personal Details: Name, Residence address, Aadhaar no, Ration card no, Bank detail etc
  - Upload: Photo, Signature and Certificate Details 
  - Documents: Birth Certificate, Residential Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Caste Certificate, Bank Passbook etc
  - Finalize and "Submit" the application.


**Step-04:** Receive the reference number after the submission.
<br>

> **Check Application Status:**

**Step-01:** Click on the “Services” link on the menu and select "[Know your status](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/eSchemes/know_stat.aspx)".
**Step-02:** Enter the Reference or Mobile or Aadhaar Card Number to get the application status.
**Step-03:** Select the "Financial Year" and Fetch the details. 
<br>

> **Download the Acknowledgement slip:**

**Step-01:** Applicants have to visit the [official website](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/) and click on [Scheme](https://www.andhrabrahmin.ap.gov.in/schemes/schemes.aspx#).
**Step-02:** Click on the "Acknowledgement Slip" under the concerned scheme.
**Step-03:** Enter the Aadhaar Card Number and select the "Scheme".
**Step-04:** Click on the "Submit".

## Documents Required

- Aadhar Card of the Student & Parents (with address in Andhra Pradesh).
- White Ration Card / Income Certificate Number (issued by Mee Seva).
- Integrated Certificate (Caste, Residence and Date of Birth) Number (issued by Mee Seva).
- Study Certificate (from the College / Institute for the Academic Year 2019-20).
- Marks Sheets Of All The Previous Years (starting from Senior Secondary Certificate (SSC)) 
- SB Account Passbook (in the name of the applicant; the account should be in Andhra Bank or State Bank of India (SBI))

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I ensure my family and I are eligible for this education scheme?

To be eligible, you and your parents must belong to the Brahmin community, and your parents must reside in Andhra Pradesh. Your annual family income should not exceed  ₹ 3,00,000/-.
<br>

### What is the primary purpose of this scheme for students in Andhra Pradesh?

This scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the academic year to eligible poor Brahmin students in Andhra Pradesh to encourage them to continue their education.
<br>

### Is it necessary for me to be enrolled in the Praja Sadhikara Survey to be eligible?

Yes, you should be enrolled in the Praja Sadhikara Survey (PSS) to be eligible for this scheme.
<br>

### What is the maximum annual family income allowed to qualify for this scheme?

Your parents' or guardian's annual family income should not be more than  ₹ 3,00,000/- (Rupees Three lakhs only).
<br>

### Am I eligible if I have backlogs from previous semesters or years of study?

No, you should have passed in all subjects till the previous year of study, meaning no backlogs are allowed.
<br>

### What documents should I have ready before starting the online registration process?

You should have your Aadhar Card (student and parent with Andhra Pradesh address), White Ration Card/Income certificate Number issued by Mee Seva, and Integrated Certificate (Caste, Residence and Date of Birth) Number issued by Mee Seva ready.
<br>

### Am I eligible if I have backlogs from previous semesters or years of study?

No, you should have passed in all subjects till the previous year of study, meaning no backlogs are allowed.
<br>

### Can I receive benefits from this scheme if I am already availing another government scheme for education?

Generally, you should not have availed of any amount under any other Government scheme for the same purpose.
<br>

### Are students receiving benefits from Archaka Welfare Trust also eligible for this scheme?

Yes, students availing themselves of the benefits from Archaka Welfare Trust are eligible under this scheme, in addition to the assistance they receive from Archaka Welfare Trust.
<br>

### In which banks should my SB Account Passbook be for the financial assistance?

Your SB Account should be in Andhra Bank or SBI. However, for students of Professional Education Courses and Post Graduation outside Andhra Pradesh, an account in any Nationalised Bank is acceptable.
<br>

### Who is responsible for the selection process and sanctioning of financial assistance?

The State Level Selection Committee (SLSC) of A.P. Brahmin Welfare Corporation will handle the shortlisting, finalisation of applications, and sanction of Financial Assistance.
<br>

### What specific documents are required for Intermediate or equivalent courses like ITI or Polytechnic?

You need to upload your Study Certificate for the Academic Year, Marks Sheets of all previous years (starting from SSC), and your SB Account Passbook (Andhra Bank or SBI).
<br>

### What are the eligibility requirements for students pursuing Graduation courses (e.g., B.A., B.Com, B.Sc.)?

You must be studying in a recognized college in Andhra Pradesh for the academic year, your parents must reside in Andhra Pradesh, your family income should not exceed 3 Lakhs, and you must have cleared all subjects from previous years.
<br>
