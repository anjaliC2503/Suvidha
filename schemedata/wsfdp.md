## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide support, rehabilitation, and empowerment to persons with disabilities through financial assistance for healthcare, education, vocational training, and assistive devices.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Welfare Services for Disabled Persons&quot; under the Mizoram Grant-in-Aid framework focuses on improving the quality of life of persons with disabilities by supporting voluntary organizations in delivering specialized services. Beneficiaries include persons with locomotor, visual, hearing, and mental disabilities, and multiple disabilities such as cerebral palsy and autism.
The scheme supports activities like vocational training, early detection, medical treatment, rehabilitation, and the provision of assistive devices. It also promotes awareness regarding the rights of disabled persons and supports the establishment of special schools and institutions.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Rehabilitation & Training:**


- Vocational training for self-employment.
- Early detection and intervention services.

> **Medical Support:**


- Treatment and rehabilitation services.
- HIV/AIDS care for disabled persons.

> **Assistive Devices:**


- Prosthetics, mobility aids, hearing aids, Braille equipment.
- Education Support.
- Special schools and vocational centres.

> **Awareness:**


- Awareness programmes on disability rights.

<br>

## Eligibility

> **For Individuals (Beneficiaries):**


- The applicant must be a person with disability having at least 40% disability, duly certified by a competent medical authority.
- The applicant must belong to any category such as locomotor, visual, hearing, mental, or multiple disabilities.
- The applicant must require support for rehabilitation, medical treatment, education, or livelihood.

> **For Organizations:**


- The organization must be a registered voluntary organization/NGO/institution.
- The organization must have experience in implementing disability welfare programmes.
- The organization must have adequate infrastructure and trained personnel to provide services.
- The organization must ensure that services are provided without discrimination.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The objective of the scheme is to provide rehabilitation, support, and empowerment services to persons with disabilities.
<br>

### Who can benefit from this scheme?

Persons with disabilities having at least 40% certified disability can benefit from this scheme.
<br>

### What types of disabilities are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers locomotor, visual, hearing, mental, and multiple disabilities including cerebral palsy and autism.
<br>

### Is a disability certificate mandatory to apply?

Yes, the applicant must possess a valid disability certificate issued by a competent medical authority.
<br>

### Does the scheme provide financial assistance?

Yes, the scheme provides financial assistance for rehabilitation, treatment, and support services.
<br>

### Are assistive devices provided under this scheme?

Yes, assistive devices such as prosthetics, hearing aids, and mobility aids are provided to eligible beneficiaries.
<br>

### Does the scheme support vocational training?

Yes, the scheme provides vocational training to help persons with disabilities become self-employed.
<br>

### Can organizations apply under this scheme?

Yes, registered voluntary organizations and institutions can apply to implement welfare services.
<br>

### What kind of services can organizations provide under the scheme?

Organizations can provide services such as rehabilitation, medical care, special education, and awareness programmes.
<br>

### Is medical treatment covered under the scheme?

Yes, the scheme includes support for medical treatment and rehabilitation services.
<br>

### Are there any awareness programmes under this scheme?

Yes, awareness programmes on disability rights and inclusion are supported under the scheme.
<br>

### Does the scheme support special schools for disabled persons?

Yes, the scheme supports the establishment and running of special schools and vocational centres.
<br>

### Is there any age limit for beneficiaries?

No, there is no specific age limit, but eligibility depends on disability certification and need.
<br>
