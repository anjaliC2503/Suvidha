## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage tribal students to participate in sports and prepare them for national & international competitions. Tribal students from Classes 6 to 12 are provided with free accommodation, specialized training, nutritious meals & other necessary facilities in the sports hostels.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Operation of Sports Hostels&quot; was introduced by the Tribal Area Development Department, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to encourage tribal students in Scheduled Areas to participate in sports and prepare them for national and international competitions. Under this scheme, tribal students from Class 6 to 12 are provided with free residential facilities, training in archery and athletics by skilled coaches, nutritious meals, uniforms, and modern sports equipment. The scheme is implemented in the following District:	Banswara, Dungarpur, Sirohi, Udaipur &amp; Pratapgarh.

## Benefits

The following facilities are provided in the sports hostel:

- Free residential accommodation in sports hostels.
- Nutritious meals, snacks, and special dietary arrangements as per approved pattern.
- Sports training with modern equipment by expert coaches (especially in archery and athletics).
- Free school uniforms and educational materials.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Rajasthan.
1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribe community.
1. The applicant should be studying in class 6th to 12th.1. The applicant should not have any disability or suffering from any serious illness.
1. The applicant’s parents/guardians should not be income tax payers.
1. The applicant should not be a failed student in the last examination.

**Note:** Only those students who pass the selection test will be admitted to the sports hostel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply?

ST students from Rajasthan studying in classes 6th to 12th.

### Is there a selection test?

Yes, selection is based on battery tests and skill evaluations.

### What benefits are provided?

Free stay, food, uniforms, education materials, and expert sports training.

### Where are the hostels located?

In Banswara, Dungarpur, Sirohi, Udaipur, and Pratapgarh.

### What sports are trained?

Archery and athletics.

### What if a student fails a class?

Failed students are not eligible for admission.

### Are students with disabilities eligible?

No, students must be physically fit and not suffering from any disease.

### How is the application submitted?

Online via e-Mitra or the official website.

### Is there any application fee?

No, applying is free of cost.

### What documents are needed?

Aadhar, Jan Aadhar/Bhamashah, marksheet, and income certificate.

### Who implements the scheme?

The Tribal Area Development Department of Rajasthan.
