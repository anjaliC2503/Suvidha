## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to eligible Indian students admitted to M.Sc. (Dairying) and Ph.D. (Dairying) programmes at NDRI through monthly scholarships during the prescribed period of study.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;NDRI Institute Scholarship for M.Sc. and Ph.D. Programmes&quot; is implemented by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare to provide financial assistance to Indian students admitted to Master&#39;s and Doctoral programmes at the National Dairy Research Institute (NDRI). The scholarship is available only to students who do not receive any other financial assistance or scholarship from any source. Financial support is provided in the form of monthly scholarships during the prescribed duration of the academic programme. Continuation of the scholarship is subject to academic performance, course registration requirements, submission of progress reports, and compliance with institutional rules and regulations. No extension of scholarship is granted beyond the prescribed period.
<br>

## Benefits

<br>

**Programme****Institute Scholarships Details**Master&amp;amp;#39;s degree₹7,560/- P.M. for two years + ₹,6000/- per annum as contingencyPh.D.₹25,000/- P.M. for first two years, ₹28,000/- during third year, and ₹10,000/- per annum as contingency.Ph.D. (In-service)₹3,000/- P.M. for three years and ₹10,000/- per annum as contingency<br>

## Eligibility

- “Institute Scholarship” is offered to all students of Indian Nationality admitted to Master’s and Doctoral programmes at NDRI to provide them with financial assistance as per the rules and conditions in this document.
- Scholarships are offered to only those who do not hold any other financial Assistance/ Scholarship. As and when a student receives or accepts financial assistance from any other source, the “Institute Scholarship” will be terminated.
- The award of a scholarship will be made on receipt of the following certificates:


1. Acceptance of the award in the prescribed proforma.
1. An undertaking to follow the rules and regulations as per the conditions governing the Scholarship in the prescribed proforma.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Application URL:** https://education.icar.gov.in/

**Step 1:** Obtain admission to the eligible Master's or Doctoral programme at NDRI.
**Step 2:** Submit the [Acceptance of the Award](https://ndri.res.in//sites/default/files/2025-06/UNDERTAKING_Conditions-of-Award.pdf) in the prescribed format.
**Step 3:** Submit the Undertaking to comply with scholarship rules and regulations.
**Step 4:** Complete registration for all prescribed courses in each semester.
**Step 5:** Submit periodic Progress Reports as prescribed.
**Step 6:** Maintain the required academic performance for continuation and renewal of the scholarship.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the NDRI Institute Scholarship?

The scheme provides financial assistance to eligible Indian students pursuing Master's and Doctoral programmes at NDRI.

<br>

### Who can apply for this scholarship?

Indian students admitted to eligible Master's or Doctoral programmes at NDRI can apply for the scholarship.
<br>

### Can a student receive this scholarship along with another scholarship?

No, a student receiving any other financial assistance or scholarship is not eligible for this scholarship.

<br>

### How much scholarship is provided to Master's students?

Eligible Master's students receive ₹7,560/- per month.

<br>

### How much scholarship is provided to Doctoral students?

Eligible Doctoral students receive ₹25,000/- per month during the first 2 years and ₹28,000/- per month during the 3rd year.
<br>

### What is the scholarship amount for in-service Ph.D. students?

Eligible in-service Ph.D. students receive ₹3,000/- per month subject to prescribed conditions.
<br>

### When does the scholarship begin?

The scholarship begins from the date of joining the programme.
<br>

### What is the duration of the scholarship for Master's students?

The scholarship continues until thesis submission or completion of the prescribed scholarship period, whichever is earlier.
<br>

### What is the duration of the scholarship for Doctoral students?

The scholarship continues until thesis submission or completion of the prescribed scholarship period, whichever is earlier.
<br>

### Is extension of the scholarship allowed?

No, extension of the scholarship is not granted under any circumstances.
<br>

### What academic performance is required for continuation of the scholarship?

The student must obtain at least 65% marks or 6.5 Grade Point Average out of 10.0 in aggregate.
<br>

### What happens if a student fails to achieve the required academic standard?

The student may improve performance through supplementary or semester-end examinations and become eligible for renewal after meeting the required standard.
<br>

### Is submission of a Progress Report mandatory?

Yes, submission of the prescribed Progress Report is mandatory for release of the scholarship.
<br>
