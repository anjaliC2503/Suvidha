## Brief Description

This scheme aims to provide safe housing for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Extremely Backward Classes whose existing group housing under previous schemes has become broken down.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Mukhyamantri Gramin Awas Yojana&quot; was introduced by the Rural Development Department, Bihar, to address the housing needs of beneficiaries belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Extremely Backward Classes whose cluster housing, built under previous schemes before January 1996, has deteriorated. As these families are ineligible for the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana due to prior benefits, this scheme provides financial assistance in three installments directly to the beneficiaries&#39; accounts for reconstructing their homes.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,20,000/-  (Provided in three installments: ₹40,000/- each through Direct Benefit Transfer or DBT).

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must belong to rural area.
   1. The applicant must belong to the Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, or Extremely Backward Class category.
   1. The applicant must reside in a cluster housing scheme built under previous government programs before January 1996.
   1. The applicant must have a house that is currently in a dilapidated condition.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Applicants must visit the Block Development Officer (BDO) office in their respective area.
**Step 2:** Collect the Mukhyamantri Gramin Awas Yojana application form from the office and fill properly.
**Step 3:** Submit the filled form along with the required documents at the BDO office. Ensure you obtain an acknowledgment receipt for your submission.
> **Note:** Field officers may visit the site to inspect the condition of the existing house. Upon successful verification, the application is approved, and the financial assistance is sanctioned.

1. Aadhaar card of the applicant.

   1. Bank account details.
   1. Proof of ownership of damaged/broken house (if applicable).
   1. Recent passport-sized photograph.
   1. Address proof.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Mukhyamantri Gramin Awas Yojana"?

The purpose of the "Mukhyamantri Gramin Awas Yojana" is to provide financial assistance for the reconstruction of dilapidated houses belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Extremely Backward Classes who previously benefited from pre-1996 housing schemes.

### Who can apply for this scheme?

The scheme is open to individuals belonging to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, or Extremely Backward Classes with dilapidated houses constructed under cluster housing schemes before January 1996.

### How much financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance of [?]1,20,000/-, which is disbursed in three installments of [?]40,000 each.

### How are the funds disbursed to beneficiaries?

The funds are disbursed directly to the beneficiary's bank account through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system.

### What are the required documents for the application?

The required documents include an Aadhaar card, caste certificate, bank account details, proof of ownership of the dilapidated house, recent photograph, and address proof.

### Can beneficiaries of the "Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana" apply for this scheme?

No, individuals who have already benefited from the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana are not eligible for this scheme.

### Is there any fee to apply for the "Mukhyamantri Gramin Awas Yojana"?

No, there is no fee to apply for the scheme.

### Can the application be rejected?

Yes, the application can be rejected if the applicant fails to meet the eligibility criteria or submit the required documents.

### What happens after the application is submitted?

After submission, the documents are verified, and a field inspection may be conducted to assess the condition of the existing house. Upon approval, the funds are disbursed in installments.

### Are widows or single mothers eligible for this scheme?

Yes, widows or single mothers who meet the eligibility criteria can apply for the scheme.

### Is the scheme applicable to urban areas?

No, the Mukhyamantri Gramin Awas Yojana is specifically designed for rural areas.

### What is the eligibility period for cluster housing under this scheme?

The scheme is applicable to houses constructed under cluster housing schemes initiated before January 1996.

### Where can applicants get more information about the scheme?

Applicants can get more information about the scheme from the local Block Development Officer's office or the official scheme portal.
