## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support small and marginal jute growers with adequate pre and post harvesting operations to improve fiber quality and receive higher prices. The scheme provides 100% certified seeds at subsidized rates, free mechanical equipment, microbial consortiums, retting ponds.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Improved Cultivation And Advanced Retting Exercise (I-CARE)" was launched by the National Jute Board, Ministry of Textiles. The scheme aims to support the small and marginal jute growers with adequate pre and post harvesting operations so that they can grow good quality jute and receive a higher price for their produce. The scheme provides 100% certified high yielding seeds at a 50% subsidized rate, demonstration and use of scientific jute cultivation practices with mechanical intervention, free distribution of a microbial consortium for advanced retting, construction of retting ponds, and a ₹5,000/- one-time maintenance assistance. The applicant must be a registered small or marginal jute grower willing to provide land consent for retting ponds and adopt scientific agronomic techniques. The scheme is implemented by the National Jute Board in association with the Jute Corporation of India and the Central Research Institute For Jute And Allied Fibres. The applications for this scheme are accepted through local agencies, cooperative bodies, and farmers' clubs at the block level.
<br>

## Benefits

- Provision of 100% certified high yielding jute seeds at a 50% subsidized rate supplied by the National Seed Corporation and the Central Research Institute For Jute And Allied Fibres to ensure better crop quality and yield.
- Demonstration of scientific jute cultivation practices and free usage of mechanical interventions, including multi-row seed drills, nail weeders, and single wheel jute weeders, reducing manual weeding costs by approximately 35%.
- Free distribution of a microbial consortium to improve the quality of jute fiber by at least one grade, increase fiber productivity by 10%, and reduce retting time from 18 to 12 days.
- Construction of small retting ponds in the farmers’ fields through the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana, and Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana.
- A one-time financial assistance of ₹5,000/- per pond towards maintenance costs.

##### ***Disbursal Conditions for Financial Assistance***

- The ₹5,000/- maintenance cost is transferred directly to the farmers’ bank accounts through Direct Benefit Transfer after the completion of the retting pond and the submission of a confirmation report, photographic evidence, and Global Positioning System coordinates by the field officials.
- Extensive farmer training and awareness programs on the use of seed drills, single wheel jute weeders, and microbial retting techniques.
- Short Message Service advisories and voice messages detailing land preparation, sowing, de-weeding, plant protection, and retting to educate the growers.

##### ***Conditions for Availment***

- The farmers must utilize the provided inputs such as certified seeds and the microbial consortium strictly for jute cultivation and retting, and successfully maintain the constructed in-situ retting ponds on their fields.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a small and marginal jute grower.
- The applicant must be registered in the scheme by the Jute Corporation Of India in association with different farmers’ societies, co-operative bodies, Primary Agricultural Credit Societies, Krishi Vigyan Kendras, or farmers’ clubs under the National Bank For Agriculture And Rural Development.
- The applicant must provide formal consent for their land to be used by the State Government Department for the construction of the retting pond.
- The applicant should be willing to transition from traditional stem retting to scientific methods of cultivation and microbial retting.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

##### ***Registration Process***
**Step 1:****** ***Contacting Local Agencies***
The applicant must approach the local farmers' societies, co-operative bodies, Primary Agricultural Credit Societies, Krishi Vigyan Kendras, or farmers' clubs to initiate the offline registration process. These local bodies act as the primary touchpoints for the scheme at the village level.
**Step 2:****** ***Submitting Registration Details***
The applicant must fill out the required registration and feedback forms provided by the Jute Corporation Of India through their respective local agency. The forms collect the farmer's details and establish their registration within the scheme.
<br>

##### ***Application Process***
**Step 3:****** ***Providing Land Consent For Retting Ponds***
The applicant must submit formal consent for using their land if they wish to have an in-situ retting pond constructed. This consent is collected by the field supervisors of the Jute Corporation Of India and forwarded to the State Government Department for prompt construction.
**Step 4:****** ***Providing Bank Details For Maintenance Fund***
The applicant must submit their valid bank account details to the field officials and regional managers of the Jute Corporation Of India. This ensures the seamless transfer of the one-time ₹5,000/- maintenance cost through the Direct Benefit Transfer system.
<br>

##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 5:****** ***Undertaking Training And Receiving Inputs***
The registered applicant must attend the training sessions organized by the Central Research Institute For Jute And Allied Fibres on the use of seed drills, weeders, and microbial consortiums. Following the training, the applicant receives the 50% subsidized seeds, free machinery, and the microbial retting supplies through the local agencies.
**Step 6:****** ***Completion And Verification Of Retting Pond***
The applicant must cooperate with the field officials who will visit the completed retting pond to capture photographic evidence and Global Positioning System coordinates. Once this confirmation report is successfully submitted by the officials, the ₹5,000/- financial assistance is credited directly to the applicant's bank account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Since there is a financial grant provided for the upkeep of the newly constructed water pool, how exactly is the money sent to me, and what personal financial details must I submit to receive it?

The ₹ 5,000/- one-time maintenance cost is transferred directly to your bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer, meaning you must provide your valid bank details to the field officials.
<br>

### I usually save seeds from my previous harvest to plant the following year, so why is it so strictly recommended that I purchase the officially approved seed bags distributed by the local agricultural agencies instead?

Using the 100% certified high-yielding seeds ensures better plant growth, higher crop quality, and an improved overall yield of 10 to 15% compared to using traditional methods like broadcasting older seeds.
<br>

### If I am interested in having a dedicated water body constructed on my property to process the harvested stems, what sort of formal permission must I provide to the local authorities before the digging work can commence?

You must provide formal consent for your land to be used by the State Government Department for the construction of the retting pond through the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act or similar state initiatives.
<br>

### Extracting the fibre from the plant stems traditionally takes nearly three weeks and uses a lot of water, so what specific biological product will be given to me to speed up this exact process?

You will receive a free microbial consortium such as Central Research Institute for Jute and Allied Fibres SONA or National Institute of Natural Fibre Engineering and Technology SATHI, which can reduce processing time to 12 to 14 days.
<br>

### If the plants in my field suddenly develop a strange disease or an insect infestation starts destroying the leaves before harvest, who can I contact or how will I receive immediate guidance to save my crop?

Field staff can collect infected plant parts or photos to share with the Central Research Institute for Jute and Allied Fibres, whose scientists will then provide you with specific crop protection information and advice.
<br>

### I have been growing agricultural crops for many years on a very limited plot of land, and I would like to know if cultivators with a small scale of operations are allowed to apply for these agricultural benefits?

Yes, the initiative is specifically designed to support small and marginal jute growers with adequate pre-harvesting and post-harvesting operations to help them produce better quality fibre and increase their overall income.
<br>

### Removing unwanted plants from my fields by hand takes a lot of time and money, so what kind of modern tools will I be allowed to use to make this specific farming task easier and more affordable?

You will receive free access and field demonstrations for mechanical interventions, including multi-row seed drills, nail weeders, and single-wheel weeders, which can dramatically reduce manual weeding costs by approximately 35%.
<br>

### Throughout the agricultural season, from the moment I prepare my soil to the final extraction of the fibres, how will I receive ongoing technical advice and reminders directly to my personal mobile device?

You will receive Short Message Service advisories and voice messages detailing land preparation, sowing, de-weeding, plant protection, and the extraction process to keep you fully educated and regularly updated.
<br>

### In order to officially start participating and receiving the agricultural inputs mentioned, do I need to enroll my name somewhere, and who is responsible for keeping the official records of my enrollment at the local village level?

You must be registered as a beneficiary farmer by the Jute Corporation of India in association with different farmers’ societies, co-operative bodies, Primary Agricultural Credit Societies, or Krishi Vigyan Kendras.
<br>

### After the excavation of the water pool on my property is finally finished, what specific proof must be collected by the inspecting officials before the maintenance funds can be officially released into my account?

Field officials must successfully submit a confirmation report along with photographic evidence and Global Positioning System coordinates of the completed pond before the financial assistance is approved and transferred.
<br>

### Since I have only ever used traditional farming techniques my entire life, will there be any educational sessions organized to teach me how to properly use the new planting machines and the special biological powders?

Yes, scientists and master trainers will conduct dedicated awareness and training programs at fixed locations to teach you the correct usage protocols for seed drills, weeders, and microbial retting techniques.
<br>

### Because I often struggle to afford high-quality planting materials at the beginning of the season, what exact type of planting materials will be given to me, and will I have to pay the full market price for them?

You will be provided with 100% high-yielding certified seeds recommended by the Central Research Institute for Jute and Allied Fibres, and these are successfully distributed at a highly beneficial 50% subsidized rate.
<br>

### I cultivate my crops in the northeastern part of the country, and I am wondering if the benefits and mechanical tools provided by this initiative are actively distributed to agricultural workers residing in my specific geographic region?

Yes, the initiative covers several agricultural states, ensuring that farmers in regions including West Bengal, Assam, Bihar, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Meghalaya, and Tripura are fully eligible to participate and receive assistance.
<br>

### When soaking the harvested stalks in the water pool, I typically weigh them down using dirt and heavy plant logs from my farm; will I be allowed to continue this traditional weighing practice while using the new methods?

No, you must actively participate in awareness programs that specifically demonstrate how to perform the soaking and extraction process correctly without the traditional use of soil or banana trees on the jak.
<br>
