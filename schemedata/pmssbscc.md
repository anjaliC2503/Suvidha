## Brief Description

The scheme is to provide financial assistance to students belonging to the Scheduled caste community pursuing Post-Matriculation recognized courses in recognized institutions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Pre Matric Scholarship for Students Belonging to the Scheduled Caste Community" was launched by the Mizoram Scholarship Board, Government of Mizoram. The scheme aims to support parents from the Scheduled Caste community in the education of their children studying in classes 9th and 10th so that the incidence of drop-out, especially in the transition from the elementary to the secondary stage, is minimized. The scheme also aims to improve participation these children in the pre-matric stage of education so that they perform better and have a better chance of progressing to the post-matric stage of education.
<br>

## Benefits

Financial assistance is provided to the students.
_*The scheme is funded by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and the Government of Mizoram in 90:10 ratio._
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be permanently settled in Mizoram.
1. The applicant must belong to Scheduled Caste.
1. The income of the parents/guardians of the applicant (from all sources) should not exceed ₹2,50,000/- per annum.
1. The applicant must be studying either in 9th or 10th standard.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Normally application is open during July- September through offline where application form is available in Mizoram Scholarship Board during Office working hours.  


- Application Form duly filled-in along with documents mentioned above must be submitted to the following address -  

***Mizoram Scholarship Board, Kaisara Rd, Aizawl, Mizoram 796001***

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the specific class requirements for students who want to apply for financial assistance under this educational support program?

Only students currently enrolled in 9th or 10th standard are eligible to apply. The program specifically targets these two grades to support the transition from elementary to secondary education.
<br>

### Does my family's total annual income from all sources affect my eligibility for this scholarship program?

Yes, your parents or guardians' combined annual income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,50,000/-. This income limit applies to the total earnings of your family unit.
<br>

### As someone belonging to the Scheduled Caste community, what residential requirements must I fulfill to be considered for this scholarship?

You must be permanently settled in Mizoram to qualify for this program. Temporary residence or recent migration to the state would not meet the eligibility criteria.
<br>

### What is the primary objective behind launching this particular scholarship program for students from marginalized communities?

The program aims to minimize dropout rates during the critical transition from elementary to secondary education. It also seeks to improve academic participation and performance to enhance progression chances to post-matric education.
<br>

### Which specific community classification must students belong to in order to qualify for this educational financial assistance?

Applicants must belong to the Scheduled Caste community as recognized by the government. This community classification is a mandatory eligibility requirement for the scholarship program.
<br>

### During which months of the year can students typically submit their applications for this scholarship program?

Applications are normally accepted during July through September each year. The application process follows an offline format with forms available during office working hours.
<br>

### What is the maximum annual income threshold that parents or guardians can have while their children remain eligible for this scholarship?

The combined annual income of parents or guardians from all sources should not exceed ₹2,50,000/-. Income above this limit would disqualify the student from receiving financial assistance.
<br>

### Where exactly should completed application forms along with supporting documents be submitted for processing and consideration?

All application forms with required documents must be submitted to Mizoram Scholarship Board, located at Kaisara Road, Aizawl, Mizoram 796001. Submission should be done in person during office hours.
<br>

### What type of financial support can eligible students expect to receive through this government-sponsored educational program?

The program provides direct financial assistance to eligible students. The exact amount and disbursement details would be specified in the official application guidelines and forms.
<br>

### Which government departments are responsible for funding this scholarship program and what is their respective contribution ratio?

The scheme is jointly funded by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and the Government of Mizoram. The funding is shared in a 90:10 ratio between these two government entities.
<br>

### How does this scholarship program specifically target the critical educational transition period for students from disadvantaged backgrounds?

The program focuses on 9th and 10th standard students to address the vulnerable transition phase from elementary to secondary education. This targeted approach helps reduce dropout rates during this crucial academic period.
<br>
