## Brief Description

The scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy" by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote organic agriculture by providing financial assistance for certification, transportation, and organic inputs to farmers across the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote sustainable agricultural practices by encouraging farmers to adopt organic farming methods. The scheme provides financial support for the registration and certification processes conducted by the Gujarat Organic Products Certification Agency (GOPCA) and Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA)-approved agencies. It also offers input cost assistance per hectare, enabling farmers to reduce chemical usage and enhance soil health.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Organic Farming Registration and Certification Assistance (GOPCA):** The scheme provides 75% at-source assistance in cost to the farmer for registration and certification when undertaken through the Gujarat Organic Products Certification Agency (GOPCA).- **Organic Farming Registration, Certification, and Transportation Assistance (APEDA-approved agencies):** When certification is done through APEDA-approved organic certification agencies, the scheme offers 50% assistance in registration, certification, and transportation fees. The maximum benefit for certification support is limited to ₹2,000/- per hectare.- **Input Assistance for Organic Farming:** Farmers are also eligible for input assistance amounting to ₹5,000/- per hectare to promote organic farming practices.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a farmer. 
  - The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be engaged in organic farming activities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy"?

The main purpose of the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy" is to encourage farmers in Gujarat to adopt organic farming practices by providing them with financial assistance for organic certification, registration, transportation, and organic input costs in order to promote sustainable agriculture and reduce the dependency on chemical-based farming.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy"?

Any individual engaged in farming and residing in Gujarat, who wishes to pursue or is already practicing organic farming, is eligible to apply under the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy", provided they fulfill basic requirements such as possessing land records and registering on the iKhedut portal.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What type of financial assistance is provided under the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy"?

Under the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy", farmers receive up to 75% assistance for certification through the Gujarat Organic Products Certification Agency (GOPCA), 50% assistance for registration, certification, and transportation through APEDA-approved agencies, and input assistance of ₹5,000/- per hectare to support organic cultivation.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any upper limit on the assistance provided under the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy"?

Yes, under the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy", the maximum assistance available for certification is capped at ₹2,000/- per hectare, while the input assistance is limited to ₹5,000 per hectare, ensuring that the support is fairly distributed among eligible farmers.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme applicable to all districts of Gujarat?

Yes, the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy" is applicable across the entire state of Gujarat, thereby allowing all eligible farmers from different regions of the state to benefit from the support offered under this initiative.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any cost associated with applying under the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy"?

There is no cost associated with applying for the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy", as the application process is completely free and can be carried out online through the government’s official portal.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can individuals from outside Gujarat apply for this scheme?

No, individuals who are not residents of Gujarat are not eligible to apply for the scheme, as the scheme is exclusively for residents of the state.
<br>

### Are there any income restrictions for applying to this scheme?

There are no income restrictions or income limits imposed under the scheme. Individuals from all economic backgrounds may apply.
<br>

### What are the steps to apply for the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy"?

To apply for the “Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy,” the applicant must visit the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, fill out the online application form, upload the required documents, and submit the form.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy"?

To apply for the scheme "Promotion of Organic Farming under State Organic Policy", a farmer must submit key documents such as the 7/12 Land Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Bank Pass Book or a cancelled cheque, and, in applicable cases, certification documents from GOPCA or APEDA-approved agencies.
<br>
