## Brief Description

The “Generator Subsidy” scheme provides a 25% subsidy on the cost of generator sets up to 125 KVA capacity for manufacturers in Tamil Nadu.

## Detailed Description

The “Generator Subsidy” scheme provides a 25% subsidy or maximum ₹1,50,000/- on the cost of generator sets up to 125 KVA capacity for manufacturers in Tamil Nadu. The subsidy must be claimed within six months from the date of purchase or installation of the generator.
<br>

## Benefits

**Quantum of Assistance:** 25% subsidy or maximum ₹1,50,000/- on the cost of generator sets (up to 125 KVA capacity).
<br>

## Eligibility

- Beneficiaries must be new and existing Micro, Small and Medium Manufacturing Enterprises.
  - The generator set must have a capacity of up to 125 KVA.
  - Subsidy claims must be made within six months from the date of purchase or installation.

1. Proof of purchase or installation of the generator set.
   1. Invoice and receipt of the generator.
   1. Proof of enterprise registration (MSME registration certificate).
   1. Bank account details for subsidy transfer.
   1. Installation certificate from a certified technician.
   1. Any other documents (if required).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Generator Subsidy" scheme?

The Generator Subsidy scheme provides a 25% subsidy or maximum [?]1,50,000/- on the cost of generator sets up to 125 KVA capacity for Micro, Small, and Medium Manufacturing Enterprises (MSMEs) in Tamil Nadu.

### Who is eligible for the Generator Subsidy?

New and existing Micro, Small, and Medium Manufacturing Enterprises (MSMEs) registered in Tamil Nadu are eligible.

### What is the maximum capacity of the generator covered under the scheme?

The scheme provides a subsidy for generator sets with a capacity of up to 125 KVA.

### How much subsidy is provided under the scheme?

A 25% subsidy is provided on the cost of the generator set.

### How long is the subsidy claim period?

The subsidy claim must be made within 6 months from the date of purchase or installation of the generator set.

### What documents are required to apply for the subsidy?

Applicants must provide proof of purchase, an installation certificate, an MSME registration certificate, and bank account details for the transfer of the subsidy.

### Is the subsidy available for both new and existing MSMEs?

Yes, both new and existing MSME manufacturing enterprises in Tamil Nadu are eligible.

### Is there a deadline to apply for the subsidy after purchasing or installing the generator?

Yes, the subsidy must be claimed within 6 months of purchasing or installing the generator set.

### Can the subsidy be availed for multiple generators?

The subsidy is available for generator sets up to 125 KVA, but manufacturers should verify if multiple generators are covered based on their enterprise's requirements.

### What happens if an MSME misses the six-month deadline for applying?

If the deadline is missed, the MSME will no longer be eligible for the subsidy.

### Where can manufacturers submit their applications for the Generator Subsidy?

Applications along with the required documents must be submitted to the relevant state authority in Tamil Nadu Regional Joint Director of Industries and Commerce Industrial Estate, Chennai-600 032.
