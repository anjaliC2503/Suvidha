## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide standardized world-class Bed & Breakfast accommodation facilities, supplement tourist accommodation, and promote clean, affordable, and homely stays for domestic and foreign tourists in States and Union Territories where no State/UT approval scheme exists.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Incredible India Bed &amp; Breakfast Establishments&quot; scheme implemented by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, aims to provide standardized world-class Bed &amp; Breakfast accommodation by classifying eligible establishments in States and Union Territories where no State or Union Territory approval or accreditation scheme exists. The scheme promotes clean, affordable, and homely accommodation for domestic and foreign tourists while encouraging them to experience Indian traditions and hospitality. Eligible establishments are classified under Silver and Gold categories after inspection by the Regional Classification Committee. Approved establishments are publicized by the Ministry of Tourism and included in an official directory, and hospitality training may also be organized for interested operators.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Standardized Classification:** Eligible establishments receive classification as Silver or Gold category.  - **Official Recognition:** Approved establishments are recognized by the Ministry of Tourism.  - **Publicity:** Approved establishments are publicized by the Ministry of Tourism. These are also included in the official directory for tourists.  - **Hospitality Training:** Interested operators may receive short-term hospitality training.  - **Tourist Promotion:** The scheme promotes domestic and international tourism through quality accommodation.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be the owner or promoter of the establishment.
  - The owner or promoter must not reside in the establishment.
  - The applicant must appoint an agent or operator who resides at the establishment premises.
  - The agent or operator must provide necessary services to guests.
  - The establishment must have a minimum of 1 lettable room.
  - The establishment must have a maximum of 6 lettable rooms.
  - The establishment must have a maximum capacity of 12 beds.
  - The establishment must operate on a bed and breakfast basis.
  - The breakfast type offered must be specified.
  - The applicable charges must be displayed.
  - Guests must be informed of the charges in advance.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Visit the [official portal](https://wvvw.hotelclould.nic.in/).
**Step 2:** Select the appropriate category (Silver or Gold) for the application.
**Step 3:** Fill in the online application form with the required details.
**Step 4:** Upload the prescribed documents and complete the online checklist, wherever applicable.
**Step 5:** Pay the prescribed application fee through digital mode only.
**Step 6:** Submit the online application for approval.
**Step 7:** If applying for an extension of the approval, submit the extension application online through the same portal and pay the applicable fee through digital mode.
**Step 8:** After submission, the application will be processed by the Ministry of Tourism in accordance with the scheme guidelines.
> **Contact Us:** [**Click here**](https://nidhi.tourism.gov.in/home/page/contact-us)**.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of this scheme?

The scheme aims to classify eligible Bed & Breakfast establishments and provide standardized accommodation for domestic and foreign tourists.
<br>

### Who implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India.
<br>

### Who can apply under this scheme?

Owners or promoters of eligible Bed & Breakfast establishments fulfilling the prescribed conditions can apply.
<br>

### How many rooms are permitted under the scheme?

An establishment must have at least 1 lettable room and not more than 6 lettable rooms with a maximum of 12 beds.
<br>

### What categories are available under the scheme?

The establishment may be classified under Silver or Gold category.
<br>

### Is the owner required to stay at the establishment?

No. The owner does not reside at the establishment, but an appointed agent or operator must reside there to provide services.
<br>

### What type of accommodation should be provided?

The establishment must operate on a bed and breakfast basis.
<br>

### How long is the classification valid?

The classification remains valid for 2 years.
<br>

### How is the establishment evaluated?

The Regional Classification Committee evaluates the facilities and services through inspection.
<br>

### Is there any preference during evaluation?

Yes. Preference is given to establishments offering authentic Indian décor, cuisine, and cultural experiences.
<br>

### What happens if deficiencies are found during inspection?

The establishment must rectify the deficiencies within the stipulated period, which cannot exceed 3 months.
<br>

### What happens if deficiencies are not rectified?

The application is rejected if the deficiencies are not rectified within the prescribed time.
<br>

### How long does it take to process an application?

Applications complete in all respects are finalized within 30 days after online submission and confirmation of fee payment.
<br>

### Can an applicant appeal against the classification decision?

Yes. An appeal may be submitted to the Chairman, Hotel and Restaurant Approval and Classification Committee, Ministry of Tourism, within 30 days of receiving the communication.
<br>

### What happens if facilities are changed after approval?

The establishment must inform the Regional Director, Indiatourism Office, within 30 days of any change in facilities, failing which the classification may be withdrawn or terminated.
<br>
