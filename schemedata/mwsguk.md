## Brief Description

The scheme aims to enhance the interest of Class 5 students in Mathematics and English by organizing competitions at the school, block, district, and state levels, and encouraging them through rewards.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Maths Wizard &amp; Spelling Genius” is implemented under the Samagra Shiksha Programme by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to enhance the interest of Class 5 students in Mathematics and English by organizing competitions at the school, block, district, and state levels, and by encouraging students through rewards.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, to enhance the interest of Class 5 students in Mathematics and English, the Government of India organized the “Maths Wizard” and “Spelling Genius” competitions at the school, block, district, and state levels under AWP-B for the year 2023-24, wherein participating students were encouraged through rewards.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a Class 5 student of a government school in Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

The selection of beneficiaries for first, second, and third positions is done through school, block, district, and state-level competitions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to participate in this scheme?

Students of Class 5 studying in government schools of Uttarakhand are eligible.
<br>

### What is the main objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to enhance students’ interest in Mathematics and English through organized competitions and reward-based encouragement.
<br>

### Which subjects are covered under this scheme?

Mathematics and English.
<br>

### At what levels are the competitions organized?

Competitions are organized at the school, block, district, and state levels.
<br>

### How are students selected as winners?

First, second, and third positions are selected at each level—school, block, district, and state—based on their performance in the competitions.
<br>

### Is there any reward for participating students?

Yes, students participating in competitions are encouraged and rewarded for their performance.
<br>

### Are students from private schools eligible?

No, only Class 5 students from government schools of Uttarakhand are eligible.
