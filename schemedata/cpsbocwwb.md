## Brief Description

"Cash Prizes Scheme" was launched by the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Students securing 60% or more in the 10th and 12th exams receive cash prizes of up to ₹25,000/- per year. Upto two children of a registered construction worker are eligible.

## Detailed Description

"Cash Prizes Scheme" was launched by the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar. Students securing 60% or more in the 10th and 12th exams receive cash prizes of up to ₹25,000/- per year. Upto two children of a registered construction worker are eligible.

## Benefits

- On securing 80% or more in the 10th or 12th examination: ₹25,000/- per year.
- On securing 70% to 79.99% in the 10th or 12th examination: ₹15,000/- per year.
- On securing 60% to 69.99% in the 10th or 12th examination: ₹10,000/- per year.

## Eligibility

**For the Worker**

- The worker should be engaged in building or construction work.
- The worker should be registered with the "Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board".
- The worker's membership with the Welfare Board must be active at the time of application.
- The worker should have at least one year of membership with the Board.
- A maximum of two children of the registered construction worker will be eligible for the benefit.

**For the Child**

- The child should have passed the 10th or 12th examination conducted by a board in Bihar.
- The child should have scored 60% or above in the examination.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What support does the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board provide for students who score well in their board exams?

The Board offers cash prizes ranging from ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 per year for children of registered construction workers who secure 60% or above in their 10th or 12th board examinations in Bihar.

### How much cash prize is awarded to students who score above 80% in their board exams?

Students securing 80% or more in their 10th or 12th examinations are eligible for a cash prize of ₹25,000 per year.

### Can all children of a registered construction worker apply for this scheme?

No, only up to two children of a registered construction worker are eligible for this benefit.

### What is the minimum percentage required to be eligible for the cash prize scheme?

Students must secure a minimum of 60% marks in their 10th or 12th examination to be eligible for the cash prize scheme.

### How long must a worker be registered with the Welfare Board to avail this benefit?

The worker must have at least one year of membership with the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

### What is the cash prize amount for students scoring between 70% to 79.99%?

Students scoring between 70% to 79.99% in their 10th or 12th examination are eligible for a cash prize of ₹15,000 per year.

### How can applicants track the status of their application?

Applicants can track their application status online on the official website by entering their registration number.

### What is the cash prize amount for students scoring between 60% to 69.99%?

Students scoring between 60% to 69.99% in their 10th or 12th examination receive a cash prize of ₹10,000 per year.

### How do I apply for the scheme after completing the registration process?

Visit the official website, click on "Scheme Application," select "Apply for Scheme," enter your registration number, and complete the application form with required documents.

### Can a construction worker apply without being registered with the Welfare Board?

No, the worker must be registered with the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board to apply for the scheme.

### What type of work should the applicant be engaged in to be eligible?

The worker should be specifically engaged in building or construction work.

### Are applicants required to pay any fee during the registration process?

Yes, applicants must pay an application fee online during the registration process.

### Is it necessary to fill all fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the registration form?

Yes, all fields marked with an asterisk (*) are mandatory and must be filled in.

### Can students who scored below 60% apply for this scheme?

No, students must score a minimum of 60% or above to be eligible for the cash prize scheme.
