## Brief Description

The "Girls Literacy Promotion Scheme Class 11th" by the Madhya Pradesh Government encourages girls from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities to study. Girls enrolling in Class 11 for the first time receive ₹3,000/-

## Detailed Description

The "Girls Literacy Promotion Scheme Class 11th" by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to encourage girls from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities to pursue education. Under this scheme, an incentive amount of ₹3,000 is provided to girls enrolling in Class 11 for the first time. The scholarship distribution is managed through the Directorate of Public Distribution, Government of Madhya Pradesh.

## Benefits

An incentive of ₹3,000/-.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be from Denotified, Nomadic, or Semi-Nomadic Community.
1. The applicant should be a girl.
1. The applicant should be enrolled in the 11th Standard.
1. The applicant should be studying in a recognized institution.
1. The applicant should have studied continuously without any academic breaks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the Girls Literacy Promotion Scheme?

The applicant should be a girl from De-notified, Nomadic, or Semi-Nomadic tribes and enrolled in Class 11 for the first time.

### What is the incentive amount provided under the scheme?

An incentive amount of [?]3,000 is provided to eligible girls.

### Is the scheme available for students in private institutions?

The scheme is applicable only for students studying in recognized institutions as per the guidelines.

### Do students need to reapply for the incentive every year?

No, the incentive is provided as a one-time benefit when enrolling in Class 11.

### What happens if a student discontinues their studies?

The applicant must be continuously studying; discontinuing studies may disqualify them from receiving the incentive.

### How is the incentive distributed?

The incentive is distributed through the Directorate of Public Instruction under the Integrated Scholarship Scheme.

### Is this scheme applicable to boys?

No, the scheme is specifically designed to promote girls' education.

### Can a student receive this incentive if they repeat Class 11?

No, the incentive is only for students enrolling in Class 11 for the first time.

### Are there any specific income criteria for eligibility?

The scheme does not mention any income criteria, but the student must belong to De-notified, Nomadic, or Semi-Nomadic tribes.

### Can students apply for other scholarships along with this scheme?

Yes, students may be eligible for other scholarships, depending on their qualifications and criteria.
