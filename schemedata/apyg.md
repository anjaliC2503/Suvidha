## Brief Description

The scheme "Ambubhai Purani Award" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Gujarat, aims to recognize outstanding contributions in the field of gymnasiums (Akhadas) through the conferment of the Gymnasium Jyotirdhar Ambubhai Purani Award and financial assistance of ₹51,000/-.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Ambubhai Purani Award&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote the development of gymnasiums and traditional Indian physical culture by honoring individuals who have shown exceptional contribution in this field. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹51,000/- and state-level recognition to outstanding individuals, fostering motivation and continuity in preserving indigenous physical training traditions. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹51,000/-.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a male individual.
   1. The applicant should have performed excellent work in the field of Gymnasiums (Akhadas).

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official homepage of the [Sports Authority of Gujarat](https://sportsauthority.gujarat.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** On the homepage,  navigate to the Player Registration option after clicking on the &quot;Register&quot; button in the top-right corner.
**Step 3:** Fill out the Player Registration Form with the required personal details and click on &quot;Register&quot;.
**Step 4:** After completion, the login ID and password information will be shared via email.
<br>

> **Login and Application Process:**

**Step 1:**  Go to the [Login page](https://sportsauthority.gujarat.gov.in/login) and select the user type as &quot;Player&quot;.
**Step 2:** Log in using the credentials received via email.
**Step 3:** After logging in, select the concerned scheme.
**Step 4:** Fill in the required details and upload documents as required. Click on &quot;Submit&quot; to apply.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme?

The main purpose of the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme is to recognize and honor individuals who have demonstrated outstanding contribution in the field of gymnasiums, particularly in traditional Indian Akhadas, by awarding them a certificate of recognition along with financial assistance.
<br>

<br>

### Who implements the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme in Gujarat?

The "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat under the supervision of the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Male individuals who have done excellent work in Gymnasiums (Akhadas) in Gujarat and are residents of the state are eligible to apply for the "Ambubhai Purani Award."
<br>

<br>

### What financial benefit is provided under the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme?

Under the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme, a selected individual is given a one-time financial assistance of ₹51,000 as recognition for their excellence in the field of gymnasiums.
<br>

<br>

### Is the "Ambubhai Purani Award" open to female applicants?

As per the current eligibility criteria of the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme, only male individuals are eligible to apply, and female applicants are not considered under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit specified to be eligible for the "Ambubhai Purani Award"?

There is no income limit, either individual or family-based, specified under the eligibility criteria of the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme, allowing participation from all economic backgrounds.
<br>

<br>

### Does the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme require any specific educational qualifications?

No specific educational qualifications are required to be eligible for the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme, making it accessible to individuals from all levels of education.
<br>

<br>

### Can an NGO or organization apply for the "Ambubhai Purani Award"?

Yes, the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme allows applications not only from individual contributors but also from NGOs that are actively involved in the development or support of gymnasiums in Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any application fee for applying under the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme?

There is no application fee required to apply under the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme, and the application form can be accessed and submitted free of cost through the designated portal.
<br>

[Link](https://sportsauthority.gujarat.gov.in/login)

### Where can eligible individuals or organizations apply for the "Ambubhai Purani Award"?

Applications for the "Ambubhai Purani Award" must be submitted online through the official portal of the Sports Authority of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to be submitted while applying for the "Ambubhai Purani Award"?

The required documents for applying under the "Ambubhai Purani Award" scheme include a valid Domicile Certificate of Gujarat and the Applicant's Photo.
