## Brief Description

The Shri Hari Om Ashram Prerit Doctor Vikram Sarabhai Research Awards are biennial awards established with funds from Pujya Shri Mota of Hari Om Ashram, Nadiad, to recognize outstanding contributions by Indian scientists under the age of 45 in the fields of Space Sciences etc.

## Detailed Description

The Shri Hari Om Ashram Prerit Dr. Vikram Sarabhai Research Awards are biennial awards established with funds from Pujya Shri Mota of Hari Om Ashram, Nadiad, to recognize outstanding contributions by Indian scientists under the age of 45 in the fields of Space Sciences, Space Applications, Electronics, Informatics, Telematics, Automation, and Systems Analysis or Management. The awards consist of a cash prize of ₹50,000, a medal, and recognition for original research work done in India.
> ***Fields of Disciplines:***


- Space Sciences (including Astronomy, Astrophysics, Planetary and Atmospheric Sciences).
- Space Applications (in the areas of Meteorology, Hydrology, Remote Sensing, and related Ground Truths).
- Electronics, Informatics, Telematics and Automation.
- Systems Analysis or Management, including non-linear, non-equilibrium systems in natural and social sciences and technology.

<br>

## Benefits

- **Cash Prize:** An amount of ₹50,000/- and a medal.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be an Indian citizen.
  - The applicant should be a scientist. 
  - The applicant&#39;s age is below 45 years (born on or after January 2, 1972, for the 2017 awards).
  - The applicant&#39;s contributions should be in the areas mentioned, such as Space Sciences, Space Applications, Electronics, Informatics, Telematics, Automation, etc.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant visits the [PRL website](https://www.prl.res.in/prl-eng/) and navigate to the "Opportunities" section.
**Step 2:** Click on "Awards Administered By PRL" and select the scheme "Hari Om Ashram Prerit Doctor Vikram Sarabhai Research Award" 
**Step 3:** The candidate downloads the prescribed format of the application form. 
**Step 4:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 5:** Candidate can email or post their applications in the prescribed format to: hoa@prl.res.in OR The Director, Physical Research Laboratory, Navrangpura, Ahmedabad-380009.
<br>

## Documents Required

1. Nomination Form (downloadable from the PRL website).
1. Significant contributions to the area of specialization by the nominee (two pages) during his/her research career.
1. Impact of the contributions in the field concerned, basic and/or applied
1. Specific contributions for which the nomination is being made
1. Complete list of Publications (with the names of authors in the order in which they appear in the publications).
1. List of TEN BEST publications, of which five should preferably be from the last five years (A separate listing of this is essential)
1. Reprints of five important publications in the last five years (e-file only).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the prize for this award?

The award includes a cash prize of ₹50,000 and a medal.

### Who is eligible for the award?

Scientists under the age of 45 (born on or after January 2, 1972) are eligible.

### In which fields are contributions considered?

Space Sciences, Space Applications, Electronics, Telematics, Automation, and Systems Analysis or Management.

### How should nominations be submitted?

Nominations must be submitted via email to hoa@prl.res.in.

### What documents are required for the nomination?

The nomination form, two-page summary of contributions, impact statement, complete list of publications, list of ten best publications, and five reprints (in e-file format) are required.

### Who can submit nominations?

Nominations can be submitted by Heads of academic and research institutions, Vice-Chancellors, Fellows of Indian Academies of Science and Engineering, and past recipients.

### Can previous nominees be re-nominated?

Yes, candidates from 2013 and 2015 can be re-nominated by submitting a fresh nomination following the current guidelines.

### What is the selection process?

The selection is based on the originality and impact of the work done by the nominee, with priority given to contributions made in India.

### Is there an application fee for the nomination?

No, there is no application fee for submitting a nomination.

### Where can I download the nomination proforma?

The nomination proforma can be downloaded from the PRL website under the "National Awards" section.
