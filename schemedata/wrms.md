## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support the rehabilitation of widows by encouraging marriage through a monetary incentive. It provides a grant to bona fide Himachali men (above 21 years) and women (above 18 years) at the time of marriage.

## Detailed Description

The Widow Remarriage Scheme under the Government of Himachal Pradesh aims to support the rehabilitation of widows by encouraging men to marry widows through a monetary incentive. Under this scheme, a grant is provided to bona fide Himachali men (above 21 years) and women (above 18 years) at the time of marriage.
<br>

## Benefits

If any widow re-marries, a cheque of ₹ 20,000/- is given to the women and a Fix Deposit worth ₹ 30,000/- for the 5 years term is given to the couple.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The widow should have remarried.
1. The applicant should be a resident of Himachal Pradesh.
1. Groom should not be less than 21 years of age
1. Women should not be less than 18 years of age.

## Application Process

### Offline

Step 1: Visit the CDPO (Child Development Project Officer) office and collect the [application form.](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s348aedb8880cab8c45637abc7493ecddd/uploads/2018/11/2018111253.pdf)
Step 2: Fill out the form with required details and attach all necessary documents.
Step 3: Submit the completed application form at the CDPO office for verification and further processing.

## Documents Required

1. Proof of Date / year of the first marriage
1. Proof of Date / year on which she became widow
1. Proof of Date of Re-Marriage
1. Proof of Name & Address of person to whom widow is marrying
1. Himachal Bonafide Certificate of Women and men
1. Proof of Annual Family Income of applicant
1. BPL Certificate

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who Is Eligible For The Widow Re-Marriage Scheme?

The scheme is available for widows who have remarried within one year of the death of their first husband and are residents of Himachal Pradesh.

### What is the amount provided under the scheme?

If any widow re-marries, a cheque of ₹20,000/- is given to the women and a Fix Deposit worth ₹30,000/- for the 5 years term is given to the couple.
<br>

### Can A Widow Apply For The Scheme If She Remarried After One Year Of Her First Husband's Death?

No, the scheme is only applicable for those who have remarried within one year of their first husband's death.

### Is There Any Income Limit To Apply For The Scheme?

No, there is no income limit to apply for the scheme.

### Can A Widow Who Has Remarried Outside Himachal Pradesh Apply For The Scheme?

No, the scheme is only applicable for residents of Himachal Pradesh.

### Can A Widow Who Has Remarried Apply For The Scheme More Than Once?

No, the scheme is only applicable for a one-time grant.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Bona fide Himachali men (above 21 years) and women (above 18 years) at the time of remarriage are eligible.
<br>

### Is there any application fee?

No, there is no fee required to apply for this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Can applicants from outside Himachal Pradesh apply?

No, only bona fide residents of Himachal Pradesh are eligible.
<br>

### Where can I get help if I face issues while applying?

You can contact the concerned department or visit the nearest CDPO (Child Development Project Officer) office for assistance.
<br>
