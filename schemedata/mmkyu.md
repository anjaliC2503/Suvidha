## Brief Description

The scheme aims to improve the health and well-being of both mothers and their newborn girl children by providing essential item kits after the institutional delivery of pregnant women registered at an Anganwadi Centre.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Mukhyamantri Mahalakshmi Kit Yojana&quot; has been launched by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to improve the health and well-being of both mothers and their newborn girl children by providing essential item kits after the institutional delivery of pregnant women registered at an Anganwadi Centre.
<br>

## Benefits

In the state, after the institutional delivery of a pregnant woman, the following benefits will be provided:

- The woman will receive one Mahalakshmi kit for the birth of each of her first two girl children.
- In the case of twins, the woman will be given one Mahalakshmi kit, and each child will receive a separate Mahalakshmi kit.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiary must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
   1. The beneficiary must be a pregnant woman registered with an Anganwadi Centre and eligible to receive a Mahalakshmi Kit after delivery.
   1. The beneficiary must not be an income tax payer.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

After the institutional delivery of a pregnant woman registered at an Anganwadi Centre, a Mahalakshmi Kit is provided as a benefit.
The following items are included in the kit for mothers and their newborn girl children:

- **For Mothers:** Dry fruits, socks, scarf, towel, shawl, blanket, bedsheet, sanitary pads, mustard oil, soap, and nail cutter.- **For Newborn Girl Child:** Cotton/warm cap, socks, diaper, towel, baby soap, rubber sheet, warm blanket, and vaccination card.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Pregnant women registered with an Anganwadi Centre, who are not income tax payers, are eligible to receive the Mahalakshmi Kit after delivery.
<br>

### Where should a pregnant woman be registered to avail benefits?

She must be registered at an Anganwadi Centre in Uttarakhand.
<br>

### When is the Mahalakshmi Kit provided?

The kit is provided after the institutional delivery of the pregnant woman.
<br>

### How many kits does a woman receive?

A woman receives one kit for each of her first two girl children.
<br>

### What happens in the case of twins?

For twins, the woman receives one Mahalakshmi Kit, and each child is given a separate Mahalakshmi Kit.
<br>

### Can women who are income tax payers receive the kit?

No, only women who are not income tax payers are eligible.
<br>

### Is the scheme available for mothers of boy children?

No, the scheme is specifically for mothers of girl children.
<br>

### Is registration at the Anganwadi Centre mandatory?

Yes, only women registered at an Anganwadi Centre are eligible for the kit.
<br>

### Does the kit support home deliveries?

No, the kit is provided only after institutional delivery at recognized health facilities.
<br>
