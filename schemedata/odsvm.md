## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide a common platform for officers, teachers, and students from universities, colleges, schools, and other departments to actively participate and gain knowledge about the simplified curriculum of Vedic Mathematics and its easy-to-apply methods.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “One-day Seminar on Vedic Mathematics” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Sanskrit Academy, Haridwar, under the Department of Sanskrit Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a common platform for officers, teachers, and students from universities, colleges, schools, and other departments to actively participate and gain knowledge about the simplified curriculum of Vedic Mathematics and its easy-to-apply methods.
<br>

## Benefits

Participants of the seminar will gain knowledge about the simplified course of Vedic Mathematics and learn its easy and practical methods.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. Officers, teachers, and students from universities, colleges, schools, and other departments are eligible to participate in the seminar.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

After finalizing the date, time, and venue of the seminar, the convener will submit the list of invitees, who will then be formally invited through letters issued by the Academy Secretary.
<br>

Documents are required for attending the seminar, as per the guidelines of the Uttarakhand Sanskrit Academy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can participate in the seminar?

Officers, teachers, and students from universities, colleges, schools, and other departments are eligible to participate.
<br>

### What are the benefits of attending the seminar?

Participants gain knowledge of the simplified curriculum of Vedic Mathematics and learn easy methods to solve mathematical problems efficiently.
<br>

### How long is the seminar?

The seminar is conducted for one day.
<br>

### How is the seminar organized?

The seminar is organized by deciding a date, time, and venue. The list of invitees is submitted by the convener, and invitations are sent by the Academy Secretary.
<br>

### Can students from private institutions participate?

Yes, students from recognized universities, colleges, and schools, including private institutions, may participate if invited.
<br>

### How will participants be invited?

Invitations are sent through official letters by the Academy Secretary after the list of participants is finalized.
<br>
