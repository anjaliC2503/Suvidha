## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension of ₹1,000/- to construction workers registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board who have attained the age of 60 years.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Old Age Pension Scheme” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension to registered construction workers who have attained the age of 60 years.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Pension Amount:**  ₹1,000/- per month
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a construction worker registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
   1. The applicant must have completed 60 years of age.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online - via CSC

- The online application can be submitted through Shramik Suvidha Kendras.
  - During the application process, the applicant is required to provide the Shramik Card, Aadhaar Card, Bank Passbook, a Self-Declaration Certificate, and an affidavit stating that no benefit from any other scheme is being availed. 
  - If the documents are found correct during departmental investigation/verification, the pension is sanctioned and disbursed on a half-yearly basis. 
  - After every six months, the worker must submit a life certificate to continue receiving the pension.
