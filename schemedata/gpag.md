## Brief Description

The scheme "Gaurav Puraskar Award" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to honour artistes for their significant achievements in music, dance, drama, and folk art by providing a cash award, trophy, and appreciation letter.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Gaurav Puraskar Award" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to recognize and encourage individuals who have demonstrated excellence in the fields of music, dance, drama, and folk art through a combination of financial and honorary benefits. The scheme confers a cash award of ₹1,000/-, a trophy, and a letter of appreciation to 12 deserving artistes every year. It also facilitates opportunities for awardees to contribute to state-sponsored cultural events and festivals in various expert capacities. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Sangeet Natak Academy.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Cash Prize:** 



  - Each awardee receives a cash prize of ₹1,000/-.


> **Honorary Recognition:**



  - Awardees are presented with a trophy and a letter of appreciation.


> **Post-Award Engagement:**



  - Awardees may be included as members in committees of the Academy.
  - Awardees can offer their services as adjudicators or experts in state cultural programs such as Rajya-level Navratri festivals, Kalamahakumbh, and other events.


<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
   1. The applicant must have made a significant contribution or demonstrated excellence in the fields of music, dance, drama, or folk art.

<br>

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should download the prescribed format of the [application form](https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/ViewForm.aspx) for the &quot;Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts&quot; scheme.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, including career details in the field of art, photographs, or color slides of work (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the Gujarat State Sangeet Natak Academy.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority at the Academy office to whom the application has been submitted.
**Note:** Applications for book publication are invited every year through advertisements in daily newspapers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" scheme?

The primary objective of the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" scheme is to recognize and honor individual artistes in Gujarat who have made significant contributions to the fields of music, dance, drama, and folk art by awarding them with a cash prize, a trophy, and a letter of appreciation as a mark of state-level recognition.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award"?

Any individual residing in Gujarat who has demonstrated excellence or significant achievements in the disciplines of music, dance, drama, or folk art is eligible to apply for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award", irrespective of their income level, gender, profession, education, or social background.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How many artistes are selected under the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" scheme each year?

Under the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" scheme, a total of 12 artistes are selected every year by the implementing agency to receive the award based on their merit and contribution to cultural fields.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What benefits are provided to the recipients of the "Gaurav Puraskar Award"?

Each recipient of the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" receives a cash award of ₹1,000/- along with a trophy and a letter of appreciation, which collectively serve to acknowledge their cultural contributions and promote further engagement in state-sponsored artistic activities.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which government body is responsible for implementing the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" scheme?

The "Gaurav Puraskar Award" scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Sangeet Natak Academy, which functions under the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department of the Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit for applying under the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" scheme?

No, there is no income limit applicable under the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" scheme; both individual and family income limits are not considered during the eligibility evaluation.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can applicants from outside Gujarat apply for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award"?

No, only those applicants who are residents of Gujarat are eligible to apply for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award", as the scheme is meant to recognize the contributions of artistes within the state.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the application process for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award"?

To apply for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award", an individual must collect and submit the application to the Member Secretary of the Gujarat State Sangeet Natak Akademi at Dr. Jivaraj Mehta Bhawan, Gandhinagar, along with the required documents.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any cost associated with obtaining or submitting the application for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award"?

No, there is no cost involved in obtaining or submitting the application for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" scheme, and the application can be submitted free of charge.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award"?

Applicants for the "Gaurav Puraskar Award" must submit a copy of their Aadhaar Card, attested PAN Card, Bank Passbook or cancelled cheque, Identity Card, passport-size photographs, and a formal application letter written on plain paper.
