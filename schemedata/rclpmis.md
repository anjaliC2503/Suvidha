## Brief Description

The scheme is designed to assist Cured Leprosy Persons who face social and economic challenges in arranging marriages for their daughters.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Marriage Incentives &amp; Support&quot; is a component of the “Rehabilitation of Cured Leprosy Persons” scheme, implemented by the Social Security and Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (SSEPD) Department, Government of Odisha. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to the Cured Leprosy Persons who face social and economic challenges in arranging marriages for their daughters.

## Benefits

**Financial Support:** A one-time grant of up to ₹50,000/- for the marriage of daughters of Cured Leprosy Persons.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant is a bonafide resident of Odisha.
- The applicant should be a Cured Leprosy Person.
- The applicant’s daughter should be unmarried.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Download the application form ([Annexure C](https://ssepd.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-04/Guideline%20on%20An%20umbrella%20scheme%20for%20Rehabilitation%20of%20Cured%20Leprosy%20Persons.pdf) or Refer to Page No. 15) and fill out all required sections of the application form accurately.
**Step 2:** Submit the completed application form along with all attached documents to the Block/ ULB Administration office.
**Step 3:** Ensure you receive a submission receipt for future reference.

## Documents Required

- Proof of permanent domicile in Odisha.
- Aadhaar Number of parents (in case the guardian enclose Certificate of guardianship obtained from Competent Authority).
- Birth certificate or proof of age (Daughter and one of the Parents).
- Proof for being Cured Leprosy Person.
- Any other documents if required.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for the “Marriage Incentives & Support” scheme?

The scheme is available to Cured Leprosy Persons who face financial and social challenges in arranging marriages for their daughters.

### What is the maximum amount of financial support available?

The scheme provides a maximum financial support of [?]50,000/- for the marriage of a daughter.

### How do I apply for the scheme?

Complete the prescribed application form (Annexure - C) and submit it along with the required documents to the Block/ Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) Administration office.

### What documents do I need to provide with my application?

Required documents include proof of cured leprosy status, financial status, daughter's identification, marriage proposal details, and the completed application form.

### Can I apply if I have already received marriage support from another scheme?

The scheme is intended for those who have not received similar support from other sources. You may need to declare any other assistance received when applying.

### How will I know if my application is approved?

You will be notified of the approval status through the contact details provided in your application.

### When will the financial support be disbursed?

Disbursement will occur after the application is reviewed and approved, according to the scheme's guidelines.

### What if I cannot provide all the required documents?

Incomplete applications may be rejected. Ensure you provide all necessary documents or seek assistance if you have difficulties obtaining them.

### What should I do if I need assistance filling out the application form?

Seek help from local social services or community organizations that assist with application processes for similar schemes.

### How long does the application review process take?

The review process duration can vary. You should contact the designated authority for an estimated timeline.

### Can I appeal if my application is rejected?

Yes, if your application is rejected, you may be able to appeal or request a review by contacting the Block/ Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) Administration office.
