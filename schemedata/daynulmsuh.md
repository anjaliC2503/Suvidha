## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide safe and accessible night shelter facilities to homeless people living under the open sky in urban areas, ensuring they have protection and support at any time.

## Detailed Description

The “Support for Urban Homeless” is an initiative under the Deen Dayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Urban Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NULM), implemented by the Nagar Nigam Dehradun under the Department of Urban Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide safe and accessible night shelter facilities to homeless people living under the open sky in urban areas, ensuring they have protection and support at any time.
<br>

## Benefits

People living under the open sky are provided with benefits in night shelters.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. Any person living under the open sky within the municipal areas of Uttarakhand is eligible to stay in a night shelter.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

A person can come and stay in the night shelters established under the municipal body at any time.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the aim of the scheme?

The aim is to provide safe and accessible night shelter facilities to homeless people living under the open sky in urban areas, ensuring protection and support at any time.
<br>

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Any person living under the open sky within the municipal areas of Uttarakhand is eligible to stay in a night shelter.
<br>

### What benefits are provided under this scheme?

Homeless individuals are provided with access to night shelters, offering safety, basic amenities, and support during their stay.
<br>

### Can a person access the night shelter at any time?

Yes, any eligible person can visit and stay in the night shelters at any time.
<br>

### Where are the night shelters located?

Night shelters are established by the Nagar Nigam Dehradun across various urban areas in Uttarakhand.
