## Brief Description

The scheme aims to bring about all round development of Popular Tiatr through annual competitions. It provides cash prizes and awards to tiatr groups and individual artists performing in traditional tiatr format across Goa.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Popular Tiatr Competition" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to bring about all round development of Popular Tiatr by organizing annual competitions in different parts of Goa, preserving and promoting the traditional format of tiatr performances. The scheme provides cash prizes ranging from ₹2,000/- to ₹2,00,000/- for various categories including performance, direction, acting, singing, and technical aspects, along with mementos and certificates for winners. The applicant must have completed at least 5 performances including Kantaram during the period from 1st April 2018 to 31st December 2018 and must perform in specified auditoriums across Goa. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline through submission of application forms along with required documents.
<br>

##### ***Cash Prizes for Performance Categories***
1st Prize: ₹2,00,000/- and Memento; 2nd Prize: ₹1,50,000/- and Memento; 3rd Prize: ₹1,00,000/- and Memento

- 1st Prize (Female): ₹10,000/-; 2nd Prize (Female): ₹5,000/-

1st Prize: ₹15,000/-; 2nd Prize: ₹10,000/-; 3rd Prize: ₹5,000/-
##### ***Technical and Other Aspects***

- Best Setting: ₹10,000/-; Best Light Effects: ₹10,000/-
- Best Live Music: ₹15,000/- (To be shared by all Musicians)
- Best Make-up: ₹5,000/-; Best Costumes: ₹5,000/-
- Best Background Music: ₹10,000/-

##### ***Best Singer Awards (Solo)***

- 1st Prize (Male): ₹10,000/-; 2nd Prize (Male): ₹5,000/-
- 1st Prize (Female): ₹10,000/-; 2nd Prize (Female): ₹5,000/-
- Child Artist (Male): ₹2,000/-; Child Artist (Female): ₹2,000/-

##### ***Best Actor (Child Artists)***
Child Artist (Male): ₹2,000/-; Child Artist (Female): ₹2,000/-
##### ***Other Songs Categories***

- Best Duet: ₹10,000/-; Best Duo: ₹10,000/-; Best Trio: ₹15,000/-

- Best Choral Song (Maximum four singers): ₹10,000/-
- Best Comedy Kantar (solo): ₹5,000/-

##### ***Script and Lyrics Awards***

- Best Lyrics (1st Prize): ₹5,000/-
- Best Script (1st Prize): ₹10,000/-
- Best Script (2nd Prize): ₹5,000/-

- Performance Fee: ₹5,000/- (only if the applicant gives consent to video record the performance).

<br>

_*Awards will be announced and distributed in a specially organized Award Distribution Ceremony._
_*All prizes are one-time cash awards._
<br>

## Exclusions

- The applicant cannot include Kantaram presented in the Kala Academy Tiatr Competitions in the Popular Tiatr Competition, unless a tiatr from the Kala Academy's Competition is admitted in the Popular Tiatr Competition.
- The applicant cannot participate artists including technicians and backstage artists in more than one group and one Tiatr, however musicians and stage setting personnel can participate in more than 2 Tiatrs.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must have completed at least 5 performances including Kantaram during the period from 1st April to 31st December.
- The applicant must perform in specified auditoriums including Ravindra Bhavan Margao, Gomant Vidya Niketan Margao, Hanuman Natyagraha Mapusa, Kala Academy Panjim, Rajiv Gandhi Kala Mandir Ponda, Ravindra Bhavan Vasco, Ravindra Bhavan Sanquelim, or Ravindra Bhavan Curchorem.
- The applicant must present popular tiatrs having traditional format of the tiatr.
- The applicant must maintain the traditional format strictly as specified.
- The applicant must communicate the date, time and place of staging of the tiatr in the competition to the organizers at least 15 days in advance from the date of performance.
- The applicant must arrange for 6 passes for the Judges and the Organizers at the performance of the Competition show.
- The applicant must ensure live music with a band consisting of at least 5 instruments which shall include drums, keyboard, bass, guitar and 2 blowing instruments.
- (Optional) The applicant may include child artists up to 12 years of age for special awards in singing and acting.

***Other Conditions***

- The tiatr must be strictly presented as per the given traditional format wherever it is staged.
- If the format is not maintained, the tiatr will not be eligible for 1st, 2nd and 3rd prize, however individual prizes will be given for deserving artists.
- The decision to award or not to award the prizes shall depend entirely on the recommendations of the panel of judges.
- Political songs should be performed without exceeding the limits of freedom of expression, and criticizing individuals by taking name or displaying images or character assassination will not be allowed.

## Application Process

### Offline

***Applications will be invited by releasing press notes and advertisements in the local dailies. Applicants should regularly monitor these announcements for application opening dates.***
**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
<br>

##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 1:****** ***Await Scheduling Communication***
After successful submission, wait for communication from organizers regarding the date, time and venue allocation for the competition performance. The judgment will be conducted from 5th January 2019 to 5th February 2019 at respective auditoriums.
**Step 2:****** ***Confirm Performance Schedule***
Once the performance schedule is communicated, confirm participation and ensure all logistical arrangements including arranging 6 passes for judges and organizers at the performance venue.
**Step 3:****** ***Stage the Performance***
Present the tiatr performance strictly as per the traditional format at the assigned auditorium during the competition period. Ensure compliance with all technical requirements including live music band with minimum 5 specified instruments.
_*The judgment will be conducted in January-February at respective auditoriums._
_*The decision of the panel of judges will be final and binding, and no correspondence regarding results will be entertained._

- Complete Cast and Crew Information Including Names of Characters and Artists.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum number of performances required for my tiatr group to be eligible for participation in this competition?

Your tiatr group must have completed at least 5 performances including Kantaram during the period from 1st April 2018 to 31st December 2018 to be eligible for participation.
<br>

### Can I submit handwritten copies of my script and song lyrics along with the application form for this competition?

No, handwritten copies of scripts and song lyrics will not be accepted. You must submit three typed copies of the script and three typed copies each of the lyrics of the songs.
<br>

### How much advance notice do I need to give the organizers about my performance schedule during the competition?

You must communicate the date, time and place of staging of your tiatr in the competition to the organizers at least 15 days in advance from the date of performance.
<br>

### What is the highest cash prize amount that can be won for the best overall performance in this competition?

The highest cash prize is ₹2,00,000/- along with a memento for the 1st Prize in the performance category.
<br>

### Are there any specific auditoriums where I must have performed to qualify for participation in this annual competition?

Yes, you must have completed your 5 shows in specified auditoriums including Ravindra Bhavan Margao, Gomant Vidya Niketan Margao, Hanuman Natyagraha Mapusa, Kala Academy Panjim, Rajiv Gandhi Kala Mandir Ponda, Ravindra Bhavan Vasco, Ravindra Bhavan Sanquelim, or Ravindra Bhavan Curchorem.
<br>

### What documents do I need to submit along with my application form to participate in this cultural competition?

You need to submit self-attested copies of Aadhaar Card and Permanent Account Number Card along with your application form.
<br>

### Can my band members and technical crew participate in performances with multiple tiatr groups during the same competition?

Artists, technicians and backstage artists can participate in only one group and one tiatr. However, musicians and stage setting personnel can participate in more than 2 tiatrs.
<br>

### What happens if my script is based on another writer's story and I want to participate in this competition?

If your script is based on another writer's story, you must obtain a No Objection Certificate from that writer and submit it along with your application.
<br>

### How many complimentary passes do I need to arrange for the judges and organizers during my performance?

You must arrange for 6 passes for the judges and organizers at the performance of your competition show.
<br>

### What is the maximum age limit for child artists to be eligible for special awards in this competition?

Child artists up to 12 years of age are eligible for special awards in singing and acting categories.
<br>

### Will I receive any compensation for participating in this competition even if I don't win any prizes?

Yes, you will receive ₹5,000/- only if you give consent to video record your performance, and the video recording expenditure will be borne by the organizing body.
<br>

### What are the minimum instrumental requirements for the live music band that must accompany my tiatr performance?

Your live music band must consist of at least 5 instruments including drums, keyboard, bass, guitar and 2 blowing instruments.
<br>

### Can I include political songs in my tiatr performance without facing any restrictions or disqualification from the competition?

Political songs are allowed as they are a tradition of tiatr, but you cannot criticize individuals by taking names, displaying images, or engaging in character assassination, as this will lead to disqualification.
<br>

### What will happen if I fail to maintain the traditional format of tiatr during my performance in the competition?

If you don't maintain the traditional format, your tiatr will not be eligible for 1st, 2nd and 3rd prizes, however individual prizes will still be given for deserving artists.
<br>
