## Brief Description

This scheme has been introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat. It aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a 30% share capital contribution by the State Government for setting up new sugar cooperative factories in Gujarat.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;State Government Share Capital Contribution to Co.operative Sugar Factories (Normal Area) (Tribal Area)&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to facilitates the establishment of new sugar cooperative factories by providing a 30% share capital contribution from the State Government. The objective is to strengthen the cooperative sugar industry and support economic development in the state.

## Benefits

To the extent of 30% of the project cost to the concerned Sugar Co-operative Societies who are eligible<br>

## Eligibility

- Newly established sugar co-operative factories shall be eligible for this scheme.

## Application Process

### Offline

- The concerned sugar co-operative societies who are eligible, should send the proposal through the District Registrar of the concerned district to the Director of Sugar, Gujarat State, Gandhinagar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to newly established sugar cooperative factories in Gujarat by contributing 30% of the project cost as share capital.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Newly established sugar cooperative factories located in Gujarat are eligible to apply for financial assistance under this scheme.

### What percentage of the project cost does the State Government contribute?

The State Government contributes 30% of the total project cost as share capital to eligible sugar cooperative factories.

### Are existing sugar factories eligible for this scheme?

No, only newly established sugar cooperative factories are eligible to apply for the scheme.

### Can a privately-owned sugar factory apply for financial assistance under this scheme?

No, only sugar factories operating under the cooperative model are eligible to apply.

### Can a sugar factory apply for this scheme without a sanctioned project loan?

No, a sanctioned project loan is a mandatory requirement for applying under this scheme.

### What is the process for applying to this scheme?

Eligible sugar cooperative societies must submit their proposal through the District Registrar of the concerned district, which is then forwarded to the Director of Sugar, Gujarat State, Gandhinagar, for approval.

### Where can applicants find more information about this scheme?

Applicants can contact the District Registrar of their respective district or the Director of Sugar, Gujarat State, Gandhinagar, for more information.
