## Brief Description

Financial assistance scheme providing up to ₹36,000 annually to visually challenged permanent college teachers for reader assistance, Braille books, and learning aids. Implemented by University Grants Commission to support teaching, research, and academic independence.

## Detailed Description

**Objective**
To provide facility to help visually challenged permanent teachers to achieve self-dependence by using various aids for teaching, learning and research.
<br>

**Purpose**
The scheme has been formulated to help visually challenged permanent teachers to pursue teaching and research with the help of a Reader and by using teaching and learning aids, by way of providing Reader&#39;s Allowance and funds for purchase of Braille books, recorded materials, and other related materials/equipment.
<br>

## Benefits

The scheme provides the following benefits to visually challenged permanent teachers:
**1. Financial Support**
An allowance of **Rs. 36,000/- per annum** is provided to meet expenses related to teaching, learning and research.
**2. Reader Support**
Provision of funds for payment to a Reader, enabling the teacher to carry out academic duties effectively.
**3. Access to Learning Materials** Funds can be used for purchase of:

  - Braille Books/Material
  - Recorded Materials
  - Any other related/required material or equipment for research, teaching and learning

**4. Self-Dependence**
The scheme enables visually challenged teachers to achieve self-dependence in their teaching and research activities.
<br>

## Exclusions

**1. Non-Permanent Teachers**
The scheme is applicable only to permanent teachers. Teachers who are temporary, contractual, or ad-hoc are not covered under the scheme.
**2. Non-UGC Recognized Colleges**
Teachers working in colleges not recognized under Sections 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act are not eligible for the scheme.
**3. Cap on Reader&#39;s Honorarium**
Payment to the Reader exceeding **Rs. 50/- per hour** is not reimbursable under the scheme.
**4. Excess over Annual Limit**
Any expenditure beyond Rs. 36,000/- per annum is not covered, regardless of actual expenses incurred.
**5. Bills Not Submitted**
Reimbursement will not be made unless original bills are submitted by the teacher to the college.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent teacher in a college. Temporary, contractual, or ad-hoc teachers are not covered under the scheme.
2. The scheme covers teachers with the following nature of blindness:

  - Fully Blind
  - Low Vision

A certificate from a Government Hospital is mandatory to establish the nature of visual impairment.
3. The college where the teacher is employed must be recognized under:

  - Section 2(f) of the UGC Act
  - Section 12(B) of the UGC Act

4. The eligible teacher&#39;s details must be included in the consolidated list of visually challenged teachers submitted by the college to the UGC Regional Office/NRCB along with the Twelfth Plan General Development Grant proposal.
<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

1. **Prepare the Proposal:** The College prepares the proposal in the [prescribed proforma format](https://www.sanskrit.nic.in/uploads/2025_09_08_FA_Visually_Disabled_Teachers.pdf) only, including the consolidated list of all visually challenged teachers in the Institute.   1. **Submit the Proposal:** The proposal may be sent directly to the respective Regional Offices/NRCB of the UGC along with the proposal for the Twelfth Plan General Development Grant.   1. **Approval by UGC:** The UGC would analyze and approve the proposal at its own level.   1. **Release of First Installment:** Once the proposal is approved, the first installment of the admissible grant will be released by the UGC with the letter of approval.   1. **Release of Subsequent Installments:** Subsequent installments would be paid on receipt of:

  - Utilization Certificate of the previous grant
  - Statement of Expenditure incurred in the previous year
  - Details pertaining to visually challenged teachers

<br>

## Documents Required

**1. Disability Certificate**
A certificate from a Government Hospital to establish the nature of blindness:

  - Fully Blind, OR


  - Low Vision

**2. Reader&#39;s Receipt**
A receipt from the Reader with revenue stamp indicating the honorarium paid to the Reader.
**3. Original Bills**
Original bills to be submitted by the teacher to the college towards actual expenditure incurred.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

All visually challenged teachers who are working as permanent teachers in colleges recognized under Sections 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act are eligible to apply for this scheme.
<br>

### What is the amount of financial assistance provided under this scheme?

The allowance provided to visually challenged permanent teachers is **Rs. 36,000/-** per annum. This amount can be utilized for payment to a Reader, purchase of Braille Books/Material, purchase of Recorded Materials, and any other related/required material or equipment for research, teaching and learning.
<br>

### What is the maximum amount payable to the Reader?

The amount payable to the Reader shall not exceed Rs. 50/- per hour.
<br>

### What documents are required to be submitted by the teacher to the college for reimbursement?

The teacher is required to submit the following documents to the college:

   1. Original bills of actual expenditure incurred
   1. Receipt from the Reader with revenue stamp
   1. Certificate from a Government Hospital establishing the nature of blindness

<br>

### How and when will the teacher receive the financial assistance?

The college will reimburse the amount to the visually challenged teacher on receipt of the actual amount paid to the Reader and actual expenditure incurred, subject to a maximum of Rs. 36,000/- per annum. The first installment is released by UGC with the letter of approval and subsequent installments are released on receipt of the Utilization Certificate and Statement of Expenditure of the previous year.
<br>
