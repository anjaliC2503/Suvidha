## Brief Description

This initiative offers free coaching to the students belonging to the backward class (BC) and extremely backward class (EBC) of Bihar, helping them prepare for competitive exams like NET, GATE, JRF, Ph.D., and M.Phil.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Mukhyamantri Vyavsayik Pathyakram Margdarshan Evam Utpreran Yojana&quot; was launched by the Backward Class and Extremely Backward Class Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, aims to provide free pre-examination coaching to BC and EBC students. This initiative supports students aiming for competitive exams such as NET, GATE, JRF, and Ph.D. Programs. Coaching is offered at select universities and career guidance centers.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Free Coaching:** Free coaching in the renowned [Career Guidance Centers(Page 26)](https://state.bihar.gov.in/cache/41/Department%20Info/Schemes/varsik2025s.pdf).
**Note:****** There is no hostel provision in the centers.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Bihar.
   1. The applicant should belong to the Backward Class (BC) or Extremely Backward Class (EBC) categories.
   1. The applicant’s annual family income should not exceed ₹3,00,000/-.
   1. The applicant must meet the minimum educational qualifications required for the chosen competitive examination.

**Note:** 

  - Applicants will appear for competitive exams under their respective courses. Selection will be based on a multiple-choice written test following the prescribed syllabus.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Download the [application form](https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/CitizenHome.html).
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Send the completed application form and supporting documents to the concerned Director of the Carrier Guidance Centre through registered post or speed post to the below-mentioned address:

1. Director, Vocational Course Guidance and Induction Centre, PMIR Department, Darbhanga House, Patna University, Patna – 800005 (Mobile No. 947303070).

## Documents Required

1. Two (02) passport-size color photographs.
1. Birth Certificate.
1. Educational Qualification Certificates.
1. Caste Certificate.
1. Income Certificate.
1. Aadhaar Card.
1. A self-addressed envelope with a postage stamp of appropriate value for registered post.

**Note:** Each document should be Self-Attested by the applicant.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme offers coaching for backward class (BC) and extremely backward class (EBC) students of Bihar, helping them prepare for competitive exams like NET, GATE, JRF, Ph.D., and M.Phil.

### Who can apply for the coaching program?

Applicants must be residents of Bihar, belonging to the Backward Class (BC) or Extremely Backward Class (EBC), with a family income below ₹3,00,000/-.

### What exams are covered under the program?

The program supports preparation for exams such as NET, GATE, JRF, Ph.D., and M.Phil.

### Where can applicants find the application form?

The application form can be downloaded from the following link: [https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/CitizenHome.html](https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/CitizenHome.html)


<br>

### How should the application be submitted?

Applications must be submitted via registered post or speed post to the concerned Director of the Vocational Course Guidance and Induction Center.

### Is accommodation provided during the program?

No, accommodation is not provided as part of the program.

### How are applicants selected?

Selection is based on a multiple-choice written test aligned with the prescribed syllabus.

### Can applicants apply to more than one center?

No, applications can be submitted to only one center.

### What is the age limit for applicants?

Applicants must meet the age and academic criteria specified for the competitive exams.

### Are there reserved seats for specific groups?

Yes, a percentage of seats are reserved for female applicants and specific categories as per the guidelines.
