## Brief Description

It aims to provide landowners in Haridwar district with model maps for residential construction according to the size of their plots.

## Detailed Description

The “Providing Model Maps for Residential Construction” is implemented by the Haridwar - Roorkee Development Authority under Housing Department, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to provide landowners in Haridwar district with model maps for residential construction according to the size of their plots.
<br>

## Benefits

Model maps for residential construction are provided to all landowners across Haridwar district.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. All landowners in the Haridwar district of Uttarakhand are eligible.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

For the convenience of landowners in Haridwar district, model maps have been uploaded on the authority’s website. Landowners can select a model map according to the area of their plot and avail the facility.
<br>

The required documents must be submitted as per the guidelines of the Haridwar-Roorkee Development Authority.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can benefit from this scheme?

All landowners in Haridwar district are eligible to use the model maps provided under this scheme.
<br>

### What is the main objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to assist landowners by providing ready-made model maps for residential construction suitable for their plot size.
<br>

### How can landowners access the model maps?

Model maps have been uploaded on the Haridwar-Roorkee Development Authority website, and landowners can download them directly.
<br>

### Can a landowner select a map according to their plot area?

Yes, landowners can select a model map based on the size of their plot.
<br>

### Can commercial or non-residential construction use these maps?

No, these model maps are specifically for residential construction only.
<br>
