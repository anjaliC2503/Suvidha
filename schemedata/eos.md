## Brief Description

The scheme provides capital assistance for setting up small-scale Recirculatory Aquaculture Systems (RAS) and Biofloc culture systems to promote high-yield, sustainable fish farming.

## Detailed Description

The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Establishment of small RAS / Biofloc Culture System, introduced by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide  financial assistance for establishing small-scale aquaculture units, either through 1 RAS tank (100 m³ capacity) or 7 Biofloc tanks (4 m diameter × 1.5 m height). It aims to promote sustainable and scientific fish farming for individual farmers with smaller land or capital access. It is a centrally funded scheme with a 50:50 cost-sharing pattern between the Central and State Governments.

## Benefits

- The project cost is ₹7,50,000/-

> **Subsidy Distribution:**


- A 40% subsidy is available for the General Category.
- A 60% subsidy is offered for SC (Scheduled Caste) and Women beneficiaries.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Haryana and possess a valid Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).
1. The applicant must own land or have a registered lease of at least 10 years, free from legal encumbrances.
1. The applicant must have access to a water source such as a canal, well, tube well, or similar.
1. The applicant must be trained in aquaculture or fisheries and hold a valid certification.
1. The applicant must be willing to maintain and operate the system at their own cost post-installation.
1. The applicant must submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or a Self-Contained Proposal (SCP).
1. The applicant must sign an agreement with the Fisheries Department to be eligible under the scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much subsidy will I get if I belong to the General Category?

As a General Category applicant, you will receive a 40% subsidy on the total project cost.

### If I am a woman or belong to the SC category, what subsidy am I eligible for?

You are eligible for a 60% subsidy on the project cost if you are a woman or from the SC category.

### Is having a Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) mandatory to apply for the scheme?

Yes, it is compulsory to have a valid Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) to apply for this scheme.

### What land documents do I need to submit along with my application?

You need to provide Jamabandi, Nakal Akshizra, Farad, or a registered lease deed with a minimum 10-year duration.

### Is borewell water allowed as a water source for this scheme?

Yes, water from open wells, tube wells, borewells, canals, rivers, and springs is allowed.

### Will the government take care of maintenance after the construction is completed?

No, you will be responsible for the operation, management, and maintenance of the unit at your own cost.

### What specific infrastructure is required to be set up under this scheme?

The infrastructure must include essential elements required for RAS or Biofloc, including water treatment units.

### Am I required to submit a Detailed Project Report during application?

Yes, you need to submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or Self Contained Proposal (SCP) as per PMMSY guidelines.

### Can I apply online or do I have to visit an office?

The document does not specify application mode, but support is available via **saral.haryana@gov.in** and 0172-3968400.

### Is this scheme open to first-time applicants or only experienced ones?

The scheme does not restrict first-time applicants, but a training certificate is mandatory.
