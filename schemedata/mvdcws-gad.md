## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide social security to registered drivers and conductors by offering gratuity assistance for funeral expenses to families in the event of an accidental death.

## Detailed Description

The “Motor Vehicle Driver-Conductor Welfare Scheme- Gratuity Assistance in case of Death” scheme has been launched by the Transport Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to provide social security and welfare support to registered transport vehicle drivers and conductors. Under this scheme, gratuity assistance for funeral and last rites is provided to the family in case of death due to an accident. The scheme covers both the registered beneficiary and eligible family members. 
<br>

## Benefits

> **Gratuity Assistance in Case of Death**


- A one-time gratuity assistance of ₹2,000 is provided for funeral and last rites.
- The assistance is payable in case of accidental death of the registered driver or conductor and the family members. .

> **Mode of Disbursement**


- The assistance amount is disbursed immediately by the local body.
- The local body later claims reimbursement from the Social Justice Department.

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be a registered transport vehicle driver or conductor under the scheme.
1. The applicant should possess a valid commercial driving or conductor license issued in Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant’s name should be registered on the Samagra Portal.
1. The death should have occurred due to an accident.
1. The applicant should not be a government employee.
1. The applicant’s family member should fall under the defined [family category](https://transport.mp.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/yojna_20150001.pdf) (**Page No. 3**) of the scheme.
<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

> **Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official website: [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on the option “Register Family” or “Register Member”.
**Step 3:** Enter the Aadhaar number and other required information.
**Step 4:** Enter the OTP received on the registered mobile number.
**Step 5:** Carefully fill in all the required details and upload the necessary documents.
**Step 6:** Enter the captcha code and click on “Submit Application.”
> **Offline Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the nearest Lok Seva Kendra, MP Online Kiosk, or Common Service Center (CSC).
**Step 2:** Provide the required documents and necessary information to complete the registration process.
> **After Samagra Registration:**

**Step 1: Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://transport.mp.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/yojna_20150001.pdf) (Page No. 9) from the official website. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District or Regional Transport Office.

## Documents Required

- Passport-size Photograph
- Death Certificate
- Accident Report or Police FIR (in case of accidental death)
- Samagra Family ID
- Samagra Member ID
- Valid Commercial Driving License or Conductor License (for registered beneficiary)
- Proof of Relationship with Deceased (if family member benefit is claimed)
- Bank Account Details

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of this benefit?

It provides immediate financial assistance to meet funeral and last rites expenses.
<br>

### Who is eligible for gratuity assistance?

Registered drivers, conductors, or their eligible family members are eligible.
<br>

### What is the amount of gratuity assistance?

A fixed amount of ₹2,000 is provided.
<br>

### Is the benefit applicable for natural death?

No, the benefit is applicable only in cases of accidental death.
<br>

### Who disburses the assistance amount?

The assistance amount is disbursed by the local body.
<br>

### Is reimbursement involved?

Yes, the Social Justice Department reimburses the amount to the local body.
<br>

### Is Samagra registration mandatory?

Yes, Samagra registration is mandatory.
<br>

### Can the assistance be cancelled?

Yes, the assistance can be cancelled if false information is provided.
<br>

### Who verifies the claim?

The claim is verified by the local body and Transport Department authorities.
<br>

### Can other states people apply for this scheme?

No, only residents of Madhya Pradesh are eligible.
<br>
