## Brief Description

The scheme "Student Program Under AcSIR" implemented by the Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR), aims to promote high-quality research and leadership development in Earth Sciences through doctoral education. It provides research opportunities to geoscience postgraduate students.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Student Program Under AcSIR&quot; by the Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR), Government of India aims to promote high-quality interdisciplinary research by enrolling eligible postgraduate candidates into PhD programmes.  Through this scheme, students receive fellowships, training, and research opportunities in Earth Sciences, with the broader goal of preparing future leaders in the geoscience domain of the Himalayas.

## Benefits

- **Advanced Research Opportunities:** The program provides opportunities for conducting high-quality, interdisciplinary research in various fields of Himalayan Geo-science.  - **Leadership Development:** The scheme aims to develop leaders in the field of Himalayan Geo-science through the PhD program.  - **Career Advancement:** Successful completion of the PhD program leads to a postgraduate degree, enhancing career prospects in the field.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must have a postgraduate degree (M.Sc./M.Tech.) in any discipline of Earth Sciences.
- The applicant must hold a valid national-level fellowship such as:


1. NET-JRF (UGC/CSIR).
1. INSPIRE Fellowship.
1. Any other equivalent national fellowship.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the official online [application portal](https://acsiriphd.emli.in/) and create a Username/Application Log-In by clicking on the “Sign Up here” option.
**Step 2:** Log in using the credentials received on your registered email ID, and click on “Complete your application” to proceed.
**Step 3:** Fill out the application form as per the instructions mentioned in the portal and upload the following mandatory and applicable documents in the specified format and size
**Step 4:** Choose the relevant Admission Route for the iPhD Program:

  - **Fresh Candidate (Not registered in PhD at AcSIR)**: Choose a research area and lab preference. Eligible for AcSIR-NIF Innovation Doctoral Fellowship.  - **AcSIR PhD Student:** Submit Enrolment Letter and Thesis Research Proposal. Eligible for lump sum research support through AcSIR faculty.  - **NIF Employee:** Submit Permission from Director, NIF and Proposed Research Problem. No additional financial support provided.  - **Industry Sponsored Candidate:** Submit &#39;No Objection Certificate&#39; from the employer and Proposed Research Problem.
**Step 5:** Confirm the application details, review the entire form carefully, and then submit the application.
> **Note:** There is no application fee for this process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the "Student Program Under AcSIR"?

The main objective of the "Student Program Under AcSIR" is to promote interdisciplinary and high-quality scientific research by enrolling meritorious postgraduate students into doctoral programmes in Earth Sciences with a special focus on Himalayan geology.

### Which organization implements the "Student Program Under AcSIR"?

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Student Program Under AcSIR"?

Any applicant who has completed a postgraduate degree in Earth Sciences and holds a valid national-level fellowship such as NET-JRF (CSIR/UGC), INSPIRE Fellowship, or any equivalent national fellowship is eligible to apply for the Student Program Under AcSIR.
<br>

<br>

### Is it mandatory to have a national fellowship to apply for this program?

Yes, it is mandatory for the applicant to possess a valid national-level fellowship to be considered eligible for admission to the "Student Program Under AcSIR".
<br>

<br>

### How often does the admission process occur for the "Student Program Under AcSIR"?

The admission process for the Student Program Under AcSIR is conducted twice every year, allowing applicants to apply during either of the two cycles.
<br>

<br>

### Where should interested candidates apply for the "Student Program Under AcSIR"?

Interested candidates must submit their application through the official online platform known as the AcSIR Admission Portal, which is accessible via the website: [https://acsiriphd.emli.in/](https://acsiriphd.emli.in/).
<br>

<br>

### What types of research are undertaken through this doctoral program?

The research undertaken through this doctoral programme typically involves advanced studies in Earth Sciences, with emphasis on geoscientific problems and exploration specific to the Himalayan region.
<br>

<br>

### Can a student without a postgraduate degree apply for this program?

No, a student without a postgraduate degree in Earth Sciences or a related field is not eligible to apply for the Student Program Under AcSIR.
<br>

<br>

### Are there any reservation or relaxation criteria applicable in the admission process?

While the scheme primarily focuses on merit and fellowship eligibility, candidates are advised to check the latest AcSIR guidelines for any applicable reservation or relaxation criteria based on government norms.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any fee that an applicant needs to pay for applying under this scheme?

There is no application fee required to apply for this program.
