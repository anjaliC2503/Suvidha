## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote and develop a healthy lifestyle among school children through the principles and practices of AYUSH. Under this initiative, free AYUSH-based health services and medicines are provided to children registered in government schools.

## Detailed Description

The “Healthy Lifestyle for School Children through Ayurvidya-AYUSH” is implemented by the Ayush Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote and develop a healthy lifestyle among school children through the principles and practices of AYUSH.
<br>

## Benefits

- Promoting a healthy lifestyle among school children through AYUSH and providing free distribution of medicines based on their specific health conditions. This facility is provided free of cost to the students.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiary must be a student enrolled in a government school in Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

School children may contact the concerned authorities of their respective government schools to avail the benefits under this scheme.
<br>

## References

- [Guidelines (Page No. 147)](https://uk.gov.in/department92/library_file/file-04-12-2023-06-02-23.pdf)

## Documents Required

Documents are to be submitted as per the directions of the respective schools.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to benefit from this scheme?

All children enrolled in government schools across Uttarakhand are eligible to benefit from this scheme.
<br>

### What does the scheme provide to students?

The scheme provides free AYUSH-based health check-ups, medicines, and guidance on healthy lifestyle practices.
<br>

### Is there any cost involved for the students?

No, all the services and medicines under this scheme are provided completely free of cost.
<br>

### Where can students avail the benefits of this scheme?

Students can avail the benefits directly in their respective government schools.
<br>

### Are private school students eligible for the scheme?

No, the scheme is applicable only to students studying in government schools of Uttarakhand.
<br>
