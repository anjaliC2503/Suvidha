## Brief Description

The scheme aims to rehabilitate drivers and conductors who suffer permanent disability due to accidents by providing skill training for alternative employment to the beneficiaries.

## Detailed Description

The “Motor Vehicle Driver-Conductor Welfare Scheme- Training and Rehabilitation in Case of Permanent Disability due to Accident” scheme has been launched by the Transport Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. aims to ensure the welfare and rehabilitation of registered transport vehicle drivers and conductors. The scheme aims to provide training and rehabilitation support to drivers and conductors who suffer permanent disability due to accidents, enabling them to regain livelihood opportunities. 
<br>

## Benefits

**Nature of Benefit**

- Skill development training for alternative employment.
- Rehabilitation support based on aptitude and interest assessment.
- Training services are provided through Skill Development Mission–approved institutions.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be a registered transport vehicle driver or conductor.
1. The applicant should possess a valid commercial driving or conductor license issued in Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be registered on the Samagra Portal.
1. The applicant should have suffered permanent disability due to an accident.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

> **Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official website: [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on the option “Register Family” or “Register Member”.
**Step 3:** Enter the Aadhaar number and other required information.
**Step 4:** Enter the OTP received on the registered mobile number.
**Step 5:** Carefully fill in all the required details and upload the necessary documents.
**Step 6:** Enter the captcha code and click on “Submit Application.”
> **Offline Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the nearest Lok Seva Kendra, MP Online Kiosk, or Common Service Center (CSC).
**Step 2:** Provide the required documents and necessary information to complete the registration process.
> **After Samagra Registration:**

**Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://transport.mp.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/yojna_20150001.pdf) (Page No. 9) from the official website. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District or Regional Transport Office.

## Documents Required

- Passport-size Photograph
- Samagra Member ID

- Permanent Disability Certificate (Issued by Authorized Medical Board)
- Accident Details or Supporting Record (If required)
- Aadhaar Card
- Proof of Residence
