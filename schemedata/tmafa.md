## Brief Description

The scheme "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" aims to promote and honor women’s contributions to Indian classical music by recognizing two outstanding female artistes with a cash award of ₹5,00,000, a shawl, and a certificate at a national-level event.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to commemorate the legacy of legendary musical artistes Beldi Tana and Riri and promote the cultural heritage of Indian classical music by honoring two exceptional female artistes from across the country. These artists are felicitated at a prestigious national-level award ceremony held at Vadnagar, Gujarat, with a cash prize of ₹5,00,000, a shawl, and a certificate each.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Cash Prize:** Each of the two selected female artists is awarded a cash prize of ₹5,00,000/-.- **Other Benefit:** In addition to the monetary award, recipients receive a shawl and a certificate recognizing their contribution.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of India.
  - The applicant must be a female artiste in the field of Indian classical music.
  - The applicant must have made notable contributions to classical music and demonstrated a distinguished record of achievement.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme?

The objective of the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme is to preserve and promote the rich legacy of Indian classical music by recognizing and honoring two outstanding female artistes each year for their significant contributions to the art form through a prestigious national-level award.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who implements the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme?

The "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme is implemented by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department under the Government of Gujarat, which organizes the award ceremony and oversees the entire selection and disbursement process.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to receive the award under the scheme?

Under the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme, only female artistes who are residents of India and have made notable and distinguished contributions in the field of Indian classical music are eligible for consideration.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income restriction for applicants under this scheme?

There is no income-based eligibility restriction under the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme, and both individual and family income are not considered as criteria for selection.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How many artistes are awarded under the scheme each year?

As part of the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme, a total of two female artistes are selected and honored annually with awards during a nationally organized ceremony at Vadnagar.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the components of the award given under the scheme?

Each selected awardee under the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme receives a cash prize of ₹5,00,000, along with a shawl and a certificate, as a token of appreciation and recognition of their excellence.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where is the award ceremony for the scheme conducted?

The award ceremony under the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme is conducted in Vadnagar, Gujarat, as a tribute to the historical and cultural significance associated with the legendary artistes Beldi Tana and Riri.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there a specific application form required for this scheme?

There is no pre-designed form mandated under the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme; applicants are required to submit their applications on plain paper, providing all relevant personal and professional details.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any application fee required to apply under this scheme?

No application fee is charged for applying under the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme, and submission of the application is completely free of cost.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any application fee required to apply under this scheme?

No application fee is charged for applying under the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme, and submission of the application is completely free of cost.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant submit the application for this award?

Applications under the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" scheme can be submitted in offline mode to the Gujarat State Sangeet Natak Academy.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any age limit to apply for the award under this scheme?

The scheme guidelines for the "Tanariri Music Awards Festival and Awards (National Level)" do not mention any specific age restriction, but the applicant must have a credible and recognized record in Indian classical music.
