## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) to enhance their financial independence and social inclusion. Through this scheme, PwDs are employed as Clerical Assistants for 89 Days under the District Panchayat offices of their respective islands.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Special Job to PwDs - Clerical Assistant&quot; was introduced by the Department of Social Welfare &amp; Tribal Affairs, Union Territory of Lakshadweep, with the aim of providing temporary employment opportunities to Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) having a minimum of 40% disability in the UT. Under this scheme, 11 PwD candidates are employed as Clerical Assistants on an 89-day rotation basis in the District Panchayat offices of their respective islands to address issues related to disabled persons and other associated matters.

## Benefits

1. Persons with Disabilities are employed in the District Panchayat offices of their respective islands.
1. A monthly salary of ₹11,000/-.

**Note:** Employment is provided on 89 days rotation basis.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a local resident of the Union Territory of Lakshadweep.
1. The applicant should have a minimum of 40% disability and be physically capable of performing clerical work.
1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category.
1. The age of the applicant should be between 18 and 45 years.
1. The applicant should have passed SSLC or a higher qualification.
1. The applicant should have knowledge of typewriting/computers.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Eligible applicants can collect the application form from the Village (Dweep) Panchayat Office or District Panchayat Office of their respective islands.
**Step 2**: Carefully fill out the application form and attach all required supporting documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the completed application form along with the necessary documents to the concerned officer at the Village (Dweep) Panchayat Office or District Panchayat Office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is the job duration under this scheme?

The job is offered on a rotational basis for 89 days per appointment.

### Are there any additional skill requirements?

Having typewriting or computer knowledge is desirable but not mandatory for selection.

### What is the application process for this scheme?

Applicants must submit their forms and documents at their respective Village (Dweep) Panchayat Office or District Panchayat Office.

### What documents are required for application?

Applicants need to submit a Disability Certificate, ST Certificate, Residential Proof, Age Proof, Educational Qualification Certificate, and Identity Proof.

### How is the selection process conducted?

Selection is based on eligibility criteria and merit.

### Will I get a permanent job through this scheme?

No, the scheme only offers temporary employment on an 89-day rotation basis.

### Can candidates from other states apply for this scheme?

No, only local residents of Lakshadweep belonging to the ST category are eligible.

### Is there a reservation or preference for any specific category?

Yes, the scheme is exclusively for PwDs belonging to the ST category in UT of Lakshadweep.

### Can female candidates apply for the Clerical Assistant post?

Yes.

### Can a candidate with less than 40% disability apply?

No, applicants must have a minimum of 40% disability to be eligible.

### Can candidates with higher educational qualifications apply?

Yes, applicants with SSLC and above are eligible.

### How can I get more details or assistance regarding this scheme?

Applicants should visit the Village (Dweep) Panchayat Office or District Panchayat Office in their respective islands for detailed information.
