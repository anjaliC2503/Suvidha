## Brief Description

The "Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana" by the Labour, Employment, Training & Skill Development Department, Government of Jharkhand, provides financial assistance to unemployed candidates who have completed skill training programs aligned with NSQF.

## Detailed Description

The "Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana" by the Labour, Employment, Training & Skill Development Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to support unemployed individuals who have completed short-term skill training programs aligned with the National Qualification Framework (NSQF). Under this scheme, candidates receive a financial incentive to enhance their employability.

## Benefits

- A financial incentive of ₹5,000/- per annum for eligible candidates.
- Special categories like widows, abandoned women, primitive tribes, and persons with disabilities receive an additional 50% incentive.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be unemployed (not employed in the public/private sector or self-employed).
1. The applicant must be registered in a Jharkhand employment exchange.
1. The applicant should have completed a skill training course conducted by a government ITI, polytechnic, or other government vocational institutes aligned with NSQF.
1. The applicant must not have availed of any similar incentive under another scheme.
1. The applicant must be a domicile of Jharkhand.
1. The applicant should have a valid bank account and Aadhaar card.
1. The applicant should not have been convicted of a crime with a sentence of 48 hours or more.
1. The applicant must be between 18 to 35 years old on the date of employment exchange registration (relaxation as per government norms for SC/ST candidates).

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the application form [(Annexure-I)](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s32b8a61594b1f4c4db0902a8a395ced93/uploads/2021/06/2021062549.pdf) from the official website. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the respective training institute/department.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the respective training institute/department to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Unemployed individuals who have completed skill training from government ITIs, polytechnics, or other government vocational training institutes in Jharkhand and meet the eligibility criteria can apply.

### How much financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

The candidates receive [?]5,000 for one year. Special categories like widows, abandoned women, primitive tribes, and persons with disabilities receive an additional 50% incentive.

### Can a self-employed individual apply for this scheme?

No, only unemployed individuals who are not engaged in public/private sector jobs or self-employment are eligible.

### What documents are required for application?

Applicants need to submit domicile proof, an Aadhaar card, bank details, a training completion certificate, and other applicable documents.

### How can I apply for the Mukhyamantri Protsahan Yojana?

The candidates must obtain and submit a duly filled application form along with required documents at the training department/institute where they completed their course.

### Where can I get the application form?

The application form is available at the respective training department/institute where the applicant completed their training.

### Is there any age limit for applying?

Yes, applicants must be between 18 to 35 years on the date of employment exchange registration. SC/ST candidates receive age relaxation as per government norms.

### Can I apply if I have availed benefits from another similar scheme?

No, the scheme does not allow duplication of benefits from other similar government incentive programs.

### Can I apply online for this scheme?

No, applications must be submitted physically at the training institute/department.

### Is there any reservation or special preference for specific groups?

Yes, widows, abandoned women, persons with disabilities, and primitive tribes receive an additional 50% incentive under this scheme.

### Can an applicant who has completed training from a private institution apply?

No, the scheme is only for candidates trained at government ITIs, polytechnics, and other government vocational institutes that are aligned with NSQF.

### Where can I get more information about this scheme?

For more details, visit the official portals: https://rojgar.jharkhand.gov.in, https://ranchi.nic.in, or check notices at the Sub-Regional Employment Exchange, Ranchi.
