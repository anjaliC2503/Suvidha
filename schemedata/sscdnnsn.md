## Brief Description

The "State Scholarship for Classes 6th to 10th" scheme provides an annual scholarship ranging from ₹200/- to ₹1,200/- (for 10 months) to the students belonging to De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities studying in classes 6th to 10th.

## Detailed Description

The "State Scholarship for Classes 6th to 10th" scheme by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides an annual scholarship ranging from ₹200/- to ₹1,200/- (for 10 months) to the students belonging to De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities studying in classes 6th to 10th. The scholarship is distributed by the Directorate of Public Distribution, Government of Madhya Pradesh.

## Benefits

> **Annual Scholarship (for 10 months):**

**For Classes 6th to 8th**

- Boys: ₹200/-
- Girls: ₹600/-

**For Classes 9th to 10th**

- Boys: ₹600/-
- Girls: ₹1200/-

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a student.
1. The applicant should be from a Denotified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Community.
1. The applicant should be studying in Class 6th to 10th.

_*The applicant should be eligible for the scholarship even if they have failed one year._
_*The applicant should not need to reapply for renewal if they continue in the same category of classes (e.g., 6th to 8th or 9th to 10th)._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scholarship?

Students belonging to De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities are eligible.

### What is the scholarship amount for boys in classes 6 to 8?

Boys in classes 6 to 8 receive an annual scholarship of Rs. 200.

### What is the scholarship amount for girls in classes 6 to 8?

Girls in classes 6 to 8 receive an annual scholarship of Rs. 600.

### What is the scholarship amount for boys in classes 9 to 10?

Boys in classes 9 to 10 receive an annual scholarship of Rs. 600.

### What is the scholarship amount for girls in classes 9 to 10?

Girls in classes 9 to 10 receive an annual scholarship of Rs. 1200.

### Is an income certificate required for the scholarship?

No, an income certificate is not required for this scholarship.

### Do students need to reapply every year?

No, students do not need to reapply for renewal if they are in the same category of classes (e.g., 6th to 8th or 9th to 10th).

### Can a student receive the scholarship if they have failed a year?

Yes, students are eligible for the scholarship even if they have failed a year.

### What documents are required to apply for the scholarship?

An attested photocopy of the Permanent Caste Certificate issued by the Sub-Divisional Officer (Revenue) is required.

### How long is the scholarship provided for each year?

The scholarship is provided for 10 months each academic year.
