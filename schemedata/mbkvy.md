## Brief Description

The scheme aims to develop skills of youth through new age technology-based courses in manufacturing, service and other sectors to create skilled manpower in the state. It provides quality training and certification to Gujarat domicile students.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Mukhyamantri Bhavishyalakshi Kaushalya Vikas Yojana (MBKVY)" by the Labour, Skill Development and Employment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide skill development training to youth in manufacturing, service and other sectors through new age technology-based courses to meet current industry requirements and create skilled manpower in the state. The scheme provides short-term courses, digital training, future skill courses and new age courses with quality training according to current technology, along with practical training as per the latest technology requirements. The applicant must be a domicile of Gujarat state and must have passed at least Standard 10/12/Industrial Training Institute/Degree-Diploma. The scheme is implemented by Gujarat Skill Development Mission under the guidance of Steering Committee and Implementation and Monitoring Committee. The applications for this scheme are accepted online through skills.gujarat.gov.in portal.
<br>

## Benefits

**Training Cost Coverage:** Training cost will be directly paid to the Training Provider's bank account based on Common Skilling Norms for National Skill Qualification Framework courses.
**Assessment Fee Coverage:** Assessment fee for National Skill Qualification Framework courses will be paid to the concerned Assessment Agency as per set standards.
**Industry-Demand Based Course Fees:** For new courses based on industry demand, training cost will be paid to training centers as determined by the Steering Committee.
**Special Course Fees:** For special types of courses conducted by Skill Institutes/Industrial Training Institutes/Kaushalya The Skill University, training cost and method will be as approved by the Steering Committee.
**Infrastructure Support:** Raw material, guest faculty, Training of Trainers, tools equipment, office expenses etc. will be covered under training cost for Industrial Training Institutes.
**Certification:** Certification will be provided through concerned Sector Skill Councils/Gujarat Council for Vocational Training/Kaushalya The Skill University.
<br>

**Payment Milestones** _(as reviewed by Mission Director, Gujarat Skill Development Mission)_

- 30%: After commencement of training batch against valid candidates.
- 40%: After certification of trainees (with respect to passed out trainees).
- 30%: Based on placement results (final payment as per Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana guidelines).

<br>

## Eligibility

- The trainees must be a domicile of Gujarat.
- The trainees must have passed at least 10th Standard / 12th Standard / Industrial Training Institute / Degree-Diploma.
- Trainees must be registered on the portal with Aadhaar number submission by Training Provider.
- Training must be conducted only through trainers as per course guidelines and syllabus.
- On Job Training must be made mandatory as per course requirements, with On Job Training/soft skills to be covered for others.
- The Training Centers must carry branding of the MBKVY scheme.

_*Monitoring will be portal-based and payments will be made based on portal data only._
_*For National Skill Qualification Framework courses, assessment and certification will be done through concerned Sector Skill Councils/Gujarat Council for Vocational Training/Kaushalya The Skill University._ 
_*For new courses based on industry demand, assessment will be conducted as per standards set by Gujarat Council for Vocational Training or Kaushalya The Skill University, with certification provided through Gujarat Council for Vocational Training and Kaushalya The Skill University._
<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process**

**Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://skills.gujarat.gov.in) of Gujarat Skill Development Mission (GSDM). At the top right corner, click "Register". You will be taken to the registration page.
**Step 2:** On the Registration Page, fill in all the mandatory fields: User group, Name of SPOC, Email Address, Mobile Number. If prompted, verify your Email ID and Mobile Number via OTP. Provide the Captcha Code and click "Register".
<br>

> **Application Process**

**Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://skills.gujarat.gov.in/) of Gujarat Skill Development Mission (GSDM). At the top right corner, click "Login". Login using your credentials created during the Registration Process.
**Step 2:** To obtain center accreditation and affiliation for providing training, navigate to the online application form of the scheme. In the form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size.
**Step 3:** Carefully review all the information provided and the documents uploaded. Make any necessary corrections. Finally, submit your application. You'll receive a confirmation message.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum educational qualification required for me to enroll in these skill development training programs?

You must have passed at least Standard 10/12/Industrial Training Institute/Degree-Diploma to be eligible for enrollment in the training courses.
<br>

### Can I participate in these technology-based skill training courses if I am originally from another state but currently residing in Gujarat?

No, you must be a domicile of Gujarat state to be eligible for these skill development training programs under this scheme.
<br>

### What types of courses are available under this skill development program and do they focus on current industry requirements?

The scheme provides short-term courses, digital training, future skill courses and new age courses with quality training according to current technology in manufacturing, service and other sectors.
<br>

### How will I receive certification after completing my training course and which organizations will issue these certificates?

Certification will be provided through concerned Sector Skill Councils/Gujarat Council for Vocational Training/Kaushalya The Skill University depending on the course type.
<br>

### What documents do I need to submit during the application process to prove my eligibility for these training programs?

You need to submit Domicile Certificate of Gujarat State, Educational Certificate (Standard 10/12/Industrial Training Institute/Degree-Diploma), and Aadhaar Card.
<br>

### Is practical training included as part of these skill development courses and how does it align with industry technology requirements?

Yes, the scheme includes practical training as per the latest technology requirements, with On Job Training made mandatory as per course requirements.
<br>

### Where can I register online for these skill development training programs and what is the official website portal?

You can register through the skills.gujarat.gov.in portal, which is the official website of Gujarat Skill Development Mission for scheme registration.
<br>

### What government department is responsible for implementing this skill development program and who oversees its execution?

The scheme is implemented by Gujarat Skill Development Mission under the guidance of Steering Committee and Implementation and Monitoring Committee, Labour, Skill Development and Employment Department.
<br>

### Are there any special provisions or relaxations available for students from government institutions when applying for these training programs?

Yes, government institutions will be given relaxation as per requirement, though specific details of these relaxations are determined by the implementing authority.
<br>

### How are the training costs covered for different types of courses offered under this skill development scheme?

Training costs are directly paid to Training Provider's bank accounts based on Common Skilling Norms for National Skill Qualification Framework courses and industry-demand based determinations.
<br>

### What is the payment structure for training providers and how are payments distributed throughout the training program duration?

Payments are made in milestones: 30% after training commencement, 40% after trainee certification, and 30% based on placement results as per guidelines.
<br>

### Is Aadhaar registration mandatory for enrollment and who is responsible for submitting my Aadhaar details during the application process?

Yes, trainees must be registered on the portal with Aadhaar number submission, and this registration is done by the Training Provider.
<br>

### What additional support is provided to Industrial Training Institutes under this skill development program beyond basic training costs?

Industrial Training Institutes receive infrastructure support including raw material, guest faculty, Training of Trainers, tools equipment, and office expenses coverage under training cost.
<br>

### How are new industry-demand based courses assessed and certified compared to standard National Skill Qualification Framework courses?

For new courses based on industry demand, assessment is conducted as per standards set by Gujarat Council for Vocational Training or Kaushalya The Skill University with certification through these institutions.
<br>
