## Brief Description

The objective of the scheme is to provide facilities to Goan talented students to undergo advanced training in the field of various disciplines of Art, i.e. Indian Classical Music and Dance, Drama, Western Music, Folk Art, Puppetry, Painting, Sculpture, etc.. Scholarship of ₹3,000/- per month.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Scholarship to Undergo Advanced Training in the Field of Art" is a state-level cultural scheme by the Kala Academy, Department of Art and Culture, Government of Goa. The objective of the scheme is to provide facilities to Goan talented students to undergo advanced training in the field of various disciplines of Art, i.e. Indian Classical Music and Dance, Drama, Western Music, Folk Art, Puppetry, Painting, Sculpture, etc..

## Benefits

- Advanced training in the field of various disciplines of Art, i.e. Indian Classical Music and Dance, Drama, Western Music, Folk Art, Puppetry, Painting, Sculpture, etc. 
- Scholarship of ₹3,000/- per month is given, initially for two years and can be extended for another three years.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be from Goa.
- The applicant should be a student.
- The applicant should be in the age group of 16 to 30.

_*A copy of the rules and regulations is available at Kala Academy free of charge._

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applications are invited by releasing an announcement in local newspapers. The announcement consists of the proforma prescribed by the Kala Academy for application to the scholarship scheme.
**Step 2:** In the provided application form, the interested applicant should fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the supporting documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the Member Secretary, Kala Academy, Goa, Campal, Panaji.
**Step 4:** After proper scrutiny, the candidates are interviewed with practical demonstrations in performing arts for the ion. 
_*The Kala Academy reserves the right to the students for advance training._ 
_*Authority for Redressal of Grievances: Member Secretary/Chairman, Kala Academy, Goa._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scholarship program for advanced training in arts?

The scholarship aims to support talented Goan students by providing facilities for advanced training in various art disciplines like Indian Classical Music, Dance, Drama, Western Music, Folk Art, Puppetry, Painting, and Sculpture.

### What is the monthly financial assistance provided under this scholarship scheme?

Selected candidates receive a monthly scholarship amount of ₹3,000/- for their advanced training in the arts.

### For how long is the scholarship initially granted, and can it be extended?

The scholarship is initially awarded for two years, with the possibility of extension for an additional three years.

### What is the age range required to be eligible for this arts scholarship?

Applicants must be between 16 and 30 years of age to qualify for this scholarship.

### Where can I find a copy of the rules and regulations for this scholarship?

The complete rules and regulations are available free of charge at Kala Academy, Goa.

### How are applications for this arts scholarship typically announced?

Applications are invited through announcements published in local newspapers.

### What documents must be submitted along with the scholarship application?

Applicants must submit their birth certificate, certificates of previous training, and other required documents in the prescribed proforma.

### What is the selection process for this advanced arts training scholarship?

After scrutiny of applications, candidates undergo an interview and practical demonstration in their respective performing arts for final selection.

### Can students from outside Goa apply for this scholarship program?

The scholarship is specifically for Goan students, as mentioned in the objective.

### What types of art disciplines are covered under this scholarship scheme?

Eligible disciplines include Indian Classical Music, Dance, Drama, Western Music, Folk Art, Puppetry, Painting, and Sculpture.

### Is there any provision for scholarship renewal after the initial two years?

Yes, the scholarship can be extended for an additional three years after the initial two-year period.

### Do applicants need prior training certificates to be eligible for this scholarship?

Yes, certificates of previous training must be submitted along with the application.

### Who can be contacted for suggestions regarding this scholarship program?

Suggestions can be sent to the Member Secretary, Kala Academy Goa.

### Can applicants in visual arts like painting and sculpture also apply?

Yes, disciplines like Painting and Sculpture are explicitly mentioned as eligible fields.

### Does the scholarship cover additional expenses like materials or travel?

Only the monthly stipend of ₹3,000/- is mentioned, with no additional allowances specified.
