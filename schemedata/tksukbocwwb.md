## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide registered construction workers with a toolkit, including hand gloves and other construction-related tools, worth up to ₹10,000/-.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Toolkit Assistance” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide registered construction workers with a toolkit, including hand gloves and other construction-related tools, worth up to ₹10,000/-.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, registered construction workers are provided with a toolkit worth up to ₹10,000/-.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a construction worker registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online - via CSC

- The online application can be submitted through Shramik Suvidha Kendras. 
  - A self-attested photocopy of the Shramik Card, Aadhaar Card, and Self-Declaration Certificate must be submitted along with the application form. 
  - After departmental verification, the construction worker is provided with a toolkit, which may include hand gloves and other construction-related tools. 
  - Each beneficiary is eligible to receive this benefit only once in their lifetime.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can benefit from this scheme?

Only construction workers registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board are eligible.
<br>

### How much assistance is provided under this scheme?

Eligible beneficiaries receive a toolkit worth up to ₹10,000/-.
<br>

### How can I apply for this scheme?

Applications can be submitted online through Shramik Suvidha Kendras.
<br>

### Is there a limit on the number of times a beneficiary can avail of this scheme?

Yes, each beneficiary can receive the toolkit only once in their lifetime.
<br>

### What happens after submitting the application?

After submission, the department conducts a verification process to confirm eligibility.
<br>

### What items are included in the toolkit?

The toolkit may include hand gloves, construction-related tools, and other related equipment necessary for construction work.
<br>

### Can family members apply on behalf of a construction worker?

No, only the registered construction worker can apply and receive the benefit.
<br>

### Can an unregistered construction worker apply?

No, registration with UKBOCWWB is mandatory to avail of this benefit.
<br>
