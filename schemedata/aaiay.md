## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide residential facilities to homeless families through a registration process and lottery-based allotment.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Indralok Aawasiy Yojana (Group Housing)” is implemented by the Haridwar-Roorkee Development Authority under the Housing Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide residential facilities to homeless families through a registration process and lottery-based allotment.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, residential facilities are provided to homeless families.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

The Authority selects residential units through registration and allotment is done through lottery draw.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

All homeless families who are residents of Uttarakhand are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What is the aim of the scheme?

The scheme aims to provide residential facilities to homeless families through a registration and lottery-based allotment process.
<br>

### How are residential units allocated?

Residential units are allocated through a lottery draw after completing the registration process.
<br>

### Can non-residents of Uttarakhand apply for the scheme?

No.
<br>

### How can eligible families register for the scheme?

Eligible families can register through the official registration process conducted by the Haridwar-Roorkee Development Authority.
<br>

### How are the beneficiaries selected?

Beneficiaries are selected through a transparent lottery draw conducted by the Authority.
<br>
