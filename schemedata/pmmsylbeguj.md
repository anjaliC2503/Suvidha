## Brief Description

The scheme aims to increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs, provide employment to people involved in inland fisheries activities, accelerate inland fish farming activities in freshwater bodies, and boost inland fisheries.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Large Biofloc Establishment (50 tanks 4-meter diameter and 1.5-meter height) Culture System” is a component under the “Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY-Inland)” scheme, implemented by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs, provide employment to people involved in inland fisheries activities, accelerate inland fish farming activities in freshwater bodies, and boost inland fisheries. Under this scheme, subsidies for establishing Biofloc and Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS) are provided to individuals, societies, self-help groups, associations, reservoir lease holders, and private entities involved in inland fisheries activities. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries of the State Governments, with 60% contribution from the Government of India and 40% from the Government of Gujarat.
> **Objective:** 


- Assistance is provided for accelerating inland fish farming activities in freshwater bodies, increasing inland fish production and boosting inland fisheries. 
- Except for water bodies or ponds, in less land, less water, Biofloc with source as well, Recirculating Aquaculture System (RAS). By creating fish tanks with this method, more fish production can be obtained in less land and less water. If more beneficiaries get the benefit of the subsidy of this scheme, more fish production can be achieved with less water sources and less land.
- Inland fish production capacity can be increased by the establishment of freshwater hatcheries.

<br>

## Benefits

The scheme provides financial assistance for the establishment of a Large Biofloc Establishment (50 tanks, 4 meter diameter and 1.5 meter height) culture system. The unit cost is ₹50,00,000/- per unit.

1. **For General Category:** The scale of assistance is 40% of the unit cost or 40% of the actual cost, whichever is less, paid as a subsidy.1. **For Women/Scheduled Caste (S.C.) and Scheduled Tribe (ST):** The scale of assistance is 60% of the unit cost or 40% of the actual cost, whichever is less, paid as a subsidy.
> **Mode of Disbursement:** Assistance is paid through a 60% contribution from the Government of India and a 40% contribution from State Governments.

> **Frequency of Disbursement:** The benefit can be reclaimed once in a lifetime.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be an individual, society, self-help group, association, reservoir lease holder, or private entity involved in inland fisheries activities.
1. The applicant must be above 18 years of age.
1. The applicant must have their own bank account.
1. The applicant must have their own private land or a minimum 10-year lease agreement for land to avail construction-related assistance on land. In case of co-owners, the consent form of the co-owners must be submitted.
1. The applicant must have a Voter ID/Ration Card/Aadhaar Card/Passport as proof of identity.

> **Preference/Priority/Reservation/Relaxation:**


- All categories (General, Women, Scheduled Caste, and Scheduled Tribe) will receive benefits under the Inland Schemes.
- Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe beneficiaries are required to have a Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribe certificate to avail benefits under the inland components.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of setting up 50 Biofloc tanks with specific dimensions?

It is aimed at increasing fish production in areas with limited land and water through a controlled, high-density fish farming method.
<br>

### What is the size of each Biofloc tank supported under the component?

Each tank must have a diameter of 4 meters and a height of 1.5 meters.
<br>

### How many tanks are required for a complete unit under this component?

A total of 50 tanks are required to constitute one Biofloc unit.
<br>

### Is financial assistance available for establishing Biofloc tanks?

Yes, financial assistance is available in the form of a subsidy on the unit or actual cost.
<br>

### How much subsidy is provided to General category beneficiaries?

General category beneficiaries are eligible for a subsidy of 40% of the unit cost or actual cost, whichever is less.
<br>

### What is the subsidy percentage for SC, ST, and Women beneficiaries?

SC, ST, and Women beneficiaries are eligible for 60% of the unit cost or actual cost, whichever is less.
<br>

### Is the subsidy based on the unit cost or actual expenditure?

The subsidy is calculated on either the unit cost or the actual cost, whichever is lower.
<br>

### Can the assistance for setting up Biofloc tanks be availed more than once?

No, the assistance is available only once in a lifetime.
<br>

### Is owning land mandatory for setting up the Biofloc system?

Yes, applicants must have private land or a lease agreement for at least 10 years.
<br>

### What document is needed if the land is co-owned by multiple people?

A consent form signed by all co-owners must be submitted with the application.
<br>

### Can women apply for assistance under the Biofloc tank setup?

Yes, women are eligible and receive a higher subsidy rate of 60%.
<br>

### Are applicants required to be of a certain minimum age?

Yes, applicants must be at least 18 years old.
<br>

### Where can one apply to receive assistance for Biofloc tank construction?

Applications must be submitted through the iKhedut Portal ([https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)).
<br>

### Are any groups or organizations eligible to apply under the scheme?

Yes, societies, self-help groups, associations, and private entities involved in inland fisheries are eligible.
