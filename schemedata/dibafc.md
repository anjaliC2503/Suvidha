## Brief Description

The scheme provides complimentary iron boxes to support individuals from Backward Classes (BC), Most Backward Classes (MBC), and Denotified Communities (DNCs) engaged in washing work, aiming to improve their livelihood.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Distribution of Iron Boxes at Free of Cost&quot; scheme launched by the Backward Classes, Most Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to provide complimentary iron boxes to support individuals from Backward Classes (BC), Most Backward Classes (MBC), and Denotified Communities (DNCs) engaged in washing work.

## Benefits

- Free provision of iron boxes to eligible individuals.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.
   1. The beneficiary should belong to Backward Classes (BC)/ Most Backward Classes (MBC)/ and Denotified Communities (DNCs).
   1. The beneficiary should be engaged in washing work.
   1. Annual income should not exceed ₹40,000/- in rural areas and ₹60,000/- in urban areas.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The interested applicant should collect the application form by contacting the District Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Officer of the concerned District.
**Step-2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step-3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the concerned authority.
**Step-4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme provides complimentary iron boxes to support individuals from Backward Classes (BC), Most Backward Classes (MBC), and Denotified Communities (DNCs) engaged in washing work, aiming to improve their livelihood.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Individuals who are residents of Tamil Nadu, belong to BC, MBC, or DNC communities, are engaged in washing work, and meet the income criteria can apply.

### Is the scheme available for both rural and urban residents?

Yes, the scheme is available for both, with income limits of [?]40,000/- for rural areas and [?]60,000/- for urban areas.

### Where can one apply for the scheme?

Applications can be submitted at the District Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Office.

### What documents are required for the application?

Applicants need to provide community and income certificates, residence proof, and occupation proof.

### Is there an income limit for eligibility?

Yes, annual income should not exceed [?]40,000/- in rural areas and [?]60,000/- in urban areas.

### Is there a fee to apply for the scheme?

No, applying for this scheme is free of cost.

### Can someone reapply if their application is rejected?

Yes, reapplication is possible if the eligibility criteria are met.

### Can individuals not currently engaged in washing work apply?

No, only those actively engaged in washing work are eligible.

### Who can be contacted for more information?

Applicants should reach out to the District Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Officer for further details.
