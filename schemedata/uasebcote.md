## Brief Description

The scheme "Uniform Assistance for Socially and Educationally Backward Class Students Studying Standard 1st to 8th" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to promote educational inclusivity among SEBC students by providing financial assistance of ₹900/- for uniforms.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Uniform Assistance for Socially and Educationally Backward Class Students Studying Standard 1st to 8th&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote education and reduce the economic burden of students from the Socially and Educationally Backward Class by providing them with uniform assistance. The scheme ensures that eligible students from classes 1 to 8 studying in government and aided primary schools receive ₹900/- to cover the cost of three sets of school uniforms.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹900/- is provided to each eligible student for the purchase of three pairs of school uniforms.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a student.
  - The applicant should belong to the state of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be studying in Class 1st to Class 8th.
  - The applicant should be enrolled in a government or government-aided primary school.
  - The applicant must belong to the Socially and Educationally Backward Class under the minority category.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Uniform Assistance for Socially and Educationally Backward Class Students Studying Standard 1st to 8th" scheme?

The scheme "Uniform Assistance for Socially and Educationally Backward Class Students Studying Standard 1st to 8th" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide uniform assistance to SEBC students enrolled in classes 1st to 8th. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹900 for three pairs of uniforms is provided to eligible students studying in government and aided primary schools.
<br>

<br>

### Who can apply for the "Uniform Assistance for Socially and Educationally Backward Class Students Studying Standard 1st to 8th" scheme?

Students enrolled in standards 1 to 8 in government or government-aided primary schools in Gujarat, who belong to the Socially and Educationally Backward Class (SEBC), are eligible to apply under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What financial support is offered through this scheme?

The scheme provides ₹900 per eligible student as monetary support for the purchase of three pairs of school uniforms to help ensure access to proper school attire.
<br>

<br>

### Which class groups are eligible for this benefit?

This scheme is applicable to all SEBC students from standard 1 through standard 8, studying in approved government or aided primary schools across the state.
<br>

<br>

### Are private school students allowed to apply for this scheme?

Students attending private unaided schools are not eligible, as the benefit is limited exclusively to those in government and government-aided institutions.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any financial eligibility limit for families under this scheme?

No income threshold is prescribed, which means all SEBC students in the eligible schools can avail themselves of the benefit regardless of their family’s financial condition.
<br>

<br>

### Which authority is responsible for managing this scheme?

The Social Justice and Empowerment Department of the Government of Gujarat administers and oversees this benefit program across the state.
<br>

<br>

### Is this scheme applicable to children in private schools?

No, the "Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th" scheme is strictly for students enrolled in government or government-aided primary schools; private school students are not covered.

### Does a student need to meet any income-related criteria to be eligible?

There is no income threshold under this scheme, making it open to all eligible minority OBC students, regardless of how much their family earns.

### How does a student or guardian apply for the scheme?

The entire application process is carried out online through the [Digital Gujarat](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/) portal, where applicants can log in, fill in the form, and upload documents without needing to visit any office physically.

### Is there any fee for applying?

No, there is absolutely no cost to apply; the application for the "Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th" scheme is completely free of charge.

### Can girls apply for this scheme too?

Yes, the scheme is gender-inclusive, and girl students are equally eligible; in fact, implementation guidelines often prioritize the participation of girls in such initiatives.

### Is the scheme available to students across all districts of Gujarat?

Yes, the scheme is applicable uniformly across all districts in the state of Gujarat.
