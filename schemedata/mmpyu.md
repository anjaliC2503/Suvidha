## Brief Description

The scheme aims to improve the health of pregnant and lactating women by providing nutritional food such as eggs twice a week and dates twice a week at Anganwadi Centres.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Mukhyamantri Mahila Poshan Yojana&quot; has been launched by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to improve the health of pregnant and lactating women by providing nutritional food such as eggs twice a week and dates twice a week at Anganwadi Centres.
<br>

## Benefits

Under the scheme, pregnant and lactating women are provided eggs two days a week and dates two days a week. Dates are given to those registered women who do not consume eggs.
<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant must be a pregnant or lactating woman.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Women who are registered at the Anganwadi Centre receive the benefits at the centre itself.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

All pregnant and lactating women who are residents of Uttarakhand and registered at an Anganwadi Centre are eligible.
<br>

### What benefits are provided under this scheme?

Eligible women are given eggs two days a week and dates two days a week at Anganwadi Centres.
<br>

### What if a woman does not eat eggs?

Women who do not consume eggs are provided dates as an alternative.
<br>

### Where are the benefits distributed?

The benefits are provided directly at the Anganwadi Centres to registered beneficiaries.
<br>

### Is there any cost for receiving these benefits?

No, the benefits are provided free of cost to eligible women.
<br>

### How can women register for the scheme?

Women need to enroll at the nearest Anganwadi Centre during pregnancy or lactation to avail the benefits.
<br>

### How often are eggs and dates given?

- Eggs: 2 days a week
- Dates: 2 days a week (or as a substitute for eggs if not consumed)

<br>

### Are these benefits available throughout pregnancy and lactation?

Yes, benefits are provided during the entire period of pregnancy and lactation to ensure maternal health.
<br>
