## Brief Description

The scheme aims to help Adivasi farmers and tribal cultivators increase crop production and improve their livelihood. Through this scheme, subsidies are provided for various agricultural inputs and equipment such as seeds, fertilizers, plant protection equipment, and pump sets.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Distribution of Seeds of more productive varieties / Hybrid varieties Seeds and fertilizer at subsidies, etc,. to Adivasi farmers in Tribal Area (TASP)" with a component "100% Soluble Chemical Fertilizer/Nano Fertilizer" is introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to assist Adivasi and Scheduled Tribe farmers in tribal and non-tribal areas to achieve higher agricultural production and improve their livelihoods. Through this scheme, subsidies are provided on items such as seeds of more productive/hybrid varieties, fertilizers, plant protection equipment, tarpaulins, micronutrients, water carrying pipelines, and pump sets. 
<br>

## Benefits

> **100% Soluble Chemical Fertilizer/Nano Fertilizer:**


- 100% soluble chemical fertilizers/nano-fertilizers of FCO-approved grade subject to 75% of the purchase price or ₹ 750/- per hectare, will be eligible for subsidy. (subject to a maximum of four hectares per account)

<br>

> **Remark:** As per conditions No. 5, 16, 17, 19 and 22 of [Appendix-1](https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx). (Refer Scheme No. 35).

> **Frequency:** Every year.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to a Scheduled Tribe.
1. The applicant should be a farmer.
1. The applicant should be located in tribal areas OR be a tribal cultivator residing outside tribal areas.
1. The scheme excludes farmers from Dang district.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Approach the Gram Sevak in your village for access to the "100% Soluble Chemical Fertilizer/Nano Fertilizer" under the scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main aim of this scheme for farmers?

The main aim is to help Adivasi farmers achieve higher agricultural crop production and improve their livelihood.
<br>

### What kind of agricultural products and equipment are provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides seeds of more productive/hybrid varieties, fertilizer, plant protection equipment, tarpaulins, and other agricultural inputs.
<br>

### Are there any other specific components covered besides seeds and fertilizers?

Yes, the scheme also covers field demonstrations, micronutrients, agriculture fairs, water carrying pipelines, and pump sets.
<br>

### What is the assistance for 100% soluble chemical fertilizer or nano-fertilizer?

A subsidy is provided for 100% soluble chemical fertilizers/nano-fertilizers of FCO-approved grade.
<br>

### Is there a maximum limit on the subsidy for 100% soluble chemical fertilizer?

Yes, the subsidy is subject to a maximum of four hectares per account.
<br>

### How often can I claim the benefit for 100% soluble chemical fertilizer/nano-fertilizer?

The benefit for this component can be reclaimed every year.
<br>

### Are there any specific conditions related to the assistance for 100% soluble chemical fertilizer/nano-fertilizer?

Yes, the assistance is provided as per conditions No. 5, 16, 17, 18, 19, and 22 of Appendix-1.
<br>

### Are there any farmers who are not eligible for this scheme?

Yes, scheduled tribe farmers from the Dang district are not eligible for the scheme.
<br>

### Is there a minimum time limit to re-apply for benefits?

For the 100% Soluble Chemical Fertilizer/Nano Fertilizer component, the minimum time limit for reclaiming benefits is every year.
