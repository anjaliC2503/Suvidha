## Brief Description

To motivate the growers to boil the turmeric hygienically using steam boiling unit and to produce quality turmeric suitable for exports

## Detailed Description

The Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom: Post-Harvest Improvement – Turmeric Boiler (Nos.) is implemented by the Spices Board, India, under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. The scheme aims to encourage turmeric growers to adopt hygienic steam boiling units for processing turmeric, thereby improving the quality of the produce and making it suitable for export markets. By promoting modern post-harvest processing practices, the scheme helps enhance product quality, reduce contamination, and increase the export potential of Indian turmeric. The scheme is being implemented in accordance with the modalities and guidelines approved by the Competent Authority under the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

## Benefits

1. 50 % of cost of unit, subject to a maximum of ₹ 1,88,000/- per unit for General Category and 75% subject to maximum of ₹ 2,28,000/- per unit for NE/SC/ST & 90% Spice Producer's Groups in Identified Clusters subjected to maximum of ₹ 3,38,000/-
1. Individual farmers having a minimum of 0.40 ha - 8 ha under turmeric are eligible to benefit under the scheme.
1. Assistance for groups/FPOs will be as in QGBG

<br>

## Eligibility

1.All turmeric growing States and regions/UT in India.
2. Designated Officials will conduct final inspection and recommend the eligible cases.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Turmeric Boiler scheme?

The scheme aims to encourage turmeric growers to adopt hygienic steam boiling technology for processing turmeric. This helps improve the quality of turmeric, enhances its market value, and makes it more suitable for export.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for assistance under this scheme?

Individual farmers cultivating turmeric on land holdings ranging from 0.40 hectare to 8 hectares are eligible to apply for assistance under the scheme.
<br>

### Can Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and farmer groups avail assistance?

Yes. Farmer groups and FPOs engaged in turmeric cultivation are eligible to avail assistance as per the provisions applicable to group beneficiaries under the scheme guidelines.
<br>

### What financial assistance is available for General Category beneficiaries?

General Category beneficiaries are eligible for assistance equal to 50% of the cost of the turmeric boiler unit, subject to a maximum limit of ₹1,88,000 per unit.
<br>

### What financial assistance is available for NE, SC, and ST beneficiaries?

Beneficiaries belonging to the North-Eastern Region, Scheduled Castes, and Scheduled Tribes can receive assistance equal to 75% of the cost of the unit, subject to a maximum limit of ₹2,28,000 per unit.
<br>

### What assistance is available for Spice Producers' Groups in identified clusters?

Spice Producers' Groups operating in identified clusters can receive assistance equal to 90% of the cost of the unit, subject to a maximum limit of ₹3,38,000 per unit.
<br>

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for applying under the scheme?

Yes. Aadhaar is a mandatory document required for identification and processing of the application.
<br>

### In which areas is the scheme implemented?

The scheme is available in all turmeric-growing states, regions, and Union Territories across India.
<br>
