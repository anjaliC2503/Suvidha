## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide an opportunity and exposure to future graduates and postgraduates in Engineering and General Sciences to the research and development activities carried out by DRDO in state-of-the-art technology in fields relevant to the defense sector.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Scheme of Internship of Students to DRDO Labs/Estts for Project Training&quot; has been launched by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), Ministry of Defence. It aims to provide an opportunity and exposure to prospective graduates and postgraduates in Engineering and General Sciences to the research and development activities carried out by DRDO in state-of-the-art technology fields relevant to the defense sector.

## Benefits

1. Internship Training is provided in the areas relevant to DRDO.
   1. Provide opportunity to interns to be associated with projects.

**Note:** DRDO shall not be liable for any compensation in case of personal injury resulting from an accident occurring during the students&#39; attachment to DRDO Labs/Establishments.
***Duration of Training:***

  - The period of training generally is 4 weeks to 6 Months.

**Note:** The duration of training will depend on the type of course and is subject to the discretion of the Lab Director.

## Eligibility

1. Students pursuing undergraduate or postgraduate degrees in Engineering or General Sciences are eligible.
   1. Interns will be permitted access only to UNCLASSIFIED areas of DRDO Labs/Establishments.

**Note 1:** The provisions of the Apprentices Act, 1961, do not apply to this scheme.
**Note 2:** DRDO is under no obligation to offer employment to students upon completion of their training.

## Application Process

### Offline

- Students who are interested can apply through their respective institutes or colleges by corresponding with concerned laboratories or establishments that align with their academic disciplines.
  - Internship training will be permitted subject to available vacancies in labs and approval by the Lab Director.

**Note:** Students are requested to identify the lab as per subject discipline and may apply directly to the concerned lab and not to DRDO HQ, in this regard.

   1. Declaration Form – Stating compliance with DRDO rules and liability disclaimer
   1. Any other documents as required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this internship scheme?

The main objective is to provide prospective graduates and postgraduates exposure to DRDO’s state-of-the-art research and development activities in defence technologies.

### Who is eligible to apply for this internship?

Students pursuing undergraduate or postgraduate degrees in Engineering or General Sciences are eligible.

### Is there any restriction on the type of labs the interns can access?

Yes, interns will be permitted access only to UNCLASSIFIED areas of DRDO Labs/Establishments.

### What is the duration of the internship?

The internship duration generally ranges from 4 weeks to 6 months, depending on the course and at the discretion of the concerned Lab Director.

### Can the internship duration be extended?

Any extension is subject to approval by the Lab Director and availability of resources.

### How can a student apply for the DRDO internship?

Students can apply through their institutes or colleges by directly corresponding with the concerned DRDO laboratories or establishments aligned with their academic discipline.

### Can students apply directly to DRDO HQ for internships?

No, students must apply directly to the relevant DRDO Lab/Estt and not to DRDO HQ.

### Is there any financial compensation offered during the internship?

There is no mention of stipend or compensation. Additionally, DRDO shall not be liable for any compensation in case of personal injury during the internship.

### Is the internship offered under the Apprentices Act, 1961

No, the Apprentices Act, 1961 does not apply to this internship scheme.

### Does this internship guarantee a job at DRDO?

No, DRDO is under no obligation to offer employment after the completion of the internship.

### What disciplines are accepted for this internship?

Students from Engineering and General Sciences disciplines are considered for internship opportunities.
