## Brief Description

The scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" aims to promote natural farming and hybrid seed cultivation through financial assistance to farmers. The scheme provides up to ₹20,000 per hectare to encourage sustainable agricultural practices.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support vegetable farmers in enhancing their cultivation areas using hybrid seeds and to promote natural farming practices. The financial assistance provided under the scheme helps reduce input costs and encourages environmentally sustainable agricultural methods among the farming community.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Subsidy Assistance for Area Expansion Using Hybrid Seeds:** 40% of unit cost or up to ₹20,000/- per hectare, whichever is less.- **Promotion of Natural Farming in Vegetable Crops:** Maximum Assistance: ₹20,000/- per hectare.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be a farmer.


- The applicant should be engaged in vegetable crop cultivation.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed"?

The main objective of the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" is to provide financial assistance to farmers in Gujarat for expanding the area under vegetable cultivation using hybrid seeds and to encourage the adoption of natural farming practices to improve productivity and sustainability.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed"?

Any farmer who resides in Gujarat and is engaged in vegetable cultivation is eligible to apply for the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed."
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How much financial support is provided under the scheme for using hybrid seeds in vegetable farming?

Under the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed," the Government of Gujarat provides a financial subsidy amounting to 40% of the unit cost of ₹50,000 per hectare or a maximum of ₹20,000 per hectare, whichever is less, to eligible farmers.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Does the scheme also support natural farming practices along with hybrid seed cultivation?

Yes, the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" also promotes natural farming by offering financial assistance of up to ₹20,000 per hectare to farmers who adopt chemical-free and eco-friendly farming methods in vegetable cultivation.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" applicable across all districts of Gujarat?

Yes, the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" is implemented across the entire state of Gujarat and is available to eligible farmers from all districts.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Are there any gender or income-related restrictions for applicants under this scheme?

There are no gender-specific or income-based restrictions for applicants, as the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" is open to all farmers irrespective of their gender or income status.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where can farmers apply online for this scheme?

Farmers can apply online for the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" by visiting the [ikhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/iKhedutPublicScheme/) portal, where they must register and fill out the application form with the necessary documents.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the cost of applying to the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed"?

The application process for the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" is completely free of cost.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of land documents must a farmer submit while applying for the scheme?

While applying for the "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" scheme, the farmer must submit the 7/12 certificate, which serves as proof of land ownership or cultivation rights for the proposed area under vegetable farming.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents are necessary to apply under this scheme?

The documents required to apply under the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion of Vegetable Crops through Hybrid Seed" include the Aadhaar Card for identity proof, 7/12 Certificate for land ownership, and a bank passbook or cancelled cheque to facilitate direct benefit transfer.
<br>
