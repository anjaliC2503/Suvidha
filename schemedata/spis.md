## Brief Description

The scheme "Sero Positive Illness Scheme" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat aims to provide educational scholarships to orphaned and destitute children affected by Sero Positive illness.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 2009, the scheme &quot;Sero Positive Illness Scheme&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat aims to support the education of children affected by or born into families impacted by Sero Positive illness. Through this scheme, financial scholarships and support for school uniform, books, tuition fees, food, and hostel expenses are provided to destitute and orphaned children across education levels—from primary school to postgraduate studies. The scheme is implemented by the Director, Social Security Department, Gujarat State, Gandhinagar, and at the district level, by the District Social Security Officer or District Child Protection Officer.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a child affected by Sero Positive illness or should have one or both parents who are Sero Positive.
1. The applicant should be a student.
1. The applicant should be studying in any following standard:



  - Standard 1 to 5.
  - Standard 6 to 10.
  - Higher Secondary Class-11 and 12.
  - B.A./B.Com./B.AC. and equivalent degree courses.
  - Degree courses like B.E., B.Tech., LLB., MBA and equivalent courses as well as Diploma courses in Medical and Ayurvedic and Homeopathic courses.
  - Postgraduate level courses like M.Sc./ M.Phil./ LLM./ M.Ed./ M.B.A. etc.


<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the office of the District Social Security Officer or the District Child Protection Officer and request a hard copy of the prescribed application form for the Sero Positive Illness Scheme.
**Step 2:** In the application form, the applicant should provide all the mandatory details and attach self-attested copies of all the required documents, including Aadhaar Card, Birth Certificate, Civil Surgeon’s Certificate, and other supporting documents.
**Step 3:** The applicant should submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the District Social Security Office or District Child Protection Office.
**Step 4:** The applicant should request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted for future reference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Sero Positive Illness Scheme"?

The scheme "Sero Positive Illness Scheme" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat aims to provide educational scholarships to orphaned and destitute children affected by Sero Positive illness. Through this scheme, financial aid is offered to eligible students from primary to postgraduate levels.
<br>

<br>

### When was the "Sero Positive Illness Scheme" launched and by whom?

The "Sero Positive Illness Scheme" was launched in the year 2009 by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department under the Government of Gujarat with the aim of supporting the educational needs of orphaned and destitute children affected by Sero Positive illness.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the Sero Positive Illness Scheme?

A student is eligible to apply for the Sero Positive Illness Scheme if they are Sero Positive themselves or if one or both of their parents are Sero Positive, provided the child is pursuing education in any recognized institution within the state of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### What type of financial support is provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides financial support in the form of scholarships that cover school uniform expenses, tuition fees, costs of books, and in the case of hostellers, also includes food and hostel charges depending on the level of education.
<br>

<br>

### Is this scheme available for students pursuing higher secondary or college-level education?

Yes, the scheme is available for students studying at higher secondary, undergraduate, professional, and even postgraduate levels, with varying scholarship amounts based on the student's course and whether they are day scholars or hostellers.
<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the "Sero Positive Illness Scheme"?

To apply for the scheme, the applicant must submit several documents including their Aadhaar Card, Birth Certificate, latest Mark Sheet, Ration Card, School Leaving Certificate (if applicable), and a Certificate from a Civil Surgeon verifying the Sero Positive status.
<br>

<br>

### Where should the application form be submitted?

The completed and signed application form, along with the necessary documents, must be submitted to the office of the District Social Security Officer or the District Child Protection Officer in the applicant’s home district.
<br>

<br>

### How are the applications for the scheme reviewed and approved?

After submission, each application is reviewed by the Sponsorship and Approval Committee (SFCAC) which operates under the chairmanship of the District Social Security Officer, and only after their approval is the scholarship sanctioned and disbursed.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit to apply for the scheme?

No, there is no individual or family income limit specified for this scheme, making it accessible to all children who meet the health-related and educational eligibility criteria regardless of their financial background.
<br>

<br>

### Can both boys and girls apply for the scheme?

Yes, the scheme is open to all genders without any restrictions, and both boys and girls who fulfill the eligibility conditions are equally entitled to apply and receive the scholarship benefits.
<br>

<br>

### Can students from all over Gujarat apply for this scheme?

Yes, the scheme is applicable across the entire state of Gujarat, and students residing in any district of the state can apply through their respective district offices.
<br>

<br>

### Where can applicants seek help or more information about the scheme?

Applicants or their guardians can approach the office of the District Social Security Officer or the District Child Protection Officer for any assistance, clarification, or guidance regarding the application process and benefits of the "Sero Positive Illness Scheme".
<br>
