## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support Socially and Educationally Backward Class (SEBC) and Economically Backward Class (EBC) students with coaching fee assistance. Up to ₹20,000 or actual fee is provided for one exam preparation.

## Detailed Description

The “Coaching Sahay for the Preparation of NEET, JEE, GUJCET Examination to both Socially and Educationally Backward Class Trainees/Students and Female Students and Economically Backward Class Students” scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to provide financial aid to underprivileged students for coaching support. Under this scheme, students from SEBC or Economically Backward Class categories receive coaching fee (whichever is lower) for one exam-NEET, JEE, or GUJCET. 
<br>

## Benefits

**Financial Assistance:** 

- ₹20,000/- or actual coaching fee paid, whichever is lower.

**Applicable Exams:** 

- NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) 
- JEE (Joint Entrance Examination)
- GUJCET (Gujarat Common Entrance Test)

**Mode of Disbursement:**

- Amount is credited via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the student’s bank account.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a student.
1. The applicant must be from the SEBC or Economically Backward Class category.
1. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
1. The applicant must have passed the Secondary School Certificate (SSC/Class 10).
1. The applicant must be enrolled in or preparing for NEET, JEE, or GUJCET.
1. The applicant must have paid a coaching fee and be able to submit a valid receipt.
1. The applicant’s annual family income must not exceed ₹6,00,000/-.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 01:** The eligible applicant may visit the e-Samaj Kalyan Portal: [https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 02:** On the home page, under the tab ‘Citizen Login’, click on New User ‘[Please Register Here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/Registration.aspx)’.
**Step 03:** Enter your Full Name as per Aadhaar Card, Gender, Date of Birth, Aadhaar Card Number, Email ID, Caste etc. and then click on ‘Register’.
**Step 04:** After successful registration, the applicants can login through their User ID and Password.
**Step 05:** Now, click on ‘User Profile’ to update your profile.
**Step 06:** Fill in all the mandatory information and then click on ‘Update’. 
**Step 07:** After updating the profile successfully, select the scheme appearing on the home page; an application form will open.
**Step 08:** Fill in all the mandatory details in the application form and upload all the relevant documents.
**Step 09:** Now agree with the terms &amp; conditions and click on ‘Save Application’.
**Step 10:** Submit the application form and take out the print of the application for future reference. The applicant can note down the application number to track the application status by logging into the portal using their User ID and Password.
[User Manual](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/HelpManual/Citizen-HelpManual.pdf)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Students from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC) or Economically Backward Classes (EBC), residing in Gujarat.
<br>

### What exams are covered?

The scheme covers NEET, JEE, and GUJCET exam preparation.
<br>

### How much financial assistance is given?

Up to ₹20,000 or the actual fee paid for coaching, whichever is lower.
<br>

### What educational qualification is required?

The applicant must have passed Class 10 (SSC).
<br>

### How is the benefit amount disbursed?

The amount is transferred through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the applicant’s bank account.
<br>

### Can students outside Gujarat apply?

No, only permanent residents of Gujarat are eligible.
<br>

### Can I apply offline?

No, the application must be submitted online
<br>

### What documents are required?

Aadhaar card, caste certificate, income certificate, SSC mark sheet, bank details, age proof, and fee receipt.
<br>

### Is there any application fee?

No, applying under this scheme is completely free.
<br>

### Where can I get more information?

Visit https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/ddcw or the eSamajKalyan portal https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in for details.
