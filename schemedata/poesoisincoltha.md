## Brief Description

To promote the cultivation of Lakadong turmeric for exports by providing 50% of the cost of planting material, subject to a maximum of ₹ 30,000 per Ha, to turmeric growers of the North East having land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Promotion of Exportable Surplus of Identified Spices in the North East: Cultivation of Lakadong Turmeric (Ha)” under the Spices Board, India, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India, aims to promote the cultivation of Lakadong turmeric for exports by providing 50% of the cost of planting material, subject to a maximum of  ₹30,000 per Ha, to turmeric growers of the North East having land holdings of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha, as per the modalities and scheme guidelines approved by the Competent Authority for implementation of the “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom” during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26), under the component Export Oriented Production.
<br>

## Benefits

1. 50% of cost of planting material subject to maximum of ₹ 30,000 per Ha
1. Turmeric growers of NE having land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicable to turmeric growers in Sikkim, Darjeeling & Kalimpong district of West Bengal, and other North Eastern States.
1. Eligible cases will be finalized based on inspection and recommendation by designated officials.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to promote the cultivation of Lakadong turmeric for exports.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Turmeric growers of the North East having land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha are eligible.
<br>

### Which areas are covered under this scheme?

The scheme is applicable in Sikkim, Darjeeling & Kalimpong district of West Bengal, and other North Eastern States.
<br>

### What financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

50% of the cost of planting material, subject to a maximum of ₹ 30,000 per Ha.
<br>

### What is the minimum land holding required?

The minimum land holding required is 0.10 Ha.
<br>

### What is the maximum land holding allowed under the scheme?

The maximum land holding allowed is 8 Ha.
<br>

### Is any additional document required if land is not in the applicant’s name?

Yes, a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Panchayat is required.
<br>

### How are beneficiaries selected under the scheme?

Designated officials conduct final inspection and recommend eligible cases.
<br>

### What is the basis of financial assistance calculation?

It is calculated as 50% of the cost of planting material.
