## Brief Description

The objective of this scheme is to implement various rehabilitation programs for persons with disabilities through the Community-Based Rehabilitation Worker (CBRW) network and to support their rehabilitation by providing assistive devices.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;National Programme for Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities&quot; has been launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Himachal Pradesh. The programme implements various rehabilitation schemes for persons with disabilities through the Community-Based Rehabilitation Worker (CBRW) network and supports their rehabilitation by providing assistive devices.
<br>

## Benefits

- Identifies persons with disabilities and supports their treatment.
- Provides therapy services such as physiotherapy and occupational therapy.
- Rehabilitates persons with disabilities by providing assistive devices like artificial limbs, hearing aids, and wheelchairs.
- Offers guidance for education, training, and job skills.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a person with disability.
1. The applicant should be a resident of Himachal Pradesh.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

> Applicants can apply offline by contacting the Project Officer, District Rural Development Agency, or the District Welfare Officer in Kangra or Hamirpur.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the National Programme for Rehabilitation of Persons with Disabilities?

It is a government programme that provides rehabilitation services and support to persons with disabilities.
<br>

### Who implements this programme in Himachal Pradesh?

The programme is implemented by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment through District Rehabilitation Centres in Hamirpur and Kangra.
<br>

### What is the objective of this programme?

The objective is to rehabilitate persons with disabilities through various schemes using the Community-Based Rehabilitation Worker (CBRW) network.
<br>

### What kind of assistance is provided under this programme?

The programme provides assistive devices and rehabilitation support to persons with disabilities.
<br>

### What services are offered at District Rehabilitation Centres?

The centres provide identification, prevention, treatment, therapy services, and rehabilitation support.
<br>

### What specialist services are available under this programme?

Specialist services include physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and audiological assessment.
<br>

### Are assistive aids and appliances provided under this scheme?

Yes, assistive devices such as artificial limbs, hearing aids, wheelchairs, and other appliances are provided.
<br>

### Does the programme support education and skill development?

Yes, counselling is provided for education, training, and vocational skill development.
<br>
