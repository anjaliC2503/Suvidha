## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial assistance for the marriage of a registered female worker herself (including one-time remarriage) and for up to two daughters of a registered worker. The scheme applies to the marriage of the female beneficiary herself and the legal, adopted, or stepdaughter of worker.

## Detailed Description

"Marriage Assistance Scheme" by the Madhya Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Department of Labour, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides financial assistance for the marriage of a registered female worker herself (including one-time remarriage) and for up to two daughters of a registered worker. The scheme applies to the marriage of the female beneficiary herself or the legal, adopted, or stepdaughter of a registered construction worker, provided that the bride is at least 18 years old at the time of marriage.

## Benefits

- ₹55,000/- Total Assistance
- ₹11,000/- Cash Support
- ₹38,000/- For Materials
- ₹6,000/- For Mass Marriage Organizer

## Eligibility

**For Registration**

- The applicant should be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.
- The applicant should be a Building/ Construction Worker.
- The applicant should be between 18 and 60 years.
- The applicant should have engaged in any building or other construction work for at least 90 days during the preceding 12 months.
- The registration of the applicant should be renewed annually.

**For Application**

- The applicant should be a registered female worker or the daughter of a registered worker.
- The applicant should participate in the "Chief Minister Kanya Vivaah/Nikah Scheme".
- The bride should be at least 18 years old.
- The applicant should not have received similar assistance before.

## Application Process

### Online

> **For Registration**

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the prescribed authority mentioned below. 
For Rural Area: Chief Executive Officer, Janpad Panchayat
For Urban Area: Commissioner/Chief Municipal Officer, Urban Bodies
**Step 2:** Registration can be done by paying a cash payment of Rs. 15/- (Rs. 5/- as registration fee and Rs. 10 as contribution for five years) to the prescribed authority in the prescribed form for five years.
**Step 3:** The receipt of fee receipt is given to the applicant by the prescribed authority. The approval / rejection of the application and accordingly the registration order is decided by the prescribed authority.
**Step 4:** The process of registration of labour is done through the departmental portal https://labour.mp.gov.in/.
**Step 5:** On completion of the online registration process, the identity card with the photo received from the portal is issued under the signature of the prescribed authority.
_*Arrangements have been made to register the construction worker on the basis of self-certification as a proof of 90 days of work in a year._
<br>

> **For Application**

**Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://labour.mp.gov.in/Default.aspx) of the &quot;Shram Seva Portal&quot;. In the top ribbon, click &quot; योजना आवेदन&quot;, then click &quot; [योजना लाभ हेतु आवेदन दर्ज करें](https://labour.mp.gov.in/Public/Application/RegsiterSchemeApplication.aspx)&quot;.
**Step 2:** On the next screen, provide your &quot;श्रमिक पंजीयन क्रमांक (पोर्टल कोड)&quot; (Labour Registration Number), fill in the Captcha Code, and click &quot;View Information&quot;.
**Step 3:** On the next screen, a list of the Board&#39;s schemes will be displayed. Click on the name of the scheme that you intend to apply for. The online application form for the scheme will open.
**Step 4:** In the form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size.
Carefully review all the information provided and the documents uploaded. Make any necessary corrections.
**Step 5:** Click &quot;Submit&quot; to submit your application. You&#39;ll receive a confirmation message.

## Documents Required

> **For Registration**


- Copy of the Composite ID.
- Copy of the First Page of the Passbook of the Applicant's Own Bank Account.
- 02 Passport-size Photographs of the Applicant.
- Proof of Residence (Voter ID/license/Electricity Bill/Ration Card/aadhaar Card).
- Self-attested Signed Proof/Thumb Impression Certificate Regarding 90 Days' Employment.

> **For Application**


- Identity Card of the Registered Worker
- Bank Account Details
- Proof of Residence (e.g., Utility Bill, Voter ID, etc.)
- Marriage Invitation Card (original)
- Proof of Relationship with the Registered Worker
- Age Proof of the Bride

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum number of daughters for whom a registered worker can claim benefits?

A registered worker can receive assistance for the marriage of up to two daughters under this scheme.

### What is the total financial assistance provided under this marriage support scheme?

The total assistance is ₹55,000/-, which includes ₹11,000/- in cash, ₹38,000/- for materials, and ₹6,000/- for mass marriage organizers.

### What is the minimum age requirement for the bride to qualify for assistance?

The bride must be at least 18 years old at the time of marriage to be eligible for the scheme.

### Can a registered female worker receive assistance for her own remarriage?

Yes, a registered female worker can receive assistance for one-time remarriage under this scheme.

### Is there any restriction on receiving similar assistance from other schemes?

The applicant should not have received similar financial assistance from any other scheme before applying.

### What is the age range required for a construction worker to register under this welfare board?

The applicant must be between 18 and 60 years old at the time of registration.

### How many days of construction work must an applicant have completed to be eligible for registration?

The applicant must have worked in building or construction for at least 90 days in the last 12 months.

### Is there a renewal requirement for registered workers to remain eligible?

Yes, the registration must be renewed annually to maintain eligibility for benefits.

### Where should rural applicants go to register under this scheme?

Rural applicants must visit the Chief Executive Officer of the Janpad Panchayat for registration.

### Where should urban applicants submit their registration application?

Urban applicants should approach the Commissioner or Chief Municipal Officer of their local urban body.

### What is the registration fee for construction workers under this scheme?

The registration fee is ₹15/-, which includes ₹5/- as a registration fee and ₹10/- as a five-year contribution.

### Is self-certification accepted as proof of 90 days of work for registration?

Yes, self-certification is sufficient as proof of 90 days of employment in a year for registration.

### What information is needed to access the online application form for benefits?

The applicant must enter their Labour Registration Number (Portal Code) and a captcha code to proceed.

### How can an applicant select the correct scheme while filling out the online form?

After entering registration details, a list of schemes appears, and the applicant must click on the relevant scheme name.

### What documents are needed for registration as a construction worker?

Required documents include a Composite ID copy, bank passbook first page, two passport photos, residence proof, and a self-certified 90-day employment proof.

### Is an Aadhaar card acceptable as proof of residence for registration?

Yes, an Aadhaar card is accepted as valid residence proof along with other documents like a Voter ID or utility bill.
