## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage inter-caste marriages in Assam. It provides a one-time financial assistance of ₹2,50,000/- in a joint fixed deposit for eligible couples where one spouse belongs to a Scheduled Caste and the other to a General Caste Hindu.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Financial Incentive For Inter-Caste Marriages Under Protection Of Civil Rights Act 1955 And Scheduled Castes And Scheduled Tribes (Prevention Of Atrocities) Act 1989 In Assam" was launched by the Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Assam. It aims to financially support eligible inter-caste married couples. The scheme provides a one-time financial assistance of ₹2,50,000/- in a fixed deposit, jointly in the name of the couple, with the first name being of the woman, for a strict lock-in period of three years without the facility for its premature encashment. The applicant must submit the completely filled application form along with the mandatory self-attested documents. The scheme is officially implemented by the Director, Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Assam.
<br>

## Benefits

- A one-time financial assistance of ₹2,50,000/- is provided to the eligible inter-caste marriage couple.
- The financial assistance is granted directly in the form of a fixed deposit.
- The fixed deposit is opened jointly in the name of the couple in a Government or Nationalized bank.

***Conditions***
_*The opening of joint accounts for the eligible inter-caste marriage couple will be initiated by the_ ***Office of the Director Welfare Of Scheduled Castes And Backward Classes, Assam***_._
_*The first name on the joint fixed deposit account must exclusively be that of the woman._
_*The financial incentives provided are strictly subject to the availability of budget provision and the sanction of funds from the Government._
_*The joint fixed deposit has a mandatory lock-in period of three years._
_*There is absolutely no facility permitted for the premature encashment of the fixed deposit amount during the lock-in period._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a spouse in an inter-caste marriage.
- The applicant must ensure that one spouse of the inter-caste marriage belongs to the Scheduled Caste.
- The applicant must ensure that the other spouse of the inter-caste marriage belongs to the General Caste Hindu.
- The applicant must have had their marriage officially performed between 1st January 2025 and 31st January 2025.
- The applicant must be applying for their first marriage, as there will be no incentive provided for a second or subsequent marriage.
- The applicant must have a combined annual income of the couple from all sources of income of ₹5,00,000/- and below.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format ](https://directorwsc.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/public_utility/INTER%20CASTE%20MARRIAGE%20APPLICATION%284%29%20%284%29.pdf)of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Both the bridegroom and the bride must personally sign the declaration section at the end of the application form to authenticate all the details provided. This acts as a formal and legally binding certification that all the furnished particulars are accurate and true.
**Step 4:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the ***Office of the Director, Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Assam***.

## Documents Required

- Self-Attested Digital Caste Certificate of the Husband or Wife Issued by the Deputy Commissioner.
- Self-Attested Proof of General Caste of the Husband or Wife in the Form of an Affidavit from a Notary.
- Self-Attested Marriage Certificate Issued by the Office of the Sub-Registrar.
- Self-Attested Marriage Certificate from a Gazetted Officer Confirming that It Is the Couple's First Marriage.
- Self-Attested Digital Income Certificate of the Couple from the Concerned Circle Officer (In Case the Person or Persons Are Unemployed).
- Self-Attested Salary Statement Signed by the Employer (In Case of Private Sector Employees).
- Self-Attested Salary Statement Generated in Fin Assam (In Case of Government Employees).
- Self-Attested Affidavit from a Notary Stating that the Wife Is a Homemaker Without any Income Source (If Applicable).
- Self-Attested Aadhaar Card of the Couple as Identity Proof.
- Self-Attested Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card of the Couple as Identity Proof.
- Self-Attested Voter Identity Card, Driving Licence, or Ration Card as Residence Proof of the Couple.
- Self-Attested Joint Photograph of the Couple.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I want to apply for this financial assistance, but my partner and I both work; is there a specific maximum annual income limit we must stay under to qualify?

The combined annual earnings of the couple from all possible sources must be strictly ₹5,00,000/- and below to be considered eligible.
<br>

### My spouse belongs to the general category, so I am wondering exactly what official document needs to be submitted to properly prove their caste status during the application process?

You must provide proof of the general caste status by submitting an official affidavit obtained directly from a Notary.
<br>

### When the financial assistance is finally granted and the fixed deposit account is created for us, whose name needs to appear first on that joint bank account?

The fixed deposit will be opened jointly, but it is a mandatory requirement that the first name on the account must exclusively be that of the woman.
<br>

### I was previously married and divorced before marrying my current partner; are we still eligible to receive this financial support if this is my second marriage but their first?

No, you are not eligible, as the financial incentive is explicitly not provided for any second or subsequent marriages.
<br>

### As one of us belongs to a reserved category, which specific government authority is responsible for issuing the required digital caste certificate that we need to submit?

The required digital caste certificate for the relevant spouse must be officially issued by the Deputy Commissioner.
<br>

### Neither my spouse nor I currently hold a job, so we do not receive a salary statement; what alternative document must we provide to verify our annual income?

If the applicants are unemployed, they must submit a digital income certificate for the couple issued by the concerned Circle Officer.
<br>

### Since I currently work as an employee for the state government, what precise format or system must my salary statement be generated from to be accepted as valid proof?

Government employees are required to submit a salary statement that has been specifically generated through the Fin Assam system.
<br>

### After the financial assistance is successfully deposited into our joint bank account, are we allowed to withdraw those funds early if we face a sudden financial emergency?

No, the funds are placed in a fixed deposit with a strict lock-in period of three years, and there is absolutely no facility permitted for premature encashment.
<br>

### My wife completely manages our household full-time and does not earn a separate salary; do we need to submit any special legal document declaring her current employment status?

Yes, if the wife is a homemaker without any income source, an affidavit from a Notary stating this fact must be submitted.
<br>

### What are the exact caste requirements for the two partners involved in the marriage to ensure that our application for this financial incentive is not immediately rejected?

One spouse in the marriage must belong to the Scheduled Caste, while the other spouse must belong to the General Caste Hindu community.
<br>

### We are preparing our paperwork for submission and need to confirm which specific national identity cards are mandatorily required to serve as our primary identification proof?

You must enclose copies of both the Aadhaar card and the Permanent Account Number card for the couple as proof of identity.
<br>

### How exactly does the government verify that this union is indeed the very first marriage for both of us, and what specific official's certification is required for this?

You are required to provide a marriage certificate issued by a Gazetted Officer, which formally confirms that it is the couple's first marriage.
<br>

### Aside from our basic identification cards, what other officially recognized documents can we submit to properly establish our current residential address for the application process?

As valid proof of residence for the couple, you may submit either a Voter card, a Driving licence, or a Ration Card.
<br>
