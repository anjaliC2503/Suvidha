## Brief Description

The scheme component "Harvesting Tools" launched under the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm" scheme aims to increase the production of oil palm and reduce its import by providing subsidies for various harvesting tools to farmers in the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme component &quot;Harvesting Tools&quot; launched under the &quot;National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm&quot; scheme by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote oil palm cultivation by increasing production in the state and reducing dependency on imported oil palm. The scheme provides financial assistance to farmers for purchasing harvesting tools and equipment required for efficient oil palm cultivation.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Cutter:** ₹2,500/- per unit.- **Wire Mesh:** ₹20,000/- per unit.- **Motorized Chisel:** ₹15,000/- per unit.- **Portable Ladder/Polls:** ₹5,000/- per unit.- **Chaff Cutter:** ₹50,000/- per unit.- **Tractor Trolley:** ₹2,00,000/- per unit.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a farmer.
  - The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component under the National Mission on Edible Oil – Oil Palm?

The main purpose of the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component is to enhance the production of oil palm in Gujarat and reduce dependency on imported oil palm by providing farmers with financial assistance for purchasing essential harvesting tools and equipment required for oil palm cultivation.
<br>

### Which government department is responsible for implementing the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component?

The "Harvesting Tools" scheme component is implemented by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat, which ensures that eligible farmers receive subsidies for purchasing the specified tools and equipment in accordance with the scheme guidelines.
<br>

### Who can apply for the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component?

Any individual farmer who possesses valid land records in their name and is engaged in farming activities is eligible to apply for the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component as per the stated eligibility criteria.
<br>

### What type of support is offered to beneficiaries under the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component?

Under the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component, financial support is provided to farmers in the form of subsidies for purchasing various tools and machinery such as cutters, wire mash, motorized chisels, portable ladders or polls, chaff cutters, and tractor trolleys.
<br>

### How much financial assistance is provided for purchasing a cutter under the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component?

The "Harvesting Tools" scheme component provides a subsidy amount of ₹2,500/- per unit for the purchase of a cutter, which is one of the essential tools for harvesting oil palm effectively.
<br>

### What is the subsidy amount for purchasing a wire mash under the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component?

The subsidy amount provided under the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component for purchasing a wire mesh is ₹20,000/- per unit, which helps farmers acquire this equipment at a reduced cost.
<br>

### How much assistance is provided for a motorized chisel under the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component?

The "Harvesting Tools" scheme component provides a subsidy of ₹15,000 per unit for purchasing a motorized chisel, enabling farmers to invest in mechanized harvesting tools for increased efficiency.
<br>

### What is the financial assistance available for a portable ladder or polls under the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component?

Under the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component, farmers are eligible for a subsidy of ₹5,000/- per unit for purchasing a portable ladder or polls, which are vital for safe and effective harvesting of oil palm bunches.
<br>

### Can a farmer receive benefits from the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component more than once?

No, the benefit under the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component can only be availed once per account, meaning that a farmer cannot apply for the same subsidy multiple times.
<br>

### How can eligible farmers apply for the scheme?

Eligible farmers can apply for the scheme by visiting the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, filling out the application form online, uploading the required documents, and submitting it for departmental verification.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the "Harvesting Tools" scheme component?

Applicants are required to submit their Aadhaar Card, Land Records 7-12 and 8-A, Bank Passbook or Cancelled Cheque, and if applicable, a Caste Certificate or Disability Certificate issued by the competent authority.
<br>
