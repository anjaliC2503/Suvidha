## Brief Description

Launched in 1976-77, the "Post Matric Students of Class 11th &12th " scheme is a welfare initiative by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides a facility for the reimbursement of scholarship fees to students.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 1976-77, the "Post Matric Students of Class 11th & 12th" scheme is a welfare initiative by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides reimbursement of scholarship fees to students in Class 11th and 12th. It aims to encourage students from the Scheduled Caste category in the field of education and provide them with financial assistance.

## Benefits

The facility of reimbursement of scholarship fee is provided to the students of class 11th and 12th.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.
1. The student should be enrolled in Class 11th or 12th in a recognized educational institution.
1. The student should provide a valid income certificate of the family, confirming that the annual income is below the prescribed limit.
1. The guardian should provide a declaration stating that there has been no increase in the family income for the last financial year.

## Application Process

### Online

**Registration Process:**
**Step1:** Go to the official [State Scholarship Portal 2.0](https://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx) for Madhya Pradesh.
**Step2:** Scroll down the page and find the "Student Corner" section on the left side of the screen.
**Step3:** Under the Student Corner, click on the “Student Registration” option.
**Step4:** Complete the online registration form by filling in all mandatory fields and uploading the required documents in the specified format and size.
**Step5:** Carefully review the details entered and the documents uploaded. Once you have verified everything, click on the "Submit" button.
**Step6:** After submitting, you will receive your registration number on the registered email address or mobile number.
<br>

**Application Process:**
**Step1:** Go to the [State Scholarship Portal 2.0](https://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx) for Madhya Pradesh.
**Step2:** Scroll down to the "Student Corner" section on the left and click on "Student Login." Enter your User ID and Password to log in.
**Step3:** After logging in, a dashboard will appear in front of you. From your dashboard, select the scheme you wish to apply for and click on it.
**Step4:** Complete the application form by filling in all required fields and uploading necessary documents in the specified format and size.
**Step5:** Double-check the information and documents provided, then click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Post Matric Students of Class 11th & 12th" scheme?

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students of the Scheduled Caste category studying in Class 11th and 12th to encourage their education.

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Students belonging to the Scheduled Caste category, studying in Class 11th and 12th in recognized educational institutions, are eligible to apply.

### What is the amount provided under the scheme?

The scheme offers reimbursement of scholarship fees, and students receive financial assistance for their studies.

### Where can I apply for the scheme?

Applicants can apply through the State Scholarship Portal 2.0, Madhya Pradesh.

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme?

Aadhaar card, caste certificate, income certificate, bank account details, educational documents, and passport-sized photograph are required.

### How do I register for the scheme?

Visit the State Scholarship Portal 2.0, go to the Student Corner, click on "Student Registration," and fill in the required details.

### Is there an application fee for the scheme?

No, there is no application fee for this scheme.

### Can I apply for the scheme offline?

No, the application process for this scheme is online through the State Scholarship Portal 2.0.

### How will I receive the scholarship amount?

The scholarship amount will be credited directly to the applicant's bank account.

### What is the last date for applying for the scheme?

The last date for applying varies each year, so applicants should check the State Scholarship Portal for updated deadlines.
