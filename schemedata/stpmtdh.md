## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote mechanization for the development of horticulture by providing subsidies to farmers for purchasing modern agricultural machinery used in horticultural crops.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Scheme to Promote Mechanization for the Development of Horticulture&quot; launched by the Horticulture and Food Processing Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to encourage mechanization in horticulture through providing subsidies on various modern machines to eligible farmers.
<br>

## Benefits

<br>

MachineryMaximum Subsidy (₹)Potato Planter/Digger30,000Garlic/Onion Planter/Digger30,000Tractor Mounted Agri-Blast Sprayer75,000Power Operated Pruning Machine20,000Fogging Machine10,000Mulch Laying Machine30,000Power Tiller75,000Power Weeder50,000Tractor with Rotavator (Up to 20 H.P.)1,50,000Onion/Garlic Marker500Post Hole Digger50,000Tree Pruner45,000Plant Hedge Trimmer35,000Mist Blower30,000Power Spray Pump25,000<br>

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a farmer.
  - The applicant must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The applicant should contact the District Office of the Horticulture Department.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The objective of this scheme is to encourage farmers to use modern machines in horticultural crops by providing subsidies.
<br>

### Which department implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Horticulture and Food Processing Department, Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Who can apply for this scheme?

All farmers are eligible to apply for this scheme.
<br>

### What percentage of subsidy is provided under this scheme?

A subsidy of 50% of the unit cost is provided.
<br>

### What is the maximum subsidy amount for a potato planter or digger?

The maximum subsidy amount is ₹30,000.
<br>

### What is the subsidy for a power tiller?

The subsidy for a power tiller is ₹75,000.
<br>

### What is the maximum subsidy available for a tractor with rotavator?

The maximum subsidy available is ₹1,50,000.
<br>

### How can farmers apply for the scheme benefits?

Farmers can contact the District Office of the Horticulture Department.
<br>

### What is the benefit of using mechanized horticultural equipment?

Mechanized equipment helps in improving efficiency and productivity in horticultural farming.
<br>

### Where should the applicant contact for more information?

The applicant should contact the District Office of the Horticulture Department, Madhya Pradesh.
<br>
