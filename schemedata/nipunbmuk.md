## Brief Description

The scheme aims to ensure that all children aged 3 to 9 achieve foundational literacy and numeracy by creating an enabling learning environment across all schools in Uttarakhand.

## Detailed Description

The “NIPUN Bharat Mission” is a scheme under the Samagra Shiksha Programme, implemented by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to ensure that all children aged 3 to 9 achieve foundational literacy and numeracy by creating an enabling learning environment across all schools in Uttarakhand.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this mission, all children can get admission in Class 1 as per the admission norms of all schools in the state.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiary must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
   1. All children aged 3 to 9 years are eligible to benefit from the scheme to achieve foundational literacy and numeracy.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the Mission?

### Who are the beneficiaries of the mission?

All children in the age group of 3 to 9 years are the beneficiaries.

### Can children get admission in Class 1 under this mission?

Yes, all eligible children can get admission in Class 1 as per the admission norms of all schools in the state.
<br>

### Is the mission applicable to all schools in Uttarakhand?

Yes, the mission is implemented across all government and recognized schools in the state.
