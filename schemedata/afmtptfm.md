## Brief Description

The scheme “Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program”  aims to support mechanization in agriculture through financial aid for the purchase of mini tractors and power tillers to all eligible farmers across the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program” by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to improve agricultural productivity and reduce manual labor burden by providing financial assistance for the purchase of mini tractors and power tillers to farmers. The scheme offers a subsidy based on the horsepower of the machinery and the applicant&#39;s category to promote inclusive agricultural mechanization and improve efficiency in farming operations. 
<br>

## Benefits

> **Mini Tractors up to 20 HP and Power Tillers above 8 BHP:**


- **General Category Farmers:** Financial assistance of ₹45,000/- or 40% of the equipment cost, whichever is less.- **SC/ST Farmers:** Financial assistance of ₹60,000/- or 50% of the equipment cost, whichever is less.
> **Power Tillers Below 8 BHP:**


- **General Category Farmers:** Financial assistance of ₹40,000/- or 40% of the equipment cost, whichever is less.- **SC/ST Farmers:** Financial assistance of ₹50,000/- or 50% of the equipment cost, whichever is less.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a farmer.
  - The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be engaged in agricultural practices and require mechanization support.
  - The applicant should intend to purchase either a mini tractor up to 20 HP, or a power tiller (either above or below 8 BHP).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme "Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program"?

The scheme "Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program" has been launched with the primary objective of encouraging agricultural mechanization by providing financial assistance to farmers for the purchase of mini tractors and power tillers to improve productivity and reduce manual labor in farming operations.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

All farmers residing in the state of Gujarat, who are engaged in agricultural activities and require mini tractors or power tillers for farming purposes, are eligible to apply for the scheme "Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program".
<br>

### Is there any special benefit provided to Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) farmers under this scheme?

Yes, under the scheme, farmers belonging to SC or ST communities are eligible for a higher financial subsidy of 50% of the equipment cost or a maximum of ₹60,000/- for mini tractors and power tillers above 8 BHP, and ₹50,000/- for power tillers below 8 BHP, whichever is less.
<br>

### What is the financial assistance amount for general category farmers under the scheme?

General category farmers can receive financial assistance up to ₹45,000/- or 40% of the equipment cost for mini tractors and power tillers above 8 BHP, and ₹40,000/- or 40% of the cost for power tillers below 8 BHP, whichever amount is lower, under the scheme "Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program".
<br>

### Is there any income limit prescribed for applying under this scheme?

No, there is no specific individual or family income limit prescribed for availing benefits under the scheme "Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program", making it inclusive for all farmers irrespective of their income.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can female farmers apply for this scheme?

Yes, female farmers are equally eligible to apply under the scheme "Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program", as there is no gender-based restriction mentioned in the eligibility criteria.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is this scheme applicable in all regions of Gujarat?

Yes, the scheme "Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program" is applicable across the entire state of Gujarat, ensuring uniform access to farm mechanization support for farmers in all districts.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What type of farm equipment is supported under this scheme?

The scheme "Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program" provides financial assistance specifically for the purchase of mini tractors up to 20 HP and power tillers, including both below and above 8 BHP capacities.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the steps to apply for the scheme?

To apply for the scheme, the applicant must visit the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, fill out the online application form, upload the required documents, and submit the form electronically.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply under the scheme?

The documents required to apply under the scheme "Assistance to Farmers for Mini Tractor and Power Tiller in Farm Mechanization Program" include the 7/12 Certificate (proof of land ownership), Aadhaar Card (identity proof), and a copy of the Bank Pass Book or a Cancelled Cheque (for DBT purposes).
