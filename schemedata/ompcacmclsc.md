## Brief Description

The scheme aims to organize camps & awareness programs across urban & rural areas to inform and empower vulnerable groups such as women, children, senior citizens, workers in the unorganized sector, persons with disabilities, acid attack survivors & victims of trafficking about their legal rights.

## Detailed Description

The Scheme “Organization of Multi-Purpose Camps/ Public Awareness Camps/ Medical Camps/L egal Service Camps” has been implemented by the Uttarakhand State Legal Services Authority, Nainital under the Department of Law, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to organize camps and awareness programs across urban and rural areas to inform and empower vulnerable groups such as women, children, senior citizens, workers in the unorganized sector, persons with disabilities, acid attack survivors and victims of trafficking about their legal rights.
<br>

## Benefits

For the effective implementation of public welfare schemes of the Hon’ble National Legal Services Authority (NALSA), New Delhi, the District Legal Services Authorities, Tehsil Legal Services Committees, and the Uttarakhand State Legal Services Authority also organize multi-purpose camps, awareness programs, debate competitions, painting competitions, and essay competitions across urban areas and remote rural regions.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. Individuals, women, and children from all walks of life particularly workers in the unorganized sector, persons with mental illness or disabilities, acid attack survivors, senior citizens, and victims of trafficking and commercial sexual exploitation are provided information about their legal rights.
   1. Multi-purpose camps are also organized to support the effective implementation of poverty alleviation schemes.
   1. These camps are conducted in the presence of representatives from all departments.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Before organizing any awareness camp or medical camp, the District Legal Services Authorities publicize the event through daily newspapers and inform public representatives and village panchayat-level officers of the concerned area so that local residents can avail the benefits of the multi-purpose or public awareness camps. 
  - Participation in these camps is completely free, and underprivileged individuals in need of certificates, medical examinations, or legal aid can obtain them at these camps. 
  - Updates regarding upcoming camps are also available on [https://slsa.uk.gov.in](https://slsa.uk.gov.in/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which groups are targeted by this scheme?

The scheme targets women, children, senior citizens, workers in the unorganized sector, persons with disabilities, acid attack survivors, and victims of trafficking or commercial sexual exploitation.
<br>

### What types of camps are organized under the scheme?

The scheme organizes multi-purpose camps, public awareness camps, medical camps, legal service camps, and outreach programs including debate, painting, and essay competitions.
<br>

### How are these camps helpful?

They provide information about legal rights, access to certificates, medical examinations, and other welfare services for underprivileged and vulnerable populations.
<br>

### Who conducts these camps?

The camps are organized by the District Legal Services Authorities, Tehsil Legal Services Committees, and the UKSLSA, in coordination with NALSA.
<br>

### How are local residents informed about upcoming camps?

District Legal Services Authorities publicize the camps through daily newspapers and inform public representatives and village panchayat-level officers.
<br>

### Is participation in these camps free?

Yes, participation in all multi-purpose, public awareness, and medical camps is completely free.
<br>

### How can one get updates about upcoming camps?

Updates are available on the UKSLSA website: [https://slsa.uk.gov.in](https://slsa.uk.gov.in/).
<br>
