## Brief Description

The scheme aims to recognize and support academically outstanding undergraduate students by providing monthly financial assistance of ₹800/- in the form of scholarships to students in their 3rd year of Medical and 4th year of Veterinary undergraduate (UG) programs.

## Detailed Description

The “Merit Scholarship (Top 3 UG Students)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to recognize and support academically outstanding undergraduate students by providing monthly financial assistance in the form of scholarships to students in their 3rd year of Medical and 4th year of Veterinary undergraduate (UG) programs.
<br>

## Benefits

**Rate of Scholarship:** ₹800/- per month.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the 3rd year of the Medical or 4th year of the Veterinary undergraduate (UG) program at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Based on the scores provided by the Registrar&#39;s Office, the Dean of Student Welfare prepares a list of the top three students in each undergraduate program. The list is then approved by the Registrar and the Vice-Chancellor, after which the scholarship is disbursed to the selected students.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scholarship?

The scholarship is open to 3rd-year Medical and 4th-year Veterinary undergraduate (UG) students at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
<br>

### How much financial assistance is provided?

Selected students receive ₹800 per month as scholarship.
<br>

### How many students receive this scholarship?

The scholarship is awarded to the top three students in each eligible undergraduate program.
<br>

### How are the top students selected?

The Registrar's Office provides the academic scores, and the Dean of Student Welfare prepares a list of the top three students based on these scores.
<br>

### Who approves the list of selected students?

The list is approved by the Registrar and the Vice-Chancellor before disbursal.
<br>

### How is the scholarship disbursed to students?

Once approved, the scholarship amount is paid directly to the selected students by the university.
<br>
