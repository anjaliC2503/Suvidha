## Brief Description

The scheme "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance personality and promote physical, mental, and spiritual well-being through training for tribal youth by organizing seven-day camps.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;TASP Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to empower Scheduled Tribe (ST) youth across 17 districts of the state by providing a structured seven-day training program. This camp focuses on personality development and yogasana training, helping the participants to develop their physical, mental, and spiritual health. The scheme seeks to uplift tribal youth through skill-building and self-improvement techniques in a culturally inclusive and health-oriented environment.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Training and Development:** The scheme offers a seven-day residential training camp focused on personality development and yogasana. The training covers aspects of physical fitness, mental well-being, and spiritual awareness.
> **Note:** A total of 50 tribal youths (both male and female) are selected from each of the 17 targeted districts.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.


  - The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should download the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the office of the District Youth Development Officer.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)"?

The main objective of the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" is to support the physical, mental, and spiritual development of tribal youth through personality enhancement sessions and yogasana training.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to participate in the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)"?

Any tribal youth who belongs to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category and resides in Gujarat is eligible to participate in the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which department is responsible for implementing the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)"?

The "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" is implemented by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department of the Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### In which areas of Gujarat is the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" conducted?

The "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" is conducted in 17 districts of Gujarat that have a significant population of Scheduled Tribe communities.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any cost involved in participating in the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)"?

No, there is no cost involved for the participants, as both the application form and the training camp under the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" are completely free of charge.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the duration of training under the "Persona Training Camp (TASP)"?ality Development and Yogasan

The training under the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" is conducted over a continuous period of seven days.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What activities are included in the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)"?

The "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" includes activities such as personality development sessions and yogasana practices aimed at improving participants’ confidence, discipline, and overall well-being.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How many youth are selected per district for the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)"?

A total of 50 tribal youths, comprising both male and female candidates, are selected from each of the 17 eligible districts to attend the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)".
<br>

### Is there any income limit to apply for the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)"?

No, there is no income limit, either individual or family-based, for applicants to be eligible for the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Are any reservations or preferences given under the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)"?

Yes, preference is given to tribal youth residing in districts with a higher concentration of Scheduled Tribe populations under the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where can the application form for the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" be obtained?

The application form for the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)" can be downloaded free of cost in both Gujarati and English languages from the official website of the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)"?

To apply for the "Personality Development and Yogasana Training Camp (TASP)", the applicant must submit a copy of their Aadhar Card along with the completed application form in the prescribed format.
