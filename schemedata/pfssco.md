## Brief Description

This scheme has been introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat. It aims to support sugar co-operatives by providing financial assistance for modernization, diversification, organic fertilizer production to enhance sugarcane farming and the sugar industry.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Package for strengthening to sugar co-operatives as may be policy decided by the State Government&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial aid to sugar co-operative societies for projects such as modernization, upgradation, tissue culture labs, molasses-based distilleries, storage system improvements, and cost reduction initiatives. It also offers direct assistance to farmers for the sale of bio-compost, phospho-compost, and vermicompost.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of this scheme?

The scheme aims to modernize sugar co-operatives, improve infrastructure, enhance productivity, and provide financial assistance for bio-compost, phospho-compost, and vermicompost sales.

### Who is eligible to apply for financial assistance under this scheme?

Only registered sugar co-operative societies and their farmer members are eligible to apply for financial assistance under this scheme.

### What types of financial assistance are available under this scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance for compost sales, modernization projects, infrastructure development, tissue culture labs, and cost reduction initiatives in sugar co-operatives.

### What is the maximum financial assistance provided for compost sales?

1. [?]100/- per metric ton for bio-compost sales (maximum [?]20,00,000/-).
2. [?]100/- per 50 kg bag of phospho-compost (maximum [?]20,00,000/-).
3. [?]50/- per 50 kg bag of vermicompost (maximum [?]6,50,000/-).

### Is financial assistance available for setting up tissue culture labs?

Yes, the scheme provides financial assistance of up to [?]40,00,000/- for setting up new tissue culture labs and up to [?]20,00,000/- for expanding existing labs.

### Can a sugar co-operative society apply for assistance for multiple projects?

Yes, a sugar co-operative society can apply for multiple projects, but only one project will be considered based on priority and funding availability.

### How is financial assistance disbursed to the beneficiaries?

For compost-related projects, financial assistance is transferred directly to the farmers' accounts. For other projects, funds are allocated to the sugar co-operative societies.

### How can a sugar co-operative society apply for this scheme?

The sugar co-operative society must submit a proposal through the District Registrar of the concerned district, which will then be forwarded to the Director of Sugar, Gujarat State, Gandhinagar, for approval.

### Can an individual farmer apply directly for this scheme?

No, individual farmers cannot apply directly. Only sugar co-operative societies can apply on behalf of their members.

### Where can applicants get more information about the scheme?

Applicants can contact the District Registrar of their respective district or the Director of Sugar, Gujarat State, Gandhinagar, for more information.
