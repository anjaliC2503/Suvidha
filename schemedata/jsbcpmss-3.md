## Brief Description

The "Jharkhand State Backward Classes Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme" aims to enhance educational opportunities and participation among students belonging to backward classes in Jharkhand. It provides financial assistance to reduce dropout rates from classes 1 to 10 and promote equality in education.

## Detailed Description

The "Jharkhand State Backward Classes Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme" was launched by the Department of Scheduled Tribe, Scheduled Caste, Minority and Backward Class Welfare, Government of Jharkhand. The scheme aims to enhance educational opportunities and participation among students belonging to backward classes in Jharkhand. It provides financial assistance to reduce dropout rates from classes 1st to 10th and promote equality in education.

## Benefits

> **Financial assistance to cover educational expenses:**

**S.NO****Class****Rate for Hostel (For 10 months)****Rate for Daylight (For 10 months)**11st to 5th₹1,500/-₹1,500/-26th to 8th₹2,000/-₹2,500/-39th to 10th₹4,500/-₹4,500/-<br>

**NOTE:** 

- Direct transfer of scholarships to beneficiaries’ bank accounts via PFMS.
- Residential students receive higher scholarship amounts compared to non-residential students.
- Class 9th and 10th scholarships are funded jointly by the state (40%) and central (60%) governments.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant/parents/guardians should be a native of Jharkhand.
1. The applicant should be a student.
1. The applicant must belong to the Other Backward Class category.
1. The applicant must be enrolled in a state government-recognized school.
1. There is no income limit for students from Class 1st to 8th.1. The applicant's annual family income should not exceed ₹2,50,000/- for Class 9th and 10th.

## Application Process

### Online

> ***Application Process for Student:***

**Document Preparation:**

- Ensure all required documents are ready, including Aadhaar, caste certificate, income certificate (if applicable), and Aadhaar-linked bank account details.

**Submission to School:**

- Submit the required documents and information to the school principal for entry into the e-Kalyan portal.

**Aadhaar Authentication:**

- Ensure Aadhaar authentication is completed to generate a unique ID for the application.

<br>

> ***Application Process for Schools (Principal/INO Role):***

**Login Setup:**
- Principals or headmasters (Institute Nodal Officers) register schools using their UDISE code on the [e-Kalyan portal](https://ekalyan.cgg.gov.in/Login.do).**Student Registration:**
- The school head enters eligible students’ details (name, DOB, Aadhaar).
- New students must be registered immediately upon enrollment.
**Aadhaar Authentication:**
- A unique ID is generated post-verification and is valid for subsequent years.
**Approval Workflow:**
- Applications are verified by District Nodal Officers (DNOs) and forwarded to the District Level Approval Committee.
- Approved applications are sent to the State Nodal Officer (SNO) for integration and payment processing.
**Scholarship Disbursement:**
- Payments are made through the Aadhaar Payment Bridge System (APBS) or PFMS/NACH for non-linked accounts.

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar Card (mandatory for authentication).
- Caste Certificate.
- Income Certificate (for classes 9th to 10th).- Bank Passbook with Aadhaar-linked account details.
- Admission or Enrollment Certificate from the school.

<br>

**NOTE:** The income certificate for Class 9th remains valid for Class 10th.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

To increase the participation of backward-class students in pre-matric education and reduce dropouts.

### Who is eligible to apply?

Students from the backward classes studying in government schools in Jharkhand.

### What is the income limit for classes 1 to 8?

There is no income limit for students in classes 1 to 8.

### Can private school students apply?

No, the scheme is only for students in state government schools.

### What is the application deadline?

The deadlines will be announced on the e-Kalyan portal.

### Is the income certificate mandatory?

Yes, for students in classes 9th and 10th not required for classes 1st to 8th.

### How will the scholarship amount be disbursed?

The amount will be transferred directly to the applicant's bank account.

### How can I check the status of my application?

Log in to the e-Kalyan portal and view the application status.

### Can students from other states apply?

No, only residents of Jharkhand are eligible.

### Will the scholarship be renewed automatically?

No, students must apply each year to avail of the benefits.

### What is the helpline for queries related to this scheme?

The helpline details are available on the e-Kalyan portal.
