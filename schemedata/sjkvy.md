## Brief Description

The "Saksham Jharkhand Kaushal Vikas Yojana" is an initiative by the Jharkhand government to equip youth aged 18-35 years with quality skills training aligned with the National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF). The scheme aims to create job opportunities for 70% of successful trainees.

## Detailed Description

The "Saksham Jharkhand Kaushal Vikas Yojana"  is a component of the umbrella scheme “Mukhymantri Sarthi Yojana” launched by the Department of Labour, Employment, Training and Skill Development, Jharkhand. The scheme aims to create job opportunities for 70% of successful trainees through placement-linked and self-employment training across various sectors. The program operates through 72 training centers and covers trades.

## Benefits

Payment terms and schedule are guided by the Common Norms notified by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE). 

- **Training Cost per Hour:**



   1. Category I Sectors/Trades: ₹49/- per hour.
   1. Category II Sectors/Trades: ₹42/- per hour.
   1. Category III Sectors/Trades: ₹35/- per hour.


<br>


- **Boarding and Lodging Costs:** For residential training or trainees who need to travel significant distances
   1. X Category Cities: ₹375/- per day per trainee.
   1. Y Category Cities: ₹315/- per day per trainee.
   1. Z Category Cities: ₹250/- per day per trainee.
   1. Rural Areas or Non-Municipal Areas: ₹220/- per day per trainee.


<br>


- Placement opportunities for 70% of trainees.
- Training in industry-relevant sectors like Tourism & Hospitality, Telecom, Security, Retail, Plumbing, Logistics, IT/ITES, Electronics, Domestic Worker, Construction, Capital Goods, BFSI, Beauty & Wellness, Automotive, Apparel, and Agriculture, Food Processing, Healthcare, Iron & Steel, Mining

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Jharkhand.
1. The applicant's age should be 18-35 years.
1. The applicant must belong to one of the priority groups (SC/ST, women, PwD, transgender).

## Application Process

### Offline

> ***Candidate registration by TSP:***


- The candidate visits the nearest [Training Service Provide Center](https://jsdm.jharkhand.gov.in/jsdm/cms/en/) for registration under the "Mukhymantri Sarthi Yojana" umbrella scheme. 
**NOTE:** Candidate registration would be Aadhaar-based & hence Aadhaar number at the time of candidate registration would be mandatory.

### Online

> ***Self-Registration by Candidate:***



  - Visit the [Jharkhand Skill Development Mission](https://jsdm.jharkhand.gov.in/jsdm/cms/en/#) official website.  - Click on ‘Candidate Registration’ under the ‘Registration’ tab.


> ***Create Profile:***



  - Provide basic information such as First Name, Last Name, Username, Password, Identity Type (e.g., Aadhaar), Identity Number, Date of Birth, Gender, Mobile Number, and Email.
  - Submit to receive a User ID and Password via SMS and email.


> ***Login and OTP Validation:***



  - Log in using the provided Username and Password.
  - Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number to activate the User ID.


> ***Manage Profile:***



  - After login, access the ‘Registration’ link.
  - Update and save user profile details such as Personal information and optional bank account details. 


> **Contact and Educational Qualification** **Information:**



  - Enter and save your contact details, including address and communication preferences.
  - Fill in education details if literate, or select preferred languages if illiterate. 
  - Save and submit information to the Training Service Provider (TSP).


**Registration ID Generation:**


  - On final submission, the system generates a unique Registration ID and notifications for successful registration will be sent via SMS and email.


<br>

**NOTE:** Aadhaar number is mandatory for registration and prevents duplicate entries.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of SJKVY?

To provide quality skills training to Jharkhand youth and ensure employment opportunities.

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Residents of Jharkhand aged 18-35 years with an interest in the offered trades.

### What sectors are covered under SJKVY?

IT, healthcare, construction, beauty & wellness, agriculture, logistics, and more.

### How can I apply for this scheme?

Register on the official portal (https://jsdm.jharkhand.gov.in/jsdm/cms/en/) and submit the required documents.

### What is the duration of the courses?

Course duration depends on the trade and is compliant with NSQF standards.

### How many training centers are operational?

72 centers are functional across Jharkhand.

### What is the infrastructure requirement for training centers?

10,000 sq. ft. in Ranchi, Dhanbad, Jamshedpur, Hazaribagh, and Bokaro; 5,000 sq. ft. in other districts.

### What is NSQF?

National Skills Qualification Framework ensures standardized and industry-relevant training.

### Are there placement opportunities?

Yes, the scheme aims to place 70% of successful trainees.

### Is self-employment supported under the scheme?

Yes, training includes support for self-employment and entrepreneurship.

### What is the role of Sector Skill Councils (SSC)?

SSCs ensure industry alignment and certification of courses.
