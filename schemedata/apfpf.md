- **Nodal Department Name:** Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department

## Brief Description

The scheme offers financial assistance to farmers in Gujarat for the cultivation of floriculture crops, including loose, cut, and tuberous flowers, based on crop type and farmer category.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance Program for Planting in Floriculture&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to encourage floriculture in Gujarat by offering financial support to farmers cultivating various types of flowers. The assistance amount varies depending on the type of flower and whether the farmer is classified as a small/marginal or other category.

## Benefits

> **Loose Flowers:**


- ₹16,000 per hectare or 40% of cost for small/ marginal farmers.
- ₹10,000 per hectare or 25% of cost for other farmers.

<br>

> **Stem (Cut) Flowers:**


- ₹40,000 per hectare or 40% of cost for small/ marginal farmers.
- ₹25,000 per hectare or 25% of cost for other farmers.

<br>

> **Tuberous Flowers:**


- ₹60,000/- per hectare or 40% of cost for small/ marginal farmers.
- ₹37,500/- per hectare or 25% of cost for other farmers.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a farmer.
   1. The applicant must be engaged in floriculture farming (loose, cut, or tuberous flowers).

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on &quot;Apply New&quot; button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the &quot;Update Application&quot; button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application.
**Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) 
> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Assistance Program for Planting in Floriculture" scheme?

The scheme aims to offer financial assistance to farmers in Gujarat for the cultivation of floriculture crops, including loose, cut, and tuberous flowers, based on crop type and farmer category.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Farmers who are engaged in floriculture cultivation are eligible to apply for the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Which types of flowers are covered under this scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance for the cultivation of loose flowers, stem (cut) flowers, and tuberous flowers.
<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum financial assistance provided under the scheme?

Depending on the category of the farmer and the type of flower cultivated, the assistance ranges from ₹10,000/- to ₹60,000/- per hectare.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit for farmers to apply under this scheme?

No, there is no income limit for individual or family income to be eligible for this scheme.

### How can an applicant apply for this scheme?

Applicants can apply on the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/)portal to avail the benefits of the scheme.

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme?

Applicants are required to submit an Aadhaar card and Vehicle purchase invoice.
.

### Who should the applicant contact for help or more information about the scheme?

The applicant should contact the nearest agriculture office or visit the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/)portal for more information.

### Can farmers belonging to any caste or social group apply for this scheme?

Yes, the scheme is open to all farmers, regardless of caste, category, or social group.
<br>
