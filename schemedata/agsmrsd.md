## Brief Description

The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries" aims to strengthen the overall industrial ecosystem in the State and also enable a larger set of manufacturing companies to emulate the adoption of clean energy and make them future-ready to compete in global markets.

## Detailed Description

The &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries&amp;quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&amp;#39;s dynamic mega-industry sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2032, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&amp;quot; and &amp;quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&amp;quot;
The component &amp;quot;Reimbursement of Stamp Duty and Registration Fees&amp;quot; offers reimbursement of stamp duty and registration fees to eligible Mega Industrial Units in Gujarat for land purchases or leases related to their projects. It aims to encourage industrial development and ease the financial burden of acquiring land for business purposes.

## Benefits

- **Reimbursement:** Eligible units can claim 100% reimbursement of stamp duty and registration charges paid to the Government of Gujarat for the purchase and/or lease of land meant for the project.

## Eligibility

1. The mega industry must operate as a manufacturing unit.
   1. The unit must have a minimum investment of ₹2,500/- crore in plant and machinery.
   1. The unit must provide direct employment to at least 2,500 persons.
   1. The unit must belong to one of the thrust sectors as defined in the [Guidelines](https://cmogujarat.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-01/AatmaNirbhar-Gujarat_Industrial-Policy_0.pdf) (Refer Page No. 9).   1. The land must be purchased or leased during the eligible investment period.
   1. The unit must have commenced commercial production.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on "New Investor Registration" to access the registration form.
**Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on "Submit". 
**Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID.
**Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.
<br>

> **Post-Registration Process:**

**Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on "Login".
**Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration.
**Step-3:** Click the "Login" button to access your account.
**Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries"?

The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to Mega Industries" is a state initiative designed to support and strengthen the Mega Industry sector in Gujarat. It aims to enhance growth, encourage investments, and contribute to the vision of self-reliant Gujarat and India.

### When is the scheme operative?

The scheme is operative from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2032, covering a period of 10 years.

### What are the objectives of the scheme?

The scheme aims to boost Mega Industry growth, enhance the industrial ecosystem, encourage investments, and contribute to Gujarat's and India's self-reliance vision.

### Who is eligible to benefit from the scheme?

Mega Industries established and operating within Gujarat are eligible to benefit from the scheme, as per the criteria defined in the policy framework.

### What is the "Reimbursement of Stamp Duty and Registration Fees" component?

The scheme provides 100% reimbursement of stamp duty and registration fees to eligible Mega Industrial Units in Gujarat for land purchases or leases related to their projects.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Mega Industrial Units in Gujarat that meet the specified eligibility criteria are eligible for this scheme.

### What expenses are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers stamp duty and registration fees paid for the purchase or lease of land meant for the project.

### Can the reimbursed amount be counted as part of fixed capital investment?

The reimbursed stamp duty and registration fees cannot be included as part of Eligible Fixed Capital Investment.

### When can an industrial unit apply for reimbursement?

An industrial unit can apply for reimbursement only after the commencement of commercial production.

### What is the eligible investment period?

The eligible investment period is the time frame specified by the Government of Gujarat during which land transactions must occur to qualify for reimbursement.

### What is the quantum of reimbursement provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides 100% reimbursement of stamp duty and registration fees paid to the Government of Gujarat for eligible land transactions.

### Is the scheme applicable to land rented for a project?

No, the scheme is only applicable to land that is purchased or leased for the project.

### Is there any restriction on the timing of the land transaction?

Yes, the land must be purchased or leased during the eligible investment period to qualify for reimbursement.

### Can multiple land transactions be reimbursed under the scheme?

Yes, multiple land transactions can be reimbursed as long as they are part of the project and meet the eligibility criteria.
