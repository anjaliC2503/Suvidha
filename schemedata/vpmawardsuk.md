## Brief Description

The scheme aims to recognize and encourage B.Sc. Agriculture students in their 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year of study by awarding them a cash prize of ₹10,000/- for their academic excellence.

## Detailed Description

The “Shri Varun Panwar Memorial Award for B.Sc. Agriculture (2nd Year, 3rd Year, and 4th Year) Students” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to recognize and encourage B.Sc. Agriculture students in their 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year of study by awarding them a cash prize for their academic excellence.
<br>

## Benefits

1. Award Amount: ₹10,000/- per year
1. Number of Awards: One award for each class


- 2nd Year: 1 student
- 3rd Year: 1 student
- 4th Year: 1 student

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the B.Sc. Agriculture program and be in their 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year of study at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The award is granted for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the selected students receive the payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this award?

The applicant must be a student enrolled in the B.Sc. Agriculture program at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, in their 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year.
<br>

### What is the award amount?

The award carries a cash prize of ₹10,000 per year.
<br>

### For how long is the award granted?

The award is granted for a duration of one year only.
<br>

### Can students from other universities apply?

No.
<br>

### How many awards are given each year?

A total of three awards are given annually — one each for 2nd, 3rd, and 4th-year students.
<br>

### How can students apply for this award?

Students must submit their applications using the prescribed form provided by the university.
