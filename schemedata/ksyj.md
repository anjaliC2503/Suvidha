## Brief Description

The "Kisan Samriddhi Yojana" by the Department of Agriculture, Animal Husbandry & Cooperative, Government of Jharkhand, promotes solar-powered irrigation systems to reduce agricultural costs and dependency on fossil fuels. Farmers receive up to 90% subsidy for solar units under this initiative.

## Detailed Description

The "Kisan Samriddhi Yojana" by the Department of Agriculture, Animal Husbandry & Cooperative, Government of Jharkhand, aims to promote solar energy-powered irrigation systems to reduce dependency on fossil fuels and lower agricultural costs. Through this scheme, farmers receive up to 90% subsidy for solar irrigation units, enhancing their income and fostering sustainable agricultural practices.

## Benefits

1. Farmers receive a 90% subsidy on solar-powered irrigation units, reducing their agricultural costs.
1. Two types of solar units are available: 5 HP fixed and 2 HP portable, catering to varied irrigation needs.
1. Solar units eliminate the need for electricity or diesel, ensuring year-round irrigation.
1. The portable 2 HP unit is mobile, making it suitable for multi-location usage.
1. Reduced maintenance costs for solar-powered systems.
1. Encourages sustainable energy use, contributing to environmental conservation.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a farmer, self-help group, cooperative, or farmer producer organization registered in Jharkhand.
1. The applicant must have a valid Aadhaar card and Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
1. A registered office of the group/organization must be located in Jharkhand.
1. The applicant must own or have access to land near a water source.
1. Only one member per family is eligible for benefits.
1. The applicant should be aged 18 years or above.

## Application Process

### Online - via CSC

**Step-1:** The applicant should gather copies of the necessary documents needed to apply for the concerned scheme.
**Step-2:** The applicant should visit the nearest Common Service Center (CSC) and complete the Biometric Verification Process.
**Step-3:** The applicant should ensure that the CSC agent completes all mandatory fields in the application form and uploads copies of all required documents.
**Step-4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).
<br>

**NOTE:** 

- Applicants must link their ration card with Aadhaar for e-KYC verification.
- Pay the 10% contribution fee as per the unit cost.

**Group Registration Process:**
**Step-1:** The applicant may visit the official website "[Kisan Samriddhi Yojna](https://ksy.jharkhand.gov.in)"
**Step-2:** Click on "Group Registration" and fill the details like: Group Type, Group Name, Enter Group Name, Registration Number, GST/PAN/TAN Number, Registration Date etc.
**Step-3:** Click on "Submit"

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Farmers, self-help groups, cooperatives, and farmer producer organizations registered in Jharkhand with access to a water source and Aadhaar-linked mobile numbers are eligible.

### How much subsidy is provided under this scheme?

The government provides up to 90% subsidy on the cost of solar-powered irrigation units, with farmers contributing only 10%.

### What are the types of irrigation units available under this scheme?

The scheme offers two types: 5 HP fixed solar pump sets for large-scale irrigation and 2 HP portable solar pump sets for smaller, mobile usage.

### Can an individual benefit from the scheme more than once?

No, individual beneficiaries can avail of the scheme only once. Groups benefiting from the scheme must wait five years before reapplying.

### Is there any age limit for applicants?

Yes, the applicant must be 18 years or older to apply for the scheme.

### Can a farmer without land access to a water source apply?

No, the farmer must own or have access to land near a water source to qualify for the scheme.

### How can one apply for the scheme?

Applications are submitted online through the Jharkhand Agriculture Department portal, with Aadhaar-based e-KYC verification.

### Is the scheme applicable across Jharkhand?

Yes, the scheme is implemented in all 24 districts of Jharkhand.

### What happens if the land details provided are incorrect?

Applications with incorrect or unverifiable land details will be rejected during the inspection.

### What is the irrigation capacity of the units provided?

The 5 HP solar pump can irrigate up to 5 acres, while the 2 HP unit is suitable for 1 acre.
