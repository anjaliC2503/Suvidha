## Brief Description

The scheme enables artisans and handicraft organizations to participate in government-sponsored melas across Gujarat and other states, fostering market access and preserving traditional crafts. Benefits include subsidized stall fees, promotional support, sales opportunities for handmade products.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Handicraft Mela Participation" by the Industrial Extension Cottage (iNDEXT-C), Industries & Mines Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote traditional handicrafts by providing artisans and organizations with platforms to showcase and sell their products at state and national-level fairs. The scheme facilitates participation in designated melas (fairs) by covering logistical support and stall allocations. Eligible applicants include individual artisans, self-help groups (SHGs), and registered handicraft organizations. The scheme is implemented by Indext-C (Industrial Extension Bureau, Gujarat), and applications are accepted offline through district offices or the Indext-C portal.

## Benefits

**Stall Allocation:** Free or subsidized stalls at designated melas.
**Promotional Support:** Marketing and branding assistance for participants.
**Sales Platform:** Direct access to customers and bulk buyers.
**Logistical Aid:** Basic infrastructure (tables, signage) provided at mela venues.
**Financial Incentives:** Priority for artisans from SC/ST/SEBC/EWS categories.

## Eligibility

**For Individual Artisans**

- The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
- The applicant must possess a valid Artisan ID Card issued by Indext-C or a recognized authority.
- The applicant must specialize in a traditional handicraft listed under the scheme (e.g., embroidery, pottery, woodwork).

<br>

**For Organizations/SHGs**

- The applicant must be a registered cluster/cooperative society/NGO.
- The applicant must have a minimum of 10 artisans affiliated with the organization.
- The applicant must submit audit reports and registration certificates.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://craftofgujarat.gujarat.gov.in/Documents/SubDeal__2025-2-13_96.pdf) of the application form from the Indext-C website.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields ( personal/organization details, artisan ID, and handicraft category, etc), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form, along with the documents, either to your nearest [District Industries Office](https://www.cottage.gujarat.gov.in/Home-2140795B5D2D-282472282472-2824725B5D2D) or to the following address: **Indext-C Headquarters, Industrial Extension Cottage, Block No. 7, 1st Floor, Udyog Bhavan, Sector-11, Gandhinagar - 382 011.**
The Indext-C will review the applications and notify the selected participants via SMS/email.

## Documents Required

- Artisan ID Card (for Individuals).
- Aadhaar Card (Proof of Residence).
- Caste Certificate (if applying under SC/ST/SEBC/EWS Quota).
- Organization Registration Proof (for SHGs/Clusters).
- Bank Account Details (for Disbursements).
- Recent Passport-Sized Photograph.
- Handicraft Samples (Photographs or Physical Samples, if requested).
- Audit Reports (for Organizations).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can artisans share or sublet their allocated stalls to others?

No, stall transfers are prohibited; violators will face penalties and future participation bans.

### What types of handicrafts are eligible for display at these fairs?

Eligible crafts include 62 listed categories like embroidery, metalware, terracotta, woodwork, and traditional textiles.

### What happens if an artisan fails to attend after registering for a fair?

The stall allocation will be canceled, and any paid fees may be forfeited unless prior notice is given to Indext-C.

### Are there any awards or recognition for outstanding artisans at these fairs?

Artisans with awards can attach certificates (max 4) in their application, but no competition details are specified.

### How are artisans identified during the fair for verification purposes?

They must carry and display their artisan ID card and recent photograph at the stall.

### What are the rules regarding display and arrangement of products at the stall?

Artisans must display only within allocated space without obstructing pathways or other stalls, following Indext-C's guidelines.

### Can artisans participate in fairs outside their home district or state?

Yes, the scheme covers both intra-state and inter-state fairs organized by Indext-C.

### What banking details are required for application processing?

Artisans must provide bank account number, IFSC code, branch name, and account holder name.

### Is there any insurance coverage for artisans' goods during the fair?

No, artisans are solely responsible for their goods' safety, though security may be provided at the venue.

### Are artisans allowed to consume alcohol or tobacco at the fair venue?

No, narcotics, alcohol, or flammable materials are strictly prohibited at stalls or venues.

### What are the eligibility criteria for artisans to participate in the handicraft fairs organized by the government?

Artisans must produce handmade crafts listed in the official Gujarat handicraft list and possess a valid artisan identification card issued by recognized institutions like Indext-C.

### How does one prove domicile status as a Gujarat resident for participation?

The application form includes a domicile declaration section; supporting documents may be required.
