## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for vehicle purchases in the transport sector for SEBC unemployed individuals. A loan is provided under the scheme.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Parivahan Scheme (GBCDC)" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance for the purchase of vehicles in the transport sector to promote self-employment among the Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC).
Through this scheme, a loan is provided to unemployed individuals for purchasing vehicles like auto-rickshaws, loading vehicles, etc. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Backward Classes Development Corporation (GBCDC).
<br>

## Benefits

1. The applicant can avail a loan of up to ₹2,00,000/- for purchasing vehicles such as auto-rickshaws, loading vehicles, and other transport vehicles.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to the Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC) category.
1. The applicant can be of any gender.
1. The applicant should be unemployed.
1. The applicant should have any level of education.
1. The applicant should have an individual and family annual income up to ₹3,00,000/-.
1. The applicant should hold a valid Driving Licence for the type of vehicle intended for purchase (specifically for passenger vehicles).

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in/) of the Gujarat Backwards Caste Development Corporation.
**Step 2:** On the top menu bar, click on "Apply Online".
**Step 3:** If you’re a new user, click on "New Registration" Enter personal details (name, mobile number, email, aadhaar number, etc.), create a password, and complete OTP verification.
**Step 4:** Use your registered credentials (User ID and password) to log in.
**Step 5:** Click on “New Application”. Select “Parivahan Scheme” from the list of available schemes.
**Step 6:** Fill out the Application Form, enter the following details:


  - Personal Information: Name, gender, date of birth, caste, etc.
  - Address Information: Permanent and correspondence address
  - Income Details: Monthly family income and source
  - Loan Requirement: Amount needed, proposed use (e.g., business, self-employment)
  - Bank Account Details: For loan disbursement


**Step 7:** Upload scanned copies of the Caste certificate, Income certificate, Aadhaar card, Passport-size photo, Bank passbook (first page), and Residence proof.
**Step 8:** Double-check all details. Click on “Preview” to verify. If all details are correct, click “Submit”.
**Step 9:** You will receive an Application ID/Reference Number. Save or print the acknowledgement for future tracking.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of this scheme provided by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department?

This scheme aims to support unemployed individuals from SEBC communities by providing loans to purchase vehicles in the transport sector for self-employment.
<br>

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Any unemployed individual belonging to the Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC) category can apply.
<br>

### Can an individual from any educational background apply for this scheme?

Yes, individuals with any level of education are eligible to apply.
<br>

### Is there any professional restriction to apply for this scheme?

No, any unemployed individual, irrespective of previous profession, can apply.
<br>

### Is a driving licence necessary to apply under this scheme?

Yes, a valid driving licence is required, especially if applying for a loan to purchase a passenger vehicle.
<br>

### Can I apply for this scheme if I want to buy a loading vehicle?

Yes, the scheme covers loans for purchasing loading vehicles.
<br>

### Where can I apply online for this scheme?

You can apply online through the official portal of GBCDC at https://gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in.
<br>

### Is there any cost to access or submit the application form for this scheme?

No, the application form is available free of cost.
<br>

### What should I do if I am a new user on the GBCDC portal?

New users must complete the "New Registration" by providing personal details, creating a password, and verifying through OTP.
<br>

### What details are required to fill in the application form for this scheme?

You need to provide personal details, address, income information, loan requirements, and bank account details.
