## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide free, high-quality e-learning materials to students of classes 9 to 12, covering subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, etc. in collaboration with reputed ed-tech platforms.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Development and Dissemination of e-Content” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Science Education and Research Centre (USERC) under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free, high-quality e-learning materials to students of classes 9 to 12, covering subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, etc. in collaboration with reputed ed-tech platforms.
<br>

## Benefits

The high-quality e-learning materials from online education providers (platforms such as Byju&#39;s and Vedantu) are made available to students of classes 9 to 12 through free bilingual online and offline lectures in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and English.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student from Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

- Students can access certain e-learning materials by visiting the USERC website, [https://www.userc.in](https://www.userc.in). However, to access the full course, they can download the Vidyasaar app from the Google Play Store and register their mobile number free of charge.  - In schools without internet access, USERC provides a device to the school, enabling students to access the entire course offline.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which classes are covered?

Students of classes 9th to 12th are eligible. 
<br>

### Which subjects are available?

The subject like Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, etc. are available. 
<br>

### Is the content multilingual?

Yes, it is available in both Hindi and English.
<br>

### How can students access the content?

Through the Vidya Saar mobile app or offline devices in schools.
<br>

### Is the app free to use?

Yes, the Vidya Saar app is completely free.
<br>

### What if a student has no internet access?

Offline devices with preloaded content are provided to schools.
<br>

### Can students access content from home?

Yes, if they have internet and a mobile device with the Vidya Saar app.
<br>

### Is registration mandatory?

Yes, students must register with a valid mobile number on the app.
<br>

### Are there any charges for lectures?

No, the lectures are provided free of cost.
<br>
