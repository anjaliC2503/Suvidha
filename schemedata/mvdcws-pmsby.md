## Brief Description

This scheme provides accident insurance coverage to registered drivers and conductors, offering financial protection in the event of accidental death or permanent disability.

## Detailed Description

The “Motor Vehicle Driver-Conductor Welfare Scheme- Pradhanmantri Suraksha Bima Yojana” has been launched by the Transport Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to ensure social security and financial protection for registered transport vehicle drivers and conductors. Under this scheme, accidental insurance coverage is provided to the registered drivers and conductors.
<br>

## Benefits

**Accident Insurance Coverage**

- ₹2,00,000 is payable in case of accidental death.
- ₹2,00,000 is payable in case of total and irreversible loss of both eyes, both hands, both feet, or loss of one eye and one limb.
- ₹1,00,000 is payable in case of total and irreversible loss of one eye or one limb.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant's age should be between 18 to 70 years. 
1. The applicant should be a registered transport vehicle driver or conductor in Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should possess a valid commercial driving or conductor license issued in Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be registered on the Samagra Portal.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

> **Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official website: [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on the option “Register Family” or “Register Member”.
**Step 3:** Enter the Aadhaar number and other required information.
**Step 4:** Enter the OTP received on the registered mobile number.
**Step 5:** Carefully fill in all the required details and upload the necessary documents.
**Step 6:** Enter the captcha code and click on “Submit Application.”
> **Offline Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the nearest Lok Seva Kendra, MP Online Kiosk, or Common Service Center (CSC).
**Step 2:** Provide the required documents and necessary information to complete the registration process.
> **After Samagra Registration:**

**Step 1: Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://transport.mp.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/yojna_20150001.pdf) (Page No. 9) from the official website. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District or Regional Transport Office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of this component scheme?

The primary purpose of this component is to provide comprehensive accident insurance coverage specifically tailored for registered drivers and conductors.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which insurance scheme is applicable?

The insurance coverage is governed by the guidelines and provisions of the Prime Minister’s Security Insurance Scheme.
<br>

### Who is eligible for accident insurance?

Eligibility for this accident insurance is strictly limited to registered transport vehicle drivers and conductors.
<br>

### What is the age limit for coverage?

Applicants must fall within the eligible age bracket of 18 to 70 years to qualify for the coverage.
<br>

### What is the insurance amount for accidental death or permanent disability?

For cases involving accidental death or permanent total disability resulting from an accident, the insurance payout is set at ₹2,00,000.
<br>

### What is the insurance amount for permanent partial disability?

If an accident results in permanent partial disability, the insured individual is entitled to a payout of ₹1,00,000.
<br>

### Is Samagra registration mandatory?

It is a mandatory requirement for all applicants to have a valid Samagra registration to avail of these benefits.
<br>

### Can other states people apply for this scheme?

No, only residents of Madhya Pradesh are eligible.
﻿
<br>

### How can I apply for the scheme?

The applicant submits the application at the District or Regional Transport Office (RTO) for verification and further processing.
<br>

### What documents are needed to apply?

The following documents are required: Samagra Family Identification Number (Samagra ID), Samagra Member Identification Number, Aadhaar Card, Proof of Residence (Madhya Pradesh), Valid Commercial Driving or Conductor License, Bank Account Details, Passport-size Photograph, etc.
