## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage college and university students to undertake research in Tiatr, its song, and music. It provides scholarships and publication opportunities for research work, targeting students pursuing Graduation, Post-Graduation, and PhD studies.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Tiatr Research Scholarship Program" was launched by the Tiatr Academy (TAG) under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to encourage young students to undertake research in different aspects of Tiatr, its song, and music. The scheme provides scholarships to college and university students for research related to Tiatr, which must be conducted in English and Konkani (Roman script only). It also includes the publication of the approved research work in book form by TAG, with 100 copies provided to the student concerned in addition to the scholarship amount. The applicant must submit a report of the research carried out duly signed by either the guide or the head of the institution.
<br>

## Benefits

> ***Scholarship Amount***

The amount of each scholarship will be decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) from time to time.
> ***Publication of Research Work***

The research work of the students will be printed by Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) in book form after approval by the Recommendation Board.
> ***Copies of Published Work***

Each student concerned will receive 100 copies of their printed research work in addition to the scholarship amount.
> ***Number of Scholarships***

TAG will offer scholarships in three categories every year:
    ◦ PhD: 1 scholarship
    ◦ Post-Graduation: 3 scholarships
    ◦ Graduation: 6 scholarships
_*Scholarships will be for every academic year._
_*A scholarship will be given only once in a particular category; however, the same applicant is free to apply for other categories in subsequent academic years._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a bonafide student who does research work or a project related to Tiatr.
- The applicant must conduct research work in English and Konkani (Roman script only).
- The applicant should have secured at least 50% marks in the last examination.
- The applicant must submit a report of the research carried out, duly signed either by the guide or the head of the institution.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields: name of the applicant. Attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
<br>

## Documents Required

> ***At Application Stage***


- Self-Attested Copy of Aadhaar Card
- Self-Attested Copy of PAN Card
- Report of The Research Carried Out (Duly Signed Either By The Guide Or The Head of The Institution)
- Copy of Mark Sheet / Certificate of Last Examination (Inferred, to substantiate the requirement of at least 50% marks)
- Proof of Bonafide Student Status / Enrolment In College/University (Inferred, for undertaking research work related to Tiatr)

> ***At Benefit Disbursal / Post-Selection Stage***


- Copy of Research Work (Duly certified by the guide, as undertaken in the application form)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific academic qualification do I need to have completed to be eligible for applying under this research program?

You must have secured at least 50% marks in your last examination to be eligible for the scholarship program.
<br>

### Am I allowed to conduct my research work in any Indian language of my choice for this scholarship opportunity?

No, your research work must be conducted strictly in English and Konkani using Roman script only.
<br>

### Can I receive this scholarship multiple times if I apply in the same academic category in different years?

You can receive the scholarship only once in each particular category, but you are free to apply for other categories in subsequent academic years.
<br>

### What happens to my research work after I complete it and submit it under this scholarship program?

The Tiatr Academy of Goa will print your research work in book form after approval by the Recommendation Board, and you will receive 100 copies of the published work.
<br>

### How many scholarship opportunities are available each year for students pursuing doctoral level studies under this program?

There is 1 scholarship available for PhD students every academic year under this research program.
<br>

### Do I need to have my research report verified by any specific authority before submitting my application?

Yes, you must submit a report of your research work duly signed by either your guide or the head of your institution.
<br>

### What is the maximum number of scholarships offered annually to undergraduate students through this research initiative?

6 scholarships are offered to Graduation level students every academic year under this program.
<br>

### Is there a predetermined fixed amount that I will receive as scholarship money under this research program?

The scholarship amount is decided by the Tiatr Academy of Goa from time to time and is not fixed.
<br>

### What specific subject area or theme must my research work focus on to qualify for this scholarship?

Your research work must be related to Tiatr, its song, and music to qualify for this scholarship program.
<br>

### How many students pursuing master's degree programs can receive scholarships each year under this research scheme?

3 scholarships are available for Post-Graduation level students every academic year.
<br>

### What identity documents do I need to provide along with my application for this research scholarship?

You need to provide self-attested copies of your Aadhaar Card and PAN Card along with your application.
<br>

### Do I need to prove that I am currently enrolled as a student when applying for this scholarship?

Yes, you need to provide proof of bonafide student status or enrollment in college/university to demonstrate your eligibility.
<br>

### What academic performance documentation is required to demonstrate that I meet the minimum qualification standards?

You must provide a copy of your mark sheet or certificate from your last examination to prove you secured at least 50% marks.
<br>

### Will I need to submit any additional documentation after being selected for the scholarship program?

Yes, you must provide a copy of your research work duly certified by your guide at the benefit disbursal stage.
<br>
