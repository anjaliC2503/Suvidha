## Brief Description

In view of the difficulty faced by poor families regarding the marriage of the girl child, the Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana has been implemented.

## Detailed Description

Redressal of economic difficulties faced by poor families in the context of girls&#39; marriage, prevention of extravagance on the occasion of marriage and promotion of simple marriages, increase in morale/self-esteem through organizing group marriages and improvement in their social status. Promotion of group marriages and prevention of dowry transactions in marriages.

## Benefits

A financial assistance of up to ₹50,000 per daughter is provided for marriage.

1. Up to ₹7,000/- can be given as material assistance.
1. ₹35,000/- is provided to the bride through a bank draft.
1. Up to ₹8,000/- can be spent on the mass marriage ceremony per beneficiary.

## Eligibility

1. Families living below the poverty line.
1. A maximum of two girls above the age of 18 years of the BPL cardholder family.
1. Widows/orphans/destitute girls have also been included.
