## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote renewable energy and reduce electricity expenses. Through this scheme, a 75% subsidy is provided for setting up solar plants (1KW to 40KW) in milk cooperative societies in tribal areas.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme of Capital Assistance for Setting Up Solar Plants in Milk Cooperative Societies in the State of Gujarat (Tribal Areas)&quot; by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation, Government of Gujarat aims to promote renewable energy and reduce electricity expenses. Through this scheme, a 75% subsidy is provided for setting up solar plants (1KW to 40KW) in milk cooperative societies in tribal areas.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** 75% subsidy on capital cost for installation of solar systems ranging from 1KW to 40KW.
<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1. A registered milk cooperative society located in the tribal areas of Gujarat shall be eligible under this scheme.
   1. The society must extensively use electricity for milk collection, cooling, and processing.
   1. The society must be willing to install a solar power plant with a capacity between 1KW and 40KW.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Collect the plain paper application form from the District Registrar&#39;s office.
**Step 2:** Fill in all required details in the application form and attach the necessary documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the completed application to the District Registrar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of the "Scheme of Capital Assistance for Setting Up Solar Plants in Milk Cooperative Societies in the State of Gujarat (Tribal Areas)"?

The main purpose of the scheme is to financially support milk cooperative societies in tribal areas by subsidizing the cost of setting up solar power plants, thereby promoting renewable energy and reducing operational electricity costs.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Only registered milk cooperative societies located in the tribal areas of Gujarat are eligible to apply for this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What type of financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Under this scheme, eligible cooperative societies are provided a subsidy of 75% on the capital cost for setting up solar power plants with a capacity between 1KW and 40KW.
<br>

<br>

### Which government department is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation, Government of Gujarat, is responsible for implementing this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Where can an applicant obtain the application form for this scheme?

The application form for the scheme can be obtained from the office of the District Registrar.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any fee charged for the application form?

No, there is no cost associated with obtaining the application form; it is available free of charge.
<br>

<br>

### What is the permissible range of solar plant capacity under the scheme?

The scheme allows for the installation of solar power plants with a capacity ranging from 1 kilowatt (KW) to 40 kilowatts (KW).
<br>

<br>

### Does the scheme cover the full cost of installing a solar plant?

No, the scheme covers only 75% of the capital cost; the remaining 25% must be borne by the cooperative society.
<br>

<br>

### How should the completed application be submitted?

The completed application must be submitted on plain paper along with the required documents to the District Registrar.
<br>

<br>

### Whom should applicants contact for more information or assistance regarding the scheme?

Applicants should contact the District Registrar office in their respective areas for detailed information, clarification, or assistance related to the scheme.
