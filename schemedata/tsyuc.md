## Brief Description

The scheme "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)" by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance of ₹15,000/- annually to unreserved category students in the science stream of Class 11th and 12th.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)&quot; by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Gujarat aims to support meritorious students from the unreserved category pursuing science in Classes 11 and 12 by providing them annual tuition fee assistance. This scheme ensures that students who scored 70% or more in their Class 10 board examination receive ₹15,000 through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to pursue science education. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Unreserved Educational &amp; Economic Development Corporation.

## Benefits

- **Monetary Assistance:** ₹15,000/- per academic year is provided as tuition assistance to each eligible student.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must belong to the unreserved category.
  - The applicant must be studying in Class 11th or Class 12th in the science stream.
  - The applicant must have secured 70% or more marks (from total marks in all subjects) in Class 10.
  - The applicant’s family income must not exceed ₹6,00,000 annually.
  - The applicant must apply in the same academic year.
  - The institution should be registered under a valid Act (such as Trust Act, Companies Act, etc.), with at least 3 years of experience in Class 11-12 science tuition.
  - The institution should have filed IT returns for the past 3 years, including income from tuition classes, 
  - The institution should have a mandatory GST.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Go to the Corporation's [official website](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/) and navigate to the "Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation" section under the Corporation menu.
**Step 2:**Click on "Tuition Sahay Yojana" and then Apply Now.
**Step 3:** Click on "New User? Please Register Here".
**Step 4:** Enter the required details and click Register.
> **Application Process:**

**Step 1:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, then click "Login".
**Step 2:** Read the scheme details carefully and click OK.
**Step 3:** If your permanent address is correct, confirm it. Otherwise, enter your correct address.
**Step 4:** Click Save & Next to proceed. Upload the relevant documents.
**Step 5:** If you need to make changes, click "No, Cancel". If all details are correct, click "Yes, Submit".
**Step 6:** Click Save Application. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout by clicking Print Application.
> **Check Application Status:** [Click here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx).

> **Post Online Application Procedure:** After submitting the online application, the applicant must take a printout of the application, sign it, and submit it along with all uploaded documents (certificates, mark sheets, etc.) to the concerned Deputy Director (Non-Reserved Category) or the District Social Welfare Officer through courier, post, or by visiting in person.

> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in/contact-us.html).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)" launched by the Government of Gujarat?

The objective of the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)" launched by the Government of Gujarat is to provide annual financial support to meritorious students belonging to the unreserved category who are studying in Class 11 and 12 in the science stream, so that they can continue their education without facing financial hardship in paying private tuition fees.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to benefit from the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)"?

A student is eligible to benefit from the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)" if they belong to the unreserved category, have secured at least 70% marks in their Class 10 board examinations, are studying in Class 11 or 12 in the science stream, and their family income does not exceed ₹6,00,000 per year.
<br>

<br>

### What is the amount of financial assistance provided under the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)"?

Under the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)", the Government of Gujarat provides financial assistance of ₹15,000 per academic year directly to the bank account of each eligible student through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to cover tuition-related expenses.
<br>

<br>

### How many times can a student avail the benefit of the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)"?

A student can avail the benefit of the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)" once every academic year, and since it applies to both Class 11 and Class 12, the assistance can be received for a maximum of two years if the student remains eligible in both years.
<br>

<br>

### What is the income limit for the applicant’s family under the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)"?

To be eligible for the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)", the applicant’s total annual family income should not exceed ₹6,00,000, and an official income certificate must be provided as proof during the application process.
<br>

<br>

### Is it necessary for the student to be studying in the science stream to avail the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)"?

Yes, it is absolutely necessary for the student to be enrolled in the science stream in either Class 11 or 12, and they must submit a bonafide certificate from their school confirming their enrollment in the science stream.
<br>

<br>

### Can students from reserved categories apply for the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)"?

No, students belonging to reserved categories are not eligible for the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)" as it is specifically designed for students from the unreserved category only.
<br>

<br>

### What is the minimum academic requirement for the student to be eligible for the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)"?

The student must have secured a minimum of 70% marks in Class 10 based on the total marks of all subjects, and percentile scores will not be considered for eligibility under the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)".
<br>

<br>

### What is the mode of application for the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)"?

The mode of application for the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)" is online, and the student must submit the application form along with scanned copies of the required documents through the [official portal](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/index.aspx?HodID=ifU15XBnALM5xkQSOKg0PQ==).
<br>

<br>

### What is the process after submitting the online application?

After submitting the online application for the scheme, the student must also submit a self-attested hard copy of the application form and supporting documents to the district office where they are currently studying within 30 days from the date of online submission.
<br>

<br>

### Are there any specific conditions for the tuition institution under the "Tution Sahay Yojana (Unreserved Category)"?

Yes, the tuition institution must be registered under an appropriate law such as the Trust Act, Companies Act, or Shop and Establishment Act, must have at least 3 years of operational experience in providing science stream tuition for Class 11 and 12, and must possess a valid GST number along with filed IT returns for the last 3 years.
<br>
