## Brief Description

The purpose of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to poor families at the time of marriage, to encourage girls' education, and prevent child marriage.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana&quot; was introduced by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Bihar, and aims to provide a grant at the time of marriage to girls from BPL and other economically disadvantaged families, to offer financial assistance to their families during the marriage. 
Under the scheme, girls are encouraged to complete their education, and upon getting married at the appropriate age, they receive financial assistance from the government. This initiative aims to raise awareness among the public, thereby contributing to an increase in the literacy rate of Bihar. With the introduction of this scheme, the state government has also made a concerted effort to curb social evils like dowry and bring about a positive change in societal mindset.
<br>

## Benefits

- Under the scheme, an amount of ₹5,000/- will be given at the time of marriage of the girl child.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a girl who is 18 years of age or older at the time of application.
1. The applicant should be a native of Bihar.
1. The applicant's family should be Below Poverty Line (BPL) card holders.
1. The annual income of the applicant's family should not exceed ₹60,000/-.
1. The applicant should be marrying a boy who is 21 years of age or older.

## Documents Required

1. Aadhaar card
1. Basic address proof
1. Caste certificate
1. BPL ration card
1. Date of birth of the girl
1. Income certificate
1. Bank account number
1. Mobile number
1. Passport-size photo
1. Birth certificate or age certificate of the boy and the girl
1. Self-attested declaration of non-payment of dowry

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is objective of the scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance to poor families during their daughter’s marriage and also promotes girl education, registered marriages, and prevention of child marriage.
<br>

### Will the amount be given in cash or transferred to my bank account?

The money will be directly deposited into your bank account.
<br>

### How does the government ensure this scheme is properly implemented in my district?

Regular monthly review meetings are held by the District Programme Officer and other authorities.
<br>

### What is the Minimum age of the Boy/Groom for the scheme ?

The Age Of The Boy/Groom Child Should Be 21 Years Or More.

### Who is responsible for preparing the utilization certificate after I receive the scheme benefits?

The concerned institutions or assistant directors prepare it and submit it to the District Child Protection Unit.
<br>

### Where do I need to submit the application form for this scheme?

You need to submit your filled application form at the RTPS counter in your block office.
<br>

### Who will approve my application?

Your application will be verified and approved by the Block Development Officer (BDO).
<br>

### My family has a BPL card. Does that make me eligible for this scheme?

Yes, BPL cardholders are eligible under this scheme.
<br>

### My marriage is not registered yet. Can I still apply for this scheme?

No, you can only apply after your marriage is officially registered.
<br>
