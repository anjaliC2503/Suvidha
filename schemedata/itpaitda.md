## Brief Description

The policy aims to promote the growth of IT, ITES, Internet Service Provider, and Telecom Service Provider industries in Uttarakhand by supporting startups, and creating employment opportunities for local youth through policy incentives and initiatives.

## Detailed Description

The policy “IT Policy and Amendments-2020” is implemented by the Information Technology Development Agency (ITDA) under Department of Information Technology, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote the growth of IT, ITES, Internet Service Provider, and Telecom Service Provider industries in Uttarakhand by supporting startups, and creating employment opportunities for local youth through policy incentives and initiatives.
<br>

## Benefits

Under the IT Policy, the focus is on promoting the IT and IT Enabled Services (ITES) sectors, as well as the Internet Service Provider and Telecom Service Provider industries in the state, by encouraging investment through various beneficial schemes and policy incentives offered by the Industries Department.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. IT, ITES, Internet Service Providers, Telecom Service Providers, IT-based industries, investors, startups, and youth from Uttarakhand are eligible to avail of the benefits offered under the policy.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

The benefits under this policy can be availed through the Information Technology Development Agency (ITDA), an organization under the State’s IT Department. Beneficiaries can receive subsidies and other incentives as per the procedures prescribed by the State’s Industries Department.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who are the primary beneficiaries of this policy?

Beneficiaries include IT and ITES companies, Internet Service Providers, Telecom Service Providers, IT-based industries, investors, startups, and local youth from Uttarakhand.
<br>

### How does this policy promote investment in the IT sector?

The policy encourages investment through beneficial schemes, subsidies, and policy incentives provided by the Industries Department of Uttarakhand.
<br>

### Can startups avail benefits under this policy?

Yes, eligible startups registered in Uttarakhand can avail of the benefits and incentives provided under this policy.
<br>

### How can companies apply for benefits under this policy?

Companies can apply through the Information Technology Development Agency (ITDA) by submitting the required documents as per the prescribed guidelines.
<br>

### Are Internet and Telecom Service Providers eligible for benefits?

Yes, Internet Service Providers and Telecom Service Providers operating in Uttarakhand are eligible under this policy.
