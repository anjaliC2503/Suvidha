## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide monthly financial assistance of ₹800/- in the form of scholarships to undergraduate students who excel in sports.

## Detailed Description

The “Sports Scholarship (UG)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide monthly financial assistance in the form of scholarships to undergraduate students who excel in sports.
<br>

## Benefits

**Rate of Scholarship:** ₹800/- per month.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the first year of an undergraduate course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The applicant must be one of the six selected students eligible to receive this benefit.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

First, applications are invited from students using a prescribed form. Thereafter, a committee constituted by the Vice-Chancellor evaluates the received applications as per the established selection process. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice-Chancellor approves the selected students, following which the scholarship amount is disbursed to them.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture & Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand.
<br>

### What is the amount of scholarship provided under this scheme?

Selected students receive a scholarship of ₹800/- per month.
<br>

### How many students are selected under this scheme?

A total of six undergraduate students are selected each year to receive the scholarship.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scholarship?

Students enrolled in the first year of an undergraduate course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture & Technology, Pantnagar, are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What is the application process for the scheme?

Students must submit their applications on a prescribed form provided by the university within the specified timeframe.
<br>

### Who is responsible for selecting the students?

A committee constituted by the Vice-Chancellor reviews the applications and recommends eligible students for the scholarship.
<br>

### How are the final selections approved?

The final approval of selected students is granted by the Vice-Chancellor, based on the recommendations of the selection committee.
<br>

### How is the scholarship amount disbursed?

After approval, the scholarship amount is paid directly to the selected students as per university procedures.
<br>
