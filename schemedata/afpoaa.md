## Brief Description

The "Assistance for Purchase of Aids/Appliances" scheme is to provide support to individuals with disabilities by facilitating access to assistive devices and appliances.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Assistance for Purchase of Aids/Appliances&quot; scheme under the Social Welfare, Women &amp; Child Development Chandigarh financial assistance is being provided to physically handicapped persons for the purchase of aids/appliances to increase their mobility in their day-to-day working. The applicant should be a resident of UT, Chandigarh to avail of benefits under the scheme. By providing support for their purchase, the scheme empowers individuals to perform tasks and activities independently, thereby enhancing their self-esteem and overall well-being.

## Benefits

> **100% cost on purchase of artificial limbs/ aids/ appliances subject to a maximum of ₹40,000/-**

## Eligibility

1. Annual income of the applicant from all sources should not exceed  ₹1,50,000/- per annum.
   1. Applicant must be resident of UT, Chandigarh for more than 3 years.
   1. Applicant&#39;s Disability should be to the extent of 40% and above.

## Documents Required

> **Required Documents:- (Self-Attested Photocopy)**

<br>


1. Two passport-size photographs.
1. Residence Proof for the last three years duly attested (Voter Identity Card, Ration Card, Electricity Bill)
1. Copy of Disability Certificate/Identity Card with 40% Disability.
1. Affidavit regarding income as per specimen enclosed with the application form.
1. Recommendations of Medical Authorities (General Hospital, PGIMER, GMCH, or any Govt. Dispensary) regarding aid/ appliance required by the applicant. Driving license in case apply for a vehicle with gear.
