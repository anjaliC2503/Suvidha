## Brief Description

The scheme aims to raise awareness about aromatic plants and their benefits, and to provide guidance on cultivation practices suitable to different agricultural climates.

## Detailed Description

The “Aromatic Awareness Programme” is implemented by the Centre for Aromatic Plants (CAP), Selaqui, Dehradun, under the Department of Horticulture, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to raise awareness about aromatic plants and their benefits, and to provide guidance on cultivation practices suitable to different agricultural climates. 
<br>

## Benefits

- To spread information about aromatic plants and the benefits of their cultivation to the general public.
  - To provide information about crops according to the agricultural climate.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. Any interested individual who is a resident of Uttarakhand can avail the benefits of the programme.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Any person interested in aromatic plant cultivation can participate in the field-level awareness programmes organized by the Centre for Aromatic Plants (CAP), Selaqui, Dehradun.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this programme?

The main objective is to educate the general public about aromatic plants, their benefits, and cultivation practices suitable for different agricultural climates.
<br>

### Who can participate in this programme?

Any individual interested in aromatic plant cultivation and who is a resident of Uttarakhand can participate.
<br>

### What are the key activities under this programme?

- Spreading information about aromatic plants and their benefits.
- Providing guidance on crop selection according to agricultural climate.
- Organizing field-level awareness programmes.

<br>

### How can one avail the benefits of this programme?

Interested individuals can attend field-level awareness programmes organized by the Centre for Aromatic Plants (CAP), Selaqui, Dehradun.
<br>

### What kind of information will be provided in the programme?

Participants will receive information about aromatic plants, their medicinal and economic benefits, cultivation techniques, and climate-suitable crop selection.
