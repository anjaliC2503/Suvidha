## Brief Description

In the scheme "Mentally Retarded Children Benefit" by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, an amount of ₹20,000/- per annum is provided to the registered workers to take care of their mentally retarded/disabled children.

## Detailed Description

In the scheme "Mentally Retarded Children Benefit" by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Government of Himachal Pradesh, an amount of ₹20,000/- per annum is provided to the registered workers to take care of their mentally retarded/disabled children.

## Benefits

₹20,000/- per annum

## Eligibility

***For Registration***

1. The applicant should be a resident of Himachal Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be a Building or Construction Worker.
1. The age of the applicant should be between 18 and 60 years.
1. The applicant should have worked in building or construction activities for at least 90 days in the preceding 12 months.

_*The registration should be renewed annually._
***For Application***

1. The worker should be engaged in building or construction work under the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
1. The worker should be registered with the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
1. The worker's membership with the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board must be active.
1. The child of the worker should be mentally retarded/disabled and dependent on the registered worker.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Registration**
**Step 1:** Take the print of the [prescribed format](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/pdf/formno27_A1b.pdf) of the registration form.
**Step 2:****** In the form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste a passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:****** Submit the duly filled and signed registration form to the Labour Officer.
_* A one-time registration fee must be paid at the office, and it will be valid for three years._
_* After three years, the worker must visit the office to pay a renewal fee and submit proof of 90 days of work each year to maintain eligibility for benefits._

**Step 1:** Take the print of the [prescribed format](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/pdf/pension3539_A1b.pdf) of the application form.

**Step 3:****** Submit the duly filled and signed application form to the Labour Officer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Mentally Retarded Children Benefit Scheme?

The scheme, introduced by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HPBOCWWB), provides annual financial assistance of [?]20,000 to support the care of mentally retarded or disabled children of registered construction workers.

### How much financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

A financial aid of [?]20,000 per year is provided for the care and well-being of the mentally retarded or disabled child.

### Can both parents apply for assistance separately?

No, the benefit is provided per eligible child and only one parent can apply for assistance under the scheme.

### How can a worker register with HPBOCWWB?

A worker can register by:
Visiting the Labour Welfare Office in their jurisdiction.
Self-registering on the official portal of HPBOCWWB.

### What are the eligibility criteria for HPBOCWWB registration?

To register, a worker must:
Be between 18 and 60 years old.
Have completed at least 90 days of construction work.
Be a resident of Himachal Pradesh.

### Can a worker apply if they have more than one mentally retarded or disabled child?

Yes, if a registered worker has more than one eligible child, they can apply separately for each child.

### Is there a deadline for applying for the benefit?

There is no specific deadline, but the worker must have an active membership at the time of application. It is advisable to check with HPBOCWWB for any updates.

### How can the financial assistance be used?

The financial assistance is meant for the care and well-being of the mentally retarded or disabled child, including medical, educational, and other necessary expenses.

### How is the amount disbursed to the beneficiary?

The financial assistance is directly transferred to the bank account of the registered worker.

### Can a retired worker apply for the scheme?

No, only active registered workers under HPBOCWWB are eligible for the scheme.
