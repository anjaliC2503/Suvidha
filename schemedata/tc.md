## Brief Description

The "Tiatr Competition" aims to encourage and promote the unique Goan theatre form of Tiatr. Participation in this scheme is open to Goan institutions registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (with renewed registration). Entries are invited through press notes in local dailies.

## Detailed Description

The "Tiatr Competition" is organised annually by the Kala Academy under the Department of Art and Culture, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to encourage and promote the unique Goan theatre form of Tiatr. Participation in this scheme is open to Goan institutions registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (with renewed registration). Entries are invited through press notes in local dailies. The institutions fulfilling the scheme’s requirements and submitting entries within the stipulated date are allowed to stage their Tiatr performances as per the sequence finalised by Kala Academy.

## Benefits

- Free auditorium with permissible infrastructure (extra facilities chargeable).
- Hire charges paid for two vehicles for transportation of artistes and stage materials (as per Kala Academy norms).
- 80% of gate collection from ticket sales is given to the participating institution.
- Prizes awarded based on recommendations from a panel of judges.

1. The institution should be registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Kala Academy, Goa, and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from  the [**Office of Tiatr Academy of Goa**](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/3RD-ALL-GOA.pdf#:~:text=Detailed%20Rules%20and%20Regulations%20of%20the%20competition%2C,is%20June%206%2C%202025%20during%20office%20hours.),  Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Campal, Panaji, Goa.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the academy staff.
_*For more information, contact_ ***9158044466***_._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the eligibility criteria for institutions to participate in the annual Tiatr competition?

Only Goan institutions registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, with renewed registration, are eligible. The Tiatr must be staged as per the programme finalized by Kala Academy.

### How can my institution apply for the Tiatr competition, and what documents are required?

Submit the prescribed entry form before the deadline, along with a registration/renewal copy, ₹500 entry fee, and ₹5,000 refundable security deposit.

### Is there a fee for participating in the competition, and is any part of it refundable?

Yes, a non-refundable entry fee of ₹500 and a refundable security deposit of ₹5,000 must be paid at the time of application.

### What facilities are provided to participating institutions for staging their Tiatr performances?

Kala Academy provides an auditorium with basic infrastructure free of cost, while additional facilities are charged as per their rates.

### Are there any financial benefits for participating institutions apart from prizes?

Yes, institutions receive 80% of gate collections from ticket sales and vehicle hire charges for transporting artists and stage materials as per norms.

### How are the performances scheduled for participating institutions?

Kala Academy decides the sequence of performances, and all eligible entries submitted on time are allowed to stage their shows.

### What are the prize categories and amounts for the Best Performance in the competition?

In 'A' Group, prizes are ₹1,00,000 (1st), ₹75,000 (2nd), and ₹50,000 (3rd). 'B' Group offers ₹50,000 (1st), ₹40,000 (2nd), and ₹30,000 (3rd).

### Are there consolation prizes, and under what conditions are they awarded?

Consolation prizes of ₹25,000 each (for 'A' Group) and ₹20,000 (for 'B' Group) are given if there are more than 10 performances.

### What prizes are available for individual artists in acting categories?

Prizes range from ₹7,000 (1st prize) to ₹2,000 (2nd prize) for male/female actors, negative roles, and child artists in different groups.

### Are there separate awards for comedians, and how much are they worth?

Yes, best comedian (male/female) awards are ₹5,000 ('A' Group) and ₹2,500 ('B' Group).

### Are technical aspects like stage setting and lighting also awarded in the competition?

Yes, categories like Best Stage Setting, Light Effects, and Music offer prizes ranging from ₹2,500 to ₹9,000 depending on the group.

### What awards are given for music-related performances such as solo singing and choral groups?

Prizes include ₹2,000 (Best Solo Singer), ₹4,000 (Best Choral Singing), and ₹3,000 (Best Trio), varying between groups.

### Is there a category for Best Lyrics, and what is the prize amount?

Yes, Best Lyrics awards are ₹2,000 ('A' Group) and ₹1,000 ('B' Group).

### How are the winners selected in the competition?

A panel of judges appointed by Kala Academy evaluates performances and recommends prize winners as per scheme guidelines.
