## Brief Description

The scheme "International Financial Assistance" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, aims to promote participation of Gujarat sportspersons in international competitions through financial support of ₹75,000/- for travel, accommodation, and other related expenses.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "International Financial Assistance" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to encourage and support sportspersons from Gujarat participating in international-level competitions by providing a one-time financial assistance of ₹75,000/- to cover costs related to boarding, lodging, air travel, kit, or miscellaneous expenses. This initiative ensures that deserving athletes are not hindered by financial constraints in their pursuit of excellence on global platforms.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹75,000/- is provided to eligible sportspersons to cover expenses for boarding, lodging, travel (including air tickets), sports kits, and other related miscellaneous expenses.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a sportsperson representing Gujarat in any recognized international-level sports competition.
  - The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be male.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of the "International Financial Assistance" scheme?

The main purpose of the "International Financial Assistance" scheme is to provide financial support to Gujarat-based sportspersons so they can participate in international-level sports events without being hindered by the financial burden of travel, accommodation, and other related expenses.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the "International Financial Assistance" scheme?

Any male sportsperson who is a permanent resident of Gujarat and has been selected to participate in an officially recognized international-level sports competition is eligible to apply for the "International Financial Assistance" scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What amount is provided under the "International Financial Assistance" scheme?

Under the "International Financial Assistance" scheme, an amount of ₹75,000/- is provided to each eligible sportsperson to help cover the cost of air travel, lodging, boarding, sports kits, and other miscellaneous expenses related to participation in an international sports event.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme available for both male and female sportspersons?

As per the current eligibility criteria of the "International Financial Assistance" scheme, it is applicable only to male sportspersons; female athletes are not eligible under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit to avail the benefits under this scheme?

There is no individual or family income limit specified for availing the benefits under the "International Financial Assistance" scheme, making it accessible to all eligible male athletes regardless of their economic background.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of sports competitions are covered under this scheme?

The "International Financial Assistance" scheme covers officially recognized international-level sports competitions in which the sportsperson is representing Gujarat or the country as an official participant.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there a limit to the number of times a sportsperson can apply under the scheme?

The scheme allows sportspersons to apply on a per-event basis, which means a sportsperson can apply multiple times during different international competitions, provided each application meets the scheme's eligibility conditions and is submitted separately.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How can a sportsperson apply for the "International Financial Assistance" scheme?

A sportsperson can apply for the "International Financial Assistance" scheme by registering on the official website of the Sports Authority of Gujarat, logging in as a player, and filling out the application form along with uploading the necessary documents.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the key documents required to apply for the scheme?

To apply for the "International Financial Assistance" scheme, the applicant needs to submit documents such as the Accounts Audited by a Chartered Accountant, a valid Domicile Certificate proving Gujarat residency, and the duly filled online application form available on the portal.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the official website to apply for this scheme?

The official website where eligible sportspersons can register and apply for the "International Financial Assistance" scheme is: [https://sportsauthority.gujarat.gov.in](https://sportsauthority.gujarat.gov.in/).
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can the assistance amount be used for personal expenses not related to the sports event?

No, the financial assistance provided under the "International Financial Assistance" scheme is strictly meant to be used for expenses directly related to participation in the international sports competition, such as travel, accommodation, and equipment, and not for unrelated personal expenses.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is responsible for implementing and monitoring the scheme?

The implementation and monitoring of the "International Financial Assistance" scheme is managed by the Sports Authority of Gujarat, which is the designated implementing agency under the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>
