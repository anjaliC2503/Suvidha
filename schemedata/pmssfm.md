## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide scholarships to students belonging to minority communities who are studying in Classes 1 to 10 in any recognized government/non-government educational institution in Uttarakhand.

## Detailed Description

The “Minority Scholarship Scheme for Class 1 to 10” was launched by the Minority Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide scholarships to students belonging to minority communities who are studying in Classes 1 to 10 in any recognized government/non-government educational institution in Uttarakhand.
<br>

## Benefits

At present, the State Government provides the following **monthly scholarships** for students from Class 1 to 10:

- **Class 1 to 5:** ₹50/-- **Class 6 to 8:** ₹80/-- **Class 9 to 10:** ₹120/-
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must belong to a minority community (Muslim, Sikh, Christian, Parsi, Buddhist, or Jain).
1. The applicant must be studying in Classes 1 to 10.
1. The applicant must be enrolled in a recognized government/non-government educational institution in the state of Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Application URL:** https://scholarships.gov.in/#

- Minority community students studying in Classes 1 to 10 can register at any time through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP 2.0) at [https://scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/). Registration requires an Aadhaar number and an Aadhaar-linked mobile number.   - During registration, students provide basic information such as their name, parents’ names, and educational qualifications. Upon successful registration, a student ID and password are generated. 
  - For applying to the scholarship, the department publishes advertisements in newspapers or on the NSP portal, inviting online applications within a stipulated period. 
  - Students must log in using their registered ID and password or register if not already done, and select their school or institution during the application process.
  - While applying, students are required to upload mandatory documents, including educational certificates, Aadhaar card, bank account linked with Aadhaar, permanent residence certificate, self-declaration certificate of minority community, income certificate, certificate of studying in school, and a passport-size photograph. In addition, offline copies of these documents must be submitted to the school or institution for verification. 
  - Once submitted, the application reaches the school’s Institute Nodal Officer (INO), who verifies the documents and forwards the application to the State Nodal Officer (SNO). The SNO then forwards the applications to the Ministry of Minority Affairs, Government of India after a random selection process (2% of applications). 
  - If all documents and eligibility criteria are verified and found correct, the scholarship amount is directly transferred to the student’s bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for this scholarship?

Students belonging to minority communities, studying in Classes 1 to 10 in recognized educational institutions of Uttarakhand, are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What are the scholarship amounts provided under this scheme?

The monthly scholarship amounts are as follows: Class 1 to 5 – ₹50, Class 6 to 8 – ₹80, and Class 9 to 10 – ₹120.
<br>

### How can a student register for the scholarship?

Students can register anytime through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP 2.0) at [www.scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/) using their Aadhaar number and an Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
<br>

### What information is required during registration?

During registration, students must provide basic details such as their name, parents’ names, educational qualifications, and other personal information.
<br>

### How will students know when to apply for the scholarship?

The department publishes advertisements in newspapers and on the National Scholarship Portal inviting applications within a specified period.
<br>

### Do students need to submit physical copies of documents?

Yes, along with online submission, students must submit offline copies of documents to their school or institution for verification.
<br>

### How is the scholarship payment made?

Once the application and documents are verified and eligibility is confirmed, the scholarship amount is directly transferred to the student’s bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
<br>

### Is Aadhaar mandatory to apply for this scholarship?

Yes, students must have an Aadhaar number, and their bank account should be linked to Aadhaar.
