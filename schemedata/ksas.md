## Brief Description

In the scheme "Kharmore Sonchidia Award" by the Forest Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, the farmers are rewarded with ₹5,000/- if a Kharmor bird is sighted in the private land of the farmer and ₹10,000/- if a Golden bird is sighted in the private land of the farmer.

## Detailed Description

In the scheme "Kharmore Sonchidia Award" by the Forest Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, the farmers are rewarded with ₹5,000/- if a Kharmor bird is sighted in the private land of the farmer and ₹10,000/- if a Golden bird is sighted in the private land of the farmer. The farmers are rewarded provided they ensure the protection of the birds, their eggs, and their habitat on their private land.

## Benefits

**Reward of ₹5,000/- on Sighting of Kharmor Bird**

- Installment 1: ₹1,000/- if sighted in July-August
- Installment 2: ₹4,000/- if sighted in the same area in September-October

**Reward of ₹10,000/- on Sighting of Golden Bird**

- Installment 1: ₹2,000/- for the first sighting
- Installment 2: ₹8,000/- if sighted again in the same area

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a farmer or landowner.
1.  The applicant should own private land.
1. Either Kharmor Bird or Golden Bird should have been sighted on the applicant’s land.
1. The applicant should have ensured the protection of the birds, their eggs, and their habitat without causing harm.

## Application Process

### Offline

The interested farmer should make an appeal to the Concerned Divisional Forest Officer/Deputy Director.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Kharmore Sonchidia Award Scheme?

It is a reward scheme by the Forest Department of Madhya Pradesh for sighting and protecting rare birds on private land.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Farmers or landowners in Madhya Pradesh who spot Kharmor or Golden Bird on their land.

### How much reward is given for sighting the Kharmor bird?

[?]5,000 in two installments ([?]1,000 for July-August sighting and [?]4,000 for September-October sighting).

### How much reward is given for sighting the Golden Bird?

[?]10,000 in two installments ([?]2,000 for first sighting and [?]8,000 for a second sighting in the same area).

### What documents are required to claim the reward?

ID proof, land ownership proof, photographic evidence, verification report from the Forest Department, and bank details.

### How can I report a bird sighting?

Contact the nearest Forest Department office and provide necessary details.

### Who verifies the bird sighting?

The Forest Department officials will verify and confirm the sighting.

### Can I harm or capture the bird to claim the reward?

No, harming, capturing, or disturbing the birds or their eggs will disqualify you.

### How is the reward amount transferred?

The amount is transferred directly to the applicant's bank account.

### Where can I get more information?

Visit the nearest Forest Department office or check the Madhya Pradesh Forest Department website.
