## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote physical fitness, adventure awareness, and confidence-building among children through a 6-day rock climbing adventure course for 100 children aged 9 to 12 years, organized at Mount Abu/ Junagadh Center.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to develop a spirit of adventure, encourage physical endurance, and boost confidence and discipline among children belonging to Scheduled Tribes through a structured training program. The scheme provides selected students with an opportunity to participate in a 6-day residential rock climbing adventure course conducted at either Mount Abu or Junagadh. It focuses on holistic development by engaging youth in outdoor sports activities, thereby fostering physical and mental growth.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Training:** A 6-day rock climbing adventure course for 100 children aged 9 to 12 years, conducted at Mount Abu or Junagadh Center.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category.
  - The applicant should be between 9 and 12 years.
  - The applicant must be physically fit.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant should download the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** All required fields in the form should be completed carefully, and copies of the necessary documents must be enclosed (self-attestation may be required).
**Step 3:** Submit the properly filled and signed application form, along with all attached documents, to the District Youth Development Office.
**Step 4:** Ensure to obtain a submission receipt or acknowledgment slip from the official receiving the application for future reference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course"?

The objective of the scheme "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" is to encourage adventure-based physical training among children of the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category in Gujarat by providing them with the opportunity to attend a structured and supervised 6-day rock climbing training camp conducted at Mount Abu or Junagadh.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course"?

Children who are permanent residents of Gujarat, belong to the Scheduled Tribe category, and are between 9 and 12 years of age are eligible to apply under the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the duration and nature of the training provided under the scheme?

The training provided under the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" scheme is of 6 days’ duration and includes adventure activities focused on rock climbing along with physical fitness.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How many children are selected for the training under this scheme?

Under the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" scheme, a total of 100 children are selected per training session to participate in the adventure course at the designated centers.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which authority is responsible for implementing the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" scheme?

The District Youth Development Officer is responsible for implementing the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" scheme at the district level in coordination with the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the locations where the training is conducted?

The training under the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" scheme is conducted at two main centers, namely Mount Abu and Junagadh, depending on the training batch and schedule.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any fee charged for applying or participating in this scheme?

No fee is charged either for applying or for participating in the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course", as the application form is provided free of cost and the entire training, including accommodation and meals, is fully sponsored by the government.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

To apply under the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" scheme, the applicant is required to submit a self-attested copy of their date of birth certificate, caste certificate (ST), Aadhaar card, bank account details, fitness certificate, a consent letter from the guardian, and, if applicable, proof of participation in NCC/NSS or sports competitions.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Are any preferences given during the selection process?

Yes, during the selection process of the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" scheme, preference is given to children who are associated with NCC/NSS, have participated in the National Parade on 15th August or 26th January, or have secured 1st to 3rd position in Taluka, District, or State-level sports festivals.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme open to children from all communities or categories?

No, the "Rock Climbing Adventure Training Course" scheme is specifically designed for children belonging to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category and does not cover children from other social categories.
<br>
