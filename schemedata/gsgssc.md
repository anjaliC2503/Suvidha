## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage and motivate girl students studying in Classes 6 to 12 at Sanskrit schools in the state by providing financial scholarships to support their education.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Gargi Sanskrit Balika Chhaatravrtti Yojana” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Sanskrit Academy, Haridwar, under the Department of Sanskrit Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to encourage and motivate girl students studying in Classes 6 to 12 at Sanskrit schools in the state by providing financial scholarships to support their education.
<br>

## Benefits

**Scholarship Amount:** 

  - Girl students from classes 6 to 12 are provided ₹251/0 per month, at the rate of ₹3,012/- annually.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a girl student of any category.
1. The girl student must be studying from class 6 to class 12 in a Sanskrit school of Uttarakhand.
1. A total of 175 enrolled girl students will be eligible.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Institutional girl students of all categories studying in Sanskrit schools or colleges, who possess an Aadhaar card and a bank account, will submit their documents to the concerned Principal. The Principal will forward the documents along with their recommendation to the Assistant Director, Sanskrit Education, Uttarakhand. The Assistant Director will then forward them to the Director, and after the Director’s recommendation, the scholarship will be disbursed online by the Academy.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which students are eligible for the scholarship?

Girl students from class 6 to class 12 studying in Sanskrit schools of Uttarakhand, belonging to any category, are eligible.
<br>

### What is the amount of scholarship provided?

The scholarship amount is ₹251/- per month, totaling ₹3,012/- annually.
<br>

### How many students are covered under this scheme?

A total of 175 institutionally registered girl students are eligible for the scholarship.
<br>

### Are students of all categories eligible?

Yes, the scholarship is available for girl students of all categories studying in Sanskrit schools.
<br>

### How should a student apply for the scholarship?

Students must submit their documents to the Principal of their Sanskrit school or college.
<br>

### What documents are required for submission?

Aadhaar card, bank account details, and any other documents as required by the school for verification.
<br>

### Who approves the scholarship after the Principal’s recommendation?

The Assistant Director forwards the recommendation to the Director, whose approval is required before disbursement.
<br>

### How is the scholarship disbursed to the student?

After the Director’s recommendation, the scholarship is transferred online to the student’s bank account by the Uttarakhand Sanskrit Academy.
<br>
