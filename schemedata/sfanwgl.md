## Brief Description

The scheme aims to financially support new writers in the Gujarati language who wish to publish original works of creative and literary merit.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature&quot; has been designed to encourage first-time authors who lack financial means to publish original and high-quality creative works in the Gujarati language. Administered by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy, the scheme provides grants to cover partial printing costs for eligible manuscripts. Works eligible for assistance include novels, short stories, essays, poetry, and plays that promote cultural awareness, national unity, and intellectual development. The manuscript must be original, unpublished, and reviewed positively by expert evaluators.

## Benefits

> **Financial Assistance:**


- **For manuscripts with up to 112 pages:** Up to ₹10,000/-.- **For manuscripts exceeding 112 pages:** Up to ₹15,000/-.
> **Printing Size Guidelines:**


- **For “Crown” size:** ₹2,100/- (paperback), ₹2,200/- (hardcover).- **For “Demy” size:** ₹2,300/- (paperback), ₹2,500/- (hardcover).- **For “Double Crown” size:** ₹2,700/- (paperback), ₹2,800/- (hardcover).
> **Note:** The lower of the printing cost or the above ceiling will be granted as assistance.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a first-time writer in the Gujarati language and literature.
  - The manuscript must be an original literary work such as a novel, play, essay, poetry collection, or autobiography.
  - The manuscript must not be edited work or related to non-literary subjects.
  - The applicant must not have received more than five grants from any language academy affiliated with Gujarat Sahitya Academy.
  - The manuscript must be typed, bound, paginated, and submitted in two copies.
  - The applicant must not change the title, content, or format after submission without written permission from the Academy.

Note: Only one application per financial year is allowed per author.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature"?

The purpose of the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature" is to provide financial support to emerging Gujarati writers who wish to publish their first original literary work but are unable to afford printing costs due to financial constraints.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature", only new writers of the Gujarati language who are publishing their first book of original literary content are eligible to apply for financial assistance.
<br>

<br>

### What kinds of literary works are eligible for financial assistance?

Only original creative works in Gujarati such as novels, short stories, essays, plays, poetry collections, and autobiographies are eligible for financial assistance under this scheme, while edited compilations or non-literary books are not considered.
<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum financial assistance available under the scheme?

The maximum financial assistance provided under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature" is ₹10,000 for manuscripts up to 112 pages and ₹15,000 for those exceeding 112 pages, subject to the Academy's standard cost norms.
<br>

<br>

### Can a writer apply more than once under this scheme in the same financial year?

No, a writer can apply only once in a financial year under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature" and must not submit multiple applications.
<br>

<br>

### Are writers who have received financial assistance before eligible to apply again?

Writers who have already received financial assistance five or more times under any scheme of the Gujarat Sahitya Academy or its affiliated academies in other languages are not eligible to apply again under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What format should the manuscript be submitted in?

The manuscript must be submitted in two hard-bound, typed copies with proper pagination, clearly labeled with the author’s name, address, mobile number, and the title, and should not be submitted with loose pages or incomplete formatting.
<br>

<br>

### Can the author make changes to the manuscript after submission?

No, the author cannot make any changes to the manuscript, including its title, content, or formatting, after submission unless they receive written permission from the Gujarat Sahitya Academy.
<br>

<br>

### How is the final decision regarding financial assistance made?

The final decision under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature" is made after the manuscript is reviewed by expert evaluators, and only those manuscripts deemed of high literary merit are approved.
<br>

<br>

### What is the requirement for acknowledging the Academy’s support in the printed book?

The printed book must clearly state “Published with financial assistance from Gujarat Sahitya Academy” along with the Academy’s logo on the back cover and the inside front matter of the book.
<br>

<br>

### Can the grant amount be transferred to a publisher or a third party?

No, the financial assistance granted under this scheme is non-transferable and cannot be redirected to any private publisher or third-party organization under any circumstance.
<br>

<br>

### How many copies of the book need to be submitted to the Academy?

After publishing, the writer is required to submit 15 printed copies of the book free of cost to the Gujarat Sahitya Academy along with the original printing bills and approval documents.
<br>

<br>

### Within what time frame must the book be published after receiving approval?

The approved manuscript must be printed and submitted within one year of the grant approval under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to New Writers of Gujarati Language and Literature for Publishing Original Works of Creative and Fine Literature".
