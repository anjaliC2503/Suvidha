## Brief Description

Under this scheme, recognition is provided to Indian seafarers who risk their lives to save others or protect the marine environment.

## Detailed Description

The "Sagar Samman Award- Sagar Samman Award for Gallantry" scheme has been launched by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways under the Directorate General of Shipping, Government of India, aims to recognize extraordinary bravery in maritime situations. Under this scheme, recognition is provided to Indian seafarers who risk their lives to save others or protect the marine environment.
<br>

## Benefits

- Trophy
- Citation
- Individual Medals (if multiple persons)

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be an Indian seafarer serving on any vessel.
1. The applicant should have performed an act of exceptional bravery in adverse maritime conditions.
1. The applicant’s act should involve saving lives, property, or environment beyond normal duty.

<br>

### Online

**Step 02:** Register/Login using the official credentials of the recommending authority

**Step 04:** Upload required documents, including citation and verification certificate

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the Award?

Individuals working in maritime activities who demonstrate exceptional bravery and courage are eligible.
<br>

### What type of acts are considered for this award?

Acts involving life-saving, rescue operations, or extraordinary courage in challenging maritime situations are considered.
<br>

### Can the award be given posthumously?

Yes, the award may be conferred posthumously in deserving cases of gallantry.
<br>

### Is self-nomination allowed for this award?

Yes, self-nomination may be allowed as per the prescribed guidelines.
<br>

### How can nominations be submitted?

Nominations must be submitted through the official award portal.
<br>

### Who evaluates the nominations?

An expert committee evaluates the nominations based on the merit of the gallantry act.
<br>

### What does the award consist of?

The award generally includes a medal, citation, and formal recognition.
<br>

### How many awards are given each year?

The number of awards depends on eligible and deserving candidates each year.
<br>

### What is the main objective of this award?

The objective is to honor bravery and encourage courageous actions in maritime operations.
<br>

### When is the award announced?

The award is typically announced annually as per the official schedule.
<br>

### Can a nomination be rejected?

Yes, nominations can be rejected if they are incomplete or do not meet the eligibility criteria.
<br>
