## Brief Description

The "Khel Chhatravriti (Vritika) Yojana" implemented by the Directorate of Art, Culture, Sports, and Youth Affairs, Government of Jharkhand, provides financial assistance to athletes based on their performance to encourage sports as a career.

## Detailed Description

The "Khel Chhatravriti (Vritika) Yojana" implemented by the Directorate of Art, Culture, Sports, and Youth Affairs, Government of Jharkhand aims to financially support athletes and encourage them to focus on sports as a livelihood. Under this scheme, scholarships are provided to athletes based on their performance in recognized national and state-level competitions.

## Benefits

> ***Monetary Assistance Based on Performance***

**National championship winners:**

- First, Second, and Third Place: ₹6,000 per month.
- Fourth to Eighth Place: ₹4,000 per month.

<br>

**State-level winners qualifying for national championships:**

- First to Third Place: ₹3,000 per month.

<br>

**National record-equivalent performances:**

- ₹4,000 per month.

<br>

***Recognition for National Representation***

- Athletes representing Jharkhand after obtaining a No Objection Certificate from other states or organizations may receive an allowance equal to their existing honorarium/salary.

***Additional Support***

- Financial aid can be used for training, nutrition, and purchasing sports equipment.

## Exclusions

- The applicant availing any other sports-related stipend, scholarship, or honorarium are not eligible.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Jharkhand.
1. The applicant should be a sportsperson. 
1. Scholarships are awarded based on performance in the previous year's recognized national or state-level sports competitions.
1. The applicant must not receive any other scholarship or benefits under any other sports scheme, such as free diet, accommodation, or stipend.

<br>

<br>

**NOTE:** If the applicant is already receiving such benefits, they must forfeit them before applying for this scholarship.

## Application Process

### Offline

> **Visit the Official Website**


- Go to the [official website](https://sports.jharkhand.gov.in/Home.aspx) Directorate of Art, Culture, Sports, and Youth Affairs.
<br>

> **Download Application Form**


- Navigate to the "Notice" section, select "Khel Vritika (Sports Scholarship)," and download the [application form](https://sports.jharkhand.gov.in/notice_docs/6f2a344cFormat%20of%20Scholarship%20Application.pdf).- Fill Out the Form (details such as personal information, competition performance, and bank details).

<br>

> **Attach Documents**


- Attach all required documents as listed above. Ensure proper attestation wherever needed.

<br>

> **Submit the Application**


- Submit the completed form and attached documents to the Directorate of Art, Culture, Sports, and Youth Affairs or the Jharkhand State Sports Authority.

## Documents Required

1. Passport-Sized Photographs
1. Proof of Residence
1. Aadhaar Card
1. Certificates of Performance in Recognized Competitions (State/National level)
1. No Objection Certificate (if representing Jharkhand while employed/registered with another state/organization)
1. Proof of Age (e.g., Birth Certificate or School Leaving Certificate)
1. Bank Account Details (Copy of Passbook or Canceled Cheque)

<br>

**NOTE:** Applicants must submit detailed results, including dates, distances, points, and scores, verified by the respective associations.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for this scholarship?

Any athlete who is a permanent resident of Jharkhand and has performed exceptionally in recognized national or state-level competitions can apply.

### What is the amount provided under this scheme?

Scholarships range from [?]3,000 to [?]6,000 per month based on performance.

### Can the scholarship be renewed?

Yes, the scholarship is valid for one year and can be renewed if the athlete demonstrates progress or retains their skill level.

### Can an applicant receive benefits from other schemes simultaneously?

No, the applicant must forfeit any other sports-related benefits before applying.

### What are the selection criteria?

Athletes are selected based on their performance in recognized competitions and eligibility criteria.

### How is the scholarship amount disbursed?

The amount is disbursed monthly through the Directorate of Sports or Jharkhand State Sports Authority.

### Who reviews the applications?

Applications are reviewed by a selection committee headed by the Secretary of the Directorate of Art, Culture, Sports, and Youth Affairs.

### Are there any exclusions under this scheme?

Yes, athletes receiving other sports-related stipends or scholarships are not eligible.

### What documents are required for application submission?

Documents include proof of residency, performance certificates, age proof, and bank details.

### Where can I get the application form?

The application form is available on the official website: sports.jharkhand.gov.in.
