## Brief Description

The scheme aims to enhance the knowledge, skills, and productivity of farmers in Uttarakhand by providing them with the latest technical training in agriculture, horticulture, and forestry.

## Detailed Description

The “Various Training and Support Schemes of Kisan Vigyan Kendra” is implemented by the Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali Uttarakhand University of Horticulture and Forestry under the Department of Horticulture, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to enhance the knowledge, skills, and productivity of farmers in Uttarakhand by providing them with the latest technical training in agriculture, horticulture, and forestry.
<br>

## Benefits

Kisan Vigyan Kendra provides farmers with the latest technical training in agriculture, forestry, and horticulture, along with improved seeds and plant varieties.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. All farmers in Uttarakhand are eligible to receive the benefits.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

From time to time, the Krishi Vigyan Kendra contacts farmers and provides training through various programmes, as well as shares information through different media to promote improved seeds and plant varieties.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can benefit from this scheme?

All farmers of Uttarakhand are eligible to avail themselves of the benefits of this scheme.
<br>

### What types of training are provided under this scheme?

Farmers receive training in modern agriculture, horticulture, forestry, crop management, pest control, soil health, water management, and organic farming techniques.
<br>

### Are improved seeds and plants provided to farmers?

Yes, the Kisan Vigyan Kendra provides improved seeds and plant varieties to farmers as part of the training and support.
<br>

### How do farmers get information about these programs?

Farmers are contacted directly by Kisan Vigyan Kendra staff and also receive information through various media channels, including print, radio, and digital platforms.
<br>

### How can a farmer enroll in these training programs?

Farmers can enroll by contacting the nearest Kisan Vigyan Kendra.
<br>
