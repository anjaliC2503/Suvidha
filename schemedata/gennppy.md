## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide free textbooks to all students from Classes 1 to 8 studying in government and government-aided schools, as well as recognized madrasas that follow the state government curriculum.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Gunavatta Evam Navaachar Nishulk Paathy Pustak&quot; is a scheme under the Samagra Shiksha Programme, implemented by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free textbooks to all students from Classes 1 to 8 studying in government and government-aided schools, as well as recognized madrasas that follow the state government curriculum.
<br>

## Benefits

Every year, in accordance with the guidelines of the Directorate of Elementary and Secondary Education, free textbooks are provided to all students from Classes 1 to 8 in government schools, government-aided schools, and recognized madrasas following the state government curriculum.
<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student from Classes 1 to 8.
   1. The applicant must be studying in a government or government-aided school, or a recognized madrasa that follows the Uttarakhand state government curriculum.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

There is no selection process. Textbooks are distributed free of cost to all students from Classes 1 to 8 studying in the eligible schools.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who are the beneficiaries of this scheme?

All students from Classes 1 to 8 studying in government and government-aided schools, as well as recognized madrasas following the state curriculum, are eligible.
<br>

### Which classes are covered under the scheme?

Students from Classes 1 to 8 are covered under this scheme.
<br>

### Which schools are included in the scheme?

Government schools, government-aided schools, and recognized madrasas that follow the Uttarakhand state government curriculum are included.
<br>

### Is there any fee for the textbooks?

No, textbooks are provided completely free of cost.
<br>

### Is there any selection process to receive the textbooks?

No, there is no selection process.
<br>

### Are both boys and girls eligible?

Yes, textbooks are provided to all boys and girls studying in the eligible schools.
