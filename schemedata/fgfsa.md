## Brief Description

The scheme "Financial Grant for Sports Associations" implemented by the Tourism, Art-Culture, Sports, and Youth Affairs Department, Government of Jharkhand, provides financial assistance to recognized sports associations for organizing sports activities and promoting talent.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Financial Grant for Sports Associations" by the Tourism, Art-Culture, Sports, and Youth Affairs Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to support recognized sports associations in organizing and promoting various sports activities in the state. Under this scheme, financial grants are provided to sports associations that meet the eligibility criteria to conduct training camps, host competitions, and encourage sports development.

## Benefits

- Financial grants are provided to recognized state-level sports associations for organizing sports competitions and training camps.
- Assistance is offered for conducting training camps before national-level competitions for selected players.

<br>

**NOTE:** 

- The grant includes coverage for competition organization costs, training camp expenses, and administrative support.
- The funds are disbursed based on the recommendations of the committee constituted under Jharkhand Sports Policy 2022.

## Exclusions

- Associations operated by individuals or private groups are not eligible.
- If two or more associations apply for the same sport, only the association recognized by the Jharkhand Olympic Association and National Federation will be considered.

## Eligibility

1. The association should be a recognized state sports association registered under the Jharkhand State Registration Department.
1. The association must be affiliated with the Jharkhand Olympic Association and the relevant National Sports Federation.
1. The association association should have district-level units functioning in at least 18 districts of the state.
1. Para-sports associations or associations related to sports included in the Olympics, Asian Games, or Commonwealth Games are eligible. Associations for other sports require at least 15 affiliated districts.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- **Obtain the Application Form:** Download the prescribed [application form](https://sports.jharkhand.gov.in/notice_docs/5a00b45dKhel%20Sangho%20ko%20Anudan%20Prapatra%20Annexure-I.pdf) from the Directorate of Sports and Youth Affairs' official website.- **Fill Out the Form:** Provide complete details about the association, including registration details, affiliations, and proposed activities.- **Attach Documents:** Attach all required documents, such as audit reports, PAN card, and bank account details.- **Submit the Application:** Submit the filled application form along with documents to the Directorate of Sports and Youth Affairs, Ranchi, or through the online application portal.
<br>

***Additional Requirements:***

- The association must have a functional independent website.
- A PAN card and a nationalized bank account are mandatory.
- The association must submit the audit report of the last three years.
- Associations affiliated with National Federations receiving central government grants for specific projects cannot apply for state grants for the same purpose.

## Documents Required

1. Registration Certificate (from the Jharkhand State Registration Department)
1. Affiliation Letter (from the Jharkhand Olympic Association and the National Sports Federation)
1. PAN Card
1. Bank Account Details (Nationalized Bank) with Proof
1. Audit Reports of the Last 3 Years
1. An Undertaking Ensuring Compliance with Departmental Guidelines
1. Details of District-Level Units (at least 18 districts)
1. Supporting Documents for the Proposed Sports Activities
1. An Official Letter Declaring the Association’s Independent Website Link

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for the grant?

State-level sports associations registered with the Jharkhand Registration Department and affiliated with the Jharkhand Olympic Association and National Sports Federations are eligible.

### Are para-sports associations eligible for this scheme?

Yes, para-sports associations are eligible if they meet the criteria mentioned under the scheme.

### Can multiple associations for the same sport apply for the grant?

No, only the association recognized by the National Federation and Jharkhand Olympic Association will be considered.

### What is the mode of fund disbursement?

Funds are disbursed directly to the association's nationalized bank account after approval.

### Are associations required to submit utilization reports?

Yes, associations must provide audited utilization reports for the grant received.

### Can private clubs or institutions apply for the grant?

No, private clubs or institutions are not eligible for this scheme.

### What documents are required for application?

Key documents include registration certificates, affiliation letters, audit reports, PAN card, and bank account details.

### Is there any restriction on the type of sports supported under the scheme?

Sports included in the Olympics, Asian Games, Commonwealth Games, and other recognized events are prioritized.

### Where can I get the application form?

The application form can be downloaded from the official website of the Directorate of Sports and Youth Affairs, Jharkhand.

### Is it mandatory to have an independent website?

Yes, having an independent, functional website is mandatory for all associations applying for the grant.
