## Brief Description

This scheme provides capital subsidy for setting up fish feed plants with a minimum production capacity of 100 tons per day, under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) to general , schedule caste and women beneficiaries .

## Detailed Description

The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Fish Feed Plants of Production Capacity of at least 100 Ton/Day, introduced by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry &amp; Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide capital subsidy for setting up fish feed plants with a minimum production capacity of 100 tons per day. The objective is to enhance the availability of high-quality and affordable feed for fish farmers, supporting the expansion of aquaculture in Haryana. This is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme, under which the project is jointly funded by the Central and State Governments: 50% of the subsidy amount is contributed by the Central Government, and 50% is provided by the State Government. Beneficiaries are expected to operate and maintain the plant independently and ensure the supply of feed to fishers at affordable prices.
<br>

## Benefits

- The project cost is ₹650,00,000/-

> **Subsidy Distribution :**


1. A 40% subsidy is available for the General Category.
1. A 60% subsidy is offered for SC (Scheduled Caste) and Women beneficiaries.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Haryana and possess a valid Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).
1. The applicant must own land or hold a registered lease of at least 10 years.
1. The applicant must be willing to operate, maintain, and manage the feed plant after its construction.
1. The applicant must ensure that the feed plant provides an affordable feed supply to fishers and fish farmers.
1. The applicant must submit a valid Detailed Project Report (DPR) or Self-Contained Proposal (SCP) in accordance with PMMSY guidelines.
1. The applicant must hold a valid training certificate relevant to feed mill operation and maintenance.
1. The applicant must ensure that the land is free from legal disputes and must provide a valid land title.

## Documents Required

1. Date of Birth Proof (Voter ID/Birth Certificate/Aadhaar/Matriculation)
1. Caste Certificate (if applicable – from Tehsildar/SDM)
1. Agreement Deed with Fisheries Department
1. Training Certificate related to fish feed plant operation/maintenance
1. Land Records (Jamabandi, Fard, Registered Patta – with clear title)
1. GST Bills of project purchases
1. Photographs of feed plant with beneficiary
1. Bank Account Details and PAN Card
1. DPR or SCP as per PMMSY norms
1. CA Certificate verifying bill payments follow GST/Income Tax rules
1. All bills above ₹50,000 should comply with GST/e-way/IT norms
1. CLU/NOC/Fire/Pollution Clearance from respective departments
1. NOC from Gram Sabha / Local Body / Municipal Authority
1. Clear Site Plan/Area Plan
1. All documents must be in legible/readable form

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much subsidy can I get as a General Category applicant?

You are eligible for a 40% subsidy on the total project cost if you belong to the General Category.

### I am a woman applicant. How much subsidy will I receive under this scheme?

As a woman applicant, you will receive a 60% subsidy on the total project cost.

### Do I need to submit any identity document while applying?

Yes, you must submit a valid Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) to be eligible for the scheme.

### If I own the land, what kind of land records do I need to submit?

You need to provide Jamabandi, Nakal Akshizra, Fard, or Patta Nama with a clear land title and latest records.

### What kind of training certificate do I need to attach?

You must attach a training certificate related to the operation or maintenance of a fish feed mill.

### Do I need to submit a project plan while applying for the scheme?

Yes, you must submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or a Self Contained Proposal (SCP) as per PMMSY guidelines.

### Is it necessary to attach photographs of the plant setup?

Yes, you must attach photographs of the fish feed mill with the beneficiary.

### Are there any fees I need to pay for applying to this scheme?

There is no government charge, but a service charge of ₹10 is applicable at the Atal Seva Kendra.

### How long will it take for my application to be processed?

The processing time for your application is 50 days under the Right to Service (RTS) Act.

### Do I need to submit a site plan of the fish feed plant with my application?

Yes, a clear Site Plan or Area Site Plan must be submitted along with your subsidy application.
