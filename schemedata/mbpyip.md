## Brief Description

The Mukhyamantri Balika (Intermediate Pass) Protsahan Yojana is a scheme by the Bihar government aimed at promoting girl child education, curbing child marriages, reducing fertility rates, and empowering girls to become self-reliant.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Mukhyamantri Balika (Intermediate Pass) Protsahan Yojana&quot;, a component of the &quot;Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana&quot;, launched by the Education Department, Bihar, focuses on the socio-economic upliftment of girls in Bihar. The scheme provides monetary incentives to unmarried girls who have cleared their intermediate examinations from recognized schools or madrasas within Bihar.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹10,000/- for girls passing intermediate in 2021; ₹25,000/- in 2022 and later.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Bihar.
- The applicant must be an unmarried girl.
- She must have passed the intermediate examination from a recognized school, madrasa, or institution in Bihar.
- The examination must have been conducted in 2021 or later.
- The applicant must have a valid bank account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the the "Mukhyamantri Balika (Intermediate Pass) Protsahan Yojana"?

The scheme aims to promote girl child education, reduce child marriages, lower fertility rates, and empower girls to become self-reliant.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Unmarried girls who have passed their intermediate (Class 12) examinations from recognized schools or madrasas within Bihar are eligible for the scheme.

### How much financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

Eligible girls are provided [?]10,000/- if they have passed the intermediate examination in 2021 and [?]25,000 for those who have passed in 2022 or later.

### How will the financial assistance be provided?

The financial assistance is directly transferred to the beneficiary's bank account through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system.

### Can married girls apply for the scheme?

No, only unmarried girls are eligible for the scheme.

### Are students from private schools eligible?

Only students from recognized government or government-aided schools, madrasas, or project schools in Bihar are eligible.

### Can an applicant apply more than once?

No, the scheme benefits can be availed only once.

### Is there any fee for registration or application?

No, the registration and application process for the scheme is free of cost.
