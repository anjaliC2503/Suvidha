## Brief Description

The scheme provides early-stage funding to support idea validation and proof of concept for technology-based and deep-tech innovations through PRAYAS Centres (PCs) and Advance PRAYAS Centres (APCs).

## Detailed Description

NIDHI – Promoting and Accelerating Young and Aspiring Innovators and Startups (PRAYAS) is a scheme of the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India. The scheme supports young innovators and startups in transforming innovative ideas into prototypes or proof of concept (PoC) by providing financial assistance of up to ₹10 lakh. It also offers access to incubation infrastructure, mentorship, technical and financial guidance, and industry linkages through Technology Business Incubators (TBIs) and PRAYAS Shalas (Fab Labs). The scheme aims to promote indigenous innovation, technology-based entrepreneurship, and the development of commercially viable products in line with the Atmanirbhar Bharat Mission.
**Objectives**
**The objectives of NIDHI – PRAYAS are :-**

- To enable translation of an innovative idea to a prototype.
- To provide a platform for faster experimentation and modify approaches in the idea to market journey.
- To indigenously generate innovative solutions relevant to the local and global problems.
- To attract a large number of youth who demonstrate problem solving zeal and abilities to work on their new technology/knowledge/innovation-based startups.
- To enhance the pipeline in terms of quality and quantity of innovative start ups to the incubators. To build a vibrant innovation ecosystem, by establishing a network between innovators, academia, mentors and incubators.

<br>

## Benefits

• Level 1 – PCs: Each innovator/startup supported by a Level 1 Centre PC may receive up to ₹20 Lakhs grant for prototyping. DST provides up to ₹2 crore per annum to each PC.
• Level 2 – APCs: Provides prototyping grants of up to ₹40 lakh per innovator/ startup. DST provides up to ₹4 crore per annum to each APC.
<br>

## Eligibility

- A DPIIT recognised startup or an individual innovator are eligible to apply under the program.


- In case of a recognised startup, it has to be incorporated in India, not more than 5 years of existence and its annual turnover for any financial year should not have exceeded ₹1 crore.

## Application Process

### Online

**Application For Startup Incubation Centres**
Startup Incubation Centres / TBIs looking to implement the NIDHI-PRAYAS programme may apply for the same through open call-for-proposals published on the NIDHI-PRAYAS [PMU Website.](https://www.nidhi-prayas.org/)
**Application For Startups and Innovators**
Interested Students and Innovators may apply for support under NIDHI – PRAYAS through [NIDHI – PRAYAS Centres ](https://www.nidhi-prayas.org/prayascenters.html)(at selected TBIs).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the NIDHI–PRAYAS 2.0 scheme?

NIDHI–PRAYAS 2.0 is an initiative of the Department of Science and Technology (DST) that provides early-stage funding and prototyping support to help innovators convert technology ideas into prototypes and viable startups.
<br>

### Who can apply for the scheme?

Individual innovators and DPIIT-recognised startups can apply through a PRAYAS Centre (PC) or Advance PRAYAS Centre (APC).
<br>

### What types of innovations are supported?

The scheme supports technology-based and deep-tech innovations across all sectors that have the potential for commercialization.
<br>

### Is the scheme limited to any specific sector?

No. NIDHI–PRAYAS 2.0 is a sector-agnostic scheme and supports innovations from any technology domain.
<br>

### What can the grant be used for?

The grant can be used for prototype development, proof of concept (PoC), product validation, testing, and other approved project-related expenses.
<br>

### Are incubator facilities provided?

Yes. Selected innovators receive access to incubation facilities, technical infrastructure, mentorship, business guidance, and industry networking through the PRAYAS Centres.
<br>

### Can an individual without a registered startup apply?

Yes. Individual innovators can apply. If selected, they may be required to register a startup as per the scheme guidelines before receiving support.
<br>

### How can I apply for the scheme?

Applications are submitted online through the designated PRAYAS Centre or Advance PRAYAS Centre via the NIDHI–PRAYAS portal.
<br>

### How are applications selected?

Applications are evaluated by the respective PRAYAS Centre or Advance PRAYAS Centre based on innovation, technical feasibility, commercialization potential, and overall impact.
<br>

### Where can I find the official guidelines and apply?

Applicants can visit the official NIDHI–PRAYAS portal or the DST website to access the operational guidelines and submit applications through the designated incubators.
<br>
