## Brief Description

"Back Ended Investment Subsidy to Paddy Growers" component of the "Crop Production Technology" scheme was introduced by the Dept of Agriculture and Farmer's Welfare, UT of Puducherry. The paddy farmers residing in the Yanam region are provided a back-ended investment subsidy at ₹4,000/- per hectare.

## Detailed Description

The "Back Ended Investment Subsidy to Paddy Growers" component of the "Crop Production Technology" scheme was introduced by the Department of Agriculture and Farmer's Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the paddy farmers residing in the Yanam region are provided a back-ended investment subsidy at ₹4,000/- per hectare.

## Benefits

Back-ended investment subsidy to paddy growers @ ₹4,000/- per hectare.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident/native of Puducherry.
- The applicant should be residing in the Yanam region.
- The applicant should be a Farmer.
- The applicant should possess land holdings.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) one of the offices given below, and request the hard copy of the [prescribed format](https://agri.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/forms/backendpad.pdf) of the application form from the concerned authority.

- Agricultural Officers of the [Uzhavar Udhaviyagam](https://agri.py.gov.in/emailcontact.html).- [Office ](https://agri.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/citchart/response.pdf)of the Deputy Director of Agriculture, Yanam.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form and the documents to the concerned authority.
**Step 4:** On receipt of the application, necessary entries will be made in the application receipt register and a unique number is assigned to the applicant for identifying each applicant which shall be used for all further references and also as acknowledgement for the receipt of the application.

**Step 4:** Visit the [Official Website](https://edistrict.py.gov.in/) of the e-District Portal of the Government of Puducherry.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the subsidy applicable to all farmers in Puducherry?

No, only paddy farmers residing in the Yanam region are eligible for the subsidy.

### Name the offices from where an applicant can obtain the application form.

The Agricultural Officers of the Uzhavar Udhaviyagam and the Office of the Deputy Director of Agriculture, Yanam.

### What is the amount of the back-ended investment subsidy provided to paddy growers?

Paddy growers receive a subsidy of [?]4,000/- per hectare under this scheme.

### Who administers the "Crop Production Technology" Scheme in the Union Territory of Puducherry?

The Department of Agriculture and Farmer's Welfare, Union Territory of Puducherry, administers the scheme.

### Are there provisions for additional incentives or bonuses within the subsidy scheme?

There are no explicit provisions for additional incentives or bonuses within the current scheme.

### Are there any provisions for adjusting the subsidy amount based on crop yield or quality?

No, the subsidy amount remains fixed at [?]4,000/- per hectare and is not adjusted based on crop yield or quality.

### Can the subsidy amount be disbursed in instalments, or is it a lump sum payment?

The subsidy amount is provided as a back-ended investment and is typically disbursed as a lump sum payment.

### Is there a maximum limit on the total subsidy amount a farmer can receive under this scheme?

There is no specified maximum limit; however, the subsidy is calculated per hectare of land cultivated.

### Is the subsidy amount subject to income tax, and does the farmer need to declare it?

The subsidy amount is generally exempt from income tax, but farmers are advised to consult with a tax professional for specific details.

### Can the subsidy amount be transferred directly to the bank account of the beneficiary?

Yes, the subsidy amount is often transferred directly to the bank account of the eligible paddy farmer.

### How does the government verify the authenticity of the applicant's residency in Yanam?

The Residence-cum-Nativity Certificate, issued by the competent authority, serves as a crucial document for verifying residency authenticity.

### Can a farmer with holdings in multiple regions of Puducherry avail the subsidy for each region?

No, the subsidy is specific to the Yanam region, and holdings in other regions do not make the farmer eligible for this scheme.
