## Brief Description

The "Production of Foundation and Certified Seeds" scheme, provides subsidy to farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified seeds to the Department of Agriculture.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Production of Foundation and Certified Seeds” by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, is aimed at encouraging farmers to produce high-quality seeds. Under this initiative, farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds to the Department of Agriculture are eligible to receive a subsidy.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,000/- per quintal.

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary should be a Farmer.
  - The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.
  - The beneficiary should produce and supply Foundation or Certified seeds to the Department.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office.
**Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). 
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office:
> **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer.

> **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer.

> **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.

## Documents Required

- Proof of identity (Aadhar card, Voter ID)
  - Land ownership documents or lease agreement.
  - Certification of seed production and supply.
  - Bank account details.
  - Any other documents (if required).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the “Production of Foundation and Certified Seeds” scheme?

It is a state-sponsored scheme that offers a subsidy to farmers for producing and supplying Foundation and Certified seeds.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Any farmer who produces Foundation or Certified seeds and supplies them to the Department.

### What is the subsidy provided under this scheme?

Farmers receive [?]1,000/- per quintal as a subsidy for the seeds they produce and supply.

### How to apply for the scheme?

Applications can be submitted to the Agricultural Officer at the village, block, or district level.

### What documents are required to apply?

You need proof of land ownership, identity proof, certification of seed production, and bank account details.

### Is there any income restriction to apply for the scheme?

No, there are no income criteria for this scheme.

### Is the scheme available to all communities?

Yes, the scheme is open to all farmers, irrespective of community.

### Is there an age limit to apply for the scheme?

No, there is no age restriction for applicants.

### What kind of seeds are eligible under the scheme?

Only Foundation and Certified seeds are eligible for the subsidy.

### Whom to contact for more information?

You can contact the Agricultural Officer at the village, block, or district level.
