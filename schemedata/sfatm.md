## Brief Description

The Government of Gujarat has introduced the "Scheme for Awards to MSMEs" under the Industrial Policy 2020. This scheme aims to recognize and reward Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) for their outstanding achievements in production, quality improvement, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme for Awards to MSMEs&quot; was introduced by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the  Gujarat Industrial Policy. This scheme aims to recognize and reward Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) for their outstanding achievements in production, quality improvement, innovation, and entrepreneurship. The scheme shall be effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

> **1. Separate awards to be given for Micro, Small and Medium category:**

**Category:** Three awards to be awarded in each category-

  - Growth in production and profit
  - Quality and environment improvement measures
  - Innovation in technology for new product or process development

**Award Money:** Amount of ₹2,00,000/-, Trophy and Appreciation letter to each winner.
<br>

> **2. Awards to Best MSEs Entrepreneur in the following category one to each:**

**Category:** One award to be awarded in each category-

  - Woman Entrepreneur
  - Young Entrepreneurs (first generation Entrepreneurs below 35 year age)
  - SC Entrepreneur
  - ST Entrepreneur

**Award Money:** Amount of ₹2,00,000/-, Trophy and Appreciation letter to each winner..
<br>

> **3. Awards for Commercially Successful Best MSME Start-up:**

**Award Money:** Amount of ₹2,00,000/-, Trophy and Appreciation letter to each winner.

## Eligibility

1. Enterprises shall be in continuous production for the previous three years.
   1. Enterprises shall have valid statutory clearances and licenses as may be applicable.

<br>

**Note****:** MSME/Start-up will be entitled for only one award in each category during operative period of the scheme in each category.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** MSME Commissionerate invites applications through advertisements in local newspapers.
 **Step-2:** Enterprises must submit the prescribed application form along with the required documents.
 **Step-3:** A working committee reviews applications and ranks them.
 **Step-4:** The State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) selects the awardees.
 **Step-5:** MSME Commissionerate publishes the final list of selected enterprises.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Scheme for Awards to MSMEs"?

The scheme aims to recognize and reward MSMEs in Gujarat for their excellence in production, innovation, quality improvement, and entrepreneurship.

### Who is eligible to apply for the MSME awards?

Any Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise (MSME) registered under the MSME Development Act, 2006, that has been in continuous production for the past three years and holds valid statutory clearances is eligible to apply.

### What is the prize for the winners of the MSME awards?

Each award winner receives [?]2,00,000/- , a trophy, and an appreciation letter from the Government of Gujarat.

### How many award categories are available under this scheme?

The scheme includes separate awards for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises based on growth in production and profit, quality and environmental improvement, and technological innovation. Additionally, there are special awards for Women, Young, SC, and ST entrepreneurs, as well as a category for the Best MSME Start-up.

### Can an enterprise receive multiple awards under this scheme?

No, an enterprise is eligible for only one award in each category during the operative period of the scheme.

### How are applications evaluated under this scheme?

Applications are reviewed by a working committee based on various criteria, including production growth, innovation, quality improvements, and environmental measures. The final selection is made by the State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC).

### When will the results of the awards be announced?

The MSME Commissionerate announces the list of selected awardees after the evaluation process is completed.

### Can newly established MSMEs apply for the awards?

No, only MSMEs that have been in continuous production for at least three years are eligible to apply.

### Is the scheme applicable to enterprises located outside Gujarat?

No, only MSMEs registered and operating in Gujarat are eligible for the scheme.

### How can an enterprise apply for the awards?

The MSME Commissionerate invites applications annually through advertisements in local newspapers. Enterprises must submit the prescribed application form along with the necessary documents.

### Where can enterprises get more information about the scheme?

Enterprises can contact the MSME Commissionerate, Gujarat, for further details regarding the application process, eligibility, and award selection criteria.
