## Brief Description

The objective of the scheme is to arrange for education and rehabilitation of children with disabilities by providing them free hostel, education-training, food, clothing and residential facilities.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Government Institutions for Teaching and Training of Persons with Disabilities&quot; was implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. The objective of the scheme is to arrange for the education and rehabilitation of children with disabilities by providing them free hostel, education-training, food, clothing, and residential facilities.

## Benefits

- Under the scheme, beneficiaries will receive education, pre-professional training, and free hostel facilities in the concerned government institutions.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Chhattisgarh.
   1. The applicant should have a disability of 40% or more.
   1. The applicant&#39;s age should be more than 6 years and less than 10 years.
   1. The monthly income of the applicant&#39;s parents/guardians should be less than ₹8,000/-.

## Application Process

### Offline

On receipt of application, admission will be given as per eligibility on the recommendation of the committee constituted in the concerned institution.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the key benefits provided under this scheme?

Beneficiaries receive education, pre-professional training, and free hostel facilities in government institutions.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

The applicant must be a resident of Chhattisgarh, have a disability of 40% or more, be between 6 and 10 years old, and have a family income of less than [?]8,000 per month.

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

Required documents include proof of residence, disability certificate, age proof, income certificate, and any other documents specified by the concerned institution.

### Where can I obtain the application form for this scheme?

The application form can be obtained from the concerned government institution or the Social Welfare Department's official website.

### What is the application process for the scheme?

Submit the completed application form along with required documents to the concerned government institution.

### How are admissions determined under this scheme?

Admissions are based on eligibility criteria and are determined by a committee constituted in the concerned institution.

### Can a child with a disability less than 40% apply for the scheme?

No, only children with a disability of 40% or more are eligible.

### What age group is eligible for this scheme?

Children aged between 6 and 10 years are eligible to apply.

### What is the income limit for applicants' families?

The monthly income of the applicant's parents/guardians should be less than [?]8,000/-.

### What type of training is provided under the scheme?

The scheme offers pre-professional training suited to the needs of children with disabilities, preparing them for future educational and professional opportunities.

### What facilities are provided in the government institutions?

Facilities include free education, hostel accommodation, food, clothing, and necessary rehabilitation services.
