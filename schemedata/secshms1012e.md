## Brief Description

Under this scheme, children of sanitation workers who have achieved first, second, or third rank in the board exams of Class 10 and Class 12 (General Stream and Science Stream) across the state are awarded cash prizes and certificates of appreciation.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme to Encourage the Children of Sanitation Workers Who Have Passed the Class 10/12 Examination&quot; is implemented by the Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam (GSKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. This scheme has been in operation since the year 2012-13.
The objective of the scheme is to promote and encourage the academic excellence of children of sanitation workers who have passed the Class 10 or Class 12 board examinations with top ranks. Under this scheme, students securing first, second, or third rank in the board examinations (General and Science streams) across the state are awarded cash prizes and certificates of appreciation.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Cash Prize Details:**

**Rank****10th Class****12th Class (Science Stream/General Stream)**First Rank₹ 41,000/-₹ 31,000/-Second Rank₹ 21,000/-₹ 21,000/-Third Rank₹ 11,000/-₹ 11,000/-<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
1. The applicant should be a student.
1. The applicant should be a child of a sanitation worker engaged in scavenging activities.
1. The applicant should have passed the SSC (Class 10) or HSC (Class 12) board examinations conducted during the current academic year to be eligible under this scheme.
1. There is no annual income limit to avail the benefits of this scheme.

> **Note:** Applicants who secure the first, second, or third rank among the online applications received will be eligible for benefits under this scheme.

## Application Process

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Students who are children of sanitation workers engaged in scavenging activities and have passed Class 10 or Class 12 board exams with top ranks are eligible.
<br>

### Is there an income limit to apply?

No. There is no income restriction for eligibility.
<br>

### Which department implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam (GSKVN) under the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>

### Is it necessary for the applicant to be a resident of Gujarat?

Yes, the applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
<br>

### What types of board exams are considered under this scheme?

The SSC (Class 10) and HSC (Class 12 – Science/General stream) board exams held in March/April of the respective year are considered.
<br>

### What ranks are eligible for the award?

Students who secure first, second, or third rank among all online applicants will be eligible for the award.
<br>

### Where can I check the status of my application?

On the e-Samaj Kalyan Portal under “[Application Status](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx)”.
<br>

### Is the scheme available every year?

Yes, the scheme is implemented annually based on board examination results.
