## Brief Description

This scheme provides free BIS type II concentrate feed to pregnant cattle owned by General Category livestock owners in Gujarat to ensure their good health, boost immunity, prevent metabolic diseases, and enhance milk production.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Assistance of Concentrate Feed for Pregnant Animals (General Category)" introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support livestock owners during the last trimester of cattle pregnancy—a critical period for both the animal and the calf. Through this scheme, 150 kg of BIS Type II concentrate feed is provided free of cost to eligible beneficiaries to enhance milk yield, extend the milking period, and prevent health complications.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a livestock owner.
1. The applicant must belong to the General Category.
1. The animal/ cattle should be in the last trimester of pregnancy.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Animal Husbandry Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on &quot;Apply New&quot; button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the &quot;Update Application&quot; button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application.
**Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) 
> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).

> **Note:** Required Documents can also be submitted in hard copies to the office mentioned in the application form within 7 days.

## Documents Required

1. Barcoded ration card.
1. Government approved photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.).
1. Certificate of natural breeding or artificial insemination of the animal.
1. Animal pregnancy diagnosis certificate.
1. Disability certificate from a competent authority (only if the applicant is disabled).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of the 'Assistance of Concentrate Feed for Pregnant Animals (General Category)' scheme?

The scheme is designed to provide nutritional support to pregnant animals during their last trimester to improve their health, enhance immunity, prevent metabolic diseases, and increase milk production.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to benefit from this scheme?

Livestock owners who belong to the General Category are eligible to receive assistance under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What type and quantity of feed is provided under the scheme?

Under the scheme, each eligible beneficiary receives 150 kg of BIS Type II concentrate feed free of cost.
<br>

<br>

### How often can a beneficiary receive the feed assistance?

The assistance can be claimed once every year by eligible livestock owners.
<br>

<br>

### Are livestock owners from Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes eligible under this scheme?

No, this scheme is specifically intended for livestock owners from the General Category.
<br>

<br>

### Does the beneficiary have to pay anything for the concentrate feed?

No, the concentrate feed is provided as 100% assistance, and the beneficiary does not have to bear any cost.
<br>

<br>

### What is BIS Type II concentrate feed?

BIS Type II concentrate feed is a balanced, high-quality nutritional supplement approved by the Bureau of Indian Standards, suitable for pregnant animals.
<br>

<br>

### Can the same livestock owner apply again next year?

Yes, the benefit can be availed once every year by the same eligible livestock owner.
<br>

<br>

### How can a livestock owner apply for the scheme?

A livestock owner can apply by completing an online registration on the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)portal.
