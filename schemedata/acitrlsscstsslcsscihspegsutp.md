## Brief Description

The scheme aims to award cash incentives to encourage students in their education by recognizing top three regional rank holders and those scoring centum in theory subjects in Secondary School Leaving Certificate/Secondary School Certificate/Intermediate/Higher Secondary Exams in Govt. Schools.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Award of Cash Incentive to the Toppers at Regional Level and Students Scoring Centum in the Subjects Involving Theory in Secondary School Leaving Certificate/Secondary School Certificate/Intermediate/Higher Secondary Public Examinations in Government Schools in the Union Territory of Puducherry" was launched by the Department of School Education, Puducherry. The quantum of Award ranges from ₹10,000/- up to ₹20,000/- for top ranks depending on the position, and ₹5,000/- per subject for centum scores.
<br>

## Benefits

- For students securing top three ranks at the regional level in Secondary School Leaving Certificate/Secondary School Certificate/Intermediate/Higher Secondary Public Examinations in Government Schools: 1st Rank: ₹20,000/-; 2nd Rank: ₹15,000/-; 3rd Rank: ₹10,000/-.
- For students scoring centum in the subjects involving theory in Secondary School Leaving Certificate/Higher Secondary Course Examinations: ₹5,000/- per subject.


- **Mode of Disbursement:** The award will be given through Direct Benefit Transfer or by Demand Draft.- **Frequency:** The award is disbursed to the eligible beneficiaries every year.- **Tie-Breaker Condition:** In the event of more than one student getting equal marks, the concerned full prize amount will be distributed to each of them in full. This distribution includes the marks secured by way of revaluation at a later date as prescribed by the concerned Board.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a citizen of India.
- The applicant must be a resident of the Union territory of Puducherry, by virtue of birth or continuous residence of not less than five years.
- The applicant must be a student appearing in Secondary School Leaving Certificate/Secondary School Certificate/Intermediate/Higher Secondary Public Examinations in Government Schools.
- The applicant must have passed the qualifying examination in the first attempt.
- The applicant's marks from consecutive attempts must not be taken into account.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

****The process is primarily an internal selection methodology conducted by the administering department based on examination results.***
##### **Step 1:****** ***Preparation of the List of Eligible Students***

- The Directorate of School Education, Puducherry, will prepare the initial list of eligible students for the Award of cash incentive based on the total marks obtained in the respective Public Examination.
- This preparation utilizes the result analysis published by the concerned Board (Secondary School Leaving Certificate/Secondary School Certificate/Intermediate/Higher Secondary Public Examinations).

##### **Step 2:****** ***Final Determination in Case of Disputes***

- If any doubts or disputes arise concerning the selection or eligibility during the implementation of the Scheme, the matter shall be referred to the Secretary to Government (Education).
- The decision made by the Secretary to Government (Education) regarding the interpretation or clarification of any provision shall be considered final.

##### ***Disbursement of the Award***

- Once the list of eligible beneficiaries is finalized annually, the corresponding cash award will be disbursed through either Direct Benefit Transfer or by Demand Draft.
- If multiple students secure equal marks, the full prize amount corresponding to the rank achieved will be distributed in full to each student, even if the marks were secured via revaluation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### If I achieve a centum score in a qualifying theory subject, how much money will I receive for each individual subject award?

The cash award provided for scoring a centum in subjects involving theory in Secondary School Leaving Certificate or Higher Secondary Course Examinations is ₹5,000/- per subject.
<br>

### What is the specific quantum of the cash incentive that a student is eligible to receive for achieving the Second Rank at the regional level?

The award amount instituted for students who secure the Second Rank at the regional level in the respective Public Examination is ₹15,000/-.
<br>

### To be considered eligible for this financial recognition program, is it a mandatory requirement that the applying student possesses Indian citizenship?

Yes, the student must be a citizen of India to fulfill the basic requirements for receiving the award.
<br>

### What is the established procedure if two or more students happen to obtain the exact same total marks, resulting in a tie for a ranked position?

In the event of a tie, the concerned full prize amount corresponding to that rank will be distributed equally and in full to each of the students who share the equal marks.
<br>

### If I achieve the First Rank at the regional level in my Public Examination, what is the exact amount of the cash award I will receive?

Students securing the First Rank at the regional level in either the Secondary School Leaving Certificate or Higher Secondary level receive ₹20,000/-.
<br>

### Besides being a citizen of India, what specific requirements regarding my residence must I meet within the Union territory of Puducherry?

The student must be a resident of the Union territory of Puducherry, either by virtue of birth or through continuous residence for a minimum period of five years.
<br>

### Which specific administrative regions within the Union territory of Puducherry are covered under the scope of this regional ranking scheme?

The awards for regional toppers are applicable across the four regions: Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe, and Yanam.
<br>

### Is there a specific condition related to the number of attempts I must take to pass the qualifying examination to be eligible for the financial award?

Yes, the student must have successfully passed the qualifying examination on their first attempt to meet the eligibility requirements.
<br>

### Could you please clarify which specific Public Examinations qualify a student for receiving a cash incentive under these guidelines?

The examinations covered are the Secondary School Leaving Certificate, Secondary School Certificate, Intermediate, and Higher Secondary Public Examinations.
<br>

### Are students attending any type of recognized educational institution allowed to apply for these merit-based cash incentives?

No, the award is applicable only to students (both boys and girls) appearing in the Public Examinations from Government Schools.
<br>

### Do the cash incentives offered for achieving a perfect score apply to all subjects, including both theory and practical components of the curriculum?

The cash incentive for a perfect score is awarded specifically to students scoring centum only in subjects that involve a theory component.
<br>

### If I secure the Third Rank among all eligible Government School students within my specific region, what financial incentive will be provided?

Students who achieve the Third Rank at the regional level in either the Secondary School Leaving Certificate or Higher Secondary level are awarded ₹10,000/-.
<br>

### If I attempt the qualifying examination more than once, will the scores from those subsequent or consecutive examinations be considered for ranking?

No, marks obtained from any subsequent or consecutive attempts after the initial sitting shall not be taken into account for determining eligibility or ranking.
<br>

### If my final marks increase after requesting a revaluation from the concerned Board, will these new, higher marks be considered for the cash award?

Yes, marks secured by way of revaluation at a later date, as prescribed by the concerned Board, are included for considering the awards and resolving ties.
<br>
