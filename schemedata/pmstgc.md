## Brief Description

The scheme aims to train and create a professional cadre of geriatric caregivers to meet the increasing demand for specialized care for senior citizens. It offers skill development and certification in geriatric care job roles to Indian citizens, ensuring professional services for the elderly.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "PM-Special Training of Geriatric Caregivers" was launched by the Department of Social Justice & Empowerment, Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Govt. of India. The scheme aims to bridge the gap in supply and increasing demand in the field of geriatric caregivers, provide more professional services to senior citizens, and create a cadre of professional caregivers in geriatrics. The scheme provides training in four specific job roles: Geriatric Caregiver (Institutional & Home Care), Elderly Caretaker (Non-Clinical), Elderly Care Companion, and Wellness Therapist for Elderly, with an emphasis on physical, mental, and overall well-being of the elderly through a multi-disciplinary approach, including on-job training for specific courses. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Justice & Empowerment with the help of the National Institute of Social Defence. The applications for this scheme are accepted through registration on the Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH).
<br>

## Benefits

> ***Skill Training***

The scheme provides training to candidates in four specific job roles: Geriatric Caregiver (Institutional & Home Care) (480 hours), Elderly Caretaker (Non-Clinical) (360 hours), Elderly Care Companion (450 hours), and Wellness Therapist for Elderly (570 hours).
> ***On-Job Training (OJT)***

For job roles as mandated by the National Council for Vocational Education and Training, On-Job Training is provided, with its duration guided by the approved qualification pack.
> ***Assessment and Certification***

The skill assessment is conducted by empaneled Assessment Agencies, and a certificate is made available to candidates in physical and digital form, along with corresponding 'credits' as per the National Credit Framework of MSDE. The assessment fee is provided under the scheme.
> ***Re-assessment Opportunity***

Every candidate is offered one chance of re-assessment free of cost, to be borne from the scheme.
> ***Placement Support***

Apprenticeship is promoted, and training linked to captive placements (training and placement within the same industry) will be encouraged. Trainees are also encouraged to participate in Rozgar Melas for placement, with Training Institutes having tie-ups for proper placement.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a citizen of India.
- The applicant must meet the minimum age as specified in the respective job role.
- The applicant must be less than 40 years of age.
- The applicant must have the minimum education qualification as per the required job roles.
- The applicant must not indulge in any substance abuse.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

##### **Official Website**
[https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in](https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in)
<br>

**Step 1:****** ***Account Registration***
Navigate to the Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH) at https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in, and click "Register". Provide your mobile number and verify via OTP. Provide your email ID. Set a strong password of minimum 8 characters. Complete the basic registration form and proceed to profile creation.
**Step 2:****** ***Profile Creation and e-KYC***
Fill in your complete personal details including full name, date of birth, gender, category, and current address. Enter your Aadhaar number and click "Verify Aadhaar" for automatic e-KYC. The system will auto-populate your basic details and send OTP to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number for final authentication. Ensure all information matches exactly with your Aadhaar card. Upload the required documents in the specified file format and file size.
**Step 3:****** ***Course and Institute Selection***
Browse through available geriatric caregiver training courses. Select your preferred training course. Choose up to three empaneled training institutes in order of preference. The system will allocate seats based on availability and your preference order during final selection process.
**Step 4:****** ***Application Review and Submission***
Carefully review all entered information, uploaded documents, and selected preferences in the application preview section before submission. Read and accept the terms and conditions, scheme guidelines, and declaration. Click "Submit Application" to generate unique Application Reference Number (ARN) and receive confirmation SMS and email.
<br>

##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 1:****** ***Verification and Selection***
The application verification (15-30 working days) by Project Appraisal Committee under Joint Secretary of DoSJE. Selected candidates receive training institute allocation, batch schedule, and joining instructions through registered communication channels.
**Step 2:****** ***Training Enrollment and Support***
Report to assigned training institute as per schedule provided in selection notification with original documents for physical verification. Complete the free training program. Upon successful completion, receive government-recognized certification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What types of training courses are available for aspiring geriatric caregivers and what are their respective durations?

Four distinct courses are offered: Geriatric Caregiver (Institutional & Home Care) for 480 hours, Elderly Caretaker (Non-Clinical) for 360 hours, Elderly Care Companion for 450 hours, and Wellness Therapist for Elderly for 570 hours. All courses focus on comprehensive elderly care with varying specializations.
<br>

### Do I need to maintain a minimum attendance percentage during the training period to be eligible for assessment?

Yes, you must maintain more than 70% biometric attendance throughout the entire training duration to qualify for the final assessment. This attendance requirement applies to all types of training courses offered under this program.
<br>

### What essential documents must I possess to prove my eligibility for enrollment in the training program?

You need valid documentary proof of your age, educational qualifications, and current residence address. Additionally, possession of a valid Aadhaar card is mandatory for registration and profile verification through the Skill India Digital Hub platform.
<br>

### Are there any age restrictions that I need to meet to be considered eligible for this training program?

You must meet the minimum age requirement specified for your chosen job role, but your age should not exceed 40 years. The exact minimum age varies depending on the specific course you wish to pursue.
<br>

### What educational qualifications are required for different job roles offered in the geriatric caregiver training program?

The minimum education qualification varies according to each specific job role you choose to pursue. You must verify that you meet the educational requirements specified for your selected course before applying for enrollment.
<br>

### Can I participate in this training program if I am not an Indian citizen or permanent resident?

This training program is exclusively available to all citizens of India only. You must be an Indian citizen to be eligible for enrollment in any of the four geriatric caregiver training courses offered.
<br>

### Is there any restriction regarding substance abuse that could affect my eligibility for the training program?

Yes, you should not be involved in any form of substance abuse to be eligible for this program. This is a mandatory requirement that ensures you can provide professional and reliable care services to elderly individuals.
<br>

### How will my profile verification be conducted after registering on the Skill India Digital Hub platform?

Your profile verification will be completed through mobile-based One Time Password electronic Know Your Customer verification. This digital verification process ensures the authenticity of your registration details and facilitates secure access to the training platform.
<br>

### What is the maximum number of trainees allowed in practical training sessions compared to theoretical classes?

Practical training classes are limited to a maximum of 25 trainees per session, while theoretical classes can accommodate up to 50 trainees. This difference ensures adequate hands-on learning opportunities and personalized attention during practical skill development.
<br>

### Will I receive any study materials or handbooks as part of the training program without additional cost?

Yes, you will receive a participant handbook and study materials at the start of your training program. These materials are provided as part of the training cost and do not require any additional payment from your side.
<br>

### How many free re-assessment opportunities will I receive if I fail the initial certification assessment?

You will be offered one free re-assessment opportunity under this program if you do not pass the initial certification assessment. This re-assessment cost will be covered by the scheme without any additional expense to you.
<br>

### What happens if I need to undergo On the Job Training as part of my selected course curriculum?

If your chosen course includes On the Job Training, the Training Provider will arrange suitable establishments and coordinate your placement. You must maintain proper attendance during this period, and the final assessment will occur only after completing the On the Job Training component.
<br>

### Is the training conducted entirely through online mode or do I need to attend physical classroom sessions?

The training follows a 100% classroom-based approach where both theoretical and practical sessions are conducted physically at the affiliated Training Centre. Online or remote learning options are not available under this program structure.
<br>

### Will I receive both physical and digital certificates upon successful completion of the training and assessment?

Yes, upon successful completion, you will receive certificates in both physical and digital formats. Additionally, you will receive corresponding credits as per the National Credit Framework of Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship.
<br>
