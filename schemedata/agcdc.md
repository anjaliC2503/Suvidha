## Brief Description

The scheme "All Goa Classical Dance Competition" is open for Bharatnatyam and Kathak disciplines. Only solo dance will be considered for the competition. Costumes and accompanists are to be arranged by the participants at their own cost. Open to local students between 12 to 20 years of age.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "All Goa Classical Dance Competition" by the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Goa, is open for Bharatnatyam and Kathak disciplines. Only solo dance will be considered for the competition. Costumes and accompanists are to be arranged by the participants at their own cost. Local students in the age group of 12 and 20 years can participate.

## Benefits

- 1st Prize: ₹5,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹3,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹2,000/-, Five Consolation Prizes: ₹500/- each.
- Each participant will receive ₹500/- (lump sum) towards travel expenses, along with one accompanist.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a local student.
- The applicant should be between 12 and 20 years of age.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should request the prescribed format of the application form free of charge from the **Kala Academy, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Campal, Panaji, Goa.**
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the **Member Secretary, Kala Academy, Goa**. 
**Authority for Redressal of Grievances:** Member Secretary/Chairman, Kala Academy, Goa.
_*Applications in the prescribed format will also be invited through the local newspapers_

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the classical dance forms eligible for participation in this competition?

Only Bharatnatyam and Kathak dance disciplines are eligible for participation in this competition.

### Is group or solo performance allowed for this dance competition?

The competition only considers solo dance performances; group performances are not allowed.

### Who is eligible to participate in terms of age and residency?

Local students between the ages of 12 and 20 years are eligible to participate.

### Do participants need to arrange their own costumes and accompanists?

Yes, participants must arrange their own costumes and accompanists at their own cost.

### Is there any entry fee required to participate in this competition?

No, there is no entry fee for participation in this competition.

### How can applicants submit their entries for the competition?

Applications must be submitted in the prescribed format as advertised in local newspapers.

### Who prepares the final competition schedule and programme?

The final programme of the competition will be prepared by Kala Academy.

### Is there any financial assistance provided for travel expenses?

Yes, each participant and their accompanist will receive a lump sum of ₹500 for travelling expenses.

### What is the prize money for the first-place winner?

The first prize winner will receive ₹5,000.

### Are there any consolation prizes, and how much are they worth?

Five consolation prizes of ₹500 each will be awarded.

### Can the general public attend the competition, and is there an entry fee?

Admission to the competition is free for all members of the public.

### Where should applicants submit their competition applications?

Applications must be submitted to the Member Secretary, Kala Academy Goa.

### Who can be contacted for grievance redressal regarding the competition?

The Member Secretary or Chairman of Kala Academy Goa can address any grievances.

### Is there a specific format for the application, and where can it be found?

Applications must be in the prescribed format, which will be advertised in local newspapers.
