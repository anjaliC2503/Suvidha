## Brief Description

The “Pay & Play Scheme” aims to provide state-level players with training and practice opportunities at the Mega Sports Complex, Hotwar, Ranchi.

## Detailed Description

The “Pay & Play Scheme” was launched by the Department of Sports & Youth Affairs, Government of Jharkhand. The scheme aims to provide state-level players with training and practice opportunities at the Mega Sports Complex, Hotwar, Ranchi. The scheme includes 11 sports disciplines: athletics, badminton, lawn tennis, table tennis, shooting, gymnastics, basketball, taekwondo, volleyball, wrestling, and swimming.

## Benefits

- Training in 11 diverse sports disciplines: athletics, badminton, lawn tennis, table tennis, shooting, gymnastics, basketball, taekwondo, volleyball, wrestling, and swimming under professional supervision.
- Free registration and membership for school students (up to Class 10th) with the principal’s recommendation.- Free registration and membership for international/national participants and medalists.
- Athletics players enjoy 15 days of free access (non-repetitive).

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Jharkhand.  
1. The applicant should be a sportsperson.
1. The applicant should actively participate in state-level sports.
1. The students up to class 10th with a recommendation from the school principal and National/international participants or medalists are eligible to apply.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant downloads the [application form](https://sports.jharkhand.gov.in/documents/Pay-and-Play.pdf) the official website. 
**Step 2:** Fill out the form with accurate details and attach the required documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the form at the Jharkhand State Sports Promotion Society office.
**Step 4:** Pay the registration fee (₹50) and the relevant membership fee, if applicable.
<br>

**NOTE:** 

- Other athletes are willing to pay registration and membership fees.
- Fee Table (https://sports.jharkhand.gov.in/documents/Pay-and-Play.pdf)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Pay and Play Scheme?

It is a sports training initiative by the Jharkhand government to provide state players access to world-class training facilities.

### What sports are included in the scheme?

Athletics, badminton, lawn tennis, table tennis, shooting, gymnastics, basketball, taekwondo, volleyball, wrestling, and swimming.

### Who can apply for the scheme?

Any Jharkhand resident actively involved in sports or willing to train under the scheme.

### Are there any fee exemptions?

Yes, school students (up to Class 10th) and national/international medalists are exempt from fees.

### How can I register for the scheme?

Registration can be done via the Jharkhand State Sports Promotion Society's office or website.

### What is the registration fee?

[?]50 for general registration, with additional membership fees based on the chosen sport.

### Are there any free training provisions?

Athletics players can avail of a 15-day free training period (non-repetitive).

### Is there a limit to the number of participants?

Yes, slots are allocated on a "first-come, first-served" basis.

### Where is the training conducted?

At the Mega Sports Complex, Hotwar, Ranchi.

### What documents are required for registration?

A filled application form, ID proof, and relevant supporting documents such as medal certificates or recommendation letters.

### Can athletes from outside Jharkhand apply?

No, this scheme is exclusively for Jharkhand residents.

### Can I register online?

Forms can be downloaded online, but submission must be done at the office.

### Who to contact for more information?

Contact Mr. S.S. Prasad (8986788422) or Mr. Chintu Singh (99358830).
