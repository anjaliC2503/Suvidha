## Brief Description

Loan scheme offering up to ₹15 lakh at 4% simple interest to unreserved category students for MBBS, PG, or equivalent courses abroad with flexible collateral and repayment conditions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme provides **financial loan assistance** to students from the **unreserved category** to pursue **higher education abroad.** Loans up to **₹15 lakh** are granted at simple interest of **4% per annum**, with flexible repayment options. Security and repayment conditions depend on the loan amount. This scheme aims to support meritorious, economically weaker students for global education opportunities.

## Benefits

**Loan Amount:** Up to ₹15.00 lakh
**Interest Rate:** Simple interest at 4% per annum
**Collateral:**
**Up to ₹7.50 lakh:** Property valued at 1.5 times the loan amount
**Above ₹7.50 lakh:** Property equal to the loan amount
**Loan Repayment:**
**Up to ₹5 lakh:** repay in 5 years after 1-year moratorium
**Above ₹5 lakh**: repay in 6 years after 1-year moratorium
<br>

**Borrower can pre-pay without penalty**

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a **citizen of India** and a **resident of Gujarat.**1. The applicant must belong to the **Unreserved category**.1. The applicant must have passed **Class 12 with 60% or more marks**.1. The applicant must be seeking admission to **MBBS, Master’s, PG Diploma, or equivalent programs abroad.**1. The applicant’s annual family income must be **₹6.00 lakh or less.**

## Application Process

### Online

1. Visit the Official Website:[ **https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in**](https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in)1. Click on “**Register**” and fill in your basic details such as name, email, mobile number, and create a password.1. **Login** to Your Account.1.  From the dashboard, select the “**Foreign Study Loan**” scheme .1. Fill the Online Application Form.
1. Upload Required Documents.
1. Submit the Application.
1.  Save or note down your **unique application ID** for future reference.1. Print the Application Form.
1. Submit the signed hard copy and original documents for verification **at the GUEEDC office or as directed**.

## Documents Required

1. Class 12 Marksheet (60% or above)
1. Unreserved category proof
1. Income certificate (₹6 lakh or less)
1. Admission letter to foreign university
1. Collateral documents (immovable property details):
1. For up to ₹7.5 lakh: property worth 1.5 times of loan amount
1. Above ₹7.5 lakh: property worth 100% of loan amount
1. Post-dated cheques (5 nos.) in favor of Corporation
1. Identity proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID/Passport)
1. Passport copy and visa (once issued)
1. Bank passbook or cancelled cheque

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this loan?

Students from unreserved category with **60%+ in Class 12** and **annual income ≤ ₹6 lakh.**

### What courses are covered under this scheme?

MBBS, Master's degrees, PG diplomas, or equivalent programs abroad.

### What is the maximum loan amount available?

₹15 lakh.

### What is the rate of interest?

Simple interest at **4%** per annum.

### Is there any collateral required?

**Yes.** Property must be mortgaged—1.5× value for loans ≤ ₹7.5 lakh, 1× for higher amounts.

### When does the repayment start?

One year after course completion.

### How long is the repayment period?

**5 years** for loans ≤ ₹5 lakh, and **6 years** for loans > ₹5 lakh.

### Can I repay the loan early?

**Yes,** prepayment is allowed without penalty.

### Do I need to submit post-dated cheques?

**Yes,** five post-dated cheques in favor of the Corporation.

### Where do I apply?

**Visit the Official Website:** [**https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in**](https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in)
