## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support eligible NCC cadets through financial incentives to encourage continued participation in education and NCC training. Under this scheme, scholarship amounts ranging from ₹600/- to ₹2,400/- are provided annually to eligible NCC cadets at group and unit levels.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Mukhyamantri Chhaatrvrtti Yojana&quot; has been launched by the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to support eligible NCC cadets through financial incentives to encourage continued participation in education and NCC training. Through this scheme, scholarship amounts ranging from ₹600/- to ₹2,400/- are provided annually to eligible NCC cadets at group and unit levels. Annual cash scholarships are awarded at both group and unit levels to junior and senior division NCC cadets. The scheme is implemented by designated Group Headquarters and Unit Offices under the administrative control of the State Directorate of NCC.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Group Level:**



  - For Senior Division/Senior Wing cadets: ₹2,400/-.
  - For Junior Division/Junior Wing cadets: ₹1,200/-.


> **Unit Level:**



  - For Senior Division/Senior Wing cadets: ₹1,200/-.
  - For Junior Division/Junior Wing cadets: ₹600/-.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be an NCC cadet.
   1. The applicant must have passed the 10th or 12th class or an equivalent examination in the previous academic session.
   1. The applicant must have completed two years of NCC training.
   1. The applicant must have maintained a minimum of 80% attendance in each year of NCC training.
   1. The applicant must be currently pursuing higher education.
   1. The applicant must not be receiving any other general or special scholarship from the State Government.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The Group Headquarters/Units collect applications in the prescribed format from cadets of schools/colleges where NCC is functional.
**Step 2:** Each Group Headquarters constitutes a &quot;Board of Officers&quot; to review and verify applications.
**Step 3:** The board also handles the processing of cadets’ application forms and all related documents.
**Step 4:** The complete application set, including the filled form and caste certificate certified by the competent authority, is submitted to the Directorate.
**Step 5:** Upon approval at the Directorate level, the scholarship amount is sanctioned and disbursed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Mukhyamantri Chhatravritti Yojana"?

The objective of the "Mukhyamantri Chhatravritti Yojana" is to provide financial support to deserving NCC cadets so that they can continue their education and remain motivated to actively participate in NCC training and activities.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Mukhyamantri Chhatravritti Yojana"?

Students who are enrolled as NCC cadets and have successfully passed either Class 10 or Class 12 in the previous academic session, and who are currently pursuing higher education, are eligible to apply under this scholarship scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Is the scholarship available to both Junior Division and Senior Division NCC cadets?

Yes, the scheme is available to cadets in both the Junior Division/Junior Wing and the Senior Division/Senior Wing categories who meet the required eligibility criteria.
<br>

<br>

### What is the scholarship amount provided under the "Mukhyamantri Chhatravritti Yojana"?

Under the scheme, a scholarship amount of ₹2,400/- is awarded to 3 senior cadets at the group level, ₹1,200/- to 3 junior cadets at the group level, ₹1,200/- to 18 senior cadets at the unit level, and ₹600/- to 16 junior cadets at the unit level.
<br>

<br>

### What are the training requirements for applicants of this scheme?

To be considered eligible, the applicant must have undergone a minimum of two years of NCC training and must have maintained at least 80% attendance in NCC parades during each year of training.
<br>

<br>

### Are cadets receiving other government scholarships eligible for this scheme?

No, cadets who are already receiving any special scholarships from the government, other than the regular state scholarships for general students, are not eligible to apply under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any limitation on the number of cadets selected from each group or unit?

Yes, from each group, only 3 senior and 3 junior cadets are selected, and from each unit, 18 senior and 16 junior cadets are selected based on merit and eligibility.
<br>

<br>

### Is it mandatory for cadets to continue their education to receive this scholarship?

Yes, only those cadets who are currently pursuing higher education are eligible, as continuing education is a mandatory criterion for receiving this scholarship.
<br>

<br>

### Can applications for the "Mukhyamantri Chhatravritti Yojana" be submitted online?

No, the application process for this scheme is entirely offline, and the applications are collected through designated Group Headquarters or Units in schools and colleges where NCC is active.
<br>

<br>

### How are cadets selected for the scholarship under this scheme?

A Board of Officers constituted by the Group Headquarters evaluates and verifies the application forms and supporting documents, and forwards the recommended applications to the Directorate for final approval and disbursement.
<br>
