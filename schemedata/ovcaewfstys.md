## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote and enhance scientific attitude and skill development of women, women scientists, teachers, and young students of the state by organizing conclaves and felicitating them.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Organization of Various Conclaves and Felicitations to Encourage Women, Women Scientists, Teachers and Young Students” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Science Education and Research Centre (USERC) under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote and enhance scientific attitude and skill development of women, women scientists, teachers, and young students of the state by organizing conclaves and felicitating them.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, to promote and enhance scientific attitude and skill development among students, teachers, and women of the state, the following conclaves are organized and felicitations are given:

  - Yuva-Bal Samagam: Medhavi Chhatra Samman
  - Teacher/Science Conclave: Vigyan Prasar Samman
  - Woman Scientists Conclave: Utkrisht Yuva Mahila Vaigyanik Samman
  - Mahila Prayogdharmi Samman

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand state. 
1. The applicant must be a student, teacher, woman, and the general public.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- USERC organizes district-wise Bal Yuva Samagams. The top five meritorious students from each district, who achieve the highest marks in High School/12th Grade under the Uttarakhand Board, are honored. The merit list is provided by the District Education Officer of the respective district.
  - The Science Teachers&#39; Conclave honors Uttarakhand’s science teachers and science-oriented educators with the Vigyan Prasar Samman to encourage and promote the exchange of ideas. Applications are invited from teachers across the state for the award, and advertisements are also published in newspapers.
  - USERC organizes the Women Scientists Conclave and the Utkrisht Yuva Mahila Vaigyaanik Samman Programme every year to encourage women scientists. Applications are invited from women scientists across the state, and advertisements are published in newspapers.
  - To identify and encourage women making exemplary contributions to the integration of science in the state, they are honored with the Mahila Prayogdharmi Samman on the occasion of International Women’s Day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to participate in this scheme?

Students, teachers, women, and the general public of Uttarakhand are eligible.
<br>

### What are the main conclaves organized under this scheme?

- Bal Yuva Samagam
- Teacher/Science Conclave
- Women Scientists Conclave
- Mahila Prayogdharmi Samman

<br>

### Who is honored in the Teacher/Science Conclave?

Uttarakhand's science teachers and science-oriented educators are honored with the Vigyan Prasar Samman.
<br>

### Which award is given in the Women Scientists Conclave?

The Utkrisht Yuva Mahila Vaigyaanik Samman is awarded to women scientists.
<br>

### When is the Mahila Prayogdharmi Samman awarded?

It is awarded on the occasion of International Women’s Day to women doing exemplary work in science integration.
<br>

### How are students selected for Bal Yuva Samagam?

The top five meritorious students from each district who achieve the highest marks in High School/12th Grade under the Uttarakhand Board are selected.
<br>

### Who provides the merit list for students?

The District Education Officer of the respective district provides the merit list.
<br>

### How are women scientists selected for the Utkrisht Yuva Mahila Vaigyaanik Samman?

Applications are invited from women scientists across Uttarakhand, and shortlisted candidates are honored.
<br>

### What is the frequency of these conclaves?

These conclaves and awards are organized annually.
