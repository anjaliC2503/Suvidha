## Brief Description

The "Diesel VAT Relief Scheme for Fishermen" introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance by subsidizing the VAT levied on high-speed diesel used in fishing boats.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Diesel VAT Relief Scheme for Fishermen&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support fishermen by subsidizing the VAT on diesel, making fuel more affordable for their mechanized boats. This helps reduce the operational cost of fishermen using mechanized boats.

## Benefits

**Name of Component****Standard of Assistance****Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits**Diesel VAT assistancePer litre maximum assistance up to ₹15/-Assistance is available as per fixed diesel quota throughout the year

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a registered diesel cardholder.
  - The applicant must own a mechanized fishing boat.
  - The boat should be less than 20 meters in length.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Fishermen must obtain a token number from [https://fisheries.gujarat.gov.in/Registration.aspx using their personal details.](https://fisheries.gujarat.gov.in/Registration.aspx)
**Step 2:** Diesel cardholder fishermen must purchase fuel from government-approved diesel pumps.
**Step 3:** The VAT relief is credited automatically via an online system.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Diesel VAT Relief Scheme for Fishermen" scheme?

The "Diesel VAT Relief Scheme for Fishermen" provides financial assistance by subsidizing the VAT levied on high-speed diesel used in fishing boats. This helps reduce the operational cost of fishermen using mechanized boats.

### Who is eligible to get benefit from this scheme?

Fishermen who own mechanized fishing boats that are less than 20 meters in length and possess a valid diesel card issued by the government are eligible for this scheme.

### How much financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Under this scheme, eligible fishermen can receive a maximum VAT subsidy of [?]15 per litre of diesel.

### Is it necessary for fishermen to submit an application to receive the subsidy?

No, fishermen do not need to apply separately, as the subsidy is automatically processed through an online system.

### Where must eligible fishermen purchase diesel to avail of the subsidy?

Fishermen must purchase diesel from government-approved diesel pumps to be eligible for the VAT subsidy.

### Is there a limit to the amount of diesel a fisherman can purchase under this scheme?

Yes, the government sets a fixed diesel quota per fisherman, which determines the amount of diesel that can be purchased at the subsidized rate.

### Are fishermen owning boats longer than 20 meters eligible for the scheme?

No, only those who own mechanized fishing boats under 20 meters in length are eligible for this scheme.

### What documents are required to avail of this scheme?

Fishermen need a valid diesel card, a boat registration certificate, and identification proof such as an Aadhaar Card or Fishermen ID. Additional documents may required as per the authority along with the above-mentioned documents.
