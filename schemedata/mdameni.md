## Brief Description

The scheme "Assistance to MSME Manufacturing Sector Participation in the Exhibition (National and International)" under the "Market Development Assistance to MSMEs" aims to offer reimbursement for stall rent, product literature, and display materials to MSMEs participating in exhibitions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance to MSME Manufacturing Sector Participation in the Exhibition (National and International)&quot; under the &quot;Market Development Assistance to MSMEs&quot; introduced by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat, aims to offer reimbursement for stall rent, product literature, and display materials to MSMEs participating in exhibitions at state, national, and international levels.

## Exclusions

- The Enterprise that has received assistance under this scheme will not be entitled to avail benefit of any other state Government Scheme for the same component/purpose, unless specifically specified under that scheme.

## Eligibility

1. The MSME unit should be engaged in the manufacturing sector.
   1.  The MSME unit must be located in Gujarat.
   1. The MSME unit must be in production during the scheme&#39;s operative period.
   1. MSMEs participating in Exhibitions/Trade fairs in India for State, National, and level Exhibition/Trade fairs organized by industry associations, federations, chamber of commerce and State Government (or its Agency) and / or outside India will be eligible under the scheme.
   1. The MSME unit must apply within six months of exhibition participation.
   1. International level exhibition must be approved by the Ministry of MSME, India Trade Promotion Organization (ITPO), Engineering Exports Promotion Council (EEPC), Federation of Indian Export Organization (FIEO) etc. and as approved by SLEC will be eligible under the scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Assistance to MSME Manufacturing Sector Participation in the Exhibition (National and International)" scheme?

The scheme "Assistance to MSME Manufacturing Sector Participation in the Exhibition (National and International)" under the "Market Development Assistance to MSMEs" introduced by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat, aims to offer reimbursement for stall rent, product literature, and display materials to MSMEs participating in exhibitions at state, national, and international levels.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Any MSME enterprise engaged in manufacturing, registered in Gujarat, and participating in approved exhibitions or trade fairs is eligible to apply.

### What types of exhibitions qualify for financial assistance?

The scheme covers State, National, and International level exhibitions organized by industry associations, federations, chambers of commerce, the State Government (or its agencies), and recognized international organizations.

### Is participation in international exhibitions outside India eligible for assistance?

Yes, MSMEs participating in international exhibitions outside India are eligible for 60% assistance up to [?]5,00,000/-, provided the exhibition is approved by recognized bodies like ITPO, EEPC, FIEO, or the Ministry of MSME.

### How many times can an MSME avail the benefits of this scheme?

An MSME can participate a maximum of 5 times in State, National, and International (Inside India) exhibitions combined.
An MSME can participate a maximum of 3 times in International (Outside India) exhibitions during the scheme's operative period.

### What expenses are covered under the scheme?

The scheme provides reimbursement for stall rent, product literature, and display material costs.

### What is the application process for claiming assistance?

The MSME must apply through the IFP Portal of the District Industries Centre (DIC) within six months from the last date of the exhibition. The required documents must be uploaded for verification.

### Can an MSME avail benefits under both State and Central Government schemes?

Yes, an MSME may avail benefits under both schemes, but not for the same components. The total assistance from both sources cannot exceed the actual expenditure.

### What happens if an MSME applies after six months of exhibition participation?

If an MSME submits the application after six months, the request for reimbursement will not be considered.

### What if an MSME receives assistance from another Gujarat Government scheme for the same exhibition?

If an MSME has already received financial support under another Gujarat Government scheme for the same purpose, it will not be eligible for this scheme unless explicitly mentioned otherwise.
