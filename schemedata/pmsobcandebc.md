## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide educational empowerment and financial assistance to OBC, EBC, and DNT students. Through this scheme, ₹4,000/- per annum is provided to eligible students studying in Classes 9 and 10 in Government Schools.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Pre-Matric Scholarship for OBC, EBC and DNT Students” by the Department of Social Justice & Empowerment, Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India aims to provide educational empowerment, welfare, and motivation to students belonging to Other Backward Classes (OBCs), Economically Backward Classes (EBCs), and De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes (DNT/S-NT) by providing financial assistance at the pre-matriculation or secondary stage. Through this scheme, a consolidated academic allowance of ₹4,000 per annum is provided to eligible students studying in Classes 9 and 10 in Government Schools. The respective Union Territory Administrations of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu implement the scheme. The scholarship is available for studies in India only and is awarded by the State/Union Territory to which the applicant belongs. The scheme operates through an online scholarship portal with the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode of payment. The scholarship may be renewed every academic year, subject to satisfactory academic performance and minimum attendance requirements. The scheme also aims to improve educational access for socio-economically backward communities through a transparent and technology-driven scholarship system.
> **Objectives:**


- To provide welfare and motivation to children belonging to OBC, EBC, and DNT categories through financial assistance at the pre-matriculation or secondary stage.
- To support the educational empowerment of socio-economically backward classes.
- To facilitate completion of education for vulnerable sections of the target population.
- To widen the outreach of scholarship benefits through a uniform and competitive scholarship platform.

<br>

## Benefits

> **Coverage Value of Scholarship:**


  - The students shall be given a consolidated academic allowance of ₹4,000/- per annum.

**Note:** The entire scholarship amount both from the State and Central Government will be paid directly into the account of the students or parents ONLY through DBT preferably through an Aadhaar Based Payment System (Aadhaar Payment Bridge-AePS).
<br>

## Exclusions

1. The applicant should not avail benefits under any other pre-matric scholarship scheme.
1. The applicant repeating the same class will not receive scholarship again for that class.
1. Scholarship may be suspended or cancelled if the student violates school discipline or scheme conditions.
1. Scholarship obtained through false statements shall be cancelled and recovered.
1. Such students may be blacklisted and debarred from scholarship schemes permanently.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The scholarships are open to all the students of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.
   1. Scholarship awards will be sanctioned in the case of students whose parents/guardians&#39; income from all sources does not exceed ₹2,50,000/- per annum.
   1. The students should be studying in Classes IX and X on a full-time basis in Government Schools only.
   1. They should belong to either of the OBC, EBC, or DNT (who are not covered under SC, ST, or OBC) categories through a valid certificate.
   1. A scholarship holder under this scheme will not hold any other educational scholarship for the purpose of studying in Class IX and X. If awarded any other educational scholarship for studying in classes IX and X, the students can avail of either of the two scholarships as per his/her choice and should inform the awarding authority to the Head of the Institution about the option made.
   1. Income Certificate is required to be taken once only, i.e. at the time of admission to courses. 

**Note:** The condition stipulated in this Ministry’s Notification No. 428 dated 16.02.2017 relating to provisions contained in Section 7 of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and other Subsidies, Benefits, and Services) Act 2016 are to be followed with the further stipulation that if, for any reason, the authentication of Aadhaar fails in respect of any applicant, such an individual should be permitted to establish his/her identity by any other means so that genuine persons are not deprived of their benefits.
> **Other Conditions for Award:**


   1. If a student violates school discipline or any other terms and conditions of the scholarship, the scholarship may be suspended or even cancelled, subject to the satisfaction of the competent school authority. The State Government/ Union Territory Administrations can also directly cancel the award if duly satisfied with the reasons for violation of these regulations governing the scheme.
   1. Migration of students from one institution to another would not normally be allowed during the course of the academic year except under exceptional circumstances and in the interest of the student&#39;s academic career.
   1. The scholar obtaining benefits under this scheme shall not be allowed to avail of benefits under any other pre-matric scholarship scheme.
   1. Only two boys of the same parents/guardians will be entitled to receive scholarships. This restriction will, however, not apply to girls. Accordingly, scholarships availed by girls of the same parents/guardian will not adversely affect the admissibility of availing scholarships upto two boys of the same parents/guardian.
   1. A regular attendance record of not less than 75% in the previous year should be a prerequisite for availing scholarship.
   1. At least 5% of students with disabilities should be covered for the grant of the scholarships. 30% of the beneficiaries should be reserved for girl students.
   1. If a student is found to have obtained a scholarship by false statements, his/ her scholarship will be cancelled forthwith and the amount of the scholarship paid will be recovered, at the discretion of the concerned State Government. The student concerned will be blacklisted and debarred for scholarship in any scheme forever.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the this scholarship about?

The PM–YASASVI scheme is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme launched by the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India to provide financial assistance for educational empowerment of OBC, EBC, and DNT students studying in Classes 9 and 10.
<br>

### Who can apply for the PM–YASASVI Pre-Matric Scholarship?

Students belonging to Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Backward Classes (EBC), and De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes (DNT/S-NT), studying in Classes 9 and 10 in Government Schools, can apply for the scholarship subject to fulfillment of eligibility conditions.
<br>

### What is the annual scholarship amount provided under the scheme?

Eligible students are provided a consolidated academic allowance of ₹4,000 per annum under the scheme.
<br>

### What is the income limit for applying under the scheme?

The annual income of the applicant’s parents or guardians from all sources should not exceed ₹2,50,000/-.
<br>

### Can students studying in private schools apply for the scholarship?

No. The scholarship is available only for students studying in Government Schools.
<br>

### Is it mandatory to have Aadhaar for the scholarship application?

Applicants are required to provide Aadhaar details. However, if Aadhaar authentication fails, the applicant may establish identity through any other approved means.
<br>

### Can a student receive another scholarship along with PM–YASASVI scholarship?

No. A student receiving benefits under this scheme cannot avail benefits under any other pre-matric scholarship scheme for Classes 9 and 10.
<br>

### What are the attendance requirements for renewal of scholarship?

The student should maintain at least 75% attendance in every academic year for continuation or renewal of the scholarship.
<br>

### How is the scholarship amount disbursed to beneficiaries?

The scholarship amount is transferred directly into the bank account of the student or parent through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), preferably using Aadhaar Based Payment System.
<br>

### Is there any reservation for girl students and students with disabilities under the scheme?

Yes. Under the scheme, 30% of the beneficiaries should be girl students and at least 5% of scholarships should cover students with disabilities.
<br>
