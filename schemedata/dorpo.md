## Brief Description

The “Distribution of Rhizobium Packets-Oilseeds” scheme provides a subsidy on the cost of Rhizobium, to eligible farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds.

## Detailed Description

The “Distribution of Rhizobium Packets-Oilseeds” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides a subsidy to farmers on the cost of Rhizobium. It targets farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds to the Department of Agriculture.

## Benefits

- **Quantum of Assistance:** 50% on the cost of Rhizobium or ₹100/- per ha. whichever is less.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary should be a Farmer.
  - The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.
  - The beneficiary should produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds to the Department.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office.
**Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). 
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office:
> **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer.

> **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer.

> **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Distribution of Rhizobium Packets-Oilseeds” scheme?

The "Distribution of Rhizobium Packets-Oilseeds" scheme provides a subsidy on the cost of Rhizobium, to eligible farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Farmers who produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds to the Department are eligible.

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme?

The applicant needs to provide proof of identity, land ownership, seed production and supply certificate, and bank details.

### Where should the application be submitted?

Applications should be submitted to the Assistant Agricultural Officer at the village level or the Agricultural Officer/Deputy Agricultural Officer at the block level.

### How much subsidy will be provided?

A 50% subsidy on the cost of Rhizobium or [?]100/- per hectare, whichever is less, will be provided.

### What is Rhizobium and how does it benefit crops?

Rhizobium is a soil bacterium that fixes nitrogen, improving soil fertility and crop yield.

### Is there an age limit for applying to the scheme?

There is no age limit specified for this scheme.

### Can applicants from any community apply?

Yes, the scheme is open to farmers of all communities.

### Is it necessary to produce a specific type of seed to be eligible?

Yes, the applicant must produce and supply Foundation and Certified Class seeds to the Department.

### What should be done if the application is rejected?

The applicant may contact the concerned agricultural officer for further assistance and clarification.
