## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide artists practising Indian art forms with an opportunity to perform abroad. Funding for international travel, performance opportunities, and logistical support is provided to Indian artists, dancers, musicians, and cultural troupes selected for performance abroad.

## Detailed Description

The "Global Engagement Scheme: Festival of India" component of the Global Engagement Scheme by the ICR Division, Ministry of Culture, Government of India aims to provide artists practising Indian art forms an opportunity to perform abroad under the banner of 'Festival of India'. Through this component, funding for international travel, performance opportunities, and logistical support is provided to Indian artists, dancers, musicians, and cultural troupes selected for performance abroad. The component is implemented by the Ministry of Culture in coordination with Indian Missions abroad, through a Standing Committee, a Festival of India (FOI) Cell, and a Screening Committee.
> **Objectives:**


- Promote Indian Culture abroad.
- Strengthen bonds of foreign countries with India.
- Promote bilateral cultural contracts.
- Project India's cultural image abroad.
- Promote inbound tourism.

> **Institutional Set-up:**


- A Standing Committee, chaired by the Secretary (Culture), includes representatives from ICCR, Ministry of Tourism, Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, Planning Commission, Financial Advisor of the Ministry of Culture, DG, Archaeological Survey of India (ASI), and other related departments/agencies; it decides on the selection of countries, events, and time span.
- A Festival of India Cell coordinates with participating agencies to prepare a consolidated proposal covering artist selection, delegation size, travel dates, venues, timings, logistics, accommodation, cargo handling, and other details.
- A Screening Committee facilitates participation of non-government agencies/persons having expertise in performing arts, literature, and exhibitions; graded and empanelled persons are listed on the Ministry of Culture website.

> **Events covered:** 


- Dance (classical, folk, contemporary, fusion), 
- Music (vocal, instrumental, sufi, classical, semi-classical, carnatic, contemporary), 
- Exhibitions, 
- Theatre, 
- Folk Arts including Mehendi artists and puppetry, 
- Literary Festival (from Sahitya Akademi), 
- Food Festival, 
- Film Festival, 
- Yoga, 
- Fashion Show/Textile Exhibition

<br>

## Benefits

1. Ministry of Culture meets expenditure on the selected delegation, including international passage, performance fees, visa fee, travel insurance, cost of extra baggage, and lodging in 3 or 4 star hotels.
1. Ministry also supports venue hiring charges and publicity expenses.
1. Missions are expected to explore sponsorship through local government and/or public-private funding for venues, other infrastructure (sound system, lighting), local hospitality, and local transportation, to the extent possible, before including such expenditure in the budget — this ensures maximising the limited budget for Festival of India.
1. Indian Missions are encouraged to organize Food Festivals, Film Festival, Yoga, and Fashion Show or Textile Exhibition as part of the Festival, for which the Ministry facilitates contacts with the concerned Ministry or organization.
1. Ministry of Culture also sends cultural troupes from its Zonal Cultural Centres for folk dances/music, and from autonomous organizations such as Sangeet Natak Akademi and Kalashetra for classical dance/music; exhibitions and/or lectures through museums, Lalit Kala Akademi, or Sahitya Akademi are also supported.

> **Mode of Disbursement:**


- Funds are not given directly to artists; the Ministry identifies a nodal agency in India for making arrangements for international passage booking, visas, and payment of performance fees to the artists.

> **Frequency/Timing of Disbursement:**


- The approved budget is released in two instalments 75 percent after financial and administrative approval, and the balance on conclusion of the festival, on receipt of utilization certificates.

> **Validity/Conditions:**


- Ministry of Culture expects constant feedback before, during, and after the festival period; Missions should send reports, photographs, and video recordings, if made.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should have expertise in the field of performing arts, literature, exhibitions, or related disciplines.
1. The applicant should be graded by the Screening Committee and empanelled; the list of empanelled persons is uploaded on the website of the Ministry of Culture.
1. The applicant should belong to the outstanding or promising category of artists on the empanelled list, from which the Indian Mission selects the art form.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Once the dates of the Festival and suggestions from the Missions are received for activities proposed to be included, the approval of the Hon'ble Minister of Culture is obtained.
- After approval of activities by the Minister, Missions work out the detailed programme of the festival, including precise dates for each performance and venues in each city, along with the estimated budget for activities to be funded by the Ministry of Culture.
- The Ministry identifies a nodal agency in India for making arrangements for the cultural troupes, such as booking of international passage, visas, and payment of performance fees to the artists.
- The approved budget is released in two instalments 75 percent after financial and administrative approval, and the balance on conclusion of the festival, on receipt of utilisation certificates.
- Ministry of Culture expects constant feedback before, during, and after the festival period; Missions should send reports, photographs, and video recordings (if made).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Festival of India (FOI)?

Festivals of India abroad are events organised to promote India's rich cultural heritage and enhance India's image in the global arena in a concerted manner, with the aim of having a lasting impact on the people of the host country.
<br>

### Under which scheme does Festival of India fall?

Festival of India is one of the three components of the "Global Engagement Scheme" (earlier known as the "Scheme for Promotion of International Cultural Relations"), implemented by the ICR Division, Ministry of Culture.
<br>

### When was the Festival of India scheme revived?

The Scheme of conducting Festivals of India abroad was revived in 2013.
<br>

### What are the objectives of Festival of India?

- Promote Indian Culture abroad.
- Strengthen bonds between foreign countries and India.
- Promote bilateral cultural contacts.
- Project India's cultural image abroad.
- Promote inbound tourism.

<br>

### What kind of events are included in a Festival of India?

Dance (classical, folk, contemporary, fusion), Music (vocal, instrumental, sufi, classical, semi-classical, carnatic, contemporary), Exhibitions, Theatre, Folk Arts including Mehendi artists and puppetry, Literary Festival (from Sahitya Akademi), Food Festival, Film Festival, Yoga, and Fashion Show/Textile Exhibition.
<br>

### Who decides which country and events are selected for a Festival of India?

The Standing Committee, under the Chairmanship of Secretary (Culture) — with representatives from ICCR, Ministry of Tourism, Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, Planning Commission, Financial Advisor of Ministry of Culture, DG, Archaeological Survey of India (ASI), and other related departments/agencies takes the decision on selection of country, events, and time span.
<br>

### What is the role of the Festival of India Cell?

The Festival of India Cell coordinates with all participating agencies to prepare a consolidated proposal containing detailed information on all aspects of the Festival — from selection of artist to budgetary requirement, including performances, delegation size and details, dates of travelling, local venues, timings of shows, local logistics and accommodation arrangements, cargo handling charges, contact details of concerned agencies, logo, and posters.
<br>

### Who is eligible to participate as an artist in a Festival of India?

Agencies/persons other than those from Government organizations, having expertise in the field of performing arts, literature, exhibitions, etc., can participate. They are graded by the Screening Committee and empanelled. Indian Missions select the art form from this empanelled list.
<br>

### Where is the list of empanelled artists available?

The list of persons empanelled is uploaded on the website of the Ministry of Culture.
<br>

### How does an Indian Mission select which art form to include?

The Missions proposing to organize a Festival of India should select the art forms from the outstanding or promising category of artists on the empanelled list. While deciding the art form, Missions are expected to keep in mind recent cultural events held in their country, with a view to broad-base the projection of Indian culture, local tastes, and sensitivities.
<br>

### Does the Ministry of Culture send its own cultural troupes for Festival of India?

Yes. The Ministry of Culture also sends cultural troupes from its Zonal Cultural Centres for folk dances and music, and cultural troupes for classical dance and music from its autonomous organisations such as Sangeet Natak Akademi and Kalashetra. Exhibitions and/or lectures through museums under the Ministry of Culture and academics such as Lalit Kala Akademi or Sahitya Akademi are also supported.
<br>

### Can Food Festivals, Film Festivals, Yoga, or Fashion Shows be part of a Festival of India?

Yes. For a comprehensive presentation of Indian culture, Indian Missions are encouraged to organize Food Festivals, Film Festival, Yoga, and Fashion Show or Textile Exhibition as part of FOI. The Ministry of Culture facilitates the contacts with the concerned Ministry or organization.
<br>

### What is the procedure for approval of a Festival of India?

Once the dates of the Festival and suggestions from the Missions are received for activities proposed to be included, the approval of the Hon'ble Minister of Culture is obtained. After approval of activities by the Minister, Missions are asked to work out the detailed programme of the festival, including the precise dates for each performance and venues in each city, along with the estimated budget for the activities to be funded by the Ministry of Culture.
<br>

### Who makes the travel and payment arrangements for the artists?

The Ministry identifies a nodal agency in India for making arrangements for the cultural troupes in India, such as booking of international passage, visas, and payment of performance fees to the artists, etc.
<br>

### How is the approved budget released?

The approved budget is released in two instalments 75 percent after financial and administrative approval, and the balance on conclusion of the festival, on receipt of utilization certificates.
<br>

### What is expected from the Missions during and after the Festival?

Ministry of Culture expects constant feedback before, during, and after the festival period. The Missions should send reports, photographs, and video recordings (if made).
<br>

### Is there a separate budget outlay for the overall Global Engagement Scheme (which includes Festival of India)?

Yes. The scheme has an outlay of ₹ 62,70,00,000/- (Revenue) for the period 2021-22 to 2025-26.
