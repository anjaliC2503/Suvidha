## Brief Description

This scheme has been introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat. It aims to enhance fish seed production and promote inland fisheries in non-tribal areas.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;To Increase Fish Seed Production and Inland Fisheries Equipment in Non-Tribal Areas&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to enhance fish seed production and promote inland fisheries in non-tribal areas. It provides financial assistance for fish farming, fish seed rearing, and procurement of essential fisheries equipment, benefiting individuals, societies, and self-help groups involved in inland fisheries.

## Eligibility

- Individuals/ societies/ self-help groups/ associations/ reservoir monopolies/ private units etc. involved in inland fisheries activities can avail the benefits of inland schemes.
  - The applicant should be a Fisherman.
  - Applicant should be above 18 years of age.
  - Applicant must have own private land/leased land to avail new pond construction assistance for fish farming (In case of two or more land holders consent form of other land holders must be submitted).
  - The beneficiary fisherman must have his own bank account.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on &quot;Apply New&quot; button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the &quot;Update Application&quot; button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application.
**Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

This scheme aims to enhance fish seed production and promote inland fisheries in non-tribal areas through providing financial assistance for fish farming, fish seed rearing, and procurement of essential fisheries equipment, benefiting individuals, societies, and self-help groups involved in inland fisheries.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Individuals, societies, self-help groups, associations, and private units engaged in inland fisheries activities are eligible to apply for the scheme.

### What is the age requirement for applicants?

The applicant must be above 18 years of age to be eligible for the scheme.

### Can women avail benefits under this scheme?

Yes, women, including those from Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), and Other Backward Class (OBC) categories, are eligible for benefits under the scheme.

### What financial assistance is provided for fish pond construction?

General category beneficiaries receive 40% subsidy up to [?]2,80,000/-, while SC/ST beneficiaries receive 60% subsidy up to [?]4,20,000/- per hectare.

### Is it mandatory to have private or leased land for fish farming?

Yes, applicants must have private or leased land to avail assistance for new pond construction. In cases where multiple landowners are involved, a consent form from all landowners must be submitted.

### How much subsidy is available for purchasing boats and nets?

Beneficiaries can receive a 50% subsidy up to [?]7,500/- for Tin Boat-Net and up to [?]15,000/- for FRP Boat-Net.

### How can an applicant apply for the scheme?

Applications can be submitted online through the ikhedut portal: https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in, along with the necessary documents.

### Can multiple landowners jointly apply for assistance?

Yes, multiple landowners can apply, but they must submit a consent form from all landowners involved in the project.
