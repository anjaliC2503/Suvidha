## Brief Description

The scheme aims to equip beneficiaries with skills for employability and entrepreneurship at taluka/village level across Gujarat. It provides skill development training through Short-Term Training and Special Projects for diverse beneficiaries.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "SAKSHAM - KVK 2.0"was launched by the Gujarat Skill Development Mission (GSDM), Labour and Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide access to skill development programmes to a wide range of beneficiaries at taluka/village level, enhancing employability and competitiveness in the job market while promoting entrepreneurship. The scheme provides Short-Term Training in National Skills Qualifications Framework Level 1, 2, and 3 courses along with Gujarat Council of Vocational Training courses, and implements Special Projects for marginalized and vulnerable groups. The applicant must be a domicile of Gujarat, looking for fresh skills, reskilling, upskilling opportunities. The scheme is implemented by GSDM through empanelled Implementation Partners across all 33 districts of Gujarat. The applications are accepted online through the GSDM portal.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Training Benefits***

- Short-Term Training of approximately 300 hours duration in National Skills Qualifications Framework/Gujarat Council of Vocational Training aligned courses.
- On-the-Job Training as an integral part of Short-Term Training with duration determined by specific job roles.
- Specialized training courses for Special Projects tailored to meet specific needs of marginalized and vulnerable groups.
- Training conducted at Saksham Centers established at taluka/village level.
- Access to certified Training of Trainers trainers endorsed by respective Sector Skill Councils.
- Industry expert and entrepreneur guest lectures on rotational basis.

##### ***Assessment and Certification Benefits***

- Assessment and certification carried out by Sector Skill Councils/Gujarat Council of Vocational Training.
- Assessment and certification fees paid directly by Gujarat Skill Development Mission.
- Candidates eligible for assessment with minimum 70% attendance for classroom training and 70% for On-the-Job Training.

##### ***Employment Support Benefits***

- Placement assistance and employment opportunities from Implementation Partners.
- Multiple placement options including wage employment, probation with stipend/salary, service aggregator portal registration, job work acceptance, self-employment, and apprenticeship opportunities.
- Post-placement tracking and support services.

##### ***Infrastructure and Facility Benefits***

- Training centers with basic amenities including drinking water and separate toilets for boys and girls.
- Aadhaar-based biometric attendance system and Internet Protocol-based Closed-Circuit Television cameras with Digital Video Recorder systems.
- Teaching aids, furniture, fittings, and equipment as per job role requirements.

##### ***Conditions***

- Minimum 70% attendance required for classroom training to be eligible for completion.
- Minimum 70% attendance required for On-the-Job Training to be eligible for completion.
- Original Aadhaar card verification required during assessment to prevent duplication.
- Adherence to batch timings from earliest 7:00 AM to latest 7:00 PM with minimum 4 hours and maximum 8 hours per day training.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a domicile of Gujarat.
- The applicant must be minimum 14 years of age as per labour law for non-hazardous occupations.
- The applicant must be maximum 59 years of age for Short Term Training.
- The applicant must be maximum 65 years of age for Special Projects.
- The applicant must be looking for fresh skills, reskilling, or upskilling opportunities for Short Term Training.
- The applicant must be out of education, school/college dropout, or unemployed youth for Short Term Training.
- The applicant must belong to marginalized, vulnerable groups requiring special attention or specific job roles for Special Projects.

> ***Eligibility for Special Projects***

The applicant must belong to one of the following categories: _Jail Inmates, Former Prisoners, Juvenile homes, Scheduled Castes, Women, Members of the Transgender Community, Members of the Beggar Community, Senior Citizens and Old age homes, Patients recovered from Mental illness, Children in Shelter Homes and Orphanages, Scheduled Tribes, Economically Backward Communities, Persons with Disabilities, Rural Artisans, or Migrant Workers._

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:****** ***Registration, Login and Profile Setup***

- Visit the [official website](https://skills.gujarat.gov.in) of Gujarat Skill Development Mission and navigate to the SAKSHAM - KVK 2.0 application portal.

- Create a new user account on the portal by providing basic personal information including name, mobile number, and email address. Verify your mobile number through OTP sent via SMS and verify email address through verification link.
- Login to your registered account using credentials and complete your detailed profile including personal information, educational qualifications, employment status, and contact details. Upload a recent passport-size photograph in JPEG format with maximum file size of 100kb.

**Step 2:****** ***Selection of Scheme, Job Role, and Training Center***

- Navigate to the SAKSHAM - KVK 2.0 scheme section and select the appropriate training category - either Short Term Training or Special Projects based on your eligibility and requirements. Browse through the available NSQF/GCVT aligned job roles listed on the portal. 
- Select your preferred job role based on your interest, aptitude, and career objectives from the available options in your district/taluka.
- View the list of empanelled Implementation Partners and their Saksham Centers in your preferred taluka/village location. 
- Select your preferred training center based on location convenience and course availability.

**Step 3:****** ***Filling Application Form and Document Upload***

- Fill the comprehensive application form with mandatory fields including personal details, educational background, employment history, family information, and preference details. Ensure all information is accurate and matches with supporting documents.
- Upload all required documents in PDF format with maximum file size of 2MB per document. Ensure documents are clear, legible, and properly scanned with all four corners visible.

**Step 4:****** ***Application Review and Submission***

- Review all entered information and uploaded documents for accuracy and completeness. Make necessary corrections if required and submit the application form. Note down the application reference number for future tracking.
- Receive application submission confirmation message on registered mobile number and email address. Download and save the application acknowledgment receipt for future reference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum age requirement I need to meet to apply for the Short Term Training courses available under this scheme?

You must be at least 14 years of age as per labor law requirements for non-hazardous occupations. This is the minimum age to be eligible for participation in any training program.
<br>

### I am currently 62 years old and interested in Special Projects - am I still eligible to apply for training opportunities?

Yes, you are eligible as the maximum age limit for Special Projects is 65 years. However, for Short Term Training, the age limit is 59 years maximum.
<br>

### What specific attendance percentage do I need to maintain during my training to become eligible for the final assessment and certification?

You must maintain a minimum 70% attendance for both classroom training and On-the-Job Training components. This attendance is mandatory to qualify for assessment by Sector Skill Councils.
<br>

### As someone who belongs to a Scheduled Tribe community, which category of training would be most suitable for my application?

You would be eligible for Special Projects as Scheduled Tribes are specifically mentioned as one of the marginalized groups. Special Projects provide specialized training tailored for vulnerable communities.
<br>

### I want to know if there are any fees I need to pay for the assessment and certification process after completing my training?

No, you do not need to pay any assessment or certification fees. These fees are paid directly by the Gujarat Skill Development Mission to the respective certifying bodies.
<br>

### What are the different types of employment opportunities and placement assistance that will be available after I complete my training successfully?

You will receive placement assistance including wage employment, probation with stipend or salary, service aggregator portal registration, job work acceptance, self-employment guidance, and apprenticeship opportunities from Implementation Partners.
<br>

### How many hours of training can I expect to receive if I enroll in the Short Term Training program offered?

Short Term Training consists of approximately 300 hours of training duration. This includes both classroom sessions and On-the-Job Training components as per specific job role requirements.
<br>

### Can you tell me about the daily training schedule and timing flexibility that will be available during my course?

Training batches operate from earliest 7:00 AM to latest 7:00 PM with minimum 4 hours and maximum 8 hours per day. You must adhere to the specific batch timings assigned to you.
<br>

### I am a college dropout who is currently unemployed - does this educational and employment status make me eligible for applying?

Yes, you are eligible for Short Term Training as the scheme specifically covers individuals who are out of education, school or college dropouts, or unemployed youth seeking skill development opportunities.
<br>

### What kind of infrastructure and facilities can I expect at the training centers where classes will be conducted?

Training centers provide basic amenities including drinking water, separate toilets for boys and girls, teaching aids, furniture, equipment as per job role requirements, and Aadhaar-based biometric attendance systems.
<br>

### I am a Person with Disabilities - which specific training category should I apply for and what additional support can I expect?

You should apply for Special Projects as Persons with Disabilities are listed as eligible for this category. You will receive specialized training courses tailored to meet specific needs of marginalized groups.
<br>

### Do I need to be a resident of Gujarat to be eligible for this training program, or can applicants from other states also apply?

You must be a domicile of Gujarat to be eligible for this scheme. Applicants from other states are not eligible, and you will need to provide a Domicile Certificate of Gujarat as proof.
<br>

### What document verification process should I expect during the assessment phase, and which original documents will be required for checking?

During assessment, original Aadhaar card verification is mandatory to prevent duplication. You must also present your Training Completion Certificate and attendance records showing minimum 70% attendance for both components.
<br>

### If I am interested in learning new skills for the first time or want to upgrade my existing skills, which training option would be appropriate?

Short Term Training is designed for individuals looking for fresh skills, reskilling, or upskilling opportunities. This category covers National Skills Qualifications Framework Level 1, 2, and 3 courses along with Gujarat Council of Vocational Training courses.
<br>
