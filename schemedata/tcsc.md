## Brief Description

The "Tiatr Cantaram Singing Competition", organised by the Kala Academy under the Department of Art, Culture and Museums, Government of Goa, aims to promote singing talent, preserve and revive the tradition of Tiatr songs, and encourage both traditional and new compositions that reflect Goa.

## Detailed Description

The "Tiatr Cantaram Singing Competition", organised by the Kala Academy under the Department of Art, Culture and Museums, Government of Goa, aims to promote singing talent, preserve and revive the tradition of Tiatr songs, and encourage both traditional and new compositions that reflect Goa’s rich Tiatr heritage. The competition is open to all Goan artists.

## Benefits

- A common music band is provided by Kala Academy free of cost for practice and competition.
- Each participant gets ₹150/- as travel and daily allowance.
- Winners receive cash prizes in different categories.

<br>

**Solo Singing [Male & Female]:** 1st Prize: ₹2,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹1,500/-, 3rd Prize: ₹1,000/-
**Duet Singing:** 1st Prize: ₹4,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹3,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹2,000/-
**Duo Singing:** 1st Prize: ₹4,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹3,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹2,000/-
**Trio Singing:** 1st Prize: ₹6,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹4,500/-, 3rd Prize: ₹3,000/-
**Quartet [Group of 4]:** 1st Prize: ₹8,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹6,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹4,000/-
**Choral [Group Singing]:** 1st Prize: ₹8,000/-, 2nd Prize: ₹6,000/-, 3rd Prize: ₹4,000/-

## Eligibility

The Competition is open to all Goan artists.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Kala Academy, Goa, and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form free of charge from the  [**Program & Development Section, Tiatr Academy of Goa**](https://kalaacademygoa.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/Cantaram-Competition.pdf#:~:text=Academy%20Complex%2C%20Panaji%2DGoa.%20All%20the%20interested%20cantaram,which%20will%20be%20available%20in%20the%20Prog.)**,  Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Campal, Panaji, Goa.**
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the **Member Secretary, Kala Academy, Goa**. 
**Authority for Redressal of Grievances:** Member Secretary/Chairman, Kala Academy, Goa.
_*All participants fulfilling the requirements should submit their applications within the stipulated date._ 
_*The right to accept or reject the entry is vested with Kala Academy._

## Documents Required

- Four typed copies of the song lyrics.
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the original composer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to participate in this singing competition focused on traditional Goan songs?

The competition is open to all Goan artistes who wish to showcase their talent in singing Tiatr Cantaram.

### How can I obtain the entry form and rules for the singing competition?

Entry forms and rules are available free of charge at Kala Academy, and no fees are required for submission.

### What documents must I submit along with my entry form for the competition?

You must submit four typed copies of your song lyrics along with the filled entry form and a No Objection Certificate (N.O.C.) from the original composer.

### Is there any fee required to participate in this traditional singing competition?

No, there is no entry fee for participating in the competition.

### What is the last date to submit my application for the singing competition?

Applications must be submitted within the stipulated date mentioned in the press release by Kala Academy.

### Will there be any musical accompaniment provided during the competition?

Yes, Kala Academy provides a common music band free of charge for rehearsal and competition performances.

### Is there any financial support provided to participants for travel and other expenses?

Each participant is eligible for ₹150/- as travel and daily allowance (T.A. & D.A.).

### What are the different performance categories available in this singing competition?

The competition includes Solo (Male & Female), Duet, Duo, Trio, Quartet (Group), and Choral performances in Decent and Comedy styles.

### What is the prize money for the first prize in the Solo Male category?

The first prize winner in the Solo Male category receives ₹2,000/-.

### How much is the second prize in the Solo Male and Female categories?

The second prize winners in both Solo Male and Female categories receive ₹1,500/- each.

### What is the third prize amount for Solo Male and Female participants?

Third prize winners in Solo Male and Female categories receive ₹1,000/- each.

### What is the prize money for the first prize in the Duet category?

The first prize in the Duet category is ₹4,000/-.

### What is the prize amount for the third position in the Duet category?

The third prize in the Duet category is ₹2,000/-.

### How much do second prize winners receive in the Quartet (Group) category?

Second prize winners in the Quartet (Group) category receive ₹6,000/-.
