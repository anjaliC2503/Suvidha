## Brief Description

Self-employment loan scheme for Rabari and Bharwad women in Gujarat, offering up to ₹2 lakh at 5% annual interest to promote economic independence.

## Detailed Description

The **New Swarnima Scheme** is a financial empowerment initiative by the **Gujarat Gopalak Vikas Nigam** under the **Social Justice and Empowerment Department**. It aims to provide **100% loan** support to women from the **Rabari and Bharwad communities** to promote self-employment and entrepreneurship.
Eligible women between the **age of 21 and 45 years**, with an **annual family income up to ₹3 lakh**, can receive a **loan of up to ₹2 lakh** at a subsidized **interest rate of 5% per annum** to start a business of their choice. The scheme is designed to increase economic independence and livelihood generation among backward class women.

## Benefits

- **100% loan** assistance provided to eligible women- Loan amount up to **₹2.00 lakh**- Interest rate fixed at **5% per annum**- Loan can be used to **start a business** of the beneficiary’s choice- Meant for **economically backward women** of specific castes- **Encourages entrepreneurship and self-reliance**

## Eligibility

1. Be a permanent **resident of Gujarat**1. Be a **woman**1. Belong to the **Rabari or Bharwad caste**1. Belong to a **socially and educationally backward class** as recognized by the Government of Gujarat1. Be between **21 to 45 years** of age1. Have a **total annual family** income not exceeding **₹3.00 lakh**1. Intend to start your **own business or self-employment** venture using the loan

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the New Swarnima Scheme?

A loan scheme for **Rabari and Bharwad women** to start self-employment ventures.

### Who is eligible to apply?

Women from Rabari or Bharwad castes, **aged 21–45**, with income under ₹3 lakh/year.

### What is the maximum loan amount?

**₹2.00 lakh.**

### Is there any subsidy or grant in the scheme?

**No subsidy**, but interest is subsidized at 5% per annum.

### Is collateral required?

**No collateral** is mentioned in the scheme guidelines.

### Can I use the loan for any type of business?

**Yes,** any legal small business or trade.

### Can a man apply under this scheme?

**No,** only eligible women can apply.

### What is the rate of interest?

**5% per annum.**

### Is it necessary to belong to a backward class?

**Yes,** only socially and educationally backward Rabari and Bharwad castes.

### Where can I apply?

To apply visit the official [**DAADC**](https://daadconline.gujarat.gov.in/) online portal.
