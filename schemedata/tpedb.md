## Brief Description

The scheme aims to train youth in biotechnology and develop entrepreneurial skills by providing technical knowledge, mentorship, and guidance to help them initiate biotech startups.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Training Programme for Entrepreneurship Development in Biotechnology” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Council for Biotechnology under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to train youth in biotechnology and develop entrepreneurial skills by providing technical knowledge, mentorship, and guidance to help them initiate biotech startups.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this training programme, training and technical information are provided to the youth of the state to promote entrepreneurship development and support them in establishing startups in the field of biotechnology, along with guidance as per their specific needs.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand. 
1. The applicant must be a student or researcher at the graduate, postgraduate, or Ph.D. level in Biotechnology, Microbiology, Molecular Biology, Genetic Biology, Environmental Biology, or related subjects.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

The Council organizes training programmes for entrepreneurship development. Prior to each event, information is published on the official website and in newspapers. Colleges, universities, and related institutions are informed through email or social media platforms, and applications are accepted via Google Forms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this training programme?

Graduate, postgraduate, and Ph.D. students, as well as researchers in subjects such as Biotechnology, Microbiology, Molecular Biology, Genetic Biology, and Environmental Biology, are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What kind of training is provided under this programme?

Participants receive training in biotechnological techniques & startup management.
<br>

### How can interested candidates apply for the programme?

Applications are accepted through Google Forms, the link for which is shared in official notifications and announcements.
<br>

### How are applicants informed about upcoming training programmes?

Information about upcoming programmes is published on the council's website and advertised in newspapers.
<br>

### What are the benefits of participating in this programme?

Participants gain practical knowledge, technical expertise, and mentorship to develop business ideas, along with exposure to startup procedures.
<br>

### Does the programme help participants start their own biotech startup?

Yes.
<br>
