## Brief Description

The "Civil Service Incentive" by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, offers an incentive of ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 for successful candidates from these communities in MPPSC and UPSC civil service exams.

## Detailed Description

The "Civil service incentive" by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides an incentive amount ranging from ₹20,000/- to ₹50,000/- to qualified candidates from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities for their success in various levels of civil service examinations conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC) and the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC).

## Benefits

**On Passing the Preliminary Examination:**

- ₹20,000/- (Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission)
- ₹40,000/- (Union Public Service Commission)

**On Passing the Main Examination:**

- ₹30,000/- (Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission)
- ₹60,000/- (Union Public Service Commission)

**On Passing the Interview:**

- ₹25,000/- (Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission)
- ₹50,000/- (Union Public Service Commission)

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be from De-notified, Nomadic, or Semi-Nomadic communities.
- The applicant should have passed one of the following stages of the MPPSC or UPSC Examination: Preliminary Exam, Main Exam, or Interview.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "De-Notified, Nomadic and Semi-nomadic communities "?

It is a scheme by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Madhya Pradesh, which provides financial incentives to qualified candidates from these communities who succeed in various levels of civil service exams.

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

The applicant should belong to De-notified, Nomadic, or Semi-Nomadic communities and be eligible for civil service exams like MPPSC or UPSC.

### What is the incentive amount for passing the preliminary exam?

The incentive amount is [?]20,000 for M.P. Public Service Commission (MPPSC) and [?]40,000 for Union Public Service Commission (UPSC).

### What is the incentive amount for passing the main exam?

The incentive amount is [?]30,000 for MPPSC and [?]60,000 for UPSC.

### What is the incentive amount for passing the interview?

The incentive amount is [?]25,000 for MPPSC and [?]50,000 for UPSC.

### Is the incentive only available for women candidates?

Yes, the scheme specifically focuses on promoting girls' literacy and incentivizes female candidates from the eligible communities.

### Do I need to clear all stages of the exam to receive the full incentive?

Yes, you must pass the preliminary exam, the main exam, and the interview to receive the full incentive amount.

### How is the incentive disbursed?

The incentive is provided after each stage of the exam (preliminary, main, and interview) is successfully passed.

### Can I receive incentives for both MPPSC and UPSC exams?

Yes, the scheme offers incentives for success in both MPPSC and UPSC exams, with different amounts for each.

### Is there any age limit for applying to the scheme?

The age limit for applying depends on the respective MPPSC or UPSC eligibility criteria. You must meet the age requirements for these exams.

### Can this incentive be claimed if I have already passed a civil service exam?

No, the incentive is only available for those who pass the exams in the current year. Past success is not eligible for this scheme.
