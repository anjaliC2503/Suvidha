## Brief Description

The scheme aims to set up production centres to promote eco-friendly jute products and generate sustained employment. It provides skill training, machine subsidies, and financial incentives to collaborating agencies, targeting artisans, women self-help groups, and entrepreneurs.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Jute Diversification Scheme" was launched by the National Jute Board, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. The scheme aims to spread jute diversification programs, provide training, and create sustained employment by setting up Jute Resource-cum-Production Centres across the country. The scheme provides basic, advanced, and design training in jute diversified products manufacturing, financial incentives for collaborating agencies, and subsidies for procuring machinery. The applicant must be a registered agency or entrepreneur with at least three years of experience in product identification, bulk production, skill training, and marketing of jute diversified products. The scheme is implemented by the National Jute Board. The applications for this scheme are accepted online through the official website, followed by a hard copy submission.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Financial Assistance for Activities***

- The scheme provides an estimated cost of ₹12,40,000/- for organizing 5 cycles of basic, advance, and design training for 24 beneficiaries per cycle.
- Incentive for Collaborating Agencies: The agency receives ₹2,48,000/- (calculated as 20% of the activity cost) for organizing the activities and maintaining the infrastructure.

##### ***Infrastructure Support***
The scheme provides ₹12,000/- for the installation of a biometric machine, internet connectivity, and maintenance.
##### ***Machinery Subsidy***
The scheme offers ₹9,00,000/- per year per centre to procure 72 machines (at 50% subsidy or a maximum ceiling of ₹12,500/- per machine, whichever is less) for successful beneficiaries engaged in production.
##### ***Disbursal***

- 50% of the activities cost is released as an advance along with the approval of the action plan.
- The remaining 50% of the activities cost, the cost of the biometric machine, and the incentive are released upon successful completion of the activities and submission of final claims and reports.
- The machine subsidy is released in two installments, where 50% is released within one month of installation, and the remaining 50% is released after six months upon inspection certifying that the beneficiaries are engaged in manufacturing and sales.
- The payments of such incentives are paid strictly through Direct Bank Transfer.

##### ***Conditions***

- The collaborating agency must ensure the engagement of at least 50% of the trainees in jute diversification activities post-training.
- In the case of self-employment, the beneficiary must achieve sales of at least ₹5,000/- per month or ₹15,000/- per quarter.
- The agency must maintain a separate bank account for all advances and limit cash transactions to a maximum of ₹5,000/- per activity.
- The installation of an Aadhaar linked biometric attendance machine and a webcam is mandatory at the training site.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a bonafide Indian agency or an Indian citizen.
- The applicant must be a reputed registered jute entrepreneur, manufacturer, exporter, merchant trader, non-governmental organization, cooperative society, federation, public sector undertaking, or organization.
- The applicant must be engaged in the product identification, bulk production, skill trainings, and marketing of jute diversified products for at least the last three years.
- The applicant should possess recognized experience in the training, promotion, and development of Women Self Help Groups in the field of production and marketing of jute diversified products, handlooms, or handicrafts.
- The applicant must have their own office of at least 250 sq.ft. equipped with a telephone or mobile connection, a computer or laptop, and email facilities.
- The applicant must have at least 500 sq.ft. of space available for setting up the Jute Resource-Cum-Production Centre.
- The applicant must obtain a minimum of 50 marks in the evaluation criteria to be eligible for consideration for selection.

<br>

##### ***Preference***
Preference is given to recognized agencies with at least three years of experience in training, promotion, and development of Women Self Help Groups in the handloom, handicrafts, and textiles sector.
##### ***Earmarking/Reservation***
At least 70% of the training beneficiaries must be individual women or women belonging to Women Self Help Groups.
##### ***Relaxation***
If an entrepreneur collaborates with the purpose of converting the centre into a full-fledged manufacturing unit by employing at least 50% of its trainees, the condition of training over 70% women will not be applicable.
##### ***Post-Selection Conditions***

- The applicant must sign a Memorandum of Understanding with the National Jute Board.
- The applicant must submit an annual action plan supported by a list of confirmed bonafide trainees.
- The applicant must submit a bank guarantee equivalent to 50% of the activities cost of the approved cycles.
- The applicant must set up the centre by procuring and installing new branded machineries within one month from the date of the in-principle approval.

## Application Process

### Online

##### **Step 1:****** ***Accessing the Portal***
The applicant must access the [official website](https://www.jute.com) of the National Jute Board to begin the application procedure. The application must be initiated through the designated online portal.
##### **Step 2:****** ***Filling the Online Proposal***
The applicant must complete the online application by filling in all the relevant details, including agency profile, infrastructure facilities, past experience in jute diversified products, and proposed action plans. All requested information must be accurately entered into the online system to generate an acknowledgement.
##### **Step 3:****** ***Submitting the Hard Copies***
After successfully submitting the online proposal, the applicant must print the online acknowledgement. The printed acknowledgement, accompanied by a covering letter on the official letterhead of the company, must be prepared for offline submission.
##### **Step 4:****** ***Physical Document Dispatch***
The applicant must compile all the prescribed supporting documents and attach them to the printed acknowledgement. This complete application package must be submitted physically to the Head Office or the respective Regional Office of the National Jute Board, depending on the jurisdiction of the proposed centre.
##### **Step 5:****** ***Evaluation and Selection***
The submitted proposals will be scrutinized and evaluated by a Sub-Committee based on predefined criteria such as agency experience, proximity of location, and infrastructure. Agencies scoring a minimum of 50 marks will be shortlisted and recommended to the Monitoring Committee for final approval and selection.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### In order to successfully proceed after receiving the initial approval, what financial assurances or security deposits do I need to provide to the implementing authority for the approved training cycles?

You must submit a bank guarantee equivalent to 50 percent of the activities cost. This amounts to ₹1,24,000/- for 1 cycle or ₹2,48,000/- if you plan to conduct 2 cycles simultaneously.
<br>

### What are the exact physical space and area requirements that my organization must possess in order to be considered eligible for establishing the proposed production and training facility?

Your organization must have an available space of at least 500 square feet specifically dedicated to setting up the resource and production center.
<br>

### Are there any specific citizenship or nationality requirements that my organization and its core team members need to fulfill to be considered as a valid applicant?

Yes, your organization must be a bonafide Indian agency, and all its functionaries must be citizens of India.
<br>

### If the individuals I train decide to start their own independent business instead of taking up a job, what minimum financial targets must they achieve?

Self-employed beneficiaries must achieve a minimum sales target of jute diversified products worth ₹5,000/- per month or ₹15,000/- per quarter.
<br>

### While selecting individuals for the upcoming skill development batches, is there any specific demographic quota or reservation that I need to maintain among the participants?

At least 70 percent of the total training beneficiaries must be individual women or women belonging to Women Self Help Groups.
<br>

### Under what specific circumstances can my manufacturing business be exempted from the mandatory rule of ensuring high female participation in the skill development batches?

The condition of training over 70 percent women does not apply if you intend to convert the center into a full-fledged manufacturing unit by employing at least 50 percent of the individuals you train.
<br>

### Apart from the space required for the actual production setup, what are the mandatory infrastructural requirements for my administrative office to qualify for the collaboration?

You must have an independent office measuring at least 250 square feet. It must be equipped with a telephone or mobile connection, a computer or laptop, and internet email facilities.
<br>

### How many years of relevant sector experience must my enterprise or non-governmental organization possess in bulk manufacturing and skill development to apply successfully?

You must have a minimum of 3 years of active experience in product identification, bulk production, skill training, and the marketing of jute diversified products.
<br>

### Following the successful conclusion of the skill development phases, what is the mandatory placement or employment rate that my organization must ensure for the attendees?

You must ensure that at least 50 percent of the trained beneficiaries are successfully employed or engaged in manufacturing activities related to jute diversified products.
<br>

### What specific types of legal entities, businesses, or organizational structures are officially permitted to submit a proposal for running the resource and production centers?

Eligible entities include registered entrepreneurs, manufacturers, exporters, merchant traders, non-governmental organizations, cooperative societies, federations, and public sector undertakings.
<br>

### During the official review of my submitted proposal by the monitoring committee, what is the minimum qualifying score I must achieve to be considered for final selection?

Your application must secure a minimum of 50 marks out of 100 in the predefined evaluation criteria to become eligible for final selection.
<br>

### Will my organization receive any special priority or advantage during the selection process if we have a strong background in empowering community-based female groups?

Yes, preference is given to recognized agencies with at least 3 years of experience in training, promotion, and development of Women Self Help Groups in handlooms, handicrafts, or textiles.
<br>

### What mandatory documentation or specific financial accounts must the targeted beneficiaries possess before they can be officially enrolled in the proposed skill development batches?

Every selected trainee must possess a valid Aadhaar linked biometric attendance number and a mandatory Aadhaar linked bank account.
<br>

### Once my proposal receives the initial in-principle approval from the authorities, what is the strict timeline within which I must procure and completely install the required equipment?

You are required to procure and install new branded machineries to fully set up the center within exactly 1 month from the date of receiving the in-principle approval.
