## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide central assistance for the cost of cycles with ice boxes for fisheries activities. Through this scheme, central assistance is provided for newly purchased cycles with ice boxes for the initial crop.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Pradhan Mantri Matasya Sampada Yojana: Cycle with Ice Boxes" by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide central assistance for the cost of cycles with ice boxes to beneficiaries involved in fisheries activities. Through this scheme, central assistance for Cycle with Ice Box costs is provided for the initial crop only in the purchase of newly purchased cycles with ice boxes.
<br>

## Benefits

- The scheme provides a subsidy for the cycle with ice boxes for fisheries activities. 
- The project cost is ₹0.10/- lakh per unit.
- The subsidy will be limited to 40% of the project cost for beneficiaries falling under the General Category.
- The subsidy will be limited to 60% of the project cost for beneficiaries who are Scheduled Caste (SC) or Women.

<br>

> **Note:** The Government of India and the Government of Haryana shall not be responsible for any losses incurred on procurement, operation, maintenance, and management of the fish transport facilities.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. Beneficiary must have Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).
1. Beneficiaries should ensure that fish transport facilities are maintained in operational condition.
1. Beneficiaries shall be provided central assistance for Cycle with Ice box the initial crop only in the purchase of newly purchased Cycle with Ice Box
1. Central assistance for Cycle with Ice Box cost shall be released only for the fisheries activity.
1. Govt. of India and Govt. of Haryana shall not be responsible for any losses incurred on procurement, operation, maintenance and management of the fish transport facilities.

<br>

## Documents Required

1. Agreement Deed between Vendor and Fisheries Department.
1. Date of Birth Certificate (Voting Card/Birth Certificate/Aadhaar Card/Matriculation Certificate).
1. Caste Certificate from First Class Magistrate (Tehsildar).
1. Training Certificate of Vendor/Beneficiary.
1. Bills/Receipts/Vouchers.
1. Photographs of Beneficiary with Cycle with Ice-Box.
1. Detail of Bank Account and PAN Card.
1. Detailed Project Report (DPR)/Self Contained Proposal (SCP) as per Pradhan Mantri Matasya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) guidelines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of this scheme?

The scheme aims to provide central assistance for Cycle with Ice Box costs for the initial crop only in the purchase of newly purchased Cycle with Ice Box.

### Who are the intended beneficiaries of this scheme?

Beneficiaries involved in fisheries activities are the intended recipients.

### What is the financial assistance provided under this scheme?

The project cost is ₹0.10 lakh per unit.

### What percentage of subsidy can a General Category beneficiary expect?

General Category beneficiaries are eligible for a subsidy limited to 40% of the project cost.

### What percentage of subsidy is available for SC/Women beneficiaries?

SC/Women beneficiaries are eligible for a subsidy limited to 60% of the project cost.

### Will the central assistance cover the cost of multiple crops?

No, central assistance for Cycle with Ice Box costs is provided for the initial crop only.

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

Required documents include an Agreement deed between vendor and Fisheries Department, Date of Birth Certificate, Voting Card/Birth Certificate/Aadhar Card/Matriculation Certificate, Caste Certificate, Training Certificate, Bills/Receipts/Vouchers, Photographs of beneficiary with Cycle with Ice-Box, Detail of Bank Account and Pan Card, and Detailed Project Report (DPR)/Self Contained Proposal (SCP) as per PMMSY guidelines.

### Is a training certificate necessary for the application?

Yes, a Training Certificate of Vendor/beneficiary is required.

### Do I need to submit photographs with the application?

Yes, photographs of the beneficiary with the Cycle with Ice-Box are required.

### What financial details are required in the application?

Details of your Bank Account and PAN Card are required

### Is there a specific report or proposal required for the application?

Yes, the beneficiary will submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR)/Self-Contained Proposal (SCP) as per PMMSY guidelines.

### What specific condition must be maintained for the fish transport facilities?

Beneficiaries should ensure that fish transport facilities are maintained in operational condition.

### Does the scheme cover procurement, operation, maintenance, and management costs for the fish transport facilities?

No, the Government of India and the Government of Haryana are not responsible for losses incurred on these aspects.
