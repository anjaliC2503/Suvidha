## Brief Description

The Bihar Government provides incentives to individuals belonging to the EBC Categories upon passing the preliminary examination of various competitive exams such as UPSC, BPSC, NDA, CDS, Banking, Railway, SSC, etc., to support their further preparation for the main examination/interview.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;**Chief Minister EBC Civil Seva Protsahan Yojana**&quot; has been initiated by the Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes Welfare Department, Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, the Bihar Government provides incentives to individuals belonging to the Extremely Backward Classes upon passing the preliminary examination of various competitive exams such as UPSC, BPSC, Bihar Judicial Service, NDA, CDS, Banking, Railway, SSC, etc., to support their further preparation for the main examination/interview. The scheme is designed to provide financial assistance to students from the Most Backward Classes (MBC) who are preparing for civil services exams.

## Benefits

Under this scheme, candidates who pass the preliminary examination of competitive exams are provided with a prescribed incentive amount ranging from ₹30,000/- to ₹1,00,000/- as under:

## Exclusions

- Candidates who are already employed in any government department, public sector undertaking, or state-funded institution are not eligible for this scheme.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Bihar.
   1. The applicant should belong to the Extremely Backward Class (EBC) category.
   1. The applicant should have passed the preliminary examination of a competitive exam, such as UPSC, BPSC, Bihar Judicial Service, NDA, CDS, Banking, Railway, SSC, etc.
   1. The applicant should not have previously received the same incentive from any other source.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [**official website**](https://bcebconline.bihar.gov.in/cspy/EBCscholarship/Index.aspx). Click &quot;[**New Registration**](https://bcebconline.bihar.gov.in/cspy/EBCscholarship/PETC_Terms.aspx)&quot; option, in case of a new applicant. 
**Step 2:** In case of **&quot;New Registration&quot;** carefully read the &quot;Guidelines for Registration&quot; and click &quot;Continue.&quot;
**Step 3:** On successful login**,** enter the form by entering your personal details, educational qualifications, and other required information.
**Step 4:** Upload your photograph, signature, and any necessary documents.
**Step 5:** Ensure all fields marked with an asterisk (*) are filled in correctly.
**Step 6:** Once all the required information is entered, click the &quot;Submit&quot; button. Upon successful submission, you will receive a registration ID and password.
> **Process to apply for scheme:**

**Step 1:** In case already registered, then click on &quot;**A**[**pplicant Login**](https://bcebconline.bihar.gov.in/cspy/EBCscholarship/Login.aspx)&quot; option. 
**Step 2:** Enter your User ID, Password, and the verification code.
**Step 3:** Click &quot;Login&quot; to access your account and fill out the application form.
**Step 4:** Verify your entries before submitting the application. No modifications can be made after the final submission.
**Step 5:** After verifying your application form, click &quot;Submit&quot; to save your application.

  - You may print the application form as a draft.
  - An application ID will be generated after the final submission.
  - Only the final submission will be considered.

**Step 6:** Print a copy of the finally submitted application form.
**Note:** 

   1. In case the applicant faces any issue while submitting the application form, one can submit a &quot;[**Request Query**](https://bcebconline.bihar.gov.in/cspy/EBCscholarship/RequestQuery.aspx)&quot; to the department raising the concerns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Applicants must be permanent residents of Bihar, belong to the EBC category, and have passed the preliminary exam of a recognized competitive exam (e.g., UPSC, BPSC, SSC). Additionally, they should not have previously received this incentive from any other source.

### What are the benefits of this scheme?

Successful candidates can receive an incentive ranging from [?]30,000 to [?]1,00,000, depending on the competitive exam they have passed.

### Which competitive exams are covered under this scheme?

Exams such as UPSC, BPSC, Bihar Judicial Service, NDA, CDS, Banking, Railway, SSC, and other recognized exams are covered under this scheme.

### How do I apply for this scheme?

You can apply by visiting the official website: https://bcebconline.bih.nic.in/.

### Is there an age limit to apply for the scheme?

No specific age limit is mentioned, but you must meet the eligibility criteria of the competitive exam you have passed.

### Are candidates who are employed in government jobs eligible?

No, candidates already employed in any government department, public sector undertaking, or state-funded institution are not eligible for the scheme.

### Can I apply for the scheme if I have already received a similar incentive from another source?

No, you cannot apply if you have previously received the same incentive from any other source.

### How much incentive is provided for passing the UPSC preliminary exam?

The incentive amount for passing the UPSC preliminary exam can go up to [?]1,00,000.

### Do I need to submit proof of passing the preliminary exam?

Yes, you will need to submit proof of having passed the preliminary exam of the competitive exam.

### Can I apply multiple times if I pass different exams?

No, the incentive is provided only once. You cannot apply again for a different exam after receiving the incentive.
