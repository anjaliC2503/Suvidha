## Brief Description

This initiative aims to promote the physical, mental, and emotional well-being of adolescents aged 10 to 19 years by providing free health counselling and guidance.

## Detailed Description

The “Kishor Swasthya Paramarsh Clinic” is an initiative under the National Health Mission (NHM) implemented by the Department of Medical Health and Family Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand. The initiative aims to promote the physical, mental, and emotional well-being of adolescents aged 10 to 19 years by providing free health counselling and guidance.
<br>

## Benefits

Free counselling services are provided at the Adolescent Health Clinics.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiary must be an adolescent between 10 and 19 years of age from Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Free consultation is provided upon registration at 13 district hospitals, 2 sub-district hospitals, and 45 community health centres across the state.
<br>

Documents are required as per the guidelines prescribed for the dedicated Adolescent Health Clinics.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to benefit from this initiative?

All adolescents aged 10 to 19 years in the state of Uttarakhand are eligible to avail services under this initiative.
<br>

### What services are provided at the Clinics?

The clinics provide free counselling, health consultations, and awareness services.
<br>

### Are the counselling services free of cost?

Yes, all counselling and consultation services at the Adolescent Health Clinics are free of cost.
<br>

### Where are these clinics located in Uttarakhand?

Free consultation services are available at 13 district hospitals, 2 sub-district hospitals, and 45 community health centres across the state.
<br>

### How can an adolescent avail services at the clinic?

Adolescents can visit at the nearest district hospital, sub-district hospital, or community health centre offering Adolescent Health Clinic services.
<br>
