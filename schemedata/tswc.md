## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage new generation playwrights to create quality Tiatr scripts and enrich Konkani language. It provides cash prizes and publication opportunities for original traditional format scripts.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Tiatr Script Writing Competition" by the Tiatr Academy of Goa aims to encourage new generation of Tiatr playwrights to script better quality of Tiatrs and enrich the Konkani language through annual script writing competitions. The scheme provides cash prizes up to ₹15,000/- for winning scripts and opportunities for publication with honorarium payments, along with certificates for recognition of literary excellence in traditional Konkani theatre. The applicant must write original scripts in traditional tiatr format with minimum 6-7 podd'de and at least 4 Cantos, and must submit scripts that have not been published or performed earlier. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications are accepted through offline submission of typed copies with duly filled application forms.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Cash Prizes***

- 1st Prize: ₹15,000/-
- 2nd Prize: ₹10,000/-
- 3rd Prize: ₹5,000/-

##### ***Additional Benefits***

- **Certificates:** Provided to first three prize winners.- **Publication Opportunity:** Prize winning scripts can be printed in book form at Tiatr Academy of Goa's cost (subject to No Objection Certificate from script writer).- **Honorarium:** ₹5,000/- one-time payment for published scripts (in addition to prize money).- **Complimentary Copies:** 100 copies of the published book provided to script writer.
##### ***Conditions***

- Scripts must comply with content guidelines - no adverse remarks against any Governments/Individuals/Institutions or anything against caste, creed and religion
- For publication benefits, script writers must provide No Objection Certificate to Tiatr Academy of Goa.

<br>

## Eligibility

##### ***Basic Requirements***

- The applicant must write scripts on themes as specified by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
- The applicant must ensure the script has not been published or performed earlier.
- The applicant must write original scripts only.

##### ***Script Format Requirements***

- The applicant must write scripts in traditional tiatr format.
- The applicant must include minimum 6 to 7 podd'de in the script.
- The applicant must include at least 4 Cantos in the script.
- The applicant must write scripts in Konkani language using Roman script.
- The applicant must ensure comedy is inter-related to the main plot (however, comedy may be absent in religious tiatr).

##### ***Content Guidelines***

- The applicant must ensure scripts do not contain adverse remarks against any Governments/Individuals/Institutions.
- The applicant must ensure scripts do not contain content against caste, creed and religion that may hurt sentiments.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields. Attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment_ 
##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 1:****** ***Evaluation Process***
The submitted scripts will be evaluated by a panel of judges appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa, whose decision will be final and binding. The judging process will assess scripts based on quality, format compliance, and adherence to specified themes.
**Step 2:** **Result Declaration and Prize Distribution**
Winners will be announced after the judging process is completed, and the first three best scripts will receive cash prizes and certificates as specified. Prize winners will be contacted for any additional procedures related to publication opportunities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the structural requirements that my script must meet to be eligible for submission?

Your script must be written in traditional tiatr format with minimum 6 to 7 podd'de and at least 4 Cantos. Additionally, comedy should be inter-related to the main plot, though comedy may be absent in religious tiatr.
<br>

### Is there any restriction on the age of participants who can apply for this competition?

There is no age limit for participants. Writers of any age can participate in the Tiatr script writing competition.
<br>

### What language and script format should I use when writing my submission?

The script should be written in Konkani language using Roman script only.
<br>

### Can I submit a script that has been previously published or performed somewhere else?

No, the script of the Tiatr should not have been published or performed earlier. Only original, unpublished, and unperformed scripts are eligible.
<br>

### How many physical copies of my manuscript do I need to submit along with my application?

You should submit three typed copies of the manuscript along with the duly filled application form.
<br>

### What monetary rewards can I expect if my script wins one of the top positions?

The first prize is ₹15,000/-, second prize is ₹10,000/-, and third prize is ₹5,000/-. All winners also receive certificates.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions on the themes or content I can include in my script?

Yes, there should not be any adverse remarks against any governments, individuals, institutions, or anything against caste, creed, and religion in the scripts or lyrics that may hurt anyone's sentiments.
<br>

### Who will evaluate my submission and how will the winners be determined?

A panel of judges will judge the scripts and their decision will be final regarding the winners.
<br>

### What additional benefits can I receive if my script wins and I agree to its publication?

If you provide a No Objection Certificate to print your script in book form, Tiatr Academy of Goa will print it at their cost and pay you a one-time honorarium of ₹5,000/- in addition to the prize money, plus give you 100 copies of the book.
<br>

### Do I have the freedom to choose any theme for my script or are there specific requirements?

The themes will be as specified by Tiatr Academy of Goa, so you must follow the themes announced for each competition.
<br>

### What happens if my script is found to contain objectionable content after submission?

If your script contains adverse remarks against governments, individuals, institutions, caste, creed, or religion, Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to disqualify your script.
<br>

### How often is this competition organized and when can I expect the next opportunity to participate?

Tiatr Academy of Goa organizes this script writing competition every year.
<br>

### Can the organizing body make changes to the competition rules after I have submitted my application?

Yes, Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to make any changes in the rules of the competition.
<br>

### What type of declaration do I need to make when submitting my application?

You must declare that you have read and understood the rules, enclosed three typed copies of the script, confirm that the script is original and has not been performed or published earlier, and state that all information provided is true to the best of your knowledge.
<br>
