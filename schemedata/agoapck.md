## Brief Description

The scheme aims to attract youth towards dramatic activities and promote Goan culture through Konkani theatre. It provides cash awards, mementos, and certificates for best One-Act Play performances along with individual category awards for youth, clubs, and associations.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "All Goa One-Act Play Competition in Konkani" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to attract youth towards dramatic activities and provide ample opportunities to showcase their talents in dramatics while promoting Goan culture through Konkani theatre. The scheme provides cash awards for the top 3 performances along with mementos and certificates, individual awards for various categories like direction, acting, music, and stage setting, and reimbursement of transport costs as per Regional Transport Office norms. The applicant must be an individual, club, or association capable of presenting a One-Act Play in Konkani with duration between 35 to 45 minutes, and must submit a typed copy of the play in Roman script along with written permission from the playwright. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline.
<br>

## Benefits

- Cash awards for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd best performances (amount as decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa).
- Mementos and certificates for top 3 performances.
- Individual awards with mementos (no cash) for Direction, Male Actor, Female Actor, Background Music, Stage Setting, Light Effects, Make-up, and Script.
- Reimbursement of transport expenses as per Regional Transport Office norms.
- Video recording of all plays for preservation in Tiatr Academy of Goa Library.

_*Performance must not contain adverse remarks against any governments, individuals, institutions, or anything against caste, creed, and religion._
_*Participants must abide by all competition rules and regulations._
_*Any violation may result in disqualification and forfeiture of benefits._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be an individual, club, or association.
- The applicant must present a One-Act Play in Konkani language.
- The applicant must ensure the play duration is not less than 35 minutes and not more than 45 minutes.
- The applicant must ensure participation only when at least 5 groups are participating in the competition.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
_*Applications are considered on a "First come First Serve" basis, subject to the availability of funds._
_*Performance date and time will be determined by draw of lots._
_*If applicant withdraws after schedule is drawn, full security deposit will be forfeited._
<br>

## Documents Required

- Self-Attested Copy of Aadhaar Card.
- Self-Attested Copy of Permanent Account Number Card (if applicable).
- Typed Copy of the One-Act Play in Roman Script.
- Written Permission From the Playwright.
- Security Deposit of ₹1,000/- in Cash or Demand Draft in favour of Member Secretary, Tiatr Academy of Goa, payable at Panaji.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if our group decides to withdraw from the competition after the performance schedule has been officially drawn and announced?

If you withdraw after the schedule is drawn by Tiatr Academy of Goa, your complete security deposit of ₹1,000/- will be forfeited and will not be returned.
<br>

### Can we submit our security deposit payment through a personal or organizational cheque along with our application form?

No, cheques are not accepted for security deposit payment. You must pay ₹1,000/- either in cash or through a demand draft in favour of Member Secretary, Tiatr Academy of Goa, payable at Panaji.
<br>

### What are the minimum and maximum time limits that our One-Act Play performance must strictly adhere to during the competition?

Your One-Act Play performance must be not less than 35 minutes and not more than 45 minutes in total duration.
<br>

### Are there any specific individual performance categories for which separate recognition awards will be presented during the competition?

Yes, individual awards with mementos will be given for Direction, Male Actor, Female Actor, Background Music, Stage Setting, Light Effects, Make-up, and Script categories.
<br>

### What format and script requirements must we follow when submitting our One-Act Play manuscript along with the application?

You must submit a typed copy of your One-Act Play written in Roman Script format along with your application form.
<br>

### Will the organizing academy provide any financial assistance or reimbursement for our group's travel expenses to participate in the competition?

Yes, Tiatr Academy of Goa will reimburse the amount you spend on transport for participants as per Regional Transport Office norms.
<br>

### What is the minimum number of participating groups required for the competition to be officially organized and conducted?

At least 5 groups must register and participate for the competition to be organized and conducted by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
<br>

### How much time will our group be allocated for setting up our stage arrangements, lighting equipment, and other technical preparations?

Each participating group will be given exactly 15 minutes to complete stage setting, light setting arrangements, and other technical preparations.
<br>

### What documentary permission must we obtain from the original creator before submitting our entry for the competition?

You must obtain and submit written permission from the playwright or original writer of the One-Act Play along with your application.
<br>

### What types of content or themes are strictly prohibited and could lead to immediate disqualification from the competition?

Any adverse remarks written or verbal against governments, individuals, institutions, or anything against caste, creed, and religion that may hurt sentiments are strictly prohibited.
<br>

### What is the maximum number of groups that can participate in this competition and on what basis are they selected?

A maximum of 18 groups will be permitted to participate in the competition on a first come first serve basis.
<br>

### What cash prizes and recognition will be awarded to the top performing groups in this dramatic competition?

Three awards for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd positions will include mementos, certificates, and cash amounts as decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
<br>

### Who are eligible to apply and participate in this One-Act Play competition organized by the cultural academy?

Individuals, clubs, and associations are all eligible to participate in this One-Act Play competition organized by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
<br>

### How will the performance schedule and timing be determined for all the participating groups in the competition?

The date and time for performance of One-Act Plays will be determined through a draw of lots system.
<br>
