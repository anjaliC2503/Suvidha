## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide monthly financial assistance of ₹1,500/- to widows in the Union Territory of Lakshadweep. It covers widows who have not remarried and have no source of income.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Union Territory of Lakshadweep Pension Scheme for Widows&quot; was introduced by the Social Welfare &amp; Tribal Affairs Department, Union Territory of Lakshadweep, with the aim of providing monthly financial assistance to widows in the UT. The scheme covers widows who have not remarried and have no source of income. The District Panchayat and Village (Dweep) Panchayats (VDPs) of the Union Territory of Lakshadweep implement the scheme to ensure smooth processing and disbursement. The pension amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary’s bank account.

## Benefits

**Financial Assistance:**

- Eligible beneficiaries receive a pension of ₹1,500/- per month (₹18,000 per annum).

**Mode of Disbursement:**

- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the beneficiary's bank account.

**Frequency of Disbursement:**

- The pension is disbursed on a monthly basis.

## Exclusions

1. The applicant is not eligible if she has remarried.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of the Union Territory of Lakshadweep.
1. The applicant should be a widow.
1. The applicant should not have remarried.
1. The applicant should have only nominal income, not exceeding ₹1,500/- per month (₹18,000/- per annum).
1. The applicant should not be a beneficiary of any other similar pension scheme provided by the government.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Application Process:**
**Step 1:** The eligible applicants may collect the application form from the nearest Village (Dweep) Panchayat office.
**Step 2:** Carefully fill out the application form and attach all supporting documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the completed application form along with the required documents to the Executive Officer at the Village (Dweep) Panchayat office.
**Post-Application Process:**
**Step 1:** The application is verified by the members of the Evaluation Committee of the respective Village (Dweep) Panchayat.
**Step 2:** After successful verification, the application is forwarded to the Chief Executive Officer, District Panchayat (HQ), Kavaratti, for further processing.
**Step 3:** The forwarded application undergoes re-verification by District Panchayat officials.
**Step 4:** Upon successful re-verification at the District Panchayat level, the list of eligible pensioners is prepared through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) and submitted to the Social Welfare &amp; Tribal Affairs Department for payment processing.
**Step 5:** Payments are made through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode, money is paid through Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.

1. Any other documents if required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a widow who has remarried apply for this pension?

No, remarried widows are not eligible for the pension.

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

Applicants need to submit proof of widowhood, proof of age, proof of residence, an income certificate, bank details, and a self-declaration confirming non-remarriage.

### Can the pension be transferred to another family member in case of the beneficiary’s death?

No.

### How frequently is the pension credited to the beneficiary’s bank account?

The pension is credited every month through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

### Where can an applicant submit the application for this scheme?

The application can be submitted at the nearest Village (Dweep) Panchayat office.

### Can a widow residing outside Lakshadweep apply for this scheme?

No.

### What happens if a beneficiary does not receive the pension on time?

In case of delays, beneficiaries should contact the concerned Village (Dweep) Panchayat office.

### Are beneficiaries required to submit a self-declaration every year?

Yes, a self-declaration confirming non-remarriage and income status may be required periodically.

### Can an applicant without a bank account apply for the scheme?

No.

### Can a beneficiary reapply if her application was previously rejected?

Yes, if the applicant now meets the eligibility criteria, she can reapply with proper documentation.
