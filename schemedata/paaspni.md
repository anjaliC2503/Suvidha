## Brief Description

The scheme aims to strengthen values of Patriotism & National Integration among Tiatr fraternity by promoting production of Konkani audio albums. It provides financial support for producing albums with at least 10 patriotic songs for distribution to educational institutions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Production of Audio Albums of songs on Patriotism & National Integration" was launched by the Tiatr Academy of Goa under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to strengthen the values of Patriotism & National Integration among members of Tiatr fraternity, addressing the problem that audio albums of Konkani songs of Patriotism & National Integration are rarely produced in Goa despite numerous audio albums of songs (Kantaram) in Konkani being produced. The scheme provides financial support as decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa for the production of audio albums containing at least 10 songs of Patriotism & National Integration and distribution of 300 copies to schools and groups participating in various competitions. The applicant must be a tiatr artiste (commercial or non-commercial). The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications for this scheme are accepted offline.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Financial Support***
Tiatr Academy of Goa will give financial support as decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa for the production of the Album.
##### ***Distribution Channel***
Tiatr Academy of Goa will distribute 300 copies to schools & groups which participate in its various competitions and may also offer them to other schools, groups, individuals, etc. who are interested in promoting Patriotism & National Integration.
##### ***Commercial Rights***
The applicant is free to sell the remaining Compact Discs.
##### ***Sponsorship Permission***
The applicant may take sponsorship to meet the cost of production of Album.
##### ***Quality Assurance***
Lyrics will be scrutinized by the Recommendation Committee appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa with advisory support to improve quality.
<br>

**Conditions**
_*The applicant must produce the Audio album strictly following the specified norms._
_*Melody and musical arrangement must be appropriate to the content of the song._
_*Lyrics must be approved by the Recommendation Committee._
_*Official letter of acceptance must be obtained from Tiatr Academy of Goa before production._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a tiatr artiste (commercial as well as non-commercial).
- The applicant must submit a typed copy of lyrics of songs to Tiatr Academy of Goa along with the application form.
- The applicant must obtain approval of lyrics from the Recommendation Committee appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
- The applicant must receive official letter of acceptance from Tiatr Academy of Goa before production.
- The applicant must not make any adverse remark in any form either written or verbal against any Governments/Individuals/Institutions or anything against caste, creed and religion in the lyrics of songs which may hurt the sentiments of anybody.

##### ***Album Content Requirements***

- The audio album must include at least 10 songs of Patriotism & National Integration.
- The album should not have solo songs only.
- 50% of the songs must be group songs and children's songs.
- Melody and musical arrangement must be appropriate to the content of the song.
- The applicant will have to give 300 copies of Audio albums to Tiatr Academy of Goa.

##### ***Quality Control Process***

- Lyrics will be scrutinized by the Recommendation Committee.
- Committee will be free to advise the lyricists to improve the quality of lyrics.
- Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to disapprove the lyrics as per the opinion given by Recommendation Committee.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
_*The application must be submitted to the office of the Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days in advance of the proposed programme date._
_*Applications are considered on a "First come First Serve" basis, subject to the availability of funds._
<br>

##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 1:****** 
***Lyrics Scrutiny & Committee Feedback***

- The applicant must wait for the Recommendation Committee appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa to scrutinize the submitted lyrics, during which the committee may advise improvements to enhance the quality of lyrics and ensure alignment with patriotism and national integration themes.
- The applicant must incorporate any suggestions or improvements recommended by the Recommendation Committee to enhance the quality of lyrics, ensuring the content meets the required standards for patriotism and national integration themes.

**Step 2:****** 
***Official Approval & Acceptance Letter***

- The applicant must wait for the final decision from Tiatr Academy of Goa, as the academy reserves the right to disapprove the lyrics based on the opinion given by the Recommendation Committee, and will issue an official letter of acceptance if the lyrics are approved. 
- The applicant must obtain the official letter of acceptance from Tiatr Academy of Goa confirming approval of the lyrics before proceeding with the production of the audio album, as this letter is mandatory for proceeding to the production phase.

**Step 3:****** 
***Album Production & Financial Support***

- The applicant must plan the production of the audio album strictly following the approved norms including minimum 10 songs, ensuring 50% are group and children's songs, and that melody and musical arrangement are appropriate to the content of each song.
- The applicant must coordinate with Tiatr Academy of Goa to receive the financial support as decided by the academy for the production of the album, and may also arrange additional sponsorship to meet the complete cost of production if needed.

**Step 4:****** 
***Produce Audio Album & Submit Required Copies***

- The applicant must produce the audio album following all specified norms and quality standards, ensuring professional recording quality and appropriate musical arrangements that complement the patriotic and national integration themes of the songs. 
- The applicant must provide 300 copies of the completed audio albums to Tiatr Academy of Goa as per the scheme requirements, which will be used by the academy for distribution to schools and groups participating in various competitions.

**Step 5:****** 
***Complete Documentation***

- The applicant must ensure all production documentation is complete and maintain records of the album production process, quality standards met, and compliance with all scheme norms for future reference and verification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum number of songs required in the audio album and what specific themes must they address?

The audio album must contain at least 10 songs specifically focused on Patriotism & National Integration themes. Each song's melody and musical arrangement must be appropriate to the content, ensuring alignment with the patriotic values the program aims to promote.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can I include only solo performances in my audio album or are there specific composition requirements I must follow?

You cannot have an album with solo songs only. At least 50% of your songs must be group songs and children's songs, ensuring variety in vocal arrangements and making the content suitable for educational and community participation.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What type of content restrictions apply to the lyrics I compose for the patriotic songs in my album?

You must not include any adverse remarks in written or verbal form against any Governments, Individuals, or Institutions, nor anything against caste, creed, and religion that may hurt anybody's sentiments. All content must promote positive patriotic and national integration values.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How many copies of the finished audio album must I provide and what happens to these copies after submission?

You must provide 300 copies of your completed audio albums to Tiatr Academy of Goa. These copies will be distributed to schools and groups participating in various competitions, and may also be offered to other educational institutions and individuals interested in promoting patriotism.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this financial support program for producing patriotic audio albums in Konkani language?

Any tiatr artiste, whether commercial or non-commercial, can apply for this program. You must demonstrate your involvement in the tiatr fraternity and commitment to producing quality Konkani songs focused on patriotic themes.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents must I submit along with my application form to be considered for this audio album production scheme?

You need to submit a prescribed application form, self-attested copy of Aadhaar Card, self-attested copy of Permanent Account Number Card, and a typed copy of lyrics for all songs you plan to include in your album.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there a quality control process for the song lyrics I submit, and who evaluates the content before approval?

Yes, your lyrics will be scrutinized by a Recommendation Committee appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The committee has the authority to advise improvements to enhance lyrical quality and the academy reserves the right to disapprove lyrics based on committee recommendations.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can I seek additional financial support through sponsorship while participating in this government-supported audio album production program?

Yes, you are permitted to take sponsorship to meet the cost of album production in addition to the financial support provided by Tiatr Academy of Goa. This flexibility helps ensure you can produce a high-quality album meeting all specified standards.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What commercial rights do I retain after providing the required copies to the academy for distribution purposes?

You retain full commercial rights to sell the remaining Compact Discs after providing the mandatory 300 copies to Tiatr Academy of Goa. This allows you to recover additional production costs and potentially earn from your creative work.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Must I obtain any official approval before beginning the actual production of my patriotic audio album?

Yes, you must obtain an official letter of acceptance from Tiatr Academy of Goa before commencing production. This letter confirms approval of your lyrics and authorization to proceed with album production according to the specified norms.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What specific information must I provide in the application form regarding the composition breakdown of my proposed album?

You must provide details including your name, complete address, contact number, Permanent Account Number, Aadhaar Card number, album title, producer name, and detailed breakdown of Kantaram specifying the number of solos, duets, duos, trios, quartets/quintets, and choral songs.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where can I obtain the application form for this audio album production scheme and is online submission available?

You must visit the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa to collect the prescribed application form. The scheme accepts applications through offline mode only, requiring physical submission of all documents and materials.
<br>

### What happens if the Recommendation Committee suggests changes to my submitted song lyrics during the review process?

You must incorporate any suggestions or improvements recommended by the committee to enhance the quality of your lyrics. The committee provides advisory support to ensure your content meets the required standards for patriotism and national integration themes.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions on the musical arrangement and melody choices I make for the patriotic songs?

The melody and musical arrangement must be appropriate to the content of each song. This ensures that the musical elements complement and enhance the patriotic themes, creating an effective audio experience for listeners in educational settings.
<br>
