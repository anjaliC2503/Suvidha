## Brief Description

The scheme is implemented by the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Under this scheme, financial assistance of up to ₹3,500/- is provided to registered construction workers who have been members of the Board for at least one year, for purchasing a bicycle.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Grant for Purchase of Bicycle&quot; scheme is implemented by the Bihar Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar. Under this scheme, financial assistance of up to ₹3,500/- is provided to registered construction workers who have been members of the Board for at least one year, for purchasing a bicycle. The assistance will be provided to workers upon submission of the receipt for the bicycle purchase.

## Benefits

Under this scheme, financial assistance of up to ₹3,500/- is provided to registered construction workers for purchasing a bicycle.

## Eligibility

1. The worker should be engaged in building or construction work.
   1. The worker should be registered with the Bihar Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
   1. The registered worker should have a minimum of one year of continuous membership.
   1. The worker&#39;s membership with the Welfare Board should be active at the time of application.

Note: Assistance will be provided to workers upon submission of the receipt for the bicycle purchase.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration of a Construction Worker under BBOCWWB:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Website/LabourReg.aspx) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
**Step 2:** On the homepage, click on &quot;Labour Registration&quot;.
**Step 3:** If you are not registered, click on &quot;[Apply for New Registration](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Registration/AddLabourRegistrationWeb.aspx)&quot;.
**Step 4:** Enter your Aadhar card number and name, then click &quot;Authenticate&quot;.
**Step 5:** A dashboard will appear. Enter all required details, ensuring that all fields marked with an asterisk (*) are filled in correctly.
**Step 6:** Click on &quot;Submit&quot; and proceed to pay the application fee online.
**Step 7:** You will receive your construction worker registration number upon successful registration.
> **Apply for Scheme Benefits:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Website/SchemeApply.aspx) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
**Step 2:** Click on &quot;Scheme Application&quot; and select &quot;[Apply for Scheme](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Registration/SchemeTaggingWeb.aspx)&quot;.
**Step 3:** Enter your registration number and click &quot;Show&quot; to view your details.
**Step 4:** Select the scheme you wish to apply for to avail the benefits.
**Step 5:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents.
**Step 6:** Click on &quot;Submit Application&quot; to complete the process. 
> **Application Tracking:**

Applicant can track their application status online through the [official website](https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Registration/ApplicationStatus.aspx) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Registered construction workers who have been members of the BBOCWWB for at least one year are eligible, provided their membership is active at the time of applying.

### What is the amount of financial assistance provided under the scheme?

A maximum financial assistance of [?]3,500 is provided to the eligible workers for purchasing a bicycle.

### What type of workers are covered under this scheme?

Unorganized workers involved in building, road, bridge, and dam construction, masons, carpenters, painters, electricians, plumbers, and various other construction-related roles are covered under the scheme.

### Can workers from all sectors apply for this scheme?

No, workers covered under the Factory Act, 1948, and the Mines Act, 1952, are not eligible for this scheme.

### What is the minimum membership period required for workers to be eligible?

Workers need to have a continuous membership of at least one year with the BBOCWWB.

### Is it necessary to submit a purchase receipt to avail of the grant?

Yes, the receipt for the bicycle purchase must be submitted to receive financial assistance.

### How can a construction worker register with the BBOCWWB?

Workers can register online through the official website of Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Website/LabourReg.aspx

### Where can workers apply for the scheme?

Workers can apply online through the official website of Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Website/SchemeApply.aspx

### Can a worker apply if their BBOCWWB membership has expired?

No, workers must have an active membership at the time of application to be eligible for the scheme.

### How can workers track the status of their application?

Workers can track their application status online through the official website: https://bocw.bihar.gov.in/Registration/ApplicationStatus.aspx

### Can workers apply for this scheme multiple times?

The scheme provides assistance only once for the purchase of a bicycle per eligible worker.
