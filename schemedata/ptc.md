## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial grants (up to ₹3,00,000/- per organization) to registered NGOs/Trusts/Voluntary Organizations for promoting tribal culture among ST(P) communities in Assam's plain districts (outside BTC).

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Promotion of Tribal Culture" by the Directorate of Welfare of Plains Tribes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, aims to provide financial grants to NGOs, Trusts, and Voluntary Organizations working for the advancement of tribal culture among Scheduled Tribes (Plains) in Assam's plain districts (excluding BTC area). The scheme supports initiatives like heritage preservation, literature promotion, cultural activities, and local brews. Eligible organizations must be registered and have prior experience in tribal culture promotion. Applications are invited through advertisements, and grants are disbursed subject to Selection Committee approval.

## Benefits

**Financial Grant:** Maximum of ₹3,00,000/- per organization for tribal culture promotion initiatives (e.g., heritage, literature, local brews).
**Disbursement:** Subject to Selection Committee approval and fund availability.
_*Financial assistance is not a right; approval depends on eligibility and fund availability._

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a Registered NGO/Trust/Voluntary Organization under the Societies Registration Act, 1980, or equivalent.
- The applicant must have a demonstrated background in promoting tribal culture.

_*Priority is given to organizations with ST(P) members._

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The Directorate of Welfare of Plains Tribes and Backward Classes, Assam, will advertise the scheme in print and electronic media, along with the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** The interested applicant should take print of the [prescribed format](https://directoratetribalaffairsplain.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/swf_utility_folder/departments/directorate_wpt_uneecopscloud_com_oid_4/how_do_i/level_1/promotion_of_tribal_culture_form.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 3:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 4:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the Director, WPT & BC, Assam. Applicants are advised to retain a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgment for future reference.
<br>

**Scrutiny & Approval**
The Selection Committee evaluates applications and approves beneficiaries.
**Disbursement**
Approved funds are allocated to selected organizations.
<br>

_* The Selection Committee may reject applications without justification._
_* Organizations must ensure submitted information is accurate; false details may lead to disqualification._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the Promotion of Tribal Culture scheme?

The scheme aims to provide financial grants to organizations working to advance tribal culture among Scheduled Tribes (Plains) in Assam's plain districts, excluding the BTC area.

### Which types of organizations are eligible to apply for this scheme?

Registered NGOs, Trusts, and Voluntary Organizations under the Societies Registration Act, 1980 (or equivalent) with experience in promoting tribal culture may apply.

### What is the maximum financial grant available under this scheme?

Eligible organizations may receive up to ₹3,00,000/- for tribal culture promotion initiatives.

### Which geographical areas are covered under this scheme?

The scheme applies to plain districts of Assam, excluding the Bodoland Territorial Council (BTC) area.

### Is there any priority given to certain types of organizations?

Yes, organizations with Scheduled Tribes (Plains) members receive priority consideration.

### What kinds of activities does this scheme support?

Supported activities include heritage preservation, literature promotion, cultural activities, and local brews related to tribal culture.

### What are the basic eligibility requirements for organizations?

Applicants must be properly registered and have demonstrated experience in promoting tribal culture.

### What should the detailed project report include?

The report must contain the project plan, cost estimates, and complete budget breakdown.

### Can organizations consider the grant as an entitlement?

No, approval is discretionary and depends on eligibility and available funds.

### What consequences might an organization face for submitting false information?

Providing inaccurate details may lead to disqualification from the scheme.

### Are there any restrictions on how the grant money can be used?

Funds must be used for approved tribal culture promotion activities as specified in the project report.

### Can individual artists or cultural practitioners apply?

No, only registered organizations are eligible to apply.

### Is there any preference given to certain types of cultural activities?

The document mentions heritage, literature, and local brews but doesn't indicate preferences.

### Where are the scheme advertisements typically published?

Advertisements appear in print and electronic media.
