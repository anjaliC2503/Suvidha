## Brief Description

The scheme is implemented by the Social Welfare Department to provide financial assistance to individuals with disabilities. The primary objective is to provide financial benefits to eligible Divyangjan to cater to their basic needs. The scheme is targeted at Divyangjan aged between 18 to 59 years.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Pension Scheme to Disabled Persons (Divyangjan)” is implemented by the Social Welfare Department, UT of Dadra &amp; Nagar Haveli and Daman &amp; Diu. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to individuals with disabilities to cater to their basic needs and ensure social security in cases of disablement. The scheme covers Divyangjan aged between 18 and 59 years who have a disability of 80% or above and are domiciles of Dadra &amp; Nagar Haveli and Daman &amp; Diu. Additionally, the annual family income should not exceed ₹1.5 lakh per annum, and the applicant should not be availing financial assistance under any other Divyang pension schemes. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹1,000/- per month is given to eligible beneficiaries.

## Benefits

- Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries receive financial assistance of ₹1,000/- per month.

**Note:** The mode of fund transfer shall be Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a domicile of the districts of Dadra &amp; Nagar Haveli and Daman &amp; Diu.
   1. The applicant should be a person with a disability (Divyangjan).
   1. The applicant should have a disability of 80% or above.
   1. The age of the applicant should be between 18 and 59 years.
   1. The applicant&#39;s family income should not exceed ₹1.5 lakhs per annum.
   1. The applicant should not be availing financial assistance under any other Divyang Pension schemes.

<br>

**Special/General Conditions:** In case of non-availability of age proof the beneficiary shall be referred to Medical Board (Government Hospital) of the concerned district for verification of age. After attaining age of 60 years the beneficiary shall be transferred to scheme “Pension Scheme to Old Age Persons (Senior Citizen)”.
<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Application Process:**
**Step 1:** The applicant can collect the application form from the office of the District Panchayat or Municipal Council of the concerned district. Alternatively, the form can be [downloaded](https://dnhpanchayat.in/uploads/scheme/ApplicationFormsNSAP.pdf) from the official website.
**Step 2:** The applicant should carefully fill out the application form and attach all necessary documents.
**Step 3:** The duly filled application form, addressed to the Chief Executive Officer/Chief Officer of the concerned District Panchayat/Municipal Council, should be submitted to the Gram Panchayat/District Panchayat/Municipal Council of the concerned district.
<br>

**Post Application Process:**
**Step 1:** Field-level staff of the District Panchayat/Municipal Council will verify the submitted documents and scrutinize the application. They will also collect a life certificate from the beneficiaries annually.
**Step 2:** The Chief Executive Officer/Chief Officer of the respective District Panchayat/Municipal Council will review and approve the applications.
<br>

**Timelines:**
•	**Application process:** The application may be processed within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt.
•	**Payment disbursement:** Once the application is approved by the Approving / Recommending authorities, the payment shall be disbursed into the bank account of the Divyangjan by the fifth of every month.
<br>

**Grievance Redressal &amp; Contact:**
For any grievance, the aggrieved Divyangjan may approach the Chief Executive Officer/Chief Officer of District Panchayat/ Municipal Council in the concerned district or District Collector of the concerned district or Department of Social Welfare.
<br>

## Documents Required

1. Age Proof (Birth Certificate/School leaving Certificate)
   1. In case of non-availability of age proof, the beneficiary shall be referred to the Medical Board (Government Hospital) of the concerned district for verification of age
   1. Income Certificate issued by the Competent Authority
   1. Domicile Certificate issued by the Competent Authority
   1. Disability Certificate (80% &amp; more) issued by Medical Board (Government Hospital) of the concerned district
   1. Copy of Bank Pass Book (Aadhaar linked)
   1. Copy of Aadhaar Card
   1. Election Card/Passport/Ration card (any one)
   1. Passport size photographs (2 copies)
   1. Undertaking/Affidavit (Not getting any pension from any other department) in the prescribed proforma at [Annexure II](https://dnhpanchayat.in/uploads/documents/DivyangjanPensionNotification.pdf)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to disabled persons (Divyangjan) to cater to their basic needs.

### What is the financial benefit under the scheme?

The pension provides a monthly financial benefit of [?]1,000 to Divyangjan aged 18-59 years.

### Will I receive the pension every month?

Yes, the pension is disbursed every month by the fifth of the month.

### How long will I receive the pension under this scheme?

You will receive the pension until you reach the age of 60. After that, you will be transferred to the Senior Citizens Pension Scheme.

### Will the pension amount increase over time?

The pension amount is fixed as per the current policy and may be revised based on future decisions by the government.

### How is the pension amount disbursed?

The pension is disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly to the bank account of the beneficiary.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Disabled persons between the ages of 18-59 years, with 80% or more disability, who are domiciled in Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu are eligible.

### What is the minimum percentage of disability required to qualify for the scheme?

The applicant must have at least 80% disability to qualify.

### What is the income limit to be eligible for the pension?

The annual income should not exceed [?]1.5 lakh.

### Can I apply if I am already receiving another pension?

No, if you are already receiving a pension under any other Divyang Pension scheme, you are not eligible.

### Can persons over 60 years of age apply for this scheme?

No, only persons aged 18-59 years can apply. After turning 60, you will be transferred to the Senior Citizens Pension Scheme.

### Is a domicile certificate mandatory for applying to the scheme?

Yes, a domicile certificate issued by the competent authority is required.

### Can I apply if I do not have a bank account?

No, you must provide a copy of your Aadhaar-linked bank passbook as part of the application process.

### How do I apply for the Pension Scheme to Disabled Persons?

You can apply by filling out the application form and submitting it to the Gram Panchayat or District Panchayat/Municipal Council.

### What documents do I need to submit along with the application?

Documents required include age proof, income certificate, domicile certificate, disability certificate, bank passbook, Aadhaar card, election card, passport-size photos, and an affidavit confirming you are not receiving other pensions.

### How long will it take for my application to be processed?

Applications will be processed within 30 days from the date of receipt.

### Who is responsible for processing my application?

The Chief Executive Officer or Chief Officer of the concerned District Panchayat or Municipal Council will process your application.

### Who can I contact if I have issues or grievances regarding the application process?

You can approach the Chief Executive Officer/Chief Officer of the District Panchayat, Municipal Council, District Collector, or the Department of Social Welfare for grievance redressal.
