## Brief Description

The "Workers In Case of Partial Permanent Disablement" scheme by the JKBOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, provides financial assistance to registered BOC workers in the event of their partial permanent disablement.

## Detailed Description

The "Workers In Case of Partial Permanent Disablement" scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, under the MUHAFIZ Scheme (PMJJBY: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana, PMSBY: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana) from 01.01.2018, provides Rs. 1,00,000 to registered BOC workers in the case of partial permanent disablement.

## Benefits

> Under the scheme, Rs. 1,00,000 is provided to registered BOC workers in case of partial permanent disablement.

## Eligibility

> **For Registration as a Building/ Construction Worker:**


1. The applicant should be a resident of Jammu & Kashmir.
1. The applicant should be a Building/ Construction Worker.
1. The applicant should be between 18 and 60 years.
1. The applicant should have engaged in any building or other construction work for at least 90 days during the preceding 12 months.
1. The applicant should be registration is renewed annually.

> **For the Application of the Welfare Scheme:**


1. The worker must be a registered member of the Building and Other Construction Workers (BOC) Welfare Board.
1. The scheme applies to workers who suffer from partial permanent disablement.

## Documents Required

1. Original Medical Certificate issued by the Medical Board or competent authority.
1. Attested Age Proof of the registered worker.
1. Duly attested photocopy of Bank Passbook of the worker or nominee/legal heir, or a cancelled cheque bearing the name of the worker/nominee/legal heir and IFSC code of the bank branch.
1. Photocopy of Aadhaar Card/Biometric Card of the registered worker.
1. Registration Certificate of the worker under the BOC Welfare Board.
1. Proof of employment in the building/construction sector (work records or pay slips).
1. Disability Certificate from a recognized medical authority confirming the partial permanent disablement.
1. Proof of residence (Voter ID, Ration Card, Utility Bill, etc.) for address verification.
1. Photograph of the worker (passport size).
1. Income Certificate to establish eligibility under the scheme (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Workers In Case of Partial Permanent Disablement" scheme?

This scheme provides financial assistance to registered Building and Other Construction (BOC) workers in the case of partial permanent disablement.

### Who is eligible for the partial permanent disablement scheme?

Any registered BOC worker who suffers from partial permanent disablement is eligible for this scheme.

### What is the amount of financial assistance provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance of Rs. 1,00,000 to the eligible worker.

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

Key documents include a Medical Certificate from a competent authority, Age Proof, Aadhaar Card, Bank Passbook, and a Disability Certificate.

### Where can the worker apply for the scheme?

The worker can apply through the nearest BOC Welfare Board office in Jammu & Kashmir.

### Is the Aadhaar Card mandatory for applying?

Yes, a photocopy of the Aadhaar Card or Biometric Card is required for both the registered worker and the nominee/legal heir.

### How is the disability confirmed for this scheme?

The disability must be certified by a Medical Board or a competent medical authority.

### Can a nominee/legal heir apply on behalf of the worker?

Yes, if the worker is unable to apply, a nominee or legal heir can apply with the necessary documents, including their own Aadhaar and bank details.

### Is there any age limit for the worker to apply for the scheme?

The worker should be within the eligible working age as per the registration guidelines of the BOC Welfare Board.

### Is the scheme applicable for accidental disablement?

No, this scheme specifically covers partial permanent disablement due to other causes, not accidents.

### How is the financial assistance disbursed to the worker?

The financial assistance is directly credited to the registered bank account of the worker or nominee.

### Is the scheme applicable to workers in sectors other than construction?

No, the scheme is specifically for workers registered under the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
