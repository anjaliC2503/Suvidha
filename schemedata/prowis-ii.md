## Brief Description

This programme will encourage women candidates to work with the Indian/French scientific communities with an aim to accelerate their talented instincts towards carrying out advanced studies and research in the frontier areas of Science & Technology.

## Detailed Description

The “Indo-French Visiting/Mobility Programme for Women in Science (PROWIS-II)” is dedicated to the young women scientists of both India and France. This programme will encourage women candidates to work with the Indian/French scientific communities with an aim to accelerate their talented instincts towards carrying out advanced studies and research in the frontier areas of Science &amp; Technology.
> **Duration of Programme:**

The duration of the Programme will be for two weeks.

## Benefits

1. Return airfare (economy class), accommodation &amp; per diem allowances, local travel in India/France, airport transfer, insurance, and visa charges shall be given to the selected candidates.

## Eligibility

1. Women applicants must be Indian or French citizens (citizens residing in France) from a recognized university or research institution in India/France.
   1. Indian applicants should hold a PhD degree &amp; sanctioned/tenured post in a scientific/academic institution and be willing to undertake collaborative research projects in French institutions/universities.
   1. French women applicants holding a PhD degree and currently completing a postdoctoral project &amp; willing to undertake collaborative research projects in Indian institutions/universities.
   1. The mobility should take place during the employment period of the applicant in the concerned institution.
   1. The applicant should not have availed of any visiting fellowship in France/India during the last 5 years.
   1. This fellowship cannot be availed simultaneously with any other fellowship.

**Note 01:** Preference will be given to those candidates who don&#39;t have any international exposure.
**Note 02:** CEFIPRA may relax the eligibility conditions in case of potential candidates.

## Application Process

### Online

**Registration**

Step 3: Then, fill in your basic details such as your email ID, password, name, date of birth, and mobile number carefully.
Step 4: Next, enter your academic details, research area, and institution information as required.
Step 5: Finally, check all the entered details and click on the “Register” button to complete your registration.
**Login**
Step 1: First, open the[ login ](https://www.cefipraonline.in/CEFIPRA/app_srv/cefipra/gl/jsp/quickpath.jsp)page of the [website](https://www.cefipraonline.in/CEFIPRA/app_srv/cefipra/gl/jsp/quickpath.jsp) in your browser.
Step 2: Then, enter your registered email ID in the username field.
Step 3: After that, type your password and fill in the captcha code shown on the screen.
Step 4: Finally, click on the “Login” button to access your account, after which you can apply for schemes or track the status of your application.

## Documents Required

2.	Visiting proposal under the STEM and allied S&T areas (one page)
3.	No objection certificate from the employer
4.	Recent Photograph
5.	Passport
6.	Educational Certificates
7.	Any other document, as required
