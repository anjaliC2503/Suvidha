## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide stipend and financial assistance to students belonging to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community. The benefits include financial assistance and stipends for poor students from Class 9th up to normal Three Years Degree Courses (e.g. Bachelor of Arts, Bachelor of Science, etc.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Pre-Matric & Post-Matric Scholarship" was launched by the Tea Tribes Welfare Department, Government of Assam. The scheme aims to provide stipend to Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarship to the students belonging to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community for the benefit of poor students. The scheme is also intended to provide financial assistance to students of the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community pursuing their studies at the post-matriculation or post-secondary stage to enable them to complete their education. The applications for this scheme are accepted through the SIRISH-Online Application Portal.
<br>

## Benefits

- The benefits are to provide stipend to Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarship to the students belonging to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community for the benefit of poor students.
- The scheme is to provide financial assistance to the students of the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community pursuing their studies at the post-matriculation or post-secondary stage to enable them to complete their education.

##### ***Conditions for Availment/Continuation***

- _The scholarship holder under this scheme shall not hold any other scholarship or stipend disbursed by any Government Department._
- _Students who are also undergoing coaching in any of the pre-examination training centers with financial assistance from the Government will not be eligible._
- _In case of detained students, he or she shall not be eligible to apply for scholarship for the same class twice._
- _The applicant eligible for the "Post-Matric Scholarship" will not be eligible for other schemes, except for the scheme "Simon Sing Horo (Special Post-Matric) Scholarship," if eligible, in the same year._

<br>

## Exclusions

- The scholarships are not available for identified Higher Studies courses and Vocational 3 (three) years Degree Courses or equivalent.
- The scholarship holder shall not hold any other scholarship or stipend disbursed by any Government Department.
- Students applying repeatedly from different Sub-Divisions shall in no case be considered for the scholarships.
- Students who are undergoing coaching in any of the pre-examination training centers with financial assistance from the Government will not be eligible.
- In case of detained students, he or she shall not be eligible to apply for scholarship for the same class twice.
- The applicant eligible for the "Post-Matric Scholarship" will not be eligible for other schemes except for the scheme "Simon Sing Horo (Special Post-Matric) Scholarship," if eligible, in the same year.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a permanent resident of Assam.
- The applicant must belong to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi Community.
- The applicant must produce a valid Caste certificate which is duly certified or issued by the District Commissioner of the concerned district or his or her designated officer.
- The applicant must submit an undertaking on the annual family income stating that it is not exceeding ₹5,00,000/- per annum.
- The Pre-Matric Scholarship is awarded to students from Class 9th to Class 10th.
- The Post-Matric Scholarship is awarded to students pursuing studies from Class 11th to normal Three Years Degree Courses (Bachelor of Arts, Bachelor of Business Administration, Bachelor of Commerce, Bachelor of Science, Integrated Graduate plus Post Graduate courses like integrated Master of Business Administration / Bachelor of Arts-Bachelor of Law / Bachelor of Arts-Bachelor of Education / Master of Social Work, etc.).
- The scholarships are available for all recognized post-matriculation or post-secondary courses in recognized institutions.

##### ***Special Provisions***

- All children of the same parents or guardians are eligible.
- Students studying through correspondence course are also eligible to apply.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

##### ***Step 1:*** ***Portal Access and Scheme Selection***
**A. Navigating to the Portal:** The applicant must first open the official Tea Tribes Welfare Department URL, www.ttwd.assam.gov.in, and then click the "Online Application" link to be redirected to the [SIRISH application portal](https://sirishassam.in/).
**B. Initial Login or Registration:** New users must register themselves on the online portal, while existing users will log in using the username and password created during prior registration.
**C. Selecting the Scheme:** Once the applicant is logged into the Dashboard, they must select the appropriate scholarship scheme from the dropdown menu available under STEP 1: Select Scheme.
##### ***Step 2:********* **Entering Personal Details**
**A. Inputting Mandatory Data:** The applicant proceeds to STEP 2: Enter Personal Details where all required information in the application form must be accurately provided.
**B. Specifying Location Details:** Location data, including District, Assembly Constituency, and Sub-Division, must be selected, along with other essential address details like Village/Town.
**C. Saving Personal Information:** After filling in all necessary fields in this step, the applicant must click on the "Save Form Details" button to proceed further with the application.
##### ***Step 3:********* **Document Upload**
**A. Uploading Required Files:** Under the STEP 3: Upload Documents tab, the applicant must upload all required supporting documents in the specified image formats (e.g., JPG/JPEG).
**B. Document Formatting Check:** All uploaded documents must adhere to specified maximum file sizes (e.g., photo maximum 50 KB, other documents generally 100 KB) before they can be saved.
##### ***Step 4:********* **Updating Bank Details**
**A. Providing Account Information:** The applicant must enter the Bank Account Number and the name of the bank under the STEP 4: Update Bank Details tab.
**B. Entering Branch Identification:** Details essential for electronic transfer, specifically the Bank Branch Name and the corresponding IFSC Code, must be accurately provided.
**C. Securing Financial Data:** Once all bank details have been correctly entered, the applicant must click on the "Save Bank Details" button to confirm the information.
##### ***Step 5:********* **Preview and Final Submission**
**A. Application Review:** The final step, STEP 5: Preview Application and Final Submission, displays all details entered in the form for a thorough and final check.
**B. Final Confirmation:** After cross-checking all information and uploaded documents for accuracy, the applicant must confirm the application by clicking the "Proceed with Submit Application" button.
**C. Tracking Status:** Upon successful submission, an application number will be visible on the screen, allowing the applicant to view the status under the "APPLICATION STATUS" tab.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a student receiving any other form of stipend or scholarship funds from a different Government Department simultaneously eligible for this benefit?

No. The student awarded this scholarship shall not hold any other scholarship or stipend that has been disbursed by any other Government Department.
<br>

### If I qualify for the Post-Matriculation Scholarship, is there any restriction on me applying for or receiving benefits from other schemes implemented by the department?

Yes, the applicant eligible for the Post-Matriculation Scholarship will generally not be eligible for other schemes in the same year. The sole exception is the "Simon Sing Horo (Special Post-Matric) Scholarship," provided the applicant is otherwise eligible for that scheme.
<br>

### What are the primary residency and community requirements mandatory for an applicant to successfully qualify for receiving the benefits?

The applicant must be a permanent resident of Assam. Additionally, the student must belong to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi Community.
<br>

### If a student has been detained in a particular class during the previous academic session, will they be allowed to reapply for this scholarship while repeating the same class?

No. If a student is detained in a specific class, he or she shall not be considered eligible to apply for this scholarship for the identical class a second time.
<br>

### What specific documentation is required to formally verify my community membership during the online application process?

The applicant must produce a valid Caste Certificate. This certificate must be duly certified or issued by the District Commissioner of the concerned district or his or her designated officer.
<br>

### What specific courses are covered under the Post-Matriculation section of this scheme if I am pursuing studies at the post-secondary stage?

Coverage extends from Class XI up to normal Three Years Degree Courses, such as Bachelor of Arts, Bachelor of Business Administration, Bachelor of Commerce, or Bachelor of Science. Integrated Graduate plus Post Graduate courses like integrated Master of Business Administration / Bachelor of Arts-Bachelor of Law / Bachelor of Arts-Bachelor of Education / Master of Social Work are also covered.
<br>

### Are there any specific types of higher education or specialized courses that are explicitly excluded from eligibility for this financial assistance?

Yes. The scholarships are explicitly not available for identified Higher Studies courses. They are also not available for Vocational 3 (three) years Degree Courses or their equivalent.
<br>

### What evidence must I present during the application process to confirm my current academic enrollment status?

The applicant must produce a recommendation letter from the Headmaster or Principal. Alternatively, the applicant can submit their valid admission or fee receipts.
<br>

### What is the scope of the Pre-Matriculation Scholarship portion of the scheme regarding the student's current enrollment class?

The Pre-Matriculation Scholarship is awarded specifically to students currently enrolled in Class IX (9) up to Class X (10).
<br>

### Does the number of siblings or children from the same household affect the eligibility or limit how many can receive this financial benefit?

No. A special provision states that all children belonging to the same parents or guardians are eligible to apply for the scheme.
<br>

### Which types of educational institutions must my post-matriculation or post-secondary course be enrolled in for me to be considered eligible?

The scholarships are available only for all recognized post-matriculation or post-secondary courses. These courses must be pursued in institutions that are recognized by the appropriate authorities.
<br>

### What is the maximum annual family income threshold established for applicants to be considered eligible for financial assistance under this scholarship scheme?

The annual family income of the applicant’s family must not exceed ₹5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Lakh) per annum. This information must be submitted via an undertaking provided by the Parent or Guardian.
<br>

### Am I eligible to apply for this financial assistance if I am currently attending pre-examination training or coaching sponsored by the Government?

No. Students who are undergoing coaching in any of the pre-examination training centers, especially those receiving financial assistance from the Government, are not eligible.
<br>

### Is it permissible for an applicant to submit multiple scholarship applications from various Sub-Divisions during the same application cycle?

No. The application guidelines strictly state that in no case shall students who apply repeatedly from different Sub-Divisions be considered for the scholarships.
<br>
