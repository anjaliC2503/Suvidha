## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage documentation and digitisation of valuable Tiatr-related materials to prevent their loss to posterity. It provides assistance and incentives to holders of such materials and pays honorarium to documentation assistants.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Documentation And Research Assistantship Scheme" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to encourage documentation and digitisation of valuable Tiatr-related materials such as manuscripts, photos, handbills, advertisements, newspaper or magazine clippings, video and audio recordings that are with Tiatr artistes, tiatr lovers and institutions, addressing the high degree of risk that some of these materials may be lost to posterity if efforts are not undertaken to preserve and document them. The scheme provides assistance to possessors of Tiatr-related materials to organize and document their holdings.
<br>

## Benefits

- One-time Incentive: A reasonable amount as decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa will be paid to the holder for converting materials into electronic formats.
- Hourly Honorarium: Documentation assistants will be paid an honorarium on clock-hour basis as determined by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
- Reimbursement: Tiatr Academy of Goa will reimburse costs incurred towards conversion according to rates determined by it if services of other agencies become necessary.
- Assistance provided to organize and document materials in holdings.
- Copy of digitised material provided to the holder on Digital Versatile Disc or Pen-drive.
- Professional documentation and preservation services.

> ***Conditions***

_*The work done must be certified by the holder of the material._
_*Tiatr Academy of Goa will periodically review the work undertaken and accordingly extend or curtail the assistantship._
_*Holders must indicate willingness to permit and facilitate documentation tasks._
<br>

## Eligibility

> ***For Material Holders***


- The applicant must be a possessor of materials related to Tiatr such as manuscripts, photos, handbills, advertisements, newspaper or magazine clippings, video and audio recordings.
- The applicant must indicate willingness to permit and facilitate documentation and organization tasks.

> ***For Documentation Assistants***


- The applicant must be willing to serve as Documentation Assistant.
- The applicant must be capable of performing tasks including compilation and organisation of materials, typing or word-processing of textual materials, scanning and converting by Optical Character Recognition, conversion of images by scanning or photographing with digital camera, and converting video or audio recordings from analogue to digital format.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. The form requires details including name of artiste or documentation assistant, complete address, contact number, email identification, Aadhaar Card number, and specific aspect of Tiatr on which documentation can be done.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields including personal details, contact information, and specify the aspect of Tiatr on which documentation work can be undertaken. Attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
**Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001**
_*For material holders, indicate willingness to permit and facilitate documentation tasks, and may also indicate proximate candidates willing to undertake the documentation work._
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment received for future reference._
<br>

##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 1:****** ***Assignment of Tasks***
Tiatr Academy of Goa will assign documentation tasks to candidates according to the requests received from the holders of materials. The academy will match documentation assistants with appropriate holders based on proximity and requirements.
**Step 2:** ***Work Execution and Certification***
Documentation assistants will perform assigned tasks including compilation, organization, digitisation, and conversion of materials under the supervision and certification of the material holder. The quality and progress of work will be periodically reviewed by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
**Step 3:** ***Payment and Delivery***
Upon completion and certification of work, documentation assistants will receive honorarium payment on clock-hour basis as determined by Tiatr Academy of Goa. A copy of digitised material will be provided to the holder on Digital Versatile Disc or Pen-drive, while Tiatr Academy of Goa will retain a similar copy for its uses.
_*The Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to make any changes in the rules of the scheme._
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply as a documentation assistant if I have skills in typing, scanning, and converting audio-video materials from analogue to digital format?

Yes, you can apply as a documentation assistant if you are capable of performing tasks including compilation and organisation of materials, typing or word-processing of textual materials, scanning and converting by Optical Character Recognition, conversion of images by scanning or photographing with digital camera, and converting video or audio recordings from analogue to digital format.
<br>

### What types of materials related to this art form are considered eligible for documentation and digitisation under this assistance program?

Eligible materials include manuscripts, photos, handbills, advertisements, newspaper or magazine clippings, video and audio recordings that are related to this theatrical art form. These materials must be in possession of artistes, enthusiasts, or institutions.
<br>

### Do I need to provide any specific identity document along with my application form when applying for this documentation assistance?

Yes, you must submit a self-attested copy of Aadhaar Card along with your application form as it is mandatory according to the scheme rules. Ensure the copy is clear and properly self-attested.
<br>

### Will I receive any financial compensation for allowing my collection of theatrical materials to be documented and digitised?

Yes, you will receive a reasonable one-time incentive amount as decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa for converting your materials into electronic formats. The exact amount will be determined by the academy.
<br>

### What happens to the digitised copies of my materials once the documentation work is completed by the assigned assistant?

You will receive a copy of the digitised material on Digital Versatile Disc or Pen-drive, while Tiatr Academy of Goa will retain a similar copy for its institutional uses and preservation purposes.
<br>

### How much will I be paid as a documentation assistant for the work I perform under this scheme?

Documentation assistants will be paid an honorarium on clock-hour basis as determined by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The specific hourly rate will be decided by the academy.
<br>

### Is there any preference given to certain candidates when selecting documentation assistants for this preservation and digitisation work?

Yes, preference will be given to persons who are in proximity to the venue of the holdings. This ensures better coordination and accessibility for the documentation work.
<br>

### What are the key details I need to provide in the application form for this documentation and research assistance program?

The application form requires your name, complete address, contact number, email identification, Aadhaar Card number, and the specific aspect of this theatrical art form on which documentation can be undertaken.
<br>

### Who will verify and certify that the documentation work performed by the assistant has been completed satisfactorily?

The work done must be certified by the holder of the material. The material owner is responsible for verifying and certifying the quality and completion of documentation tasks.
<br>

### If external agencies need to be hired for certain documentation tasks, will those costs be covered under this scheme?

Yes, Tiatr Academy of Goa will reimburse costs incurred towards conversion according to rates determined by it if services of other agencies become necessary for the documentation work.
<br>

### As a holder of valuable theatrical materials, what commitment must I make when applying for documentation assistance under this program?

You must indicate willingness to permit and facilitate documentation and organisation tasks. This commitment ensures that documentation assistants can access and work with your materials effectively.
<br>

### How does the academy ensure quality control and monitor the progress of documentation work being carried out?

Tiatr Academy of Goa will periodically review the work undertaken and accordingly extend or curtail the assistantship based on the quality and progress of the documentation tasks.
<br>

### What specific conversion and digitisation tasks might I be expected to perform as a documentation assistant in this preservation program?

Tasks may include compilation and organisation of materials, typing or word-processing of textual materials, scanning and converting by Optical Character Recognition, conversion of images by scanning or photographing with digital camera, and converting video or audio recordings from analogue to digital format.
<br>

### Can I suggest or recommend someone who lives near my location to work as a documentation assistant for my materials?

Yes, as a material holder, you may indicate proximate candidates who are willing to undertake the documentation work when submitting your application to the academy.
<br>
