## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial assistance to Scheduled Caste girl students of Madhya Pradesh enrolled in Class 11 to encourage them to continue their education and prevent dropouts.

## Detailed Description

Under this scheme, Scheduled Caste girl students studying in Class 11 receive financial support from the Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to ensure that girls from SC communities continue their education without financial barriers. Applications are accepted online through the Shiksha Portal, and the eligible amount is directly transferred to the student’s account after verification.
<br>

## Benefits

Eligible SC girl students of Class 11 receive an annual financial incentive of ₹3,000/-, credited directly to their bank account
<br>

## Eligibility

1. Applicant must be a scheduled Caste girl students.
1. Applicant should have a attested photocopy of the permanent caste certificate from the Sub-Divisional Officer (Revenue).
1. Applicant's father or guardian must not be an income tax payer and self-declaration.
1. The student must be in continuous study.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- To avail of the benefits under this scheme, eligible students can contact the Principal of their respective government schools/institutions.

> **Note:** The student&#39;s profile is entered on the education portal [https://shikshaportal.mp.gov.in/](https://shikshaportal.mp.gov.in/) by the teacher of the educational institution as per the record/certificate provided by the student.
