## Brief Description

The "Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for the Under Graduate Meritorious Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University" provides monthly scholarships to students pursuing Under Graduate in various faculties at Junagadh Agricultural University.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for the Under Graduate Meritorious Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University&quot; launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide monthly financial assistance to eligible Under Graduate students at Junagadh Agricultural University. The objective is to attract bright rural students to seek admission in all the faculties, and after completion of their studies, they can join the University and help in the rural development thereby improving Agricultural productivity of the State.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Undergraduate Students:** ₹1,000/- per month for 12 months.- **Free Accommodation:** Free hostel accommodation for girl students in all undergraduate faculties.

- **Duration:** The duration of the scholarship will be as per duration (or total period) of Graduation including semester end breaks and vacations.- **Number of Scholarships:** 36

## Exclusions

-  The awardee under this scheme will not be allowed to receive any other regular scholarship, free ship stipend loan or any other financial assistance.

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

- Applicant must be pursuing an undergraduate program in any faculty of Junagadh Agricultural University.
  - Applicant must have secured at least 45% marks in the Science stream of the HSC examination.

**Note:** The students from College of Agriculture, Junagadh, Mota Bhandariya and Khapat Priority will be given to the girls candidates and if the sufficient number of girls candidates are not available then remaining number of fellowship will be given to economical poor boys candidates on merit basis.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Write an application on plain paper addressed to the Director, Junagadh Agricultural University.
**Step 2:** Submit the completed application along with the necessary documents to the Principal of the college.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for the Under Graduate Meritorious Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University"?

The objective of the "Scheme for the Award of Scholarship for the Under Graduate Meritorious Students of Various Faculties of Junagadh Agricultural University" is to attract bright rural students to seek admission in all the faculties and after completion of their studies, they can join the University and help in the rural development thereby improving Agricultural productivity of the State.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scholarship?

Students who have secured at least 45% marks in the Science stream of the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) examination and are admitted to an undergraduate course at Junagadh Agricultural University are eligible.

### Is there any special provision for girl students?

Yes, girl students are given priority in the selection process and are also provided free hostel accommodation.

### What is the amount provided under this scholarship scheme?

The scholarship provides a financial assistance of ₹1,000/- per month to each selected student.
<br>

<br>

### What happens if a student fails to maintain the required CGPA?

If a student fails to maintain a cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 6.5 in an even semester, the scholarship is temporarily withheld and can be renewed upon achieving the required CGPA in a subsequent semester.
<br>

<br>

### Can a student receive another scholarship along with this one?

No, a student cannot receive this scholarship if they are already receiving any other regular scholarship, stipend, or financial assistance. Such aid must be surrendered to become eligible.
<br>

<br>

### Is the scholarship renewed automatically each year?

The scholarship is renewed each academic year based on the student’s academic performance, progress report, and conduct.
<br>

<br>

### Are students from all faculties eligible for this scholarship?

Yes, students enrolled in Agriculture, Horticulture, Agricultural Engineering & Technology, Fisheries Science, and Veterinary Science are eligible.
<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant apply for the scheme?

Applicants must submit their application on plain paper with all required details and supporting documents to the Principal of the college.

### Who disburses the scholarship amount to the student?

The Principal of the respective college is responsible for disbursing the scholarship after verifying the student’s progress report.

### What role does conduct and class attendance play in the continuation of the scholarship?

Good conduct and regular class attendance are essential requirements for the continuation of the scholarship throughout the course duration.
<br>

<br>

### Is a student allowed to change their college during the scholarship period?

No, a student is not allowed to change their college during the scholarship period unless they obtain prior written permission from the university.
<br>

<br>

### Can the cancelled scholarship be restored later?

No, once the scholarship is cancelled, it cannot be restored under any circumstances, except in the case of prolonged illness.
