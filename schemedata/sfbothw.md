## Brief Description

This scheme is introduced by the Climate Change Department, Gujarat, aims to promote the adoption of eco-friendly vehicles by providing a subsidy on the purchase of battery-operated three-wheelers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-wheelers&quot; by the Climate Change Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage the use of clean energy-based vehicles for the transportation of passengers and goods, thereby reducing dependency on petrol and mitigating pollution. Beneficiaries, including rickshaw drivers, women entrepreneurs, startups, unemployed youth, educational institutions, cooperatives, NGOs, local bodies, and yatras, can avail a subsidy on a reimbursement basis after purchasing battery-operated three-wheelers from Gujarat Energy Development Agency (GEDA) authorized manufacturers and dealers.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Subsidy Assistance:** Subsidy of ₹48,000/- after the purchase of a battery-operated three-wheeler from GEDA-authorized manufacturers/dealers.<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
   1. The applicant must be an individual (such as rickshaw drivers, women entrepreneurs, startups, educated unemployed individuals) or an institution (such as cooperatives, NGOs, educational institutions, local bodies, yatras).
   1. The applicant must purchase a battery-operated three-wheeler from a GEDA-authorized manufacturer or dealer.
   1. The applicant must apply through authorized dealers linked with GEDA.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers"?

The scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers" has been launched by the Climate Change Department, Government of Gujarat, with the purpose of promoting the adoption of eco-friendly battery-operated three-wheelers by providing a financial subsidy that helps beneficiaries save fuel costs and reduce environmental pollution.
<br>

### Who is the implementing agency for the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers"?

The implementing agency for the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers" is the Gujarat Energy Development Agency (GEDA), which is responsible for verifying applications, processing subsidy claims, and reimbursing the approved amount to the beneficiaries.
<br>

### Who can apply under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers"?

Under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers," any individual such as rickshaw drivers, women entrepreneurs, startups, educated unemployed persons, and any institution such as cooperatives, NGOs, educational institutes, local bodies, and yatras are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What financial assistance is provided under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers"?

The scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers" provides a financial subsidy of ₹48,000/- per eligible purchase of a battery-operated three-wheeler, which is reimbursed to the applicant after successful purchase and submission of documents.
<br>

### What is the process of receiving the subsidy under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers"?

Under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers," the applicant purchases a battery-operated three-wheeler from a GEDA-authorized manufacturer or dealer, submits the application with required documents through the authorized dealer, and receives the subsidy on a reimbursement basis after verification.
<br>

### Are there any specific manufacturers or dealers from whom the vehicle must be purchased under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers"?

Yes, under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers," the vehicle must be purchased only from manufacturers and dealers authorized by GEDA, otherwise the applicant will not be eligible for the subsidy reimbursement.
<br>

### Are there any income criteria under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers"?

No, under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers," there are no income-based restrictions, meaning the scheme is open to all eligible applicants regardless of their social or economic background.
<br>

### From where can the application form for the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers" be obtained?

The application form for the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers" can be obtained free of cost from GEDA offices through vehicle dealers who are appointed by GEDA-authorized manufacturers.
<br>

### Where should the completed application for the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers" be submitted?

The completed application for the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers" should be submitted through the authorized vehicle dealer, who forwards it to the GEDA office for verification and further processing.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers"?

To apply under the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers," the applicant is required to submit a duly filled application form along with Aadhaar Card, Copy of License, and a valid Identity Card.
<br>

### Is the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers" applicable throughout Gujarat?

Yes, the scheme "Subsidy For Battery Operated Three-Wheelers" is applicable across the entire state of Gujarat and can be availed by eligible applicants residing in any district of the state.
<br>
