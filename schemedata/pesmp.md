## Brief Description

The "Primary Education Scholarship" scheme provides ₹150/- annually to students from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities who are studying in classes 1st to 5th.  The scholarship distribution is managed through the Directorate of Public Education, Government of Madhya Pradesh.

## Detailed Description

The "Primary Education Scholarship" scheme by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides a state scholarship of ₹150/- for 10 months at the rate of ₹15/- per month to students from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities studying in classes 1 to 5. The scholarship distribution is managed through the Directorate of Public Education.

## Benefits

Scholarship of ₹150/- for 10 months at the rate of ₹15/- per month.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a student.
1. The applicant should be from a [De-Notified](https://samarth.mp.gov.in/Public/Pages/TribesDetail?ID=2),[ Nomadic, or Semi-Nomadic](https://samarth.mp.gov.in/Public/Pages/TribesDetail?ID=1) Community.
_*The applicant should not need to apply again for renewal while continuing in classes of the same rate (e.g., classes 1 to 5)._
_*The applicant should be eligible for the scholarship even if they fail one academic year._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which department manages this scholarship scheme?

The Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, manages it, and it is distributed through the Directorate of Public Education.

### What documents do I need to submit with the scholarship application?

You need to submit a passport-size photo, Aadhaar Card, domicile certificate, caste certificate, previous class marksheet, Samagra ID, and bank account details.

### How do I register for the scholarship on the Samarth Portal?

Visit the Samarth Portal, click "Registration" in the top right corner, provide mandatory details, verify your contact information if required, and click "Register."

### If my child fails a class, will they lose the scholarship?

No, students remain eligible for the scholarship even if they fail one academic year.

### Do I need to apply for renewal every year while my child is in primary school?

No, you don't need to reapply while continuing in classes 1 to 5.

### Who is eligible for the Primary Education Scholarship?

Students from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities studying in classes 1 to 5 are eligible.

### Are students beyond class 5 eligible for this scholarship?

No, the scholarship is specifically for students studying in classes 1 to 5 only.

Yes, all fields marked with * are mentioned as mandatory in the registration process.
