## Brief Description

The scheme aims to fund the pre-matric and post-matric education of wards belonging to specific mine, beedi, and cine workers. It provides annual scholarships between ₹1,000/- and ₹25,000/- based on the student's academic level and course type.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Financial Assistance for Education to the Wards of Beedi/Cine/IOMC/LSDM Workers – Pre & Post-Matric" was launched by the Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India. The scheme aims to provide educational scholarships to the children of Beedi, Iron Ore, Manganese & Chrome Ore, Limestone, Dolomite, Mica, and Cine workers. The scheme provides annual financial assistance ranging from ₹1,000/- to ₹25,000/- directly to the beneficiaries via Direct Benefit Transfer. The applicant must be studying in a recognized institution and meet the stipulated parental occupation and income thresholds. The applications for this scheme are accepted online through the National Scholarship Portal.
<br>

## Benefits

***Financial Assistance Rates***

- Class I to IV (for the purchase of dress/books, etc.): ₹1,000/- per annum.
- Class V to VIII: ₹1,500/- per annum.
- Class IX: ₹2,000/- per annum.
- Class X: ₹2,000/- per annum.
- Class XI to XII: ₹3,000/- per annum.
- ITI: ₹6,000/- per annum.
- Polytechnic: ₹6,000/- per annum.
- Degree Courses (including B.Sc. Agriculture): ₹6,000/- per annum.
- Professional Courses: ₹25,000/- per annum.

***Mode of Disbursement:*** The benefits under the scheme are transferred through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system.
<br>

## Exclusions

> **The scholarship sanctioned is liable to be canceled on the following occasions -** 


1. If the scholar is found to have obtained a scholarship by false statements.
1. If the scholarship discontinues his/her studies, the scholarship shall be stopped from the date of such discontinuance.
1. If the scholar changes the subject of the course of study for which the scholarship was originally awarded or changes the Institution of Study without the prior approval of the Welfare Commissioner.
1. If the scholar fails to make satisfactory progress in the studies or is irregular in attendance or is guilty of misconduct during the academic year for which the scholarship has been granted.
1. If the parent(s) of the scholar ceases to be a Beedi/Mine/Cine worker.

<br>

> **The following category of students is not eligible for the award of scholarships under the scheme -** 


1. Students who, after passing one stage of education, are studying in the same stage of education in a different subject. e.g. B.Sc. after B.Com. or B.Com. after B.A. or M.A. in one subject after M.A. in another subject.
1. Students who after having completed their educational career in one professional line, continue education in a different professional line, e.g. L.L.B. after B.T. or B.Ed.
1. The Scholars pursuing studies through Correspondence are not eligible.
1. Students who receive scholarships or stipends from any other source will not be granted under this Scheme. 

<br>

**NOTE:** If the scholar is found to have committed any one or more of the above after the scholarship amount has actually been paid to him/her, the amount shall be recovered forthwith from him/her or his/her parents.
<br>

## Eligibility

- Either of the parents of the students should be **Beedi, Iron Ore Manganese & Chrome Ore Mines, Limestone & Dolomite Mines, Cine worker** with at least six months service. This also includes **Contract/Gharkhata (Home Based) workers** also.- Total monthly Income of the Worker's family from all sources should not exceed as under:


1. **Beedi Workers:** Rs.10,000/-1. **Mine Workers:** 
                 a)   The Mine workers doing manual, unskilled, highly skilled and clerical work are eligible for availing all facilities under different welfare schemes of Labour Welfare Organization irrespective of the wages paid to them.
                 b)   The persons employed in supervisory and managerial capacity are eligible for availing the facilities under different welfare schemes subject to the wage limit of Rs.10,000/- per month.
facilities under different welfare schemes of Labour Welfare Organization irrespective of the wages paid to them.
                 c)   Cine workers - "An amount not exceeding Rs.8,000/- per month where such amount is paid monthly or an amount not exceeding Rs.1,00,000/- where it is paid by way of lump sum or instalments; as remuneration of a cine worker for the purpose of this Act.

- The Applicant must have passed the last qualifying examination in first attempt. However, Students promoted to the next class are also eligible to apply for the above scholarships.
- Candidates for the award of scholarships should have taken regular admission for studies at recognized institutions in India in any course of general or technical education, including medical, engineering and agricultural studies. 
- The Educational Institute must be Government/Government Recognized Institute.
- The Scholar should have separate bank account. In case of Joint Account, the first name should be of the Scholar.
- More than one child of the same worker also should furnish separate bank account number.
- Each Scholar is required to furnish separate Mobile Number.

##### ***New Registration***
**Step 1:****** Visit the [New Registration](https://scholarships.gov.in/otrapplication/#/) page of the [National Scholarships Portal](https://scholarships.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Guidelines for Registration will appear. Scroll to the bottom. Read the undertaking carefully and Accept the Terms. Click “Next”.
**Step 3:** In the next window, enter your mobile number and OTP. Then click “Verify”. After successful verification, continue with e-KYC process (as applicable).
**Step 4:** Once completed, your reference number will be generated and the same will be sent to your registered phone number.
##### ***OTR ID Number Generation***
**Step 1:** Download and install these apps from the Google Play Store - 

  - [AadhaarFaceRD](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.uidai.facerd) services  - [NSP OTR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.gov.scholarships.nspotr&pli=1) App
**Step 2:** After opening the mobile app, select the option “eKYC with FaceAuth” highlighted in red.
**Step 3:** Enter the allotted reference number and click on “Send OTP” to receive OTP on your Mobile Number. Enter the OTP and captcha in the screen and click &quot;Next&quot;.
**Step 4:** Click on “Proceed for Face Authentication” button highlighted in red. On Successful completion of face-authentication OTR Number will be generated and will also be sent on your registered mobile no.
##### ***Fresh Application***
**Step 1:** Visit the applicant [Login ](https://scholarships.gov.in/otrapplication/#/login-page)page of the [National Scholarships Portal](https://scholarships.gov.in/). Enter your OTR Number and password. Type the Captcha and click “Login”.
**Step 2:****** On the next screen, provide the OTP received on your registered mobile number. You will be directed to the Password Reset screen. Create a new password and confirm. Click “Submit”. You will be directed to the “Applicant’s Dashboard”.
**Step 3:** On the left pane, click “Application Form”. The fields marked as * are compulsory. Fill in the details and upload the documents.
**Step 4:****** You can either click on “Save as Draft” to complete the application later. Else, click “Final Submit” to submit the application.
##### ***Track Payment Status***
The student can track their payment status from the template “Know Your Payment” in the [PFMS Portal, ](https://pfms.nic.in/static/NewLayoutCommonContent.aspx?RequestPagename=static/TrackNSPpayments.aspx)either by indicating their Aadhaar Number, Bank Account number or NSP Application ID.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Due to my current circumstances, I have enrolled in a distance education course where I receive all my study materials by mail. Am I still able to get the student allowance?

No, scholars pursuing their studies through correspondence are strictly not eligible for this financial assistance.
<br>

### Both my younger sister and I are applying for the educational support, but we currently share 1 cell phone for all our communication needs. Do we each need to provide distinct contact numbers?

Yes, each applying scholar is required to furnish a separate mobile number.
<br>

### The college I am currently attending is completely privately run and does not have any formal recognition from the state or central authorities. Does my institution meet the necessary criteria?

Unfortunately no, you are only eligible if your educational institute is a Government or Government Recognized Institute.
<br>

### My parent has been working in the manganese ore mines for the past 8 months. Will I be considered for this financial assistance program?

Yes, you are eligible if your parent is an Iron Ore, Manganese & Chrome Ore Mines worker with at least 6 months of service.
<br>

### Our household relies entirely on my father's income from beedi rolling, which fluctuates around ₹9,000/- monthly. Is there a specific earnings cap we need to worry about?

You are eligible, as the total monthly income of a Beedi worker's family from all sources must not exceed ₹10,000/-.
<br>

### I was recently awarded an academic merit-based grant from a state government agency for scoring top marks in my class. Am I barred from receiving this specific welfare-based financial aid at the same time?

You may avail of this welfare-based support even if receiving a merit-based one, provided you do not receive more than 1 scholarship within the same category simultaneously.
<br>

### After successfully finishing my Bachelor of Arts degree last year, I decided to enroll in a Bachelor of Commerce program this year. Can I get financial help for my 2nd bachelor's degree?

No, students studying in the same stage of education in a different subject after passing 1 stage are not eligible.
<br>

### If my mother is employed in a clerical position at a limestone mine and earns a reasonably high salary, does her income level disqualify me from receiving the study allowance?

No, mine workers doing manual, unskilled, highly skilled, and clerical work have no wage limit restrictions to avail of the benefits.
<br>

### I do not possess a bank account exclusively in my own name, but I do share a joint savings account where my name is listed as the primary account holder. Is this setup acceptable for receiving the funds?

Yes, in the case of a joint account, it is perfectly acceptable as long as the 1st name on the account is that of the scholar.
<br>

### Both my mother and father work from home rolling beedis as contract labourers rather than being direct employees. Are their children allowed to apply for the educational support?

Children of contract workers and home-based workers are fully eligible to apply for this financial assistance.
<br>

### Considering that my parent works in the film industry and is paid a 1-time flat fee for projects instead of a monthly wage, what is the maximum amount they can earn?

A cine worker's remuneration must not exceed ₹8,000/- per month, or it must not exceed ₹1,00,000/- if paid in a lump sum or instalments.
<br>

### I have already completed my professional training to become a teacher with a Bachelor of Education degree and now want to pursue a law degree. Will my new studies be funded?

No, students who have completed their educational career in 1 professional line and continue education in a different professional line are not eligible.
<br>

### I recently received my final grades and was successfully promoted to the next academic class on my very 1st try. Does this academic progress meet the requirements for the educational grant?

Yes, applicants who have passed their last qualifying examination in the 1st attempt and are promoted to the next class are eligible.
<br>

### My father currently holds a supervisory role in a dolomite mine and receives a monthly salary of roughly ₹9,000/-. What is the maximum permitted salary for his specific job category?

For persons employed in supervisory and managerial capacities in mines, the wage limit must not exceed ₹10,000/- per month.
<br>
