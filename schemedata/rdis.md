## Brief Description

The Research, Development & Innovation (RDI) Scheme provides funding and support to help organizations create and improve new technologies or ideas so they can boost innovation, economic growth, and global competitiveness.

## Detailed Description

Research, Development & Innovation (RDI) Scheme is a flagship initiative of the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India.
**OBJECTIVES**
• DST has launched the Research, Development, and Innovation (RDI) Scheme, approved by the Cabinet on 1st July 2025, with a total outlay of ₹1 lakh crore.
• The initiative aims to catalyze private sector participation in high-impact R&D and strengthen India’s capabilities in strategic technologies and promote technological self-reliance, aligning with the nation’s long-term innovation and Atmanirbhar Bharat vision.
<br>

## Benefits

-  Loan – Debt funding (via loan or optionally convertible debt) from the SLFM, calculated as 50% of the project cost.
- Equity – Funding via equity or equity linked instruments from the SLFM, calculated as 50% of the value of each funding round.

<br>

## Eligibility

• The RDI Fund is provided to Second Level Fund Managers (SLFM) which include Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), Development Finance Institutions (DFIs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), and Focused Research Organisations (FROs).
• The SLFM then provides funds to private companies including DPIIT recognised startups advancing RDI-intensive technologies.
<br>

## Application Process

### Online

Step 1: Visit the [RDIF portal,](https://www.e-techcom.tdb.gov.in/rdif-registration.php) register using your email ID and mobile number, verify your account, and log in.
Step 2: Go to the Application section and open the draft application created by the system. Select the Preferred Mode of Participation and the applicable Category (AIF/FoF).
Step 3: Fill in all the required details, including organization information, fund details, key personnel, investment strategy, and other mandatory fields.
Step 4: Upload all the required supporting documents in the prescribed format, ensuring that each document meets the portal requirements.
Step 5: Review the application, verify the information entered, validate all mandatory fields, and submit the application online.
Step 6: Note the application reference number and log in to the portal to track the application status and respond to any requests for clarification or additional documents.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Research, Development & Innovation (RDI) Scheme?

The RDI Scheme is a Government of India initiative by the Department of Science and Technology (DST) to promote research, development, and innovation by providing financial support for high-impact technology projects.
<br>

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to encourage private sector investment in research and innovation, strengthen India's capabilities in strategic technologies, and promote technological self-reliance.
<br>

### Which ministry implements the scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India.
<br>

### Who can apply under the RDI Scheme?

Funding is provided to Second Level Fund Managers (SLFMs), such as Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), Development Finance Institutions (DFIs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), and Focused Research Organisations (FROs). These entities then support eligible private companies, including DPIIT-recognized startups.
<br>

### Can startups apply directly under the scheme?

Yes. Eligible startups can apply through the designated SLFMs, such as the Technology Development Board (TDB), as per the scheme guidelines.
<br>

### What types of projects are supported?

The scheme supports projects involving high-impact research, development, commercialization of innovative technologies, and strategic technology development.
<br>

### Is there a maximum fund allocation for the scheme?

Yes. The Government has approved the scheme with a total outlay of ₹1 lakh crore.
<br>

### What is the role of Second Level Fund Managers (SLFMs)?

SLFMs evaluate eligible projects, provide financial assistance, monitor project implementation, and support innovation-focused enterprises.
<br>

### Does the scheme support DPIIT-recognized startups?

Yes. DPIIT-recognized startups developing research and innovation-intensive technologies are eligible to receive support through SLFMs.
<br>

### Which sectors are covered under the scheme?

The scheme primarily supports strategic and emerging technology sectors with high research and innovation potential.
<br>
