## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide tuition fee concessions to students enrolled in 3-year undergraduate programs and 4-year veterinary programs. Through this scheme, tuition fee concessions are provided to students in higher years of study whose tuition fees are less than ₹1,00,000/-.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Freeship Less than ₹1,00,000/- (UG Students Only)&quot; scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide tuition fee concessions to students enrolled in 3-year undergraduate programs and 4-year veterinary programs.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, tuition fee concessions (Freeship Tuition Fees) are provided to eligible students.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in a 3-year undergraduate program or a 4-year veterinary program at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

First, applications are invited from students using the prescribed form. Based on the applications received, the Dean of Student Welfare prepares a list of students following the prescribed procedure. This list is then approved by the Registrar and the Vice-Chancellor. The Dean of Student Welfare issues an office order announcing the selected students, who are subsequently granted tuition fee concessions.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand.

### Which students are eligible for this scheme?

Students enrolled in 3-year undergraduate programs and 4-year veterinary programs at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology are eligible.

### What benefit is provided under this scheme?

Eligible students are granted tuition fee concessions (freeship) as per the university’s prescribed norms.

### How can students apply for the scheme?

Students need to fill out the prescribed application form available from the university and submit it within the specified deadline.

### Who prepares the list of eligible students?

<br>

### Who approves the final list of selected students?

The list prepared by the Dean of Student Welfare is approved by the Registrar and the Vice-Chancellor of the university.
<br>

### How are students informed about their selection?

An office order issued by the Dean of Student Welfare announces the list of students who have been granted the tuition fee concession.
