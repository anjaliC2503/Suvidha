## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for fish seed stocking, including transportation costs, is provided to beneficiaries from Scheduled Tribes involved in inland fisheries.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Fish Seed Stocking” is a component under the “To Increase Fish Seed Production and Inland Fisheries Resources in Tribal Areas” scheme, introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to people involved in inland fisheries activities. Under this scheme, 100% financial assistance for fish seed stocking, including transportation costs, is provided to beneficiaries from Scheduled Tribes involved in inland fisheries.
> **Objectives:**


- To increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to people involved in inland fisheries activities.
- To boost fish farming or fishing activities in freshwater bodies.
- Fishing in reservoirs, aquaculture in ponds, and catch from rivers increase fish production and boost inland fisheries.
- The main objective is to provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Tribe fishermen in the state and increase fish production.

<br>

## Benefits

The 100% assistance is provided to Scheduled Tribe beneficiaries for the cost of fish seed including transportation under the Fish Seed Stocking component.
> **Validity:** Minimum 1 year required before reclaiming benefits under this component again.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be Individuals/societies/self-help groups/associations/reservoir monopolies/private units, etc., involved in inland fisheries activities can avail the benefits of inland schemes.
1. The applicant must be from the Scheduled Tribes.
1. The applicant should be above 18 years.
1. The beneficiary fisherman must have his/her own bank account.
1. The applicant must have an Aadhar Card/Voter ID/Ration Card/Passport as proof of identity.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on 'Login' from the top right-hand side, then click on "Beneficiary Registration" and select any one from the 'Farmer' or 'Institutional' or 'Except For the Farmer' as the Beneficiary Type.
**Step 3:** Enter the 'Company Name' and Address if you have selected 'Institutional' or 'Except For the Farmer' as the Beneficiary Type. And for all select the District, Taluka, and Village, choose 'what type of account holder you are?', enter your Account Number and Name, solve the Captcha, and click 'Save and Next'.
**Step 4:** Enter your Mobile Number and E-mail address, click 'Send OTP', enter the received OTP, create and confirm a Password, solve the Captcha, and click 'Submit'.
> **Login Process:** 

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on 'Login' from the top right-hand side of the homepage. On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button, and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on the "Apply New" button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the "Update Application" button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a printout of the confirmed application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply under the scheme?

Individuals, societies, self-help groups, associations, reservoir monopolies, and private units involved in inland fisheries can apply for this benefit.
<br>

### Is this scheme benefit available to all citizens or only certain communities?

The benefit is specifically available to Scheduled Tribe beneficiaries under inland fisheries schemes.
<br>

### What is the minimum age required to apply under the scheme?

Applicants must be at least 18 years old to be eligible for this benefit.
<br>

### Is financial assistance given for the cost of fish seed under this scheme?

Yes, 100% assistance is provided for the cost of fish seed and transportation for Scheduled Tribe beneficiaries.
<br>

### Do I need to have a bank account to apply for the scheme?

Yes, the applicant must have a bank account in their own name to avail the benefits.
<br>

### Is identity proof mandatory for applying to this scheme?

Yes, the applicant must have Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Ration Card, or Passport as proof of identity.
<br>

### What proof is required to show that I belong to the Scheduled Tribe category?

A valid Scheduled Tribe Certificate is required to avail benefits under this category.
<br>

### Can I receive this benefit more than once?

You can reclaim the benefit after a minimum period of 1 year from receiving the assistance.
<br>

### Where can I apply for the Fish Seed Stocking scheme benefit?

You can apply online through the iKhedut portal at [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).
<br>

### Is there any restriction on when I can reapply for benefits?

Yes, there is a minimum time limit of 1 year before you can reclaim the same benefit.
<br>
