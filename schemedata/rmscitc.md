## Brief Description

The Reimbursement of the MS-CIT Course scheme was launched by the Maharashtra Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWW), Labour Department Maharashtra.

## Detailed Description

The Reimbursement of the MS-CIT Course scheme was launched by the Maharashtra Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWW), Labour Department Maharashtra. The scheme offers reimbursement of the MS-CIT (Maharashtra State Certificate in Information Technology) course fees for up to two children of registered construction workers.

## Benefits

- Reimbursement of the MS-CIT (Maharashtra State Certificate in Information Technology) course fees for up to two children of registered construction workers.

## Eligibility

1. The children's parents/guardians should be registered construction workers.
1. The construction worker should be registered under the MBOCWW. 
1. The applicant must be enrolled in a government-recognized MS-CIT course.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://mahabocw.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/SCHEME_HEALTH_FORM-1.pdf) from the MBOCWW official website. 
**Step-2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step-3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Labor Commissioner/Government Labor Officer.
**Step-4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for the MS-CIT course fee reimbursement?

Children of registered construction workers in Maharashtra.

### Is there a limit to the number of children who can benefit from this scheme?

Yes, the reimbursement is available for up to two children per registered worker.

### What course is covered under this scheme?

The MS-CIT (Maharashtra State Certificate in Information Technology) course.

### Can the spouse of a registered worker avail of this scheme?

No, this benefit is only for the children of the registered worker.

### What documents are required to apply?

Aadhaar Card, MS-CIT course fee receipt, enrollment certificate, previous academic marksheet, and proof of worker registration.

### Is the course fee fully reimbursed?

Yes, the full course fee is reimbursed under this scheme.

### What is the age limit for children to apply for the reimbursement?

There is no specific age limit mentioned, but children must be enrolled in the course.

### How do I prove enrollment in the MS-CIT course?

Need to submit the course fee receipt and enrollment certificate from the institute.

### Can I apply for reimbursement after completing the course?

Yes, but you must submit proof of course completion and fee payment.

### Is this scheme available for children studying outside Maharashtra?

No, this scheme is only applicable for the MS-CIT course taken within Maharashtra.

### What happens if the course fees are higher than expected?

Only the official MS-CIT course fee will be reimbursed, as per the guidelines.

### Where can I submit my application?

At the nearest Labor Office or through the official Maharashtra Welfare Board.
