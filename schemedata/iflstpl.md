## Brief Description

The scheme aims to enhance the quality of teaching in schools in the State of Goa by equipping teachers with necessary Information Technology knowledge and skills. It offers an Interest Free Loan up to a maximum of ₹1,00,000/- for purchasing a Laptop to regular/permanent, full-time teachers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Interest Free Loan Scheme To Teachers For The Purchase Of Laptops" was launched by the Directorate of Education, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to get full exposure to changing technology for teachers, who need to handle computers daily and enhance the quality of teaching through visual effects, Multimedia presentation, and Graphics in schools in the State of Goa. The scheme provides an Interest Free Loan up to a maximum of ₹1,00,000/- for the purchase of a Laptop, which is issued via Cheque through Messers Goa Education Development Corporation (GEDC), Government of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted through the respective educational institution and subsequently forwarded to the Directorate of Education.
<br>

## Benefits

- An Interest Free Loan up to a maximum amount of ₹1,00,000/- is granted for the purchase of a Laptop.
- The loan is disbursed in the form of a Crossed Cheque.
- The Cheque is drawn in favor of the dealer.
- The Cheque is issued through Messers Goa Education Development Corporation (GEDC), Government of Goa.

***Conditions***

- _The purchase of the Laptop must be made from any registered or authorized dealer of the branded manufacturer._
- _The Cheque amount sanctioned shall not exceed the maximum amount of ₹1,00,000/-._

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a regular/permanent full-time teacher.
- The applicant must be teaching in Secondary schools or Higher Secondary schools.
- The applicant must be working in a Government school or in a Government Aided school.
- The applicant must be covered under the Administrative control of the Directorate of Education in the State of Goa.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant must obtain a quotation for the laptop from a registered dealer of their choice, which includes details such as a pre-receipt, specifications, and the cost of the laptop, to be submitted along with the application.
**Step 2:** The applicant must obtain the prescribed format of the application form of the scheme from the institution where he/she is currently working.
**Step 3:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 4:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the Head of the Institution. The application must be recommended by the respective Head of the Institution. The Head of the Institution must forward the recommended application to the Directorate of Education.
##### ***Post-Application Process***
**Step 1:** **Processing at the Directorate of Education and Forwarding**

- The Directorate of Education takes utmost care to process the application forwarded by the applicant teacher within a period of forty-five days.
- Once processed and approved, the Directorate of Education forwards the application and related documentation to Messers Goa Education Development Corporation (GEDC).

**Step 2:** **Final Verification and Release of Payment**

- Messers Goa Education Development Corporation processes and verifies the application for the release of the payment or Cheque.
- This processing and the release of the Cheque are dependent upon the availability of funds.

**Step 3:** **Cheque Issuance to the Dealer**

- The Crossed Cheque, amounting to the price of the Laptop, but not exceeding the maximum sanctioned amount of ₹1,00,000/-, is drawn in the favor of the dealer chosen by the teacher.
- This cheque is then released by Messers Goa Education Development Corporation to the respective party.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Approximately how long should I expect the Directorate of Education to take to process and review my application after it has been properly forwarded to their office?

The Directorate of Education takes utmost care to process the application forwarded by the applicant teacher within a period of forty-five days.
<br>

### Do teachers who are working full-time in educational institutions that are managed by private bodies but receive Government Aid also qualify?

Yes, teachers employed in Government Aided schools covered under the Administrative control of the Directorate of Education are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What specific documentation related to the intended technology purchase, such as price and specifications, must I submit with my formal application?

You must submit the quotation for the Laptop, which includes the pre-receipt, details and specifications, and the cost of the Laptop.
<br>

### Considering the high cost of modern technology, what type of financial assistance is primarily provided to the selected beneficiaries under this program?

The assistance provided under this scheme is an Interest Free Loan intended to help eligible teachers purchase necessary technological equipment.
<br>

### What is the mandated procedure for submitting the filled-out application form to the concerned authorities after I have completed all required sections?

You must submit the completed application form through the institution where you are currently working, recommended by the respective Head of the Institution.
<br>

### If I am currently employed in a Government-run educational establishment, am I considered eligible to submit an application for this loan assistance?

Yes, teachers working in Government schools, provided they are under the Administrative control of the Directorate of Education in the State of Goa, are eligible.
<br>

### What is the mandatory and singular intended purpose for which the funds obtained through this particular financial assistance program must be utilized?

The loan amount must be strictly utilized for the purchase of a Laptop computer, facilitating the use of Multimedia presentation and Graphics in teaching.
<br>

### Is there a geographical or administrative boundary requirement regarding the location of my teaching institution for eligibility purposes?

Your teaching institution must be under the Administrative control of the Directorate of Education in the State of Goa.
<br>

### What is the maximum quantifiable monetary ceiling placed on the financial assistance that a qualifying teacher can be granted for their purchase?

The maximum amount of the Interest Free Loan granted for the purchase of a Laptop is limited to ₹1,00,000/-.
<br>

### As a teacher interested in technology integration, what specific employment status must I hold to qualify for this particular assistance program?

You must be a regular/permanent and full-time teacher to meet the basic employment criteria for application.
<br>

### Which specific educational levels, such as primary, secondary, or college, must I be currently teaching at to meet the basic criteria?

You must be teaching in Secondary schools or Higher Secondary schools that fall under the administrative purview of the scheme.
<br>

### Are there specific limitations regarding the vendor or brand manufacturer from whom the technological equipment must be procured using the loan funds?

The Laptop must be purchased from any registered or authorized dealer of the branded manufacturer of your choice and configuration.
<br>

### Once the loan is approved and prepared for disbursement, who is named as the recipient on the Crossed Cheque issued by the corporation?

The Crossed Cheque, amounting to the price of the Laptop, is drawn directly in favor of the dealer chosen by the teacher.
<br>

### Which specific governmental body or corporation is responsible for the final verification and subsequent distribution of the sanctioned financial amount?

The loan is issued in the form of a Cheque through Messers Goa Education Development Corporation (GEDC), Government of Goa.
<br>
