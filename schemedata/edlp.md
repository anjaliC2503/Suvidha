## Brief Description

The scheme aims to establish Digital Learning Platform to provide training and career counseling on various subjects, including ICT orientation, emerging technologies, and more, through both online and offline modes for students of secondary schools across the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Establishment of Digital Learning Platform” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Science Education and Research Centre (USERC) under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to establish Digital Learning Platform to provide training and career counseling on various subjects, including ICT orientation, emerging technologies, and more, through both online and offline modes for students of secondary schools across the state.
<br>

## Benefits

Under the Digital Learning Platform, students of secondary schools are provided with training and career counseling on various subjects, such as ICT orientation, emerging technologies, and more, through both online and offline modes.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. All students studying in secondary schools across Uttarakhand are eligible.
<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

A five- or seven-day training on digital technology shall be provided to students through both online and offline modes in various secondary schools across the state.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can benefit from this scheme?

All students studying in secondary schools across Uttarakhand are eligible.
<br>

### What kind of training is provided under this scheme?

Training includes ICT orientation, emerging technologies, and other relevant digital skills through online and offline mediums.
<br>

### Is career counselling provided through this scheme?

Yes, students receive career counselling to guide them in choosing educational and career paths.
<br>

### How long is the training program?

The training is conducted for five or seven days, depending on the school and program schedule.
<br>

### Can training be attended online?

Yes, students can attend training online through the digital platform.
<br>

### Can training be attended offline?

Yes, offline training sessions are also conducted in various secondary schools across the state.
<br>
