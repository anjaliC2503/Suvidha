## Brief Description

The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs" aims to empower the state's dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&quot; and &quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&quot;
The component &quot;Assistance for Saving in Consumption of Energy and Water&quot; provides financial assistance to enterprises for taking measures that reduce energy and water consumption. It aims to promote sustainable practices and enhance resource efficiency in industries by providing support for energy/water audits and equipment purchases.

## Benefits

> **Energy/Water Audit Reimbursement:**



  - 75% of the cost of an energy/water audit, up to ₹50,000/- will be reimbursed.
  - Reimbursement is available once during the operative period of the scheme.


> **Equipment Cost Assistance:**



  - 25% of the cost for equipment recommended by the audit authority, up to ₹20,00,000/-, is eligible for assistance.
  - Assistance is available once during the scheme's operative period.


> **Energy/Water Savings Requirement:**



  - Assistance for equipment is available only if the energy or water savings are at least 10% of the average monthly consumption over the last 12 months before the audit.

## Eligibility

1. The firm must be a Micro/Small/Medium enterprise in the service sector.
   1. The enterprise must have obtained acknowledgment or registration from the Ministry of MSME or the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), as applicable.
   1. Enterprises must have undergone an energy/water audit conducted by a recognized institution/consultant to qualify for the audit reimbursement.
   1. Enterprises must demonstrate a minimum 10% reduction in energy/water consumption, based on the audit findings, to qualify for equipment assistance.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on "New Investor Registration" to access the registration form.
**Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on "Submit". 
**Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID.
**Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.

> **Post-Registration Process:**

**Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on "Login".
**Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration.
**Step-3:** Click the "Login" button to access your account.
**Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs"?

The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs" is a state initiative designed to support and strengthen the MSME sector in Gujarat. It aims to enhance growth, encourage investments, and contribute to the vision of self-reliant Gujarat and India.

### When is the scheme operative?

The scheme is operative from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, covering a period of five years.

### What are the objectives of the scheme?

The scheme aims to boost MSME growth, enhance the industrial ecosystem, encourage investments, and contribute to Gujarat's and India's self-reliance vision.

### What are some initiatives included in the scheme?

The scheme includes the establishment of new industrial estates, sector-specific parks, and infrastructure projects, along with amendments to the Gujarat Industrial Policy 2020 to further support MSME growth.

### Who is eligible to benefit from the scheme?

MSMEs established and operating within Gujarat are eligible to benefit from the scheme, as per the criteria defined in the policy framework.

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Assistance for Saving in Consumption of Energy and Water" component?

Both new and existing enterprises that have taken steps to save energy and water consumption are eligible to apply for assistance under this scheme.

### What type of audits are required to qualify for this scheme?

The enterprise must have a certified energy or water audit conducted by a recognized institution or consultant to be eligible for assistance.

### What is the maximum amount reimbursed for energy or water audits?

The scheme reimburses 75% of the cost of the energy/water audit, with a maximum of [?]50,000/- per audit.

### Can the enterprise apply for assistance more than once for audits?

No, the scheme provides reimbursement for the energy/water audit only once during the operative period.

### What type of equipment does the scheme cover for assistance?

The scheme covers 25% of the cost of equipment recommended by the audit authority, subject to a maximum of [?]20,00,00/-.

### What is the eligibility condition for equipment assistance?

The enterprise must demonstrate at least a 10% reduction in energy or water consumption compared to the previous 12 months before the audit to qualify for equipment assistance.

### Can enterprises claim assistance for both energy and water savings?

Yes, enterprises can apply for assistance for either energy or water savings, provided they meet the relevant eligibility requirements.

### Are there any exceptions to the 10% reduction requirement?

If consumption increases due to production growth, the claim will be reviewed separately by a committee formed by the State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC).

### Is there a time limit for submitting the application for assistance?

Yes, applications must be submitted within the operative period of the scheme, and the claims for assistance will not be accepted after the scheme's deadline.

### Who evaluates whether the enterprise qualifies for assistance under the scheme?

The eligibility and claims are reviewed and approved by the State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) based on the audit findings and consumption reductions.
