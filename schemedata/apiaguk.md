## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide free and accessible education for children from Class 1 to 8 by offering free education and essential learning materials, including books, shoe-bags, and meals, across all government and non-government schools in the state.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Admission Process in All Government and Non-Government Schools of the State&quot; scheme is implemented by the Basic Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free and accessible education for children from Class 1 to 8 by offering free education and essential learning materials, including books, shoe-bags, and meals, across all government and non-government schools in the state.
<br>

## Benefits

After admission in all Government and Non-Government schools of the state, children from Class 1 to 8 receive free education, meals, books, and shoe-bags.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant (child) must be 6 years old to get admission in Class 1.
1. The applicant (student) must be currently registered in Anganwadi Bal Vatika to be eligible for admission to Class 1.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Admission to Class 1 in government and private schools begins every year in April. 
  - For the admission process, any one of the following documents can be submitted as proof of the student’s age: the child’s hospital/ANM record, Anganwadi record, village/family register, or a declaration by the guardian/parents regarding the child’s age.
  - In case the student is transferring from another school, the school leaving/transfer certificate (TC) from the previous school is mandatory.
