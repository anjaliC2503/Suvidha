## Brief Description

The "Training Scheme for Horticulture Skill Development to Landless Farm Labour at Centre of Excellence of Department" aims to provide skill development training in horticulture to landless farm labourers through the Centres of Excellence.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Training Scheme for Horticulture Skill Development to Landless Farm Labour at Centre of Excellence of Department&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, is designed to uplift landless agricultural workers by equipping them with essential horticulture skills through training. Conducted across Gujarat, the training spans a minimum of three days to a maximum of five days and includes modules like experiential learning tours, training kits, stipend, expert-led sessions, and more.

## Benefits

- **Stipend:** ₹250/- per trainee.- **Other Facilities:** Dining facility, training literature kit, experiential education tour, trainee kit, etc.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be any of the following:


   1. All Farmers.
   1. Farm Labourer.
   1. Horticulturists.
   1. Landless Agricultural Labourers.


  - The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Obtain a plain paper application form from the District Office of the Deputy Director of Horticulture.
**Step 2:** Fill in the application form with accurate details. Attach the required documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the District Office of the Deputy Director of Horticulture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Trainning Scheme for Horticulture Skill Development to Landless farm labour at Centre of Excelence of Department" scheme?

The primary objective of the scheme is to provide structured, hands-on horticulture training to landless agricultural labourers in Gujarat to enhance their skills and enable better livelihood opportunities through specialized learning at the Centres of Excellence under the Agriculture and Co-operation Department.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this horticulture skill development training scheme?

The scheme is exclusively intended for individuals who are landless agricultural labourers residing in Gujarat and belong to any social category.

### How much stipend is provided to the beneficiaries?

Beneficiaries receive an amount of ₹250/- as a stipend.

### What facilities and support are provided to trainees during the training period?

During the training, participants are provided with a dining facility, a training literature kit, a trainee kit, access to experiential education tours, and sessions conducted by expert trainers, all funded through the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### How long does the training program last for participants enrolled in this scheme?

The duration of the training provided under the scheme ranges from a minimum of three days to a maximum of five days, during which participants engage in intensive skill development sessions related to horticulture practices.
<br>

<br>

### Where are the training sessions conducted for beneficiaries of this scheme?

The training sessions are conducted at recognized Centres of Excellence that operate under the Department of Agriculture and Co-operation within the state of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### Are there any category-wise distinctions in the implementation of this training scheme?

While the overall structure of the scheme remains consistent, it covers beneficiaries across Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, and General categories, each receiving the same financial structure and support.
<br>

<br>

### Can individuals owning agricultural land apply under this scheme?

No, the scheme is specifically designed for landless farm labourers, and individuals owning any form of agricultural land are not eligible to participate in the training program.
<br>

<br>

### Are women also eligible to benefit from this horticulture training scheme?

Yes, the scheme is gender-inclusive and is open to all landless agricultural labourers, including women, who meet the other eligibility criteria set forth by the government.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit or educational qualification required to apply for the scheme?

No, there is no minimum income or educational qualification specified for this scheme, as it is designed to uplift all landless farm labourers regardless of their economic background or formal education.
<br>

<br>

### What is the process to apply for the horticulture skill development training scheme?

Interested landless farm labourers need to write a plain paper application to the District Office of the Deputy Director of Horticulture, fill it with accurate details, attach necessary documents, and submit it to the same office.
<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to be submitted along with the application form?

Applicants are required to submit a copy of their Aadhaar Card, Bank Passbook or a Cancelled Cheque, Ration Card, and the duly filled plain paper application form.
