# A Program to Provide 90 Percent Assistance in Planting Material of Horticultural Fruit Crops

- **Scheme For:** Individual
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Nodal Department Name:** Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department

## Brief Description

This scheme offers 90% financial assistance to farmers for purchasing planting materials of fruit crops to promote horticulture.

## Detailed Description

&quot;A Program to Provide 90 Percent Assistance in Planting Material of Horticultural Fruit Crops&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support farmers by providing 90% subsidy on the cost of planting materials like grafted/tissue culture saplings and seedlings for various fruit crops.

## Benefits

- Farmers receive up to 90% financial assistance on the cost of planting materials.
> **Pre-fixed assistance rates per hectare for different crops:**


<br>

Fruit Assistance Mango₹32,000/haSapota (Chiku)₹22,000/haPomegranate₹20,000/haGuava₹16,650/haAonla (Indian Gooseberry)₹5,560/haMandarin / Orange₹5,560/haBer₹2,780/haCoconut₹13,000/ha<br>

> **Note:**


- Only saplings costing up to ₹250/- per unit are eligible for subsidy.
- **For fruit crops and cuttings:** Planting material will have to be purchased from nurseries accredited by NHB/Agricultural University/Horticulture Department.- **For coconut crops:** seedlings prepared from seeds will have to be purchased from nurseries accredited by NHB/Agricultural University/Horticulture Department.-  **For tissue culture:** Planting material (planting) will have to be purchased from tissue labs approved/accredited by DBT, GNFC, GSFC, Tissue Labs of Agricultural University, Government Public Institutions like

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a farmer.
   1. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
   1. The applicant must purchase planting materials from approved/accredited sources.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on &quot;Apply New&quot; button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the &quot;Update Application&quot; button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application.
**Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The main objective of this scheme is to encourage fruit crop cultivation by providing 90% financial assistance to farmers for purchasing planting materials.
<br>

<br>

### Who is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation, Government of Gujarat, is responsible for implementing this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Which farmers are eligible to apply for the scheme?

Farmers residing in Gujarat who are engaged in fruit crop cultivation are eligible to apply for the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What kind of financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides a subsidy of up to 90% of the cost of approved planting materials for horticultural fruit crops.
<br>

<br>

### Are the subsidy amounts different for different fruit crops?

Yes, the subsidy amounts vary depending on the crop, such as ₹32,000/ha for Mango, ₹22,000/ha for Chiku, and ₹20,000/ha for Pomegranate.
<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum cost allowed per sapling to be eligible for subsidy?

Only planting materials costing up to ₹250/- per unit are eligible for subsidy under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Can a farmer purchase planting materials from any nursery?

No, the planting materials must be purchased from nurseries accredited by the NHB, Agricultural Universities, or the Horticulture Department.
<br>

<br>

### Is tissue culture planting material covered under the scheme?

Yes, tissue culture planting material is covered if it is purchased from DBT-approved labs or government-recognized institutions.
<br>

<br>

### Is this scheme available to farmers outside Gujarat?

No, this scheme is only available to farmers who are residents of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### Does the scheme have any restrictions based on caste, income, or education?

No, the scheme is open to all farmers regardless of their caste, income level, or educational background.
<br>

<br>

### How will the subsidy be disbursed to the farmer?

The approved subsidy amount will be directly credited to the bank account of the eligible farmer after verification.
<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant apply for this scheme?

Applicants can apply on the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)portal to avail the benefits of the scheme.

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme?

Applicants are required to submit an Aadhaar card, 7/12 land record, and a copy of their bank passbook or a cancelled cheque.
<br>

<br>

### Who should the applicant contact for help or more information about the scheme?

The applicant should contact the nearest agriculture office or visit the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/)portal for more information.
