## Brief Description

The "Coaching Help Scheme for JEE-GUJCET-NEET Exams" scheme provides financial assistance to non-reserved category students from the science stream who are getting coaching in institutions with three years or more experience.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Coaching Help Scheme for JEE-GUJCET-NEET Exams" introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to support non-reserved category students in pursuing professional courses in medical and engineering fields by providing financial aid for coaching. Students who have secured 70% or more marks in Class 10th/ 11th/ 12th standard and are enrolled in coaching institutes with at least three years of experience can benefit from this scheme. The scheme is being implemented by the Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:**  ₹20,000/- per year per student or actual fee, whichever is lower.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must belong to a non-reserved  category.
   1. The applicant must have obtained 70% or more marks in Class 10th/ 11th/ 12th.
   1. The applicant must be from Science stream.
   1. The applicant must be pursuing coaching for JEE, GUJCET, or NEET.
   1. The coaching institute must have at least 3 years of experience.
   1. The annual family income of the applicant should be ₹4,50,000/- or less.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process****:**

**Step 1:** Go to the Corporation's [official website](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/) and navigate to the "Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation" section under Corporation menu.
**Step 2:**Click on "Coaching Help Scheme for JEE-GUJCET-NEET Exams" and then Apply Now.
**Step 3:** Click on "New User? Please Register Here".
**Step 4:** Enter the required details and click Register.
<br>

> **Application Process:**

**Step 1:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, then click "Login".
**Step 2:** Read the scheme details carefully and click OK.
**Step 3:** If your permanent address is correct, confirm it. Otherwise, enter your correct address.
**Step 4:** Click Save & Next to proceed. Upload the relevant documents.
**Step 5:** If you need to make changes, click "No, Cancel". If all details are correct, click "Yes, Submit".
**Step 6:** Click Save Application. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout by clicking Print Application.
<br>

> **Check Application Status:** [Click here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx).

> **Post Online Application Procedure:** After submitting the online application, the applicant must take a printout of the application, sign it, and submit it along with all uploaded documents (certificates, mark sheets, etc.) to the concerned Deputy Director (Non-Reserved Category) or the District Social Welfare Officer through courier, post, or by visiting in person.

> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in/contact-us.html).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which students are eligible for coaching assistance?

Students who are studying in the Science stream of Class 12 are eligible for coaching assistance under this scheme.

### What is the minimum percentage required in Class 10 for coaching assistance?

To be eligible for coaching assistance, students must have secured 70% or more marks in Class 10.

### How much financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

Under this scheme, students will receive financial assistance of [?]20,000 per year.

### What is the annual family income limit for coaching assistance?

The annual family income of the student's household must be less than [?]4,50,000/- to be eligible for this scheme.

### What type of fee receipt is required for coaching assistance?

The student must submit a fee receipt that includes either a GST number or the registration number of the coaching institution.

### Which documents are required for coaching assistance?

The documents required for coaching assistance include a prescribed application form, self-declaration form, non-reserved category certificate, income certificate, domicile proof, Aadhaar card copy, school/college admission letter (bonafide certificate), Leaving Certificate (LC), Class 10 marksheet copy, tuition fee receipt, bank passbook copy, proof that the coaching class has been operational for at least three years with a valid registration number, and the fee receipt from the coaching institute.

### What type of fee receipt should be submitted?

The student must submit the fee receipt from the coaching class in which they are currently enrolled.

### Should percentile rank or percentage be considered for Class 10 marks?

For this scheme, the percentage of marks obtained in Class 10 will be considered and not the percentile rank.

### Will a domicile certificate be accepted as proof of 10 years of education in Gujarat?

Yes, a domicile certificate will be accepted as valid proof of completing 10 years of education in Gujarat.

### Which authority’s income certificate is valid for this scheme?

The income certificate issued by the Taluka Development Officer (TDO), Mamlatdar, or Chief Officer will be considered valid for this scheme.

### From which authorities can the non-reserved category certificate be obtained?

The non-reserved category certificate can be obtained from the Taluka Development Officer (TDO), Mamlatdar, District Social Welfare Officer (Non-Reserved), or Deputy Director (Non-Reserved).

### Will a Leaving Certificate (LC) be accepted as proof of 10 years of education in Gujarat?

Yes, a Leaving Certificate (LC) will be accepted as valid proof of completing 10 years of education in Gujarat.

### What should be done after submitting the online application?

After submitting the online application, the applicant must take a printout of the application form, sign it, and submit all the uploaded documents, including certificates and marksheets, in attested copies to the Deputy Director (Non-Reserved) or District Social Welfare Officer of the district where they are studying. The submission can be done via courier, post, or in person.

### What happens if the hard copy of the application is not submitted after applying online?

If the applicant fails to submit the hard copy of the application along with the required documents, the application will not be considered valid.
