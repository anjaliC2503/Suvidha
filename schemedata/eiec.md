## Brief Description

The scheme "Establishment of Integrated Educational Complexes" by the Directorate of Education, Government of Goa, aims to address the inadequate infrastructure and congested conditions faced by government-aided educational institutions in urban areas such as Panaji, Margao, and Mapusa within Goa.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Establishment of Integrated Educational Complexes&quot; by the Directorate of Education, Government of Goa, aims to address the inadequate infrastructure and congested conditions faced by government-aided educational institutions in urban areas such as Panaji, Margao, and Mapusa within Goa. These complexes are envisioned as comprehensive educational hubs equipped with essential facilities like playgrounds and auditoriums, providing a conducive environment for learning.
<br>

The scheme envisages the setting up of Integrated Educational Complexes at Bambolim, Mapusa and Margao, initially at Bambolim. The Integrated Educational Complex is a unique approach to providing better education (with common facilities) to students coming from all backgrounds as it is observed that many of the schools located in Urban areas are functioning in congested areas/flats and do not have any space for infrastructure facilities such as playground, auditorium etc. which does not provide the right school atmosphere.

## Benefits

Allotment of (approx.) 3000 to 6000 square meters of land for every school/institution for separate building(s) depending on the strength of the students. The same is leased out for 33 years.

## Eligibility

1. Government-aided institutions in the city/town of Panaji/Margao/Mapusa located within 10 km. of the complex site and engaged in educational activities, if they so desire to shift from their present location due to the fact that their schools are presently functioning in areas that are congested and with insufficient infrastructure, may apply.
1. Existing Government educational institutions that have no or insufficient premises and are located within 10 km. of the proposed complex site may also apply.

## Application Process

### Offline

- The schools/institutions fulfilling the eligibility criteria should submit the proposal requesting for allotment of land, along with the required documents, to: ***Director of Education, Directorate of Education, Government of Goa, Porvorim, Goa- 403 501, 0832-2416139, dir-educ.goa@nic.in***- All proposals under the scheme shall be scrutinized by a committee comprising of Director of Education, Senior Dy. Director of Education and Joint Director of Accounts as member.

## Documents Required

1. Certificate of registration of the society and renewal certificate for educational purposes.
1. Copy of permission and recognition granted by the Department.
1. Status note of the institution since its inception highlighting its achievements.
1. A copy of the recent Managing Committee was approved by the Department.
1. The institution should give an undertaking that they will utilize the land for the purpose it was granted and that the building(s) be constructed within three years. All proposals under the scheme shall be scrutinized by a committee comprising of Director of Education, Senior Dy. Director of Education and Joint Director of Accounts as a member.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Scheme for Integrated Educational Complexes?

The scheme aims to provide better educational facilities by establishing integrated complexes with improved infrastructure like classrooms, laboratories, playgrounds, and auditoriums for government-aided institutions in congested urban areas of Panaji, Margao, and Mapusa within Goa.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Government-aided educational institutions within 10 kilometers of the designated complex sites and existing government educational institutions lacking adequate premises are eligible to apply.

### What facilities are provided at the integrated educational complexes?

The complexes offer approximately 3000 to 6000 square meters of space per institution, leased for 33 years. They include modern classrooms, labs, libraries, sports grounds, and cultural facilities, essential for comprehensive educational development.

### How does the application process work?

Eligible institutions submit applications to the Director of Education, Goa, providing necessary documentation such as society registration, permissions, achievements, and a commitment to utilize the land for educational purposes within three years.

### Who evaluates the applications?

A committee comprising the Director of Education, Senior Deputy Director of Education, and Joint Director of Accounts evaluates applications based on institutional status, achievements, and adherence to scheme guidelines.

### What happens after approval of the application?

Institutions awarded land enter into a 33-year lease agreement for construction within the integrated complex. They proceed with building their facilities as per the approved proposal and timelines.

### How are the complexes financed?

While specific financial details are not detailed, the scheme likely involves some form of government assistance or subsidy for the construction or improvement of facilities within the integrated complexes.

### What are the benefits of relocating to these complexes?

Relocating institutions benefit from improved infrastructure, stability through long-term leasing, shared amenities, and enhanced connectivity within urban centers, fostering a conducive learning environment.

### How does the scheme contribute to educational development?

By providing modern facilities and centralized locations, the scheme aims to uplift educational standards, support capacity building, and promote academic excellence among students in urban areas of Goa.

### How does the scheme ensure transparency and fairness in allocation?

The scheme employs a structured application and evaluation process conducted by a committee of educational and financial experts, ensuring transparency, fairness, and merit-based allocation of resources to eligible institutions.
