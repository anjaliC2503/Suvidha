## Brief Description

The scheme "Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage the publication of fine arts books through financial assistance provided to artists and art institutions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase awareness about fine arts and promote the publication of fine arts literature by supporting artists and art institutions financially. Under this initiative, applications are invited annually through paid newspaper advertisements, and 2 (two) artists/art institutions are provided assistance for the publication of fine arts books.. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** 50% of the total printing cost or a maximum of ₹20,000/- (whichever is lower).
<br>

## Eligibility

- An individual artist or an art institution working in the field of fine arts is eligible for this scheme.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should download the prescribed format of the [application form](https://lalitkalaacademy.gujarat.gov.in/writereaddata/Portal/Magazine/Document/1_34_3_Pustak-Prakashan-Sahay.pdf) for the &quot;Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts&quot; scheme.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, including career details in the field of art, photographs, or color slides of work (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority at the Academy office to whom the application has been submitted.
**Note:** Applications for book publication are invited every year through advertisement in daily newspapers.

1. Copy of Dummy Publication.
1. Drawings, Sketches, and Photographs of Paintings.
1. Book Text (Manuscript).
1. Printed Book (10 Copies) (to be submitted post-printing).
1. Original Printing Bills.
1. Declaration/Acknowledgment in the Book (Stating Financial Support by Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the primary objective of the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts”?

The primary objective of the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts” is to promote the creation and dissemination of literature in the field of fine arts by offering financial assistance to individual artists and art institutions for the publication of high-quality fine arts books, thereby increasing public awareness and appreciation of artistic disciplines.
<br>

### Who has launched the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts” and which agency is responsible for its implementation?

The “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts” has been launched by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, and is implemented through the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy, which is the designated agency responsible for overseeing the application process and disbursement of funds.
<br>

### What kind of support is provided under the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts”?

Under the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts”, selected applicants are provided with financial assistance amounting to 50 percent of the total printing cost or a maximum of ₹20,000, whichever is lower, to support the publication of fine arts books and related content.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who can apply for the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts”?

The scheme is open to individual artists and recognized art institutions that are actively engaged in the creation, study, or promotion of fine arts and who intend to publish content such as books or compilations that contribute to the field of fine arts.
<br>

### How many applicants are selected each year under the scheme?

Each year, only two applicants—either individual artists or art institutions—are selected to receive financial support under the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts”, making the selection highly competitive and based on the quality and relevance of the submitted work.
<br>

### What is the process for applying to the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts”?

To apply for the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts”, the applicant must respond to an annual call for applications published through paid advertisements in newspapers, submit their proposal in the prescribed format, and ensure that all required materials are included for evaluation by the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What should the selected applicant do after printing the book under this scheme?

After the book is printed, the selected applicant must submit 10 copies of the printed book along with the original printing bills to the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy, in order to become eligible for the disbursement of the financial assistance promised under the scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents must an applicant submit while applying for the scheme?

While applying for the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts”, the applicant must submit a duly filled application form in the prescribed format along with a publication dummy, drawings, sketches, photographs, the proposed manuscript or text, and any other supportive content that demonstrates the publication’s artistic value.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there a requirement to acknowledge the support received under this scheme in the published book?

Yes, it is mandatory for the applicant to clearly mention in the published book that it has been supported and funded through the financial assistance provided under the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts” by the Gujarat State Fine Arts Academy.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Are books that have already been printed before scheme approval eligible for funding?

No, books that have already been printed before receiving formal approval under the “Publication Support Scheme for Fine Arts” are not eligible for funding, as the scheme strictly requires that the printing be undertaken only after approval and in accordance with prescribed procedures.
<br>
