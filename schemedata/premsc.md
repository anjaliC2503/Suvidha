## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support the education of Scheduled Caste students studying in Classes IX and X. Through this scheme, annual scholarship assistance of up to ₹7,000/- is provided to eligible students.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Pre-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste Students" launched by the Department of Social Justice & Empowerment, Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India, aims to support the education of Scheduled Caste students studying at the pre-matric stage, reduce school drop-out rates, and encourage continuation of education up to higher levels. Through this scheme, annual scholarship assistance is provided to eligible Scheduled Caste students studying in Classes IX and X. The scheme is implemented by the UT Administration of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu. The scholarship is provided as a consolidated academic allowance to support educational expenses. The scheme seeks to improve educational participation and progression of Scheduled Caste students to the post-matric level.
> **Objectives**


- To support parents of Scheduled Caste students for the education of their wards studying at the pre-matric stage.
- To minimise school drop-out rates, especially during the transition from elementary to secondary education.
- To improve students' educational performance and increase their chances of progressing to the post-matric stage.

<br>

## Benefits

1. Financial support for SC students studying in Std. IX and X.
1. ₹3,500/- per annum assistance for day scholars.
1. ₹7,000/- per annum assistance for hostellers.
1. Additional 10% allowance for Divyang students.
1. Encourages continuation of education among economically weaker students.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to the Union Territory of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.
1. The applicant should be studying in Class IX or Class X.
1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste category.
1. The applicant's parents' or guardian's annual income from all sources should not exceed ₹2,50,000/-.
1. The applicant should possess a UT domicile certificate.
1. The applicant must study in a recognised school with a valid UDISE code.
1. The applicant must be divyang students are eligible for an additional 10% allowance.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:**   Keep ready the soft copies of the required documents.
Go to http://www.scholarships.gov.in/. And click “New Registration”.
Guidelines for Registration will appear. Scroll to the bottom. 
Read the undertaking carefully. Accept the Terms. Click “Continue”.
**Step 2:**  A Registration Form will appear. (The fields marked as * are compulsory)
Fill in the details and click “Register”. 
Your Application ID and password will be displayed.
The same will also be sent as an SMS on your registered mobile number. 
**Step 3:**  Go to https://scholarships.gov.in/fresh/newstdRegfrmInstruction 
Click on “Login to Apply”. Enter your Application ID and password.
Type the Captcha and click “Login”.
On the next screen, provide the OTP received on your registered mobile number. You will be directed to the Password Reset screen.
Create a new password and confirm.
Click “Submit”. You will be directed to the “Applicant’s Dashboard”.
 **Step 4:**  On the left pane, click “Application Form”. The fields marked as * are compulsory. Fill the details and upload the documents.
You can either click on “Save as Draft” to complete the application later.
Else, click “Final Submit” to submit the application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Pre-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste Students?

The scheme provides financial assistance to Scheduled Caste students studying in Classes IX and X to support their education and reduce school drop-out rates.
<br>

### Who can apply for this scholarship in DNH&DD?

Students belonging to the Scheduled Caste category, studying in Class IX or X on a full-time basis, and belonging to the Union Territory of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu can apply, subject to fulfilment of the income criteria.
<br>

### What is the annual income limit for eligibility?

The annual income of the applicant's parent or guardian from all sources should not exceed ₹2,50,000/-.
<br>

### How much scholarship is provided to eligible students?

Day scholars receive ₹3,500 per annum, while hostellers receive ₹7,000 per annum.
<br>

### Is the scholarship available for students studying outside DNH&DD?

Yes. The scholarship is available for studies pursued anywhere in India, provided the student belongs to the Union Territory of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu and fulfils all eligibility conditions.
<br>

### Can students studying below Class IX apply for this scholarship?

No. Under this component, only students studying in Classes IX and X are eligible.
<br>

### How can a student apply for the scholarship?

The student can apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) during the application period notified by the authorities.
<br>

### Is a Scheduled Caste certificate mandatory for applying?

Yes. The applicant must belong to the Scheduled Caste category and should provide a valid Scheduled Caste Certificate as proof, wherever required.
<br>

### Is the scholarship provided every year?

Yes. The scholarship is awarded annually for the approved academic year, subject to fulfilment of the scheme conditions and successful verification of the application.
<br>

### Which authority verifies and approves the applications in DNH&DD?

Applications are verified and processed by the competent authorities of the UT Administration of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu before the sanction of the scholarship.
