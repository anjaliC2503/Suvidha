## Detailed Description

The Buti Foundation Award, established in 2007 by the Buti Foundation, New Delhi, is a biennial award recognizing innovative and outstanding research in the field of Plasma Science & Technology. The award aims to honor a Scientist/Engineer under the age of 45 years for their significant contributions to this field. The award includes a medal and a cash prize.

## Benefits

- **Award Amount:** ₹50,000/-- **Award Recognition:** Medal

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a Scientist/Engineer.
  - The applicant’s age should be under 45 years as of 1st January 2021 (born on or after 02.01.1976).
  - The applicant should have conducted innovative and outstanding research in the field of Plasma Science &amp; Technology in India.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Download the prescribed format of the [application form](https://www.prl.res.in/prl-eng/sites/default/files/documents/national-awards/Proforma-for-Nomination-Buti-Foundation-Award-2019.doc) from the PRL official website. 
**Step 2:** Fill in all the mandatory fields in the application form and attach copies of all the required documents (self-attested, if necessary).
**Step 3:** Candidate can email their applications in the prescribed format to: DIRECTOR@PRL.RES.IN with the subject line “Nomination for The Buti Foundation Award”.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the age limit for the award?

Nominees must be under 45 years of age as of January 1, 2021 (born on or after January 2, 1976).

### What is the prize amount?

The award includes a medal and a cash prize of [?]50,000.

### What should be included in the nomination?

Significant contributions, the impact of contributions, specific contributions, a complete list of publications, TEN BEST publications, and reprints of five important publications.

### Can I submit physical copies of the documents?

No, only e-files of the reprints are accepted. All submissions should be via email.

### Can past nominees be re-nominated?

Yes, but a fresh nomination must be submitted according to the current criteria and guidelines.

### Who can make a nomination?

Nominations can be made by Heads of Academic & Research Institutions, Vice-Chancellors, Deans of Universities, Fellows of Indian Academies of Science and Engineering, and past recipients of the award.

### What should be the subject line of the nomination email?

The subject line should be 'Nomination for The Buti Foundation Award'.

### How is the impact of contributions evaluated?

The impact is evaluated based on the significance of the nominee's contributions to the Plasma Science & Technology field.

### Where can I download the nomination proforma?

The proforma can be downloaded from the PRL website under National Awards --> Buti Foundation Award.

### How should the nomination be submitted?

Nominations should be emailed to the Director, PRL at DIRECTOR@PRL.RES.IN with the required document.
