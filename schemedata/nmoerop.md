## Brief Description

The scheme component "Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden" launched under the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm" scheme aims to enhance oil palm production and lower dependency on imports by supporting farmers in uprooting and replanting old oil palm gardens.

## Detailed Description

The scheme component &quot;Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden&quot; launched under the &quot;National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm&quot; scheme by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance oil palm production and lower dependency on imports by supporting farmers in uprooting and replanting old oil palm gardens that have reached the age of 25–30 years. The scheme provides a subsidy of ₹250 per plant for uprooting, replacing, and digging new pits, thereby ensuring sustainable cultivation practices and long-term productivity.
<br>

## Benefits

**Subsidy:** ₹250/- per plant (uprooting, replacing, and digging of new pit).
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a farmer.
  - The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden"?

The main purpose of the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden" is to increase the production of oil palm in Gujarat and to reduce the dependency on imports of oil palm from abroad by supporting farmers in replanting their old oil palm gardens.
<br>

### Which department is responsible for implementing the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden"?

The scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden" is implemented by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat through its concerned agricultural authorities.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden"?

Any farmer in Gujarat is eligible to apply for the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden".
<br>

### What type of financial support is provided under the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden"?

The scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden" provides financial support in the form of a subsidy of ₹250/- per plant for uprooting, replacing, and digging new pits for replanting oil palm gardens.
<br>

### What specific activities are covered under the subsidy of the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden"?

The subsidy provided under the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden" covers the activities of uprooting old oil palm plants, replacing them with new plants, and digging new pits for proper plantation.
<br>

### Is the financial assistance under the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden" a recurring benefit?

The financial assistance under the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden" is not a recurring benefit but is given as a one-time grant to eligible farmers.
<br>

### How can eligible farmers apply for the scheme?

Eligible farmers can apply for the scheme by visiting the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, filling out the application form online, uploading the required documents, and submitting it for departmental verification.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden" scheme?

Applicants for the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden" scheme are required to submit their Aadhaar Card, Land Records 7-12 and 8-A, Bank Passbook or Cancelled Cheque, and if applicable, a Caste Certificate or Disability Certificate issued by the competent authority.
<br>

### Is there any income limit for applying to the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Replanting of Old Oil Palm Garden"?

There is no income limit specified under the scheme guideline, meaning that farmers of all income levels can apply for the financial assistance provided they fulfil the other eligibility requirements.
<br>
