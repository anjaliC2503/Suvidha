## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote the protected cultivation of commercial horticultural crops by providing financial assistance to farmers. The scheme supports greenhouse, shade net, low tunnel, plastic mulching, bird/hail net, and cultivation of vegetables and flowers under polyhouse/shade net house.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Scheme for Promotion of Protected Cultivation of Commercial Horticultural Crops&quot; launched by the Horticulture and Food Processing Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to promote protected cultivation through providing financial assistance up to 50% of the unit cost to eligible farmers.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** Eligible farmers are provided a subsidy up to a maximum of 50% of the unit cost.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a farmer.
  - The applicant must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The applicant should contact the District Office of the Horticulture Department.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme aims to promote protected cultivation practices among farmers by providing financial assistance.
<br>

### Who implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Horticulture and Food Processing Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Who can apply for this scheme?

All farmers of the state can apply for this scheme.
<br>

### What types of structures are covered under the scheme?

Greenhouse, shade net, low tunnel, plastic mulching, bird/hail net, and polyhouse/shade net house are covered.
<br>

### What percentage of financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

A maximum of 50% of the unit cost is provided as financial assistance.
<br>

### How can a farmer apply for the scheme?

A farmer can apply by contacting the District Office of the Horticulture Department.
<br>

### What crops are promoted under the scheme?

Vegetables and flowers cultivated under protected structures are promoted under this scheme.
<br>

### Where can an applicant get more information about the scheme?

The applicant can contact the District Horticulture Office for detailed information.
