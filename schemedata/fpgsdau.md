## Brief Description

The scheme "Fellowship for the Post-Graduate Students in Various Faculties Various Faculties of Sardarkrushinagar Dantiwada Agricultural University" provides a monthly fellowship to meritorious Post-Graduate students across various faculties of Sardarkrushinagar Dantiwada Agricultural University.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Fellowship for the Post-Graduate Students in Various Faculties Various Faculties of Sardarkrushinagar Dantiwada Agricultural University&quot; launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, attract the bright young talents in post-graduate studies in the field of Agricultural and other allied subjects for improvement of academic standard of teaching, research and extension education activities and better output in the field wherever they resume after obtaining higher education.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Master’s degree students:** ₹5,000/- per month for 2 years.  - **Ph.D. degree students:** ₹5,000/- per month for 3 years.
> **Note:** The University shall annually award four fellowships in each of the concerned faculties for both Master&#39;s well as Doctorate degree programmes.

## Exclusions

- The applicant must not be availing any other government fellowship or scholarship.
  - The applicant must not be an in-service candidate.
  - The applicant must not have dropped any course during the academic program (except in the case of formal withdrawal).
  - The applicant must not have been punished under university disciplinary rules or for using unfair means in examinations.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a student.
  - The applicant must be enrolled in a Master’s or Ph.D. program at Sardarkrushinagar Dantiwada Agricultural University (SDAU).
  - The applicant must have secured a first-class or its equivalent OGPA in the qualifying undergraduate or postgraduate examination from a State Agricultural University in Gujarat.
  - The applicant must be 28 years of age or below at the time of application.
  - The applicant must maintain a minimum OGPA of 7.5 and must not receive an “F” (Fail) or “I” (Incomplete) grade during the course of study.

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Obtain the prescribed application form from the University.
**Step 2:**  Submit the completed application to the Registrar by 31st December of the same year.
**Step 3:** Selection will be made by a university committee based on merit and eligibility.
> **Note:** Fellowship notification is released by 30th November every year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the " Fellowship for the Post-Graduate Students in Various Faculties Various Faculties of Sardarkrushinagar Dantiwada Agricultural University" scheme?

The main objective of this scheme is to encourage bright and talented students to pursue postgraduate (Master’s and Ph.D.) education in agriculture and allied sciences, thereby addressing the shortage of trained personnel for teaching, research, and extension activities within the University.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the fellowship under this scheme?

A student is eligible to apply for the fellowship only if they are a fresh applicant enrolled in a Master's or Ph.D. program at SDAU, have secured a first-class or equivalent OGPA in previous qualifying examinations from a State Agricultural University in Gujarat, and meet all other criteria as specified by the University.
<br>

<br>

### What is the financial assistance provided under this fellowship?

The fellowship provides financial assistance in the form of ₹5,000/- per month, which is granted to both Master's and Ph.D. students throughout the approved duration of their academic program.
<br>

<br>

### What is the duration for which the fellowship is granted to selected students?

The fellowship is granted for a period of two years for students enrolled in a Master's degree program and for three years for those pursuing a Ph.D. degree, subject to satisfactory academic performance and adherence to university guidelines.
<br>

<br>

### How are the candidates selected for the fellowship?

Candidates are selected based on merit by a University-appointed committee comprising the Director of Research and Dean of PG Studies, Deans of various faculties, Director of Student Welfare, and the Registrar, following a thorough evaluation of academic records and eligibility.
<br>

<br>

### Are students who are already employed in service eligible to receive this fellowship?

No, students who are already in service or employment at the time of application are not eligible to receive this fellowship, as the scheme is intended exclusively for fresh and full-time postgraduate students.
<br>

<br>

### Can a student avail another government fellowship or scholarship along with this SDAU fellowship?

No, the scheme strictly prohibits students from availing any other government-sponsored fellowship or scholarship simultaneously, and any such dual benefit will result in disqualification and cancellation of the fellowship.
<br>

<br>

### What academic performance is required to retain the fellowship in subsequent years?

To retain the fellowship in subsequent years, the student must maintain a minimum OGPA of 7.5, must not secure any failing or incomplete grades, and must submit a positive progress report duly endorsed by their Major Guide and approved by the Principal.
<br>

<br>

### What happens if a student fails a subject or receives a disciplinary penalty?

If a student fails a subject (receives an "F" or "I" grade), drops a course without formal withdrawal, or is subjected to any form of disciplinary action, including the use of unfair means, the fellowship will be immediately terminated.
<br>

<br>

### How will the fellowship amount be disbursed to the selected students?

The fellowship amount will be disbursed monthly through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the bank account of the beneficiary student, which must be linked with their Aadhar number to ensure secure and timely payment.
<br>

<br>

### When is the fellowship officially announced each year?

The University officially announces the availability of fellowships through a notification issued by the Registrar, typically by the 30th of November each year.
<br>

<br>

### What is the deadline for submitting the application for the fellowship?

Interested and eligible candidates must submit their completed fellowship application forms to the University Registrar no later than the 31st of December each academic year.
<br>

<br>

### Are students from other states or universities eligible for this fellowship?

No, only those students who have completed their undergraduate or postgraduate degrees from State Agricultural Universities within Gujarat are eligible to apply for the fellowship under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What actions will lead to the cancellation of a student’s fellowship?

The fellowship will be cancelled if the student engages in academic misconduct, receives grades below the minimum required standards, fails to comply with university rules, or voluntarily leaves the course incomplete without official withdrawal.
<br>

<br>

### Whom should the students contact for more details regarding the fellowship scheme?

Students seeking further clarification or assistance regarding the fellowship scheme should directly contact the office of the University Registrar or refer to the official SDAU website for updated notifications and guidelines.
