## Brief Description

The scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World" aims to promote mountaineering by providing financial aid to successful climbers, including up to ₹15,00,000 for those who scale Mt. Everest.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage and reward mountaineers who demonstrate extraordinary courage and determination by scaling some of the most difficult mountain peaks in the world. The scheme offers tiered financial assistance based on the height and difficulty of the peak climbed, ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹15,00,000, with the maximum incentive awarded to individuals who successfully scale Mt. Everest. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner, Youth Services and Cultural Activities, Gandhinagar.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Financial Support for Mountaineers:**


- Financial assistance ranging from ₹50,000/- to ₹10,00,000/- is provided to individuals depending on the height and difficulty of the mountain scaled.
- A maximum of ₹15,00,000/- is provided to those who successfully climb Mount Everest.

<br>

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

> **Eligibility Criteria for Adventurer:**



    - The applicant should have successfully scaled Mount Everest or other inaccessible mountains of the world.
    - The applicant should be aged between 18 to 45 years as on 31st December of the respective year.
    - The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat, Mount Abu, or any other Indian state.
    - The applicant from Gujarat or Mount Abu should have completed a coaching course from the Swami Vivekananda Mountaineering Institute, Mount Abu.
    - The applicant must be physically and mentally fit.


    - The applicant must not have any criminal record.


    - The applicant who has already participated once in the camp during the same year will not be allowed to participate again.


> **Eligibility Criteria for Coach or Instructor:**



    - The applicant should have provided services in Artificial Rock Climbing Courses or Coaching Courses held at Mount Abu.
    - The applicant must have completed the Method of Instructor Course and Search and Rescue Course.
    - The applicant should possess sufficient mountaineering experience and certifications relevant to instructing rock climbing activities.


> **Eligibility Criteria for Subject Experts:**



    - The applicant must be one of the following:




    - A subject expert or experienced individual from any Ministry or Department of the Government.
    - A representative of an international or national mountaineering association or institution recognized by the government.
    - A person who has received National or International awards/medals or has similar significant achievements in mountaineering from recognized associations.

&gt; **Note:** Preference will be given to applicants who are participating for the first time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World"?

The scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World" aims to promote the spirit of adventure and mountaineering among Indian citizens, especially those from Gujarat, by providing financial assistance to individuals who successfully scale Mount Everest or other globally recognized inaccessible peaks.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to receive financial assistance under this scheme?

Individuals who have successfully scaled Mount Everest or other challenging and inaccessible mountains, meet the specified age criteria, and submit valid certificates of training, fitness, and clean police record are eligible to apply under the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum financial support provided under this scheme?

Under the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World", the maximum financial assistance provided is ₹15,00,000 for those who successfully climb Mount Everest.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How much financial aid is offered for scaling mountains other than Mount Everest?

For inaccessible peaks other than Mount Everest, the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World" provides financial aid ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000, depending on the height and difficulty of the mountain climbed.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which department implements this scheme?

The scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World" is implemented by the Commissioner, Youth Services & Cultural Activities, under the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the eligibility criteria for participants of the National Level Rock Climbing Camp under this scheme?

Participants of the National Level Rock Climbing Camp under the scheme must be between 18 to 45 years of age, possess a fitness certificate from a Civil Surgeon, and have undergone recognized mountaineering training or have experience in scaling mountains, as per the rules of the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can participants from other Indian states apply under this scheme?

Yes, the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World" is open to adventurers from all parts of India, provided they fulfill the required eligibility criteria, including proper certification and experience.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is it necessary for applicants to have prior training in mountaineering?

Yes, under the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World", it is mandatory for applicants to have completed formal mountaineering training from recognized institutes such as the Swami Vivekananda Mountaineering Institute or equivalent institutions recognized by state or central governments.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What type of proof is required for physical and mental fitness?

Applicants must provide a certificate issued by a Civil Surgeon confirming their physical and mental fitness in order to be considered eligible under the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any restriction on the number of times a person can participate in the rock climbing camp?

Yes, as per the guidelines of the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World", an individual cannot participate more than once in the National Level Rock Climbing Camp in a single year.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply as a coach or instructor under the scheme?

Individuals who have completed the Method of Instructor Course and the Search and Rescue Course and have provided training services in Artificial Rock Climbing or other certified coaching programs are eligible to apply as coaches or instructors under the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the qualifications required for selection as a subject expert in the camp?

A person may be selected as a subject expert under the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World" if they are a government-recognized expert, have experience with national or international mountaineering institutions, or have received notable awards in the field of mountaineering.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Does the scheme cover travel expenses to the training camp location?

No, the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World" does not cover travel expenses to the camp location; such costs must be borne by the participant themselves.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where should the applicant submit the application for this scheme?

Applications for the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World" must be submitted to the office of the Commissioner, Youth Services & Cultural Activities, Gandhinagar.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any cost for the application form under this scheme?

No, the application form for the scheme "Financial Assistance for Scaling Mount Everest and Other Inaccessible Mountains of the World" is free of cost and can be downloaded from this [Link](https://commi-synca.gujarat.gov.in/writereaddata/Portal/Magazine/Document/1_216_3_revised-mount-everest-sahay-application-form-01092018.pdf).
