## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support senior writers facing financial hardship by offering monthly assistance to those who have made notable contributions to Gujarati or associated language literature.

## Detailed Description

The "Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers" is a welfare initiative implemented by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy to provide financial security to elderly writers who have significantly contributed to Gujarati, Hindi, Sanskrit, Sindhi, Urdu, or Kutchi literature. The scheme offers monthly assistance to writers who are 60 years of age or older, have no steady pension or major income source, and are economically disadvantaged.

## Benefits

- **Monthly Financial Support:** Eligible elderly writers receive monthly financial assistance as specified by the authority.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be 60 years or older.
  - The applicant must have made a significant literary contribution (e.g., authored books, received awards, or provided literary service).
  - The applicant must belong to one of the recognized language academies: Gujarati, Hindi, Sanskrit, Sindhi, Urdu, or Kutchi.
  - The applicant’s annual family income must be below ₹1,20,000 in rural areas and below ₹1,50,000 in urban areas, certified by the Mamlatdar.
  - The applicant must not be receiving any pension.
  - The applicant must not already be receiving financial support under similar schemes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers"?

The purpose of the "Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers" is to offer recurring financial support to senior writers who have contributed significantly to literature in Gujarati or other approved Indian languages and are currently facing economic hardship in old age.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the "Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers"?

Under the "Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers", any writer who is 60 years of age or older and has made noteworthy contributions to Gujarati, Hindi, Sanskrit, Sindhi, Urdu, or Kutchi literature may apply, provided they are not receiving any form of pension or similar financial benefit.
<br>

<br>

### What kind of literary contribution is required for eligibility under the scheme?

The scheme requires applicants to have made substantial literary contributions such as publishing books, winning literary awards, or rendering outstanding service in the field of literature recognized by the respective Sahitya Academy.
<br>

<br>

### What is the age requirement for applicants under this scheme?

The applicant must be at least 60 years old at the time of submitting the application under the "Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers".
<br>

<br>

### Are pensioners allowed to apply for this scheme?

No, individuals who are currently receiving any government, semi-government, or private pension are not eligible to receive financial assistance under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Is there a specific income limit for eligibility under the scheme?

Yes, the applicant’s total annual family income must be less than ₹1,20,000 if residing in a rural area and less than ₹1,50,000 if residing in an urban area, as certified by the Mamlatdar or competent revenue authority.
<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme limited to Gujarati language writers only?

No, the scheme also includes writers from Hindi, Sanskrit, Sindhi, Urdu, and Kutchi languages, as recognized by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy.
<br>

<br>

### What is the nature of financial assistance provided under the scheme?

The financial support provided under the "Scheme to Provide Monthly Financial Assistance to Elderly and Eligible Writers" is recurring on a monthly basis and is intended to function as a basic livelihood support for eligible senior writers.
<br>

<br>

### Will incomplete or partially filled applications be accepted?

No, incomplete or partially filled application forms or those missing mandatory documents will be rejected and not considered for financial assistance under the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### How is the decision made regarding selection of beneficiaries?

The Gujarat Sahitya Academy evaluates each application based on the applicant’s age, financial condition, literary achievements, family responsibilities, and completeness of submitted documentation before approving assistance.
