## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension of ₹20,000/- to the democracy fighters of the state who were imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period (from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977).

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Loktantra Senani Samman Pension (Police Line Yojana)&quot; is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension to democracy fighters of the state who were imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period (25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977).
<br>

## Benefits

**Pension Amount:** ₹20,000/- per month
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
- The applicant must have been imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the emergency period (25 June 1975 to 21 March 1977).

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The application must be submitted to the concerned District Magistrate.
  - Based on the report and recommendation of the District Magistrate, the pension will be sanctioned at the government level after obtaining approval from the Hon’ble Departmental Minister or the Hon’ble Chief Minister.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of this scheme?

### Who is eligible to apply for this pension?

### How much pension is provided under this scheme?

A monthly pension of ₹20,000/- is given to eligible democracy fighters.

### Where should the application be submitted?

The application must be submitted to the concerned District Magistrate of the applicant’s district.
<br>

### Who approves the pension after application submission?

The District Magistrate reviews the application and forwards it with recommendations. Final approval is granted by the Hon’ble Departmental Minister or Hon’ble Chief Minister.

The pension will be transferred directly to the beneficiary’s bank account every month.
