## Brief Description

The "Shramashakthi Special Women's Scheme" is a special scheme for the economic empowerment of widowed, divorced, unmarried women of the minority community.

## Detailed Description

The "Shramashakthi Special Women's Scheme" was launched by the Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka, and implemented by the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation. This is a special scheme for the economic empowerment of widowed, divorced, unmarried women of the minority community. Under this scheme, ₹25,000/- loan and ₹25,000/- subsidy will be provided to undertake various economic activities.

## Benefits

> **Financial Assistance:** 


- A total of ₹50,000/- is provided, comprising ₹25,000/- as a loan and ₹25,000/- as a subsidy.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a female. 
1. The applicant should be a widowed, divorced, unmarried woman. 
1. The applicant should belong to a religious minority community as defined in the Government Order.
1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Karnataka.
1. The applicant's age should be between 18 to 55 years.
1. The annual family income of the applicant from all sources should be below ₹3,50,000/-.
1. The applicant's family member should not be a State/Central Government employee. 
1. The applicant or family members should not have availed of benefits under any other scheme (Excluding the Arivu Scheme) of the KMDCL in the last 5 years.

## Application Process

### Online

Step-01: Applicant need to visits the Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation [official website](https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/home).  
Step-02: Click "[Apply Online](https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/login)" and Enter "Mobile Number" and "Submit".   
Step-03: Enter "Aadhaar Number" Fill in the "Captcha" and Select "Next".
Step-04: Applicant will get OTP in his/her Aadhaar Linked Mobile Number.
Step-05: Enter "OTP" and select "Continue", After that Allow the official notice.
Step-06: After verifying the Aadhaar again fill in the "OTP" and Submit.
Step-07: Select the scheme "Sikhligar’s Community Development Scheme" and fill in the basic details father's name, gender, qualification detail
Step-08: Select "Next" Fill in the address detail and again select "Next".
Step-09: Upload the required document passport-size photo, educational document, income certificate, etc. 
Step-10: Click "Proceed" and Select "Submit Application" and get an Application ID.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is this scheme about?

This scheme provides financial assistance to minority women for economic activities.

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Widowed, divorced, and unmarried minority women aged 18-55 from Karnataka.

### What is the financial assistance provided?

[?]25,000/- loan and [?]25,000/- subsidy.

### What documents are needed for the application?

Project report, caste/minority certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar card, bank passbook, and self-declaration forms.

### How do I apply for the scheme?

Applications can be submitted at KMDCL's official website online.

### Can I apply if I work for the government?

No, applicants or their family members should not be government employees.

### Can I reapply if I received benefits from KMDCL before?

No, you cannot apply if you have availed benefits from KMDCL in the last 5 years, except for the Arivu Education Loan Scheme.

Yes, a surety is needed for the loan application.

### What is the purpose of the subsidy?

The subsidy helps reduce the financial burden of starting an economic activity.

### Is there any application fee?

No, recently no mention of any fee for more information check with KMDCL website.
