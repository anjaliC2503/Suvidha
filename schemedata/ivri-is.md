## Brief Description

The Indian Veterinary Research Institute will award scholarships to MVSc and PhD students who need financial assistance.his provision will be utilized to assist as many students as possible who have good academic record and who are making diligent efforts to pursue higher education.

## Detailed Description

IVRI Institute Scholarship for M.V.Sc. and Ph.D. – The Indian Council of Agricultural Research under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare provides this scholarship for students pursuing M.V.Sc. and Ph.D. programmes at the Indian Veterinary Research Institute. The scholarship is admissible only to candidates admitted through a competitive entrance examination for M.V.Sc. and Ph.D. degree programmes. The scholarship for fresh students is awarded by the Joint Director (Academic) on the recommendation of the Standing Committee on Scholarship, Financial Assistance, and Academic Progress, which considers the merit of each applicant based initially on admission performance and subsequently on academic performance at the Institute.
**Master Programme at IVRI Deemed University**
The candidates for admission to Master’s programme must have Bachelor’s Degree in Veterinary Science in the concerned discipline as specified below as specified by the Veterinary Council of India with a minimum of 60% marks in aggregate (55% for SC/ST or sponsored candidates) or equivalent CGPA.
Admission for MVSc program will be held on the basis of all India Competitive Examination conducted by the Education Division, Indian Council of Agricultural Research, New Delhi. All the candidates including sponsored (in-service) for seeking admission to MVSc programme must have to appear in the written Examination. For more information related to Competitive Examination, please visit the Education Division, ICAR, New Delhi website.(www.icar.org.in.)
**The main subjects/disciplines of study in which various degrees will be offered are as follows:** 
1. 	Animal Biochemistry
2. 	Animal Biotechnology
3. 	Animal Genetics and Breeding
4. 	Animal Nutrition
5. 	Bio-Statistics
6. 	Epidemiology
7. 	Livestock Economics
8. 	Livestock Production and Management
9. 	Livestock Products Technology
10. 	Poultry Science
11. 	Veterinary Bacteriology
12. 	Veterinary Extension Education
13. 	Veterinary Gynaecology and Obstetrics
14. 	Veterinary Immunology
15. 	Veterinary Medicine
16. 	Veterinary Parasitology
17. 	Veterinary Pathology
18. 	Veterinary Pharmacology
19. 	Veterinary Physiology
20. 	Veterinary Public Health
21. 	Veterinary Surgery & Radiology
22. 	Veterinary Virology
Admission for MVSc programme will be held on the basis of all India Competitive Examination conducted by the Education Division, Indian Council of Agricultural Research, New Delhi. All the candidates including sponsored (in-service) for seeking admission to MVSc programme must have to appear in the written Examination. For more information related to Competitive Examination, please visit the Education Division, ICAR, New Delhi.

## Benefits

**For Master Programme**
The amount of the scholarship for the Master’s Degree programme is ₹ 7,560/- per month for 2 years with a contingent grant of ₹ 6,000/- per annum for two years
**For Doctoral Programme**
In the Doctoral programme, it is ₹ 13125/- per month for three years with a contingent grant of ₹ 10000/- per annum for three years.
<br>

## Eligibility

**IVRI Institute Scholarship for M.V.Sc. and Ph.D.**

- The applicant must be admitted to the M.V.Sc. or Ph.D. programme through a competitive entrance examination conducted by the Indian Veterinary Research Institute.
- Candidates must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.00 out of 10.00 at the end of each semester to continue receiving the scholarship.

**For Master’s (M.V.Sc.) Programme**

- The applicant must possess a Bachelor’s Degree in Veterinary Science in the relevant discipline as specified by the Veterinary Council of India.
- Candidates must have secured at least 60% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA.
- A minimum of 55% marks is required for SC/ST and sponsored candidates.

**For Doctoral (Ph.D.) Programme**

- The applicant must possess an M.V.Sc. degree in the relevant discipline from a recognized university in India.
- General/OBC candidates must have a minimum CGPA of:

6.50 out of 10.00 scale, or
3.25 out of 5.00 scale, or
2.60 out of 4.00 scale.

- SC/ST/Physically Challenged/Sponsored candidates must have a minimum CGPA of:

5.50 out of 10.00 scale, or
2.75 out of 5.00 scale, or
2.20 out of 4.00 scale.

- Where only marks are awarded, General/OBC candidates must have at least 60% marks, while SC/ST/Physically Challenged/Sponsored candidates must have at least 50% marks.

**Age Limit**

- The minimum age for Ph.D. candidates is 23 years as on 1st August of the academic session.
- There is no upper age limit.

## Application Process

### Online

**Application URL:** http:/ /www.ivri.nic.in

**Step 1: Apply for Entrance Examination**

1. Candidates seeking admission to the M.V.Sc. programme must apply for the All India Competitive Examination conducted by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research, New Delhi.
1. Candidates applying for the Ph.D. programme must submit an application form through the Indian Veterinary Research Institute website after the admission advertisement is released.
1. Applicants must carefully select the subject for the entrance examination and submit the application before the prescribed last date.

**Step 2: Appear in the Entrance Examination**

1. Eligible candidates must appear in the competitive written entrance examination conducted on an All India basis.
1. The examination consists of multiple-choice questions and is conducted in English only.
1. Ph.D. entrance examinations are conducted at various IVRI campuses and regional stations, including Bareilly, Bangalore, Bhopal, Kolkata, and Palampur.

**Step 3: Merit List, Counseling, and Admission**

1. Admission is granted on the basis of marks obtained in the entrance examination and fulfillment of minimum qualifying marks.
1. For the M.V.Sc. programme, final selection and counseling are conducted by ICAR based on merit.
1. For the Ph.D. programme, the merit list is prepared according to written examination marks, followed by tie-breaking based on MVSc marks, BVSc marks, and age.
1. Selected candidates are admitted to the programme and become eligible for the IVRI Scholarship.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the IVRI Institute Scholarship for M.V.Sc. and Ph.D.?

The scholarship is provided by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare for students pursuing M.V.Sc. and Ph.D. programmes at the Indian Veterinary Research Institute.
<br>

### Who can apply for the IVRI Scholarship?

Candidates admitted to M.V.Sc. or Ph.D. programmes through a competitive entrance examination are eligible to apply for the scholarship.
<br>

### What is the eligibility criteria for the M.V.Sc. programme?

Applicants must possess a Bachelor’s Degree in Veterinary Science with at least 60% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA. SC/ST and sponsored candidates require a minimum of 55% marks.
<br>

### What is the eligibility criteria for the Ph.D. programme?

Candidates must hold an M.V.Sc. degree in the relevant discipline with the prescribed minimum CGPA or percentage as specified by IVRI.
<br>

### How is admission to the M.V.Sc. programme conducted?

Admission is based on the All India Competitive Examination conducted by the Education Division of ICAR, New Delhi.
<br>

### Is it mandatory to appear in the entrance examination?

Yes, all candidates, including sponsored or in-service candidates, must appear in the competitive written examination.
<br>

### What is the scholarship amount for M.V.Sc. students?

Selected M.V.Sc. students receive ₹7,560 per month for two years along with a contingent grant of ₹6,000 per annum.
<br>

### What is the scholarship amount for Ph.D. students?

Ph.D. students receive ₹13,125 per month for three years along with a contingent grant of ₹10,000 per annum.
<br>

### What is the duration of the scholarship?

The scholarship is provided for 2 years for the Master’s programme and 3 years for the Doctoral programme.
<br>

### What minimum academic performance is required to continue the scholarship?

Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.00 out of 10.00 at the end of each semester.
