## Brief Description

This scheme provides financial assistance for setting up ice plants or cold storage units with a minimum capacity of 50 tons, supporting cold chain infrastructure under PMMSY.

## Detailed Description

The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana: Ice Plant/Cold Storage of Minimum 50-Ton Capacity , introduced by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India, aims to provide financial assistance for setting up ice plants or cold storage units with a minimum capacity of 50 tons, supporting cold chain infrastructure under PMMSY. The aim is to reduce post-harvest losses and improve fish quality. This is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme, funded in the ratio of: 50% by the Central Government, and 50% by the State Government. The beneficiary must operate and maintain the plant and ensure supply of ice to fishers at affordable prices.
<br>

## Benefits

- The project cost is ₹150,00,000/-

****   **Subsidy Distribution:**

1. A 40% subsidy is available for the General Category.
1. A 60% subsidy is offered for SC (Scheduled Caste) and Women beneficiaries.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Haryana and possess a valid Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).
1. The applicant must own land or hold a registered lease for a minimum period of 10 years.
1. The applicant must submit an undertaking within the DPR/SCP, accepting full responsibility for operating and maintaining the unit.
1. The applicant must provide ice at affordable prices to fishers.
1. The applicant must submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) or Self-Contained Proposal (SCP) in accordance with PMMSY guidelines.
1. The applicant must hold a valid training certificate in ice plant or cold storage operation and maintenance.
1. The applicant must ensure the land is free from legal issues and must provide a valid land title.

## Documents Required

1. Date of Birth Proof (Voter ID, Aadhaar, Matriculation Certificate)
1. Caste Certificate (SC/Women applicants – issued by Tehsildar/SDM)
1. Agreement Deed with Fisheries Department
1. Training Certificate in cold storage/ice plant operations
1. Land Records (Jamabandi, Fard, Registered Patta with clear title)
1. GST Bills for construction/equipment
1. Photographs of cold storage with beneficiary
1. Bank Account Details and PAN Card
1. DPR/SCP as per PMMSY
1. CA Certificate for GST/IT compliance of bills
1. Bills > ₹50,000 must follow GST/IT/e-way bill norms
1. Clearance Certificates – CLU/NOC/Fire/Pollution (as applicable)
1. NOC from local authorities (Gram Sabha/Municipality)
1. Site Plan/Area Layout Plan

## Frequently Asked Questions

### As a General Category applicant, how much subsidy can I receive for this project?

You are eligible for a subsidy of 40% of the total project cost under this scheme.

### If I belong to the SC category or I’m a woman, what subsidy am I eligible for?

You are eligible for a subsidy of 60% of the project cost if you are from the SC category or are a woman applicant.

### Is it mandatory to have a Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) to apply for the scheme?

Yes, having a valid Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) is a mandatory requirement for application.

### Can I apply if I am setting up the project on leased land?

Yes, you can apply if the land is on a registered lease for a minimum period of 10 years.

### What documents can be submitted as proof of date of birth?

You can submit a Voter Card, Birth Certificate, Aadhaar Card, or Matriculation Certificate as proof of date of birth.

### Do I need to provide any photographs while applying?

Yes, you must submit photographs of the cold storage along with the beneficiary.

### How long will it take for my application to be processed?

The application will be processed within 50 days as per the Right to Service (RTS) time limit.

### Is any local approval or NOC required for the unit?

Yes, you must obtain NOCs from the Gram Sabha or Municipal Corporation/Authority where your unit is located.

### How long will approval take?

Within 50 working days under RTS.

### Do I need to attach a site plan while submitting the application?

Yes, a clear Site Plan or Area Site Plan must be attached along with your subsidy case.
