## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to unemployed youth/women for 7 years by involving them in the transport of food grains from Madhya Pradesh State Civil Supplies Corporation centers to fair price shops. Benefits include an interest subsidy/grant.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Mukhyamantri Yuva Annadoot Yojana” was launched by the Department of Food, Civil Supplies and Consumer Protection, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to provide unemployed youth/women with employment opportunities under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises scheme by making vehicles available for lifting and transporting food grains from the Madhya Pradesh State Civil Supplies Corporation center to fair price shops, thereby enabling them to be self-reliant for 7 years. The scheme provides benefits including an interest subsidy/grant for 7 years and a maximum refund of the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises fee of up to ₹1,25,000/-. The applications for this scheme are accepted online at the Samagra Portal or through the MP Online Kiosk Center.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Interest Subsidy/Grant:** A 3% interest subsidy/grant is provided for 7 years.- **CGTMSE Fee Refund:** A maximum refund of the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises fee of up to ₹1,25,000/- is provided.- **Maximum Vehicle Cost Covered:** The scheme supports the purchase of vehicles with an approximate cost of up to ₹25,00,000/-.- **Payment for Transportation:** Payment for the transportation of food grains and handling expenses is provided as per the fixed rate for the sector.
##### ***Disbursement*** 

- **Mode of Payment:** The payment for transportation of food grains and handling expenses for the sector will be paid directly into the bank account of the beneficiary monthly by the Madhya Pradesh State Civil Supplies Corporation.- **Penalty Provision:** If the food grain is not transported on time to the designated fair price shop, 33% of the total monthly transport rent/fare on the concerned fair price shop may be deducted from the beneficiary's transport rent/fare. This penalty deduction will only occur after a hearing has been granted by the designated Supply Officer/Collector.
##### ***Conditions***

- The beneficiary must ensure the purchase of a vehicle within the capacity range of 7.5 Metric Tons (+/- 10%).
- The beneficiary must utilize the vehicle for lifting and transporting allocated food grains from the Madhya Pradesh State Civil Supplies Corporation center to the fair price shops for 7 years.
- The beneficiary must obtain a bank loan, as the Central Bank of India is designated to provide the loan for vehicle purchase.

<br>

## Exclusions

- The individuals having a maximum annual family income exceeding ₹12,00,000/-.
- The individuals involved in any criminal activity or who have a criminal background.
- The individuals currently benefiting from any other self-employment scheme.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a native resident of the rural or urban local body of the concerned district of Madhya Pradesh.
- The applicant must be aged between 18 and 45 years.
- The applicant must have passed the 8th standard in educational qualification.
- The applicant must possess a Heavy Motor Vehicle driving license to operate the vehicle.
- The applicant must have clearance from the bank for obtaining the loan (defaulter status must be 'No').
- The applicant must not be a Government servant or pensioner.
- The applicant must not have annual family income exceeding ₹12,00,000/-.
- The applicant must not be involved in any criminal activity or have a criminal background.
- The applicant must not be currently benefiting from any other self-employment scheme.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Visit the [Samagra portal](https://samast.mponline.gov.in). Click on “[Create Profile](https://samast.mponline.gov.in/portal/register)”. Fill in the required basic details such as Name, Mobile Number, Gender, Email ID, Date of Birth, etc. Click on “Next.”
**Step 2:** To verify your basic details, re-enter your Date of Birth and Mobile Number, enter the Captcha Code, and click on “Profile Banaye”. An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter the OTP to verify your profile.
**Step 3:** To complete Aadhaar e-KYC, enter your Aadhaar Number, Registered Mobile Number, and Captcha Code. Click on “Send OTP” and verify using the Aadhaar OTP to complete the e-KYC process.
**Step 4:** Navigate to the online form of the scheme. In the form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size. Ensure that all documents are clear, legible, and in the accepted file format before uploading.
**Step 5:** Information regarding the selected truck manufacturer/company, model, and maximum cost is also available on the departmental portal, and the applicant may select any truck model from the authorized list.
**Step 6:** Carefully review all the information provided and the documents uploaded. Make any necessary corrections. Click the "Submit" or "Apply" button to submit your application. You'll receive a confirmation message.
****A fee of ₹100/- is stipulated for submitting the required affidavit (Declaration of non-Government servant, no criminal case, and true information) on a stamp paper.***
<br>

##### ***Loan Processing through the Bank***
The loan application should be processed through the bank, or the applicant may seek pre-sanction for the loan themselves, as the loan for vehicle purchase will be provided by the Central Bank of India, which will review the suitability for the loan.
##### ***Post-Application Process***
**Step 1:****** ***Eligibility Assessment and Loan Processing***

- Upon receipt of the application, the Department will conduct an examination of the applicant's general eligibility. 
- The Central Bank of India will conduct the testing of the application's suitability for the loan.

**Step 2:****** ***Selection and Allotment***

- If more than one eligible application is received for a single sector (area), the beneficiary will be selected through a lottery system (drawing of lots) in a transparent manner by the State Level Committee and the Food, Civil Supplies, and Consumer Protection Department. 
- Once the loan is sanctioned, the department will facilitate the loan approval process.

**Step 3:****** ***Completion of Documents/Deficiencies (If Required)***

- If the District Supply Officer/Collector finds any document or deficiency in the submitted application, the applicant will be immediately informed. 
- The applicant must ensure the completion of the required documents/deficiencies online within 7 working days from the date of receiving the intimation.

## Documents Required

##### ***Documents Required During Application***

- Applicant’s Latest Photograph And Signature.
- Proof Of Educational Qualification.
- Proof Of Age.
- Truck/Vehicle Model Details.
- Caste Certificate.
- Proof Of Native Residence And Address (Must be a certificate issued by Tehsildar or a higher authority of the Revenue Department).
- Copy Of Project Report.
- Copy Of Bank Loan Pre-Sanction (If Applying Via Bank).
- Heavy Motor Vehicle Driving License.
- Self-Declaration Of Not Being A Government Servant Or Pensioner.
- Affidavit Of No Criminal Case Or Background And Declaration That Given Information Is True On A Stamp Paper Of ₹100/-.
- Consent To Share Equity/Initial Contribution Amount (If Applicable).
- Self-Declaration Regarding Vehicle Selection.

##### ***Documents Required Post-Application (Deficiency Completion)***

- Document/Certificate Required By District Supply Officer/Collector (Must be supplied within 7 working days).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### If an individual is currently receiving government support under a different self-employment initiative, are they still permitted to apply for this particular program?

No, the applicant must not be currently benefiting or nominated under any other self-employment scheme.
<br>

### What is the stipulated maximum limit on the total annual family income an applicant can have to qualify for financial benefits under the scheme?

The maximum annual family income of the applicant must not exceed ₹12,00,000/-.
<br>

### What is the specific permitted tonnage or capacity range of the commercial transport vehicle that beneficiaries are authorized to purchase for operations?

The vehicle must be purchased within the capacity range of 7.5 Metric Tons plus or minus 10%.
<br>

### What is the fundamental goal of this government initiative, which provides vehicles for lifting and transporting essential public goods?

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to unemployed youth and women under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises scheme. It involves transporting food grains from the Madhya Pradesh State Civil Supplies Corporation center to fair price shops for 7 years.
<br>

### When submitting the declaration stating that one is not a government employee and has no criminal record, what monetary value is required for the stamp paper?

The affidavit, which includes the self-declaration of not being a Government servant or pensioner and having no criminal case, must be submitted on a stamp paper of ₹100/-.
<br>

### Are individuals currently working as government servants or receiving a government pension eligible to participate in this employment initiative?

No, the applicant must submit a self-declaration stating they are neither a Government servant nor a pensioner.
<br>

### What is the ceiling on the total maximum cost of the specific commercial transport vehicle that will be considered eligible for financing under this support program?

The maximum allowable cost of the commercial transport vehicle sanctioned for purchase under this scheme is ₹25,00,000/-.
<br>

### What specific class or type of heavy-duty motor vehicle driving license must a successful applicant possess to operate the transport vehicle?

The applicant must possess a valid Heavy Motor Vehicle driving license.
<br>

### What is the maximum age limit for individuals seeking to apply for entrepreneurial opportunities requiring the operation of a commercial vehicle?

The applicant must not exceed 45 years of age.
<br>

### Where must an applicant legally reside, specifically relating to their rural or urban local area, to be considered eligible for this program?

The applicant must be a native resident of the concerned district's rural or urban local body.
<br>

### What is the mandatory minimum age requirement that an individual must satisfy in order to successfully submit an application for this opportunity?

The applicant must be at least 18 years old. The calculation of the applicant's age for application consideration will be based on February 28, 2023.
<br>

### If the application documents are found to be incomplete or deficient by the District Supply Officer or Collector, how much time is granted for corrections?

The applicant will be informed immediately and must ensure the completion of the required documents or deficiencies online within 7 working days from the date of receiving the intimation.
<br>

### Are there any specific requirements regarding the applicant's existing loan status or financial default history with any recognized lending institution?

The applicant must have bank clearance, meaning they must not be classified as a loan defaulter.
<br>

### Is there a mandatory minimum educational prerequisite that all applicants must satisfy before they can successfully submit their documents?

The applicant must have passed the 8th standard in educational qualification.
