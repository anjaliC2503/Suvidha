## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide one of the state's best driver "Sarathi Shri" award, which includes a cash prize, a certificate of appreciation, and a trophy.

## Detailed Description

The "Motor Vehicle Driver-Conductor Welfare Scheme- Sarathi Shri Award" has been launched by the Transport Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. Under this scheme, one of the state's best drivers will be honored with the "Sarathi Shri" award, which includes a cash prize, a certificate of appreciation, and a trophy. The state government will present this award at a special ceremony. 
<br>

## Benefits

**Award Components**

- Cash Prize: ₹1,00,000/-
- Appreciation Certificate
- Trophy

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be a registered transport vehicle driver or conductor.
1. The applicant should possess a valid commercial driving license or conductor's license issued in Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant’s name should be registered on the Samagra Portal.
1. The applicant should be actively engaged or registered as a transport driver or conductor.
1. The applicant should not be a government employee.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

> **Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official website: [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on the option “Register Family” or “Register Member”.
**Step 3:** Enter the Aadhaar number and other required information.
**Step 4:** Enter the OTP received on the registered mobile number.
**Step 5:** Carefully fill in all the required details and upload the necessary documents.
**Step 6:** Enter the captcha code and click on “Submit Application.”
> **Offline Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the nearest Lok Seva Kendra, MP Online Kiosk, or Common Service Center (CSC).
**Step 2:** Provide the required documents and necessary information to complete the registration process.
> **After Samagra Registration:**

**Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://transport.mp.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/yojna_20150001.pdf) (Page No. 10) from the official website. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District or Regional Transport Office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme's objective is to provide comprehensive welfare and social security to drivers and conductors.
<br>

### Which department launched the scheme?

The Transport Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### When was the scheme launched?

The scheme was launched in 2015.
<br>

### Who can apply for the scheme?

Registered transport vehicle drivers and conductors of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Is Samagra registration mandatory?

Yes, Samagra registration is mandatory.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable across the state?

Yes, it is applicable throughout Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Are women drivers eligible?

Yes, women drivers and conductors are eligible.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable to unorganised workers?

Yes, it primarily targets unorganized transport workers.
<br>

### Are government employees eligible?

No, government employees are not eligible.
<br>

### Can applications be rejected?

Yes, if eligibility conditions are not fulfilled.
<br>

### Is there an appeal mechanism?

Yes, appeals can be submitted to the Divisional Deputy Transport Commissioner.
