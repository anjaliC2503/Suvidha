## Brief Description

Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme "Safai Karmakar Hetu Aavashyak Upakaran Sahayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 02 September 2015.

## Detailed Description

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Safai Karmakar Hetu Aavashyak Upakaran Sahayata Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 02 September 2015. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing required equipment for their work.

## Benefits

1. Registered sanitation workers will receive a grant of ₹ 700/- (Indian Rupees seven hundred only) to purchase essential equipment such as gum boots, gloves, masks, and aprons.
1. Assistance amount will be provided every year.

## Eligibility

1. Applicant must be registered with the State Social Security Board in any district of Chhattisgarh.
1. Applicant should not receive assistance from any other parallel scheme of the State Government.
1. Applicant should be between 18 and 60 years.

## Application Process

### Online

**Unregistered Applicant :**

1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on "[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)" under "Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board".1. Enter the following details

   1. Select Group Name : "Asangathit Marmakaar Mandal"
   1. Select Service : "Asangathit Shramik Panjikaran"
   1. What do you want to do :  "Aavedan"
   1. Click next.

1. Check the eligibility criteria for registration in Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board and click next. 
1. Fill out the online "Registration form".
1. Submit.
