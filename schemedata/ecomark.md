## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage the demand for environment-friendly products that cause lesser adverse impacts on the environment. It provides an Ecomark label for products meeting specified environmental criteria to eligible producers, manufacturers, importers, and exporters.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Ecomark Scheme of India (ECOMARK)" was launched by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India. The scheme provides an "Ecomark" label for products that meet specified environmental criteria with respect to resource consumption and environmental impacts, enabling consumers to make informed purchase decisions. The applicant must submit an application for the grant of Ecomark to the Central Pollution Control Board in Form 1. The scheme is implemented by the Central Pollution Control Board. The applications for this scheme are accepted online through the Ecomark web portal.
<br>

## Benefits

- The scheme grants an Ecomark label to the product.
- The Ecomark granted to the product shall be valid for a period of three years or until a change in the Ecomark criteria for the product, whichever is earlier.

##### ***Conditions***

- Every holder of Ecomark shall submit an annual report to the Central Pollution Control Board online through the web portal on or before the 31st May of every year.
- Every person who has been granted an Ecomark in respect of a product shall affix on such product an Ecomark label granted under these rules.
- The holder of Ecomark shall allow the Central Pollution Control Board or the registered agencies to undertake such inspection and verification as may be considered necessary for ensuring compliance.
- The holder of the Ecomark shall grant access to the premises in which the product concerned is produced or kept to the Central Pollution Control Board and the registered agency.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a producer, manufacturer, exporter, or importer of products specified under the First Schedule of the rules.
- The applicant must have a license or a certificate of conformity of Indian Standards granted under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016.
- The applicant must comply with the mandate of the Quality Control Orders issued by the Central Government, if applicable.
- The applicant must fulfill the criteria for the grant of Ecomark specified under the corresponding entries of the First Schedule.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

##### **Step 1:****** ***Accessing the Ecomark Portal***
The applicant must access the [centralized online Ecomark web portal](https://cpcb.nic.in/ecomark-licence/) developed by the Central Pollution Control Board for making applications and tracking Ecomark certification.
##### **Step 2:****** ***Submitting the Application Form***
The applicant must fill and submit an application for the grant of Ecomark for a product which complies with the specified conditions to the Central Pollution Control Board.
##### **Step 3:****** ***Verification of the Product***
On receipt of the application, the Central Pollution Control Board shall, by itself or through a designated Verifier, verify whether the product complies with the required environmental conditions.
##### **Step 4:****** ***Preparation and Submission of the Report***
After making such verification, the Central Pollution Control Board or the Verifier shall prepare a report and submit it within a period of sixty days from the date of such verification.
##### **Step 5:****** ***Grant of the Ecomark Certificate***
On receipt of the verification report, if the Central Pollution Control Board is satisfied that the product complies with the conditions, it may grant the Ecomark to the product.

## Documents Required

- Application for Ecomark Certification for a Product (Form 1).
- License or Certificate of Conformity of Indian Standards Granted Under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016.
- Consent Clearance as per the Provisions of Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974.
- Consent Clearance as per the Provisions of Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981.
- Authorisation Under Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 and Rules Made Thereunder (If Required).
- Documentary Evidence by Way of Certificate or Declaration for the Collection Pay Back System for Used Batteries (Required for Automotive Lead-Acid and Dry Cell Batteries).
- Documentary Evidence With Respect to the Compliance of Regulation Under Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954 and Drugs and Cosmetic Act, 1940 (Required for Plastic Products).
- Documentary Evidence on Compliance of the Provisions Related to Noise Level and Occupational Health Under the Provisions of Factories Act, 1948 (Required for Textiles).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When my business manufactures automotive lead-cid or dry cell batteries, what specific documentary evidence must I supply to prove I handle post-consumer waste appropriately?

You must provide a certificate or declaration proving you have established a collection pay back system for used batteries.
<br>

### I understand that various government mandates apply to manufacturing quality. What specific central government directives must I ensure my product complies with before seeking this environmental certification?

Your product must strictly adhere to the mandates of any Quality Control Orders issued by the Central Government, if they are applicable to your specific goods.
<br>

### Once I successfully obtain the environmental label for my product, what recurring yearly obligation must I fulfill online to maintain my good standing and compliance?

You are required to submit an annual report to the Central Pollution Control Board through the web portal on or before the 31st May of every succeeding year.
<br>

### I am interested in applying for this environmental label, but I am unsure about the required business profile. What specific roles must an individual or company fulfill to be considered eligible for the certification?

The applicant must be either a producer, manufacturer, exporter, or importer of the specific products listed under the First Schedule of the rules.
<br>

### If my company produces plastic products, particularly those meant for food or cosmetics, what specific regulatory acts must I provide documentary evidence of compliance for?

You must provide evidence demonstrating compliance with the regulations under the Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954, and the Drugs and Cosmetic Act, 1940.
<br>

### Before submitting my application for the environmental label, what specific documentation regarding product standardization must I have already acquired from the national standards body?

You must possess a valid license or a certificate of conformity to Indian Standards granted under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016.
<br>

### After receiving the official environmental certification for my manufactured goods, what action must I physically take regarding the packaging or the product itself to show my compliance?

Every person granted the certification must appropriately affix the designated label directly onto the approved product.
<br>

### Environmental regulations strictly monitor water usage and discharge. Which specific statutory document must I obtain regarding water pollution control to satisfy the documentation requirements for this application?

You are required to submit a valid Consent Clearance as mandated by the provisions of the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974.
<br>

### Following the approval of my product's certification, what access must I provide to the authorities or their designated agencies when they wish to verify my ongoing compliance?

You must grant full access to the premises where the concerned product is produced, kept, or stored to the Central Pollution Control Board and their registered verification agencies.
<br>

### I am reviewing the criteria required to qualify for the label. Where exactly can I find the specific environmental standards that my product must fulfill to be eligible?

Your product must fulfill the exact environmental criteria outlined under the corresponding entries of the First Schedule of the rules.
<br>

### In addition to air and water pollution clearances, what broader environmental statutory authorization might I need to attach to my application file, if it is applicable to my industry?

You must include an authorisation under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, and the corresponding rules made thereunder, if your specific operations require it.
<br>

### Once I am successfully granted the environmental certification for my product, how long will this approval remain valid before it either expires or requires a renewal process?

The granted label remains valid for a period of three years, or until there is a change in the specified environmental criteria for that product, whichever occurs earlier.
<br>

### Manufacturing operations often involve emissions. To prove that my factory complies with emission standards, which exact consent clearance document must be included in the submission?

You need to provide a Consent Clearance in accordance with the provisions of the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981.
<br>

### As a manufacturer operating within the textile sector, what specific proof of compliance regarding working conditions and auditory environments must I supply during the application process?

You must submit documentary evidence demonstrating compliance with the provisions related to noise level and occupational health under the Factories Act, 1948.
