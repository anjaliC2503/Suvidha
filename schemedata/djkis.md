## Brief Description

The "Digital J&K Internship Scheme" is an opportunity for the students to secure first-hand and practical work experience. It also aims at active participation in the learning process through experimentation and putting into practice the knowledge acquired in the studies.

## Detailed Description

The "Digital J&K Internship Scheme" was launched by the J&K e-Governance Agency of the Information Technology Department. An internship is an opportunity for the students to secure first-hand and practical work experience. It also aims at active participation in the learning process through experimentation and putting into practice the knowledge acquired in the studies.
<br>

> **Duration of Internship:**

The minimum duration of the internship shall be 03 months. 
<br>

> **Place of Internship:**

The internship will be offered at Jammu and Kashmir.
<br>

## Benefits

**Stipend****:**

- A token stipend of ₹10,000/- per month would be paid to an Intern, subject to satisfactory performance. 

<br>

**Certificate of Internship****:**

- The J&K e-Governance Agency will issue certificates to the Interns upon the completion of the internship.

## Eligibility

1. The student should be studying at recognized universities in India.
1. The student should have secured at least 60% marks in the last held degree or pursuing B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech (Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)/Computer Science (CS)/ IT/ Electronics & Communications (EC)/) M.Sc. (CS/IT/) MCA/DOEACC ‘B’ level. 
1. Students in the last semester or the final year will be eligible to apply.

## Application Process

### Online

***Registration Process:***
**Step 01:** The applicant visits the [official website](https://jakega.jk.gov.in/index.html) and clicks the [notification](https://internshipjakega.jk.gov.in/) to apply. 
**Step 02:** Click on "New User" and fill in the basic details Name, Email ID, Mobile Number.
**Step 03:** Generate the Password and enter the Captcha. 
<br>

***Application Process:***
**Step 01:** Log in to the profile and enter your email ID and password
**Step 02:** Click the Apply internship scheme and enter the address, qualification, reference detail
**Step 03:** Upload the domicile certificate, signature, resume, etc.
**Step 04:** Click on the "Submit" button and your application number will be generated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which organization is offering the Digital J&K Internship Scheme?

The internship is offered by the Jammu and Kashmir e-Governance Agency (JaKeGA).

### Who can apply for the Digital J&K Internship Scheme?

Students from recognized universities in India who have secured at least 60% marks in their last degree or are pursuing degrees such as B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech in relevant fields, M.Sc. (CS/IT), or MCA/DOEACC 'B' level are eligible to apply.

### What is the minimum duration of the internship?

The minimum duration of the internship is 3 months.

### Is there any stipend for the interns?

Yes, a token stipend of [?]10,000.00 per month will be paid to an intern, subject to satisfactory performance.

### What kind of projects will interns work on during the internship?

Interns will work on projects such as e-Unnat, Auto Appeal System, e-Service platform, e-office, and dashboard application development.

### What skills are required for the internship?

Skills required include programming (C#, Dot Net, HTML, CSS, JavaScript), database administration (SQL, Oracle, Postgres SQL), creating and consuming APIs, and mobile application development.

### What are the general rules interns need to follow during the internship?

Interns must follow the rules and regulations applicable to department employees, maintain confidentiality, and adhere to work plans and schedules assigned by authorities.

### Can interns present their work in academic forums?

Yes, interns may present their work to academic bodies and at seminars/conferences with prior permission from the department, ensuring no confidential information is revealed.

### How can interested applicants apply for the internship?

Interested and eligible applicants need to apply online through the URL: https://jakega.jk.gov.in.

### What is the selection process for the internship?

Interns will be shortlisted and selected by the committee/authorities and may include personal or online interviews if necessary.

### Will the internship guarantee employment with JaKeGA?

No, the internship is neither an employment nor an assurance of employment with JaKeGA.

### What happens if an intern's performance is unsatisfactory?

If an intern's performance is unsatisfactory, they may be advised by the department to discontinue the project work before the completion of the internship term.

### What documentation is required from interns during the internship?

Interns need to submit a monthly report on the work assigned, necessary qualification certificates, and a character certificate by the competent authority to JaKeGA.

### Can an intern terminate their internship before the completion of the term?

Yes, if the intern decides to disengage, they should provide a 2-week prior notice.

### What should an intern do with departmental assets upon termination of the internship?

Upon termination, the intern must hand over any papers, equipment, or other assets given to them by JaKeGA during their project work.
