## Brief Description

"Uchch Shiksha Puraskar Yojana" aims to encourage and motivate students from weaver families to pursue higher education. ₹20,000/- is provided to talented students from weaver families who get admission in first year of Engineering/Medical/CA/MBA courses in any government college in Chhattisgarh.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 2012-13, the scheme "Uchch Shiksha Puraskar Yojana" by the Chhattisgarh State Handloom Development and Marketing Cooperative Union Limited, Raipur aims to encourage and motivate talented students from weaver families to pursue higher education. The scheme provides financial incentives to students from weaver families who secure admission to professional courses in government colleges. It specifically targets students who are children of members of Primary Weaver Cooperative Societies affiliated with the State Handloom Union. The scheme is implemented by the Chhattisgarh State Handloom Development and Marketing Cooperative Union Limited, Raipur to create awareness and competitive spirit among children of weavers engaged in handloom weaving industry mostly established in rural areas.

## Benefits

- A financial incentive of ₹20,000/- is provided as an encouragement award to eligible students.
- The financial assistance is provided only for the first year of the course.

## Eligibility

- The student must be from a family of weavers who are members of Primary Weaver Cooperative Societies affiliated to the State Handloom Union, Raipur.
- The student must be a resident of Chhattisgarh.
- The student's mother/father must be a regular member of a weaver cooperative society.
- The student must have secured admission in the first year of Engineering/Medical/CA/MBA course in any government college.
- More than 50% of the parent's income should be from weaving.

_*Weaver family refers to husband, wife, and their children._

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take print of the [prescribed format](https://www.cgapexhandloom.org.in/sites/default/files/page-documents/Bunkar-Schlorship_high_edu_0.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents within the prescribed period (if any), to the Primary Weaver Cooperative Society.
**Step 4:** The Primary Weaver Cooperative Society will verify the details, complete their formalities, add their remarks, and forward the application to the concerned District Handloom Office. The District Handloom Office will compile all received applications.
**Step 5:** A selection committee consisting of the Deputy/Assistant Director of the District Handloom Office and a nominated President/Representative of a weaver society in the district will review the applications. The committee will organize a meeting to select eligible members and verify the applications.
**Step 6:** After verification, the selection committee will send their recommendations to the Managing Director, Chhattisgarh State Handloom Development and Marketing Cooperative Union Limited, Raipur.
**Step 7:** The Managing Director has the authority to take appropriate action if any information is found to be false. After final approval, the incentive amount will be disbursed to the selected students.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I am a student from a weaver family seeking higher education support. How much financial incentive can I receive through this scheme?

You can receive a financial incentive of [?]20,000/- as an encouragement award. This amount is provided only for the first year of your course after you submit the verified photocopy of your admission fee receipt along with the prescribed application form.

### When was this educational incentive scheme for weaver families launched and who implements it?

The scheme was launched in 2012-13 and is implemented by the Chhattisgarh State Handloom Development and Marketing Cooperative Union Limited, Raipur. It aims to encourage and motivate talented students from weaver families to pursue higher education.

### I want to apply for the higher education incentive. What professional courses are covered under this scheme?

The scheme covers professional courses including Engineering, Medical, Chartered Accountancy (CA), and Master of Business Administration (MBA). You must secure admission in the first year of any of these courses in a government college to be eligible.

### My father is a weaver. Am I eligible for the education award if I get admission to a private college for engineering?

No, you are not eligible. The scheme specifically mentions that you must secure admission in a government college. Private college admissions are not covered under this scheme regardless of the course you're pursuing.

### Is the financial assistance provided for the entire duration of my professional course or just for a specific period?

The financial assistance is provided only for the first year of your course. The scheme does not mention any extension or renewal of the incentive for subsequent years of your professional course.

### My mother is a member of a weaver cooperative society. Does this make me eligible for the higher education incentive?

Yes, you are eligible if your mother is a regular member of a weaver cooperative society that is affiliated to the State Handloom Union, Raipur. The scheme specifically mentions that the student's mother or father must be a regular member.

### My uncle is a member of a weaver cooperative society. As his nephew, am I eligible to apply for the higher education incentive?

No, you are not eligible. The scheme defines a weaver family specifically as husband, wife, and their children. As a nephew, you do not fall under this definition, so you cannot apply for the incentive.

### Who decides whether I am eligible for the higher education incentive after I submit my application?

A selection committee consisting of the Deputy/Assistant Director of the District Handloom Office and a nominated President/Representative of a weaver society in your district will review your application. This committee verifies applications and selects eligible students.

### My father earns income from weaving and other agricultural activities. Am I still eligible for the higher education incentive?

You are eligible only if more than 50% of your father's income comes from weaving. The document specifically mentions that the parent's income should be more than 50% from weaving to qualify for this incentive.

### Where should I submit my completed application form for the higher education incentive program?

You should submit your completed application form to the concerned Primary Weaver Cooperative Society where your parent is a member. The society will verify your details, complete their formalities, and add their remarks before forwarding it.

### What information do I need to provide about my educational background in the application?

You need to provide details about the board examination you passed, the total marks/percentage you obtained, and the name of the school from which you passed the examination. You must also attach a verified photocopy of your marksheet.

### I am from a weaver family but my parents are not members of any cooperative society. Can I still apply for the incentive?

The scheme requires that your mother/father must be a regular member of a weaver cooperative society that is affiliated with the State Handloom Union, Raipur to be eligible.

### What action will the District Handloom Office take after receiving my application?

The District Handloom Office will compile all received applications and organize a selection committee meeting. The committee will review your application, verify the details, and if found eligible, they will recommend your name with Form-2 to the Managing Director.

### Do I need to submit my actual marksheet and fee receipt or will photocopies suffice?

You need to submit verified photocopies, not the originals. The scheme specifically mentions that you must submit a verified photocopy of your marksheet and admission fee receipt along with the application form.
