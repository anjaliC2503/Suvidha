## Brief Description

NCCS engages young scholars as Research Associates  to contribute in development of Telecom Security eco system with in India by providing an opportunity to them to work in areas like Telecom Security Standard development, Lab recognition and Security Certification of telecom products.

## Detailed Description

National Centre for Communication Security (NCCS) responsible for the implementation of ComSeC Scheme part of MTCTE. This involves development of Indian Telecom Security Assurance Standards, Security Test labs recognition and Security certification of telecom equipment and associated processes. This mandate is of significant importance considering the looming telecom/network/cyber threats globally and the threat these vulnerabilities pose to National Security and Privacy to people of the Nation. NCCS work in security domain to strengthen the security standardization eco-system in India is highly specialized in nature and unique in its mandate. It also needs to interact with multilateral agencies like 3GPP, ITU, APT, IEEE, IETF, NIST and ETSI and other national/ international standardization bodies for active collaboration &amp; development of country specific telecom security standards for India. It develops expertise to imbibe latest technologies and provides technical support/ policy inputs to DOT to advice other ministries in respects of Telecom Security Domain. 
**Positions**

   1. The number of positions available depends upon the vacancies. Please refer to the RA Notification for vacancy details.
   1. The total number of RAs at NCCS at any point of time can not be more than 18.
   1. NCCS reserves the right not to fill up all or any of the positions, if it so desires.

**Period of engagement**
Initially for a period of 2 years, extendable by one more year, on the recommendations of Performance Appraisal Committee. 
**Brief Description of Work and Responsibilities**

   1. The RAs will be primarily engaged for in-depth study, formulation, operationalization and assessment of various security standards ITSARs preparation, TSTPs preparation/programmes/ projects/ policies evaluation of test reports/DUTs etc. related to emerging ICT areas such as Telecom, Network and Cyber Security, 4G/5G Networks, Virtualisation Function &amp; Operating Systems Expertise, Cloud Security, Source Code Analysis, Cryptography, Internet of Things, Smart Networks, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, new developments in the fields of Mobile Technologies and any associated field deemed fit by NCCS. They will also be associated in preparation of contributions for various international standardization organizations such as International Telecommunication Union (ITU) Study Groups, APT, IEEE, IEC etc. Further, they will also be engaged in various works emerging from ComSec Scheme, new National Digital Communication Policy (NDCP) - 2018
   1. The RAs would The RA would be attached with one of the Divisions of the NCCS and required to provide high quality work. Depending on the requirements of NCCS and candidate’s personal skills, RAs can be rotated to other divisions within NCCS. 

**Place of posting**
The place of posting will be NCCS, City Telephone Exchange, Sampangirama Nagar, Bengaluru 560027.
<br>

## Benefits

> Remuneration


1. A consolidated amount of ₹ 75,000/- per month on pro rata basis, all-inclusive (including taxes), will be paid to every RA. There shall be an annual increase of ₹ 7,500/- subject to satisfactory performance.
1. RA shall submit execute bond as prescribed in the Advertisement.

## Eligibility

1. The candidate must have a Bachelor’s Degree in Electronics / Communications / Telecommunications / Information Technology / Computers / Electrical Engineering or a related field with at least 60% marks or equivalent CGPA.
   1. The age limit is up to 28 years for Graduates (BE/BTech), 30 years for Postgraduates (MTech/MS), and 35 years for PhD candidates.
   1. The applicant must be an Indian National.
   1. The preference will be given to candidates having a valid GATE score, postgraduate degree with minimum 60% marks, PhD in a relevant field, work experience of one year or more, or an internship of six months or more with NCCS/TEC.
   1. The candidates who decline the job offer after selection will be barred from applying for the next two NCCS notifications.

**Note :** A candidate who declines to join after the offer of appointment is issued by NCCS shall be debarred from appointment in 2 subsequent notifications issued by NCCS.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Fill out the prescribed application form (Annexure-I) and attach self-attested copies of your academic and experience certificates along with your CV and area of work interest.
**Step 2:** Submit the completed application form either by post or in person to:
ADET (SC &amp; HQ), Room 301, National Centre for Communications Security, 2nd Floor, City Telephone Exchange, Sampangirama Nagar, Bangalore – 560027.
**Step 3:** Make sure to submit your application before the last date mentioned in the notification. Late or incomplete applications will be rejected.
<br>

**Applications are invited based upon vacancy at NCCS ( usually twice a year). Currently, no applications are being invited. Please follow** [**NCCS website**](https://nccs.gov.in/) **for next Research Associate (RA) engagement notification.** [**Click here**](https://nccs.gov.in/public/latest_updates/RA%20Notification%20Signed%20(1).pdf) **for last RA notification.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the full name of NCCS? Which department manages this organization?

The full name of NCCS is "National Centre for Communication Security". It is a subordinate office of Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications, Government of India.

### What are the areas of activity of NCCS?

National Centre for Communication Security (NCCS) responsible for the implementation of ComSeC Scheme part of MTCTE. 
For implementation of ComSeC scheme NCCS has 3 divisions i.e., Security Assurance Standards (SAS), Security Lab Recognition  (SLR) and Security Certification (SC). For more details, visit the NCCS website www.nccs.gov.in. NCCS is entrusted with the task of establishment of Centre of Excellence in Telecom Security Domain. NCCS is also involved in supporting activities for evaluation of hardware and the integrity of the supply chain of Telecom Network Equipment.

### Does Research Associate comes under category of job a NCCS?

Research Associateship is neither a job nor an assurance of a job with the NCCS. Selected candidate has to enter into a contract with NCCS.
<br>

### Is the contract period extendable?

Yes, it is extendable by one year, on the recommendations of Performance Appraisal Committee.

### What are the other terms and conditions of the scheme?

Refer to the latest RA notification published on the NCCS website for more details regarding general conditions of the contract.

### What is the methodology for shortlisting of candidates?

Refer to the Annexure II of RA notification published on NCCS website.

### What is the minimum educational qualification required?

The candidate must have a Bachelor’s Degree in a relevant field such as Electronics, Communications, IT, Computers, or Electrical Engineering with at least 60% marks or equivalent CGPA.
<br>

### What is the age limit for applying?

The age limit is up to 28 years for Graduates, 30 years for Postgraduates, and 35 years for PhD candidates.
<br>

### Who can apply for this opportunity?

Only Indian Nationals are eligible to apply.
<br>

### Is a GATE score mandatory?

A GATE score is not mandatory but candidates with a valid GATE score will be given preference.
<br>

### Will higher qualifications get preference?

Yes, candidates with postgraduate degrees or a PhD in relevant fields will be given preference.
<br>

### Are internships considered for eligibility?

Yes, candidates with an internship of six months or more with NCCS/TEC will be given preference.
