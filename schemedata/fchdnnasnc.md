## Brief Description

The "Free Caste Hostel" scheme by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides a scholarship of ₹1,230 per month for boys and ₹1,270 per month for girls for 10 months.

## Detailed Description

The "Free Caste Hostel" scheme by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides a scholarship of ₹1,230 per month for boys and ₹1,270 per month for girls for 10 months. The scheme benefits students from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic tribes studying in local educational institutions.

## Benefits

- A scholarship of ₹1,230 per month for boys.
- A scholarship of ₹1,270 per month for girls.
- The scholarship is given for 10 months.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a student belonging to the De-Notified, Nomadic, or Semi-Nomadic tribes.
1. The applicant should be studying in a local educational institution.
1. The applicant should apply for admission before the new academic session.
1. The applicant should secure a vacant seat, as new admissions depend on availability.
1. The applicant should have a higher percentage in the last passed examination for priority admission.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Free caste hostel Scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance to students of de-notified, nomadic, and semi-nomadic tribes studying in local educational institutions.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Students belonging to de-notified, nomadic, and semi-nomadic tribes who are enrolled in local educational institutions are eligible.

### What is the amount of scholarship provided?

The scheme provides [?]1,230 per month for boys and [?]1,270 per month for girls for 10 months.

### How can students apply for this scheme?

Eligible students must apply using the prescribed application form before the start of the academic session.

### Is there a renewal process for existing beneficiaries?

Yes, renewal is automatic for resident students, and they do not need to reapply.

### Are there any academic requirements for admission?

Admission is based on merit, with preference given to students who have secured higher marks in the previous academic year.

### Can new students apply if seats are full?

No, new students can only apply if there are vacant seats in the hostel, ashram, or community welfare center.

### What documents are required for application?

Applicants need to submit caste certificates, proof of enrollment in an educational institution, and previous academic records.

### Is there any income limit for eligibility?

No, the scheme does not have an income limit for applicants.

### What is the duration of the scholarship?

The scholarship is provided for 10 months in an academic year.
