## Brief Description

The "Educational Assistance" scheme provides financial support of ₹10,000 per year to children of registered construction workers who are studying in the 11th or 12th standard. The benefit applies to a maximum of two children per worker and extends to the wife of a registered male worker.

## Detailed Description

The Educational Assistance to the Children of the Registered Worker Studying in 11th and 12th Standard scheme was launched by the Maharashtra Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWW), Labour Department Maharashtra. The scheme provides financial assistance ₹10,000 per year to children of registered construction workers who are studying in the 11th or 12th standard. The benefit applies to a maximum of two children per worker and extends to the wife of a registered male worker.

## Benefits

- Financial assistance of ₹10,000 per year for children studying in 11th and 12th grades.

<br>

> **Note:** Benefit applies to up to two children and the wife of a registered worker.

## Eligibility

1. The children's parents or guardians should be registered under the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
1. The child/wife must be studying in 11th or 12th standard.
1. Only children and spouse of registered construction workers are eligible to get educational assistance.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://mahabocw.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/SCHEME_EDUCATION_FORM.pdf) from the MBOCWW official website. 
**Step-2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step-3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Labor Commissioner/Government Labor Officer.
**Step-4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Documents Required

- Passport-size Photo.
- Aadhaar Card.
- Identity Card of Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
- Bank Passbook.
- MarkSheet for 11th or 12th standard.
- Proof of Residence (One of Aadhaar Card/Passport/Driving Licence/Ration Sheet/Last Month's Electricity Bill/Gram Panchayat Certificate).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the amount of educational assistance provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides [?]10,000 per year to children studying in 11th or 12th standard.

### How many children can benefit from this scheme?

A maximum of two children per registered worker can benefit.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Registered construction workers and their children or spouses are eligible.

### Is this scheme available for students outside Maharashtra?

No, only children residing in Maharashtra are eligible.

### What documents are required to apply?

Aadhaar card, 11th or 12th class mark sheet, and proof of residence.

### Where can I submit the application?

Applications can be submitted at the nearest Labour Commissioner's office.

### Is there any minimum percentage required for eligibility?

No, there is no minimum percentage required, but the student must be enrolled in 11th or 12th standard.

### Is the scheme available for higher education beyond 12th standard?

No, the scheme only covers students in the 11th and 12th standards.

### What if the child switches schools or boards?

As long as the child remains in 11th or 12th standard and is enrolled in a recognized institution in 
Maharashtra, they remain eligible.

### How long does it take for the assistance to be processed?

Processing times may vary, but you can follow up with the concerned Labour office after submission.
