## Brief Description

Supply of School Uniform and Tie belt to SC/ST Boys/ Girls Students of Std I to VIII studying in Govt Schools

## Detailed Description

Provision of a complete school uniform (shirt, pants, skirt tie, belt) to  SC/ ST Boys and all General Girls of classes I to VIII studying in government schools. Standardized uniform scheme to promote discipline, removing social distinctions based on attire.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

<br>

1. Fosters a sense of identity, equality and discipline among students.
2. Significantly reduces school absenteeism and improves performance by eliminating the financial barrier of purchasing clothes.
3. Promotes regular school attendance by supporting foundational needs
	
<br>

## Eligibility

1.	The applicant should be a student.
2.	The applicant should be studying in class 1st to 8th.
3.	The applicant should be studying in a government school in UT of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.
<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

1. No separate application is required.
1. Eligible students enrolled in government schools are automatically covered under the scheme.

### Which students are eligible?

<br>

### How many uniforms are provided?

Two pairs of uniforms per student.
<br>

### Is the scheme for both boys and girls?

Yes, it benefits both boys and girls.
<br>

### What is the purpose of this scheme?

To encourage regular attendance and continuity in education among school students.
