## Brief Description

The "Fellowship Scholarship Scheme" by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial help to students by granting an annual monetary award to those who secure 70% or more marks in Class 10th and pursue higher education.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Fellowship Scholarship Scheme&quot; by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students who secure 70% or more marks in Class 10th and are pursuing higher secondary or higher education. The primary objective is to encourage education among the Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Physically Backward Classes, and other eligible categories by providing monetary support based on their merit in Class 11th and subsequent studies.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Annual Scholarship:** Financial assistance is provided every year to eligible students.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must have secured at least 70% marks in Class 10th.
- The applicant must be pursuing higher secondary school (Class 11th onwards), college, or higher education.
- The applicant’s personal/ family annual income must not exceed ₹2,50,000/-.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Fellowship Scholarship Scheme"?

The "Fellowship Scholarship Scheme" is a financial aid program launched by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, to provide annual monetary assistance to students who secure 70% or more in Class 10 and continue their higher education.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

A student who has scored at least 70% marks in Class 10th, belongs to SC, ST, Physically Backward Class, or other eligible categories, and is pursuing higher secondary, college, or higher education can apply for the scheme.
<br>

### Is there any income limit for applying?

Yes, the applicant’s personal annual income and family annual income should not exceed ₹2,50,000/- to be eligible for the scheme.
<br>

### What type of financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides scholarships to eligible students based on their merit in Class 11th and subsequent studies.
<br>

### How often is the scholarship amount disbursed?

The scholarship amount is disbursed annually after verification of the student’s application and academic performance.
<br>

### Are students from all categories eligible?

Yes, students from SC, ST, Physically Backward Classes, and other eligible categories can apply for this scheme.
<br>

### Is there any minimum marks requirement?

Yes, the student must have secured at least 70% marks in Class 10 to qualify for the scholarship.
<br>

### How can a student apply for this scheme?

Students can apply through the [Digital Gujarat](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/) portal.
<br>

### Which documents are mandatory for applying?

Documents such as Aadhar Card, Caste Certificate (if applicable), Class 10 Mark Sheet, School Leaving Certificate, and a Passport Size Photograph etc are mandatory for applying.
<br>

### What is the geographical coverage of this scheme?

The scheme is applicable across the state of Gujarat and covers all eligible students within the state.
<br>
