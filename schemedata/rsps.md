## Brief Description

The scheme "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to ensure financial security to retired state-level medal-winning sportspersons aged above 50 years through monthly pension assistance.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to recognize and support the contributions of state-level sportspersons who have won medals in national-level sports events by offering them a financial pension after the age of 50 years. The scheme ensures dignified post-retirement sustenance for these individuals, thereby encouraging younger generations to pursue sports professionally. The scheme is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** A Pension of ₹3,000/- per month.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be a male.
  - The applicant should be a sportsperson.
  - The applicant should be aged above 50 years at the time of application.
  - The applicant should have won medals in national-level sports competitions representing the state.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant should download the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the Commissioner of Youth Services and Cultural Activities Office.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme"?

The primary objective of the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" is to offer financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension to retired male sportspersons of Gujarat who have brought recognition to the state by securing medals in national sporting events.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme"?

Any male sportsperson who is a resident of Gujarat, has attained the age of 50 years or more, and has won a medal while representing the state in a national-level sports competition is eligible to apply under the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How much monthly pension is provided under the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme"?

Under the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme", a fixed financial assistance of ₹3,000/- per month is provided to each eligible retired sportsperson as a means to support their livelihood after the age of 50 years.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" available to sportspersons from all sports disciplines?

Yes, the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" is applicable to all sportspersons who have achieved national-level recognition by winning medals, irrespective of the type of sport or discipline they were associated with.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can female sportspersons apply for the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme"?

As per the current guidelines of the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme", the benefit is applicable only to male sportspersons, and therefore, female sportspersons are not eligible to apply under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Does the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" have any income limit criteria for eligibility?

The "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" does not impose any restrictions related to the individual or family income of the applicant, and sportspersons from any economic background can avail the benefits if other eligibility criteria are met.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is the implementing authority for the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme"?

The "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" is implemented by the Sports Authority of Gujarat under the guidance of the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" available throughout Gujarat or restricted to specific districts?

The "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme" is available across the entire state of Gujarat and can be availed by any eligible sportsperson residing in any district of the state without regional restrictions.
<br>

### Is there a fee required to apply for the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme"?

There is no fee or cost associated with applying for the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme", and the application form has been made available free of charge.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to be submitted while applying for the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme"?

While applying for the "Retired Sportsperson Pension Scheme", the applicant must submit an affidavit and a domicile certificate proving Gujarat residency.
<br>
