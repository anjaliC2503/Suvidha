## Brief Description

The objective of the scheme is to encourage spice growers to adopt hygienic drying practices by using Silpaulin Sheets, thereby improving the quality of spices.

## Detailed Description

Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom: Reintroduced Subcomponent – Supply of Silpaulin Sheets (Nos.) is implemented by the Spices Board, India under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.The objective of the scheme is to encourage spice growers to adopt hygienic drying practices by using Silpaulin Sheets, thereby improving the quality of spices.
The scheme is being implemented in accordance with the modalities and guidelines approved by the Competent Authority under the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

## Benefits

- 50 % (Gen) and 75% (SC/ST) at maximum of  ₹ 2000 (Gen) and  ₹ 3000/- (SC/ST), avg cost per sheet estimated at  ₹ 4000/-.
- Spices growers having land holdings from 0.20 to 8 ha are eligible to avail assistance.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. All spice growing States and regions in India with preference for Chilli/turmeric crops.
2. Spices Board will arrange centralized purchase and supply to the farmers by collecting the non- assistance portion from the farmers and subsequent payment to the approved supplier.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Supply of Silpaulin Sheets scheme?

The scheme aims to encourage spice growers to adopt hygienic drying practices using Silpaulin Sheets to improve the quality of spices.
<br>

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Spice growers with land holdings from 0.20 hectares to 8 hectares are eligible to apply.
<br>

### Which crops are given preference under the scheme?

Preference is given to chilli and turmeric growers.
<br>

### In which areas is the scheme available?

The scheme is available in all spice-growing States and regions of India.
<br>

### What financial assistance is available for General Category applicants?

General Category applicants are eligible for 50% assistance, subject to a maximum of ₹2,000 per Silpaulin Sheet.
<br>

### What financial assistance is available for SC/ST applicants?

SC/ST applicants are eligible for 75% assistance, subject to a maximum of ₹3,000 per Silpaulin Sheet.
<br>

### What is the estimated cost of one Silpaulin Sheet?

The average estimated cost of one Silpaulin Sheet is ₹4,000.
<br>

### Is proof of land ownership or possession required?

Yes. Applicants must submit valid land documents or a No Objection Certificate (NOC), wherever applicable.
