## Brief Description

The scheme “Stipends to Students of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes for Technical and Vocational Courses after SSC” by the Social Justice and Empowerment Dept., Gujarat, supports education for Socially and Educationally Backward Classes students by providing a monthly stipend of ₹400/-.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Stipends to Students of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes for Technical and Vocational Courses after SSC” by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote inclusive education by financially supporting students from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes pursuing technical and vocational education after SSC. The initiative offers a stipend of ₹400/- per month to aid the academic progress and reduce the dropout rate among underprivileged youth. The scheme is implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department. It specifically benefits those enrolled in professional or Government ITI courses and belonging to families with an annual income not exceeding ₹6,00,000/-.

## Benefits

- **Stipend:** A monthly stipend of ₹400/- is given to Socially and Educationally Backward Classes students for one year of recognized professional and Government ITI courses.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
- The applicant should belong to Socially and Educationally Backward Classes.
- The applicant should be a student who has passed the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) examination.
- The applicant should be enrolled in a recognized professional or Government Industrial Training Institute (ITI) course.
- The applicant’s individual and family income should not exceed ₹6,00,000/- per annum.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Stipends to Students of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes for Technical and Vocational Courses after SSC"?

The scheme "Stipends to Students of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes for Technical and Vocational Courses after SSC" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat aims to provide monthly financial assistance to students belonging to socially and educationally backward classes.

### Who can apply for the stipend under this scheme?

Any student who belongs to the Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC) category, has successfully passed the SSC examination, and is currently enrolled in a recognized professional or Government ITI course in the state of Gujarat is eligible to apply for the stipend under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What is the amount of financial assistance provided through this scheme?

Under this scheme, each eligible student receives a fixed monthly stipend of ₹400/- as financial assistance to support their education in technical and vocational courses after SSC.
<br>

<br>

### What is the total duration for which the stipend is granted?

The stipend is granted for a maximum duration of one academic year and is specifically meant to support students enrolled in one-year technical or vocational courses that are officially recognized.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit prescribed for the applicant or their family?

Yes, the scheme stipulates that both the individual and family annual income of the applicant must not exceed ₹6,00,000/- in order to be eligible for receiving the benefits under this stipend program.
<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme applicable throughout Gujarat?

Yes, the scheme is implemented across the entire state of Gujarat, and eligible students from any district within the state can apply for and benefit from the stipend.
<br>

<br>

### Which courses are covered under the scheme for receiving the stipend?

The scheme covers recognized professional courses and Government ITI courses, and students enrolled in any of these programs are eligible to receive the monthly stipend.

### What is the process to apply for this scheme for eligible students?

The student must visit the official [Digital Gujarat](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/frmMain1.aspx) portal, register or log in using valid credentials, select the scheme, fill out the application form, upload the required documents, and submit the application online.

### What are the required documents that must be submitted while applying for the scheme?

Applicants are required to submit several documents including a valid caste certificate (as proof of SEBC status), a current income certificate, SSC mark sheet, the last year’s academic mark sheet, fee receipt of the enrolled course, and either a bank passbook copy or cancelled cheque for verification of bank details.
<br>

<br>

### What should a student do if there is an issue with the application?

In case of any issues with the application, the student should contact the [helpdesk ](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/SchemPortal/ContactUs.aspx)on the Digital Gujarat portal.
