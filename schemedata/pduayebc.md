## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) by offering financial aid up to ₹1,20,000/- for house construction. This scheme also ensures that Economically Weaker Sections receive support in securing shelter.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana (Developing Caste Welfare) (EBC)&quot; is a housing scheme by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to support Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) by offering financial aid up to ₹1,20,000/- for house construction. This scheme also ensures that the Economically Weaker Sections receive support in securing shelter.

## Benefits

- Financial Assistance of up to ₹1,20,000/- for house construction.

## Eligibility

- Applicant must belong to the Economically Backward Class (EBC).
- Applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
- Applicant's family income should not exceed ₹6,00,000/- per annum.
- Applicant must own land or have been allotted land/house under a government poverty housing scheme.

## Documents Required

1. Aadhaar Card.
1. Bank Passbook/Cancelled Cheque.
1. Caste Certificate.
1. Income Certificate.
1. Land Ownership Aadhaar.
1. Land Ownership Acknowledgment Form.
1. Title Deed/Sanad Deed (as applicable).
1. Map of land signed by Talati-cum-Mantri.
1. Allotment order of land/house (if received under a poverty scheme).
1. Permission letter for building construction.
1. Certificate from the Circle Inspector for housing assistance.
1. Certificate from City or Gram Panchayat Talati-cum-Mantri.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the primary objective of the "Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana (Developing Caste Welfare) (EBC)"?

The "Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana (Developing Caste Welfare) (EBC)" has been introduced by the Government of Gujarat with the primary objective of providing financial assistance to individuals belonging to the Economically Backward Classes (EBC) to help them construct their own houses and thereby improve their standard of living.

### Which department is responsible for implementing this housing scheme?

The scheme is implemented and overseen by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of the Government of Gujarat, which works towards the welfare and upliftment of socially and economically disadvantaged communities.
<br>

<br>

### Who can apply for the benefits under this scheme?

Any individual who is a permanent resident of Gujarat, belongs to the Economically Backward Class category, and has an annual family income not exceeding ₹6,00,000 is eligible to apply for the benefits under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What kind of financial support is provided under the scheme?

Under this scheme, the eligible beneficiaries are entitled to receive financial assistance of up to ₹1,20,000 from the state government for the purpose of constructing their own residential houses.
<br>

<br>

### Is this scheme available throughout the state of Gujarat?

Yes, the Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana is applicable and available to eligible applicants residing in any part of the state of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### Is the application process for this scheme online or offline?

The entire application process for the Pandit Dindayal Upadhyay Awas Yojana is conducted online through the official eSamajKalyan portal of the Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### What is the official website where eligible individuals can apply for this scheme?

Eligible individuals can submit their application for the scheme by visiting the official website: [https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in) and completing the registration and application process as required.

### Is there any application fee for submitting the form under this scheme?

Applicants are required to submit several documents including their Aadhaar card, caste certificate, income certificate, bank passbook or a cancelled cheque, proof of land ownership or allotment, building permission, and certificates from relevant authorities such as the Talati-cum-Mantri and Circle Inspector.
<br>

<br>

### Does the scheme provide any assistance for renovation or only for new construction?

The financial assistance provided under this scheme is strictly intended for the purpose of constructing new houses and does not cover renovation or repair of existing structures.
<br>

<br>

### Where can one find more official information and updates about the scheme?

For more detailed information, official guidelines, and updates about the scheme, individuals can visit the website of the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of Gujarat at [https://sje.gujarat.gov.in](https://sje.gujarat.gov.in/).
