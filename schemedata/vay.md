## Brief Description

The Department of Higher Education, Government of Madhya Pradesh has created this new scholarship scheme available for candidates belonging to the General categories.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Vikramaditya Yojana&quot; is a scholarship scheme offered by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. This scheme is presented by the Department of Higher Education, which means it is intended to help students who are pursuing further education after successfully completing Class 12. These scholarship opportunities are available only for candidates belonging to the General category, which is one of the most important aspects of this scheme.
<br>

## Benefits

- Annual financial assistance of a maximum of ₹ 2,500/-

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must belong to the General category.
1. He/she must have scored at least 60% or more marks in the Class 12 examination.
1. The annual family income of the applicant should not exceed INR 1,20,000 (for higher education) and INR 54,000 (for graduation).
