## Brief Description

The scheme aims to recognize and encourage students for their outstanding research work in the field of Vegetable Science. Under this, a cash award of ₹20,000/- is provided annually to the selected students.

## Detailed Description

The “Dr. Y.V. Singh Award for Best Thesis in Vegetable Science” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to recognize and encourage students for their outstanding research work in the field of Vegetable Science.
<br>

## Benefits

**Award Amount:** ₹20,000/- per year.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student of Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The applicant must have submitted a thesis in the field of Vegetable Science at the university.
   1. The award is granted for a period of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the selected students receive the payments.
