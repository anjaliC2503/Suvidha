## Brief Description

It aims to simplify and expedite the process of residential property map creation and approval for all landowners in Haridwar district through mobile app.

## Detailed Description

The “Map Approval through UDAY App” is implemented by the Haridwar - Roorkee Development Authority under Housing Department, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to simplify and expedite the process of residential property map creation and approval for all landowners in Haridwar district through mobile app.
<br>

## Benefits

All landowners in Haridwar district can avail themselves of the benefit of map approval through the UDAY mobile app.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. All landowners in the Haridwar district of Uttarakhand are eligible.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

Landowners in Haridwar district have been provided with the UDAY Mobile App to simplify the process of map preparation and approval. Under this system, residential maps are prepared by the authority’s architects free of charge, and support is provided to assist landowners in getting their maps approved.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can benefit from this scheme?

All landowners in Haridwar district are eligible to use the UDAY App for map creation and approval.
<br>

### Is there any fee for getting the map prepared?

No, residential maps are prepared free of charge by the authority’s architects.
<br>

### How can a landowner access the UDAY App?

The UDAY App can be downloaded on mobile devices, and landowners can register to access the map creation and approval services.
<br>

### Does the scheme provide support during map approval?

Yes, landowners receive assistance from the authority to ensure their maps are properly approved.
<br>

### Who prepares the residential maps?

The maps are prepared by architects appointed by the Haridwar-Roorkee Development Authority.
<br>
