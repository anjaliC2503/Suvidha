## Brief Description

The scheme aims to enhance the nutritional health of children aged 6 months to 6 years and pregnant and lactating mothers by providing fortified flavored milk in Anganwadi centers across 20 districts in the state.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Doodh Sanjivani Scheme&quot; launched by the Women and Child Development Department, Gujarat, is a welfare initiative designed to improve the nutritional status of children and women in tribal and underdeveloped communities. The scheme benefits children aged 6 months to 6 years and pregnant and lactating mothers registered at Anganwadi centers. It covers 138 tribal and developing communities in 20 districts and is implemented through Anganwadi centers.

## Benefits

- **Nutritional Support for Children:** 100 ml of fortified flavored milk is provided 5 days a week to children aged 6 months to 6 years.  - **Nutritional Support for Mothers:** 200 ml of fortified flavored milk is provided 2 days a week to pregnant and lactating mothers.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a child aged 6 months to 6 years or a pregnant or lactating mother.
  - The applicant must be registered at an Anganwadi center.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Visit the nearest Anganwadi center.
**Step 2:** Register yourself or your child with the Anganwadi center.
**Step 3:** Provide the required documents.
**Step 4:** Avail of the benefits during scheduled distribution days.

## Documents Required

1. Birth Proof for child.
   1. Identity proof (e.g., Aadhaar card, ration card).
   1. Pregnancy proof for pregnant mothers (e.g., medical certificate).
   1. Any other document (if required).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the "Doodh Sanjivani Scheme"?

The scheme aims to improve the nutritional status of children and mothers in tribal and underdeveloped communities.

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Children aged 6 months to 6 years and pregnant or lactating mothers registered at Anganwadi centers are eligible for the scheme.

### What type of milk is provided under the scheme?

Fortified flavored milk is provided under the scheme to enhance nutritional intake.

### How much milk do children receive under the scheme?

Children aged 6 months to 6 years receive 100 ml of fortified flavored milk 5 days a week.

### How much milk do pregnant and lactating mothers receive?

Pregnant and lactating mothers receive 200 ml of fortified flavored milk 2 days a week.

### Is there any cost to avail of the benefits of the scheme?

No, the milk is provided free of cost to all eligible beneficiaries.

### Where can beneficiaries access the benefits of the scheme?

Benefits can be accessed at Anganwadi centers in the participating districts.

### Does the scheme have an income limit for eligibility?

No, there is no income limit for eligibility under the scheme.

### How can beneficiaries register for the scheme?

Beneficiaries must register themselves or their children at their nearest Anganwadi center.

### Can children who are not registered at an Anganwadi center benefit from the scheme?

No, only children registered at Anganwadi centers are eligible for the scheme.

### In how many districts is the Doodh Sanjivani Scheme implemented?

The scheme is implemented in 20 districts of the state, covering 138 tribal and developing communities.

### What should beneficiaries do if they need more information about the scheme?

Beneficiaries can contact their nearest Anganwadi center or the Child Development Scheme Officer for further information.
