## Brief Description

The objective of the scheme is to mobilize and support farmers to use bio-pesticides and bio- agents for plant health management through distribution of bio-pesticides and other IPM tools and through sale of Sprayers and Plant Protection Equipment to encourage organic crop production.

## Detailed Description

The “Plant Protection including Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture &amp; Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to mobilize and support farmers to use bio-pesticides and bio- agents for plant health management through the distribution of bio-pesticides and other IPM tools and through the sale of Sprayers and Plant Protection Equipment to encourage organic crop production.

## Benefits

> **Pattern of Assistance:**


- Distribution of bio-pesticide and other IPM tools to farmers.
- Sale of Sprayers and Plant Protection Equipment.

> **Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If any):**


1. Free distribution of Bio-pesticides and other IPM tools.
1. 50% subsidy sale on Plant Protection Equipment.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Meghalaya.
1. The applicant should be a bonafide farmer engaged in agriculture.

## Application Process

### Offline

> **Application Process:**

**Step 01:** The eligible bonafide farmers can apply/write an application on plain paper.
**Step 02:** Along with the application, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 03:** Submit the duly filled and signed application along with the relevant documents at the nearest office of the Agriculture Development Officer of a CD Block/Agriculture Circle.
> **Mode of Selection of Beneficiary(s):**

The bonafide farmers having sufficient available cultivable land shall be selected on a first come first served basis.
> **Whom to Contact:**


   1. District Agriculture Officers of the concerned districts.
   1. Sub-Divisional Agricultural Officers of the concerned sub-divisions.
   1. District Training Officers, Tura/Shillong.
   1. Agricultural Development Officers of the concerned Community and Rural Development Blocks.
