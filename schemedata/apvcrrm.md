## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial assistance to farmers in Gujarat for setting up vegetable trellises (Mandap) for raw, semi-ripe, and ripe vegetables, with varying support for General, SC, and ST farmers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance for Planting Vegetable Crops for Raw and Ripe Mandap&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to offer financial support to farmers across Gujarat for constructing vegetable trellises (mandaps) using wooden/bamboo supports and GI/plastic wires.

## Benefits

> **Raw Mandap (Tomato/Chilli and other vegetables):**



    - General Farmers: 50% subsidy, up to ₹26,000/ha.
    - SC/ST Farmers: 75% subsidy, up to ₹39,000/ha.


> **Semi-ripe Mandap (Vine vegetables):**



    - General Farmers: 50% subsidy, up to ₹40,000/ha.
    - SC/ST Farmers: 75% subsidy, up to ₹60,000/ha.


> **Ripe Mandap (Vine vegetables):**



    - General Farmers: 50% subsidy, up to ₹80,000/ha.
    - SC/ST Farmers: 75% subsidy, up to ₹1,20,000/ha.


> **Other Support Details:**



    - Maximum area eligible: 2 hectares per farmer.
    - Construction material: 1600 wooden/bamboo poles per hectare, 400 kg GI wire (12-18 gauge) or plastic wire.
    - Recommended spacing: 2.50 x 2.50 meters.


<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a farmer.
   1. The applicant must be residing in Gujarat.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)

**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.

**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Assistance for Planting Vegetable Crops for Raw and Ripe Mandap" scheme?

The purpose of the scheme is to provide financial support to farmers in Gujarat for constructing trellises (mandaps) to improve the cultivation of vegetable crops.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Any farmer who is a resident of Gujarat and owns agricultural land is eligible to apply for this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme available to farmers from all caste categories?

Yes, the scheme is open to all farmers, including those from the General, Scheduled Caste (SC), and Scheduled Tribe (ST) categories.
<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum land area for which assistance can be availed under the scheme?

Farmers can receive assistance for a maximum of 2 hectares of land per account.
<br>

<br>

### What types of mandaps are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers raw mandaps (for crops like tomato and chilli), semi-ripe mandaps, and ripe mandaps used for vine vegetable cultivation.
<br>

<br>

### What is the financial assistance provided to SC/ST farmers for ripe mandaps?

SC/ST farmers are eligible for 75% of the cost, with a maximum assistance of ₹1,20,000/- per hectare for ripe mandaps.
<br>

<br>

### What support is provided to General category farmers for raw mandaps?

General category farmers receive 50% of the cost, with a maximum of ₹26,000 per hectare for raw mandaps.
<br>

<br>

### What materials are required for constructing a mandap under the scheme?

Farmers are required to use wooden or bamboo supports (1600 pieces per hectare) and GI wire (12–18 gauge, 400 kg/ha) or plastic wire for the construction.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit for eligibility under this scheme?

No, there is no income limit for either the individual or the family to be eligible for this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What spacing is recommended for setting up a raw mandap?

A spacing of approximately 2.50 x 2.50 meters is recommended for setting up a raw mandap.
<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant apply for this scheme?

Applicants can apply on the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)portal to avail the benefits of the scheme.

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

The required documents include the applicant's Aadhar card, a 7/12 land extract, and a bank passbook or cancelled cheque.

### Who should the applicant contact for help or more information about the scheme?

The applicant should contact the nearest agriculture office or visit the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/)portal for more information.
