## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to ex-servicemen and their legal dependents by sponsoring them for contractual posts. Under this scheme, eligible candidates are sponsored for various departmental positions based on demand and their qualifications.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Sponsorship for Employment&quot; is implemented by Uttarakhand Purv Sainik Kalyan Nigam Limited (UPNL) under the Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide employment opportunities to ex-servicemen and their legal dependents by sponsoring them for contractual posts. Under this scheme, eligible candidates are sponsored for various departmental positions based on demand and their qualifications.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, employment opportunities are provided to ex-servicemen and their legal dependents by sponsoring them for contractual posts through outsourcing.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
   1. The applicant must be an ex-serviceman (Army, Navy, or Air Force), their legal dependent, or a widow of an ex-serviceman (Veer Nari).

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

- Firstly, the eligible candidates (ex-servicemen and their legal dependents) fill out the online application/enrollment form for the desired post on the UPNL website: [www.upnl.co.in](https://upnl.co.in/default.aspx), based on their district, qualifications, and preferred location.  - Based on the district-wise demand for posts received from various departments and the eligibility/qualification criteria for those posts, UPNL forwards the names of enrolled candidates with the relevant qualifications to the concerned department for selection, following district-wise and priority order of nomination.
  - As per the demand received from the department, UPNL sends the names of six enrolled candidates for each vacant post to the concerned department for consideration.
  - The final selection of candidates is made by the department according to its rules, and the information is subsequently communicated to UPNL.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply under this scheme?

Ex-servicemen, their legal dependents, and widows.

### Is this a permanent job?

No, positions are on a contractual basis through outsourcing.

### How many candidates are sent for one vacancy?

Six candidates per post are sponsored.

### Is any fee required for enrollment?

No official fee is mentioned for enrollment on the UPNL website.

### Can civilians apply for these posts?

No, only ex-servicemen and their legal dependents.

### How are candidates selected for the job?

The department selects based on merit, qualifications, and internal rules.

### Can I apply offline?

No, applications must be submitted online via the UPNL portal.

### Are Veer Naris given any preference?

Yes, Veer Naris are eligible as primary beneficiaries.

### What is the official website for application?

The applicant should visit the official UPNL portal: [www.upnl.co.in](https://www.upnl.co.in/).
