# Promotion of Tiatr Through Academic Activities

## Brief Description

The scheme aims to foster interest in Tiatr among students and enthusiasts through academic activities like talks, symposia, seminars, and workshops. It covers activity expenditures, honorarium for resource persons, and provides materials, benefiting educational institutions and individuals.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Promotion of Tiatr Through Academic Activities" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to create interest in Tiatr among students of educational institutions and tiatr lovers in villages and cities. The scheme provides for the creation of awareness about tiatr, its song, and music through academic activities such as talks, symposia, seminars, and workshops. These activities, which must have at least 20 participants, can be organized by the Tiatr Academy of Goa alone or in collaboration with other institutions or individuals, with the Academy covering various expenditures and providing necessary materials. The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa on a 'First Come First Serve' basis, and applications for this scheme are accepted as per the availability of funds.
<br>

## Benefits

• **Awareness Creation:** Awareness about Tiatr, its song, and music will be created through academic activities such as talks, symposia, seminars, and workshops.
• **Resource Person Honorarium:** Resource persons will be paid an honorarium, as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa, for every session, with the amount dependent upon the distance from their residence to the venue.
• **Expenditure Coverage:** The Tiatr Academy of Goa will cover the expenditure incurred on Resource Persons, refreshments, and food (lunch or dinner) served to participants. Additionally, any other miscellaneous expenditure made in organizing the activity by an institution or individual by way of collaboration will be taken care of by the Tiatr Academy of Goa.
• **Provision of Materials:** The Tiatr Academy of Goa will provide essential materials, including a backdrop, certificates for the participants, and stationery.
• **Documentation Coverage:** The Tiatr Academy of Goa will document the Symposia, Seminar, or Workshop, and the expenditure incurred on this documentation will be borne by the Tiatr Academy of Goa.
<br>

> **Conditions**

_*Prior approval will be required from the Tiatr Academy of Goa before finalizing the resource persons, if the activity is organized in collaboration with others._
_*The cost of refreshments or food to be served must be verified and approved by the Tiatr Academy of Goa before finalization._
_*The scheme will be implemented strictly on a ‘First Come First Serve’ basis._
_*Applications will be considered, and benefits disbursed as per the availability of funds._
<br>

## Eligibility

• The applicant must be an educational institution or an individual interested in collaborating with the Tiatr Academy of Goa to organize academic activities for promoting Tiatr.
• The applicant must ensure the number of participants for conducting the Symposia, Seminar, or Workshop shall be at least 20.
• The applicant must obtain prior approval from the Tiatr Academy of Goa before finalizing resource persons, if the activity is organized by others in collaboration.
• The applicant must get the cost of refreshments or food to be served verified and approved by the Tiatr Academy of Goa before finalizing them.
<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the copies of the mandatory documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
*The application will be considered on a 'First Come First Serve' basis and is subject to the availability of funds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What type of organization or person is eligible to apply for support in organizing activities to promote this traditional art form?

Educational institutions and individuals interested in collaborating with the Tiatr Academy of Goa can apply. The applicant must be willing to work with the Academy to organize academic activities such as talks, symposia, seminars, and workshops for promoting Tiatr.
<br>

### Is there a minimum number of people who must attend the educational activities I want to organize about this performing art?

Yes, you must ensure that at least 20 participants attend the symposium, seminar, or workshop. This minimum participation requirement is mandatory for all activities organized under this scheme.
<br>

### What expenses will be covered by the Academy if I organize an educational event about this traditional performing art form?

The Tiatr Academy of Goa will cover expenditure on resource persons, refreshments, food (lunch or dinner) for participants, and any miscellaneous costs. They will also bear the cost of documentation and provide materials like backdrop, certificates, and stationery.
<br>

### Do I need to get approval before deciding who will be the speakers or experts for my educational event?

Yes, if your activity is organized in collaboration with others, you must obtain prior approval from the Tiatr Academy of Goa before finalizing the resource persons. This approval is mandatory before confirming any speakers or experts.
<br>

### What compensation will the speakers and experts receive for participating in the educational activities I organize?

Resource persons will receive an honorarium as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa for every session. The amount will depend upon the distance from their residence to the venue of the activity.
<br>

### Can I decide independently what food and refreshments to serve at my educational event about this art form?

No, you must get the cost of refreshments or food to be served verified and approved by the Tiatr Academy of Goa before finalizing them. Prior approval is required for all catering arrangements and their associated costs.
<br>

### What documents do I need to submit along with my application for organizing educational activities about this performing art?

You need to submit a self-attested copy of your Aadhaar Card. This is the only document specifically mentioned as required for the application process under this scheme.
<br>

### How are applications processed and selected for support under this educational initiative for promoting traditional performing arts?

The scheme is implemented strictly on a 'First Come First Serve' basis by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. Applications are considered and benefits are disbursed based on the availability of funds at the time of application.
<br>

### What materials and resources will be provided by the Academy for my educational event about this traditional art form?

The Tiatr Academy of Goa will provide essential materials including a backdrop for the event, certificates for all participants, and necessary stationery. These materials are provided as part of the scheme benefits.
<br>

### Will the Academy help document and record the educational activities I organize about this performing art tradition?

Yes, the Tiatr Academy of Goa will document the symposia, seminar, or workshop you organize. All expenditure incurred on this documentation process will be borne entirely by the Academy.
<br>

### What types of educational activities are supported under this scheme for promoting awareness of traditional performing arts?

The scheme supports academic activities such as talks, symposia, seminars, and workshops. These activities are designed to create awareness about Tiatr, its songs, and music among students and enthusiasts.
<br>

### Can I organize these educational activities independently or do I need to work with the Academy?

You must collaborate with the Tiatr Academy of Goa to organize these activities. The Academy can organize activities alone or in collaboration with institutions or individuals, but collaboration with the Academy is essential.
<br>

### What is the primary goal of organizing these educational activities about this traditional art form in educational institutions?

The scheme aims to create interest in Tiatr among students of educational institutions and tiatr lovers in villages and cities. It focuses on generating awareness about this traditional performing art through structured academic activities.
<br>

### Is funding guaranteed for all applications submitted for organizing educational events about this performing art tradition?

No, applications are accepted and benefits are disbursed as per the availability of funds. Funding is not guaranteed and depends on the financial resources available with the Tiatr Academy at the time of application.
<br>

### Who can benefit from participating in the educational activities organized under this scheme for promoting traditional performing arts?

Educational institutions, individuals, and Tiatr lovers can benefit from these activities. The scheme is designed to reach students in educational institutions as well as enthusiasts in villages and cities who are interested in this art form.
<br>
