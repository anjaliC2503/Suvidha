## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide cash subsidies for school uniforms to students from Nursery to Class XII in government and aided schools. Benefits include annual payments ranging from ₹1,100/- to ₹1,500/-, ensuring financial support for all eligible students.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Subsidy for School Uniforms to the Students" by the Department of Education (DoE), Government of National Capital Territory (GNCT) of Delhi, aims to provide financial assistance to students for purchasing school uniforms, ensuring equitable access to education. Under this scheme, a cash subsidy is provided annually to students from Nursery to Class XII, irrespective of gender or income, to alleviate the financial burden on families. The scheme covers students enrolled in government and aided schools, with varying amounts allocated based on class levels. The subsidy amounts range from ₹1,100/- to ₹1,500/- per annum, depending on the class. Eligibility is universal for students in the specified classes, with no income or gender-based restrictions. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Education, GNCT of Delhi.

## Benefits

> **Cash Subsidy Amounts**


- Nursery to Class 5th: ₹1,100/- per annum.
- Class 6th to 8th: ₹1,400/- per annum.
- Class 9th to 12th: ₹1,500/- per annum.

> **Disbursement**

**Mode:** Direct cash subsidy, disbursed annually to students or their guardians.
**Validity:** Applicable for the academic year in which the subsidy is disbursed.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a student enrolled in a government or aided school in Delhi.
- The applicant should be studying in any class from Nursery to 12th Standard.

## Application Process

### Offline

The scheme "Subsidy for School Uniforms to the Students" does not require any formal application from students, as the process is managed by the school authorities. The school verifies the students' enrollment and class details, compiles a list of eligible students, and submits it to the Department of Education.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there any income limit for families to qualify for the uniform subsidy?

No, the subsidy is available to all students in eligible classes without any income-based restrictions.

### Do students in private unaided schools qualify for this financial assistance?

No, the scheme is only for students studying in government and government-aided schools in Delhi.

### What is the amount provided to students in Nursery to Class V under this scheme?

Students from Nursery to Class V receive ₹1,300/- per annum as a uniform subsidy.

### How much do students in Class VI to VIII receive for their uniforms?

Students in Class VI to VIII are given ₹1,400/- per year to help cover uniform expenses.

### What is the subsidy amount for students in Class IX to XII?

Students in Class IX to XII receive ₹1,500/- annually to assist with uniform costs.

### Is the subsidy amount the same for all classes from Nursery to XII?

No, the subsidy varies by class group, ranging from ₹1,300/- to ₹1,500/- per annum.

### Are there any gender-based restrictions for receiving the uniform subsidy?

No, the subsidy is provided to all eligible students irrespective of gender.

### How is the uniform subsidy disbursed to the beneficiaries?

The subsidy is directly transferred to the bank account linked with the student’s application.

### Can students apply for the subsidy if they change schools within Delhi?

Yes, as long as the new school is a government or aided institution in Delhi, they remain eligible.

### Does the subsidy cover additional expenses like shoes or stationery?

No, the subsidy is specifically for school uniforms and does not include other expenses.

### Can parents collect the subsidy amount in cash instead of bank transfer?

No, the subsidy is only disbursed electronically to the registered bank account.

### Is the subsidy applicable for students in special schools under the government?

Yes, if the school is government-run or aided, students are eligible for the subsidy.

### How often is the uniform subsidy disbursed to students?

The subsidy is provided once per academic year, as per the scheme’s guidelines.

### What is the purpose of providing financial assistance for school uniforms?

The assistance aims to reduce the financial burden on families by offering cash subsidies for purchasing uniforms, ensuring all students have access to proper attire.

### Who is eligible to receive the uniform subsidy in government schools?

All students from Nursery to Class XII enrolled in government or aided schools in Delhi are eligible, regardless of income or gender.
