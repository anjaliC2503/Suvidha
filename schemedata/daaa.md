## Detailed Description

The "Disability Aids and Appliances" scheme was launched by the Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, LET&F (Labour) Department. Telangana. The scheme aims to provide artificial limbs, wheelchairs, tricycles, and other fabricated appliances to registered construction workers who have lost limbs or sustained disabilities due to accidents.

## Benefits

- **Free distribution of artificial limbs, wheelchairs/tricycles, and fabricated appliances.**

## Eligibility

1. The worker should be a registered building or construction worker with the Telangana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
1. The disablement must have occurred due to an accident at the worksite or anywhere.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The interested applicant visits the [official website](https://tbocwwb.telangana.gov.in/index.html) and clicks on "[Downloads](https://tbocwwb.telangana.gov.in/new_downloads.html)" at the top of menu options.
**Step-2:** Now click on the download button corresponding to the scheme name.
**Step-3**: In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step-4:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the concerned Assistant Labour Officer.
**Step-5:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Registered workers with the Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board who have sustained disabilities due to accidents.

### What benefits are provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides artificial limbs, wheelchairs/tricycles, and other fabricated appliances to eligible workers.

### Is there any cost involved for the workers?

No, all aids are provided free of charge.

### What type of accidents are covered under this scheme?

Accidents resulting in disability, whether occurring at the workplace or elsewhere, are covered.

### Can a worker apply for this scheme if the accident happened before May 1, 2016?

No, the scheme is only applicable for accidents that occurred on or after May 1, 2016.

### Where will the aids be sourced from?

Aids will be sourced from the Artificial Limbs Manufacturing Corporation of India (ALIMCO), a Government of India organization.

### How does one apply for this scheme?

Submit an application form with the required documents to the Assistant Labour Officer.

### Who verifies the application?

The Assistant Labour Officer verifies the application before it is sent to the District Screening Committee.

### What happens after the application is approved?

The Deputy Commissioner of Labour will arrange for the supply of the necessary aids from ALIMCO.

### Who oversees the implementation of this scheme?

The Commissioner of Labour, Telangana, Hyderabad, and the Secretary & Chief Executive Officer of the Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

### Can the aid be collected in person?

The aid will be arranged through the Hyderabad Regional Office of ALIMCO.
