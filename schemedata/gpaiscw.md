## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial compensation to deceased/disabled coir workers and their nominees. Through this scheme, insurance compensation benefits are provided to coir workers in case of accidents and disabilities.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Personal Accident Insurance Scheme for Coir Workers" by the Coir Board, Government of India, aims to provide financial compensation to deceased/disabled coir workers and their nominees. Through this scheme, insurance compensation benefits are provided to coir workers in case of accidental death, permanent total disability, permanent partial disability, and finger cut. The scheme is implemented by the Coir Board. The entire insurance premium is paid by the Coir Board to the insurance company selected through quotations. The financial compensation is provided by the insurance company to disabled coir workers or the nominee of disabled/deceased coir workers.
<br>

## Benefits

1. Accidental death: ₹ 50,000/-
1. Permanent total disability: ₹ 50,000/-
1. Permanent partial disability: ₹ 25,000/-
1. Provision for finger cut: Depending upon the finger and limited to applicable percentages of the capital sum insured.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a Coir Worker engaged in the coir industry.
1. The applicant should be aged 18 years and above.
1. There is no upper age limit for coverage under the insurance scheme.
1. The disabled coir worker can apply for the claim.
1. The nominee of the disabled/deceased coir worker can apply for the claim.
1. For women coir workers, considering their majority in the group, accidents include death and disablement arising out of and traceable to:


- Sterilisation and consequent complications.
- Pregnancy.
- Childbirth.
- Caesarean.
- Hysterectomy.
- Removal of breast.
- Murder and rape, etc.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

In the event of an eligible admissible claim, the claim form should be submitted through the offices of the Coir Board authorised for the purpose within the time limit fixed by the disabled coir worker or a nominee of the disabled/deceased coir worker.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to provide financial compensation to coir workers and their nominees in case of accidental death, disability, and other eligible accidental injuries.
<br>

### Who is eligible to be covered under the insurance scheme?

Coir workers aged 18 years and above engaged in the coir industry are covered under the insurance scheme. There is no upper age limit for coverage.
<br>

### Who can apply for a claim under the scheme?

The disabled coir worker or the nominee of the disabled/deceased coir worker can apply for the claim.
<br>

### What compensation is provided in case of accidental death?

A compensation amount of ₹50,000/- is provided in case of accidental death.
<br>

### What compensation is provided for permanent total disability?

A compensation amount of ₹50,000/- is provided in case of permanent total disability due to an accident.
<br>

### Do I need to be from a particular state in order to be eligible for this scheme?

The scheme is equally applicable to citizens across all the states of India.

### What compensation is provided for permanent partial disability?

A compensation amount of ₹25,000/- is provided in case of permanent partial disability due to an accident.
<br>

### What is the time limit for submitting a claim notice in case of accidental death?

The claim notice should be submitted before internment/cremation and within 1 month from the date of death through the Coir Board to the Insurance Company.
<br>

### What documents are required for permanent disability claims?

For permanent total disability, permanent partial disability, or temporary total disability claims, the insured person should submit the claim form along with the Doctor’s Certificate, medical bills, and diagnostic reports such as X-rays to prove the extent of accidental injuries.
<br>

### What documents are required for fatal claims?

The nominee of the insured person or legal heir should submit the Post-Mortem Report and the Original Death Certificate to the Insurance Company.
<br>

### Within what period should death occur for payment of the insured amount?

The specified capital sum insured is payable only when death occurs due to an accident within 12 months from the date of occurrence of the accident, and the accident should have occurred during the operational period of the policy.
