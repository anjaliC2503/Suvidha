## Brief Description

The "Panchayat Samiti Nandishala Jan Sahbhagita Yojana" was initiated to provide shelter for stray male cattle by constructing infrastructure in Panchayat Samitis.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Panchayat Samiti Nandishala Jan Sahbhagita Yojana&quot;, launched in 2021-22, by the Animal Husbandry Department, Rajasthan, aims to support the construction of permanent infrastructure for the shelter of stray male cattle in each Panchayat Samiti. The scheme provides financial assistance for Nandishala.

## Benefits

- One-time assistance of 90% of the total construction cost.

## Eligibility

1. Eligible entities include local bodies, Panchayati Raj institutions, registered trusts, NGOs, and government-recognized institutions.
   1. The organization must have been operational for at least 3 years.
   1. Ownership of 20 bighas of private land to sustain 250 male cattle.
   1. Minimum of 3 years of experience in operating a cattle shelter or similar activities.
   1. Availability of 10% contribution towards the project cost.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Obtain the application form from the Office of the District Joint Director, Animal Husbandry Department.
**Step 2:** Review the tender notices and apply online as per the guidelines.
**Step 3:** Submit the application form along with the required documents.
**Step 4:** Await verification and approval from the concerned authority.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Panchayat Samiti Nandishala Jan Sahbhagita Yojana"?

The objective of the scheme is to provide shelter for stray male cattle by supporting the construction of permanent infrastructure in Panchayat Samitis.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Eligible applicants include local bodies, Panchayati Raj institutions, registered NGOs, trusts, and government-recognized institutions.

### What is the financial assistance provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance for 90% of the total project cost for each Nandishala.

### How much land is required to be eligible for the scheme?

To be eligible, the organization/institution must own 20 bighas of private land to sustain 250 male cattle.

### What kind of experience is required to apply for the scheme?

The organization/institution must have at least 3 years of experience in managing cattle shelters or conducting similar activities.

### Where can an applicant obtain the application form for the scheme?

The application form can be obtained from the Office of the District Joint Director, Animal Husbandry Department.

### Is the scheme applicable in urban areas?

The scheme primarily targets Panchayat Samitis and rural institutions, but registered entities from urban areas may apply if they meet the eligibility criteria.

### Are private organizations allowed to apply for the scheme?

Yes, private organizations, including NGOs, trusts, and registered institutions, can apply if they meet the eligibility requirements.

### How is the funding distributed under the scheme?

The state government provides 90% of the total project cost, while the applicant must contribute the remaining 10%.

### Where can applicants seek assistance regarding their applications?

Applicants can seek assistance from the Office of the District Joint Director, Animal Husbandry Department for any queries or clarifications regarding the application process.
