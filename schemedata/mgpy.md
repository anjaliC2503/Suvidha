## Brief Description

The Scheme provides financial assistance for purchasing vehicles to enhance transportation facilities in rural areas. It includes grants for regular vehicles, e-rickshaws, and ambulances under specified conditions.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana&quot; launched by the Transport Department, Government of Bihar, aimed at improving transportation facilities in all panchayats of the state. It offers financial assistance to beneficiaries for purchasing vehicles.

## Benefits

- **Standard vehicles:** 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹1,00,000/-.  - **E-rickshaws:** 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹70,000/-.  - **Ambulances:** 50% of the purchase price or up to ₹2,00,000/-.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of the state.
  - The applicant must belong to a rural area.
  - The applicant should not have benefited from a similar scheme previously.
  - For ambulance purchases, applicants must be willing to use it for public health services.

## Application Process

### Online

**Registration Process:**
**Step-1:** Visit the official [Website](https://state.bihar.gov.in/transport/SectionInformation.html?editForm&amp;rowId=6353). Click on the &amp;quot;Registration&amp;quot; button.
**Step-2:** Enter your Mobile Number, New Password, Email, and Driving License No. 
**Step-3:** Click on &amp;quot;Register&amp;quot; to complete the registration process.
&lt;br&gt;

**Application Process:**
**Step-1:** Login with your registered Mobile Number and Password.
**Step-2:** On the Application page, fill in all the required details and upload the relevant documents.
**Step-3:** Then click on the declaration form and click on &amp;quot;Submit&amp;quot;.
**Step-4:** Once all details are filled in correctly, click on &amp;quot;Print Application&amp;quot; to get a copy of the application form.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is "Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana"?

The "Mukhyamantri Gram Parivahan Yojana" is a state initiative designed to provide financial assistance for purchasing vehicles to enhance rural transportation.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

The scheme is open to residents of Bihar state who belong to economically weaker sections or marginalized communities.

### What types of vehicles are covered under this scheme?

The scheme covers standard vehicles, e-rickshaws, and ambulances.

### How much subsidy is provided under the scheme?

Beneficiaries can receive:

50% of the purchase price or up to [?]1,00,000/- for standard vehicles.
50% of the purchase price or up to [?]70,000/- for e-rickshaws.
50% of the purchase price or up to [?]2,00,000/- for ambulances.

### What documents are required for the application?

Applicants must provide ID proof, income certificate, vehicle invoice, bank details, and other supporting documents.

### Can an individual apply for more than one vehicle under this scheme?

No, each applicant can only benefit from the scheme for one vehicle type.

### Can organizations or groups apply for this scheme?

No, the scheme is exclusively for individual beneficiaries.

### Can the subsidy be used to purchase a second-hand vehicle?

No, the scheme provides assistance only for the purchase of new vehicles.

### How are beneficiaries selected?

Beneficiaries are selected through a transparent process by the Panchayat or the concerned authority.

### Whom should applicants contact for grievances or queries?

Applicants can reach out to the district transport department or the local Panchayat office for assistance.
