## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students pursuing an M.Sc. in Agriculture (Agronomy) who are in their second year of study, by awarding them a monthly fellowship of ₹1000/- for one year.

## Detailed Description

The “Dr. K.C. Sharma Fellowship (M.Sc. Agriculture, Agronomy (second year) students)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students pursuing an M.Sc. in Agriculture (Agronomy) who are in their second year of study, by awarding them a monthly fellowship for one year.
<br>

## Benefits

**Fellowship Amount:**  ₹1,000/- per month.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the second year of the M.Sc. (Agriculture) in Agronomy program at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The fellowship is awarded for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the selected students receive the fellowship payments.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this fellowship?

Only students enrolled in the second year of the M.Sc. (Agriculture) in Agronomy program at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar are eligible.
<br>

### Is this fellowship available for first-year students?

No, the fellowship is specifically meant for second-year students of the M.Sc. (Agriculture) Agronomy program.
<br>

### How much financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Each selected student receives ₹1,000/- per month as a fellowship.
<br>

### For how long is the fellowship granted?

The fellowship is awarded for a duration of one year only.
<br>

### How are students selected for the fellowship?

Students must submit applications on the prescribed form, which are reviewed by a committee constituted by the Vice Chancellor. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval for the fellowship.
<br>

### How will the fellowship amount be disbursed?

The fellowship amount is credited directly to the selected student’s account on a monthly basis after approval.
<br>
