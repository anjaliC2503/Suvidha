## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support Adi Dravidar/Tribal communities by providing burial grounds and pathways in areas where such facilities are not available.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Burial Ground-Provision of Burial Grounds and Pathway to Burial Grounds&quot; scheme launched by the Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, aims to support Adi Dravidar/Tribal communities by providing burial grounds and pathways in areas where such facilities are not available. This initiative ensures that Adi Dravidar and Tribal habitants have access to dignified burial services, promoting social welfare and respect for cultural practices.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Access to Dignified Burial Services:** Provision of land for burial grounds.

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.
  - The beneficiary should belong to the Adi Dravidar/ Tribal communities.
  - The beneficiary must be residing in habitations without existing burial ground or pathway access.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should collect the application form from the office of the Director of Adi Dravidar (DAD) and Tribal Welfare Office (TWO) /Project Officer DRDA /Commissioner, Panchayat Union.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the concerned department/ office.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of this scheme ?

The "Burial Ground-Provision of Burial Grounds and Pathway to Burial Grounds" scheme aims to support Adi Dravidar/Tribal communities by providing burial grounds and pathways in areas where such facilities are not available.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

The scheme is available to members of Adi Dravidar/Tribal communities living in habitations that lack a burial ground or accessible pathway.

### Is there an income limit to apply?

There is no income limit for beneficiaries applying to this scheme.

### What documents are required for the application?

Applicants must submit a community certificate (Adi Dravidar/Tribal), proof of residence, and a completed application form.

### How does one apply for the provision of a burial ground?

Applications can be submitted to the Panchayat Union Commissioner, DAD, TWO, or Project Officer DRDA.

### Are there any costs involved for beneficiaries?

No, the scheme provides burial grounds and pathways free of charge to eligible SC/ST beneficiaries.

### Can individuals from non-Adi Dravidar/Tribal communities apply?

No, the scheme is exclusively for Adi Dravidar/Tribal communities.

### Can the scheme be availed if a burial ground already exists in the area?

No, the scheme is meant for habitations without an existing burial ground or pathway.

### How is the location for the burial ground selected?

The authorities select the burial ground location based on land availability and the needs of the community.

### What happens if an application is rejected?

Applicants may contact the authorities for clarification on the reasons for rejection and can reapply if they meet the eligibility criteria.

### Whom should applicants contact for further information?

Applicants can reach out to the District Adi Dravidar Welfare Office or their local Panchayat Union Commissioner for more information.
