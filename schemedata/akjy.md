## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide affordable electricity to farmers in Madhya Pradesh by offering power supply at concessional rates.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Atal Kisan Jyoti Yojana” launched by the Energy Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to provide concessional electricity to farmers through a flat rate power supply of ₹750/- per horsepower per year to metered agricultural pump consumers up to 10 horsepower.
<br>

## Benefits

Electricity Supply at Concessional Rate: Farmers having metered permanent agricultural pumps up to 10 horsepower are provided electricity at a flat rate of ₹750/- per horsepower per year.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a farmer.
  - The applicant must be a permanent agricultural pump consumer.
  - The applicant must have a metered connection.
  - The applicant’s pump capacity should be up to 10 horsepower.

<br>

### Offline

- The benefit will be automatically provided to eligible consumers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme aims to provide electricity at concessional rates to farmers in Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Who implements the scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Horticulture and Food Processing Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Who is eligible to benefit from this scheme?

Permanent metered agricultural pump consumers up to 10 horsepower are eligible for the benefit.
<br>

### What is the rate of electricity supply under this scheme?

Electricity is supplied at a flat rate of ₹750/- per horsepower per year.
<br>

### What type of benefit is provided under the scheme?

The benefit is provided in the form of a grant from the State Government.
<br>

### Is this scheme available to all categories of farmers?

Yes, it is available to all General category farmers having metered agricultural pumps up to 10 horsepower.
<br>

### Is there any application process to avail of this scheme?

No, the benefit is automatically available to eligible consumers.
<br>

### Is there any application fee?

No, there is no application fee for this scheme.
<br>

### Can farmers with more than 10 horsepower pumps apply for this benefit?

No, only consumers with up to 10-horsepower pumps are eligible.
<br>

### Who is the designated officer for this scheme?

The distribution center in-charge is the designated officer.
<br>
