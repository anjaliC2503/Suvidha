## Brief Description

The scheme aims to enhance rural livelihoods, promote sustainable animal husbandry practices, & contribute to self-reliance in livestock sector. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to unemployed individuals, SHGs, cooperative societies, etc. for livestock rearing & allied activities.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Atma Nirbhar Pashu Palan Yojna (ANPPY)&quot;, implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry, Veterinary &amp; Dairy Development, Government of Arunachal Pradesh, aims to enhance rural livelihoods, promote sustainable animal husbandry practices, and contribute to self-reliance in the livestock sector. Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of credit-linked support is provided to unemployed individuals, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), farmers&#39; clubs, cooperative societies, and Dairy Cooperative Societies (DCS) for livestock rearing and allied activities.

## Benefits

Under this scheme, financial assistance in the form of credit-linked support is provided to eligible beneficiaries for livestock rearing and allied activities.

## Eligibility

The following beneficiaries are eligible under this scheme:

  - Arunachal State Rural Livelihoods Mission (ArSRLM) promoted Self-Help Groups (SHGs), individuals, Farmers&#39; Clubs, Registered Cooperative Societies, and Dairy Cooperative Societies (DCS) will be eligible.
  - Individuals should belong to the Arunachal Pradesh Scheduled Tribe (APST) category and be unemployed.

## Application Process

### Online

***Interested and eligible students need to apply online on the*** [***Atma Nirbhar Yojana***](https://agri.arunachal.gov.in/atmanirbhar_yojana/) ***portal.*** 
**New User Registration**

   1. Select the &quot;[Login](https://agri.arunachal.gov.in/atmanirbhar_yojana/login)&quot; option.   1. Subsequently, click on the &quot;[Register Here&quot;](https://agri.arunachal.gov.in/atmanirbhar_yojana/user-register) link.   1. Complete the registration form with the requisite details, including your name, Aadhaar card number, email ID, and mobile number.
   1. Establish a password for your account.
   1. Upon successful registration, the applicant will receive a User ID and password.

***Login &amp; Application Process:***

   1. [Log in](https://agri.arunachal.gov.in/atmanirbhar_yojana/login) using the provided User ID and password to access the application form.   1. Fill in the complete application form.
   1. Upload the required documents, including a photograph, caste certificate, residential certificate, and land ownership proof, etc.
   1. Submit the application form.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for ANPPY?

Unemployed APST individuals, SHGs, farmers clubs, and registered cooperative societies can apply.

### What are the eligible livestock units under the scheme?

Dairy, poultry, and piggery units are eligible.

### Is the scheme loan-based?

Yes, ANPPY is a credit-linked scheme requiring bank loan sanction.

### Do SHGs need to be registered to apply?

Yes, SHGs must be promoted by ArSRLM and properly registered.

### What documents are needed to apply?

Documents include Aadhaar, APST certificate, unemployment proof, and a project proposal.

### How soon will I get the subsidy?

Subsidy is released post-loan sanction and verification by the department.

### Will training or support be provided?

Yes, technical support and guidance will be provided by the implementing agency.

### Can existing livestock businesses apply?

No, the scheme is intended for new, unemployed applicants only.

### Where can I apply for the scheme?

Applicant can apply at the [Atma Nirbhar Yojana portal](https://agri.arunachal.gov.in/atmanirbhar_yojana/user-register).
