## Brief Description

Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme "Nirmaan Shramik  Ke Bachchon Hetu Utkrasht Khel Protsaahan Yojana" for Construction worker on 23 May 2017.

## Detailed Description

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Nirmaan Shramik  Ke Bachchon Hetu  Utkrasht Khel Protsaahan Yojana&amp;quot; for Construction Workers on 23 May 2017. The main objective of the scheme to provide financial to encourage the studdents for sports.
&lt;br&gt;

&lt;br&gt;

## Benefits

Incentive amount will be provided to the children who perform excellently at the district / division / state / national level in all recognized sports as follows -

1. Amount at district level: ₹ 1,000/-
1. Amount at divisional level: ₹ 2,000/-
1. Amount at state level: ₹ 5,000/-
1. Amount at National level : ₹ 50,000/-

## Eligibility

1. Benefits will only be provided to children of construction workers who are registered in any district within the state
1. The benefit of the scheme will be given only to those children whose excellence is recognized by the Sports Department.

## Application Process

### Online

1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on "[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)" under "Chhattisgarh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board".1. Enter the following details

   1. Select Group Name : "Bhavan Evan Koi Sannirmaan"
   1. Select Service : "Yojana"
   1. What do you want to do : "Aavedan"
   1. Click next.

1. Select your district name and provide old/new registration number and click next.
1. Select the scheme name. 
1. Fill out the online with required details. 
1. Submit.
