## Brief Description

The Educational Assistance for Government Recognised Diploma Courses scheme is designed to financially support children of registered construction workers, providing ₹20,000 per year for diploma courses and ₹25,000/- per year for postgraduate diploma courses.

## Detailed Description

The Educational Assistance for Government Recognised Diploma Courses scheme was launched by the Maharashtra Building And Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (MBOCWW), Labour Department Maharashtra. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to children of registered construction workers, providing ₹20,000/- per year for diploma courses and ₹25,000/- per year for postgraduate diploma courses.

## Benefits

**Financial Assistance For:**

- Government Recognized Diploma Courses- ₹20,000/-
- Postgraduate Diploma Courses- ₹25,000/-

<br>

> **NOTE:** Benefit applies to a maximum of two children and to the wife of registered male workers.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Maharashtra.
1. The applicant should be a child or spouse of a registered construction worker.
1. The construction worker should be registered under the MBOCWW. 
1. The applicant must be enrolled in a government-recognized diploma or postgraduate diploma course.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://mahabocw.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/SCHEME_EDUCATION_FORM.pdf) from the MBOCWW official website. 
**Step-2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step-3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Labor Commissioner/Government Labor Officer.
**Step-4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Documents Required

- Passport-size Photo.
- Aadhaar Card.
- Identity Card of Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
- Bank Passbook.
- Marksheet/School Certificate of passing in the previous academic level.
- Acknowledgment/Bonafide for admission in the current academic year.
- Proof of Residence (One of Aadhaar Card/Passport/Driving Licence/Ration Sheet/Last Month's Electricity Bill/Gram Panchayat Certificate).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the assistance amount for diploma courses?

The assistance amount is [?]20,000 per year for government-recognized diploma courses.

### How much is offered for postgraduate diploma courses?

For postgraduate diploma courses, [?]25,000 per year is provided.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Children and wives of registered construction workers enrolled in government-recognized diploma or postgraduate diploma courses are eligible.

### Is this benefit applicable to more than one child?

Yes, it applies to up to two children.

### What documents are required for the application?

A mark sheet from the previous academic year and a bonafide certificate for current admission.

### Can the wife of a registered worker also apply?

Yes, the wife of a registered male worker is eligible.

### Is the scheme applicable to private institutions?

No, the scheme is only applicable to government-recognized institutions.

### What is the application process?

The form can be collected from the respective department, filled out, and submitted with the necessary documents.

### What happens if I don’t have a bonafide certificate?

A bonafide certificate from the institution is mandatory for application.

### Can both parents claim the benefit for more than two children?

No, the benefit extends to a maximum of two children per registered worker.

### Is there a minimum passing percentage required to apply?

No specific passing percentage is mentioned for eligibility.

### How frequently can the assistance be availed?

The assistance is provided annually, subject to continued enrollment in the course.

### What should be done if the worker's registration expires during the academic year?

The worker's registration must be valid at the time of applying for assistance.
