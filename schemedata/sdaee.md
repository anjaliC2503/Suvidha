## Brief Description

The scheme "Skill Development and Economic Empowerment" aims to provide vocational training and skill enhancement for visually challenged individuals. Through this scheme, free education, food, accommodation, and training in various job-oriented fields are provided to eligible candidates.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Skill Development and Economic Empowerment&quot; by the is a central sector scheme that aims to provide inclusive vocational training and empower visually challenged individuals for economic self-reliance. Through this scheme, training is provided in various trades such as computer operations, stenography, programming assistance, secretarial assistance, and other technical fields. Additionally, students are provided with free meals, hostel accommodation, library facilities, teaching aids, and medical care. The scheme is implemented by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities, Dehradun.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

> **Free Vocational Training:** 


  - Provides training in various professional courses, including Computer Operator, Stenographer, Mushroom Producer, Radio Jockey, Data Entry Operator, Reflexology, and Gardener.

> **Essential Skill Training:** 


  - Offers training in Braille orientation and mobility, computer typing, Hindi and English, and home management skills.

&gt; **Modern Learning Tools:** 


  - Senior students receive smartphones and laptops to facilitate modern and convenient studies.

> **Comprehensive Support for Trainees:**


  - Free food and accommodation are provided.
  - Commuting allowance is given for participation in the program.
  - Uniforms are provided.
  - Library facilities are accessible.
  - Medical facilities are available.
  - Accessible teaching materials are supplied.
  - Assistive devices are provided.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should belong to the Persons with Disabilities category.
  - The applicant should be a visually challenged (Blindness/ Low-Vision) individual.
  - The applicant should be willing to undergo full-time vocational training or other training courses.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun, and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.
&gt; **Website:** [niepvd.nic.in](https://niepvd.nic.in/)

&gt; **Telephone No.:** 0135-2744491

&gt; **Email:** tcab-niepvd@nivh.gov.in

   1. Income Certificate of the Parent/Guardian.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the "Skill Development and Economic Empowerment" scheme?

The main objective of the "Skill Development and Economic Empowerment" scheme is to provide comprehensive vocational training and livelihood-oriented skill development to visually challenged individuals so that they can become economically self-reliant and socially integrated.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the training programs offered under this scheme?

Any visually challenged individual who possesses the necessary educational qualifications for the chosen trade and can submit valid proof of disability and other required documents is eligible to apply under this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Which government department is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The scheme is implemented through the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities, Dehradun.
<br>

<br>

### What types of vocational courses are available under the scheme?

The scheme offers training in a variety of vocational courses such as Computer Operator and Programming Assistant, Stenography and Secretarial Assistance, Music and Radio Jockey, Data Entry Operator, Telephone Operator, and Gardener, among others.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any fee to be paid by the trainees for the courses or facilities provided under this scheme?

No, the training, accommodation, food, uniforms, educational materials, medical services, and all other facilities are provided free of cost to eligible trainees under the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What kind of support facilities are provided to the students during the training period?

The trainees are provided with full-time hostel accommodation, nutritious meals, study materials, uniforms, medical care, library access, and assistive learning devices throughout the duration of their course.
<br>

<br>

### Is prior computer knowledge required to enroll in computer-related programs?

While prior knowledge is helpful, it is not mandatory as basic computer training is part of the course curriculum.
<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme limited to visually challenged individuals only?

Yes, the scheme is specifically designed to empower and provide skill development opportunities to visually challenged individuals.
<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant apply for a training course under this scheme?

Interested applicants should visit the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun, and submit a duly filled offline application form along with the required documents directly to the institute.

### What documents need to be submitted at the time of application?

The applicant is required to submit a Disability Certificate, UDID Card, Educational Qualification Certificates, Birth Certificate, Aadhaar card, Income Certificate of the Guardian, etc.
<br>
