## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide day care facilities for children aged 6 months to 6 years of working couples at a nominal monthly fee in Crèches.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Paalna Ghar (Crèche)” is implemented by the Women Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide day care facilities for children aged 6 months to 6 years of working couples.
<br>

## Benefits

Under the scheme, a crèche is run for the children of working couples, providing day care facilities for children in the age group of 6 months to 6 years.
The following fees are applicable under the scheme:

  - For BPL families: ₹20/- per child per month
  - For families with an income less than ₹12,000/-: ₹100/- per child per month
  - For families with an income above ₹12,000/-: ₹200/- per child per month

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiary must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The beneficiary must be a child aged between 6 months and 6 years.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

A crèche is established in every district of Uttarakhand. Working couples can avail of the benefits by paying the applicable fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to benefit from this scheme?

Working couples with children aged 6 months to 6 years are eligible.
<br>

### Is the scheme available for all children?

No, children between 6 months and 6 years of age can avail the facility.
<br>

### Where are the crèches located?

Crèches are established in every district of Uttarakhand.
<br>

### What facilities are provided under the scheme?

Day care services
<br>

### What is the monthly fee for BPL families?

BPL families are required to pay ₹20 per child per month.
<br>

### What is the monthly fee for families with income less than ₹12,000?

Families with income less than ₹12,000 per month need to pay ₹100 per child per month.
<br>

### What is the monthly fee for families with income above ₹12,000?

Families with income above ₹12,000 per month need to pay ₹200 per child per month.
<br>

### How can working couples apply for this scheme?

They can approach the nearest crèche in their district and submit the required documents along with the applicable fee.
