## Brief Description

"Merit Scholarship: Polytechnic Institutions" is a scholarship scheme by the Technical Education Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. The scheme provides ₹600/- per month to eligible students for pursuing education from recognized polytechnic institutions in Chhattisgarh.

## Detailed Description

"Merit Scholarship: Polytechnic Institutions" is a scholarship scheme by the Technical Education Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. The scheme provides ₹600/- per month to eligible students for pursuing education from recognized polytechnic institutions in Chhattisgarh. Eligible applicants include (a) Students who have passed the 10th/12th class examination under the 10+2 system from any recognized institution located in Chhattisgarh with a minimum of 60% marks, (b) Students newly admitted to the second year of the Diploma program who are ITI / 12th vocational certificate holders and have passed the qualifying examination with a minimum of 60% marks.

## Benefits

(a) For students newly admitted to the first year of the Diploma program: ₹600/- per month.
(b) For students admitted to the second year through lateral entry: ₹600/- per month.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a native resident student of Chhattisgarh.


- The applicant must be currently studying in a recognized polytechnic institution in Chhattisgarh.
- The applicant must have secured a minimum of 60% marks in the qualifying examination.
- Students who have passed the 10th/12th class examination under the 10+2 system from any recognized institution located in Chhattisgarh with a minimum of 60% marks will be eligible.
- Students newly admitted to the second year of the Diploma program who are ITI / 12th vocational certificate holders must have passed the qualifying examination with a minimum of 60% marks.

<br>

**Other Conditions -** 

- Without special approval from the government, no student will receive more than one scholarship at the same time. However, if the institution where he is studying has given him any fee concession, then he will be entitled to continue receiving it.
- If any scholarship becomes vacant during its duration, then the approving authority can give such scholarships for its unexpired period to the next eligible and deserving candidate from the merit list.
- Only those students will be eligible to receive scholarships who have passed the qualifying examination from any testing body of Chhattisgarh. But children of such permanent government employees of Chhattisgarh can be exempted from this condition who are on deputation to the Government of India or any other state government.
- Such students will not be eligible to receive scholarships who are in full-time employment. In this regard, only one concession can be given that any such student, who is regularly studying in a recognized institution and is receiving any scholarship, can be allowed to do part-time work.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the **Office of the Principal of the Institution** and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the institute staff.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the staff. The Principal will verify the documents and forward the application to the Directorate for approval (New applications) or process the renewal (for existing beneficiaries).
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the staff to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the eligibility criteria for students applying to the first year of a Diploma program under this scholarship scheme?

To be eligible, you must have passed the 10th or 12th class examination under the 10+2 system from a recognized institution in Chhattisgarh with at least 60% marks.

### How much financial assistance will I receive per month if I qualify for this scholarship?

Eligible students receive ₹600 per month, regardless of whether they are in the first or second year of the Diploma program.

### Do I need to be a resident of Chhattisgarh to apply for this scholarship?

Yes, only native resident students of Chhattisgarh are eligible to apply for this scholarship.

### What is the minimum percentage required in the qualifying examination to be eligible for this scholarship?

You must have secured at least 60% marks in your 10th or 12th class examination (or equivalent qualifying exam).

### Can students admitted through lateral entry in the second year of a Diploma program apply for this scholarship?

Yes, if you are admitted to the second year through lateral entry (ITI or 12th vocational certificate holders) and have at least 60% marks in the qualifying exam, you are eligible.

### Which institutions are considered valid for eligibility under this scholarship scheme?

You must be studying in a recognized polytechnic institution located in Chhattisgarh to qualify.

### Do I need to submit my previous academic marksheets to apply for this scholarship?

Yes, you must attach a copy of your qualifying examination (10th/12th or equivalent) mark sheet with the application.

### Where can I obtain the application form for this scholarship?

You must visit the Office of the Principal of your institution and request a hard copy of the prescribed application form.

### What should I do after filling out the scholarship application form?

Submit the duly filled and signed form along with self-attested copies of required documents to the institute staff within the prescribed period.

### Will I get an acknowledgement after submitting my scholarship application?

Yes, you should request a receipt or acknowledgement containing submission details such as date, time, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

### Who verifies the submitted scholarship applications before approval?

The Principal of your institution will verify the documents and forward the application to the Directorate for approval.

### Is there any difference in scholarship amount between first-year and second-year Diploma students?

No, both first-year and lateral entry second-year students receive ₹600 per month.

### Can students from private polytechnic institutions apply for this scholarship?

Yes, as long as the institution is recognized and located in Chhattisgarh, students from both government and private polytechnics can apply.
