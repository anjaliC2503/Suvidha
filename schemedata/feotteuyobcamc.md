## Brief Description

"Free Employment Oriented Training to Educated Unemployed Youth of Backward Class and Minority Class" aims to provide quality pre-examination training for various employment-oriented competitive examinations to talented but economically disadvantaged young men and women.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Free Employment Oriented Training to Educated Unemployed Youth of Backward Class and Minority Class" by the Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to provide quality pre-examination training for various employment-oriented competitive examinations to talented but economically disadvantaged young men and women belonging to the backward and minority classes of the state. Training is offered near their place of residence through government, semi-government, or non-government institutions, along with an ideal educational environment and free coaching facilities.

## Benefits

- Free coaching for employment-oriented competitive examinations.


- Stipend support during the training period.
- Access to well-equipped coaching facilities.
- Ideal academic environment for exam preparation.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
- The applicant should belong to Other Backward Classes (OBC) or Minorities.
- The applicant's family income should not exceed the creamy layer limit.
- The applicant should be selected by the training institution approved by the government.

## Application Process

### Offline

The institutions are selected by the government to provide coaching before employment-oriented examinations, and the institutions select the trainees. Applications are made by the Office Director, State Level Employment and Training Center, Bhopal in the training centers of selected non-government organizations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to provide quality pre-examination training and free coaching for employment-oriented competitive exams to economically weak youth from backward and minority classes in Madhya Pradesh.

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Economically weak young men and women belonging to backward and minority classes in Madhya Pradesh preparing for competitive exams can apply.

### What types of exams are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers employment-oriented competitive exams, including civil services, banking, teaching, engineering, and other technical or professional entrance exams.

### What facilities are provided under the scheme?

Free coaching, an ideal educational environment, and scholarships to reduce financial difficulties during the training period are provided.

### Are scholarships included in the scheme?

Yes, a scholarship is provided to trainees to minimize financial challenges faced during the training period.

### Is there any income limit to apply for the scheme?

Yes, applicants must be economically weak as per the eligibility norms set by the department.

### Can employed individuals apply for this scheme?

No, applicants must be unemployed to be eligible for the scheme.

### What documents are required for application?

Documents required include proof of residence, caste certificate, income certificate, and educational qualification certificates.

### How many times can an applicant avail of the benefits?

An applicant can avail of the benefits only once for the same competitive examination.

### Are private institutions included in the scheme?

Yes, coaching is provided through government, semi-government, and non-government institutions.
