## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide medical check-up facilities to registered construction workers through door-to-door visits, health camps, and collection centers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Medical Check-Up Facility” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UKBOCWWB) under the Labour Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide medical check-up facilities to registered construction workers through door-to-door visits, health camps, and collection centers.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, medical check-up facilities are provided to construction workers.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a construction worker registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

To provide medical check-up benefits as part of social security to construction workers registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, medical check-up facilities are offered through door-to-door visits, health camps, and collection centers.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Construction workers registered with the Uttarakhand Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board are eligible.
<br>

### What benefits are provided under this scheme?

Medical check-up services and other preventive health services are provided.
<br>

### How are the medical check-up facilities delivered?

Facilities are provided through door-to-door visits, health camps, and collection centers.
<br>

### Can unregistered workers avail of this facility?

No, only registered construction workers are eligible for the scheme.
<br>

### Can workers choose to visit collection centers instead of door-to-door services?

Yes.
<br>

### How does this scheme benefit construction workers?

It promotes early detection of health issues, ensures preventive care, and contributes to overall health and productivity.
<br>
