## Brief Description

The scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops" aims to promote fruit crop cultivation through financial assistance for high density and ultra-high density plantations, tissue culture bananas, and papayas to farmers across the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance the production and productivity of fruit crops by providing financial assistance to farmers for adopting advanced cultivation methods such as high-density and ultra-high-density plantations like Mango, Guava, Lemon, and Pomegranate etc. This initiative supports the state’s agricultural growth and helps farmers increase their income through modern horticultural practices.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **High-Density and Ultra High-Density Plantations:** Farmers can receive assistance ranging from ₹30,000/- to ₹40,000/- per hectare for crops like mango, guava, lemon, and pomegranate, with a maximum limit of 4 hectares per beneficiary.  - **Tissue-Culture Banana Cultivation:** The assistance covers 40% of the expenditure incurred or ₹50,000/-, whichever is less, per beneficiary, with a limit of 4 hectares.  - **Papaya Cultivation:** Farmers can receive assistance of 50% of the cost or a maximum of ₹30,000/- per hectare.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be a farmer.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops"?

The main objective of the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops" is to encourage and support farmers in Gujarat to adopt high-density and ultra-high-density plantation techniques and to take up new cultivation of specific fruit crops by providing them with financial assistance for covering a portion of their cultivation costs.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is responsible for implementing the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops"?

The scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops" is implemented by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department under the Government of Gujarat.
<br>

### Which fruit crops are eligible for financial assistance under the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops"?

The fruit crops eligible for financial assistance under the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops" include mango, guava, lemon, pomegranate, tissue culture banana, and papaya, which have been identified as suitable for cultivation under high-density, ultra-high-density, or new plantation methods.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the amount of assistance provided for high-density and ultra-high-density plantation under the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops"?

Under the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops", the assistance for high-density and ultra-high-density plantations in crops such as mango, guava, lemon, and pomegranate ranges from ₹30,000/- to ₹40,000/- per hectare, subject to a maximum limit of 4 hectares per beneficiary farmer.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How much financial assistance is provided for the cultivation of tissue culture banana under the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops"?

For the cultivation of tissue culture banana, the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops" provides financial assistance amounting to either 40% of the total expenditure incurred or ₹50,000/-, whichever is less, with an upper limit of 4 hectares per beneficiary farmer.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What level of financial assistance is provided for papaya cultivation under the scheme?

The scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops" offers financial support for papaya cultivation, amounting to either 50% of the total cultivation cost or ₹30,000/- per hectare, whichever is less, enabling farmers to reduce their initial investment burden.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops"?

The scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops" is open to all farmers residing in Gujarat who are engaged in or intend to engage in the cultivation of the specified fruit crops on land that they own or cultivate, provided they meet the scheme’s conditions.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit for applying to the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops"?

There is no income limit specified under the scheme "Assistance for Area Expansion in Fruit Crops", meaning that farmers of all income levels can apply for the financial assistance provided they fulfil the other eligibility requirements.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can a farmer claim benefits for more than 4 hectares under the scheme?

The scheme has a maximum area limit of 4 hectares per beneficiary farmer, and therefore no financial assistance will be provided for any area exceeding this limit under the scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How can eligible farmers apply for the scheme?

Eligible farmers can apply for the scheme by visiting the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, filling out the application form online, uploading the required documents, and submitting it for departmental verification.
<br>

### What documents are necessary to apply under this scheme?

The documents required to apply for the scheme include the Aadhaar Card for identity proof, a 7/12 Certificate for land ownership, and a bank passbook or cancelled cheque.
<br>
