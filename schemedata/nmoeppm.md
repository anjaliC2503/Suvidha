## Brief Description

The scheme component "Planting Materials" launched under the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm" scheme aims to increase oil palm production in the state and reduce dependency on imports through financial assistance to farmers for procuring planting materials.

## Detailed Description

The scheme component &quot;Planting Materials&quot; launched under the &quot;National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm&quot; scheme by the Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance oil palm cultivation in Gujarat by providing subsidized planting materials to farmers, thereby reducing import dependency and promoting self-reliance in edible oil production. The assistance covers both locally sourced and imported seedlings, including transportation costs for imported varieties.
<br>

## Benefits

- **For Locally Sourced Planting Materials.:** ₹20,000/- per hectare   - **For Imported Plants (including transportation cost):** ₹29,000/- per hectare
> **Note:** For imported seedlings (along with transportation cost) the assistance will be admissible as per the holding of land held by the farmer.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a farmer.
  - The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme?

The objective of the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme is to increase the production of oil palm in Gujarat by providing financial assistance for procuring planting materials, thereby reducing the state’s dependence on importing oil palm from abroad.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who implements the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme?

The "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme is implemented by the Agriculture Department, Government of Gujarat, to ensure the effective delivery of subsidies and support to eligible farmers.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to benefit from the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme?

All farmers in Gujarat are eligible to benefit from the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What type of financial assistance is provided under the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme?

Under the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme, farmers receive a subsidy of ₹20,000/- per hectare for locally sourced planting materials and ₹29,000/- per hectare for imported plants, which also covers the transportation cost.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Are there any conditions for obtaining seedlings under the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme?

Yes, the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme mandates that the seedlings must be obtained from nurseries of companies that are approved and operating under the Oil Palm Scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there a limit on how often a farmer can receive benefits under the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme?

Yes, the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme has a re-benefit time limit, which is determined according to the landholding size of the cultivator.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can farmers from outside Gujarat apply for the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme?

No, the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme is specifically designed for farmers within the state of Gujarat, and farmers from other states are not eligible.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How can eligible farmers apply for the scheme?

Eligible farmers can apply for the scheme by visiting the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, filling out the application form online, uploading the required documents, and submitting it for departmental verification.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme?

Applicants for the "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials" scheme are required to submit their Aadhaar Card, Land Records 7-12 and 8-A, Bank Passbook or Cancelled Cheque, and if applicable, a Caste Certificate or Disability Certificate issued by the competent authority.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit for applying to the scheme "National Mission on Edible Oil–Oil Palm: Planting Materials"?

There is no income limit specified under the scheme guideline, meaning that farmers of all income levels can apply for the financial assistance provided they fulfil the other eligibility requirements.
<br>
