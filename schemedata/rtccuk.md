## Brief Description

The scheme "Rural Technology Complex" by the Programme Implementation Department, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to enhance livelihood opportunities in the Himalayan region through the use of local, need-based and environment-friendly technologies.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Rural Technology Complex&quot; implemented by the Govind Ballabh Pant National Institute of Himalayan Environment (NIHE), aims to support livelihood enhancement in the Himalayan region through the application of need-based, eco-friendly technologies. The scheme involves the establishment of Rural Technology Complex(RTC) within institutional premises, where local technological needs are addressed by training participants in sustainable practices. These sessions, focused on skill development, aim to create awareness and equip trainees with techniques to improve their income generation capacities using low-cost and environmentally compatible tools. Training is provided at no cost, and is open to government officers, NGO representatives, educators, students, women, and rural self-help group members.

## Benefits

- **Free Training:** All training programmes under the scheme are provided at no cost.  - **Livelihood Enhancement:** Training equips individuals with skills for sustainable, eco-friendly income generation.  - **Practical Exposure:** Training includes hands-on learning and field demonstrations.  - **No Participation Charges:** There is no fee for training or the exposure visit to the Rural Technology Campus.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should belong to any of the following categories:


   1. A central or state government officer or staff member.
   1. A representative of a non-governmental organisation (NGO).
   1. A student from a recognised university or educational institution.
   1. A teacher or faculty member from any recognised educational institution.
   1. A rural woman or man interested in acquiring environment-friendly livelihood-based technological skills.
   1. A member of a registered self-help group (SHG).

## Application Process

### Offline

- The applicant must send a request letter to the Director of the institute for a visit or training.

> **Note:**


  - For training lasting one to seven days, an advance payment must be made based on the package determined by the institute.
  - For awareness visits to rural technical premises, no fee is payable. This service is free of charge.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Rural Technology Complex"?

The "Rural Technology Complex" is a government-supported initiative established to promote the development, demonstration, and dissemination of sustainable and eco-friendly rural technologies to enhance the livelihood of rural communities.
<br>

<br>

### Who implements the activities under the "Rural Technology Complex"?

The activities under the "Rural Technology Complex" are implemented by the G. B. Pant National Institute of Himalayan Environment, functioning under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India.
<br>

<br>

### What are the key objectives of the "Rural Technology Complex"?

The main objectives of the "Rural Technology Complex" include training rural youth in eco-technologies, demonstrating low-cost innovations for sustainable living, and promoting green livelihood opportunities in ecologically sensitive zones.
<br>

<br>

### Who can participate in training programs at the "Rural Technology Complex"?

The training programmes are open to students, rural youth, members of self-help groups, entrepreneurs, farmers, and any individuals interested in sustainable and environment-based livelihoods.
<br>

<br>

### What kinds of technologies are demonstrated at the "Rural Technology Complex"?

The programs showcase a wide range of green technologies such as biogas plants, solar drying units, rainwater harvesting systems, organic farming methods, and composting technologies.
<br>

<br>

### What are the benefits of participating in programmes at the "Rural Technology Complex"?

Participants gain practical knowledge in rural and green technologies, improve their employability, and receive hands-on exposure that can help in setting up their own sustainable business or service.
<br>

<br>

### What is the role of the "Rural Technology Complex" in local development?

The "Rural Technology Complex" serves as a catalyst for local development by empowering communities through knowledge transfer, skill enhancement, and access to environment-friendly technologies.

### How long are the training programs offered at the Rural Technology Complex?

The duration of the training programmes may vary from short-term workshops lasting a few days to more intensive courses extending up to a few weeks, depending on the subject and structure of the module.
<br>
