## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide one-time financial assistance to physically disabled artists and litterateurs for disability-related medical treatment and rehabilitation.

## Detailed Description

The “Madhya Pradesh Kalakar Evam Sahityakar Kalyan Kosh Niyam- Disability Assistance” scheme has been launched by the Department of Culture, Government of Madhya Pradesh. Under this scheme, one-time monetary support is provided to eligible disabled artists and litterateurs for disability-related medical treatment. The assistance helps beneficiaries manage treatment expenses arising from permanent or severe physical disability. 
<br>

## Benefits

- One-time financial assistance of up to ₹1,00,000/-.
- Assistance is provided exclusively for the treatment of physical disability.

> **NOTE:** Direct transfer to the beneficiary’s bank account.

<br>

## Exclusions

1. The applicant should not be a government employee.
1. The applicant should not be employed in autonomous or semi-government institutions.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be a physically disabled artist or litterateur.
1. The applicant should be 21 years of age or above.
1. The applicant’s monthly income from all sources, including spouse income, should not exceed ₹10,000.
1. The total monthly family income, including dependents, should not exceed ₹20,000.

> **NOTE:** Income limits are revised annually with a 6 percent increase for eligibility calculation.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://www.culturemp.in/pdfs/kalakar-kalyan-kosh-yojna-2023.pdf) (**Page No. 8**) from the official website. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Director, Directorate of Culture, Madhya Pradesh.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of disability assistance under the scheme?

To provide one-time financial support to physically disabled artists and litterateurs for disability-related treatment expenses.
<br>

### Who is eligible for disability assistance?

Physically disabled artists or litterateurs aged 21 years or above are eligible.
<br>

### What is the maximum assistance amount?

The maximum assistance amount is ₹1,00,000.
<br>

### Is the assistance recurring?

No, the assistance is non-recurring and provided only once.
<br>

### Is there an income limit for eligibility?

Yes, the individual monthly income should not exceed ₹10,000 and the family income should not exceed ₹20,000.
<br>

### Is a disability certificate mandatory?

Yes, submission of a valid disability certificate is mandatory.
<br>

### Can government employees apply?

No, government employees and employees of autonomous or semi-government institutions are not eligible.
<br>

### Is Madhya Pradesh residency compulsory?

Yes, the applicant must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### What is the mode of payment?

The assistance is disbursed through direct bank transfer.
<br>

### Where can the application be submitted?

The application should be submitted to the Directorate of Culture, Madhya Pradesh.
<br>
