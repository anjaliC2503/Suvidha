## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial support to meritorious students pursuing a B.Sc. Agriculture course in their 2nd and 3rd years of study by awarding a one-year scholarship of ₹1,100 per month.

## Detailed Description

The “Dr. S.K. Mukherjee Scholarship for B.Sc. Agriculture Students (2nd &amp; 3rd Year)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support to meritorious students pursuing a B.Sc. Agriculture course in their 2nd and 3rd years of study by awarding a one-year scholarship.
<br>

## Benefits

**Rate of Scholarship:** ₹1,100/- per month.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the 2nd or 3rd year of a B.Sc. Agriculture course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The scholarship is awarded for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the students receive their payments.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scholarship?

Students enrolled in the 2nd or 3rd year of a B.Sc. Agriculture course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar are eligible.
<br>

### What is the value of the scholarship?

The scholarship provides ₹1,100/- per month.
<br>

### For how long is the scholarship awarded?

The scholarship is awarded for a duration of one year.
<br>

### How can students apply for the scholarship?

Students must submit their applications using the prescribed application form issued by the university.
<br>

### Who reviews and approves the applications?

A committee constituted by the Vice Chancellor reviews and evaluates all applications, and the Vice Chancellor grants final approval based on the committee’s recommendations.

### Can students from other universities apply?

No.
