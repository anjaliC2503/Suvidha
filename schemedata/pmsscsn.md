## Brief Description

The Directorate of Higher Education (DHE) has launched the Post Matric Scholarship to ST students of the Nagaland scheme for providing socio-economic welfare to the students belonging to the residentship of Nagaland state. The student must be studying or getting admission to the post-matric class.

## Detailed Description

The Directorate of Higher Education (DHE) has launched the Post Matric Scholarship To Scheduled Tribe Students Of Nagalandscheme for providing socio-economic welfare to the students belonging to the residentship of Nagaland state. The student must be studying or getting admission to the post-matric class (class 11th, 12, UG, PG Degree).Post-matric Scholarship for ST Scholarship is awarded to the students of the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India. The objective of the ST scholarship is to provide financial assistance to these students to complete their studies at the college level.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

1. To provide social security provisions to the students of post-metric classes.
1. Students will be able to pursue studies.
1. To provide financial assistance to these students to complete their studies at the college level.
1. Providing socio-economic welfare to the students belonging to the residentship of Nagaland state.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be pursuing post-matric studies (above Class X) and belong to the Scheduled Tribe of Nagaland.
- The applicant should have parents’/guardian’s annual income not exceeding ₹2.50 lakh from all sources.
- The applicant should be studying in an institution with a valid AISHE/UDISE code.
- The applicant should not be a repeater (students who failed in the last examination are not eligible).
- The applicant should not have availed this scholarship at the same stage of education in a different stream or subject.
- The applicant should not have a study break of more than two years.
- The applicant should not be a beneficiary of any other scholarship scheme.
- The applicant should not be employed in any Government or Semi-Government establishment.
- The applicant should be pursuing a Diploma or Certificate course of at least two years duration, if applicable, and must fulfil all the above criteria.
- The applicant should be studying under NIOS, if applicable, and must fulfil the above criteria and have passed Class 10 with English, Alternative English/MIL, Science, Mathematics, and Social Science for Class 11 or 12 level courses.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Application URL:** https://highereducation.nagaland.gov.in

Step 1: Institutions inform students about the scholarship and fix a deadline for submission of[ forms](https://highereducation.nagaland.gov.in) and documents.
Step 2: Institutions verify applications, prepare Annexure IV, and ensure the applicant’s bank account is in their own name.
Step 3: Institutions display the list of eligible/rejected students for correction before final submission.
Step 4: Submit Annexure IV (soft and hard copy) along with required documents to the Department within the deadline.
Step 5: The Head of Institution ensures all documents are complete and genuine.

## Documents Required

**Documents required for Fresh Application:**
 1) Class 10 Marksheet. 
 2) Mark Sheet (s) of the last class/year passed. For colleges with a semester system, mark sheets of the last two semesters. 
 3) Scheduled Tribe Certificate issued by the competent designated authority.
 4) Family Income Certificate issued by the competent authority from the Office of the Deputy Commissioner.
 5) Hostel Certificate: (For hostellers only) 
 a) Applicant staying in a recognized hostel outside Nagaland can produce a Hostel Certificate from the hostel warden. 
 b) Applicant staying in a recognized hostel within Nagaland should use the Hosteller Form issued by the Department. Hosteller Forms will be issued to the wardens of recognized hostels for distribution to the bonafide hostellers only.
 6) Disability Certificate, in case of differently abled applicant
 **Documents required for Renewal Application:**
 1) Mark Sheet (s) of the last class/year passed. For colleges with a semester system, mark sheets of the last two semesters. 
 2) Admission/Fee Receipt of the current year.
 3) Hostel Certificate: (For hostellers only) 
 a) Applicant staying in a recognized hostel outside Nagaland can produce a Hostel Certificate from the hostel warden. 
 b) Applicant staying in a recognized hostel within Nagaland should use the Hosteller Form issued by the Department. Hosteller Forms will be issued to the wardens of recognized hostels for distribution to the bonafide hostellers only
 4) Disability Certificate, in case of differently abled applicant.
