## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial grants to the OBC students for appearing in the UPSC Mains Exam. An amount upto ₹50,000/- is provided to each beneficiary. The assistance is extended depending on the applicant meeting the eligibility criteria and the documents submitted.

## Detailed Description

The 'Financial Assistance to OBC students appearing in the Mains Examination of UPSC' scheme, administered by the Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, is designed to provide financial grants to Other Backward Classes (OBC) students who qualify for the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) Mains Examination. Each eligible beneficiary will receive an amount of up to ₹50,000/-, subject to budgetary provisions. The disbursement of assistance is contingent upon the applicant satisfying the stipulated eligibility criteria and the successful submission of required documentation.
<br>

***A DigiLocker account is required for application. Please refer to the Application Process section for details.***

## Benefits

An amount of up to ₹50,000/- will be provided to each beneficiary, subject to budget provision.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must belong to a Other Backward Class (OBC) community of Assam.


- The applicant must have recently qualified for the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) Preliminary Examination held in 2025.
- The annual income of the applicant's parents/guardians must not exceed ₹5,00,000/-.

## Documents Required

- Caste Certificate (valid OBC certificate, inserting sub-caste)
- Parents' Annual Income Certificate, issued by the Circle Officer

- Proof of Qualification in UPSC Preliminary Examination

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum financial assistance provided under this scheme?

Eligible beneficiaries may receive up to ₹50,000/-, subject to budget availability.

### Who is eligible to apply for this financial assistance scheme for UPSC Mains Examination?

Only applicants belonging to the Other Backward Class (OBC) community of Assam, who have qualified for the UPSC Preliminary Examination, are eligible.

### Is there an income limit for applicants to qualify for this financial assistance?

Yes, the annual income of the applicant’s parents must not exceed ₹5,00,000/-.

### Do I need to submit proof of qualifying the UPSC Preliminary Exam to apply?

Yes, you must attach a valid proof of qualification in the UPSC Preliminary Examination along with your application.

### Do I need to self-attest my photograph and documents before submission?

Yes, you must paste a passport-sized photograph on the form and self-attest all required documents if necessary.

### What documents are required to prove my OBC status for this scheme?

You must submit a valid OBC caste certificate that clearly mentions your sub-caste.

### Is an income certificate mandatory for this financial assistance scheme?

Yes, you must provide an annual income certificate of your parents, issued by a Circle Officer.

### Do I need to submit my UPSC Preliminary Exam admit card with the application?

Yes, a copy of your UPSC Preliminary Examination admit card is required as proof of eligibility.

### Is a bank passbook necessary for applying under this scheme?

Yes, you must attach a self-attested copy of the front page of your passbook.

### Can I use my Aadhaar card as identity proof for this application?

Yes, a copy of your Aadhaar card is required as part of the identity verification process.

### Which government department is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, oversees the scheme’s implementation.

### When is the application window open for the 2025-26 cycle, and how can I apply?

Applications will be accepted online via the myScheme platform from 1st July 2025 to 31st August 2025.

### Is a DigiLocker account mandatory to apply?

Yes, a DigiLocker account is compulsory for application submission.

### What are the technical specifications for uploading photographs in the application?

Photos must be in JPEG/JPG format, with a file size between 50KB and 3MB.

### What file type and size are acceptable for uploading supporting documents?

Documents must be in PDF format, with each file sized between 300KB and 3MB.

### How do I create a DigiLocker account if I don’t have one?

Visit the DigiLocker portal/app, provide your Aadhaar number, mobile, email, and a 6-digit PIN, and submit the details to register.

### What credentials do I need to sign in to the myScheme platform?

Use your DigiLocker-registered mobile number and the 6-digit PIN created during DigiLocker setup to log in.

### Where can I download the DigiLocker mobile app for account creation?

The app is available on Google Play (Android) and the iOS App Store.

### What happens after I submit the application on myScheme?

You’ll receive a "Form Submitted Successfully!" confirmation and can print/download the form as a PDF.
