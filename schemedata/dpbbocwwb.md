## Brief Description

Under the scheme, eligible workers receive ₹1,000/- per month for permanent disabilities caused by conditions such as paralysis, leprosy, tuberculosis, or accidents. Additionally, a one-time assistance of ₹75,000/- is granted for total permanent disability, and ₹50,000/- for partial disability.

## Detailed Description

The &#39;Disability Pension&#39; scheme is implemented by the Bihar Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to registered construction workers with disabilities. Eligible workers receive ₹1,000/- per month for permanent disabilities caused by conditions such as paralysis, leprosy, tuberculosis, or accidents. Additionally, a one-time assistance of ₹75,000/- is granted for total permanent disability, and ₹50,000/- for partial disability. To avail of benefits under this scheme, workers should maintain an active membership with the Board.
<br>

## Benefits

Eligible registered construction workers under this scheme will receive the following financial assistance:

  - A monthly financial assistance of ₹1,000/- is given to workers with permanent disabilities caused by conditions such as paralysis, leprosy, tuberculosis, or accidents.
  - A one-time assistance of ₹75,000/- is given to workers in case of permanent total disability.
  - A one-time assistance of ₹50,000/- is given to workers in case of partial disability.

## Eligibility

1. The worker should be engaged in building or construction work.
   1. The worker should be registered with the Bihar Building &amp; Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
   1. The worker&#39;s membership with the Board should be active.
   1. The worker should have a permanent total or partial disability caused by illness or an accident to be eligible for the scheme.

## Application Process

### Online

&gt; **Registration of a Construction Worker under BBOCWWB:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
**Step 2:** On the homepage, click on &amp;quot;[Labour Registration](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/worker_reg)&amp;quot;.
**Step 3:** Enter your Aadhar card number and name, then click &amp;quot;Authenticate&amp;quot;.
**Step 4:** A dashboard will appear. Enter all required details, ensuring that all fields marked with an asterisk (*) are filled in correctly.
**Step 5:** Click on &amp;quot;Submit&amp;quot; and proceed to pay the application fee online.
**Step 6:** You will receive your construction worker registration number upon successful registration.
&gt; **Apply for Scheme Benefits:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [Official Website](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
**Step 2:** Click on &amp;quot;Login&amp;quot; and select &amp;quot;Labour&amp;quot;.
**Step 3:** Enter your registration number &amp;amp; year of Birth, click &amp;quot;Show&amp;quot; to view your details.
**Step 4:** Select the scheme you wish to apply for to avail the benefits.
**Step 5:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents.
**Step 6:** Click on &amp;quot;Submit Application&amp;quot; to complete the process. 
&gt; **Application Tracking:**

Applicant can track their application status online through the [official website](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home) of the Bihar Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number.

## Documents Required

1. Aadhaar Card
   1. Registration Number of worker
   1. Passport-size photograph
   1. Proof of residence
   1. Ration card

   1. Any other documents as required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Registered construction workers with permanent total or partial disabilities caused by illness or accidents are eligible, provided they have an active membership with the BBOCWWB.

### What financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Eligible workers receive ₹1,000/- per month for permanent disabilities. One-time assistance of ₹75,000/- is given for total permanent disability, and ₹50,000/- is granted for partial disability.

### Which types of disabilities are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers disabilities caused by conditions such as paralysis, leprosy, tuberculosis, accidents, and other serious health conditions resulting in permanent disability.

### Are both total and partial disabilities covered under the scheme?

Yes, both total and partial disabilities are covered. Total disability results in ₹75,000/- one-time assistance, and partial disability results in ₹50,000/- one-time assistance.

### How can a construction worker register for this scheme?

Construction workers can register through the official website of the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board: [https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home)
<br>

### Can unorganized workers apply for this scheme?

Yes, unorganized workers engaged in construction work, such as masons, carpenters, electricians, painters, and others, are eligible if they meet the criteria.

### What is the process for applying for benefits under this scheme?

Workers can apply for the scheme benefits through the official BBOCWWB website: [https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home](https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home)
<br>

### Which workers are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers a wide range of unorganized workers, including masons, carpenters, electricians, painters, laborers, and many others working in construction.

### How can I track my application for the scheme?

You can track the application status online through the official BBOCWWB website: https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home
<br>
