## Brief Description

The scheme reduces post-harvest losses and supports farmers by providing loans and subsidies to build small, weatherproof warehouses for safe storage and better marketing of crops.

## Detailed Description

The **Meghalaya Agriculture Warehouse Scheme,** under the Chief Minister’s CM-ELEVATE Program by the Planning, Investment Promotion & Sustainable Development Department, Government of Meghalaya, focuses on enhancing agricultural infrastructure through the establishment of warehouses across the state. It aims to reduce post-harvest losses, stabilize market prices, and improve farmer incomes. The scheme offers easy credit access via partner banks and substantial government subsidies to ease entrepreneurs’ financial burden. Implemented by the Meghalaya Basin Management Agency (MBMA), it supports individuals and registered entities in creating weatherproof storage for agricultural produce, enabling value addition, market linkages, and long-term storage for the farming community.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

1. 5% of project cost to be contributed by the entrepreneur 75% of project cost provided upfront by Government of Meghalaya as margin money support to the bank remaining 20% via bank loan.
1. Direct credit linkage with partner banks.
1. Guidance and handholding in the application process through implementing agencies.
1. Support in project realization for the warehouse venture.
1. Training and capacity building for entrepreneurs to manage post-harvest storage effectively.
1. Technical support for maintenance and operations of warehouses.

<br>

## Exclusions

**Individuals**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for the following:**
A) Pressure groups and organizations that are primarily political in nature shall not be ordinarily considered
B) Only one member from a family is eligible for the scheme
C) Following individuals (currently employed as full-fledged or contractual, or retired and drawing pension) and their immediate family members which includes spouse, parents, and children up to the age of 30:
i. State and Central Government employees
ii. Employees of public sector undertakings, subsidiaries of public sector undertakings, and divisions of public sector undertakings etc.
iii. Any individual whose job is concerned with politics such as MLAs, MLCs, MPs etc. or seeking to hold a similar position in government
iv. Registered class-1 contractors
v. Consultants who are currently engaged with state or central government
**Registered Entities**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for the following:**
i) State and Central Government employees
ii) Employees of public sector undertakings, subsidiaries of public sector undertakings and divisions of public sector undertakings etc.
iii) Any individual whose job is concerned with politics such MLAs, MLCs, MPs etc. or seeking to hold a similar position in government
iv) Registered class-1 contractors
v) Pressure groups and organizations that are primarily political in nature shall not be ordinarily considered
vi) Consultants who are currently engaged with state or central government
<br>

## Eligibility

**For Individuals:**

1. The applicant should be between 18 and 58 years of age.
1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Meghalaya.
1. The applicant should have ownership of the land where the warehouse is proposed or leased land for at least the next 10 years.
1. The applicant should not be a credit defaulter of any bank.

**For Registered Entities:**

1. The applicant should be based in the village where the program is being availed.
1. The applicant should have an active bank account and submit the bank statement of the last six months.
1. The applicant should have been in operation for at least two years.
1. The applicant should possess land, either self-owned or leased, duly verified by the competent authority.
1. The applicant should not be a credit defaulter of any bank.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Meghalaya Agriculture Warehouse Scheme?

It is a scheme to reduce post-harvest losses, stabilize market prices, and increase farmers’ incomes by supporting warehouse creation.
<br>

### Who implements the scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Meghalaya Basin Management Agency (MBMA).
<br>

### Who can benefit from the scheme?

Both individual farmers and registered entities or groups engaged in agriculture can apply.
<br>

### How is the project cost shared?

Entrepreneurs contribute 5%, the government provides 75% upfront, and 20% is via bank loan.
<br>

### Are government subsidies available?

Yes, substantial subsidies are provided to reduce the financial burden on entrepreneurs.
<br>

### Is guidance provided for bank loan applications?

Yes, implementing agencies provide handholding and guidance throughout the application process.
<br>

### Does the scheme provide technical support?

Yes, technical assistance is provided for warehouse maintenance and operations.
<br>

### What is the age criteria for individuals?

The applicants must be 18–58 years old.
<br>

### Do I need land to apply?

Yes, applicants must own the land or have leased it for at least 10 years.
<br>

### Where should the entity be located?

The entity should be based in the village where the scheme is being availed.
<br>

### Are government employees eligible?

No, state or central government employees, or pensioners, are generally excluded.
<br>

### Can public sector employees apply?

No, employees of public sector undertakings or subsidiaries are excluded.
