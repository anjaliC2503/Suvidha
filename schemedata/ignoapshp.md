## Brief Description

Under this scheme, a monthly pension is provided to elderly persons aged 60 years and above belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families to provide financial support. This is a centrally sponsored scheme for elderly members of BPL families.

## Detailed Description

The Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme, implemented by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Himachal Pradesh, is a centrally sponsored scheme that provides a monthly pension to elderly men and women aged 60 years and above belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families. 
<br>

The objective of the scheme is to provide social security and financial support to elderly persons who are members of families living below the poverty line.
<br>

## Benefits

- Beneficiaries aged 60 to 79 years: ₹1,000/-  per month
- Beneficiaries aged 70 years or above: ₹1,500/- per month

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Himachal Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be a senior citizen.
1. The applicant should be 60 years of age or older.
1. The applicant should belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant shall submit the prescribed application form to the concerned Panchayat, Tehsil Welfare Officer, or District Welfare Officer, along with the required documents.
**Step 2:** Eligible applicants are identified in the Gram Sabha meeting, and the verified list along with the proposal is forwarded to the Tehsil Welfare Officer for scrutiny and completion of formalities.
**Step 3:** After due verification, the applications are submitted to the District Welfare Officer, who grants final approval for sanction of benefits.
<br>

## Documents Required

- Age proof (Birth Certificate or School Certificate)
- Latest BPL Certificate
- Recommendation from Gram Panchayat
- Copy of Parivar Register
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank account details
- Identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Driving Licence)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme?

It is a centrally sponsored scheme providing monthly financial assistance to elderly persons from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families.
<br>

### Which department implements this scheme in Himachal Pradesh?

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Himachal Pradesh.
<br>

### What is the main objective of the scheme?

The objective is to provide social security and financial support to elderly individuals.
<br>

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Elderly persons aged 60 years or above who are members of BPL families.
<br>

### Is there any special provision for those above 80 years of age?

Yes, pensioners above 80 years receive a higher pension. However, the State Government reduced the age limit to 70 years for this higher pension from January 2018.
<br>

### What is the pension amount for those aged 60 to 79 years?

Pensioners aged 60 to 79 years receive ₹1,000 per month (₹200 Central share + ₹700 State share).
<br>

### What is the pension amount for those aged 70 years and above?

Pensioners aged 70 years and above receive ₹1,500 per month (₹200 Central share + ₹1,300 State share).
<br>

### How is the pension shared between the Central and State Government?

The Central Government provides ₹200 per month, and the State Government provides ₹1,300 per month for beneficiaries aged 70 years and above.
<br>

### Where should the application be submitted?

Applications can be submitted to the concerned Panchayat, Tehsil Welfare Officer, or District Welfare Officer.
<br>

### Is the scheme only for BPL families?

Yes, the primary eligibility is being from a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
<br>
