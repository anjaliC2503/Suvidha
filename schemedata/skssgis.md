## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial protection in case of accidental death or permanent disability to students or their guardians/heirs to help reduce the burden during unforeseen tragedies. The scheme covers students at Government and Non-Government Colleges, Gram Vidyapeeth GBTC colleges, etc.

## Detailed Description

The "Shahidvir Kinariwaala Student Security Group Insurance Scheme" was launched by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme provides financial protection in case of accidental death or permanent disability to students or their guardians/heirs to help reduce the burden during unforeseen tragedies. The scheme covers students at universities, Government and Non-Government colleges, Gram Vidyapeeth GBTC colleges, and technical education institutes across the state.
<br>

## Benefits

- Maximum financial assistance of ₹1,00,000/- is provided in case of accidental death or permanent disability. 
- Coverage includes accidental death, permanent total disability, loss of two limbs or two eyes, or one limb and one eye (100% coverage).
- Partial disability such as loss of one limb or one eye may also be covered at 50%.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
1. The applicant must be a student, guardian or heir of the student. 
1. The applicant must be studying in any university, government college, non-government college, Gram Vidyapeeth GBTC college, or technical education institute within Gujarat.
1. The student should have died in an accident or permanent disable. 
1. The death must have been due to natural cause or suicide.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:****** The interested applicant should visit the college and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attestted, if required).
**Step 3:** Duly filled and signed application form along with the documents send it to the office of the commissioner of Higher Education through the college in which you are studying.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Any student studying in universities, government or non-government colleges, GBTC, Gram Vidyapeeth, or technical institutes in Gujarat.
<br>

### What is the maximum financial assistance available?

Financial assistance up to ₹1,00,000.
<br>

### What incidents are covered under this scheme?

Incidents are covered under this scheme, including accidental death and permanent disability.
<br>

### Are natural deaths or suicides covered?

Yes, as per specific insurance policy conditions.
<br>

### Who receives the benefit in case of the student’s death?

The guardian or heir of the student.
<br>

### Is there any income limit for eligibility?

No, there is no income limit.
<br>

### What documents are mandatory?

Aadhaar card, accident report, affidavit, bank details, bonafide certificate, and other relevant certificates.
<br>

### How can the student apply?

By submitting the form through their college.
<br>

### Is the application form chargeable?

No, the application form is free.
<br>

### Can out-of-state students apply?

No, only students studying in Gujarat are eligible.
<br>
