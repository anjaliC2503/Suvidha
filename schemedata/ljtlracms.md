## Brief Description

The scheme "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department aims to promote classical music education by providing annual scholarships to eligible Gurus and their disciples across Gujarat.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to preserve and promote classical music in Gujarat by offering financial support to eligible Gurus for imparting training and to their disciples for receiving it. The scheme facilitates a 10-month classical music training period where selected Gurus can train up to 2 disciples, and both Gurus and disciples receive monetary assistance. The scheme is implemented by Gujarat Rajya Sangeet Natak Akademi, Gandhinagar, which releases a public advertisement annually inviting applications.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

> **For Gurus:**


- Financial assistance of ₹1,000/- per month for a duration of 10 months (i.e., ₹10,000/- per disciple) for training up to 2 disciples.

> **For Disciples:**


- A scholarship of ₹10,000/- per annum, calculated at ₹1,000/- per month for 10 months, for receiving classical music training from the selected Guru.

<br>

## Eligibility

> **For Gurus:**


- The applicant should be a classical music artist.
- The applicant should have completed formal education in classical music or must possess at least 7 years of experience in teaching classical music.
- The applicant should be capable of training a maximum of 2 disciples during the 10-month training period.

> **For Disciples:**


- The applicant should be a student of classical music residing in Gujarat.
- The applicant should be undergoing training under an eligible Guru selected under this scheme.
- The applicant should submit a certificate of training provided by the Guru.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Applications are invited once every year through a public advertisement by Gujarat Rajya Sangeet Natak Akademi.
**Step 2:** Applicants must submit the prescribed application form along with required documents to the designated address.
**Step 3:** The selection committee evaluates the applications and selects eligible Gurus and disciples.
**Step 4:** Both Guru and disciple must submit a report of training conducted and received over 10 months.
**Step 5:** Upon successful verification, scholarship amounts are disbursed directly to the beneficiary accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship" and what is its primary aim?

The "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship" is a government initiative by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, which aims to support the development and preservation of classical music by providing annual financial assistance to eligible classical music Gurus and their disciples for structured training over a period of ten months.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to receive financial assistance under this scheme?

Under the "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship", classical music Gurus who have either completed formal education in classical music or possess at least seven years of teaching experience, and their disciples who are pursuing classical music education under their mentorship, are eligible to receive financial assistance.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the amount of scholarship provided to Gurus and disciples under this scheme?

The eligible Gurus selected under the "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship" can receive up to ₹20,000/- per annum for training up to two disciples, while each eligible disciple can receive a scholarship of ₹10,000/- per annum, calculated at the rate of ₹1,000/- per month for ten months of music training.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the duration of the training period considered under this scheme?

The training period under the "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship" spans ten months, generally commencing in June and concluding in March, during which the Gurus are expected to provide regular classical music training to their selected disciples.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How are applications for this scheme invited and selected?

Applications for the "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship" are invited once every year through a public advertisement issued by the Gujarat Rajya Sangeet Natak Akademi, and the selection is carried out by a designated committee comprising classical music experts who evaluate the applicants based on eligibility and merit.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any limit on the number of disciples a Guru can train under this scheme?

Yes, as per the guidelines of the "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship", each Guru is allowed to train a maximum of two disciples during the training cycle in order to maintain the quality and focus of instruction.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents need to be submitted along with the application?

Applicants of the "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship" are required to submit documents such as Aadhaar Card, age proof, an attested copy of PAN Card, bank details or passbook, and a certificate of training issued by the Guru in the case of disciples.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit applicable for applicants under this scheme?

No, there is no individual or family income limit prescribed under the "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship", making it accessible to all eligible Gurus and students irrespective of their economic background.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Whom should applicants contact in case of queries or support related to the scheme?

For any queries, clarifications, or support related to the "Late Jaswantsinhji Thakor and Late Rasiklal Andhariya Classical Music Scholarship", applicants can reach out to the Member Secretary, Gujarat Rajya Sangeet Natak Akademi at Block No. 14/2, Dr. Jivraj Mehta Bhavan, Gandhinagar – 382010, or contact the office via phone at 079-23251693.
<br>
