## Brief Description

"Laptop Supply Scheme" by the School Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh provides ₹25,000/- for purchasing a laptop to students who score 80% or more in 12th Standard from a Government Higher Secondary School or a Madhya Pradesh Board (MP Board) affiliated school.

## Detailed Description

"Laptop Supply Scheme" by the School Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh provides ₹25,000/- for purchasing a laptop to students who score 80% or more in 12th Standard from a Government Higher Secondary School or a Madhya Pradesh Board (MP Board) affiliated school.

## Benefits

An amount of ₹25,000/- is provided for purchasing a laptop.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a student.
   1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
   1. The applicant should have passed the 12th Standard with 80% or above marks.

## Application Process

### Offline

The entire proceedings are prepared by the class teacher of the school and sent to the district education officer’s office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Talented Student Incentive Scheme?

It is a state-sponsored scheme that provides laptops/computers to students for higher education.

### When was the scheme started?

The scheme started on January 1, 2009.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Students who score 75% or more marks in their first attempt in the Class 12th Board of Secondary Education examination.

### Is this scheme beneficiary-oriented?

Yes, it is designed to benefit talented students.

### What is the benefit provided under this scheme?

Eligible students receive a laptop/computer or an amount of [?]25,000 for higher education.

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Class 12th students appearing in the Board of Secondary Education examination.

### Is this scheme applicable to both urban and rural students?

Yes, it covers students from both urban and rural areas.

### Where can students apply for this scheme?

No separate application is required. The benefit is provided automatically based on the eligibility list from the Board of Secondary Education.

### Who is the designated officer for this scheme?

The Commissioner of Public Education, Madhya Pradesh.

### How is the incentive amount transferred?

Once the State Government fixes the eligibility percentage, the amount is transferred directly to the student's bank account through one-click transfer.
