## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide an efficient online system for landowners in Haridwar district to get approval for their residential and commercial property maps quickly and easily, ensuring transparency and reducing delays.

## Detailed Description

The “Map Simplification Process” is implemented by the Haridwar - Roorkee Development Authority under Housing Department, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to simplify and expedite the approval of residential and commercial property maps through an efficient online system.
<br>

## Benefits

Approval of residential maps is being provided within 15 days, and approval of commercial maps within 30 days.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. All landowners in the Haridwar district of Uttarakhand are eligible.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

An online system has been set up for landowners in Haridwar district to obtain approval for their private residential and commercial properties. Landowners can submit applications from their homes and have their maps approved within the prescribed time period.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

All landowners in the Haridwar district of Uttarakhand are eligible.
<br>

### How can landowners apply for map approval?

Landowners can apply online through the official system from their homes.
<br>

### How long does it take to get a residential map approved?

Residential maps are approved within 15 days of submission.
<br>

### How long does it take to get a commercial map approved?

Commercial maps are approved within 30 days of submission.
<br>

### What types of properties are covered under this scheme?

Both private residential and commercial properties are covered under this scheme.
