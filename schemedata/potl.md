## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage research work in the field of Tiatr and facilitate writers to take up research work in different aspects of Tiatr, its Song & Music. Research work will be published in book form with remuneration provided to researchers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Preservation of Tiatr Literature" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to preserve the literature related to Tiatr/Khell Tiatr/Khell, their scripts, lyrics of songs (kantaram), music, scripts of one-act plays, and encourage script writers and lyricists to publish their works in book form. The scheme addresses the issue that, although thousands of tiatrs have been produced and staged and songs (kantaram) composed and sung over the decades, not many books of Tiatr/Khell tiatr/Khell/One Act Plays scripts and lyrics of songs (Kantaram) have been published. The scheme provides printing of scripts of tiatrs/khell tiatrs/khells, one-act plays, lyrics of kantaram with or without music in book form in Konkani in Roman Script, and payment of honorarium along with free copies to eligible writers.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Printing Services***

- Tiatr Academy of Goa will print scripts of tiatrs/khell tiatrs/khells, one-act plays, lyrics of kantaram with or without music in book form in Konkani in Roman Script.
- Minimum of 500 copies of each book will be printed.

<br>

##### ***Category-wise Benefits***
> ***Category I (Deceased or Inactive Tiatr Artistes)***


- One-time honorarium as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for each tiatr script/anthology of songs.
- 25 copies of books will be given to the writer or legal heir.
- Books will be published by Tiatr Academy of Goa from its funds allotted to the scheme.

> ***Category II (Active Tiatr Artistes)***


- 200 books will be given to the artiste.
- One-time honorarium as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa for each tiatr script/anthology of songs.
- Books will be published by Tiatr Academy of Goa from its funds allotted to the scheme.

> ***Category III (Individual Artiste/Publisher Self-Publishing)***


- 100% of the cost of printing will be paid by Tiatr Academy of Goa on proper verification of rates.
- Must give 100 books to Tiatr Academy of Goa free of cost.
- Not entitled for any honorarium from Tiatr Academy of Goa.

<br>

##### ***Conditions***
_*The manuscript must adhere to traditional formats as approved by Tiatr Academy of Goa._
_*There should not be any adverse remark made in any form either written or verbal against any Governments/Individuals/Institutions or anything against caste, creed and religion in the scripts/lyrics of songs which may hurt the sentiments of anybody._
_*If proved to contain adverse remarks, Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right not to print the book and refrain from making payment or recall the payment made to the concerned._
<br>

## Eligibility

##### **General Eligibility**
The applicant must be a tiatr artiste or the legal heir of a deceased tiatr artiste.
##### **Category-wise Eligibility**
***Category I:***

- The applicant must be a tiatr artiste who has expired or is living but not active on the tiatr stage.
- The applicant must submit scripts of Tiatr/Khell tiatr/Khell/One Act Plays.
- The applicant must have a minimum collection of 50 songs with or without musical scores if submitting only songs (kantaram).

***Category II:***

- The applicant must be a tiatr artist who is active on the tiatr stage.
- The applicant must submit scripts of Tiatr/Khell tiatr/Khell/One Act Plays.
- The applicant must have a minimum collection of 100 songs with or without musical scores if submitting only songs (kantaram).

***Category III:***

- The applicant must be an individual artist/publisher who wishes to publish Scripts of Tiatr/Khell tiatr/Khell/One Act Plays on his/her own.
- The applicant must ensure the book conforms to all norms of the scheme.

##### ***Preference***
Scripts/lyrics/music written by tiatr artistes of yesteryears will be given first preference.
<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields: name of the applicant. Attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
<br>

> ***Post-Application Processes***

**Step 1:** _Recommendation Board Review_
The submitted manuscript will be reviewed by the Recommendation Board consisting of 2 members appointed by Tiatr Academy of Goa who will recommend whether the books can be published or not.
**Step 2:** _Approval and Soft Copy Submission_
If approved by the Recommendation Board, submit a soft copy of the manuscript on a Compact Disc/Digital Versatile Disc/pen drive to Tiatr Academy of Goa.
**Step 3:** _Editorial Review_
The approved manuscript will be edited by a member of Editorial Board for spelling and orthography checking.
**Step 4:** _Publication Process_
Upon successful completion of all review processes, Tiatr Academy of Goa will proceed with printing minimum 500 copies of the book in Konkani in Roman Script.
**Step 5:** _Benefit Distribution_
Receive the designated benefits as per the applicable category including honorarium payment and free book copies as specified in the scheme guidelines.
<br>

> ***Application Processing***

_*Applications will be processed on 'First Come First Serve' basis._
_*Applications will be considered as per the availability of funds._
_*Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to make any changes in the rules._
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the different categories of applicants and how do the benefits differ between them for this literature preservation initiative?

There are 3 categories: Category I covers deceased or inactive tiatr artistes who receive 25 free copies and honorarium; Category II covers active tiatr artistes who get 200 free copies and honorarium; Category III covers individual artistes or publishers who get 100% printing cost coverage but no honorarium and must provide 100 books to the Academy.
<br>

### As someone wanting to submit only song lyrics without any dramatic scripts, what is the minimum collection requirement I need to meet?

If you are submitting only songs (kantaram) with or without musical scores, you need a minimum collection of 50 songs if you fall under Category I (deceased or inactive artiste), or 100 songs if you fall under Category II (active artiste).
<br>

### Can I apply for this literature preservation support if I am not personally involved in tiatr but am related to someone who was?

Yes, you can apply as a legal heir of a deceased tiatr artiste. You must be able to establish your legal relationship to the deceased artiste who was involved in tiatr performances or creation.
<br>

### What happens if my submitted manuscript contains content that criticizes government policies or makes comments about religious communities?

The Academy reserves the right not to print your book and can refrain from making payment or even recall payments already made if your scripts or lyrics contain adverse remarks against any governments, individuals, institutions, or anything against caste, creed, and religion that may hurt sentiments.
<br>

### In what specific language and script format must my manuscript be prepared for consideration under this preservation initiative?

Your manuscript must be prepared in Konkani language written in Roman Script format. All scripts, lyrics, and musical compositions must adhere to this language and script requirement as specified by the Academy.
<br>

### How many physical copies of my manuscript do I need to submit along with my application for review?

You must submit 3 typed copies of your manuscript along with your application. Additionally, you also need to provide a soft copy on a Compact Disc, Digital Versatile Disc, or Pen Drive.
<br>

### What is the minimum number of book copies that will be printed if my manuscript gets approved for publication?

The Academy will print a minimum of 500 copies of each approved book. This ensures adequate distribution and preservation of the tiatr literature for future generations and cultural documentation.
<br>

### If I am currently performing and writing for tiatr productions, which category should I apply under and what benefits can I expect?

As an active tiatr artiste, you should apply under Category II. You will receive 200 copies of your published book, a one-time honorarium fixed by the Academy for each script or anthology, and the Academy will fund the entire publication from its allocated scheme funds.
<br>

### Are there any financial documents I need to provide as part of my application submission process?

Yes, you must submit a self-attested copy of your Permanent Account Number Card along with a self-attested copy of your Aadhaar Card as part of the required documentation.
<br>

### What type of dramatic works are eligible for consideration beyond traditional full-length tiatr productions?

Besides full-length tiatrs, you can submit scripts of Khell tiatr, Khell productions, and One Act Plays. The scheme covers various forms of dramatic literature within the tiatr tradition and related theatrical formats.
<br>

### If I want to publish my work independently but still receive financial support, what obligations do I have toward the Academy?

Under Category III, you must ensure your book conforms to all scheme norms, provide 100 books to the Academy free of cost, and you will receive 100% printing cost reimbursement but no honorarium payment.
<br>

### How can I obtain the official application form required for submitting my manuscript for consideration?

The prescribed application form is available at the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa. You need to collect this specific form as it is required along with your other documents for application submission.
<br>

### What additional documentation do I need if I am applying as a publisher on behalf of an author rather than as the original creator?

If you are applying as a publisher, you must provide a written No Objection Certificate from the original author in addition to all other required documents including manuscript copies and identification papers.
<br>

### Which type of tiatr literature receives priority consideration during the evaluation and selection process?

Scripts, lyrics, and music written by tiatr artistes of yesteryears are given first preference during the evaluation process. This prioritization helps preserve historical and culturally significant works from earlier generations.
<br>
