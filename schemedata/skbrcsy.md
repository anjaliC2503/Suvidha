## Brief Description

Chhattisgarh State Government started a scheme "Safai Karmakar Bahay Rogi Chikitsa Sahayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 11 March 2015.

## Detailed Description

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &quot;Safai Karmakar Bahay Rogi Chikitsa Sahayata Yojana&quot; for unorganized worker on  11 March 2015. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing required financial assistance for medical situation.

## Benefits

> Medical assistance up to Rs. 2,000/- in a year.

## Eligibility

1. Safai Worker must be registered Unorganized worker of State Social Security Board.
1. Safai worker must be between 18 to 60 years.
1. Family members details must be mentioned in the registration card. 
1. Benefits will be provided only safai worker and their family members (husband, wife, son and daughter) mentioned in the registration card.
1. The benefit of the scheme will be available only up to two children.
1. If the benefit of the scheme is being received from any other parallel scheme of the State Government, then the benefit of this scheme will not be available.

## Application Process

### Online

**Unregistered Applicant :**

1. All the applicant have to visit the official [website](https://cglabour.nic.in) Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 1. On the home page click on "[Apply](https://cglabour.nic.in/Departments.aspx?id=4)" under "Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board".1. Enter the following details

   1. Select Group Name : "Asangathit Marmakaar Mandal"
   1. Select Service : "Asangathit Shramik Panjikaran"
   1. What do you want to do :  "Aavedan"
   1. Click next.

1. Check the eligibility criteria for registration in Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board and click next. 
1. Fill out the online "Registration form".
1. Submit.
