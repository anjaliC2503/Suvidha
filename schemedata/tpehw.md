## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide community-service-based skill development and employment opportunities for women in the field of animal husbandry. Eligible women members of Self-Help Groups under the Uttarakhand State Rural Livelihood Mission can apply.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Training Program for A-HELP Women Workers” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Livestock Development Board (ULDB) under the Department of Animal Husbandry, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide community-service-based skill development and employment opportunities for women in the field of animal husbandry.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, community-service-based job creation or job filling is carried out between the Animal Husbandry Department and Animal Husbandry by A-HELP workers.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a woman member of a self-help group formed under the Uttarakhand State Rural Livelihood Mission.
   1. The applicant must have a minimum educational qualification of eighth pass.
   1. The applicant must have completed 7 days of Pashu Sakhi training.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Eligible women selected by the Uttarakhand State Rural Livelihood Mission may apply.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this training program?

Women members of self-help groups formed under the Uttarakhand State Rural Livelihood Mission, with a minimum educational qualification of eighth pass, are eligible.
<br>

### Is prior training required for applicants?

Yes, applicants must have completed 7 days of Pashu Sakhi training.
<br>

### How are eligible women selected?

Eligible women are selected by the Uttarakhand State Rural Livelihood Mission based on their qualifications and participation in relevant training programs.
<br>

### What type of employment does the scheme provide?

The scheme facilitates job creation and deployment of A-HELP workers in coordination with the Animal Husbandry Department, allowing women to work in animal husbandry services.
<br>

### How long is the Pashu Sakhi training?

The Pashu Sakhi training is conducted for 7 days.
<br>

### How can women apply for this scheme?

Eligible women are selected and notified by the Uttarakhand State Rural Livelihood Mission.
<br>
