## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote the establishment and growth of aromatic industries in Aroma Park, SIIDCUL, Kashipur by providing financial incentives, concessional facilities, and technical support to entrepreneurs, thereby fostering industrial development and economic growth in the region.

## Detailed Description

The “Incentives by MSME Department for Setting Up of Aromatic Industries in Aroma Park located at SIIDCUL, Kashipur” scheme is implemented by the Centre for Aromatic Plants (CAP), Selaqui, Dehradun, under the Department of Horticulture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote the establishment and growth of aromatic industries in Aroma Park, SIIDCUL, Kashipur by providing financial incentives, concessional facilities, and technical support to entrepreneurs, thereby fostering industrial development and economic growth in the region.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, industries to be set up in Aroma Park are provided with investment promotion assistance, interest subsidy, reimbursement of SGST, exemption from stamp duty, exemption from market fees, and supply of electricity at concessional rates, as detailed in the table below:
**Sr. No.****Criteria****Incentives for units to be set up**1Investment promotion assistance40% (maximum ₹40 lakh) on the fixed capital investment made in plant and machinery and workshop building of the enterprise2Interest Subsidy10% of the interest payable on term loans taken for construction of workshop building and purchase of plant &amp;amp;amp; machinery for a period of 5 years from the date of commencement of production (maximum ₹8 lakh per year per unit)3Reimbursement of SGST100% of the net SGST liability on sales made to customers within the state (B2C) for a period of 5 years from the date of commencement of production4Exemption from Stamp DutyFull exemption from stamp duty charges payable on registration of sale deed/lease deed for land for establishment of the enterprise5Exemption from Market Fee100% exemption from market fee on raw materials for a period of 5 years from the date of commencement of production6Supply of Electricity at Concessional RatesFor a period of 5 years from the date of commencement of production, electricity will be supplied at concessional rates applicable for irrigation/tubewell (currently ₹1.55 per unit) along with uninterrupted supply<br>

## Eligibility

1. Entrepreneurs/ individuals interested in setting up an aromatic industry in Aroma Park will be eligible under this scheme.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- For the establishment of aromatic industries in Aroma Park, SIIDCUL invites online applications through advertisements posted on its website: [https://esiidcul.com/eprocure/home](https://esiidcul.com/eprocure/home). Applications for vacant plots in Aroma Park are open for a fixed period. Details regarding plot sizes and the Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) are available on the SIIDCUL website.  - Applicants can apply online for their preferred plot based on the plot size and submit the required EMD. After considering all applications received for specific plots within the specified period, industrial plots will be allotted, subject to availability, following the procedure outlined by SIIDCUL. Online bidding is conducted, and the highest bidder is awarded the plot. The successful applicant is issued an allotment letter.
  - More information about vacant plots and advertisements can be obtained from w[ww.siidcul.com](https://siidcul.com/). The Centre for Aromatic Plants (CAP) provides technical assistance to entrepreneurs interested in setting up industries in Aroma Park.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Entrepreneurs or individuals interested in setting up an aromatic industry in Aroma Park, SIIDCUL, Kashipur, are eligible to avail of the scheme benefits.
<br>

### What types of incentives are provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides:

- Investment promotion assistance
- Interest subsidy on term loans
- Reimbursement of SGST
- Exemption from stamp duty
- Exemption from market fees
- Electricity supply at concessional rates

<br>

### What is the Investment Promotion Assistance offered?

40% of the fixed capital investment made in plant and machinery and workshop building of the enterprise is provided as assistance, up to a maximum of ₹40 lakh.
<br>

### How does the SGST reimbursement work?

100% of the net SGST liability on sales made to intra-state customers (B2C) is reimbursed for a period of 5 years from the date of commencement of production.
<br>

### How can one apply for a plot in Aroma Park?

Applications are invited online through advertisements posted on SIIDCUL’s e-procurement website: [https://esiidcul.com/eprocure/home](https://esiidcul.com/eprocure/home).
<br>

### What information is required to apply?

Applicants need to select their preferred plot size and submit the Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) online as specified in the advertisement.
<br>

### How are plots allotted?

Industrial plots are allotted based on the online bidding process. The highest bidder is awarded the plot, subject to availability, following the process outlined by SIIDCUL.
<br>

### When is the allotment letter issued?

The successful applicant is issued an allotment letter after the completion of the bidding and selection process.
<br>

### Where can one find details about vacant plots?

Details about vacant plots, plot sizes, EMD, and advertisements are available on the SIIDCUL website: [www.siidcul.com](https://siidcul.com/).
<br>
