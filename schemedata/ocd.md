## Brief Description

The "Orientation Courses in Dramatics" scheme by the Tiatr Academy of Goa aims to improve the quality of tiatr performances. It offers preliminary training and workshops in various aspects of dramatics, such as script writing and acting, to individuals and existing tiatr artistes.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Orientation Courses in Dramatics" by the Tiatr Academy of Goa aims to improve the quality of tiatr performances. The scheme provides orientation courses and workshops covering script writing, lyrics composition, direction, acting, stage setting, stage craft, background music, light effects, costumes, make-up, and the history of tiatr. These workshops are conducted as per the syllabus formulated by the Tiatr Academy of Goa, led by graduates of the National School of Drama or Kala Academy School of Drama, with tiatr-specific aspects handled by personnel involved in the field. The training is preliminary, intended to help tiatr artistes enhance performance standards. The applicant must be an individual interested in training in tiatr's various aspects, including song and music performances, and must submit a self-attested copy of their Aadhaar Card with the application. The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. Applications for this scheme are accepted through the local press or by invitation to Konkani Dramatic Troupes.
<br>

## Benefits

• **Training and Skill Enhancement:** The scheme provides orientation courses and workshops in various aspects of dramatics, including script writing, lyrics composition, direction, acting, stage setting, stage craft, background music, light effects, costumes, make-up, and the history of tiatr. These courses and workshops are intended to help tiatr artistes improve the standard and quality of tiatr performances.
• **Expert Instruction:** Workshops are conducted by graduates of the National School of Drama or Kala Academy School of Drama, with tiatr-specific aspects handled by personnel involved in the field of tiatr, to ensure high-quality and relevant training for participants.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be an individual.
- The applicant must be interested in getting trained in various aspects of tiatr, including its song and music performances.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the copies of the mandatory documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
*The application will be considered on a 'First Come First Serve' basis and is subject to the availability of funds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What documentation do I need to provide when applying for training in traditional Goan theatrical arts and performance techniques?

You must submit a self-attested copy of your Aadhaar Card along with your application. This is the only document required for participation in the orientation courses.
<br>

### Can individuals who have no prior experience in theatrical performances still apply for these comprehensive dramatics training programs?

Yes, any individual can apply as long as they have a genuine interest in learning various aspects of traditional Goan theatre, including song and music performances. Prior experience is not mentioned as a requirement.
<br>

### What specific areas of theatrical training and performance skills are covered in these comprehensive orientation workshops?

The courses cover script writing, lyrics composition, direction, acting, stage setting, stage craft, background music, light effects, costumes, make-up, and the history of traditional Goan theatre forms.
<br>

### How does the selection process work for applicants wanting to participate in these theatrical training courses and workshops?

All applicants must undergo an interview conducted by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. The Academy will evaluate candidates during this interview process to determine their selection for the courses.
<br>

### Who are the qualified instructors responsible for conducting these specialized theatrical training sessions and dramatic arts workshops?

The workshops are led by graduates from the National School of Drama or Kala Academy School of Drama. Traditional theatre-specific aspects are handled by personnel actively involved in the field.
<br>

### What is the primary objective and intended outcome for participants completing these orientation courses in dramatic arts?

The training is preliminary and specifically designed to help traditional theatre artistes enhance their performance standards and improve the overall quality of theatrical presentations.
<br>

### Are there any specific conditions or rules that participants must follow throughout the duration of these training programs?

Yes, participants are expected to abide by all scheme rules. The Tiatr Academy of Goa also reserves the right to make changes to the scheme rules as needed.
<br>

### How are applications typically accepted and processed for these theatrical training and orientation programs?

Applications are accepted through announcements in local press or by direct invitation to Konkani Dramatic Troupes. The Academy manages the application process through these channels.
<br>

### What type of individual applicant profile is most suitable for these comprehensive dramatics and performance training courses?

The applicant must be an individual with a genuine interest in learning various aspects of traditional Goan theatre, including song and music performances. No other specific qualifications are mentioned.
<br>

### Can existing theatrical groups or troupes apply collectively for these orientation courses and specialized training workshops?

Applications are accepted from individuals only. However, invitations may be extended to Konkani Dramatic Troupes, suggesting possible group participation through invitation processes.
<br>

### What is the overall structure and level of training provided through these theatrical arts orientation courses?

The training is described as preliminary and follows a syllabus specifically formulated by the Tiatr Academy of Goa to ensure systematic learning progression.
<br>

### Who is responsible for implementing and overseeing these comprehensive theatrical training programs and orientation courses?

The Tiatr Academy of Goa is the implementing agency responsible for conducting these courses and managing all aspects of the training program.
<br>

### How does the Academy determine the scheduling and timing for these theatrical training classes and workshop sessions?

The Tiatr Academy of Goa decides specific class days based on member suggestions or course requirements, ensuring flexible scheduling to meet participant needs.
<br>

### What happens after successful completion of the interview process for these dramatic arts training programs?

Upon selection through the interview, participants will attend the preliminary training sessions designed to improve traditional theatre performance standards and overall presentation quality.
<br>
