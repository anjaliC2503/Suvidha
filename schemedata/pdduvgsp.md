## Brief Description

The project aims to promote sustainable rural development through the application of science & technology to enhance livelihoods in selected villages throughout Uttarakhand. Under the project, rural development initiatives are implemented through the formation of village clusters across the state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyay Vigyaan Gram Sankul Pariyojana” is funded by the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India, and implemented by the Uttarakhand State Council for Science and Technology (UCOST) under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Uttarakhand. The project aims to promote sustainable rural development through the application of science and technology to enhance livelihoods in selected villages throughout Uttarakhand.
<br>

## Benefits

- Under the project, rural development work is carried out by forming clusters of villages across various districts of the state. 
  - Based on the natural resources available in the selected villages, various units are established to improve farmers&#39; livelihoods through the application of science and technology.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiaries of the project include selected farmers, entrepreneurs, self-help groups, and general public residing in Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

The project is being implemented through the formation of Gramodaya Cooperative Societies, with experienced farmers selected from the chosen clusters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who implements the project?

The project is implemented by the Uttarakhand State Council for Science and Technology (UCOST) under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Uttarakhand.
<br>

### Which areas are covered under the scheme?

The project is implemented in selected villages across various districts of Uttarakhand.
<br>

### Who can benefit from this project?

Selected farmers, entrepreneurs, self-help groups, and members of the general public residing in Uttarakhand are eligible beneficiaries.
<br>

### How are the villages selected for the project?

Villages are selected based on the availability of natural resources and the potential for livelihood improvement through technology interventions.
<br>

### What is the role of Gramodaya Cooperative Societies in the project?

Gramodaya Cooperative Societies are formed by selecting experienced farmers from the village clusters to manage and implement project activities locally.
