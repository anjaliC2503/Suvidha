## Brief Description

The “Training of Farmers on Pulses Production Technologies” scheme aims to educate farmers on advanced pulses production technologies to enhance crop yield.

## Detailed Description

The “Training of Farmers on Pulses Production Technologies” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, offers financial assistance for training programs to educate farmers on advanced pulses production technologies.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹15,000/- per batch for 50 farmers.- **Training Program:** Training on advanced pulse production techniques.

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary should be a Farmer.
  - The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.
  - The beneficiary should raise seed farms with Pulses crop.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office.
**Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). 
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office:
> **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer.

> **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer.

> **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.

## Documents Required

1. Proof of identity (Aadhar card, Voter ID).
1. Land ownership documents or lease agreement.
1. Proof of cultivation of pulses.
1. Caste Certificate (for SC/ST applicants).
1. Any other documents (if required).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the “Training of Farmers on Pulses Production Technologies” scheme?

This scheme provides training on modern techniques for pulses production, along with financial assistance to participating farmers.

### Who qualifies for this scheme?

Farmers who cultivate pulses are eligible, with special focus on SC/ST (24%) and women farmers (20%).

### Are there any age or income limitations?

No, there are no specific age or income requirements for this scheme.

### What financial support is provided under this scheme?

Each batch of 50 farmers receives [?]15,000/- in financial assistance for the training.

### How can farmers apply for this scheme?

Applications can be submitted to the Assistant Agricultural Officer at the village level, or to the relevant agricultural officials at the block and district levels.

### What documents are necessary for the application?

Applicants need to provide identity proof, land ownership documents, caste certificates (if applicable), and evidence of pulse cultivation.

### Is there a reservation for women farmers?

Yes, 20% of the beneficiaries under this scheme are reserved for women farmers.

### Is the scheme available for farmers cultivating other crops?

No, it is specifically designed for farmers growing pulses.

### What are the advantages of participating in the training program?

Farmers will gain knowledge on advanced techniques that can help improve the yield and profitability of pulse crops.
