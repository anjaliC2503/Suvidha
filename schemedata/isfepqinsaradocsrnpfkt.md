## Brief Description

Under this scheme to motivate growers to improve production and productivity through systematic replanting of diseased, old, and senile plantations, and to undertake area expansion.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom – Small Cardamom Replanting/New Planting for Kerala & Tamil Nadu” is implemented by the Spices Board under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. The scheme aims to motivate growers to improve production and productivity through the systematic replanting of diseased, old, and senile plantations, along with area expansion initiatives, as per the modalities and guidelines approved by the Competent Authority for the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

## Benefits

1. 33.33% of cost of cultivation of subject to a maximum of ₹1,00,000/- per Ha for General Category and 75% of cost of cultivation ₹2,10,000/- per Ha for SC/ST payable in two annual installments of ₹50,000/- and ₹1,05,000/- each year respectively.
1. Individual registered cardamom growers having a land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha.
1. Minimum area to be replanted under the programme is 0.10 Ha in a continuous block and the maximum area is limited to 2 Ha per beneficiary per year and a maximum of 8 Ha. in block year of 5 years from 2021-2026.
1. The plant population is minimum 1100 Nos. per ha.
1. Replanting cycle is considered as 8 years and a second time benefit for the same area replanted by a beneficiary will be available only on completion of 8 years except on exclusive circumstances like natural calamities, devastating pest and disease attacks certified by a committee constituted by the Board.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to Kerala or Tamil Nadu and undertake Small Cardamom replanting/new planting activities under the scheme.
1. The plantation/site will be subject to final inspection by the Designated Officials, and only eligible cases recommended by them will be considered for assistance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme aims to improve the production and productivity of Small Cardamom through systematic replanting of diseased, old, and senile plantations and by promoting area expansion.
<br>

### Which states are covered under this scheme?

The scheme is applicable only in Kerala and Tamil Nadu.
<br>

### What is the minimum area required for replanting/new planting?

The minimum area required is 0.10 hectare in a continuous block.
<br>

### What is the maximum area eligible for assistance?

A maximum of 2 hectares per beneficiary per year is eligible, subject to an overall limit of 8 hectares during the scheme period from 2021–2026.
<br>

### What financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

General Category growers are eligible for 33.33% of the cost of cultivation, subject to a maximum of ₹1,00,000 per hectare.
SC/ST growers are eligible for 75% of the cost of cultivation, subject to a maximum of ₹2,10,000 per hectare.
<br>

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for availing benefits?

Yes, submission of Aadhaar Card is mandatory for processing the application.
<br>

### What is the minimum plant population required?

The plantation should maintain a minimum plant population of 1100 plants per hectare.
<br>

### Can a beneficiary avail the scheme benefit again for the same land?

Yes, but only after completion of the replanting cycle of 8 years.
<br>

### Are there any exceptions for reapplying before 8 years?

Yes. In exceptional cases such as natural calamities or severe pest and disease attacks, benefits may be considered earlier based on certification by the committee constituted by the Spices Board.
<br>

### Who will inspect the plantation?

Designated Officials of the Spices Board will conduct the final inspection and recommend eligible cases.
<br>

### Under which period is this scheme implemented?

The scheme is implemented during the 15th Finance Commission period from 2021–22 to 2025–26.
<br>
