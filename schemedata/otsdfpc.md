## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide supplementary financial assistance to displaced families for assured and sustained earnings. Through this scheme, one-time financial assistance of ₹5,50,000/- per family is provided to eligible displaced families of PoK and Chhamb settled in Jammu & Kashmir.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Central Assistance for One Time Settlement of Displaced Families from PoK and Chhamb under Prime Minister's Development Package" has been launched by the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India, and provides one-time financial assistance to displaced families of PoK and Chhamb settled in Jammu & Kashmir. aims to provide supplementary financial assistance so as to enable the families to run small business or to undertake scientific farming, livestock or other land-based activities, which can provide the displaced families some assured and sustained earning. The amount of assistance can also be invested in any annuity scheme, which can give an assured monthly income on a sustainable basis. The scheme will have a State share of ₹308 and a Central share of ₹5,49,692 per family, amounting to ₹5,50,000 per displaced family.
<br>

## Benefits

1. The applicant is provided with one-time financial assistance of ₹5,50,000 per displaced family.
1. The assistance consists of:

  - Central Government share: ₹5,49,692 per family.
  - State Government share: ₹308 per family.

1. The financial assistance can be used by displaced families for:

  - Running small businesses.
  - Undertaking scientific farming.
  - Undertaking livestock activities.
  - Undertaking other land-based activities for assured and sustained earnings.

1. The assistance amount may also be invested in an annuity scheme to provide assured monthly income on a sustainable basis.

> **Mode of Disbursement:**


- The Central Assistance of ₹5,49,692 per family is transferred directly to eligible beneficiaries through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) by the Central Government.
- The State Government's share of ₹308 per family is transferred to beneficiaries through DBT.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be the Claimant/Head of the Displaced Person (DP) family or his/her successor or heir residing within the State of Jammu & Kashmir.
1. The applicant should belong to the overall category of 36,384 displaced families.
1. The applicant should belong to any of the following categories:


- Pakistan Occupied Jammu & Kashmir 1947 Displaced Persons.
- Chhamb 1965 and 1971 (camp/non-camp) Displaced Persons.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The applicant details are collected by the Government of Jammu & Kashmir from eligible beneficiaries.
- The applicant has to provide details such as Aadhaar Number and Bank Account details.
- The Government of Jammu & Kashmir authenticates the beneficiary details and uploads the information in the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) module.
- After verification and recommendation by the State Government, the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India, transfers the Central Assistance directly to eligible beneficiaries through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- The State Government transfers its share of ₹308 per family directly into the beneficiary’s bank account through DBT.
- All transactions are completed through bank transfer, and no cash transactions are made.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will the financial assistance under this scheme be provided on an yearly basis?

No. The financial assistance will be a one-time assistance.

### Can I invest the amount of the financial assistance in an annuity scheme?

Yes, the amount of assistance can also be invested in any annuity scheme, which can give an assured monthly income on sustainable basis.

### What if the total amount of assistance that a displaced family will receive under this scheme?

The assistance will be Rs 5,50,000/- per displaced family.

### What would be the mode of payment of the assistance?

The central assistance will be distributed through DBT.

### Can I opt to receive the financial assistance in cash?

No. All transaction should be through Bank and no cash transaction shall be made.

### I have a nuclear family. Am I eligible to apply?

Yes, this scheme is open to nuclear families as well.

### My family was displaced from Ladakh. Is my family eligible to receive assistance under this scheme?

Yes, displaced families from Ladakh are also eligible.

### I adopted a child months after I was displaced from Chhamb. Will she be considered as part of the family for the purpose of this scheme?

Yes, adopted child will also be considered as part of the family.

### Is the amount of financial assistance subject to the number of members in the family?

No, the financial assistance of Rs 5,50,000/- will be the same for all the eligible families.

### How will my family be identified under this scheme?

For identification of the beneficiaries, the government of J&K may stipulate credible and verifiable documents. The state government will verify the documents and recommend the name of the beneficiaries along with copy of Aadhar card and Aadhar linked bank account number of the beneficiaries to the MHA.

### How will the govt. recognize/identify a displaced family?

For identification of the beneficiaries, the government of J&K may stipulate credible and verifiable documents. The state government will verify the documents and recommend the name of the beneficiaries along with copy of Aadhar card and Aadhar linked bank account number of the beneficiaries to the MHA.

### Is there a deadline to this scheme?

No, as per the scheme guidelines, there is no deadline.
