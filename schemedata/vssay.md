## Brief Description

Chhattisgarh state Government started a scheme "Viranarayan Singh Shram Ann Yojana" for unorganized worker on 17 January 2018.

## Detailed Description

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme &amp;quot;Viranarayan Singh Shram Ann Yojana&amp;quot; for unorganized worker on 17 January 2018. The main objective of the scheme to provide the food assistance for unorganized worker at their work places.

## Benefits

1. Hot food is being distributed to construction workers at ₹ 5/- in food centers.
1. Cooked rice, dal, vegetable or khichdi, fai dal, vegetable or cumin rice, fried dal, vegetable.
1. Pickles will also be payable along with the food.

## Eligibility

- Registered unorganized workers are eligible for benefits.

## Application Process

### Offline

- There is no need to apply to avail the benefits.
- Beneficiaries can avail benefits by visiting their nearest food distribution center.

### What is this scheme ?

### What are the benefits?

Provide food at subsidized rate.

### Who can get the benefits?

All eligible registered unorganized labor individuals from Chhattisgarh State.

### Can applicant get the benefits multiple times?

Yes.

### What is the eligible age to get the benefits?

There is no age restriction for the scheme.

### What is the procedure to apply for the scheme?

There is no need to apply for the scheme. Beneficiaries will get the benefits at the their nearest food centers.

### How can applicant register under Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board" ?

1. All the applicant have to visit the official website Chhattisgarh Labour Department. 
2. On the home page click on "Apply" under "Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board". 
3. Enter the following details, 
Select Group Name : "Asangathit Marmakaar Mandal" 
Select Service : "Asangathit Shramik Panjikaran" 
What do you want to do :  "Aavedan"Click next.
4. Check the eligibility criteria for registration in Chhattisgarh Unorganized Workers State Social Security Board and click next. 
5. Fill out the online "Registration form".
6. Submit.
