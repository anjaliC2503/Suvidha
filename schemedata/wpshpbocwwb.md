## Brief Description

The "Widow Pension Scheme" launched by HPBOCWWB is designed to support widows of registered beneficiaries. Through this initiative, the Board will disburse a monthly amount of ₹1,500/- to help widows manage their expenses and improve their quality of life.

## Detailed Description

The "Widow Pension Scheme" launched by the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HPBOCWWB) is designed to support widows of registered beneficiaries. Through this initiative, the Board will disburse a monthly amount of ₹1,500/- to help widows manage their expenses and improve their quality of life.

## Benefits

Monthly Pension of ₹1,500/-

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be the widow of a deceased construction worker.
1. The deceased worker should have been registered with the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HPBOCWWB).
1. The deceased worker should have maintained regular membership with the Board.

## Application Process

### Online

**Scheme Application Process:**
**Step 1:** The applicant must visit the [official website](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/index.aspx) of the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
**Step 2:** Click on the option "[Apply for Benefits.](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/PublicPages/ApplyForSchemes.aspx)"
**Step 3:** Enter your beneficiary details and date of birth, then click "Search" to view your information.
**Step 4:** Select the scheme you wish to apply for to avail of the benefits.
**Step 5:**  Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted.
**Step 6:** Finally, click on "Submit Application" to complete the process.

**Application Tracking:**
Applicants can track their application status online through the[ official website](https://bocw.hp.nic.in/index.aspx) of the Himachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Widow Pension Scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance of [?]1,500 per month to widows of registered construction workers under HPBOCWWB.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

The applicant must be the widow of a deceased construction worker who was registered with HPBOCWWB and maintained an active membership.

### How much pension is provided under this scheme?

A monthly pension of [?]1,500 is provided to eligible widows.

### Is this scheme available to all widows?

No, only widows of registered construction workers under HPBOCWWB can apply.

### How can I apply for the Widow Pension Scheme?

Applications can be submitted at the Labour Welfare Office or online through the HPBOCWWB official portal.

### Will the pension be transferred to my bank account?

Yes, the pension is directly credited to the widow's registered bank account.

### Can a widow apply if the worker’s membership was inactive?

No, the deceased worker must have had an active membership with HPBOCWWB at the time of death.

### Is there any time limit to apply after the worker's death?

There is no strict deadline, but early application is advised for timely financial support.

### Will the pension continue for a lifetime?

The pension continues as long as the widow meets the eligibility criteria. Changes in marital status may affect eligibility.

### Can a widow receive this pension along with other government pensions?

Yes, this scheme can be availed along with other eligible government pensions.
