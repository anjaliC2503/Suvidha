## Brief Description

The "Scheme of Assistance for Environment Protection Measures" under the Gujarat Industrial Policy promotes sustainable industrial growth by supporting industries in adopting eco-friendly practices and ensuring compliance with environmental norms, effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme of Assistance for Environment Protection Measures&quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative promotes sustainable industrial growth by supporting industries in adopting eco-friendly practices and ensuring compliance with environmental norms.
The component &quot;Scheme for Assistance to Environment Management&quot; is designed to promote the adoption of cleaner production technologies and advanced pollution control equipment to improve environmental management. It aims to optimize raw material use, reduce energy and water consumption, and minimize waste generation in industries.

## Benefits

<br>

**Eligible Activity****Quantum of Assistance per project** 1. Implementation of cleaner production technology in place of existing processes such as substitution and optimization of raw materials, reduction water consumption or energy consumption, or waste generation1. Upto 35% of the cost of plant and machinery with a ceiling of ₹35,00,000/- during the operative period of the scheme for MSME.        2. Upto 10% of the cost of plant and machinery with a ceiling of ₹35,00,000/- during the operative period of the scheme for large projects.2. Any other environment management project with the use of Clean, Efficient, and Innovative Pollution Control Equipment1. Upto 25% of the cost of plant and machinery with a ceiling of ₹35,00,000/- during the operative period of the scheme for MSME.                    2. Upto 10% of the cost of plant and machinery with a ceiling of ₹35,00,000/- during the operative period of the scheme for large projects.<br>

**Sanctioning Authority:** Assistance under the scheme will be sanctioned by State Level Empowered Committee and assistance will be disbursed by Industries Commissionerate.

## Eligibility

1. The industry/unit must be registered under relevant industrial laws in Gujarat.
   1. The industry/unit must comply with existing environmental regulations and standards.
   1. The industry/unit must be engaged in manufacturing or production activities.
   1. The industry/unit must undertake projects focused on environmental protection measures.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The applicant enterprise has to fill out the prescribed [application form](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2020/Application-form%20for%20Scheme-1%20Assistance%20to%20Environment%20Management.pdf).
**Step-2:** Attach all relevant [documents ](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2020/Checklist%20for%20Scheme-1%20EPM%20IP-2020.pdf)as required under the scheme guidelines.
**Step-3:** Submit the completed application form and documents to the Industries Commissionerate or the concerned District Industries Centre.
**Step-4:** Ensure that the application is submitted within six months from the date of completion of the activity or project.

## Documents Required

1. Copy of the Constitution of Enterprise.
   1. Project Completion Certificate/ Commissioning Certificate with project completion date.
   1. Expenditure statement approved by Chartered Accountant in prescribed proforma as per Annexure ES1.
   1. Justification of the comparative benefits of the implemented project with details of data and % reduction in raw material/ water/ energy consumption or waste generation.
   1. Copy of EM registration/ IEM Part-B/ Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM)/ Udyam Registration (UR) (as the case may be).
   1. GPCB NOC/CCA for the project.
   1. Process Diagram with brief note on existing and new technology adopted for the project.
   1. Last year balance sheet of enterprise.
   1. Bank statements for expenditures incurred in the project.
   1. Photographs of Project (minimum 3 photographs).
   1. Copy of any other financial assistance granted by GoG and/or GoI for the same component/project (if not granted, then give undertaking).
   1. Undertaking for outstanding Government dues and any pending court case against the government.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Scheme for Assistance for Environment Protection Measures"?

The "Scheme of Assistance for Environment Protection Measures" under the Gujarat Industrial Policy promotes sustainable industrial growth by supporting industries in adopting eco-friendly practices and ensuring compliance with environmental norms, effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025.

### What is the purpose of the "Scheme of Assistance for Environment Protection Measures" component?

The "Scheme of Assistance for Environment Protection Measures" component aims to promote sustainable industrial growth by supporting industries in adopting eco-friendly practices and complying with environmental norms.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Industries or units registered under relevant industrial laws in Gujarat and engaged in manufacturing or production activities are eligible to apply.

### What types of projects are supported under the scheme?

Projects involving cleaner production technologies, pollution control equipment, and other environmental protection measures are supported.

### What is the maximum financial assistance provided under the scheme?

Financial assistance varies depending on the project type and size, with a ceiling of [?]35,00,000/- per project.

### What is the deadline for submitting an application?

Applicants must submit their applications within six months from the date of completion of the activity or project.

### Where should the application be submitted?

Applications should be submitted to the Industries Commissionerate or the concerned District Industries Centre.

### Can an applicant apply for assistance for multiple projects?

Yes, an applicant can apply for assistance for multiple projects, provided each meets the scheme's eligibility criteria.

### Are there any restrictions on the type of industries that can apply?

Only industries compliant with environmental regulations and located in Gujarat are eligible; industries with significant environmental violations are ineligible.

### How is the assistance amount determined?

The assistance amount is based on the cost of the proposed project, subject to the prescribed percentage and ceiling limits.

### Can large enterprises apply for this scheme?

Yes, large enterprises are eligible, though the quantum of assistance is lower compared to MSMEs.

### What role does the Industries Commissionerate play in the scheme?

The Industries Commissionerate is responsible for receiving, reviewing, and approving applications under the scheme.
