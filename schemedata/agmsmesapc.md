## Brief Description

The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for assistance to MSMEs" aims to empower the state's dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs&quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, this initiative supports growth, investments, and the vision of &quot;Aatmanirbhar Gujarat&quot; and &quot;Aatmanirbhar Bharat.&quot;
The component &quot;Assistance for Power Connection Charges&quot; financial assistance to eligible Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) to offset costs incurred for power connection charges. It covers charges for new connections, additional load for expansion, or shifting connections or service lines, subject to conditions.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** Assistance of 35% on charges paid to the distribution licensee for LT/HT service lines maximum assistance of up to ₹5,00,000/- during the operative period of the scheme.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be registered as an MSME.
   1. The enterprise must have obtained acknowledgment or registration from the Ministry of MSME or the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), as applicable.
   1. The enterprise must be located outside GIDC/approved industrial parks.
   1. Charges must be paid to the distribution licensee during the scheme&amp;#39;s operative period.
   1. Assistance applies to new power connections, additional load for expansion, or shifting of connection/service lines.
   1. Applications must be submitted within one year from the date of payment to the distribution licensee.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on "New Investor Registration" to access the registration form.
**Step-2:** Fill in the Registration Form. Click on "Submit". 
**Step-3:** Verify the E-mail ID using the link sent on your registered E-mail ID.
**Step-4:** After verification, you will be registered successfully.
<br>

> **Post-Registration Process:**

**Step-1:** Visit the [Investor Facilitation Portal (IFP)](https://ifp.gujarat.gov.in/DIGIGOV/) portal. Click on "Login".
**Step-2:** Enter the email address and password you used during registration.
**Step-3:** Click the "Login" button to access your account.
**Step-4:** Now fill in the required details and mandatory documents about your business to complete the application process.
<br>

## Documents Required

1. Copy of Udyam/IEM/LOI Registration certificate.
   1. Copy of the Constitution of the enterprise.
   1. CA Certified Balance sheet /Annual Audit Report. (if applicable).
   1. CA certificate for capital investment.
   1. Land Document such as NA Permission/ copy of Index/sale Deed.
   1. PAN card of Enterprise, Promoters/partners/directors and Authorized Signatory.
   1. Estimates of Power Distribution company (DISCOM) and Receipt.
   1. Copy of bills issued by Power Distribution company.
   1. Copy of Power Distribution License company Certificate (HT/LT).
   1. Copy of certificate for category (if applicable).
   1. Copy of Self-certified Shareholding pattern.
   1. Copy of sanctioned and connected Load Certificate issued by Power Distribution License Company.
   1. Self-certified expenditure statement supported by DISCOM Documents.
   1. Copy of GPCB approved NOC/Consent &amp; other departments approval (if applicable).
   1. Copy of GST registration (if applicable).
   1. Bank details for payment (Cancelled cheque).
   1. Self-Attested documents/details to be submitted by the Authorized Person.
   1. Power of attorney/Board resolution/Consent letter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs"?

The "Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs" is a state initiative designed to support and strengthen the MSME sector in Gujarat. It aims to enhance growth, encourage investments, and contribute to the vision of self-reliant Gujarat and India.

### When is the scheme operative?

The scheme is operative from October 5, 2022, to October 4, 2027, covering a period of five years.

### What are the objectives of the scheme?

The scheme aims to boost MSME growth, enhance the industrial ecosystem, encourage investments, and contribute to Gujarat's and India's self-reliance vision.

### What are some initiatives included in the scheme?

The scheme includes the establishment of new industrial estates, sector-specific parks, and infrastructure projects, along with amendments to the Gujarat Industrial Policy 2020 to further support MSME growth.

### Who is eligible to benefit from the scheme?

MSMEs established and operating within Gujarat are eligible to benefit from the scheme, as per the criteria defined in the policy framework.

### What is the purpose of the component?

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to eligible MSMEs for costs incurred on LT/HT service line charges paid to distribution licensees.

### What is the maximum amount of assistance provided under the scheme?

The maximum assistance provided under the scheme is [?]5,00,000/- per enterprise during the operative period.

### What percentage of the charges is reimbursed under this scheme?

The scheme reimburses 35% of the charges paid to the distribution licensee for LT/HT service lines.

### What types of power connection expenses are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers expenses for new connections, additional load for expansion, and shifting of connection or service lines.

### What is the time limit to apply for the assistance?

Applicants must submit their applications within one year from the date of payment to the distribution licensee.

### Can an enterprise located inside GIDC or approved industrial parks apply for the scheme?

No, the scheme is exclusively for MSMEs located outside GIDC or approved industrial parks.

### Can multiple applications be submitted by the same enterprise?

No, the scheme allows for reimbursement up to the maximum limit of [?]5,00,000/- per enterprise during the operative period.
