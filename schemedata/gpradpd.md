## Brief Description

Under this scheme, a grant of up to ₹3,500/- is provided for the purchase of individual aids, which may be increased to a maximum of ₹7,000/- in special cases. Additionally, ₹500/- is provided for the repair of prosthetic limbs and other assistive equipment.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Grant for Purchase and Repair of Various Assistive Devices/Equipment for Persons with Disabilities” has been implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to enhance the mobility and functional independence of persons with disabilities by providing financial assistance for the purchase and repair of essential assistive devices. Under this scheme, a grant of up to ₹3,500/- (maximum ₹7,000/- in special cases) is provided for the purchase of individual aids and ₹500 for the repair of prosthetic limbs and assistive equipment.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Grant Amount:**


- ₹50/- to ₹3,500/- for the purchase of assistive devices (maximum ₹7,000/- in special cases).
- ₹500/- for repair of artificial limbs and assistive equipment.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant should be a person with a disability.
1. The applicant should have a minimum disability of 40%.
1. In the case of a minor beneficiary, the monthly income of parents/guardians should not exceed ₹4,000/-.
1. The applicant should have a medical recommendation from a Government Medical Officer or specialist recommending the device or its repair.

## Application Process

### Offline

The medical officer must recommend the applicant for fitting with a prosthetic limb or hearing aid. The applicants should complete the application form himself/herself and submit it to the concerned Assistant Social Welfare Officer. The District Social Welfare Officer’s office will process the application as per the prescribed rules. Thereafter, payment will be made to the beneficiary through the treasury.
<br>

1. Identity proof

1. Recommendation Letter (from a government doctor for the device or repair)
1. Any Other Document if Required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply under this scheme?

Persons with at least 40% disability and a valid disability certificate.

### What is the grant amount provided?

₹50 to ₹3,500 for devices and ₹500 for repairs. Maximum up to ₹7,000 in specific cases.

### Is the scheme applicable to minors?

Yes, but the parent's/guardian's income must not exceed ₹4,000 per month.

### Is there any application fee?

No, the application process is free of charge.
<br>

<br>

### Where should I submit the application?

At the office of the Assistant Social Welfare Officer in your district.

### Is medical approval necessary?

Yes, a government doctor must recommend the need for the device or repair.

### Can I apply online?

The process is offline through the district office.

### Who can I contact for help?

The applicant may contact the District Social Welfare Officer or the Assistant Social Welfare Officer.
<br>

<br>

### How is the amount disbursed?

The sanctioned amount is paid via the treasury directly to the applicant's bank account.

### Who verifies the application?

The District Social Welfare Office verifies documents and eligibility.
