## Brief Description

The "Workers In Case of Partial Permanent Disablement" scheme by the JKBOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, provides financial assistance to registered Building and Other Construction (BOC) workers in the case of permanent disablement.

## Detailed Description

The "Workers In Case of Partial Permanent Disablement" scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, under the MUHAFIZ Scheme (PMJJBY: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana, PMSBY: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana) from 01.01.2018, provides ₹2,00,000 to registered BOC workers in the case of permanent disablement.

## Benefits

> Under the scheme, ₹2,00,000 is provided to registered BOC workers in case of partial permanent disablement.

## Eligibility

> **For Registration as a Building/ Construction Worker:**


1. The applicant should be a resident of Jammu & Kashmir.
1. The applicant should be a Building/ Construction Worker.
1. The applicant should be between 18 and 60 years.
1. The applicant should have engaged in any building or other construction work for at least 90 days during the preceding 12 months.
1. The applicant should be registration is renewed annually.

> **For the Application of the Welfare Scheme:**


1. The worker must be a registered member of the Building and Other Construction Workers (BOC) Welfare Board.
1. The scheme applies to workers who suffer from permanent disablement.

## Documents Required

1. Original Medical Certificate issued by the Medical Board or competent authority.
1. Attested Age Proof of the registered worker.
1. Duly attested photocopy of Bank Passbook of the worker or nominee/legal heir, or a cancelled cheque bearing the name of the worker/nominee/legal heir and IFSC code of the bank branch.
1. Photocopy of Aadhaar Card/Biometric Card of the registered worker.
1. Registration Certificate of the worker under the BOC Welfare Board.
1. Proof of employment in the building/construction sector (work records or pay slips).
1. Disability Certificate from a recognized medical authority confirming the partial permanent disablement.
1. Proof of residence (Voter ID, Ration Card, Utility Bill, etc.) for address verification.
1. Photograph of the worker (passport size).
1. Income Certificate to establish eligibility under the scheme (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Workers In Case of Permanent Disablement" scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance to registered Building and Other Construction (BOC) workers in case of permanent disablement.

### How much financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

A financial assistance of [?]2,00,000 is provided to the worker in case of partial permanent disablement.

### Is the worker required to be registered with the Welfare Board?

Yes, the worker must be a registered member of the Building and Other Construction Workers (BOC) Welfare Board.

### How can a worker register with the BOC Welfare Board?

The worker should be a resident of Jammu & Kashmir, engaged in construction work for at least 90 days in the preceding 12 months, and renew their registration annually.

### What is the age requirement for registering as a BOC worker?

The worker must be between 18 and 60 years of age.

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme?

Original Medical Certificate from the Medical Board, attested age proof, bank passbook or cancelled cheque, and Aadhar card/biometric card of the worker.

### Is there a minimum period of work required for the worker to qualify?

Yes, the worker must have worked in construction for at least 90 days in the last 12 months.

### Does the worker need to provide proof of the disability?

Yes, the worker must submit an Original Medical Certificate issued by a competent authority to confirm the partial permanent disablement.

### How will the funds be disbursed to the worker?

The funds are disbursed to the registered worker's bank account after submission of all required documents.

### Are there any other conditions for applying under the scheme?

The worker must be actively engaged in construction work, and the disablement should be partial and permanent, as confirmed by the Medical Board.
