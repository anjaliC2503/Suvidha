## Brief Description

The objective of the scheme is to improve the health and well-being of the local population. It promotes physical fitness and encourages participation in sports and wellness activities.

## Detailed Description

The **Sports and Wellness Scheme**, under the **Planning Department** of the **Government of Meghalaya**, is an initiative designed to encourage both registered and unregistered firms to establish modern sporting facilities in Meghalaya. The scheme has the dual objectives of enhancing well-being and improving the health of the local population. By promoting physical fitness, mental health, and healthier lifestyles through sports and wellness activities, the scheme aims to benefit the community by providing opportunities for physical exercise, sports participation, and other wellness-related activities that contribute to better overall health outcomes.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

1. Upfront subsidy up to 25% of total project cost (maximum ₹37.5 lakh for most applicantssemi_colon ₹75 lakh for top 5 promising entrepreneurs)
1. Backend support up to 10% of project cost (maximum ₹12.5 lakhsemi_colon ₹25 lakh for top 5 promising entrepreneurs)
1. 5% entrepreneur’s own contribution required, with remaining balance via bank loan (up to ₹2.1 crore for top 5semi_colon up to ₹1.05 crore for others)
1. 18-month moratorium before EMI repayment beginssemi_colon 15-year total loan term
1. Mentoring and acceleration coaching support through PRIME Meghalaya
1. One-time financial assistance for setting up sports and wellness infrastructure (gyms, swimming pools, football grounds, etc.) 
1. Priority selection ensuring one facility per district before additional approvals

<br>

## Exclusions

**Individuals**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for the following**
A. Following individuals (currently employed as full-fledged or contractual, or retired and drawing pension) and their Immediate family members which includes spouse, parents, and children up to the age of 30
i. State and Central Government employees
ii. Employees of public sector undertakings, subsidiaries of public sector undertakings and divisions of public sector undertakings etc.
iii. Any individual whose job is concerned with politics such MLAs, MLCs, MPs etc. or seeking to hold a similar position in government
iv. Registered class-1 contractors
v. Consultants who are currently engaged with state or central government
B. Only one member from a family is eligible for the scheme.
C. Pressure groups and organizations that are primarily political in nature
**Registered Entities**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for the following**
A. Only one member from a family is eligible for the scheme.
B. Pressure groups and organizations that are primarily political in nature
C. Following individuals (currently employed as full-fledged or contractual, or retired and drawing pension) and their Immediate family members which includes spouse, parents, and children up to the age of 30
i. State and Central Government employees
ii. Employees of public sector undertakings, subsidiaries of public sector undertakings and divisions of public sector undertakings etc.
iii. Any individual whose job is concerned with politics such MLAs, MLCs, MPs etc. or seeking to hold a similar position in government
iv. Registered class-1 contractors
v. Consultants who are currently engaged with state or central government.

## Eligibility

**Individuals**

1. The applicant must be a resident of Meghalaya and provide valid government ID proof.
1. The applicant must be between 18 and 58 years of age.
1. The applicant must possess land, either self-owned or on lease, verified by the competent authority.
1. The entrepreneur must provide proof of relevant experience in the field through past projects, portfolios, certifications, or testimonials.

**Registered Entities**

1. The firm or company must be registered in Meghalaya, such as Pvt. Ltd., LLP, or Sole Proprietorship, and must operate as a “for-profit” organization.
1. The organization must provide an Incorporation Certificate or registration certificate, whichever is applicable.
1. The entity must ensure that at least two members possess a training certificate in the relevant sector from a recognized institution, preferably a government institute.
1. The entity must possess land, either self-owned or on lease, verified by the competent authority.
1. The entrepreneur must not be a credit defaulter with any bank.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Sports and Wellness Scheme?

The Sports and Wellness Scheme is an initiative by the Planning Department of the Government of Meghalaya to encourage the development of modern sports and wellness facilities in the state.
<br>

### Are NGOs, SHGs, and societies eligible for the scheme?

No, NGOs, SHGs, and societies may not be eligible based on their constitution.
<br>

### What is the age requirement for individual applicants?

Individual applicants must be between 18 and 58 years of age.
<br>

### Is land ownership required to apply for the scheme?

Yes, the applicant must possess land either through ownership or lease, verified by the competent authority.
<br>

### What types of projects are supported under the scheme?

The scheme supports projects related to sports and wellness infrastructure such as gyms, swimming pools, football grounds, and other fitness facilities.
<br>

### What is the maximum upfront subsidy provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides an upfront subsidy of up to 25% of the total project cost, with a maximum of ₹37.5 lakh for most applicants and up to ₹75 lakh for the top five promising entrepreneurs.
<br>

### Is there any required contribution from the entrepreneur?

Yes, the entrepreneur must contribute at least 5% of the total project cost.
<br>

### How is the remaining project cost financed?

The remaining balance can be financed through a bank loan.
<br>

### What is the loan repayment structure?

The loan includes an 18-month moratorium period before EMI repayment begins and a total loan tenure of up to 15 years.
<br>
