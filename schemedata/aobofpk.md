## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to fishermen owning small boats with Outboard Motors (OBM) by subsidizing the cost of kerosene or petrol.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance to OBM Boat Owner Fishermen on Purchase of Kerosene/Petrol&quot; has been launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to fishermen owning small boats with Outboard Motors (OBM) by subsidizing the cost of kerosene or petrol. This initiative supports the fishing community and promotes fisheries in Gujarat.
<br>

## Benefits

**Assistance under the scheme:**
**Name of Component****Standard of Assistance****Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits**Assistance on the purchase of Kerosene/Petrol Maximum assistance of ₹50/- per literBenefits are available as per the prescribed quota throughout the year.<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
1. The applicant must be a fisherman.
1. The applicant must be a Kerosene Card holder.
1. The applicant must own a small boat operated by an Outboard Motor (OBM).

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Fishermen must purchase kerosene or petrol from government-approved fuel stations Gujarat Fisheries Central Co-operative Association Limited (G.F.C.C.A. Ltd.).
**Step 2:** Collect original bills of purchase.
**Step 3:** Submit an application along with the required documents to the concerned district office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance to fishermen owning small boats with Outboard Motors (OBM) by subsidizing the cost of kerosene or petrol. This initiative supports the fishing community and promotes fisheries in Gujarat.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Fishermen who are residents of Gujarat, own a small boat with an Outboard Motor (OBM), and possess a government-issued Kerosene/Petrol Card are eligible to apply for the scheme.

### What type of assistance is provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹50/- per liter of kerosene or petrol to eligible fishermen to help reduce their fuel expenses.

### Is there a limit on the amount of fuel eligible for assistance?

Yes, the assistance is available as per the fixed quota set by the government.

### Where must the fuel be purchased to avail of the subsidy?

Fishermen must purchase kerosene or petrol from government-approved fuel stations operated by G.F.C.C.A. Ltd. to be eligible for assistance.

### Are fishermen from other states eligible for this scheme?

No, only fishermen who are permanent residents of Gujarat can apply for the scheme.

### How frequently can an applicant claim the benefit?

The benefit is available throughout the year based on the fixed fuel quota allotted by the government.

### Is there a minimum income requirement to be eligible for the scheme?

No, the scheme does not have a minimum income requirement, and all eligible fishermen can apply regardless of their income level.

### Can an applicant apply for the scheme if the boat is operated by an Inboard Motor (IBM)?

No, the scheme is only applicable to fishermen who own small boats operated by Outboard Motors (OBM).

### Can an applicant apply for the scheme online?

Currently, the application process requires submission at the concerned district office along with the necessary documents. 


<br>

### Where can an applicant get more information about the scheme?

More details about the scheme can be obtained from the concerned district fisheries office or through government notifications.
