## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote trade and entrepreneurship by allotting canteens for selling tea and other food items to eligible residents of Uttarakhand through a fair and transparent tender process.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Allotment of Canteens” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Agricultural Production &amp; Marketing Board under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote trade and entrepreneurship by allotting canteens for selling tea and other food items to eligible residents of Uttarakhand through a fair and transparent tender process.
<br>

## Benefits

The scheme facilitates the promotion of trade in tea and other food items by allotting canteens to eligible residents of Uttarakhand.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Application Process:**

   1. Information regarding the allotment of canteens is published in newspapers and through public notices displayed at public places, and interested applicants can obtain the tender form by paying the prescribed amount at the committee office.
   1. Submit the completed tender form in the tender box on the due date.

***Selection Process:***

   1. The allotment of canteens is carried out by a committee formed at the committee level.
   1. The tenders are opened by the committee in the presence of the tenderers.
   1. The canteen is allotted to the applicant/tenderer offering the highest amount for a period of one year.

All residents of Uttarakhand who meet the committee’s eligibility criteria are eligible to apply.

### What types of trade are covered under this scheme?

The scheme primarily covers trade in tea and other food items through allotted canteens.

### Who is responsible for allotting the canteens?

A committee formed at the committee level is responsible for the allotment.

### How are tenders evaluated?

Canteens are allotted for a period of one year.
