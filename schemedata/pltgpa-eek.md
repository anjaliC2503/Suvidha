## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide latest technology and equipment to pottery artisans for better productivity, improved quality and reduce health risks. It provides 100% financial assistance for energy-efficient kilns to groups of registered clay artisans and potters in Gujarat state.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Providing Latest Technology to Group of Pottery Artisans (Energy Efficient Kiln)" by the  Gujarat Matikam Kalakari & Rural Technology Institute, Industries & Mines Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide latest technology and equipment to pottery artisans for better productivity, improved quality and reduce health risks. The scheme provides 100% financial assistance on development of the kiln with unit cost of ₹83,000/- and energy-efficient furnaces to groups of registered clay artisans and potters. The applicant must be directly involved in the pottery business and be in the age group of 18 to 58 years, with at least 800 to 1000 square feet of open space available for pottery business. The scheme is implemented by the relevant Gujarat State Government Department and applications are accepted through prescribed application forms with required details and certificates.
<br>

## Benefits

**Financial Assistance:** 100% financial assistance is provided on development of the kiln with unit cost of ₹83,000/-.
**Technology Provision:** Energy-efficient furnaces are provided to improve productivity and quality.
**Health Benefits:** Reduced health risks for pottery artisans through improved technology.
<br>

> **Mode and Frequency of Disbursement**


- The assistance will be given only in the form of an energy saving furnace.
- No assistance related to the kiln will be paid in cash.

<br>

> **Conditions for Availment/Continuation**


- The energy-saving furnace must be used only for pottery business.
- Beneficiaries must send a report about the utility of the furnace to the organization every 3 months after the furnace is ready.
- Cost of maintenance and repair of the furnace must be borne by the beneficiaries at their own expense after construction and installation.
- Misuse of the furnace will result in legal action by the institute.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be registered clay artisans and potters eligible for the scheme on 100% subsidy basis.
- The applicant must be directly involved in the pottery business and carry out pottery activity.
- The applicant must be in the age group of 18 to 58 years.
- The applicant must be part of a group doing at least 2 (two) pottery.
- The applicant must have at least 800 to 1000 square feet of open space in their own rent lease or for at least five years on the date on which they apply for pottery business.
- The applicant must be associated with pottery in the state of Gujarat only.

**Post-Selection Conditions**

- The applicant must adhere to the policy rules of the institute as well as the norms regarding the construction of the kiln.
- The applicant must use the energy-saving furnace only for pottery business.
- The applicant must compulsorily use the energy-saving furnace as per the guarantee letter given unanimously.

## Application Process

### Online

**Steps for New Registration**
**Step 1:** Visit the [eKutir portal](https://e-kutir.gujarat.gov.in). In the right pane of the landing page, click "[For New Individual Registration, click Here](https://e-kutir.gujarat.gov.in/Registration.aspx)".
**Step 2:** On the registration page, fill in all mandatory fields (marked with an asterisk): Full Name (in English), Aadhaar Card Number, Gender, Date of Birth, Mobile Number, E-mail, Password (confirm the password by typing it again). Fill in the Captcha Code, and click "Register".
**Step 3:** A confirmation pop-up will display the entered name, mobile number, and other details. If all the details are correct, click "Confirm" to proceed. If any information is incorrect, click "Cancel", make the necessary corrections, and then click "Register" again.
**Step 4:** Upon successful registration, the User ID and Password will be sent to your registered mobile number via SMS. If an email address has been provided, the credentials will also be sent via email.
<br>

**Steps for Login Procedure and User Profile Update**
**Step 1:** Visit the [eKutir portal](https://e-kutir.gujarat.gov.in). In the right pane of the landing page (in the "Login to Portal" section), provide your User ID, Password, and the Captcha Code. Then click "Login".
**Step 2:** Upon first login, the user must complete the "User Profile" by providing personal and organisational details. All fields marked with an asterisk are mandatory. Some fields will already be pre-filled based on the information provided during registration.
_*Most of these details can later be updated from the “View Profile” section, except for the fields initially filled during registration._
<br>

**Steps for Scheme Application**
**Step 1:** After logging in and completing the profile, the system displays schemes relevant to the user on the homepage. Click on the name of the scheme that you intend to apply for. You will be redirected to the online application form of the scheme. The application form is divided into multiple tabs. 
**Step 2:** In the form, fill in all the mandatory fields and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size. For each uploaded document, the corresponding document number must be entered wherever required. Click the “Save & Next” button to proceed to the subsequent tabs. 
**Step 3:** Carefully review all the information provided and the documents uploaded. Make any necessary corrections. Acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions, declaration, and privacy policy (if any) by ticking the designated checkbox.
**Step 4:** Clicking “Save & Next” in the last tab of the application form generates an application number, which is displayed on the screen. The applicant must record this number for future reference. If the applicant wishes to print the application, they may click the “Print Application” button.
_*If you do not wish to continue with the application at that point, you may click "Cancel"._
<br>

**Application Printing and Modification**

- If the user wishes to print the application, they may click “Print Application.” 
- If the application has been returned for revision, a “Modify” button will appear. The user can then edit the application and resubmit it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the specific requirements regarding my age and involvement in pottery work to be eligible for this financial assistance program?

You must be between 18 to 58 years of age and be directly involved in the pottery business, actively carrying out pottery activities. Simply owning or being associated with pottery work is not sufficient.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the exact amount of financial support provided and how is it disbursed to successful applicants?

The program provides 100% financial assistance worth ₹83,000/- for developing an energy-efficient kiln. However, no cash payment is made; instead, you receive the energy-saving furnace directly as equipment.
<br>

### What are the minimum space requirements for my pottery business premises to qualify for this assistance?

You must have at least 800 to 1,000 square feet of open space available for your pottery business. This space can be owned by you, rented, or leased for a minimum period of 5 years from your application date.
<br>

### Do I need to apply individually or can I apply as part of a group, and what are the group requirements?

You must apply as part of a group that includes at least 2 pottery artisans or potters. Individual applications are not accepted under this scheme; group participation is mandatory for eligibility.
<br>

### What type of registration or certification do I need to have before applying for this energy-efficient kiln assistance?

You must be a registered clay artisan or potter to be eligible for this scheme. The program specifically targets registered pottery professionals who can avail the 100% subsidy basis assistance.
<br>

### Is this financial assistance program available to pottery artisans working anywhere in India or are there geographical restrictions?

This assistance is exclusively available to pottery artisans who are associated with pottery work within Gujarat state only. Artisans from other states are not eligible to apply.
<br>

### What is the primary purpose and expected outcome of receiving this energy-efficient kiln technology for my pottery business?

The kiln technology aims to enhance your productivity, improve the quality of your pottery products, and significantly reduce health risks associated with traditional pottery firing methods through modern energy-efficient equipment.
<br>

### What are my ongoing responsibilities after receiving the energy-efficient furnace regarding reporting and communication with the implementing organization?

You must submit a utility report about the furnace's performance and usage to the organization every 3 months after the furnace becomes operational and ready for use.
<br>

### Who bears the financial responsibility for maintaining and repairing the energy-efficient furnace after it is installed at my premises?

All costs related to maintenance and repair of the furnace after its construction and installation must be borne entirely by you and your group at your own expense.
<br>

### What are the consequences if I use the energy-efficient furnace for purposes other than pottery business activities?

Misuse of the furnace for non-pottery activities will result in legal action being initiated against you by the Gujarat Matikam Kalakari & Rural Technology Institute.
<br>

### What specific application form and documentation process must I follow to submit my application for this assistance program?

You must submit the prescribed application form with all required details and necessary documents as specified by the Gujarat Matikam Kalakari & Rural Technology Institute along with supporting certificates.
<br>

### What type of legal guarantee document do I need to provide as part of my group application for the furnace?

Your group must provide a notarized guarantee on stamp paper worth ₹300/- stating unanimously that the energy-saving furnace will be used exclusively for pottery business by the group.
<br>

### What land ownership or tenancy documents are required to prove my eligibility for installing the energy-efficient kiln?

You must provide necessary documents proving ownership, rental agreement, or lease agreement for the land where the furnace will be built, valid for at least 5 years.
<br>

### What permissions do I need to obtain from local authorities before applying for this energy-efficient kiln installation assistance?

You must obtain construction permission or leave letter from your Gram Panchayat, Municipality, or other competent local authority where the furnace is planned to be installed.
<br>
