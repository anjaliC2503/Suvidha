## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹20,000/- as a one-time lump sum benefit to families facing financial hardship due to the death of their primary breadwinner.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;The Delhi Family Benefit Scheme&quot; is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of NCT of Delhi. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to families whose primary breadwinner has died while being more than 18 years of age and less than 60 years of age. Under the scheme, a one-time lump sum financial benefit of ₹20,000/- is provided to the surviving member of the household who is determined to be the head of the household after due local inquiry. The assistance is intended to support low-income families facing financial distress following the death of the earning member. The payment is made directly into the beneficiary&#39;s bank account through the Aadhaar Payment Bridge System.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** A one-time lump sum benefit of ₹20,000/- is provided to the eligible surviving member of the deceased breadwinner's household.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a surviving member of the household of the deceased primary breadwinner.
  - The deceased primary breadwinner must have been more than 18 years of age and less than 60 years of age at the time of death.
  - The applicant&#39;s family income from all sources must not exceed ₹1,00,000/- per annum.
  - The applicant must be a resident of the National Capital Territory of Delhi for at least 5 years immediately preceding the date of application.
  - The applicant must have a singly operated bank account for receiving payment through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS).
  - The applicant must have an Aadhaar Number or must have enrolled with the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) for Aadhaar.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Visit the Delhi [e-District Portal](https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in/in/en/Account/Register.html) and register as a new user using an Aadhaar Card or Voter Identity Card.
**Step 2:** Log in to the portal using the User ID and Password received after registration.
**Step 3:** Select &quot;Delhi Family Benefit Scheme&quot; from the services offered by the Department of Social Welfare.
**Step 4:** Fill in the online application form with details of the deceased breadwinner, applicant, family income, Aadhaar, and bank account.
**Step 5:** Upload the required documents and the recommended/attested application, wherever applicable.
**Step 6:** Review the application and submit it online through the portal.
**Step 7:** The application will be verified by the concerned authority. Upon approval, a one-time financial assistance of ₹20,000/- will be credited to the beneficiary&#39;s bank account through the Aadhaar Payment Bridge System (APBS).
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Delhi Family Benefit Scheme"?

The Delhi Family Benefit Scheme provides a one-time financial assistance of ₹20,000/- to eligible families after the death of their primary breadwinner.
<br>

### Which department implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of NCT of Delhi.
<br>

### Who can apply under the scheme?

A surviving member of the household of the deceased primary breadwinner can apply under the scheme.
<br>

### What is the amount of assistance provided?

The eligible beneficiary receives a one-time lump sum assistance of ₹20,000/-.
<br>

### What should be the age of the deceased breadwinner?

The deceased breadwinner must have been more than 18 years of age and less than 60 years of age at the time of death.
<br>

### What is the maximum family income allowed under the scheme?

The applicant's family income must not exceed ₹1,00,000/- per annum from all sources.
<br>

### Is Delhi residency mandatory for applying?

Yes, the applicant must have been residing in Delhi for at least 5 years before applying.
<br>

### Is Aadhaar mandatory under the scheme?

Yes, the applicant must possess an Aadhaar Number or have enrolled with UIDAI for Aadhaar.
<br>

### Is a bank account required for receiving the benefit?

Yes, the applicant must have a singly operated bank account for receiving the benefit through PFMS.
<br>

### Are there any exceptions to the bank account requirement?

Yes, relaxation may be provided for minors, mentally challenged applicants, and persons under legal guardianship.
<br>

### How is the benefit amount disbursed?

The benefit amount is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account through the Aadhaar Payment Bridge System.
<br>

### Can proof of residence be established through witness statements?

Yes, witness statements may be accepted when documentary proof of residence is unavailable.
<br>

### Who can provide witness statements for residence verification?

Eligible witnesses may include public representatives, Resident Welfare Association office bearers, neighbours, Self-Help Group representatives, Integrated Child Development Services supervisors, Accredited Social Health Activists, and Gazetted Officers.
<br>

### Is local inquiry conducted before sanctioning the benefit?

Yes, the authorities conduct a local inquiry to determine the eligible head of the household and verify the claim.
<br>
