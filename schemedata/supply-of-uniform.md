## Brief Description

Supply of School Uniform, Shoes Socks to all Students for IX to XII studying in Govt Schools

## Detailed Description

Provision of complete uniform(Shirt, Pants, Skirt, Tie, Belt) socks, shoes  to all students in classes IX to XII of Government schools. It ensures that every child, regardless of their socio-economic background, has the basic physical tools required for a productive learning environment. Standardized uniform scheme to promote discipline and equality among male and female students, removing social distinctions based on attire.
<br>

<br>

<br>

## Benefits

<br>

1. Fosters a sense of identity, equality and discipline among students.
2. Significantly reduces school absenteeism and improves performance by eliminating the financial barrier of purchasing clothes.
3. Providing shoes and socks promotes better foot health and hygiene for students commuting to school.
4. Promotes regular school attendance by supporting foundational needs
	
<br>

## Eligibility

1.	The applicant should be a student.
2.	The applicant should be studying in class 9th to 12th.
3.	The applicant should be studying in a government school in UT of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.
<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

1. No separate application is required.
1. Eligible students enrolled in government schools are automatically covered under the scheme.

### What is the purpose of this scheme?

### Which students are eligible?

<br>

### Are private school students eligible?

No, the scheme is only for government school students.
<br>

### How many uniforms are provided?

Two pairs of uniforms per student.
<br>

### Is there any application fee?

No, the application is completely free.
