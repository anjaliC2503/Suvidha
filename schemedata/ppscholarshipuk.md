## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students pursuing a B.Sc. in Agriculture who are in their fourth year of study, by awarding them a monthly scholarship of ₹ 800/- for one year.

## Detailed Description

The “Priyank Pathak Scholarship for B.Sc. Agriculture (4th Year) Students” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to meritorious students pursuing a B.Sc. in Agriculture who are in their fourth year of study, by awarding them a monthly scholarship for one year.
<br>

## Benefits

**Rate of Scholarship:** ₹800/- per month.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the 4th year of the B.Sc. Agriculture program at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The scholarship is awarded for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the selected students receive the scholarship payments.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scholarship?

Only students who are currently enrolled in the fourth year of the B.Sc. Agriculture program at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar are eligible.
<br>

### Is this scholarship available for students of other universities?

No.
<br>

### What is the amount of the scholarship?

Each selected student receives a monthly scholarship of ₹800.
<br>

### What is the duration of the scholarship?

The scholarship is awarded for a period of one year only.
<br>

### How can a student apply for the scholarship?

Eligible students must submit an application on the prescribed form made available by the university.
<br>

### Who reviews the applications?

A committee constituted by the Vice Chancellor evaluates all the applications following a defined and transparent procedure.
<br>

### Who approves the final list of selected students?

The Vice Chancellor grants the final approval based on the recommendations of the selection committee.
<br>

### How are scholarship payments made to selected students?

After approval, scholarship payments are disbursed to the selected students as per the university’s established process.
<br>
