## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide ₹10,000/- as a one-time scholarship to extremely backward class students passing class 10th in the first division from the Bihar School Examination Board.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Mukhyamantri Atyant Pichhda Varg Medhavriti Yojana&quot; was launched by the Backward and Extremely Backward Class Welfare Department, Government of Bihar. The scheme aims to encourage academic excellence among students belonging to extremely backward classes. A one-time financial incentive is provided to students who have passed the 10th class (Matric) examination in the first division from the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB).

## Benefits

- A one-time scholarship of ₹10,000/- is provided per student.

**Note:** Payment will be made via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the beneficiary’s bank account.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Bihar.
1. The applicant should be a student. 
1. The applicant should belong to the Extremely Backward Class (BC-I) category.
1. The applicant should have passed the 10th class (Matric) examination in the first division from the Bihar School Examination Board, Patna.

## Application Process

### Offline

***Application Process:***

  - The eligible students may contact their respective schools and ensure the availability of required documents such as a caste certificate, mark sheet, Aadhaar card, etc.
  - The Bihar School Examination Board provides the list of eligible students to the Education Department.
  - Documents and identity of eligible students are verified by the concerned authorities.
  - The authorized officer of the Education Department verifies and approves the disbursement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Students from Bihar's Extremely Backward Class (BC-I) who passed Class 10 in the first division from BSEB.

### Is the scheme applicable to all boards?

No, only students who passed from the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) are eligible.

### Is the scholarship a one-time benefit?

Yes, it is a one-time incentive after passing the 10th class.

### What is the mode of payment?

Payment is made via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the student’s bank account.

### Is there any application fee?

No, there is no fee to avail this scholarship.

### What is the scholarship amount?

The financial assistance of ₹10,000 per eligible student.
<br>

<br>

### Do I need to apply individual?

No individual application is needed. Eligible students are selected based on the BSEB-provided list.

### Who is implement the scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Education Department, Government of Bihar.

### What if my documents are not verified?

The student will not receive the scholarship without proper verification.

### When will the amount be disbursed?

After successful verification of documents and authorization by the Education Department.
