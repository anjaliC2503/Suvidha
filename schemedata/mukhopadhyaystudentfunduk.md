## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹25,000/- per year to meritorious & needy students pursuing B.Sc. Agriculture course in their final year of study, based on their genuine financial need, thereby helping to reduce their economic burden & support the continuation of their education.

## Detailed Description

The “Dr. A.N. Mukhopadhyay Needy Student Fund (B.Sc. Agriculture - Final Year Student)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to needy students pursuing the B.Sc. Agriculture course in their final year of study, based on their genuine financial need, thereby helping to reduce their economic burden and support the continuation of their education.
<br>

## Benefits

**Amount of Assistance:** ₹25,000/- per year.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student in need of financial support to continue their education.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the final year of the B.Sc. Agriculture course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The financial assistance is awarded for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the selected students receive the payments.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Students enrolled in the final year of the B.Sc. Agriculture course at the University who require financial assistance to continue their education are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What is the amount of financial assistance provided under this scheme?

Each selected student receives ₹25,000/- per year as financial assistance.
<br>

### For how long is the financial assistance provided?

The financial assistance is provided for a duration of one year only.
<br>

### How can students apply for the scheme?

Students must submit their applications in the prescribed form made available by the University within the notified time period.
<br>

### Who evaluates the applications submitted by the students?

A committee constituted by the Vice Chancellor reviews and evaluates all applications following a defined procedure.
<br>

### Who makes the final decision on the selection of students?

The Vice Chancellor grants the final approval based on the recommendations of the selection committee.
<br>

### How is the scholarship amount disbursed to the selected students

After approval, the selected students receive the scholarship payments directly as per the University’s disbursement process.
