## Brief Description

The scheme "Petrol Subsidy Scheme" implemented by the Food, Public Distribution & Consumer Affairs Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide a petrol subsidy of ₹250 per month for two-wheelers to eligible NFSA/Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme beneficiaries.

## Detailed Description

The "Petrol Subsidy Scheme" implemented by the Food, Public Distribution & Consumer Affairs Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide financial relief to economically disadvantaged families by offering a petrol subsidy for their two-wheelers. Under this scheme, beneficiaries under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) or Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme receive ₹25/- per liter of petrol for up to 10 liters monthly.

## Benefits

***Subsidy Amount***

- ₹25/- per liter of petrol for up to 10 liters monthly (₹250/month).

<br>

***Mode of Disbursement:*** 

- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the beneficiary's bank account.

## Eligibility

> ***General Eligibility:***


1. The applicant should possess a valid ration card under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) or Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme.
1. All family members listed in the ration card must have Aadhaar numbers linked and verified.
1. The applicant's Aadhaar should be linked to a valid bank account and an updated mobile number.

<br>

> ***Vehicle Eligibility:***


1. The applicant's two-wheeler must be registered in their name and in the state of Jharkhand.
1. The applicant must possess a valid driving license.

## Application Process

### Online

> ***Registration Process:***


- Download the CMSUPPORT app or visit the official portal [jsfss.jharkhand.gov.in](https://jsfss.jharkhand.gov.in/).- Enter your ration card number and Aadhaar number.
- An OTP will be sent to the mobile number linked to the Aadhaar. Verify the OTP.

> ***Login Credentials:***


- Use your ration card number as the login ID and the last 8 digits of your Aadhaar as the password.

<br>

> ***Filling the Form:***


- Select your name from the family members listed on the ration card.
- Enter your two-wheeler's registration number and driving license details.

> **Verification Process:**


- Submitted vehicle details are sent to the DTO (District Transport Office) for verification.
- Post verification, the data is forwarded to the District Supply Officer (DSO) for final approval.

<br>

> ***Approval and Disbursement:***


- Once approved, the subsidy amount is transferred directly to the applicant's linked bank account through DBT.

1. Bank Passbook.
1. Valid Driving License.
1. Vehicle Registration Certificate (Two-Wheeler Registered in Jharkhand).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the Petrol Subsidy Scheme?

Applicants possessing a valid ration card under NFSA or Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme are eligible.

### What is the maximum subsidy provided under this scheme?

Beneficiaries receive a maximum of [?]250 per month for petrol ([?]25/liter for up to 10 liters).

### Can beneficiaries apply offline for this scheme?

No, applications can only be submitted through the CMSUPPORT app or jsfss.jharkhand.gov.in.

### What documents are required for the application?

Documents include a valid ration card, Aadhaar card linked to a bank account, vehicle registration certificate, and driving license.

### How is the subsidy amount disbursed?

The amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary's bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

### What if my vehicle is not registered in Jharkhand?

Only vehicles registered in Jharkhand are eligible for the subsidy.

### What is the helpline number for the scheme?

The applicant can contact the helpline at 1800-345-6598.

### Is this subsidy applicable for other types of vehicles?

No, the scheme is exclusively for two-wheelers registered in the applicant's name.

### How long does it take for the subsidy to reflect in the bank account?

The subsidy is credited monthly after successful verification and approval.

### Can I update my mobile number or bank details later?

Yes, updates can be made through the CMSUPPORT app or the official portal.
