## Brief Description

The scheme "Post Matric Scholarship for Students belonging to Scheduled Caste Community" was launched by the Mizoram Scholarship Board, Government of Mizoram. The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to students belonging to Scheduled Castes pursuing post-matriculation.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Post Matric Scholarship for Students belonging to Scheduled Caste Community" was launched by the Mizoram Scholarship Board, Government of Mizoram. The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to students belonging to Scheduled Castes pursuing post-matriculation recognized courses in recognized institutions. The scheme covers professional, technical as well as non-professional and non-technical courses at various levels and the scheme also includes correspondence courses including distance and continuing education.
<br>

## Benefits

Financial assistance is provided to the students.
_*The scheme is funded by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and the Government of Mizoram in 90:10 ratio._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be permanently settled in Mizoram.
- The applicant must belong to Scheduled Caste.
- The income of the parents/guardians of the applicant (from all sources) should not exceed ₹2,50,000/- per annum.
- The applicant's ongoing educational qualification must be between 11th Standard and Post-Doctoral Fellowship.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Normally application is open during July- September through offline where application form is available in Mizoram Scholarship Board during Office working hours.  


- Application Form duly filled-in along with documents mentioned above must be submitted to the following address -  

***Mizoram Scholarship Board, Kaisara Rd, Aizawl, Mizoram 796001***

1. Aadhaar Card.
1. Bank Account (self-account).
1. Income certificate issued by competent authority (up to date).
1. Previous year mark sheet.
1. Permanent Residential Certificate (up to date).
1. Scheduled Caste Certificate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum annual family income limit that allows me to remain eligible for this financial assistance program?

Your parents or guardians' combined income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,50,000 per annum. This income ceiling applies to the total earnings from every source of the family.
<br>

### Can students from communities other than Scheduled Caste apply for this educational financial support scheme?

No, this scholarship is exclusively available for students who belong to the Scheduled Caste community only. You must possess valid documentation proving your Scheduled Caste status to be eligible.
<br>

### Is it mandatory for applicants to have been residing permanently in a specific state to qualify for this scholarship?

Yes, you must be permanently settled in Mizoram State to be eligible for this scholarship. A valid Permanent Residential Certificate is required as proof of your permanent settlement in Mizoram.
<br>

### What educational levels and types of courses are covered under this financial assistance program for continuing education?

The scheme covers all recognized Post-Matriculation courses from Class 11 up to Post-Doctoral Fellowship levels. This includes professional, technical, non-professional, non-technical, correspondence, distance learning, and continuing education courses in recognized institutions.
<br>

### Do I need to provide my previous academic performance records as part of the application documentation process?

Yes, you must submit your previous year mark sheet as one of the mandatory documents. This academic record serves as proof of your educational progression and performance.
<br>

### Is it necessary to have a personal bank account in my own name for receiving the scholarship benefits?

Yes, you must have a bank account in your own name (self-account). The scholarship amount will be directly transferred to your personal bank account, so joint or family accounts are not acceptable.
<br>

### What specific document do I need to prove my community status for this educational financial assistance?

You must provide a valid Scheduled Caste Certificate issued by the competent authority. This certificate serves as official proof of your belonging to the Scheduled Caste community.
<br>

### During which months of the year can I typically submit my application for this scholarship program?

The application period normally opens during July to September each year. Applications are processed through offline mode only, so you need to visit the Mizoram Scholarship Board during this period.
<br>

### Is it acceptable to submit photocopies of income-related documents, or do they need to be recently issued by authorities?

You must provide an up-to-date Income Certificate issued by the competent authority. The certificate should be current and not outdated to ensure your family's present financial status is accurately reflected.
<br>

### Where exactly can I obtain the application form for this educational financial support scheme?

Application forms are available at the Mizoram Scholarship Board office during regular working hours. You need to visit their office personally during July-September to collect the form.
<br>

### What identity verification document is mandatory for all applicants under this scholarship scheme?

An Aadhaar Card is mandatory for all applicants. This serves as your primary identity verification document and must be submitted along with your application.
<br>

### Can I submit my scholarship application through online portals or email, or is physical submission required?

Physical submission is required as the application process is conducted offline only. You must submit your duly filled application form along with all required documents directly to the Mizoram Scholarship Board office.
<br>

### Is there any flexibility in the residency requirement, or must the certificate be current and valid?

You must provide an up-to-date Permanent Residential Certificate as proof of your permanent settlement in Mizoram State. The certificate should be current and issued by the competent authority.
<br>

### After I submit my application to the scholarship board, what happens to my documents during the processing phase?

The Mizoram Scholarship Board will first verify your submitted application and documents, then forward your complete application to the concerned Ministry for final processing and approval.
<br>
