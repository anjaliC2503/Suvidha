## Brief Description

The scheme "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana" by the Women and Child Development Department, Haryana, aims to provide a cash incentive of ₹5,000/- during maternity to eligible pregnant and lactating women on the birth of a second child (boy).

## Detailed Description

Launched on 8th March, 2022, the scheme &quot;Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana&quot; by the Women and Child Development Department, Government of Haryana, aims to offer partial compensation for wage loss to promote adequate rest before and after childbirth, and to encourage health-seeking behaviour among pregnant and lactating women. This initiative particularly targets women delivering their second child (boy) and intends to promote institutional delivery and immunization adherence. The benefit of ₹5,000/- is disbursed through direct benefit transfer (DBT) into the beneficiaries’ accounts. The is provided after the fulfillment of specified conditions including child registration and first immunization cycle.

## Benefits

- **Monetary Benefit:** A cash incentive of ₹5,000/- is provided in one instalment after the birth of a second child (boy).

## Eligibility

> **The following are eligible to apply for the scheme:**


  - Women belonging to the Scheduled Castes(SC) and the Scheduled Tribes (ST).
  - Women who are partially (40%) or fully disabled (Divyang Jan).
  - Women holding of BPL ration card.
  - Women beneficiaries under Pradhan Mantri Jan Aarogya Yojana (PMJAY) under Ayushman Bharat.
  - Women holding E-shram card.
  - Women farmers who are beneficiaries under Kishan Samman Nidhi.
  - Women holding MGNREGA job card.
  - Women whose net family income is less than Rs. 8 lakh per annum.
  - Pregnant and Lactating AWWs/ AWHs/ ASHAs.
  - Any other category as may be prescribed by the Central Government.

> **Conditions for Disbursement:**


  - The child born on or after 08.03.2022 in any family in state of Haryana.
  - Child birth is registered.
  - Child has received first cycle of BCG, OPV, DPT and Hepatitis-B or its equivalent/substitute.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana"?

The main objective of the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana" is to provide financial assistance of ₹5,000 to support women during maternity, compensate partial wage loss, and encourage proper healthcare practices among pregnant and lactating mothers, especially after the birth of their second child.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to receive benefits under the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana"?

Women who belong to specific eligible categories such as Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, persons with disabilities (40% or more), Below Poverty Line (BPL) cardholders, beneficiaries of PMJAY under Ayushman Bharat, E-shram cardholders, women farmers under the Kisan Samman Nidhi, MGNREGA job cardholders, and those with an annual family income below ₹8,00,000 are eligible to receive benefits under the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana".
<br>

<br>

### What are the conditions that must be met to receive the ₹5,000 benefit under this scheme?

To receive the ₹5,000/- benefit under the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana", the applicant must register her pregnancy, undergo at least one antenatal check-up within six months from the Last Menstrual Period (LMP), ensure the birth of the child is officially registered, and confirm that the newborn has received the first cycle of immunizations including BCG, OPV, DPT, and Hepatitis-B.
<br>

<br>

### Is the benefit amount under this scheme disbursed in multiple instalments or as a single payment?

The benefit amount under the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana" is disbursed as a single payment of ₹5,000, provided all specified conditions and eligibility criteria are fulfilled by the applicant.
<br>

<br>

### Can the scheme be availed for the birth of the first child?

No, the scheme specifically provides financial benefits only for the birth of a second child who is a boy, and it does not cover the first child or girl children under its current guidelines.
<br>

<br>

### Who is responsible for implementing the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana"?

The "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana" is implemented by the Women and Child Development Department, Government of Haryana, which ensures the proper execution of the scheme through Anganwadi Centres and approved health facilities.
<br>

<br>

### Are pregnant women working as Anganwadi Workers, Anganwadi Helpers, or ASHAs eligible for this scheme?

Yes, pregnant and lactating women who are employed as Anganwadi Workers (AWWs), Anganwadi Helpers (AWHs), or Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs) are eligible to receive benefits under the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana" if they meet the other eligibility requirements.
<br>

<br>

### How is the financial benefit provided to the beneficiaries under this scheme?

The financial benefit under the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana" is transferred directly to the bank account of the eligible beneficiary through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mechanism after verification of all required documents and fulfilment of scheme conditions.
<br>

<br>

### Can a woman avail the scheme more than once for different children?

No, a woman can avail the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana" only once for the birth of her second child who is a boy, and it cannot be availed for any other births or subsequent children.
<br>

<br>

### From which date are children born eligible under this scheme?

Children born on or after 8th March 2022 are eligible for benefits under the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana", provided all other conditions and eligibility criteria are fulfilled.
<br>

<br>

### What is the role of Anganwadi Centres in the implementation of this scheme?

Anganwadi Centres play a vital role in the implementation of the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana" as they facilitate pregnancy registration, provide antenatal services, assist in documentation, and coordinate with health facilities to ensure scheme benefits are delivered to eligible women.
<br>

<br>

### What is the process for applying to the "Mukhya Mantri Matritva Sahayata Yojana"?

The applicants may visit the [Antyodaya-SARAL Portal](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) to apply for the scheme.
