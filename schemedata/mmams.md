## Brief Description

The "Mukhya Mantri Agricultural Mechanization Scheme" launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, offers financial assistance ranging from ₹1,200/- to ₹11,00,000/- to farmers in Gujarat for purchasing agricultural machinery and farm equipment.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Mukhya Mantri Agricultural Mechanization Scheme&quot; launched by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to provide financial aid to farmers for the purchase of various modern farm implements and machinery. The scheme promotes mechanized farming and ensures timely agricultural operations for better yield and efficiency.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** An amount between ₹1,200/- to ₹11,00,000/- for purchasing various farm implements.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a farmer.
- The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.

<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on &quot;Apply New&quot; button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the &quot;Update Application&quot; button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application.
**Step 8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) 
> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Mukhya Mantri Agricultural Mechanization Scheme"?

The "Mukhya Mantri Agricultural Mechanization Scheme" is a state government initiative in Gujarat that provides financial assistance to farmers for purchasing modern agricultural machinery.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Any individual who is a farmer and a resident of Gujarat is eligible to apply for this scheme, regardless of caste, income, or social status.
<br>

<br>

### What types of machinery are supported under this scheme?

The scheme supports the purchase of various farm implements such as tractors, power tillers, seed drills, threshers, and other agricultural tools.
<br>

<br>

### How much financial assistance can a farmer receive under this scheme?

A farmer can receive financial assistance ranging from ₹1,200/- to ₹11,00,000/- depending on the type and cost of the machinery.
<br>

<br>

### How is the subsidy amount disbursed to the applicant?

The approved subsidy amount is directly credited to the applicant's bank account.
<br>

<br>

### How can a farmer apply for the scheme?

A farmer can apply online through the official  [i-Khedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/) portal of the Gujarat government by filling in the application form and uploading the required documents.
<br>

<br>

### What documents must be submitted with the application?

The applicant must submit a copy of the 7/12 land record, Aadhaar card, and a bank passbook or a cancelled cheque.
<br>

<br>

### What are the consequences of submitting false information in the application?

If false or incorrect information is submitted, the application may be rejected, and the applicant could face legal action.
<br>

<br>

### Where can applicants seek help if they face difficulties during the application process?

Applicants can visit the nearest agriculture office or contact the helpline number provided on the ikhedut portal for assistance.
