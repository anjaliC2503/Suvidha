## Brief Description

The "Chief Minister BC & EBC Chhatravas Anudan Yojana", implemented by the Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes Welfare Department, provides financial assistance to students from Backward Classes (BC) and Extremely Backward Classes (EBC) residing in government hostels.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Chief Minister BC &amp; EBC Chhatravas Anudan Yojana&quot;, implemented by the Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes Welfare Department, aims to support students belonging to Backward Classes (BC) and Extremely Backward Classes (EBC) residing in government hostels by providing them a financial grant to cover their essential needs.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:**  ₹1,000/- per month for students residing in government hostels.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must belong to the BC or EBC category.
1. The annual family income should be less than ₹3,00,000/-.
1. The student must be residing in a government hostel.

## Application Process

### Online

Step 1: The applicant must first register on the [e-Kalyan portal](https://bcebconline.bihar.gov.in/cay/Login.aspx?Id=7).
Step 2: To complete the registration, the applicant must fill in the following details: Personal details &amp; Contact details
Step 3: After registration, the applicant must log in using the provided User ID and Password.
Step 4: Once logged in, the applicant must carefully fill out the application form.
Step 5: All required documents must be uploaded to the portal.
Step 6: The application form, along with the uploaded documents, will be verified by the concerned authorities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Chief Minister BC & EBC Chhatravas Anudan Yojana"?

The "Chief Minister BC & EBC Chhatravas Anudan Yojana", implemented by the Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes Welfare Department, aims to support students belonging to BC and EBC categories who stay in government hostels by providing them a financial grant to cover their essential needs.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Students belonging to the Backward Classes (BC) and Extremely Backward Classes (EBC) who reside in government hostels in Bihar are eligible to apply.
<br>

<br>

### What is the financial assistance provided under this scheme?

Eligible students receive ₹ 12,000/- per year as financial assistance.
<br>

<br>

### Which department has implement this scheme?

This scheme has been implemented by the Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes Welfare Department, Government of Bihar.
<br>

<br>

### Can students from private hostels apply for this scheme?

No, only students residing in government hostels are eligible for this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Is there an income limit for eligibility?

Yes, the applicant's annual family income must be less than ₹3,00,000/-.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any age limit for applicants?

Yes, applicants should be between 11 to 40 years to be eligible for this scheme.

### How can an applicant apply for this scheme?

The applicant can apply online through the [e-Kalyan portal.](https://bcebconline.bihar.gov.in/cay/Login.aspx?Id=7)

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

The applicant needs to submit the following documents: Caste Certificate, Income Certificate, Residence Certificate etc. Any other documents may also be required as per the authority.

### Can students from general categories apply?

No, only BC and EBC students are eligible.
