## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Anganwadi workers, mini workers, and assistants of the state upon attaining the superannuation age of 60 years. A minimum amount of ₹30,000/- is granted, while those completing 20 years of service receive ₹1,30,000/-.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Anganwadi Workers Welfare Fund&quot; was launched by the Department of Women Empowerment and Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to Anganwadi workers/mini workers/assistants of the state upon attaining the superannuation age of 60 years.
<br>

## Benefits

- A minimum amount of ₹30,000/- will be provided.
- Upon completing 20 years of service, ₹1,30,000/- will be provided.

**Note**: There is a provision for a 5% annual increase on the deposited amount.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant must be an Anganwadi woman worker/mini worker/assistant working on an honorarium basis.
1. The applicant must have completed 60 years of age.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

To receive the amount under the Anganwadi Workers Welfare Fund, Anganwadi workers/mini workers/assistants must apply at the concerned CDPO office.
A retirement certificate from the Anganwadi Centre, Aadhar card, and bank account details must be attached with the application.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who are eligible for this scheme?

Anganwadi workers, mini workers, and assistants working on an honorarium basis in Uttarakhand who have attained the age of 60 years are eligible.
<br>

### What is the minimum financial assistance under the scheme?

The minimum amount provided under the scheme is ₹30,000/-.
<br>

### What is the maximum financial assistance under the scheme?

On completion of 20 years of service, an eligible worker can receive ₹1,30,000/-.
<br>

### Is there any annual increment on the fund?

Yes, there is a provision of a 5% increase on the deposited amount every year.
<br>

### Where should the application be submitted?

Applications must be submitted to the concerned Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) office.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply?

Applicants must attach a retirement certificate from the Anganwadi Centre, Aadhar card, and bank account details with their application.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable to regular salaried Anganwadi workers?

No, the scheme is only for workers, mini workers, and assistants serving on an honorarium basis.
<br>

### What is the age criterion for eligibility?

The applicant must have attained 60 years of age.
<br>
