## Brief Description

Under this scheme, scholarships are provided to Scheduled Caste students who are studying in Class 1 to 10 in Government/Grant-in-Aid Schools/Private Schools.

## Detailed Description

The “Pre-SSC Scholarship for Scheduled Caste Students” scheme is administered by the Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, scholarships are provided to Scheduled Caste students who are studying in Class 1 to 10 in Government/Grant-in-Aid Schools/Private Schools.

## Benefits

**Assistance Details:**
Sl. No.StandardBoys/GirlsScholarship Rate (Yearly)1Standard 1 to 5Both₹750/-2Standard 6 to 8Boys₹750/-3Standard 6 to 8Girls₹1000/-4Standard 9 to 10Both₹1000/-

## Eligibility

1. The student should belong to the Scheduled Caste Category of Gujarat.
   1. The student should be enrolled in Standards 1 to 10.
   1. There is no income limit for the scheme.

## Application Process

### Offline

1. To avail the benefits of the scheme, eligible students need to contact the principal of their school.
   1. The Principal of concerned school has to submit the online proposal on the Digital Gujarat Portal (Portal: [https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/)).
