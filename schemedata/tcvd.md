## Brief Description

The scheme aims to conduct training classes in Veda, Devaram, and Nalayira Divya Prabantham for divine worship/Poojas. It benefits eligible candidates by providing religious training in Saivaite and Vainavaite temples, and provides an honorarium of ₹1,000/- per month to the instructing pandits.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Training Classes in Veda/Devaram" was launched by the Hindu Religious Institutions and Wakf Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. The scheme offers specialized training classes in Veda, Devaram, and Nalayira Divya Prabantham. These subjects are required for divine worship/Poojas. The classes are conducted in various Saivaite and Vainavaite temples in the Puducherry region to provide instruction to eligible candidates. The instructing pandits (teachers) are provided an honorarium of ₹1,000/- per month. Applications are collected by receiving them from some of the important temples of the Puducherry region.
<br>

## Benefits

- Provision of training classes in Veda, Devaram, and Nalayira Divya Prabantham, which are required for divine worship/Poojas.
- Instructing pandits are provided an honorarium of ₹1,000/- per month.
- The honorarium is given once every three months.
- The honorarium is disbursed upon the pandits furnishing attendance particulars of the students who attended the Veda classes.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be an eligible candidate to receive the training.
- The applicant must be a resident of Puducherry.
- The applicant must know how to read, write, and speak Tamil fluently.
- The applicant must be interested in receiving specialized training classes in Veda, Devaram, and Nalayira Divya Prabantham.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format of the application form](https://hri.py.gov.in/resources/docs/Citizen_Charter.pdf).
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form, along with the documents, to the concerned authority at the temple.
_*The processing time for the application shall be 15 days._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to list my previous academic achievements or formal schooling levels within the official application paperwork?

Yes, the applicant must clearly state their complete educational qualification in the required section of the application.
<br>

### If I am selected for training, do I need to indicate which specific religious institution I would prefer to attend for receiving the specialized classes?

Yes, the applicant must clearly state the name of the temple they have chosen for undertaking the necessary training.
<br>

### Are there specific linguistic requirements that applicants must fulfill, particularly concerning their ability to engage with the instructional material in Tamil?

The applicant must explicitly confirm whether they know how to read, write, and speak the Tamil language fluently.
<br>

### What condition must the instructing pandits satisfy in order to successfully receive the authorized monthly honorarium payment?

The honorarium is disbursed to the pandits upon them furnishing the attendance particulars of the students who actively attended the Veda classes.
<br>

### What kind of supporting documentation, specifically related to the applicant’s character, must be provided from individuals within their local community?

The application requires the recommendation of two responsible persons who are residents of the applicant’s locality.
<br>

### What types of religious centers are utilized for conducting these specific instruction sessions in the prescribed traditional texts?

The training classes are conducted in various Saivaite and Vainavaite temples.
<br>

### What are the main scriptural or traditional texts that form the core curriculum for the candidates enrolling in this program?

The classes offer specialized training in Veda, Devaram, and Nalayira Divya Prabantham.
<br>

### Does the application require disclosure regarding whether or not the candidate is currently engaged in employment at a temple?

Yes, the application mandates disclosing whether the applicant is currently working as a temple employee.
<br>

### What is the primary objective of these specialized courses, and how is the knowledge gained expected to be practically utilized after completion?

These training classes are designed to provide the knowledge required for performing divine worship or Poojas.
<br>

### Is it a mandatory prerequisite for the candidate applying for this religious instruction program to be a current resident of the Union Territory of Puducherry?

Yes, the candidate must confirm that they are a current resident of the Union Territory of Puducherry when submitting their application materials.
<br>

### What are the required personal and demographic details regarding my background that must be compulsorily provided in the application form?

The applicant must furnish their date of birth, age, place of birth, religion, and caste.
<br>

### Where exactly should an interested candidate submit the application form and all accompanying documents to formally enroll in the training course?

Applications are collected by receiving them from some of the important temples located within the Puducherry region.
<br>

### Once a completed application is submitted through the appropriate channel, what is the standard duration required for processing?

The established processing time for the application shall be 15 days.
<br>

### Which specific annexure or standardized document number must I use to ensure I am filling out the correct application form for these classes?

The prescribed Application Form for this training program is provided in Annexure-IV (or Annexure-4).
<br>
