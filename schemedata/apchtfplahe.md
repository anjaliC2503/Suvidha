## Brief Description

The scheme aims to generate awareness among the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community regarding child/human trafficking, family planning, legal awareness, and health. It generates awareness benefits for the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community through Non-Governmental Organizations proposals.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Awareness Programme on Child/Human Trafficking, Family Planning, Legal Awareness, Health etc." was launched by the Tea Tribes and Adivasi Welfare Department, Government of Assam. The scheme aims to generate awareness among the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community on critical social issues. The scheme provides a platform to generate awareness regarding child/human trafficking, family planning, legal awareness, and health issues such as the reduction of Maternal Mortality Rate, Infant Mortality Rate, and Alcoholism, along with maintaining hygiene. The applications for this scheme are accepted by inviting proposals through Expression of Interest (EOI).
<br>

## Benefits

- The benefit is to generate awareness among the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community on issues like child/human trafficking, family planning, legal awareness, and health (reduction of Maternal Mortality Rate, Infant Mortality Rate, Alcoholism, and maintenance of hygiene).
- This awareness generation aims towards bringing effective social and behavioural change amongst the community.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a Non-Governmental Organization.
- The applicant must have valid registration under Societies Act, XXI of 1860.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The implementation of the scheme is initiated by issuing proposals inviting Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs).
**Step 2:** Eligible NGOs must submit their proposals for the implementation of the scheme by responding to the Expression of Interest (EOI).
**Where to Apply**
The details of the scheme guidelines will be available at the official website ([**http://ttwd.assam.gov.in**](http://ttwd.assam.gov.in)).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific legal requirement must our organization meet concerning our official registration status before submitting a proposal for implementation?

Non-Governmental Organizations applying for this scheme must possess valid registration under the Societies Act.
<br>

### What is the mandatory nature of the entity that must apply for this particular program designed for social awareness generation?

The applicant must strictly be a Non-Governmental Organization possessing valid registration under the relevant Societies Act.
<br>

### Is there any preferential treatment or weightage provided during the selection process based on the operational location of our Non-Governmental Organization?

Yes, preference will be extended to Non-Governmental Organizations that are based within the Tea Garden areas.
<br>

### What is the rationale behind offering an advantage or prioritization to Non-Governmental Organizations situated specifically in Tea Garden locations?

Preference is given to Tea Garden based Non-Governmental Organizations because they are presumed to be well aware of the specific environment and dynamics of the garden area.
<br>

### What specific range of social and health issues should our organization have a prior track record of experience in conducting awareness programs for?

Your organization should have experience in awareness programs covering child/human trafficking, family planning, reducing child labour, and improvement of health, among others.
<br>

### What types of supplementary documentation should be included in the submission package to prove the organization’s previous experience and track record?

You must provide documentary evidence of your track record, which should include an Audit Report, Photographic Evidence, and any other relevant proof.
<br>

### Does the proposal need to detail the internal capacity of the organization regarding personnel and logistical setup necessary for managing the scheme activities?

Yes, the proposal must provide proper details regarding the availability of well-trained staff, adequate infrastructure, and the required managerial expertise necessary to carry out the activities under the scheme.
<br>

### How should the Non-Governmental Organization quantify or elaborate on the geographical scope of its past awareness work during the application submission?

Applicants must clearly specify the extent of their experience by indicating the number and specific names of the Districts where activities have been previously conducted.
<br>

### What form of confirmation or proof is required to certify the total number of years the Non-Governmental Organization has been operational?

The Non-Governmental Organization needs to submit documentary evidence demonstrating its total years of experience in the relevant field.
<br>

### If our organization has previously received monetary support from government bodies, is documentation of such financial assistance required for this application?

Yes, you must provide documentary evidence detailing any financial assistance that your Non-Governmental Organization has received from the Government.
<br>

### Is it necessary to provide an accurate estimate of the number of individuals or communities that the organization has successfully served or impacted thus far?

Yes, the organization must indicate the number of the total population covered by its awareness activities till the date of application.
<br>

### Should the proposal outline future operational strategies, specifically detailing the intended outreach efforts for the selected district?

Yes, the Non-Governmental Organization must include an action plan detailing the proposed number of camps and the specific Geographical area intended to be covered under the selected district.
<br>

### Through what official mechanism or process does the implementing agency invite organizations to submit their proposals for this awareness scheme?

Implementation of this specific scheme is carried out by issuing proposals that invite Non-Governmental Organizations to respond through an Expression of Interest (EOI).
<br>

### Are there particular health metrics, such as mortality rates or substance abuse, on which the organization should demonstrate a history of conducting impactful awareness programs?

Yes, required experience includes programs aimed at the reduction of Maternal Mortality Rate, Infant Mortality Rate, Alcoholism, and maintaining hygiene.
<br>
