## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote postgraduate and Doctor of Philosophy studies among talented students from Goa, encourage admission to reputed institutions in India and abroad, and foster quality education, research, development, and innovation.

## Detailed Description

The "Manohar Parrikar Goa Scholars Scheme, 2025" has been launched by the Directorate of Higher Education, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to promote postgraduate and Doctor of Philosophy studies among talented students from Goa, encourage admission to reputed institutions in India and abroad, and strengthen quality education, research, development, and innovation. It provides financial assistance for studies in India and abroad to meritorious scholars pursuing postgraduate or Doctor of Philosophy programmes. 
**Objectives of the Scheme**
To promote postgraduate and Doctor of Philosophy studies among talented youth of Goa.
To recognize and support meritorious and outstanding candidates.
To encourage admission in reputed institutions in India and abroad.
To motivate students to undertake cutting-edge research.
To promote quality education, research, development, and innovation.
To contribute towards sustainable development through human capital creation.
<br>

## Benefits

**Scholarship for Studies in India:**

- Financial assistance of ₹6,00,000 is provided to each selected scholar pursuing postgraduate or Doctor of Philosophy studies in India.

**Scholarship for Studies Abroad:**

- Financial assistance of ₹15,00,000 is provided to each selected scholar pursuing postgraduate or Doctor of Philosophy studies abroad.

**Number of Awards:**

- Up to 40 scholars are selected every year.
- In exceptional circumstances, the number of awards may be increased.
- The total number of awards shall not exceed 50 in a year.

**Mode of Disbursement:**

- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the beneficiary's bank account.

<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should not be more than 35 years of age as on 1 January of the year for which the scheme is disbursed.
1. The applicant should be a resident of Goa for a minimum period of 15 years.
1. The applicant who is of Goan origin may also apply, provided the applicant has resided in Goa for at least 7 years.
1. The applicant of Goan origin should submit proof that either parent or grandparent was born in Goa.
1. The applicant should have passed the qualifying degree examination from an institution, preferably located in Goa.
1. The applicant should have secured a minimum of 60% marks or equivalent grade in the qualifying degree examination.
1. The applicant should have obtained a minimum aggregate score of 70% as per the criteria prescribed under the scheme for shortlisting.
1. The applicant should be a bonafide student of the institution in which the applicant is pursuing a postgraduate or Doctor of Philosophy programme.
1. The applicant should be pursuing the postgraduate or Doctor of Philosophy programme during the academic year for which assistance is sought.
1. The applicant should provide a bonafide certificate mentioning the programme name and the start and end dates of study.
1. The applicant may pursue a postgraduate or Doctor of Philosophy programme in any discipline.
1. The applicant should provide rank, grade, position, or accreditation details if the institution is listed in QS World University Rankings, Times Higher Education Rankings, National Institutional Ranking Framework rankings, or accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council.

## Application Process

### Online

- Register on the [SUGAM portal](https://sugam.gshec.edu.in/) and log in using your credentials.- Select the Bursary Scheme from the available schemes on the dashboard.
- Click “New Application”, choose the applicable academic year, and create a new application.
- Fill in the required personal details and proceed to the next section.
- Enter your address details and provide information on past and current academic qualifications. Upload the bonafide certificate.
- Enter fee details and upload the fee receipt and textbook fee receipt (if applicable).
- Provide parent/guardian details, family annual income information, and upload supporting documents such as income certificates and death certificates (if applicable).
- Enter bank account details and upload the required banking and identity documents.
- Complete the undertaking by downloading it through the e-sign process using Aadhaar authentication, then upload the signed undertaking.
- Preview the application, submit it online, and receive a confirmation email upon successful submission.

> **NOTE:** [**Student User Manual**](https://goascholar.dhe.goa.gov.in/Manual) **to know more about the application process.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the studies for which I can avail Goa Scholar?

The applicant can avail Goa Scholar for PG / PhD programmes.
<br>

### Should the University/ Institution be located in India?

No. You can apply to any institution/ University within and outside India.
<br>

### Can I get selected as a Goa Scholar for more than one year?

No. Once you are selected for a particular year your application for the subsequent years will not be considered. For example, if you are selected as Goa Scholar for the year 2024-25 then your application for the academic year for 2025-26 will not be considered.
<br>

### How many awards are given every year under the Goa Scholar Scheme?

Every year 40 outstanding Scholars shall be selected and awarded under the Scheme. However, in certain circumstances for reasons to be recorded more awards can be granted on the recommendation of the Selection Committee with the approval of the Government provided the total number of awards does not exceed 50. However, there are no separate seats earmarked for students pursuing PG and Doctoral Programmes.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the minimum number of years of residence I should complete in Goa for applying under the Goa Scholar Scheme?

You should be a resident of Goa for a minimum period of 15 years. However, if you are of Goan origin with a minimum period of 7 years of residence in Goa then in such cases, Birth certificate of either of the parents/grandparents born in Goa shall be submitted.
<br>

### Should I pass the qualifying examination from an institution located in Goa?

You must have passed the qualifying Degree examination from an institution preferably located in Goa.
<br>

### How is the Selection done?

Initially, applications are invited from the candidates. The Screening Committee then shortlists the candidate for the final interview. The Selection Committee selects the Goa Scholars based on the criteria laid down under the Clause X of the Scheme.
<br>

### What is the criteria for shortlisting eligible candidates by the Screening Committee?

The Screening Committee shortlists the eligible candidates only on the basis of Curricular/Academic achievements as mentioned under Clause VIII of the Scheme.
<br>

### How many marks are assigned for Curricular/Academic achievements by the Screening Committee?

100 Marks are assigned for Curricular/Academic achievements. Marks obtained for Std. 10th gets 50% weightage, 30% weightage for Std. 12th and 20% weightage for the Graduation marks.
<br>

### Why do the marks scored at Std. 10th examinations have a higher weightage?

That is because it is a common examination.
<br>

### How many candidates are shortlisted by the Screening Committee?

The Screening Committee recommends the top 100 candidates with a minimum aggregate score of 70 marks after assigning the respective weightages for academic and curricular achievements.
<br>

### What happens if more than one candidate secures the same marks for the 100th position?

In such a situation, all candidates at the 100th position securing the same marks will be called for the interview.
<br>

### Should I submit CV, Write-up and Extra Curricular Certificates along with the application?

No. CV, write-up of 500 words and Extra Curricular Certificates will be collected in phase two from only those candidates who get shortlisted for the interview.
<br>

### What should I do after submitting the online form?

After successfully submitting the online form on the Goa Scholars Portal, you will receive an acknowledgement email. You should then go the Dashboard tab available on the Goa Scholars Portal and download the application submitted by you. Take a printout of the downloaded application as it is and submit this hardcopy/printout to the Directorate of Higher Education.
<br>

### Do I have to submit hardcopy of the application?

Yes, you have to submit hardcopy/printout of the downloaded application and send it by post/hand delivery to: The Director, Directorate of Higher Education, Goa Education Complex, Alto-Porvorim, Goa, 403521.
<br>

### Should I write anything at the top of the envelope?

Yes. For example, if you are applying for Goa Scholars Scheme 2025-26, the right-hand-top corner of the envelope should have the inscription “APPLICATION FOR MANOHAR PARRIKAR GOA SCHOLAR SCHEME 2025-26”.
