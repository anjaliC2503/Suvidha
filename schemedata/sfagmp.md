## Brief Description

The scheme "Kishori Balika Yojana" by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to provide vocational, health, and hygiene training to adolescent girls. Through this scheme, training and material assistance are provided to the girls.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Kishori Balika Yojana" by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Madhya Pradesh aims to provide vocational, health, and hygiene training to adolescent girls. Through this scheme, girls from each Anganwadi center are selected and trained by master trainers, who in turn provide training at the Gram Panchayat level. The scheme is implemented by Anganwadi Workers and includes Two-day Master Trainer Training Programs provided at monthly intervals.

## Benefits

- Each Anganwadi center selects 2 adolescent girls, 1 Anganwadi worker, and 1 Auxiliary Nurse Midwife (ANM) for a two-day master trainer training at monthly intervals.
- Master trainers provide vocational, health, and hygiene training at the Gram Panchayat level.
- A two-day master trainer training program is provided at monthly intervals.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.
   1. The applicant should be a girl.
   1. The applicant should be aged between 11 and 18 years.
   1. The applicant should be from one of the following categories: General, Other Backward Class (OBC), Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST).

## Application Process

### Offline

The interested applicants should visit the nearest Anganwadi Center for registration, along with the necessary documents.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Scheme for Adolescent Girls?

This scheme, under the Women and Child Development Department of Madhya Pradesh, provides vocational, health, and cleanliness training to adolescent girls through master trainers at Anganwadi Centres.

### Who is eligible to participate in the scheme?

Adolescent girls selected at the Gram Panchayat level are eligible for the training.

### How many adolescent girls are trained at each Anganwadi Centre?

Two adolescent girls are selected for training at each Anganwadi Centre.

### Who provides the training to the girls?

The training is provided by master trainers who conduct a 2-day training session every month.

### What topics are covered in the training?

The training includes vocational skills, health education, and cleanliness practices.

### How often does the training take place?

The training is held every month, with each session lasting 2 days.

### Do I need to pay any fee to participate in the scheme?

No, the scheme is free of charge for eligible adolescent girls.

### How can I apply for the scheme?

Eligible girls can register at the nearest Anganwadi Centre by filling out an application form.

### Can I participate in the scheme if I am not part of the Gram Panchayat list?

No, only adolescent girls selected at the Gram Panchayat level are eligible for the scheme.

### Can I attend the training if I am already receiving other support programs?

Yes, participation in other programs does not affect eligibility for this scheme.
