## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide free travel facilities in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation (UTC) buses to the widowed daughters-in-law of freedom fighters. Beneficiaries under this scheme are entitled to avail complimentary travel across all UTC bus services within the state.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Free Travel in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation Buses to Widowed Daughters-in-Law of Freedom Fighters&quot; scheme is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free travel facilities in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation buses to the widowed daughters-in-law of freedom fighters.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, widowed daughters-in-law of freedom fighters are entitled to free travel in Uttarakhand Transport Corporation (UTC) buses across the state.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a widowed daughter-in-law of a freedom fighter from Uttarakhand.
   1. The beneficiary must possess a valid identity card for the journey.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- To obtain an identity card, the widowed daughter-in-law of a freedom fighter must contact the concerned District Magistrate&#39;s office, inquire about the required documents, and submit the application.
  - The District Magistrate&#39;s office will then issue her an identity card, on the basis of which she can travel free of cost in the buses of the Uttarakhand Transport Corporation.

<br>

## Documents Required

1. Identity proof (Aadhaar card, voter ID, etc.)
      1. Death certificate of husband
      1. Address proof
      1. Passport-size photographs (recent)
      1. Other documents as required
