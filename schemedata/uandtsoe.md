## Brief Description

The scheme "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes students Studying in Class 1st to 8th" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, provides ₹900/- for three pairs of uniforms to students from NT/DNT Castes in government and aided primary schools.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes students Studying in Class 1st to 8th&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support students from Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes by reducing the burden of education-related expenses and promoting school attendance. The scheme ensures that eligible students from Classes 1st to 8th studying in government and aided schools receive ₹900/- for three pairs of school uniforms annually, enhancing their dignity and inclusion in the school environment.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹900/- is provided to each eligible student for the purchase of three pairs of school uniforms.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a student.
  - The applicant should belong to the state of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be studying in Class 1st to Class 8th.
  - The applicant should be enrolled in a government or government-aided primary school.
  - The applicant should belong to the Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes students Studying in Class 1st to 8th" scheme?

The primary objective of the "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes students Studying in Class 1st to 8th" scheme is to support NT and DNT students by reducing the financial burden of education through the provision of funds specifically meant for the purchase of school uniforms, thereby encouraging regular school attendance and promoting educational inclusion.

### Who is eligible to apply under the scheme "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes Students Studying in Class 1st to 8th"?

Students who belong to the Notified Tribes or Denotified Tribes communities and are currently studying in Class 1 to 8 in government or government-aided primary schools in Gujarat are eligible to apply under the scheme "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes Students Studying in Class 1st to 8th".
<br>

<br>

### What kind of financial support is provided under the scheme?

Under the scheme "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes Students Studying in Class 1st to 8th", a financial amount of ₹900/- is provided to each eligible student for the purpose of purchasing three pairs of school uniforms.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit to be eligible for the scheme "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes Students Studying in Class 1st to 8th"?

There is no income limit specified under the scheme "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes Students Studying in Class 1st to 8th", which means students from all economic backgrounds within the eligible communities can benefit from this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Can a student from a private school apply under the scheme?

Students who are enrolled in private unaided schools are not eligible to receive benefits under the scheme "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes Students Studying in Class 1st to 8th" as it is specifically meant for students studying in government or government-aided primary schools.
<br>

<br>

### Which government body is responsible for implementing the scheme?

The Social Justice and Empowerment Department of the Government of Gujarat is the primary authority responsible for the implementation of the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Where can students apply for the scheme "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes Students Studying in Class 1st to 8th"?

Applicants can apply through the [Digital Gujarat](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/) portal.

### Can students of any gender apply for the scheme?

Yes, students of any gender who meet the community and school eligibility criteria are allowed to apply for the scheme "Uniform Assistance for Notified Tribes and Denotified Tribes Castes Students Studying in Class 1st to 8th".
