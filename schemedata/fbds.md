## Brief Description

The "Free Bicycle Distribution Scheme" by the Welfare Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide financial aid for bicycle purchases to Scheduled Caste (SC) students studying in Class 8th. Each student receives ₹3000 through DBT.

## Detailed Description

The "Free Bicycle Distribution Scheme" by the Welfare Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to provide financial support for purchasing bicycles to Scheduled Caste (SC) students studying in Class 8th. Through this scheme, ₹3000 is directly transferred to the bank accounts of eligible students via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
<br>

> ***Objective:***


- Promotes education by ensuring better accessibility to schools for students.
- Available to both Below Poverty Line (BPL) and Above Poverty Line (APL) students.

<br>

## Benefits

- Monetary assistance of ₹3000 per student for purchasing bicycles.

<br>

> **Note****:** 


- The amount is disbursed directly to the beneficiary’s bank account via DBT.
- Ensures purchase of new bicycles conforming to ISI/ISO standards.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a student.
1. The applicant should be studying in Class 8th.1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.
1. The applicant’s family should either belong to the BPL or APL category.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the District Welfare Officer and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications.
**Step-2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step-3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District Welfare Officer/Block Welfare Officer and School Headmaster.
**Step-4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the District Welfare Officer/Block Welfare Officer and School Headmaster to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for the scheme?

Only Scheduled Caste (SC) students studying in Class 8 and belonging to BPL or APL families are eligible to apply.

### How is the benefit amount disbursed to the beneficiaries?

The benefit amount of [?]3000 is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account via DBT.

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

Documents include a caste certificate, proof of Class 8 enrollment, bank account details, residential proof, and an income certificate (if applicable).

### Are bicycles provided directly to students?

No, the scheme provides financial assistance for purchasing bicycles, ensuring they meet ISI/ISO standards.

### Can students use the money for purposes other than buying bicycles?

No, the amount is strictly intended for purchasing bicycles, and misuse may lead to disqualification from the scheme.

### How do I apply for the scheme?

The applicant can apply by submitting the application form along with the required documents to your District Welfare Office.

### Can APL students also apply for this scheme?

Yes, both APL and BPL students belonging to the Scheduled Caste category are eligible.

### Is there any reservation or relaxation in the eligibility criteria?

No specific reservations or relaxations are mentioned apart from the SC category requirement.

### Can the scheme be applied for in multiple years?

No, the scheme is applicable only for students currently studying in Class 8.

### Who ensures the proper utilization of the funds?

District Welfare Officers and school principals are responsible for ensuring that the funds are used exclusively for purchasing bicycles.
