## Brief Description

Under this scheme, a monthly pension of ₹1500/- is provided to persons with disabilities in the age group of 18 to 79 years who belong to Below Poverty Line (BPL) families to support them financially. This scheme is centrally sponsored.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension&quot; Scheme was introduced by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Himachal Pradesh, to provide social security to persons with disabilities. The scheme covers disabled individuals aged 18 to 79 years who are members of selected families living below the poverty line. Eligible persons must have a certificate of severe disability (80% or more) issued by a medical board constituted under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries will receive a monthly pension of ₹1,500/-. 
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Himachal Pradesh.
   1. The applicant should be a person with disabilities.
   1. The applicant should have a disability of 80% or more.
   1. The applicant’s age should be between 18 and 79 years.
   1. The applicant should belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant shall submit the prescribed application form to the concerned Panchayat, Tehsil Welfare Officer, or District Welfare Officer, along with the required documents.
**Step 2:** Eligible applicants are identified in the Gram Sabha meeting, and the verified list along with the proposal is forwarded to the Tehsil Welfare Officer for scrutiny and completion of formalities.
**Step 3:** After due verification, the applications are submitted to the District Welfare Officer, who grants final approval for sanction of benefits.

## Documents Required

1. Age proof (Birth Certificate or School Certificate)
1. Latest BPL Certificate
1. Copy of the Parivar Register
1. Disability certificate
1. Bank Passbook/Bank account details
1. Identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Driving Licence)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme?

It is a centrally sponsored scheme that provides a monthly pension to persons with disabilities to ensure social security.
<br>

### Which department implements this scheme in Himachal Pradesh?

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Himachal Pradesh.
<br>

### What is the main objective of the scheme?

The objective is to provide social security and financial support to persons with disabilities.
<br>

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Persons with disabilities aged 18 to 79 years, belonging to selected families living below the poverty line, with severe disability of 80% or more certified by a medical board.
<br>

### What qualifies as severe disability?

A disability of 80% or more, as certified by a medical board constituted under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
<br>

### What is the pension amount under this scheme?

Eligible beneficiaries receive ₹1,500 per month.

### How is the pension shared between the Central and State Government?

The Central Government provides ₹300 per month, and the State Government provides ₹1,200 per month from its budget.
<br>

### Where should applicants submit their application?

Applications must be submitted to the concerned Panchayat, Tehsil Welfare Officer, or District Welfare Officer.
<br>

### Is there an age limit for eligibility?

Yes, applicants must be between 18 and 79 years of age.
<br>

### Is there an income criterion for the applicant?

Yes, applicants must belong to selected families living below the poverty line.
