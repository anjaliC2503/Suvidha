## Brief Description

The scheme "Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, aims to promote educational inclusivity among minority OBC students by providing financial assistance of ₹900/- for uniforms.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote education and reduce the economic burden on families of minority students from the Other Backward Classes (OBC) by providing them with uniform assistance. The scheme ensures that eligible students from classes 1 to 8 studying in government and aided primary schools receive ₹900/- to cover the cost of three sets of school uniforms.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹900/- is provided to each eligible student for the purchase of three pairs of school uniforms.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a student.
  - The applicant should belong to the state of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be studying in Class 1st to Class 8th.
  - The applicant should be enrolled in a government or government-aided primary school.
  - The applicant must belong to the Other Backward Classes (OBC) under the minority category.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the core idea behind the "Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th" scheme?

At its core, the "Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th" scheme strives to ensure that no child from a minority OBC background misses school due to the lack of basic essentials like uniforms.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who introduced the "Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th" scheme?

The scheme was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of the Government of Gujarat as a welfare measure targeted at improving access to education among minority OBC children.
<br>

<br>

### Who can benefit from this scheme?

Only students who belong to the Other Backward Classes under the minority category and are currently studying in classes 1 to 8 in either government or aided primary schools in Gujarat can avail the benefits of the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### How much money does each student get under the "Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th" scheme?

Under this scheme, every eligible student receives ₹900/- to purchase three sets of school uniforms, helping to reduce the financial strain on their families.
<br>

<br>

### Is this scheme applicable to children in private schools?

No, the "Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th" scheme is strictly for students enrolled in government or government-aided primary schools; private school students are not covered.
<br>

<br>

### Does a student need to meet any income-related criteria to be eligible?

There is no income threshold under this scheme, making it open to all eligible minority OBC students, regardless of how much their family earns.
<br>

<br>

### How does a student or guardian apply for the scheme?

The entire application process is carried out online through the [Digital Gujarat](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/) portal, where applicants can log in, fill in the form, and upload documents without needing to visit any office physically.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any fee for applying?

No, there is absolutely no cost to apply; the application for the "Uniform Assistance for Other Backward Classes (Minority) Students Studying in Standard 1st to 8th" scheme is completely free of charge.
<br>

<br>

### Can girls apply for this scheme too?

Yes, the scheme is gender-inclusive, and girl students are equally eligible; in fact, implementation guidelines often prioritize the participation of girls in such initiatives.
<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme available to students across all districts of Gujarat?

Yes, the scheme is applicable uniformly across all districts in the state of Gujarat.
