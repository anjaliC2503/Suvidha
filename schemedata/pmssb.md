## Brief Description

The “Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme-Bihar” aims to provide financial assistance to eligible students from Extremely Backward Classes (EBC) and Other Backward Classes (OBC) for their post-secondary education.

## Detailed Description

The “Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme-Bihar” is designed to support students from disadvantaged communities by covering their educational expenses for post-secondary courses. The scholarship is intended for students belonging to categories from Extremely Backward Classes (EBC) and Other Backward Classes (OBC). This initiative promotes access to higher education and helps reduce the financial burden on families from these communities.
<br>

## Benefits

- Scholarship and maintenance allowance will be payable on the basis of the criteria prescribed in the resolution issued by the Government of India from time to time.

## Exclusions

- After passing one level course, studying in another equivalent course will not be entitled to scholarship, for example, after passing I.S.C. examination, studying in I.A., after passing B.A. examination, studying in B.Com. and after doing M.Sc., studying in M.A. class will not be given scholarship.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Bihar state. 
   1. The caste of the applicant should be included in the list of Backward Class and Extremely Backward Class issued by the state government.
   1. The applicant should be studying in higher classes after passing the Matriculation/Entry Examination.
   1. The family’s annual income must not exceed the below-mentioned limit:

    - Extremely Backward Classes (EBC): ₹2,50,000/-.
    - Other Backward Classes (OBC): ₹3,00,000/-.

   1. The scholarship will be awarded to only two (2) sons of the applicant&#39;s parents. This rule will not apply to daughters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the “Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme-Bihar”?

The scheme aims to support students from Extremely Backward Classes (EBC) and Other Backward Classes (OBC), by covering their educational expenses for post-secondary courses.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Applicants must be permanent residents of Bihar, belong to the Backward Class or Extremely Backward Class categories, and be studying in higher classes after passing the Matriculation/Entry Examination.

### What is the income limit for families under this scheme?

The family's annual income should not exceed [?]2,50,000/- for EBC applicants and [?]3,00,000/- for OBC applicants.

### Are female students subject to the two-sons rule for scholarship eligibility?

No, the rule restricting scholarships to two sons of the applicant's parents does not apply to daughters.

### What benefits are provided under the “Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme-Bihar”?

The scheme offers scholarships and maintenance allowances based on the criteria prescribed by the Government of India.

### Can students receive scholarships for equivalent courses after completing one level?

No, students studying in another equivalent course after completing one level, such as transitioning from I.S.C. to I.A. or B.A. to B.Com., are not eligible for the scholarship.

### How can students apply for this scheme?

Students can apply by visiting the official Post-Matric Scholarship Portal and following the registration process.

### What is the maximum number of scholarships allowed per family?

A maximum of two sons from a family can receive the scholarship, but this restriction does not apply to daughters.

### Can students apply for this scholarship if their caste is not listed under the Backward Class or Extremely Backward Class categories?

No, only students whose caste is included in the government-issued Backward Class or Extremely Backward Class list are eligible.

### Is the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme available for students from other states?

No, the scheme is only available to permanent residents of Bihar.

### What courses are covered under the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme?

The scholarship covers post-secondary courses for students who have passed the Matriculation/Entry Examination.

### Is the scholarship transferred directly to the student’s account?

Yes, the scholarship amount is directly transferred to the student's bank account through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer).
