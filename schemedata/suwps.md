## Brief Description

The scheme “Sikkim Unmarried Women Pension Scheme (SUWPS)” was launched in 2013 by the Department of Women & Child Welfare, Govt. of Sikkim. Under this scheme, the unmarried women of Below Poverty Line (BPL) families in Sikkim would receive a pension of ₹ 2000/- per month.

## Detailed Description

The Govt. of Sikkim launched a scheme named “Sikkim Unmarried Women Pension Scheme (SUWPS)” in 2013 for unmarried women of Below Poverty Line (BPL) families in Sikkim. Under this scheme, the unmarried women of BPL families in the age group 45-59 years without any source of income are eligible for payment of ₹ 2,000 as a monthly pension. This scheme is 100% State Innovative Scheme and the unmarried woman should not have been covered by any other existing pension schemes of the State or Central government.

## Benefits

A monthly pension of ₹ 2,000/- per beneficiary.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of the state of Sikkim.
1. The applicant should be a woman.
1. The applicant should be unmarried.
1. The applicant should be from Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
1. The applicant should be in the 45-59 years age group.
1. The applicant should not have any source of income.

## Documents Required

- Recent passport size photographs – 3 Number
- Attested photocopy of Sikkim Subject Certificate / Certificate of Identification as the case may be i.e. Income certificate, Caste certificate.
- Attested photocopy of the Below Poverty Line Certificate.
- Birth Certificate or Electoral photo Identity Card or any other valid document having recorded the date as proof of age.
- Attested copy of Aadhar Card.
- Unmarried Certificate/ Non-Marriage Certificate issued by the Gram Vikash Adhikari / Sub-Divisional Magistrate.
- Attested photocopy of first page of the Bank/Post Office Passbook along with the photocopy of latest updated account.

### How long will a beneficiary receive a monthly pension under this scheme?

The beneficiary will get a pension as long as she is unmarried.

### What is the age limit under this scheme?

Unmarried women of the age group 45-59 years are eligible for the scheme.

### Is this scheme for employed unmarried woman?

No, this scheme is applicable only to unmarried women without any source of income.

### Is this scheme applicable for all women?

No, this scheme is only for unmarried women of BPL families of Sikkim state.

No, the unmarried woman should not have been covered by any other existing pension schemes of the State or Central government.

Yes, this scheme is 100% State Innovative Scheme.

### Can a beneficiary avail of the benefits after permanent migration to another state?

No, to continue the benefits of the scheme, the beneficiary should not be migrated permanently outside the state of Sikkim.

The applicant may fill application both online & offline mode.

The pension shall be distributed through Direct Benefit Transfer System or any other system as may be decided by the Sanctioning / Competent Authority from time to time.
