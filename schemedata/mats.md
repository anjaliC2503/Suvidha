## Brief Description

The objective of the Meghalaya Adventure Tourism Scheme is to promote Meghalaya as a premier eco-adventure destination while generating local livelihoods through sustainable and community-based tourism. It also ensures safety, environmental protection, and sectoral growth.

## Detailed Description

The **Meghalaya Adventure Tourism Scheme,** under the Chief Minister’s CM-ELEVATE Program and implemented by the Department of Tourism, Government of Meghalaya, aims to position Meghalaya as a leading eco-adventure destination. It leverages the state’s unique natural landscapes to promote sustainable tourism and generate local livelihoods. The scheme addresses barriers such as high equipment costs, limited credit, and safety gaps through 75% capital subsidies, credit linkage via partner banks, standardized training, eco-infrastructure support, and marketing assistance. Strict environmental safeguards and global safety standards are mandatory. It supports individual entrepreneurs, registered operators, and community-based groups in activities such as caving, rafting, paragliding, and more—fostering employment, community engagement, and sectoral growth.
<br>

## Benefits

1. 75% of total equipment cost supported (5% by entrepreneur, 75% as front-ended subsidy by Government of Meghalaya)
1. Credit linkage through partnered banks (Meghalaya Rural Bank, MCAB, SBI, Shillong Co-operative Urban Bank)
1. Loan of up to 20% of project cost, repayable over a 2-year term, with a 6-month moratorium
1. Financial support for eco-friendly infrastructure (e.g., eco-campsites, bio-toilets, safety signage)
1. Financial assistance for training and certification with national bodies (e.g., IMF, NIWS)
1. Workshops on first aid, CPR, wilderness rescue, risk management, and customer service
1. Marketing support via official Meghalaya Tourism channels and promotional materials
1. Support in building linkages with tour operators and participation in tourism marts
1. Pre-approved vendor list available, but entrepreneurs may choose other vendors within capped prices
1. Mandatory environmental safeguards and compliance with national/international safety standards
1. Capacity building programs and SOP development support for adventure operators

## Exclusions

**Individuals**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for:**
A) Pressure groups and organizations primarily political in nature
B) More than one member from a single family
C) Following individuals and their immediate family members (spouse, parents, children up to age 30):
i. State and Central Government employees
ii. Employees of public sector undertakings, their subsidiaries, or divisions
iii. Politically affiliated individuals (MLAs, MLCs, MPs, etc.) or those seeking public office
iv. Registered class-1 contractors
v. Consultants currently engaged with State or Central Government
**Registered and Unregistered**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for:**
A) Pressure groups and organizations primarily political in nature
B) Entities led by or involving:
i) State and Central Government employees
ii) Employees of public sector undertakings, subsidiaries, or divisions
iii) Politically affiliated individuals (MLAs, MLCs, MPs, etc.)
iv) Registered class-1 contractors
v) Consultants currently engaged with State or Central Government

## Eligibility

**For Individuals:**

1. The applicant must be between 18 and 58 years of age.
1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Meghalaya.
1. The applicant must be involved in the tourism sector, specifically in adventure tourism (e.g., 4x4 vehicles).
1. The applicant must provide a valid government-issued ID with address (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
1. The applicant shall not be a credit defaulter with any bank.
1. The applicant must have experience as a commercial driver if applying for the 4x4 category.

**For Registered and Unregistered**

1. The entity must provide an Incorporation Certificate or a valid Registration Certificate, as applicable.
1. The entity must be based in Meghalaya and actively engaged in the tourism sector.
1. The entity must have an active bank account and submit the last six months’ bank statement.
1. The key office bearers (President/Secretary) must provide valid government ID proof with address (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
1. The entity shall not be a credit defaulter with any bank or financial institution.
1. The entity must hold land ownership or have a lease for the project site, certified by competent authorities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Meghalaya Adventure Tourism Scheme?

The scheme, under the CM-ELEVATE Program and implemented by the Department of Tourism, aims to develop Meghalaya as a top eco-adventure destination, promoting sustainable tourism and generating local livelihoods through activities like caving, rafting, and paragliding.
<br>

### What financial support is available?

The scheme provides 75% subsidy on equipment costs, credit linkage through partner banks, and loans up to 20% of the project cost, repayable over 2 years with a 6-month moratorium.
<br>

### Does the scheme support eco-friendly infrastructure?

Yes. Financial assistance is provided for eco-friendly infrastructure like eco-campsites, bio-toilets, and safety signage to promote sustainable tourism.
<br>

### Is training and certification included?

Yes. The scheme funds training and certification with bodies like IMF and NIWS and offers workshops on first aid, CPR, rescue operations, risk management, and customer service.
<br>

### How is marketing support provided?

Entrepreneurs receive promotion through official Meghalaya Tourism channels, promotional materials, connections with tour operators, and opportunities to participate in tourism marts.
<br>

### What safety and environmental standards are required?

Operators must follow national and international safety standards and adhere to strict environmental safeguards for sustainable operations.
<br>

### What are the criteria for individuals?

Applicants must be 18–58 years old, permanent residents of Meghalaya, active in adventure tourism, not be credit defaulters, provide valid ID proof, and have commercial driving experience if applying for 4x4 activities.
<br>

### Which adventure activities are supported?

The scheme supports activities like caving, rafting, paragliding, trekking, 4x4 off-roading, and other eco-adventure tourism experiences.
<br>

### How does the scheme support local communities?

By funding individual entrepreneurs, operators, and community groups, the scheme creates employment, skill development, and active community participation in tourism.
<br>
