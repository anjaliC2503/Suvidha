# Scheme for Promotion of Affordable Rental Smart Housing (SPARSH 2.0): Government Affordable Rental Housing Development Support

## Brief Description

The scheme aims to increase affordable rental housing infrastructure. Through this scheme, land identification and government housing development support are provided.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Scheme for Promotion of Affordable Rental Smart Housing (SPARSH 2.0)” implemented through verticals "***Incentivising government departments, PSUs, autonomous bodies to either use their vacant residential complexes for stay of urban poor by renting them out on outsourcing model or construction of such new SPARSH 2.0 housing complexes on vacant land under their ownership***" by the Labour & Employment Department, UT Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu aims to expand affordable rental housing stock through government participation and institutional support. Through this scheme, land identification, housing development support, and conversion of existing vacant government housing are provided for affordable rental housing purposes. The scheme is implemented by Municipalities, District Panchayats, Public Sector Undertakings, Autonomous Bodies, and other government agencies in coordination with the Department of Labour & Employment. The scheme promotes the creation of at least 1 SPARSH 2.0 housing complex by each participating authority. Existing government housing stock may also be converted into affordable rental housing complexes. Construction may be carried out through own funds or through Grant-in-Aid support. The scheme contributes towards the creation of long-term affordable housing infrastructure.
> **Objectives:**


- To address the vision of “Aatma Nirbhar Bharat Abhiyan” by creating a sustainable ecosystem of affordable rental housing solutions for workers in industries, construction sites, and other weaker sections.
- To achieve the objective of “Housing for All” by promoting affordable rental housing for workers and labourers.
- To create a conducive environment by incentivising public and private entities to invest in affordable rental housing stock.
- To increase the productivity of industries by improving the health and well-being of workers.
- To reduce the financial burden on workers due to treatment costs and wage loss.
- To increase the happiness and quality of life of workers.
- To harmonize the Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHC) Scheme guidelines with the SPARSH Scheme.

<br>

## Benefits

1. All municipalities, District Panchayats, PSUs, and Autonomous bodies within UT of DNH-DD shall be obliged to identify lands in their respective jurisdictions with the help of concerned revenue departments, and to construct at least one SPARSH 2.0 housing or to run at least one SPARSH 2.0 housing on houses already under their possession in their respective jurisdictions
1. Such municipalities, District Panchayats, PSUs, and Autonomous bodies within UT of DNH-DD shall do so either through their own funds, or if the same is not viable, through Grant in Aid allotted to them.
1. In order to be eligible under SPARSH 2.0, the units constructed under this vertical shall follow the same norms as mentioned in Vertical 2.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to Dadra And Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.
1. The applicant should be the owner or authorised representative of the housing complex.
1. The housing complex should fulfil SPARSH 2.0 infrastructure norms.
1. The housing complex should possess a valid construction permission and/or occupancy certificate.
1. The applicant should agree to reserve at least 66% dwelling units for Economically Weaker Section beneficiaries.
1. The applicant should agree to maintain the housing complex and waste management systems.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The owner or authorised representative of the housing complex should obtain Form A prescribed under the scheme.
**Step 2:** The applicant should fill out Form A and attach all required supporting documents.
**Step 3:** The completed application should be submitted to the concerned officer designated by the Secretary, Labour, for the respective district.
**Step 4:** The nodal officer shall verify the application within 30 days. Technical verification may also be conducted through the Associate Town Planner or other competent authority.
**Step 5:** After successful verification, the nodal officer shall issue the Registration Certificate in Form B.
**Step 6:** The nodal officer shall execute an agreement with the owner as prescribed under the scheme.
**Step 7:** The registration shall be verified annually. Registration may be cancelled if the scheme conditions are violated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is this SPARSH 2.0 vertical about?

This vertical promotes the use of vacant residential complexes and vacant land owned by government departments, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), and autonomous bodies for affordable housing under SPARSH 2.0.
<br>

### Which organisations are covered under this vertical?

The following organisations within the Union Territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu are covered:

- Municipalities
- District Panchayats
- Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs)
- Autonomous Bodies

<br>

### What is the objective of this vertical?

The objective is to increase affordable rental housing availability for the urban poor through government-owned infrastructure and land.
<br>

### Can vacant government residential complexes be used under this vertical?

Yes. Vacant residential complexes may be used to accommodate the urban poor by renting them out through an outsourcing model.
<br>

### Can new SPARSH 2.0 housing complexes be constructed?

Yes. New SPARSH 2.0 housing complexes may be constructed on vacant land owned by government departments, PSUs, or autonomous bodies.
<br>

### Are authorities required to identify land for housing development?

Yes. Authorities are required to identify suitable land within their jurisdictions with the assistance of the concerned revenue departments.
<br>

### Is construction of at least one SPARSH 2.0 housing complex mandatory?

Yes. Municipalities, District Panchayats, PSUs, and autonomous bodies are required to construct at least one SPARSH 2.0 housing complex or operate at least one SPARSH 2.0 housing facility using existing houses under their possession.
<br>

### Can existing houses already under possession be used?

Yes. Existing houses under the possession of the concerned authority may be used for operating SPARSH 2.0 housing.
<br>

### How can the housing projects be funded?

Projects may be funded:

- Through the organisation’s own funds, or
- Through Grant-in-Aid support, if self-funding is not viable.

<br>

### During which period is SPARSH 2.0 operational?

SPARSH 2.0 is operational from 2022 to 2027.
<br>

### What are the general documents that may be required?

Authorities may need:

- Land Ownership Documents
- Project Proposal
- Construction Plans
- Government Approval Documents
- Grant-In-Aid Approval Documents (If Applicable)
