## Brief Description

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) Scheme was launched on 25th June 2015 by the Hon’ble Prime Minister to provide all-weather pucca houses to all eligible urban households across the nation with the vision of ‘Housing for All’.

## Detailed Description

The “Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Urban (PMAY-U)” was launched on 25th June 2015 by the Ministry of Housing &amp; Urban Affairs, Government of India with the vision of “Housing for All” to provide pucca houses to eligible urban households. Implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, the scheme addresses urban housing shortages, especially for EWS, LIG, and MIG categories, including slum dwellers. It promotes affordable and sustainable housing through components like the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS), offering interest subsidies on housing loans. The mission covers all statutory towns and notified urban areas as per Census 2011. Aadhaar is mandatory for all eligible beneficiaries under the scheme.

## Eligibility

1. The family identifies as one of the following -
a)    Economically Weaker Section (EWS): households with annual income up to ₹3,00,000.
b)    Low Income Group (LIG): households with annual income between ₹3,00,001 and ₹6,00,000.
c)    Middle Income Group-1 (MIG-1): households with annual income between ₹6,00,001 and ₹12,00,000.
d)    Middle Income Group-2 (MIG-2): households with annual income between ₹12,00,001 and ₹18,00,000.
2. The applicant or his/her family members must not own a pucca house in any part of the country.
3. The family must comprise of husband/wife and unmarried children.
4. The town/city in which the family resides must be covered under the scheme.
5. The family MUST NOT have previously availed the benefits of any housing-related schemes set up by the Government of India.

## Application Process

### Online

**Application URL:** https://pmaymis.gov.in/

**Step 1:** Interested beneficiary who wants to own a house under PMAY-U 2.0 can register herself/himself on the [PMAY-U 2.0 website](https://pmay-urban.gov.in/), wherein it is required to click on ‘Apply for PMAY-U 2.0’ tab. 
**Step 2:** The User is required to read and understand the instructions carefully, her/his eligibility criteria, the option to choose any one vertical out of the four verticals of PMAY-U 2,0 before availing the benefits.
**Step 3:** After proceeding, the User needs to undergo the eligibility check for the scheme.
**Step 4:** Once the Eligibility check is completed, the user shall give consent for Aadhaar authentication. 
**Step 5:** User will then be taken to the Beneficiary Survey Form for all verticals. The survey form has different sections.
> User Manual: [Click here](https://pmaymis.gov.in/PMAYMIS2_2024/PDF/UserManual/BeneficairyApplication_for_SoP_27_12_24.pdf).

### Offline

**Application URL:** https://cscindia.info/

**Step 1:** Visit a [Common Service Centre (CSC) near you](https://cscindia.info/). 
**Step 2:** Purchase the application form by paying ₹ 25 (plus GST) at the counter.
**Step 3:** In the form, fill in all the mandatory details, and attach copies of the required documents. The details to be filled include name, contact number, other personal details, bank account and income details, among others.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form at the CSC.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the entire mission implemented as Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS)?

Except for the component of Credit Linked Subsidy which is implemented as a Central Sector Scheme, the rest of the mission is implemented as Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS).

### I need to know the technical specifications, construction standards of the houses/slums that will be built under this scheme?

The houses must conform to the standards provided in National Building Code (NBC) and Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). For a detailed description of the houses/slums, please refer Section-2 of the scheme guidelines: https://pmay-urban.gov.in/uploads/guidelines/60180813309f2-Updated%20guidelines%20of%20PMAY-U.pdf

### Are there any sections that would be given preference while providing the benefits?

Preference will be given to differently-abled persons, senior citizens, SCs, STs, OBCs, Minority, single women, transgender and other weaker & vulnerable sections of the society.

### Will I still be eligible to apply for government’s ongoing or upcoming schemes if I apply for PMAY-U?

The benefits of all existing and future rural schemes will not be denied to a beneficiary solely on the grounds that she/he has availed a house under PMAY(U).

### I am a beneficiary of PMAY (G) but I am in the permanent waitlist. What are my options?

The beneficiaries in the permanent wait list of PMAY (G) will have the flexibility for opting for a house under PMAY(G) or PMAY(U).

### How do I know if my town is covered under the scheme?

You can visit https://pmay-urban.gov.in/pmayprogress to check the same. Alternatively, you can visit https://pmay-urban.gov.in/ and head to "City Wise Progress".

### For how am I required to be residing in an urban area to become a beneficiary under this scheme?

The States/UTs, at their discretion, may decide a cut-off date on which beneficiaries need to be a resident of that urban area for being eligible to take benefits under the scheme.

### Will central assistance be provided for rehabilitation of dwellers from slums built on private land?

No, slums built on private land are not covered under central assistance.

### Is there a private entity authorised by MoHUA to collect fee from the beneficiary under PMAY-U?

No private entity/persons have been authorised by MoHUA to collect this fee from any beneficiary under this scheme.

### How do I know if a field in the application form is mandatory?

The mandatory fields have a red asterisk (*) mark at the end.

### How do I check my application status?

There are two ways in which the applicant can check his/her application status:
Method-1: Using Assessment ID.
Step 1: Log on to the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana website: http://pmaymis.gov.in/Track_Application_Status.aspx or go to the 'Citizen Application' option and then choose the 'Check Status' button.
Step 2: When you hit the track button, there will be two options displayed: 'By Name, Father's Name & Mobile No' and 'By Assessment ID'.
Step 3: Choose the 'By Assessment ID' option.
Step 4: Enter the Assessment ID & Mobile Number that was assigned when the form was filled and submitted.
Step 5: After filling in the details, click 'Submit'.
Step 6: The user can view the progress of his/her application.
 

Method-2: Using Name, Father's Name and Mobile Number.
Step 1: Log on to the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana website (pmaymis.gov.in) and go to the 'Citizen Application' option and then choose the 'Check Status' button.
Step 2: After clicking on the know your status button, choose the 'By Name, Father's Name & Mobile No' option.
Step 3: Once this is done, provide the State, District and City details.
Step 4: The next step is to fill in the Name, Father's name and Mobile Number.
Step 5: Once this done, Click 'Submit'.
Step 6: Once this done, the status can be viewed.
