## Brief Description

Launched in 1976-77, the "Post Matric College Scholarship" is a scheme by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides financial assistance to encourage Scheduled Caste students to pursue higher education.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 1976-77, the "Post Matric College Scholarship" is a scheme by the Scheduled Caste Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides financial assistance to encourage Scheduled Caste students to pursue higher education. One-third of the subsistence allowance is payable to students residing in departmental hostels who are provided free lodging and boarding facilities.

## Benefits

Post-matric students are provided with scholarships.

## Exclusions

1. Non-Govt Institutions: Students whose parents' income exceeds ₹2.50 lakh are not eligible for the subsistence allowance.
1. Govt Institutions: Students with an income above ₹2.50 lakh are not eligible for the subsistence allowance but are eligible for full fee reimbursement.
1. Sambal Yojana Families: Students from Sambal Yojana families do not have to pay fees at government schools and are not eligible for fee reimbursement.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
1. The student should belong to the Scheduled Caste category.
1. The student must come from a family with an income limit of up to ₹6.00 lakh to qualify.

<br>

**Note:** One-third of the subsistence allowance will be payable to students residing in departmental hostels who are provided with free lodging/boarding facilities.

## Application Process

### Online

**Registration Process:**
**Step1:** Go to the official [State Scholarship Portal 2.0](https://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx) for Madhya Pradesh.
**Step2:** Scroll down the page and find the "Student Corner" section on the left side of the screen.
**Step3:** Under the Student Corner, click on the “Student Registration” option.
**Step4:** Complete the online registration form by filling in all mandatory fields and uploading the required documents in the specified format and size.
**Step5:** Carefully review the details entered and the documents uploaded. Once you have verified everything, click on the "Submit" button.
**Step6:** After submitting, you will receive your registration number on the registered email address or mobile number.
<br>

**Application Process:**
**Step1:** Go to the [State Scholarship Portal 2.0](https://scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx) for Madhya Pradesh.
**Step2:** Scroll down to the "Student Corner" section on the left and click on "Student Login." Enter your User ID and Password to log in.
**Step3:** After logging in, a dashboard will appear in front of you. From your dashboard, select the scheme you wish to apply for and click on it.
**Step4:** Complete the application form by filling in all required fields and uploading necessary documents in the specified format and size.
**Step5:** Double-check the information and documents provided, then click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.
