## Brief Description

Under this scheme to motivate the growers to bring more area under irrigation and land development by constructing rain water harvesting structures for increasing the productivity and improving quality of small cardamom.

## Detailed Description

The Rain Water Harvesting Structure Scheme implemented by the Spices Board under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India, and as per the Modalities and Scheme Guidelines approved by the Competent Authority for the implementation of the “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion and Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom” during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26), financial assistance is provided to motivate growers to bring more area under irrigation and land development through the construction of rain water harvesting structures. The scheme aims to increase productivity and improve the quality of small cardamom.
<br>

## Benefits

1. 33.33 % of cost of construction subject to a maximum of ₹18,000 per structure for General and 75% subject to maximum of ₹40,000 /- per structure for SC/ST.
1. The above assistance will be for a structure of 200 cu. Mt. capacity lined with silpauline sheet. For lesser capacity the assistance will be limited proportionate to the water holding capacity.
1. Devices can be for harvesting water from direct rainfall as well as surface run off without blocking the natural flow of water including streams.
1. Registered cardamom growers having land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a registered small cardamom grower eligible under the scheme guidelines of the Spices Board.
1. The rain water harvesting structure should not block the natural flow of water, streams, or drainage channels, and should not create objections from the local community or concerned authorities.
1. The constructed structure must be inspected by the designated officials, and assistance will be recommended only for eligible cases as per the approved delegation.
1. The Inspecting Officer must ensure that all scheme conditions are complied with before recommending the case for sanction.
1. Random checking of the approved structures may be conducted by the respective controlling officers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Rain Water Harvesting Structure Scheme?

The scheme aims to encourage small cardamom growers to bring more area under irrigation and land development by constructing rain water harvesting structures to improve the productivity and quality of small cardamom.
<br>

### Which states are covered under the scheme?

The scheme is implemented in Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka.
<br>

### Who can apply for the scheme?

Registered small cardamom growers having land holdings from 0.10 hectare to 8 hectares are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What type of assistance is provided under the scheme?

Financial assistance is provided for constructing rain water harvesting structures for irrigation and land development purposes.
<br>

### What is the subsidy available for SC/ST growers?

SC/ST growers are eligible for assistance of 75% of the construction cost subject to a maximum of Rs. 40,000 per structure.
<br>

### What is the standard capacity considered under the scheme?

The assistance is provided for a rain water harvesting structure having a capacity of 200 cubic metres lined with silpauline sheet.
<br>

### Will assistance be reduced for smaller structures?

Yes. For structures having lesser capacity, the assistance will be limited proportionately based on the water holding capacity.
<br>

### What types of water sources can be harvested under the scheme?

The structures can be used for harvesting water from direct rainfall as well as surface runoff.
<br>

### Can the structure block natural water flow or streams?

No. Structures blocking the natural flow of water, including streams and drainage channels, will not be considered for assistance.
<br>

### Is Aadhaar Card mandatory for availing assistance?

Yes. Aadhaar Card is a mandatory document for applying under the scheme.
<br>

### Who conducts the inspection of the structures?

Designated officials will inspect the rain water harvesting structures and recommend eligible cases as per the approved delegation.
<br>
