## Brief Description

The scheme aims to recognize and reward organizations for their exemplary efforts in energy conservation and energy efficiency.

## Detailed Description

The National Energy Conservation Award under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, recognizes organizations for their outstanding efforts in energy conservation and energy efficiency.A total of 65 awards are given under different categories, including Tier-I and Tier-II. The award ceremony is held every year on 14th December on the occasion of National Energy Conservation Day.
**Objective**
a) Scope: The scope of the Awards is to cover sectors(Industries, Building, Transport, Institutions, Equipments) which have a share of around 56% of India’s total Electricity consumption(Energy Statistics 2022, MOSPI).
b) Purpose: The purpose of the awardis to recognize/reward organizations for the exemplary efforts on Energy Conservation and Energy Efficiency. It can be categorized into National Award(Tier-I/Tier-II) category.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Certificate & Trophy**

<br>

## Exclusions

**However, the following organizations cannot apply for the Award:**
a. Organizations having legal issues, blacklisted, or defaulters in any of the government schemes.
b. Defaulters/repeated defaulters under the Perform Achieve and Trade scheme of the Bureau of Energy Efficiency, Ministry of Power.
<br>

## Eligibility

All organizations are eligible to apply free of cost for the National Energy Conservation Award subject to the condition that their sector should be eligible for Award for a particular year.
<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Registration:**
**Step 1:** Visit the o[fficial website](https://awards.gov.in/) and click on the[ Registration button.](https://awards.gov.in/Registration/SignUp)
**Step 2:** Select your category: Individual or Organization.
**Step 3:** Fill in the required details:
Individual: Name, Date of Birth, Nominator Type, Mobile, Email, Aadhaar Number.
Organization: Type, Authorized Person Name, Date of Birth, Organization Description, Designation, Mobile, Email, Aadhaar Number.
**Step 4:** Click Submit to complete registration.
**Apply:**
**Step 1:** [Log in](https://awards.gov.in/Login) to the portal using your registered credentials.
**Step 2:** Select the scheme from the list of awards and click "[Nominate / Apply Now".](https://awards.gov.in/Home/AwardLibrary)
**Step 3:** Fill in all mandatory fields in the online application form.
**Step 4:** Click Submit to complete your application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the National Energy Conservation Award?

The National Energy Conservation Award recognizes organizations for their outstanding efforts in energy conservation and energy efficiency.
<br>

### Which ministry is responsible for this award?

The award is administered by the Ministry of Power, Government of India through the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE).
<br>

### Which sectors are covered under this award?

The award covers industries, buildings, transport, institutions, and equipment sectors contributing significantly to electricity consumption.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the award?

All organizations can apply free of cost, provided their sector is eligible for the award in that particular year.
<br>

### Are there any organizations that are not eligible to apply?

Yes, organizations with legal issues, blacklisted entities, or defaulters under government schemes are not eligible.
<br>

### Can defaulters under the PAT scheme apply?

No, repeated defaulters under the Perform Achieve and Trade (PAT) scheme are not allowed to apply.
<br>

### How many awards are given under this scheme?

A total of 65 awards are given across various sectors and categories.
<br>

### Who can nominate for the award?

Any eligible organization can nominate itself for the award.
<br>

### How is the final selection made?

The Selection Committee evaluates the data and recommends candidates to the Award Committee for final approval.
<br>

### Who approves the final list of awardees?

The Competent Authority/Award Committee chaired by the Secretary, Power, approves the final list.
<br>

### Why is this award important for organizations?

It encourages organizations to adopt energy-efficient practices and contributes to reducing overall energy consumption in India.
<br>
