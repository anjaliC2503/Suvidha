## Brief Description

The "Maanki Munda Scholarship for Female Students" scheme was launched by the Department of Higher & Technical Education, Govt. of Jharkhand. This scholarship program aims to make the girl students aware of enrollment in technical education, making them self-reliant & providing financial assistance.

## Detailed Description

The "Maanki Munda Scholarship for Female Students" scheme was launched by the Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Jharkhand, a wonderful opportunity for female students (Regular and Lateral Entry) pursuing B.Tech. at BIT Sindri. This Scholarship program aims to make the girls of Jharkhand state aware of enrollment in technical education, making them self-reliant, and providing financial assistance.

## Benefits

- ₹15,000/- per year for female students enrolled in Diploma courses.
- ₹30,000/- per year for female students enrolled in B.Tech/B.E. courses.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Jharkhand state. 
1. The applicant should be a female student.
1. The student should be enrolled in B.Tech. Programme at BIT Sindri in 2nd, 3rd, and 4th year. 
1. The student already availing/applied for any other scholarship from any other source viz state govt. or central govt. cannot apply for this scholarship.
1. The student should have obtained a minimum of 50% marks in the previous year without any back paper.
1. The student's annual family income from all sources should not exceed ₹8,00,000/-. 
1. The student must have passed the 10th and 12th examinations from schools based in Jharkhand state. For lateral entry, the student must have passed their class 10th and diploma or equivalent courses from schools/institutes based in Jharkhand state.

## Application Process

### Online

_Applications must be submitted online via the_ [_official portal_](https://www.bitsindri.ac.in/)_._
**Step 01:** The student needs to fill out the Google form and submit a handwritten application along with necessary documents to the Academic Section.
**Step 02:** Submit a handwritten application to the Academic Section, forwarded by the HOD.
**Step 03:** Submit two sets of the printed Google form and self-attested documents in the Academic Section.

1. Handwritten Application (from the student addressed to the Academic Section).
1. Google Form Printout (Two Sets).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if I am eligible for the Maanki Munda Scholarship?

Eligibility is based on academic performance (50% marks minimum), family income (below [?]8 lakh), and enrollment in a technical course in Jharkhand.

### Can I apply for this scholarship if I am already receiving another government scholarship?

No, you cannot receive two government scholarships simultaneously.

### What if I have a back paper in one subject, can I still apply for this scholarship?

No, you must have no back papers to be eligible.

### How do I apply for the Maanki Munda Scholarship?

Fill out the online application, submit the required documents, and have your department's HOD forward it.

### Is there any reservation or preference for specific categories of students?

No specific reservations are mentioned in the scheme.

### Can I apply for the scholarship if I am a lateral entry student?

Yes, lateral entry students are eligible if they meet the criteria.

### If I fail one subject, can I still receive the scholarship next year?

No, you must pass all subjects to remain eligible for the scholarship.

### How many students will benefit from the Diploma course?

Around 3,000 female students will benefit.

### How many students will benefit from the B.Tech/B.E. course?

Approximately 1,200 female students will benefit.

### Can students receive benefits from two schemes in the same year?

No, only one scheme can be availed.

### Is this scheme only for students from Jharkhand?

Yes, it's for female students enrolled in institutions within Jharkhand.
