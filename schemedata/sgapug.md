## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote agro-processing and value addition by providing capital investment assistance to private entrepreneurs operating in regulated APMC areas (general zones).

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)&quot; by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage establishment of agro and food processing units in the general areas of Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs) to increase farmers’ income, reduce post-harvest losses, generate employment opportunities, and improve supply chain efficiencies. The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of capital subsidy to co-operatives institutions that set up processing units.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Variable Grant Amount:** The grant percentage is tailored to the needs of different market committees, offering 25% for 'A' and 'B' class committees and 50% for 'C' and 'D' class committees.- **Maximum Assistance:** The maximum financial assistance provided under the scheme is capped at ₹1,50,00,000/-, ensuring substantial support for eligible projects.
<br>

## Eligibility

-  Co-operative institutions are eligible under this scheme.
  - The institutions must set up a processing unit in the general area of an APMC.

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should prepare the application on plain paper.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the District Registrar's Office.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the “Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)”?

The “Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)” aims to provide financial support in the form of capital investment subsidy to private entrepreneurs who establish agro and food processing units in the general (non-tribal) areas of Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs), thereby encouraging local-level value addition, reducing post-harvest losses, and enhancing farmers’ income.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to avail benefits under the “Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)”?

Cooperative societies that set up an agro or food processing unit within the jurisdiction area in Gujarat, and fulfill all the specified criteria laid out in the scheme guidelines, are eligible to apply under the “Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)”.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of financial assistance is provided under this scheme?

Under the “Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)”, eligible beneficiaries are provided a capital subsidy amounting to 25% of their total fixed capital investment, subject to a maximum ceiling of ₹1,50,00,000/-, as support for establishing agro or food processing infrastructure.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of processing units are supported under this scheme?

The “Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)” supports various types of agro and food processing units including those engaged in grading, sorting, cleaning, packaging, and value addition of agricultural produce within the general APMC areas.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which department is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The implementation of the "Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)" is undertaken by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>

### Is there a provision for online application under this scheme?

No, as per the guidelines currently, there is no provision for online application.
<br>

### What is the format of the application form required under this scheme?

Applicants seeking financial assistance under the "Scheme for Granting Assistance to Processing Units in APMC (General Area)" must submit their application on plain paper, along with the required supporting documents.
<br>

### Where should the application be submitted?

The application for this scheme should be submitted to the District Registrar's Office.
<br>
