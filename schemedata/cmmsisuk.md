## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote academic excellence among students entering Classes 9 and 10 by providing financial assistance to meritorious students, based on their performance in a competitive examination conducted by the SCERT, Uttarakhand.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Mukhyamantri Medhavi Chhatra Protsahan Scholarship Scheme (Secondary Level)” is implemented by the Secondary Education Department under the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote academic excellence among students entering Classes 9 and 10 by providing financial assistance to meritorious students, based on their performance in a competitive examination conducted by the State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT), Uttarakhand.
<br>

## Benefits

Rate of Scholarship:

- **For Class 9th Students:** ₹900/- per month for 1 year.- **For Class 10th Students:** ₹900/- per month for 1 year.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The student must have passed Class 8 (institutional) from a Government School or Government-aided private school of the state (excluding Central and Residential schools) and must be studying in Class 9.
   1. The applicant must qualify in the competitive examination conducted by SCERT, Uttarakhand.
   1. The selected student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance and secure at least 60% marks in Class 9.  For SC/ST students, a relaxation of 5% is provided in both marks and attendance requirements.
   1. Based on the examination results, a student securing a position in the top 10% of the total participants will be eligible to avail the benefits of this scheme.
   1. There is no income criterion for the parents/guardians of the student under this scholarship.
   1. A student cannot avail double benefits under multiple state scholarship schemes.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- To appear in the scholarship examination, a student can collect the application form from the Block Education Officer’s office or download it from the School Education Department’s website ([https://schooleducation.uk.gov.in](https://schooleducation.uk.gov.in/)) or the SCERT Uttarakhand website ([https://scert.uk.gov.in/](https://scert.uk.gov.in/)) during the months of June-July every year. At present, the application process is offline, and no application or examination fee is required.  - While filling the application form, the student must attach a passport-size photograph, Aadhaar card, Class 8 passing certificate, admission card for Class 9, caste certificate (if applicable), disability certificate (if applicable), and Aadhaar-linked bank account details. The student must ensure that all information provided in the form is correct and then submit it to the Principal of the school where he or she is studying.
  - The Principal verifies the form on the basis of documents and school records, attests the photograph, and forwards the form to the Block Education Officer’s office by the stipulated date, which is usually around 21st August. After verification, the Block Education Officer issues the admit card.
  - Students can collect the admit card from their school or from the Block Education Officer’s office. The examination is conducted every year in September in an objective-type multiple-choice format. Students who are placed in the merit list are informed by the Principal, after which the scholarship amount is directly credited to the student’s bank account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scholarship?

Students who have passed Class 8 (institutional) from a Government school or Government-aided private school (excluding Central and Residential schools) in Uttarakhand and are studying in Class 9.
<br>

### What is the scholarship amount under this scheme?

₹900/- per month for 1 year for Class  9 & 10 students.
<br>

### Is there any minimum attendance or marks requirement?

Yes. Students must maintain 75% attendance and secure at least 60% marks in Class 9. SC/ST students get a 5% relaxation in both marks and attendance.
<br>

### Is there an income limit for the student’s family?

No. There is no parental/guardian income criterion under this scheme.
<br>

### Can a student avail benefits from multiple state scholarship schemes?

No. Students cannot avail double benefits under multiple state scholarship schemes.
<br>

### How can a student apply for the scholarship?

Students can collect the application form from the Block Education Officer’s office or download it from:

- School Education Department, Uttarakhand: [https://schooleducation.uk.gov.in/](https://schooleducation.uk.gov.in/)- SCERT Uttarakhand : [https://scert.uk.gov.in/](https://scert.uk.gov.in/)
The forms are available during June–July every year.
<br>

### Is there any application or examination fee?

No. The application and examination are free of cost.
<br>

### Where should the completed application form be submitted?

The student must submit the form to the Principal of their school, who will verify and forward it to the Block Education Officer’s office.
<br>

### When is the scholarship examination conducted?

The competitive examination is conducted every year in September in an objective-type multiple-choice format.
<br>

### How is the scholarship amount disbursed?

The scholarship amount is directly credited to the student’s Aadhaar-linked bank account.
<br>
