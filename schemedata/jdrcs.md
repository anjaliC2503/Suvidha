## Brief Description

The scheme aims to foster innovation and product diversification in the Jute Diversified Products sector. It provides innovative designs, skill training, and technical specifications to Jute Artisans, Micro Small and Medium Enterprises, Exporters, and Women Self Help Groups.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Jute Design Resource Centre" was launched by the National Jute Board, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. The scheme aims to foster innovation and product diversification in the Jute Diversified Products sector by establishing a centralized design development hub. The scheme provides continuous development, designing, and dissemination of market-worthy modern, innovative, and trend-forecasting designs, including unit-specific product development on a cost-sharing or free basis.
<br>

## Benefits

- Provides up to 2 free designs per unit per quarter, with the cost of raw materials and accessories borne by the National Jute Board on an actual basis.
- Offers an unlimited number of designs on a First-In-First-Out basis under the cost-sharing model, where the design ideas and making charges are provided free of cost by the Jute Design Resource Centre.
- Grants the exporter or manufacturer the exclusive right to market and sell the developed cost-shared design in the domestic and export market for a period of one year without disclosure to others.
- Permits the manufacturer to have full rights to manufacture, market, and export the product up to the registered trademark period if a trademark is obtained within the initial one-year confidentiality period.
- Provides free technical specification sheets with required technical tips and showcases sample bags in display for reference.
- Organises specific workshops or skill training programmes at Patsan Bhavan or at different National Jute Board specified centres without any charges.

##### ***Conditions***

- The exporter or manufacturer must bear the entire cost of trademark registration without claiming any financial assistance from the Jute Design Resource Centre or the National Jute Board.
- The exporter or manufacturer must take up legal action with the concerned trademark authorities against impersonating units themselves, as the National Jute Board is not responsible for the copying of trademarked products.
- The entire raw material cost and accessories used for developing new jute samples under the cost-sharing basis must be borne by the exporter, artisan, or manufacturer.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a Jute Artisan, a Micro Small and Medium Enterprise unit, an Exporter, a Women Self Help Group, or a Jute Raw Material Centre & Production Centre trained beneficiary.
- The applicant must provide a formal request format for the sharing of jute bag designs developed and stored by the Jute Design Resource Centre.

##### ***Post-Selection Conditions***

- The applicant must maintain confidentiality regarding the technical and design specification sheets provided for trademarked jute products.
- The applicant must bear the full cost of trademark registration if they plan to obtain a trademark for the newly developed product.
- The applicant must share the cost of raw materials and accessories used for developing the jute designer bags if applying under the cost-sharing basis.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant must access the [official application format](blob:https://jute.com/02a0581f-c5bb-4961-b817-3fbe5c3040c2) provided within the scheme guidelines and take a printout of the requisition form. The physical form must be used for requesting the supply of jute bags and made-up designs from the centre.
**Step 2:** The applicant must fill in all required details, including the **unit's registration status, Goods and Services Tax number, Importer - Exporter Code, and valid Registration Cum Membership Certificate number**. The applicant must clearly indicate whether the designs are required for the domestic market or the export market.
**Step 3:** The applicant must explicitly specify if the design request is on a free basis or a cost-sharing basis. If applying for a trademark, the applicant must disclose the intent to register the developed product within the application form.
**Step 4:** The applicant must submit the completely filled physical application form along with the necessary documentation to the relevant regional or head office. The form must be signed with the date, place, name, and designation of the applicant.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When filling out the formal requisition form to receive new product concepts and sample patterns, do I need to declare where I intend to sell the finalized goods?

Yes, you must clearly specify whether the requested items are required for marketing within the domestic market or for the export market.
<br>

### If I opt to receive the newly developed product concepts without sharing the manufacturing costs, what is the maximum number of items I can request within a specific timeframe?

Under the free distribution option, you are strictly limited to receiving a maximum of 2 product concepts per manufacturing unit during each quarter of the year.
<br>

### In the event that I decide to legally register a trademark for the exclusively developed product, will I receive any financial assistance or reimbursement for the registration expenses?

You must bear the entire cost of trademark registration yourself, as no financial assistance can be claimed from the operating authorities for this specific legal process.
<br>

### If I choose the option where expenses are shared, which specific financial responsibilities will fall upon my manufacturing unit, and what will be provided to me free of charge?

You are responsible for the entire cost of raw materials and accessories, while the actual design ideas and making charges are provided completely free of cost.
<br>

### Besides just looking at the physical sample bags on display, what other detailed instructional materials can I receive to help my manufacturing unit accurately reproduce the items?

Upon request, you will be provided with relevant technical specification sheets containing precise measurements, material types, and necessary technical tips completely free of charge.
<br>

### How exactly will the newly formulated product concepts be taught and transferred to my manufacturing unit, and are there any participation fees required for these educational sessions?

The concepts will be transferred through specific workshops and skill training programs organized at designated centers, and these sessions are conducted without charging any participation fees.
<br>

### I run a small women-led group focused on creating eco-friendly fabric products; am I legally recognized as an eligible candidate to access these newly developed product concepts?

Yes, eligible candidates include Women Self Help Groups, along with artisans, Micro Small and Medium Enterprises, exporters, and specific officially trained beneficiaries.
<br>

### If I successfully secure a formal trademark for an officially developed product during the initial 1-year exclusivity period, how long will I retain the exclusive manufacturing and marketing rights?

If a trademark is obtained within that first year, you will possess full rights to manufacture, market, and export the product until the registered trademark period expires.
<br>

### What specific procedural steps must I take to formally request the supply of the officially developed bag samples and made-up patterns for my manufacturing unit's use?

You must submit a formal physical requisition format, filling out all required details and legally signing it, to access the repository of stored product concepts.
<br>

### If my manufacturing unit applies to receive the strictly limited free product concepts, who will take the financial responsibility for purchasing the actual raw materials and necessary accessories?

For those strictly limited free items, the cost of raw materials and accessories used for development and dissemination will be fully borne by the National Jute Board.
<br>

### If my manufacturing unit agrees to financially cover the raw materials and accessories under the expense-sharing option, is there a maximum cap on how many concepts I can request?

Under the expense-sharing option, there is no maximum limit, and any number of product concepts will be provided to you on a strictly First-In-First-Out basis.
<br>

### Once I receive a newly developed product concept under the expense-sharing arrangement, how long will I hold the exclusive rights to sell it before it is shared with others?

You will hold the exclusive right to market and sell the developed product in both domestic and export markets for exactly 1 year without it being disclosed to others.
<br>

### When filling out the formal paperwork to request new product concepts, what specific official registration numbers and identifying certificates must I possess and attach as proof?

You must provide a valid Registration Status, a Goods and Services Tax number, an Importer - Exporter Code, and a valid Registration Cum Membership Certificate number.
<br>

### If another manufacturer illegally copies the specific product that I have officially trademarked with my own funds, will the government authorities take legal action against that impersonating unit?

No, the authorities are not responsible for copied products, and you must independently initiate legal action with the concerned trademark authorities against any impersonating units.
<br>
