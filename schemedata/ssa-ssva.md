## Brief Description

This scheme aims to recognize outstanding maritime contributions by providing top national recognition to distinguished maritime professionals.

## Detailed Description

The "Sagar Samman Award- Sagar Samman Varuna Award" scheme has been launched by the Ministry of Ports,Shipping and Waterways under the Directorate General of Shipping, Government of India aims to recognize exceptionally outstanding and lifelong contributions in the Indian maritime sector. Under this scheme, the highest level of national recognition in the maritime domain is provided to distinguished individuals or entities demonstrating leadership and excellence.
<br>

## Benefits

- Statue of Lord Varuna
- Citation Certificate
- National-level recognition and prestige

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be an Indian citizen, Person of Indian Origin (PIO), or Overseas Citizen of India (OCI).
1. The applicant should have at least 5 years of outstanding contributions.
1. The applicant should have contributed significantly in at least 2 major maritime areas.
1. The applicant should demonstrate leadership, innovation, and institutional impact.
1. The applicant should have impeccable integrity and reputation.
1. The applicant should be nominated by at least 2 entities or peers; self-nomination requires 2 recommendations.
1. The applicant should not have any reputational or ethical issues.
1. Government serving officers are not eligible.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many awards are given each year?

Only one Sagar Samman Varuna Award is given in a year.
<br>

### Can the award be skipped in a year?

Yes, the award may not be conferred if no suitable candidate is found.
<br>

### Is self-nomination allowed for this award?

Yes, self-nomination is allowed, but it must be supported by 2 recommendations.
<br>

### What is the minimum duration of contribution required?

The applicant must have made contributions in the maritime field for at least 5 years.
<br>

### Who can apply for the award?

Both individuals and entities involved in the maritime sector can apply for the award.
<br>

### What does the award consist of?

The award includes a statue and a citation as recognition of excellence.
<br>

### Are government officers eligible for this award?

No, government officers are not eligible for the Sagar Samman Varuna Award.
<br>

### Who evaluates the applications for the award?

The applications are evaluated by an expert committee constituted for this purpose.
<br>

### What type of contributions are recognized under this award?

The award recognizes innovation, leadership, and significant contributions in the maritime sector.
<br>

### How can applications be submitted?

Applications must be submitted online through the award portal.
<br>

### When is the award announced?

The award is announced in the month of March.
<br>

### What is the significance of this award?

The award reflects national-level prestige and recognition in the maritime domain.
<br>
