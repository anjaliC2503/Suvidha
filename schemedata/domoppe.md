## Brief Description

The "Distribution of Manually Operated Plant Protection Equipment" scheme provides farmers with a subsidy of 50% on the cost of equipment to enhance agricultural practices.

## Detailed Description

The “Distribution of Manually Operated Plant Protection Equipment” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, provides financial assistance to farmers by offering a subsidy for manually operated plant protection equipment. Farmers who raise seed farms with pulse crops are eligible to receive this subsidy.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** A subsidy of 50% on the cost of equipment or ₹800/- whichever is less.

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary should be a Farmer.
  - The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.
  - The beneficiary should raise seed farms with Pulses crop.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office.
**Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). 
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office:
> **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer.

> **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer.

> **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.

## Documents Required

1. Proof of Identity (Aadhar Card, Voter ID, etc.).
   1. Proof of Land Ownership.
   1. Proof of Seed Farm with Pulses Crop.
   1. Bill or receipt of purchased equipment(s).
   1. Caste Certificate (for SC/ST applicants).
   1. Proof of being a Farm Woman (if applicable).
   1. Bank Account Details.
   1. Any other documents (if required).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Distribution of Manually Operated Plant Protection Equipment" scheme?

The "Distribution of Manually Operated Plant Protection Equipment" scheme provides farmers with a subsidy of 50% on the cost of equipment to enhance agricultural practices.

### Who is eligible for the subsidy under this scheme?

All farmers who raise seed farms with pulse crops are eligible. Special allocations are available for SC/ST (24%) and Farm Women (20%).

### What is the subsidy amount provided under this scheme?

The subsidy is 50% of the cost of the equipment or [?]800/-, whichever is less.

### How to apply for the subsidy?

Applications should be submitted to the Assistant Agricultural Officer at the Village Level, Agricultural Officer/Deputy Agricultural Officer at the Block Level, Assistant Director of Agriculture at the Block Level, or the Joint Director of Agriculture at the District Level.

### What documents are required for the application?

Required documents include proof of identity, proof of land ownership, bill or receipt of purchased equipment(s), proof of seed farm with Pulses crop, caste certificate (for SC/ST applicants), proof of being a Farm Woman (if applicable), and bank account details.

### How long is the scheme valid?

The validity of the scheme is subject to the availability of funds.

### Is there a specific allocation for SC/ST and Farm Women?

Yes, 24% of the subsidy allocation is reserved for SC/ST farmers and 20% for Farm Women.

### Can the applicant receive subsidy if he/she is not a farmer but involved in related agricultural activities?

No, the scheme is specifically for farmers who raise seed farms with Pulses crops.

### How to verify the status of the application?

Status of the application can be checked by contacting the office where the applicant has submitted the application.

### What to do if the application is rejected?

If the application is rejected, the applicant may contact the respective agricultural officer for more details.
