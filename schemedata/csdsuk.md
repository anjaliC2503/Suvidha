## Brief Description

The scheme "Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students" by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun, aims to support students with disabilities in preparing for competitive government examinations.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students&quot; by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun, aims to empower students with benchmark disabilities by supporting their preparation for competitive exams such as SSC, RRB, Banking, Insurance, and UPSC. Through this scheme, free coaching, book allowance, transport/hostel allowance, disability and other allowances are provided to eligible candidates. The scheme is implemented by NIEPVD.

## Benefits

> **Financial Allowances:**


- Coaching fees.
- Transport or hostel allowance.
- Disability allowance.
- Book allowance.

## Exclusions

- The applicant should not have availed benefits under any other coaching scheme of the Center or  State Government.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a student with a benchmark disability (40% or more) as defined.
- The applicant must hold a valid disability certificate issued by a competent authority.
- The applicant must possess a Unique Disability ID (UDID).
- The applicant must have an Aadhaar number or Aadhaar enrollment number.
- The total annual family income of the applicant should not exceed ₹8,00,000/-.

## Documents Required

1. Disability Certificate.
2. Aadhaar number or Aadhaar enrolment Number.
3. Unique Disability Id or UDID enrolment number with disability certificate.
4 Graduation marksheets or completion of second year at graduation level.
5. High School marksheet and certificate in support of Date of Birth.
6. Family income certificate of ₹8,00,000/- or less per annum from all sources from the revenue officer not below the rank of Tehsildar/ Naib Tehsildar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students"?

The "Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students" aims to support students with benchmark disabilities by providing them with free coaching and essential allowances to help them prepare for competitive government job examinations.
<br>

<br>

### Who is responsible for implementing the "Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students"?

The scheme is implemented by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD), Dehradun.

### What kinds of exams are covered under the coaching provided by this scheme?

Under the "Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students", eligible candidates receive coaching for government competitive exams such as those conducted by the Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Railway Recruitment Board (RRB), banking services, insurance sector exams, and Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) exams.
<br>

<br>

### What financial benefits are offered to students under this scheme?

The scheme offers multiple financial benefits including free coaching, transport or boarding-lodging allowance, book allowance, and a disability stipend to ensure holistic support for the applicant.
<br>

<br>

### Is the coaching provided under the scheme completely free of cost?

Yes, the "Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students" provides coaching free of charge, and applicants are not required to pay any fee for the coaching services.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students"?

The applicant must have a benchmark disability of 40% or more, a valid disability certificate, a Unique Disability ID (UDID), and an annual family income not exceeding ₹8,00,000.
<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum income limit allowed under the scheme for applicants?

The annual income of the applicant’s family should not exceed ₹8,00,000 in order to qualify for the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### How can a student apply for the "Coaching Scheme for Divyang Students"?

Students can apply online by filling out the application form available through a [Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdKNwhS-U9zGHNRRzwkpW8XUmjD3MgUUEq1faabeEi3Hwv5Hw/viewform) link published by NIEPVD.
<br>

### Are additional allowances provided apart from the coaching fee waiver?

Yes, students may also receive additional allowances such as for transport or accommodation, book grants, and other related support depending on their specific needs.
<br>

<br>

### What are the consequences if false or incorrect information is detected in the application form?

If any false or incorrect information is found during the verification process, the applicant may be disqualified from receiving coaching benefits and may also face legal action including recovery of the disbursed amount with 15% compound interest.
<br>

<br>

### Can an applicant apply if they are already benefiting from another coaching scheme?

No, the applicant must declare that they are not currently availing or have not previously availed benefits under any other coaching scheme offered by either the Central Government or State Government.
<br>

<br>

### Is document verification mandatory for admission to the coaching?

Yes, the selected candidates are required to present all original documents for verification at the time of admission to the coaching programme.
