## Brief Description

The Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board offers a comprehensive 15-day residential skill up-gradation training program designed to enhance the skills of registered construction workers.

## Detailed Description

The "Skill Development Training - 15 Days Residential Skill Up-gradation Training Programme" was launched by the  Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, LET&F (Labour) Department, Telangana. The scheme offers a comprehensive 15-day residential skill upgradation training programme designed to enhance the skills of registered construction workers. This programme aims to improve proficiency in various construction trades, ensuring better safety standards, productivity, and employability.
<br>

> **Trades Offered:**


- Masonry.
- Bar Bending.
- Plumbing & Sanitation.
- Electrical House Wiring.
- Painting.
- Welding.
- Dry Wall & False Ceiling.
- Building Carpentry.
- Formwork Carpentry.

## Benefits

**Stipend:** 

- ₹300/- per day per trainee

<br>

**Duration:**

- 15 working days (120 Hours total: 24 Hours of Theory, 96 Hours of Practical Training)

## Eligibility

1. The worker must be registered with the Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Hyderabad.
1. The worker's age should be between 18-45 years old.
1. The worker should have knowledge of Telugu, Hindi, and English language.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The interested applicant visits the [official website](https://tbocwwb.telangana.gov.in/index.html) and clicks on "[Downloads](https://tbocwwb.telangana.gov.in/new_downloads.html)" at the top of menu options.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the 15 Days Residential Skill Upgradation Training Programme?

The programme aims to enhance the skills of registered construction workers in various trades, improving their proficiency, safety, and employability.

### Who is eligible to apply for the training programme?

Registered workers with the Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, aged between 18-45 years, are eligible.

### How much does the training programme cost?

The training fee is [?]7,000/- per trainee.

### Will I receive any financial support during the training?

Yes, trainees will receive a daily stipend of Rs. 300/- to cover living expenses during the training period.

### What is the duration of the training programme?

The programme lasts for 15 working days, including 120 hours of training (24 hours of theory and 96 hours of practical training).

### What trades are covered in the training?

The training includes Masonry, Bar Bending, Plumbing & Sanitation, Electrical House Wiring, Painting, Welding, Dry Wall & False Ceiling, Building Carpentry, and Formwork Carpentry.

### Where will the training be conducted?

Training will be held at two to three centers in each district of Telangana, based on demand.

### What are the educational qualifications required for this programme?

There are no specific educational qualifications required as the programme is for workers already engaged in their trades.

### What is the age limit for participating in the programme?

Participants must be between 18 and 45 years old.

### What languages will the training be conducted in?

The training will be conducted in Telugu, Hindi, and English.

### What is the batch strength for the training programme?

Each training batch consists of 30 trainees per trade.

### How is the training fee covered for the participants?

The Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board bears the entire cost of the training.
