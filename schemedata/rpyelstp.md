## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide free, self-employment-oriented training courses to unemployed/school dropouts/other disadvantaged youth of the state. It is a state-sponsored scheme that offers training in fields such as IT, healthcare, agriculture, beauty and wellness, electronics, and more.

## Detailed Description

The “Employment Linked Skill Training Programme” scheme was launched by the Department of Skill Development and Employment, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free, self-employment-oriented training courses to unemployed/school dropouts/other disadvantaged youth of the state. It is a state-sponsored scheme that offers training in fields such as IT, healthcare, agriculture, beauty and wellness, electronics, and more.
<br>

## Benefits

- This training is free of cost and has a duration of 3 to 6 months. 
  - Training offers employment/self-employment-oriented courses in areas such as IT, health, agriculture, beauty, wellness, electronics, and more. 
  - The training provider also strives to facilitate employment opportunities for participants.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant must be an unemployed, school dropout, or other underprivileged youth of the state.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

- Candidates can register for training on the Uttarakhand Skill Development Committee website, [www.uksdm.org](https://www.uksdm.org/), and apply to the private training provider centers listed on the website.  - Interested youth can contact the organizations by selecting the relevant job role. The list of active organizations is available on the departmental website. 
  - During the application process, candidates will need to provide their Aadhaar card and educational qualification certificate for the respective course.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Unemployed youth, school dropouts, and other disadvantaged youth who are residents of Uttarakhand.
<br>

### Is there any fee for the training?

No, the training is completely free of cost.
<br>

### What types of courses are offered under this scheme?

Courses include IT, health, agriculture, beauty, wellness, electronics, and other self-employment-oriented skills.
<br>

### How long is the training?

The duration of the training ranges from 3 to 6 months, depending on the course.
<br>

### How can I apply for this scheme?

Candidates can register on the Uttarakhand Skill Development Committee website: [www.uksdm.org](https://www.uksdm.org/) and apply to the centers listed under private training providers.
<br>

### Do I need any documents to apply?

Yes, candidates need their Aadhaar card and educational qualification certificate for the relevant course.
<br>

### How can I find the list of training centers?

The list of private training provider centers is available on the UKSDM website ([www.uksdm.org](https://www.uksdm.org/)) and the departmental website.
<br>
