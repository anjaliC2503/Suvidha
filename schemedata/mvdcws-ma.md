## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide monetary support for the marriage of daughters of registered drivers and conductors.

## Detailed Description

The “Motor Vehicle Driver-Conductor Welfare Scheme- Marriage Assistance” scheme has been launched by the Transport Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the marriage of daughters of registered transport vehicle drivers and conductors belonging to the unorganized sector to reduce the financial burden on low-income families. 
<br>

## Benefits

1. Financial assistance for the marriage of daughters of registered transport vehicle drivers or conductors.
1. Assistance is provided as per the prevailing rates and conditions of the "[Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana](https://socialjustice.mp.gov.in/schemes/view/SWhoak9tZGNSMnpISG9jbndQckN4UT09)".

- Under this scheme, the following assistance is provided:
- Each eligible bride (including girls, widows, or abandoned women) participating in the mass marriage ceremony will receive a total of ₹49,000/- through an Account Payee Cheque, on the day of the mass marriage ceremony.The organizing body that conducts the mass marriage ceremony will receive ₹6,000/- per beneficiary.

> **NOTE:** 


- A total of ₹55,000/- is provided per beneficiary under the scheme.
- The monetary amount is as notified by the Social Justice Department under the Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana and may change from time to time.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be a registered transport vehicle driver or conductor.
1. The applicant should possess a valid commercial driving license or conductor's license issued in Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant’s name should be registered on the Samagra Portal.
1. The applicant’s daughter should be unmarried at the time of application.
1. The marriage should be conducted as per the conditions of the Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana.
1. The applicant should not be a government employee.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

> **Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official website: [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on the option “Register Family” or “Register Member”.
**Step 3:** Enter the Aadhaar number and other required information.
**Step 4:** Enter the OTP received on the registered mobile number.
**Step 5:** Carefully fill in all the required details and upload the necessary documents.
**Step 6:** Enter the captcha code and click on “Submit Application.”
> **Offline Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the nearest Lok Seva Kendra, MP Online Kiosk, or Common Service Center (CSC).
**Step 2:** Provide the required documents and necessary information to complete the registration process.
> **After Samagra Registration:**

**Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://transport.mp.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/yojna_20150001.pdf) (Page No. 9) from the official website. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District or Regional Transport Office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of this benefit?

To provide financial assistance for the marriage of daughters of registered drivers and conductors.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply?

Registered transport vehicle drivers and conductors are eligible to apply.
<br>

### Is the benefit available for sons’ marriages?

No, the benefit is available only for daughters’ marriages.
<br>

### Is Samagra registration mandatory?

Yes, Samagra registration is mandatory for availing the benefit.
<br>

### Is the benefit provided more than once?

The benefit is provided as per the norms of the Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana.
<br>

### Are government employees eligible?

No, government employees are not eligible.
<br>

### Can applications be rejected?

Yes, applications can be rejected if eligibility conditions are not fulfilled.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable statewide?

Yes, the scheme is applicable across Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Is the assistance amount fixed?

No, the assistance amount is as per prevailing government norms.
<br>

### Who verifies the application?

The application is verified by the District or Regional Transport Office.
