# Assistance for Cultivation of Medicinal/ Aromatic Crops

- **Scheme For:** Individual
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Nodal Department Name:** Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department

## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial assistance up to 75% of the cost or ₹11,250/- per hectare (whichever is lower) to farmers in Gujarat cultivating medicinal or aromatic crops on land between 0.20 and 4 hectares.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance for Cultivation of Medicinal/ Aromatic Crops&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to encourage the cultivation of medicinal and aromatic plants to promote alternative agriculture and improve farmers&#39; income.

## Benefits

- Financial assistance up to 75% of the cost or ₹11,250/- per hectare, whichever is lower.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a farmer.
   1. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
   1. Applicant must possess cultivable land ranging from 0.20 hectares to 4 hectares.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step-1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at I-Khedut Portal: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step-2:** On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step-3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button and an application page will open.
**Step-4:** Click on &quot;Apply New&quot; button and submit a new application.
**Step-5:** Click on the &quot;Update Application&quot; button to add corrections to the application.
**Step-6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step-7:** Take a print out of the confirmed application.
**Step-8:** Applicant can also check their Application Status/View/Print/Upload Application details from the below link: [https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/public/frm_Applicant_Corner.aspx) 
> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/Public/frm_Public_Contacts.aspx).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the scheme 'Assistance for Cultivation of Medicinal/ Aromatic Crops'?

The purpose of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to farmers for cultivating medicinal and aromatic crops, thereby encouraging sustainable agriculture and enhancing farmers' income.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Any farmer residing in Gujarat who owns or cultivates land between 0.20 hectare and 4.00 hectare is eligible to apply for this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum financial assistance a farmer can receive under this scheme?

A farmer can receive financial assistance up to 75% of the cultivation cost or ₹11,250/- per hectare, whichever is lower.
<br>

<br>

### What is the minimum and maximum landholding required to apply for this scheme?

The scheme applies to farmers cultivating land with a minimum area of 0.20 hectares and a maximum of 4.00 hectares.
<br>

<br>

### Are there any income restrictions to be eligible for this scheme?

No, there are no income restrictions for individual or family income to be eligible for this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Can farmers belonging to any caste or social group apply for this scheme?

Yes, the scheme is open to all farmers, regardless of caste, category, or social group.
<br>

<br>

### How is the assistance amount disbursed to the beneficiary?

After the application is verified and approved, the assistance amount is directly credited to the beneficiary’s bank account.
<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant apply for this scheme?

Applicants can apply on the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/)portal to avail the benefits of the scheme.

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme?

Applicants are required to submit an Aadhaar card, 7/12 land record, and a copy of their bank passbook or a cancelled cheque.

### Who should the applicant contact for help or more information about the scheme?

The applicant should contact the nearest agriculture office or visit the [iKhedut ](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/)portal for more information.
