## Brief Description

The "Excellent Work Award Scheme for Social Workers of De-notified Communities" by the Government of Madhya Pradesh awards ₹1,00,000/- to a social worker from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities for outstanding contributions to their social and economic upliftment.

## Detailed Description

The "Excellent Work Award Scheme for Social Workers of De-notified Communities", implemented by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to recognize and reward outstanding contributions. Under this scheme, a cash award of ₹1,00,000/- is given to a social worker from the De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities who have made significant contributions to the social and economic upliftment of these communities.

## Benefits

Award of ₹1,00,000/- to a social worker.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a member of the De-notified, Nomadic, or Semi-Nomadic communities.
1. The applicant should be a social worker actively engaged in the all-round development, educational advancement, social service, economic development, and eradication of social evils within the De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities.
1. The applicant should be an original resident of Madhya Pradesh to qualify for the award.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Excellent Work Award Scheme for Social Workers of De-notified Communities?

The scheme provides a [?]1.00 lakh award to social workers from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities who have made outstanding contributions to the social and economic upliftment of these communities.

### Who is eligible for this award?

The applicant should be a social worker belonging to the De-notified, Nomadic, or Semi-Nomadic communities and should have significantly contributed to their welfare.

### Does the applicant need to be a resident of Madhya Pradesh?

Yes, the applicant should be an original resident of Madhya Pradesh to qualify for the award.

### What type of work qualifies for this award?

The applicant must be involved in social service, educational development, economic empowerment, and the removal of social evils within the De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities.

### Can a nominee apply for this award?

Yes, individuals can be nominated by others or apply for the award themselves, provided they meet the eligibility criteria.

### Can a person who has already received a similar award apply?

No, the applicant should not have received a similar state or national-level award for the same work.

### What documents are required to apply?

Applicants must submit proof of social work, community certificate, Madhya Pradesh residency proof, and any other relevant supporting documents.

### Is there any age limit for applicants?

No specific age limit is mentioned, but the applicant must have significant contributions in the field of social work.

### How will the winner be selected?

A selection committee will evaluate applications based on impact, consistency, and effectiveness of the applicant's work for the community.

### Can an organization apply for this award?

No, this award is specifically for individual social workers and not for organizations or groups.
