## Brief Description

The scheme is implemented by the Sainik and Ardh Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹21,000/- is provided to the war widows for marriage of their dependent daughters, for up to two daughters who are at least 18 years old.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Marriage Grant to War Widows’ Daughters of Ex-Servicemen&quot; scheme is implemented by the Sainik and Ardh Sainik Welfare Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹21,000/- is provided to the war widows for the marriage of their dependent daughters, for up to two daughters who are at least 18 years old.

## Benefits

Financial assistance of ₹21,000/- is given to the war widows for the marriage of their dependent daughters.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a bonafide resident of Haryana.
- The applicant should be the widow of an ex-serviceman who died in the war.
- The daughter should be at least 18 years old.
- The widow can receive the benefit for up to two of her daughters.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process on Antyodaya-SARAL Portal:**

**Step 1:** The eligible applicant may apply online through the Official Portal- [Antyodaya-SARAL Portal](https://saralharyana.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** If the applicant is not registered on the portal, he/she gets registered there. 
**Step 3:** For registration, click “[New User](https://saralharyana.gov.in/citizenRegistration.html)” under ‘Sign In Here’ and fill in all the mandatory details i.e. Name, Email ID, Mobile Number, Password &amp; State. Click on ‘Submit’.
**Step 4:** Verify your mobile number and email ID through the received OTP and complete the registration process.
> **Login to apply for the scheme:**

**Step 1:** To avail the benefits of the scheme, visit the Official [Antyodaya-SARAL Portal](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) and log in through the email ID used while registering.
**Step 2:** Enter Password &amp; Captcha and click on ‘Login’.
**Step 3:** After successful login, click on ‘Apply for Services’ and then ‘View all Available Services’.
**Step 4:** Now, you can search for the scheme and download the pre-defined documents form/template and you will need to upload the filled-in/signed form/template in the &#39;Attach Enclosures&#39; screen.
**Step 5:** Click on ‘Proceed to Apply’ to fill out the application form. Enter your Parivar Pehchan Patra number- Family ID and click on “Click here to Fetch Family Data” which displays the family members registered under the given Family ID.
**Step 6:** Select the beneficiary/applicant name and enter the OTP that is being sent to the selected family member for verification. Click on “Click to Verify”
**Step 7:** Fill in all the mandatory details and upload all the mandatory documents.
**Step 8:** Preview the application form and click on ‘Submit’.
> **Tracking of Application:**

Applicants can track their applications through the Official [Antyodaya-SARAL Portal](https://status.saralharyana.nic.in/) by entering their Department Name, Scheme Name, and Application Reference ID.

## Documents Required

1. Copy of Ex-serviceman/Widow of Ex-serviceman Identity Card
   1. Parivar Pehchan Patra
   1. Original Discharge Book of Ex-serviceman (Complete)
   1. Domicile Certificate of Haryana
   1. Copy of Bank Passbook linked with Aadhaar Card

   1. Battle Casualty Report from Military Authority

   1. Age Proof- Matric Exam Certificate for Date of Birth verification (attested)
   1. Wedding Card
