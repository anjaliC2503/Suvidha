## Brief Description

This scheme aims to recognize high-level maritime contributions by providing recognition to individuals with sustained excellence.

## Detailed Description

The "Sagar Samman Award- Sagar Samman Award for Excellence" scheme has been launched by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways under the Directorate General of Shipping, Government of India, aims to recognize consistently high-quality contributions in the maritime sector. Under this scheme, a prestigious award is provided to individuals demonstrating excellence in leadership, innovation, and sector development.
<br>

## Benefits

- Ship Model
- Citation Certificate
- National recognition

<br>

## Exclusions

- Government officers are not eligible.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be an Indian national or PIO.
1. The applicant should have consistent high-level contributions.
1. The applicant should contribute in leadership, innovation, or HR development.
1. The applicant should have measurable impact in the maritime sector.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 01:** Visit the National Awards Portal: [https://www.awards.gov.in.](https://awards.gov.in/)
**Step 02:** Register/Login using the official credentials of the recommending authority.
**Step 03:** Fill in the nomination form with complete details.
**Step 04:** Upload required documents, including citation and verification certificate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for the Award?

Individuals and organizations involved in maritime activities and demonstrating excellence are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What type of contributions are considered for this award?

Contributions related to innovation, leadership, sustainability, and development in the maritime sector are considered.
<br>

### Is self-nomination allowed for this award?

Yes, applicants can nominate themselves for the Sagar Samman Award for Excellence.
<br>

### How can applications be submitted?

Applications must be submitted online through the award portal.
<br>

### Is there any application fee for this award?

No, there is no application fee required to apply for this award.
<br>

### Who evaluates the applications?

Applications are evaluated by an expert committee constituted by the concerned authority.
<br>

<br>

### What does the award include?

The award typically includes a citation, trophy, or certificate recognizing excellence.
<br>

### When is the award announced?

The award is usually announced annually as per the official schedule.
<br>

### What is the purpose of this award?

The purpose is to promote excellence, innovation, and best practices in the maritime sector.
<br>

### Can an application be rejected?

Yes, applications can be rejected if they do not meet eligibility criteria or contain incomplete or incorrect information.
