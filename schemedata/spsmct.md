## Brief Description

This scheme has been introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat. It aims to provide financial assistance to State Market Committees and Taluka-District Co-operative Purchase and Sale Unions for constructing large-sized warehouse godowns

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme to Provide Assistance to Sate Market Committees as well as Taluka-District Co-operative Purchase and Sale Union to Construct Large Size Warehouse Godowns&quot; introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to address the storage needs of farmers through providing financial assistance for warehouse godowns construction within market committees. This will allow farmers to store their produce during the harvest season when prices are low and sell it later when market prices improve.

## Benefits

<br>

**Component****Support Standard**Large size warehouse godowns up to 5000 MT in market committeesAll State Market Committees, each without considering the class standards will be paid ₹6,500/- per metric ton up to 50% of the project cost will be available for godowns up to 5000 mt	<br>

## Eligibility

- All State Market Committees in Gujarat are eligible to apply.
- The godowns must be used exclusively for the storage of farmers' produce.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The market committee must identify land for constructing the warehouse.
**Step 2:** Engineers from the approved panel will prepare maps and estimates based on the uniform design set by the Director of Agricultural Marketing and Rural Finance, Gujarat.
**Step 3:** The Managing Director, Gujarat Agricultural Marketing Board, will oversee implementation.
**Step 4:** The beneficiary must hire government-approved contractors of class &quot;AA&quot; to &quot;D&quot; for construction.
**Step 5:** The construction work must be carried out through the e-tendering process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to assist Market Committees in constructing large-sized warehouse godowns for storing farmers' agricultural produce, helping them sell their produce at better prices.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

All State Market Committees in Gujarat are eligible to apply for assistance under this scheme.

### What financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides [?]6,500/- per metric ton, covering up to 50% of the project cost for constructing warehouse godowns with a capacity of up to 5000 MT.

### Can private individuals or companies apply for this scheme?

No, only registered State Market Committees in Gujarat are eligible to apply for assistance under this scheme.

### What is the maximum storage capacity allowed under the scheme?

The scheme provides assistance for constructing godowns with a maximum capacity of 5000 MT.

### Who is responsible for implementing the scheme?

The Managing Director of the Gujarat Agricultural Marketing Board, along with engineers from the approved panel, is responsible for implementing the scheme.

### Are there any specific requirements for constructing the godowns?

Yes, the godowns must follow a uniform design, be used exclusively for agricultural storage, and be constructed by government-approved contractors from class "AA" to "D".

### What happens if the godown is used for non-agricultural purposes?

If a godown is used for any purpose other than storing farmers' produce, the financial assistance may be revoked.

### Who approves the construction plan for the godowns?

Who approves the construction plan for the godowns?

### What is the process for applying to the scheme?

The Market Committee must submit an application along with required documents, including a project plan and land details, and follow the e-tendering process for construction.
