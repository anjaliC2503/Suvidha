## Brief Description

"Incentive For Inter Caste Marriage" aims to promote inter-caste marriages between SC and non-SC communities to combat casteism. The scheme provides a financial incentive of ₹50,000/- to eligible couples, with either spouse being a permanent resident of NCT of Delhi.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Incentive for Inter Caste Marriage" by the Department for the Welfare of SC/ST/OBC/Minorities, Government of NCT of Delhi, aims to promote marriages between non-SCs and SCs as an effective instrument to combat casteism. Under the scheme, an incentive of ₹50,000/- is provided to couples where one spouse belongs to the SC community and the other to a non-SC community. The scheme mandates marriage registration and requires applicants to apply within two years of marriage. The Secretary (DSCST) is the competent authority to authorize the concerned DC to disburse the incentive.

## Benefits

**Financial Incentive:** One-time payment of ₹50,000/- per eligible couple.
**Mode of Disbursement:** Through the concerned Deputy Commissioner (DC) as authorized by the Secretary (DSCST).
**Conditions for Disbursement:** The couple should provide proof of living together at the time of disbursement of benefits.

## Eligibility

- The couple should have married legally.
- The couple should have had their marriage registered at the Office of the Registrar, Marriages.
- The marriage should have been between a Scheduled Caste (SC) and a Non-SC spouse.
- At least one of the spouses should be a permanent resident of NCT of Delhi.
- If the non-SC spouse belongs to another state, their parents must have resided in NCT of Delhi for at least five years.
- The non-SC spouse should provide proof that their caste is not listed under untouchability in their home state and that they follow the Hindu religion.
- After marriage, the couple should be residing in the NCT of Delhi.
- The couple should provide proof of living together at the time of fund disbursement.

_*A widow of up to 35 years of age with no children can avail of the incentive upon inter-caste remarriage._

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the Office of the Area SDM, and request the hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the authorised staff.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Get attested the the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents by the Gazetted Officer to the Area SDM.
**Step 4:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the authorised staff.
**Step 5:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the staff to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date of submission.
_* The application must be submitted within two years of the marriage._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the financial incentive amount provided to couples under this inter-caste marriage scheme?

The scheme provides a one-time financial incentive of ₹50,000/- to eligible couples where one spouse belongs to the SC community and the other to a non-SC community.

### Who is the competent authority responsible for authorizing the disbursement of the incentive amount?

The Secretary (Department of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes) is the competent authority who authorizes the concerned Deputy Commissioner to disburse the incentive.

### Where can applicants obtain the prescribed application form for this scheme?

The application form is available at the office of the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) in the respective area where the couple resides.

### What is the time limit for applying under this scheme after marriage?

Couples must apply within two years from the date of their marriage to be eligible for the incentive.

### Is marriage registration compulsory to avail the benefits under this scheme?

Yes, registration of marriage in the Office of the Registrar of Marriages is mandatory to qualify for the incentive.

### What residency requirement must be fulfilled by the couple to be eligible?

Either the husband or wife must be a permanent resident of the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi.

### Are there any special conditions for non-SC spouses from other states?

Non-SC spouses from other states must prove their caste is not covered under untouchability and that they follow the Hindu religion.

### What additional requirement applies to parents of non-SC spouses from other states?

Parents of the non-SC spouse must have been residing in NCT of Delhi for the last five years.

### Can widows or widowers avail the benefits under this scheme?

Yes, widows or widowers up to 35 years of age with no children can avail the incentive after an inter-caste marriage.

### What proof must the couple provide to confirm they are living together after marriage?

They must submit proof of cohabitation at the time of release of the fixed deposit amount.

### Who verifies the application and documents submitted by the couple?

The Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) verifies the application and supporting documents before forwarding them for approval.

### Where are the completed application forms stored after submission?

The fully processed application forms are kept in the custody of the respective SDM office.

### Is there any preference or relaxation for certain categories of applicants?

Widows and widowers up to 35 years with no children are given preference under this scheme.

### How is the incentive amount disbursed to the beneficiaries?

The Secretary (DSCST) authorizes the concerned Deputy Commissioner to disburse ₹50,000/- per eligible case.

### Can couples where both spouses belong to SC communities avail this benefit?

No, the scheme is specifically for inter-caste marriages between SC and non-SC individuals.

### What is the role of the Gazetted Officer in the application process?

The completed application form must be attested by a Gazetted Officer before submission.

### Is there any age restriction for widows or widowers applying under this scheme?

Yes, widows or widowers must be below 35 years of age to be eligible.

### What happens if the couple fails to submit proof of cohabitation?

The incentive amount will not be released without proof that the couple is living together after marriage.
