## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial aid to promote education. Scholarships are provided to eligible students of Standards 1 to 10.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Pre-Matric Scholarship for the Students of Socially and Educationally Backward Class" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide financial assistance to promote education among socially and educationally backwards students.
Through this scheme, scholarships per annum are provided to boys and girls studying in Standards 1 to 10 in government and aided schools. The scheme is implemented by the Director, Developing Caste Welfare, Gandhinagar, Gujarat.
<br>

## Benefits

1. **For Boys (Std. 1 to 8):** ₹750/- scholarship.1. **For Boys (Std. 9 to 10):** ₹1000/- scholarship.1. **For Girls (Std. 1 to 5):** ₹500/- scholarship.1. **For Girls (Std. 6 to 10):** ₹1000/- scholarship.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to the Socially and Educationally Backward Class (SEBC).
1. The applicant should be studying in Standard 1 to 10.
1. The applicant should be enrolled in a Government or Aided Primary/Secondary School.
1. For applicants studying in Standard 1 to 8, there is no family income limit.
1. For applicants studying in Standard 9 to 10, the annual family income of the applicant’s family should not exceed ₹6,00,000/-.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Students belonging to the Socially and Educationally Backward Class (SEBC) studying in Standards 1 to 10 in Government or Aided Schools can apply.
<br>

### Is this scheme available for students studying in private schools?

No, this scheme is only applicable for students studying in Government or Aided Primary or Secondary Schools.
<br>

### Can students from all classes apply for this scheme?

Yes, students from Standard 1 to Standard 10 can apply under this scheme.
<br>

### Is there any income limit to apply for this scheme?

There is no income limit for students studying in Standard 1 to 8, but for Standard 9 and 10, the family income should not exceed ₹6,00,000 per annum.
<br>

### Is this scheme applicable for students studying outside Gujarat?

No, this scheme is applicable only for students studying within the state of Gujarat.
<br>

### Where can I apply for this scheme?

Applications can be submitted online through the Digital Gujarat portal at www.digitalgujarat.gov.in.
<br>

### Do I need to pay any fee to apply for this scheme?

No, there is no cost involved in applying for this scheme. The application is free.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply for this scheme?

You need a Caste Certificate, Income Certificate (for Standard 9 and 10), School Bonafide Certificate, Last Year’s Marksheet, Bank Passbook, Aadhar Card, and a Passport Size Photograph.
<br>

### What if I need help while applying online?

You can contact the Digital Gujarat Help Desk at 18002335500 for assistance.
