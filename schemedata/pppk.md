## Brief Description

The scheme provides free pre-examination training to eligible candidates for various competitive exams to enhance their preparation and improve their chances of success.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendra&quot; is an initiative launched by the Backward Classes and Extremely Backward Classes Welfare Department, Bihar, aimed at supporting underprivileged candidates aspiring to appear for competitive exams. It offers free coaching for exams like Civil Services, SSC, and others. The program includes expert guidance, study materials, and mentoring, enabling candidates to prepare effectively and achieve their career goals.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

- **Free Coaching:** Free coaching in [Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendras](https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/cache/41/30-Dec-24/SHOW_DOCS/Letter%20No-I-179552.pdf).  - **Incentive:** Students will be provided an incentive amount of ₹3,000/- (three thousand rupees) based on 75% attendance.
**Note:****** There is no hostel provision in the centers.

## Eligibility

1. The student must be a permanent resident of Bihar state.
   1. The student must belong to the Backward Class or Extremely Backward Class.
   1. The maximum annual income of the student or their guardian, from all sources, should not exceed ₹3,00,000/-.
   1. The student’s age and minimum educational qualifications must meet the eligibility criteria for the competitive exam related to the respective course.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step-1:** Download the [application form](https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/cache/41/30-Dec-24/SHOW_DOCS/Letter%20No-I-179552.pdf).
**Step-2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step-3:** The application must be submitted to the concerned Director, Pre-Exam Training Center, via registered mail or speed post. Applicants may also personally submit the application form at the office of the concerned Pre-Exam Training Center (Refer page no 3 of the [Guidelines ](https://state.bihar.gov.in/bcebcwelfare/cache/41/30-Dec-24/SHOW_DOCS/Letter%20No-I-179552.pdf)to see the training center list).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendra"?

The purpose of the "Prak Pariksha Prashikshan Kendra" is to provide free pre-examination training to candidates preparing for various competitive exams.

### Who is eligible to apply for the training program?

Candidates who are residents of the specified state, belong to the eligible categories, and meet the educational and socio-economic criteria can apply.

### What types of exams are covered under this scheme?

The scheme covers exams such as Civil Services, SSC, and other government competitive examinations.

### Is there any fee for participating in the training program?

No, the training program is offered free of cost to all eligible candidates.

### What documents are required to apply for the program?

Applicants must submit proof of identity, proof of residence, educational certificates, caste or community certificates (if applicable), and passport-sized photographs.

### Are candidates from other states allowed to apply?

No, only residents of the state where the training center operates are eligible to apply.

### What is the mode of coaching provided under the scheme?

The coaching is conducted through classroom sessions, workshops, and mock tests facilitated by the center.

### Are there any age limits for applying to the program?

Yes, age limits are applicable and are determined based on the eligibility criteria of the exams for which the training is provided.

### How long does the training program last?

The duration of the program varies based on the specific exam being targeted by the candidates.

### How are candidates selected for the training program?

Candidates are selected based on their fulfillment of eligibility criteria and verification of submitted documents.

### Does the program provide any financial assistance to participants?

Yes, the program offers financial assistance of [?]3,000/- on the basis of 75% attendance to the candidates.

### Where can candidates find more information about the program?

Candidates can find detailed information on the official website or by contacting the designated centers associated with the training program.
