## Brief Description

The scheme "Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Tribes Students" by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, and Government of Mizoram aims to provide financial assistance to ST students pursuing post-matriculation education. It covers various recognized courses.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Tribes Students&quot; by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, and the Government of Mizoram aims to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Tribe students pursuing post-matriculation education. Through this scheme, financial support is provided to ST students enrolled in recognized professional, technical, non-professional, and non-technical courses, including correspondence, distance, and continuing education. The scheme is implemented by the Mizoram Scholarship Board, Government of Mizoram. It ensures that students can pursue higher education without financial constraints. The assistance supports education at various levels and promotes educational advancement among Scheduled Tribes. The funding is shared in a 90:10 ratio between the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and the Mizoram State Government.
<br>

## Benefits

1. Financial assistance for post-matric education.
   1. Support for ST students pursuing recognized professional, technical, non-professional, non-technical, and correspondence courses.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribes community.
1. The applicant should be permanently settled in the state of Mizoram.
1. The applicant should be pursuing studies from Class XI up to a Post-Doctoral Fellowship.
1. The applicant’s parents’/guardians’ income from all sources should not exceed ₹2,50,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand only) per annum.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the benefits of Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Tribes Students?

The Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Tribes Students is a government scholarship scheme that provides financial assistance to ST students to pursue post-matriculation studies.

### What expenses are covered under the scholarship?

The scholarship covers the expenses of tuition fees, maintenance fees, and other incidental expenses such as books and stationery.

### How much scholarship amount is provided under the scheme?

The scholarship amount varies from state to state, but in general, it covers the expenses of tuition fees, maintenance fees, and other incidental expenses such as books and stationery.
<br>

### Is the scholarship renewable?

Yes, the scholarship is renewable, subject to the academic performance of the student and the availability of funds.

### What is the duration of the scholarship?

The duration of the scholarship is for the entire course, subject to satisfactory academic performance and the availability of funds.

### Who is eligible for the scholarship?

ST students who have passed matriculation or higher secondary examination and are pursuing post-matriculation studies in any recognized institution are eligible for the scholarship

### Is there any income limit for the scholarship?

Yes, there is an income limit for the scholarship. The exact limit varies from state to state, but in general, the annual family income of the applicant should not exceed a certain amount.

### How can I apply for the scholarship?

Through official website of NSP [https://scholarships.gov.in/](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
<br>

### What documents are required to apply for the scholarship?

The documents required to apply for the scholarship include caste certificate, income certificate, mark sheet of the previous examination, and bank account details.

### What is the selection process for the scholarship?

The selection process for the scholarship is based on the merit of the applicant and the availability of funds.
