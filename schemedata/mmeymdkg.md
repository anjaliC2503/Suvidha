## Brief Description

The scheme aims to form groups of interested women and men from Gram Panchayats across the state, who are 18 years of age or above, enabling them to avail the benefits of various schemes of the Youth Welfare and PRD Department and to collectively undertake business or livelihood activities.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Formation of Mahila Mangal and Yuvak Mangal Dal” was launched by the Department of Youth Welfare and prantiy rakshak dal (PRD), Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to form groups of interested women and men from Gram Panchayats across the state, who are 18 years of age or above, enabling them to avail the benefits of various schemes of the Youth Welfare and PRD Department and to collectively undertake business or livelihood activities.
<br>

## Benefits

It is mandatory to form a group in order to avail the benefits of various schemes of the Youth Welfare and PRD Department and to collectively undertake any business activity.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be an interested woman or man.
1. The applicant must be a resident of a Gram Panchayat in Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant must be above 18 years of age.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Formation of Mahila Mangal and Yuvak Mangal Dals – The Youth Welfare &amp; PRD Department forms one Mahila Mangal Dal and one Yuvak Mangal Dal in each Gram Panchayat.
  - Any individual wishing to become a member of the Mahila Mangal Dal or Yuvak Mangal Dal must have a related proposal passed by the Gram Sabha and submitted to the concerned Regional Youth Welfare and PRD Officer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can become a member of the Mahila Mangal Dal or Yuvak Mangal Dal?

Interested women and men of Gram Panchayats in Uttarakhand who are 18 years of age or above.
<br>

### Is forming a group mandatory to avail benefits?

Yes, it is mandatory to be part of a Mahila Mangal Dal or Yuvak Mangal Dal to avail benefits under several schemes of the Youth Welfare and PRD Department.
<br>

### How many Dals are formed in each Gram Panchayat?

One Mahila Mangal Dal and one Yuvak Mangal Dal are formed in each Gram Panchayat.
<br>

### Who forms the Mahila Mangal and Yuvak Mangal Dals?

The Youth Welfare & PRD Department is responsible for forming these Dals.
<br>

### What is the minimum age requirement to join a Dal?

The applicant must be 18 years or older.
<br>

### How can an individual become a member?

A proposal must be passed by the Gram Sabha and submitted to the Regional Youth Welfare and PRD Officer.
<br>
