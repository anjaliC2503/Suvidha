## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial support for tuition fees to meritorious students from economically weaker sections of Delhi pursuing undergraduate education in Government higher educational institutions, ensuring that no deserving student is deprived of quality higher education.

## Detailed Description

The Delhi Higher &amp; Technical Education Support Scheme is implemented by the Higher Education Department, Government of NCT of Delhi under the aegis of The Delhi Higher Education Aid Trust. The scheme aims to provide financial support to meritorious students belonging to economically weaker sections of society through reimbursement of Tuition Fee Only for eligible undergraduate courses. It is applicable to students admitted to Government Universities, Colleges, and Institutions located in Delhi from the academic session 2022-2023 onwards. Applications are processed through the concerned College, University, and the Department of Higher Education, and the approved financial assistance is transferred directly to the beneficiary&#39;s Aadhaar-linked bank account through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Tuition Fee Assistance:** Financial assistance covering the tuition fee for eligible undergraduate students.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a bona fide resident of Delhi.
  - The applicant must have passed Class XII from a recognized school located in Delhi.
  - The applicant must be enrolled in a regular undergraduate programme in a Government Delhi State Public University, Government College, or Government Institution located in Delhi.
  - The applicant must be pursuing a regular degree course.
  - The applicant must not be pursuing the course through correspondence, distance mode, or diploma programmes.
  - The applicant must have a gross annual family income of up to ₹3,00,000/- from all sources.
  - The applicant must possess a valid Family Income Certificate issued by the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) or other competent authority of the Government of NCT of Delhi.
  - The applicant must have secured at least 75% marks in Class XII for assistance during the first year.
  - The applicant must secure at least 75% marks in the undergraduate course for assistance in subsequent years.
  - The applicant must not be receiving financial assistance under any other scholarship scheme for the same course.
  - The applicant must apply every academic year through the online portal.
  - The applicant must pass all subjects during the course.
  - The applicant must maintain good conduct and must not be involved in indiscipline, criminal behaviour, or ragging.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant must apply through the online portal of the Government of NCT of Delhi.
**Step 2:** The applicant must submit the printed application along with the required documents to the concerned College, University, or Institution.
**Step 3:** The concerned College or Institution verifies the application and recommends eligible cases to the affiliated University.
**Step 4:** The concerned University verifies the applications and forwards eligible applications with the fund requirement to the Department of Higher Education.
**Step 5:** The Department of Higher Education submits the proposal to the Trustees of the Delhi Higher Education Aid Trust for approval.
**Step 6:** After approval, funds are released to the concerned University.
**Step 7:** The University transfers the approved financial assistance to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Delhi Higher & Technical Education Support Scheme"?

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance towards tuition fees to meritorious students from economically weaker sections of Delhi pursuing undergraduate education.
<br>

### Which department implements the scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Higher Education Department, Government of NCT of Delhi under the aegis of the Delhi Higher Education Aid Trust.
<br>

### Who can apply for the scheme?

A bona fide resident of Delhi who has passed Class XII from a recognized Delhi school and is enrolled in a regular undergraduate programme in a Government institution located in Delhi can apply.
<br>

### What financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance towards the Tuition Fee Only.
<br>

### What is the maximum annual family income allowed?

The applicant's gross annual family income from all sources must not exceed ₹3,00,000/-.
<br>

### What minimum marks are required to become eligible?

The student must secure at least 75% marks in Class XII for the first year and at least 75% marks in the undergraduate course for subsequent years.
<br>

### Is there any relaxation for SC/ST students?

Yes. Students belonging to the SC/ST category receive a 5% relaxation in the minimum qualifying marks.
<br>

### Can students studying in self-financing institutions apply?

No. The scheme is applicable only to students studying in Government Universities, Colleges, and Institutions located in Delhi.
<br>

### Are students pursuing distance education eligible?

No. Students pursuing correspondence, distance education, or diploma courses are not eligible.
<br>

### Can a student receiving another scholarship apply for this scheme?

No. A student receiving financial assistance for the same course under any other scheme is not eligible.
<br>

### Is it necessary to apply every year?

Yes. Eligible students must submit a fresh application every academic year.
<br>

### What happens if a student fails in any subject?

A student who fails in any subject during the course becomes ineligible for financial assistance.
<br>

### How is the scholarship amount disbursed?

The approved financial assistance is transferred through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account via the concerned University.
<br>

### How are beneficiaries selected when applications exceed available scholarships?

When eligible applications exceed the available scholarships, beneficiaries are selected based on the merit of the qualifying aggregate percentage, subject to the availability of funds.
<br>
