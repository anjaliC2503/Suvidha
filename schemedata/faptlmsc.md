## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote youth participation in adventure sports by providing financial assistance for training, workshops, and competitions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain &amp; Sea Adventure Sports Competitions&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote adventure sports among the youth of Gujarat by encouraging their participation in training programs, workshops, expeditions, and competitions organized within and outside the state. The scheme offers financial support to cover expenses such as fees, equipment, insurance, transportation, and other relevant costs.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** Up to ₹30,000/- or 75% of the actual expenditure (whichever is lower).
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
   1. The applicant should be up to 45 years of age at the time of application.
   1. The applicant should have participated in or completed a training program, workshop, expedition, or competition related to land, sea, or air adventure sports.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of the scheme "Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain & Sea Adventure Sports Competitions"?

The main purpose of the scheme "Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain & Sea Adventure Sports Competitions" is to promote the active participation of youth from Gujarat in adventure sports by supporting them financially for attending recognized training programs, workshops, expeditions, and competitions held within or outside Gujarat in the domains of land, sea, and air sports.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to receive the benefits of this scheme?

Any individual who is a permanent resident of Gujarat and is aged 45 years or below at the time of application is eligible to apply for financial assistance under the scheme "Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain & Sea Adventure Sports Competitions", provided they have completed an eligible event or training in adventure sports.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What types of sports or events are considered valid under this scheme?

The scheme "Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain & Sea Adventure Sports Competitions" covers adventure sports and events that are conducted in the categories of land, sea, and air, including training programs, workshops, expeditions, and competitive events recognized by relevant authorities.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum financial support provided under this scheme?

Under the scheme, a maximum financial assistance of ₹30,000 or 75% of the actual expenditure incurred by the applicant—whichever is less—is provided after successful completion of the training or event and upon submission of the required documents.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What expenses are covered under the scheme?

The scheme provides reimbursement for various types of expenses incurred during participation in adventure sports programs, including but not limited to training fees, cost of equipment, insurance charges, transportation, and other relevant expenditures.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How is the financial assistance disbursed to the applicant?

The financial assistance under the scheme is disbursed through a reimbursement process, where the applicant receives the approved amount directly in their bank account after submitting valid proofs of expenditure and completion of the training or event.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is the financial assistance given in advance or after the event?

Financial assistance under the scheme "Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain & Sea Adventure Sports Competitions" is strictly provided after the event or training has been successfully completed and not in advance.
<br>

### What documents must be submitted to apply for this scheme?

Applicants are required to submit essential documents such as Aadhar Card, Bank Passbook or a Cancelled Cheque, Birth Certificate, Certificate of the Professional Course or Training Completed, Identity Card, Passport Size Photographs, and a plain paper application addressed to the Commissioner, Youth Service & Cultural Activities, Gandhinagar.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where should the application be submitted?

The complete application, along with all supporting documents, must be submitted to the office of the Commissioner, Youth Service & Cultural Activities, located in Gandhinagar, Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any fee or charge for submitting the application under this scheme?

There is no application fee or charge for availing the benefits under the scheme "Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain & Sea Adventure Sports Competitions", and the application can be submitted free of cost.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Are there any income-based eligibility restrictions under the scheme?

There are no individual or family income limits prescribed for availing the benefits under this scheme, making it accessible to all eligible youth regardless of their economic background.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which government authority is responsible for implementing and managing this scheme?

The scheme "Financial Assistance for Preparation of Training Camp and Participation in Land, Mountain & Sea Adventure Sports Competitions" is implemented and managed by the Commissioner, Youth Service & Cultural Activities, functioning under the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>
