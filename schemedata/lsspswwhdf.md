## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension of ₹20,000/- to the widowed wife or widower husband of democracy fighters of the state who were imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Loktantra Senani Samman Pension Scheme&quot; is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension to the widowed wife or widower husband of democracy fighters of the state who were imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977.
<br>

## Benefits

**Pension Amount:** ₹20,000/- per month
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
1. The applicant must be a widow or widower of a recognized democracy fighter.
1. The applicant’s deceased spouse must have been imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- The application has to be submitted to the concerned District Magistrate. 
  - Based on the report/recommendation of the District Magistrate, the pension will be sanctioned at the Government level after obtaining the approval of the Hon’ble Departmental Minister or the Hon’ble Chief Minister.
  - The prescribed application format is not available. As proof, the applicant must submit evidence that his/her late husband or wife was receiving the Loktantra Senani Pension, or that he/she was imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency period (from 25.06.1975 to 21.03.1977).

1. Bank account details
1. Passport-size photographs of the applicant
1. Other documents as required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who are eligible beneficiaries under this scheme?

Only the widowed wife or widower husband of democracy fighters of the state who were imprisoned under MISA/DIR during the Emergency are eligible.
<br>

### What is the pension amount provided under this scheme?

The eligible beneficiary receives ₹20,000/- per month as pension.
<br>

### Is this pension given to democracy fighters directly?

No. This specific scheme is for the widowed wife or widower husband of democracy fighters who were imprisoned under MISA/DIR.
<br>

### How can one apply for the scheme?

The application has to be submitted to the concerned District Magistrate.
<br>

### What happens after submitting the application?

The District Magistrate will verify and recommend the application. Based on this, pension will be sanctioned by the Government after approval from the Hon’ble Departmental Minister or Hon’ble Chief Minister.
<br>

### Is there a prescribed application format?

No.
<br>

### Can children of democracy fighters avail this pension?

No. The scheme is limited to the widowed wife or widower husband only.
