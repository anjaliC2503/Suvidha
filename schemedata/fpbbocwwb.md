## Brief Description

The scheme "Family Pension" by the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar, provides pension to the dependent of the deceased pensioner of the Board.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Family Pension" by the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BBOCWWB), Labour Resources Department, Government of Bihar, provides pension to the dependent of the deceased pensioner of the Board.

## Benefits

50% of the pension amount or ₹100/-, whichever is higher.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a dependent of a deceased worker.
- The worker, in his/her lifetime, should have been registered with the "Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board".
- The worker, in his/her lifetime, should have been in receipt of regular pension from the Board.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### As a dependent of a deceased construction worker, what specific registration requirement must the worker have fulfilled during their lifetime for me to be eligible?

The worker must have been officially registered with the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board during their lifetime. This registration should have been active and valid at the time of their death.

### What is the exact amount of pension that would be provided to eligible dependents of deceased pensioners under this welfare scheme?

You will receive either 50% of the original pension amount that was being given to the deceased worker, or ₹100/-, whichever amount is higher.

### I want to apply for the pension scheme through the official website, but I don't know how to access my deceased family member's details online?

You need to visit the official website [https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home], click on "Scheme Application," select "Apply for Scheme," and then enter the registration number of the deceased worker. Clicking "Show" will display all relevant details.
<br>

### What steps should I follow after viewing the deceased worker's details on the welfare board's online portal to complete my pension application?

After viewing the details, select this specific pension scheme, fill in all mandatory information fields, and upload all required documents in the specified format and size. Finally, click on "Submit Application" to complete your application.

### How can I check whether my pension application has been approved or is still being processed by the welfare board?

You can track your application status anytime by visiting the official website of the Bihar Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board and entering the registration number provided during application.

### What identity documents do I need to submit along with my pension application as proof of being the deceased worker's dependent?

You need to submit your Aadhaar Card or Voter Card as identity proof, along with a Nominee/Legal Heir Certificate proving your relationship with the deceased worker.

### What documents related to the deceased worker's registration must I submit while applying for the dependent pension?

You must submit the Identity Card of the Registered Worker and Proof of Membership with the welfare board, along with the death certificate of the worker.

### As an applicant for the pension scheme, what banking information do I need to provide to receive the pension amount?

You need to provide your complete bank account details, including account number, bank name, branch details, and IFSC code, to facilitate the pension transfer.

### Where Can I Find The Link To The Scheme Guidelines?

You can access the comprehensive Scheme Guidelines, which provide intricate details and instructions, at the following web address: https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/bihar/assets/images/scheme.pdf
<br>

### Is There Any Compensation For The Delay In The Disbursal Of The Scheme Benefits?

The guidelines do not mention any provision for compensation in the event of delayed disbursement of scheme benefits.

### Where Can I Find The URL To The Website Of The Board?

The official website of the Board is: https://bocwscheme.bihar.gov.in/home.
<br>

### Is It Mandatory to Have a Bank Account for This Scheme?

Yes, possession of a valid bank account is a mandatory requirement for participation in this scheme. All scheme benefits are disbursed directly to the beneficiary's registered bank account.

### Is there an application deadline for this scheme?

The applications for this scheme are open throughout the year.

### Are there any specific provisions for persons with disabilities under this scheme?

No specific provisions for persons with disabilities are mentioned in the guidelines of this scheme.
