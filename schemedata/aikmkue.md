## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide a platform for farmers, agri-entrepreneurs, and stakeholders to access, learn about, and showcase the latest agricultural innovations, technologies, and practices.

## Detailed Description

The “Akhil Bhartiya Kisan Mela and Krishi Udyog Pradarshani” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide a platform for farmers, agri-entrepreneurs, and stakeholders to access, learn about, and showcase the latest agricultural innovations, technologies, and practices.
<br>

## Benefits

The Fair/Exhibition is organized twice a year on the university campus.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. Participants should be farmers, farmer entrepreneurs, or agriculture-related firms interested in showcasing and promoting the latest agricultural technologies.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

The exhibition is organized after agriculture and agriculture-related firms deposit the prescribed participation fee, while farmers participate by registering themselves for the event.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often is the exhibition organized?

The Akhil Bhartiya Kisan Mela and Krishi Udyog Pradarshani is organized twice a year on the university campus.
<br>

### Who can participate in the exhibition?

Farmers, farmer entrepreneurs, and agriculture-related firms are eligible to participate in the exhibition.
<br>

### Is there a participation fee?

Yes. Agriculture and agriculture-related firms must deposit the prescribed participation fee, while farmers can participate by registering themselves.
<br>

### How can farmers register for the exhibition?

Farmers can register through the official communication channels or registration counters set up by the university before the event.
<br>

### Who organizes the event?

The event is organized by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, Uttarakhand.
<br>
