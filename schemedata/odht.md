## Brief Description

The scheme aims to preserve the oral history of tiatr for future generations through documentation of veteran artistes' experiences. It provides video recording and remuneration to eligible veteran tiatr artistes.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Oral Documentation of History of Tiatr" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to preserve the rich experiences of veteran tiatr artistes who have contributed immensely to the development of Tiatr, as their experiences form a part and parcel of the history of Tiatr for future generations. The scheme provides video recording of artistes' experiences on High Definition or higher format camera with preservation on Digital Versatile Disc at Tiatr Academy of Goa's Library and a onetime remuneration as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applicant must have made at least 20 years contribution to the Konkani stage and must be a script writer, director, actor, singer, musician, backstage artiste or organizer involved in tiatrs. The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications for this scheme are accepted offline.
<br>

## Benefits

> ***Financial Benefits***


- Onetime remuneration as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa to the artiste to be documented.
- Additional amount as decided by the Tiatr Academy of Goa for artistes living outside Goa.
- Remuneration to the interviewer as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa.

> ***Non-Financial Benefits***


- Video recording of artiste's experiences on High Definition or higher format camera.
- Preservation of documentation on Digital Versatile Disc at Tiatr Academy of Goa's Library.
- One copy of the Digital Versatile Disc provided to the artiste concerned.
- Potential upload of recording on Tiatr Academy of Goa's website, other internet sites, or other media channels.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a veteran artist who has contributed to the development of Tiatr.
- The applicant must be a script writer, director, actor, singer, musician, backstage artiste involved in stage setting, light effects, background music, costumes, make-up, or those involved in organizing tiatrs.
- The applicant must have made at least 20 years of contribution to the Konkani stage.
- The applicant who is a writer or director must have scripted at least 15 tiatrs.
- The applicant who is other than writer or director must have participated in at least 20 tiatrs.

> ***Preference, Selection and Implementation***


- At least five artistes will be considered for documentation in a financial year.
- Artistes will be selected by the Managing Committee of Tiatr Academy of Goa.
- Preference will be given to old and sick artistes.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. The application form requires details including the name of the artiste, address, contact number, email, Aadhaar Card number, and aspects of tiatr on which the artiste has contributed to the tiatr stage.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Provide detailed information about your contribution to the tiatr stage on a separate sheet as indicated in the form. Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
**Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001**
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment received for future reference._
_*The Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to make any changes in the rules of the scheme._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific roles or contributions to the Konkani stage would make me eligible for this oral documentation initiative?

You must be a script writer, director, actor, singer, musician, backstage artiste involved in stage setting, light effects, background music, costumes, make-up, or those involved in organizing tiatrs.
<br>

### If I live outside Goa, will I receive any additional compensation beyond the standard remuneration offered to participants?

Yes, artistes living outside Goa receive an additional amount as decided by the Tiatr Academy of Goa, in addition to the onetime remuneration.
<br>

### What are the minimum requirements for writers and directors in terms of the number of productions they must have created?

Writers and directors must have scripted at least 15 tiatrs to meet the eligibility criteria for this documentation program.
<br>

### How many years of active participation in the Konkani stage are required to be considered for this documentation opportunity?

You must have made at least 20 years contribution to the Konkani stage to be eligible for participation in this oral documentation initiative.
<br>

### Will I receive a personal copy of my recorded documentation, and in what format will it be provided to me?

Yes, one copy of the Digital Versatile Disc containing your documented experiences will be provided to you upon completion of the recording process.
<br>

### What types of supporting materials should I prepare or bring along during the video recording session for enhanced documentation?

You should provide any available photographs, video clippings, or other supporting materials to enhance the documentation process during your recording session.
<br>

### Are there any specific categories of artistes who receive priority consideration during the selection process conducted by the Managing Committee?

Yes, preference will be given to old and sick artistes during selection by the Managing Committee of Tiatr Academy of Goa.
<br>

### For artistes other than writers and directors, what is the minimum number of productions they must have participated in?

Artistes other than writers or directors must have participated at least in 20 tiatrs to meet the eligibility requirements.
<br>

### What comprehensive topics and aspects of my life and career will be covered during the video recording interview process?

The interview covers family background, childhood, education, entry on tiatr stage, acting, singing, direction, script writing, production of tiatrs, tiatr stage experiences, production of Audio and Video albums, production of films, felicitations, awards received, contribution through All India Radio, Doordarshan or other Television channels, publication of tiatr literature, and other achievements in life.
<br>

### What essential documents must I submit along with my application form to complete the initial application process?

You must submit a self attested copy of Aadhaar Card and the completed Application Form for Oral Documentation Of History Of Tiatr Data Form.
<br>

### How many artistes are typically selected for documentation during each financial year, and is this number fixed or variable?

At least five artistes will be considered for documentation in a financial year, though this appears to be a minimum number.
<br>

### What technical specifications and quality standards are used for recording the video documentation of my experiences and contributions?

Video recording of your experiences will be conducted on High Definition or higher format camera to ensure quality preservation of your documentation.
<br>

### Where will my documented experiences be permanently stored and preserved for future generations to access and reference?

Your documentation will be preserved on Digital Versatile Disc at Tiatr Academy of Goa's Library for permanent storage and future reference.
<br>

### Is there any possibility that my recorded documentation might be made available to the public through digital platforms?

Yes, there is potential for your recording to be uploaded on Tiatr Academy of Goa's website, other internet sites, or other media channels.
<br>
