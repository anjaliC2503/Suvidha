## Brief Description

The scheme “Grant for the Purchase of Working Tools” by the BOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, Odisha, is a welfare scheme that provides one-time reimbursement to each registered beneficiary of the Board for purchasing tools.

## Detailed Description

The “Tool Purchase Grant for the Construction Workers” scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Odisha, is a welfare scheme for the registered workers of the board. Under this scheme, the Board shall make one time reimbursement to each beneficiary who is registered with the Board for purchasing tools on production of original bill/ voucher.

## Benefits

- **Quantum of Assistance:** ₹4,000/-.

## Eligibility

> **For Registration as a Building/ Construction Worker:**


  - The applicant should be a resident of Odisha.
  - The applicant should be a Building/ Construction Worker.
  - The applicant should be between 18 and 60 years.
  - The applicant should have engaged in building and construction work for at least 90 days in a given year.
  - The applicant should not be a member of any other Welfare Fund established under any law.

> **For the Application of the Welfare Scheme:**


  - The applicant should be a registered member of the Odisha Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
  - The applicant should be registered for a period of one year or more under the board.
  - The items purchased by the beneficiary must be related to his/her nature of job/ occupation or trade as mentioned in the Identity Card.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process as a Building/ Construction Worker:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official [website ](https://nirmanshramik.odisha.gov.in/obocwwb/public/cwLogin)of the Odisha BOCWW Board.
**Step 2:** Click on “One Time Registration” on the top right side of the page.
**Step 3:** Click on the Declaration box and then on the “Proceed” button.
**Step 4:** In the next window fill in all the required details carefully and click on “Proceed”.
**Step 5:** Enter the received OTP and click on “Submit”.
**Step 6:** Your account will be successfully created and a Username will be displayed (Keep the Username for future references).
> **Application Process of the Welfare Scheme:**

**Step 1:** Visit the Applicant [Login page](https://nirmanshramik.odisha.gov.in/obocwwb/public/cwLogin) of the Odisha BOCWW Board.
**Step 2:** Enter your Username, OTP and fill in the captcha then click on “Sign In”.
**Step 3:** In the next window, fill in your General Details, Address Details, Family &amp; Nominee Details, Details of the establishment(s) where the applicant worked during the last one year, Bank Details etc. and then click on “Submit”.
**Step 4:** Now in the Menu bar section, click on “Benefit”. From the list select the concerned benefit you want to apply.
**Step 5:** Fill in all the mandatory details carefully and click on “Submit” to complete the application process.
> **Check Your Application Status:** Registered workers can check their application status through visiting this [link ](https://nirmanshramik.odisha.gov.in/obocwwb/public/applicationTracking)upon entering their Application Tracking Number.

> **Pay Annual Contribution:** Registered workers can pay the Annual Contribution through visiting this [link ](https://nirmanshramik.odisha.gov.in/obocwwb/public/payAnnuealContribution)upon entering their Registration Number.

### Offline

> **Registration Process as a Building/ Construction Worker:**

**Step 1:** To register as a beneficiary under the BOCW Welfare Board, an applicant worker should download [Form-XXVII](https://bocboard.labdirodisha.gov.in/Building/homecontent/BeneficiaryForms/REGISTRATION%20FORM.pdf) and attach all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 2:** Application shall be submitted to the District Labour Officers (D.L.O)/ Asst. Labour Officers/ Rural Labour Inspectors of your area.
> **Application Process of the Welfare Scheme:**

**Step 1:** A registered worker should download [Form-XLIV](https://bocboard.labdirodisha.gov.in/Building/homecontent/BeneficiaryForms/WORKING%20TOOLS.pdf) and attach all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 2:** Application shall be submitted to the District Labour Officers (D.L.O)/ Asst. Labour Officers/ Rural Labour Inspectors of your area.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the age requirement for registration as a beneficiary under the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of Odisha?

The building worker must be at least 18 years old and not older than 60 years old.

### Is there a minimum service requirement to register as a beneficiary?

Yes, the building worker must have completed 90 days of service as a building worker in the preceding 12 months.

### Who can apply for the welfare scheme?

Any registered worker of the Board can apply for the welfare scheme.

### Who is responsible for registering a worker as a beneficiary?

The District Labour Officers (D.L.O)/ Asst. Labour Officers/ Rural Labour Inspectors are acting as Registering Officers for the registration of beneficiaries under the Board.

### Who is eligible for registration as a beneficiary of the BOCW Welfare Board?

Every building worker who is between the age of 18-60 years and who has been engaged in any building or other construction work for not less than 90 days during the preceding one year is eligible for registration as a beneficiary of the BOCW Welfare Board of Odisha.

### Can Odia migrant workmen be registered as beneficiaries under the Odisha Building & Other Construction Workers’ Welfare Board?

Yes, Odia migrant workmen engaged in building and other construction works in other states can be registered as beneficiaries under the Odisha Building & Other Construction Workers' Welfare Board.

### What is the procedure for applying for the registration?

To register as a beneficiary under the BOCW Welfare Board, an applicant worker should download Form-XXVII and attach all the mandatory documents (self attested, if required) and a fee of [?]20/- to the District Labour Officers (D.L.O)/ Asst. Labour Officers/ Rural Labour Inspectors of your area.
Alternatively, registration can also be done through the official website of the Odisha BOCWW Board: https://nirmanshramik.odisha.gov.in/obocwwb/public/cwLogin .

### Is there a specific form for the registration through offline mode as a beneficiary under the BOCW Welfare Board?

Yes, the application for registration must be made in Form-XXVII. You can obtain this form from the District Labour Officers (D.L.O)/ Asst. Labour Officers/ Rural Labour Inspectors in your area.

### How much is the fee associated with the registration process?

The registration fee is [?]20/- which is payable during the registration.

### What happens after I pay the application fee?

After you pay the application fee of  [?]20/-, your application will be reviewed by the registering officer. The decision to approve or reject your application will be based on the merits of your application.

### Who decides whether my application will be approved or rejected?

The registering officer is responsible for deciding whether your application will be approved or rejected based on its merits.

### Can my application be rejected even if I have paid the application fee?

Yes, your application can be rejected even if you have paid the application fee. The final decision is made by the registering officer based on the merits of your application.

### How much is the fee associated with the renewal of the registration?

The renewal fee is [?]50/- which is payable during the renewal process of the registration.

### What criteria must an applicant meet to be considered for this benefit?

A registered member under the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of Odisha, shall be eligible for this benefit.

### What kind of assistance is provided under this scheme?

A member is eligible for one-time reimbursement of [?]4,000/- under this benefit.

### What form needs to be used to apply for this welfare scheme?

The application must be made in the prescribed Form-XLIV. The form can be downloaded from https://bocboard.labdirodisha.gov.in/Building/homecontent/BeneficiaryForms/WORKING%20TOOLS.pdf or can be collected from the District Labour Officers (D.L.O)/ Asst. Labour Officers/ Rural Labour Inspectors of your area.

### Where do I submit the application for this welfare scheme?

Application can be submitted online on: https://nirmanshramik.odisha.gov.in/obocwwb/public/cwLogin . Alternatively , the offline application for this welfare scheme should be submitted to the District Labour Officers (D.L.O)/ Asst. Labour Officers/ Rural Labour Inspectors in your area.

### What happens if the application is rejected?

In case of rejection, the applicants would be intimated about such rejection, with due intimation to the Board.

### What are the consequences of submitting fraudulent documents for assistance?

In case of receipt of assistance on the basis of fraudulent documents, the amount released towards any assistance would be recovered from the applicant under the provisions of the OPDR Act, and his/her membership would be ceased.
