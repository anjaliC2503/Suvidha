## Brief Description

The scheme aims to compensate Goan dramatic troupes for transportation expenses when staging Tiatrs in Mumbai, Poona, and other parts of India, and vice versa. It offers financial assistance to promote Goan Tiatr, benefiting directors and writers of Goan and Mumbai/Poona troupes.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Financial Assistance to Troupes Going to Mumbai/Poona and Other Parts of the Country & Vice Versa" by the Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG), Goa, aims to compensate Goan dramatic troupes towards their transportation costs, recognizing that staging Tiatrs in Mumbai, Poona, and other parts of the country is not always financially viable. The scheme provides financial support to cover transport expenses for Goan troupes staging a minimum of five shows of the same Tiatr in Mumbai/Poona, or for Mumbai/Poona troupes staging five shows in Goa. Benefits can also be availed for only one show staged in other locations such as Kolkata, Delhi, Gujarat, Nagpur, Bangalore, or Udaipur. Applications for this scheme are accepted one month in advance of the performance date, on a 'First Come First Serve' basis, and are subject to the availability of funds.
<br>

## Benefits

- The scheme provides financial support to compensate for transportation expenses incurred while staging Tiatrs, thereby promoting Goan Tiatr.
- The extent of the financial support shall be fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa.

<br>

> ***Conditions***

_*The Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) may appoint a representative to witness the performance, and payments will be released based on their report._
_*A compulsory announcement stating ‘This Tiatr is organized in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG)’ must be made both before the commencement of the Tiatr and again during the interval._
_*No adverse remarks must be made, either in written or verbal form, against any Governments, Individuals, Institutions, or on the basis of caste, creed, or religion within the scripts, song lyrics, performances, or speeches. If such remarks are proven, the Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) reserves the right to disqualify the performance and either withhold payment or recall any payment already made to the applicant._
_*For performances at Mandd Sobhann, Mangalore, the payment will only be made after a certificate of performance is issued by the Mandd Sobhann._
<br>

## Eligibility

1.  The applicant must be the writer or director of the Tiatr.
2.  The applicant must stage the Tiatr as per its traditional format.
3.  The applicant's Tiatr must have 6 or 7 acts (podd’dde).
4.  The applicant's Tiatr must include the following - 

- At least 4 ‘cantos’ and comedy linked to the story of the play.
- At least 10 ‘Kantaram’.

5.  The applicant's 'Kantaram' must include the following - 

- At Least 2 Duets.
- At Least 1 Duo.
- At Least 1 Trio.
- At Least 1 Quartet/quintet.
- At Least 1 Choral Song and Solos.

6.  The applicant must not have any adverse remark made in any form (written or verbal) against any Governments, Individuals, Institutions, or anything against caste, creed, and religion in the scripts, lyrics of songs, performances, speeches, etc., which may hurt the sentiments of anybody.
7.  All advertisements, press notes, banners, tickets, and handbills must explicitly state that ‘This Tiatr is organized in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG)’.
8.  The applicant's troupe must stage a minimum of 5 shows of the same Tiatr in Mumbai/Poona (if a Goan troupe) or 5 shows in Goa (if a Mumbai/Poona troupe) to qualify for transport support.
9.  The applicant's troupe can avail benefit even if only one show is staged in other places like Kolkata, Delhi, Gujarat, Nagpur, Bangalore, Udaipur, etc.
10.  The applicant can avail the scheme only twice in a financial year for two different Tiatrs.
<br>

> ***Earmarking***

1.  For performances at Mandd Sobhann at Mangalore, financial assistance is extended to two troupes from Goa.
2.  Of these two Tiatrs for Mandd Sobhann, one should be from Commercial Troupes and one from a prize-winning Troupe of Goa Kala Academy ‘A’ Tiatr Competition.
<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form. The form may also be obtained directly from the office of the Tiatr Academy of Goa, requiring a physical visit to the office for collection.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the copies of the mandatory documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
**Deadline:** The submission must be made at least one month in advance of the scheduled performance date.
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
*The application will be considered on a 'First Come First Serve' basis and is subject to the availability of funds.
<br>

> ***Post-Application Processes***

**Step 1:** _Await Performance Witnessing and Verification_
Upon application, the Tiatr Academy of Goa may choose to appoint and depute its own representative to witness the Tiatr performance. This representative will observe the show in Mumbai, Poona, or any other specified location and subsequently submit a report to the Tiatr Academy of Goa for review.
<br>

**Step 2:** _Release of Financial Assistance_
Based on the report provided by the Tiatr Academy of Goa's representative, the financial assistance payments will be released to the concerned applicant. For Tiatrs performed at Mandd Sobhann in Mangalore, the payment will be disbursed only after a formal certificate of performance has been issued by Mandd Sobhann, Mangalore.
<br>

## Documents Required

***At the Application Stage***
• Typed Copy of Script And Lyrics
• Hall Booking Proofs (Required one month in advance with the application)
• Self-Attested Copy of Aadhaar Card
• Self-Attested Copy of Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card
• Letter of Authority (Required if hall/auditorium is booked in the name of a person other than the applicant)
• Letter from Organizers (Required as proof instead of hall booking receipts if the Tiatr is performed in Parishes)
***At the Benefit Disbursal Stage***
• Receipts of Hall/Auditorium Booking (To be submitted to Tiatr Academy of Goa for making payments)
• Certificate of Performance (Issued by Mandd Sobhann, Mangalore, required for payment for performances there)
• 5 Photographs of the Performance on Stage (To be sent to the Tiatr Academy of Goa for records)
 
_*If the halls/auditoriums are booked in the name of any person other than the applicant, the applicant must provide a letter of authority to that person stating authorization for booking._
_*In case the Tiatr is performed in Parishes, a letter from the organizers to this effect should be produced as proof instead of hall booking receipts._
_*The applicant must send 5 photographs of the performance on stage to the Tiatr Academy of Goa (TAG) for records._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific musical elements must be included in my performance to meet the traditional format requirements?

Your performance must include at least 4 'cantos' with comedy linked to the story, and at least 10 'Kantaram'. The 'Kantaram' must feature at least 2 Duets, 1 Duo, 1 Trio, 1 Quartet/quintet, 1 Choral Song and Solos.
<br>

### How many times can I apply for financial assistance within a single financial year period?

You can avail the scheme only twice in a financial year, but it must be for two different Tiatrs. This limitation ensures fair distribution of benefits among various applicants.
<br>

### What documentation do I need to provide at the time of submitting my initial application?

You must submit a typed copy of script and lyrics, hall booking proofs, self-attested copies of Aadhaar Card and Permanent Account Number Card. If the hall is booked under someone else's name, provide a Letter of Authority, or a letter from organizers for parish performances.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions on the content or messaging that can be included in my performance?

You must not include any adverse remarks in written or verbal form against any Governments, Individuals, Institutions, or content based on caste, creed, or religion in scripts, song lyrics, performances, or speeches that may hurt anyone's sentiments.
<br>

### What is the minimum number of shows required if I am performing outside Mumbai or Poona?

For performances in other locations such as Kolkata, Delhi, Gujarat, Nagpur, Bangalore, or Udaipur, you can avail benefits even if only one show is staged, unlike the 5-show requirement for Mumbai/Poona.
<br>

### Who determines the actual amount of financial support that will be provided under this assistance program?

The extent of financial support is fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. The scheme provides compensation for transportation expenses, but the specific amount is determined by the Academy based on their assessment.
<br>

### What structural requirements must my performance meet in terms of acts and traditional format compliance?

Your performance must be staged as per traditional format and must have 6 or 7 acts (podd'dde). This ensures adherence to authentic presentation styles while maintaining cultural integrity.
<br>

### What promotional acknowledgments am I required to include in all my marketing and performance materials?

All advertisements, press notes, banners, tickets, and handbills must explicitly state that 'This performance is organized in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa'. This acknowledgment is mandatory for all promotional materials.
<br>

### What documents must I submit after completing my performance to receive the financial disbursement?

You must submit receipts of hall/auditorium booking, 5 photographs of the performance on stage for records, and for Mandd Sobhann performances specifically, a certificate of performance issued by Mandd Sobhann, Mangalore.
<br>

### What specific verbal announcements am I required to make during my actual performance presentation?

You must make a compulsory announcement stating 'This performance is organized in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa' both before the commencement and again during the interval of your performance.
<br>

### How many shows must Mumbai or Poona troupes perform in Goa to qualify for transportation support?

Mumbai/Poona troupes must stage a minimum of 5 shows of the same performance in Goa to qualify for transport support, which is the reciprocal arrangement for Goan troupes performing outside.
<br>

### What are the eligibility requirements regarding my role and involvement in the performance I am applying for?

You must be either the writer or director of the performance. This ensures that financial assistance reaches the creative professionals directly responsible for the artistic content and direction.
<br>

### When is the deadline for submitting applications relative to my scheduled performance dates?

Applications are accepted one month in advance of the performance date on a 'First Come First Serve' basis, subject to availability of funds. Early submission increases your chances of approval.
<br>

### How many Goan troupes can receive financial assistance annually for performing at Mandd Sobhann in Mangalore?

Financial assistance is extended to two Goan troupes annually for performing at Mandd Sobhann in Mangalore. One troupe must be commercial and the other must be a prize-winner from the Goa Kala Academy 'A' Competition.
<br>
