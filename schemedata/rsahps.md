## Brief Description

The scheme promotes fitness among rural youth by providing financial assistance to the mangal dals for setting up open gyms in each Gram Panchayat. Financial assistance of ₹17,960/- is allocated per gram panchayat for installing open gym/community fitness equipment to encourage fitness & well-being.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Rural Sports and Health Promotion Scheme&quot; was launched by the Department of Youth Welfare and prantiy rakshak dal (PRD), Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote physical fitness and well-being among rural youth by establishing open gyms in village communities.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, an amount of ₹17,960/- is allocated to Mangal Dals for setting up community fitness equipment (open gym) in each Gram Panchayat to promote the health and fitness of the youth.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. All Gram Panchayats in Uttarakhand are eligible.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- At the Gram Panchayat level, a site is selected for the construction of an open gym. 
  - The proposal is approved by the Gram Sabha and then submitted to the regional Youth Welfare and PRD officer. 
  - Once verified and approved, the allocated amount is disbursed to the Mangal Dal associated with the Gram Panchayat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much financial support is provided?

The amount is ₹17,960/- allocated to the per Gram Panchayat.

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Every Gram Panchayat in Uttarakhand is eligible.

### Is the grant recurring?

No, it is a one-time grant.

### Who receives the fund?

The responsible Mangal Dal is associated with the Gram Panchayat.

### What is the process for applying?

The proposal is approved by the Gram Sabha and then submitted to the regional Youth Welfare and PRD officer.
<br>

### Who verifies the proposals?

The Regional Youth Welfare and Provincial Rakshak Dal Officer.
