## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide free irrigation facilities to farmers in Scheduled Tribe-dominated areas through the construction of artesian wells, thereby enhancing agricultural productivity. It is a 100% state government-funded scheme.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Construction of Artesian Wells in Scheduled Tribe Areas” was launched by the Department of Minor Irrigation, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide free irrigation facilities to farmers in Scheduled Tribe-dominated areas through the construction of artesian wells, thereby enhancing agricultural productivity. It is a 100% state government-funded scheme.
<br>

## Benefits

- Under the scheme, free irrigation facilities will be made available to farmers. 
  - Artesian wells are established in areas dominated by Scheduled Tribes.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiary must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
   1. The beneficiary must be a farmer engaged in agricultural activities.
   1. Collective irrigation projects will be constructed under the scheme.
   1. The name of the proposed village must be included in the list of Scheduled Tribe-dominated villages issued by the Department of Social Welfare.
   1. The project site must be suitable for the construction of artesian wells.
   1. A sufficient irrigated area must be available for the project.
   1. The project cost must not exceed ₹4.00 lakh per hectare, and the benefit-cost ratio must be greater than 1.0.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Generally, only certain selected areas of Udham Singh Nagar district and some areas of Nainital district are suitable for the construction of artesian wells. The proposal may be submitted to the Division, Sub-Division, or other departmental offices through the Gram Sabha, Gram Panchayat, District Panchayat, or other authorized channels. Based on site inspection and the suitability of the received proposals, a Detailed Project Report (DPR) will be prepared.
  - After examination by the departmental TAC, the DPR is forwarded to the headquarters. After which the proposal is sent to the government for approval. The construction work of the projects is initiated after receiving financial approval from the Government.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can benefit from this scheme?

Farmers residing in Scheduled Tribe-dominated areas of Uttarakhand and actively engaged in agricultural activities are eligible for the scheme.
<br>

### In which areas is the scheme implemented?

The scheme is generally implemented in selected areas of Udham Singh Nagar and some areas of Nainital districts where artesian wells are suitable.
<br>

### What types of projects are constructed under the scheme?

Collective irrigation projects are constructed under this scheme.
<br>

### How can a village become eligible for this scheme?

The proposed village must be included in the list of Scheduled Tribe-dominated villages issued by the Department of Social Welfare.
<br>

### What is the maximum cost allowed per hectare for the project?

The project cost should not exceed ₹4.00 lakh per hectare.
<br>

### How can proposals be submitted?

Proposals can be submitted through Gram Sabha, Gram Panchayat, Zila Parishad, or other departmental channels to the division, sub-division, or other offices.
<br>

### How is financial approval obtained?

After DPR scrutiny, the proposal is sent to the Government of Uttarakhand for financial approval.
<br>

### Is there any cost to farmers for irrigation under this scheme?

No, free irrigation facilities are provided to farmers through the constructed artesian wells.
