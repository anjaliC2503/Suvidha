## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support meritorious students pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics Engineering who are in their 4th year of study by providing a one-year scholarship amounting to ₹7,000/-.

## Detailed Description

The “Amit Gautam Memorial Scholarship (B.Tech in Electronics Engineering - 4th Year)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to support meritorious students pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics Engineering who are in their 4th year of study by providing a one-year scholarship.
<br>

## Benefits

The eligible student will receive ₹7,000/- per year as a scholarship.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in the 4th year of the B.Tech (Electronics Engineering) course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The scholarship is awarded for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

First, applications are invited from students in the prescribed format. Thereafter, a committee constituted by the Vice-Chancellor evaluates the received applications as per the prescribed procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice-Chancellor grants approval, following which the selected students receive the scholarship amount.
<br>
