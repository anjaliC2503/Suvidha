## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote Konkani songs by presenting them with choreography and dancing to attract youth towards Konkani cultural performances. It provides cash prizes, mementos, certificates and traveling allowances to participating groups in organized competitions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Promotion of Konkani Songs with Choreography & Dancing" was launched by the Tiatr Academy of Goa, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to promote Konkani songs by presenting them with choreography and dancing, attracting youth towards Konkani 'kantar' and improving the presentation quality of Konkani cultural performances. The scheme provides cash prizes ranging from ₹2,000/- to ₹7,000/- for winners along with mementos and certificates, and traveling allowances between ₹1,000/- to ₹2,000/- based on distance traveled by participating groups. The applicant must form a group of 6 to 10 participants and submit a recording of one selected Konkani song at the time of application. The scheme is implemented by Tiatr Academy of Goa and applications for this scheme are accepted offline.
<br>

## Benefits

##### ***Cash Prizes for Performance***

- First Prize: ₹7,000/- plus memento and certificate.
- Second Prize: ₹5,000/- plus memento and certificate.
- Third Prize: ₹3,000/- plus memento and certificate.
- Special Prize for Best Choreographer: ₹2,000/- plus memento and certificate.

##### ***Traveling Allowance***

- Groups traveling up to 10 kilometers radius: ₹1,000/-.
- Groups traveling within 10-20 kilometers radius: ₹1,500/-.
- Groups traveling 20 kilometers or more: ₹2,000/-.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must form a group consisting of 6 to 10 participants.
- The applicant must submit a recording of one selected Konkani song at the time of application.
- The applicant must not perform fusion of songs as it will result in automatic disqualification.
- The applicant must abide by all rules and regulations of the competition organized by Tiatr Academy of Goa.
- The applicant must accept that the decision of the judges will be final and binding.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
_*The application must be submitted to the office of the Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days in advance of the proposed programme date._
_*Applications are considered on a "First come First Serve" basis, subject to the availability of funds._
> ***Post-Application Process***

**Step 1:** Competition Details
Wait for Tiatr Academy of Goa will communicate the venue, date, and other competition details to participating groups.
**Step 2:** Participate in Competition
Attend the competition at the designated venue and perform the registered Konkani song with choreography and dancing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum and maximum number of participants required to form a group for this cultural competition?

Each participating group must consist of 6 to 10 participants. Groups with fewer than 6 or more than 10 members will not be eligible to participate in the competition.
<br>

### How much traveling allowance will be provided to groups participating in the competition based on their location?

The traveling allowance varies based on distance: ₹1,000/- for groups within 10 kilometers radius, ₹1,500/- for groups between 10-20 kilometers radius, and ₹2,000/- for groups traveling 20 kilometers or more.
<br>

### What are the cash prize amounts awarded to the first three winners in the performance category?

The first prize winner receives ₹7,000/- plus memento and certificate, second prize winner gets ₹5,000/- plus memento and certificate, and third prize winner receives ₹3,000/- plus memento and certificate.
<br>

### Can a participating group perform a fusion of multiple songs during the competition?

No, performing fusion of songs will automatically disqualify the group from the competition. Only one selected Konkani song should be performed with choreography and dancing.
<br>

### What format should be used to submit the recorded song along with the application?

The recorded Konkani song must be submitted on Compact Disc or Digital Versatile Disc format at the time of application submission.
<br>

### Is there a special recognition or prize available specifically for choreographers in this competition?

Yes, there is a separate prize for the best choreographer who will be awarded ₹2,000/- cash prize along with a memento and certificate.
<br>

### Who will be responsible for judging the performances and what expertise will they possess?

A panel of judges well versed in song, music, and choreography will evaluate the performances. Their decision will be final and binding for all participants.
<br>

### What type of song content is acceptable for participation in this cultural promotion competition?

The song must be a recorded Konkani song (Kantar). Only Konkani language songs are acceptable, and participants must submit the title of their selected song in the application form.
<br>

### Where will the competition venue be located and who decides the location?

The competition will be organized at a place identified and decided by Tiatr Academy of Goa. The specific venue details will be communicated to participants after registration.
<br>

### What happens if participants want to make changes to their group members after submitting the application?

The source material does not specify procedures for changes after submission. However, correct names must be provided initially as they will appear on certificates, suggesting changes may not be easily accommodated.
<br>

### What authority reserves the right to modify rules and regulations of this competition?

Tiatr Academy of Goa reserves the right to make any changes in the rules and regulations of the competition at their discretion.
<br>

### What documentation is required to prove the identity and participation of group members?

While specific identity documents are not mentioned, participants must provide correct names of all group members and the choreographer as these will appear on the certificates.
<br>

### How should applicants provide their contact information and what details are mandatory?

Applicants must provide complete address, contact numbers, and email address in the application form. Multiple contact numbers can be provided for better communication.
<br>

### What binding commitments must participants agree to before joining this cultural promotion competition?

Participants must read, understand, and agree to abide by all rules and regulations of the competition organized by Tiatr Academy of Goa, and accept that judges' decisions are final and binding.
<br>
