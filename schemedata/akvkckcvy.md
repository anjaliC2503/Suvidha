## Brief Description

Under this scheme, free bicycles will be distributed to 5,800 girl students belonging to the Economically Weaker Section (EWS), who are studying in government schools from Class 6 to Class 8, to facilitate their commute between home and school.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Bicycle Distribution Scheme&quot; was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan, for girl students belonging to the Economically Weaker Section (EWS) of Rajasthan. Under this scheme, free bicycles will be distributed to 5,800 girl students from the EWS category, who are studying in government schools from Class 6 to Class 8, to facilitate their commute between home and school.

## Benefits

Under this scheme, free bicycles will be distributed to girl students from the EWS category.
***Number of beneficiaries:***

1. Total: 5,800 beneficiaries
1. Number of Beneficiaries Class-wise:


- Class 6th: 1,933- Class 7th: 1,933- Class 8th: 1,934

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a native of Rajasthan.
1. The applicant should be a girl student.
1. The applicant should be studying in Class 6, 7, or 8 in a government school in Rajasthan.
1. The applicant should belong to the Economically Weaker Section (EWS) category.
1. The annual income of the applicant’s parents/guardian should be less than ₹1,00,000/-.
1. The applicant should have secured at least 60% marks in the previous examination.

**Note:** Preference will be given based on merit (marks obtained).

## Application Process

### Online

**Application Process:**
**Step 1:** To avail of the benefits of the scheme, the applicant needs to contact their respective school.
**Step 2:** Submit copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required) to the school.
**Step 3:** The school will visit the [Shala Darpan](https://rajshaladarpan.rajasthan.gov.in/SD2/BSP/Home/HomePage.aspx), Rajasthan Portal.
**Step 4:** Using the school&amp;#39;s login credentials (Login ID and Password), the school will help the student to fill out and submit the application form. 
**Post-Application Process:**
**Step 1:** After verification, the Head of the Institution will forward the application to the concerned District Education Officer (Headquarters), Primary/Secondary Education.
**Step 2:** The District Education Officer (Headquarters), Primary/Secondary Education, will compile and forward all the applications from the district to the Directorate of Secondary Education, Rajasthan, Bikaner, for further action.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Girl students of Classes 6 to 8 studying in government schools and belonging to EWS families.

### How many students benefit from this scheme?

A total of 5,800 girl students benefit—1,933 each from Classes 6 and 7, and 1,934 from Class 8.

### What benefit do students get under this scheme?

Eligible students receive a free bicycle.

### How often is the bicycle given?

It is a one-time benefit for the academic year.

### When was the scheme launched?

It was launched as part of the 2022-23 Budget Announcement (Announcement No. 55).

### Can private school students apply?

No, only students of government schools are eligible.

### How is selection done if applications exceed the quota?

Selection is based on merit and marks obtained.

### Is there any fee to apply?

No, the application and distribution are completely free.

### What is the mode of application?

The application is processed online by the school principal.

### What documents are needed?

Aadhaar/Jan Aadhaar, EWS Certificate, Income Certificate, Marksheet, and Affidavit.
