## Brief Description

The scheme provides a financial incentive to girls who achieve a first division in their matriculation examination.

## Detailed Description

&quot;Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan Yojana&quot;, launched by the Education Department, Bihar, aims to promote education among girls and encourage academic excellence. The scheme rewards girls who secure a first division in their matriculation examination with a one-time financial incentive of ₹10,000/-.

## Benefits

- **Financial Incentive:** ₹10,000/-.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of the respective state.
  - The applicant must be a girl student.
  - She must have secured a first division in her matriculation examination.

### Offline

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the Registration [page](https://medhasoft.bih.nic.in/medhamat2022/(S(isowffkahr0cb41idfmeymls))/Default.aspx).
**Step 2:** Scroll down below and click on "Apply For Matric 2022 Scholarship Only".
**Step 3:** Read the instructions and click on the check-boxes. Click on "Continue".
**Step 4:** Enter all the mandatory details and create a new password. Validate the Captcha code.
**Step 5:** Click on “Submit” to generate the login ID and Password.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is "Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan Yojana"?

"Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan Yojana" is a government scheme that provides a financial incentive of [?]10,000 to girl students who secure a first division in their matriculation examination.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Only girl students who have secured a first division in their matriculation examination and are residents of the respective state are eligible for this scheme.

### Is there any income limit to avail of this scheme?

No, there is no income limit for eligibility under this scheme.

### How is the incentive amount disbursed?

The incentive amount is transferred directly to the applicant's registered bank account.

### Is the scheme applicable to boys?

No, the scheme is exclusively for girl students.

### Can students who scored a second division apply for the scheme?

No, only students who have secured a first division in their matriculation examination are eligible.

### Can a student from another state apply for this scheme?

No, the scheme is only available to students who are residents of the state where it is implemented.

### Where can applicants find more information about the scheme?

Applicants can find more information by visiting the official website of the education department.
