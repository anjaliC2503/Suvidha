## Brief Description

Under this scheme to support farmer groups/FPOs in acquiring organic certification for the farm which is a prerequisite for marketing of organic spices.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Organic Certification Groups” under Spices Board India, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, is implemented as per the Modalities and Scheme Guidelines approved by the Competent Authority under the “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom” during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
The objective of this scheme is to support farmer groups and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) in obtaining organic certification for farms, which is a prerequisite for the marketing and export of organic spices.
<br>

## Benefits

- 50% subject to maximum of ₹ 1,50,000/- for ONER groups and 90% subject to a maximum of ₹ 2,70,000/- for NER groups.
- Preference on eligibility/selection First:
- Group which produces organic spices Second:
- Above 50% of the farmers in the approved list of farmers producing organic spices adopting any cropping system.
- A group/FPO is eligible for assistance thrice in a block year of 5 years from 2021 to 2026 consecutively or in staggered manner as per availability of funds in the component.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The scheme is applicable to all spice-growing States, regions, and Union Territories (UTs) in India.
1. Spices Board India reserves the right to conduct inspections or checks at any stage of the chain of custody, either before or after providing assistance under the scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Organic Certification Groups Scheme?

The scheme aims to support farmer groups and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) in obtaining organic certification for farms cultivating spices, which is essential for marketing and export of organic spices.
<br>

### Who can apply under this scheme?

Farmer groups and FPOs engaged in spice cultivation and seeking organic certification can apply under the scheme.
<br>

### Which areas are eligible under the scheme?

The scheme is applicable to all spice-growing States, regions, and Union Territories (UTs) in India.
<br>

### What expenses are covered under the scheme?

The scheme supports expenses related to organic certification, including certification body charges and related documentation.
<br>

### Is organic certification mandatory for availing assistance?

Yes. The assistance is specifically meant for obtaining organic certification for spice farms.
<br>

### Which groups are given preference under the scheme?

Preference is given in the following order:
Groups producing organic spices
Groups where more than 50% of farmers in the approved list cultivate organic spices under any cropping system
<br>

### How many times can a group or FPO avail assistance?

A group or FPO can avail assistance three times during the block period from 2021 to 2026, either consecutively or in a staggered manner, subject to availability of funds.
<br>

### Is the assistance available every year?

Assistance is subject to fund availability under the scheme component.
<br>

### Can the Spices Board verify the certification process?

Yes. The Board reserves the right to conduct checks or inspections at any stage of the chain of custody before or after providing assistance.
<br>

### Is certification through any agency acceptable?

No. Certification must be obtained through a recognized Certification Body (CB).
<br>

### Under which broader scheme is this component implemented?

The scheme is implemented under the “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom” during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>
