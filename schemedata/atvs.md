## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote high-value tourism in Meghalaya by encouraging the development of agro-tourism villas, empowering local youth through entrepreneurship, improving accommodation facilities, and preserving the state’s cultural and natural heritage.

## Detailed Description

The Agro Tourism Villa Scheme is a component of the Chief Minister's Elevate Program launched under the Tourism Department of the Government of Meghalaya. The program aims to employ, engage, and empower the youth of Meghalaya by promoting entrepreneurship and providing easy access to credit through the onboarding of various banks as financial partners. To meet the rising demand and promote high-value tourism, the Meghalaya government is investing in luxury tourist facilities and infrastructure. However, there is a need to further enhance accommodation facilities to meet the expectations of high-value tourists while preserving the state's heritage.
<br>

## Benefits

1. 35% of total project cost supported (5% by entrepreneur, 25% as front-ended subsidy, 10% as EMI copayment by Government of Meghalayasemi_colon max project cost ₹1.5 Cr, max subsidy ₹52.5 lakh).
1. Loan up to 95% of project cost with a 15-year repayment term and 18-month moratorium Government-funded villa design consultancy blending ethnic aesthetics with modern comfort, tailored to location.
1. Priority allocation in scenic rural/agricultural/tourist clusters to boost visibility and occupancy.
1. Training in hospitality and bookkeeping with reputed institutes/hotels in Meghalaya.
1. Regular, monitoring, and need-based training during the initial years, with a dedicated app/dashboard for progress tracking.

<br>

## Exclusions

**Individuals**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for:**
A. Pressure groups and organizations primarily political in nature
B. More than one member from a single family
C. Following individuals and their immediate family members (spouse, parents, children up to age 30):
i. State and Central Government employees
ii. Employees of public sector undertakings, their subsidiaries, or divisions
iii. Politically affiliated individuals (MLAs, MLCs, MPs, etc.) or those seeking public office
iv. Registered class-1 contractors
v. Consultants currently engaged with State or Central Government
**Registered Entities**
**Applications shall not be ordinarily considered for:**
A. Pressure groups and organizations primarily political in nature
B. Entities led by or involving:
i. State and Central Government employees
ii. Employees of public sector undertakings, subsidiaries, or divisions
iii. Politically affiliated individuals (MLAs, MLCs, MPs, etc.)
iv. Registered class-1 contractors
v. Consultants currently engaged with State or Central Government
<br>

## Eligibility

**Individuals should:**

1. The applicant should be a resident of Meghalaya.
1. The applicant must be between 18 and 58 years of age.
1. The applicant should not be a defaulter at any financial institution.
1. The applicant should apply only for new projects sanctioned under the scheme.
1. The applicant should own the land for the project and possess valid land documents, or the applicant should have a notarized lease agreement for a minimum period of 15 years.

**Registered Entities should:**

1. The entity should be a registered society, cooperative, or similar organization involved in tourism-related activities.
1. The entity should be legally registered with the Government of Meghalaya.
1. The entity should provide an Incorporation Certificate or Registration Certificate, whichever is applicable.
1. The entity should not be a defaulter at any financial institution.
1. The entity should hold land ownership for the project site or have a valid lease certified by competent authorities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Agro Tourism Villa Scheme?

The Agro Tourism Villa Scheme is part of the Chief Minister’s Elevate Program of the Government of Meghalaya. It promotes high-value tourism by supporting the development of agro-tourism villas and encouraging entrepreneurship.
<br>

### Who can apply for the scheme?

Residents of Meghalaya aged between 18 and 58 years can apply. Registered tourism-related entities such as societies and cooperatives are also eligible.
<br>

### What is the financial support provided under the scheme?

The scheme supports up to 35% of the total project cost. This includes 5% by the entrepreneur, 25% as a subsidy, and 10% as EMI co-payment by the Government of Meghalaya.
<br>

### What is the maximum project cost allowed?

The maximum project cost allowed under the scheme is ₹1.5 crore. The maximum subsidy provided is ₹52.5 lakh.
<br>

### Is a bank loan available under the scheme?

Yes, applicants can avail a loan of up to 95% of the total project cost. The loan has a repayment period of up to 15 years.
<br>

### Is there a moratorium period for loan repayment?

Yes, the scheme provides a moratorium period of up to 18 months. This allows entrepreneurs time to establish and stabilize their business before repayment begins.
<br>

### Can existing tourism businesses apply for the scheme?

No, assistance under the scheme is provided only for new projects. Existing businesses are not eligible for financial assistance under this scheme.
<br>

### Are registered organizations eligible to apply?

Yes, registered entities such as societies, cooperatives, or tourism organizations can apply. They must be legally registered with the Government of Meghalaya.
<br>

### Can applicants with existing loan defaults apply?

No, applicants must not be defaulters at any financial institution. Financial credibility is a mandatory eligibility requirement.
<br>

### Does the government provide design support for villas?

Yes, the government offers professional villa design consultancy. The designs combine ethnic aesthetics with modern comfort suited to the location.
<br>

### Is there any digital monitoring system for the scheme?

Yes, a dedicated app or dashboard will track project progress. It will help monitor performance and provide timely support.
<br>
