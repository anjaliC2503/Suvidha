## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide hostel facilities for women with higher education or professional training, as well as working women, to stay in urban areas by paying a nominal fee.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Sakhi Niwas - Working Women&#39;s Hostel&quot; is implemented by the Department of Women Empowerment &amp; Child Development, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide hostel facilities for women with higher education or professional training, as well as working women, to stay in urban areas.
<br>

## Benefits

- Under this scheme, hostels have been arranged for women to stay in urban areas by paying a nominal fee.
- Facilities include: Accommodation, Mess, Library, Gym, TV Hall, Creche, and more.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
- The applicant must be a woman pursuing higher education or professional training, or a working woman.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- At present, hostel facilities are operational in the districts of Dehradun, Haridwar, and Udham Singh Nagar. 
  - Applicants need to visit the hostel, submit a form along with their Aadhaar card and family details, and pay the required fees to stay in the hostel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for the hostel?

Women who are pursuing higher education, professional training, or working women are eligible to apply.
<br>

### In which districts are the hostels currently operational?

Hostels are currently operational in Dehradun, Haridwar, and Udham Singh Nagar districts.
<br>

### What facilities are provided in the hostel?

Facilities include accommodation, mess, library, gym, TV hall, creche, and other essential amenities.
<br>

### How can one apply for admission to the hostel?

Applicants need to visit the hostel in person and submit the application form along with the required documents.
<br>

### What documents are required for admission?

Applicants must submit their Aadhaar card and details of family members along with the application form.
<br>

### Is there a fee for staying in the hostel?

Yes, women can stay in the hostel by paying a nominal fee.
<br>

### Is the hostel only for women from Uttarakhand?

Yes, the applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
<br>

### Are meals provided in the hostel?

Yes, mess facilities are available for residents.
<br>
