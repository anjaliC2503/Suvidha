## Brief Description

The "Special Health Scheme for Primitive Tribes" scheme by the Welfare Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to improve healthcare services for primitive tribal communities. Financial assistance of ₹38,41,632 is provided for targeted healthcare benefits.

## Detailed Description

The "Special Health Scheme for Primitive Tribes" scheme by the Welfare Department, Government of Jharkhand, aims to improve healthcare access and outcomes for primitive tribal communities. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹38,41,632 is allocated for health-related services and programs to ensure better medical support and infrastructure for the targeted groups.

## Benefits

- Focused healthcare services for primitive tribal communities.
- Distribution of funds to districts such as Dumka, Sahebganj, Pakur, and Godda.
- Utilization for better medical facilities, treatments, and preventive care programs.
- Priority to regions with high tribal populations and pressing health needs.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to a primitive tribal community.
1. The applicant should reside in districts covered under the scheme, including Dumka, Sahebganj, Pakur, and Godda.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 01:** Visit the district welfare office in your region and obtain the application form for the Special Health Scheme for Primitive Tribes.
**Step 02:** Fill in the required details, including personal, income, and community information.
**Step 03:** Attach the necessary documents.
**Step 04:** Submit the completed form to the welfare officer and keep the acknowledgment receipt for future reference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Special Health Scheme for Primitive Tribes?

The scheme aims to improve healthcare facilities and provide financial support for health-related services to primitive tribal communities in Jharkhand.

### Who can apply for this scheme?

Applicants from primitive tribal communities residing in Dumka, Sahebganj, Pakur, and Godda districts are eligible.

### What benefits are provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides financial support for better healthcare facilities, treatments, and preventive health programs, totaling [?]38,41,632.

### How are the benefits disbursed?

Benefits are disbursed through the district welfare offices via direct transfer to approved applicants.

### What documents are needed to apply for the scheme?

Documents include proof of identity, residence, community certificate, medical reports, bank account details, and photographs.

### What is the process for application?

Applicants must visit the district welfare office, fill out the application form, attach necessary documents, and submit it for approval.

### What happens after the application is submitted?

The welfare office verifies the application and documents. Upon approval, benefits are provided to the applicant.

### What is the role of district welfare officers in this scheme?

They manage applications, verify eligibility, disburse funds, and ensure implementation as per guidelines.

### Can I apply online for the scheme?

No, the application process is offline through the district welfare offices.

### Where can I get more information about this scheme?

Visit the district welfare office or contact the Tribal Welfare Commissioner's office for detailed information
