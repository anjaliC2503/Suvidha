## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage spice growers to adopt proper polishing methods for spices such as small cardamom, large cardamom, and turmeric to improve their appearance, quality, and marketability.

## Detailed Description

The Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom: Post-Harvest Improvement – Polisher for Small Cardamom, Large Cardamom, Turmeric, etc. (Nos.) is implemented by the Spices Board, India, under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. The scheme aims to encourage spice growers to adopt proper polishing methods for spices such as small cardamom, large cardamom, and turmeric to improve their appearance, quality, and marketability. By promoting the use of modern polishing equipment, the scheme helps produce high-quality spices that meet export standards and enhances the competitiveness of Indian spices in international markets. The scheme is being implemented in accordance with the modalities and guidelines approved by the Competent Authority under the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

## Benefits

1. 50 % of cost of unit, subject to a maximum of ₹ 94,000/- per unit for General Category and 75% subject to maximum of ₹ 1,40,000 per unit for NE/SC/ST & 90% Spice Producer's Groups in Identified Clusters subjected to maximum of ₹ 1,70,000/-
1. Individual farmers having minimum of 0.40 ha - 8 ha of spices.
1. Assistance for groups/FPOs will be as in QGBG

<br>

## Eligibility

1.All spice growing States and regions in India/UT
2.Designated Officials will conduct final inspection and recommend the eligible cases.
> The facility can be used for polishing other suitable spices also and for availing assistance necessary approval may be taken from Director(Dev.) Spices Board with recommendation of Regional Officer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Polisher for Spices scheme?

The scheme aims to encourage spice growers to adopt proper polishing methods for spices such as small cardamom, large cardamom, turmeric, and other suitable spices. The use of polishing equipment helps improve the quality, appearance, and marketability of spices, making them more suitable for export.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Individual farmers cultivating spices on land holdings ranging from 0.40 hectare to 8 hectares are eligible to apply for assistance under the scheme.
<br>

### Can Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and farmer groups avail assistance?

Yes. Farmer groups and FPOs engaged in spice cultivation are eligible to receive assistance as per the provisions applicable to group beneficiaries under the scheme guidelines.
<br>

### What financial assistance is available for General Category beneficiaries?

General Category beneficiaries are eligible for assistance equal to 50% of the cost of the polishing unit, subject to a maximum limit of ₹94,000 per unit.
<br>

### What financial assistance is available for NE, SC, and ST beneficiaries?

Beneficiaries belonging to the North-Eastern Region, Scheduled Castes, and Scheduled Tribes can receive assistance equal to 75% of the cost of the unit, subject to a maximum limit of ₹1,40,000 per unit.
<br>

### What assistance is available for Spice Producers' Groups in identified clusters?

Spice Producers' Groups operating in identified clusters can receive assistance equal to 90% of the cost of the unit, subject to a maximum limit of ₹1,70,000 per unit.
<br>

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for availing assistance?

Yes. Aadhaar is a mandatory document required for identity verification and processing of the application.
<br>

### In which areas is the scheme implemented?

The scheme is implemented in all spice-growing states, regions, and Union Territories across India.
<br>

### Which spices can be polished using the equipment?

The equipment is primarily intended for small cardamom, large cardamom, turmeric, and similar spices. It may also be used for polishing other suitable spices with the necessary approvals.
<br>
