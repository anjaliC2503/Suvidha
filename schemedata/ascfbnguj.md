## Brief Description

The scheme aims to assist boat nets, which are provided to Scheduled Caste (SC) fishermen and fish farmers, to increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and providing employment to SC individuals involved in inland fisheries.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries", with the component of Boat Net, introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and providing employment to Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals involved in inland fisheries. Through this scheme, assistance is provided for boat nets, which are provided to Scheduled Caste fishermen and fish farmers. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries, Government of Gujarat.
**Objectives:**

- To increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to scheduled caste (SC) people involved in inland fisheries activities.
- To boost fish farming or fishing activities in freshwater bodies by scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen.
- Fishing in reservoirs, aquaculture in ponds, and catch from rivers increase fish production and boost inland fisheries through scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen.
- The main objective is to provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Caste fishermen in the state and increase fish production.

<br>

## Benefits

1. **Tin Boat-Net Unit:** 50% of unit cost or ₹7,500/- (Boat: ₹5,000/-; Net: ₹2,500/-), whichever is less.1. **F.R.P. (Fibre-Reinforced Plastic) Boat-Net Unit:** 50% of unit cost or ₹15,000/- (Boat: ₹12,500/-; Net: ₹2,500/-), whichever is less.
> **Conditions/Aspects/Criteria Associated:****** Benefit amount is calculated based on 50% of the unit cost or the fixed maximum amount mentioned above, whichever is less.

> **Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits:** 6 Years

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) community.
1. The applicant should be above 18 years of age.
1. The applicant should be involved in inland fisheries activities.
1. The applicant can be an individual, a society, a self-help group, an association, a reservoir monopoly, or a private unit.
1. The applicant must have a bank account in their own name.
1. The applicant must have an Aadhar Card/Voter ID/Ration Card/Passport as proof of identity.

<br>

> **Preference/Reservation:**

The scheme specifically benefits Scheduled Caste fishermen under the inland fisheries scheme.
<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on 'Login' from the top right-hand side, then click on "Beneficiary Registration" and select any one from the 'Farmer' or 'Institutional' or 'Except For the Farmer' as the Beneficiary Type.
**Step 3:** Enter the 'Company Name' and Address if you have selected 'Institutional' or 'Except For the Farmer' as the Beneficiary Type. And for all select the District, Taluka, and Village, choose 'what type of account holder you are?', enter your Account Number and Name, solve the Captcha, and click 'Save and Next'.
**Step 4:** Enter your Mobile Number and E-mail address, click 'Send OTP', enter the received OTP, create and confirm a Password, solve the Captcha, and click 'Submit'.
> **Login Process:** 

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on 'Login' from the top right-hand side of the homepage. On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button, and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on the "Apply New" button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the "Update Application" button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can individuals, societies, and other groups apply for the scheme?

Yes, individuals, societies, self-help groups, associations, reservoir monopolies, and private units involved in inland fisheries can all avail themselves of the scheme's benefits.
<br>

### What are the main objectives of the scheme?

The scheme aims to increase fish production by encouraging fish rearing in reservoirs and boosting inland fisheries activities for Scheduled Caste (SC) individuals. It also seeks to provide employment opportunities to SC fishermen.
<br>

### What is the eligibility age for an applicant?

The applicant must be above 18 years of age.
<br>

### What are the essential documents required to apply for the scheme?

Applicants need to provide a bank account, an Aadhar Card/Voter ID/Ration Card/Passport as proof of identity, and a Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribe certificate.
<br>

### Do I need my own bank account to apply?

Yes, the beneficiary fisherman must have their own bank account to receive the benefits.
<br>

### Is the scheme only for Scheduled Caste applicants?

Yes, the scheme is specifically for Scheduled Caste fishermen to boost inland fisheries through their involvement.
<br>

### Where can the application for this assistance be submitted?

Applications can be submitted online through the iKhedut portal at https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in.
<br>

### Is it necessary to be involved in inland fisheries to apply for the assistance?

Yes, only those involved in inland fisheries activities are eligible for this component.
<br>

### What is the purpose of providing assistance for boat-net units?

The support is aimed at increasing fish production and generating employment among Scheduled Caste fish farmers and fishermen.
<br>

### How often can one receive support for the boat-net component?

Assistance for boat-net units is available once every 6 years for eligible beneficiaries.
<br>
