## Brief Description

The scheme "Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana" aims to support SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthen MSMEs, making them globally competitive.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Dr.Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana&quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat. It aims to empower the state&#39;s dynamic MSME sector, which significantly contributes to employment, GDP, and exports. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative supports SC/ST entrepreneurs and strengthens MSMEs, making them globally competitive.
The component &quot;Assistance to Micro and Small Enterprises (MSE) of SC/ST for Plot Developed by GIDC&quot; provides financial assistance for the development of essential industrial infrastructure, including water supply, gas connections, and approach roads. The objective is to support industries by improving last-mile connectivity and access to utilities, thereby fostering a conducive business environment.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Quantum of Assistance:**


   1. An enterprise will be eligible for assistance up to 70% of the allotment price of GIDC. 
   1. On payment of 30% of the allotment price, GIDC will hand over the possession of the plot and submit the claim of subsidy up to 70% of the allotment price to the commissioner MSME.

## Exclusions

- The trading activity will not be eligible under the scheme.

## Eligibility

1. The firm must be a Micro/Small/Medium enterprise run by SC/ST entrepreneur.
   1. The applicant must apply for a plot within an estate approved by GIDC.
   1. The maximum plot size should not exceed 1000 Sq. Mt. (with exceptions for irregular plots).

&gt; **Note****:** 


  - **SC/ST Enterprise:** SC/ST Enterprise means the enterprise in which contribution of SC/ST entrepreneur is 51% or more.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Udyog Uday Yojana"?

It is a special scheme introduced under the Industrial Policy 2020 to provide financial assistance and support to Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) entrepreneurs in the MSME sector.

### What sectors are prioritized under the Industrial Policy 2020?

The policy prioritizes industries such as Auto and Auto Components, Chemicals and Petrochemicals, Pharmaceuticals, Cement, Textiles, Engineering, Gems and Jewellery, and Ceramics.

### How does the scheme promote export competitiveness?

The scheme emphasizes "Vocal for Local to become Global," strengthening integrated value chains and encouraging MSMEs to enhance their export potential.

### What is the duration of the scheme?

The scheme is effective from August 7, 2020, and will remain in operation until August 6, 2025.

### What is the purpose of the "Assistance to Micro and Small Enterprises (MSE) of SC/ST for Plot Developed by GIDC" component?

The "Assistance to Micro and Small Enterprises (MSE) of SC/ST for Plot Developed by GIDC" component aims to support Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) owned by SC/ST entrepreneurs by providing financial assistance for acquiring plots in estates developed by Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation (GIDC).

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Entrepreneurs belonging to the SC/ST category who own a Micro or Small Enterprise (MSE) are eligible to apply.

### What is the maximum financial assistance available under this scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance of up to 70% of the plot allotment price.

### Is it necessary for the applicant to pay the full allotment price before receiving the subsidy?

No, the applicant needs to pay only 30% of the allotment price, after which the possession of the plot will be handed over.

### What types of businesses qualify for assistance under this scheme?

Only manufacturing and service sector businesses are eligible, while trading businesses do not qualify.

### What is the maximum plot size that can be allotted under this scheme?

The maximum plot size allowed under the scheme is 1000 Sq. Mt., except for irregular plots resulting from layout constraints.

### What happens if an enterprise fails to commence production within the scheme’s operative period?

If an enterprise fails to commence production within the stipulated period, it may become ineligible for the subsidy.

### What steps follow after the applicant pays 30% of the allotment price?

Once the applicant pays 30% of the allotment price, GIDC will hand over the possession of the plot and submit the subsidy claim to the MSME Commissionerate.

### When will the subsidy amount be disbursed to the eligible enterprises?

The subsidy will be disbursed based on the availability of government funds.

### Are trading businesses allowed to apply for this scheme?

No, trading businesses are not eligible under this scheme.

### Can an enterprise apply if the required plot size exceeds 1000 Sq. Mt.?

No, enterprises cannot apply for plots exceeding 1000 Sq. Mt., except in cases where the plot size is irregular due to layout constraints.
