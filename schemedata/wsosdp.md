## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide care, protection, and financial assistance to elderly persons and socially disadvantaged individuals lacking family or financial support.

## Detailed Description

The "Welfare Services for Other Socially Disabled Persons (Senior Citizens & Destitute)" under the Mizoram Grant-in-Aid framework focuses on elderly persons (65+), destitute individuals, and socially disabled persons who lack means of livelihood. It supports NGOs in running old age homes, day care centres, mobile medical units, and specialized care services.
The scheme ensures access to healthcare, shelter, companionship, and mental health support, promoting dignified living for senior citizens.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Care & Shelter**


- Old age homes and respite care homes.

> **Healthcare**


- Mobile medical units.
- Physiotherapy clinics.

> **Day Care Services**


- Multi-service centres for elderly.

> **Special Care**


- Dementia/Alzheimer’s care centres.

> **Support Services**


- Counseling and helplines.

<br>

## Eligibility

> **For Individuals (Beneficiaries):**


- The applicant must be a senior citizen aged 65 years or above.
- The applicant must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.
- The applicant must be a destitute person without any regular means of livelihood.
- The applicant must not have family members or relatives to provide financial or social support.

> **For Organizations:**


- The applicant must be a registered voluntary organization/NGO/institution.
- The applicant must be engaged in providing welfare services for senior citizens or destitute persons.
- The applicant must have adequate infrastructure such as old age homes, day care centres, or care facilities.
- The applicant must have experience in implementing elderly care or social welfare programmes.
- The applicant must ensure that services are provided without discrimination on any grounds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The objective of the scheme is to provide care, protection, and financial assistance to senior citizens and destitute persons who lack adequate means of livelihood and support.
<br>

### Who are eligible beneficiaries under this scheme?

Senior citizens aged 65 years and above and destitute individuals without financial or family support are eligible beneficiaries under the scheme.
<br>

### What is the age requirement for senior citizens?

The applicant must be at least 65 years of age to qualify as a senior citizen under this scheme.
<br>

### Does the scheme provide financial assistance to individuals?

Yes, the scheme provides financial assistance to eligible elderly and destitute individuals as per prescribed norms.
<br>

### Are shelter facilities available under the scheme?

Yes, the scheme supports the establishment and maintenance of old age homes and care facilities for beneficiaries.
<br>

### What types of institutions are supported under the scheme?

The scheme supports old age homes, respite care homes, day care centres, and continuous care homes.
<br>

### Are healthcare services provided under this scheme?

Yes, healthcare services such as mobile medical units, physiotherapy, and basic medical care are provided.
<br>

### Is there any support for elderly persons with serious illnesses?

Yes, the scheme provides specialized care through continuous care homes and medical support services for seriously ill elderly persons.
<br>

### Does the scheme include mental health support?

Yes, mental health care and specialized services for conditions such as dementia and Alzheimer’s disease are included.
<br>

### . Can NGOs apply to implement the scheme?

Yes, registered voluntary organizations and institutions can apply for financial assistance to implement welfare services.
<br>

### Are services available in rural and remote areas?

Yes, the scheme includes mobile medical units and outreach services for beneficiaries in rural and remote areas.
<br>

### Are day care services provided under the scheme?

Yes, day care centres provide healthcare, recreational activities, and social interaction for elderly persons.
<br>
