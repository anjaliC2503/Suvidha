## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide research assistantship to undergraduate and postgraduate research scholars to support their academic and research activities.

## Detailed Description

The “Research Assistantship” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote higher education and research through providing research assistantship to undergraduate and postgraduate research scholars.
<br>

## Benefits

Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to undergraduate and postgraduate researchers in the form of research assistantships.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a graduate or postgraduate research scholar pursuing research at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

The selection of undergraduate and postgraduate research scholars is based on a written examination and academic performance (OGPA), in accordance with the criteria established by the university.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Graduate and postgraduate students actively pursuing research at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, are eligible.
<br>

### What type of assistance is provided?

Financial assistance is provided in the form of a research assistantship to eligible undergraduate and postgraduate research scholars.
<br>

### How are candidates selected for the scheme?

Selection is based on a written examination and academic performance (OGPA), as per the criteria established by the university.
<br>

### How can a student apply for the Research Assistantship?

Students can apply through the official notification issued by the university on its website or through the concerned department.
<br>

### Can students from other universities apply?

No.
