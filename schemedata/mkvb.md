## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide subsidized mushroom kits and training to farmers, promoting mushroom cultivation as a sustainable livelihood opportunity.

## Detailed Description

The “Mushroom Kit Vitaran” scheme launched by the Department of Agriculture, Bihar, aims to encourage farmers and individuals to engage in mushroom cultivation by providing high-quality mushroom kits at a 90% subsidy and through training programs.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Subsidized Mushroom Kits:** Kits provided at ₹60/- each with a 90% subsidy.  - **High-Quality Materials:** Kits weigh 5 kg and include 100 grams of superior mushroom spawn.  - **Training Support:** 2-days group training is provided, with a daily allowance of ₹1000/- per farmer.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Bihar.
1. The applicant should be a farmer.
1. The applicant must be registered on the DBT portal.
1. Farmers who have taken advantage of the scheme in the last three years (202-22, 2022-23 and 2023-24) will not be given the benefit of the scheme this year.

<br>

> **Note:**  


1. The benefit of this scheme will be given only once to one member of the family.
1. Beneficiaries will be selected on first come first serve basis.

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **DBT Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [registration page](https://dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in/RegFarmer/) of the DBT portal. 
**Step 2:** Select Aadhaar verification type: OTP/ BIO-AUTH/ IRIS.
**Step 3:** Enter your Aadhaar number and Name as per Aadhaar. Click on “Authentication”.
**Step 4:** Enter the OTP. Click on “Validate OTP”. 
**Step 5:** Now confirm the details about the “Kisan Credit Card (KCC)” Details. Click on “Farmer Registration”.
**Step 6:** In the Registration Page, fill in all the details properly i.e. Farmer details, Land information, Bank account details and click on “Submit”.
**Step 7:** Validate with OTP. Click on “Register” to generate the Registration Id.
> **Note:**  After 48 hours of registration, farmers can apply for the scheme with the help of 13 digit registration number.

<br>

> **Online Application of the Scheme:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official [website](https://horticulture.bihar.gov.in/HORTMIS/InputBasedStateScheme/OnlineAppMushroomKitState.aspx). 
**Step 2:** Scroll to bottom and confirm the checkbox. Click on “Proceed to apply”.
**Step 3:** In the next page select the “Type of Applicant” and enter the “DBT Registration Number of Kisan”. Click on “Get details”.
**Step 4:** Fill in the required details i.e. Address, Land Details, etc, and upload documents.
**Step 5:** Confirm the checkbox and then click on “Submit”.
**Step 6:** An “Application Number” will be generated. Keep this for future reference.
**Step 7:** Printout of the filled in application also can be taken using the Print option.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the “Mushroom Kit Vitaran” scheme?

The Mushroom Kit Vitaran scheme is a government initiative aimed at promoting mushroom cultivation by providing farmers with highly subsidized mushroom kits and necessary training.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Farmers who have not benefited from the scheme in the last three years are eligible to apply.

### Is it mandatory for applicants to own land to avail of this scheme?

No, land ownership is not mandatory for availing of the benefits of this scheme.

### What is the cost of a mushroom kit under this scheme?

The unit cost of a mushroom kit is [?]60/-, and the government provides a 90% subsidy, significantly reducing the cost for farmers.

### What is the quantity of kits an applicant can receive under the scheme?

An applicant can receive a minimum of 25 kits and a maximum of 100 kits under this scheme.

### What are the specifications of the mushroom kits provided?

Each mushroom kit weighs 5 kg and contains 100 grams of high-quality mushroom spawn.

### Where is the training for mushroom cultivation conducted?

Training is conducted at locations such as the Center of Excellence, Krishi Vigyan Kendras, Agricultural Universities, and Horticulture Colleges.

### What is the duration and support provided for the training?

The training is a two-day program and a provision of [?]1,000/- per farmer per day is allocated.

### Can more than one member of a family benefit from this scheme?

No, only one member per family is eligible to receive benefits under this scheme.

### What percentage of subsidy is provided on mushroom kits?

The government provides a 90% subsidy on the unit cost of the mushroom kits.

### What support does the scheme provide during the training program?

The scheme allocates [?]1,000/- per farmer per day to cover expenses related to the training program, ensuring farmers receive proper guidance and resources.
