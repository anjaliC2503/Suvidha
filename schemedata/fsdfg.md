## Brief Description

Under this scheme, meritorious girl students from government and private schools who have scored 85% or above in the Class 10th and 12th (Arts, Commerce, Science streams) examinations are provided with a free scooty, along with a helmet and petrol.

## Detailed Description

The "Free Scooty Distribution for Girls" scheme was launched by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Rajasthan in the year 2017-18, aims to encourage meritorious girl students belonging to the economically weaker section of the general category in the state. Under this scheme, meritorious girl students from government and private schools who have scored 85% or above in the Class 10th and 12th (Arts, Commerce, Science streams) examinations are provided with a free scooty, along with a helmet and petrol.

## Benefits

Under this scheme, Eligible girl students will receive a scooty, a helmet, and 2 liters of petrol.

## Exclusions

- The applicant should not have received a scooty through any other scheme.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a native of Rajasthan.
1. The applicant should be a girl student. 
1. The applicant should belong to the Economically Weaker Section (EWS) of General Category. 
1. The applicant's family annual income should not exceed ₹2,50,000/-.
1. The applicant should have studied in a government or private school in Rajasthan.
1. The applicant should have secured at least 85% marks in class 10th or 12th.

## Application Process

### Offline

***Application process:***
**Step 1:** The interested applicant visits the [official website](https://evaluation.rajasthan.gov.in/scheme/detail/337) and clicks on the application form to download it. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the respective school where the education is completed.

## Documents Required

1. Passport-size Photo (A seal on the verified photo of the student by the school principal)
1. Birth Certificate (Self-certified secondary examination certificate)
1. Domicile Certificate (Self-certified copy issued by the competent authority)
1. 10th Marksheet (Education Qualification Marksheet/Certificate Copy)
1. 12th Marksheet (Education Qualification Marksheet/Certificate Copy)
1. Caste Certificate/EWS Certificate (Self-certified copy issued by the competent authority)
1. Income Certificate Copy (Self-certified copy issued by the competent authority)
1. Jan Aadhar/Bhamashah Card Copy (Scooty not receive certificate in any other scheme)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the Free Scooty Distribution scheme?

Girls from the general category who belong to the EWS group and have scored above the cut-off marks in class 10th or 12th are eligible.

### What is the income limit for applying under this scheme?

The family income of the applicant must be less than ₹2,50,000 per year.

### What are the benefits provided under this scheme?

Selected beneficiaries receive a scooty, helmet, and 2 litres of petrol.

### Is this scheme applicable to students of private schools also?

Yes, it is applicable to both government and private school students.

### Can I apply if I already received a scooty under another government scheme?

No, such applicants are excluded from this scheme.

### What is the mode of application for this scheme?

The application is submitted offline at the respective school.

### Is there any application fee for this scheme?

No, the application form is free of cost.

### How long does it take to receive the scooty after application?

The scooty is distributed within 45 days of the selection process.

### Who is the designated authority for this scheme?

The District Education Officer (DEO) is responsible for implementation.

### Will I receive any monetary assistance under this scheme?

No, the scheme provides physical items (scooty, helmet, petrol) only.
