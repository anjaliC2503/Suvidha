## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage cardamom growers to adopt Improved Cardamom Curing Devices (ICCD) for drying small cardamom, thereby improving the quality of the produce and making it more suitable for export.

## Detailed Description

The Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research & Development of Cardamom: Post-Harvest Improvement – Improved Cardamom Curing Devices (ICCD) for Small Cardamom (Nos.) is implemented by the Spices Board India under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. The scheme aims to encourage cardamom growers to adopt Improved Cardamom Curing Devices (ICCD) for drying small cardamom, thereby improving the quality of the produce and making it more suitable for export. It is being implemented in accordance with the modalities and guidelines approved by the Competent Authority under the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research & Development of Cardamom during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

## Benefits

- 33.33 % of cost of unit, subject to a maximum of ₹ 1,50,000/- per unit for General Category and 75% subject to maximum of ₹ 3,37,500, per unit for SC/ST & 90% Spice Producer's Groups in Identified Clusters subject to maximum of ₹ 4,00,000/-
- Registered cardamom growers having a land holding size of 0.40 ha to 8 ha Assistance for groups/FPOs will be as in QGBG

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to Kerala, Tamil Nadu & Karnataka.
2. Designated Officials will conduct final inspection and recommend the eligible cases.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the ICCD for Small Cardamom scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance to registered cardamom growers for installing Improved Cardamom Curing Devices (ICCD) to improve the quality of dried cardamom and enhance its export potential.
<br>

### Which organization implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Spices Board India under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India.
<br>

### What is the objective of the scheme?

The objective is to encourage growers to adopt improved curing technologies for drying cardamom and producing high-quality cardamom suitable for export markets.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for assistance under the scheme?

Registered cardamom growers having a landholding size between 0.40 hectares and 8 hectares are eligible to apply.
<br>

### In which states is the scheme available?

The scheme is available in the cardamom-growing states of Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka.
<br>

### What subsidy is available for General Category beneficiaries?

General Category beneficiaries can receive 33.33% of the unit cost, subject to a maximum subsidy of ₹1,50,000 per unit.
<br>

### What subsidy is available for SC/ST beneficiaries?

SC/ST beneficiaries are eligible for 75% of the unit cost, subject to a maximum subsidy of ₹3,37,500 per unit.
<br>

### What subsidy is available for Spice Producer Groups in identified clusters?

Spice Producer Groups in identified clusters can receive 90% of the unit cost, subject to a maximum subsidy of ₹4,00,000 per unit.
<br>

### Is it mandatory to purchase the curing device from an approved manufacturer?

Yes. The equipment must be purchased from a valid manufacturer empaneled by the Spices Board.
<br>
