## Brief Description

The scheme aims to empower individuals and organizations by providing financial, technical, and institutional support for livelihood, entrepreneurship, and employment generation. It also promotes sustainable socio-economic development and youth engagement in Mizoram.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Mizoram Bana Kaih (Handholding) Scheme&quot; by the Planning &amp; Programme Implementation Department, Government of Mizoram, aims to promote socio-economic development through providing financial, technical, and institutional support to selected Progress Partners. It facilitates livelihood generation, entrepreneurship, and sustainable development. The scheme is implemented for an initial period of 5 years starting from 19th September, 2024.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Handholding Support:**


- Comprehensive support including advisory services, training, technical assistance, and supervision.
- Credit linkage through bank loans with collateral-free provisions.
- Full interest subvention and bank guarantee support.
- Provision of catalytic capital grant and venture funding.

> **Support to Implementing Departments:**


- Funding for development of common infrastructure and facilities.
- Research and development support for innovation and localization.
- Financial support for technical training programmes.
- Administrative expense support for implementation and monitoring.

> **Cluster Development Support:**


- Promotion of agriculture and allied sectors through cluster (zau) approach.
- Development of industrial clusters to attract investments.
- Support based on agro-climatic suitability and employment potential.

> **Market Support:**


- Price support for priority crops.
- Market intelligence and information dissemination.
- Establishment of market linkages.
- Financial support for infrastructure and machinery (fixed capital creation).

> **Participative Planning & Innovation:**


- Support for decentralized planning at district and local levels.
- Promotion of innovation through initiatives like the "Chief Minister’s Innovation Challenge".
- Integration of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be an individual, group, organization, entrepreneur, or innovator.
  - The applicant should have high potential for wealth and employment generation.
  - The applicant should be selected as a “Progress Partner” by the Apex Body.
  - The applicant should be engaged in livelihood, business, startup, or development activities.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Progress Partners registration using &quot;[Register New User](https://handholding.mizoram.gov.in/register)&quot;. One Individual and/or One Organization Project may be submitted from a single user account.
**Step 2:** After registration, the Application (Loan or Chief Minister Package) may be submitted online by Progress Partners through the portal. Upon successful submission, the applicant will receive a confirmation SMS at the registered phone number.
**Step 3:** Based on the Trade selected, the respective department will perform verification and then forward it to the Planning &amp; Programme Implementation Department.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Mizoram Bana Kaih (Handholding) Scheme"?

The scheme is a government initiative to support individuals and organizations through financial, technical, and institutional assistance for livelihood and entrepreneurship development.
<br>

### Who implements the scheme?

The scheme is implemented by various departments under the Government of Mizoram with the Planning & Programme Implementation Department as the nodal department.
<br>

### Who are Progress Partners?

Progress Partners are individuals or organizations selected for their potential to generate employment and wealth.
<br>

### What kind of support is provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance, technical support, training, market linkage, and infrastructure support.
<br>

### Is there any financial assistance available?

Yes, support includes collateral-free loans, interest subvention, grants, and venture funding.
<br>

### How are beneficiaries selected?

Beneficiaries are identified by departments and selected by the Apex Body based on merit.
<br>

### What is the duration of the scheme?

The scheme is implemented for an initial period of 5 years starting from 19.09.2024.
<br>

### Is the scheme available across Mizoram?

Is the scheme available across Mizoram?
<br>

<br>

### Does the scheme support startups?

Yes, startups as defined by DPIIT are eligible under the scheme.
<br>

### What is credit linkage?

Credit linkage refers to the facilitation of bank loans for selected beneficiaries.
<br>

### Is training provided under the scheme?

Yes, technical training and capacity building are key components of the scheme.
<br>

### What is cluster development under the scheme?

Cluster development focuses on promoting agriculture and industries in specific zones for better productivity and employment.
