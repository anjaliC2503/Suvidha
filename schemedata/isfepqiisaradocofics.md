## Brief Description

Under this scheme to support the grower groups / FPOs to establish grower group certification in order to prepare farmers on group formation, organic regulations, certification procedures, training, documentation, internal inspection etc. for organic certification and aggregation of the produce.

## Detailed Description

Under the scheme “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom: Organic Farming – Internal Control System (ICS) (Nos)” of the Spices Board India, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, support is provided to grower groups and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) for establishing grower group certification systems. The scheme aims to prepare farmers through group formation, awareness of organic regulations, certification procedures, training, documentation, internal inspection, and other activities related to organic certification and aggregation of produce. This is implemented as per the modalities and scheme guidelines approved by the competent authority under the “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom” during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

## Benefits

> 50% subject to maximum of ₹ 1,12,000/- for ONER groups and 75% subject to a maximum of ₹1,68,000/- for NER groups.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The scheme is applicable to all spice-growing States, regions, and Union Territories (UTs) in India.
1. The Spices Board India reserves the right to conduct checks at any stage of the chain of custody, either before or after providing assistance.
1. An Internal Control System (ICS) is eligible to receive assistance a maximum of three times within the five-year block period from 2021–22 to 2025–26, either consecutively or in a staggered manner, subject to the availability of funds under the component.

**Preference for Selection**
**Preference will be given in the following order:**

- Grower groups/ICSs producing organic spices.
- Grower groups/ICSs in which more than 50% of the farmers in the approved farmer list are producing organic spices under any cropping system.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Internal Control System (ICS) assistance scheme?

The scheme aims to support grower groups and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) in establishing grower group certification systems for organic spices. It helps farmers with group formation, organic regulations, certification procedures, training, documentation, internal inspection, and aggregation of produce.
<br>

### Which organization implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by Spices Board India under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the scheme?

Grower groups, farmer groups, and FPOs involved in organic spice cultivation are eligible to apply under the scheme.
<br>

### In which areas is the scheme applicable?

The scheme is applicable in all spice-growing States, regions, and Union Territories (UTs) of India.
<br>

### How many times can an ICS receive assistance?

An ICS is eligible for assistance a maximum of three times during the five-year block period from 2021–22 to 2025–26, either consecutively or in a staggered manner, subject to fund availability.
<br>

### Is the assistance automatic every year?

No. Assistance is subject to eligibility, submission of required documents, and availability of funds under the scheme component.
<br>

### Which groups receive preference under the scheme?

Preference is given first to groups producing organic spices.
<br>

### Is there any additional preference criteria?

Yes. Additional preference is given to groups where more than 50% of the farmers in the approved list cultivate organic spices under any cropping system.
<br>

### Can the Spices Board inspect or verify the applicants?

Yes. The Spices Board India reserves the right to conduct checks at any point in the chain of custody before or after providing assistance.
<br>

### What is a Scope Certificate?

A Scope Certificate is a document issued by the Certification Body confirming that the grower group or ICS complies with the prescribed organic certification standards.
<br>
