## Brief Description

The scheme "Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area" aims to promote cultural infrastructure through financial assistance to trusts and institutions for building or renovating cultural centres within municipal areas.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to strengthen cultural development and encourage the preservation and promotion of arts and heritage by supporting the creation and improvement of cultural infrastructure in urban regions. Under this initiative, eligible trusts and institutions are provided financial assistance either for constructing new cultural centres or for renovating existing ones. The scheme is implemented by the Sangeet Natak Academy, Gujarat.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Assistance for New Construction:** 50% of the total cost or ₹30,00,000/- whichever is lower.  - **Assistance for Renovation:** Up to ₹15,00,000/-.
<br>

## Eligibility

- A trust or institution operating within the municipal areas of Gujarat shall be eligible for this scheme.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Applicants must prepare a proposal on plain paper detailing the purpose, estimated cost, and intended use.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the Gujarat State Sangeet Natak Academy.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the “Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area” scheme?

The scheme "Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote cultural infrastructure through financial assistance to trusts and institutions for building or renovating cultural centres within municipal areas.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme “Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area”?

A registered trust or institution located within the municipal areas of the state of Gujarat is to be considered eligible for the scheme “Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area”.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What type of financial support is offered under the scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance either for the construction of a new cultural centre or for the renovation of an existing one, with the objective of strengthening cultural infrastructure in urban areas.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum amount of financial assistance provided for constructing a new cultural centre?

Under the scheme “Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area”, the maximum assistance for constructing a new cultural centre is limited to 50% of the actual project cost or ₹30,00,000/-, whichever is less.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the financial limit for renovation of an existing cultural centre under this scheme?

For renovation purposes, the scheme provides financial support up to a maximum of ₹15,00,000, enabling the applicant institutions to improve their existing infrastructure for cultural engagement.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can individuals apply for this scheme?

The applicant must be a trust or institution; therefore, individuals are not eligible to apply under the scheme “Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area”.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What are the documents required for applying under this scheme?

The applicant must submit key documents including Aadhaar Card, an attested copy of the PAN Card, bank details of the authorized representative or heir, a copy of the bank passbook or a cancelled cheque, along with a detailed application written on plain paper.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How can one apply for the scheme “Assistance to Trusts/Institutions for Setting Up Cultural Centers/Auditoriums in the Municipal Area”?

The applicant must prepare a formal application on plain paper and submit it along with the required documents through the official website of Sangeet Natak Academy, Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### What kind of cultural institutions are encouraged to apply under this scheme?

The scheme encourages the participation of trusts and institutions that are involved in promoting traditional, classical, folk, or contemporary art forms and are seeking to expand or improve their infrastructure to host cultural events.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Does the applicant need to contribute a share of the project cost?

Yes, the applicant institution is expected to bear at least 50% of the cost for constructing a new cultural centre, as the government’s contribution is limited to the remaining 50% or ₹30,00,000, whichever is less.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where can the applicant find more information or track updates regarding the scheme?

The applicant can visit the official portal of the Sangeet Natak Academy at [https://sangeetnatak.gujarat.gov.in](https://sangeetnatak.gujarat.gov.in/) for more information.
<br>
