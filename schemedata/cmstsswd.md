## Brief Description

"CMST: Scholarship of Students with Disabilities" is a Scholarship Scheme by the Govt. of Manipur. In this scheme, the Students with Disability who are a domicile of the State of Manipur, and are studying in Class 1st and and above are awarded with scholarship of upto ₹ 8,000/-.

## Detailed Description

"Chief Ministergi Shotharabasingi Tengbang (CMST): Scholarship of Students with Disabilities" is a Scholarship Scheme by the Social Welfare Department, Govt. of Manipur. In this scheme, the Students with Disability (SwD) who are a domicile of the State of Manipur, and are studying in Class 1st and above are awarded with scholarship of upto ₹ 8,000/-. The student must be enrolled in a school/college/university/institution recognized by the Govt. of Manipur. The amount of the scholarhip shall be disbursed directly into the bank account of the beneficiary.

## Benefits

- Class: 1st to 5th; Rate of Scholarship (per annum): ₹ 3,000/-.
- Class: 6th to 8th; Rate of Scholarship (per annum): ₹ 4,000/-.
- Class: 9th to 12th; Rate of Scholarship (per annum): ₹ 6,000/-.
- Graduate & Above (including all Technical Courses): ₹ 8,000/-.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a Person with Disability (PwD).
1. The applicant should be a Student.
1. The applicant should be a domicile of the State of Manipur.
1. The applicant should be studying in Class 1st and above.
1. The applicant should be enrolled in a school/college/university/institution recognized by the Govt. of Manipur.
1. The applicant should not be availing scholarship under any other similar scheme from any source.
