## Brief Description

The scheme aims to help farmers belonging to scheduled tribes and tribal cultivators residing outside tribal areas achieve higher production of agricultural crops and improve their livelihood.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Distribution of Seeds of more productive varieties / Hybrid varieties Seeds and fertilizer at subsidies etc., to Adivasi farmers in Tribal Area (TASP)" with a component "Power Operated Plant Protection Equipment" is introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to help farmers belonging to scheduled tribes and tribal cultivators residing outside tribal areas achieve higher production of agricultural crops and improve their livelihood. Through this scheme, subsidies are provided on seeds of more productive/hybrid varieties, plant protection equipment, and agricultural inputs to the intended beneficiaries. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>

## Benefits

1. The assistance provided is 75% of the purchase price of power/machine-driven equipment or ₹ 4500/- whichever is less. (Maximum one unit per account is allowed.)

<br>

> Remark: As per conditions No. 5, 16, 17, 19 and 22 of [Appendix-1](https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx). (Refer Scheme No. 35)

> Frequency: Once in 3 Years.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to a Scheduled Tribe.
1. The applicant should be a farmer.
1. The applicant should be located in tribal areas OR be a tribal cultivator residing outside tribal areas.
1. The scheme excludes farmers from Dang district.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme for Adivasi farmers in tribal areas?

The scheme provides subsidies on seeds, fertilizers, and farm equipment to Scheduled Tribe farmers in tribal areas to help them increase crop production and improve their livelihood.
<br>

### Can tribal farmers living outside tribal areas also apply?

Yes, tribal cultivators living outside tribal areas are also eligible for the scheme.
<br>

### How often can a farmer claim the subsidy for plant protection equipment?

The subsidy for power-operated plant protection equipment can be claimed once in 3 years.
<br>

### How often can a farmer claim the subsidy for plant protection equipment?

The subsidy for power-operated plant protection equipment can be claimed once in 3 years.
<br>

### Is there a limit to the number of equipment units that can be purchased under subsidy?

Yes, only one equipment unit per account is allowed under this scheme.
<br>

### What conditions apply to availing subsidies for plant protection equipment?

The subsidy is provided as per conditions 5, 16, 17, 19, 22, and 24 of Appendix-1.
<br>

### What is the purpose of providing power-operated plant protection equipment under the scheme?

The equipment helps in protecting crops scientifically, ensuring better production and improving farmers’ livelihood.
<br>

### Can farmers use the subsidy for other types of equipment?

The specific component here is power-operated plant protection equipment, with assistance as mentioned in the scheme guidelines.
<br>

### When will the subsidy be given to the farmer?

The subsidy is provided after verification of the application and purchase documents by the Agriculture Department.
<br>

### What happens if a farmer from the Dang district applies under this scheme?

Farmers from the Dang district are excluded and cannot avail benefits under this scheme.
<br>

### Why is the benefit restricted to once in three years for plant protection equipment?

The restriction ensures fair distribution of benefits and prevents repeated claims within a short time frame.
