## Brief Description

This scheme awards cash incentives to NCC cadets who demonstrate outstanding performance in the current year's RDC, TSC, VSC, or NSC camps, recognizing six gold medal recipients with ₹3,000/- and six silver medal recipients with ₹2000/-.

## Detailed Description

&quot;Rajya / Mukhyamantri Swarn / Rajat Padak Protsahan Puraskar&quot; implemented by the Department of Education, Government of Uttarakhand, aims to promote excellence in National Cadet Corps (NCC) training by incentivizing high-performing cadets. Through this scheme, cadets who win gold or silver medals during RDC (Republic Day Camp), TSC (Thal Sainik Camp), VSC (Vayu Sainik Camp), or NSC (Nav Sainik Camp) are awarded cash prizes. The scheme is implemented by NCC Group Headquarters through a structured selection and recommendation process.

## Benefits

- **Gold Medal Winners:** A cash award of ₹3,000/- each is given to 6 cadets who achieve gold medals during NCC training and camps.- **Silver Medal Winners:** A cash award of ₹2,000/- each is given to 6 cadets who achieve silver medals during NCC training and camps.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be an NCC cadet.
   1. The applicant must have demonstrated outstanding performance in one of the following camps during the current training year: RDC (Republic Day Camp), TSC (Thall Sainik Camp), VSC (Vayu Sena Camp), or NSC (Nau Sainik Camp).
   1. The applicant must be among the top 6 performers for the gold medal or the next 6 performers for the silver medal.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Application forms are filled by all NCC Units.
**Step 2:** Filled applications are forwarded by the Units to the Group Headquarters.
**Step 3:** Group Headquarters conducts necessary verification.
**Step 4:** After verification, Group Headquarters sends the applications to the Directorate.
**Step 5:** At the Directorate, a Board of Officers is formed to scrutinize the applications and related documents/board proceedings.
**Step 6:** Based on performance and scrutiny, cash awards are allocated to the top 6 cadets (gold) and next 6 cadets (silver).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Rajya/Mukhyamantri Swarn/Rajat Padak Protsahan Puraskar" scheme?

The "Rajya/Mukhyamantri Swarn/Rajat Padak Protsahan Puraskar" scheme has been introduced to recognize and encourage exceptional performance by NCC cadets who secure medals during various national-level training camps conducted in the current training year.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to receive the cash incentive under this scheme?

Only those NCC cadets who are actively participating in the current training year and have secured either a gold or silver medal during camps such as the Republic Day Camp (RDC), Thal Sainik Camp (TSC), Vayu Sainik Camp (VSC), or Nav Sainik Camp (NSC) are eligible to receive the cash incentive.
<br>

<br>

### How much monetary reward is given under this scheme to gold medal winners?

Under this scheme, each NCC cadet who is selected as a gold medal recipient is awarded a monetary prize of ₹3,000/- in recognition of their outstanding performance during training.
<br>

<br>

### What is the amount of reward provided to silver medal-winning cadets?

NCC cadets who have secured silver medals in the mentioned training camps are awarded a cash incentive of ₹2,000 under the scheme as a form of encouragement and acknowledgment of their efforts.
<br>

<br>

### How many NCC cadets are selected annually for the gold and silver medal rewards?

A total of 12 NCC cadets are selected each year for this reward, out of which 6 cadets are chosen for the gold medal category and 6 cadets are selected for the silver medal category.
<br>

<br>

### Are cadets from all NCC units within the state eligible for this scheme?

Yes, cadets from all recognized NCC units operating under the jurisdiction of Uttarakhand are eligible to apply for and benefit from this scheme if they meet the prescribed eligibility criteria.
<br>

<br>

### What are the main camps considered for medal-based selection under this scheme?

The main camps considered for evaluating and selecting cadets for this award include the Republic Day Camp (RDC), Thal Sainik Camp (TSC), Vayu Sainik Camp (VSC), and Nav Sainik Camp (NSC), where cadets must have won medals.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any online application process available for this scheme?

No, the application process for this scheme is entirely offline and involves submitting physical application forms and supporting documents through the concerned NCC units and Group Headquarters.
<br>

<br>

### How is the final list of awardees prepared and approved?

The final list of awardees is prepared based on merit, evaluated by the Board of Officers constituted by the Directorate, and includes only those cadets who obtain the highest scores in performance and meet all eligibility norms.
<br>

<br>

### When is the cash award generally disbursed to the selected cadets?

The disbursement of the cash award generally takes place after all applications are scrutinized, approved by the Directorate, and the funds are released to the selected cadets through the respective NCC authorities.
