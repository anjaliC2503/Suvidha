## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide scholarship support for the education of registered drivers, conductors, and their dependents.

## Detailed Description

The “Motor Vehicle Driver-Conductor Welfare Scheme- Scholarship” scheme has been launched by the Transport Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme supports the educational advancement of registered drivers, conductors, and their dependents by providing scholarships, based on prevailing government norms, to eligible students from Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Other Backward Class, and economically weaker general category families.
<br>

## Benefits

- The scholarship amount is provided to eligible students for education.
- The scholarship was provided as per the norms of the concerned department.
- Categories covered include Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Other Backward Class, and the economically weaker general category.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be a registered transport vehicle driver or conductor in Madhya Pradesh. 
1. The applicant should possess a valid commercial driving or conductor license issued in Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant’s name should be registered on the Samagra Portal.
1. The applicant should belong to a Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Other Backward Class, or economically weaker general category.
1. The applicant should meet educational and income criteria prescribed under the relevant scholarship scheme.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

> **Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the official website: [samagra.gov.in](https://samagra.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on the option “Register Family” or “Register Member”.
**Step 3:** Enter the Aadhaar number and other required information.
**Step 4:** Enter the OTP received on the registered mobile number.
**Step 5:** Carefully fill in all the required details and upload the necessary documents.
**Step 6:** Enter the captcha code and click on “Submit Application.”
> **Offline Samagra ID Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit the nearest Lok Seva Kendra, MP Online Kiosk, or Common Service Center (CSC).
**Step 2:** Provide the required documents and necessary information to complete the registration process.
> **After Samagra Registration:**

**Step 1:** The interested applicant downloads the [application form](https://transport.mp.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/yojna_20150001.pdf) (Page No. 9) from the official website. 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District or Regional Transport Office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of this scholarship benefit?

The scheme aims to support educational expenses of drivers, conductors, and their eligible dependents.
<br>

### Who is eligible for the scholarship?

Registered drivers, conductors, and their eligible dependent students.
<br>

### Which categories are covered?

Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Other Backward Class, and economically weaker general category.
<br>

### Is Samagra registration mandatory?

Yes, Samagra registration is mandatory.
<br>

### Is the scholarship amount fixed?

No, it is as per prevailing government norms.
<br>

### Can students from other states apply for this scheme?

No, only students who are natives/residents of Madhya Pradesh are eligible.
<br>

### Who launched the scheme?

The Transport Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### How can I apply for the scheme?

The applicant submitting the application at the District or Regional Transport Office (RTO) for verification and further processing.
<br>

### What documents are needed to apply?

The following documents are required: Samagra Family Identification Number (Samagra ID), Samagra Member Identification Number, Aadhaar Card, Proof of Residence (Madhya Pradesh), Valid Commercial Driving or Conductor License, Bank Account Details, Passport-size Photograph, etc. 
<br>

### How Can I register for Samagra ID?

The applicant visits the Samagra [official website](https://samagra.gov.in/) for the registration.
