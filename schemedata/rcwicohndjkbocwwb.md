## Brief Description

The "Registered Construction Worker in Case of His/Her Natural Death" scheme by the JKBOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, provides financial assistance to the nominee or dependents of a registered construction worker in the event of their natural death.

## Detailed Description

Launched on 1st January 2018, the scheme "Registered Construction Worker in Case of His/Her Natural Death" is a Welfare Scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir. Through this scheme, financial assistance of ₹2,00,000/- is provided to the nominee or dependents in the event of natural death of a registered BOC worker. The applications are accepted online as well as offline.

## Benefits

₹2,00,000/- is provided to the nominee/dependent of the deceased construction worker.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a nominee/dependent of a registered member/worker who has deceased due to natural causes.

## Application Process

### Online

***Application -*** 
**Step 1:** Go to the [official website ](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/Index.aspx)of the Jammu & Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.
**Step 2:** Click on ["Login,"](https://portal.jkbocw.gov.in/LoginGuest.aspx) enter your details, and sign in to your account.
**Step 3:** After logging in, click on "Scheme Registration" and select "Apply for Scheme."
**Step 4:** Choose the scheme you wish to apply for in order to avail the benefits.
**Step 5:** Enter all required information and upload the necessary documents as prompted.
**Step 6:** Finally, click on "Submit Application" to complete the process.
<br>

***Status Tracking -*** 
Applicant can track their application status online through the [official website](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/Index.aspx) of the  Jammu & Kashmir Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. Relevant updates will be sent to the registered mobile number.

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the JKBOCWWB office during office hours and request a hard copy of the prescribed application form from the staff responsible for issuing and collecting filled-in applications.
OR
The interested applicant can print the Scheme [application form](https://www.jkbocw.gov.in/downloads/circ.pdf) (Page no. 19).
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all mandatory fields, paste a passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the required documents to the Assistant Labour Commissioner  (ALC ) of your district.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority upon submission. Ensure the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission and a unique identification number (if applicable).
**NOTE:** Ensure that the application is submitted within the prescribed period, if any.

## Documents Required

1. Original Death Certificate of the Worker.
1. Attested Age Proof of the Worker.
1. Duly attested photocopy of Bank Passbook of the nominee/legal heir or a cancelled cheque bearing the name of the nominee/legal heir and the IFSC code of the bank branch.
1. Photocopy of the Aadhaar Card or Biometric Card of the deceased.
1. Photocopy of the Aadhaar Card or Biometric Card of the nominee/claimant.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Registered Construction Worker in Case of His/Her Natural Death" scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance to the nominee or dependents of a registered construction worker in the event of their natural death.

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

The nominee or dependents of a registered construction worker who has passed away due to natural causes.

### What is the financial assistance provided under this scheme?

The nominee or dependents are eligible for [?]2,00,000 under the MUHAFIZ scheme (PMJJBY & PMSBY).

### How can I register as a Building/Construction Worker?

To register, the worker must be a resident of Jammu & Kashmir, between 18 and 60 years, and must have worked in construction for at least 90 days in the past 12 months.

### Is there an age limit to register as a construction worker?

Yes, the worker must be between 18 and 60 years old.

### How often should the registration be renewed?

The registration should be renewed annually.

### Can a non-registered construction worker avail the benefits of this scheme?

No, only registered construction workers or their dependents are eligible for the financial assistance under this scheme.

### Is the nominee required to submit the original documents?

Yes, the original Death Certificate of the deceased worker must be submitted, along with other attested copies.

### What happens if the deceased worker did not have a bank account?

If the deceased worker did not have a bank account, the nominee must provide the bank details of their own account.
