## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial support and encourage meritorious students pursuing an M.Sc. in Mathematics, thereby assisting them and reducing their financial burden. The assistance of ₹20,000/- per year is provided to eligible students.

## Detailed Description

The “Dr. V.N. Mathur Award (M.Sc. Mathematics)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial support and encourage meritorious students pursuing an M.Sc. in Mathematics, thereby assisting them and reducing their financial burden.
<br>

## Benefits

**Award Amount:** ₹20,000/- per year.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in an M.Sc. Mathematics course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The financial assistance is awarded for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the selected students receive the payments.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

Students enrolled in the M.Sc. Mathematics course at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar are eligible.
<br>

### What is the financial assistance provided under the scheme?

Each selected student receives ₹20,000/- per year.
<br>

### For how long is the scholarship awarded?

The scholarship is awarded for a duration of one year only.
<br>

### How can students apply for the scholarship?

Students are invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form issued by the university.
<br>

### Who evaluates the applications?

A committee constituted by the Vice Chancellor evaluates the applications based on defined criteria.
<br>

### Who grants the final approval for the scholarship?

The Vice Chancellor grants approval for the selected students based on the committee’s recommendations
<br>

### How are the scholarship funds disbursed?

After approval by the Vice Chancellor, the scholarship amount is paid directly to the selected students.
