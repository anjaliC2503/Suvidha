## Brief Description

The "Scheme for Enhancement of Technical Competence and Manpower" under the Gujarat Industrial Policy aims to bridge the industry’s skill gap, boost employment, and align workforce training with evolving technology and business needs.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme for Enhancement of Technical Competence and Manpower&quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to bridge the industry’s skill gap, boost employment, and align workforce training with evolving technology and business needs.
The component &quot;Scheme of Financial Assistance for Tuition Fees for Enhancement of Skill&quot; aims to support individuals in skill development by providing financial aid for training programs. It ensures access to industry-relevant training, enhancing employability and career growth opportunities.

## Benefits

> **Financial Assistance:**


- Up to ₹15,000 for training programs of 121 hours or more.
- Up to ₹10,000 for training programs of 61 to 120 hours.
- Up to ₹5,000 for training programs of 40 to 60 hours.

> **Full Fee Coverage:**


- 100% assistance for BPL, Physically Handicapped, SC, ST, SEBC, and Women candidates.
- 75% assistance for other eligible candidates.

> **Additional Assistance:**


  - **Awareness Programs &amp; Seminars:** A maximum support of ₹1,00,000/- per training center per year will be provided for awareness programs and seminars.

  - **Other Expenditures:** SLEC/IC/CED can approve the expenditure on any study, renovation/refurbishing of existing skill centers/institute, remuneration to third party evaluation of scheme, monitoring/audit expenses, assessment agency, etc.

  - **Trainee Transportation Reimbursement:** Trainees who complete skill upgradation training under any scheme will be eligible for reimbursement of Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation (GSRTC) bus passes for travel between their residence and training center as per the following rates:

   1. **For BPL candidates, Physically Handicapped, SC, ST, SEBC, and Women:** 100%   1. **For Other candidates:** 75%

## Eligibility

- The training institute must have at least 5 years of experience in skill development or relevant industry.
  - The training program must be industry-responsive and recognized by skill development bodies.
  - The first training batch must commence within 3 months of the sanction order.
  - Minimum 10 trainees per batch must complete the training and certification process.
  - Candidates must not have availed of similar government assistance for the same course.
  - The training duration should not exceed 6 months unless specified by authorities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Scheme for Enhancement of Technical Competence and Manpower"?

The "Scheme for Enhancement of Technical Competence and Manpower" is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, it aims to bridge the industry's skill gap, boost employment, and align workforce training with evolving technology and business needs.

### What is the objective of the "Scheme of Financial Assistance for Tuition Fees for Enhancement of Skill” component?

The component "Scheme of Financial Assistance for Tuition Fees for Enhancement of Skill" aims to support individuals in skill development by providing financial aid for training programs. The scheme ensures access to industry-relevant training, enhancing employability and career growth opportunities.

### What is the maximum financial assistance provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides up to [?]15,000/- as financial assistance, depending on the duration of the training program.

### Is it necessary for the candidate to pay the full training fee before receiving reimbursement?

Yes, the candidate must initially pay the full fee, which will be reimbursed as per the scheme guidelines after successful training and placement.

### What percentage of the training fee is reimbursed under the scheme?

The scheme covers 100% of the fee for BPL, Physically Handicapped, SC, ST, SEBC, and Women candidates, while other candidates receive 75% reimbursement.

### What happens if a candidate does not complete the training program?

If a candidate fails to complete the training, they will not be eligible for fee reimbursement under the scheme.

### Is there a deadline for claiming reimbursement after completing the training?

Yes, the candidate must claim the reimbursement within six months from the date of training completion; otherwise, the claim will be rejected.

### How is the candidate’s placement verified for reimbursement?

Placement verification is done through the submission of an appointment letter and a bank statement showing salary credited for a minimum of three months.

### Can a candidate receive financial assistance for training in any industry?

Yes, as long as the training is industry-responsive and aligns with the skill development objectives set by the scheme.

### Yes, as long as the training is industry-responsive and aligns with the skill development objectives set by the scheme.

No, only training programs conducted within India by approved institutions are covered under the scheme.

### Are there penalties for training institutes that fail to meet placement targets?

Yes, training institutes that do not meet the placement requirement of 75% may lose their performance bank guarantee.

### Can a candidate apply for multiple training programs under this scheme?

No, a candidate is eligible for financial assistance for only one training course under this scheme.
