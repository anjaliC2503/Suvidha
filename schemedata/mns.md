## Brief Description

The “Micro Nutrient Spray” scheme provides a 50% subsidy or ₹70/- per hectare to eligible farmers, with a focus on ensuring benefits to SC/ST and women farmers.

## Detailed Description

The “Micro Nutrient Spray” scheme by the Agriculture-Farmers Welfare Department, Tamil Nadu, is aimed at helping farmers improve crop nutrition by providing a subsidy for micro-nutrient sprays. The scheme offers a subsidy of 50% or ₹70/- per hectare, whichever is less. All farmers are eligible to apply, with a target of 24% of the benefits flowing to SC/ST farmers and 20% to women farmers or women groups.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** 50% subsidy or ₹70/- per hectare for micro-nutrient spray.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary should be a Farmer.
  - The beneficiary should be a resident of Tamil Nadu.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Collect the application form from the nearest Agricultural Office.
**Step 2:** Fill out the application form with the required details and attach mandatory documents (self-attested, if required). 
**Step 3:** Submit the form to the concerned office:
> **Village Level:** Submit the application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer.

> **Block Level:** Submit to the Agricultural Officer or Deputy Agricultural Officer.

> **District Level:** Submit to the Joint Director of Agriculture.

- Proof of identity (Aadhar card, Voter ID)

  - Caste certificate (for SC/ST applicants).
  - Any other documents (if required).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Micro Nutrient Spray scheme?

The "Micro Nutrient Spray" scheme provides financial assistance to farmers by offering a subsidy for applying micro-nutrient spray to their crops.

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

All farmers are eligible for the scheme. There is also a focus on ensuring 24% of the benefits go to SC/ST farmers and 20% to women farmers or women groups.

### How much subsidy is offered under the scheme?

The scheme provides a subsidy of 50% or [?]70/- per hectare, whichever is less.

### How can a farmer apply for the scheme?

Farmers can apply by submitting their application to the Assistant Agricultural Officer at the village level, or to other agricultural officers at the block or district level.

### Are there any special provisions for certain communities?

Yes, 24% of the benefits are allocated for SC/ST farmers, and 20% for women farmers or women groups.

### What documents are required to apply for the scheme?

Farmers need to provide proof of identity, land ownership documents, a caste certificate (if applicable), proof of farming activity, and the completed application form.

### Is there any age or income limit for this scheme?

There is no specific age or income limit for eligibility under this scheme.

### Can women farmers benefit from the scheme?

Yes, women farmers and women groups are eligible, with 20% of the benefits reserved for them.

### How will the subsidy be provided to the farmers?

The subsidy is provided based on the area covered by micro-nutrient spray and upon submission of the required documents.

### Where can farmers get the application form?

Application forms can be obtained from the Assistant Agricultural Officer or the block and district-level agricultural offices.

### Can farmers apply online for the scheme?

Currently, applications need to be submitted in person to the designated agricultural officers. There is no online application process available.
