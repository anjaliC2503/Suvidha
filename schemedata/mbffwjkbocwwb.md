## Brief Description

The "Maternity Benefit for Female Workers" scheme by the JKBOCW Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, is a welfare program that provides maternity benefits to eligible registered female workers up to two times in their lifetime.

## Detailed Description

The "Maternity Benefit for Female Workers" scheme by the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Jammu & Kashmir, provides financial assistance to registered female workers during maternity. Under this scheme, female beneficiaries are eligible to receive ₹5,000 (for up to two children), provided they have been engaged in building or construction work for at least 90 days in the preceding 12 months.

## Benefits

Under the scheme, maternity benefits for female workers amount to ₹5,000 for up to two children.
Note: The benefit is allowed only twice in the beneficiary's lifetime.
<br>

## Eligibility

**For Registration as a Building/ Construction Worker:**

1. The applicant should be a resident of Jammu & Kashmir.
1. The applicant should be a Building/ Construction Worker.
1. The applicant should be between 18 and 60 years.
1. The applicant should have engaged in any building or other construction work for at least 90 days during the preceding 12 months.
1. The applicant should be registration is renewed annually.

**For the Application of the Welfare Scheme:**

1. The applicant should be a registered female member Under the Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of Jammu & Kashmir.
1. The applicant should be pregnant.
1. The worker's membership with the Welfare Board must be active at the time of application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Maternity Benefit Scheme for Female Workers?

The Maternity Benefit Scheme provides financial assistance to eligible registered female construction workers during maternity. It can be availed up to two times in a worker's lifetime.

### Is there a minimum service requirement to register as a beneficiary?

Yes, the building worker must have completed 90 days of service as a building worker in the preceding 12 months.

### Who can apply for the welfare scheme?

Any registered worker of the Board can apply for the welfare scheme.

### How many times can a worker avail the Maternity Benefit?

A registered female worker can avail the Maternity Benefit up to two times in her lifetime.

### What is the amount provided under the Maternity Benefit Scheme?

The financial benefit amount may vary based on state policies. You can check the exact benefit amount on the JKBOCWWB website or contact the board.

### How do I register as a construction worker under JKBOCWWB?

To register, visit the official JKBOCWWB website, apply through the "Labour Registration" section, and follow the steps to fill out your details, upload documents, and submit the form online.

### What documents are required for registration as a construction worker?

Documents like Aadhaar Card, proof of residence, bank passbook, passport-size photograph, age certificate, and employment certificate are needed for registration.

### Can I apply for multiple welfare schemes under JKBOCWWB?

Yes, registered construction workers can apply for multiple welfare schemes, including maternity benefits, medical assistance, and education assistance, as long as they meet the eligibility criteria.

### How can I apply for welfare schemes under JKBOCWWB?

Log in to your account on the JKBOCWWB website, click on "Scheme Registration," select the scheme you wish to apply for, fill in the required information, upload documents, and submit your application.
