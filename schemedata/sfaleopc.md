## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage intellectual and cultural activities by providing financial support to institutions conducting seminars, workshops, symposiums, and literary programs.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide grants to registered institutions for organizing events such as seminars, dialogues, conventions, workshops, and literary festivals. The scheme is implemented by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy, and financial assistance is disbursed based on actual expenses, subject to a maximum limit. Institutions must comply with language usage norms, use appropriate branding and logos, and submit reports and financial documentation after event completion. Events can be state-level, national, or international, and must involve recognized speakers, literary scholars, and culturally relevant topics.
<br>

<br>

## Benefits

> **Funding Assistance for Institutions:**  



    -  Up to ₹50,000/- or 75% of the actual expenditure, whichever is lower, is provided for organizing approved literary or educational events.


> **Honorarium for Speakers:** 



  - An honorarium of up to ₹2,000/- per speaker can be paid to invited speakers participating in the event.


> **Note:** 



  - Any honorarium amount exceeding ₹2,000/- will not be reimbursed by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy and must be borne by the organizing institution.
  - Events must be reported with bills, photos, invitation cards, and video recordings.


<br>

## Eligibility

1. A registered literary or educational institution shall be eligible.
   1. Only 2 programs can organize per year by the institution.
   1. The program must be related to literature, education, or cultural discourse.
   1. The institution must use the Gujarati language in all materials (alongside any other language).
   1. The event must clearly acknowledge Gujarat Sahitya Academy through logos and name placement.
   1. The program must involve a minimum 5 speakers.
   1. All payments above ₹5,000/- must be made through banking channels only (RTGS/NEFT/Cheque).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences"?

The "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences" aims to promote literary, cultural, and intellectual engagement by supporting institutions financially in conducting programs such as seminars, conferences, workshops, and symposiums.
<br>

<br>

### Who can apply under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences"?

Only registered literary and educational institutions are eligible to apply under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences" to receive grants for organizing eligible events.
<br>

<br>

### Can an individual apply for financial support under this scheme?

No, individuals are not eligible to apply, as the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences" is exclusively intended for recognized institutions, not for personal events or individual organizers.
<br>

<br>

### What types of programs are eligible under this scheme?

Programs such as literary seminars, educational workshops, academic conferences, symposiums, and cultural dialogues are considered eligible under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences."
<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum amount of financial assistance provided under the scheme?

Under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences", institutions may receive financial support of up to ₹50,000/- or 75% of the actual expenditure incurred—whichever amount is lower.
<br>

<br>

### Is prior approval from Gujarat Sahitya Academy necessary before conducting the program?

Yes, institutions must obtain prior approval from the Gujarat Sahitya Academy before organizing the event in order to be eligible for financial assistance under the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### How many programs can an institution organize under the scheme in one year?

An institution may organize a maximum of two programs in a single financial year under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences."
<br>

<br>

### What is the maximum honorarium that can be paid to a speaker under this scheme?

As per the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences", the honorarium payable to each speaker must not exceed ₹2,000/-, and any excess will not be reimbursed by the Academy.
<br>

<br>

### How should financial transactions and payments be made under the scheme?

All financial transactions over ₹5,000/- must be made through RTGS, NEFT, cheque, or other approved banking methods, and cash payments are not permitted for major expenses.
<br>

<br>

### Are institutions required to submit documentation after the event?

Yes, institutions must submit a comprehensive report, photographs, video documentation, original bills, vouchers, and a utilization certificate after the completion of the event to claim the approved grant.
<br>

<br>

### Will the Academy reimburse the costs for non-speaker participants or attendees?

No, the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences" does not cover any expenditure incurred for non-speaking participants or audience members.
<br>

<br>

### How can an institution apply for the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences"?

To apply under the "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Literary/Educational Institutions for Organizing Programs/Conferences", an institution must submit the online [application form](https://gujaratsahityaacademy.com/user/yojana/add/Njc1MjQxNQ==).
