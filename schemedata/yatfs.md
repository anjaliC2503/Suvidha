## Brief Description

The scheme aims to encourage youth in arts by providing a monthly fellowship and professional training through government institutions.

## Detailed Description

The “Youth Art Training Fellowship Scheme" has been launched by the Department of Culture, Government of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme aims to promote interest and skill development among youth in various art forms such as music, dance, theatre, painting, and crafts. Under this scheme, monthly training fellowships are provided to selected young artists to support their artistic training. 
<br>

## Benefits

- Monthly training fellowship of ₹10,000 per selected participant.
- Professional art training at [government-recognized art and music institutions (Page No. 3)](https://www.culturemp.in/pdfs/fellowship-2023.pdf).- Certification after successful completion of training.
- Opportunity to receive structured guidance in classical, tribal, and folk art forms.
- The fellowship is valid only for the approved training period of 3 months.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be interested in training in one art discipline such as singing, instrumental music, dance, theatre, painting, or crafts.
1. The applicant should be between 18 and 35 years of age.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-01:** Download the prescribed format of the application form from the [official website](https://www.mptribalmuseum.com/). 
**Step-02:** Fill in all the mandatory fields in the application form and attach copies of all the required documents (self-attested, if necessary).
**Step-03:** Email or post the completed application in the prescribed format to:
Post: Director, Madhya Pradesh Tribal Museum, Shyamla Hills, Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh 462002. India  
E-mail: mptmfellowship@gmail.com
***NOTE:***

- Applications must be submitted within the deadline mentioned in the advertisement.
- Late applications will not be accepted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who launched this scheme?

The scheme was launched by the Department of Culture, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
<br>

### Who implements the scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Madhya Pradesh Tribal Museum.
<br>

### What is the age limit to apply?

Applicants must be between 18 and 35 years of age.
<br>

### What is the duration of the fellowship?

The fellowship is provided for a duration of three months.
<br>

### How much fellowship amount is provided?

A fellowship amount of ₹10,000 per month is provided to selected candidates.
<br>

### How many youth are selected under the scheme?

Approximately 1,000 youth artists are selected across the state.
<br>

### How many candidates are selected from each district?

Around 20 candidates are selected from each district.
<br>

### What art disciplines are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers music, dance, theatre, painting, and crafts.
<br>

### How is the selection done?

Selection is conducted through an examination followed by an interview.
<br>

### Who conducts the selection process?

The selection process is conducted by a committee constituted by the Directorate of Culture.
<br>

### Is certification provided after training?

Yes, participants receive a certificate upon successful completion of training.
<br>

### Can applications be submitted offline?

Yes, applications can be submitted via post or email.
