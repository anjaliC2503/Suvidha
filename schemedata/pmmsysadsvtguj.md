## Brief Description

The scheme aims to strengthen fish storage infrastructure for hygienic preservation. Through this scheme, beneficiaries receive financial assistance for the support for acquisition of deep-sea fishing vessels for traditional fishermen.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)” with the component of "Support for Acquisition of Deep Sea Fishing Vessels for Traditional Fishermen", introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to modernise cold chain infrastructure to preserve fish health, uplift fishermen, and enhance value chain efficiency. Through this scheme, financial support is provided to fishermen and related beneficiaries for constructing ice plants or storage units with a minimum capacity of 30 tonnes, ensuring hygienic fish storage and better returns. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries of the State Governments, with 60% contribution from the Government of India and 40% contribution from the State Government.
<br>

> **Objective of the scheme**


- Marine production in the Gujarat state is one of the leading in the country. The state lags behind in exports due to non-norms. So cold storage/ice plant modernization is necessary to keep the fish in healthy condition.
- Insulated vehicle, three wheeler with ice box including e-rickshaw, and live fish vending center to deliver the fish in hygienic condition to the consumer, so that the consumer (customer) can get fresh and clean fish from the local area and get good returns to the sellers.
- Around 27,000 small and large boats are operating in the state. To strengthen the safety and security of the fishermen and increase the income of the boat owner, replacement of the existing boats is necessary to increase the sea growth of the existing boats.

<br>

## Benefits

- Unit cost is ₹1,20,00,000/-. 40 percent assistance for general category i.e. assistance up to ₹48,00,000/-
- And for women and ST/SC category beneficiaries 60 percent assistance i.e. assistance up to ₹72,00,000.
- The benefit will be available as per the target (category) allocated by the Government of India.

<br>

> **Remarks:** 60% contribution from Government of India and 40% contribution from State Governments is paid.

> **Minimum Time Limit (in Years) for Reclaiming Benefits:** Once in a lifetime.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a native of Gujarat state.
1. The applicant should have a fishing boat registered under Real Craft software.
1. The applicant should hold a fishing license.
1. The applicant should complete the online registration on the [iKhedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/).1. The applicant should obtain in-principle approval before purchase of the floor plan.
1. The applicant should have his/her own bank account.

> **Preference/Reservation/Relaxation:**



  - Women, SC, and ST beneficiaries are eligible for 60% assistance of the unit cost.
  - General category beneficiaries are eligible for 40% assistance of the unit cost.


<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on 'Login' from the top right-hand side, then click on "Beneficiary Registration" and select any one from the 'Farmer' or 'Institutional' or 'Except For the Farmer' as the Beneficiary Type.
**Step 3:** Enter the 'Company Name' and Address if you have selected 'Institutional' or 'Except For the Farmer' as the Beneficiary Type. And for all select the District, Taluka, and Village, choose 'what type of account holder you are?', enter your Account Number and Name, solve the Captcha, and click 'Save and Next'.
**Step 4:** Enter your Mobile Number and E-mail address, click 'Send OTP', enter the received OTP, create and confirm a Password, solve the Captcha, and click 'Submit'.
<br>

> **Login Process:** 

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut Portal](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/).
**Step 2:** Click on 'Login' from the top right-hand side of the homepage. On the home page, click on ‘Schemes’ and then click on ‘Agricultural Schemes’.
**Step 3:** Now select the scheme, click on the ‘Apply’ button, and an application page will open.
**Step 4:** Click on the "Apply New" button and submit a new application.
**Step 5:** Click on the "Update Application" button to add corrections to the application.
**Step 6:** Once the application is done, confirm it.
**Step 7:** Take a printout of the confirmed application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for support to acquire a deep sea fishing vessel?

The applicant should be a native of Gujarat state, have a registered fishing boat under Real Craft software, and possess a valid fishing license.
<br>

### Is there any requirement for bank details before applying?

Yes, the beneficiary must have their own bank account to receive the assistance.
<br>

### Where should I register to apply for this benefit?

You must do online registration on the iKhedut portal at https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in.
<br>

### Do I need approval before purchasing the vessel?

Yes, in-principle approval must be obtained before purchasing the floor plan for the vessel.
<br>

### Is the benefit amount fixed or does it depend on government targets?

The benefit will be available as per the target (category) allocated by the Government of India.
<br>

### How is the contribution shared between the Centre and State Government?

60% contribution is provided by the Government of India and 40% by the State Government.
<br>

### Can a beneficiary avail this benefit more than once?

No, the benefit can only be availed once in a lifetime.
<br>

### Is there a specific time limit to reclaim this benefit?

Yes, there is a minimum time limit of 1 year to reclaim the benefit.
<br>

### How many boats are currently operating in the state?

Around 27,000 small and large boats are operating in the state.
<br>

### Why is support for acquiring deep sea fishing vessels necessary?

It helps strengthen the safety and security of fishermen and increases the income of boat owners.
<br>

### Does the scheme also aim to modernize fishing practices?

Yes, it focuses on modernization to enhance safety, efficiency, and income of traditional fishermen.
<br>

### What is the main goal of providing assistance for deep sea vessels?

The main goal is to provide fishermen with safer, modern vessels that increase income and strengthen marine production.
