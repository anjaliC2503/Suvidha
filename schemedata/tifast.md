## Brief Description

The scheme "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote love and awareness for nature among youth by organizing trekking programs in forest areas for selected Scheduled Tribes young men and women.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to instill a deep love for nature, pristine landscapes, wild animals and birds, trees, mountains, springs, and ravines among the Scheduled Tribes youth of the state by directly engaging them in a forest trekking experience. Under this initiative, a forest area rotation program is conducted for 100 selected young men and women, giving them immersive exposure to natural surroundings and biodiversity.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Nature Immersion Experience:** The selected beneficiaries will have the opportunity to participate in a guided forest trekking program designed to foster a love for nature and environmental awareness.  - **Exposure to Biodiversity:** Participants will get firsthand experience of pristine natural sites, including forests, wildlife, birds, trees, springs, mountains, and ravines.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribes (ST) category.
  - The applicant should be between 15 and 35 years of age.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should download the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the office of the District Youth Development Officer.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main purpose of the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme?

The main purpose of the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme is to provide selected young men and women from the Scheduled Tribes (ST) category with direct exposure to the beauty of nature and forest ecosystems, thereby cultivating in them a lasting appreciation for wildlife, biodiversity, and natural landscapes.
<br>

### Which department is responsible for implementing the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme?

The "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme is implemented and managed by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department of the Government of Gujarat, which oversees the selection and organization of the trekking programs.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme?

Only applicants who belong to the Scheduled Tribes (ST) category, are residents of Gujarat, and fall within the youth age group are eligible to apply under the "Trekking in Forest Area" scheme.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable to individuals from other social categories or states?

No, the scheme is specifically intended for Scheduled Tribes youth residing in Gujarat, and individuals from other social categories or states are not eligible to benefit from the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme.
<br>

### What kind of benefits are offered under the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme?

Under this scheme, selected participants are offered a structured and guided trekking program in designated forest areas, allowing them to experience natural elements such as wild animals, birds, trees, springs, and mountain terrain in a safe and educational environment.
<br>

### Is any monetary assistance given to participants of this scheme?

No direct monetary assistance is provided to the participants; instead, the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme offers its benefits in the form of an organized service—namely, a government-sponsored trekking experience.
<br>

### How many participants are selected for each cycle of the trekking program?

A total of 100 young men and women are selected for each cycle or batch of the trekking program conducted under the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme.
<br>

### Is there any application fee to participate in the scheme?

There is no fee required to apply for the scheme, as the application form for the "Trekking in Forest Area" scheme is available free of cost to all eligible applicants.
<br>

### What are the documents required to apply for the scheme?

Applicants must submit a duly filled application form along with a valid Aadhaar Card while applying for the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme.
<br>

### How can one access the application form for the scheme?

The application form for the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme can be downloaded from the scheme guidelines section.
<br>

### Is the scheme available for both male and female candidates?

Yes, the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme is open to all genders, allowing both young men and women from the ST category to participate equally in the trekking program.
<br>

### Are there any educational or professional qualifications required to apply?

There are no specific educational or professional qualifications required to apply for the "Trekking in Forest Area (Scheduled Tribes)" scheme, as it is open to all ST youth regardless of their academic or employment background.
<br>
