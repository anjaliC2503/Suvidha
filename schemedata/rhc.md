## Brief Description

It aims to register cultivators who grow medicinal and aromatic plants, make the process of using their cultivated herbs easier, and stop the illegal collection of herbs from forests.

## Detailed Description

The “Registration of Herb Cultivators” is implemented by the Herbal Research and Development Institute (HRDI), mandal gopeshwar chamoli under the Department of Horticulture, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to register cultivators who grow medicinal and aromatic plants, make the process of using their cultivated herbs easier, and stop the illegal collection of herbs from forests.
<br>

## Benefits

Registration of farmers cultivating medicinal and aromatic plants, simplification of the extraction process for cultivated herbs, and prevention of illegal exploitation of herbal resources from forest areas.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
   1. The applicant must be a cultivator of medicinal and aromatic plants.
   1. The applicant must either have their own resources on their measured land or be a cultivator encouraged by the Herbal Research and Development Institute, or a cultivator beneficiary supported by any other organization, institute, department, or voluntary organization.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

After physical verification by the Institute’s Surveyor Assistant, the actual cultivated area is registered. The Surveyor Assistant prepares the registration form in the prescribed format, attaches the applicant’s Aadhaar card and the land’s Khasra details, and sends it to the Director of the Herbal Research and Development Institute, mandal gopeshwar chamoli. After obtaining the Director’s approval, the concerned District Coordinator completes the registration.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for registration?

Farmers cultivating medicinal and aromatic plants on their own land or those encouraged by HRDI or other organizations can apply.
<br>

### Do farmers need to own land to apply?

Yes, the farmer must have measured land, or they must be a beneficiary encouraged by HRDI or any other recognized organization or department.
<br>

### Who prepares the registration form?

The Surveyor Assistant prepares the registration form in the prescribed format after verifying the cultivation area.
<br>

### Where is the registration form submitted?

The form, along with required documents, is sent to the Director of HRDI.
<br>

### Is physical verification required for registration?

Yes, the Institute’s Surveyor Assistant conducts physical verification of the cultivated area.
<br>

### Who prepares the registration form?

The Surveyor Assistant prepares the registration form in the prescribed format after verifying the cultivation area.
<br>

### Who approves the registration?

The Director of the Herbal Research and Development Institute approves the registration.
<br>

### Who completes the final registration?

After approval, the concerned District Coordinator completes the registration process.
