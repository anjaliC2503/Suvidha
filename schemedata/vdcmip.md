## Brief Description

The programme aims to harness the energy and innovative ideas of the youth pursuing graduation and post-graduation of India, which will be implemented in policy and decision making, to make Delhi a smart, clean, caring and prosperous metropolis.

## Detailed Description

The “Viksit Delhi CM Internship Programme” is a scheme of the Delhi Government to engage young interns pursuing graduation/post-graduation from various fields. The programme aims to harness the energy and innovative ideas of the youth pursuing graduation and post-graduation in India, which will be implemented in policy and decision-making, to make Delhi a smart, clean, caring and prosperous metropolis. The summary of the scheme is as following:-

  - This internship scheme is for 89 days (3 months approx.) referred to as “Term”.
  - The time period of this scheme starts on 30 Jun, 2025 and ends on 28 September, 2025.
  - Initially screened candidates are required to bring original certificates and their candidature/selection is subject to verification of certificate.
  - Reservation for SC, ST, OBC, EWS &amp; PHYSICALLY DISABLED applicable as per Delhi and Central Government rules.

***Boot Camp:***
300 candidates shall be selected through prelim/online screening test. These 300 candidates shall be allowed to attend boot camp wherein 150 candidates shall be shortlisted based on performance in essay writing during boot camp.

  - Boot camp shall be held on 23rd July 2025.
  - Candidates screened through online exam mode will be given a topic for essay writing.
  - 150 interns shall be finalized on the basis of performance in essay writing.
  - Candidates are required to bring their original documents for verification.

**Note:** The venue and timing of boot camp will be announced shortly on the [website](https://viksitdelhiyuva.org/). It is advised to check the website frequently for details.
<br>

## Benefits

1. Interns will be eligible for 5 days&#39; leave during the entire duration of the internship.
   1. Each intern shall be paid an amount of ₹20,000/- per month, i.e. ₹60,000/- for a complete term, subject to attendance.
   1. At the end of the internship, interns will submit their report to the HODs of the concerned department.
   1. Interns who successfully complete the programme will receive a certificate signed by the concerned Head of the Department/Authorised signatory.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be pursuing a graduation or post-graduation course.
1. The applicant must be enrolled in the electoral roll of Delhi or have any residential proof in Delhi.
1. The applicant must have scored 70% or above marks in the Class 12th examination.
1. The applicant must be between 18 and 30 years of age as on the date of application.

**Reservation/Relaxation:** 

- Reservation for Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Other Backward Class (OBC), Economically Weaker Section (EWS), and Physically Disabled candidates is applicable as per Delhi and Central Government rules.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Visit to [official website](https://viksitdelhiyuva.org/about.php) of Viksit Delhi Yuva. 
**Step 2:** On the top-right corner of the page, click on ‘Registration'.
**Step 3:** A new [registration form](https://viksitdelhiyuva.org/registration_form.php) page will appear
**Step 4:** Begin by clicking on the ‘Take Your Selfie’ option and uploading your photograph.
**Step 5:** Fill in the required personal details, i.e., Your Full Name, Email ID, Password & Confirm Password, Building/House Number, Locality, District Name, Pincode, and Aadhaar Number.
**Step 6:** Upload the following documents: Aadhaar Card, Address Proof, Category Certificate (if applicable), Proof of Date of Birth, and 12th Class Certificate.
**Step 7:** Enter your 12th Class Percentage and Date of Birth.
**Step 8:** Provide your Mobile Number, click Send OTP, and enter the OTP received for verification.
**Step 9:** Tick the declaration box confirming the correctness of the details and uploaded documents.
**Step 10:** Click the Submit button to complete your application.
> **After Registration:** 


- 300 candidates shall be selected through preliminary/online screening test. These 300 candidates shall be allowed to attend boot camp, wherein 150 candidates shall be shortlisted based on performance in essay writing during boot camp.
- Initially screened candidates are required to bring original certificates, and their candidature/selection is subject to verification of certificates.
- The venue and timing of boot camp will be announced shortly on the website. It is advised to check the website frequently for details.
- Selected interns will start joining on August 01, 2025. They will be given a 3-day time frame to join the office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this scheme available for all Indian citizens?

No, this scheme is for individuals pursuing graduation/post-graduation who are enrolled in the electoral roll of Delhi or have residential proof of Delhi.
<br>

### What is the primary goal of this internship programme?

The programme aims to engage young interns to harness their energy and innovative ideas, which will be implemented in policy and decision-making to make Delhi a smart, clean, caring, and prosperous metropolis.
<br>

### When does the internship period begin and end?

The internship period starts on June 30, 2025, and ends on September 28, 2025, lasting for 89 days (approximately 3 months).
<br>

### How many candidates will be selected for the internship?

Initially, 300 candidates will be selected through a prelim/online screening test, and then 150 candidates will be finalised after the boot camp.
<br>

### What is the first step in the selection process?

The first step is a prelim/online screening test for candidates.
<br>

### What happens after the prelim/online screening test?

The 300 candidates selected through the prelim/online screening test will be allowed to attend a boot camp.
<br>

### When is the boot camp scheduled to be held?

The boot camp will be held on July 23, 2025.
<br>

### What should I bring to the boot camp?

Initially screened candidates are required to bring their original certificates for verification.
<br>

### How will the final 150 candidates be selected during the boot camp?

The final 150 interns will be finalised based on their performance in essay writing during the boot camp.
<br>

### When will the preliminary screening results be declared?

The preliminary/screening results will be declared on July 21, 2025.
<br>

### What is the age limit for applicants to this internship programme?

Candidates must be in the age range of 18 and 30 years as of the date of application.
<br>

### What documents are required to be uploaded during registration?

A copy of your student ID card, residence proof, 12th marks certificate, and 10th certificate for verification of age are to be uploaded during registration.
<br>

### When does the registration for the scheme begin and end?

Registration will start on June 30, 2025, and end on July 10, 2025, at 11:59 P.M. sharp.

### Can I apply multiple times for this internship?

No, a candidate can apply/register only one time.
<br>

### Is it important to stay logged in until the exam is complete?

Yes, do not log out of the portal before completing the exam.
<br>

### Will interns receive a certificate upon successful completion of the programme?

Yes, interns who successfully complete the programme will receive a certificate signed by the concerned Head of the Department/Authorised signatory.
<br>

### Is the use of AI tools allowed for the application or essay writing?

No, the use of AI is not allowed, and candidates are advised not to cut/copy and paste.
