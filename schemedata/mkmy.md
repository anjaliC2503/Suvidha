## Brief Description

The scheme aims to create necessary infrastructure by the distribution company for providing permanent pump connections to farmers or groups of farmers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Mukhyamantri Krushak Mitra Yojana” launched by the Energy Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to create infrastructure for permanent agricultural pump connections through the distribution company. The scheme provides grants and cost-sharing support to farmers and distribution companies for infrastructure development.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Cost Sharing Support:** Only 50% of the infrastructure development cost will be borne by the concerned farmer or group of farmers.

- **Government Grant:** 40% of the cost will be provided directly by the State Government to the power distribution company as a grant.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a landholding farmer/group-farmer.

<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The objective of this scheme is to create necessary infrastructure by the distribution company for providing permanent pump connection to the farmer/group of farmers
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Landholding farmers are eligible under this scheme.
<br>

### What percentage of cost is borne by the farmer?

The farmer or group of farmers has to bear 50% of the infrastructure development cost.
<br>

### How much cost will be provided by the government?

The State Government will provide 40% of the cost directly to the power distribution company as a grant.
<br>

### Who bears the remaining 10% of the cost?

The remaining 10% of the cost will be borne by the power distribution company.
<br>

### Who is the designated officer for the scheme?

The Distribution Center In-Charge is the designated officer for the scheme.
<br>

### What is the time limit for completing the work after payment?

The infrastructure work will be completed within six months after the farmer deposits the required amount.
<br>

### Is there any application fee for applying?

Yes, there is an application fee as per the rules of the distribution company.
<br>
