## Brief Description

Pratibha Kiran Scholarship has been launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. Through this scheme, scholarship will be provided every year to girl students living below the poverty line.

## Detailed Description

The scheme" Pratibha Kiran Scholarship", under the Higher Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, provides financial assistance to girls from urban areas to pursue higher education. The scholarship is awarded for a duration of 10 months. This scheme aims to encourage and promote education among girl students. Girls who have secured 60% or more marks in Class 12 are eligible to avail the benefits of this scheme.
<br>

## Benefits

- Assistance up to ₹5000/- is provided annually to the girl student.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant of the scheme should be a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant can be a resident of the urban area of MP state.
1. The candidate of the scheme should have passed class 12th with 60% or more then. 
1. The candidate who wants to apply for the scheme should belong below the poverty line. Financial assistance under the scheme will only be provided to BPL girl students.

## Application Process

### Online

**Application URL:** http://www.scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx

Step 1: Go to the[ portal ](http://www.scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/Index.aspx)and register as a new user. This is for SC/ST/OBC/General girl students.
Step 2: Click on New Registration for the Pratibha Kiran Scholarship and fill in all the required details.
Step 3: After registration, log in using your Registration ID at http://www.scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in/sLogin.aspx
 and apply for the scholarship.
