## Brief Description

The scheme aims to facilitate trade in products other than agricultural produce by providing commercial shops in Kisan Bazaars. The shops are allotted to eligible residents of Uttarakhand through public notices published in newspapers and displayed at public places.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Allotment of Shops in Kisan Bazaar” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Agricultural Production & Marketing Board under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to facilitate trade in products other than agricultural produce by providing commercial shops in Kisan Bazaars.
<br>

## Benefits

The scheme facilitates trade in products other than agricultural produce by allotting shops in the Kisan Bazaars to eligible residents of Uttarakhand.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

***Application Process:***

  - Upon receiving information regarding the allotment of shops in the Kisan Bazaar (through notices published in newspapers and displayed at public places), applicants can obtain the tender form from the committee office by paying the prescribed fee.
  - Complete the tender form and submit it in the tender box on or before the specified due date.

***Selection Process:***

  - The allotment of shops in the Kisan Bazaar is carried out by the Shop Allotment Committee.
  - Tenders are opened by the Shop Allotment Committee in the presence of the applicants/tenderers.
  - The committee invites bids from applicants, and selection is based on the highest tender amount.
  - The shop is allotted to the highest bidder.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

The general public who are residents of Uttarakhand are eligible to apply.
<br>

### What is the main objective of the scheme?

The scheme aims to facilitate trade in products other than agricultural produce in Kisan Bazaars.
<br>

### How will I know when shops are available for allotment?

Information is published through notices in newspapers and displayed at public places in the Kisan Bazaar area.
<br>

### How can I obtain the tender form?

The tender form can be obtained from the committee office by paying the prescribed fee once the notice for shop allotment is issued.
<br>

### How do I submit the tender form?

Fill in the required details and submit the completed tender form in the designated tender box on or before the specified due date.
<br>

### Who is responsible for allotting shops in Kisan Bazaars?

The Shop Allotment Committee is responsible for the allotment of shops.
<br>

### How are the shops allotted?

Shops are allotted to the applicant who offers the highest tender amount for the shop.
<br>

### Can anyone bid for a shop?

Yes, any eligible resident of Uttarakhand can participate, provided they submit a valid tender form and pay the prescribed fee.
<br>

### Is it necessary to trade only in non-agricultural products?

Yes, the shops under this scheme are intended for trading products other than agricultural produce.
<br>
