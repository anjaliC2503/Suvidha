## Brief Description

The scheme "Skill Development" by the Wadia Institute of Himalayan Geology, aims to impart practical training to postgraduate students in various Earth Science disciplines, including structural and evolutionary geology, geochemistry, geomorphology, glaciology, magnetostratigraphy, etc.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Skill Development" implemented by the Wadia Institute of Himalayan Geology aims to enhance the practical knowledge and research capabilities of students pursuing Earth Science and allied subjects. Through this scheme, specialised summer and winter training programmes are offered to postgraduate and integrated postgraduate students in disciplines such as geochemistry, sedimentology, tectonics, geomorphology, environmental geoscience, and paleontology.

## Benefits

- **Research Experience:** Provides opportunities for self-sponsored research work, allowing students to gain practical experience in Earth Sciences.  - **Seasonal Training:** Offers valuable summer and winter training programs, enhancing practical skills and knowledge in specialized geological disciplines.  - **Exposure to Diverse Fields:** Exposes students to a wide array of sub-disciplines within Earth Sciences, such as structural geology, geochemistry, glaciology, engineering geology, palaeontology, and environmental geology.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must possess a postgraduate degree in any discipline of Geosciences, Earth Sciences, or allied fields.
   1. The applicant may also be pursuing an integrated postgraduate degree course and should be in the fourth or fifth year of the curriculum.
   1. The applicant must be from fields such as geophysics, geochemistry, structural geology, sedimentology, paleontology, environmental geoscience, and related disciplines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the "Skill Development" program?

The main objective of the "Skill Development" program is to provide practical training and research exposure to postgraduate students in various branches of Earth sciences to enhance their academic and applied skills.
<br>

<br>

### Who implements the "Skill Development" program?

The "Skill Development" program is implemented by the Wadia Institute of Himalayan Geology (WIHG), under the Government of India.
<br>

<br>

### Which students are eligible to apply for the "Skill Development" program?

Students who are pursuing postgraduate degrees or are in the fourth or fifth year of integrated postgraduate programs in Earth sciences or related disciplines are eligible to apply for the program.
<br>

<br>

### What types of Earth science disciplines are covered under this program?

The program covers a wide range of Earth science disciplines including structural geology, geochemistry, sedimentology, geomorphology, tectonics, environmental geology, economic geology, paleontology, and related fields.
<br>

<br>

### Is there a specific degree requirement for eligibility under the "Skill Development" program?

Yes, applicants must be pursuing or should have completed a postgraduate degree in any Earth science, geophysics, geology, or allied field.
<br>

<br>

### What kind of training is provided under the "Skill Development" program?

The program provides hands-on, field-based and laboratory-based training along with participation in institution-led research activities during the training period.
<br>

<br>

### Does the "Skill Development" program include seasonal training opportunities?

Yes, the program offers both summer and winter training sessions to accommodate the academic calendars of different institutions.
<br>

<br>

### How can students apply for the "Skill Development" program?

Students can apply through the official [website ](https://www.wihg.res.in/)of the Wadia Institute of Himalayan Geology where information regarding the program and application instructions are regularly updated.
<br>

<br>

### Is there a selection process for the "Skill Development" program?

Yes, the selection process typically involves screening of applications based on academic background, relevance of the research interest, and available seats for the training period.
<br>

<br>

### What are the long-term benefits of attending the "Skill Development" program?

Participants benefit by acquiring advanced field and laboratory skills, networking with Earth science professionals, and enhancing their academic and research profiles, which helps in future higher education or employment opportunities.
