## Brief Description

The scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)" aims to enhance agricultural productivity by financially supporting co-operative irrigation societies in tribal areas through the installation of water distribution infrastructure.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase farm productivity by offering financial support to irrigation co-operative societies formed by tribal area farmers. The scheme focuses on forming such societies and facilitating water distribution by setting up electric pumps and motors on wells and agar creeks. 
<br>

## Benefits

- **Monetary Support:** Financial help is provided for the installation of electric motors and pumps on wells and agar creeks to enhance irrigation facilities.
<br>

## Eligibility

- Cooperative irrigation societies formed by small and marginal farmers, or any other farmers, are eligible under this scheme.
  - The society should be located within the state of Gujarat.
  - The Scheme is applicable to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should prepare the application on plain paper.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the District Registrar's Office.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.

## Documents Required

1. Proof of Co-operative Society Registration.
1. Proof of ST Category (Caste Certificate).
1. Address Proof of the Society (Electricity Bill, Rent Agreement, etc.).
1. Identity Proof of Applicant Members (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID).
1. Land Ownership or Usage Proof for Well/Agar Creek Installation.
1. Project Proposal Detailing the Irrigation Setup.
1. Bank Account Details of the Society.
1. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Related Authorities (if applicable).
1. Resolution Copy of Society for Installation of Irrigation Setup.
1. Electricity Connection Certificate or Application Copy (if applicable).
1. Any other documents as required.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)"?

The objective of the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)" is to enhance agricultural productivity in tribal regions by supporting the formation and functioning of irrigation co-operative societies through the provision of financial assistance for the installation of electric pumps and motors on wells and agar creeks.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)"?

The scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)" is open exclusively to registered co-operative societies formed by farmers belonging to Scheduled Tribe (ST) communities who are residing in the tribal areas of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of financial support is provided under the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)"?

Under the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)", financial assistance is provided specifically for establishing water distribution infrastructure, such as the installation of electric pumps and motors on wells or agricultural creeks, aimed at enhancing irrigation for agricultural purposes.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the geographical coverage of the scheme?

The "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)" scheme is applicable across the entire state of Gujarat and can be availed by any qualifying cooperative irrigation society within the state.
<br>

### Can societies from non-tribal areas apply for benefits under the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)"?

Societies that are not located in the officially designated tribal areas of Gujarat are not eligible to apply for benefits under the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)", as the scheme is specifically targeted at supporting tribal region development.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which department is responsible for the implementation of the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)"?

The scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies (Tribal Area)" is implemented by the District Registrar Office functioning under the Agriculture and Co-operation Department of the Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where should the application be submitted?

The application for this scheme should be submitted to the District Registrar's Office.
<br>

### Is there any preference or reservation for specific groups under this scheme?

The Scheme is applicable to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category.
<br>
