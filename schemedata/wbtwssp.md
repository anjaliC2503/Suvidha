## Brief Description

The scheme provides pension benefits to transport workers of the state upon reaching 60 years of age.

## Detailed Description

The “West Bengal Transport Workers’ Social Security Scheme: Pension” by the Labour Department, is designed to support unorganized transport workers upon retirement. Eligible beneficiaries receive a monthly pension of ₹1500/-, which increases by ₹10/- for each year of enrolment beyond 5 years, provided they have been continuously enrolled in the scheme for at least 5 years. This initiative aims to ensure financial stability and dignity for transport workers during their retirement years.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Pension Amount:** ₹1,500/- per month for beneficiaries aged 60 and above.  - **Incremental Benefit:** Additional ₹10/- per month for every completed year of enrolment beyond five years.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be between 18 and 60 years of age at the time of enrolment.
   1. The applicant must be an unorganized worker employed in the operation of commercial vehicles, such as cars, taxis, luxury taxis, light commercial vehicles, vans, auto-rickshaws, tempos, lorries, trucks, or buses.
   1. The applicant’s vehicle must be used for commercial purposes and not solely for personal or family use.
   1. The applicant must have been continuously enrolled in the scheme for at least 5 years to qualify for pension benefits.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step-1:** Visit the “[eDistrict 2.0](https://edistrict.wb.gov.in/portal/home)” portal. Click on Log In/Sign Up.
**Step-2:** In the User Login window, scroll down to the bottom and click on “Register”.
**Step-3:** Fill in all the details carefully, enter a username, and click on “Register”.
**Step-4:** Upon entering the One Time Password received on your mobile, you will be successfully registered.
**Step-5:** Now in the User Login window, enter your Username or email and captcha and click on “Sign In”.
**Step-6:** Enter the received O.T.P and click on “Submit”.
**Step-7:** Upon successful Login, You will be redirected to the “Dashboard”. From the right side menu apply for the concerned scheme.

&gt; **For New Registration:**



      1. Certification by Prescribed Authority in the Prescribed format,
      1. Passport Size Photo (self and for each dependent member).
      1. Age Proof Document.
      1. Driving Licence in case of Drivers.
      1. EPIC/ AADHAR Card.

      1. Original copies of Form - I, II, IIA.
      1. Life Certificate.
      1. Bank Details.
      1. Joint Passport Size Photo with Spouse (3 copies).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the scheme?

The scheme provides financial security in the form of a pension to unorganized transport workers upon reaching 60 years of age.

### What is the monthly pension amount provided under the scheme?

Beneficiaries receive a monthly pension of [?]1,500/-, with an additional [?]10/- increment for each completed year of enrollment beyond 5 years.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

The scheme is open to unorganized transport workers aged 18-60 years, engaged in operating commercial vehicles.

### Are individuals using private vehicles eligible for the scheme?

No, the scheme excludes individuals whose vehicles are used solely for personal or family purposes.

### What is the minimum enrollment period required to qualify for pension?

Beneficiaries must have been continuously enrolled in the scheme for at least 5 years.

### When will the pension payments begin for eligible beneficiaries?

Pension payments start once the beneficiary attains the age of 60 years and fulfills all eligibility criteria.

### How are pension benefits disbursed under the scheme?

Pension amounts are directly transferred to the beneficiaries' bank accounts through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

### What documents are required for submitting a pension application?

Applicants need identity proof, age proof, employment proof, bank account details, and the enrollment certificate under the scheme.

### What if a worker does not complete the required five years of enrollment?

If the minimum continuous enrollment period of 5 years is not met, the worker may not qualify for pension benefits.

### Where can workers seek additional information or assistance regarding the scheme?

Workers can contact the respective Regional Labor Offices or Labor Welfare Facilitation Centers in their area for more details.
