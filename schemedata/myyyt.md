## Brief Description

The scheme aims to digitally empower students and help them leverage the advantages of an interconnected world.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Mukhyamantri Yuba Yogayog Yojana-Special" by the Directorate of Information Technology, Government of Tripura, aims to digitally empower students and help them leverage the advantages of an interconnected world. Through this scheme, a grant or the cost of the phone, whichever is lower, is provided for the purchase of a smartphone to students who pursued a final-year undergraduate degree in Government Institutes in Tripura. The Directorate of Information Technology is responsible for implementing the scheme.
> **Objective:**

The need for a Smart Phone in this digitally connected world cannot be over emphasised. The Smart Phone is helpful in opening up huge opportunities for developing skills, finding resources to enhance knowledge and availing employment opportunities. Providing a grant for the purchase of smart phones to the students of the final year will empower them digitally and help them leverage the advantages of an interconnected world.
<br>

## Benefits

1. Eligible students receive a grant of ₹5,000/- or the cost of the phone, whichever is lower, for the purchase of a smartphone.

<br>

> **Mode of Disbursement:** The grant is credited directly to the eligible applicant's registered bank account using the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode.

> **Expected Time of Disbursement:** The payment processing by the State Level Authority is expected to take place from the launch date of the scheme to 2 months after.

> **Frequency of Disbursement:** The benefit can be availed once in a lifetime.

<br>

<br>

## Exclusions

1. The payment processing by the State Level Authority is expected to take place from the launch date of the scheme to 2 months after.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must belong to Tripura and be registered in a Tripura Ration Card.
1. The applicant must have pursued a final year undergraduate degree course during the academic year 2022-23.
1. The course must have been from a Government College/Institute/University in Tripura.
1. The phone must be purchased on or after July 1, 2022.
1. The phone must be purchased in the name of the applicant or their parents.
1. The applicant must have passed the previous academic year or semester before the final year.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Application URL:** Yuba-Yogayog-Official-Notification.pdf (pmmodiyojana.in)

**Step 1:** Visit the [Beneficiary Management System (BMS) portal](https://bms.tripura.gov.in) and click the "Citizen" link.
**Step 2:** Click on "Beneficiary Schemes" to see the available schemes.
**Step 3:** Find "Mukhyamantri Yuba Yogayog Yojana-Special (AY 2022-2023)" from the list and click "Enroll".
**Step 4:** First-time users must click "New User? Register" and provide their full name, mobile number, and email ID.
**Step 5:** Enter the One-Time Passwords (OTPs) received on the mobile number and email to complete registration.
**Step 6:** Log in with the registered email ID or mobile number and the new OTP received.
**Step 7:** Enter your 12-digit Ration Card number, select your name from the family members list, and fill out your personal and address details.
**Step 8:** Provide your bank account details, including the bank name, account number, IFSC code, and account holder name.
**Step 9:** Fill in the scheme-specific information, such as college name, year of study, invoice details (invoice number, date, amount, GSTIN), and phone's IMEI number.
**Step 10:** Upload all mandatory documents. All documents except the photograph must be in PDF format with a maximum size of 2 MB. The photograph must be an image file (e.g., JPG, JPEG) with a maximum size of 2 MB.
**Step 11:** Submit the application. An acknowledgement slip will be generated for future reference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the "Mukhyamantri Yuba Yogayog Yojana-Special" scheme open to all students enrolled in universities, colleges, or institutes in Tripura?

No, the scheme is only for students who pursued a final year undergraduate degree during the academic year 2022-23 in a Government College, Institute, or University in Tripura.
<br>

### Can students who are not from Tripura apply for this scheme?

No, to be a beneficiary, you must be a domicile of Tripura with a valid Ration Card.
<br>

### Can I apply for the scheme at any time during the year?

No, you can only apply online through the Beneficiary Management System (BMS) portal for a specific period of time as notified by the Government of Tripura. The time limit for online applications is from July 2, 2024, to August 2, 2024.
<br>

### Does the scheme cover all types of courses?

No, the benefit is only awarded to students of undergraduate degree courses.
<br>

### Are students enrolled in private universities eligible to apply for this scheme?

No, students in private universities not funded by the Central or State Government are not eligible.
<br>

### Am I, a final-year undergraduate student, eligible to apply for this scheme?

Yes, only those students who pursued a final year undergraduate degree course in the academic year 2022-23 in any Government College, Institute, or University in Tripura are eligible to apply.
<br>

### Is it necessary to upload the mobile phone's invoice along with the online application form?

Yes, you must upload a GST-enabled invoice of the purchased mobile phone. This invoice must be duly countersigned and stamped by the Government College, Institute, or University Authority.
<br>

### What are the mandatory documents I need to upload for the scheme?

You must upload a photograph, your final year marksheet, a Ration Card, a bank account document (passbook), and the mobile phone's invoice.
<br>

### Is there a minimum marks requirement to be eligible for the grant under this scheme?

No, there is no minimum mark required to receive the grant.
<br>

### How will I be notified about the status of my application?

You will be notified about the status of your application through email or SMS.
<br>

### How will I receive the grant amount if my application is approved?

The grant amount will be credited directly to your registered bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
<br>

### Where can I find my ration card number if it is not in the correct format?

The correct 12-digit ration card number can be found on the receipt printed from the Point of Sale (POS) machine at the Fair Price shop.
<br>

### How will I know if my application has been forwarded for payment?

You can track your application status online from your login, and you will also be notified via email or SMS.
<br>
