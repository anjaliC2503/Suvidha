## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote high-quality literary works for children by providing financial support to Gujarati authors for publishing original, unpublished children's books that contribute to cultural and moral development.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme to Provide Financial Assistance for the Production and Publication of Excellent Original Children&#39;s Literature&quot; by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, is designed to encourage the creation and dissemination of original, educational, and culturally enriching children’s literature in Gujarati. Financial assistance is provided to eligible authors after their manuscripts are evaluated by expert reviewers. Approved authors are required to publish the book within one year, provide acknowledgment of the Academy’s support in the published work, and submit printed copies and printing-related documents for reimbursement. Previously published or edited works and authors who have received five grants under any Sahitya Academy scheme are ineligible.

## Benefits

> **Financial Assistance:**


- **For books up to 64 pages:** Up to ₹5,000/-.- **For books exceeding 64 pages:** Up to ₹10,000/-.
> **Printing Size Guidelines:**


- **For “Demy” size:** ₹2,300/- (paperback).- **For “Double Crown” size:** ₹2,500/- (paperback), ₹2,700/- (hardcover).
> **Note:** Whichever is lower between actual expense and ceiling will be granted.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be the original author of a children&#39;s book manuscript written in Gujarati.
  - The book must be unpublished, original, and appropriate for children’s literature in terms of content and presentation.
  - Authors who have received five grants or more under any Sahitya Academy schemes are not eligible.
  - Only one application per financial year is allowed.
  - Manuscript must be typed, bound, paginated, and submitted in two copies by the deadline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Scheme to Provide Financial Assistance for the Production and Publication of Excellent Original Children's Literature"?

The "Scheme to Provide Financial Assistance for the Production and Publication of Excellent Original Children's Literature" aims to promote the creation and dissemination of high-quality, original, and culturally valuable children's literature in Gujarati by providing financial support to eligible authors for publishing their manuscripts.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Under the "Scheme to Provide Financial Assistance for the Production and Publication of Excellent Original Children's Literature", any author who has written an original and unpublished manuscript in Gujarati for children, and who has not received five or more grants from any Sahitya Academy scheme, is eligible to apply.
<br>

<br>

### What type of literary works are supported under this scheme?

This scheme supports original children’s books in Gujarati that contain creative stories, poetry, or cultural narratives designed to foster moral values, national pride, imagination, and social awareness among young readers.
<br>

<br>

### Can previously published works be considered under this scheme?

No, the scheme strictly supports only those manuscripts that are original and unpublished at the time of application; previously published or printed works are not eligible for financial assistance.
<br>

<br>

### What is the amount of financial assistance provided under the scheme?

Authors may receive ₹5,000 for books up to 64 pages and ₹10,000 for books exceeding 64 pages, subject to the cost ceiling fixed for different book sizes and formats as specified by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy.
<br>

<br>

### Is there a maximum number of grants an author can receive from the Academy?

Yes, authors who have already received financial assistance five times under any of the Sahitya Academy’s publishing-related schemes are not eligible to apply under this scheme again.
<br>

<br>

### How many applications can an author submit in a financial year?

An author can submit only one application under this scheme during a financial year, and multiple submissions for different manuscripts in the same year will not be entertained.
<br>

<br>

### What are the formatting requirements for manuscript submission?

The manuscript must be typed in Gujarati, properly bound, clearly paginated, and submitted in two hard copies along with the completed application form, author details, and declaration documents.
<br>

<br>

### Can the author change the manuscript title or content after submission?

No, the author cannot change the title, content, or format of the manuscript after submission unless written permission is obtained from the Gujarat Sahitya Academy; any unauthorized changes may lead to cancellation of the application.
<br>

<br>

### How is the final selection of manuscripts carried out?

The manuscript is reviewed by expert literary evaluators appointed by the Gujarat Sahitya Academy, and only those manuscripts that meet high literary standards and are suitable for children's reading are approved for assistance, with the Academy's decision being final.
<br>

<br>

### What acknowledgment must be printed in the final published book?

Every book published under the scheme must include the sentence “Published with financial assistance from Gujarat Sahitya Academy” and should display the Academy’s logo on the back cover and inner page, as per the prescribed format.
<br>

<br>

### How many printed copies must be submitted to the Academy?

The author is required to print a minimum of 300 copies of the approved book and submit 15 free copies to the Gujarat Sahitya Academy for record, distribution, or library purposes.
