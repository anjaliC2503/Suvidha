## Brief Description

This scheme offers a financial incentive of up to ₹10,000/- to meritorious Other Backward Classes (OBC) students who have achieved 80% or higher marks in the HSSLC examination. The objective is to motivate and encourage OBC students to pursue higher education.

## Detailed Description

The 'Special Incentive to OBC Meritorious Students (Boys & Girls)' scheme, administered by the Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, provides financial assistance to deserving Other Backward Classes (OBC) students. Eligibility is extended to those who have achieved 80% or higher marks in the HSSLC examination. The scheme's objective is to motivate and encourage OBC students to pursue higher education, thereby fostering their academic parity with the broader population through a substantial incentive. Assistance is contingent upon the applicant satisfying the stipulated eligibility criteria and the successful submission of required documentation. An incentive of up to ₹10,000/- will be disbursed to students as approved by the Selection Committee.
<br>

***The applications will be accepted through the myScheme platform from 04/07/2025 to 31/10/2025.***
***A DigiLocker account is required for application. Please refer to the Application Process section for details.*** 
<br>

## Benefits

A financial incentive of upto ₹ 10,000/-.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be currently enrolled as a student.
- The applicant must belong to the Other Backward Class (OBC) community of Assam.
- The applicant must have secured a minimum of 80% marks in the HSSLC examination held in 2025.
- The annual income of the applicant's parents/guardians must not exceed ₹5,00,000/-.


_*In instances where multiple applicants achieve an identical percentage in the HSSLC examination, preference will be given to those with a higher percentage in the HSLC examination._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum financial assistance provided under this scheme?

Selected students will receive a one-time incentive of up to ₹10,000/- as financial support.

### Which education boards are considered eligible for this scholarship?

The scheme recognizes HSSLC results from SEBA (Assam Board), CBSE, and ICSE.

### Is there any income limit for the applicant’s family to qualify?

Yes, the annual income of the applicant’s parents must not exceed ₹5,00,000/-.

### What happens if multiple applicants have the same HSSLC percentage?

In case of a tie, preference will be given to students with a higher percentage in their HSLC (Class 10) examination.

### Who is eligible to apply for this financial incentive scheme for meritorious students?

The scheme is open to OBC (Other Backward Class) students from Assam who have scored 80% or above in their HSSLC (SEBA/CBSE/ICSE) examination.

### Do I need to submit a caste certificate for this scheme?

Yes, a valid OBC certificate (mentioning sub-caste) issued by the competent authority is mandatory.

### What document serves as proof of income for the application?

An Annual Income Certificate, issued by the Circle Officer, must be submitted.

### Is the HSLC mark sheet required even if I’m applying based on my HSSLC results?

Yes, the HSLC mark sheet is necessary, especially if there’s a tie in HSSLC scores among applicants.

### Do I need to submit my Class 10 admit card for verification?

Yes, the Class 10 admit card is required as proof of date of birth.

### Which identification documents are needed along with the application?

You must submit a PAN Card, or Aadhaar Card for identity verification.

### Is a bank account mandatory to receive the incentive amount?

Yes, you must provide a self-attested front page of your passbook for direct transfer of funds.

### Which government department is responsible for implementing this scheme?

The Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes, Government of Assam, oversees the scheme’s implementation.

### When is the application window open for the 2025-26 cycle, and how can I apply?

Applications will be accepted online via the myScheme platform from 1st July 2025 to 31st August 2025.

### Is a DigiLocker account mandatory to apply?

Yes, a DigiLocker account is compulsory for application submission.

### What are the technical specifications for uploading photographs in the application?

Photos must be in JPEG/JPG format, with a file size between 50KB and 3MB.

### What file type and size are acceptable for uploading supporting documents?

Documents must be in PDF format, with each file sized between 300KB and 3MB.

### How do I create a DigiLocker account if I don’t have one?

Visit the DigiLocker portal/app, provide your Aadhaar number, mobile, email, and a 6-digit PIN, and submit the details to register.

### What credentials do I need to sign in to the myScheme platform?

Use your DigiLocker-registered mobile number and the 6-digit PIN created during DigiLocker setup to log in.

### Where can I download the DigiLocker mobile app for account creation?

The app is available on Google Play (Android) and the iOS App Store.

### What happens after I submit the application on myScheme?

You’ll receive a "Form Submitted Successfully!" confirmation and can print/download the form as a PDF.
