## Brief Description

The "Scheme for Celebration of Birth/Death Anniversary of Eminent Personalities" scheme aims to provide financial assistance for the celebration of anniversaries/Jayanti's of Sant Kabir, Sant Gadge, and Sant Durbalnath and in addition to Anniversaries of DR. B.R. Ambedkar, etc.

## Detailed Description

The "Scheme for Celebration of Birth/Death Anniversary of Eminent Personalities" was launched by the Department for the Welfare of SC / ST / OBC / Minorities, Government of NCT of Delhi. The scheme aims to assist in the organization of events for the Birth/Death anniversaries of prominent figures who have significantly contributed to social justice and the upliftment of weaker sections of society.  
<br>

The programs that may be organized to celebrate the Birth/Death Anniversary of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, Sant Ravidas, and Maharishi Balmiki are indicated below as an illustration:

1. Appropriate Cultural Programmed.
1. A conference or symposium on themes like

  - Life & Work of the Great Saint.
  - Contribution of eminent personality to Social Justice.
  - Road map for building a society based on the principles of social justice, equity, equality of opportunity, etc.
  - Other related subjects.

1. A quiz/debate/essay competition etc. on the life and work of Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar for the promotion of social justice, for its students. The winners to be given cash prize along with a book(s) on Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar.

## Benefits

- Assistance for the arrangements of celebrations up to a maximum of ₹50,000/- per occasion, for a maximum of two occasions in one year.

**Note:** Assistance for the celebration of Birth/Death Anniversaries of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, Sant Ravidas, and Maharishi Balmiki will be provided through empaneled agencies.

## Eligibility

1. The NGOs should be registered under the Societies Registration Act.
1. The registered NGOs should have audited accounts.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit (during office hours) the Department for the Welfare of SC/ST/OBC and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the Department for the Welfare of SC/ST/OBC.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).
<br>

**Note-1:** The application is submitted at least 15 days in advance.
**Note-2:** The recommendation letter is not mandatory during the enforcement of the Model Code of Conduct during elections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who are the personalities celebrated under this scheme?

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, Sant Ravidas, Maharishi Balmiki, Sant Kabir, Sant Gadge, and Sant Durbalnath.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

The registered NGOs under the Society Registration Act with audited accounts.

### Is there a financial limit for the assistance provided?

Yes, assistance for the arrangements of celebrations up to a maximum of [?]50,000 per occasion.

### Can the financial limit be relaxed?

Yes, by the committee under the Hon'ble Minister's chairmanship.

### When should the application be submitted?

At least 15 days in advance.

### Can NGOs without audited accounts apply?

Yes, for arrangements through approved vendors only.

### Are MLA recommendations mandatory?

Not during the enforcement of the Model Code of Conduct during elections.

### What is the static limit on NGO assistance per year?

NGOs can seek assistance for a maximum of two occasions in one year.

### Is photography required for the event?

Yes, the applicant organization must provide a set of photographs to the department.

### Can the arrangement be made through other government departments?

Yes, arrangements can be made through empaneled vendors of other government departments.
