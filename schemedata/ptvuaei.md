## Brief Description

The scheme aims to explore and develop hidden singing and acting talents among youths in villages by promoting tiatr activities. It provides financial and technical support to produce tiatr in original format for local communities.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Production of Tiatr in Villages/Urban Areas/Educational Institutions" by the Tiatr Academy of Goa under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa, aims to explore hidden singing and acting talents among the youths in villages and provide encouragement to develop such talents by promoting tiatr activities among villagers, addressing the need to nurture local artistic capabilities in rural and urban areas including educational institutions. The scheme provides financial and technical support for various aspects including direction, live band, outside artistes, stage setting, background music, costumes, make-up, and light effects, along with technical guidance through directors from Tiatr Academy of Goa's Panel of Directors.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Support:** Tiatr Academy of Goa will provide financial support to the extent as fixed by Tiatr Academy of Goa towards various aspects: Direction, Live Band (with at least 2 blowing instruments), Outside Artistes, Stage Setting, Background Music, Costumes, Make-up, Light Effects.- **Technical Guidance:** Provision of director from Tiatr Academy of Goa's Panel of Directors who will direct village talents and produce the tiatr.- **Director Expenses:** The expenditure incurred in engaging the director will be borne by Tiatr Academy of Goa.- **Marketing Materials:** Provision of one banner which should be compulsorily displayed on one of the two front side wings of the stage.- **Documentation:** Tiatr Academy of Goa may video film the performance at Kala Academy.- **Observer Support:** Tiatr Academy of Goa may depute an observer from its Panel of Observers to see whether the performance has been made as per the norms of the scheme.- **Payment Method:** Payment will be made directly to the service providers.- **Sponsorship Policy:** Sponsorship from businessmen, firms etc. may be taken.
<br>

> ***Conditions***

_*The performance of the tiatr should be staged in the village/urban area of production._
_*The tiatr should be shown to the people free without charging any tickets._
_*Submit statement of expenditure enclosing all original bills (stamped receipt if the amount exceeds ₹5,000/-)._
_*Provide 20 invitation cards/entry passes to Tiatr Academy of Goa well in advance from the middle of rows A, B, or C._
_*Return banner to Tiatr Academy of Goa after the show is staged._
_*Payment will be made only after the receipt of the observer's certificate._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be an Educational Institution, Cultural Association, Dramatic Troupe, or Individual.
- The applicant must be interested in promoting Tiatr culture in the village.
- The applicant must be from the particular village/urban area/parish (in case of Mumbai, Sindhudurg and other parts of the country).
- The participating artistes must be from the particular village/urban area/parish (Educational Institutions only).
- A maximum of 3 artistes from outside the village/urban areas/parish must be allowed to act as well as sing in the tiatr.
- The tiatr must be produced in its original format including 6 or 7 acts (podd'dde) with at least 4 'cantos' and comedy linked to the story of the play.
- The tiatr must include at least 14 'Kantaram' which includes at least 2 Duets, 1 Duo, 2 Trios, 1 Quartet/Quintet, 2 Choral Songs and Solos.
- The applicant must not make any adverse remark in any form either written or verbal against any Governments/Individuals/Institutions or anything against caste, creed and religion in the scripts/lyrics of songs/performances/speeches which may hurt the sentiments of anybody.
- The applicant must communicate the schedule of practices to Tiatr Academy of Goa well in advance.
- The applicant must discuss with Tiatr Academy of Goa and decide who will be the Chief Guest/Guest of Honour at the performance well in advance.
- The applicant must ensure this is the first performance of the tiatr for which financial assistance is sought from Tiatr Academy of Goa.

<br>

***Script and Performance Requirements***

- The script and songs may be written/composed by anyone from within the village or elsewhere but acting and singing must be done by the artistes from the particular village/urban area/parish.
- Musicians may be from anywhere and not necessarily from the village.
- Advertising Requirements: In all advertisements, oral, or print or electronic media, banners, etc. the following must be inserted/published/announced 'This Tiatr is organized in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa'.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
_*Applications are considered on a "First come First Serve" basis, subject to the availability of funds._
_*The application should reach Tiatr Academy of Goa 30 days in advance of the date of staging the tiatr._
_*The cheque will be issued in the name appearing at Serial Number 1 of the application form (name of individual/registered association/dramatic troupes/educational institution)._
<br>

##### ***Post-Application Processes***
**Step 1:****** ***Schedule Coordination & Chief Guest Arrangement***
The applicant must communicate the schedule of practices to Tiatr Academy of Goa well in advance. Discuss with Tiatr Academy of Goa and decide regarding the Chief Guest/Guest of Honour at the performance well in advance.
**Step 2:****** ***Collect Banner & Provide Invitation Cards***
The applicant must collect the banner from the office of Tiatr Academy of Goa and arrange for its compulsory display on one of the two front side wings of the stage during the performance. The applicant must provide 20 invitation cards/entry passes to Tiatr Academy of Goa well in advance, ensuring these seats are from the middle of the rows A, B, or C for proper viewing of the performance.
**Step 3:****** ***Stage Performance***
The applicant must stage the tiatr in the village/urban area of production ensuring all conditions are met including free admission for public, proper acknowledgment of Tiatr Academy of Goa collaboration in all advertisements and media, and maintaining the approved script and format.
**Step 4:****** ***Observer Coordination, Documentation and Reporting***
The applicant must cooperate with the observer deputed by Tiatr Academy of Goa from its Panel of Observers who will certify that the performance was in accordance to Tiatr Academy of Goa's norms, as payment will be made only after receipt of the observer's certificate. The applicant must document the performance through photographs and submit 5 copies to Tiatr Academy of Goa for records, ensuring two photographs show the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner during performance.
**Step 5:****** ***Submit Financial Documentation & Return Materials***
The applicant must prepare and submit a detailed statement of expenditure with all original bills (stamped receipts if amount exceeds ₹5,000/-) to Tiatr Academy of Goa for direct payment to service providers. The applicant must return the Tiatr Academy of Goa banner to the office after the show is staged to complete the programme requirements and maintain the academy's inventory.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific types of organizations or individuals are allowed to apply for financial assistance to produce theatrical performances in local communities?

You must be an Educational Institution, Cultural Association, Dramatic Troupe, or Individual to be eligible. All applicants must demonstrate genuine interest in promoting local theatrical culture within their specific village, urban area, or parish community.
<br>

### How many performers from outside my local area am I permitted to include in the cast for this theatrical production?

You are allowed to include a maximum of 3 performers from outside your village, urban area, or parish. These outside performers may both act and sing in the production while the majority of cast members must be local residents.
<br>

### What are the mandatory structural requirements that my theatrical performance must include to qualify for this financial support program?

Your performance must follow the original format with 6 or 7 acts, include at least 4 cantos with comedy elements linked to the main story, and feature at least 14 musical numbers including 2 Duets, 1 Duo, 2 Trios, 1 Quartet or Quintet, 2 Choral Songs, and Solo performances.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions on the content or messaging that can be included in the script and songs of my performance?

You cannot include any adverse remarks in written or verbal form against any governments, individuals, institutions, or content targeting caste, creed, and religion. All scripts, lyrics, performances, and speeches must avoid hurting anyone's sentiments or promoting divisive content.
<br>

### What documentation do I need to provide if my performers are located in areas outside of Goa state?

For performers in Mumbai, Sindhudurg, and other parts of the country, you must attach address proof documents to verify that each performer belongs to the specific parish or local area where the performance will be staged.
<br>

### How far in advance must I submit my complete application before the scheduled date of my theatrical performance?

Your application with all required documents must reach the office at least 30 days before your planned staging date. Incomplete applications will not be accepted, so ensure all documentation is properly prepared and submitted within this timeframe.
<br>

### What specific acknowledgment must I include in all promotional materials and advertisements for my supported theatrical production?

In all advertisements, oral announcements, print media, electronic media, and banners, you must include the statement "This performance is organized in collaboration with the organization." This acknowledgment is mandatory across all promotional channels and materials.
<br>

### What financial documentation must I provide after completing my theatrical performance to receive the promised support payments?

You must submit a detailed statement of expenditure with all original bills attached. For any amount exceeding ₹5,000, stamped receipts are required. Payment will only be processed after receiving the observer's certificate confirming compliance with program norms.
<br>

### Can I charge admission fees to audience members who attend my locally produced theatrical performance under this support scheme?

No, the performance must be shown to the public completely free of charge without any ticket sales or admission fees. The show must be accessible to all community members at no cost as a condition of receiving financial support.
<br>

### What are my obligations regarding the promotional banner provided by the supporting organization during my performance?

You must display the provided banner compulsorily on one of the two front side wings of your stage during the performance. After the show concludes, you are required to return this banner to the supporting organization's office.
<br>

### How many invitation passes must I provide to the supporting organization and where should these seats be located in the venue?

You must provide 20 invitation cards or entry passes well in advance of your performance. These reserved seats should be positioned in the middle sections of rows A, B, or C to ensure optimal viewing of the performance.
<br>

### What photographic documentation requirements must I fulfill after staging my theatrical performance for the support program records?

You must submit 5 photographs of your performance for official records. Two of these photographs must clearly show the supporting organization's banner displayed during the performance to verify compliance with promotional requirements.
<br>

### Who has the authority to decide on the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour for my supported theatrical performance?

You must discuss and coordinate with the supporting organization well in advance to jointly decide who will serve as Chief Guest or Guest of Honour. The organization's President, Vice President, or their nominee will typically fulfill this role.
<br>

### What happens if I later stage the same theatrical production at other competitive events or festivals after receiving this support?

If you stage the same production at specific annual competitions, you will become ineligible for certain other financial assistance schemes. This restriction ensures that supported productions maintain their community-focused purpose rather than becoming commercial ventures.
<br>
