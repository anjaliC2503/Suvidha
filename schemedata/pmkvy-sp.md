## Brief Description

The scheme aims to address the skilling needs of marginalised groups and difficult geographies. Project-based short-term skill training is provided to such candidates.

## Detailed Description

Launched in 2015, with its 4.0 phase being implemented between Financial Year 2022 and Financial Year 2026, the scheme "Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana 4.0 – Special Projects" by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Government of India, aims to meet the skilling needs of marginalized and vulnerable groups, difficult geographies, and specialized training courses that are likely to be offered outside of regular short-term skilling programmes, and can also focus on new-age job roles with future skills.  Through this scheme, project-based short-term skilling initiatives — which can be residential or non-residential and may avail certain exemptions granted with the approval of the Executive Committee are provided to marginalised and vulnerable groups and candidates in difficult geographies that require special attention. The scheme is implemented by the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC), which supports the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE), and is delivered on the ground through Special Projects Implementing Agencies (SPIAs).
> **Objectives of Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana 4.0**


- The scheme promotes an enabling ecosystem for the youth to get skilled and choose a career path aligned with their abilities and aspirations.
- The scheme enables delivery of skill training in a market-oriented and demand-driven manner, with emphasis on improving the employability of candidates.
- The scheme simplifies processes of the skill ecosystem by leveraging technology and digitalisation.
- The scheme enhances access to skilling by setting up skill development infrastructure in remote and difficult geographies such as hilly terrain, Left-Wing Extremism (LWE)-affected areas, and border areas, by designing special projects.
- The scheme improves inclusivity by ensuring that Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), women, and other marginalised communities can undertake skill training and access gainful wages and self-employment.
- The scheme provides an opportunity for lifelong skilling through upskilling and reskilling to address the dynamic needs of the ever-changing market.
- The scheme ensures quality training delivery through trained pedagogy, standardised assessments, and industry-relevant curriculum.
- The scheme facilitates enhanced employability through training in transferable skills and incentives for employment generation.
- The scheme provides candidate-centric training with emphasis on industry-relevant skills.

<br>

## Benefits

The following benefits are provided to candidates under Special Projects:

1. **Base Training Cost:** Provided as per the rationalised hourly base cost of ₹27.89 per hour.1. **Free Short-Term Skill Training:** On-the-Job Training (OJT) is provided in applicable job roles for practical, live-work exposure.1. **Uniform, Induction Kit, and Participant Handbook:** Maximum up to ₹500 per candidate. The induction kit consists of a T-shirt (Male) or Jacket (Female) and a bag.1. **Assessment and Certification:** ₹600 per assessment per candidate, funded under the scheme. Every candidate is offered one free-of-charge chance of re-assessment.1. **Boarding, Lodging, and Transportation:** Provided to special groups (Women and Persons with Disability) and Special Areas, and may also be extended for training in Aspirational, Border, Tribal-dominated, and LWE-affected districts. Special Projects may also be taken up with fully residential training.1. **Conveyance / Transportation Cost:** Permissible for Women and Persons with Disability (PwD) in case of non-residential training, as per Common Norms.1. **Additional Support to Persons with Disability (PwD) Candidates:** ₹5,000 towards assistive devices, aids, and appliances.1. **Accidental Insurance:** Every certified candidate is provided with 1-year accidental insurance, as per prevailing rates (₹14.16).1. **Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System (AEBAS) Transaction Fees:** Up to ₹10 per user, borne under the scheme.1. **Post-Certification Tracking Support:** Information Technology (IT)-based tracking for up to 1 year from the date of certification, with an allowance of up to ₹50 per certified candidate.1. **Certification:** A Quick Response (QR) code-based digital certificate is issued (on DigiLocker, on email/phone number, and on the Skill India Digital profile), and one printed certificate is provided free of cost by the Training Provider.1. **Post-Certification Linkages:** Certified candidates are linked with Rojgar Melas (including virtual) and Apprenticeship Melas and gain access to jobs, apprenticeship opportunities, and loans for entrepreneurship through Skill India Digital.
<br>

## Exclusions

1. The applicant should not be enrolled again in the same job role in which he/she was trained earlier.
1. The applicant, if already certified once under PMKVY, will be discouraged from opting for a lower National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF) level course in the same sector; however, a higher job role in the same sector is permitted.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be of Indian nationality.
1. The applicant should possess a valid Aadhaar.
1. The applicant should fulfil the eligibility criteria of the respective job role.
1. The applicant should be in the age group of 15 to 45 years.
1. The applicant should belong to a marginalised or vulnerable group, or another group requiring special attention, or should be undertaking job roles with a focus on future skills.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Application URL:** https://www.skillindia.gov.in/

**Stage 1:** The applicant first registers on the [Skill India Digital (SID)](https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in/home) platform or the mobile application developed for the purpose; profile verification is completed through mobile-based OTP / eKYC, and a de-duplication check is carried out.
**Stage 2:** The applicant is counselled by the PIA/trainer on areas including a brief about MSDE, the Skill India programme, and RPL processes; eligibility for RPL certification; benefits and how to avail them; possible career progression (vertical and horizontal); and employment opportunities. The Induction Video on RPL is also shown at this stage.
**Stage 3:** A de-duplication check is carried out against the data available in the Skill India Digital database, so that the applicant is not enrolled again in the same job role in which he/she was trained earlier.
**Stage 4:** The applicant's profile is completed, with existing details fetched from other portals such as e-Shram and National Career Services (NCS).
**Stage 5:** The applicant avails online counselling offered through Skill India Digital to select an appropriate course and Training Centre.
**Stage 6:** The applicant selects the nearest Training Centre based on the availability of the preferred job role and the quality of training infrastructure (rating, centre profile), or is mapped automatically through logic-based mapping to the nearest Training Centre.
**Stage 7:** On selection and enrolment, the applicant undergoes training, followed by On-the-Job Training (OJT) in applicable job roles.
**Stage 8:** The applicant appears for assessment by an NCVET-approved Awarding Body / Assessment Agency after completion of On-the-Job Training, requiring more than 70% biometric attendance to be eligible.
**Stage 9:** On successful completion, the applicant is awarded a Quick Response (QR) code-based digital certificate, issued via DigiLocker, an email/phone download link, and the Skill India Digital profile page, along with one printed certificate provided free of cost by the Training Provider.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Special Projects under Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) 4.0?

Special Projects are project-based short-term skilling initiatives that primarily meet the skilling needs of marginalized and vulnerable groups, difficult geographies, and specialized training courses likely to be offered outside of regular short-term skilling programmes. These projects can also focus on new-age job roles with future skills.
<br>

### Who can apply as a candidate under Special Projects?

Candidates of Indian nationality, possessing a valid Aadhaar and fulfilling the eligibility criteria of the respective job role, who belong to marginalized or vulnerable groups or other groups requiring special attention, can apply.
<br>

### What is the age limit for candidates under Special Projects?

The age group for Special Projects is 15 to 45 years.
<br>

### Is the training free of cost for candidates?

Yes. The base training cost, assessment and certification, induction kit, accidental insurance, and other applicable support are funded under the scheme. The training cost is borne under the scheme as per the standard cost norms.
<br>

### Can Special Projects be residential?

Yes. Special Projects can be residential or non-residential. Boarding and lodging facilities can be provided under Special Projects for fully residential training.
<br>

### What support is available for women and Persons with Disability (PwD) candidates?

Boarding, lodging, and transport facilities are provided to special groups (Women and PwD). A conveyance facility is permissible for women and PwD candidates in case of non-residential training. PwD candidates additionally receive up to ₹5,000 towards assistive devices, aids, and appliances.
<br>

### Is On-the-Job Training (OJT) part of Special Projects?

Yes. On-the-Job Training has been introduced under Short-Term Training and Special Projects to provide practical exposure, and is applicable in job roles as mandated by the National Council for Vocational Education and Training (NCVET). In districts where OJT facilities are not available, candidates may carry out project work with special exemption from the Executive Committee.
<br>

### How is attendance recorded during training?

Daily attendance on entry and exit is recorded through the Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System (AEBAS) for both candidates and trainers. More than 70% biometric attendance is required for a candidate to appear in the assessment.
<br>

### What certificate does a candidate receive on completion?

On successfully passing the assessment, the candidate is awarded a Quick Response (QR) code-based digital certificate, issued via DigiLocker, an email/phone download link, and the Skill India Digital profile page. One printed certificate is also provided free of cost by the Training Provider.
<br>

### Is there any support after certification?

Yes. Information Technology (IT)-based tracking is carried out for up to 1 year from the date of certification. Certified candidates are linked with Rojgar Melas (including virtual) and Apprenticeship Melas, and gain access to jobs, apprenticeship opportunities, and loans for entrepreneurship through Skill India Digital.
