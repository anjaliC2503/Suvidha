## Brief Description

The scheme aims to develop specialists, coaches, officials, and researchers in sports. Under this scheme, fellowships, training support, research grants, and publication assistance are provided to sports personnel and institutions.

## Detailed Description

The "Scheme of Human Resources Development in Sports- Assistance to Match Officials, Coaches and Support Personnel" by the Department of Sports, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India. The scheme aims to provide assistance to match officials, coaches, and other support personnel for appearing in qualifying examinations and for attending training and courses.
> **Scheme Implementation:**

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Sports in association with its autonomous bodies, namely the Sports Authority of India, the Lakshmibai National Institute of Physical Education, the National Dope Testing Laboratory, the National Anti-Doping Agency, and the National Sports University.
> **Components of the Scheme:**

The scheme will have the following components:
(i) Fellowship Programme
(ii) Exchange of Knowledge/ Research/ Ideas
a) For attending seminars/ workshops/conferences of repute in India or abroad
b) For holding training programmes/ seminars/ workshops/ clinics and conferences within the country
(iii) Assistance to Match Officials, Coaches and Supporting Personnel
(iv) Assistance for Research
(v) Publication of Outstanding Works on sports related Subjects
<br>

## Benefits

> Assistance for undergoing specialised training abroad (not available within the country) is provided to an individual for a period not exceeding 1 year, and covers:


- Economy class air fare by the shortest route from the point of embarkation in India to the point of disembarkation in the foreign country, both ways, along with airport tax, local transport, and other incidentals; for journeys inside the country, train fare by Air-Conditioned 2nd Class, both ways, and local transport, with air travel by economy class allowed where the distance exceeds 500 kilometres.
- Maintenance allowance up to a maximum of US$ 1,000 per month, payable in local currency.
- Actual cost of books, essential apparatus, study tours (coach class fare), and other items specifically recommended by the course in-charge, subject to a maximum of US$ 350 per annum.
- Tuition fee, examination fee, and medical and health insurance charges of a mandatory nature, as per actuals.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should not be more than 55 years of age, except that there is no age bar for match officials appearing in qualifying examinations.
1. The match official should have been officiating in official tournaments, and the coach should have been coaching sportspersons at the State level for at least 2 years.
1. For specialised training, match officials (Judge, Umpire, Referee, Arbitrators) should have participated or officiated in the Olympics, Commonwealth Games, Asian Games, or World Championship, and should hold an 'A' grade certificate issued by the recognised international body.
1. For undergoing specialised training abroad, the applicant should have a confirmed admission from a reputed institution.
1. The application or proposal should be routed through the Sports Authority of India, any recognised National Sports Federation, or the Indian Olympic Association.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 01:** The applicant should submit the application online through the dedicated portal, [dbtyas-sports.gov.in](https://dbtyas-sports.gov.in/). Only online applications received through this portal are accepted.
**Step 02:** The applications, along with the recommendations of the Appraisal Committee, are then placed before the Designated Committee, which meets bi-monthly in the first or second week of every alternate month.
**Step 03:** The decision of the Designated Committee in respect of selection of any application or proposal is final.
**Step 04:** Selected candidates are intimated by the Department and are required to submit all documents in original to the Department of Sports for verification.
**NOTE:**

- The applicant should ensure the application is submitted well before the time of the scheduled meeting of the Designated Committee, and not later than the 20th day of the preceding month of the scheduled meeting.
- Applications and proposals received on or before the 20th day of the preceding month, and complete in all respects, are first placed before the Appraisal Committee for examining the eligibility and technical aspects. 


- For match officials and coaches, the application should be routed through the Sports Authority of India, a recognised National Sports Federation, or the Indian Olympic Association.

## Documents Required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Ministry and Department implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Sports under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India, in association with its Autonomous Bodies.
<br>

### Who is covered under this component?

Match officials such as Judges, Umpires, Referees, and Arbitrators, along with coaches and supporting personnel such as physiotherapists, psychologists, nutritionists, and masseurs.
<br>

### What support is provided?

Assistance for appearing in qualifying examinations organised by international bodies or National Sports Federations, and for undergoing specialised training in relevant disciplines in India or abroad.
<br>

### What is the eligibility for match officials and coaches?

The match official should have been officiating in official tournaments, and the coach should have been coaching sportspersons at the State level for at least 2 years.
<br>

### What is the additional requirement for specialised training of match officials?

For specialised training, match officials should have participated or officiated in the Olympics, Commonwealth Games, Asian Games, or World Championship, and should hold an 'A' grade certificate issued by the recognised international body.
<br>

### Is there an age limit?

The applicant should not be more than 55 years of age, except that there is no age bar for match officials appearing in qualifying examinations.
<br>

### For how long is assistance for training abroad provided?

Assistance for undergoing specialised training abroad, the equivalent of which is not available within the country, is provided to an individual for a period not exceeding 1 year.
<br>

### What financial assistance is available for training abroad?

Economy class air fare by the shortest route, both ways, with incidentals; maintenance allowance up to a maximum of US$ 1,000 per month in local currency; up to US$ 350 per annum for books, essential apparatus, and study tours; and tuition fee, examination fee, and mandatory medical and health insurance charges as per actuals.
<br>

### How is the application submitted?

The application or proposal should be routed through the Sports Authority of India, any recognised National Sports Federation, or the Indian Olympic Association.
<br>

### Is a service bond required?

Yes, coaches and support personnel (other than match officials) are required to execute a bond for serving the Government for a minimum period of 3 years on completion of the training, failing which they refund the amount along with interest.
