## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students belonging to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi Community who have cleared the Civil Services Preliminary Examination. The benefits support their preparation for the Mains Examination to ensure their success.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Financial Assistance to Students who Cleared Civil Services Prelims Conducted by UPSC/APSC” was launched by the Tea Tribes and Adivasi Welfare Department, Government of Assam. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students belonging to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi Community who have cleared the Civil Services Preliminary Examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission or Assam Public Service Commission during the current year, so that they are supported in their preparation for the Mains Examination and succeed. The applications for this scheme are accepted online.
<br>

## Benefits

- Financial assistance to students belonging to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi Community who have cleared the Civil Services Preliminary Examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission/Assam Public Service Commission during the current year.
- The assistance is intended to support them in their preparation for the Mains Examination and succeed.

<br>

## Exclusions

- The applicants who have cleared both the Civil Services Preliminary Examinations Conducted by the Union Public Service Commission and Assam Public Service Commission in the same year shall be considered for scholarship only for the Union Public Service Commission Prelims.
- The applicant should not be a Government service holder.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a permanent resident of Assam and must belong to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community.
- The applicant must produce a valid Caste certificate which is duly certified/issued by the District Commissioner of the concerned district or his/her designated officer.
- The applicant should have cleared the Civil Services Preliminary Examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission/Assam Public Service Commission during the current year.
- The applicant must produce his/her copy of the admit card and result of the Civil Services Preliminary Examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission/Assam Public Service Commission.
- The applicant should not be a Government service holder.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

##### ***Step 1:*** ***Portal Access and Scheme Selection***
**A. Navigating to the Portal:** The applicant must first open the official Tea Tribes Welfare Department URL, www.ttwd.assam.gov.in, and then click the "Online Application" link to be redirected to the [SIRISH application portal](https://sirishassam.in/).
**B. Initial Login or Registration:** New users must register themselves on the online portal, while existing users will log in using the username and password created during prior registration.
**C. Selecting the Scheme:** Once the applicant is logged into the Dashboard, they must select the appropriate scholarship scheme from the dropdown menu available.
##### ***Step 2:********* **Entering Personal Details**
**A. Inputting Mandatory Data:** The applicant proceeds to enter Personal Details where all required information in the application form must be accurately provided.
**B. Specifying Location Details:** Location data, including District, Assembly Constituency, and Sub-Division, must be selected, along with other essential address details like Village/Town.
**C. Saving Personal Information:** After filling in all necessary fields in this step, the applicant must click on the "Save Form Details" button to proceed further with the application.
##### ***Step 3:********* **Document Upload**
**A. Uploading Required Files:** Under the Upload Documents tab, the applicant must upload all required supporting documents in the specified image formats (e.g., JPG/JPEG).
**B. Document Formatting Check:** All uploaded documents must adhere to specified maximum file sizes (e.g., photo maximum 50 KB, other documents generally 100 KB) before they can be saved.
##### ***Step 4:********* **Updating Bank Details**
**A. Providing Account Information:** The applicant must enter the Bank Account Number and the name of the bank under the "Update Bank Details" tab.
**B. Entering Branch Identification:** Details essential for electronic transfer, specifically the Bank Branch Name and the corresponding IFSC Code, must be accurately provided.
**C. Securing Financial Data:** Once all bank details have been correctly entered, the applicant must click on the "Save Bank Details" button to confirm the information.
##### ***Step 5:********* **Preview and Final Submission**
**A. Application Review:** "Preview Application and Final Submission", displays all details entered in the form for a thorough and final check.
**B. Final Confirmation:** After cross-checking all information and uploaded documents for accuracy, the applicant must confirm the application by clicking the "Proceed with Submit Application" button.
**C. Tracking Status:** Upon successful submission, an application number will be visible on the screen, allowing the applicant to view the status under the "APPLICATION STATUS" tab.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I demonstrate successful completion of the initial examination stage that qualifies me for this specific financial assistance?

You must have successfully cleared the Civil Services Preliminary Examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) or Assam Public Service Commission (APSC) during the current year.
<br>

### What specific records relating to the preliminary examination must I submit alongside my application materials?

You are required to produce and submit a copy of your admit card and the official result of the Civil Services Preliminary Examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission/Assam Public Service Commission.
<br>

### If this is my first time seeking benefits through this departmental portal, what is the initial required step before starting the application form?

All fresh applicants are required to first register themselves in the `www.sirishassam.in` online portal before following the necessary steps to complete the application process.
<br>

### If I happen to clear the preliminary examinations for both the Union Public Service Commission and the Assam Public Service Commission in the same year, for which one will my application be considered?

If you have cleared both Civil Services Preliminary Examinations in the same year, your application for financial assistance shall be considered only for the Union Public Service Commission Preliminary Examination.
<br>

### What is the residency and community requirement I must meet to be considered for financial support under this specific program?

You must be a permanent resident of Assam and must demonstrate that you belong to the Tea Tribes and Adivasi community.
<br>

### When providing my financial information during the application process, whose bank account details should I ensure are entered?

All applicants must ensure they provide correct details of their own Bank Account within the application form.
<br>

### What type of documentation must I provide to confirm my community status for the purpose of receiving benefits through this scheme?

You must provide a valid Caste certificate that has been duly certified or issued by the District Commissioner (DC) of the concerned district or their designated officer.
<br>

### In which financial year was this specific scheme designed to assist preliminary examination qualifiers officially launched by the department?

The financial assistance scheme was launched during the 2024-25 financial year.
<br>

### Before I proceed with filling out the application form, are there any preliminary actions or review steps I should undertake?

Applicants are strongly advised to carefully read the respective guidelines of the schemes for eligibility and terms and conditions prior to filling out the application.
<br>

### What is the overall purpose of providing this financial assistance to candidates who have successfully progressed past the initial testing phase?

The scheme’s purpose is to provide financial assistance to support students in their preparation for the Mains Examination and help them succeed.
<br>

### If I have successfully registered on the portal in a previous year for a different scheme, what steps should I take to apply now?

Already registered applicants need to login using their previous User ID and Password and then upload only the necessary relevant documents to avail the financial grant.
<br>

### Who is the designated agency responsible for the implementation and management of this financial assistance scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Tea Tribes and Adivasi Welfare, Assam.
<br>

### Am I eligible to apply for this financial support if I am currently employed in a position as a holder of a government service job?

No, an applicant for this financial assistance scheme is specifically required not to be a Government service holder.
<br>
