## Brief Description

"Merit cum Means Scholarship: Polytechnic Institutions" is a scholarship scheme by the Technical Education Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. The scheme provides ₹600/- per month to eligible students for pursuing education from recognized Polytechnic Institutions in Chhattisgarh.

## Detailed Description

"Merit cum Means Scholarship: Polytechnic Institutions" is a scholarship scheme by the Technical Education Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. The scheme provides ₹600/- per month to eligible students for pursuing education from recognized Polytechnic Institutions in Chhattisgarh. To be eligible, the student must have passed the 10+2 examination or the 10th/12th examination from any recognised institution located in Chhattisgarh with a minimum of 45% marks. The total annual income of the student's father/guardian should not exceed ₹ 2,50,000/-.

## Benefits

Scholarship of ₹600/- per month.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a native resident of Chhattisgarh.
- The applicant must be a Student.
- The applicant must be currently studying in a recognised Polytechnic Institution in Chhattisgarh.
- The applicant must have passed the 10+2 examination or the 10th/12th examination from any recognised institution located in Chhattisgarh with a minimum of 45% marks.
- The total annual income of the student's father/guardian should not exceed ₹ 2,50,000/-.

<br>

**OTHER CONDITIONS**

- Without special approval from the government, no student will receive more than one scholarship at the same time. However, if the institution where he is studying has given him any fee concession, then he will be entitled to continue receiving it.
- If any scholarship becomes vacant during its duration, then the approving authority can give such scholarships for its unexpired period to the next eligible and deserving candidate from the merit list.
- Only those students will be eligible to receive scholarships who have passed the qualifying examination from any testing body of Chhattisgarh. But children of such permanent government employees of Chhattisgarh can be exempted from this condition who are on deputation to the Government of India or any other state government.
- Such students will not be eligible to receive scholarships who are in full-time employment. In this regard, only one concession can be given that any such student who is regularly studying in a recognised institution and is receiving any scholarship can be allowed to do part-time work.
- In special cases not covered under the provisions of these rules, the government will provide special scholarships to eligible students at the rate prescribed for each course of study, based on poverty and merit. The total annual expenditure incurred in this regard will not exceed the budget limit.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the **Office of the Principal of the Institution** and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the institute staff.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the staff. The Principal will verify the documents and forward the application to the Directorate for approval (New applications) or process the renewal (for existing beneficiaries).
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the staff to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the basic eligibility requirements to apply for this scholarship for polytechnic education?

To be eligible, you must be a native resident of Chhattisgarh, currently studying in a recognized polytechnic institution, and have passed 10+2 or 10th/12th with at least 45% marks from a recognized institution in Chhattisgarh.

### Is there any income limit for my family to qualify for this scholarship?

Yes, the total annual income of your father or guardian must not exceed ₹2,50,000/- to be eligible for this scholarship.

### How much financial assistance will I receive each month under this scholarship scheme?

You will receive a scholarship amount of ₹600/- per month for your studies in a recognized polytechnic institution.

### Do I need to be a resident of Chhattisgarh to apply for this scholarship?

Yes, only native residents of Chhattisgarh are eligible to apply for this scholarship.

### Can I apply if I am studying in a polytechnic institution outside Chhattisgarh?

No, you must be studying in a recognized polytechnic institution located within Chhattisgarh to be eligible.

### What is the minimum academic requirement in terms of marks for this scholarship?

You must have secured at least 45% marks in your 10+2 or 10th/12th examination from a recognized institution in Chhattisgarh.

### Can I receive this scholarship if I am already getting another scholarship?

No, you cannot receive more than one scholarship at the same time without special government approval.

### What happens if a scholarship seat becomes vacant during the academic year?

The approving authority may allocate the vacant scholarship to the next eligible candidate from the merit list for the remaining duration.

### Are children of government employees on deputation eligible for this scholarship?

Yes, children of permanent Chhattisgarh government employees on deputation to the Government of India or other states may be exempted from the qualifying exam condition.

### Am I eligible if I am working full-time while studying?

No, full-time employed students are not eligible, but part-time work may be allowed if you are already receiving a scholarship.

### What documents do I need to submit along with my scholarship application?

You must provide a Chhattisgarh residential certificate, qualifying exam mark sheet, and proof of admission/enrollment in the polytechnic institution.

### Where can I obtain the application form for this scholarship?

You must visit the office of your institution’s principal and request a hard copy of the prescribed application form.

### How should I fill and submit the scholarship application form?

Fill all mandatory fields, attach required documents (self-attested if needed), and submit the form to the institution’s staff within the prescribed period.

### Who decides the last date for submitting the scholarship application?

The principal of your institution will determine the last date for application submission.
