## Brief Description

The scheme recognizes Anganwadi Workers under the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) Schemes for their exemplary performance in improving the quality and coverage of services provided to children and mothers. Each awardee receives a cash award of ₹5,000/- and a citation.

## Detailed Description

The "State Award To Two Anganwadi Workers Under Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) Schemes" was launched by the Directorate of Women and Child Development (DW&CD), Goa. The scheme aims to recognize the exemplary performance of Anganwadi Workers. The objective is to highlight their success in improving the coverage and quality of services delivered to children and pregnant and lactating mothers under the Integrated Child Development Services Scheme. Furthermore, the scheme recognizes their contribution towards the implementation of various programmes and schemes of the central Government, state Government, or local bodies. The award provides each selected Anganwadi Worker with a cash prize of ₹5,000/- and a citation. The selection process is based on recommendations made by various authorities, which are then forwarded to a Scrutiny Committee.
<br>

## Benefits

- Each selected awardee receives a Cash Award of ₹5,000/-.
- Each selected awardee receives a citation.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be an Anganwadi Worker under the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) Schemes.
- The applicant must demonstrate exemplary performance in improving the coverage and quality of services provided to children and pregnant and lactating mothers under the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) Scheme.
- The applicant must show contribution towards the implementation of programmes or schemes of the central Government, state Government, or local bodies.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

****The process detailed below describes the pathway for nomination and selection, as the Anganwadi Worker does not apply directly for this award.***
**Step 1:** The recommendation for the award must be initiated by authorized officials or bodies, which include District/Block level Public Representation Institutions (PRIs), Members of Legislative Assembly (MLAs), Members of Parliament (MPs), Women’s Organisations, Child Development Project Officers (CDPO), District Programme Officers of Integrated Child Development Services Scheme, or the District Collector.
**Step 2:** The initiated recommendation, detailing the exemplary performance of the Anganwadi Worker, must then be put forward to the designated Scrutiny Committee for further review and selection.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific job designation must I currently hold within the government social service framework to be considered for this prestigious recognition?

The award is specifically meant for Anganwadi Workers who are currently working under the framework of the Integrated Child Development Services Schemes.
<br>

### What general level of professional conduct or achievement must I achieve in my role to qualify for consideration under the award scheme criteria?

You must demonstrate exemplary performance as an Anganwadi Worker to meet the core eligibility requirements for this State Award.
<br>

### What is the precise financial incentive amount that will be provided to the selected individuals who demonstrate outstanding dedication to the program goals?

Each selected awardee will receive a Cash Award of ₹5,000/- (Rupees five thousand only) in recognition of their exemplary work and contribution.
<br>

### Besides the financial compensation, what additional non-monetary formal recognition or documentation will be given to the award recipients?

In addition to the monetary reward, each Anganwadi Worker selected for the award will also be presented with a citation.
<br>

### How many total award recipients are recognized under this program specifically designed for excellence in performance?

Only two Anganwadi Workers will be selected to receive this award recognizing their contribution to the Integrated Child Development Services Schemes.
<br>

### Regarding the core work responsibilities, is the ability to improve the accessibility or reach of the services a mandatory consideration for selection?

Yes, eligibility requires demonstrating success in improving the coverage of the services delivered under the Integrated Child Development Services Scheme.
<br>

### When performance is assessed, do the selecting authorities also place a high value on maintaining or enhancing the standard of the services provided?

Yes, the selection criteria specifically mandates recognition of exemplary performance in improving the quality of services provided to beneficiaries.
<br>

### Are my efforts recognized if my exceptional performance focuses primarily on providing assistance and services to expectant beneficiaries?

Yes, improving the coverage and quality of services provided to pregnant mothers under the Integrated Child Development Services Scheme is a key criterion.
<br>

### Do the services I provide need to specifically target the demographic of young individuals covered under the Integrated Child Development Services framework?

Yes, your exemplary performance must specifically relate to improving services provided to children under the Integrated Child Development Services Scheme.
<br>

### Is the dedicated support provided to beneficiaries who are currently nursing also considered a vital part of the overall performance evaluation?

Yes, outstanding performance in improving services for lactating mothers is explicitly considered for the State Award under the Integrated Child Development Services Scheme.
<br>

### Does the evaluation process also consider my contribution towards the successful implementation of other government programmes beyond my primary Integrated Child Development Services duties?

The award recognizes contribution towards the implementation of various programmes or schemes managed by the central Government, state Government, or local bodies.
<br>

### Which local governance institutions or elected representatives are authorized to initiate a formal recommendation for an applicant?

Recommendations may be made by District/Block level Public Representation Institutions (PRIs), Members of Legislative Assembly (MLAs), Members of Parliament (MPs), or a Women’s Organisation.
<br>

### Which supervisory officials within the Integrated Child Development Services structure are permitted to nominate Anganwadi Workers for this award?

Nominations can be put forward by the Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) or the District Programme Officer of the Integrated Child Development Services Scheme.
<br>

### Is the administrative head of the district also empowered to formally recommend a worker for this specific State Award?

Yes, a recommendation for the award can also be initiated by the District Collector as one of the specified recommending authorities.
<br>
