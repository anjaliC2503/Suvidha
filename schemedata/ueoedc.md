## Brief Description

The scheme aims to establish and operate Entrepreneurship Development Centers to provide structured training in entrepreneurship to students, women, and the general public of Uttarakhand.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Establishment and Operation of Entrepreneurship Development Centers” is implemented by the Uttarakhand Science Education and Research Centre (USERC) under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to establish and operate Entrepreneurship Development Centers to provide structured training in entrepreneurship to students, women, and the general public of the state.
<br>

## Benefits

In the U-SERC Entrepreneurship Development Centres, training in entrepreneurship development is provided to students of the area, women, and the general public.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. All students, women, and general public in Uttarakhand will be eligible to receive the training.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Tissue culture labs and hi-tech nurseries have been developed through the establishment of Harela Peeth at P. L. M. Sharma, Shri Dev Suman University campus, Rishikesh, and Sovan Singh Jeena University, Almora. Additionally, HARC was established in Naugaon, Uttarkashi, and CIMS, Dehradun, to facilitate high-quality spawn mushroom production and related training activities.
  - The U-SERC Entrepreneurship Development Centre was set up in Satpuli, Pauri, to provide entrepreneurship development and self-employment-oriented training for students, regional women, and the general public. 
  - Benefits and training opportunities can be availed by contacting the respective Entrepreneurship Development Centre.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can benefit from the scheme?

All students, women, and members of the general public across Uttarakhand are eligible to receive training under this scheme.
<br>

### Where are the Entrepreneurship Development Centres located?

Some of the key centers include:

- Harela Peeth, P. L. M. Sharma, Shri Dev Suman University campus, Rishikesh
- Sovan Singh Jeena University, Almora
- HARC, Naugaon, Uttarkashi
- CIMS, Dehradun
- U-SERC Entrepreneurship Development Centre, Satpuli, Pauri

<br>

### What kind of training is provided at these centers?

Training focuses on entrepreneurship development, self-employment skills, innovation, business management, and sector-specific skills such as tissue culture and mushroom spawn production.
<br>

### Who can attend the training sessions?

Students, women, and the general public of Uttarakhand who are interested in developing entrepreneurial skills can attend.
<br>

### How can one apply for training?

Interested individuals can contact the nearest Entrepreneurship Development Centre to register for training.
<br>

### Can training help in starting a business?

Yes.
<br>

### How can one contact the centers for more information?

Individuals can contact the nearest Entrepreneurship Development Centre directly.
<br>
