## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide accessible, affordable, and holistic healthcare services based on Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani and Homeopathy systems to people of all age groups.

## Detailed Description

The “AYUSH Health and Wellness Centre - Ayushman Bharat Yojana” is implemented by the Ayush Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide accessible, affordable, and holistic healthcare services based on Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani and Homeopathy systems to people of all age groups.
<br>

## Benefits

A total of 300 AYUSH Health and Wellness Centres are operating in the state. At these centres, medicines are distributed according to the specific disease, and yoga and naturopathy treatments are provided as per the condition.

  - Counselling based on nature testing and seasonal routines- screening and diagnosis of non-communicable diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, oral cancer, breast cancer, and cervical cancer, are conducted.
  - Medicinal plants are also distributed.
  - OPD charges have been fixed at ₹1.00 per person in rural areas and ₹2.00 per person in urban areas.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The beneficiary must be a resident of Uttarakhand.
- The beneficiary may belong to any age group.
- The beneficiary must be registered at a government-recognized Ayurvedic, Unani, or Homeopathic dispensary or hospital.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

The beneficiary needs to register at a government-recognized Ayurvedic, Unani, or Homeopathic dispensary or hospital.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to benefit from this scheme?

Any resident of Uttarakhand, irrespective of age, is eligible to avail services under the AYUSH Health and Wellness Centre scheme.
<br>

### What is the eligibility condition for registration?

The beneficiary must be registered at a government-recognized Ayurvedic, Unani, or Homeopathic dispensary or hospital in Uttarakhand.
<br>

### How many AYUSH Health and Wellness Centres are operating in Uttarakhand?

A total of 300 AYUSH Health and Wellness Centres are currently operating across the state.
<br>

### What type of healthcare services are provided under this scheme?

The centres offer Ayurvedic, Unani, and Homeopathic treatments, yoga and naturopathy sessions, disease-specific medicines, counselling, and preventive health services.
<br>

### What kind of diseases are screened or diagnosed at these centres?

Screening and diagnosis for non-communicable diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, oral cancer, breast cancer, and cervical cancer are carried out.
<br>

### Are counselling services available?

Yes.
<br>

### How can a person register under this scheme?

A person can register by visiting the nearest government-recognized Ayurvedic, Unani, or Homeopathic dispensary or hospital and enrolling as a beneficiary.
