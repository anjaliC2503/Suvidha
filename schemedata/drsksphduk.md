## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹25,000/- per year to deserving scholars pursuing a Ph.D. in Mathematics, supporting advanced research and reducing their financial burden.

## Detailed Description

The “Dr. S.K. Sharma (Ph.D. Mathematics)” scheme is implemented by Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar, under the Department of Agriculture, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to deserving scholars pursuing a Ph.D. in Mathematics, supporting advanced research and reducing their financial burden.
<br>

## Benefits

**Amount of Financial Assistance:** ₹25,000/- per year.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be enrolled in a Ph.D. program in Mathematics at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
   1. The financial assistance is provided for a duration of one year only.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Students are first invited to submit their applications on the prescribed form. A committee, constituted by the Vice Chancellor, then reviews and evaluates these applications following a defined procedure. Based on the committee’s recommendations, the Vice Chancellor grants approval, after which the selected students receive the payments.
<br>
