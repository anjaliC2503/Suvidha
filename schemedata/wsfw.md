## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide welfare support and financial assistance for women in distress, including widows, victims of violence, trafficked women, and economically disadvantaged women.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Welfare Services for Women&quot; component under the Mizoram Grant-in-Aid framework implemented by the Social Welfare Department, Mizoram, is designed to address the needs of vulnerable women facing social, economic, and psychological challenges. The scheme supports voluntary organizations in implementing programs that ensure safety, rehabilitation, and empowerment of women in difficult circumstances.
Target beneficiaries include widows without support, women affected by domestic violence, survivors of trafficking and sexual crimes, women affected by disasters, HIV/AIDS-affected women, and women without any means of livelihood.
The scheme provides financial assistance to organizations for establishing and running facilities such as working women hostels, short stay homes, training centres, and support services like legal aid, counseling, and helplines. It also promotes awareness, skill development, and income-generating activities to help women achieve self-reliance.
Special focus is given to providing safe accommodation, healthcare, legal support, and vocational training, enabling women to reintegrate into society with dignity and independence.
<br>

## Benefits

> **Shelter & Protection:**


- Establishment of Working Women Hostels.
- Short Stay Homes for women and girls.

> **Financial Support:**


- Assistance to widows without income.
- Support for women in distress.

> **Skill Development & Livelihood:**


  - Vocational training programmes.
  - Training-cum-production centres.
  - Support for self-employment.

> **Support Services:**


- Legal aid and counseling services.
- Helplines and crisis intervention support.

> **Health & Rehabilitation:**


- HIV/AIDS care, treatment, and counselling.
- Clinical and psychological support.

> **Awareness & Empowerment:**


- Workshops, seminars, and awareness programmes.
- Education on rights and entitlements.

<br>

## Eligibility

The following individual types of applicants are eligible under this scheme:

- Widows without family or financial support
- Women facing domestic violence
- Trafficked or rescued women/girls
- Women affected by natural disasters
- Women with HIV/AIDS
- Economically backward women
- Women labourers, including domestic workers

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- **Identification:** Eligible women identified through NGOs or authorities.  - **Proposal Submission (for NGOs):** Organizations submit project proposals to the Social Welfare Department.  - **Verification:** Authorities verify beneficiary eligibility and organization capability.  - **Approval:** Project approved based on merit and need.  - **Implementation:** Services like shelter, training, and counseling are provided.
