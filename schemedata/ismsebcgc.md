## Brief Description

Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of ₹15,000/- along with a certificate of appreciation is awarded to top 100 meritorious students of Class 10 & each stream of Class 12, who are listed in state-level merit list of Rajasthan Board of Secondary Education, Ajmer.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Incentive Scheme for Meritorious Students of Economically Backward Class of General Category&quot; was launched by the Education Department, Government of Rajasthan. Under this scheme, a one-time financial assistance of ₹15,000/- along with a certificate of appreciation is awarded to the top 100 meritorious students of Class 10 and each stream (Science, Commerce, Arts) of Class 12, who are listed in the state-level merit list of the Rajasthan Board of Secondary Education (RBSE), Ajmer. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Secondary Education, Rajasthan, Bikaner.

## Benefits

> **Financial Assistance:**


  - One-time financial assistance of ₹15,000/- is provided to economically backward students from the general category studying in government and private schools.

> **Certificate of Appreciation:**


  - An official certificate is awarded along with the monetary benefit.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a native of Rajasthan.
   1. The applicant should be a student.
   1. The applicant should belong to the economically backward class from the general category.
   1. The applicant should be studying in a government or private school.
   1. The applicant should have passed Class 10th, Praveshika, Class 12th (Science/Commerce/Arts), or Varishta Upadhyay examination conducted by Rajasthan Board of Secondary Education (RBSE), Ajmer.   1. The applicant should have secured a minimum of 75% marks in the qualifying examination.
   1. The applicant’s parents’ combined annual income from all sources should be less than ₹2,50,000.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Application Process:**

**Step 1:** To avail of the benefits of the scheme, students should fill out the online application form through the institution head (principal) of their respective school.
**Step 2:** Eligible students are identified by the head of their institution.
**Step 3:** Submit copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required) to the respective school.
**Step 4:** The school will visit the [Shala Darpan, Rajasthan](https://rajshaladarpan.rajasthan.gov.in/SD4/Home/Public2/Default.aspx) Portal.
**Step 5:** Using the school&#39;s login credentials (Login ID and Password), the school will fill and submit the application form. 
> **Post-Application Process:**

**Step 1:** The institution head forwards the online application to the concerned District Education Officer (DEO) Headquarters (Primary/Secondary Education).
**Step 2:** The District Education Officer compiles and forwards the verified applications of all eligible students from the district to the Directorate of Secondary Education, Rajasthan, Bikaner.
**Step 3:** Post-verification, the incentive amount is disbursed to the students&amp;#39; bank accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who implements the scheme?

The Directorate of Secondary Education, Rajasthan, Bikaner.
<br>

<br>

### How much financial benefit is provided under the scheme?

A one-time financial assistance of ₹15,000/-.

### What additional benefit is provided apart from the cash incentive?

A certificate of appreciation.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

General category students with 75% or above marks and parental income below ₹2,50,000/- per annum.

### Which classes' students are eligible for the scheme?

Students of Class 10th, Praveshika, Class 12th (Science, Commerce, Arts), and Varishta Upadhyay.

### How many students benefit each year?

A total of 400 students annually.

### What is the minimum percentage required for eligibility?

At least 75% marks in the qualifying examination.

### What documents are required to apply?

Aadhaar/Jan Aadhaar Card, Marksheet, and Income Certificate.

### How can a student apply for this scheme?

Through their school Principal via online application.

### Who verifies and forwards the applications?

The concerned District Education Officer (Primary/Secondary Education).

### When is the amount disbursed?

After verification of merit and eligibility.

### Is this benefit provided every year?

The benefit is given only once for each qualifying examination.
