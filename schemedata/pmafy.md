## Brief Description

The purpose of the Award is to recognize and felicitate individual(s)/ organization(s) who have made a significant impact on society for a sustained period by way of promotion and development of Yoga

## Detailed Description

The Prime Minister’s Award for Yoga under the Ministry of Ayush, Government of India, aims to recognize and felicitate individuals and organizations that have made a significant and sustained contribution to the promotion and development of Yoga, creating a positive impact on society.
**Number of Awardees:**

- 2 Awards for Individuals (National/International)
- 2 Awards for Organizations/Institutions (National/International)

<br>

## Benefits

The winners will be felicitated by means of a Trophy, Certificate and a Cash Award. The value of each Cash Award would be ₹ 25 lakh.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should have rich experience and a deep understanding of Yoga.
1. The applicant (individual category) must be at least 30 years of age.
1. The applicant should have a minimum of 10 years of service with an excellent track record in promoting and developing Yoga.

**Note:** The applicant can apply or be nominated for only one category (Individual or Institutional) in a particular year.
<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Registration:**
**Step 1:** Visit the o[fficial website](https://awards.gov.in/) and click on the[ Registration button.](https://awards.gov.in/Registration/SignUp)
**Step 2:** Select your category: Individual or Organization.
**Step 3:** Fill in the required details:
Individual: Name, Date of Birth, Nominator Type, Mobile, Email, Aadhaar Number.
Organization: Type, Authorized Person Name, Date of Birth, Organization Description, Designation, Mobile, Email, Aadhaar Number.
**Step 4:** Click Submit to complete registration.
**Apply:**
**Step 1:** [Log in](https://awards.gov.in/Login) to the portal using your registered credentials.
**Step 2:** Select the scheme from the list of awards and click "Nominate / Apply Now".
**Step 3:** Fill in all mandatory fields in the online application form.
**Step 4:** Click Submit to complete your application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Prime Minister’s Award for Yoga?

It is an award to recognize individuals and organizations for their significant contribution to the promotion and development of Yoga.
<br>

### Which ministry administers this award?

The award is administered by the Ministry of Ayush, Government of India.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this award?

Individuals and organizations with significant experience and contribution in Yoga are eligible.
<br>

### What is the minimum age required for individuals?

The applicant must be at least 30 years old.
<br>

### Can organizations apply for the award?

Yes, both individuals and institutions can apply under separate categories.
<br>

### Can an applicant apply for multiple categories?

No, an applicant can apply for only one category (Individual or Institutional) in a year.
<br>

### What does the award include?

The award includes a trophy, certificate, and a cash prize of ₹ 25 lakh.
<br>

### How many awards are given each year?

A total of 4 awards are given—2 for individuals and 2 for institutions.
<br>

### Are international applicants eligible?

Yes, both national and international applicants can be considered.
<br>

### What happens after screening?

The Selection Committee evaluates shortlisted candidates and makes final decisions.
<br>

### Does the award consider social impact?

Yes, impact on society and promotion of ethical and spiritual values are key factors.
<br>
