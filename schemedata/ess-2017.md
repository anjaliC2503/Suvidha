## Brief Description

The scheme aims to create an environment conducive for attracting investment in Goa, incentivise existing units, and boost local employment. It provides a subsidy based on the percentage of Goan employees hired, restricting reimbursement to ₹7,000/- per Goan employee per month.

## Detailed Description

The “Employment Subsidy Scheme, 2017” was launched by the Department of Industries, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to create an environment conducive for attracting investment in the State of Goa and to incentivise existing units to be competitive while giving boost to local employment. The scheme provides a proportional subsidy based on the percentage of Goan manpower employed, ranging from 60% to 100%. The applicant must be a Micro, Small, Medium, or Large manufacturing industry, or a Hinterland Eco tourism project, and must engage a minimum of 60% of Goan manpower on regular employment. The scheme is implemented by the Directorate of Industries, Trade and Commerce. The applications for this scheme are accepted by submitting the application for at least one claim to the Directorate of Industries, Trade and Commerce (DITC).
<br>

## Benefits

- Reimbursement shall be restricted to ₹7,000/- per Goan employee per month.
- The total subsidy for Micro, Small, Medium enterprises and eco-tourism projects shall not exceed ₹25,00,000/- per annum, on a pro rata basis depending on the date of production.
- The total subsidy for Large enterprises shall not exceed ₹50,00,000/- per annum, on a pro rata basis depending on the date of production.
- Subsidy is calculated based on the ratio of percentage of Goan employment, which provides a proportional increase from 60% benefit for 60% Goan employees, up to 100% benefit for 80% and above Goan employees.

##### ***Benefit Disbursal***

- For Micro, Small, Medium enterprises and eco-tourism projects, the total wages paid to Goan employees shall be restricted to 50% before applying the reimbursement ratio.
- For Large enterprises, the total wages paid to Goan employees shall be restricted to 25% before applying the reimbursement ratio.
- The benefits under this scheme are subject to budgetary allocation.
- The period of grant of subsidy shall not exceed seven years.

##### ***Conditions***

- _The enterprise must allow and/or grant free access to any official authorized by the Directorate of Industries, Trade and Commerce for conducting inspection/supervision of the unit or the registers or holding discussions with the employed employees for ensuring proper utilisation of the financial incentives/subsidies granted by the State Government._
- _The enterprise must submit all necessary reports, information, and documents specified under the scheme or as required or directed by the Directorate of Industries, Trade and Commerce (DITC) from time to time or as and when required._

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be classified as a Micro, Small, Medium, or Large manufacturing industry.
- The applicant must be listed under the white category, green category, or orange category.
- The applicant must engage a minimum of 60% of Goan manpower on regular employment.
- The applicant must ensure the employees counted for the percentage calculation are employed on the enterprise’s payroll continuously during the claim year.
- The applicant must ensure the Goan employees considered for subsidy calculation are up to 60 years of age.
- The applicant must ensure the Goan employees considered for subsidy calculation are paid a gross salary of more than ₹8,000/- per month.
- The applicant must ensure that the relations defined under section 56 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, are not counted as employees for the purpose of this scheme.
- The applicant must not have availed of the Chief Minister’s Rojgar Yojana (CMRY) Scheme or any other employment generation scheme of the Government of India or the Government of Goa.
- The applicant must not be taking or have taken any benefit under any policy of the Department of Information Technology, Government of Goa.
- The applicant must not have claimed similar benefit under any other scheme/program of the Government of Goa.
- The applicant must submit the application for at least one claim to the Directorate of Industries, Trade and Commerce (DITC) during the scheme's validity (up to September 30, 2022).

##### ***Eligibility for Hinterland Eco-Tourism Projects***

- The applicant must be a project of Hinterland Eco tourism in lesser developed talukas.
- The applicant must be registered with/recognised by the Department of Tourism/Goa Investment Promotion Board (IPB)/Town & Country Planning/Planning & Development Authority (PDA)/Eco Sensitive Zone Authority.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/employment_new.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the **Directorate of Industries, Trade and Commerce, Goa**.

## Documents Required

##### ***Claim Submission Documents***

- A Self-Attested Copy Of Registration Under Umbrella Scheme.
- A Self-Declaration In The Format Provided.
- A Copy Of Payroll Of The Unit Highlighting The Number Of Total Employees, And Monthly Salaries Paid To Them In That Year.
- A Statement Of Goan Employees Excluding Non-Eligible Goans And Monthly Salaries Paid To Them In That Year.
- Documentary Evidence To Support That The Employees Are Goans As Specified In Clause (3) Above (E.g., Person Born In Goa, Or Person Having 10 Years Residence Certificate Issued By The Mamlatdar, Or Other Documents Indicating Residence In Goa Issued Prior To 10 Years Like Driving License, Ration Card, Election Photo Identity Card (EPIC), Passport).
- A Copy Of Audited Balance Sheet Of The Unit For The Claim Year.
- A Copy Of Goods And Services Tax (GST) Returns Filed By The Unit For The Claim Year.
- Copies Of Employees’ State Insurance (ESI)/Provident Fund (PF) Returns (Wherever Applicable).
- A Copy Of Partnership Deed, Memorandum Of Association/Articles Of Association, As The Case May Be.

##### ***Specific Documents for Hinterland Eco Tourism Applications***

- Certificate Of Registration Or Approval Issued By The Department Of Tourism/Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) Authority/Goa Investment Promotion Board (IPB)/Eco Sensitive Zone Authority Shall Be Submitted.

##### ***Mandatory Chartered Accountant (CA) Certificates***

- A Chartered Accountant Certificate Certifying The Amount Paid To The Goan Regular Employees Towards Salary During That Year.
- A Certificate From Chartered Accountant Giving Details Of Salary Paid To The Employees Unit-Wise (In Case Of More Than One Unit Of Any Kind Of The Same Entity).
- Certificate From Chartered Accountant Stating That No Claim From The Department Of Information Technology, Government Of Goa Has Been Made For Any Incentives.
- A Certificate From Chartered Accountant Certifying That No Similar Benefit Has Been Claimed By The Applicant Under Any Other Scheme/Program Of Government Of Goa.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum duration for which an enterprise may continue to receive the subsidy benefits once they have been deemed eligible under the terms of this policy?

The maximum period for which the grant of subsidy shall be permitted to all eligible enterprises is restricted to seven years, subject to the general conditions outlined in the Umbrella Scheme.
<br>

### If my business unit has already received funding or assistance from the Chief Minister's Rojgar Yojana Scheme, can I still receive financial aid under this policy?

No, units that have availed of the Chief Minister’s Rojgar Yojana Scheme or any other employment generation scheme provided by the Government of India or the Government of Goa are not eligible for benefits under this scheme.
<br>

### What specific types of manufacturing units are qualified to apply for incentives, especially regarding their environmental risk classification?

Micro, Small, Medium, and Large manufacturing industries are eligible, provided they are officially listed under the white category, Green category, or Orange category.
<br>

### Since the program provides financial reimbursement, is the ultimate payment of the funds guaranteed upon meeting all eligibility and compliance requirements?

No, all benefits provided under this scheme are explicitly stated to be subject to the availability of budgetary allocation. No Promissory Estoppel shall be applicable if benefits are discontinued because the allocation is exceeded.
<br>

### If I have Goan employees earning below the minimum salary threshold, do they count towards meeting the mandatory percentage of local manpower required?

Yes, those Goan employees paid a gross salary of less than ₹8,000/- per month are eligible for counting towards the percentage of Goan manpower, but they are excluded from the actual subsidy calculation.
<br>

### Is there a specific deadline by which my company must initiate the application process if we wish to avail of benefits that extend beyond the scheme's formal validity date?

Yes, to be eligible for benefit disbursement beyond the scheme's validity (September 30, 2022), the applicant must submit an application for at least one claim to the Directorate of Industries, Trade and Commerce during the scheme’s validity period.
<br>

### How is an employee defined as a "Goan" for the strict purpose of calculating the percentage of local manpower required for eligibility?

A "Goan" is defined as a person born in Goa, or a person having a ten years Residence Certificate issued by the Mamlatdar, or a person possessing other suitable documentation proving residence in Goa issued prior to ten years, such as a Driving license, Ration card, Election Photo Identity Card (EPIC), or Passport.
<br>

### Is there any maximum age limit specified for Goan employees whose salaries are included when determining the amount of the employment subsidy?

Yes, for Goan employees to be considered eligible for inclusion in the subsidy calculation, they must be up to sixty years of age.
<br>

### If my company claims financial support for employment benefits under this program, am I barred from claiming assistance for similar expenses elsewhere?

Yes, this scheme specifically states that it shall not be applicable to cases where a similar benefit has already been claimed under any other scheme or program operated by the Government of Goa.
<br>

### Are projects specifically related to tourism also allowed to apply, and if so, what special criteria must these projects meet?

Yes, projects categorized as Hinterland Eco tourism in lesser developed talukas are eligible, provided they are registered with or recognized by the Department of Tourism, Goa-Investment Promotion Board, Town & Country Planning, Planning and Development Authority, or Eco Sensitive Zone Authority.
<br>

### Will an enterprise be eligible for this employment subsidy if they are currently receiving or have previously received incentives from the Department of Information Technology?

No, enterprises that are currently taking or have previously taken any benefit under any policy offered by the Department of Information Technology, Government of Goa, shall not be eligible.
<br>

### For the purpose of meeting the required 60% Goan employment threshold, how must the employees be maintained on the enterprise’s records?

The employees counted for the percentage calculation must be employed on the enterprise’s payroll continuously throughout the duration of the claim year to be considered for both the benefit calculation and the percentage requirement.
<br>

### What is the minimum percentage of local manpower that must be engaged by the enterprise to qualify for receiving benefits under this program?

Enterprises must engage a minimum of 60% of Goan manpower on regular employment to be eligible for benefits under this scheme.
<br>

### Should I include family members or individuals related to me when calculating the total number of eligible employees for this employment program?

No, relations defined under section 56 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, cannot be counted as employees for the purpose of calculating eligibility or subsidy under this scheme.
<br>
