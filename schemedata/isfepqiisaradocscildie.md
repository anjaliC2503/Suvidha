## Brief Description

The objective of the scheme is to encourage small cardamom growers to bring more area under irrigation and land development in order to increase productivity and improve the quality of small cardamom.

## Detailed Description

The scheme for Irrigation Equipment (Nos.) implemented by the Spices Board under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, and as per the modalities and scheme guidelines approved by the Competent Authority for the implementation of the “Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion and Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom” during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26), financial assistance is provided to motivate growers to bring more area under irrigation and land development. The scheme aims to increase productivity and improve the quality of small cardamom through the adoption of different irrigation methods. 
**Assistance is provided for the installation of the following equipment:** 
(a) Gravity Irrigation Equipment, 
(b) Irrigation Pump Sets (IP Sets), and 
(c) Sprinkler, Drip, and Micro-sprinkler Sets.
<br>

## Benefits

1. 50 % of cost of equipments subject to maximum of ₹ 5000 (Gen) and, ₹11250 (SC/ST) for Gravity; ₹15,000 (Gen) and ₹45,000 (SC/ST) for IP set and ₹ 32,000/- (Gen) and ₹ 95,000/- (SC/ST) for Sprinkler/ micro sprinkler sets.
1. Registered cardamom growers having land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha are eligible for Gravity and Irrigation pump set (IP Set).
1. Registered cardamom growers having land holding size of 0.4 Ha to 8 Ha are eligible for Sprinklers / Drip / Microirrigation equipments.

**Note:** Any farmer who has availed this benefit earlier is not eligible to avail the benefit.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a small cardamom grower from the states of Kerala, Tamil Nadu, or Karnataka.
1. The applicant should install the approved irrigation equipment, and the installation must be inspected and recommended as eligible by the designated officials.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Registration**
Step 1: The applicant should visit the [ServicesPlus](https://serviceonline.gov.in/dbt/) website.
Step 2: Click on the “[Register” ](https://serviceonline.gov.in/dbt/citizenRegistration.html)option on the homepage.
Step 3: Fill out the registration form by entering Full Name, Email ID, Mobile Number, Password, selecting State, and Captcha.
Step 4: Review all details carefully and submit the registration form.
Step 5: An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number or email; enter the OTP and click “Verify.”
Step 6: Registration will be completed successfully, and a confirmation message will be displayed or sent via email.
**Login**
Step 1: The applicant should visit the ServicesPlus website and click on [“Login.”](https://serviceonline.gov.in/dbt/loginWindow.do)
Step 2: Enter your Email ID, Password, and Captcha.
Step 3: Select the relevant service/scheme name.
Step 4: Fill in the required details in the application form.
Step 5: Submit the application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Irrigation Equipment Scheme?

The scheme aims to encourage small cardamom growers to bring more area under irrigation and land development to improve productivity and quality of small cardamom.
<br>

### Who can apply for the scheme?

Registered small cardamom growers from Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka can apply.
<br>

### What is the eligible land holding size for Gravity Equipment and IP Sets?

Registered cardamom growers having land holdings from 0.10 hectare to 8 hectares are eligible.
<br>

### What is the eligible land holding size for Sprinkler/Drip/Micro-irrigation systems?

Registered cardamom growers having land holdings from 0.40 hectare to 8 hectares are eligible.
<br>

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for applying?

Yes, submission of Aadhaar Card is mandatory.
<br>

### Can a farmer who already received assistance apply again?

No. Farmers who have already availed benefits under this scheme are not eligible to apply again.
<br>

### Who conducts the inspection of installed equipment?

Designated officials will conduct the final inspection of the installed irrigation equipment.
<br>

### When is the subsidy approved?

Subsidy is recommended only after successful inspection and approval by designated officials.
<br>

### Can quotations from local dealers be submitted?

Yes. If there is no authorized dealer, applicants may submit three competitive quotations from local dealers.
