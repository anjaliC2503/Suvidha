## Brief Description

The "Scheme for Assistance for Research & Development Activities" under the Gujarat Industrial Policy promotes innovation by encouraging R&D among companies and institutions, fostering technological advancements to drive "Atmanirbhar Gujarat."

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Scheme for Assistance for Research &amp; Development Activities&quot; is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative promotes sustainable industrial growth by supporting industries in adopting eco-friendly practices and ensuring compliance with environmental norms.
The component &quot;Assistance for Contract/Sponsored Research Work&quot; provides financial support to eligible institutions for establishing R&amp;D or product development centers. It aims to promote innovation, technological advancements, and product design improvements.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** Up to 50% of the project cost (excluding land and building costs).  - **Maximum Funding:** A maximum of ₹50,00,000/- per project.  - **Support for Research Development:** Facilitates technological advancements through sponsored research.

## Eligibility

> **Eligible institution:**


   1. All Research and Development Institutions recognized by the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) / Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR).
   1. Institutions set up by the Government of Gujarat (GoG) / Government of India (Gol).
   1. All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) approved institutions/professional colleges, (including autonomous as well as those colleges affiliated with Universities).
   1. Any other institution as approved by SLEC.

<br>

> **Eligible Activities:**


   1. Proposals for contract/sponsored research by eligible institutions from Industrial Associations or individual industrial undertaking or government or any other agency as approved by SLEC.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step-1:** The R&amp;D institution must apply to the Industries Commissionerate in the [prescribed format](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2023/2.%20CLAIM%20APPLICATION%20FORM%20SCHEME%202.pdf), along with all necessary [documents](https://ic.gujarat.gov.in/documents/commondoc/2023/1.%20CLAIM%20CHECKLIST%20SCHEME%202.pdf).
**Step-2:** The office of the Industries Commissioner will scrutinize the proposals to ensure compliance with the scheme&#39;s eligibility and guidelines.
**Step-3:** The proposals will be presented to the State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) for review and approval.
**Step-4:** Upon approval by SLEC, the reimbursement of the sanctioned assistance will be processed, and the claim will be disbursed by the office of the Industries Commissioner.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the "Scheme for Assistance for Research & Development Activities" scheme?

The "Scheme for Assistance for Research & Development Activities" is an umbrella scheme launched by the Industries and Mines Department, Gujarat under the Gujarat Industrial Policy. Effective from August 7th, 2020 to August 6th, 2025, this initiative promotes sustainable industrial growth by supporting industries in adopting eco-friendly practices and ensuring compliance with environmental norms.

### What is the purpose of the component "Assistance for Contract/Sponsored Research Work"?

The component "Assistance for Contract/Sponsored Research Work" provides financial support to eligible institutions for establishing R&D or product development centers. It aims to promote innovation, technological advancements, and product design improvements.

### Who is eligible to apply for the assistance for contract/sponsored research work?

Eligible institutions recognized by CSIR/DSIR, those set up by the Government of Gujarat (GoG) or Government of India (GoI), AICTE-approved institutions, and professional colleges are eligible to apply.

### Can an individual apply for the assistance?

No, the assistance is available only to eligible institutions in collaboration with industrial associations, government bodies, or other approved sponsors.

### What is the maximum amount of assistance available under the scheme?

The maximum assistance provided is [?]50,00,000/- per project during the operative period of the scheme.

### Are land and building costs eligible for funding under the scheme?

No, the assistance excludes costs related to land and buildings.

### What percentage of the total project cost is covered by the assistance?

The scheme covers 50% of the total project cost, excluding land and building expenses.

### Is there a specific format required for submitting the research proposal?

Yes, institutions must submit a detailed research proposal according to the format specified by the SLEC.

### Can an institution apply for more than one project under this scheme?

Yes, institutions can apply for multiple projects, provided each project meets the eligibility criteria.

### Is there a specific cost breakdown format required for the proposal?

Yes, the cost breakdown must exclude land and building expenses and adhere to the financial guidelines set by the SLEC.

### Can the assistance be used to purchase equipment for the research?

Yes, the assistance can be used for research-related expenses, including equipment, but it cannot be used for land and building costs.
