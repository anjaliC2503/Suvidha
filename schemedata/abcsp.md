## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide an opportunity for personnel from various government departments of the state to participate annually in the All-India level competitions in various sports, organized by the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, Government of India.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Akhil Bharatiya Civil Services Pratiyogita” was launched by the Department of Sports, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide an opportunity for personnel from various government departments of the state to participate annually in the All-India level competitions in various sports, organized by the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, Government of India.
<br>

## Benefits

There is a provision to provide travel allowance, accommodation allowance, food allowance, sports kits, and other necessary support to all players and team members participating in the &#39;Akhil Bharatiya Civil Services Pratiyogita&#39;, in accordance with departmental standards.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be an employee of a government department in Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

- Selection trials for the competition are conducted at the district and state levels, and the selected players are given the opportunity to participate in the Akhil Bharatiya Civil Services Pratiyogita.
  - Every year, approximately 150 personnel from various government departments of Uttarakhand benefit from this scheme by participating in the All-India Level Competition in various sports, organized by the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances, and Pensions, Government of India, for Central and State Government employees.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to participate in the scheme?

Any employee of a government department in Uttarakhand is eligible to participate.
<br>

### How are players selected for the competition?

Players are selected through trials conducted at the district and state levels.
<br>

### What types of support are provided to the selected participants?

Selected participants are provided travel allowance, accommodation allowance, food allowance, and sports kits as per departmental standards.
<br>

### How often is the competition held?

The competition is organized annually at the All-India level.
<br>

### How many personnel benefit from the scheme each year?

Approximately 150 personnel from various government departments of Uttarakhand benefit annually.
<br>

### At what levels are the selection trials conducted?

Selection trials are conducted at both the district and state levels.
<br>

### Can employees from all government departments participate?

Yes, employees from all state government departments in Uttarakhand are eligible.
<br>
