## Brief Description

The scheme aims to propagate and develop literature in the field of law in Hindi and other regional languages. It provides financial grants to voluntary organisations for publishing legal books, commentaries, and journals.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Assistance to Voluntary Organisations for Promotion of the Official Languages in the Field of Law" was launched by the Legislative Department, Government Of India. The scheme aims to propagate and develop literature in the field of law in Hindi and other regional languages. The scheme provides financial assistance covering up to 85% of the anticipated net expenditure for producing legal literature, such as commentaries, treatises, and law journals. The applicant must be a voluntary organisation undertaking activities conducive to the enrichment and use of Official Languages in the legal field. The scheme is implemented by the Ministry Of Law And Justice. The applications for this scheme are accepted offline via submission to the Official Languages Wing at Shastri Bhawan, New Delhi.
<br>

## Benefits

- Financial assistance in the form of a grant not exceeding 85% of the anticipated net expenditure involved in the implementation of the project or activity.
- The grants are paid in installments depending on the nature of activities to be undertaken and the progress of the work.

##### ***Conditions***

- _The organisation must bear the balance of the estimated expenditure on the scheme or project._
- _All assets created out of the grant must not be transferred to any person or institution without the prior concurrence of the Government of India._
- _If the organisation ceases to exist, the equipment purchased out of the central grant will revert to the Government of India._
- _The accounts of the organisation must be properly maintained and submitted as required for checking by the Legislative Department or Indian Audit And Accounts Department._
- _The organisation must exercise the utmost economy in its working._
- _The grant must be utilized strictly for the purpose for which it was sanctioned._
- _Progress reports about the project must be furnished at regular intervals._
- _An audited statement of expenditure and a report on the work done must be furnished immediately after the completion of the project or before drawing a subsequent installment._
- _The organisation must submit a Utilisation Certificate in respect of the grant received during the preceding year._
- _Such number of copies, not exceeding five, of all publications brought out with central assistance must be supplied to the Legislative Department free of cost._
- _The organisation must maintain an audited record of the assets acquired or created wholly or substantially with the help of the grant in the prescribed proforma._
- _The author of a published book must use the Hindi legal terms occurring in the authoritative texts of the Central Acts, State Acts, or the Legal Glossary published by the Official Languages Wing._
- _The organisation must give an undertaking to complete the project within a reasonable time fixed by the Government._

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a voluntary organisation or institution.
- The applicant must propose a project or activity for the development and propagation of literature in the field of law in Hindi or other regional languages.
- The applicant must undertake activities such as proposing commentaries, treatises, books, law journals, compendiums, or other relevant publications.
- The applicant must agree to bear the balance of the estimated expenditure for the proposed project.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

##### **Step 1:****** ***Procuring and Preparing the Application Form***
The applicant must obtain the [prescribed application form](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/71d6d251531656818d22b048aa08fff8.pdf) for financial assistance under the Central Government. The applicant must carefully fill out the application form in duplicate and ensure all required details regarding the organisation, assets, and project features are accurately provided.
##### **Step 2:****** ***Gathering and Attaching Required Documents***
The applicant must compile all the necessary supplementary documents, such as the institutional prospectus, audited accounts, and manuscript copies. The applicant must ensure that these duplicate documents are securely attached alongside the completed application forms.
##### **Step 3:****** ***Submitting the Application Offline***
The applicant must submit the complete application package to the ***Joint Secretary and Legislative Counsel at the Official Languages Wing, Room No. 742, 7th Floor 'A' Wing, Shastri Bhawan, New Delhi-110001.***

- Application in Duplicate
- Prospectus or Note Giving Aims and Objects of the Institution or Organisation
- Constitution of the Board of Management and Particulars of Each Member
- Latest Annual Report
- Audited Accounts for the Last Financial Year, along with Copies of Certified Balance Sheets
- Estimate of Income and Expenditure with Full Justification
- Statement of Grants Received from the State Government or Other Bodies
- Information Relating to Requests Made to Other State Governments or Bodies for Grants
- Undertaking That Estimates Will Not Be Modified without Prior Approval
- Copy of the Manuscript (in the case of a request for new publication work)
- Certificate from the Author Authorising the Institution to Undertake the Publication (in the case of a request for new publication work)
- Previous Publications of the Institution (for first applications)
- Publications Brought Out in the Interim Period (for subsequent requests)
- Statement of Qualifications and Experience of Staff Employed on the Project

## Frequently Asked Questions

### While our approved legal publication project is actively ongoing, do we need to keep the authorities informed about our progress, and if so, how should we do that?

You are required to furnish regular progress reports detailing the advancement of your approved project or activity at specified intervals.
<br>

### Once our organization has successfully published a legal book using the provided financial assistance, are we obligated to provide any free copies of the finished work to the government?

You must supply the Legislative Department with a specified number of free copies of all publications brought out with central assistance, not exceeding 5 copies.
<br>

### If it is discovered that our organization has not utilized the sanctioned money for the exact approved purpose, what actions can the government take against us?

The payment of further installments may be stopped, and you will be liable to refund the whole grant amount along with any interest decided by the Central Government.
<br>

### As a voluntary institution applying for financial backing to develop legal literature, what is the maximum percentage of the total project costs that the government will cover?

The financial assistance will cover a maximum of 85 percent of the anticipated net expenditure for your approved projects or activities.
<br>

### After receiving an installment of the grant, is there a specific document our organization must submit in the following year to prove that the funds were used correctly?

You are mandatorily required to submit a formal Utilisation Certificate detailing how the grant received during the preceding year was applied toward the approved project.
<br>

### In the unfortunate event that our voluntary institution stops operating or ceases to exist, what will happen to the equipment and assets that were purchased using the central grant?

If your institution ceases to exist at any time, all equipment and assets purchased out of the central grant will immediately revert to the Government of India.
<br>

### Our voluntary organization is planning to produce several different types of legal literature; what specific formats or types of publications are eligible for receiving this financial assistance?

You can receive grants for developing commentaries, treatises, books on legal subjects, law journals, law compendiums, and other publications that enrich the use of official languages in law.
<br>

### Since the financial assistance is released in multiple installments, what specific conditions must our organization fulfill before we can request and receive the next portion of the funds?

Subsequent installments are paid only after a major portion of the previous installment is utilized, an audited statement is furnished, and the Legislative Department is satisfied with the work progress.
<br>

### When our authors are drafting the manuscripts for the proposed legal books, are there any specific guidelines or glossaries they must follow regarding the legal terminology used in the text?

Authors must use legal terms from the authoritative texts of Central or State Acts, or the Legal Glossary published by the Official Languages Wing of the Ministry of Law and Justice.
<br>

### Once our project proposal and its associated cost estimates have been reviewed and approved by the authorities, are we permitted to make any subsequent modifications to these financial estimates?

You must give an undertaking that the approved estimates will not be modified without obtaining prior formal approval from the Legislative Department.
<br>

### Knowing that the financial assistance only covers a portion of the costs, who is responsible for providing the remaining funds required to successfully complete our proposed legal publication project?

Your organization is required to bear the remaining balance of the estimated expenditure necessary to execute and finalize the proposed project.
<br>

### To ensure transparency and proper utilization of the financial assistance, will our institution's accounts and project activities be subject to any kind of formal inspection by government authorities?

Your organization will be open to inspection by an officer from either the Legislative Department of the Ministry of Law and Justice or the Indian Audit and Accounts Department.
<br>

### When applying for the grant, are we required to commit to finishing our proposed publication activities within a specific timeframe, or is the timeline completely open-ended?

You must provide an undertaking before drawing the grant that the project will be completed within a reasonable time frame fixed by the Government.
<br>

### If our institution purchases specific equipment using the grant money provided by the government, are we allowed to transfer or sell these assets to another entity in the future?

Any assets acquired wholly or substantially with the grant cannot be transferred, disposed of, encumbered, or utilized for other purposes without prior approval from the Legislative Department.
<br>
