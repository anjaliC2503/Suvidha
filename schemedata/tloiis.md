## Brief Description

The Lokpal of India Internship Scheme offers students exposure to anti-corruption laws and institutions. Interns learn about governance, legal frameworks, and contribute ideas to enhance transparency and accountability in public administration.

## Detailed Description

The Lokpal of India launched “The Lokpal of India Internship Scheme”. The interns will get an opportunity to understand anti-corruption mechanisms, including the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013, and India’s commitment under the United Nations Convention Against Corruption and related frameworks. They will also learn about the impact of corruption on governance and contribute innovative ideas to strengthen transparency, accountability, and effective functioning of institutions like the Lokpal and other vigilance bodies.
<br>

> **Duration:**

The period of internship shall be up to 3 months.
<br>

> **Attendance:**

The interns will have to attend the office on all working days. 
<br>

## Benefits

- A stipend of ₹10,000/- per month. 
- The Lokpal of India gives a certificate to the interns completing the internship successfully.
- The Lokpal of India provides working space, internet, and other appropriate facilities to the interns.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a student. 
1. The applicant must be enrolled in an institution affiliated with a recognized university within India.
1. The applicant should be pursuing a regular undergraduate or postgraduate degree course in the field of law, political science, public administration, public policy, management, economics, or human rights.
1. The applicant must have secured at least 75% or equivalent marks in the 12th class.1. The undergraduate student must have completed at least two years of study in the undergraduate course.
1. The undergraduate student must have a minimum aggregate of % or equivalent marks in years completed till the date of application.
1. The postgraduate students must have a minimum aggregate of 55% or equivalent in the graduation course.
1. The applicant must have his own laptop for the internship. 

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested candidate can apply through their institute/college/university using the [Annexure-I](https://lokpal.gov.in/pdfs/Invite_internship.pdf) [application form](https://lokpal.gov.in/pdfs/Advertisement_2023-24_Internship_Scheme_04012024.pdf). 
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents by post:
> **Under Secretary (Establishment), Lokpal of India, 6, Vasant Kunj Institutional Area, Phase-II, New Delhi - 110070.**

**NOTE:** The final list of selected interns will be displayed on the Lokpal of India website/notice board.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When was the Lokpal of India established?

The Lokpal of India was established under the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013 to combat corruption in public administration.
<br>

### Which international convention has India ratified to combat corruption?

India has ratified the United Nations Convention Against Corruption in 2011.

### What is the duration of the internship?

The duration of the internship is up to 3 months.
<br>

### Which students are eligible to apply for the internship?

Students pursuing regular undergraduate or postgraduate courses in Law, Political Science, Public Administration, Public Policy, Management, Economics, or Human Rights, meeting the prescribed academic criteria, are eligible.


<br>

### Where can students find information about internship vacancies?

Applications are considered based on availability of vacancies, which are managed by the Lokpal of India.
<br>

### What is the deadline for submitting internship applications?

Applications must be submitted within one month of the publication or issuance of the advertisement.

### What is the role of the applicant's institute in the application process?

The application must be duly recommended by the applicant’s institute.
<br>

### Who is responsible for selecting the interns?

Interns are selected by a committee appointed by the Hon’ble Chairperson of the Lokpal of India.


<br>

### What criteria will be used for selecting the interns?

The selection criteria include the course pursued and academic performance.

### Are interns required to attend the office every day?

Yes, interns are required to attend the office on all working days.

### Is there a stipend provided for the internship, and if so, how much?

Yes, a stipend of Rs. 10,000 per month will be provided to each intern upon successful completion of the internship. A proportionate amount will be paid for part of the month.

### Are interns required to bring any equipment for the internship?

Yes, interns are required to bring their own laptops.
