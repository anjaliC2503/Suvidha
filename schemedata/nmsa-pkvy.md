## Brief Description

The scheme "National Mission For Sustainable Agriculture-Parmparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (NMSA-PKVY)" is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme that aims to promote organic farming practices through providing financial assistance for organic inputs and infrastructure support to farmers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "National Mission For Sustainable Agriculture-Parmparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (NMSA-PKVY)" is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme that aims to promote traditional and sustainable organic farming practices among farmers by providing financial support and infrastructure assistance. The scheme extends financial assistance for a maximum of 2 hectares per farmer for organic farming inputs, infrastructure development for on-farm organic input production, and related activities.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** Assistance is provided for a maximum of 2 hectares per farmer for inputs for organic farming, on-farm input production infrastructure, etc.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a farmer.
  - The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

&gt; **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/) Portal.
**Step 2:** Click on &amp;#39;Login&amp;#39; from the top right-hand side, then click on &amp;quot;Beneficiary Registration&amp;quot; and select any one from the &amp;#39;Farmer&amp;#39; or &amp;#39;Institutional&amp;#39; or &amp;#39;Except For the Farmer&amp;#39; as the Beneficiary Type.

**Step 4:** Enter your Mobile Number and Email address, click &amp;#39;Send OTP&amp;#39;, enter the received OTP, create and confirm a Password, solve the Captcha, and click &amp;#39;Submit&amp;#39;.

**Step 1:** The applicant may visit and apply online at the [I-Khedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) Portal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "National Mission For Sustainable Agriculture-Parmparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (NMSA-PKVY)"?

The "National Mission For Sustainable Agriculture-Parmparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (NMSA-PKVY)" is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme aimed at promoting organic farming through financial assistance for inputs and infrastructure.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the NMSA-PKVY scheme?

Any farmer of Gujarat state is eligible to apply for the NMSA-PKVY scheme.
<br>

### What is the maximum landholding limit for availing benefits under this scheme?

The applicant farmer can avail financial assistance for a maximum of 2 hectares of land under the scheme.
<br>

### What kind of financial help is provided under the NMSA-PKVY scheme?

The scheme provides financial assistance for organic farming inputs and infrastructure for on-farm input production.
<br>

### Is there any restriction on gender or category for availing this scheme?

The scheme is open to all farmers irrespective of gender, caste, or social and economic standards.
<br>

### Who implements the NMSA-PKVY scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department of the Government of Gujarat in collaboration with the Central Government.
<br>

### Is there any income limit for applying under the scheme?

No, there is no individual or family income limit prescribed for the scheme.
<br>

### Is there any fee charged for applying to the scheme?

No, there is no application fee required for applying to the NMSA-PKVY scheme.
<br>

### How can eligible farmers apply for the scheme?

Eligible farmers can apply for the scheme by visiting the [iKhedut](https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in/site/) portal, filling out the application form online, uploading the required documents, and submitting it for departmental verification.
<br>

### What documents are required for applying under the NMSA-PKVY scheme?

The applicant needs Aadhaar Card, 7/12 Certificate, Bank Pass Book or Cancelled Cheque, and the filled application form.
<br>
