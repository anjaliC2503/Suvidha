## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide BPL-equivalent benefits to families with two or more specially-abled members. Under this scheme, eligible families receive free medical treatment, ration supplies, and other welfare benefits.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Aastha Yojana&quot; was launched by the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to provide BPL-equivalent benefits to families with multiple specially-abled members. Under this scheme, eligible families receive free medical treatment, ration supplies, and other welfare benefits. To avail of the benefits, a family must possess an Aastha Card, and their annual income should not exceed ₹1.20 lakh.

## Benefits

- Aastha cardholder families receive benefits similar to BPL families, including free medical treatment and ration supplies.

**Note:** Aastha cardholders are entitled to benefits from all BPL-related schemes run by different state government departments.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a native of Rajasthan.
1. The two or more members should be specially abled from the same family.
1. The applicant must have a disability of 40%.
1. The annual family income should not exceed ₹1,20,000/-.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit office of the District Social Justice &amp; Empowerment Office and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the staff exclusively entrusted to issue and collect filled-in applications.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to the District Social Justice &amp; Empowerment Office.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the District Social Justice &amp; Empowerment Office to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the Aastha Scheme?

Families with at least two specially-abled members (40% or more disability) and an annual income below [?]1,20,000 are eligible.

### What benefits does the scheme provide?

The scheme provides free medical services, ration supplies, and access to BPL-equivalent welfare schemes.

### Is there any fee for applying for the Aastha Card?

No, applying for the Aastha Card is completely free of cost.

### Can an individual apply for this scheme?

No, this scheme is for families with at least two eligible members with disabilities.

### Can Aastha Cardholders avail of other government schemes?

Yes, they are eligible for all BPL-category welfare schemes run by various departments.

### Whom should I contact for more information?

The applicant can contact the District Social Justice & Empowerment Office for queries and application assistance.

### Can I apply online for this scheme?

No, the application process is offline.

### Will the Aastha Card expire?

The card remains valid as long as the eligibility criteria are met and for the latest update, contact the department.

### Where can I apply for the Aastha Card?

The applicant can apply at the District Social Justice & Empowerment Office in your district.

### What documents are needed to apply?

Need an Aadhaar Card, Residence Proof, Disability Certificate, etc.
