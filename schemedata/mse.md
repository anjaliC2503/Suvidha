## Brief Description

The scheme provides ₹1000/- per month to eligible students for pursuing B.E. from a recognized engineering college in Chhattisgarh.  The applicants can either be students who have passed their 10th and 12th standard examinations, or Diploma holders newly admitted to the second year of B.E.

## Detailed Description

"Merit Scholarship: Engineering University" is a welfare scheme by the Technical Education Department, Government of Chhattisgarh. The scheme provides ₹1000/- per month to eligible students for pursuing B.E. from a recognized engineering college in Chhattisgarh. The applicants can either be students who have passed their 10th and 12th standard examinations with a minimum of 60% marks, or Diploma holders newly admitted to the second year of B.E. must have passed the final year examination of their diploma with a minimum of 60% marks.

## Benefits

- For studying in the first year of the B.E. (Bachelor of Engineering) program: ₹1,000/- per month.
- For students newly admitted to the second year of B.E. through lateral entry: ₹1,000/- per month.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a native resident student of Chhattisgarh.


- The applicant must be currently studying in a recognized engineering college in Chhattisgarh.
- The applicant must be pursuing an engineering degree course.
- Students who have passed their 10th and 12th standard examinations with a minimum of 60% marks from any recognized institution located in Chhattisgarh will be eligible.
- Diploma holders newly admitted to the second year of B.E. must have passed the final year examination of their diploma with a minimum of 60% marks will also be eligible.

<br>

**Other Conditions -** 

- Without special approval from the government, no student will receive more than one scholarship at the same time. However, if the institution where he is studying has given him any fee concession, then he will be entitled to continue receiving it.
- If any scholarship becomes vacant during its duration, then the approving authority can give such scholarships for its unexpired period to the next eligible and deserving candidate from the merit list.
- Only those students will be eligible to receive scholarships who have passed the qualifying examination from any testing body of Chhattisgarh. But children of such permanent government employees of Chhattisgarh can be exempted from this condition who are on deputation to the Government of India or any other state government.
- Such students will not be eligible to receive scholarships who are in full-time employment. In this regard, only one concession can be given that any such student, who is regularly studying in a recognized institution and is receiving any scholarship, can be allowed to do part-time work.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the **Office of the Principal of the Institution** and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the institute staff.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to the staff. The Principal will verify the documents and forward the application to the Directorate for approval (New applications) or process the renewal (for existing beneficiaries).
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the staff to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the eligibility criteria for students applying for the first year of Bachelor of Engineering under this scholarship scheme?

Applicants must have passed both 10th and 12th standard examinations with at least 60% marks from a recognized institution in Chhattisgarh and be currently enrolled in a recognized engineering college in the state.

### How can diploma holders qualify for this scholarship when joining the second year of Bachelor of Engineering through lateral entry?

Diploma holders must have secured a minimum of 60% marks in their final year diploma examination and be newly admitted to the second year of B.E. in a recognized engineering college in Chhattisgarh.

### Is there any income limit or family income requirement to be eligible for this scholarship?

No, there is no income limit specified for applicants under this scholarship scheme.

### What is the monthly financial assistance provided to eligible students under this scholarship program?

Eligible students receive ₹1,000/- per month for the duration of their Bachelor of Engineering studies.

### Do I need to be a resident of Chhattisgarh to apply for this scholarship?

Yes, only native resident students of Chhattisgarh are eligible to apply.

### Which educational courses are covered under this scholarship scheme?

The scholarship is exclusively for students pursuing a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) degree in a recognized engineering college in Chhattisgarh.

### What minimum academic performance is required in the qualifying examination to be eligible for this scholarship?

Applicants must have secured at least 60% marks in their 10th and 12th standard examinations or in their final year diploma examination, depending on their entry route.

### Can students from any recognized board or institution in Chhattisgarh apply for this scholarship?

Yes, students who have passed 10th and 12th from any recognized institution located in Chhattisgarh with 60% marks are eligible.

### What documents are mandatory for submitting a scholarship application?

Applicants must provide a residential certificate of Chhattisgarh and mark sheets of their qualifying examination.

### Where should I obtain the application form for this scholarship?

Interested applicants must visit the Office of the Principal of their institution and request a hard copy of the prescribed application form.

### How should I fill out and submit the scholarship application form?

Fill in all mandatory fields, attach a passport-sized photograph (signed if required), and submit self-attested copies of required documents to the institute staff within the prescribed period.

### Who verifies the submitted scholarship application, and what is the next step after submission?

The Principal verifies the documents and forwards the application to the Directorate for approval (new applications) or renewal processing (existing beneficiaries).

### Should I obtain any proof of submission after applying for the scholarship?

Yes, request a receipt or acknowledgement containing essential details like submission date, time, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

### Can students already receiving this scholarship renew it for subsequent years?

Yes, the Principal processes renewals for existing beneficiaries after verification.
