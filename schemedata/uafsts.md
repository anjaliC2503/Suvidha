## Brief Description

The scheme "Uniform Assistance for Scheduled Tribe Students" by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat, provides ₹900/- for three pairs of uniforms to ST students in government and aided primary schools studying in Classes 1 to 8.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Uniform Assistance for Scheduled Tribe Students&quot; by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to support students from the Scheduled Tribe category by reducing the burden of education-related expenses and promoting school attendance. The scheme ensures that eligible students from Classes 1st to 8th studying in government and aided schools receive ₹900/- for three pairs of school uniforms annually, enhancing their dignity and inclusion in the school environment.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹900/- is provided to each eligible student for the purchase of three pairs of school uniforms.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a student.
  - The applicant should belong to the state of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be studying in Class 1st to Class 8th.
  - The applicant should be enrolled in a government or government-aided primary school.
  - The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) category.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Uniform Assistance for Scheduled Tribe Students" scheme?

The objective of the "Uniform Assistance for Scheduled Tribe Students" scheme is to provide financial support to Scheduled Tribe students studying in Classes 1st to 8th by offering monetary assistance for school uniforms in order to reduce the financial burden on their families and promote regular school attendance.

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Any student who belongs to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) community, is currently enrolled in Class 1st to Class 8th in a school located in Gujarat, and resides in the state of Gujarat is eligible to apply for the Uniform Assistance for Scheduled Tribe Students scheme.
<br>

<br>

### How much monetary benefit is provided under this scheme?

Under this scheme, each eligible student is provided with ₹900/- per academic year, which is meant specifically for the purchase of three pairs of school uniforms.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit for the applicants to be eligible for this scheme?

There is no individual or family income limit specified under this scheme, which means that all students belonging to the Scheduled Tribe community are eligible regardless of their economic background.
<br>

<br>

### How often is the financial benefit disbursed to the beneficiary under the scheme?

The financial assistance is disbursed once every academic year, and eligible students can apply for and receive the benefit annually.
<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant apply for this scheme?

Applicants can apply through the [Digital Gujarat](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/) portal.

### Can a student studying in a private school also apply for this scheme?

Yes, students belonging to the Scheduled Tribe community who are enrolled in any recognized school, whether government or private, are eligible to apply as long as they meet the academic and category requirements specified under the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### What happens if an applicant provides incorrect or incomplete information in the form?

If the applicant provides incorrect or incomplete information in the application form, the application may be rejected during the verification process, and the student will not be eligible to receive the financial assistance until the correct information is submitted and approved.
