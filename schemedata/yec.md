## Brief Description

The scheme aims to promote environmental awareness and sustainable practices among students by organizing programmes and activities such as plastic reduction initiatives, kitchen gardening, etc. along with various competitions to engage and empower students in protecting the environment.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Youth and Eco Club” is implemented under the Samagra Shiksha Programme by the Secondary Education Department, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to promote environmental awareness and sustainable practices among students by organizing programmes and activities such as plastic reduction initiatives, kitchen gardening, etc. along with various competitions to engage and empower students in protecting the environment.
<br>

## Benefits

Under the Youth and Eco Club in schools, awareness programmes, competitions, and activities were organized on plastic reduction, kitchen gardening, tree plantation, environmental protection, waste management, cleanliness, and water conservation, etc.

  - Bagless Day and Environment Friendly Awareness.
  - Awareness Through Skits and Plays.
  - Poster makings, Paintings, Slogan writing etc. Competition and &quot;Say No&quot; to Single Use plastic.
  - Cleaning Drives of the School and Surrounding and Keeping Toilets Usable.
  - Composting Food Waste in the School.
  - Planting of Trees in Schools.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. All primary, upper primary, and secondary government schools in Uttarakhand are eligible.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

There is no selection process; all primary, upper primary, and secondary government schools in Uttarakhand are included.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main objective of the scheme?

The main objective is to engage students in activities that encourage environmental protection, reduce plastic use, promote cleanliness, and build a culture of sustainability.
<br>

### Who is eligible under this scheme?

All primary, upper primary, and secondary government schools of Uttarakhand are eligible under this scheme.
<br>

### Is there any selection process for schools?

No, there is no selection process. All government schools at the primary, upper primary, and secondary levels are automatically included.
<br>

### What types of activities are organized under the Youth and Eco Club?

Activities include plastic reduction drives, kitchen gardening, tree plantation, cleanliness campaigns, waste management, water conservation, poster-making, skits, and competitions.
<br>

### Are competitions organized under the Youth and Eco Club?

Yes.
<br>

### Is private school participation allowed in this scheme?

No.
<br>
