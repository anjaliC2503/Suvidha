## Brief Description

The scheme "Harit Kaushal Vikas Karyakram" implemented by the Govind Ballabh Pant National Institute of Himalayan Environment (NIHE), aims to impart eco-based livelihood skills to youth and communities in the Himalayan regions.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Harit Kaushal Vikas Karyakram" implemented by the Govind Ballabh Pant National Institute of Himalayan Environment (NIHE), aims to promote sustainable and eco-friendly livelihoods through nature-based skill development. Through this scheme, free skill training is provided to farmers, students, and youth to support self-employment and prepare them for government-backed programs as master trainers. It covers subjects like ornithology, wildlife conservation, natural interpretation, and biodiversity communication. The program is aligned with the Green Skill Development Program (GSDP) and is accessible through online applications via the official portal.

## Benefits

- **Skill Development Training:** Free-of-cost training in environment-related fields such as bird identification, wildlife conservation, biodiversity interpretation, and eco-tourism education.  - **Self-Employment Opportunities:** Empowers youth and farmers to pursue independent livelihood avenues through knowledge-based interventions.  - **Government Scheme Readiness:** Makes candidates eligible for roles under various central/state schemes and government-supported training projects.

## Eligibility

- The applicant should be a student.
  - The applicant should have completed at least 10th standard.
  - The applicant should be a farmer/ youth or individual seeking self-employment.
  - The applicant should be below 55 years of age at the time of application.
  - The applicant should have an interest in environmental and ecological training topics such as wildlife, biodiversity, or natural resource interpretation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)" and what is its main objective?

The "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)" is an initiative of the G.B. Pant National Institute of Himalayan Environment that aims to equip youth, farmers, and students with environmentally relevant skills to promote sustainable livelihoods and green employment.
<br>

<br>

### Who is responsible for implementing the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)"?

The "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)" is implemented through G.B. Pant National Institute of Himalayan Environment.

### What types of training modules are covered under the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)"?

The training modules under the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)" include bird identification, wildlife handling and documentation, traditional knowledge systems, biodiversity conservation, and eco-tourism, among others.
<br>

<br>

### What is the eligibility criterion related to education for participating in the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)"?

The applicant must have passed at least Class 10 (matriculation) to be eligible for participating in any course under the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)".
<br>

<br>

### Is there any age restriction for those applying to the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)"?

The applicant must be below 55 years of age to be considered eligible for enrolment in the training courses under the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)".
<br>

<br>

### Does the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)" require participants to pay any training fees?

No, the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)" offers its training completely free of charge to all selected participants.
<br>

<br>

### Who are the ideal candidates for the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)"?

The "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)" is best suited for farmers, unemployed youth, women, and students who wish to gain practical skills in environmental sectors for employment or self-employment.
<br>

<br>

### How can an applicant apply for the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)"?

An applicant may apply by downloading the official [application form](https://gbpihed.gov.in/PDF/spotlight/App_form_ENVIS_GSDP_PBR_2019_20.pdf) and submitting it either online via dedicated emails or offline to the G.B. Pant National Institute of Himalayan Environment.
<br>

<br>

### What is the process for selection into the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)"?

The selection process involves screening of applications by the host institution based on eligibility criteria, course relevance, and seat availability.
<br>

<br>

### How will the selected applicants be notified about their acceptance into the "Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP)"?

Selected candidates are contacted through email or phone, using the contact details provided in their submitted application form.
