## Brief Description

The scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)"  aims to increase agricultural production by supporting irrigation cooperative societies through the provision of pumps and electric motors for efficient water distribution.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)&quot; by the Agriculture and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to enhance agricultural yield in the state by offering assistance to irrigation cooperative societies formed by farmers of a common area. The support involves establishing water distribution systems using pumps and electric motors on wells and agar creeks to facilitate effective irrigation.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Monetary Benefit:** Financial assistance for setting up irrigation infrastructure (such as pumps and motors) for cooperative societies.
<br>

## Eligibility

- Cooperative irrigation societies formed by small and marginal farmers, or any other farmers, are eligible under this scheme.
  - The society should be located within the state of Gujarat.
  - The Scheme is applicable to the General category.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should visit the District Registrar's Office and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the District Registrar's Office.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.
<br>

## Documents Required

1. Application of the association for getting assistance.
2. Copy of registration certificate of the association.
3. Certified copy of the resolution of the prescribed form of the association to get assistance under MNR-10 scheme and to accept the terms and conditions of MNR-10 scheme.
4. Name, caste (general, scheduled caste, scheduled caste) of the association, land holdings and members&#39; caste/unscheduled caste Certificate from the competent authority (except for general purpose) of being a tribal.
5. Certified copy of the final rough survey of the village.
6. Original copy of the plan/estimate of the project with the signature and stamp of the Executive Engineer/N.K.E of the District Panchayat and the technical approval of the Executive Engineer.
7. Certificate from the competent officer of the Irrigation Department regarding the feasibility of the project.
8. Certificate from the Government Engineer that the project estimate is supported by a valid SAR of the Public Works/Irrigation Department.
9. Water survey from river, canal, creek To obtain a copy of the written permission order of the Government&#39;s Irrigation Department for a period of 10 (ten) years. 
10. The cooperative societies selected for the well/bore should submit the permission order of the Water Resources Development Department or a certificate from the competent authority stating that there is no proper well/bore available near the command extension. 
11. How will the society raise the remaining amount required for the project apart from the assistance received under the scheme? Annual report of the scheme.
12. Gross area of total irrigation covered in all three seasons i.e. Fall, Winter and Summer.
and Annual report of assistance available on the basis of gross area.
13. Copies of 8-A extract of the land of the members coming under the command area of the scheme.
14. Written consent/rent agreement of the farmer/member for the use of the land of any farmer member for the scheme work (on stamp paper of Rs. 300/-)
15. Scheme registration form.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)"?

The objective of the scheme "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)" is to enhance agricultural productivity in Gujarat by providing financial and technical support to cooperative irrigation societies formed by farmers from a common geographical area, enabling them to establish water distribution systems using pumps and electric motors on wells and agar creeks.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for the "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)" scheme?

The scheme is exclusively open to cooperative irrigation societies that are legally formed by groups of farmers who collectively own or cultivate agricultural land within the state of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What type of assistance is provided under this scheme?

Under the "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)" scheme, financial support is provided to enable the installation of water distribution systems, including pumps and electric motors, to aid irrigation efforts for member farmers of the cooperative societies.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the geographical coverage of the scheme?

The "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)" scheme is applicable across the entire state of Gujarat and can be availed by any qualifying cooperative irrigation society within the state.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Which department is responsible for overseeing this scheme?

The Agriculture and Co-operation Department of the Government of Gujarat is responsible for formulating, monitoring, and administering the "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)" scheme.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Where can the application form be obtained for this scheme?

The application form for the "Assistance to Co-operative Irrigation Societies, M. N. R.-10 (General Area)" scheme can be obtained free of cost from the District Registrar's Office, and it can be downloaded from the official website.
<br>

### What is the cost of the application form for this scheme?

There is no fee associated with the application form for the scheme; it is provided free of cost by the District Registrar Office and through the scheme’s official portal.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any preference or reservation for specific groups under this scheme?

The Scheme is applicable to the General category.
<br>
