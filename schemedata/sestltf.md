## Brief Description

"Transport/ Logistics/ Travels/ Food Court Wyaaj Sahaay" is a component under the "Self-Employment Scheme" that provides 5% interest subsidy on a bank loan of ₹6,00,000/- for purchasing vehicles and setting up the required infrastructure.

## Detailed Description

&quot;Transport/ Logistics/ Travels/ Food Court Wyaaj Sahaay&quot; is a component under the &quot;Self-Employment Scheme&quot; introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Gujarat. Under this scheme, eligible beneficiaries can avail of a 5% interest subsidy on a bank loan of ₹6.00 lakh for purchasing vehicles and setting up the required infrastructure for businesses like transport, logistics, travelers, food courts, etc.

## Benefits

- **Interest Subsidy:** A 5% subsidy on the interest for a bank loan of ₹6,00,000/-.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
   1. The applicant must belong to the unreserved category.
   1. The applicant&#39;s age should be between 18 years and 50 years.
   1. The applicant must have a valid driving license.
   1. The vehicle purchased under the scheme must be mortgaged (hypothecated) to the corporation.
   1. The applicant must be willing to repay the loan in equal monthly installments over 5 years.
   1. The annual income limit of the family should be ₹6,00,000/- or less.

<br>

> **Note**: 


  - The interest rate on the loan will be 5 percent simple interest per annum.
  - The simple interest will be calculated based on the amount of the loan given per year.
  - Only 1 person from a family can avail of the scheme benefits.
  - A maximum of 5 beneficiaries per village will be selected.

## Application Process

### Online

> **Registration Process:**

**Step 1:** Go to the Corporation's [official website](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/) and navigate to the "Gujarat Unreserved Education and Economical Development Corporation" section under the Corporation menu.
**Step 2:**Click on "Transport/ Logistics/ Travels/ Food Court Wyaaj Sahaay" under "Self-Employment Scheme" and then Apply Now.
**Step 3:** Click on "New User? Please Register Here".
**Step 4:** Enter the required details and click Register.
<br>

> **Application Process:**

**Step 1:** Enter your User ID, Password, and Captcha Code, then click "Login".
**Step 2:** Read the scheme details carefully and click OK.
**Step 3:** If your permanent address is correct, confirm it. Otherwise, enter your correct address.
**Step 4:** Click Save & Next to proceed. Upload the relevant documents.
**Step 5:** If you need to make changes, click "No, Cancel". If all details are correct, click "Yes, Submit".
**Step 6:** Click Save Application. Note down your Application Number for future reference. You can also take a printout by clicking Print Application.
> **Check Application Status:** [Click here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/ViewApplicationStatus.aspx).

> **Post Online Application Procedure:** After submitting the online application, the applicant must take a printout of the application, sign it, and submit it along with all uploaded documents (certificates, mark sheets, etc.) to the concerned Deputy Director (Non-Reserved Category) or the District Social Welfare Officer through courier, post, or by visiting in person.

> **Contact Us:** [Click here](https://gueedc.gujarat.gov.in/contact-us.html).
