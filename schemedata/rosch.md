## Brief Description

The "Running of Scheduled Caste Hostels" scheme by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to provide housing and a conducive study environment for Scheduled Caste students. Separate junior, senior, and college hostels are operated for boys & girls.

## Detailed Description

The "Running of Scheduled Caste Hostels" scheme by the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste (SC) Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to provide housing and a conducive study environment for Scheduled Caste students. Separate junior, senior, and college hostels are operated for boys and girls, offering free accommodation and food facilities to support their education.

## Benefits

- Scholarship, Free Food and Accommodation, Sports Equipment, Toiletry Kit, and Library Facilities.
- 90% of the scholarship amount is paid in the joint account of the hostel superintendent and mess secretary (student) for running the mess.
- 10% is paid in the student's account.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a Student.
1. The applicant should be from a Scheduled Caste.
1. The applicant should be a native of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be enrolled in a recognized institution.

_*The applicant should note that there is no income limit for admission to senior hostels._

## Application Process

### Offline

> **Application**

**Step 1:** Obtain the application form from the Divisional District Office or the Designated Hostel.
**Step 2:** Fill in all the mandatory fields and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the completed application to the concerned hostel within the stipulated deadline fixed by the District President. Ensure the application is forwarded by the Head of the Institution before submission.
> **Verification and Approval**

**Step 1:** The concerned Divisional District Officer will verify the application and supporting documents.
**Step 12:** Upon approval, admission will be granted based on hostel capacity and compliance with prerequisites.
> **Timeline**

Applications must be submitted within one month after the date fixed for admission in schools.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

The applicant must belong to the Scheduled Caste category, be a native of Madhya Pradesh, and be a regular student of a recognized institution.

### Is there an income limit for admission to the hostels?

No, there is no income limit for admission to senior hostels under this scheme.

### What facilities are provided in the hostels?

Free accommodation and food facilities are provided to the students.

### Are there separate hostels for boys and girls?

Yes, there are separate junior, senior, and college hostels for boys and girls.

### Can students from other states apply for this scheme?

No, only students who are natives of Madhya Pradesh are eligible.

### What documents are required for admission?

Caste certificate, proof of residence in Madhya Pradesh, and proof of enrollment in a recognized institution are required.

### What is the process to apply for admission?

Students need to apply through the respective district or block-level offices of the Tribal Welfare and Scheduled Caste Department.

### Are the hostels available for all educational levels?

Yes, hostels are available for junior, senior, and college-level students.

### Is there any fee for accommodation or food?

No, the scheme provides free accommodation and food facilities.

### What is the duration of stay allowed in the hostel?

Students can stay in the hostel for the duration of their academic course.
