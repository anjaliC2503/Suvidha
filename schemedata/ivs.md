## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide a subsidy per unit for purchasing insulated vehicles for the beneficiaries to promote cold-chain logistics in fish transportation.

## Detailed Description

The "Insulated Vehicle" scheme was launched by the Ministry Of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India, under the umbrella scheme "Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana". It aims to promote efficient and hygienic fish transportation systems. Under this scheme, a financial grant is provided to the beneficiaries for purchasing insulated (reefer) vehicles used in fish transportation. It seeks to minimize post-harvest losses, improve fish value chains, and enhance earnings of fish farmers by enabling cold-chain logistics.
<br>

## Benefits

**Financial Assistance:**

- Financial grant of up to ₹20,00,000/- per unit for the purchase of an insulated (reefer) vehicle.

**Grant Limits:**

- 40% of the unit cost for general category beneficiaries.
- 60% of the unit cost for Scheduled Caste (SC) and women beneficiaries.

> **Note**


- Subsidy is applicable for one vehicle per beneficiary only.
- Central assistance is released only after initiating fishery activity.
- The applicant should agree that any losses in procurement, operation, or maintenance are their own responsibility, and not that of the Government of India or Haryana.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a fish farmer.
1. The applicant must possess a Family Identity Card (Parivar Pehchan Patra).
1. The applicant must ensure that the fish transportation facility is maintained in working condition.
1. The applicant must not have availed a grant for a similar insulated (reefer) vehicle earlier.

## Documents Required

- Agreement 1 (Agreement deed between the beneficiary and the fisheries department)
- Birth Certificate (Voting Card/Birth Certificate/Aadhar Card/Matriculation Certificate)
- Caste Certificate (Caste certificate from a first-class magistrate (Tehsildar))
- Training Certificate of the beneficiary 
- Land Record (Land record from Tehsil (own land-Jamabandi, Nakal Akshizra, Farad) or registered lease deed of minimum 7 years)
- Bills/Receipts/Vouchers
- Photographs of insulated vehicles with beneficiary
- Bank Account Detail
- Pan Card
- Detailed Project Report (DPR)/ Self Contained Proposal (SCP) (as per PMMSY guidelines)
- Driving Licence of the beneficiary/driver and insurance of the vehicle

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply?

Any fishery activity practitioner with a valid Family ID card who has not previously availed such a grant.

### What is the maximum grant provided?

₹20,00,000 per unit (subject to subsidy percentage).

### Can I apply for more than one vehicle?

No, only one insulated vehicle is allowed per beneficiary.

### Will I receive the subsidy before starting fisheries activity?

No, the subsidy is released after the initiation of the fishery activity.

### Are there any government liabilities if the vehicle gets damaged?

No, all risks are borne by the beneficiary.

### What is the application processing time?

The application process takes approximately 40 days.

### Are the service charges fixed?

Yes, ₹10 for department service and ₹10 for Atal Seva Kendra.

### Where can I apply for this scheme?

The applicant can apply at the local District Fisheries Office or the[ Antyodaya SARAL portal](https://saralharyana.gov.in/).

### What documents are required to apply?

Attached: Birth Certificate, Caste Certificate (For SC beneficiaries, issued by First Class Magistrate or Tehsildar), Agreement Form, Fisheries Training Certificate (Proof of having undergone training in fisheries-related activities), Bill/Receipt/Voucher, Bank Account Details (Copy of Passbook) and PAN Card, etc.

### Is a driving license compulsory?

Yes, a copy of the driving license must be provided.
