## Brief Description

The scheme aims to improve the management of groundwater resources in water-stressed areas. It provides institutional strengthening and financial incentives to Gram Panchayats and local communities for sustainable groundwater management and water-efficient practices.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Atal Bhujal Yojana" was launched by the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation, Ministry of Jal Shakti, in 2020. The scheme aims to improve the management of groundwater resources in the water-stressed areas of the selected states. The scheme provides institutional strengthening, capacity building, and financial incentives for the preparation of community-led Water Security Plans, adoption of efficient water use practices, and improvement in the rate of decline of groundwater levels. The applicant must ensure community participation, measure groundwater levels, prepare a water budget, and develop a Gram Panchayat-level Water Security Plan. The scheme is implemented by the National Program Management Unit at the central level and State Program Management Units at the state level. The applications for this scheme are accepted through the submission of Water Security Plans to the District Program Management Units for further action and consolidation.
<br>

## Benefits

- The scheme provides an overall financial outlay of ₹60,00,00,00,000/- over five years, which includes ₹30,00,00,00,000/- as a loan from the World Bank and ₹30,00,00,00,000/- as a matching contribution from the Government of India.
- The Institutional Strengthening and Capacity Building Component offers ₹14,00,00,00,000/- for training, community mobilization, and the procurement of equipment such as Digital Water Level Recorders, rain gauges, and water quality testing kits.
- The Incentive Component provides ₹46,00,00,00,000/- in financial rewards to states and Gram Panchayats for achieving predefined targets in groundwater management.
- The scheme funds demand-side interventions, including micro-irrigation practices like drip and sprinkler systems, the use of recycled or reused water for irrigation, underground pipelines, and crop diversification.
- The scheme supports supply-side interventions, including the construction of artificial recharge and water conservation structures such as check dams, percolation ponds, contour trenches, sub-surface dykes, and farm ponds.
- The scheme provides for the upgrading of state-level groundwater quality laboratories to receive accreditation from the National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories.

##### ***Conditions***

- The beneficiary must form or strengthen a Village Water and Sanitation Committee to function as the Participatory Groundwater Management Committee.
- The beneficiary must continuously engage in regular water budgeting exercises, preferably season-wise or at least once a year.
- The beneficiary must update the Water Security Plan on an annual basis to reflect changing water needs and interventions.
- The beneficiary must actively conduct social audits to gather community feedback regarding the activities undertaken during the implementation of the scheme.
- The beneficiary must maintain proper documentation and submit audited financial statements and utilization certificates to avoid the recall of scheme funds.

<br>

## Exclusions

The applicant MUST NOT utilize the scheme funds for - 

- Construction of major or minor dams.
- Construction of new large-scale irrigation systems.
- Major industrial wastewater collection, treatment, and injection systems.
- Payment of salaries to government employees.
- Construction or maintenance of religious structures such as temples, mosques, churches, or tombs.
- Development of commercial ventures, including hotels, restaurants, or resorts.
- Activities of a military or paramilitary nature.
- Purchase of vehicles, land, arms, ammunition, alcohol, or tobacco.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must reside or operate within the identified water-stressed districts, blocks, or Gram Panchayats selected for the scheme.
- The applicant must be located in one of the seven participating states, namely Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, or Uttar Pradesh.
- The applicant must formulate a Water Security Plan with active community participation.
- The applicant must obtain the final approval of the prepared Water Security Plan from the Gram Sabha.
- The applicant must ensure the presence of at least 33% female members in the Gram Panchayat-level Water User Associations or Village Water and Sanitation Committees.
- The applicant should identify and train local volunteers or community resource persons to handle data collection equipment.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

##### **Step 1:****** ***Accessing The Portal And Submitting Baseline Data***
The applicant must access the official Management Information System portal developed for Atal Bhujal Yojana to enter the required baseline data. The designated community resource persons must regularly feed the collected groundwater level, rainfall, and water quality data into the digital application to facilitate accurate water budgeting.
##### **Step 2:****** ***Submitting The Water Security Plan For Approval***
The applicant must convene a community meeting to review the water budget and pass a formal resolution approving the finalized Water Security Plan. The applicant must then submit the approved Water Security Plan to the District Program Management Unit for further review, allocation, and action.
##### **Step 3:****** ***District And State Level Consolidation***
The District Program Management Unit must consolidate and validate the submitted plans at the district level before forwarding the recommendations to the State Program Management Unit. The State Program Management Unit must review the state-level plans and submit them to the State Level Steering Committee for final budget allocation, convergence mapping, and approval.
##### ***Grievance Redressal / Help & Support***
A dedicated online Grievance Redressal Mechanism has been developed and integrated with the Management Information System for the early resolution of complaints. The applicants can utilize the official **Toll-Free Number (011-1800110121)** allocated for the immediate registration and redressal of grievances and complaints.

##### ***At The Application And Planning Stage***

- Demographic Data And Beneficiary Census Report.
- Gram Panchayat Level Water Budget Document.
- Gram Panchayat Level Water Security Plan.
- Gram Sabha Resolution Approving The Water Security Plan.

- Audited Financial Statements Of The Gram Panchayat.
- Monthly Expenditure Statements With Supporting Documentation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### If our local community wants to repair the village temple or mosque using the provided financial support, are we allowed to allocate a portion of the budget for this purpose?

Financial assistance provided cannot be utilized for the construction or maintenance of any religious structures, including temples, mosques, churches, or tombs.
<br>

### What is the mandatory requirement regarding the inclusion and representation of female members in the local water management committees to qualify for the associated administrative benefits?

At least 33 percent of the total members in the Gram Panchayat-level Water User Associations or Village Water and Sanitation Committees must be women.
<br>

### Can the local administrative bodies utilise a part of the received financial incentives to pay the monthly salaries of the government employees assigned to assist with the project workload?

The allocated funds cannot be used for the payment of salaries to any regular government employees assigned to assist with the implementation activities.
<br>

### How frequently must the participating community evaluate and update their assessment of available water resources and current usage to remain compliant with the mandatory administrative guidelines?

The community must engage in regular water budgeting exercises at least once every year, though it is highly recommended to conduct them on a season-wise basis.
<br>

### Once our village has successfully prepared the required strategy for managing local groundwater and meeting demands, who holds the ultimate authority to officially sanction this critical document?

The finalized Water Security Plan must be formally reviewed, discussed, and officially approved through a formal resolution passed by the local Gram Sabha.
<br>

### In order to successfully apply for and receive the interventions and financial incentives, which specific regions or geographic locations must our community be currently situated in?

The applicant must be located in identified water-stressed districts or blocks within the states of Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, or Uttar Pradesh.
<br>

### Would our proposal be accepted if we plan to use the allotted financial resources to develop a community-owned resort or restaurant to boost local tourism and the overall economy?

Financial support is strictly prohibited from being utilized for the development of any commercial ventures whatsoever, including hotels, restaurants, resorts, or similar business operations.
<br>

### What kind of specific local committee needs to be formed or strengthened by the community to take charge of the participatory planning and resource management processes effectively?

The beneficiaries must form or strengthen a Village Water and Sanitation Committee to function specifically as the dedicated Participatory Groundwater Management Committee.
<br>

### Are we permitted to submit a proposal that involves the construction of major or minor dams to improve the surface water availability in our agricultural district?

The financial provisions cannot be used for the construction of major or minor dams, nor for setting up any new large-scale irrigation systems.
<br>

### Who is supposed to be responsible for the regular handling of the measurement equipment and the recording of essential groundwater and rainfall data in our village?

Local volunteers or community resource persons must be specifically identified and trained to handle the provided data collection equipment and input the relevant information.
<br>

### Can the implementation committee use a portion of the financial grant to purchase land for water conservation structures or buy vehicles for official community monitoring visits?

The provided funds are strictly restricted and cannot be used for the purchase of any vehicles, land, arms, ammunition, alcoholic beverages, or tobacco products.
<br>

### How is the local administration expected to gather feedback from the residents regarding the effectiveness and proper execution of the activities undertaken during the implementation period?

The beneficiaries must actively conduct regular social audits through a designated committee to gather community feedback on the undertaken activities and to ensure overall transparency.
<br>

### What specific financial records and proofs must our local administrative body maintain and submit to ensure that the received incentive funds are not recalled by the higher authorities?

The Gram Panchayat must maintain proper documentation and submit audited financial statements along with monthly expenditure statements and utilization certificates for the received funds.
<br>

### After the initial strategy for managing community water resources is drafted and approved, does it remain permanent for the entire five-year period or does it require regular modifications?

The drafted Water Security Plan must be reviewed and systematically updated on an annual basis to accurately reflect changing local water needs and the progress of ongoing interventions.
<br>
