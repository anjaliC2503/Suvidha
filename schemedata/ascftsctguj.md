## Brief Description

The scheme aims to increase fish production and provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Caste (SC) people engaged in inland fisheries activities.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries" with the component of "Training of Scheduled Caste Trainees" introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to increase fish production and provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Caste (SC) people engaged in inland fisheries activities. Through this scheme, financial assistance in the form of a stipend is provided to SC beneficiaries undergoing fisheries training to enhance their knowledge and skills. The scheme is implemented by the Commissioner of Fisheries, Government of Gujarat.
**Objectives:**

- To increase fish production by rearing fish in reservoirs and provide employment to scheduled caste (SC) people involved in inland fisheries activities.
- To boost fish farming or fishing activities in freshwater bodies by scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen.
- Fishing in reservoirs, aquaculture in ponds, and catch from rivers increase fish production and boost inland fisheries through scheduled caste (SC) fish farmers and fishermen.
- The main objective is to provide employment opportunities to Scheduled Caste fishermen in the state and increase fish production.

<br>

## Benefits

1. The trainee is paid a stipend of ₹1,250/- after completing ten days of training.
1. The stipend is paid at the rate of ₹125/- per day.

<br>

> **Training Duration:** 10 days of fisheries training.

> **Frequency of Disbursement:** Once in a lifetime.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) community.
1. The applicant should be above 18 years of age.
1. The applicant should be involved in inland fisheries activities.
1. The applicant can be an individual, a society, a self-help group, an association, a reservoir monopoly, or a private unit.
1. The applicant must have a bank account in their own name.
1. The applicant must have an Aadhar Card/Voter ID/Ration Card/Passport as proof of identity.

<br>

> **Preference/Reservation:**

The scheme specifically benefits Scheduled Caste fishermen under the inland fisheries scheme.
<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Visit the Nearest District Fisheries Office in your respective district.
**Step 2:** Collect the Prescribed Application Form for the training component, i.e, "Training of Scheduled Caste Trainees" under Assistance to Scheduled Castes for Fisheries.
**Step 3:** Fill the Application Form completely with accurate details.
**Step 4:** Attach the Required Documents, including identity proof, SC certificate, and bank account proof.
**Step 5:** Submit the Application in person to the concerned official at the District Fisheries Office.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for the training provided under this fisheries scheme?

Individuals from the Scheduled Caste (SC) community involved in inland fisheries activities are eligible to apply for the training.
<br>

### What is the minimum age required to apply for the training benefit?

The applicant must be above 18 years of age to apply for the training benefit.
<br>

### How many times can I avail the training benefit under this scheme?

The training benefit can be availed only once in a lifetime by an eligible Scheduled Caste beneficiary.
<br>

### Is there an online application option for the training component?

No, applications for the training must be submitted in physical form at the District Fisheries Office.
<br>

### Where should I apply to enroll for the fisheries training?

You need to apply in the prescribed form at the concerned District Fisheries Office.
<br>

### Is it necessary to have a bank account to receive the stipend?

Yes, the applicant must have a bank account in their own name to receive the stipend.
<br>

### Do I need to provide a caste certificate while applying for training?

Yes, a Scheduled Caste certificate is required to avail benefits under this scheme component.
<br>

### Will I receive the stipend during the training or after completion?

The stipend is paid after successful completion of the 10-day training program.
<br>

### What is the purpose of providing this fisheries training?

The training aims to increase fish production and provide employment to Scheduled Caste individuals involved in inland fisheries.
<br>
