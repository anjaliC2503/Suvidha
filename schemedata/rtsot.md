## Brief Description

The scheme aims to improve the quality and standard of Tiatr, the popular dramatic form of Goa. It benefits actors, directors, script writers, and backstage artists by reimbursing ticket costs for witnessing high-standard dramas to enhance performance quality.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Raising the Standard of Tiatr" was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to improve the quality and standard of Tiatr performances, which is the most popular dramatic form of Goa. The scheme provides opportunities for actors, directors, backstage artists, and script writers to witness high-standard dramas in Hindi, Marathi, English, and other languages, by reimbursing the amount spent on purchasing tickets for up to two persons. The applicant must submit a brief report about the drama witnessed, highlighting its positive and negative points, along with the counter-foils of the tickets, for the reimbursement to be processed. The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa. The applications for this scheme are accepted by submitting required documents to the office.
<br>

## Benefits

- Reimbursement of the value of ticket(s) for witnessing high-standard dramas.
- To cover the cost incurred by artists for purchasing tickets to watch high-standard plays, thereby encouraging them to improve their performance quality.

_*The value of the ticket(s) will be reimbursed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa after a brief report about the drama seen and the counter-foils of tickets are submitted to the office._
_*The artist who has witnessed the drama must submit a brief report highlighting the positive and negative points of the play._
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a director, script writer, artist, or backstage artist.
- The applicant must submit a self-attested copy of their Aadhaar Card along with the application.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the copies of the mandatory documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
*The application will be considered on a 'First Come First Serve' basis and is subject to the availability of funds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many people can receive ticket reimbursement under this program when I attend a drama performance with others?

The program provides reimbursement for ticket costs for up to 2 persons maximum. You can claim reimbursement for yourself and one additional person when attending high-standard drama performances.
<br>

### What types of performances are eligible for ticket reimbursement under this cultural development initiative?

You can claim reimbursement for attending high-standard dramas performed in Hindi, Marathi, English, or any other languages. The performances must meet high-quality standards to qualify for the reimbursement benefit.
<br>

### Do I need to submit any documentation along with my initial application form for this arts enhancement program?

Yes, you must attach a self-attested copy of your Aadhaar Card along with the completed application form. This documentation is mandatory for your initial application submission to the office.
<br>

### What specific information about the drama performance must I include when filling out my application form?

Your application form must include the Title of the Drama or Performance you witnessed, along with complete details of the Place, Date, and Time of the performance. All personal details like name, address, contact number, email, and Aadhaar Card number are also required.
<br>

### If I am involved in backstage work for dramatic productions, am I eligible to apply for this scheme?

Yes, backstage artists are specifically eligible to apply for this scheme. The program covers directors, script writers, artists, and backstage artists who work in dramatic productions.
<br>

### What happens to the ticket counter-foils that I need to retain after watching a performance under this program?

You must securely attach the counter-foils of your tickets to the brief report about the drama. These counter-foils, along with your performance report, must be submitted to the Tiatr Academy of Goa office for reimbursement processing.
<br>

### Am I required to provide both positive and negative feedback about the drama I watched for reimbursement?

Yes, your brief report must thoughtfully highlight and elaborate on both positive aspects and negative points observed during the performance. This critical assessment of the drama is mandatory for reimbursement approval.
<br>

### Which government department oversees the implementation and management of this cultural improvement scheme in the state?

The scheme is implemented by the Tiatr Academy of Goa under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. All applications and reimbursement processes are handled through this academy.
<br>

### Can script writers for dramatic productions apply for ticket reimbursement benefits under this cultural development program?

Yes, script writers are specifically eligible to apply for this scheme. The program is designed for directors, script writers, artists, and backstage artists involved in dramatic productions.
<br>

### What is the primary objective behind offering ticket reimbursements to those involved in dramatic arts production?

The scheme aims to improve the quality and standard of Tiatr performances by providing opportunities to witness high-standard dramas. This exposure helps artists enhance their own performance quality and skills.
<br>

### After submitting my reimbursement claim documents, what should I do to maintain proper records of my application?

You should keep personal copies of your submitted application form, the prepared performance report, and the ticket counter-foils. These copies are important for your records and any future reference or inquiries.
<br>

### What must I do immediately after watching a high-standard drama to ensure I can claim my reimbursement?

You must prepare a brief but comprehensive report about the performance you witnessed. This report should provide critical assessment highlighting both positive and negative aspects of the drama before submitting for reimbursement.
<br>

### As a director of dramatic productions, what initial steps must I take to participate in this arts development scheme?

You must obtain and complete the application form with all required personal details, then attach a self-attested copy of your Aadhaar Card. Submit this initial application to formally apply for the scheme benefits.
<br>

### What specific dramatic art form does this government scheme primarily aim to enhance and develop in the region?

The scheme specifically focuses on improving the quality and standard of Tiatr, which is recognized as the most popular dramatic form of Goa. The program helps artists enhance their Tiatr performance skills.
<br>
