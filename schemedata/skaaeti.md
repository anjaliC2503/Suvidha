## Brief Description

The "Strengthening Skills, Arts, and Employment through ITIs" initiative by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to connect these skills with modern technology, short-term, employment-oriented training has been provided through ITIs.

## Detailed Description

The "Strengthening Skills, Arts, and Employment through ITIs" initiative by the Department of De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh, aims to bridge traditional knowledge and art with modern technology. Communities under this department have historically relied on traditional knowledge and art for their livelihood. To connect these skills with modern technology, short-term, employment-oriented training has been provided through ITIs. The target for the financial year 2022-23 is to train 180 students, with a goal of benefiting 250 students in the coming year. After completing the training, there is a provision for employment or self-employment under the self-employment scheme.

## Benefits

The scheme provides employment-oriented training through ITIs, aiming to enhance traditional skills and offer employment or self-employment opportunities.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be from the De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities listed in Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should be a native of Madhya Pradesh.
1. The applicant should not be an income taxpayer.
1. The applicant's educational qualification should meet the course requirements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What type of training does the scheme offer?

The scheme offers short-term, employment-oriented training that links traditional art, knowledge, and business with modern technology, delivered through Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs).

### Who is eligible for the "Strengthening Skills, Arts, and Employment through ITIs" scheme?

The scheme is available to individuals from De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic communities listed in the Madhya Pradesh State's Memorandum No. /6109/XXY/GNK/IK/63, dated 21 September 1963.

### Do I need to be a resident of Madhya Pradesh to apply?

Yes, the applicant must be a native of Madhya Pradesh to qualify for the scheme.

### What kind of certificate do I need to apply?

The applicant should have been issued a certificate of free, nomadic, and semi-nomadic caste by the competent authority to prove eligibility.

### Can I apply for this scheme if I am an income taxpayer?

No, the applicant should not be an income taxpayer to be eligible for the scheme.

### What educational qualifications are required for the scheme?

The applicant's educational qualifications should meet the requirements specified for the course they wish to apply for under this scheme.

### How many students will benefit from the scheme?

The scheme aims to train 180 students in the financial year 2022-23, with a target of benefiting 250 students in the following year.

### What happens after completing the training?

After completing the training, students will have access to employment opportunities or self-employment through the self-employment scheme.

### Where will the training take place?

The training will be conducted at Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) and related institutions offering the required skill-building courses.
