## Brief Description

The scheme aims to support transgender students pursuing post-matric education. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a monthly scholarship of ₹1,000/- to cover educational expenses for their post-matric studies.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Transgender Utthan Kosh - Post Matric Scholarship for Studying Transgender&quot; scheme was introduced by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan. It aims to support transgender students pursuing post-matric education. Under this scheme, transgender students receive a monthly scholarship of ₹1,000/- to cover educational expenses for their post-matric studies.

## Benefits

***Post-Matric Scholarship:***

- Scholarship @ ₹1,000/- per month per student for 10 months.

**NOTE:** The scholarship amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary's bank account.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Rajasthan.
1. The applicant should belong to the transgender community.
1. The applicant should be studying at the post-matric level.
1. The applicant should be enrolled in a recognized school/college in Rajasthan.
1. The applicant’s family annual income should not exceed ₹8,00,000/-.

## Application Process

### Offline

***Application Submission:***

- The applicant/parents/guardians must fill out the scholarship application form and attach the required documents. 
- Submit the complete application form to their school principal/institution.

***Institutional Verification:***

- The school principal/institution verifies the application and forwards it to the Social Justice and Empowerment Department's district office.

***Approval Process:***

- The district office verifies the documents and approves/rejects the application within one month.

***Disbursement:***

- Upon approval, the scholarship amount is transferred to the applicant's bank account.

1. Previous Year’s Mark Sheet 
1. Aadhaar Card 
1. Parents/Guardians Income Certificate 
1. Domicile Certificate/Ration Card/Voter ID
1. Bank Passbook Copy
1. Any other documents as required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for this scholarship?

Transgender students who are residents of Rajasthan and enrolled in post-matric education.

### What is the financial assistance provided?

A monthly scholarship of [?]1,000/- for 10 months.

### Can students from other states apply?

No, only residents of Rajasthan are eligible.

### How is the scholarship amount disbursed?

Through Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) to the applicant's account.

### Is this scholarship available for all courses?

Yes, as long as the course is post-matric and recognized.

### Can students apply online?

Currently, the application process is offline through the school/institution.

### When will I receive the scholarship amount?

Within one month of application approval.

### Is there any reservation or preference?

No specific reservations, but all eligible transgender students are encouraged to apply.

### How can I apply for the scholarship?

Submit the application to your institution, which will forward it to the district office.

### What documents are required for the application?

Transgender Identity Card, enrollment certificate, previous year marksheet, Aadhaar card, income certificate, domicile proof, and bank details.

### Can the scholarship be renewed annually?

Yes, subject to continued eligibility and academic progress.
