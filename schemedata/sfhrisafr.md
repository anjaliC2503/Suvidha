## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide financial assistance for sports-related research projects that contribute to the improvement of sports and games in India and promote innovation in sports sciences, medicine, psychology, performance enhancement, and other sports-related disciplines.

## Detailed Description

The scheme component &quot;Assistance for Research&quot; under the &quot;Scheme of Human Resources Development in Sports&quot; is implemented by the Department of Sports, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India to promote research and development in sports-related disciplines through financial assistance to eligible scholars, universities, and institutions. The scheme supports research projects that address sports-specific challenges and contribute to the overall improvement of sports and games in the country. Research may cover areas such as sports psychology, sports medicine, injury management, performance enhancement, sociological studies related to sports, and identification of talent. The scheme also encourages collaborative research under bilateral and multilateral Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) signed by the Ministry and its autonomous bodies. Grants are provided for approved research projects based on merit and expected contribution to sports development in India.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Research Grant:** Financial assistance is provided for sports-related research projects.  - **Standard Research Funding:** Grant is provided as per University Grants Commission (UGC) norms, subject to a maximum limit of ₹10,00,000/-.  - **Mega Research Projects:** Financial assistance of up to ₹50,00,000/- may be provided for exceptional mega research projects.  - **Research Areas Supported:** Support is available for studies related to sports psychology, sports medicine, injury management, performance improvement, talent identification, and sports sociology.  - **Institutional Research Support:** Government institutions and UGC-recognised universities engaged in sports-related activities are encouraged to undertake research projects.  - **Individual Research Support:** Subject specialists and research scholars may receive assistance for eligible research and development studies.  - **Collaborative Research:** Support may be provided for research undertaken under bilateral and multilateral Memoranda of Understanding signed by the Ministry.  - **Validity of Assistance:** The duration of the grant is up to 2 years.
<br>

## Exclusions

- Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) and Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) cardholders are not eligible.

<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be an Indian national currently based in India.
  - The applicant must not be more than 58 years of age.
  - The applicant must possess a Ph.D. or Master&#39;s Degree in specific sports-related subjects.
  - The applicant must either hold a permanent position in an institution or have a suitable arrangement with an institution.
  - The applicant must have the research proposal or project approved by the concerned University or Institute.
  - The proposed research must have a direct bearing on sports-related issues or contribute to the improvement of sports and games in India.
  - The applicant may submit details of past publications or research work in related areas, if available.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** The applicant will open the [website](https://dbtyas-sports.gov.in/) to register and apply for the Online Schemes of the Department of Sports.
**Step 2:** The applicant will click on the ‘[Registration](https://dbtyas-sports.gov.in/Registration)’ tab to start the one-time registration process. Here, the applicant will fill in the required personal details, including verification of their mobile number through OTP.
**Step 3:** After successful registration, the applicant will get a message that the login credentials have been sent to the registered email ID.
**Step 4:** The applicant will use the login credentials received on the registered email ID to log in to the online portal. Please remember to refresh the captcha before clicking on the Log In button.
**Step 5:** After the first time login, the applicant will be required to update their other basic details. After filling in the details, click on the ‘Update’ button and the profile will be successfully updated.
**Step 6:** The applicant will click on the ‘Schemes’ tab and apply for the desired scheme.
> **User Manual:** [Click here](https://dbtyas-sports.gov.in/assets/cms_files/User_Manual_for_Registration_for_Online_Schemes.pdf).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Assistance for Research" component?

The objective of the scheme is to promote sports-related research that contributes to the development and improvement of sports and games in India.
<br>

### Who implements this scheme?

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Sports under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India.
<br>

### Who can apply under this scheme?

An Indian national based in India who meets the prescribed educational and institutional requirements can apply under the scheme.
<br>

### Are Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) and Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) cardholders eligible?

No, Persons of Indian Origin and Overseas Citizen of India cardholders are not eligible under this scheme.
<br>

### What is the maximum age limit for applicants?

The applicant must not be more than 58 years of age.
<br>

### What educational qualifications are required?

The applicant must possess a Ph.D. or Master's Degree in a sports-related subject.
<br>

### What is the maximum financial assistance available?

Financial assistance is available up to ₹10,00,000/- as per UGC norms, while certain mega projects may receive up to ₹50,00,000/-.
<br>

### What is the duration of the research grant?

The research grant is normally provided for a period of up to 2 years.
<br>

### What types of research are supported under the scheme?

The scheme supports research in areas such as sports psychology, sports medicine, injury management, performance enhancement, talent identification, and sports sociology.
<br>

### Can individual research scholars apply for assistance?

Yes, individual subject specialists and research scholars may apply for assistance if they meet the eligibility requirements.
<br>

### Is institutional approval required for applying?

Yes, the research proposal must be approved by the concerned University or Institute.
<br>

### How are applications evaluated?

Applications are evaluated based on academic performance, the quality of the research topic, and the expected contribution of the research to sports development.
<br>

### How is the grant released?

The sanctioned grant is released to the Institute or University in two or more installments.
<br>

### Can a researcher change the research topic after receiving the grant?

No, the researcher is not permitted to change the field of research after the grant has been sanctioned.
<br>
