## Brief Description

The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹4,800/- per month as pension to all first-generation living legal heirs of freedom fighters residing in the state of Uttarakhand.

## Detailed Description

The scheme &quot;Samman Pension Yojana&quot; is implemented by the Police Department under the Department of Home, Government of Uttarakhand. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a pension to all first-generation living legal heirs of freedom fighters residing in the state of Uttarakhand.
<br>

## Benefits

**Samman Pension Amount:** ₹4,800/- per month
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a permanent resident of Uttarakhand.
- The applicant must be a first-generation living legal heir of a freedom fighter.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

1. The application form can be obtained from the office of the District Magistrate of the concerned district and must be submitted there along with the required documents. 
   1. The Samman Pension will be sanctioned only after verification at the level of the District Magistrate. 
   1. The pension will commence only after the lifetime of the widowed wife or widower husband of the deceased freedom fighter. All surviving first-generation legal heirs of the deceased freedom fighter, including sons and daughters (married or unmarried), as well as the widow of a first-generation heir, will be jointly entitled to receive the pension.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible for the scheme?

All living legal heirs of the first generation of freedom fighters who are permanent residents of Uttarakhand are eligible.
<br>

### What is the pension amount provided under the scheme?

The scheme provides ₹4,800/- per month as pension.
<br>

### Is the pension provided to individual heirs or jointly?

The pension is admissible jointly to all the living legal heirs of the first generation of the freedom fighter.
<br>

### When does the pension become payable?

The pension becomes payable only after the lifetime of the widowed wife or widower husband of the deceased freedom fighter.
<br>

### Who among the heirs are entitled to receive the pension?

All living sons and daughters (married or unmarried) of the deceased freedom fighter, as well as the widow of a first-generation heir, are entitled to receive the pension jointly.
<br>

### How can one obtain the application form?

The application form can be obtained from the office of the District Magistrate of the concerned district.
<br>

### Where should the application be submitted?

The completed application form, along with the required documents, must be submitted to the District Magistrate’s office.
<br>

### Who approves the pension?

The pension is sanctioned only after verification and approval at the level of the District Magistrate.
