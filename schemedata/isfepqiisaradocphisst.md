## Brief Description

To motivate the farmers to adopt hygienic threshing practices for production of quality seed spices using mechanical seed spices threshers (power operated)

## Detailed Description

Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom: Post-Harvest Improvement: Seed spice thresher (nos) is implemented by the /Spices Board, India, under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India.
To motivate the farmers to adopt hygienic threshing practices for production of quality seed spices using mechanical seed spices threshers (power operated)	
The scheme is being implemented in accordance with the modalities and guidelines approved by the Competent Authority under the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion & Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

## Benefits

1. 50 % of cost of unit, subject to a maximum of ₹ 75,000/- per unit for General Category and 75% subject to maximum of ₹1,12,000/- per unit for NE/SC/ST & 90% Spice Producer's Groups in Identified Clusters subject to maximum of ₹ 1,35,000/-
1. Assistance for groups/FPOs will be as in QGBG

<br>

## Eligibility

1.Preferably in all seed spice growing states and regions in India/UT.
2. Designated Officials will conduct final inspection and recommend the eligible cases.
3. An individual grower having 0.40 ha and up to 8 ha is eligible to apply.
<br>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the Seed Spice Thresher scheme?

The scheme aims to encourage farmers to adopt hygienic threshing practices by using power-operated seed spice threshers. This helps improve the quality of seed spices and reduces post-harvest losses.
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Individual seed spice growers having land holdings of at least 0.40 hectare and up to 8 hectares are eligible to apply for assistance under the scheme.
<br>

### Can Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and farmer groups avail assistance?

Yes. Farmer groups and FPOs engaged in seed spice cultivation are eligible to avail assistance as per the provisions applicable to group beneficiaries under the scheme guidelines.
<br>

### What financial assistance is available for General Category beneficiaries?

General Category beneficiaries are eligible for assistance equal to 50% of the cost of the unit, subject to a maximum of ₹75,000 per unit.
<br>

### What financial assistance is available for NE, SC, and ST beneficiaries?

Beneficiaries belonging to the North-Eastern Region, Scheduled Castes, and Scheduled Tribes can receive assistance equal to 75% of the cost of the unit, subject to a maximum of ₹1,12,000 per unit.
<br>

### What assistance is available for Spice Producers' Groups in identified clusters?

Spice Producers' Groups in identified clusters are eligible for assistance equal to 90% of the cost of the unit, subject to a maximum of ₹1,35,000 per unit.
<br>

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for availing the benefit?

Yes. Aadhaar is a mandatory document required for identity verification and processing of the application.
<br>

### In which areas is the scheme implemented?

The scheme is implemented in seed spice-growing states, regions, and Union Territories across India.
<br>

### How does the use of a seed spice thresher benefit farmers?

The equipment helps reduce labour costs, improve threshing efficiency, minimize contamination, reduce post-harvest losses, and ensure better-quality produce for the market.
<br>

### Is inspection of the installed equipment mandatory?

Yes. Designated officials conduct a final inspection of the installed thresher and recommend eligible cases before assistance is sanctioned.
<br>
