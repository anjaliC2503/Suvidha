The scheme supports students in their first year and fills vacant awards in subsequent years (II/III/IV). Key benefits include financial aid to cover educational expenses and encouragement for students to pursue quality education. Eligible applicants must be Chhattisgarh domicile.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Scholarship for C.G. Domicile Students Pursuing Engineering Courses Outside Chhattisgarh State" by the Directorate of Technical Education, Chhattisgarh Government, aims to provide financial assistance to students domiciled in Chhattisgarh who are pursuing B.E./B.Tech courses in recognised engineering colleges outside the state. The scheme supports students in their first year and fills vacant awards in subsequent years (II/III/IV). Key benefits include financial aid to cover educational expenses and encouragement for students to pursue quality education beyond state borders. Eligible applicants must be Chhattisgarh domicile holders and enrolled in AICTE-approved institutions.

## Benefits

Financial assistance of ₹2000/- month to cover educational expenses.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a domicile of Chhattisgarh.
- The applicant must be enrolled in a recognized engineering college outside Chhattisgarh.
- The applicant should be studying in IIT / NIT / IISc or in an AICTE-approved institution.
- The applicant should be enrolled in the first year of a B.E./B.Tech. course.
- The applicant should have secured at least 60% marks in the 12th examination.
- The applicant must not be receiving any other scholarship from the state/central government or other sources.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take print of the [prescribed format](https://cgdteraipur.cgstate.gov.in/sites/default/files/admissioninfo0310.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, paste the passport-sized photograph (signed across, if required), and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents, within the prescribed period (if any), to - 
***The Director, Directorate of Technical Education, Chhattisgarh***
***Indrawati Bhavan, Block-3, 3rd/4th Floor, Nawa Raipur, Atal Nagar, Dist..- Raipur - 492002***
***Phone: 0771-2331331, 2221376 | Fax: 0771-2253620 | Website: http://www.egdleraipur.ac.in***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgement for future reference._
_*Ensure that the application is submitted within the prescribed period mentioned in the scheme guidelines._
<br>

**Post-Application Processes**
The Directorate will verify the submitted documents and approve eligible applications. The scholarship amount will be disbursed to the applicant’s bank account upon approval.

- Admission Letter From a Recognized Engineering College.
- Attested Copies of Mark Sheets From the 12th Standard Onwards.
- Domicile Certificate of Chhattisgarh Issued by a Competent Authority.
- AICTE Approval Letter (for Applicants from Private Institutions).
- Certificate From the Head of the Institution.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the domicile requirements to be eligible for this engineering scholarship scheme?

The applicant must hold a valid domicile certificate of Chhattisgarh issued by a competent authority.

### Which courses qualify for financial assistance under this scholarship program?

Only students pursuing a B.E. or B.Tech degree in a recognized engineering college outside Chhattisgarh are eligible.

### Is there a specific type of institution where I must be enrolled to qualify?

Yes, you must be studying in an IIT, NIT, IISc, or any other AICTE-approved engineering institution.

### Can students in any year of engineering apply for this scholarship?

First-year B.E./B.Tech students are eligible, and vacant awards may be filled for second, third, or fourth-year students.

### What is the minimum academic performance required in 12th grade to apply?

You must have secured at least 60% marks in your 12th standard examinations.

### What documents are needed to prove my admission to an engineering college?

You must submit an admission letter from a recognized engineering college where you are enrolled.

### Which mark sheets are required as part of the application process?

Attested copies of your 12th standard mark sheets and any subsequent academic records must be submitted.

### Is an AICTE approval letter required for all institutions?

Yes, if you are enrolled in a private institution, you must submit an AICTE approval letter.

### Do I need any certification from my college as part of the application?

Yes, a certificate from the head of your institution is required for verification.

### What details must I fill in the scholarship application form?

All mandatory fields must be completed, including personal, academic, and institutional details.

### Where should I submit my completed scholarship application?

The filled application must be submitted to the Director, Directorate of Technical Education, Chhattisgarh, at the given address in Nawa Raipur.

### Should I keep any records after submitting my application?

Yes, applicants must save a copy of the submitted application and acknowledgement for future reference.

### How will I receive the scholarship amount if my application is approved?

The financial assistance will be directly disbursed to your bank account upon approval.

### Can I apply if I am studying in an engineering college within Chhattisgarh?

No, this scholarship is only for students pursuing engineering courses outside the state.

### Can students from diploma or non-engineering courses apply for this scholarship?

No, only B.E./B.Tech students are eligible under this scheme.
