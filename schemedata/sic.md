## Brief Description

The "Solar Inverter Charger" by the Renewable Energy Department, Government of Haryana, aims to promote the use of renewable energy and reduce electricity dependence by providing a subsidy of ₹6,000/- or ₹10,000/- for solar inverter charger systems to eligible households.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Solar Inverter Charger&quot; by the Renewable Energy Department, Government of Haryana, aims to promote sustainable energy practices, reduce the dependency on grid electricity, and provide financial support for the installation of solar inverter chargers in domestic households. This initiative targets families with existing inverters who have not yet adopted solar technology, thereby enabling energy-efficient solutions and reducing carbon footprint.
<br>

## Benefits

- **For 320-watt Solar Inverter Charger System:** A subsidy of ₹6,000/- is provided.  - **For 640-watt Solar Inverter Charger System:** A subsidy of ₹10,000/- is provided.
<br>

<br>

## Exclusions

- The applicant should not have installed a Solar Inverter Charger in the past 5 years under the Department Scheme.

## Eligibility

1. Any resident family of Haryana with Pariwar Pahichan Patra (PPP) to apply.
   1. All the households who have domestic inverter and not installed solar inverter charger on his/her existing inverter.
   1. Applicants who have not installed Solar Inverter Charger in past 5 years under the Department Scheme.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "Solar Inverter Charger"?

The "Solar Inverter Charger" by the Renewable Energy Department, Government of Haryana, aims to promote the use of renewable energy and reduce electricity dependence by subsidizing solar inverter chargers. Through this scheme, a subsidy of ₹6,000/- or ₹10,000/- is provided for solar inverter charger systems to eligible households in Haryana.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under the scheme?

The following are eligible to apply for this scheme:

   1. Any Haryana resident family with Pariwar Pahichan Patra (PPP) to apply.
   1. All the households who have domestic inverter and not installed solar inverter charger on his/her existing inverter.
   1. Applicants who have not installed Solar Inverter Charger in past 5 years under the Department Scheme.

### What type of financial support is provided through the scheme?

Under the scheme, financial assistance in the form of a subsidy is provided, which includes ₹6,000 for a 320-watt solar inverter charger system and ₹10,000/- for a 640-watt system.
<br>

<br>

### Can a person without an inverter apply for the scheme?

A person who does not already have a domestic inverter is not eligible for the scheme, as one of the key conditions is that the applicant must own a conventional inverter prior to installing a solar inverter charger.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit or caste-based reservation for this scheme?

The scheme does not impose any income ceiling or provide caste-based reservations, and all Haryana residents who meet the technical and procedural criteria are eligible to apply.
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>

### Can a household choose the wattage of the system as per their requirement?

Yes, the applicant has the flexibility to choose between a 320-watt or a 640-watt solar inverter charger system depending on their household's power requirements and budget.
<br>

<br>

### Can residents of urban areas also apply for the scheme?

Any resident of Haryana, whether from urban or rural areas, who meets the eligibility criteria and has a valid Pariwar Pahichan Patra (PPP), may apply.

### What is the process for applying to the "Solar Inverter Charger"?

The applicants may visit the [Antyodaya-SARAL Portal](https://saralharyana.gov.in/) to apply for the scheme.

### Is it mandatory to submit the Pariwar Pahichan Patra (PPP) while applying for the scheme?

Yes, it is mandatory for the applicant to submit a valid Pariwar Pahichan Patra (PPP) as it serves as the primary eligibility document for applying to the "Solar Inverter Charger".
<br>

### What is the offline process for applying the scheme?

To apply for the "Solar Inverter Charger", the applicant may visit the nearest Atal Seva Kendra (Common Service Centre).
<br>

### Is there any fee applicable for applying this scheme through Atal Seva Kendra (Common Service Centre) ?

Yes, a nominal service fee of ₹10/- is charged by the Common Service Centre for the application.
