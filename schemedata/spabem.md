# A Scheme to Provide Assistance for Building E-Market Facilities in Market Committees

- **Slug:** `spabem`
- **Scheme ID:** `680b6978197a2fd77e4bb473`
- **myScheme page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/spabem
- **Dbt Scheme:** No
- **Tags:** Agriculture, E-Market
- **Scheme Name:** A Scheme to Provide Assistance for Building E-Market Facilities in Market Committees
- **Scheme Short Title:** SPABEM
- **Level:** State/ UT
- **Scheme Category:** Agriculture,Rural & Environment
- **Scheme Sub Category:** Entrepreneurship development
- **Scheme Open Date:** Not specified
- **Target Beneficiaries:** Registered Societies
- **Scheme For:** Infra
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Nodal Department Name:** Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department

## Brief Description

This scheme provides financial assistance to market committees in Gujarat to establish e-market facilities, aligning with the National Agriculture Market Policy for a unified national e-market.

## Detailed Description

"A Scheme to Provide Assistance for Building E-Market Facilities in Market Committees" introduced by the Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation Department, Gujarat, aims to support market committees to set up e-market facilities, enhancing transparency and efficiency in agricultural marketing across the state.

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** ₹1,00,000/- (One Lakh Rupees) per eligible market committee.
<br>

<br>

## Eligibility

1. Co-operative Market Committees shall be eligible under this scheme.
   1. The committee must operate within Gujarat.

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** Fill in the application form on plain paper as per the instructions.
**Step 2:** Attach all required documents.
**Step 3:** Submit the completed application to the District Registrar for processing.

## References

- [Guidelines](https://mariyojana.gujarat.gov.in/Schemeatoz.aspx)

## Documents Required

1. Proof of cooperative market committee registration.
   1. Details of the proposed e-market facility setup.
   1. Any other documents as required.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of the "A Scheme to Provide Assistance for Building E-Market Facilities in Market Committees"?

The objective of the scheme is to provide financial support to cooperative market committees in Gujarat for establishing digital e-market (e-NAM) facilities under the National Agriculture Market Policy.
<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply for this scheme?

Only registered cooperative market committees operating within the state of Gujarat are eligible to apply for this scheme.
<br>

<br>

### How much financial assistance is provided under the scheme?

Under the scheme, each eligible market committee is granted a financial assistance of ₹1,00,000/- (One Lakh Rupees).
<br>

<br>

### Is there any fee for obtaining or submitting the application form?

No, there is no fee required for obtaining or submitting the application form; it is completely free of cost.
<br>

<br>

### Can private companies or individuals apply for the scheme?

No, only cooperative market committees are eligible to apply; private entities or individuals are not eligible.
<br>

<br>

### Is the scheme applicable outside Gujarat?

No, the scheme is limited to cooperative market committees located within the state of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>

### Is online application available for this scheme?

No, currently, there is no provision for submitting applications online; applications must be submitted physically to the District Registrar.
<br>

<br>

### Which department is responsible for implementing the scheme?

The Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Cooperation, Government of Gujarat, is responsible for implementing the scheme.
