## Brief Description

The scheme "Incentive for the Simple Marriage" was launched by the Tribal Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka. The scheme provides an incentive of ₹50,000/- to ST individuals residing in Karnataka who marry another ST individual on or after 11th August 2015.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Incentive for the Simple Marriage" was launched by the Tribal Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka. The scheme provides an incentive of ₹50,000/- to ST individuals residing in Karnataka who marry another ST individual on or after 11th August 2015. The scheme aims to reduce expenses, prevent dowry, and promote a more accepting society. The combined income of the couple should be less than ₹2,00,000/-.

## Benefits

An incentive of ₹50,000/- is provided to the married couple.

## Eligibility

1. The couple should be residing in Karnataka.
1. Both partners should belong to the Scheduled Tribe community.
1. The combined annual income of the couple should be less than ₹2,00,000/-.
1. The application should be submitted within one year of marriage.
1. The marriage should have taken place on or after 11th August 2015.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Visit the [official website](https://twd.karnataka.gov.in/) of the Tribal Welfare Department, Government of Karnataka.
**Step 2:** In the right pane, click "Incentives for Marriage" and select the "Incentives to Simple Marriage" scheme. You will be directed to the department's dedicated page for that scheme.
**Step 3:** On the scheme's homepage, scroll down to the bottom and click on "[Registration](https://twd.karnataka.gov.in/Twincentive/SIM/SmRegistrationForm.aspx)".
**Step 4:** In the online registration form, fill in all the mandatory fields of the form and upload all the mandatory documents in the specified format and size.
**Step 5:** Carefully review all the information provided by you. After completing the form, click "Submit". You will receive a confirmation message via SMS from the Department, and an acknowledgement form will be generated.
**Step 6:** Print the acknowledgement form for future reference.
<br>

**Post Application Processes**
The Tribal Welfare Department will conduct a spot verification to verify the details provided by the couple and process the application further. After successful spot verification, the incentive will be credited to the couple’s joint account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Incentive for the Simple Marriage" scheme?

The scheme provides a [?]50,000 incentive to Scheduled Tribe (ST) individuals who marry another ST individual, aiming to reduce marriage-related expenses, prevent dowry, and promote a more socially accepting society.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

The scheme is available to couples who both belong to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) community and reside in Karnataka.

### What is the financial assistance provided under the scheme?

Eligible couples can receive a one-time incentive of [?]50,000 for a simple marriage.

### What is the income eligibility requirement for this scheme?

The combined annual income of the couple must be less than [?]2.0 lakhs to qualify for the scheme.

### Is there a specific marriage date requirement to qualify?

Yes, the marriage must have taken place on or after August 11, 2015, to be eligible for the incentive.

### Is the scheme open to all ST individuals?

Yes, the scheme is open to all ST individuals who marry another ST individual and meet the other eligibility criteria.

### Do both partners need to be from the same sub-caste within the ST community?

No, both partners just need to belong to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) community, but they can belong to different sub-castes.

### Can couples who marry outside of Karnataka apply for this scheme?

No, the couple must be residing in Karnataka to be eligible for the scheme.

### Are inter-caste marriages eligible for this scheme?

No, only marriages between two ST individuals are eligible. Inter-caste married couples are not eligible for the scheme.

### How soon after marriage can the application be submitted?

The application must be submitted within one year of the marriage.
