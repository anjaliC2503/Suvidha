## Brief Description

The scheme aims to commemorate the significant contributions of deceased Tiatr artistes, making the younger generation aware of their legacy. It provides financial assistance, publicity, and venue support for organizing cultural programmes celebrating jubilee/birth anniversaries of these pioneers.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Commemoration of Jubilees/Birth Anniversaries of Tiatr Artistes of Yesteryears"  was launched by the Tiatr Academy under the Art, Culture and Museums Department, Government of Goa. The scheme aims to make our younger generation aware of the contribution and sacrifices of Tiatr artistes of yesteryears. The scheme provides for organizing functions to celebrate jubilee/birth anniversaries of deceased Tiatr artistes, including wide publicity through print and electronic media, display and garlanding of portraits, cultural programmes (tiatr and songs), and financial assistance along with provision of auditoriums/halls and additional amounts for village programmes. Applications for this scheme are accepted at least 30 days in advance on a "First come First Serve" basis, subject to the availability of funds.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Financial Assistance:** An amount as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa.- **Auditorium/Hall Provision:** Provision of an auditorium or hall by the Tiatr Academy of Goa to stage the programme.- **Additional Financial Assistance for Village Programmes:** An additional amount as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa for hall charges or stage erection charges if the programme is held in a village.- **Programme Staging:** A maximum of 4 shows at 4 different places, including 3 shows in Panaji, Margao, Curchorem, Vasco, or any other city, and 1 show compulsorily staged in the artiste’s area of residence.- **Optional Additional Shows:** An additional 2 shows may be granted for performance in villages only, subject to the availability of funds, at an amount (as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa) plus an additional amount (as fixed by the Tiatr Academy of Goa) for Pandal/Hall/Auditorium including Stage, Sound, etc..- **Publicity:** Wide publicity will be given through print and electronic media.- **Banner Provision:** One banner for Tiatr performances, to be collected from and returned to the office of the Tiatr Academy of Goa, must be displayed on the front side wing of the stage.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be an organizing institution or an individual.
- The applicant must propose a programme to celebrate jubilee/birth anniversaries of artistes of yesteryears who are no longer living.
- The applicant must ensure the programme aims to make the younger generation aware of the contribution and sacrifices of these Tiatr artistes.
- The applicant must ensure the programme includes the narration of the history of the life of the late artiste to whom the programme is dedicated.
- The applicant must ensure the family members of the deceased artiste are compulsorily invited for the function.
- The applicant must give the proposal well in advance, providing all details of the programme, including typed script and lyrics to be presented, and the names of the artistes.
- The applicant must insert/publish/announce "This programme is held in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa" in all advertisements, whether oral, print, or electronic media, and on banners.
- The applicant must display the Tiatr Academy of Goa’s banner on the front side wing of the stage if it is a Tiatr, and return it after all shows.
- The applicant must display a banner of minimum size 6ft.x10 ft. carrying the Tiatr Academy of Goa’s logo as a back drop if it is a Musical Show.
- The applicant must discuss with the Tiatr Academy of Goa and decide about the Chief Guest/Guest of Honour well in advance.
- The applicant must ensure the President/Vice President of the Tiatr Academy of Goa will either be the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour at the function.
- The applicant must ensure the duration of the programme is at least 2 hours.
- The applicant must ensure there are no adverse remarks made in any form, either written or verbal, against any Government/Individual/Institution, etc., or anything against one’s caste, creed, and religion which may lead to hurting religious sentiments.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The interested applicant should take a printout of the [prescribed format](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Citizen-Charter-TAG.pdf) of the application form.
**Step 2:** In the application form, fill in all the mandatory fields, attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required). Sign the application form and mention the date.
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the documents to - 
***Tiatr Academy of Goa, AS-1 / AS-4, Block A, 2nd Floor, Campal Trade Centre, Opposite Kala Academy, Campal, Panaji, Goa, India – 403001***
_*Applicants are advised to save a copy of the submitted application form and any acknowledgment._
_*The application must be submitted to the office of the Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days in advance of the proposed programme date._
_*Applications are considered on a "First come First Serve" basis, subject to the availability of funds._

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I want to organize a cultural programme to honor a legendary performer from earlier generations who has passed away?

You can apply for this commemoration scheme which provides financial assistance, venue support, and publicity for celebrating jubilee or birth anniversaries of deceased Tiatr artistes. The programme must aim to educate younger generations about their contributions and sacrifices.
<br>

### Can both organizations and individual people apply for financial support to commemorate deceased cultural performers?

Yes, the scheme accepts applications from both organizing institutions and individuals who wish to celebrate jubilee or birth anniversaries of Tiatr artistes of yesteryears. The applicant can be either an institution or an individual person.
<br>

### What type of approval do I need from the deceased artist's relatives before I can proceed with organizing a commemorative event?

You must obtain and submit a No Objection Certificate from the nearest family member or members of the deceased artiste. This certificate is mandatory and must be included with your application to the Tiatr Academy of Goa.
<br>

### How far in advance should I submit my application if I want to organize a memorial programme for a departed cultural figure?

Your complete application must reach the office of the Tiatr Academy of Goa at least 30 days before your proposed programme date. Applications are processed on a "First come First Serve" basis, subject to fund availability.
<br>

### What documentation about my programme content and performers do I need to provide when applying for this cultural commemoration scheme?

You must submit a detailed proposal that includes the complete typed script, all lyrics to be presented during the programme, and the names of all artistes who will be performing. This comprehensive documentation is required at the application stage.
<br>

### Are there any restrictions on what I can say or present during the commemorative programme about government policies or religious matters?

Yes, you must ensure no adverse remarks are made in written or verbal form against any Government, Individual, or Institution, and avoid anything against caste, creed, or religion that may hurt religious sentiments. Content must remain respectful and non-controversial throughout.
<br>

### What is the minimum duration requirement for cultural programmes organized under this commemorative scheme?

The programme duration must be at least 2 hours. This minimum time requirement ensures adequate coverage of the artiste's history and provides sufficient cultural content to honor their legacy properly.
<br>

### How many performance venues and shows am I allowed to organize if my application gets approved for this memorial scheme?

You can organize a maximum of 4 shows at 4 different locations, including 3 shows in cities like Panaji, Margao, Curchorem, or Vasco, and 1 show compulsorily in the deceased artiste's area of residence. Additionally, 2 more village shows may be granted subject to fund availability.
<br>

### What specific information about the honored artiste must be included as part of every commemorative programme under this scheme?

The programme must compulsorily include narration of the complete history and life story of the late artiste to whom the programme is dedicated. This biographical content is essential for educating the audience about their contributions.
<br>

### Do I need to invite specific people to attend the commemorative function, and are there any mandatory guests required?

Family members of the deceased artiste must be compulsorily invited to the function. Additionally, the President or Vice President of the Tiatr Academy of Goa will serve as either the Chief Guest or Guest of Honour at your event.
<br>

### What acknowledgment requirements must I follow in all promotional materials and advertisements for my commemorative cultural programme?

All advertisements, whether oral, print, electronic media, or banners, must include the phrase "This programme is held in collaboration with Tiatr Academy of Goa." This acknowledgment is mandatory across all promotional activities and materials.
<br>

### What banner and logo display requirements do I need to follow during Tiatr performances versus Musical Shows?

For Tiatr performances, you must display the Tiatr Academy of Goa's banner on the front side wing of the stage and return it afterward. For Musical Shows, display a minimum 6ft x 10ft banner carrying the Tiatr Academy logo as backdrop.
<br>

### What financial documentation and receipts do I need to submit after completing my commemorative programme to receive reimbursement?

You must submit a statement of expenditure with all original bills enclosed. If any bill exceeds ₹5,000/-, you need a stamped receipt. This financial documentation is required for benefit disbursement and audit purposes.
<br>

### What photographic evidence and records do I need to provide to the Academy after completing my commemorative cultural programme?

You must send 5 copies of photographs from the function to the Tiatr Academy of Goa for their records. Two of these photographs must specifically show the Tiatr Academy banner prominently displayed during the performance.
<br>
