## Brief Description

The scheme "Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Schedule Caste Youth" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to promote the physical, mental, and spiritual development of Scheduled Caste youth through 7-day training camps.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Schedule Caste Youth" by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to foster the overall development—physical, mental, and spiritual—of youth  14–35 years)belonging to the Scheduled Castes through structured yogasana and personality development programs. Each camp runs for 7 days and selects 50 beneficiaries per district from across Gujarat. The initiative serves to empower socially disadvantaged youth and enhance their self-esteem, discipline, and holistic well-being. The scheme is implemented by the District Youth Development Officers across all districts of Gujarat.
<br>

## Benefits

- **Training:** The selected Scheduled Caste youth will receive 7 days of training focused on personality development and yogasana.
<br>

## Eligibility

- The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.
  - The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
  - The applicant should be between the age of 14 to 35 years.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant should download the application form or applicants may visit the Sports Directorate and request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form.
**Step 2:** All required fields in the form should be completed carefully, and copies of the necessary documents must be enclosed (self-attestation may be required).
**Step 3:** Submit the properly filled and signed application form, along with all attached documents, to the District Youth Development Office.
**Step 4:** Ensure to obtain a submission receipt or acknowledgment slip from the official receiving the application for future reference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Schedule Caste Youth" scheme about?

The "Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Schedule Caste Youth" is a state-run initiative by the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat, which aims to enhance the physical, mental, and spiritual well-being of Scheduled Caste youth by conducting structured 7-day training camps across the state.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Who is eligible to apply under this scheme?

Any youth belonging to the Scheduled Caste category, who is a resident of Gujarat and falls within the age group of 14 to 35 years, is eligible to apply for the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any educational qualification required to be eligible for this scheme?

No educational qualification is mandated for availing benefits under this scheme, and youth from any educational background are eligible to apply as long as they meet the caste and age criteria.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit for individuals or families to apply for the scheme?

There is no specified income limit, either individual or family, for applying to the "Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Schedule Caste Youth (14–35 years)".
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What kind of training is provided in the camps conducted under the scheme?

The training includes various personality development modules, physical exercises, and guided yogasana sessions that aim to promote discipline, self-awareness, confidence, and mental peace among the participating youth.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How many youths are selected for each training camp?

Each camp conducted under this scheme selects a total of 50 Scheduled Caste youth from the respective district to participate in the 7-day residential or non-residential program.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What is the duration of the training camp?

The training camps organized under the "Personality Development and Yogasan Training Camp for Schedule Caste Youth (14–35 years)" scheme are held over a continuous period of 7 days.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### How can an interested applicant apply for the scheme?

Interested applicants can download the application format or visit the District Youth Development Office to obtain the application form, fill in all the required information, attach the necessary documents, and submit the completed form to the same office.
<br>

<br>

<br>

### What documents are required to apply under this scheme?

The applicant is required to submit a copy of their Aadhar Card along with the application form in the prescribed format provided by the District Youth Development Office.
<br>

### Who is the implementing authority for this scheme?

The implementation of the scheme is carried out under the supervision of the Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities Department, Government of Gujarat.
<br>

<br>
