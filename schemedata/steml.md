## Brief Description

It aims to promote hands-on learning in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics by establishing labs across all blocks, giving students and teachers practical exposure to STEM experiments.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “STEM Lab” is implemented by the Uttarakhand State Council for Science and Technology (UCOST) under the Science and Technology, Government of Uttarakhand. It aims to promote hands-on learning in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics by establishing labs across all blocks, giving students and teachers practical exposure to STEM experiments.
<br>

## Benefits

STEM labs are being established in all the blocks of the state, through which students and teachers will be provided with information about experiments in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a student enrolled in a school located in Uttarakhand.

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

Interested students may contact their respective schools to participate and avail benefits through training and workshops.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the STEM Lab?

The STEM Lab is an initiative by the Uttarakhand State Council for Science and Technology (UCOST) under the Government of Uttarakhand to promote hands-on learning in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics for school students.
<br>

### Who is eligible for the STEM Lab?

School students studying in Uttarakhand are eligible to benefit from the STEM Lab.
<br>

### What kind of activities are conducted in the STEM Labs?

STEM Labs conduct hands-on experiments, practical demonstrations, workshops, and training sessions in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.
<br>

### What subjects are covered in the STEM Labs?

STEM Labs cover topics in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics, offering practical experiments aligned with school curricula.
<br>

### Which schools can participate in the STEM Lab?

All schools located in Uttarakhand can participate.
<br>
