## Brief Description

The scheme aims to enhance maternal and child health by providing nutritional support to first-time pregnant women and mothers of young children during the crucial first 1000 days of life.

## Detailed Description

The &quot;Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana (MMY)&quot; launched in 2022 by the Women and Child Development Department, Gujarat, focuses on improving the health and nutrition of pregnant women and mothers with children under 2 years. The scheme offers a nutritional kit containing groundnut oil, tuver dal, and chana to support calorie and protein needs. The initiative addresses the critical first 1000 days of life, aiming to enhance maternal and child health, reduce infant mortality, and prevent low birth weight.

## Benefits

- **Commodities:** The nutrition kit includes 1 Litre of Groundnut oil, 1 Kg Tuver Dal, & 2 Kg Chana.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
1. The applicant must be a woman who is conceiving for the first time.
1. The applicant must be a mother with her first child who is under the age of 2 years.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Open the [registration portal ](https://1000d.gujarat.gov.in/)and select the &quot;Self Enrollment&quot; option under &quot;Services&quot; tab.
**Step 2:** Fill in the beneficiary’s details, including Aadhaar card number, name in English, date of birth, and beneficiary&#39;s affidavit.
**Step 3:** Validate the Aadhaar by clicking on the &quot;Validate Aadhaar&quot; button.
**Step 4:** The beneficiary’s ration card ID (NFSA number) and name will be displayed automatically.
**Step 5:** If the Anganwadi name is not visible in the list, manually select the appropriate Anganwadi.
**Step 6:** The name of the Anganwadi worker will be displayed automatically.
**Step 7:** Enter the mobile number you wish to use for registration and click on the “SEND OTP” button.
**Step 8:** Enter the 4-digit OTP sent to the registered mobile number in the online form.
**Step 9:** After entering the OTP, click on the “Save &amp; Next” button to proceed.
**Step 10:** A message will appear saying, “After your general information, add your health-related information and submit the form.” Click “OK.”
**Step 11:** Enter the ID number or email address provided by the health center and click the &quot;Search&quot; button.
**Step 12:** The beneficiary’s name, status, P.A.C. center name, last month&#39;s status, and probable start date will auto-fill.
**Step 13:** Click on the “Confirm and Submit” button to complete the registration process.
**Step 14:** After submitting, click on the “Print Application” button to take a printout of the registration.
**Step 15:** If corrections are needed, click on “Edit Enrollment,” enter the Enrollment Number, Aadhaar Card Number, or Mobile Number, and click &quot;Edit.&quot; Enter the OTP received to make corrections.
**Step 16:** If needed, click on “Print Enrollment,” enter the registration number, Aadhaar card number, or mobile number, click “Verify,” and enter the OTP to take a printout of the registration form.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the "Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana"?

The Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana is a government initiative designed to improve the health and nutrition of first-time pregnant women and mothers with children under two years of age in Gujarat.

### Who is eligible for the "Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana"?

The scheme is available to women who are pregnant for the first time and mothers whose child is under two years of age.

### What does the scheme offers to beneficiaries?

The scheme provides a nutritional kit that includes 1 liter of groundnut oil, 1 kg of tuver dal, and 2 kg of chana to support the nutritional needs of mothers and children.

### How can someone apply for the Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana?

Eligible beneficiaries can apply through the official online portal by registering using their Aadhaar number and other required details.

### Is there any age restriction to apply for the Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana?

No specific age restriction is mentioned; however, applicants must be first-time pregnant women or mothers with children aged under two years.

### Can non-residents of Gujarat apply for the scheme?

No, only residents of Gujarat are eligible to benefit from the Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana.

### What is included in the nutritional kit provided by the scheme?

The nutritional kit contains 1 liter of groundnut oil, 1 kg of tuver dal, and 2 kg of chana.

### What outcomes are expected from the "Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana"?

The scheme is expected to improve maternal and child health, reduce preterm births, lower infant mortality rates (IMR), and reduce maternal mortality rates (MMR).

### How does the scheme help in improving maternal and child health?

By providing essential nutrition, the scheme helps improve the overall health, growth, and development of mothers and children, particularly during the critical first 1000 days.

### Can beneficiaries collect the nutritional kit from any Anganwadi center?

Yes, eligible beneficiaries can collect their nutritional kit from the nearest Anganwadi center.

### When was the "Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana" launched?

The scheme was launched on June 18, 2022, by the Hon'ble Prime Minister of India in Vadodara, Gujarat.

### How can the benefits of the Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana be accessed?

After registering on the official portal, beneficiaries can visit their nearest Anganwadi center to receive the benefits of the scheme.
