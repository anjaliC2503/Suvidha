## Brief Description

The scheme aims to provide marriage-related financial aid of ₹12,000 per daughter (up to two daughters per family) to socially and educationally backward classes and economically backward class families.

## Detailed Description

The "Kunwar Bai Nu Mameru Yojana- SEBC & EBC" scheme was launched by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to families from Socially and Educationally Backward Classes and Economically Backward Classes to help cover the wedding expenses of their daughters. Under this scheme, an amount of ₹12,000 is provided for each daughter’s marriage, subject to a maximum of two adult daughters per family.
<br>

## Benefits

**Monetary Assistance:**

- ₹12,000 is provided for the marriage of a daughter.

> **NOTE:** If the marriage occurred before April 1, 2021, assistance of ₹10,000 is provided.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a native of Gujarat.
1. The applicant must be a woman belonging to a Socially and Educationally Backward Class (SEBC) or Economically Backward Class (EBC).
1. The annual family income should not exceed ₹6,00,000/-.
1. The marriage must be legally registered.
1. The assistance is limited to two daughters per family.

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 01:** The eligible applicant may visit the e-Samaj Kalyan Portal: [https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/)
**Step 02:** On the home page, under the tab ‘Citizen Login’, click on New User ‘[Please Register Here](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/Registration.aspx)’.
**Step 03:** Enter your Full Name as per Aadhaar Card, Gender, Date of Birth, Aadhaar Card Number, Email ID, Caste etc. and then click on ‘Register’.
**Step 04:** After successful registration, the applicants can login through their User ID and Password.
**Step 05:** Now, click on ‘User Profile’ to update your profile.
**Step 06:** Fill in all the mandatory information and then click on ‘Update’. 
**Step 07:** After updating the profile successfully, select the scheme appearing on the home page; an application form will open.
**Step 08:** Fill in all the mandatory details in the application form and upload all the relevant documents.
**Step 09:** Now agree with the terms &amp; conditions and click on ‘Save Application’.
**Step 10:** Submit the application form and take out the print of the application for future reference. The applicant can note down the application number to track the application status by logging into the portal using their User ID and Password.
[User Manual](https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/HelpManual/Citizen-HelpManual.pdf)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much assistance is provided under this scheme?

₹12,000 per eligible daughter for marriages after April 1, 2021; ₹10,000 for earlier marriages.
<br>

### How many daughters can get the benefit in one family?

A maximum of two adult daughters per family can avail the benefit.
<br>

### Can I apply offline?

No, only online applications are accepted.
<br>

### Is there any application fee?

No, the application is free of cost.
<br>

### What documents are mandatory for application?

Aadhaar Card, Income Certificate, Marriage Certificate, Bank Passbook, and Passport Photos.
<br>

### Can I apply for assistance for more than two daughters?

No, the benefit is restricted to two daughters per family.
<br>

### Is the scheme applicable across Gujarat?

Yes, it is applicable throughout the state.
<br>

### Can widows or divorced women apply under this scheme?

No, this scheme is specifically for first-time marriages of eligible daughters.
<br>

### Where can I check the application status?

You can log in at https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in to track your application status.
