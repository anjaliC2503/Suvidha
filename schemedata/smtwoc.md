## Brief Description

The "Sanction of Motorized Three Wheeler to Orthopedically Challenged" scheme aims to provide motorized three wheelers to orthopedically challenged persons. This initiative ensures that eligible individuals receive support to enhance their mobility and independence.

## Detailed Description

The A.P. Differently Abled & Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation, Andhra Pradesh launched scheme "Sanction of Motorized Three Wheeler to Orthopedically Challenged" under its Action Plan 2017-18, aims to provide Motorized Three Wheelers to orthopedically challenged persons. The Motorized Three Wheelers are distributed by the Assistant Directors, Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled & Senior Citizens, and District Managers, A.P. Differently Abled & Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation.

## Benefits

- Eligible persons with disabilities are provided with a motorized three-wheeler (customized/adapted for their use) to enhance their independence, mobility, and access to education or livelihood opportunities.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be between 18 to 45 years of age.
1. The applicant should have a disability of 70% or above.
1. The applicant's family annual income should not exceed ₹3,00,000/-.
1. The applicant should be a bonafede student pursuing graduation or higher courses regularly.
1. The applicant should be self-employed (including in agriculture and allied activities) or wage/salary employed for a minimum period of one year, and must have passed at least the 10th class.
1. The applicant should obtain a valid driving licence for driving an adapted vehicle (previously called an invalid carriage) as per the Motor Vehicle Act within two months from the date of selection.
1. The applicant is eligible for sanction only once in a lifetime.

## Documents Required

- Disability Certificate
- Aadhaar Card
- SSC Certificate
- Caste Certificate in case of SC/ST/BC
- Passport size photo
- Latest Income Certificate
- Bonafide Certificate from the institution for the student category
- Self/Wage employment Certificate from the concerned MPDO/Municipal Commissioner for Self/Wage Category
- Self Declaration on punishment for fraudulently availing any benefit meant for Persons with Disabilities.
- Self Declaration that he/she has not availed the benefit of a Motorized Vehicle earlier from the Government, Government Agency/ Private Agency, or any other source, and he/she should not have any Motorized Vehicle two/three/or four-wheeler of his/her own

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to receive a Motorized Three Wheeler under this scheme?

To be eligible for this scheme, the applicant should:
Age between 18-45 years.
70% & above Disability.
Annual Income of family should not be more than Rs.3.00 lakhs.
Bonafide students studying Graduation and above courses regularly.
Self employed including Agriculture & Allied activities or wage/salary employed for minimum one year period with 10th Class Pass
Should acquire valid driving licence for driving an adopted vehicle (earlier it was invalid carriage) issued under Motor Vehicle Act within two months from the date of selection
Sanctioned once in a life time.
<br>

### Can the Motorized Three Wheeler be transferred to another person?

No, the Motorized Three Wheeler is issued to the individual and is non-transferable.

### How often can an individual receive a Motorized Three Wheeler under this scheme?

An individual can receive a Motorized Three Wheeler once in their lifetime.

### Who is responsible for distributing the Motorized Three Wheelers?

The Motorized Three Wheelers are distributed by the Assistant Directors and District Managers of the respective departments.

### What documents are required to apply for a Motorized Three Wheeler?

1. Disability Certificate
1. Aadhaar Card
1. SSC Certificate
1. Caste Certificate in case of SC/ST/BC
1. Passport size photo
1. Latest Income Certificate
1. Bonafide Certificate from the institution for student category
1. Self/Wage employment Certificate from concerned MPDO/ Muncipal Commissioner for Self/Wage Category
1. Self Declaration on punishment for fraudulently availing any benefit meant for Persons with Disabilities.
1. Self Declaration that he/she has not availed the benefit of Motorized Vehicle earlier from the Government, Government Agency/ Private Agency or any other source and he/she should not have any Motorized Vehicle two/three/four wheeler of his/her own.

<br>

### Is there a deadline for submitting applications?

Yes, a minimum of one month from the date of notification will be provided for submitting applications.

### What happens if there are more eligible candidates than available units?

Preference will be given to students whose remaining period for course completion is longer and those from remote areas.

### What measures are in place to prevent double or false claims?

Records of beneficiaries are maintained to avoid double or false claims, and verification is conducted before approval.

### Can individuals with mental retardation or illness apply for this scheme?

No, individuals with mental retardation or illness are not eligible.

### What should a beneficiary do if they no longer need the Motorized Three Wheeler?

They should return the Motorized Three Wheeler to the distribution office or follow the specified guidelines provided by the Corporation.
