## Brief Description

The scheme “Stipends to Students of Minority Caste for Technical and Vocational Courses after SSC” by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, supports education for Minority Caste students by providing a monthly stipend for one year.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Stipends to Students of Minority Caste for Technical and Vocational Courses after SSC” by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat aims to promote inclusive education by financially supporting students from economically weaker sections pursuing technical and vocational education after SSC. The initiative offers a stipend of ₹400 per month to aid the academic progress and reduce the dropout rate among underprivileged youth. The scheme is implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department. It specifically benefits those enrolled in professional or Government ITI courses and belonging to families with an annual income not exceeding ₹6,00,000/-.

## Benefits

- **Stipend:** A monthly stipend of ₹400/- is given to Minority Caste students for one year of recognized professional and Government ITI courses.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Gujarat.
1. The applicant should belong to Minority Caste.
1. The applicant should be a student who has passed the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) examination.
1. The applicant should be enrolled in a recognized professional or Government Industrial Training Institute (ITI) course.
1. The applicant’s individual and family income should not exceed ₹6,00,000/- per annum.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the scheme “Stipends to Students of Minority Caste for Technical and Vocational Courses After SSC” and what does it aim to achieve?

The scheme “Stipends to Students of Minority Caste for Technical and Vocational Courses After SSC” is an initiative by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, which aims to provide monthly financial support to students belonging to Minority Caste communities who are pursuing one-year recognized professional or Government ITI courses, in order to reduce the financial burden and promote technical and vocational education.
<br>

<br>

### Who is responsible for implementing this stipend scheme for minority students in Gujarat?

The scheme is implemented by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department of the Government of Gujarat, which is responsible for managing its operations, disbursement of funds, and monitoring eligibility criteria.
<br>

<br>

### What is the amount of financial assistance provided under this scheme to each eligible student?

Under this scheme, each eligible student receives a financial assistance of ₹400 per month for the duration of one year, making it a monthly stipend scheme designed to support education-related expenses.
<br>

<br>

### What type of courses are covered under this scheme for minority students?

The scheme specifically covers students who are enrolled in one-year recognized professional courses and Government Industrial Training Institute (ITI) courses that are approved by the concerned authorities.
<br>

<br>

### What is the educational qualification required for a student to apply under this scheme?

To apply under this scheme, a student must have successfully passed the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) examination, which serves as the minimum educational qualification requirement.
<br>

<br>

### Is there any income limit for students or their families to be eligible for this scheme?

Yes, the annual income of the student individually as well as the student’s family should not exceed ₹6,00,000, and this income ceiling is a mandatory criterion for eligibility under the scheme.
<br>

<br>

### Can both male and female students apply for this stipend scheme?

Yes, the scheme is inclusive and does not discriminate on the basis of gender, and therefore both male and female students belonging to Minority Castes are eligible to apply for the stipend.
<br>

<br>

### What is the process to apply for this scheme for eligible students?

The student must visit the official [Digital Gujarat](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/frmMain1.aspx) portal, register or log in using valid credentials, select the scheme, fill out the application form, upload the required documents, and submit the application online.

### Which documents are required to complete the application process under this scheme?

To complete the application process, students need to submit a valid Caste Certificate, an Income Certificate, SSC and last year’s mark sheet, a receipt of the fee paid to the institution, and a copy of their Bank Passbook or a Cancelled Cheque for bank account verification.
<br>

<br>

### What should a student do if there is an issue with the application?

In case of any issues with the application, the student should contact the [helpdesk ](https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/SchemPortal/ContactUs.aspx)on the Digital Gujarat portal.
