## Brief Description

The “Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) Students-Bihar” aims to provide financial assistance to eligible students from Schedule Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) for their post-secondary education.

## Detailed Description

The “Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) Students-Bihar” is designed to support students from disadvantaged communities by covering their educational expenses for post-secondary courses. The scholarship is intended for students belonging to Schedule Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST). This initiative promotes access to higher education and helps reduce the financial burden on families from these communities.
<br>

## Benefits

- Scholarship and maintenance allowance will be payable on the basis of the criteria prescribed in the resolution issued by the Government of India from time to time.

## Exclusions

- After passing one level course, studying in another equivalent course will not be entitled to scholarship, for example, after passing I.S.C. examination, studying in I.A., after passing B.A. examination, studying in B.Com. and after doing M.Sc., studying in M.A. class will not be given scholarship.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Bihar state. 
   1. The caste of the applicant should be included in the list of Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) issued by the state government.
   1. The applicant should be studying in higher classes after passing the Matriculation/Entry Examination.
   1. The family’s annual income must not exceed ₹3,00,000/-.

## Documents Required

1. Proof of identity (Aadhaar Card).
   1. Caste certificate.
   1. Domicile certificate.
   1. Income certificate of the family.
   1. Bank account details.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme aims to support students from Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST), by covering their educational expenses for post-secondary courses.

### Who is eligible for this scheme?

Applicants must be permanent residents of Bihar, belong to the Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) category, and be studying in higher classes after passing the Matriculation/Entry Examination.

### What is the income limit for families under this scheme?

The family's annual income should not exceed [?]3,00,000/- to be eligible for this scheme.

### What benefits are provided under this scheme?

The scheme offers scholarships and maintenance allowances based on the criteria prescribed by the Government of India.

### Can students receive scholarships for equivalent courses after completing one level?

No, students studying in another equivalent course after completing one level, such as transitioning from I.S.C. to I.A. or B.A. to B.Com., are not eligible for the scholarship.

### How can students apply for the scheme?

Students can apply by visiting the official Post-Matric Scholarship Portal and following the registration process.

### Can students apply for this scholarship if their caste is not listed under the Backward Class or Extremely Backward Class categories?

No, only students whose caste is included in the government-issued Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) list are eligible.

### Is the Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme available for students from other states?

No, the scheme is only available to permanent residents of Bihar.

### What courses are covered under this scheme?

The scholarship covers post-secondary courses for students who have passed the Matriculation/Entry Examination.

### Is the scholarship transferred directly to the student’s account?

Yes, the scholarship amount is directly transferred to the student's bank account through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer).
