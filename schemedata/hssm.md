## Brief Description

Under this scheme, provide financial support to differently-abled students for purchasing essential items like books and uniforms. Students enrolled in both government and private schools are eligible to receive a monthly stipend.

## Detailed Description

The Handicapped Students Stipend scheme was launched by the Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs Department, Government of Mizoram. Under this scheme, provide financial support to differently-abled students for purchasing essential items like books and uniforms. Students enrolled in both government and private schools are eligible to receive a monthly stipend.

## Benefits

- Upto Class 4th - ₹30/- per month per student (360/- per annum) 
- Class 5th to Class 7th - ₹40/- per month per student (480/- per annum) 
- Class 8th to  Class 12th - ₹85/- per month per student (1020/- per annum)

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a resident of Mizoram.
1. The applicant should be a disabled person. 
1. The applicant should be a student enrolled in government or private school.
1. The applicant should be from the Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.

<br>

<br>

## Application Process

### Offline

**Step 1:** The applicant writes the application on plain paper. 
**Step 2:** In the application form attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attest, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application along with the documents to the District Social Welfare Office
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgment from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted. Ensure that the receipt contains essential details such as the date and time of submission, and a unique identification number (if applicable).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Handicapped Students Stipend Scheme?

The scheme provides a stipend to students with disabilities for educational needs.

### What is the stipend amount for each class level?

Up to Class IV: [?]30/month; Class V-VII: [?]40/month; Class VIII-XII: [?]85/month.

### Can students from private schools apply?

Yes, both government and private school students are eligible.

### Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?

Students with disabilities, with a valid disability certificate and BPL status, residing in Mizoram.

### Is there a specific quota per district?

Yes, there is a district-specific quota for the stipend.

### Where should the application be submitted?

Submit the application to the District Social Welfare Office (DSWO).

### What documents are required?

Passport-size photo, Disability Certificate, BPL proof, and Bank Passbook.

### Can students apply if they have received a stipend from another scheme?

Confirm with DSWO as it depends on specific guidelines.

### Who can assist with the application if needed?

The DSWO and Social Welfare Department staff are available for assistance.

### How often is the stipend distributed?

Monthly, based on the student's class level.
