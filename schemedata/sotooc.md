## Brief Description

The "Sanctioning of Tricycles of Orthopedically Challenged" scheme aims to provide tricycles to orthopedically challenged individuals to enhance their mobility and independence. This initiative ensures that eligible individuals receive the necessary support to lead active and self-reliant lives.

## Detailed Description

The A.P. Differently Abled & Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation, Andhra Pradesh, under its Action Plan 2017-18, aims to provide tricycles to orthopedically challenged individuals to enhance their mobility and independence. The tricycles are distributed by the Assistant Directors, Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled & Senior Citizens, and District Managers, A.P. Differently Abled & Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation.

## Benefits

- Eligible beneficiaries receive a free tricycle to improve their mobility and independence. 
<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should have 40% or above disability and meet the eligibility criteria as specified in the [RPwD Act, 2016](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e58aea67b01fa747687f038dfde066f6/uploads/2023/10/202310161053958942.pdf).1. The applicant should be eligible for sanction only once in three years.
1. The applicant's family annual income should not exceed ₹3,00,000/-.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the purpose of the Tricycle distribution scheme?

The scheme aims to provide tricycles to orthopedically challenged individuals to enhance their mobility and independence.

### Who is eligible to receive a tricycle under this scheme?

To be eligible for the DAISY Player Sanction Scheme, the applicant must meet the following criteria:

1. The applicant should have 40% or above disability and must fulfill the eligibility conditions as specified under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016.
1. The applicant is eligible for sanction only once in three years.
1. The applicant's family annual income should not exceed ₹3.00 lakhs.

<br>

### How often can an individual receive a tricycle under this scheme?

An individual can receive a tricycle once every three years.

### What happens if the tricycle is damaged or requires maintenance?

Maintenance and repair responsibilities are usually not covered by the scheme; beneficiaries should refer to the guidelines or contact the distribution office for specific assistance.

### What measures are in place to prevent double or false claims?

Records of beneficiaries are maintained by the Assistant Directors and District Managers to avoid double or false claims.

### Who is responsible for distributing the tricycles?

The tricycles are distributed through the Assistant Directors, Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled & Senior Citizens, and the District Managers of the A.P. Differently Abled & Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation.

### How is the tricycle distributed to the beneficiary?

The tricycle is distributed directly by the Assistant Directors or District Managers of the respective departments.

### What should a beneficiary do if their application is denied?

They can inquire about the reasons for denial and may reapply if they address the specified issues or seek assistance from the concerned authorities.

### What documents are required to apply for a tricycle?

- Disability Certificate.
- Aadhaar Card.
- Caste Certificate in case of SC/ST/BC.
- Passport size photo.
- Latest Income Certificate/ BPL Ration Card.
