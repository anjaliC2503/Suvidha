## Brief Description

Under this scheme, financial assistance will be provided for purchase of a handcart along with essential items such as stove, gas chulha, utensils, etc., for fish and fish product sales. A subsidy of 60% on the total project cost of ₹60,000/- will be granted, amounting to ₹36,000/- per beneficiary.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Subsidy on Rehri along with Stove, Gas Chulha, Utensils, etc. for Sale of Fish/Fish Products” is implemented by the Fisheries Department, Government of Haryana. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to Scheduled Caste fish farmers for the purchase of a Rehri (handcart) equipped with stove, gas chulha, utensils, and other essentials for selling fish and fish products. A 60% subsidy is provided on the total project cost of ₹60,000/- per beneficiary.

## Benefits

- Under this scheme, financial assistance will be provided for the purchase of a Rehri (Handcart) along with essential items such as stove, gas chulha, utensils, etc., for selling fish or fish products. 
  - A subsidy of 60% on the total project cost of ₹60,000/- will be granted, amounting to ₹36,000/- per beneficiary.

## Exclusions

1. The applicant should not be a Government/Semi-Government employee.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should have a Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP).
1. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Haryana.
1. The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste category.
1. The applicant's age should be a minimum of 18 years.
