## Brief Description

The scheme aims to motivate the growers to bring more area under irrigation by constructing water storage structures, rain water harvesting structures and installation of irrigation equipments for increasing the productivity and improving quality of large cardamom.

## Detailed Description

The scheme “Large Cardamom Irrigation Land Development: Irrigation Equipment (Nos)” is a component of the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion and Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom implemented during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26) under the Export-Oriented Production component. The scheme aims to motivate growers to bring more area under irrigation by supporting the construction of water storage structures, rainwater harvesting systems, and the installation of irrigation equipment, thereby increasing productivity and improving the quality of large cardamom.
<br>

## Benefits

1. 50 % of cost of equipment subject to a maximum of ₹ 15,000 for General and 75% subject to maximum of ₹  20,000/- for SC/ST
1. Large cardamom growers having land holding size of 0.10 Ha to 8 Ha

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant should be a large cardamom grower located in Sikkim, Darjeeling and Kalimpong districts of West Bengal, or other North Eastern States.
1. The applicant should submit the application for consideration under the scheme in the prescribed format.

<br>

1. Document of ownership or legal possession of land / Parcha
1. Aadhaar card
1. Bank Pass Book
1. No Objection Certificate from panchayats if the Land is not in the beneficiary’s name.
1. Quotation from authorized dealer or three competitive quotations if there is no authorized dealer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the objective of this scheme?

The scheme aims to promote export-oriented production by encouraging large cardamom growers to adopt irrigation facilities, thereby improving productivity and quality.
<br>

### Under which programme is this scheme implemented?

It is implemented under the Integrated Scheme for Export Promotion and Quality Improvement in Spices and Research and Development of Cardamom during the 15th Finance Commission period (2021–22 to 2025–26).
<br>

### Who is eligible to apply?

Large cardamom growers cultivating in eligible regions can apply, subject to scheme guidelines.
<br>

### Which areas are covered under the scheme?

The scheme covers Sikkim, Darjeeling and Kalimpong districts of West Bengal, and other North Eastern States.
<br>

### What type of support is provided?

Financial assistance is provided for the installation of irrigation equipment to improve irrigation coverage.
<br>

### Why is irrigation equipment important under this scheme?

It helps ensure better water management, leading to increased yield and improved quality of large cardamom.
<br>

### Is the scheme linked to export promotion?

Yes, it falls under the Export-Oriented Production component aimed at improving quality for better export potential.
<br>

### What documents are required to apply?

Applicants typically need land ownership/possession proof, Aadhaar card, bank details, and other documents as specified in the guidelines.
<br>

### What role do designated officials play?

They assess applications, conduct inspections, and recommend eligible cases for approval.
