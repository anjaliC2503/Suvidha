## Brief Description

The scheme "Namo Laxmi Yojana" by the Education Department, aims to reduce the dropout rate of girls in secondary and higher secondary education by providing financial assistance for completing studies from Class 9th to 12th.

## Detailed Description

The scheme "Namo Laxmi Yojana" by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to encourage girls studying in government, aided, and private schools in Gujarat to complete their education from Class 9th to 12th by providing direct financial assistance. Each eligible girl will receive a total financial support of ₹50,000/- on completion of classes 9th to 12th under this scheme.
<br>

## Benefits

> ***Total Financial Assistance:***


- Each eligible girl will receive a total financial support of ₹50,000/- on completion of classes 9th to 12th in government, aided, and private schools.

<br>

> **Class 9th-10th Benefit:**


- ₹10,000/- per annum (₹500/- per month based on attendance × 10) and the remaining 50% will be credited via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) after passing the Class 10th board examination.

<br>

> **Class 11th-12th Benefit:**


- ₹15,000/- per annum (₹750/- per month based on attendance × 10) and the remaining 50% will be credited via DBT after passing the Class 12th board examination.

<br>

## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a girl.
   1. The applicant must be a student.
   1. The applicant must be studying in Class 9th to 12th in any Government, Government-aided, or private secondary/higher secondary school in Gujarat.
   1. The applicant must have an annual family income of less than or equal to ₹6,00,000/-.
   1. The applicant must maintain regular attendance to be eligible for monthly assistance.

<br>

## Application Process

### Online

**Step 1:** Applicants should request a hard copy of the prescribed format of the application form from the School authority.
**Step 2:** In the application form, provide all the mandatory details, and attach copies of all the mandatory documents (self-attested, if required).
**Step 3:** Submit the duly filled and signed application form along with the supporting documents to the District Education Officer.
**Step 4:** Request a receipt or acknowledgement from the concerned authority to whom the application has been submitted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

"Namo Laxmi Yojana" is a scholarship scheme launched by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, which aims to provide financial assistance to girls studying in classes 9th to 12th so that they can complete their education without dropping out.
<br>

### Who is eligible for "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

The applicant must be a girl studying in classes 9th to 12th in any government, aided, or private secondary or higher secondary school in Gujarat and must have an annual family income less than or equal to ₹6,00,000/-.
<br>

### What is the total benefit amount under "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

Under "Namo Laxmi Yojana," every eligible girl receives a total financial assistance of ₹50,000/- for completing her studies from class 9th to class 12th in government, aided, or private schools.
<br>

### How much assistance is given for classes 9 and 10 under "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

Under "Namo Laxmi Yojana," each girl receives ₹10,000/- per year for classes 9th and 10th, with monthly amounts given based on attendance and the remaining 50% credited after passing the class 10th board examination.
<br>

### How much assistance is given for classes 11 and 12 under "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

Under "Namo Laxmi Yojana," each girl receives ₹15,000 per year for classes 11th and 12th, with monthly amounts given based on attendance and the remaining 50% credited after passing the class 12th board examination.
<br>

### What is the mode of payment for the scholarship under "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

Under "Namo Laxmi Yojana," the scholarship amount is transferred directly to the bank account of the beneficiary through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to ensure transparency and timely payment.
<br>

### Is there an attendance requirement under "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

Yes, under "Namo Laxmi Yojana," the applicant must maintain regular attendance in school to become eligible for receiving the monthly disbursement of the scholarship amount.
<br>

### What is the income limit for applying to "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

Under "Namo Laxmi Yojana," the annual family income of the applicant must not exceed ₹6,00,000/- to qualify for the financial assistance benefits provided by the scheme.
<br>

### Are private school students eligible for "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

Yes, "Namo Laxmi Yojana" is applicable to girls studying in private secondary and higher secondary schools in Gujarat along with those in government and aided schools.
<br>

### Is there any fee to apply for "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

No, there is no application fee to apply for "Namo Laxmi Yojana," and students can apply completely free of cost through the prescribed online process.
<br>

### Where can the applicant get the application form for "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

The application form for "Namo Laxmi Yojana" can be collected from the school level and must be filled online through the official application portal provided by the government.
<br>

### Where must the applicant submit the filled application form for "Namo Laxmi Yojana"?

The applicant must submit the completed application form for "Namo Laxmi Yojana" to the District Education Officer for verification and further processing.
<br>
